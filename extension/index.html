Radarak

Radarak Apply: fill job applications in one click

A free extension for your computer's browser. It fills application forms with your Apply kit: your details, the CV that fits the job best and your cover letter. You check everything and press submit yourself.

Download Radarak Apply (.zip)

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox on Windows, Mac and Linux. Phones don't support browser extensions; on a phone, use the copy buttons in the Apply kit.

How it works

1. Fill your Apply kit once
On radarak.online → Apply kit: details, up to 5 CVs and cover letters.
2. Open a job and click Apply
Radarak remembers which of your CVs fits that job best and fills the cover letter with its company and title.
3. Click “Fill with Radarak”
On the application form. Filled fields turn green. Answer the other questions, then submit.

Knows the forms of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Teamtailor, BambooHR, Personio, Breezy and Pinpoint. On any other site, click the extension's icon → “Fill this page”.

Install

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera
  1. Download the .zip above and unzip it to a folder you keep (for example Documents\radarak-apply).
  2. Open the extensions page: chrome://extensions, edge://extensions, brave://extensions or opera://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open radarak.online → Apply kit once. It says “extension connected”.
Firefox
  1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → choose manifest.json in the unzipped folder.
  2. Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it closes; the store version will stay installed.

Store versions (Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, Firefox Add-ons) are on the way and will install with one click.

Privacy

  • Your details, CVs and letters are copied from radarak.online into the extension's storage on your computer only.
  • The extension has no server and sends nothing anywhere. It reads radarak.online only to copy your kit, and fills forms only when you click.
  • It never submits an application for you.
  • To remove everything: delete your kit in the Apply kit tab, or remove the extension.

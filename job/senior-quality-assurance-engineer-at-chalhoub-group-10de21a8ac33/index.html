What you'll be doing

Work fully hands-on on complex functional, automated, integration, regression, and performance testing while providing senior technical guidance to other QA engineers, ensuring committed scope is delivered on time with high and measurable quality.

• Execute complex functional, integration, end-to-end, automated, and regression testing hands-on for assigned scope.

• Build and maintain test automation using frameworks such as Playwright and Tosca and run automated regression suites.

• Lead defect triage, root cause support, and release readiness reviews with engineering and product teams to ensure issues are closed in time for launch.

• Conduct performance, load, stress testing, and API and integration testing to validate system reliability.

• Execute test strategy and design complex test scenarios, managing test data and enforcing quality gates.

• Drive defect analysis, root cause support, and defect resolution, ensuring reliable release validation.

• Coordinate test data, environment readiness, and UAT support for critical releases to prevent avoidable delays in validation.

• Provide senior technical guidance and mentoring to other QA engineers, upholding QA standards and risk-based testing.

• Anticipate quality and delivery risks and support Agile delivery through estimation, prioritization, and troubleshooting for on-time, high-quality delivery.

• Contribute to continuous improvement of testing,

What you’ll need to succeed

• 5+ years of hands-on quality engineering experience across complex functional, automated, integration, regression, and performance testing.

• Strong test automation skills with frameworks such as Playwright and Tosca, including automated regression and load and stress testing.

• Expertise in test strategy execution, complex test scenario design, test data management, and quality gates.

• Strong defect analysis, root cause support, release validation, and risk-based testing skills, with a technical mentoring capability.

• Working knowledge of Agile delivery and continuous improvement, with familiarity in AI-assisted test analysis.

• Experience driving QA across enterprise ecommerce releases, integrations, and production cutovers in an Agile delivery model.

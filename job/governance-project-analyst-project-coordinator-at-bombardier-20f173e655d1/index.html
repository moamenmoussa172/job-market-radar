About the job

When applicable, Bombardier promotes flexible and hybrid work policies.

Why join us?

At Bombardier, we design, build and maintain the world’s peak-performing aircraft for the world’s most discerning people and businesses, governments and militaries. We have been successful in setting the highest standards by putting our people at the heart of it all, and defining excellence, together.
Working at Bombardier means operating at the highest level. Every day, you are part of a team that delivers superior experiences and products, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in our industry and beyond. By prioritizing employee growth and development, we empower everyone to reach their full potential on their own terms, because the best work happens when you are free to be yourself and share your unique expertise.

Our Benefits

With our employees’ well-being top of mind, we offer a comprehensive and competitive Benefits Program, which includes the following:

  • Insurance plans (Medical, life insurance, and more)
  • Employee Assistance Program
  • Competitive base salary

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Governance Leadership: Drive effective governance across multiple organizations by planning, coordinating, and supporting key meetings and activities, including management of the Master Plan and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
  • Financial Leadership: Support engineering budget planning and management, including annual planification and quarterly reforecast exercises.
  • Manpower Strategy Leadership: Assist in strategic manpower planning and ensure alignment with engineering and product development priorities.
  • Leadership Alignment: Partner with the Engineering and Product Development leadership team to guide and influence alignment toward common strategic objectives.
  • Executive Communication: Create clear, concise, and impactful documentation and presentations for executive-level audiences.
  • Strategic Workshop Leadership: Prepare and facilitate workshops with multidisciplinary teams to validate, refine, and implement strategic plans.
  • Data-Driven Leadership: Collect, analyze, and interpret data to support evidence-based decision-making and strategy implementation.
  • Innovation Leadership: Lead or support the development of innovative tools, dashboards, and reports to enhance data analysis and performance tracking.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You have a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Engineering, or related field. A master’s degree in Strategy, Project Management, or MBA is an asset.
  • You have 5+ years of relevant experience in project management, strategy, engineering, or related disciplines. Experience in the business aircraft or aviation industry is preferred.
  • You have strong strategic thinking, project management, financial, and analytical skills.
  • You are assertive, results-oriented, and demonstrate leadership and initiative.
  • You have excellent organizational and prioritization abilities, with a proactive mindset.
  • You have strong Microsoft Office Skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Power Bi).
  • You have proven experience engaging and aligning cross-functional stakeholders (Engineering, Program Management, HR, Finance, etc.) in a large, matrixed organization.
  • You have exceptional written and verbal communication skills.
  • You have ability to synthesize and present complex information to executive audiences.
  • You have a high degree of discretion in handling confidential information.

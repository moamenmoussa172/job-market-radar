About the job

The Capital & Inventory Controller is responsible for the financial oversight, reporting, analysis, and control of the Charleston Plant's inventory and capital assets for the VM division, ensuring accurate valuation, proper accounting treatment, and compliance with company policies and IFRS.

Working closely with Operations, Engineering, Supply Chain, the Controller provides financial insights and analysis that support effective asset management, strong internal controls, operational performance, and informed business decisions. Through proactive monitoring, issue resolution, and continuous process improvement, this position helps maintain the accuracy and integrity of financial records while identifying opportunities to enhance efficiency, accountability, and financial performance.

Capital Expenditure Controlling

  • Lead the end-to-end capital expenditure (CAPEX) controlling process for the division, ensuring effective financial planning, monitoring, reporting, and governance of capital investments.
  • Manage the accounting and financial reporting of capital assets, including project capitalization, asset-in-service tracking, fixed asset records, month-end close activities, and compliance with company policies and IFRS.
  • Monitor capital project performance against approved authorizations, budgets, and forecasts; analyze variances, identify risks and opportunities, and provide actionable recommendations to management.
  • Develop capital expenditure forecasts, annual budgets, and long-range investment plans to divisional objectives.
  • Drive continuous improvement in capital planning, reporting, and internal control processes while supporting audit requirements and ensuring compliance with corporate governance standards.

Inventory Controlling

  • Lead all inventory controlling activities for the VM division, ensuring accurate valuation, financial reporting, reconciliation, and compliance with company policies and IFRS.
  • Manage inventory accounting and month-end close processes, including journal entries, accruals, reconciliations, variance analysis, and reporting related to inventory balances, reserves, obsolescence, and working capital.
  • Monitor inventory performance and analyze financial variances affecting cash flow, balance sheet accuracy, and asset utilization, providing actionable recommendations to management.
  • Partner with Supply Chain and Operations teams to improve inventory accuracy, optimize working capital, and support operational planning and decision-making.
  • Support physical inventory counts, cycle count programs, and inventory audit activities while maintaining strong internal controls and data integrity.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives related to inventory processes, reporting accuracy, cost performance, and inventory optimization.

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or related field required.
  • MBA, CMA, or equivalent professional certification a plus.

Experience

  • 2+ years of progressive experience in manufacturing finance, controlling, FP&A, or accounting.
  • Experience with capital expenditure management and inventory accounting within manufacturing.
  • Comprehensive understanding of manufacturing operations, cost accounting and financial reporting processes.
  • Experience supporting month-end close and financial analysis activities.
  • Experience working with ERP and reporting systems such as SAP, Hyperion Essbase, etc

Core Competencies

  • Financial analysis and business acumen.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Project management and organizational effectiveness.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Continuous improvement mindset.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to financial accuracy.
  • Ability to influence cross-functional teams and drive results through collaboration.

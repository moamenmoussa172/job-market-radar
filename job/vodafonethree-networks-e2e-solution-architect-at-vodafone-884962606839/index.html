What you’ll do

  • You will lead the end-to-end technical solution design for major network and technology initiatives, ensuring alignment with business priorities and technology strategy.
  • You will guide pre-project activities, including bids, consultancy engagements, and feasibility assessments, helping to shape holistic solutions across Network and IT.
  • You will collaborate with architects, designers, and technical specialists to secure approval through governance forums, including the Cross Functional Architecture Board (XAB).
  • You will translate business requirements into practical, future-focused architectures that are scalable, operationally ready, and commercially sound.
  • You will oversee the production and maintenance of key solution artefacts, including Technology Requirement Specifications (TRS) and High-Level Service Designs (HLSDs).
  • You will support domain teams in creating detailed designs while maintaining an end-to-end view of the overall solution.
  • You will identify technical risks, evaluate options, and facilitate effective resolution of issues throughout the project lifecycle.
  • You will ensure solutions meet security, regulatory, operational, and architectural compliance standards.
  • You will foster collaboration across Network, IT, and business teams, building alignment and trust to achieve shared outcomes.
  • You will champion continuous improvement, helping to enhance design quality, governance effectiveness, and solution delivery practices across the organisation.

Who you are

  • You will bring extensive experience in end-to-end solution architecture within telecoms or other large-scale technology environments.
  • You will have a strong understanding of network and IT architecture principles, frameworks, governance, and systems integration.
  • You will be able to influence and engage a wide range of stakeholders, balancing business requirements with optimised technical solutions.
  • You will have a proven track record of leading complex, multi-domain technical projects and driving successful outcomes.
  • You will be experienced in producing, reviewing, and assuring high-quality solution artefacts such as TRS and HLSD documentation.
  • You will possess deep expertise across telecoms networks and IT systems, with detailed knowledge of at least two domains, such as RAN, Core, BSS, OSS, or related technology areas.
  • You will be comfortable communicating complex technical concepts in a clear and accessible way to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • You will demonstrate curiosity, sound judgement, and a collaborative approach when solving challenges and navigating change.
  • You will thrive in a fast-paced environment, showing resilience, adaptability, self-awareness, and a willingness to support and develop those around you.
  • You will be committed to delivering high-quality, compliant, and customer-focused solutions that create lasting value.
  • You will ideally hold a degree in Telecommunications, Engineering, or a related discipline, with advanced qualifications viewed favourably.

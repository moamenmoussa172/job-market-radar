What you’ll do

The Senior Manager People Data & Analytics is responsible for the leadership of the People Data & Analytics function at Vodafone, ensuring that Vodafone has access to the right data and insights, at the right time to enable strategic and operational decision making. The role holder will be instrumental in shaping and leading how we use data-driven insights to inform and enhance our people strategy, whilst driving operating model change across the people data & analytics value chain.

The team vision is to enable strategic partnering to the business by deriving rich insights from our data and enabling our HR teams and people leaders to access and understand key insights and recommendations clearly and ahead of time. This role will drive the most transformational activities across People Data & Analytics globally, (E.g., LeadForward HR Data squad) whilst ensuring timely delivery of BAU activities.

Leading the an onshore team comprising of Data Governance and Product Delivery, and Data Science roles, the role holder is responsible for setting the team strategy, influencing and aligning a high volume of senior stakeholders across markets and functions, prrioritising global demand and managing the outcomes for HR data, reporting and analytics delivery.
The role holder is also accountable for the external reporting delivery requirements (ESG and CSRD), ensuring we are adhering to data governance standards, policy and legislation and liaising with the CHRO to ensure timely approval of the external reporting.

Shaping the People Data and Analytics strategy to:
- Ensure the team strategy enables strategic HR partnering to the business
- Ensure HR (and people leaders) have access to the right tools and expertise to deliver on the needs of the business
- Maximise the investment in HR data lake and supporting tools

Ensures the team create insights that support delivery of the people strategy
-Understands the business priorities and supports the business by creating products that support decision making
-Lead the Group and VOIS teams to create regular data insights as well as one off analysis to support strategic priorities and KPI setting, helping the team to prioritise where required

Ensure data integrity, governance, privacy and compliance with GDPR and other relevant regulations
-Drive privacy by design for all HR Data & Analytics products, mainaining close relationships with Group Privacy to enable
-Ensure HR Data & Analytics products comply with all relevant data regulations e.g., GDPR, Responsible AI act etc.,

Leads continuous improvement of HR analytics capability
-Stay current with industry trends and advancements in People Analytics
-Identify opportunities for process improvements and innovation, partnering with VOIS to deliver.
-Provide training and development opportunities for HR team
-Ensure services delivered through partners in technology and VOIS are meeting business needs and ensure services are delivered to the right quality

Lead the team ensuring connection to broader HR strategy and support their development
-Ensure sufficient opportunities for development for the team

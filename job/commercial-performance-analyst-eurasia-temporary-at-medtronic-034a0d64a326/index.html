A Day in the Life

The NextGen Academy is a 12-month immersive program designed to launch your career in medical technology, offering hands-on experience and collaboration with diverse teams to support therapies and introduce innovative products across the region. The program is designed to develop you into a confident and capable future Commercial Performance Analyst, aligned with Medtronic's mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life.
The Commercial Performance Management function supports business leaders across the region by enabling performance visibility, standardized reporting, operational alignment, and data-driven decision-making. The team partners with commercial, finance, analytics, and operational stakeholders to improve performance monitoring processes, streamline reporting activities, identify process improvement opportunities, and support the adoption of digital, automation, and artificial intelligence-enabled solutions. This position is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates and follows an onsite work model.

The Commercial Performance Analyst supports performance management activities by helping collect business requirements, analyze existing reporting processes, monitor key performance indicators, and improve reporting consistency across the organization. Through the NextGen Academy, this role provides hands-on experience in commercial analytics, business performance management, reporting standardization, and digital transformation initiatives that improve organizational effectiveness and support data-driven decision-making.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Gather and document performance management requirements, objectives and key results (OKRs), reporting needs, and existing business processes.
  • Analyze current reporting processes to identify inefficiencies, duplication, manual activities, and standardization opportunities.
  • Support recurring performance monitoring activities by preparing reports and identifying key trends, variances, and performance drivers.
  • Maintain and improve performance reporting structures, templates, definitions, and governance practices.
  • Partner with commercial, finance, analytics, and operational stakeholders to support consistent performance measurement across the organization.
  • Assist in the development of dashboards, reporting tools, and automated reporting solutions.
  • Support the identification, testing, and adoption of automation and artificial intelligence-enabled performance management capabilities.
  • Document, track, and monitor process improvement initiatives to support scalability and organizational adoption.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Engineering, Supply Chain, or Operations Management completed within the last 12 months.
  • Experience through academic projects, internships, or coursework involving business analytics, performance reporting, commercial analysis, finance, or process improvement.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including data analysis, reporting, pivot tables, and data visualization.
  • Experience using Microsoft Power BI through academic projects, coursework, or internships.
  • Fluency in English, written and verbal.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting commercial performance management, business planning, sales operations, finance, or business analytics activities.
  • Experience creating dashboards, performance scorecards, or business intelligence reporting solutions.
  • Knowledge of automation tools, artificial intelligence applications, or digital transformation initiatives.
  • Experience documenting business processes, reporting frameworks, or operational procedures.
  • Experience working in multicultural or regional business environments.

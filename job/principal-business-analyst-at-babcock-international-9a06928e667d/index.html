The Role

As a Principal Business Analyst, you'll play a pivotal role in shaping and optimising transformation across a complex portfolio of strategic programmes within the Naval Nuclear business. This is more than a traditional business analysis position. You'll operate at an enterprise level, providing critical insight that helps senior leaders make investment decisions, prioritise transformation initiatives and deliver long-term business value.

Working across a diverse portfolio of projects and change programmes, you'll help connect strategy with delivery, ensuring that transformation activities align with organisational objectives and deliver meaningful outcomes. This is an excellent opportunity to expand your influence, work alongside senior stakeholders and contribute to programmes that support national defence and security, while advancing your own career in a dynamic and highly respected organisation.

What you’ll do:

  • Developing and maintaining an integrated view of transformation initiatives, dependencies and strategic outcomes.
  • Analysing business cases, proposals and change initiatives to support prioritisation and investment decisions.
  • Identifying and managing dependencies, risks and impacts across multiple programmes, projects and business functions.
  • Facilitating workshops, governance activities and stakeholder discussions to support informed decision-making.
  • Providing strategic recommendations and insight to senior leaders, helping maximise value and benefits realisation.

What you’ll need:

  • Significant experience in business analysis, strategic transformation, portfolio management or enterprise-wide change environments.
  • Demonstrable experience working across large, complex transformation portfolios or programmes.
  • Experience analysing and managing interdependencies between multiple projects, business functions and workstreams.
  • Experience influencing and engaging senior stakeholders within complex or highly regulated environments.
  • Experience translating complex information into clear, actionable recommendations that support business objectives.
  • Degree-level qualification or equivalent professional experience.
  • Professional Business Analysis qualification - Desirable
  • Benefits Management, Lean or Continuous Improvement qualification - Desirable

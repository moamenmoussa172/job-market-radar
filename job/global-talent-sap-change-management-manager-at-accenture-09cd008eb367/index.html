The Role

As an SAP Change Management Manager, you will lead the day-to-day delivery of Change Management workstreams within SAP transformation programs.

You will translate SAP-driven business and technology changes into structured people, communications, training, readiness, and adoption interventions that enable successful implementation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute Change Management Strategies and OCM plans aligned with the SAP transformation roadmap.
  • Lead Stakeholder Identification, Stakeholder Mapping, Analysis, and Engagement.
  • Plan and facilitate Change Impact Assessment / Change Impact Analysis workshops.
  • Translate SAP system, process, and operating-model changes into clear business and people impacts.
  • Develop and manage Change Communication and Employee Engagement strategies.
  • Lead Organizational Readiness and Business Readiness Assessments.
  • Develop Learning and Training Strategies, including training needs analysis and end-user enablement.
  • Build and coordinate Change Champion / Change Agent Networks.
  • Monitor User Adoption, Change Readiness, and Resistance and develop mitigation actions.
  • Partner with SAP functional teams, PMO, technology teams, and business stakeholders.
  • Facilitate stakeholder workshops and leadership engagement sessions.
  • Manage and coach Consultants and Analysts supporting the OCM workstream.
  • Ensure Change Management deliverables remain aligned with SAP deployment and go-live milestones.

Skills & Experience

  • Experience: 8+ years of relevant professional experience, including strong hands-on Change Management / OCM experience and experience leading change workstreams within SAP S/4HANA, ERP, or large-scale transformation programs.
  • Strong Organizational Change Management experience, ideally within SAP S/4HANA or ERP transformation programs.
  • Hands-on experience in Change Impact Assessment, Stakeholder Management, Change Communications, Training, Readiness, and User Adoption.
  • Understanding of the SAP transformation lifecycle and associated business and organizational change.
  • Knowledge of Prosci, ADKAR, or similar OCM methodologies.
  • Strong workshop facilitation and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication and presentation capabilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple OCM workstreams and collaborate across business and technology teams.

About the Role

We are seeking a Product Manager to own Tailscale's networking features — the layer that decides how traffic finds its destination by name on top of Tailscale’s seamless mesh connectivity solution. Concretely, you will own four feature sets: App Connectors, DNS, Funnel, and Tailscale Serve & Services.

A tailnet gives every device an identity and an address. Networking features are what turn addresses into something a person, a script, or an AI agent can actually use — a name, a service, an app, a URL.

  • App Connectors route traffic to specific SaaS and private applications by name, so access follows the app rather than the IP.
  • DNS — MagicDNS, split DNS, custom name servers, search domains
  • Tailscale Serve & Services make a workload — not a machine — the unit of connectivity and access: addressable, load-balanced, and durable across the instances behind it. This is the wedge that lets customers replace a service registry, and eventually much of what they use a service mesh for, with Tailscale.
  • Funnel is Tailscale's ingress story: securely exposing a Service running inside the tailnet to the public internet, from a hobbyist sharing a dev server to production webhooks and public endpoints.

This is the most user-visible role in Tailscale's Dataplane Platform, and the work is as much product design as it is networking. DNS settings, connector topologies, and service definitions are configured by network engineers and IT admins in the admin console, API, and CLI, and the distance between "magical" and "unusable" is the object model, the defaults, and the error messages.

See How Tailscale Works, Tailscale Funnel, and Four ways to Tailscale a service to learn more.

  • End-to-end ownership of a product area: strategy, requirements, launch (docs, enablement, rollout), and the iteration after launch
  • Evidence of data-informed decisions — metrics you defined, instrumentation you pushed for, and at least one time the data changed your mind

  • Abuse prevention, rate limiting, or trust and safety work on a publicly exposed service

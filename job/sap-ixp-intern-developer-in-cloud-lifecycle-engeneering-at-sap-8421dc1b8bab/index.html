About the job

PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

The Cloud Lifecycle Engeneering organization designs and implements the infrastructure to handle the requirements of the rapidly growing SAP Cloud Business. We are implementing the automation of delivering the whole portfolio of SAP Software to our customers, also we automate the process on update/upgrade and decommission of our software. We work with the latest cloud native technologies and we have solutions both in Cloud and on-Premise. We focus on and embrace AI enablement of our products and AI automation in our daily work.

EXPECTATIONS AND TASKS

Are you looking for job with focus on all modern cloud and AI-native technologies, and project with huge impact on the business?
Do you have background in cloud or UI development? Are you eager to learn new technologies? Do you want to work with the latest cloud technologies in the world?
We are seeking experienced and highly motivated students. You will be part of the teams working on the core lifecycle management components for SAP’s cloud business. Our team is fully responsible for the automation, orchestration and scalability of various lifecycle management operations. Our customers are among the biggest and successful companies in the world. You will get the opportunity to work with most trending technologies, in a highly motivated team, established from young talents as well as experienced professionals.

Specifically, you will:

  • Design, develop, test and deliver platform features
  • Embrace the exciting opportunity to join a high-profile strategic and transformational project to deliver the next generation of software lifecycle management services
  • Participating in all phases of the software services development, from design discussions, implementation, testing, to deployment and operations
  • Work in an agile team applying Scrum, agile development practices and DevOps, thus contributing to team goals, requiring the flexibility and the ability to work on different topics.

SKILLS AND COMPETENCIES
Required skills

▪ Student in Computer Science or related field
▪ Good knowledge in at least one OOP language, preferably Java
▪ Excellent theoretical and practical background in OOP, data structures, design patterns, algorithms
▪ Diverse coding skills, preferably with Java or UI frameworks
▪ Experience with Linux/UNIX
▪ Easily adapting to change and learning quickly in a fast-paced environment
▪ Excellent team player, self-motivated, passionate about technology
▪ Excellent verbal and written communication skills
▪ Fluency in English
Advantage:

▪ Experience in AI-native software development
▪ Experience with Kubernetes or Cloud Foundry
▪ Experience with various cloud services in AWS, GCP and Azure
▪ Use and knowledge of common build tools, repositories and CD/CI tooling, experience in test automation
▪ Knowledge and experience with micro-service architectures
▪ Knowledge and experience with agile methodologies for software and service development

WORK EXPERIENCE

▪ Previous employment is not mandatory but certain practical experience is important

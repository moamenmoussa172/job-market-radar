About the job

Join Us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers – we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact. The CVM Performance & Insights Analyst is responsible for monitoring commercial and campaign performance, evaluating A/B tests and personalization initiatives, and translating data into clear, actionable insights and recommendations that influence business decisions.This role focuses on understanding why performance changes, identifying key drivers, and supporting optimization of targeting, offers and customer engagement strategies across the customer lifecycle. The analyst works on top of existing data foundations and dashboards, using deep analytical thinking and SQL-based analysis when required to inform decisions.

What you’ll do

  • Own monitoring of campaign and commercial KPIs;
  • Lead A/B test and experimentation analysis;
  • Deliver clear, actionable business recommendations;
  • Drive SQL deep‑dives on performance drivers;
  • Act as trusted analytics partner to squads and stakeholders.

Who you are

  • Degree in Management, Economy, Math, Engineering or Data Science;
  • Analytical and critical thinking, with the ability to move from data to recommendation;
  • Experience in performance analysis, business analytics, marketing analytics or CVM/CRM analytics;
  • Ability to communicate insights clearly and influence decision‑making;
  • Experience interpreting dashboards and analytical outputs;
  • SQL – Mandatory; experience with Google BigQuery is a plus.
  • English and Portuguese fluency.

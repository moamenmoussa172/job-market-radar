About the job

Meet the Team

Cisco ThousandEyes is a Digital Experience Assurance platform that empowers organizations to deliver flawless digital experiences across every network - even the ones they don’t own. Powered by AI and an unmatched set of cloud, internet and enterprise network telemetry data, ThousandEyes enables IT teams to proactively detect, diagnose, and remediate issues - before they impact end- user experiences.

ThousandEyes is deeply integrated across the entire Cisco technology portfolio and beyond, helping customers deploy at scale while also delivering AI-powered assurance insights within Cisco’s leading Networking, Security, Collaboration, and Observability portfolios.

The Endpoint Engineering team is leading this charge, competing to dominate its market segment as well as becoming part of the fabric of Cisco’s wireless, security and collaboration systems. We are a small team where every engineer is hands-on and has a voice in where the product and technology goes. This creates a rare opportunity for you to work with one eye on the future, while staying closely rooted in delivering exciting new innovations today.

Your Impact

As part of the Endpoint team, you will contribute to building intuitive frontend experiences that help customers quickly troubleshoot network issues and analyze end-user experience over time. You will develop user-facing features with a frontend focus while also contributing to the APIs, data flows, and backend services that support them. You will collaborate with product managers, designers, and engineers to deliver reliable, accessible, and high-performing solutions to real customer problems. You will strengthen your full-stack skills by working across the application and learning how the product processes and presents complex endpoint data.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Professional production-level experience with JavaScript/TypeScript and Java/Kotlin.
  • Professional experience with Vue + Vuex/Pinia OR React + Redux (or other state management libraries).
  • Experience designing state-driven frontends with a proven ability to process, manage, and performantly render high-volume, real-time data streams and asynchronous events.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in AI-assisted development tools.
  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field, with a strong foundation in computer science fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with micro-frontends.
  • Experience consuming or contributing to an enterprise design system.
  • Experience designing distributed, event-driven systems with a proven ability to manage asynchronous high-load challenges.
  • Understanding of networking fundamentals

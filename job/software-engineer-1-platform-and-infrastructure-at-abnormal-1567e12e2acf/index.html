About the job

At Abnormal AI, we’re building the infrastructure that powers our AI-native cybersecurity platform, which protects the world’s largest enterprises from sophisticated attacks. The Platform & Infrastructure (PI) division provides the core systems that enable all Abnormal products to operate reliably, at scale, and with high performance.

You’ll join a team that owns critical services in cloud infrastructure, data platforms, observability, and development tooling.

What We’re Looking For:

● Ownership & Impact: You’re growth-oriented, take ownership of your work, and are eager to make a significant impact.

● Continuous Learner: Looking to grow as an engineer as part of a strong team, learning from established engineers, product managers, and designers.

● Strong Communicator: You excel in communication, with the ability to workautonomously and asynchronously with different teams.

Must Have Skills

● 2+ years of experience as a software engineer with a strong foundation in coding and problem-solving.

● Hands-on experience in backend development using Python, with exposure to cloud-native environments.

● Solid understanding of databases, REST APIs, and data pipelines.

● Familiarity with LLM's and AI development tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or Claude

● Demonstrated passion for building scalable, enterprise-grade systems that operate reliably in production.

● Curiosity and eagerness to learn about distributed systems, orchestration, and cloud infrastructure.

Nice to Have

● Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).

● Exposure to observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana).

● Interest in data platforms, streaming systems, or infrastructure-as-code.

What You’ll Do

As part of our Platform & Infrastructure team, you will:

● Leverage AI-powered Development – Use Cursor, Copilot, and other AI tools to

accelerate development, automate workflows, and improve reliability.

● Build Foundational Systems – Design and maintain core backend services, infrastructure tooling, and data pipelines that support all Abnormal products.

● Optimize for Scale – Improve performance and resilience of systems handling billions of events daily.

● Collaborate Across Teams – Partner with Detection, Data Platform, and Product Engineering teams to ensure seamless integration and platform consistency.

● Drive Reliability and Observability – Build automated monitoring, alerting, and logging to ensure system health and performance at scale.

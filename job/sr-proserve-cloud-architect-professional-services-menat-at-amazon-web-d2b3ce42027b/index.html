About the job

AWS Professional Services (ProServe) is seeking a Senior Cloud Architect to support enterprise customers across the Middle East, with a focus on the UAE and Saudi Arabia. You will work directly with customers in Financial Services, Telecommunications and the Public Sector to design and implement cloud solutions on AWS that move their business forward. As a trusted technical advisor, you will lead complex architecture engagements from strategy through delivery, mentor teams, and help shape best practices across the region.
Acting as the technical bridge between ProServe Account Executives and our delivery teams, you will translate complex business challenges into scalable, secure AWS solutions. You will own the technical narrative of each engagement, from discovery and solution design to delivery, and make sure every engagement delivers measurable business outcomes for some of the region's most important organizations.
Key job responsibilities
- Lead the design and implementation of complex, enterprise-scale cloud architectures for enterprise customers
- Serve as a trusted technical advisor to C-level executives and senior technical stakeholders, translating business requirements into scalable cloud solutions
- Drive cloud adoption strategies including migration, modernization, data analytics, AI/ML, and hybrid architectures tailored to industry-specific needs
- Develop and deliver technical architectures, reference implementations, and proof-of-concepts that accelerate customer outcomes
- Mentor and guide junior architects, contributing to team development and raising the technical bar across engagements
- Collaborate with AWS service teams to influence product roadmaps based on customer feedback and industry trends
- Contribute to reusable assets, frameworks, and methodologies that scale ProServe's impact across the region
- Navigate regulatory, compliance, and data sovereignty requirements across regulated industries like Financial Services, Telecom and Public Sector.
A day in the life
You might start your morning reviewing an architecture design with a customer's engineering team, then shift to a working session where you refine a generative AI proof-of-concept alongside solution architects and partners. After lunch, you could be presenting a program update to senior customer executives, articulating how technical milestones connect to their strategic goals. Between engagements, you contribute reference architectures and mentor colleagues, ensuring the insights you gain reach the broader team.
About the team
At AWS ProServe, you won't just build architectures — you'll shape the cloud journeys of most innovative companies in across multiple industries in the Middle East. Your work will have direct, measurable impact on organizations that drive the region's economy.
AWS Professional Services works with enterprise customers and partners to help them realize their desired business outcomes through cloud adoption. Our team assists customers in achieving the greatest value from AWS by providing guidance, best practices, and hands-on delivery support throughout their cloud journey.
- 7+ years of technical specialist, design and architecture experience
- 5+ years of database (eg. SQL, NoSQL, Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, Kinesis) experience
- 7+ years of consulting, design and implementation of serverless distributed solutions experience
- 5+ years of software development with object oriented language experience
- 3+ years of cloud based solution (AWS or equivalent), system, network and operating system experience
- 7+ years of external or internal customer facing, complex and large scale project management experience
- Bachelor's degree, or 7+ years of professional or military experience
- Knowledge of AWS services including compute, storage, networking, security, databases, machine learning, and serverless technologies
- Experience in consulting, design and implementation of serverless distributed solutions

About the job

We are looking for the right people- people who want to innovate, achieve, grow and lead. We attract and retain the best talent by investing in our employees and empowering them to develop themselves and their careers. Experience the challenges, rewards and opportunity of working for one of the world’s largest providers of products and services to the global energy industry.

The role:

As an AI Engineering Team/Project Lead on our Data & AI team, you will work on a project team with two other engineers, leading the work of turning real engineering and operations workflows into agents that people across Halliburton rely on every day. You will build on our established agent platform, which spans Databricks and Azure, and extend it as you go.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role with no direct reports. You will write code every day, set the technical direction for your team, and put working agents in people's hands. You lead by building, not by managing tickets or running status meetings. Success is measured by what is live and in use, not by plans and presentations.

What you'll do:

  • Lead from the keyboard- Work on a project team with two other engineers, write production code every day, and take on the hardest technical problems yourself.
  • Ship workflows that get used- Lead your team in turning real engineering and operations workflows into production agents on our platform, from first conversation to daily use.
  • Own requirements and roadmap- Work with business stakeholders and the people who do the work to define requirements, cut scope and keep a roadmap focused on what delivers the most value.
  • Set and review architecture- Define the architecture for your team's agents, review designs and code, and hold a high bar for reliability, security and maintainability.
  • Connect agents to the business- Use secure tool and data connectors (including MCP servers) to give agents governed access to Halliburton data and engineering applications.
  • Prove it works- Use the platform's evaluation and tracing to show each agent is reliable, then track adoption and time saved in production.
  • Keep agents on track- Build optimization routines and monitoring that catch drift, regressions and failures early, so agents keep operating as designed long after launch.
  • Raise the team's speed- Make AI-first development the norm on your team, using coding agents, shared templates and code reviews to help every engineer ship faster.
  • Own what you ship- Fix what breaks and keep improving your team's agents based on real use.
  • Add to the platform- When you hit a gap, contribute the template, connector or evaluation that closes it, so the next workflow ships faster.

Who you are:

  • AI-first by default- You reach for an agent before you open a ticket. Your own workflow is already heavily automated, and you can show us how.
  • A builder with high agency- You don't wait for a perfect spec. You get the smallest useful version live fast, then harden and expand it.
  • A leader who builds- You earn trust through your code and decisions, not status meetings.
  • Allergic to repeated work- When you see the same task done twice, you build a tool so nobody has to do it a third time.
  • Comfortable in the mess- Legacy systems, messy data and shifting requirements don't slow you down.
  • Results over demos- A demo is not the finish line. You measure success by evaluation results, adoption and demonstrated time savings for the business.
  • Fluent in business and code- You turn business needs into a roadmap, then build it with your team.

What you'll need:

  • Must have 6+ years building and shipping production software, or equivalent demonstrated work.
  • Degree in Computer Science, Data Science or a related technical field, or equivalent experience.
  • Experience leading the technical work of a small engineering team (setting architecture, reviewing code and mentoring) while remaining a hands-on contributor.
  • Track record of working directly with business stakeholders to turn ambiguous needs into clear requirements and a delivery roadmap.
  • Strong Python, and comfort working across the stack (APIs, services, simple internal user interfaces).
  • Shipped LLM-powered agents to real users, ideally multi-agent systems rather than single prompt chains: tool calling, RAG, structured outputs, and prompt and context engineering.
  • Hands-on experience with agent frameworks and protocols (for example LangGraph, Microsoft Agent Framework / Semantic Kernel, OpenAI Agents SDK, Model Context Protocol) — and the judgment to know when not to use one.
  • Experience testing non-deterministic systems: building eval sets, automated scoring and regression checks.
  • Solid engineering fundamentals: Git, automated testing, CI/CD, containers and cloud deployment.
  • Hands-on Azure experience, such as Microsoft Foundry (Azure AI Foundry) Agent Service, Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search, Azure Kubernetes Service or Container Apps, and Entra ID.

Nice to have:

  • Databricks- Mosaic AI Agent Framework / Agent Bricks, MLflow tracing and evaluation, Unity Catalog, Model Serving, Vector Search.
  • Developer tooling- You have contributed to SDKs, internal platforms or shared libraries that other engineers use.
  • Total ownership- You treat what you build as your own. You take it from idea to production and stay accountable for the outcome, not just your piece of it.
  • Open source- Contributions to AI or developer tooling projects.
  • Energy or industrial exposure- Helpful, not required. We will teach you the domain; we can't teach you to build.

What success looks like:

  • First 30 days- You know the platform and your team, and have agreed on the team's first workflow, its success measure and an initial roadmap with business stakeholders.
  • First 90 days- Your team's first workflow is shipped and in regular use by the people it was built for, with a measured result such as hours saved. You wrote a meaningful share of the code.
  • First 6 months- Your team has several workflows live and in daily use, stakeholders trust the roadmap, and the architecture and review standards you set help the team ship faster.

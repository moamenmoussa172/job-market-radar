About the job

Title:

Portfolio Integration & Excellence Lead

"Belong, Connect, Grow, with KBR!

The KBR team of teams delivers future-forward science, technology and engineering solutions and mission-critical services that help governments and companies around the world accomplish their most important objectives, while also helping achieve their sustainability goals.

KBR Sustainable Technology Solutions provides holistic and value-added solutions across the entire asset life cycle. These include world-class licensed process technologies, differentiated advisory services, deep technical domain expertise, energy transition solutions, high-end design and engineering capabilities, and smart solutions to optimize planned and operating assets."

KBR is looking for a Portfolio Integration & Excellence Lead for a PMO portfolio PMC projectto be based in Abu Dhabi. The successful candidate will be responsible for portfolio-wide performance, assurance, and continuous improvement across major Oil & Gas megaprojects. The role will focus on portfolio integration, KPI management, performance benchmarking, early warning systems, project assurance, and excellence initiatives to support executive decision-making and project delivery success.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead portfolio integration, assurance, and performance excellence initiatives across multiple projects.
  • Develop and monitor portfolio KPIs, performance metrics, and executive dashboards.
  • Establish early warning systems to identify emerging risks, issues, and performance deviations.
  • Conduct portfolio assurance reviews, performance assessments, and health checks.
  • Drive benchmarking, lessons learned, and continuous improvement programs.
  • Consolidate and analyze cost, schedule, risk, and delivery performance across the portfolio.
  • Provide strategic insights and recommendations to senior leadership.
  • Ensure alignment of governance, reporting, and performance management frameworks across projects.

Experience required:

  • Minimum 15 years of experience in project assurance, project controls, portfolio performance, or PMO functions.
  • Proven experience on large-scale Oil & Gas Megaprojects within EPC, PMC, or Owner environments.
  • Strong expertise in KPIs, project assurance, benchmarking, early warning systems, portfolio reporting, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Experience developing executive dashboards and performance management frameworks.
  • Strong analytical, reporting, and stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • ADNOC project experience is an advantage.
  • Bachelor's Degree in Engineering is a MUST.

Interested and suitable candidates please apply for the role. Suitable candidates will be contacted directly.

About the job

At Quest Analytics, provider network management is our focus. For more than 20 years, we’ve helped health plans, healthcare organizations, and government agencies navigate complex regulatory and compliance requirements while building networks that give members access to the care they need.

As a Product Designer at Quest Analytics, you’ll shape how our clients experience the QES platform. You’ll design the workflows and interfaces that health plans and network teams rely on, and you’ll play a key part in growing our design system into a standardized, documented, developer-ready component library that product design and engineering teams share.

Your work will include building reusable UI components, writing the documentation behind them, and creating workflows that make consistency, accessibility, and efficiency the norm across our product suite. You’ll partner closely with engineering and explore how AI-assisted development tools, including Claude and Claude Code, can strengthen the connection between design and development and make the system easier to implement and maintain.

About the job

About Accenture

Accenture is a leading global professional services company helping the world's leading enterprises reinvent — building their digital core, unleashing the power of AI, and creating value at speed and scale. We bring together deep industry expertise, cutting-edge technology, and bold thinking to deliver outcomes that matter.

You'll play a central role in shaping and delivering security solutions for enterprise clients — from defining requirements through to design, build, and testing. This is a consulting role with real delivery accountability: you'll be expected to bring subject matter expertise, drive team performance, and contribute to decisions that matter across multiple workstreams.

  • Define security requirements and translate them into practical, well-architected security components and solutions
  • Lead and support the design, build, and testing of security deliverables across client engagements
  • Develop security strategies that align with client organizational goals and risk appetite
  • Conduct assessments to identify security vulnerabilities and recommend actionable remediation approaches
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure security measures are embedded comprehensively across people, process, and technology
  • Facilitate knowledge-sharing and training sessions to elevate team capability on security protocols and practices
  • Contribute to key decisions across multiple teams, providing solutions to both immediate and broader delivery challenges
  • Stay current with the latest security trends, technologies, and threat landscape developments to continuously sharpen delivery quality

What You'll Need

  • Expert proficiency in Security Operations Center (SOC) planning — including design, implementation, and operationalization
  • Advanced experience with Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) platforms
  • Proven ability to define security requirements and deliver solutions in complex, multi-stakeholder environments
  • Strong leadership and team management skills — comfortable owning decisions and driving cross-functional collaboration
  • Clear communicator with the ability to engage effectively at both technical and senior stakeholder levels
  • Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field

Bonus Points

  • Experience delivering managed security services or SOC transformation programs
  • Familiarity with SOAR platforms and security automation
  • Exposure to cloud security delivery across Azure, AWS, or GCP
  • Relevant certifications such as CISSP, CISM, GCIH, or equivalent

About the job

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced Project Manager to lead and oversee software delivery engagements from initiation through completion.

In this role, you will be responsible for managing project scope, timelines, budgets, resources, risks, and client expectations while ensuring successful delivery within agreed requirements and quality standards.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including Data & AI, Web Engineering, Experience Design, Product, and Quality, ensuring alignment across all project activities and maintaining strong client relationships throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Project Planning & Management: Own project scope, schedules, budgets, and risks. Develop and maintain integrated project plans based on team estimates and agreed deliverables.
  • Delivery Management: Lead day-to-day project execution, manage project meetings, track progress, maintain status reports, and proactively identify and escalate potential risks or delays.
  • Scope & Change Management: Monitor project scope, identify changes in requirements, assess their impact, and ensure all changes are properly documented, communicated, and agreed upon with clients.
  • Client Relationship Management: Act as the primary point of contact for clients throughout project delivery, manage expectations, communicate progress, and address challenges while maintaining strong working relationships.
  • Cross-functional Team Leadership: Coordinate with different capability teams, communicate resource and delivery needs, manage priorities and dependencies, and ensure alignment across stakeholders.
  • Budget & Financial Management: Monitor project budgets, effort, and overall financial performance. Ensure project milestones and deliverables are completed and properly documented to support timely invoicing.
  • Reporting & Risk Management: Maintain accurate project documentation, including status reports, risk and issue logs, and change records. Provide regular updates and escalate concerns when needed.
  • Project Closure & Handover: Oversee project closure and ensure a smooth handover to relevant support teams, including complete documentation, knowledge transfer, and resolution of outstanding issues.

Requirements

Experience

  • 4-7 years of experience delivering software projects, including at least 3 years in a Project Management role.
  • Proven experience managing client-facing projects end to end, with ownership of scope, timelines, budgets, and delivery.
  • Previous experience working in a software development company, digital agency, consultancy, or IT services environment.
  • Experience managing multiple projects or engagements simultaneously.

Technical Skills

  • Solid understanding of Agile and milestone-based, fixed-scope project delivery methodologies.
  • Hands-on experience with project management tools such as Jira and Confluence.
  • Strong knowledge of project planning, effort tracking, budgeting, and resource coordination.
  • Experience with scope management, change control, and risk and issue management.
  • Ability to understand and review technical solution designs related to web applications, APIs, and data platforms.

Communication & Leadership Skills

  • Strong ownership, accountability, and follow-through.
  • Excellent client-facing communication and stakeholder management skills in both English and Arabic.
  • Ability to manage client expectations, negotiate scope changes, and handle challenging conversations professionally.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • Ability to lead and influence cross-functional teams without direct reporting authority.
  • Proactive approach to identifying risks, addressing challenges, and escalating issues when necessary.

Education & Certifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field.
  • PMP, PRINCE2, PMI-ACP, or Scrum certifications (PSM/CSM) are considered an advantage.

Nice to Have

  • Experience delivering data platform, data warehouse, or analytics projects.
  • Familiarity with Microsoft Fabric, Azure, and data engineering concepts such as ETL, data models, and data governance.
  • Experience delivering web portals or CMS-based solutions.
  • Previous experience working with enterprise, financial services, or non-profit clients.
  • Experience coaching junior Project Managers or contributing to the development of a Project Management practice.

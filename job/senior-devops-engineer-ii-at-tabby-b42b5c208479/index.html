About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Serbia, United Arab Emirates.

Tabby creates financial freedom in the way people shop, earn and save by reshaping their relationship with money. Over 15 million users choose Tabby to stay in control of their spending and make the most out of their money.

The company’s flagship offering allows shoppers to split their payments online and in-store with no interest or fees. Over 40,000 global brands and small businesses, including Amazon, Noon, IKEA, and SHEIN use Tabby to accelerate growth and gain loyal customers by offering easy and flexible payments online and in stores.

Tabby generates over $10 billion in annual transaction volume for its partner brands and is the highest-rated, most-reviewed, largest, and fastest-growing FinTech in the GCC region.

Tabby launched in 2019 and has since raised +$1 billion in equity and debt funding from global and regional investors, and is now valued at $6.5 billion.

We are looking for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join our infrastructure team. The role focuses on operating, maintaining, and improving production infrastructure in Google Cloud Platform (GCP), with a strong emphasis on reliability, automation, and production ownership.

We are looking for an engineer who can independently manage production infrastructure, troubleshoot complex issues, respond to incidents, and proactively improve existing systems.

  • Google Cloud Platform (GCP): 3+ years of hands-on experience operating and maintaining production infrastructure.
  • Kubernetes (GKE): Strong practical experience managing, troubleshooting, and maintaining production Kubernetes clusters.
  • Terraform & Terragrunt: Solid hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) in production environments.
  • GitLab CI/CD: Experience managing pipelines, runners, and deployment workflows.
  • Production Ownership: Proven ability to independently troubleshoot production issues, resolve incidents, and make operational decisions.
  • On-call: Experience participating in production on-call rotations and willingness to respond to incidents outside regular working hours.
  • Linux & Networking: Strong troubleshooting skills and understanding of Linux systems, TCP/IP, DNS, load balancing, and networking fundamentals.

Nice to Have

  • Python or Go: Ability to write scripts and small tools for infrastructure automation.
  • Cloudflare: Understanding of Cloudflare services, WAF rules, security policies, and configuration management.
  • Security Hardening: Experience with infrastructure hardening, vulnerability management, and security tooling.
  • Experience with monitoring, logging, and observability solutions.
  • Participate in production on-call rotations, investigate incidents, troubleshoot issues, and restore services.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve production infrastructure hosted in GCP.
  • Manage and support production Kubernetes clusters and related infrastructure components.
  • Maintain and evolve infrastructure using Terraform and Terragrunt.
  • Deploy, configure, and integrate new infrastructure tools and services.
  • Automate repetitive operational tasks to reduce manual work and improve operational efficiency.
  • Maintain and improve GitLab CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes.
  • Collaborate with engineering and security teams to ensure infrastructure reliability, performance, and security.
  • Proactively identify infrastructure risks, operational bottlenecks, and opportunities for improvement.

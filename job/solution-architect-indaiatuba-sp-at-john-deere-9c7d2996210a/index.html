About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Technology (CA)
Title: Solution Architect - Indaiatuba/SP - 125680

As a Solution Architect at John Deere in Indaiatuba/SP, you will lead the design of modern Finance solutions and future-state architectures that drive business transformation, reduce complexity, and enable scalable, secure, and intelligent digital capabilities. This role steers key architectural decisions, defines solution patterns and technology standards, and establishes strategic roadmaps aligning business goals with modern technology. This role emphasizes practical leadership, active hands-on exploration through proofs of concept, and close partnership with business stakeholders to modernize platforms and unlock value through AI, data, and automation. You will collaborate closely with engineering, product, and architecture teams to solve complex technical challenges, deliver maintainable solutions, and mentor team members in adopting architectural best practices.

Main Responsibilities

  • Lead the design of modern Finance solutions and future-state architectures that drive business transformation, reduce complexity, and enable scalable, secure, and intelligent digital capabilities.
  • Lead architecture decisions, define solution patterns and technology standards, and create roadmaps that align business and technology strategy.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies and identify opportunities to create value through AI, data, automation, and modernization.
  • Work hands-on with proofs of concept, technology evaluations, and architecture experiments to validate new capabilities.
  • Partner with business stakeholders to simplify processes, modernize platforms, and improve business outcomes.
  • Collaborate with engineers, product teams, and architects to deliver secure, reliable, and maintainable solutions.
  • Support delivery teams through implementation, helping solve complex technical challenges and ensuring solution success.
  • Share technical knowledge, mentor team members, and help teams adopt architecture best practices.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience;
  • Advanced English (broad vocabulary, strong grammar, able to lead meetings, argue, negotiate, and conduct training sessions);
  • Experience designing and delivering enterprise solutions, including SAP S/4HANA, BTP, Integration Suite, and related SAP technologies supporting Finance business capabilities.
  • Strong understanding of solution architecture, integration patterns, APIs, cloud technologies, data platforms, and enterprise application ecosystems.
  • Experience creating technology roadmaps, defining future-state architectures, and guiding technology decisions that balance business value, scalability, security, maintainability, and cost.
  • Hands-on experience developing proofs of concept, troubleshooting complex technical challenges, and working alongside engineering teams to validate and implement solution designs.
  • Experience applying architecture governance practices, including architecture modeling and technical decision records (TDRs).
  • Ability to translate business objectives into practical technical solutions and influence decisions across business and technology teams.
  • Strong critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills with the ability to explain complex concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Knowledge of AI-enabled solutions, automation technologies, and emerging technology trends.
  • Full-stack troubleshooting and analysis experience within complex enterprise environments.
  • Experience working in Agile delivery models and partnering closely with product and engineering teams.

About the job

As a Senior Software Developer – Agentic Coder, you will play a key role in developing modern software solutions in an engineering environment where AI coding agents are an integral part of everyday development.

We are looking for an experienced, hands-on developer who knows how to leverage AI coding agents beyond simple code generation. You will independently deliver complex software solutions, combining agent-assisted development with strong engineering judgment to ensure high standards of quality, security and maintainability.

Our main technology stack includes Spring Boot, Java and Kotlin, complemented by modern frontend technologies such as React and Angular. You will have the opportunity to explore new development approaches, influence technical decisions and contribute to the evolution of AI-assisted software engineering within Bosch.

Your tasks:

► Independently design, develop and deliver complex software solutions from requirements analysis to production-ready implementation

► Leverage AI coding agents throughout the software development lifecycle, including planning, implementation, testing, debugging, refactoring and code review

► Structure development tasks and provide AI agents with the appropriate context, technical constraints and acceptance criteria

► Critically review and validate AI-generated code, ensuring correctness, security, performance and maintainability

► Develop and continuously improve backend applications using Spring Boot, Java and Kotlin, contributing to frontend development when needed

► Participate in architectural and technical decisions, balancing business requirements, scalability and long-term solution quality

► Design and maintain automated tests and utilize CI/CD pipelines, static analysis and quality checks to validate implementations

► Identify technical risks, investigate complex issues and proactively improve existing applications and development processes

► Explore emerging agentic development tools and techniques and evaluate their practical value for software engineering

► Share knowledge and best practices with colleagues, supporting the adoption of effective AI-assisted development approaches

► Several years of professional software development experience at Senior level, with a proven ability to independently deliver complex solutions

► Strong hands-on experience with Spring Boot and professional development experience in Java and/or Kotlin

► Substantial practical experience with modern AI coding agents, including their use in complex, multi-step software development activities

► Ability to effectively guide AI agents, provide appropriate context and critically evaluate generated solutions

► Strong understanding of software architecture, APIs, databases, automated testing and clean coding principles

► Experience working with existing and unfamiliar codebases, including debugging, refactoring and technical improvements

► Ability and willingness to contribute across different parts of a modern web application stack

► Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured, independent and quality-oriented working style

► Proactive mindset, strong ownership and willingness to explore new technologies and development approaches

► Good communication and collaboration skills in an international engineering environment

► Very good command of English, both written and spoken

What gives you further advantages:

► Experience with React, Angular, TypeScript or other modern frontend technologies

► Practical experience with Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot or comparable coding-agent environments

► Knowledge of context engineering, repository-level agent instructions and advanced agent-assisted workflows

► Experience with relational and NoSQL databases

► Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes and cloud technologies

► Experience with microservices, distributed systems and enterprise applications

► Experience with CI/CD processes and production operation of software

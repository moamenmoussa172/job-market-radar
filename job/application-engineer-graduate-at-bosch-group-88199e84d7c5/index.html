About the job

Calibration Engineer:

Support Development & calibration of Anti-Lock Brakes System (ABS), Traction Control System (TCS), Vehicle Dynamics Control (VDC) and/or value added functions (VAF) in close collaboration with individual UK car manufacturers and to their requirements.

  • Testing, supporting calibration and verifying the software and its application in test vehicles and via simulation.
  • Taking part in numerous tests trips all over Europe (including Germany, Sweden, Spain, Italy).
  • Travelling and working for periods at our headquarters in Abstatt, Germany to build up a network there and gain the knowledge you need to be effective in your role.
  • Analyse measurement data and support calibration improvements under guidance.
  • Contribute to structured handling of changes throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Work closely with senior engineers, project management, and cross-functional teams.
  • Support technical discussions with customers and internal stakeholders.

To be eligible for this Graduate role at Bosch, you must hold an undergraduate degree in a relevant field.

This is a 24-month graduate role beginning in September 2027. This position is open to recent graduates looking for a graduate level opportunity.

Location: you will be based at the Nuneaton office.

As a Graduate at Bosch, you will participate in an exclusive learning and development programme designed to support you through your graduate journey. It includes in-person sessions on topics including Assertiveness and How to Manage Conflict provided by expert external trainers.

Interview Process and Right to Work Information:

Shortlisted candidates will then be invited to participate in a face-to-face interview by the hiring team and/or an online assessment centre.

You must have the right to live and work in the UK when you start your graduate role and for the full duration of your graduate programme. Please note that we cannot offer visa sponsorship for this role at this current time.

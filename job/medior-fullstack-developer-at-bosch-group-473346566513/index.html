About the job

Job Description

We are looking for an experienced Fullstack Developer to join our innovative department, where we develop solutions for next-generation, fully electric vehicles. In this role, you will work closely with international development teams to design and build software tools that support engineers worldwide throughout the entire product development lifecycle.

Your responsibilities will include:

In this position, you will contribute to the design and development of modern software solutions used by a large global user community. You will play a key role in transforming business requirements into scalable and reliable applications by:

  • Developing modern frontend applications using Angular 17+, with a strong focus on performance, maintainability, and user experience.
  • Designing and implementing robust backend services in .NET/C#, including secure and scalable RESTful APIs.
  • Creating and maintaining clean software and database architectures that support long-term scalability and reliability.
  • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver stable, high-quality software solutions for large user communities with more than 1,000 users.
  • Contributing to application lifecycle management, deployment processes, and DevOps practices to ensure efficient software delivery.
  • Analyzing complex technical challenges, troubleshooting issues, and continuously improving existing systems and services.
  • Driving software quality through best practices in architecture, coding standards, testing, and documentation.

If you are passionate about fullstack development, enjoy building scalable enterprise solutions, and would like to work in an international environment where your contribution has a real impact, we would love to meet you!

Qualifications

Architecture Skills (Application & System Design):

Best Have:

  • Understanding of application lifecycle, deployment pipelines, and DevOps principles.
  • Ability to understand and adapt technical architecture diagrams, data flow diagrams, and component communication patterns.

Backend Development Skills:

Must Have:

  • Hands-on experience with .NET Core, C#, Web API, and Entity Framework.
  • Experience designing and implementing RESTful APIs with secure, scalable interfaces.
  • Familiarity with SQL databases (PostgreSQL and SQL Server preferred).
  • Strong command of object-oriented programming.
  • Clean Architecture experience

Frontend Development Skills (Angular):

Must Have:

  • Minimum 1 year (better >2) of Angular experience, including version 17+.
  • Solid understanding of Angular standalone components, lazy loading, and modules.
  • Strong understanding of RxJs observables

Best Have:

  • Automatic unit and system testing with Angular
  • Experience with Angular Signals
  • Server Side Rendering in Angular + Deployment
  • Familiar with responsive design, cross-browser compatibility, and performance best practices.

Soft Skills & Work Ethic:

Must Have:

  • Ability to work independently and manage time efficiently.
  • Problem-solving and analytical skills to debug and resolve complex issues.
  • Passion for learning new technologies and adapting to changing requirements.
  • Strong communication skills and commitment to quality and deadlines.

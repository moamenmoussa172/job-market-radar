Shape the Future of Defence Systems Engineering

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a Senior Systems Engineer at Ashton Vale, near Bristol, Gloucestershire.

As a Senior Systems Engineer within our Integrated Test Evaluation Acceptance team, you'll have the opportunity to work across the full engineering lifecycle on a high-profile, complex defence programme. This is a role where your expertise will directly contribute to the delivery of critical capability for our customers, while giving you the chance to further develop your technical leadership, systems engineering and requirements management skills in a collaborative and supportive environment.

The role

As a Senior Systems Engineer, you'll provide requirements, verification, validation and acceptance engineering expertise across a complex programme. Working closely with subject matter experts across software, integration, safety, security, installation and human factors disciplines, you'll play a key role in ensuring systems are delivered to the highest standards of quality, performance and safety.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Prepare test and integration specifications which will include the formal verification planning, execution, and results capture across functional and non-functional events. Ensuring test baselines are agreed, planning and conducting test readiness and exit reviews, and supporting the execution of integration and test schedules to enable system qualification and acceptance.
  • Pro-actively manage, review and develop system requirements, building acceptance and test cases and linking test events to the managed Verification and Validation Requirements Matrix.
  • Lead engineers to accomplish critical milestones, working with project managers and other systems engineering teams and Subject Matter Experts to understand, develop and manage timescales and assess resource needs.
  • Ensure that engineering processes and deliverables meet quality standards and are aligned with best practices achieving Product Quality and Safety aims and objectives.
  • Identify opportunities for process improvements and drive initiatives to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of engineering operations.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements with days onsite and days working from home.

Essential experience of the Senior Systems Engineer

  • Demonstrable systems engineering and requirements management experience.
  • Full Lifecycle engineering management experience, including requirements management and acceptance in Factory Testing, commissioning and harbour/sea trials phases.
  • Knowledge of UK MoD practices and ways of working.
  • Experience working with other technical specialists such as HFI, installations, commissioning, safety and combat systems integration and leading small teams and/or projects.
  • Experience using Requirements Management tools, ideally DOORS NG.

Qualifications for the Senior Systems Engineer

  • Engineering Degree or equivalent HNC/HND with equivalent, relevant experience is valued and welcomed.
  • Incorporated Engineer or working towards.

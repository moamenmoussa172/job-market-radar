[BDO] IT Operations & Communications Intern

Bosch Group · Ho Chi Minh, Vietnam

Onsite · Intern / Student · Other tech role · Posted 18 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Join the BDO team and support administrative coordination, purchasing, travel, events, associate communications, and onboarding. The role also includes newsletter content, operational announcements, and maintaining key administrative information.

Responsibilities

  • Support purchasing for accessories, stationery, and ad hoc needs by contacting vendors, obtaining quotations, preparing purchase order and work order requests, following up with BGSW, receiving goods, and supporting payment processing.
  • Arrange meetings, workshops, department meetings, and town halls; support invitations, event preparation, printing, posters, and other event materials.
  • Prepare invitation letters and coordinate guest visits, including accommodation, transportation, hotel bookings, refreshments, fruit, and lunch or dinner arrangements.
  • Collect information for the BDO newsletter, help create newsletter videos, and prepare announcements about tools, downtime, and other operational updates.
  • Maintain onboarding guidance for new associates, including the overall process and IDM-related roles; support organization chart maintenance and manage the company distribution list.
  • Take on other administrative tasks as required.
  • Third- or fourth-year university students in Business Administration, Secretarial Studies, IT, Communications or a related field
  • An official university recommendation letter with stamp is mandatory for university students.
  • Available full-time or at least four days per week for a six-month internship.
  • Excellent English skills in writing, reading, speaking, and listening.
  • Very good communication, interpersonal, and teamwork skills, with a professional approach when working with vendors, associates, and guests.
  • Good knowledge of administrative and clerical procedures.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, PowerPoint, and Excel; basic video editing or visual content creation skills are an advantage.
  • Well organized and detail-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Hard-working, enthusiastic, dynamic, and confident when coordinating tasks and communicating with different stakeholders.
  • Interested in learning internal processes and willing to take responsibility for assigned tasks.

WHY BOSCH?

Because we do not just follow trends, we create them. Together we turn ideas into reality, working every day to make the world of tomorrow a better place.

Do you set high expectations for your learning journey? So do we. At Bosch, you’ll explore, grow, and challenge yourself in a dynamic and innovative environment.

Internship Benefits & Learning Opportunities at Bosch

  • Monthly Internship Allowance, plus Meal and Parking support.
  • Paid leave day + 1 sick leave day for each internship month.
  • 1 birthday leave day if your birthday falls during the internship period.
  • Accident insurance provided throughout the internship.
  • Opportunity to observe and engage in international projects and innovation.
  • Access to our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professionalism.
  • Participation in various company activities such as football, yoga, badminton, and team building.

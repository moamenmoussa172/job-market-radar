Senior Tax  Analyst - International Taxes

Toast · Dublin, Ireland

Onsite · Senior · Data Analyst · Posted 10 November 2026

Apply on Toast's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

ABOUT THE ROLE

At Toast, we’re building the future of the restaurant industry—and our Global Tax team is building the foundation that makes it all possible.

The Senior Tax Analyst, International Tax, is a hands-on role supporting Toast's indirect tax requirements across its growing footprint. The role will have focus on foreign VAT/GST technical support, e-invoicing and digital reporting requirements, international business initiatives, and ongoing monitoring of indirect tax developments. This role will also support key Ireland-based tax and incentive activities, including ongoing monitoring and reporting of IDA grant commitments.

Working closely with the Global Indirect Tax team, and teams in Dublin and India, business partners and external advisors, this role will help translate international indirect tax requirements into practical processes and support Toast as its international operations continue to evolve.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

International VAT / GST & Indirect Tax

  • Research and analyze VAT/GST implications of new products, business initiatives, and changes to Toast’s international operations.
  • Support the Global Indirect Tax Team on international VAT/GST technical matters, including documenting tax positions and coordinating with external advisors where appropriate.
  • Monitor VAT/GST legislative and regulatory developments across Toast’s international jurisdictions and communicate relevant business impacts.
  • Support international indirect tax audits, registrations, and other jurisdiction- specific requirements.
  • Partner with the India Tax Operations team and Global Indirect Tax team to support consistent execution of VAT/GST compliance process.

E-Invoicing & Digital Tax Compliance

  • Monitor emerging e-invoicing, digital reporting, and real-time tax compliance requirements across Toast’s international jurisdictions.
  • Support implementation of e-invoicing and digital reporting requirements by translating tax requirements into business and system requirements.
  • Partner with Finance, Accounting, Legal, Product, and technology teams to support implementation, testing, and ongoing compliance.
  • Coordinate with external advisors to understand jurisdiction-specific requirements and help operationalize those requirements within Toast’s processes and systems.

Ireland Grant & Incentive Support

  • Coordinate ongoing monitoring and reporting of IDA grant commitments, including maintaining supporting documentation and working with relevant business stakeholders.
  • Monitor grant activity and communicate potential impacts on tax incentives or other tax matters to the International Tax Manager and Corporate Tax Team
  • Support data gathering and documentation for Ireland tax incentive activities, as needed

Process Improvement & Governance

  • Help standardize international tax compliance processes, calendars, workpapers, and documentation.
  • Identify opportunities to improve the quality and efficiency of international tax compliance and reporting processes.
  • Support process improvement, automation, and technology initiatives across the global tax function.

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

Required

  • 4+ years of progressive VAT/GST, indirect tax, or related tax experience, ideally with a combination of public accounting and in-house experience
  • Strong working knowledge of VAT/GST principles and experience researching and applying indirect tax requirements
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to understand complex transactions, identify tax implications, and communicate findings clearly
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to work effectively across functions, culture, and geographies
  • Demonstrated ability to operate independently, exercise sound judgment, manage multiple priorities, and drive work to completion
  • Willingness to work flexible hours with global business
  • Must be able to multitask, work efficiently under tight deadlines and proactively track and report progress
  • Self-motivated to apply learned experiences and leverage best practices

Preferred

  • Experience working in a technology, SaaS, fintech, or other high-growth multinational environment preferred
  • Experience with e-invoicing, digital tax reporting, or other technology-enabled tax compliance requirements
  • Experience with R&D tax credits, government incentives, or other local tax incentive programs
  • Experience driving process improvement, automation, data transformation, or AI-enabled initiatives within a tax, finance, or accounting function

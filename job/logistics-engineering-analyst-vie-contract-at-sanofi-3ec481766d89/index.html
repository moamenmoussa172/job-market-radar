About the job

Logistics Engineering Analyst - VIE Contract

  • Location: Spain, Barcelona

iMove, the Sanofi VIE Program, is available to citizens of the European Economic Area (EU + Norway, Liechtenstein and Iceland) aged between 18 and 28.

PLEASE NOTE that since this program is primarily an international development program, candidates cannot apply to a VIE assignment in their own country of citizenship.

PLEASE NOTE that applications that are only submitted in French cannot be considered by our non-French speaking partners at Sanofi worldwide. Therefore, only applications that are submitted in English will be considered. Please make sure to apply with your personal email address.

About the job


As Logistics Engineering Analyst VIE within our Supply Chain team, you will support the ongoing transformation of Sanofi's export platforms in Germany and France, which serve as critical distribution centers exporting to regional distribution hubs and markets across Europe and beyond.

Join a global network that powers how Sanofi delivers — seamlessly, purposefully, and at scale. In Manufacturing & Supply, you’ll help reimagine how life-changing treatments reach people everywhere, faster.

Ready to get started?

About Sanofi

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main responsibilities:

  • Support the operational design of export platforms by assessing warehouse resource allocation needs based on volume and capacity requirements.
  • Contribute to the definition of material and financial flows within the warehouse setup.
  • Collect and consolidate data to assess operational volumes for warehouse sizing and resource allocation.
  • Map and document processes in collaboration with transversal Global Supply Chain functions, including Finance, Procurement, and Business Operations.
  • Plan and prepare content for governance meetings related to externalization projects.
  • Track project milestones and ensure timely follow-up on action items across stakeholders.
  • Prepare clear and concise communication materials (reports, presentations) for stakeholders and leadership.

About you

Experience:

  • Prior experience or exposure to project management is preferred.


Soft and technical skills:

  • Strong analytical mindset with proficiency in Excel (data analysis, pivot tables, dashboards); knowledge of Power BI, SAP (ERP), or WMS is a plus.
  • Ability to work with large datasets, perform process mapping, and identify improvement opportunities.
  • Critical thinking and assertiveness to challenge the status quo and translate data into actionable insights.
  • Strong organizational skills, autonomy, and ability to manage multiple priorities independently.
  • Excellent communication skills and comfort working in cross-functional, international environments.
  • Proactive mindset and willingness to learn in a dynamic, transformation-focused environment.


Education:

  • Master's degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, Management, or a related field.


Languages:

  • Fluent English (written and verbal).
  • French is a plus.

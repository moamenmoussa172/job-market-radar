About the role

LemFi Europe is authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland as a payment institution and serves customers across the EU. As the business grows, so does the number of investigations, high-risk customer reviews and potential suspicious transaction reports that reach the MLRO. We need a Senior AML Analyst to make sure every one of those cases arrives complete, well evidenced and ready for a decision.

You will sit in the second line of defence, reporting directly to the MLRO. Our Operations team carries out the first review of alerts; you pick up the cases they escalate, test whether their assessment holds up, build the case file and recommend an outcome. The MLRO then reviews, challenges and makes the final call, including whether a suspicious transaction report is filed and any engagement with FIU Ireland or the Central Bank. Your job is to make those decisions quicker and better evidenced.

We want to be upfront: a large part of this role is operational. Building case files, saving evidence, keeping trackers accurate and chasing missing information. Done well, that work is what lets a small AML function keep pace with a fast-growing business and stand up to scrutiny from auditors and the Central Bank. If you like order, take pride in a clean audit trail and want close exposure to how MLRO decisions are made, you will get a lot out of it.

What you'll build and own

Case preparation

  • Review cases escalated by our Operations team as potential suspicious activity, and check the initial assessment is complete, logical and supported by the customer and transaction data.
  • Build the case file for each investigation, gathering customer due diligence records, transaction history, previous alerts, information requests and any other supporting evidence.
  • Prepare the case pack for the MLRO, including the investigation summary, a transaction timeline and the supporting narrative.
  • Flag gaps or inconsistencies, and recommend an outcome on each case for MLRO decision.

Investigation tracking and reporting

  • Own the central tracker of all LemFi Europe AML investigations, potential suspicious transaction reports and filings.
  • Monitor case ageing and make sure every open case has a clear owner and a next step.
  • Produce weekly and monthly management information for the MLRO on open cases, ageing, filings and overdue actions.

High-risk customer reviews

  • Gather source of funds, source of wealth and transaction information for high-risk customer, PEP and enhanced due diligence reviews, and draft the review summary for MLRO sign-off.
  • Track outstanding information, approvals and review dates so reviews are completed on time.

Working across the business

  • Work day to day with our transaction monitoring, customer due diligence and Operations teams, following up where documents or responses are missing.
  • Keep records audit-ready and pull together evidence for internal and external audits, regulatory requests and remediation work.
  • Spot recurring process or data problems and raise them with the MLRO, with a proposed fix.
  • Use AI tools to speed up drafting of case summaries, timelines and reports, checking everything thoroughly before it reaches the MLRO.

Your experience

  • At least 3 years in an AML, financial crime or investigations role at a payment institution, e-money institution, bank or remittance business regulated in Ireland.
  • Hands-on experience preparing investigation files or suspicious activity reports, and a clear sense of what makes a case defensible.
  • Working knowledge of the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010, as amended, and the Central Bank's AML/CFT guidelines for the financial sector.
  • Clear, concise writing. You can turn a messy transaction history into a chronology and narrative a decision-maker can follow in one read.
  • Meticulous record keeping. You notice when a document is missing or a date does not line up.
  • Confident with spreadsheets and transaction data
  • Able to manage your own caseload, prioritise and follow up without being chased.

Nice to have

  • A good understanding of the EU AML framework
  • CAMS, the ICA Diploma in AML, or an equivalent professional qualification.
  • Experience in cross-border payments or remittances, ideally with corridors into Africa and Asia, and hands-on use of screening, case management or transaction monitoring tools.

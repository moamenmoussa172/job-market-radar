About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Java Backend Developer to design and deliver robust, high-performing backend solutions for Amdocs Digital One CRM – Care. You will translate business and functional requirements into reliable, reusable technical designs using Core Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs and microservices.

Based in Pune, this role requires 4 to 10 years of hands-on backend development experience, including practical experience with Amdocs Digital One tools and telecom processes. You will collaborate with Product Owners, Business Analysts and technology stakeholders to support solution design and delivery.

What you’ll do

  • Act as the backend technology representative for business and IT stakeholders supporting Amdocs Digital One CRM – Care solution delivery.Analyse business drivers and functional requirements and translate them into backend services, integrations, APIs and microservices aligned with business objectives.
  • Collaborate with Product Owners, Business Analysts and Business Engagement Managers to understand solution requirements and convert them into technical implementation plans.
  • Design backend solutions that follow engineering best practices and support performance, reliability and maintainability.
  • Work with central and local Technology stakeholders to define the appropriate application, infrastructure and deployment approach.
  • Develop reusable backend solutions using Core Java, Spring Framework, Spring Boot, REST APIs microservices and Apache Kafka.
  • Apply Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Git and Maven/Gradle practices to backend development and cloud-native deployments.Contribute effectively within Agile/Scrum delivery environments and support module-level delivery where required.

Who you are

  • You have 4 -8 years of hands-on backend development experience.
  • You have strong practical expertise in Core Java, Spring Framework/Spring Boot, REST APIs, Apache kafka and microservices architecture.
  • You have hands-on experience with Digital One CRM – Care and its associated tools.
  • You have experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Git and Maven/Gradle.
  • You understand telecom product, order and/or catalogue processes.
  • You have experience working in Agile/Scrum delivery models.
  • You can translate business and functional requirements into robust IT designs and reusable backend solutions.
  • You bring effective communication, stakeholder engagement, influencing and decision-making skills and can work within a matrix environment.

Desired

  • Previous experience leading a team, mentoring developers or owning module-level delivery.
  • Knowledge of cloud technologies and cloud-native deployment patterns.
  • Exposure to the Telco BSS/OSS domain, including product modelling, order management or catalogue capabilities.
  • Experience combining hands-on backend development with Product Owner or Business Analyst responsibilities.

What skills you will learn

  • Deeper expertise in Amdocs Digital One CRM – Care solution delivery.Advanced application of Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs and microservices in telecom environments.
  • Practical experience with Docker, Kubernetes and cloud-native deployment patterns.
  • Greater understanding of Telco BSS/OSS, product modelling, order management and catalogue processes.
  • Enhanced experience translating business requirements into scalable backend designs and technical implementation plans.
  • Broader stakeholder management experience across business, product and technology teams.

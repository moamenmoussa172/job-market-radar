About the job

CNS are looking for a DevOps Support Analyst to support the delivery, maintenance, and continuous improvement of CNS's critical customs and logistics platforms. This role is responsible for supporting cloud infrastructure, DevOps tooling, automation, CI/CD pipelines, and operational reliability across business-critical applications and services.

The DevOps Support Analyst will work closely with software development, infrastructure, security, QA, and product teams to ensure the availability, performance, security, and continuous operation of CNS systems. The successful candidate will combine strong technical support capabilities with a passion for automation, continuous improvement, and service excellence, helping to drive modern DevOps practices across the organisation.

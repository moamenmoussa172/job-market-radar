About this Role

We are seeking an IT Budget & Financial Controls Analyst to join the PMO Centre of Excellence within VOIS. This role is responsible for supporting financial governance and budget management activities across technology projects and programmes, ensuring alignment with approved budgets and financial processes. The successful candidate will partner with Finance, Procurement, Project and Programme Managers to provide budget oversight, financial reporting, forecasting, spend analysis, and purchase order support while helping drive effective financial controls and decision-making.

What you’ll do

  • Partner with Budget, Project, and Programme Managers to ensure budgets are controlled, monitored, and aligned with approved financial plans.
  • Conduct regular meetings with project and programme stakeholders to review budget performance and provide guidance on budget-related activities and decisions.
  • Analyse budget forecasts, identify variances, communicate changes, and support financial planning activities.
  • Manage project accounting activities including budget tracker maintenance, spend analysis, variance reporting, forecasting, commitment receipting, and month-end reconciliation.
  • Support the preparation and consolidation of detailed bottom-up budget submissions.
  • Produce regular budget status reports for project teams, Operational Excellence management, and Finance Decision Support stakeholders.
  • Manage and consolidate financial reporting requirements across multiple projects and programmes, including ad hoc analysis and reporting requests.
  • Support purchase order related activities, reporting, and query resolution.
  • Track financial requests and ensure accurate reporting and follow-up.
  • Monitor approval processes for Shopping Carts and Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs), escalating issues where appropriate.
  • Collaborate with Finance, Vodafone Procurement Company (VPC), and other stakeholders to ensure effective programme financial control.
  • Ensure compliance with agreed reporting standards, governance requirements, and financial processes.

Who you are

  • Previous experience working with SAP Supply Chain modules.
  • Minimum of two years’ experience in Finance, Supply Chain, Budget Control, Financial Planning, or a related field.
  • Good understanding of financial principles, including cost centre structures and project WBS structures.
  • Knowledge of accounting cost treatment rules and financial governance processes.
  • Good understanding of CAPEX and OPEX cost treatment methodologies.
  • Strong analytical and numerical skills with attention to detail.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Microsoft Excel.
  • Able to build effective working relationships with stakeholders across multiple functions.
  • Educated to degree level, preferably in Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or a related discipline.
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

About the job

Property Finder is the leading property portal in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, dedicated to shaping an inclusive future for real estate while spearheading the region’s growing tech ecosystem. At its core is a clear and powerful purpose: To change living for good in the region.

Founded on the value of great ambitions, Property Finder connects millions of property seekers with thousands of real estate professionals every day. The platform offers a seamless and enriching experience, empowering both buyers and renters to make informed decisions. Since its inception in 2007, Property Finder has evolved into a trusted partner for developers, brokers, and home seekers. As a lighthouse tech company, it continues to create an environment where people can thrive and contribute meaningfully to the transformation of real estate in MENA.

Role Summary

We're looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join the team building the region's leading property marketplace. You'll work across the stack on high-traffic, consumer-facing products, from the Next.js web experience to the services behind it, where latency, reliability and cost efficiency directly shape the experience of millions of buyers, renters and agents.

This is a hands-on full-stack role. You'll build and own features end to end, with senior engineers around you to learn from and grow with.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build, ship and operate features across the web experience and the backend services that power core marketplace experiences
  • Own your features end to end: implementation, testing, deployment, observability, and iteration based on production data
  • Build fast, accessible and SEO-friendly user-facing features in React and Next.js
  • Build new services and features in TypeScript (Node.js), and help support and evolve our existing Go services
  • Work with product managers, designers and mobile engineers to turn requirements into maintainable systems and polished user experiences
  • Take part in operational work: monitoring, alerting, on-call and incident follow-ups
  • Review pull requests, contribute to design discussions, and help improve the team's standards and best practices

Desired Qualifications

  • 4+ years of software engineering experience across frontend and backend, including building production, consumer-facing applications
  • Strong proficiency in TypeScript on both the backend (Node.js) and the frontend
  • Production experience with React and Next.js, including server-side rendering and data fetching
  • Willingness to work in Go.
  • Our existing production services are written in Go, and you'll maintain them alongside new development (prior Go experience is a plus)
  • Experience building and consuming APIs (REST/gRPC) in a microservices environment
  • Hands-on experience with relational databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL), OpenSearch/Elasticsearch or Redis, including debugging and improving query performance
  • Experience owning software in production: understanding how your services behave, diagnosing issues, and improving reliability and performance
  • Solid engineering fundamentals: testing, maintainable code, code quality and pragmatic system design

Nice To Have

  • Production experience with AWS (ECS/EKS, Lambda, SQS/SNS, CloudFront, RDS, ElastiCache or equivalents) and containerised deployments on Kubernetes
  • Experience with event-driven architectures and asynchronous processing (Kafka, SQS or similar)
  • Experience with web performance (Core Web Vitals), SEO, internationalisation or right-to-left (Arabic) layouts
  • Experience integrating AI/LLM-backed features into production services Experience in marketplace, classifieds, or other two-sided platform products

How You Work

  • You take ownership beyond your ticket: you notice problems, suggest fixes and follow through
  • You explain technical trade-offs clearly to engineers and non-technical colleagues, and use data to back up your point of view
  • You give and receive thoughtful code review, and you help keep the team's quality bar high
  • You're pragmatic: you ship the simple thing when that's enough, and you recognise when a problem needs more investment
  • You're curious and keen to grow, and you actively look for feedback on your work

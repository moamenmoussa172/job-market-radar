About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
• 4-5 years of experience in managing Microsoft Dynamics 365 Manufacturing / Advance warehouse management. We are looking for someone who has done production & advance warehouse management in D365

• Must have performed at least three full lifecycle implementations of D365 Manufacturing & AWMS implementations.

• Hands on experience in the AWMS and Production industry with a sound understanding of the value flow within ERP systems.

• Import and Export experience

• Good at client interactions, able to set manage stakeholder expectation.

• Ability to manage requirements/scope and risks appropriately.

• Ability to identify risks, issues, find solution and plan/mitigate/resolve them.

• Good exposure to SCM and Warehouse industry knowledge

• Ability to build the necessary test artefacts to test the functionality of the modules deployed.

• Good presentation and communication skills

• Coordinating with the customer to understand their current as in business process and create process maps.

• Hands-on with creating BRD, FDD, Solution blueprint.

• Breakdown client’s and product business processes, to map out and link any functional and nonfunctional requirements.

• Analyzing and designing solutions for applications, matching processes that fit application requirements seamlessly.

• Documenting the process, informing and preparing clients during critical moments in the project.

• Creating test scenarios to cover the business processes being implemented.

• Training users and explaining complex reasoning behind changes.

• Good understanding ERP Business Processes, industry vertical knowledge –Discrete & Process manufacturing.

• Must have industry experience and understands challenges and risks when implementing vertical applications.

• Played functional roles for 4-5 end to end projects.

• Implementation of Core and advanced Microsoft Dynamics D365 or any other competitor ERP

• Flexible to travel to client location and work out of customer office.

Educational qualification:

B.E/B.Tech/MCA or relevant degree

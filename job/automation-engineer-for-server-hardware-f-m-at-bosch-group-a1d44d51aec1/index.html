About the job

Tasks:

  • Development and managing of PowerShell scripting and automation, the role involves creating automated solutions for routine administrative tasks and developing scripts for system deployment and configuration. Responsibility for maintaining and updating existing automation frameworks, building reporting and dashboards, and implementing robust error handling and logging mechanisms in scripts.
  • Managing and maintaining physical x86 server infrastructure. This includes performing regular health checks and preventive maintenance, diagnosing and resolving hardware-related issues, managing firmware updates and patch management, and coordinating with vendors for hardware support and replacement strategies.
  • Server performance monitoring requires configuring and maintaining monitoring solutions, setting up alerting thresholds and response procedures. This includes analyzing performance metrics and trending data and implementing proactive monitoring solutions to prevent outages.
  • Creating and maintaining detailed documentation of system configurations, including network diagrams, server specifications, and infrastructure dependencies. This encompasses developing and updating standard operating procedures, creating step-by-step guides for complex processes, and maintaining a knowledge base for troubleshooting common issues.
  • Optimization of the Enterprise firmware management strategy, implementation of standardized BIOS configurations across multiple server generations, optimization of hardware performance through detailed configuration of system components (including power management, CPU settings, memory parameters, and storage controllers), while maintaining security compliance.

Profile:

  • Education: A bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Systems Engineering, or related technical field is required
  • Personality and Work Style: Strong communication skills, team spirit, and discretion.
  • Personality and working practices: You are characterized by strong communication skills, teamwork skills, and discretion; loyalty, empathy, and a professional and confident demeanor are among your strengths, as are intercultural competence.
  • Languages: Excellent English skills, written and verbal.

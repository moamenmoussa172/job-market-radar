About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the Team

The ClickPipes team builds the services that power data ingestion into ClickHouse Cloud. As a primary entry point for customers’ data, ClickPipes is critical to product adoption and the company’s growth. We work at petabyte scale, solving challenges in distributed systems, fault tolerance, and high-throughput ingestion. You’ll help make these pipelines performant, reliable, and easy to operate, with ownership of technical decisions and the platform as a whole.

You can find some of the team’s recent work highlighted in our blog:

  • GCP Pub/Sub connector for ClickPipes is now in Private Preview
  • Terraform & OpenAPI for ClickPipes is now Generally Available
  • Unordered mode for GCS ClickPipes is now available

We’re looking for backend engineers with experience in data-intensive systems who enjoy working across technologies. You’ll collaborate with engineering and product partners and drive projects from design through production.

What will you do?

  • Build and evolve integrations with streaming platforms and object storage, including Kafka, Kinesis, Pub/Sub, S3, GCS, and Azure Blob Storage.
  • Continuously improve our systems based on operational metrics, customer feedback, and evolving business requirements.
  • Drive technical discussions and contribute to architectural decisions that impact our platform's scalability and resilience.
  • Participate in on-call rotations to ensure system reliability and respond to production incidents.

About you:

  • 5+ years of software development experience building and operating distributed systems.
  • Strong knowledge of Go and its ecosystem.
  • Experience designing systems for scalability and fault tolerance, with an understanding of concurrency, consistency, and failure recovery.
  • Understanding of cloud-native architecture and practical experience with at least one major cloud provider.
  • Experience diagnosing and resolving production issues in distributed systems.
  • Ability to communicate technical ideas clearly and collaborate across engineering and product teams.
  • Experience with data ingestion pipelines, streaming platforms, or object storage is a plus.

Perks

  • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
  • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
  • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity.
  • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries.
  • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee.
  • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

Culture - We All Shape It

As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture.

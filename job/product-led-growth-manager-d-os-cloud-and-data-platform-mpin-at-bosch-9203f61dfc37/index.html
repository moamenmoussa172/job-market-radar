About the job

What the role owns

Self-serve funnel ownership - Own the end-to-end funnel: discovery, sign-up, install, activation, conversion and expansion. Define the activation event and the metrics for every step, and report them regularly.

Marketplace listings and billing - Own listings and commercial setup on the major cloud marketplaces (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud): public and private offers, metering, billing and drawdown against customer commitments. Work with Finance on invoicing and revenue recognition.

Pricing and packaging - Design free tiers, usage thresholds and paid plans. Keep the free product clearly separate from the enterprise offering so that free usage builds demand and does not erode paid value.

Onboarding and time to value - Design the first-run experience, documentation, sample data and guided set-up that take a new user to a first valuable result quickly, together with engineering and design.

Lifecycle and expansion - Build upgrade triggers, in-product prompts, lifecycle communication and account-expansion plays. Identify where usage signals a team is ready for more modules or for an enterprise conversation.

Analytics and experimentation - Instrument the product, build funnel and cohort dashboards, and run structured experiments on pages, onboarding, pricing and messaging. Make decisions from evidence and share what was learned.

Go-to-market alignment - Work with global marketing and sales on demand generation, partner channels and incentives that reward self-serve conversion. Agree clearly when an account moves from self-serve to a sales-assisted motion.

Product feedback loop - Bring usage data and user feedback into the roadmap. Prioritize the product changes that move activation, conversion and retention.

Community and developer experience - Own the quality of documentation, tutorials and the community presence that technical buyers use to evaluate a product.

Entry strategy and expansion across products - Choose the entry products and customer segments, for example AI spend and cloud cost management, and sequence the next D.OS products into the same self-serve model.

How the role is measured

The role is accountable for business outcomes, not only for product documentation. Effectiveness is assessed on:

  • Number of self-serve sign-ups and activation rate
  • Time to first value
  • Free-to-paid conversion
  • Share of revenue that is self-serve and billed through cloud marketplaces
  • Expansion and net revenue retention of self-serve accounts
  • Software attach on cloud accounts served by MPS
  • Cost to acquire and payback on the self-serve funnel
  • Speed and quality of experiments run

Who we are looking for

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, business or a related field; a master's degree or MBA is an advantage.

Experience

  • 8 to 12+ years in product management, growth or product marketing, with at least 4 years owning a self-serve or product-led growth motion in B2B software, developer tools or cloud infrastructure.
  • Has launched a product on a cloud marketplace (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud) or run a free-to-paid funnel with measurable results.
  • Experience in a thin, early funnel, with little data and a product that is being separated from an enterprise offering, is a strong advantage.

Domain and technical knowledge

  • Product-led growth fundamentals: activation, time to value, conversion, expansion, free tiers and usage-based pricing.
  • Cloud marketplace mechanics: listings, private offers, metering, commit drawdown and co-sell programmes.
  • Familiarity with cloud cost management (FinOps), observability or data platforms, and with the technical buyer who evaluates them.
  • Product analytics and experimentation: funnel and cohort analysis, A/B testing, SQL or equivalent, and tools such as Amplitude, Mixpanel or similar.
  • Lifecycle marketing and in-product messaging, and the basics of SEO and developer content.

Commercial skills

  • Pricing and packaging, unit economics and payback thinking.
  • Ability to align sales, marketing and partners around a motion that changes how people are rewarded.

Personal attributes

  • Curious, analytical and hands-on; comfortable building the first version of a funnel and improving it weekly.
  • Strong written communication, and clarity in explaining a metric or a decision to senior stakeholders.
  • Collaborative influencer who works across engineering, design, marketing, finance and sales without formal authority.
  • Ownership of outcomes, and comfort with setting up the metrics that hold the role itself to account.

Who the role works with

  • Product Area Leader for D.OS (MPS/PAD) and D.OS engineering and security teams
  • Global marketing and global go-to-market leadership
  • Finance (pricing, billing, revenue recognition) and Legal
  • Cloud marketplace and alliance managers at the hyperscalers
  • Channel and technology partners
  • Product owners of other MPS stacks that share the platform

How to read this role

This is a growth and product role with business accountability. It is not a sales role and not a pure marketing role. The ideal candidate has built a self-serve motion from a weak starting point and knows how cloud marketplaces turn usage into revenue.

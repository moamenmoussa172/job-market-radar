Regional Network & Peering Engineer

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Support the operation, monitoring, and maintenance of regional aggregation routers, local WAN edge devices, and interconnect switches across designated domestic hubs, helping ensure stable and reliable regional network connectivity.
  • Assist senior engineers in reviewing and optimizing regional routing policies, including route propagation, local preference, and AS-path prepending, with the objective of keeping regional traffic localized and aligned with defined routing strategies.
  • Perform initial troubleshooting and technical analysis of network incidents affecting domestic interconnected circuits, including link flaps, increased latency, packet loss, routing anomalies, and other connectivity-related issues.
  • Support incident resolution by collecting relevant technical information, performing initial diagnostics,
  • Document findings and escalate complex routing or infrastructure issues to the appropriate engineering teams when required.
  • Maintain accurate network and peering-related documentation, including PeeringDB entries, IPAM records, cabling diagrams, port assignments, and related infrastructure documentation.
  • Ensure network asset and connectivity records remain up to date and aligned with implemented changes, supporting operational transparency, troubleshooting, and effective coordination across technical teams.
  • Contributing to automation development and infrastructure as code (DevOps/IaC).
  • Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Telecommunications, Network Engineering, or a comparable technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • English Language Level: B2/C1
  • 2+ years of relevant professional experience in a similar network management, technical implementation or consulting role
  • CCNP/CCIE level or equivalent.
  • Practical knowledge of IP networking and routing fundamentals, including IPv4/IPv6, subnetting, TCP/IP, VLANs, Ethernet, and network switching concepts.
  • Good understanding of BGP routing concepts, including route propagation, routing policies, local preference, AS paths, and AS-path prepending.
  • Deep knowledge of routing principles and the interaction between regional, WAN, and interconnected network environments.
  • Familiarity with routers, WAN edge devices, switches, interfaces, network links, and interconnection infrastructure.
  • Ability to collect and interpret network diagnostic information, including routing tables, interface statistics, device logs, connectivity tests, and network monitoring information.
  • Knowledge of cisco IOS-XE and NXOS operating systems.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Proactive mindset with a strong focus on automation and DevOps, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.
  • Ability to work independently while maintaining a collaborative team-oriented approach

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Support SAP transformation, implementation, migration, and rollout projects, ensuring alignment with project objectives, global SAP templates, organizational standards, and established delivery methodologies.
  • Coordinate and support SAP initiatives, including migration planning, process harmonization, organizational readiness, and change management activities.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, functional consultants, technical teams, and project management teams throughout the SAP project lifecycle, from discovery and planning through implementation, go-live, and stabilization.
  • Coordinate Fit-to-Standard and Gap Analysis activities, ensuring identified deviations, business requirements, and process harmonization needs are documented, assessed, and addressed by the responsible functional teams.
  • Support global SAP template definition, development, deployment and rollout preparation activities, coordinating local responsibilities such as data cleansing, process alignment, authorization readiness, testing preparation, and end-user training.
  • Apply SAP Activate (or similar) methodology and established project management practices across relevant project phases, including Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy, and Run.
  • Coordinate cross-functional activities, dependencies, deliverables, milestones, risks, and issues to ensure alignment between business, functional, and technical workstreams.
  • Support project planning, progress tracking, status reporting, stakeholder communication, and project governance activities throughout the transformation and rollout lifecycle.
  • Coordinate organizational change management activities, including stakeholder engagement, communication, training readiness, and adoption of standardized SAP processes.
  • Coordinate testing activities, including System Integration Testing (SIT), User Acceptance Testing (UAT), regression testing, defect tracking, and resolution follow-up in collaboration with the responsible functional and technical teams.
  • Support data migration planning and coordination, including migration readiness, data validation activities, dependency tracking, and issue escalation.
  • Contribute to cutover planning, cutover rehearsals, go-live readiness assessments, and coordination of cutover execution activities to ensure controlled system transitions and minimal business disruption.
  • Coordinate hypercare and post-go-live stabilization activities, including issue tracking, escalation management, stakeholder communication, and transition to operational support.
  • Support preparation, review, and formal sign-off of project deliverables, ensuring compliance with applicable quality, security, governance, and audit requirements.
  • Maintain project documentation, status reports, risk and issue logs, action trackers, meeting records, and other relevant project management deliverables.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement, knowledge transfer, standardization of project delivery practices, and evolution of SAP transformation and rollout methodologies.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable organizational procedures, project governance standards, and interdepartmental processes relevant to the assigned responsibilities.
  • Support identification, documentation and validation of functional requirements related to system integration and business processes, in collaboration with relevant stakeholders.
  • Lead workshops with stakeholders to define business requirements and ensure alignment with operational needs.
  • Collaborate with development teams on solution design, configuration, and implementation to meet business requirements and follow industry’s best practices.
  • Fulfils any other tasks related to area of responsibility, as requested by direct superior.
  • Advanced English skills
  • Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in SAP functional consulting, implementation, or rollout projects, including business process analysis, user training, and support activities.
  • Proven experience in end-to-end SAP implementations and rollouts, including involvement in data migration, functional and integration testing, and cutover activities.
  • Hands-on experience in SAP system configuration and customizing, particularly within SAP SD (Sales and Distribution), is mandatory.
  • Strong understanding of end-to-end SAP Order-to-Cash (O2C) process chains and their functional implementation within SAP S/4HANA environments.
  • Advanced functional knowledge of SAP SD, including integration with related SAP modules such as MM, WM/EWM, SAP TM, and FI.
  • Solid knowledge of SAP shipping and logistics execution processes in SAP ECC (R/3) and/or SAP S/4HANA, covering domestic and international/export shipment scenarios.
  • Experience in global and local SAP O2C rollouts, including Fit-to-Standard/Gap Analysis, integration testing, defect analysis and resolution, functional documentation, user training, and cutover support.
  • Experience with SAP Fiori applications relevant to the O2C process area is considered an advantage.
  • Ability to combine comprehensive end-to-end business process knowledge with strong SAP S/4HANA functional consulting, configuration, and integration expertise.
  • Understanding the integration between shipping execution, Post Goods Issue (PGI), Advanced Shipping and Receiving (ASR), and subsequent billing processes is considered an advantage.
  • Strong ability to analyze local business processes, assess deviations from standardized global SAP templates, and identify opportunities for process harmonization and standardization.
  • Familiarity with SAP Activate methodology and tools such as SAP Signavio, SAP Solution Manager, Jira, or equivalent solutions for business process modeling, documentation, testing, and change management.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex business and logistics requirements into clear functional and technical specifications.
  • Excellent time management, organizational, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively prioritize tasks and manage competing project demands.
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams and independently with minimal supervision.
  • Experience working in Agile project environments and/or holding a recognized Agile certification is considered an advantage.
  • Willingness and flexibility to travel internationally based on project requirements

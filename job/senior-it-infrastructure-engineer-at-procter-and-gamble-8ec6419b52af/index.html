Job Location

WARSAW DOWNTOWN OFFICE

Job Description

Located at Warsaw within CF-IT's TOP organization, this Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer role offers the opportunity to drive best practices, adoption, standardization, optimization, and automation of hardware and software. You will be part of the design team to drive a multi-year and multi-vendor infrastructure roadmap. Ideal candidates will also have hands on experience to build and implement infrastructure solutions. Additionally, you'll have the opportunity to conceptualize and understand abstract ideas, to then translate them into concrete hardware/software builds and installs.

As a Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer, you'll be responsible for designing, building, and supporting scalable hyperconverged infrastructure solutions and platforms to support P&G's key business operations. You will analyze current systems to identify automation opportunities, create, test, deploy, and monitor automation scripts. You will also drive investigation and resolution of Infrastructure and security incidents.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Own end-to-end large-scale applications, products, services, projects, or foundational technologies.
  • Shape frameworks of work and define the evolution of their area of scope, ensuring operational excellence and scalability.
  • Influence technology decisions and lead teams, engaging in detailed, hands-on work as required.
  • Demonstrate breadth of applied IT skills across at least two IT job profiles and apply Integrated Risk Management, IT Operations, IT Project Management, and Enterprise Architecture skills relevant to the work.
  • Requires at least two relevant roles and experience managing or influencing people or organizations to achieve significant IT outcomes.
  • Define strategy and lead the delivery and transformation of business outcomes through IT within major sub-OU services, products, or programs.
  • Actively participate in developing others in the function, with completion of Leadership Strength Assessment (LSA) and a sustainable career journey ahead.

Job Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.
  • A minimum of 5 years' experience in a similar role.
  • Demonstrated experience with Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) and Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS).
  • Familiarity with Linux/UNIX and Windows server administration.
  • Strong understanding of networking principles.
  • Experience with database management is a plus.
  • Familiarity with IT governance standards and compliance including ITIL, PCI, and SOC.
  • Experience / understanding of OT systems in a manufacturing environment (PLCs, HMI, MES, Ethernet/IP networks, Historians, etc.).
  • 1+ years general of manufacturing experience/knowledge.
  • Veeam Certification: VMCE+.
  • Experience with other VMware Products: VCF Operations, VCF Automation, NSX, Tanzu/VKS.
  • Experience with other hypervisors and Cloud platforms: Hyper-V, Nutanix, other/Google, Azure, AWS).

Preferred:

  • 10+ years of experience with Veeam Backup & Replication.
  • 3+ years of experience with Database infrastructure, preferred: Microsoft SQL, Oracle Database.
  • 3+ years of experience with hardware systems, preferred: Dell PowerScale/Isilon, Dell PowerEdge.
  • 2+ Years VMware vSphere with VSAN Experience.
  • Intermediate Enterprise Networking (Cisco & SDN).
  • Understanding and practice of Agile/DevOps.
  • Experience with Automating infrastructure builds / configuration management and vSphere Lifecycle management.
  • Cloud Knowledge (Azure /AWS).
  • Master’s degree in Computer Science, Informatics, Engineering.

About the job

Job Title

Senior IT Specialist (HLC)

Job Description

Reports to: Group Leader

Location: London - Heathrow Airpor, Terminal 5

Contract Type: Permanent

Introduction

The Vanderlande IT Support team is responsible for maintenance and optimization of the baggage handling system (High Level Controls). You will be located at Heathrow working 5 days a week and on call together with wider support teams to make a positive difference for our customers in a challenging environment.

Roles and Responsibilities

You will work closely with other suppliers to deliver an effective process to maintain multiple platforms and business critical systems with vulnerability management, patching (co-ordination through test facility) and configurations. There will be regular reporting to numerous stakeholders and collaboration with other business partners to deliver a highly visible response.

  • Part of service team organisation to deliver Baggage Handling System (BHS) support and patch management / updates
  • Liaison with project teams and architects to understand roadmaps for managing obsolescence
  • Perform threat monitoring using customer tools as directed
  • Ideally, technically skilled with SUSE Linux, Oracle and Microsoft Operating Systems & HP hardware.
  • Coordinate with supplier organisations at the customer site and internal Vanderlande departments for scheduling updates.
  • Will be responsible for Incident Management (working in a team) and contributing to the IT & OT teams’ response and escalation within Vanderlande, as appropriate.
  • Provide Vulnerability Management on Baggage Systems (OT and IT) including tools to provide compliancy for our customer (following customer policies).
  • Involvement with cyber teams on the customer site in daily operational meetings and topic discussions and forums to influence best practices on site.
  • Actively contribute to customer IT policies and ensuring the details are shared with the Vanderlande user community / stakeholders for compliance.
  • Represent Vanderlande when engaging with our customer and other suppliers to deliver a highly visible IT Specialist Baggage System support service.
  • Assure access control using the Heathrow applications / processes. Preparing access request information before submission to HAL IT for approval.
  • Produce detailed technical reports about security incidents and produce monthly performance reporting

Role Qualification and Skills

  • Linux admin experience (ideally SUSE)
  • Microsoft Server operating system experience essential
  • Experience with application testing
  • Familiar with working on a VMware environment
  • Experience with Cyber activities of compliance (OS patching and application updates)
  • Backup tooling experience – Idera and Veeam desirable
  • Awareness of Cyber topics – vulnerabilities, patching practices, security methodology

Experience of Baggage Handling / Airport Systems will be beneficial but not essential as training will be provided

  • A challenging work environment with lots of opportunities of career progression.
  • Cycle to work scheme
  • Yellow Nest is a salary exchange scheme that reduces childcare costs for parents and employers
  • Pension with Aviva
  • Access to Achievers an award-winning recognition platform that inspires to recognise your coworkers Where points are awarded that can be exchanged for a range of goods and discounts.

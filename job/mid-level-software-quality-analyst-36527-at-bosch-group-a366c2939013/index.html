About the job

Manage the entire requirements lifecycle, from elicitation and analysis to documentation and validation with stakeholders.

- Lead the testing process, including strategic planning, test case design, manual test execution, and end-to-end defect lifecycle management.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree in Systems Analysis, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Experience in Quality Assurance (QA), with a focus on software testing.
  • Familiarity with Agile methodologies.
  • Experience with test management tools (e.g., HP ALM, Jira, Azure DevOps).
  • Experience in creating and executing manual test cases, test scripts, and test plans.
  • Ability to gather, analyze, and document requirements clearly and concisely.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills to interact with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Ability to assess requirements for testability and completeness, identifying gaps and inconsistencies and proposing solutions.
  • Ability to handle complex requirements and understand how different parts of a system interact and impact one another.
  • Ability to work in a dynamic and collaborative environment, managing multiple stakeholders.
  • Intermediate English proficiency.

Desirable Qualifications

  • Software Testing Certification (ISTQB).
  • Knowledge of SAP and/or Brazilian localization requirements.
  • Ability to work with data and reports to validate requirements and test results.

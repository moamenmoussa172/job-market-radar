About the job

Your contribution to something big:

As a ServiceNow Delivery & Implementation Lead, you will drive ServiceNow implementation and transformation initiatives while taking operational ownership of our HR platform. You will lead cross-functional teams, align business and IT stakeholders, and ensure successful delivery of scalable digital solutions.

Your responsibilities:

  • Lead ServiceNow implementation, transformation and rollout projects end-to-end, from initiation through go-live and stabilization.
  • Take accountability for scope, timeline, quality, resources, budget and project deliverables.
  • Define and manage project plans, milestones, governance, dependencies, risks and escalation measures.
  • Lead cross-functional teams across business, IT, ServiceNow experts, developers and external implementation partners.
  • Manage senior stakeholders and ensure transparent status reporting, decision preparation and steering committee communication.
  • Translate business and process requirements into executable ServiceNow implementation plans and drive standardization and out-of-the-box solutions.
  • Coordinate requirements, solution design, configuration/development, integrations, data migration, testing, UAT and deployment.
  • Ensure compliance with relevant security, data protection, governance and works council requirements.
  • Drive change management, communication, training and user adoption throughout the implementation.
  • Ensure structured transition into operations, including documentation, ownership, support setup and continuous improvement.
  • Take operational ownership of the HR platform, ensuring smooth day-to-day Maintenance and Support, including Incident Management, Demand Management and Release Management, while coordinating priorities and continuous improvement.

What distinguishes you:

  • Education
  • Degree in IT, Business Informatics, Business Administration or a comparable field, or equivalent professional experience.
  • Experience
  • Experience leading complex IT, digital transformation or enterprise platform implementation projects.
  • Experience coordinating cross-functional teams and external partners.
  • Experience managing senior stakeholders and international projects.
  • Know-how
  • Good understanding of ServiceNow and enterprise workflow solutions.
  • Knowledge of IT integration, business processes and digital transformation.
  • Experience with Agile and traditional project management methodologies.
  • Familiarity with tools such as Jira, Confluence or Azure DevOps.
  • Understanding of project governance, risk management and change management.
  • HR platform or service management experience is an advantage.
  • Languages
  • Excellent English skills, both written and spoken.
  • Working Style and Methods
  • Structured, proactive and solution-oriented approach.
  • Strong organizational and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to manage competing priorities and drive results.
  • Personality
  • Collaborative mindset and intercultural awareness.
  • Strong sense of ownership and accountability.
  • Ability to influence and lead teams without direct disciplinary authority.

Job Description Summary

Help turn the patient voice into evidence that can shape better development decisions. As Senior Manager, Patient Experience Data Science, you’ll bring scientific and methodological expertise to the generation of patient experience data, including patient preference studies, while helping strengthen how published information is delivered across research and development programmes. Working across Patient Engagement and global project teams, you’ll apply robust methodologies to generate impactful evidence and help embed patient-centricity into new drug development programs.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

· Lead patient experience data science activities, including patient preference studies, across research and development programmes.

· Provide methodological leadership for literature reviews, ensuring scientifically robust, high-quality and timely outputs.

· Collaborate with Patient Engagement scientists and global project teams to strengthen patient experience evidence generation.

· Guide and mentor literature-review researchers, supporting consistent methodological standards and quality.

· Oversee progress and delivery of literature-review activities, ensuring adherence to relevant procedures.

· Evaluate fit-for-purpose artificial intelligence approaches for evidence identification and literature reviews with appropriate scientific oversight.

· Drive continuous improvement in literature-review quality, methodologies and processes through cross-functional collaboration.

· Contribute to innovative patient-focused initiatives, including benefit-risk assessment and Patient-Focused Drug Development.

Ideal Background

· Master’s degree in health economics, epidemiology, public health, statistics, health services research or a comparable discipline, with approximately five or more years of relevant experience; or a doctorate with approximately three or more years of relevant experience.

· Demonstrated experience in literature-review methodology, including desk research, targeted and systematic reviews, critical appraisal and evidence synthesis.

· Demonstrated experience in patient experience data science and patient preference studies.

· Experience contributing to the design, conduct, analysis, interpretation and application of patient preference studies.

· Knowledge of relevant literature-review guidelines and frameworks, with the ability to assess and implement new methodological approaches.

· Experience providing methodological or matrix leadership, guidance and mentorship to scientific colleagues.

· Ability to manage multiple projects, prioritise resources and deliver high-quality outputs within agreed timelines.

· Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with an innovative, continuous-improvement mindset and the ability to engage scientific and business stakeholders.

· Fluent English, both written and spoken.

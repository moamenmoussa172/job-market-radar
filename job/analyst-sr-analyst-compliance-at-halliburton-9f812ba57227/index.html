About the Role

Halliburton is seeking a Senior Compliance Investigator to actively handle a broad range of Code of Business Conduct (COBC) investigations, including internal investigations of fraud, theft, conflicts of interest, policy violations, financial wrongdoing, and alleged violations of U.S. and international laws. The Senior Compliance Investigator will also be responsible for programmatic initiatives including compliance training and global risk assessments.

Responsibilities

  • Lead internal investigations of alleged violations of the COBC, as well as U.S. and international laws, including creating investigation plans, analyzing potential evidence, managing data and asset collections, conducting interviews, preparing investigation reports, and presenting investigative results to management.
  • Supervise and collaborate with compliance professionals in triaging allegation reports, assigning matters for investigation, and managing closure and documentation processes.
  • Supervise and collaborate with other departments, such as Human Resources and Corporate Security, conducting COBC investigations under the direction of the Law Department.
  • Collaborate with key stakeholders to implement corrective action and enhanced internal controls.
  • Prepare documentation in response to subpoenas, government inquiries, and other internal investigations.
  • Conduct compliance audits globally to ensure continued compliance with the Company’s policies and business practices.
  • Plan, coordinate, and execute global initiatives to assist in managing compliance risks. Support training initiatives.
  • Track national and international developments in investigation best practices to update or modify the Company’s investigation process.
  • 20% travel expectation.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university.
  • Minimum 10 years of experience independently conducting internal investigations for an international company, law enforcement unit, law firm, or accounting firm.
  • Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and high degree of organizational skills
  • Ability to multi-task and handle a high-volume caseload
  • Exceptional written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills
  • Team player, with the ability to work independently, collaboratively with others, and to lead group projects

Preferred Qualifications

  • Multilingual candidates, specifically Arabic skills, will receive enhanced consideration
  • Experience providing investigation summaries and recommendations to business leadership
  • Experience working at a multinational company handling investigations across various jurisdictions in the Eastern Hemisphere
  • Working knowledge of investigative tools and enterprise resource planning software

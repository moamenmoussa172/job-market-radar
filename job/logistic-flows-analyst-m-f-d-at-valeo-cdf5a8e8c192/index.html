About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Valeo is an automotive supplier and a partner to all automakers worldwide. As a technology company, Valeo proposes innovative products and systems that contribute to the reduction of CO2 emissions and to the development of intuitive driving.

Valeo is an independent industrial Group fully focused on the design, production and sale of components, integrated systems and modules for the automotive industry.

Valeo Service Eastern Europe is the automotive aftermarket commercial and service company operating in the 12 countries of Central and Eastern Europe belonging to the Valeo Group. It offers tens of thousands product items adapted to the profile of a car park in a given country. The company has its headquarter in Warsaw and a warehouse, training and technical support center Tech'Care in Stryków near Łódź, employing in total ca. 100 people.


Currently for our entity Valeo Service Eastern Europe in Warszawa we recruit Logistic Flows Analyst

YOUR CHALLENGES:

  • Lead the regional S&OP process: consolidate multi-source data, analyze deviations, identify risks and opportunities, present monthly reviews
  • Tracking and analyzing key performance indicators (KPIs) of the supply chain and proposing improvement measures
  • Analyze and control logistics operational costs (Dicos): 3PL storage, packaging, pallets, inbound/outbound freight
  • Performance reviews with logistics service providers - transportation, 3PL
  • Identify risks regarding goods avaiability, overstock, capacity (Warehouse storrage, WH production capacity)
  • Manage and continuously review planning parameters and MRP statuses, challenging and optimizing them to ensure effective supply planning
  • Play an active role in budgeting and planning, preparing and validating input data and assumptions to support accurate planning
  • Coordination of optimization procejts in collaboration with local and central teams
  • Close colaboration with 3pl, Warehouse, Procurenemt, Demand Fcsting, Sales, Controilling

JOIN OUR TEAM IF YOU HAVE:

  • Bachelor's / Master’s degree (preference for students of Mathematics, Statistics, Econometrics, or related fields)
  • english
  • SAP experience - MM/SD
  • experience in Planning, Logistics, Data analyze 3-5 years
  • fluent in Excel, VBA/programiing experience nice to have
  • Proactiveness and problem solving approach

WE CAN OFFER YOU:

  • Full-time employment based on an employment contract in a stable, global company.
  • Private healthcare (Medicover), group life insurance, a pension plan, and a flexible cafeteria benefits system, including subsidies for sports activities, cultural events and purchases at selected stores.
  • 2 days of Home Office/week possibility

Valeo works to promote diversity in all its forms (gender, disability, social and cultural, generational) through local policies and actions, mentoring and training programs, networks and partnerships.

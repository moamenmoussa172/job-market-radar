[SX] SAP PP Senior Consultant

Bosch Group · Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, Vietnam

Onsite · Senior · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 12 November 2026

SAP S/4HANA

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Lead requirement analysis and Fit-to-Standard workshops for production planning and manufacturing processes.
  • Design and configure SAP PP solutions in SAP S/4HANA On-premise & Cloud Public Edition.
  • Translate business requirements into scalable SAP standard solutions while minimizing unnecessary customization.
  • Challenge non-standard requirements and recommend standard SAP best practices.
  • Own functional design from requirement through configuration, testing, deployment, and hypercare.
  • Roll-out Production Planning template for customers.
  • Implement new business change request based on current solutions.
  • Manage and monitor the deliverables in projects.
  • Consult and make sure daily operation run smoothly and effectively.
  • Analyze customer requirements, identify gaps and solutions to these gaps.
  • Perform system configuration, functional test, SIT, UAT support, go-live and post go-live support
  • Create functional specification and work with technical team for change requests
  • Cooperate with central team for the new development and rollout

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Supply Chain, or related field.
  • Minimum of 5 years of SAP PP / Production Planning, including 1+ year in SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud.
  • Strong understanding of discrete and/or repetitive manufacturing processes.
  • Hands-on experience with Fiori apps, Manage Your Solution, and cloud extensibility tools.
  • Experience with SAP Activate Methodology and Fit-to-Standard workshops.
  • Knowledge of MRP Live, production order management, and capacity evaluation in S/4HANA Cloud.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work in cross-functional environments.
  • SAP PP or S/4HANA Manufacturing Public Cloud Certification is a plus.

Preferred Skills:

  • Experience with integration to MES systems or Shop Floor Control solutions.
  • Experience covering implementation phases such as Explore, Realize, Test, Deploy and Hypercare.
  • Strong understanding of SAP PP integration with MM, SD, CO and related manufacturing processes.
  • Experience in manufacturing transformation programs.
  • Knowledge of SAP BTP and SAP Integration Suite is an advantage.
  • Experience across multi-country or multi-plant implementations.
  • Experience supporting migration from SAP ECC or legacy ERP systems to S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition.
  • Strong understanding of SAP Activate methodology.
  • Understanding of Quality Management (QM) or Warehouse Management (EWM) in the cloud context.

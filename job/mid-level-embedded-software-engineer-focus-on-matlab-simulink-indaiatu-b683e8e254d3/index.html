About The Role

As a Software Engineer at John Deere, based at our Research & Development Center in Indaiatuba, SP, you will contribute to the development, execution, and continuous improvement of automated test solutions for embedded software systems. Working closely with your manager and cross-functional teams, you will help ensure software quality, reduce manual testing efforts, and accelerate feedback cycles for development teams.

Main Responsabilities

  • Design, implement, and maintain automated test scripts for embedded software platforms.
  • Drive improvements in test coverage, execution efficiency, and defect detection.
  • Collaborate with developers, systems engineers, and validation teams to define test strategies and acceptance criteria.
  • Execute tests in SIL, HIL, MIL and/or VIL environments and analyze results to identify defects.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to test framework enhancements.
  • Document test plans and Test Cases, procedures, and results in compliance with internal quality standards based on Requirements
  • Support continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) pipelines with automated test integration.
  • Mentor junior engineers and interns on automation best practices and tools.

What Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree in software engineering, Electronic Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering or related field.
  • Experience developing, testing, and debugging software using MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Experience with Git
  • Advanced/Fluent English: broad vocabulary, good grammar, conducts meetings, argues, negotiates and provides training.
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
  • Ability to lead initiatives and coach junior engineers and interns.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with ECU-Test or other Test Automation tools.
  • Knowledge of Transmission Systems.
  • Experience programming using different programming languages such as C, C++, Python.
  • Understanding of CAN/Ethernet protocols.
  • Knowledge of RTOS.
  • Knowledge on Code Best Practices and Code Standards.
  • Experience in Agroindustry and/or Automotive Industry.
  • AI applied to Software Engineering experience.
  • ISTQB certification or equivalent.
  • Basics Knowledge on J1939 and/or UDS.
  • Experience in BUGs classification, analysis and fixing.
  • Agile/Scrum methodology familiarity.

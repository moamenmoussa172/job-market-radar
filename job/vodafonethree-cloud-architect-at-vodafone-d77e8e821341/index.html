What you’ll do
You’ll shape the cloud foundations of the UK Digital Domain, turning complex needs into simple, secure and cost-effective platforms. You’ll combine technical direction with collaborative problem-solving, helping teams deliver reliable services while influencing how the domain’s cloud architecture evolves.

  • You’ll create a two-year cloud roadmap covering adoption, application design, management and monitoring, aligned with the domain’s target architecture.
  • You’ll guide infrastructure design and deployment, balancing standardisation, consolidation, total cost of ownership, security, regulatory compliance and quality.
  • You’ll lead supportive design reviews throughout the software development lifecycle and sign off low-level designs, migrations, implementation plans, testing approaches and operational handovers.
  • You’ll constructively challenge requirements, balance functional and non-functional needs, and make the most of out-of-the-box capabilities, designing infrastructure to remain fit for purpose for at least 18 months.
  • You’ll help early-stage projects move forward with rough-order-of-magnitude cost and timescale estimates.
  • You’ll explore emerging technologies and market trends, assessing where they could make a practical difference.
  • You’ll share your expertise through the Architecture Guild, coaching colleagues and contributing to shared learning and technical craftsmanship.
  • You’ll build trusted relationships with external partners and Group and local technology leads, working alongside architects, engineers, Operations, Security and delivery teams.

Who you are

  • You’ll bring cloud infrastructure architecture experience across multiple teams or a whole domain, including landing zones, hybrid connectivity, managed services, resilience and disaster recovery.
  • You’ll apply sound security judgement to infrastructure design, drawing on IAM, SSO, SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, segmentation and risk acceptance.
  • You’ll bring experience reviewing and signing off technical designs, helping teams make clear, well-reasoned architecture decisions.
  • You’ll draw on in-depth web architecture knowledge, including CDNs, caching, edge protection, microservices and RESTful APIs.
  • You’ll set technical direction while listening to different perspectives, influencing without direct authority and communicating clearly with technical colleagues and senior stakeholders.
  • You’ll bring experience in a large corporate environment, supported by SAFe certification or extensive experience with scaled Scrum or Kanban.
  • You’ll have opportunities to draw on any additional experience in telecoms, multiple cloud platforms, mentoring or architecture communities. Familiarity with Terraform, CloudFormation, containers, Kubernetes and FinOps will also be welcome, rather than essential.
  • You’ll be considered for the architectural scope and judgement you demonstrate—not whether your job title includes “architect”. If your background is in cloud, DevOps or platform engineering, you’ll be welcome to apply.

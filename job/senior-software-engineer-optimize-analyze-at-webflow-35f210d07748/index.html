About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Argentina.

Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for modern marketing teams, helping organizations build, manage, and optimize high-performing web experiences that drive predictable growth and strengthen brand trust. Building at Webflow will need grit, because we move fast, without ever sacrificing craft or quality.

We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer, Optimize/Analyze to help develop and scale Webflow Optimize products.

You’ll design high-performance systems that adapt user experiences in real time, working with large-scale data processing and distributed computing to lift conversion and personalization at scale. Our team is fast-moving, data-driven, and experienced in personalization, ML infrastructure, and large-scale analytics.

The next chapter is ambitious. We’re serving some of the world’s largest enterprises and rethinking what an AI-native conversion platform can be. This is a cross-team technical role: you’ll work across a large, revenue-generating system, help decide what it needs to become, and partner directly with Engineering, Product, and Design to ship it.

About the role:

  • Location: Remote-first (Argentina)
  • Permanent, full-time employee (this is not a contractor position)
  • Application Information:
  • Application deadline: applications accepted on an ongoing basis until position is closed and filled
  • This posting is for a new position.

As a Senior Software Engineer on the Optimize team, you'll:

  • Architect, design, and build scalable multi-tenant backend services and APIs for our Optimize product, including our MCP server.
  • Build with MongoDB, Druid, Airflow, AWS (EC2, S3), Python, and TypeScript/Node.js.
  • Define clean service and API boundaries, and design and optimize the MongoDB data models and queries that power recommendations.
  • Improve scalability and reliability through distributed architecture, caching strategies, and event-driven systems tuned for low latency.
  • Lead projects from design through launch that move team and engineering org goals.
  • Write and review maintainable, tested, performant code, and raise the quality bar for the team.
  • Author, collaborate on, and evaluate design documents.
  • Use AI tools in your daily work, and help the team build reliable systems on top of non-deterministic, LLM-backed components.
  • Partner with product engineers, PMs, and designers to ship end to end.

About you

Requirements:

  • BA/BS degree or equivalent experience
  • Business-level fluency to read, write and speak in English

You’ll thrive as a Senior Software Engineer if you:

  • 5+ years building and shipping production backend services, with a track record of performant, high-quality code.
  • Depth in distributed systems: concurrency, asynchronous and eventually consistent processing, queues or streaming, and designing for failure.
  • Strong Python and/or TypeScript/Node.js, plus data modeling experience in MongoDB or a comparable database.
  • Experience with real-time analytics, event-driven architectures, and REST APIs.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS, containers, CI/CD, and observability, and comfort owning infrastructure.
  • Strong production debugging skills across services and layers of the stack.
  • A habit of valuing testing, documentation, and reliability as much as shipping, and communicating trade-offs clearly.
  • Curiosity and a proactive embrace of AI tools to work faster and expand the team’s impact.

It would be a bonus if you had even one of the following:

  • Experience with Apache Spark, Airflow, or TensorFlow.
  • Familiarity with Kafka or other event streaming platforms.
  • Prior experience working on large-scale personalization, experimentation, or recommendation systems.

Our Core Behaviors

  • Build lasting customer trust. We build trust by taking action that puts customer trust first.

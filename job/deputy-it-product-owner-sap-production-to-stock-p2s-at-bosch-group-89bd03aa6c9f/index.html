About the job

We are looking for an experienced SAP P2S Senior Consultant for the deputy IT PO role. The role will lead the P2S team and be responsible for delivering P2S continuous development business requirements for SAP R/3 & S4 HANA. It requires strong Production execution domain knowledge, proven experience in both SAP R/3 and S4 technologies, expertise in repetitive manufacturing, interfaces – SOAP, OData, Idoc, RFC. process.

Roles & Responsibility :

  • You are leading a team of experienced IT consultants and developers and actively develop the team to work in an agile organization.
  • Furthermore, you actively participate in the process design, keep focus on technical aspects, drive re-usability and the usage of SAP Standard.
  • Cross-functional integration with SAP PP- WM/EWM, ePPDS, QM, SD.
  • You will consult User Department and Governance Functions in all P2S related questions and align dependencies with adjoining process areas.
  • You will conduct the Product Lifecycle Management for your process chain, incl. Design & Build, Rollout, Support & Maintenance.
  • Additionally, you are an active part of the development team, identifying dependencies or constraints and prioritizing the backlog refinement
  • Collaborating with other IT Product Development Teams and Chief Product Owners to identify synergies and drive standardization will be one of your tasks.
  • You address cross-team prioritization conflicts and drive resolution with other Product Owners.
  • As a deputy product owner you define the team set-up within E2E process, identify required competencies and ensure staffing.

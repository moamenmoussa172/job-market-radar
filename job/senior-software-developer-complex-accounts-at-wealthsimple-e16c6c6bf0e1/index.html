About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Build something people love

Wealthsimple is Canada’s leading financial innovator. The company offers a full suite of simple, sophisticated financial products across managed investing, do-it-yourself trading, cryptocurrency, tax filing, spending and saving. Wealthsimple currently serves more than 4 million Canadians and holds over $169 billion in assets under administration. The company was founded in 2014 by a team of financial experts and technology entrepreneurs, and is headquartered in Toronto, Canada.

We're proud of what we've built — and we're just getting started. Read our Culture Manual and learn more about how we work.

About the team

The Complex Accounts team builds the accounts that don't fit a standard mould. We own how businesses, from sole proprietorships to corporations with layered ownership, open and manage their accounts with Wealthsimple, along with joint accounts and registered education savings plans (RESPs). As we expand into more account types, the list keeps growing. It's work where accuracy, security, and regulatory requirements are non-negotiable, and we partner closely with operations, compliance, design, and product to make complicated financial structures feel simple for the people using them. We’re part of Data & Engineering, where quality, security, and scalability are the standard, not trade-offs.

In this role, you'll have the opportunity to:

  • Build and evolve the systems that let businesses, joint owners, and families open and manage accounts with Wealthsimple
  • Design data models for complex ownership — corporations, co-owners, and the people behind them
  • Work with compliance and operations partners to turn regulatory requirements into reliable, well-tested software
  • Improve the account opening experience across mobile and web, removing friction wherever clients get stuck
  • Build tools that help our operations teams serve clients faster and handle unusual cases on their own
  • Share on-call responsibilities and fix the root causes of recurring issues
  • Use AI tools as a core part of how you build, and help shape how the team works with them
  • Raise the technical bar through design reviews, code review, documentation, and mentorship

Skills you bring:

  • 5+ years of professional software development, with depth in back-end systems and a track record of shipping production systems that clients and teams depend on
  • Full-stack experience: you’re as comfortable contributing to a client-facing flow on web or mobile as you are to the services running underneath it
  • Experience modelling complex domains and designing systems that handle sensitive data, including authentication, fine-grained authorization, and auditability
  • A track record of shipping in a regulated domain alongside compliance or operations partners
  • An AI-first development workflow that doesn’t compromise on rigour: you use agentic tools day to day and know how to verify their output before it reaches production
  • Clear communication: you can explain a technical trade-off to engineers, compliance partners, and leadership alike

Nice-to-have's:

  • Experience with Ruby on Rails, React / React Native, or TypeScript
  • Familiarity with KYC/KYB processes, including business verification and beneficial ownership
  • Knowledge of Canadian business structures (corporations, trusts, partnerships) or registered accounts like RESPs
  • Experience at a fintech or financial services company

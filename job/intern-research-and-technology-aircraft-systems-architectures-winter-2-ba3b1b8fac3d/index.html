About the job

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • evaluate PLM data interoperability needs and standards
  • devise a schema to integrate process and need-specific cross-disciplinary vendor and in-house tooling
  • evaluate available data interoperability solution frameworks and perform a gap analysis
  • initiation, development and testing of APIs to support integration of:
    SysML v2 data using REST APIs, vendor and in-house design tooling, centralized PLM repository.
  • investigate model query schemas employing native AI tools and protocols (e.g. Copilot, MCP)

How to thrive in this role?

  • familiarity with SE, MBSE, Simulation, PLM concepts and tools
  • experience in integrating software tools across API implementations
  • awareness of challenges and means to best implement cross-tool interoperability schemes and implementations
  • awareness of with preferrable exposure and implementation experience, to data exchange frameworks, protocols and formats (e.g DoDAF, MOSA, OSLC, REST APIs, STEP, ReqIF, JSON, XML) supporting object-based interoperability
  • experience in software development workflows (version control, debugging, configuration management) using Python, C++, Java, Git

  • Duration: 8 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.

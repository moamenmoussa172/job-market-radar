About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and maintain test strategies for AI-powered products, APIs, and web applications.
  • Build and improve automation frameworks using Python.
  • Develop UI automation using Selenium and/or Playwright.
  • Create and maintain automated API and end-to-end test coverage.
  • Integrate automated tests into GitHub Actions pipelines.
  • Support test execution on GCP instances.
  • Validate releases across DEV, ACP, and PROD environments.
  • Evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, reliability, context adherence, and business usefulness.
  • Partner with product, engineering, and stakeholders to ensure quality is measured against real business needs.
  • Contribute to shift-left quality practices, release readiness, and continuous improvement.

Professional Experience & Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electronics, Mechatronics, Communications, or a related field.
  • 3+ years of experience in software testing, test automation, or systems testing.
  • Strong Python scripting and automation skills.
  • Hands-on experience with Selenium and/or Playwright.
  • Solid background in API testing and automated regression.
  • Experience with GitHub Actions or similar CI/CD tools.
  • Familiarity with cloud-based testing, preferably on GCP.
  • Understanding of AI/GenAI concepts, LLM behavior, prompt testing, or RAG is a strong plus.
  • Ability to think beyond acceptance criteria and evaluate product quality from a business perspective.

Nice to Have

  • Experience testing chatbot, or workflow applications.
  • Exposure to AI evaluation methods or LLM benchmarking.
  • Experience with DEV / ACP / PROD release flows.
  • ISTQB certification or equivalent quality training.
  • Experience testing SDKs, orchestration layers, or cloud-native applications.

Why Join Us

  • Work on advanced AI products with real business impact.
  • Help shape the quality strategy of a growing AI platform.
  • Work in a modern engineering setup with Python, GitHub Actions, and GCP.
  • Join a team that values ownership, curiosity, and quality thinking.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Build Products That Shape the Future of Healthcare Network Compliance

At Quest Analytics, we are helping health plans and regulators navigate some of healthcare’s most complex challenges, from network adequacy and provider access to regulatory compliance and network performance.

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager – Regulatory who wants to do more than manage an existing roadmap. This is an opportunity to identify unmet market needs, build new solutions, and help shape the future of our regulatory product portfolio.

You’ll own product strategy from discovery through launch and optimization, working directly with regulator customers and users to understand their challenges and translate them into scalable SaaS solutions. You’ll partner closely with Engineering, Client Success, Sales, Product Marketing, Analytics, and other Product leaders to turn ideas into products that deliver measurable customer and business value.

We’re also building an AI-forward Product organization. We want someone who is curious about what’s next and actively uses AI and emerging technology to rethink how products are researched, designed, built, tested, and brought to market.

Own Product Strategy & Build What’s Next

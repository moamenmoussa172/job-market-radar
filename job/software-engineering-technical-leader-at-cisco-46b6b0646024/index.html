About the job

Join us as we pursue our innovative vision to make machine data accessible, usable and valuable to everyone. We are a company filled with people who are passionate about our product and seek to deliver the best experience for our customers. At Splunk, we’re committed to our work, our customers, having fun and most meaningfully, to each other’s success.

Would you like the opportunity to work at a growing company that is changing the way that information is used to support business decisions? If this resonates with you, we would love to speak with you.

Your Impact

Our AI Data Management Team builds and operates AI-powered services that help customers onboard data and connect complex workflows across diverse systems. We develop shared agents and reusable skills, and work with partner teams to make these capabilities reliable, secure, and useful across the platform.

We are looking for a Software Engineering Technical Leader to shape the technical direction of these services and help turn product and customer needs into engineering priorities. You will lead major technical initiatives across teams, guide architectural decisions, and set the standard for how we design, deliver, and operate production software. You will work across software engineering, product management, security, and operations, while mentoring senior engineers and influencing technical outcomes without direct people-management responsibility.

This role combines broad technical judgment with a strong understanding of software delivery and production systems. You will help the team build AI-powered capabilities that customers can depend on, including services that use large language models, agentic architectures, and retrieval-augmented generation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Translate product strategy, customer needs, and organizational priorities into a clear technical vision and engineering priorities for AI Data Management services.
  • Lead the architecture and technical direction of large initiatives that span services or teams, making system-level trade-offs across security, scalability, reliability, performance, and cost.
  • Guide major features from technical design through delivery, aligning partner teams on scope, dependencies, milestones, risks, and outcomes.
  • Set and promote strong engineering practices across the team and partner teams, including design and code reviews, automated testing, secure development, documentation, and maintainable service design.
  • Influence the development and operation of AI-powered services, including shared agents and reusable skills, with appropriate attention to service quality, observability, and customer impact.
  • Define and use product and service metrics to assess reliability, adoption, and customer value, and use findings to guide technical and product decisions.
  • Strengthen production readiness and operations by improving monitoring, incident response, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement practices.
  • Serve as a technical escalation point for complex cross-service or production issues, coordinating investigation and sustainable resolution.
  • Mentor and influence senior engineers, support technical growth across teams, and improve knowledge-sharing practices.

Minimum Qualification

  • Extensive experience designing, building, and operating production software systems, including large-scale backend or distributed services.
  • Strong software engineering and coding skills in Python or a comparable backend language, with the ability to guide architecture, API design, testing, and secure, maintainable implementation
  • Proven ability to set technical direction and lead complex initiatives involving multiple teams and cross-functional partners.
  • Experience improving the reliability and quality of production services through observability, incident response, root-cause analysis, and post-incident learning.
  • Strong communication skills, including the ability to explain technical trade-offs and build alignment with engineering and non-engineering partners.

Desirable Skills

  • Experience building or leading AI- or LLM-powered production services is strongly preferred. Familiarity with agentic architectures, retrieval-augmented generation, and approaches to evaluating AI service quality is valuable.
  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP and modern service deployment practices, including containers and CI/CD.
  • Experience building reusable platform components or developer-facing services adopted by multiple product teams, including managing versioning and backward compatibility.
  • Experience with multi-tenant SaaS platforms, data management, data onboarding, or data pipeline services.
  • Familiarity with Splunk products or the Splunk platform.

What we offer

  • A team of incredibly capable and dedicated peers, all the way from engineering to product management and customer support.
  • A stable, collaborative, and supportive work environment. We work in an open environment, work together to get things done, and adapt to the changing needs for the team. We keep it real by being open and honest.
  • Balance. We want you to be successful outside of work too. We trust our colleagues to be responsible with their time and commitment, and believe that balance helps cultivate a positive environment.

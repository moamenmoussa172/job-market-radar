About the job

Meet the Team

Splunk’s Observability Platform team builds and operates the data and platform capabilities that power Splunk Observability Cloud. We work on large-scale systems that help customers understand the health and performance of their services, while enabling engineering teams to deliver reliable platform capabilities.

Team is advancing two important areas: building tools that provide customers with insights into their Observability license costs and help them optimize usage, and modernizing the platform architecture and technology that support wide spectrum of o11y capabilities. Our work spans data lakes, distributed query and analytics systems, data pipelines, and AI-enabled capabilities. We collaborate across engineering, product, and platform organizations to turn complex technical challenges into improvements that matter to customers.

Your Impact

As a Software Engineering Technical Leader, you will be the technical owner for the team’s domain. You’ll set direction and lead delivery across Observability license cost insights and optimization tools, as well as platform modernization. You’ll combine hands-on engineering with technical leadership across teams.

You will shape the architecture and roadmap for data platform capabilities, guide engineers through complex design and implementation choices, and help advance engineering practices across the organization. You’ll work with partners to understand customer and business needs, clarify tradeoffs, and deliver capabilities that make license usage and cost drivers easier to understand and act on, while improving platform reliability, scalability, and maintainability.

  • Lead technical strategy and end-to-end delivery for platform initiatives, from problem definition and architecture through implementation, launch, and operation.
  • Drive development of tools and product capabilities that give customers actionable insights into Observability license usage and cost, helping them make informed optimization decisions.
  • Modernize platform components and data workflows, improving maintainability, scalability, and operational reliability.
  • Build and improve production backend services, applying sound distributed-systems and software-design practices.
  • Identify practical opportunities to apply AI to platform capabilities, engineering workflows, or data processing.
  • Lead technical discussions across teams, communicate decisions and tradeoffs clearly, and influence roadmaps beyond the immediate team.
  • Support production readiness by improving observability, troubleshooting complex issues, and driving root-cause analysis and follow-up actions.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree with 8+ years of related experience, Master’s degree with 6+ years, or PhD with 3+ years.
  • Strong experience developing and operating production software, including substantial experience with one of object-oriented languages.
  • Experience designing distributed systems or data platforms for large-scale query or data processing. Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP and modern service deployment practices, including containers and CI/CD.
  • Experience leading significant technical initiatives from design through delivery and production operations.
  • Experience applying AI or machine learning to software engineering, data platforms, or operational workflows.
  • Strong communication skills, including the ability to explain technical trade-offs and build alignment with engineering and non-engineering partners.
  • Experience mentoring engineers and improving code quality through reviews, testing, and engineering practices.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Apache Spark, Trino, Apache Pinot, ETL pipelines, or comparable data lake and analytics technologies.
  • Experience developing production software in Java.
  • Experience building cost analytics, usage insights, or optimization tools for SaaS, cloud, or observability products.
  • Experience modernizing production platforms, including evolving architecture, services, or data workflows.
  • Familiarity with Splunk products is a plus.

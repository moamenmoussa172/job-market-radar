About the job

Meet the Team

Cisco Networking Engineering, formerly known as Distributed Systems Engineering, operates as a multi-billion-dollar business driving innovation across the Web, Service Provider Edge, Core, and Optical markets. We manage the development of high-end optical products including the NCS1010, NCS1014, NCS1004, NCS4000, and NCS2000 series. Our team is a globally distributed group of engineers working at the intersection of hardware and software to solve real-world connectivity challenges. We are currently tackling the exponential growth of AI infrastructure by redesigning optical systems to be more cost-efficient and secure. This is an exciting time to join us as we manage the entire product lifecycle, from initial design through to global deployment.

Your Impact

Develop software applications to manage complex optical systems and automation stacks to improve overall network reliability and performance. Collaborate with globally distributed engineering teams to ensure seamless software and hardware integration. Identify and resolve software bottlenecks and bugs to accelerate the delivery of high-quality technical solutions. Optimize system configurations and automation processes to support the scaling requirements of next-generation AI infrastructure. Deliver robust software tools that directly contribute to the success and deployment of our multi-billion-dollar optical portfolio.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor, Master, or PhD degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 2+ years of professional experience in full-stack programming using Java.
  • Demonstrated proficiency with software version control and configuration management tools such as Jira, Github, or Bitbucket.
  • Technical understanding of computer networks and fundamental protocols including TCP/IP.
  • Documented experience participating in software and hardware integration testing.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proficiency in C, C++, or Python programming languages.
  • Experience developing and deploying applications within a Linux environment.
  • Familiarity with optical infrastructure and DWDM systems.
  • Ability to thrive in a globally distributed team focused on rapid prototyping and iterative development.
  • Strong creative problem-solving skills with a track record of proposing new technical ideas.

Employees on sales plans earn performance-based incentive pay on top of their base salary, which is split between quota and non-quota components, subject to the applicable Cisco plan. For quota-based incentive pay, Cisco typically pays as follows:

  • .75% of incentive target for each 1% of revenue attainment up to 50% of quota;

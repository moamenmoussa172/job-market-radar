About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

Doctolib is looking for a Workday Integration & Extend Engineer to join its People Systems & Performance (PSP) team. You will own the Integration and Extend development layer of Doctolib's HR tech stack in Workday. You will design and maintain integrations and custom Extend applications across all HR systems, and drive the team's upskilling on integration and Extend competencies.

Reporting to the HRIS Tech Lead, you will work in close partnership with People Strategy, Finance, Social Impact, Hiring, Onboarding teams, and the Doctolib Data team. You will play a key role in making HR data actionable for decision-making, while ensuring seamless data flows across platforms including Workday, Payroll tools (ADP), other HR and IT integrations, and future solutions.

This role is built around three pillars: Integration Architecture, Workday Extend development and Analytics. You will design connectors, APIs, and data pipelines across the HR ecosystem, and build custom Workday Extend applications to close functionality gaps — collaborating with the Data team and other IT teams, and exploring AI-powered automation (DUST our main internal AI platform, Workday AI Agents, MCP). Workday reporting is also a strong supporting skill to enable integrations by RaaS and support ad hoc requests on reportings.

As a nice-to-have, you also support day-to-day team's activities on functional scope of a Workday module (HCM, Compensation, Absence, Time Tracking) performing configuration and unit testing (autonomously for basic configuration, relying on an external integrator for complex ones), assessing impacts and being responsible for alignment with business stakeholders.

What you'll do

Integration Architecture & Engineering

  • Design, build, and maintain all integrations between Workday and the HR ecosystem (Peakon, PeopleDoc, ADP Decidium / Celergo, Deel, Ashby, Sana, and future platforms) and act as key point of contact for integration with non-HR tools
  • Own the integration architecture end-to-end: APIs (REST/SOAP), middleware connectors, Workday Studio, Cloud Connect, Boomi, and Workday Extend, in collaboration with the Delivery Factory
  • Ensure integration monitoring, incident management, and quality standards across the data layer
  • Key contributor to the PeopleData Hub architecture — coordinating closely with business stakeholders and the Doctolib Data team — to design the central data flow layer connecting HR systems to downstream consumers (Finance, BI, Payroll)

AI & Automation (Workday AI Agents, DUST/MCP)

  • Define and prioritize AI use cases together with our AI PM, end users, HR teams and choose best solution for automation (Workday AI, MCP, Agents, integration or orchestration automations, Extend applications)
  • Prototype and build AI capabilities and Workday-MCP for HR and end-users (self-service, process automations,, proactive insights), collaborate on Dust / Claude integration with Workday to enable AI-powered HR self-service
  • Evaluate build vs. buy vs. integrate on AI tooling for the HR stack

Workday Extend Development

  • Design, build, and maintain custom Workday Extend applications (Extend Studio) to close functionality gaps across the HR ecosystem and expose them though IA tools
  • Define and implement custom objects, business processes, security groups, and orchestrations within Extend
  • Integrate Extend applications with Workday APIs and external systems as part of the broader integration architecture
  • Establish Extend development standards, testing practices, and reusable components for the team

Analytics

  • Maintain the Workday reporting layer at a solid working level: standard reports, custom reports, and calculated fields, leveraging Reporting-as-a-Service (RaaS) to enable integrations
  • Support ad hoc reporting requests from HR and business stakeholders; advanced/complex reporting and dashboard design is a plus, not a core requirement
  • Partner with the Data team on our data/reporting architecture to ensure alignment with downstream consumers (Finance, BI, Payroll)

Team Upskilling & Knowledge Sharing

  • Elevate the team's integration and Extend capabilities through documentation, hands-on workshops, and shared architecture standards
  • Build runbooks and integration playbooks that enable autonomous operation and handover to operational teams
  • Act as the go-to expert on integration patterns within PSP

Who you are

Before you read on — if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

You could be our next teammate if you have:

  • 6+ years of Workday experience, including at least one full integration implementation
  • Proven integration architecture experience: Workday Studio, Cloud Connect, APIs (REST/SOAP), Boomi or equivalent middleware
  • Demonstrated experience owning end-to-end Workday integrations (design, build, test, monitor) and experience with HR data flows: payroll integrations (ADP, Deel), benefits/LMS connectors
  • Exposure to AI tooling in an HR context (Workday AI Agents, chatbots, agentic workflows, DUST, or similar)
  • Hands-on Workday Extend development experience (Extend Studio, custom objects/business processes, APIs, security configuration) —
  • Strong data modeling and governance mindset — you understand the HR data model and its downstream impacts
  • Working knowledge of Workday reporting basics (standard/custom reports, calculated fields) — advanced/complex reporting is a plus, not required
  • Ability to work autonomously in a lean team with direct ownership and minimal process overhead
  • Fluency in French and English (working language is English; stakeholders are multi-country)
  • Multi-country HRIS experience (France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands) with GDPR data constraints

Now, it would be fantastic if you have:

  • Functional expertise in at least one Workday module: HCM, Compensation & Advanced Compensation, or Absence & Time Tracking
  • Advanced/complex Workday reporting and dashboard design experience
  • WD Pro certification (Reporting, Integration, or HCM)
  • Experience with Zapier or other integration platforms to interact with other IT teams on integrations through global tools

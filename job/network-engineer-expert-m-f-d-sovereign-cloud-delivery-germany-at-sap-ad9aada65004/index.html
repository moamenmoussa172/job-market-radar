Where you belong:
We are looking for an experienced Network Engineer (f/m/d) for the newly founded Cloud Delivery Germany as part of SAP’s Sovereign Cloud Organization. With the buildout of new Sovereign Cloud regions, you will be taking part in laying the foundation towards a unified and competitive business in the regulated industry in Germany/ Europe
Ideal candidates will have a strong understanding of network infrastructure and the ability to administer and troubleshoot network infrastructure to deliver exceptional support to our customers.
What you'll build:
- Operate and maintain complex Hyperscaler/SAP Cloud Infrastructure networks, track significant problems, monitor performance, and perform upgrades as required.
- Engage with customers in network architecture discussions to meet their security requirements.
- Improve our network concepts based on customer feedback.
- Respond to and resolve escalated customer requests in a timely fashion.
- Participate in deep level incident troubleshooting.
- Participate in complex change preparation and execution.
- Other support tasks, duties, and responsibilities, e.g., hardware installations, troubleshooting, and administration
What you'll bring:
- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Computer Engineering, or related field
- Strong experience with Hyperscaler networking (AWS, Azure or GCP) and/or SAP Cloud Infrastructure.
- Experience managing networking for SAP’s solutions, e.g. S/4, BTP, etc.
- Experience implementing security on AWS with VPC, IAM, Security Groups, UTM solutions, NACLS, etc.)
- Perform and lead diagnostics of problems, troubleshoot and develop solutions on AWS, Azure & GCP
- Implement security requirements across all Cloud platforms
- A willingness to work in a 24/7 environment with on-call duties and flexibility to work in shifts is required, ensuring consistent coverage and support. The role includes an attractive remuneration package for shift work and on-call duties
- Commitment to maintaining daily office presence for efficient team collaboration. Possible office locations: Berlin and St. Leon-Rot.

We value the skills you bring and support you to build on them through our learning and development opportunities. These are the skills associated with this role:
Agentic AI Day-to-Day Practice, Learning Agility, Cloud Architecture, AI Adoption Capability, Collaboration, High Availability Engineering, AI Output Quality Assurance, Complex Problem Solving, Troubleshooting (Problem Solving), Context Engineering, Creative Thinking, Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), AI-Assisted Automation and Prototyping, Effective Communication, Application Security, Adaptive Leadership, Customer Focus, Team Performance Management
Due to the potentially classified nature of our work, your willingness is required to subject yourself to a governmental security clearance process

Solutions Architect (Pre Sales) - Defence

About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

Are you looking to make a real impact on national security and play a meaningful role in the growth of our company? As a Solutions Architect for Defence at Elastic, you will serve as the technical authority and trusted advisor to our sales team, Ministry of Defence (MoD) customers, defense primes, and the broader intelligence community. You will understand and solve complex, mission-critical data challenges using the Elastic Stack—enabling secure, resilient, and scalable search, observability, and security solutions. A successful SA at Elastic will be focused on excellence; taking the initiative to improve both themselves and the team through continuous learning, while confidently navigating the unique compliance, architectural, and operational landscapes of the Defence sector.
What You Will Be Doing

  • Serving as the primary technical point of contact for MoD accounts, defense systems integrators (Primes), and account managers within your assigned territory.
  • Developing a deep understanding of defence mission goals, operational requirements, and digital transformation initiatives (such as Multi-Domain Integration and Cloud-First strategies), articulating how Elastic offerings securely address these needs.
  • Creating and owning value-based relationships at all levels within customer organizations, from front-line technical operators to senior digital commands and stakeholders.
  • Navigating complex procurement and technical assurance frameworks, actively participating in all phases of territory execution from initial discovery to achieving the technical win.
  • Demonstrating the value of Elastic offerings in secure environments, at industry events (e.g., defence technology days), and within closed community forums.
  • Advising the sales team on effective ways of positioning Elastic products, solutions, and services specifically for high-assurance, tactical, and enterprise defence environments.
  • Onboarding, educating, and enabling defense partners and Primes, supporting them through joint architectures and complex sales cycles.
  • Creating targeted collateral, technical architectural blueprints (including hybrid, air-gapped, and cross-domain solutions), and collaborating with other Elasticians to meet stringent client requirements.
  • Being the voice of the Defence customer to communicate specific compliance, security, and feature enhancements back to our engineering and leadership teams.
  • Deepening your sales, defence-domain, and technical skills through self-driven education while taking advantage of professional development opportunities provided by Elastic.

What You Bring

  • A track record of success in a technical presales role within the UK Public Sector, specifically working with the Ministry of Defence, defense primes, or national security agencies.
  • Active UK Security Clearance (SC level minimum, DV preferred) or the clear ability and willingness to obtain it.
  • A demonstrable ability to articulate and sell the benefits of modern platforms, containerisation (Kubernetes), and software architectures within high-security, low-bandwidth, or air-gapped environments.
  • A real passion for curiosity and continuous learning, staying ahead of both modern technology trends and shifting defence data standards.
  • A history of successful customer relations where you devised robust, secure architectures that helped protect critical infrastructure, accelerate data-driven decision-making, or outpace adversarial capabilities.
  • An ability to influence and challenge the status quo, with a proven knack for guiding public sector teams toward modern, open-source-based architectural patterns.
  • The ability to inspire groups, both large and small, ranging from technical engineers to high-ranking military officials.
  • A willingness to travel 30% within the region to visit customer sites, secure facilities, and occasionally travel internationally.

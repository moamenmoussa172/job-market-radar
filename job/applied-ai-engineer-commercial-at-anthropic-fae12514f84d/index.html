About the job

About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

As an Applied AI Engineer on the Commercial team at Anthropic, you will be a trusted technical advisor to the companies that run the real economy, across software, financial services, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and professional services, as they go from using Claude to building on it. Commercial is one of Anthropic's largest and fastest-moving books of business, covering thousands of mid-market and small companies. Some of these companies put Claude inside the product they sell, and others put agents to work inside their own engineering, operations, finance, and support teams, and this role works with both. You'll lead deep technical engagements, partnering directly with customer engineering teams to take their hardest agent builds from idea to production on the Claude API, the Agent SDK, Claude Managed Agents, Claude Code, and Claude for Enterprise. Many of these teams are lean, with years of existing systems and no dedicated AI function, and you will bridge the gap between what Claude can do and what it takes to run it inside a business that already has customers depending on it.

Working closely with Applied AI Architects, Account Executives, and Anthropic's Product, Engineering, and Research teams, you'll go deep where hands-on engineering changes the outcome, then turn what you learn into something the rest of the book can use. You'll leverage your AI engineering expertise to develop custom evaluation frameworks, design scalable architectures, and create the reference implementations, tooling, and technical resources that enable Commercial customers to succeed with Claude.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as a deep technical advisor to high-potential Commercial customers, whether Claude sits inside the product they sell or inside the way they run their own business, bringing them use cases worth building and pushing the boundaries of what agents can do in engineering, operations, finance, customer support, and more
  • Work hands-on with customer engineering teams through build sprints, pair programming, and architecture reviews that move agents from idea to production, and understand their stack, data, and workflows end to end so that your guidance fits the systems, security requirements, and team they have today
  • Ship working code. Develop prototypes, near-production examples, and technical documentation, including evaluation suites that become the customer's acceptance criteria, AI engineering techniques, and architecture diagrams, that enable customers to build, operate, and scale with Claude
  • Create reference implementations, blueprints, and sample code that many companies can adopt without starting from scratch, for example multi-agent systems on the Agent SDK or Claude Managed Agents, MCP servers for common business systems, or Claude Code running across the software development lifecycle
  • Collaborate closely with Applied AI Architects and Account Executives to decide where engineering depth changes the outcome, and to maintain context and continuity across customer engagements
  • Identify patterns across engagements, including what is hard about running agents inside established companies such as legacy systems, messy data, permissions and security review, cost and latency at scale, and model migrations, then build repeatable evaluations and solutions and contribute insights back to Product, Engineering, Research, and the broader Applied AI team
  • Build the demos, agents, and internal tooling that scale the Commercial Applied AI team itself, and pair with architects on their hardest technical problems
  • Create technical content for customer and partner engineering audiences including documentation, tutorials, and sample code
  • Foster community engagement through hackathons, webinars, technical office hours, and customer events
  • Travel to customer sites for workshops, implementation guidance, and relationship building

You may be a good fit if you have

  • Deep software engineering experience, gained by designing, building, and operating production systems, along with strong opinions of your own about how to bridge the gap between what AI can do today and how established companies get work done
  • 4+ years of technical experience as a software engineer, forward deployed engineer, ML engineer, product engineer, or technical founder, with production experience building LLM-powered applications, including prompting, context engineering, agent architectures, MCP, evaluation frameworks, and deployment at scale, and the ability to work in a real codebase in Python or TypeScript, not just notebooks
  • A systems mindset and comfort with ambiguity. You'd rather build one thing that serves ten customers than ten things that serve one each, and you have a track record of building structure where the playbook doesn't exist yet
  • Strong technical communication skills, with the presence to earn the trust of a customer's engineering leaders, the ability to translate complex AI concepts into architectural decisions and actionable implementation plans for both technical and business audiences, and the ability to run technical workshops, hackathons, or developer-focused events
  • A collaborative approach to working across teams, the range of a generalist who can context-switch from a fintech to a hospital network to a manufacturer and get up to speed on a new business quickly, and a passion for making powerful technology safe and beneficial

About the job

About Us

At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale.

We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry.

More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale.

About the role

We're hiring an IT Systems Administrator to join the founding IT team in San Francisco. You'll run most of our SaaS stack day to day, from maintenance and configuration changes to access and troubleshooting. You'll also build the automation that keeps that stack running as we grow.

This is a role for someone who does a bit of everything: helping an employee that’s locked out, shipping a terraform change, and building an automation that removes a manual task. The ideal candidate has startup experience, is comfortable with ambiguity, and heavily leans into automation.

What You'll Do

SaaS Administration

  • Own the day-to-day administration of our SaaS stack, including maintenance, configuration changes, and license management
  • Administer LangSmith, our product, and use APIs to automate admin work
  • Administer identity and permissions across core SaaS platforms such as Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Zoom, and Microsoft 365

Automation and Tooling

  • Develop scripts, integrations, and reusable workflows using low or no code tools
  • Connect systems through APIs and event-driven workflows to reduce manual work

Identity Management

  • Administer Okta and user access
  • Manage Okta group rules that help define our RBAC policy
  • Establish repeatable standards and automation for employee lifecycle, group management, application provisioning, and access reviews

Client Platform Administration

  • Administer our MDM platform, Iru, to enforce policy configurations, patching, zero-touch deployment, and custom desktop environment configurations
  • Automate the hardware lifecycle from procurement, assignment, return, and recycling

Core IT Operations

  • Provide hands-on troubleshooting and support to employees in the office and remotely across hardware, software, networking, and SaaS tools
  • Build and maintain the harness for our AI ITSM agent, including the tools, playbooks, and knowledge it needs to resolve issues on its own
  • Write documentation and playbooks, and use recurring support issues to guide what to automate next

What You'll Bring

  • 3+ years of experience in IT systems administration or IT engineering, ideally at a fast-growing startup
  • Experience administering a broad SaaS stack that includes Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, and AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT
  • Hands-on experience administering Okta, including SSO, SCIM provisioning, and Okta Workflows
  • Comfort with REST APIs, webhooks, and API authentication methods
  • Experience using coding agents (e.g. Claude Code) as part of your day-to-day workflow
  • Experience with a macOS MDM platform and endpoint security tooling
  • Solid networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPNs, wireless)
  • Clear communication, with patience for non-technical users
  • Annual salary range: $120,000-$160,00 USD

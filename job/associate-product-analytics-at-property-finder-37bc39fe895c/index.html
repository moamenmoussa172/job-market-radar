About the job

Property Finder is the leading property portal in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, dedicated to shaping an inclusive future for real estate while spearheading the region’s growing tech ecosystem. At its core is a clear and powerful purpose: To change living for good in the region.

Founded on the value of great ambitions, Property Finder connects millions of property seekers with thousands of real estate professionals every day. The platform offers a seamless and enriching experience, empowering both buyers and renters to make informed decisions. Since its inception in 2007, Property Finder has evolved into a trusted partner for developers, brokers, and home seekers. As a lighthouse tech company, it continues to create an environment where people can thrive and contribute meaningfully to the transformation of real estate in MENA.

Role Summary:

The Product Analytics Associate sits within Property Finder’s Strategy & Analytics team. The role turns data into actionable insights that help Product Management leadership make better, faster decisions.

You’ll work alongside an exceptional group of ex-consultants from McKinsey, Bain, BCG and the Big 4, owning analyses that directly shape how our products grow.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own work-streams end to end, delivering data-driven business and product insights across strategic initiatives.
  • Run complex, detailed analyses to find areas for improvement, increase efficiency and help set targets for teams.
  • Work closely with Product, CRM, Growth and other functions to turn insights into action.
  • Present findings regularly to senior leaders with a clear, compelling storyline.

The Person

Experience

  • 3+ years in business analytics, including a minimum of 2 years at an MBB firm (McKinsey, Bain, BCG) or another Tier 1 management consulting firm.
  • Exposure to Product, CRM and Growth teams.
  • Strong collaboration skills, with a track record of working across functions and driving insights into action.

Technical proficiency

  • Exceptional SQL and Tableau skills.
  • Hands-on product analytics experience, including Google Analytics, A/B testing, tracking and tag management.

Communication

  • Proven ability to build and deliver presentations for senior audiences with a compelling storyline.
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication, including explaining technical content simply.

Competencies

  • Strong understanding of analytics end to end.
  • Consistent high performance throughout education and career.
  • Curious, proactive, practical and solution-oriented, with a “get it done” attitude.
  • Thrives in a fast-paced environment.
  • Demonstrates extreme ownership.

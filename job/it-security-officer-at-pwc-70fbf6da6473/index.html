Job Description & Summary

Meet your future team

Join our IT Security team and contribute to protecting PwC Luxembourg’s systems, data and technology environment. You will work closely with IT operations, development teams and other stakeholders to strengthen security across technology projects, solutions and day-to-day operations.

What your role will look like

As an experienced IT Security Officer, you will take ownership of key cybersecurity activities and help ensure that security is embedded throughout our technology environment. Your work will combine security governance, technical assessments and collaboration with IT teams to identify risks early, strengthen controls and support secure delivery.

  • Maintain and apply IT security policies, standards and controls, supporting compliance with relevant requirements including NIS and ISO 27001 and following up on related audit findings.
  • Assess applications, developed software, solution architectures and technical designs to identify security risks, evaluate controls and define practical remediation actions.
  • Drive vulnerability management by performing security and configuration assessments, coordinating penetration testing and working with technical teams to ensure timely remediation of significant findings.
  • Guide IT projects from initial security assessment through changes, deployments and upgrades, providing the security input required before production release and CAB validation.
  • Embed security into the software development lifecycle and CI/CD pipelines by working with development and IT operations teams on automated security checks and remediation before release.
  • Act as a trusted security contact for IT stakeholders, translating technical findings and risks into clear, actionable guidance while maintaining accurate records of controls, exceptions and risk acceptance decisions.

Your growth and opportunities

You will deepen your expertise across security governance, application security, vulnerability management and secure technology delivery while gaining exposure to a broad range of IT projects and stakeholders. As your experience grows, you will take increasing ownership of security assessments and contribute to strengthening how cybersecurity is integrated into technology decisions and delivery.

The skills you bring

  • A bachelor’s or master’s degree in Cybersecurity, Information Security, Information Technology or a related field, combined with at least three years of relevant experience.
  • Advanced cybersecurity knowledge, with the ability to independently assess application security, security controls, technology risks and vulnerabilities and advise teams on appropriate remediation.
  • Working knowledge of recognised cybersecurity standards and frameworks, including ISO 27001, NIST, OWASP, OWASP LLM, OWASP API and MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Proficient knowledge of secure development practices and CI/CD security controls, including SAST, DAST and SCA, with the ability to support their practical application within the software development lifecycle.
  • Fluent written and spoken English; French is an asset.

Ways of working

  • Analyse complex information and security risks in a structured way, proactively identifying issues and opportunities and translating technical findings into a clear business and risk context.
  • Collaborate effectively across IT, development and other stakeholder groups, sharing expertise, building trust and helping resolve differing perspectives constructively.
  • Take ownership of your development, actively building your cybersecurity knowledge and applying what you learn as technologies, threats and business needs evolve.
  • You act with integrity, handle confidential information with care, and uphold high ethical standards in every situation.

Additional skills that would support your success

  • Professional cybersecurity certifications such as CISSP, CISM or CISA.
  • Cloud security certifications covering Microsoft Azure or AWS.

The skills listed reflect what matters most to succeed in this role. If your experience aligns with them, even if your path looks different, we encourage you to apply and start the conversation with us.

Take your next step

Bring your cybersecurity expertise to a role where your judgement can strengthen the security of our technology environment and influence how solutions are designed and delivered. Work alongside technology teams, tackle evolving security challenges and build expertise that grows with the cybersecurity landscape. Grow here. Go further.

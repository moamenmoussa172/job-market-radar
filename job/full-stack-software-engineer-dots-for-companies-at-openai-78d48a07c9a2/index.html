About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the Team

Dots for Companies helps organizations put AI to work. Our scope includes increasing adoption and retention in Business and Enterprise plans, exploring shared AI teammates with their own responsibilities, and building the tools people need to manage their work. We build on the broader Dots platform while developing experiences specific to companies.

About the Role

We are looking for Senior and Staff Full Stack Software Engineers to build Dots product experiences from the interface through the services that power them. You will help define how people delegate tasks to enterprise agents, follow their progress, and collaborate with them over time. This role combines hands-on product development with the systems work needed to make persistent, asynchronous agent experiences reliable.

In this role, you will:

  • Build full stack experiences that help teams discover, adopt, and continue using Dots at work.
  • Develop workflows for shared AI teammates, including how people assign responsibilities, inspect work, and give feedback.
  • Build management and observability experiences that help users understand what Dots are doing and how well they are performing.
  • Partner on enterprise identity, permissions, and connector experiences so users understand what a Dot can access and which identity it acts under.
  • Turn open questions into focused experiments, ship useful increments, and use feedback and product data to guide iteration.
  • Own application architecture and production quality across the features you ship.
  • Work independently across team boundaries to resolve dependencies and keep projects moving.

You might thrive in this role if you have:

  • Experience shipping user facing software across frontend and application backend systems, with clear ownership of the resulting user experience.
  • Strong product judgment: you can explain the user problem, the tradeoffs you made, and what changed after launch.
  • Systems thinking that connects interface behavior, APIs, data models, integrations, and operational needs.
  • The ability to make progress when requirements are incomplete, seek the right input, and adjust as you learn.
  • Interest in AI tools that help people do work, with a willingness to experiment with unfamiliar product patterns.
  • A collaborative approach and clear communication about decisions, dependencies, and results.

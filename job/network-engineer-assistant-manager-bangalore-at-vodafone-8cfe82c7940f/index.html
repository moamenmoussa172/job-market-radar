About this Role

We are seeking a Network Consultant to provide first-line diagnosis and troubleshooting for IPVPN, Internet and other IP network faults. You will handle customer-reported incidents from initial diagnosis through resolution, working with customers, internal support teams, service providers and vendors to restore services within agreed service levels. The role operates in a 24/7 environment and requires shift working, including night shifts.

The position requires practical telecommunications and IT service knowledge, particularly across IP and transmission networks, alongside effective customer communication and incident management capabilities.

What you’ll do

  • Perform first-line diagnosis for IP-related incidents, including IPVPN and Internet faults.
  • Handle customer calls, raise incident tickets and gather the technical information required to investigate reported problems
  • Diagnose and troubleshoot technical issues, including network configuration and complex faults involving Cisco routers, switches and Alcatel routers.
  • Manage fault reporting, isolation and escalation in line with established processes and agreed service levels.
  • Work with internal support teams, service partners and vendors to resolve circuit and network faults.
  • Coordinate with global service providers and follow incident management processes to resolve customer-reported incidents.
  • Take ownership of customer issues throughout the incident journey and provide prompt, accurate updates.
  • Identify when escalation is required and support effective escalation and de-escalation throughout incident resolution.
  • Support timely fault resolution within agreed SLAs and KPIs, helping minimise the risk of service credits and maintain a positive customer experience.
  • Work within a 24/7 shift environment, including night shifts.

Who you are

You bring a combination of technical network troubleshooting expertise, telecommunications knowledge and customer-focused communication. The role requires:

  • 1+ years' experience within a telecom service provider or systems integrator environment.
  • Good knowledge of troubleshooting transmission and IP networks.
  • Technical troubleshooting knowledge across IP networks, including Cisco routers, switches and Alcatel routers.
  • Knowledge of telecommunications and IT service models and their practical application.
  • Experience escalating incidents and working with technology vendors, other service providers and third-party suppliers to drive resolution.
  • Experience contributing to process, system and capability improvements.
  • Good customer-handling, communication and collaborative teamwork skills.
  • An Engineering degree or equivalent technical background. The JD specifies a Bachelor of Engineering degree with CCNA as essential, while Diploma/BE qualifications in Telecom or Computer Science and CCNA/CCNP certifications are identified as desirable.
  • ITIL Foundation-level qualification.
  • An understanding of submarine cable systems and basic CCNA Voice knowledge.
  • Previous fixed-line IP troubleshooting and customer service experience is desirable.
  • Curiosity and willingness to develop knowledge across different technologies and technical disciplines.

What's in it for you

This role provides hands-on exposure to global telecommunications operations, customer-facing incident management and troubleshooting across IP and transmission networks. You will collaborate with customers, internal teams, service providers and technology vendors while developing practical experience in resolving network incidents within an SLA-driven environment.

What skills you will learn

Based on the responsibilities and technical environment described in the JD, the role offers opportunities to further develop:

  • IPVPN, Internet, transmission and IP network troubleshooting.
  • Structured incident diagnosis, escalation and end-to-end resolution.
  • Practical troubleshooting across Cisco routers and switches and Alcatel routers.
  • Working effectively with global service providers, technology vendors and internal technical teams.
  • Customer communication throughout the incident lifecycle.
  • SLA- and KPI-focused network service management.
  • Continuous improvement across processes, systems and technical capabilities.

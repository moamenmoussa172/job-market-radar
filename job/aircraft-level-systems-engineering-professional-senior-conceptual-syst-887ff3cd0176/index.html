Aircraft Level Systems Engineering Professional Sr., Conceptual System Design

As an aircraft-level systems engineering professional within the Product Integration (aircraft architecture) team, you will play a lead role in developing and governing the company’s system-level conceptual design and sizing tools. You will be responsible for advancing the methods, models, and analytical frameworks that support early-phase system sizing, parametric trade studies, and multidisciplinary analysis and optimization (MDAO), working in close collaboration with the aircraft configuration design team.

In parallel, you will contribute to the development and deployment of the company’s Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) practices. You will help define model-based methods and workflows that ensure conceptual sizing models, architecture definitions, assumptions, and early-phase analyses are captured consistently and traceably within MBSE frameworks.

You will also lead Research & Technology initiatives to evaluate and integrate emerging conceptual design methodologies, enhance architecture exploration capabilities, and pilot advanced toolchains that combine MBSE and MDAO. Your leadership will directly influence how future aircraft architectures are sized, analyzed, and matured from early trades through program launch.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Lead the development, maintenance, and governance of system-level conceptual design and sizing tools, including the methods, assumptions, parametric models, and analytical frameworks that support early-phase trades and multidisciplinary analysis and optimization (MDAO).
  • Define, mature, and document MBSE methods and model-based processes that support aircraft-level systems engineering, ensuring consistent capture of architecture definitions, conceptual sizing outputs, and early-phase analytical artefacts within MBSE frameworks.
  • Develop and integrate model-based workflows that connect conceptual sizing models with system architecture models, enabling traceable links between early trades, system requirements, functional analyses, and architecture decisions.
  • Create and maintain configuration-controlled system architecture models, including requirements, functional decompositions, interface definitions, assumptions, key drivers, and verification and validation (V&V) planning artefacts.
  • Facilitate cross-functional activities for requirements elicitation, maturation, decomposition, and validation, ensuring alignment between conceptual design results, system architecture intent, and early requirement allocation.
  • Perform and guide aircraft- and system-level analyses, including trade studies, feasibility assessments, conceptual sizing loops, and MDAO-supported architecture exploration to support Product Integration decision-making.
  • Collaborate closely with the aircraft configuration design team to ensure that conceptual sizing logic, parametric dependencies, and architecture constraints are reflected in early configuration and performance assessments.
  • Provide training, coaching, and mentoring to engineers across disciplines on conceptual design tools, MBSE methods, and optimization workflows, supporting the organization’s shift towards model-based and digital engineering.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You hold a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Aerospace or Mechanical engineering, or a related field, ideally complemented by formal training in Systems Engineering.
  • You hold a PhD in aerospace engineering, systems engineering, or a related field is an asset, particularly with focus on aircraft conceptual design, system sizing methods, MDAO, model-based systems engineering, or parametric modelling approaches.
  • You have 7+ years of experience in aircraft or complex-system development, ideally including systems engineering, conceptual design, architecture definition, preliminary sizing, or parametric modelling. Experience in a discipline such as aerodynamics, performance, mechanical/electrical systems, or structures is an advantage.
  • You have hands-on experience developing and using conceptual design and sizing tools, parametric modelling environments, and early-phase trade study workflows. Solid programming skills using Python, C/C++ or MATLAB are essential. Familiarity with MDAO frameworks or multidisciplinary simulation toolchains is an asset. Experience with CAD environments – Catia or NX – is an advantage.
  • You are proficient with model-based systems engineering, including requirements management, functional analysis, architecture modelling, and traceability. You are comfortable with tools such as Cameo, Capella, Rhapsody, and with MBSE languages and methods (SysML, UAF, OOSEM, Arcadia, etc.).
  • You are familiar with requirements and PLM ecosystems (Teamcenter, DOORS, Jama, Polarion, etc.) and understand how early-phase analyses and assumptions must flow into configuration-controlled environments.
  • You have strong scripting and automation skills, especially for connecting models, automating parametric studies, building data pipelines, or linking conceptual design tools with MBSE artefacts.
  • You have experience with system or technical simulation, parametric studies, or performance analysis. Exposure to optimization methods, surrogate modelling, or sensitivity analysis is an asset.
  • You are curious, open-minded, and proactive, comfortable exploring new methods, experimenting with modelling approaches, and driving the adoption of model-based workflows across the organization.
  • You bring strong analytical and systems-thinking skills, with the ability to structure complex problems and maintain clarity across disciplines.
  • You are passionate about innovating engineering – and aerospace technology in particular – and are motivated by long-term goals.
  • You have excellent written and verbal communication skills and naturally tailor your message to suit the audience. Experience teaching or coaching is an asset.
  • You communicate technical concepts, project status and risks clearly and succinctly to stakeholders at all organizational levels.

About the job

AWS Applied AI Solutions (AAIS) is building toward a future where every business innovates with Amazon AI teammates. To get there, we build AI solutions that improve human capabilities and transform entire business functions. We create end-to-end products that surprise and delight out-of-the-box, making complex things easy and hard things possible, with no cloud experience required. We start with customers who embrace the future and build bridges to meet the rest where they are. We pursue ambitious opportunities with conviction, and we are looking for builders who share that mindset.
AWS Enterprise Support is looking for a Support Domain Architect (SDA) to join our Strategic Industries Premium Support Business Development team. You will operate at the intersection of deep technical consulting, pre-sales architecture, and plan for delivery excellence — building customer value propositions for Unified Operations (UOps), generating pipeline through your customer relationships and Technical Field Community engagement, and serving as a domain CTO ensuring delivery excellence across your specialty.
You will perform technical consulting with customers (architecture design, modernization, migration, optimization), building trust and identifying support solution opportunities. You will partner with Business Development Managers to build pipeline, position deals, and provide technical expertise that unblocks customer conversations. You will serve as a domain CTO — collaborating with Domain Specialist Engineers, setting quality standards for delivery artifacts agreed with customers and account teams, and ensuring customers realize value from the solutions we sell. You will contribute to industry and technology solution development, ideating and shipping new support offerings that address patterns you observe across customer engagements.
Key job responsibilities
Leverage customer consulting relationships and Technical Field Community (TFC) engagement to identify UOps pipeline opportunities and share insights with BDMs. Build data-driven customer value propositions using the Strategic Value Proposition Framework, quantifying UOps impact from support case data and workload analysis. Provide on-demand technical expertise to advance deals — addressing customer objections, positioning solutions, and presenting to VP/CTO-level executives. Define proof-of-value success criteria and KPIs for customer engagements. Serve as domain CTO — collaborating with Domain Specialist Engineers, setting quality standards for CWR and CountDown Premium artifacts, and ensuring delivery consistency. Ideate and ship industry-specific and technology-specific support solutions, integrating UOps with AWS products (IDR, CDP, AMS, DevOps Agent, Security Agent). Build AI-powered tools and frameworks that scale BD effectiveness across the organization. Maintain deep technical expertise in your domain (Security, Networking, Containers, Big Data/AI-ML, SAP or Industry Specific) through hands-on consulting: architecture design, migration, modernization, and optimization. Contribute to Well-Architected Framework lenses, customer events, blog posts, and enablement programs.

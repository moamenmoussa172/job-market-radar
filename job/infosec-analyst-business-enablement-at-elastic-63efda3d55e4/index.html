About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What Is The Role :

We’re hiring an InfoSec Analyst to join the Business Enablement team within Information Security (InfoSec). InfoSec manages the security strategy, policy, and programs for the entire company. This includes risk management, compliance, security controls, threat prevention and detection, and incident response.

All of this work occurs in a globally distributed company. In this role you’ll help Elastic win and keep customer trust, and you’ll support the security requests and vulnerability work that come through the team’s queues. You’ll work closely with Sales, one of your biggest partners, along with Solution Architects, Support, Legal, Engineering and other InfoSec teams, using AI and automation to do this work faster and with a focus on what customers actually need.

What You Will Be Doing :

  • Handle customer security requests such as RFPs, RFIs, security questionnaires and due diligence, and route questions that need other teams’ expertise to the right owners.
  • Own our Trust Center. Ensure the content and security documentation for customers are accurate and up-to-date. Make it easy to navigate. This includes certifications, attestations, policies, and other customer materials.
  • Analyze incoming customer inquiries to spot recurring questions and gaps, and turn them into proposed updates to the Trust Center and customer artifacts.
  • Triage the team’s internal request queue, including product security-related tickets and CVE-related requests.
  • Design and build AI-assisted workflows, automation and process improvements that make the team faster and more scalable, with a focus on customer-centricity and self-service.
  • Explain Elastic’s security posture and compliance (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, CSA STAR) in clear terms to customers and internal teams.
  • Provide security policy guidance to employees, collaborators and partners.
  • Balance day-to-day operational work with project and creative work.
  • Learn Elastic’s technical environment, products, business processes and culture, and bring that understanding into your work.

What You Bring :

  • Experience in security GRC, customer trust, security assurance or a similar function, including answering security questionnaires, RFPs or due diligence requests.
  • Working knowledge of security domains and frameworks (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST, GDPR) and the ability to discuss technical topics with engineers and domain experts.
  • An automation-first mindset, and comfort using AI tools in your day-to-day work.
  • Familiarity with vulnerability management concepts such as CVEs and severity scoring, or the willingness to learn them quickly.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with the ability to turn technical detail into clear, accurate, customer-ready content.
  • Strong attention to detail, a data-driven approach, and comfort with deadlines and shifting priorities.
  • Experience working with fast-paced engineering and product teams, and the ability to pick up new technologies quickly.
  • Comfort with ambiguity and change.

Bonus Points :

  • A track record of improving processes through automation, including using AI in smart, practical ways: well-crafted prompts, agentic workflows, API integrations.
  • Building your own agents and tools (Python scripting is welcome, but not required).
  • Certifications such as CISA, CISM or GIAC.
  • Experience with government or other regulated customers.
  • Experience with the Elastic Stack.

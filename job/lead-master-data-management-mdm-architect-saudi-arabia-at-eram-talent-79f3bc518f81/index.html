About the job

Eram Talent, a leading talent acquisition company, is seeking a highly skilled Lead Master Data Management (MDM) Architect to join our team in Saudi Arabia. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for defining and implementing robust MDM strategies and frameworks that ensure the quality, consistency, and accessibility of master data across various platforms and applications.

The ideal candidate will possess extensive experience in master data management, data governance, and architecture. You will lead cross-functional teams in designing MDM solutions that align with business objectives while adhering to industry best practices. Additionally, you will work closely with stakeholders to understand their data needs and translate them into actionable strategies.

If selected, the work location would be onsite at Saudi Arabia.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain the Master Data Management strategy to meet business requirements and enhance data quality .
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, executives, and Technology departments to understand business requirements and translate them into Master Data Management architecture solutions.
  • Lead the design and architecture of MDM solutions considering scalability, performance and integration with existing systems
  • Define and enforce Master Data Management architectural standards, frameworks, and methodologies across the organization.
  • Define data governance policies and standards to maintain the accuracy and reliability of master data.
  • Provide expertise in data modelling, data profiling, data quality assessments and data models management
  • Collaborate with Data Quality and MDM teams to provide architectural oversight, inputs on standards & mentorship
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to understand data consumption patterns and optimize MDM solutions accordingly.
  • Evaluate and recommend emerging technologies, tools, and platforms to enhance the company's MDM capabilities.
  • Conduct the design, implementation, and maintenance of enterprise-wide Technology systems, ensuring scalability, reliability, and security and seek approval from the Master Data Quality Manager on the same.
  • Conduct regular assessments and audits to ensure compliance with Technology architecture standards and identify areas for improvement.
  • Drive the integration of new technologies and solutions, ensuring seamless interoperability with existing systems.
  • Provide technical expertise and support while, conducting the planning and execution of Technology projects.
  • Stay up-to-date with industry trends and advancements in MDM, proposing innovative solutions to enhance business operations.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer (CDPSE) preferred
  • 7+ years of experience in data management focused on Master Data Management.
  • Strong background in data governance, data quality management, and data integration.
  • Proven experience with MDM tools such as Informatica MDM, Talend, or SAP MDG.
  • Demonstrated ability to design and implement MDM frameworks and processes that drive data quality improvements.
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively with stakeholders at all levels.
  • In-depth understanding of data architecture, data modeling, and related technologies.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on delivering actionable solutions.
  • Experience in guiding and mentoring teams in a collaborative environment.
  • Fluency in English; proficiency in Arabic is a plus.

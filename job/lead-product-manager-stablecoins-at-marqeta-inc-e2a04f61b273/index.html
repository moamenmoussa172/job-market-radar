Lead Product Manager - Stablecoins

Marqeta Inc · Remote

Fully remote · Lead / Principal · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Marqeta Inc's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

As the Lead Product Manager, Stablecoins on the Flexible Payments team, you will shape the vision and drive end-to-end execution for next-generation fintech products leveraging Stablecoins and Card Issuing. You will own the strategy, roadmap, and go-to-market execution, partnering cross-functionally to build and scale transformative embedded finance solutions.

We are looking for a product leader who has already shipped Stablecoin payment products, and who thrives in a fast-paced, high-growth, highly collaborative environment. You will shape how the team works as well as what it builds: setting the bar for product craft, partnering with other product managers, and influencing senior stakeholders. If you are passionate about next-gen, customer-centric financial products and want to build from the ground up and beyond, we'd love to chat!

About the team

The Flexible Payments team is revolutionizing global embedded finance, enabling seamless integration of financial products into business platforms. We are driving marqeta’s roadmap for next-gen card issuance utilizing flexible credentials technology, and incubating new solutions based on Stablecoin payments, making financial services more accessible and scalable. As builders and innovators, we thrive on making an impact and pushing fintech boundaries.

We work Flexible First. This role can be performed remotely anywhere within the United States or from our headquarters in Oakland, CA. We'd love for you to join us!

The Impact you will have

  • Define & Drive Product Strategy – Own the roadmap for our new Stablecoin-based fintech products, aligning business goals with customer needs
  • Build & Scale Embedded Finance Solutions – Develop innovative products in Stablecoin payments, Card issuance, On-chain money movement by collaborating with engineering, design, and data teams
  • Execute with Excellence – Work cross-functionally with design, engineering, data science, legal, compliance, and external partners to build, launch, and scale compliant financial products
  • Customer & Market Research – Deeply understand customer pain points, new Payments industry trends, and competitive dynamics to shape product decisions
  • Prioritize with Data – Leverage analytics, user insights, and industry trends to identify high-impact features, optimizations, and experiments
  • Partner with Fintech Ecosystem – Work closely with banks, networks, and fintech partners to drive innovation and enhance our platform’s capabilities
  • Optimize & Iterate – Monitor key product metrics, measure impact, and iterate based on learnings to continuously enhance the customer experience.

Who You Are

  • 8+ years of product management experience , including 3+ years in a senior or lead product role in Stablecoin payments, On-chain money movement and Card issuance products
  • Proven experience building and scaling financial products from 0 to 1 in a high-growth environment
  • Strong technical acumen – ability to work closely with engineering teams and understand API-driven products
  • Deep understanding of Crypto/Stablecoin payments, card issuance, payment processing, or banking-as-a-service (BaaS) models, including how Stablecoin rails connect with traditional payment systems
  • Ability to thrive in ambiguous, fast-paced environments, balancing strategic vision with execution
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a data-driven mindset
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills – ability to work with both internal teams and external partners

Nice-To-Haves

  • Experience working with legal, compliance, risk, and regulatory frameworks in fintech is a plus
  • Hands-on experience in creating Stablecoin based card products with technology partners
  • Experience launching products with card networks or Stablecoin issuers
  • Experience leading product strategy for a new product line or business unit

Compensation and Benefits

Marqeta is a Flex First company which allows you to choose your best working environment, whether that be from home or at a company office. To support Flex First, we calibrate pay to a competitive value according to working location. Compensation is aligned according to three tiers within the United States:

  • National: A baseline tier that applies to most of the geographic territory of the United States.
  • Premium: Slightly elevated from the National tier, and oriented toward a narrower set of higher cost-of-living areas, such as Los Angeles CA and Seattle WA
  • Premium Plus: A tier for the most expensive working areas, like the San Francisco Bay area and New York City.

Visit this page or consult with a Recruiter to determine which tier would be applicable to you.

When determining salaries, we consider several factors including, but not limited to, skills, prior experience, and work location. The new-hire base salary range for this position is:

  • National: $155,200 - $194,000
  • Premium: $167,900 - $209,900
  • Premium Plus: $182,600 - $228,200

We also believe in recognizing the contributions of our people. That's why we award annual bonuses to eligible employees, rewarding both individual performance and the success of the entire company.

Along with monetary compensation, Marqeta offers

  • Multiple health insurance options
  • Flexible time off – take what you need
  • Retirement savings program with company contribution and after tax contributions
  • Equity in a publicly-traded company and an Employee Stock Purchase Program
  • Family-forming benefits, fertility support, and up to 20 weeks of Parental Leave
  • Free therapy sessions, financial and professional coaching, and legal advice
  • Monthly stipend to support our remote work model
  • Annual “development dollars” to support our people growth and development
  • Through Flex First, the freedom to live and work wherever you and your family thrive

Marqeta is on a mission to change the way money moves. We’re one of the earliest enablers of embedded finance, a market opportunity sized up in the trillions. Marqeta is powering the most well known brands in the new economy (Block, Cash App, Affirm, Instacart, Doordash, Uber, Walmart, etc). You don’t need to be a Payments expert to join the Marqeta Team, let us help you with that. This is the opportunity of a lifetime to work with innovators around the world and unlock equitable financial access for all.

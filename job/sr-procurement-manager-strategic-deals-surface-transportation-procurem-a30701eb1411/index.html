Sr. Procurement Manager, Strategic Deals, Surface Transportation Procurement

Amazon · Barcelona, Spain

Onsite · Senior · Other tech role · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Amazon's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Join us as a Sr. Procurement Manager that will architect and negotiate complex transportation deals.
We are looking for a talented individual who can solve ambiguous sourcing challenges in a fast paced and tech centric environment.
You will be joining the new strategic deals vertical in the Amazon Transportation Service Operations organization.
In this role, you are expected to drive procurement innovation as well as drive new ways of working with internal stakeholders to enable new transportation deals that impact our Supply Chain and Middle Mile Operations.
Key job responsibilities
- Negotiate transportation deals.
- Set new mechanisms and processes to engage with internal stakeholders effectively to create value propositions to suppliers.
- Drive innovation and accountability with process and mechanisms that set procurement for success.
About the team
The ST Procurement team is part of Amazon Transportation Services Operations that is in charge of Amazon Middle Mile. It includes 4 verticals :
- 3rd Party Line Haul Procurement for Middle Mile Operations ;
- Yard Procurement (shunting and offsite parkings)
- Strategics deals
- Driver and Carrier Experience.
The role reports into the strategic deals vertical within the Procurement team.
- Bachelor's degree
- Experience in strategic sourcing including vendor negotiations, global contract management, process improvement, operational and financial analysis
- Experience in transportation operations and industry knowledge
- Experience in large complex deal negotiations with a successful track record
- Business conduct in multi-cultural environment
- Master's degree
- Comfortable with AI prompting and agent building. Coding is a plus.
- German, Polish, Italian or French speaking is a plus.

About the job

Amazon is seeking a Finance Data Analyst to support the International Devices team. Over the past decade Amazon has built a portfolio of leading, innovative and fast-growing devices including Kindle, Fire tablets, Fire TV and Echo. For this role, we are looking for someone with strong analytical skills and a passion for turning data into decisions. You will work hands-on with the data behind the numbers — querying it, modelling it, and building the reporting that leadership and regional Finance teams rely on. The role has a strong focus on data, automation and BI tools, so you will spend as much time improving how the analysis is produced as producing it. This is a great opportunity for someone who is data-savvy and excited to build advanced financial analysis, reporting and automation capabilities for the team.
Key job responsibilities
• Own the automation and continuous improvement of the financial reports that support actuals and financial planning
• Build scalable reporting solutions and self-service tools that enable the team to make sound business decisions
• Partner with central and regional Finance teams, and other Devices Finance teams, on opportunities to automate, standardize and streamline financial planning and reporting processes
• Work with data source providers and the technology teams that own the data sets we rely on, ensuring key decision data is accurate, automated and timely
• Analyze opportunities and co-develop financial models for strategies and business plans that grow the Devices business
• Take on ad hoc analysis and new areas of ownership as business needs and team priorities evolve
A day in the life
• Research with your customers about their needs and objectives.
• Build a financial planning tool using your coding language skills.
• Automate data feeds, create Quick Sight dashboards and remove manual intervention.
• Consult, collaborate and influence stakeholders to find and obtain the data your customers need.
• Develop and execute data-driven ideas for innovation, automation, and efficiency.
• Have fun in an international, collaborative and fast-moving group.
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, Economics or a highly analytical field (e.g., Engineering, Math, and Computer Science)
- Experience developing financial models in Excel with advanced functions (pivot tables, macros, VBA, Power Query)
- Experience problem solving and root cause analysis
- Experience with data analytics platforms (Power BI, Python, SQL, Tableau)
- Experience with SQL or ETL
- Experience working with large-scale data mining and reporting tools (i.e. SQL, MSAccess, Tableau, Essbase or Cognos)
- Experience or equivalent experience in finance roles
- Experience defining requirements and using data and metrics to draw business insights

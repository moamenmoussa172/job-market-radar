About the job

As a user experience (UX) designer at Ubisoft Saguenay, you will shape clear, intuitive experiences for the interfaces and interactive systems of Rainbow Six Siege. You’ll work on the main game, focusing on menus, shop features, social systems, and customization flows. You’ll turn complex needs into accessible, ergonomic solutions that support player engagement and satisfaction.

What you’ll do

  • Design user flows and interaction paths for menus, shop interfaces, social features, and customization systems
  • Translate complex requirements into clear, ergonomic UX solutions
  • Collaborate with user interface (UI), gameplay, and backend teams, alongside the studio’s Graphics Team Leader, to support feature design
  • Apply user-centered design methods, including research, prototyping, and testing
  • Use player data and feedback to guide and validate design choices
  • Maintain visual consistency and usability across menus and systems
  • Create UX documentation to support teams throughout development
  • Use Figma and Miro to explore ideas and document your work

What you bring to the team

  • Understanding of information architecture and interaction concepts
  • Ability to communicate UX thinking clearly through documentation and visuals
  • Experience applying user-centered methods with multidisciplinary teams
  • Skill in reviewing user feedback and adapting concepts accordingly
  • Interest in usability, accessibility, and continuous improvement
  • Curiosity about player behaviors and motivations
  • A portfolio showcasing relevant UX work and problem-solving approaches
  • Fluency in written and spoken French

What to send our way

  • Your CV highlighting relevant skills and experiences
  • A portfolio or project links showing UX design work and user-centered processes

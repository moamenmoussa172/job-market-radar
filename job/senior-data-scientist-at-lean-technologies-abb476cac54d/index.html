About the job

About the Role

As a Senior Data Scientist on the Credit Scoring & Underwriting squad, you'll build the AI/ML models behind our credit insights and the cash flow underwriting products that come next. You'll work with transaction data from millions of bank accounts across Saudi Arabia and the UAE, and your models will end up inside lenders' credit decisions.

You'll be the squad's data scientist, working every day with software engineers and the product team. You'll help decide what's worth modelling, how to measure it, and when a model is ready to show a client. Bank data is messy, and a lot of the interesting work is figuring out what it can and can't tell you.

About Lean

Lean is the leading financial infrastructure provider in the Middle East. We power some of the region's largest companies with APIs that enable payments, account verification, financial data access, and other critical financial infrastructure.
What started as the region's pioneering Open Banking platform and quickly became the de facto leader in account aggregation and payment initiation in the region is now becoming something bigger: a multi-product infrastructure company powering payments, data, and lending across MENA.
We've processed billions of dollars in transactions. We're trusted by 350+ clients — from Binance and Careem to Etisalat — and backed by General Catalyst, Sequoia, and Shorooq. Our $67.5M Series B was just the beginning. We're expanding into new markets, opening offices, acquiring businesses, and building capabilities that didn't exist here before.

What you'll own

  • You will own the hands-on work across the whole ML lifecycle: feature engineering, experiments, training and deployment
  • Build cash flow and repayment models. That means forecasting a customer's income, spending and balance over the coming months with a clear range around each number, and predicting how they'll repay the loans they already have
  • Decide how we evaluate models, with out-of-time testing and calibration checks, so the results hold up when a lender's risk team looks closely
  • Improve the models behind our existing insights, starting with transaction categorisation and income
  • Deploy and monitor models into production on Lean's ML platform, working with engineers and DevOps
  • Sit in technical sessions with clients' risk teams and explain what the models can and can't tell them
  • Help the rest of the team understand data science/ML and get them excited about what it can do

What we’re looking for

Must-have:

  • 5+ years in data science or ML, with models you built running in production.
  • Hands-on machine learning expertise across the full ML lifecycle, from feature engineering and experimentation to model deployment.
  • You’ve built credit risk or underwriting models and has a strong understanding of how models are built and used in lending.
  • Strong Python and SQL
  • Hands-on experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch
  • You use AI coding tools such as Claude Code every day, for analysis, coding, testing and review
  • You can explain a model, its assumptions and where it breaks to people who aren’t data scientists, including product managers and lenders’ risk teams
  • You’re happy owning data science for a squad and setting the standards for how it’s done
  • Experience in fintech or with financial data

Nice-to-have:

  • You’ve used transformers or foundation models to improve a model in production
  • Open banking or bank transaction data
  • Data engineering, or building data-intensive backend systems
  • ClickHouse or another analytical database at scale
  • Arabic

NB. While we think the above experience could be important, we’re keen to hear from people that believe they have valuable experience to bring to the role. If you identify with the team and mission, but not all of our requirements, then please still apply!!

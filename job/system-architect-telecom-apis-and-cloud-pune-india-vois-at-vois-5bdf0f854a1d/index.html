About this Role

We are seeking a System Architect to lead the design and delivery of scalable, reusable, and high-performing technology solutions within the Telco IT landscape. The role involves translating business requirements into robust technical architectures, guiding cross-functional teams, and ensuring seamless implementation of APIs and cloud-based services using modern technologies such as TIBCO, Kafka, Java, and AWS.

What you’ll do

Translate business and functional telco requirements into end-to-end solution designs and oversee their implementation across multiple teamsDefine and maintain a technical solution design roadmap to enhance reusability and scalabilityDesign, develop, test, and maintain APIs using TIBCO BWCE, Kafka, and Java technologiesDevelop API management solutions for cloud environments, including reusable API proxies and application migrationsImplement traffic management strategies such as load balancing and dynamic upstream routingConfigure plugins, deployment modes, and access control mechanismsLead analytical discussions, perform system health checks, and resolve complex technical challengesDefine and configure data exchange interfaces between services and front-end applicationsPresent high-level and detailed technical designs to solution designers, testers, and DevOps teamsConduct defect analysis, manage incident resolution, and collaborate on production troubleshootingReview code and approve complex pull requests to ensure quality and reliabilityWork continuously with product and engineering teams to break down features and track delivery

Who you are

Minimum 10+ years of IT experience, including 5+ years in Solution or System Architecture rolesStrong expertise in telco BSS applications and architecture frameworksProficiency in TIBCO, Java-based applications, Apigee, Kafka, and AWS DevOps ecosystemPrior experience as a back-end developer with knowledge of Java, JavaScript, Python, and databases (relational and NoSQL)Strong understanding of telecom frameworks such as eTOM, TMForum, SID, TAM, and business process orchestrationExperience in system design, architecture, and leading cross-functional technical teamsFamiliarity with SAFe Agile methodologiesAdditional experience with tools such as Terraform, ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, Grafana, and security practices like SAST, DAST, and SCA is beneficial

Job Description

As a Frontend Developer in our team, you will play a key role in designing, modernizing, and maintaining state-of-the-art web applications. While Angular is our core frontend framework, you will bring high flexibility and adaptability across different technologies, driving architectural excellence, performance, and resilience.

  • Architect, build, and optimize responsive, high-performance web applications that deliver exceptional user experiences.
  • Maintain and modernize existing Marionette.js application, ensuring its ongoing reliability, security, and functional value.
  • Partner closely with cross-functional team members to design and implement robust, scalable frontend solutions.
  • Take ownership of complex frontend technical challenges, guiding them independently from concept to full resolution.
  • Adapt dynamically to changing requirements and seamlessly integrate with adjacent technologies across the software stack.
  • Write and execute comprehensive unit, integration, and end-to-end tests to guarantee high quality and code resilience.
  • Engage actively in code reviews, sharing constructive feedback and fostering continuous technical improvement.
  • Stay ahead of modern frontend development trends, tools, and best practices to keep our stack innovative and efficient.


Basic Requirements

  • 3 to 5 years of hands-on commercial experience crafting high-impact frontend or full-stack software solutions.
  • Deep expertise in Angular, paired with an open, versatile mindset and eagerness to adapt to other modern frontend frameworks.
  • Valuable exposure to backend development, modern DevOps practices, or software build systems that helps you see the bigger technical picture.
  • A collaborative and proactive mindset with strong analytical problem-solving skills to overcome complex technical challenges.
  • Solid mastery of HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript/TypeScript building blocks.
  • Proven experience working with RESTful APIs and modern asynchronous programming concepts.
  • A keen eye for UI/UX principles and passion for turning sleek designs into seamless user experiences.
  • Confidence navigating modern version control systems, especially Git.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with modern end-to-end and frontend testing frameworks such as Playwright, Jasmine, Karma, or Cypress.
  • Experience with build tools and continuous integration environments (e.g., Webpack, Gulp, Jenkins).
  • A security-conscious mindset, knowledgeable in mitigating vulnerabilities like XSS and CSRF.
  • Commitment to accessibility standards (WCAG) to build web applications that are inclusive and accessible for everyone.

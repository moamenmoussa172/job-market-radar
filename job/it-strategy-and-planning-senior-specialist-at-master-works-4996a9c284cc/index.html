About the job

SOOR, a subsidiary of Master Works, helps organizations stay ahead of evolving cyber threats through advanced cybersecurity and secure infrastructure solutions. SOOR delivers resilient, scalable, and regulation-aligned security across governance, cloud, networks, and managed defense.

SOOR is looking for an IT & Strategy Planning Specialist to bridge technology with business goals by developing and tracking IT strategy roadmaps, prioritizing digital investments, and ensuring technology initiatives deliver measurable business value.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop, maintain, and communicate the IT strategy and multi-year technology roadmap aligned with corporate objectives and security standards.
  • Lead annual and quarterly IT planning, including budgeting, resource allocation, and investment prioritization.
  • Assess business needs and emerging technologies, building business cases with clear cost structures, risk assessments, and expected benefits.
  • Oversee the IT project portfolio, tracking progress, project dependencies, and benefits realization through a PMO framework.
  • Define and monitor strategic KPIs, performance dashboards, and executive reporting for senior management.
  • Support enterprise architecture, digital transformation, IT governance, and cybersecurity risk management initiatives.
  • Manage vendor and stakeholder relationships, serving as a primary link between business units and IT technical teams.
  • Promote compliance with internal IT policies, security standards, and relevant regulatory frameworks.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • 3–5+ years of experience in IT Strategy, Strategic Planning, PMO, Enterprise Architecture, or Technology Consulting.
  • Strong understanding of IT portfolio management, budgeting, project prioritization, and ROI/business case development.
  • Proven experience with strategic frameworks, KPI tracking, executive dashboards, and PMO standards.
  • Solid familiarity with digital transformation methodologies, IT governance, risk management, and cybersecurity regulations.
  • Relevant professional certifications (e.g., PMP, TOGAF, COBIT, ITIL) are highly desirable.
  • Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, stakeholder management, and executive reporting skills.
  • Professional proficiency in English (written and spoken).

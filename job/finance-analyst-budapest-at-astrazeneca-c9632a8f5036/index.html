About the job

Finance Analyst (Budapest)

Diverse Minds. Bold Disruptors. Meaningful Impact.

AstraZeneca is a global, science-led, patient-focused biopharmaceutical company that focuses on the discovery, development and commercialization of prescription medicines for some of the world’s most serious disease. But we’re more than one of the world’s leading pharmaceutical companies.
It’s our patients that come first, every decision we make and action we take is centered around how we can better respond to their evolving needs, as well as those of healthcare professionals and partners. It’s why we are shaping patient care for the future, working along the whole experience to give hope to billions of lives across the world.

Build your finance career in a commercial, international environment

Are you looking to build your experience in finance and take the next step in your career?

We’re looking for a Finance Analyst to join our Finance team and work closely with Commercial and wider Finance colleagues. This is a great opportunity for someone who already has a foundation in finance and wants to develop their analytical, forecasting and business-partnering skills while gaining exposure to a broad range of commercial finance activities.

You’ll have the opportunity to work with experienced colleagues, contribute to important business decisions and develop your understanding of how finance supports commercial performance.

What you'll be doing

As a Finance Analyst, you'll support the Finance Business Partner across financial planning, reporting and analysis. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Supporting budgeting, business planning and forecasting activities.
  • Analysing financial performance and helping identify key trends, risks and opportunities.
  • Supporting the preparation of financial reports and providing clear explanations of performance.
  • Working with financial and in-market data to help understand the factors influencing business results.
  • Supporting P&L activities, including sales, investment and FTE analysis.
  • Contributing financial analysis and insights to support business and investment decisions.
  • Supporting business cases, pricing and Market Access modelling.
  • Partnering with colleagues across Commercial and Finance to ensure accurate and timely reporting and planning.
  • Building effective working relationships with internal stakeholders and developing your business-partnering skills.
  • Supporting Finance and cross-functional projects and processes.
  • Identifying opportunities to make financial processes and ways of working simpler and more effective.

What we're looking for

We're looking for someone with a strong foundation in finance, good analytical skills and the motivation to continue developing.

Requirements:

  • A degree in Finance, Business Administration, Accounting, Economics or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Some professional experience within finance, accounting, auditing, financial analysis or a related area.
  • An understanding of financial concepts such as budgeting, forecasting, reporting or financial modelling.
  • Confidence working with numbers and financial data.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication skills and an interest in working with stakeholders across the business.
  • Fluency in English, native Hungarian
  • Curiosity, attention to detail and a willingness to learn.

Why this opportunity?

This role offers the chance to broaden your finance experience beyond traditional reporting and develop a stronger understanding of commercial finance and business partnering.

You'll gain exposure to forecasting, performance analysis, P&L, planning, pricing and business cases while collaborating with colleagues across Finance and Commercial teams.

If you're analytical, commercially curious and keen to develop your finance career within a collaborative international environment, we'd love to hear from you.

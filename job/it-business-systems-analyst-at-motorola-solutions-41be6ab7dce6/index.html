Job Description

As a Business Systems Analyst, you bridge the gap between business users and technical teams, driving innovative solutions for Order Management. We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Business Systems Analyst to join our Order Management team. You will play a pivotal role in bridging the gap between business needs and technical solutions, ensuring the seamless operation and continuous improvement of our order management processes.

Responsibilities:

  • Partner with Order Management & Supply chain stakeholders to understand their needs, identify pain points, and document detailed requirements.
  • Create functional design and technical specifications to extend and enhance order management including On-prem Oracle EBS and/or Oracle Fusion cloud.
  • Leverage your expertise in Oracle EBS applications and other technologies to work with developers to design, develop, and implement solutions that meet business needs.
  • Work collaboratively with both business and IT partners to ensure solutions are delivered on time, within budget, and meet agreed-upon requirements.​
  • Proactively manage conflicting priorities, and drive prioritization discussions using strategic value.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • 8+ years of proven experience working with Oracle R12 EBS or Oracle Cloud applications, with a strong emphasis on Order Management modules.
  • Deep understanding of Oracle ERP Order Management configurations and best practices.
  • Proven experience in PL/SQL development, including troubleshooting and optimization.
  • Proven ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams and stakeholders in a geographically dispersed environment.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to articulate technical information clearly to both business and technical audiences.
  • Proven ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a focus on identifying and implementing process improvements using technology.
  • Experience with Oracle CPQ integration with Oracle EBS is an added advantage.
  • Experience with a broad set of corporate IT initiatives and applications (beyond Oracle; including Mulesoft, OIC, Oracle Fusion and emerging solutions like GenAI).
  • Experience working in agile scrum methodologies to deliver projects and enhancements.

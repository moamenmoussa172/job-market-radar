About the job

Role Based in Barcelona - 3 days onsite/ 2 days at home

AstraZeneca’s Barcelona hub brings together a dynamic, growing team at the forefront of data science, R&D, and technology. Over the past two years the site has grown rapidly, and we are continuing to expand. Barcelona’s vibrant life-sciences community and its position as one of Europe’s most dynamic cities make it an ideal location for talent from around the world — we offer relocation packages to ensure geography doesn’t stand in the way. Here you will work in an inclusive, collaborative environment with a commitment to lifelong learning, growth, and development.

About the Team

The AI for Discovery team builds and deploys AI capabilities — including agentic AI, predictive foundation models, and virtual cell models — to accelerate target discovery and drug design across AstraZeneca R&D. We are now expanding into immuno-oncology (IO) discovery and cell therapy, establishing dedicated AI support for IO target discovery, immune cell engagement, tumor microenvironment biology, and cell therapy design across both Oncology and BioPharmaceuticals. This is a new capability — you will help build it from the ground up.

The team partners closely with therapeutic area scientists, immunologists, IO discovery teams, and cell therapy groups, as well as AI engineering and AI research teams across the organisation.

What the Role Involves

This is a hands-on individual contributor role applying AI to immuno-oncology discovery, cell therapy biology, and new target discovery. You will apply your technical skills in AI and biology to build, evaluate, and deploy predictive models and agentic AI tools that support IO and cell therapy discovery across AstraZeneca’s therapeutic areas.

Key responsibilities:

  • Build, evaluate, and deploy predictive AI models on large-scale biological datasets including single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and functional genomics data.
  • Develop and benchmark virtual cell and perturbation prediction models to support immune cell engineering, IO target discovery, and cell therapy design.
  • Build predictive models of the tumor microenvironment and immune cell engagement, integrating diverse data modalities across preclinical and clinical datasets.
  • Contribute to the development of agentic AI tools and automated data integration pipelines that support discovery scientists.
  • Write production-quality code, maintain reproducible experimental pipelines, and contribute to shared infrastructure alongside AI engineers.
  • Collaborate effectively with wet-lab scientists, immunologists, and computational biologists — communicating technical findings to both expert and non-expert audiences.
  • Contribute to AstraZeneca’s external visibility through publications, conference presentations, and scientific collaborations.

Essential Skills

  • Degree in computational biology, bioinformatics, AI/ML, systems biology, immunology, or a related quantitative discipline, with relevant research or industry experience. Candidates with additional post-doctoral experience are equally encouraged to apply.
  • Experience with predictive AI modeling for biology — foundation models, graph neural networks, perturbation models, or virtual cell approaches.
  • Understanding of drug discovery biology, immuno-oncology, or cell therapy — including immune cell engagement, tumor microenvironment, target identification, mechanism of action, or translational science.
  • Strong technical proficiency in Python (required); experience building production-quality code, pipelines, and tools for scientific applications.
  • Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, or equivalent) for model development and evaluation.
  • Experience working with large-scale biological datasets (e.g., single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, bulk RNA-seq, functional genomics).
  • Effective communication of technical and scientific findings to diverse audiences.
  • Enthusiasm and persistence in applying AI methods to complex biological problems.

Desirable Skills

  • PhD in a relevant discipline.
  • Experience with immuno-oncology, immune cell engagement, cell therapy biology, or tumor microenvironment analysis.
  • Experience with agentic AI, LLM-based reasoning systems, or multi-agent architectures applied to scientific problems.
  • Experience training or fine-tuning foundation models on biological data at scale (GPU clusters, distributed training).
  • Knowledge of cell therapy modalities (CAR-T, TCR-T, engineered cell platforms) or related protein engineering.
  • Familiarity with cross-species modeling (human and mouse preclinical data).
  • Experience with cloud computing (AWS) and high-performance computing environments.
  • Proficiency in R for statistical analysis or bioinformatics workflows.
  • Experience working in pharma or biotech R&D.

In-Office Expectations

When we put unexpected teams in the same room, we unleash bold thinking with the power to inspire life-changing medicines. In-person working gives us the platform we need to connect, work at pace and challenge perceptions. That’s why we work, on average, a minimum of three days per week from the office. But that doesn’t mean we’re not flexible. We balance the expectation of being in the office while respecting individual flexibility. Join us in our unique and ambitious world.

About the job

General Summary

The Calibration and Equipment Systems Engineer I serves as a subject matter expert (SME) in
calibration and metrology within the Equipment Maintenance department. This position plays a
critical role in maintaining the integrity of measurement systems used across Production, Quality,
and R&D. The engineer leads out-of-tolerance (OOT) investigations, supports equipment
qualifications and change controls, and provides technical guidance to calibration staff. In
addition, supports the development and maintenance of equipment asset management software
tools and databases, and interfaces with IT to ensure data integrity and system reliability. Ensures
alignment with internal Quality Management System (QMS) procedures and applicable regulatory
standards, contributing to audit readiness, continuous improvement, and equipment compliance
across the organization.

Specific Duties and Responsibilities

• Serve as key point of contact for technical expertise on equipment calibration requirements, procedures, and best practices, including traceability, measurement uncertainty, and evaluation of calibration suppliers. *
• Lead out-of-tolerance (OOT) investigations, including product impact assessments, root cause analysis, and QMS documentation. *
• Collaborate cross-functionally with Equipment Maintenance, Manufacturing, and Quality teams to improve calibration programs and resolve equipment-related compliance issues. *
• Manage equipment setup and qualification processes during the construction orreorganization of production lines. *
• Collaborate with IT to support system integrations and troubleshoot database or software issues *
• Develop, maintain, and improve equipment asset database, ensuring data integrity, traceability, and system reliability. *
• Crosstrain equipment engineering specialists and end users on the calibration review system and assist in resolving issues. *
• Manage Penumbra’s inventory of production, quality, and R&D equipment including tracking of equipment status and location changes, adding new assets to, and updating the equipment database. *
• Support internal and external audits, providing calibration and equipment maintenancedocumentation. *
• Identify and implement improvements to calibration processes and equipment documentation by authoring engineering change orders (ECOs). *
• Proactively purchase and calibrate measurement equipment needed for equipment maintenance and engineering activities, maintaining Kanban inventory for production use.
• Adhere to the Company’s Quality Management System (QMS) as well as domestic and global quality system regulations, standards, and procedures. *
• Understand relevant security, privacy and compliance principles and adhere to the regulations, standards, and procedures that are applicable to the Company. *
• Ensure other members of the department follow the QMS, regulations, standards, and procedures. *
• Perform other work-related duties as assigned.

*Indicates an essential function of the role

About the job

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

Airbnb's Online Data organization runs the stores behind every booking, every message, and every payment. Storage Foundation owns the engines underneath those stores. We build and operate Airbnb's source of truth databases for the company's online OLTP traffic, and we own the control plane that provisions, upgrades, and heals them. This is engine work, not fleet administration. When our engines are slow, unavailable, or wrong, so is the product.

As Senior Engineering Manager you will lead engineers across multiple teams and you will manage managers. You will own the engine strategy, the reliability commitment that every service above you depends on, and the growth of the people who do the work. Your leverage comes from the managers and technical leads on your teams, the bar you hold in design and incident reviews, and the trust you build with the teams who stake their product on us.

The Difference You Will Make:

Set the technical direction for Airbnb's online storage engines

You will set a multi-year technical vision for the engines and the control plane beneath every transactional and key value store at Airbnb, and you will make the calls that follow from it. That includes where we invest in our own engine expertise, where we buy instead of build, how we evaluate alternative engines against real production traffic, and which capabilities have to exist before a workload can safely land on us. You will also make the cost of these systems measurable and attributable, so that investment decisions rest on evidence. You will make these calls alongside senior technical leaders across Online Data and the broader Infrastructure organization.

Make reliability the ground truth of the platform

The services built on your engines commit to availability targets that only hold if the engine underneath them is better than they are. You will own that engine level commitment and the error budgets that feed every product surface above it. You will drive fault isolation so that one tenant cannot degrade everyone else, prove regional failover with scheduled production drills, and treat capacity headroom as a reliability decision. You will own incidents in your area personally, and you will turn each one into a structural fix.

Make routine work disappear

Every ticket is a product team waiting on us and an engineer pulled off platform work. You will push your teams to build API first, so that provisioning, schema changes, grants, and quotas can be driven by an engineer, a tool, or an agent without a human in the loop. You will use AI where it takes real work off your teams.

Build and grow the team

You will coach engineering managers and senior technical leads, and grow the depth of database expertise inside Airbnb. That means taking a forward view of talent needs, building a succession pipeline, hiring senior engineers who can own a complex open source codebase, and creating the conditions where people do the most technically ambitious work of their careers. You will also represent this group to executive leadership and to our customers, and you will make the case for investment in terms the business can act on.

Your Expertise:

  • 12+ years of engineering experience with a BS or Masters, or 9+ years with a PhD, including several years managing engineers and experience managing managers or senior technical leads
  • Experience leading teams that build and operate Tier 0 distributed systems, with accountability for availability targets, on call health, and incident response at a company where downtime is visible to customers
  • Enough technical depth in databases, storage, or comparable stateful infrastructure to set direction, judge tradeoffs, and earn the trust of engineers who work on engine internals, without needing to be the architect of record
  • Experience making build versus buy and vendor decisions with evidence, including managing a critical vendor or open source dependency while building more capability in house
  • Demonstrated ability to attract, hire, and grow senior technical talent, including other managers
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, including presenting strategy, risk, and investment return to executives and explaining complex systems to non technical partners
  • Strong cross functional collaboration with customer teams, peer platform teams, and partners who do not report to you, including resolving conflict before it escalates

Nice to have:

  • Experience with distributed SQL systems, or with relational engine internals such as query execution, storage engines, replication, or consensus
  • Experience running large stateful workloads on Kubernetes
  • Experience leading migrations of business critical data under strict availability and correctness requirements
  • Experience with upstream open source contribution as a team practice, not just an individual one
  • Experience building platform products with internal customers, including onboarding and adoption

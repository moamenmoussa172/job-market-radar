About the job

Who we are

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your Mission

We're looking for a Frontend QA Engineering Intern to support our mission of delivering reliable, secure, and high-performing automated solutions. In this role, you’ll work alongside experienced QA engineers and developers to test new features, identify issues, and ensure the best possible user experience from the UI level.
Your focus will be on frontend testing across key example projects, such as operational workflows, AI integrations, and low-code automation tools. Leveraging modern low-code platforms like Appian and UiPath, your curiosity, attention to detail, and understanding of software quality principles will be key in maintaining Bitpanda’s standards of excellence, innovation, and operational scalability.
What You’ll Do

  • Execute Frontend Testing: Support QA in ensuring product quality, reliability, and performance through structured frontend testing, validating end-to-end user journeys, AI integrations, and automated business processes.
  • Collaborate Across Teams: Work closely with developers, Business Analysts, and cross-functional stakeholders to understand requirements, document test cases, identify potential quality risks early in the development cycle and support UAT cycles.
  • Test Low-Code Test Automation: Design, execute, and maintain frontend test cases using low-code automation tools such as Appian and UiPath, ensuring complete alignment with product and business objectives.
  • Monitor & Track Bugs: Assist in monitoring, reporting, and tracking defects using standard QA tools and workflows (Jira), contributing clear visual proofs and step-by-step reproduction details for efficient issue resolution.
  • Drive Quality in Agile Sprints: Participate actively in sprint planning and review meetings, sharing insights to enhance frontend test coverage and drive a culture of quality across teams.


Who You Are

  • Frontend & Quality Mindset: Detail-oriented and proactive, with a keen interest in user experience, UI workflows, and ensuring the overall reliability of software products.
  • Low-Code & Automation Curious: Familiar with core testing concepts (manual testing, test automation, agile methodologies) and eager to gain hands-on experience with low-code platforms like Appian and UiPath.
  • Methodical Problem-Solver: Possess a strong analytical mindset to test beyond happy paths, naturally exploring boundary limits, non-numeric edge cases, and unexpected UI behaviors.
  • Structured Communicator: Excellent collaborator able to document findings clearly, articulate technical issues with concrete real-world examples, and contribute to continuous team improvement.
  • Relevant Academic Background: Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field (preferred but not required).

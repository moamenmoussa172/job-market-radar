About the job

Please note: Applicants should be UK based and already have the right to work here.

The Senior Data Scientist is responsible for the data strategy, data platform and data function of Masdar's UK business. The role owns the UK's enterprise data platform (MasdarMind) and is accountable for making sure the business has accurate, timely and well-governed data for its operational, commercial and financial decisions. Working closely with the Commercial, Asset Management and Finance teams, the Senior Data Scientist sets priorities, leads delivery through internal staff and external partners, and drives the adoption of analytics, AI and automation across the UK office, in line with the data guidelines and standards set by Masdar's group data function.

Responsibilities

Data Strategy & Leadership

  • Own and deliver the UK data strategy and roadmap, aligned to business priorities and to the data guidelines and standards set by Masdar’s group data function.
  • Act as the single point of accountability for data in the UK office, prioritising requests from Commercial, Asset Management, Finance and other teams.
  • Lead, mentor and develop data team members, consultants and contractors as applicable, including recruitment and setting technical standards.
  • Represent the UK in group data forums, sharing UK practice and keeping UK work consistent with group direction.

Data Platform Ownership & Architecture

  • Own the architecture, performance, security and running cost of the UK data platform, built on Azure Databricks, ADLS Gen2 and Unity Catalog using a medallion (bronze, silver, gold) design.
  • Oversee the design, build and maintenance of reliable ETL/ELT pipelines for high-frequency operational data (e.g. BESS telemetry and wind SCADA), market and settlement data (e.g. Elexon, NESO, LCCC), optimiser reports and finance data.
  • Set and enforce engineering standards, including version control, code review, CI/CD, testing, monitoring and automated alerting.
  • Support cost control, seeking out savings across cloud compute, storage and licences.

Data Governance & Quality

  • Implement data governance in line with group guidelines, including data ownership and stewardship of critical data elements, access control, cataloguing and lineage.
  • Ensure data accuracy, completeness and timeliness through automated quality checks and monitoring, resolving issues at source with data owners.
  • Advise on the setup of cloud and data security, with the support of IT and external consultants.

Analytics, Reporting, AI & Automation

  • Deliver dashboards and reports (e.g. Power BI, Grafana) that give stakeholders actionable insight into technical, commercial and financial performance.
  • Drive the adoption of AI and automation across the UK office, for example replacing manual reporting and extracting data from documents.
  • Support the development and deployment of analytics and machine learning models, such as asset performance analysis and revenue benchmarking.
  • Build data literacy across the business through training, workshops and self-serve tools.

Stakeholder, Vendor & Partner Management

  • Manage relationships with data providers, optimisers, joint venture partners, OEMs and IT consultants to secure reliable access to data (e.g. APIs and file feeds).
  • Scope, select and manage data consultancies and technology vendors, including their deliverables and costs.
  • Communicate data plans, progress and risks to senior leadership in clear, non-technical terms.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field.

Preferred Experience:

  • At least 6 years of experience in data engineering, data science or analytics, including at least 2 years leading data platforms, projects or teams.
  • Experience in the energy sector, ideally renewables, battery storage or power trading, with an understanding of the GB power market (e.g. wholesale markets, the Balancing Mechanism and settlement).
  • Proven experience designing and running a cloud data platform end to end, ideally on Databricks and Azure (e.g. ADLS, Unity Catalog, Delta Lake).
  • Expert-level proficiency in Python, SQL and PySpark.
  • Experience working with high-volume time-series data, such as SCADA or asset telemetry.
  • Experience with BI and visualisation tools such as Power BI and Grafana.
  • Experience with data governance, CI/CD and DevOps practices (e.g. Azure DevOps).
  • Nice to have: experience with BESS optimisation, revenue benchmarking or energy trading.
  • Nice to have: experience applying AI and LLM tools to automate business processes.

Job-Specific Skills:

  • Strong leadership and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence at senior levels.
  • Excellent communication skills, including explaining technical topics to non-technical audiences.
  • Strategic, forward-thinking mindset with the ability to design scalable, future-proof data solutions that adapt to evolving technologies and business needs.
  • Hands-on and pragmatic: comfortable both setting direction and building solutions.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with attention to detail.
  • Proactive mindset with a passion for renewable energy and sustainability.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to facilitate knowledge transfer down to the field management.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, specifically managing digitalisation and data analytics.
  • Proactive and results-oriented approach.
  • Excellent attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and relevant QHSE software (e.g., environmental management systems software).

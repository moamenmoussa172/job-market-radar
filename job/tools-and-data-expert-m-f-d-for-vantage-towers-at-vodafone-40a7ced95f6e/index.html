Tools and Data Expert (m/f/d) for Vantage Towers

About the job

At Vantage Towers, we’re on a mission to power Europe’s sustainable digital transformation. As a leading tower company, we’re ushering in an era of technology-driven advances to help connect people, businesses, and internet-enabled devices like never before. We combine the scale, stability and quality of our tower network with the agility, optimism and energy of a start-up.

As a young TowerCo, we have already achieved strong results with a continued focus on accelerating growth and a special emphasis put on sustainability. As part of our team, you’ll work in a dynamic and multicultural environment that embraces open communication, collaboration and teamwork.

If you’re ready to take responsibility and shape the future of telco infrastructure with us, then let’s level up in your career and reach the top, together.

Your Responsibilities

  • IT Project Coordination: You coordinate end-to-end projects and activities aimed at developing, modifying, and transforming technical data or IT tools, databases, and applications to support Infrastructure & Operations needs.
  • Operational Bridge & Business Alignment: You serve as the vital link between Local Technology business functions and digital solutions by translating operational requirements across Site Acquisition, Deployment, Energy, Operations, and Field Maintenance into system and reporting capabilities.
  • TIMS System Administration & Ownership: You act as Vantage Greece’s Single Point of Contact and System Administrator for TIMS, ensuring data quality, user support, process alignment, training coordination, group-wide best practice sharing, and system adoption in line with local strategy.
  • Data Analytics & Reporting: You implement impactful data analytics and reporting solutions to support business requirements, with a strong focus on Energy and Spend Management needs.
  • Independent & Collaborative Execution: You function autonomously within tight timelines while fostering a team-oriented, positive attitude and collaborating seamlessly across multicultural environments.

Your Profile

  • Educational Background: Degree in Engineering or Information Technology.
  • Professional Experience: Proven experience in software tools implementation teams, including role experience as a project manager or coordinator, backed by project management certification or equivalent experience.
  • Technical Skills: Strong understanding of the telecom passive infrastructure lifecycle (Site Acquisition, Permitting, Deployment, Energy, Field Maintenance) alongside advanced expertise in MS Excel and BI reporting tools; hands-on experience with Sitetracker or similar platforms is a strong advantage.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent ability to work independently with minimal supervision, deliver under tight deadlines, and collaborate effectively within multi-cultural teams.
  • Language Proficiency: Excellent spoken and written communication skills in both Greek and English.

At Vantage Towers we operate in 8 different European countries. This role will be based in Athens.

What we offer – Flexibility & Growth Opportunities

  • Flexible work: we offer a hybrid working model, which allows you to work 3 days per week from our Athens office and 2 days remote.
  • Top Equipment: you receive a new laptop as well as a mobile device and plan. We also offer parking slots.
  • Salary & Vacation: an attractive salary package based on your experiences and external/internal benchmarking. Additionally, we offer a meal allowance and 2 extra days of annual leave.
  • Learning & Growth: LinkedIn Learning, individual support for your professional and personal development as well as international career opportunities.
  • Health & Social Care: profit from options for private health insurance, extra health and life insurances and pen plans (contributions depending on your role).
  • Team & Culture: work in a diverse, multiculture team which is committed to the values: Accountability, Respect, Teamwork and Trust. We offer the unique opportunity to shape our organization – together as OneVT.

Are you ready to create the future of digital telco infrastructure together with us? Then we are happy to receive your CV. Depending on the role, we generally invite you to two-three rounds of interviews before you receive an offer.

We are committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where everyone can thrive. Applications from all individuals, regardless of background, identity, or ability are more than welcome. If you require any reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process, please contact us so we can support you.

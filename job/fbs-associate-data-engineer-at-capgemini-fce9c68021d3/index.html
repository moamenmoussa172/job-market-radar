About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Brazil.

Our Client is one of the United States’ largest insurers, providing a wide range of insurance and financial services products with gross written premiums well over US$25 Billion (P&C). They proudly serve more than 10 million U.S. households with more than 19 million individual policies across all 50 states through the efforts of over 48,000 exclusive and independent agents and nearly 18,500 employees. Finally, our Client is part of one the largest Insurance Groups in the world.

Job Summary

The Life Data Platform (LDP) & Data Enablement team is responsible for acquiring, integrating, governing, modeling, and publishing enterprise data across on-premises and cloud platforms. The team enables trusted, scalable, and business-ready data products, analytics, reporting, and operational insights for Life Insurance functions through AWS, Dremio, Data Catalog, and modern data engineering practices.

The Associate Data Engineer will support the design, development, and maintenance of data pipelines, data models, and enterprise data products that enable reporting, analytics, and operational use cases. This role combines hands-on data engineering with stakeholder collaboration, working closely with data engineers, business partners, analysts, and technology teams to deliver reliable and scalable data solutions while growing technical expertise in cloud-based data platforms.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop, maintain, and support scalable data pipelines and data integration solutions.
  • Assist in building and supporting enterprise data products for reporting, analytics, and operational use cases.
  • Develop and maintain data models, data warehouses, and dimensional modeling solutions with guidance from senior team members.
  • Integrate data from internal systems, cloud platforms, APIs, and third-party vendors.
  • Partner with stakeholders to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Support data quality, governance, reliability, and consistency initiatives.
  • Optimize SQL queries and troubleshoot data processing issues.
  • Work within AWS-based data platforms and modern data engineering frameworks.
  • Participate in Agile ceremonies, project planning, and sprint activities.
  • Follow engineering best practices, coding standards, and governance processes.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement efforts across data architecture and platform capabilities.

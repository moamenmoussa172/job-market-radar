About the job

We are looking for a Strong Middle / Senior .NET Software Engineer to join our engineering team and contribute to the development of scalable, secure, and high-performance software solutions.

In this role, you will take an active part in designing, developing, and maintaining enterprise-level applications, building reliable backend services, and delivering high-quality solutions that support our business objectives.

You will collaborate closely with other engineers, Product Managers, System Analysts, and cross-functional teams, contributing to technical decisions and continuously improving engineering practices.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend applications and services using .NET technologies.
  • Build, maintain, and optimize RESTful APIs and backend integrations.
  • Write clean, efficient, maintainable, and well-structured code following software engineering best practices.
  • Work with Microsoft SQL Server, designing and optimizing database structures, queries, and stored procedures.
  • Apply software architecture principles and design patterns to build robust and scalable solutions.
  • Participate in architectural discussions and contribute to technical decisions.
  • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks, technical issues, and system inefficiencies.
  • Ensure application security, reliability, performance, and maintainability.
  • Write unit and integration tests to ensure code quality and system stability.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to maintaining engineering standards.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • Contribute to CI/CD processes, deployment automation, and continuous improvement of development workflows.
  • Stay up to date with modern technologies and emerging software engineering practices.
  • 5+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong hands-on experience with C# and .NET technologies, including:
  • .NET 10 or later
  • ASP.NET Core
  • ASP.NET Web API
  • Entity Framework Core
  • Solid knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), SOLID principles, and common design patterns.
  • Strong experience with Microsoft SQL Server, relational database design, and query optimization.
  • Proven experience designing, developing, and integrating RESTful APIs.
  • Good understanding of software architecture principles and application design.
  • Experience with asynchronous programming, multithreading, and dependency injection.
  • Strong knowledge of Git and version control practices.
  • Experience with unit testing and automated testing frameworks.
  • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and modern software delivery practices.
  • Ability to independently solve complex technical problems and make sound engineering decisions.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in an Agile development environment.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Microservices Architecture.
  • Knowledge of Event-Driven Architecture and messaging systems (RabbitMQ, Kafka, or similar).
  • Understanding of Domain-Driven Design (DDD).
  • Experience with CQRS and Event Sourcing.
  • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Experience with Redis, Elasticsearch, or other caching and search technologies.
  • Knowledge of observability tools, logging, and monitoring solutions.
  • Experience with GitHub Copilot or other AI-powered development tools.

Why Join Us?

  • Work on complex, business-critical applications at scale.
  • Build solutions using modern .NET technologies and engineering practices.
  • Collaborate with experienced engineers and cross-functional teams.
  • Contribute to technical decisions and architectural improvements.
  • Work in an environment that values engineering excellence, innovation, and continuous learning.
  • Grow professionally through challenging projects and opportunities to expand your technical expertise.

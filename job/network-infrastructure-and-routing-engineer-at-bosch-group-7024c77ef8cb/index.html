Network Infrastructure & Routing Engineer

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Provision, monitor, maintain, and troubleshoot external BGP (eBGP) peering sessions with public Internet Exchange Points (IXPs), ensuring stable and reliable routing connectivity.
  • Monitor traffic and link utilization across peering ports, identify traffic spikes, congestion, or capacity constraints, and support capacity planning and upgrade activities where required.
  • Maintain and validate routing filters, prefix-lists, route-maps, and related IRR/RPKI objects to support routing security, prefix validation, and compliance with defined peering policies.
  • Perform initial troubleshooting and analysis of peering-related incidents, routing anomalies, session instability, and connectivity issues, escalating complex cases where necessary.
  • Coordinate with IXP Network Operations Centers (NOCs), external peering partners, carriers, and colocation data center facilities to support cross-connect activities, incident resolution, and operational requests.
  • Manage and follow up on technical tickets related to peering connectivity, routing, and infrastructure issues,
  • Ensure proper documentation and timely coordination with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Contributing in automation development.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field.
  • English Language Level: B2/C1
  • 2+ years of relevant professional experience in a similar network management, technical implementation or consulting role.
  • Good knowledge of IP networking and Internet routing principles, with a focus on BGP/eBGP.
  • Experience in transit provider operations and supporting or troubleshooting BGP peering sessions, including public Internet Exchange Points (IXPs)
  • Understanding of traffic and capacity monitoring, including link utilization, traffic spikes, congestion, and capacity-related issues.
  • Practical knowledge of routing policies and configuration concepts, including prefix-lists and route-maps.
  • Familiarity with IRR and RPKI concepts for route validation, routing security, and policy compliance.
  • Good troubleshooting skills for network connectivity, routing, and peering-related incidents.
  • Experience working with ticketing and incident management processes.
  • Ability to coordinate effectively with IXP NOCs, external peering partners, and colocation/data center providers.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Proactive mindset with a strong focus on automation, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.
  • Ability to work independently while maintaining a collaborative team-oriented approach.

About the job

Who we are

VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.

As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. This partnership is a unique, industry-first model which brings together the best of in-house and 3rd party capability.

About Corporate IT Services

Corporate IT Services (CIS) is one of 4 VOIS service towers. We provide critical Global solutions and IT services to the entire Vodafone Plc business. Our core focus is on delivering Corporate Solutions, including the Global SAP ERP solution (EVO), and a wide portfolio of over 150 business applications supporting the key business units of Finance, HR, Supply Chain, Procurement, Shared Service centres and Group BI. The CIS team consistently leads a number of transformation initiatives, including the transition to SAP Rise, migration to SAP and other SaaS products, and the use of digital accelerators such as AI, process mining and analytics that support the Vodafone data strategy.

A cornerstone of our Vodafone strategy is our capability to execute Vodafone Mergers and Acquisitions projects. Our CIS M&A team helps Vodafone integrate acquired companies onto our EVO SAP platform and supports divested entities in leaving the Vodafone EVO platform.

About this Role

We are seeking an SAP M&A Product Portfolio Manager accountable end to end for the EVO solution through the demand, design, build and deploy stages of the system changes required to execute M&A activities. This is a senior IT product manager role responsible for managing the full M&A lifecycle and critical senior business relationships.

The role is accountable for the EVO Solution (EVO SAP) across all EVO M&A projects. These projects can involve various technical scenarios, depending on the nature of the M&A deal:

  • Onboarding of acquired companies.
  • System carve Out using SAP SLO services or other SLO service providers.
  • Balance sheet Spin Off of Vodafone business units to form new legal entities.
  • Data extraction for divested entities.
  • Joint Ventures.
  • Mergers of different dimensions: these can be mergers of major operating companies or smaller operational mergers to optimise the Vodafone portfolio of legal entities.

Our involvement in M&A deals starts during deal negotiations and covers all matters relating to the EVO ERP platform and the definition of the EVO TSA (Transition Service Agreement). The role sets and delivers the strategy for executing the M&A deal, whether integrating new organisations into our EVO platform, spinning off business units into separate legal entities or supporting divested entities in exiting EVO. It manages the complete EVO platform across Finance, Supply Chain and HR and builds a highly skilled EVO SAP product team capable of handling the various M&A scenarios.

The EVO technology suite comprises a large SAP estate, Google GCP, SaaS solutions and on-premise applications. We also use SAP Joule and other AI tools in delivery. The main products/platforms in scope are:

  • SAP S4 Hana Finance and Supply Chain.
  • SAP cloud solutions: SAP Success Factors, Ariba, Concur, Field Glass, MDG etc.
  • SAP Business Intelligence.
  • SAP Joule and other AI tools.
  • Further smaller and mid-size applications for Finance, Supply Chain and HR.

What you’ll do

  • Accountable E2E for all EVO (SAP) solution related aspects of M&A projects, ensuring that the integration of companies and the exits of divested entities from EVO are achieved within the agreed time and budget.
  • Accountable for adapting the team to changing M&A requirements.
  • Accountable for ensuring that the M&A team can support newly implemented technologies, methodologies, automation and service improvements to achieve a smooth integration or exit.
  • Accountable for Business Case inputs related to M&A projects initiated by the business, including costing the effort, landscape and delivery capabilities in line with Global architecture standards.
  • Accountable for ensuring that all M&A project activities meet IT policy guidelines in terms of SoX, IT controls, IT resilience and vulnerability management.

Who you are

We are looking for a trusted and motivated functional SAP ERP lead who can effectively manage SAP solutions in M&A projects, advise the business and demonstrate a passion for innovation.

The ideal candidate possesses comprehensive expertise in SAP Finance, HR, SCM solutions and Interfaces, along with a strong understanding of Finance, HR, and SCM business processes and their integration points.

Familiarity with SAP cloud solutions and emerging technologies such as Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning is a plus.

The role combines functional SAP expertise with business process knowledge and strong stakeholder management to achieve outcomes that meet our business needs. It drives the definition of the SAP solution in M&A projects, ensuring business alignment and delivery across matrix teams, and collaborates with software vendors and system integrators to execute our priorities.

The candidate will serve as the key ERP (SAP) point of contact, providing effective support, advice and expertise from deal preparation through to the execution of mergers, spin offs and divestitures.

The candidate will also ensure compliance with security, architecture, privacy/GDPR, SOX and audit controls.

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Proven capability as an SAP ERP product manager in large-scale, complex M&A projects for multi-functional, multi-national organisations.
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP S/4HANA systems, business processes, and software development lifecycles in the areas of Finance and SCM, with a particular emphasis on Finance.
  • Management skills, particularly in project management, networking, influencing, communication and relationship management with both external and internal stakeholders and vendors.
  • Leadership skills: aligning multiple stakeholders with different interests, delegating, empowering others and seeking input before making decisions.
  • Experience in managing offshore shared services centre resources.
  • Proven ability to build and expand high-performing teams.

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

  • Good university degree in business management or accounting.
  • Detailed knowledge of financial reporting and finance processes.
  • SAP Certified with detailed and extensive knowledge of SAP Finance and SCM, as well as SAP S4HANA knowledge and experience.
  • A minimum of 10 years’ experience in delivering ERP Projects in an international environment.

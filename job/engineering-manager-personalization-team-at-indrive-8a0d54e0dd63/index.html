About the job

We're looking for an Engineering Manager to build and lead our new Personalization team.

The team builds the decision layer of the inDrive super app, deciding what each user sees, where and when, across all our services.
This is a hands-on role: you are both the team's manager and its senior technical lead. You will own the architecture of the personalization platform, write and review code, and lead a cross-functional engineering team. You'll work closely with product, data science,

design and other engineering teams.

  • Lead the technical design of the personalization platform and its services at scale
  • Stay hands-on: write production code, review designs and pull requests, and set the engineering standards for the team
  • Define clear interfaces and ownership with partner teams
  • Automate the path from model predictions to what the user sees
  • Build in experimentation and measurement, so every change is tested and its impact is known
  • Build, grow and mentor the team, and support new joiners' onboarding and career development health
  • Plan and deliver with product, balancing quick experiments with long-term platform
  • Manage dependencies across teams, and turn stakeholder needs into a clear roadmap
  • Work with our core stack: Go, Python, MySQL, Redis, Kafka, Docker and Kubernetes
  • 7+ years in backend engineering, including 2+ years leading a team as an engineering manager or tech lead
  • Strong hands-on skills in Go (Python is a plus), SQL and Redis, and in designing distributed, high-load, low-latency microservices with sync and async communication
  • Experience building personalization, recommendation, decisioning, ad-tech or offer-management systems in production
  • Solid understanding of experimentation: A/B testing, feature flags and measuring the impact of changes
  • Experience working closely with ML or data science teams, including consuming model outputs in real-time services
  • Proven ability to drive delivery across several teams, with clear communication of complex technical topics to product and business stakeholders
  • Experience hiring and growing engineers, and building a healthy team culture
  • BS/MS in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field
  • Experience working in an international environment.

About the job

We are looking for a Reports Analyst.

Should be analytical and an excellent communicator with quantitative skills.

Our most successful candidates will have:
• At least 2 years of Reports Analyst experience.
• Experience with the usage of Google Sheets
• Bachelor/Master’s degree preferred but not required.
• Proficient in MS Office applications.
• Ability to work with minimum supervision.
• Experience in MS Excel Reporting with MS Access.
• Experience in Power point, Pivot, slicers, and PowerPivot
• Create daily, weekly, and Monthly reports for leadership team.
• Well versed in MS Excel formulas, functions, and controls
• Deliver solution that meet end to end requirements of the clients.
• Analytical mind with a problem-solving aptitude.
• Continually improve reporting process to increase the efficiency.
• Ability to learn new concepts and tools is essential.

Internal:

  • Must have FY25 PDP score of Exceeds Most or Clearly Exceeds.
  • Tenure of at least 18 months.
  • No Active DA.

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.

About the job

We are looking for Software Tester to join our PC Core team working on the technology and services behind the PC gaming experience for Ubisoft Connect users.

As a Software Tester, you will be an integral part of a development team, collaborating closely with developers, product managers, and a team of other QA specialists throughout the development lifecycle. You will contribute to the quality of new features and existing functionality through requirements analysis, test design, functional and end-to-end testing, exploratory testing, regression validation, defect investigation, and release validation.

Our team members are self-sufficient and have a strong problem-solving mindset. We are looking for people who are passionate about gaming, curious about the technology behind games, and eager to develop their technical QA skills. We expect candidates to be proactive, open-minded, comfortable investigating complex or unexpected behavior, and ready to take ownership of quality by identifying risks, communicating issues early, and working with the team to find solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Review stories, bugs, acceptance criteria, designs, and functional requirements before testing starts.
  • Identify unclear, missing, contradictory, or non-testable requirements and raise them with Product and Development.
  • Design and maintain test cases in Jira/Xray.
  • Prepare required test accounts, test data, environments, configurations, and test prerequisites.
  • Validate development tickets and features through functional, integration, and end-to-end testing in the appropriate environment.
  • Investigate edge cases, negative scenarios, and unexpected behavior.
  • Execute regression, smoke, and sanity validation for releases.
  • Identify additional checks based on release scope, affected functionality, and known risks.
  • Investigate and reproduce issues, establishing reliable reproduction steps.
  • Collect relevant evidence such as logs, screenshots, videos, build/configuration information, and test data.
  • Distinguish product defects from environment, configuration, test-data, or test-related issues.
  • Report defects in Jira with sufficient information for Development to investigate.
  • Follow defects through their lifecycle until resolution and verification.
  • Retest fixes and affected functionality, including appropriate regression checks.
  • Keep Jira/Xray test execution and defect statuses accurate and up to date.
  • Communicate blockers, significant findings, and quality risks promptly.

Requirements

  • Exploratory testing and critical thinking
  • Windows desktop application testing
  • Defect investigation and troubleshooting
  • Log analysis and troubleshooting from logs
  • Basic networking fundamentals (connectivity, online/offline behavior, client-server interactions)
  • Understanding of the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)
  • Understanding of the defect lifecycle
  • Test design techniques (boundary value analysis, equivalence partitioning, state transitions, risk-based testing)
  • Test management and defect tracking tools knowledge (Jira/Xray)
  • Clear and fluent written and verbal communication in English
  • Experience working in Agile teams
  • Ownership, autonomy, and ability to drive investigations independently
  • Experience in gaming, launcher, or consumer software products

Nice-to-have

  • Basic API testing
  • Understanding of microservices architecture
  • Experience testing complex desktop applications

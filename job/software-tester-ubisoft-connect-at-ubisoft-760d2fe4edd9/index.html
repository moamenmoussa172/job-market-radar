About the job

We are looking for a Software Tester to join our PC Core team, working on the technology and services behind the PC gaming experience for Ubisoft Connect users.

As part of an Agile development team, you will work closely with developers, product managers, and other QA specialists to ensure the quality, reliability, and stability of Ubisoft Connect. You will be involved throughout the development lifecycle, from reviewing requirements and designing test scenarios to investigating defects and validating releases.

We are looking for a proactive and independent QA specialist with strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, who enjoys exploring complex applications, investigating unexpected behavior, and taking ownership of product quality.

Responsibilities

  • Analyze user stories, functional requirements, acceptance criteria, and designs to identify potential gaps, inconsistencies, and testing risks.
  • Design, document, and maintain test cases and test scenarios in Jira/Xray.
  • Prepare and manage test data, accounts, environments, and configurations required for testing.
  • Perform functional, integration, end-to-end, exploratory, and regression testing of Windows desktop applications.
  • Validate new features, bug fixes, and changes to existing functionality.
  • Identify edge cases, negative scenarios, and potential areas of risk.
  • Investigate and reproduce defects, identifying their potential causes and providing clear reproduction steps.
  • Analyze application logs and collect relevant evidence to support defect investigations.
  • Report and track defects in Jira, ensuring clear documentation and timely follow-up.
  • Retest fixes and perform regression checks to verify that changes have not affected existing functionality.
  • Participate in smoke, sanity, and release validation activities.
  • Collaborate with developers, product managers, and other QA team members to discuss issues, clarify requirements, and improve product quality.
  • Communicate testing progress, blockers, and potential quality risks in a timely manner.

Requirements

  • 1+ years of experience in software testing, preferably in Manual QA.
  • Experience testing Windows desktop applications.
  • Strong knowledge of functional, exploratory, and regression testing.
  • Understanding of test design techniques, including boundary value analysis, equivalence partitioning, and state transition testing.
  • Experience with defect investigation, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis.
  • Ability to analyze application logs and use them to investigate unexpected behavior.
  • Basic understanding of networking fundamentals, including connectivity, online/offline behavior, and client-server communication.
  • Good understanding of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and defect management processes.
  • Experience working with Jira and test management tools such as Xray.
  • Experience working in Agile development teams.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a proactive approach to work.
  • Ability to work independently, manage testing activities, and take ownership of assigned tasks.
  • Upper-Intermediate English or higher, with the ability to communicate effectively in a professional environment.

Nice-to-have

  • Experience testing gaming applications, game launchers, or other consumer desktop software.
  • Basic knowledge of API testing.
  • Understanding of microservices architecture.
  • Experience with network troubleshooting tools and techniques.
  • Familiarity with tools for collecting and analyzing application logs.

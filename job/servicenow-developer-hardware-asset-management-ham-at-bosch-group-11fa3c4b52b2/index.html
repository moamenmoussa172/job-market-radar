About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Design, develop and enhance ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management (HAM) solutions, focusing on the physical hardware asset lifecycle from request and procurement through receiving, deployment, transfer, maintenance, retirement and disposal. Build scalable asset workflows, secure enterprise integrations and reliable asset-to-CI relationships with the CMDB.

Key Responsibilities

Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Workflows

  • HAM Development: Configure and enhance asset lifecycle processes, including request, receiving, assignment, transfer, refresh, return, retirement and disposal.
  • Stockrooms & Inventory: Develop stockroom, inventory, stock transfer and asset reconciliation workflows to improve hardware asset visibility and control.
  • Fulfilment Automation: Automate service catalog requests, approvals, procurement handoffs, fulfilment steps and asset-related notifications using Workflow Studio/Flow Designer.
  • Asset Data Quality: Implement validation, lifecycle status controls, model/category mapping and exception handling to maintain accurate hardware asset records.

Integrations, CMDB & Platform Engineering

  • Asset–CI Integration: Build and maintain reliable relationships and data synchronization between hardware assets and CMDB configuration items, including discovered device information.
  • Enterprise Integrations: Integrate approved procurement, inventory, endpoint management and Discovery data through REST APIs, IntegrationHub, import sets and transform maps.
  • ServiceNow Development: Build maintainable Business Rules, Script Includes, Glide API scripts, ACLs, reusable actions and other upgrade-safe customizations.

Quality, Governance & Production Ownership

  • Testing & Releases: Develop automated and functional tests (including ATF where appropriate), participate in peer reviews, and support controlled deployments.
  • Production Support: Investigate defects and L2/L3 issues, improve logging and documentation, and deliver sustainable fixes with stakeholders.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications

  • Experience: 7–12 years in software/platform engineering, including substantial hands-on ServiceNow development.
  • Asset & CMDB: Strong Understanding of hardware asset models, asset-to-CI relationships, Discovery integrations, data quality and operational governance.
  • HAM Specialization: Practical experience configuring or developing ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management workflows, stockrooms, inventory and asset lifecycle processes.
  • ServiceNow Development: Strong JavaScript, Glide APIs, Business Rules, Script Includes, ACLs and Flow Designer/Workflow Studio experience.
  • Integrations: Experience with REST/JSON APIs, IntegrationHub, import sets, transform maps and reusable enterprise integration patterns.
  • Engineering Delivery: Experience in technical design, debugging, ATF/functional testing, Git or version control, releases and L2/L3 support.

Additional / Preferred Experience

  • HAM Process Optimization: Experience improving stockroom operations, lifecycle controls, asset auditability, asset request fulfilment and procurement integrations.
  • Adjacent ServiceNow Capabilities: Exposure to Service Catalog, ITSM, ITOM Discovery, CMDB/CSDM or Service Graph data sources.
  • Automation & DevOps: Familiarity with Python/PowerShell, CI/CD, monitoring and automated testing for enterprise ServiceNow solutions.

Certifications

  • Mandatory: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) – Data Foundations (CMDB & CSDM).
  • Desirable: ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Hardware Asset Management (CIS-HAM).

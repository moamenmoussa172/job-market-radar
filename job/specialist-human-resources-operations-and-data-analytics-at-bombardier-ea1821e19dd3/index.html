What are your contributions to the team?

  • Manage employee data and records throughout the employee lifecycle—including hiring, promotions, transfers, terminations, and personal data updates, etc—while ensuring full compliance with country specific laws, regulations, policies, and standardized processes.
  • Provide guidance and support to requestors, assisting them in navigating data management procedures and ensuring requests are completed accurately, efficiently, and in alignment with established HR standards.
  • Identify and drive process improvement opportunities through data-driven insights and analytics to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of HR operations. Leverage HR technology solutions, including Power BI, Power Apps, and automation tools, to optimize processes, improve service delivery, and support strategic decision-making.
  • Utilize HR technology tools, such as Power BI, Power Apps, and automation solutions, to streamline workflows, enhance the employee experience, support continuous operational excellence and meet audit & compliance requirements.
  • Ensure strict adherence to the Data Privacy Policy, maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all employee information always.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities to enhance data management practices, implementing best practices, and proposing solutions to streamline workflows.
  • Partnering with other HR teams as needed to facilitate a positive employee experience.

How to thrive in this role? Skills, knowledge & experience:

  • You hold a bachelor’s degree in business administration, advanced HR analytics, business intelligence or a related field of study.
  • You have 5+ years relevant experience.
  • You are Bilingual in English. * French considered as a plus.
  • You have experience in navigating, understanding, utilizing and analyzing HR systems (e.g. SAP SuccessFactors, CMT Salesforce, ECSC)
  • You are proficient providing HR insight through data analysis tools as Power BI (DAX, data modeling, custom visuals, dashboards), Excel (advanced formulas, macros, Power Query), and Power Apps (workflow automation, app development).
  • You have the ability of conducting problem solving/kaizen initiatives including other HR team members or departments.
  • You have experience generating reports to identify deviations, set priorities, and feed KPIs for further optimization.
  • You feel motivated performing HR processes across multiple countries with diverse employee types.
  • You are able to lead team projects impacting data and other HR teams, driving continuous improvement and innovation.

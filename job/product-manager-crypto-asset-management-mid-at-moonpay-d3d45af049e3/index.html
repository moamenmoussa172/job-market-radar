About the Opportunity

In this role, you will join MoonPay’s Crypto Asset Management team as a Product Manager, helping us build and scale the liquidity and asset infrastructure that supports products across MoonPay.

The team is responsible for how liquidity is sourced and distributed across our platform, supporting high-value crypto transaction volumes across multiple assets, venues, and blockchains. As our remit expands across products including Ramps, Swaps, and Enterprise, the volume and complexity of product work is growing with it.

You will take ownership of day-to-day product execution across our on-chain and off-chain workstreams, helping turn business and technical requirements into clearly defined, prioritised, and executable work.

Working closely with the Principal Product Manager, Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Finance, Treasury, and commercial teams, you will support initiatives including liquidity-provider integrations, asset availability, routing and best-execution capabilities, custody and wallet infrastructure, and the broader on-chain services required to operate reliably at scale.

Your work will help the team move faster, improve the efficiency and resilience of our liquidity infrastructure, and enable MoonPay to support significantly more products and transaction volume as the business grows.

This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys solving complex problems, wants to deepen their understanding of crypto and financial infrastructure, and is excited to develop their product craft in a fast-moving, high-impact environment.

What You Will Do

  • Own day-to-day product execution across Crypto Asset Management initiatives, translating business, technical, operational, and compliance requirements into clear product requirements, user stories, project plans, and delivery milestones.
  • Support integrations with new liquidity sources, including centralised exchanges, OTC trading desks, and decentralised services, helping expand MoonPay’s liquidity network and improve sourcing efficiency, reliability, and execution.
  • Coordinate asset availability and infrastructure initiatives across Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Finance, Treasury, Commercial, and other Product teams, ensuring dependencies, decisions, risks, and requirements are understood and tracked through delivery.
  • Support the setup and evolution of technical infrastructure across areas such as wallets, gas sponsorship, transaction broadcasting, and other on-chain infrastructure services, working closely with engineering and operational teams to translate requirements into scalable product solutions.
  • Support roadmap development and prioritisation, using data, commercial value, operational requirements, customer impact, risk, and engineering constraints to help determine which initiatives should be addressed first.
  • Contribute to the evolution of our liquidity and asset-management infrastructure, including custody and wallet capabilities, liquidity routing, best execution, settlement efficiency, monitoring, and risk-management tooling.
  • Use data to evaluate product performance and opportunities, identifying friction, operational inefficiencies, or areas where product and infrastructure improvements can create measurable value for MoonPay and its customers.

About You

Must-have experience and skills

  • You have experience working in product management, product operations, treasury, finance, business analysis, technical programme delivery, or a similar role, and can take a problem from initial requirements through to structured execution.
  • You are comfortable working across cross-functional teams, particularly with Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Finance, Treasury, or Commercial stakeholders, and can turn complex or ambiguous requirements into clear actions and priorities.
  • You have strong product thinking and analytical skills, with the ability to understand trade-offs, use data to support decisions, and prioritise work based on business and customer impact.
  • You have a genuine interest in crypto, fintech, or financial infrastructure and are motivated to build a deeper understanding of areas such as blockchain networks, liquidity, exchanges, custody, wallets, and digital-asset infrastructure.

Nice-to-have experience

  • Previous exposure to crypto or fintech infrastructure, particularly liquidity management, exchange or OTC integrations, stablecoins, custody, wallets, or DeFi protocols.

Bonus Points

  • Familiarity with regulated financial products, crypto compliance requirements, blockchain infrastructure, or products handling high-value financial transactions.

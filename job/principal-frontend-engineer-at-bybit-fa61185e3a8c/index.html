About the job

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 90 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the technical planning and development of unified front-end frameworks and compliance solutions across business units. Define reusable architectures, components, integration standards, and technical roadmaps, and drive their adoption across different business lines.
  • Oversee To C and To B internationalization and multilingual solutions, addressing content and presentation differences across multiple websites and regions, while improving compliance site delivery efficiency and local search visibility.
  • Collaborate with Compliance, Product, and business teams to translate confirmed compliance requirements into configurable, verifiable, and traceable front-end capabilities. Lead the technical design and continuous evolution of the compliance configuration platform.
  • Drive the practical application of AI in business workflows, including AI-powered compliance configuration tagging, configuration validation, and multilingual quality checks. Establish evaluation datasets, accuracy measurement criteria, human review processes, and feedback loops to continuously improve performance, with a target of achieving over 95% accuracy in intelligent tagging.
  • Provide technical leadership for the Operations & Growth Front-End team. Coordinate project priorities and cross-team dependencies, mentor engineers, support their professional development, and proactively identify and resolve delivery risks.

Qualifications

  • Strong front-end engineering skills in TypeScript, React, and related technologies, with proven experience in designing and delivering cross-project frameworks, configuration platforms, or large-scale business systems.
  • Hands-on experience with internationalization and multilingual solutions for To C / To B products, with the ability to balance business integration efficiency, maintainability, and regional requirements.
  • Practical experience implementing AI capabilities in real business workflows. Able to design evaluation metrics, analyze error cases, and improve performance through a combination of rules, models, and human review. Experience limited to using AI coding assistants alone does not meet this requirement.
  • Experience as a technical lead or in team leadership, with the ability to collaborate effectively with Compliance, Product, Backend, Data, and other stakeholders to turn ambiguous requirements into actionable and deliverable solutions.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in fintech, trading platforms, or compliance-related businesses. Familiarity with multi-site SEO, configuration governance, and AI-assisted review or intelligent annotation systems is a plus.
  • Fluent in both Chinese and English, with the ability to communicate effectively in English in day-to-day business settings.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

Our Mission:

Omnea is the agentic operating system for procurement.

Procurement touches every pound a business spends, yet requests still stall for weeks and approvals get lost in inboxes. Our AI-first platform changes that: our agents capture requests, route approvals, onboard suppliers, flag risk and track every renewal, while anyone in the business can ask a question and get an answer grounded in their own company’s data. Buying becomes painless for everyone, procurement gets a seat at the table, and our customers have saved more than 100,000 hours of manual work so far.

Companies like Spotify, PayPal, T. Rowe Price, JustEat, Adyen and S&P Global run procurement on Omnea. We’re backed by $75M from Khosla Ventures, Insight Partners and Accel, and we’ve been named the UK’s number one startup in the Startups 100, ranked 11th on Sifted’s 2026 list of Europe’s fastest-growing companies, and recognised as one of Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies.

Talent density matters more to us than almost anything else. We keep the bar high in every team, so everyone here has real ownership and real impact. Our team is full of former founders and operators who’ve scaled unicorns, and we’re looking for people who want to do the best work of their careers alongside them.

Find out more about the team and life at Omnea here.

The Role

We're hiring a Customer Success Engineer to join the Technical Solutions team, the small team that keeps Omnea working for our customers every day. This role is based in London. This is a customer-facing role with a technical problem-solving heart: when an integration breaks, a workflow misbehaves, or a customer needs Omnea to do something it doesn't do yet, you help find the root cause and the right solution. We resolve around 95% of what comes to us ourselves, from straightforward fixes to changes in production code and small feature requests. You'll be trusted to work out what's really wrong and see it through, drawing on the team's support when you need it.

You'll go deep on the product and broad on the problems: an integration misbehaving with a customer's ERP in the morning, a feature a customer has been asking for after lunch, and helping a colleague through a tough customer conversation by the end of the day. You'll be a trusted voice customers can turn to, joining their calls, providing clear answers and working closely with the Account Management team to set them up for long-term success with Omnea. AI helps us investigate faster, tracing logs, drafting hypotheses and suggesting fixes, but people own the outcome. You'll use your judgment, and the team's expertise where needed, to make sure solutions are safe for a live enterprise environment, address the root cause and surface patterns Product needs to hear about.

The CSE team sits between Customer, Product and Engineering. It's a small, close-knit team with a lot of autonomy, and the scope of the role grows as Omnea does. There is room to lean into your strengths, whether that's guiding customers towards solutions or bringing their feedback into the product, while building your technical skills. You'll develop deep knowledge of Omnea, and your work will be felt by customers every day.

What You'll Do

  • Investigate the hard problems. One issue might be an integration failing with a customer's ERP, the next a workflow that isn't doing what anyone expected, the next a cross-system bug nobody else has cracked. You'll use AI to speed up the first pass, then confirm the cause and get it fixed.
  • Make solutions happen. Own issues through to resolution, whether that means guiding a customer through a solution, improving a workflow or working on a code change. You'll have opportunities to fix bugs and build small customer-requested features in the codebase, with support from the team as you grow. Depending on your strengths, you might lean further into customer guidance or into shaping and improving the product.
  • Back up our Implementation and Customer teams. When a colleague hits a tricky configuration, an edge case or a question they can't answer, they come to you. You'll help them think it through, step into live implementations when they get technical, and make sure the customer gets the right answer.
  • Stay close to customers. You'll have ongoing calls with some customers, join QBRs and escalation calls, get brought in on specialist projects, and represent the Technical Solutions Team when customers move from implementation to ongoing support.
  • Bring the customer's voice into Product. You'll hear what customers struggle with before anyone else does. Spot the patterns across the issues you resolve and turn them into clear, well-argued input for Product, so the same problem doesn't need solving twice.
  • Make it scale. As we grow, we can't just add people. You'll build the tooling, automation, playbooks and docs that help customers solve more on their own and let the team handle more without slowing down.

About You

  • You have at least 3 years of relevant experience, for example in technical support engineering, solutions engineering, implementation, software development or another technical, customer-facing role.
  • You're curious. You want to know how things work and why they broke, and every problem is a chance to learn something.
  • You solve problems analytically. You enjoy messy, ambiguous issues, and you don't stop at the symptom when you can find the root cause.
  • You're comfortable switching context. A bug fix, a customer call and a colleague's question can all land in the same hour, and you can move between them without losing the thread.
  • You want variety. You'd rather handle ten different problems than one big project.
  • You take ownership. You work independently, raise your hand for the hard problems, and see them through to resolution without needing the spotlight.
  • You care about customers, about getting it right, and about the team around you. You're inspired by the idea of unreasonable hospitality: going beyond the expected to make people feel genuinely looked after.
  • You explain technical issues clearly, whether you're talking to an engineer or a non-technical stakeholder.
  • You're not afraid of the technical side. You don't need a CS or STEM degree: technical aptitude can come from your work, side projects or things you've built yourself. Hands-on experience with APIs, SQL or integrations helps, and you'll pick up the codebase with AI, the docs and the team behind you.
  • You use AI tools to move faster, with the judgment to know what to trust and what to double-check.

Junior UX Requirements Specialist

About the job

Ubisoft is looking for a Junior QC Specialist to join our UX Requirements Team in Kyiv. You will operate across multiple gaming platforms, support production teams in resolving bugs, and play a key role in implementing user experience-related features.

Please note, that position is office-based in Kyiv.

Responsibilities

  • Execute epilepsy, sound, and input lag tests, using dedicated software and hardware, and verify that all Ubisoft titles are compliant with the guidelines;
  • Operate multiple platforms (PC, Luna, X1, PS4, XsX, PS5, NX);
  • Support production teams in fixing bugs and implementing UXR related features of the game;
  • Investigate, reproduce, report and track UXR issues on the projects
  • Manage projects following established team actions and processes (planning and coordinating tests, raising project risks, ensuring proper test coverage);
  • Work closely with the team and different project stakeholders (production, QC etc.) providing them with relevant feedback on the project status and quality, providing visibility, and obtaining required information;
  • Create various documentation and reports based on the project needs.

Job requirements:

  • Strong motivation to work and develop in the video game industry;
  • Upper intermediate level of English or higher (spoken and written);
  • Good knowledge and understanding of testing theory
  • Strong interest in, and genuine passion for video games
  • Good knowledge of PC and console hardware
  • Good troubleshooting skills;
  • Solid understanding of gated project management workflows;
  • Experience working with issue and project tracking systems (for example: JIRA);
  • Strong communication (ability to develop and sustain a professional network) and problem-solving skills;
  • Good organizational, time management and prioritization skills;
  • Ability to work both autonomously and as a team player;
  • Proactivity and ability to multitask;
  • Resourcefulness and ability to handle challenging situations;
  • Openness to change and self-improvement;

Would be a plus:

  • Hands-on experience with several gaming platforms;
  • Experience with multiple projects;
  • Experience in coordinating tasks;
  • Involvement in test case creation/adjustment;
  • Any sound-related background;
  • Any experience with epilepsy tests.

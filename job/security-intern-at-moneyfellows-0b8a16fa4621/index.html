About the job

Money Fellows is transforming how people save, borrow, and invest. As the world’s first and largest Money Circles app, we’ve digitized the traditional ROSCA model to make it smarter, safer, and more accessible. 💸
Since 2016, we’ve empowered over 10+ million users to take control of their financial futures with confidence. 🚀

  • Support the monitoring of security logs, alerts, and system activities to identify potential security threats and report suspicious activities to the team.
  • Assist in conducting basic vulnerability scans and security assessments, documenting identified weaknesses, and following up on remediation activities.
  • Support the Cybersecurity team in monitoring and maintaining security tools and solutions, including firewalls, endpoint protection, SIEM, and intrusion detection systems.
  • Assist in documenting and investigating security incidents under the guidance of senior team members, contributing to initial analysis and incident records.
  • Support the documentation and updating of cybersecurity policies, procedures, technical configurations, and security-related records.
  • Assist in preparing cybersecurity awareness materials and supporting internal security audits and compliance activities.
  • Collaborate with IT, Development, and Infrastructure teams to understand security requirements and support the application of basic security practices across systems and applications.
  • Only GIU students graduating next semester are eligible to apply.
  • Basic understanding of cybersecurity principles, including network security, application security, encryption, and identity and access management.
  • Basic knowledge of security monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and incident response concepts.
  • Familiarity with cybersecurity tools such as SIEM solutions, vulnerability scanners, firewalls, and endpoint protection systems is an advantage.
  • Basic understanding of networking concepts, operating systems, and system administration.
  • Awareness of cloud security concepts and platforms such as AWS or Azure is an advantage.
  • Basic knowledge of cybersecurity standards and frameworks such as ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, and GDPR.
  • Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, and attention to detail.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with technical teams.

We’re hiring a Staff Machine Learning Engineer to help advance the future of ads ranking at Pinterest. The Ads Lightweight Ranking team, also known as L1, sits before the final ranking (L2): it scores a very large pool of eligible ads and selects a smaller, high-value set for downstream ranking, all within a tight latency budget.

In this role, you’ll lead technical direction for lightweight ranking models across engagement, conversion and other supported objectives. You’ll help the team make ranking more capable and relevant while preserving serving efficiency, reliability and alignment with downstream L2 decisions. Current planning explores richer model architectures and signals, improving funnel alignment, and more efficient training and serving; priorities may evolve as experiments and business needs change.
You’ll own complex ML problems from framing and model design through training, evaluation and production readiness. You’ll remain hands-on with modeling, code and technical analysis while setting a roadmap, guiding project execution and helping engineers deliver high-quality, maintainable solutions. As the technical lead for your area, you’ll work across Ads Infra and ML serving partners to align model requirements, dependencies and launches; infrastructure implementation is coordinated with those partner teams.

This is an opportunity to shape a high-leverage ranking stage where model quality, Pinner relevance and advertiser outcomes must work together with scale, latency and cost. You’ll influence the technical roadmap beyond your own code, grow other engineers through mentorship and help the team turn experimentation into reliable production impact.

What will you be doing:

  • Own the L1 modeling technical strategy and roadmap for a meaningful area of the team; translate ambiguous ranking and business problems into a sequence of measurable, production-ready ML improvements.
  • Lead end-to-end model work: formulate objectives and prediction targets, assess data and features, shape model architectures and ranking utility, and guide training, offline evaluation, online experimentation and production rollout.
  • Make and explain quality–latency–cost–reliability trade-offs for models that score a large candidate pool. Improve capability and efficiency without compromising downstream L2 alignment or dependable operation.
  • Stay hands-on in modeling, code, technical analysis and critical reviews; establish a high bar for scalable, testable, maintainable production solutions.
  • Lead projects across contributors and partner teams, set milestones, resolve technical uncertainties, manage dependencies and communicate decisions, risks and progress to technical and cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Partner with Infra team on model-side requirements and coordinated launches.
  • Mentor engineers across levels, improve shared technical practices and documentation, and raise the quality and efficiency of the team’s development and evaluation workflows.
  • Use AI to accelerate appropriate work such as exploring modeling alternatives, summarizing experiment results, drafting analysis or automating repeatable checks; validate outputs and remain accountable for the final model, code and decisions.

What we’re looking for:

  • Minimum 7 years of industry experience in ML engineering / applied ML / software engineering.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead 0→1 ML efforts: taking ambiguous problem spaces, defining the approach, and delivering a production system with measurable impact.
  • Strong track record ML-powered systems in domains such as recommendation, ranking, retrieval, content understanding, ads, or adjacent areas with clear product impact.
  • Deep experience with evaluation and measurement: dataset strategy, labeling/review operations, metric design, regression testing, and connecting offline improvements to online outcomes.
  • Strong systems design skills building data- and ML-intensive systems, with the ability to navigate tradeoffs in performance, reliability, scalability, and cost.
  • Strong coding, review and systems judgment sufficient to remain hands-on and set a high quality bar.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to influence technical direction across teams without directly owning every implementation detail.
  • Demonstrated experience building and enhancing cross-functional partnerships with other teams and organizations.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field—or equivalent practical experience.

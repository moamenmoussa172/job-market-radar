About the Role

At Adyen, the safety and security of our people, workplaces and assets is a global priority. Our Physical Safety & Security program protects a growing international organization through a risk-based, technology-enabled approach.

Reporting to the Global Head of Physical Safety & Security, you will be the technical owner and subject matter expert for Adyen’s global physical safety and security technology environment. This includes access control and credentialing, video surveillance, intrusion detection, visitor management, emergency communication and emerging AI-enabled technologies.

You will be the primary technical point of contact for physical safety and security systems across Adyen’s global office network. You will help shape technology strategy, define technical standards, design solutions for new and existing offices, manage system administration and troubleshooting, and lead technical integrations, migrations and deployments.

For new offices, relocations and major workplace projects, you will work with Workplace, IT, contractors and specialist partners to define the physical safety and security technology architecture from design through implementation. This includes system selection, infrastructure and networking requirements, device placement, integrations, commissioning, testing and handover.

This is a hands-on global role. You will ensure systems remain reliable, secure and scalable while improving automation, integrations and AI-enabled capabilities.

What You’ll Do

Physical Safety & Security Systems

  • Own the technical lifecycle of Adyen’s physical safety and security systems, from architecture and configuration through deployment, upgrades, maintenance and decommissioning.
  • Manage and support enterprise access control, credentialing and badging, CCTV, intrusion detection, visitor management, intercoms and related technologies.
  • Support technology interfaces with fire and life-safety systems, emergency communication, mass notification and emergency response systems.
  • Ensure operational availability, reliability and appropriate configuration of physical safety and security platforms.
  • Manage system administration, device onboarding, configuration and technical dependencies.
  • Troubleshoot hardware, software, network and system issues and coordinate resolution with IT, integrators and external vendors.
  • Manage technical system incidents, provide timely updates, identify root causes and drive corrective actions.
  • Maintain accurate system documentation, architecture diagrams, technical standards and asset information.

Security Technology Architecture & Design

  • Design physical safety and security technology solutions for new offices, relocations, expansions and major workplace projects.
  • Translate security requirements, risk assessments and business needs into practical technical designs.
  • Define requirements for access control, CCTV, intrusion detection, visitor management, emergency communication and supporting infrastructure.
  • Ensure safety and security systems work together effectively during emergency scenarios.
  • Review technical drawings, system designs and vendor proposals.
  • Work with Workplace, IT, contractors and security integrators throughout design and implementation.
  • Lead technical commissioning, testing, acceptance and handover.
  • Develop and maintain global physical safety and security technology standards and reference architectures.

Access Control & Identity

  • Own the technical administration of Adyen’s global access-control environment, including Genea and related technologies.
  • Create and maintain access profiles, groups, permissions and system configurations.
  • Support employee access issues, credential problems, reader failures and routine access-control requests.
  • Partner with IT and HR on identity and employee lifecycle integrations.
  • Improve automation around joiner, mover and leaver processes.
  • Drive consistent access-control architecture and configuration across Adyen’s global offices.

Integrations, Automation & AI

  • Develop and support integrations between physical safety and security platforms and enterprise systems.
  • Work with APIs, cloud platforms, automation tools and identity systems to improve security operations and employee experience.
  • Identify opportunities to automate repetitive physical safety and security processes.
  • Support the development, testing and deployment of AI-enabled physical safety and security capabilities.
  • Work with internal technology teams and vendors to maintain and troubleshoot AI-enabled solutions.
  • Use system data, operational metrics and incident trends to identify issues, measure effectiveness and prioritize improvements.
  • Conduct technical research and proof-of-concept testing for emerging security technologies.

Projects, Migrations & Resilience

  • Lead the technical delivery of physical safety and security technology projects across Adyen offices.
  • Support new office openings, relocations, expansions, migrations and technology upgrades.
  • Plan and execute software updates, firmware upgrades, patches and configuration changes.
  • Ensure appropriate testing, validation and rollback procedures for critical changes.
  • Plan and execute migrations from legacy platforms to Adyen’s strategic systems.
  • Support backup, recovery, failover and business continuity arrangements for critical systems.

Operational Support & Investigations

  • Provide first-line technical support for routine physical safety and security technology requests across Adyen offices.
  • Troubleshoot access-control, CCTV, alarm, emergency communication and visitor-management issues.
  • Support physical security investigations through access records, video, system data and other technical evidence.
  • Provide technical guidance and support to Workplace and other dependent teams.
  • Escalate security incidents, policy questions, material risks and decisions requiring senior judgement through the appropriate channels.

Vendor & Partner Management

  • Act as the technical point of contact for physical safety and security technology vendors, integrators and specialist providers.
  • Review technical proposals, designs, statements of work and implementation plans.
  • Support vendor selection, technical evaluations and due diligence.
  • Coordinate maintenance, troubleshooting and project delivery with external partners.
  • Monitor technical performance and service levels and drive resolution of issues.

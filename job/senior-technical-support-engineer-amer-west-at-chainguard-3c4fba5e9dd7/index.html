About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Chainguard is the trusted source for open source. By delivering hardened, secure, and production-ready builds of all the open source software engineers and AI agents rely on, Chainguard helps organizations build faster, stay compliant, and eliminate risk.
Our customers include Fortune 500 enterprises and global industry leaders, including Anduril, Canva, Fortinet, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, OpenAI, Snap Inc., and Snowflake.
Chainguard is venture-backed by leading investors, including Amplify, IVP, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Mantis VC, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Spark Capital.

The role in a nutshell
Chainguard customers run our Containers, Libraries, and OS packages in their production software supply chains. When something breaks, you own it end to end: reproduce the problem, isolate root cause, and fix what you can, including shipping the fix yourself as a pull request. When you can’t, you give Engineering an escalation they can act on without follow-up questions. You’ll work across Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, CI/CD, and cloud registries, using AI Agentic tooling as a normal part of how you work, with the judgment to know when it’s wrong.

What makes Chainguard Support different?

Chainguard has a dedicated CSM team who are very technical and work closely with our customs on setup, onboarding, renewals and access to name but a few areas. This allows our support engineers to focus on engineering work.

In Chainguard, Engineering and Support Engineering are one org, with a single reporting chain. Success is shared and customer satisfaction is a common goal throughout the whole organisation.

No secret sauce in engineering. In Chainguard, the engineering codebase is not a closed shop. Support has full access to the codebase and all related tools and all engineering teams. This is another advantage of Engineering and Support Engineering being a single organisation.

We operate a boomerang program where Support engineers work with engineering teams or vice versa for 2 week stints to share knowledge and get to know our neighbours!

Of all customer issues that need an engineering fix, currently 25% of these are fixed by Support engineering and we are working to increase this month over month!

What you’ll do

  • Own complex customer issues end to end, including ambiguous ones that span the customer’s CI, registry, runtime, and our images
  • Escalate with precision (environment, repro steps, evidence, working hypothesis) and raise the bar on escalation quality across the team
  • Ship fixes, not just tickets: open reviewable PRs for package and image definitions, docs, diagnostic tooling, and scoped bug fixes
  • Communicate clearly under pressure with platform engineers, security teams, and non-technical stakeholders; manage SLAs and set honest expectations
  • Write KB articles, runbooks, and diagnostic guides built for both human readers and AI retrieval
  • Turn ticket patterns into data-backed bug reports and product feedback, and reduce toil through scripts and automation
  • Act as technical point person on high-severity issues and mentor TSEs on debugging and safe AI use
  • Participate in the on-call rotation for after-hours, weekend, and holiday support

How we use AI in Support

  • Parse logs, compare manifests and SBOMs, create repros, assist Dockerfile migrations, and draft responses
  • Be the human in the loop and verify every AI-generated command, version, config, and CVE claim before it reaches a customer
  • Respect data-handling boundaries for customer data, credentials, and regulated information
  • Fix the factory: correct incorrect answers, contribute to evaluation sets, and build agentic workflows where they create real leverage

What we’re looking for

Required

  • 5+ years of enterprise technical support engineering in production environments, or equivalent SRE/DevOps experience with significant customer-facing work
  • 4+ years hands-on with Linux, containers, and Kubernetes, including runtime-level debugging (shell-less images, permissions, package management, dynamic linking, entrypoints)
  • Bash plus Python or Go; comfortable with Git, PR workflows, and code review
  • Working experience with CI/CD, Helm, Terraform, and at least one major cloud (GCP, AWS, Azure)
  • Regular use of AI tools with a verification discipline you can explain, including a time you caught it being wrong
  • Experience in creating and orchestrating AI agents and developing skills to accelerate support velocity
  • Strong written communication, especially escalation summaries and customer-facing explanations and RCAs.

Preferred

  • Software supply chain security: SBOMs, Sigstore/cosign, SLSA, Grype/Trivy, CVE triage
  • Open source contributions in packaging (melange, apko, APK), container builds, or the Kubernetes ecosystem
  • CKA, CKAD, CKS, or KCSA; startup experience; STEM degree or equivalent experience

How success is measured

  • Escalation quality
  • P1/P2 time to resolution
  • Merged PRs that resolve issues or eliminate recurring tickets
  • Reuse of your KB contributions
  • Measurable toil reduction
  • Impact through initiative deliverables

About the job

About Gusto

At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve.

All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy.

AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process.

About the Role:

As a Staff Engineer, you will play a key role in building new products and scaling existing ones to further evolve Gusto’s personal finance platform. Your work will focus on improving financial tools for everyday employees, helping them reduce financial stress, build healthy money habits, and gain greater control over their paychecks. You will collaborate with a talented cross-functional team, contributing technical expertise throughout the product development lifecycle—from ideation and architecture to development, release, and long-term scaling.

About the Team:

The Consumer Money team is on a mission to help employees achieve financial peace of mind by bridging employment and personal finances. The team's product suite gives workers complete control over their money—including on-demand pay, high-yield cash accounts, and automated savings—all anchored to the paycheck relationship Gusto uniquely powers.

Operating as one of the fastest-growing engines within Gusto Money, the team builds at a fast, startup-like pace. Beyond scaling core worker-facing products, the team is actively incubating 0-to-1 foundational technologies to power Gusto’s broader financial platform vision. Handling people’s livelihoods in a regulated banking space requires combining rapid innovation with deep technical rigor, system reliability, and an uncompromised focus on user experience. This role represents a high-impact opportunity to help shape the future of personal and platform finance at Gusto.

Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day:

  • Build products & services and scale the platform that powers consumer money and accounting experiences at Gusto
  • Take ownership of projects, ensuring they meet high standards of quality and performance
  • Adopt the latest best practices for using AI technologies across all aspects of product development
  • Collaborate on complex and ambiguous problems with partnerships from Engineering, Product Management, Design, Data Science, Compliance, Operations, and other cross-functional teams
  • Engage in a highly supportive environment working with others to drive productivity and innovation
  • Help drive the product development process from concept to launch

Here’s what we're looking for:

  • 8+ years of professional software development experience
  • Proven track record architecting, building, and testing large-scale, resilient distributed systems and software platforms
  • High-agency ownership: a self-starter who independently takes ambiguous workstreams and drives them to the finish line, rather than waiting for assigned tasks
  • Technical leadership experience guiding multiple engineers through large, complex initiatives from concept to delivery
  • Proactive cross-functional partnership, comfortably driving alignment with Product, Design, and other functions to resolve ambiguity
  • Fluency with modern AI developer tooling, paired with an enthusiasm for modeling and mentoring the team on AI-assisted development practices
  • An aptitude for bringing clarity and simplicity to ambiguous, fast-moving environments with tight iteration cycles
  • Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to distill technical complexity and guide well-reasoned architectural and product tradeoffs

Nice, but not required:

  • Experience in the Fintech industry building scalable backend systems to power financial services and products
  • Experience with banking, lending, or personal financial systems and familiarity with these financial workflows
  • Ruby/Ruby on Rails, GraphQL, React, Typescript, AWS

Please note that a two day travel to a Gusto office for onboarding is required for this position. If you have any questions regarding this requirement, please share with your recruiter.

Our cash compensation amount for this role is targeted at $197,000–$247,000 for San Francisco. Stock equity is additional. Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors including candidate experience and expertise and may vary from the amounts listed above.

At Gusto, we don't use titles the traditional way, as a signal of rank or hierarchy. Instead, our titles describe two things at once — your craft (what you do, e.g. Engineering, Design) and your scope of ownership (what you're accountable for) — which is why you'll see something like "Head of X" instead of a rank word like "Director" that tells you neither. Because of this, the title on this posting may look different from what you'd expect based on the scope and seniority of the role, and from how similar roles are typically titled elsewhere. If you have questions about how this role compares to your current title or level, your recruiter can help translate.

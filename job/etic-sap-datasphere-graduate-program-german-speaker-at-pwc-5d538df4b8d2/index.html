Job Description & Summary

For our Transformation - AI Data Engineering & Analytics business unit, we’re looking for you as a Data Analytics & AI Trainee.

Individuality – With an 12-months training plan tailored specifically to you, you’ll be optimally prepared for a career as a business analytics consultant in the SAP environment. The graduate program will include, for example, technical and technological training, workshops, and work methods, culminating in SAP certification.

Analytics – You’ll derive the requirements for business analytics solutions from business processes and develop comprehensive applications. Our consulting teams provide support throughout all phases of project execution: from requirements and needs analysis to the development of business concepts, all the way through to implementation.

Consulting Role – We advise our clients with a focus on SAP technologies such as the SAP Business AI Platform, Reporting and Planning, SAP Cloud Computing, SAP Business Analytics, Big Data, Planning, and Predictive Analytics.

What You Bring to the Table

  • You have successfully completed your degree in (Business) Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
  • You are passionate about exploring the fields of Business and Analytics and Business AI and about developing solutions with our clients in this environment.
  • Ideally, you have some initial experience in the field of Business and Analytics or Business AI, such as through internships or student work.
  • You have good spoken German (at least Level B1) and very good written and spoken English (at least Level B2-C1).

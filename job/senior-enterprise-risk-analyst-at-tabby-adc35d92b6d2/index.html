About the job

About Us:

Runaway interest, fine print and limited access have left many behind, feeling shut out of a system that wasn't setting them up for success.
We're building a different kind of system—in a complete money app to send, spend and save. One that's clear, flexible and puts people and businesses in control. About the Role:
The role supports the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) function by keeping its core processes running to a reliable cycle — risk and incident records, policy administration, new product approval coordination and the reporting calendar — so that the risk information reaching senior management and committees is complete, accurate and on time. The role works closely with the ERM Manager and first-line stakeholders across the business.

  • Support the quarterly risk reporting cycle, including collection and validation of metrics, pack production and committee materials.
  • Maintain risk registers, incident records and action trackers, ensuring entries are complete, current and supported by evidence.
  • Administer the policy programme — tracking review and approval status, formatting, version control and the governance calendar.
  • Support the risk and control self-assessment cycle — scheduling checks, chasing evidence, and populating workpapers and trackers.
  • Support the maintenance of the risk appetite framework and key risk indicators, including data sourcing and validation with metric owners.
  • Support ERM training and awareness activity, including delivery logistics, materials and completion records.
  • Work closely with first-line stakeholders to collect, chase and quality-check risk information, escalating gaps and delays promptly.
  • Support internal audit and regulatory requests by preparing documentation and audit-ready evidence.
  • Support ERM governance and tooling, including use and administration of GRC tools and maintenance of documentation.
  • 2–4 years' experience in risk, GRC, compliance, audit support or a related operational role within Financial Services, FinTech, Payments or another regulated environment.
  • Working knowledge of risk registers, risk reporting and control documentation, with the ability to apply concepts pragmatically.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to run several recurring cycles in parallel and keep each to deadline.
  • Confident coordinating across functions and seniority levels, including remotely, and comfortable chasing stakeholders for information.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, including experience preparing clear risk reports and presentations.
  • Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint; hands-on experience using or supporting GRC tools a strong advantage.

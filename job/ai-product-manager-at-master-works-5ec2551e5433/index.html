About the job

Nabeh, a subsidiary of Master Works, delivers AI-powered solutions that help organizations automate operations, boost efficiency, and drive innovation. Its expertise spans computer vision, LLM applications, speech-to-text, data intelligence, and low-code AI solutions.

Nabeh is looking for an AI Product Manager to lead the strategy, development, and growth of AI-powered products, translating business and customer needs into scalable solutions using Generative AI, LLMs, computer vision, and AI agents.

Key Responsibilities

Product Strategy & Ownership

• Own the product lifecycle from discovery and ideation through MVP, launch, adoption, and continuous improvement.

• Define product vision, roadmaps, positioning, and measurable business objectives.

• Translate customer needs and market opportunities into product requirements and prioritized backlogs.

• Develop product strategies for B2B and enterprise AI solutions.

• Identify opportunities to transform customer-specific AI use cases into reusable product capabilities.

AI Product Development

• Collaborate with engineering teams to define features powered by LLMs, VLMs, RAG, computer vision, and autonomous AI agents.

• Evaluate AI capabilities based on feasibility, model accuracy, latency, operating cost, and business impact.

• Define acceptance criteria, evaluation metrics, and quality standards for AI-driven features.

• Understand the trade-offs between hosted AI models, local deployment, and hybrid architectures.

• Prioritize AI product improvements using customer feedback, usage analytics, and competitive research.

Product Execution

• Prepare PRDs, user stories, wireframes, acceptance criteria, and release plans.

• Work with UI/UX designers to deliver intuitive experiences for technically complex AI products.

• Coordinate product releases, demonstrations, UAT, and go-to-market readiness.

• Define KPIs for adoption, retention, feature usage, model quality, and commercial performance.

• Maintain product documentation, release notes, and feature specifications.

Commercial & Customer Collaboration

• Work with business development and presales teams to support product demonstrations and technical positioning.

• Analyze competing AI products, market trends, and customer requirements.

• Support product pricing, packaging, licensing, and commercialization strategies.

• Ensure products address enterprise security, privacy, and Saudi regulatory expectations.

Requirements

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

• 4+ years of product management experience, including AI-enabled or enterprise software products.

• Strong knowledge of Agile product development, backlog management, and product discovery.

• Practical understanding of Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, APIs, and AI integration patterns.

• Ability to communicate effectively with AI engineers and evaluate technical trade-offs.

• Experience creating PRDs, product roadmaps, and measurable success criteria.

• Strong analytical, stakeholder management, and problem-solving skills.

• Experience managing B2B SaaS or enterprise technology products.

• Strong English communication skills.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

• Experience with AI infrastructure, model inference platforms, MLOps, or LLMOps.

• Familiarity with computer vision, video analytics, or speech technologies.

• Experience with AI platforms such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Vertex AI, or open-source models.

• Knowledge of Saudi enterprise and government technology requirements.

• Experience with product pricing, API-based monetization, or usage-based business models.

• Arabic language proficiency.

SUCCESS METRICS

• Product roadmap delivery and release predictability.

• Product adoption and customer satisfaction.

• Feature usage and measurable customer value.

• Reduction in time from concept to production.

• Product revenue contribution and commercial readiness.

• Quality and completeness of product requirements and documentation.

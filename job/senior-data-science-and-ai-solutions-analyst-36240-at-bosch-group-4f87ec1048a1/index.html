About the job

As GS, we are a five-star service provider who offers services that enable people at Bosch to create technology that is invented for life. In short: "Invented for life. Empowered by us."
By using agile working methods our multicultural, cross functional purpose teams are the innovation hub for the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process to provide our Internal Business Partners state of the art services. In P2P we are focusing on: Procure to Order, Purchase request to purchase order optimization, Supplier master data and material master data, Invoice management and Data Analytics-,Data Mining- and Robotics As a data scientist in GS/P2P Analytics and Automation Hub, you are responsible for:

Advanced Analytics

  • You translate business needs into data driven technical solutions for the analysis and processing of diverse data (invoices, purchase orders, quotations etc.).
  • You work on data visualization for process transparency and KPI tracking
  • You provide technical consultancy and support to other teams in analytics and AI tools

Development, Improvement and Maintenance of data solutions:

  • You continuously develop solutions for collecting data with APIs, bots, web scraping tools, etc.
  • You ensure performance and correct orchestration of our AI Solutions and continuously improve them
  • You process (extract, clean, transform) the data in Python from different systems and make them available for different data solutions.
  • You develop and deploy APIs on cloud computing platforms
  • You analyze the technical processes, define optimization points and further enhance AI solutions to reduce manual work and increase automation rate and efficiency

Innovation and Enablement

  • You share knowledge by clearly articulating results and ideas through presentations and reports to customers, teammates, and key stakeholders.
  • You represent P2P in committees on the subject of data analytics, including trend monitoring and best practice sharing
  • You develop our new AI Framework together with the team.
  • You are encouraged and supported to stay up to date with latest research and technology trends

Minimum Qualifications

  • Experience as data scientist, AI expert or relevant fields
  • Proven expertise in building data driven and AI-powered solutions for challenging real world data sets
  • Proficiency in Python, SQL, data visualization tools such as PowerBI, Tableau
  • Languages: Fluent in English written and spoken
  • Enthusiasm: Passion for machine learning, natural language processing and statistical modelling in
    the field of Business Intelligence (BI) and Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in containerization, API development, distributed computing (Openshift,
    Kubernetes), Azure cloud or MLOps is very welcomed.

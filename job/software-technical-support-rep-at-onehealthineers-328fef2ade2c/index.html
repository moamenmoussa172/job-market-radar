About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.
Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

Completes technical-oriented service tasks of various scopes and supports in planning and managing these tasks.

Looking for an IT support career where the work you do can genuinely make a difference to people’s lives?

This is a fantastic opportunity to join Varian and be part of a team supporting healthcare professionals across the UK & Ireland who rely on our Medical Oncology solutions to help deliver patient care.

We’re looking for someone with a strong IT support background who enjoys solving problems, working with customers and getting to the root of technical issues. You don’t necessarily need previous experience within oncology or healthcare. If you bring solid Windows/server support experience, good troubleshooting skills and a genuine desire to learn, we’d like to hear from you.

What will you be doing?

Working as part of our customer support team, you’ll provide remote technical support across Varian's Medical Oncology solutions, 85% of your work will be UK based, but during peak periods there could be additional travel globally.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Diagnosing and resolving technical issues remotely for customers.
  • Supporting the maintenance, configuration, upgrades and troubleshooting of our Medical Oncology systems.
  • Managing multiple customer support cases and ensuring customers are kept informed of progress.
  • Monitoring and prioritising technical support queues.
  • Supporting system changes, software releases and upgrades.
  • Troubleshooting Windows, server, virtualisation, networking and database-related issues.
  • Working collaboratively with colleagues, customers and third-party service providers.
  • Sharing knowledge and contributing to technical documentation and knowledge articles.
  • Helping us continually improve our products, processes and customer experience.

What are we looking for?

We’re particularly interested in people who have experience supporting Windows-based IT environments, including desktop and server platforms, knowledge or experience across some of the following would be beneficial:

  • Microsoft Windows Server
  • VMware and virtual environments
  • Citrix
  • Microsoft SQL Server
  • Networking
  • Hardware and software troubleshooting
  • Enterprise application support

Relevant IT qualifications such as CompTIA A+, CompTIA Network+, Microsoft or Citrix certifications are welcome, although equivalent practical experience is equally valuable.

Just as importantly, you'll be someone who…

  • Enjoys investigating and solving technical problems.
  • Communicates confidently with customers.
  • Can explain technical issues clearly to both IT and non-IT audiences.
  • Has excellent listening and questioning skills.
  • Is comfortable managing several support cases simultaneously.
  • Can work independently while also enjoying being part of a collaborative team.
  • Is willing to support customers outside normal working hours when required.

Experience within healthcare would be useful, but we’re equally interested in transferable experience from other technically complex, customer-focused IT environments.

Applicants must have the right to work in the UK. Please note that visa sponsorship is not available for this position

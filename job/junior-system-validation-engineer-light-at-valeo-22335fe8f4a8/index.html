About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

He/She defines the product system test strategy based on Customer and Valeo standards specifications and risk analysis (PCA, PHA, DFMEA, Newness analysis).

He/She analyzes system and functional requirements and defines test strategy allocation.

He/she defines functional test plan. And support test bench, scripts specification, tools development required.

Regarding requirements assigned to functional validation scope, he/she executes test cases script on the test bench with DUT.

He/She extends test coverage with some robustness tests and end user test cases.

He/She performs tests, and reports test case results.

He/She secures that functional validation conditions & planning feasibility are within project commitments :

.Validation data (technical and system requirements, technical inputs for testing…) to be supplied by the product architect and metier design leaders

. Labs means availability (tests equipment, mission profiles, skills, suppliers)

. State with Validation Leader that test prerequisites checklist deliverables are ok regarding part level and functional features maturity required until SSSR.

Nota1 : prototypes, samples and pre-series parts procurements & audit check + purchase admin are not managed by labs.

Nota 2: Functional test benches setup are developed by Test Bench Engineer.

He/she secures reports archiving in PLM.

He/She reports issues at the first level of analysis and progress through SyVSR dashboard (KPIs).

He/She reports project functional validation risks assessment to the Validation leader

He/She contributes to Functional DR Validation metier checklist review

He/She documents and updates IVV Plan & Report

SENIOR SAP WM / EWM FUNCTIONAL CONSULTANT - 36511

About the job

We are seeking an SAP WM/EWM Functional Consultant to analyse warehouse business processes, lead requirements and fit-to-standard workshops, and translate business needs into scalable SAP solutions.

This role combines strong functional knowledge of SAP EWM and SAP WM with hands-on expertise in integrations, enhancements, and technical design. You will collaborate with business stakeholders, functional teams, architects, and developers throughout the implementation lifecycle—from solution design and development to testing, deployment, and production support. Knowledge of SAP BTP, clean-core extensibility, and side-by-side extension patterns is preferred.

The successful candidate will also support existing SAP WM solutions and use available AI tools effectively to improve delivery efficiency.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's Degree
  • Advanced English level
  • Strong experience in SAP EWM, and WM.
  • Strong understanding of end-to-end SAP EWM process flows and warehouse solution design.
  • Experience with application design, technical documentation, system analysis, development, testing, and troubleshooting.
  • Ability to evaluate technical alternatives and communicate clear, sustainable recommendations.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills for both technical and business audiences.
  • Ability to work independently, lead assigned technical topics and build effective stakeholder relationships.
  • Warehouse Process Expertise
  • Inbound, outbound, and internal warehouse processes
  • Put away, picking, packing, staging, and replenishment
  • Physical inventory and handling units
  • RF framework
  • Integration of EWM with MM, SD, PP/QM, and Transportation Management
  • Decentralized and embedded EWM deployment models
  • Technical and Integration Skills
  • ABAP knowledge for debugging and troubleshooting

*Preferred Qualifications:

  • Practical experience with SAP BTP, clean-core architecture, and side-by-side extension scenarios for SAP S/4HANA and EWM.
  • Experience with migration or transformation programs.
  • Knowledge of agentic AI solutions and advanced use of AI-assisted delivery tools.
  • Hands-on SAP EWM technical development experience, including RF screens, PPF, enhancements, CIF, ITS technology, Warehouse Monitor, and alerts.
  • ABAP, and Object-Oriented ABAP
  • IDoc, RFC, BAPI, OData, and qRFC/CIF
  • Clean-core principles, including evaluation of in-app versus side-by-side extensibility

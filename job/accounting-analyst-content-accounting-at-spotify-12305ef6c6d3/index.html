About the job

Our Global Content Accounting Operations team makes sure the songwriters behind the music on Spotify are accurately accounted for and paid, everywhere in the world. Reporting to the Manager of Content Accounting, you’ll support songwriter royalty accounting globally and partner closely with Legal, Licensing, Technical Accounting, and Engineering. You’ll also help shape what comes next: rolling out claiming processes in new territories as Spotify expands, finding new ways to show the accounting impact of business decisions, steering updates to our claiming systems, and streamlining month-end close in a fast-growing region.

What You'll do

  • Execute the monthly global content accounting close, delivering accurate results on time for reporting, including account reconciliations, roll-forward analyses, and insight-driven flux commentary.
  • Review licensor claim files and invoices promptly and accurately, meeting all contractual deadlines and flagging discrepancies for resolution.
  • Review and interpret legal agreements to ensure royalty payments are calculated and disbursed correctly, and give clear, timely feedback to internal and external stakeholders on royalty calculations and invoice reviews.
  • Develop and refine content accounting processes and procedures, and support internal, external, and third-party audit and SOX requirements for your areas of responsibility.
  • Partner with Technical Accounting, Finance, Legal, Content, Sales, Sales Operations, FP&A, Billing, AR, IT, Tax, and business owners on policies, processes, and programs, helping drive key system initiatives and improvements.
  • Mentor, teach, and review the work of supporting members of the global accounting team, and help our offshore team with questions on invoicing and claims.
  • Take part in special projects such as ERP implementation, system enhancements, third-party audits, and ad hoc analyses.
  • Travel occasionally and be available for late nights, weekends, or holidays when the close or business needs require it.
  • Who You Are
  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, ideally with a CPA or CA, and have 3+ years of professional experience, with Big 4 or entertainment and media experience a plus.
  • You have exceptional time management and organizational skills, with a track record of prioritizing multiple concurrent responsibilities and consistently meeting deadlines.
  • You take a proactive approach to problem-solving and continuous process improvement.
  • You’re curious about AI tools and have hands-on experience using them to drive process improvements, automations, and efficiencies.
  • You have advanced Excel skills, experience with ERP systems such as NetSuite, Oracle, or SAP, and proficiency in SQL and BigQuery.
  • You thrive in a fast-paced, constantly changing environment, and experience with IFRS and/or SOX is an advantage.
  • You communicate clearly and know how to escalate potential issues to the right team early to prevent process delays.
  • Where You'll Be This role is based in New York. We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home. We ask that you come in 3 times per week.

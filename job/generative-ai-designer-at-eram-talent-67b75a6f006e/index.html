About the job

We are looking for a Generative AI Designer to create high-quality AI-generated visual content for digital marketing, branding, social media, advertising, product design, and multimedia campaigns. The ideal candidate combines strong graphic design fundamentals with expertise in Generative AI tools to produce creative, innovative, and production-ready assets.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design visually compelling graphics, illustrations, and marketing materials using AI-powered creative tools.
  • Create AI-generated images, artwork, concepts, and brand assets while maintaining consistency with brand guidelines.
  • Develop effective prompts to generate high-quality visual outputs using text-to-image and image-to-image models.
  • Refine, edit, and enhance AI-generated content using professional design software.
  • Produce creative assets for social media, websites, presentations, digital advertising, and print.
  • Collaborate with marketing, branding, and product teams to develop creative concepts.
  • Stay updated with emerging Generative AI technologies, design trends, and creative workflows.
  • Ensure all AI-generated content complies with copyright, licensing, and ethical AI guidelines.
  • Build reusable prompt libraries and AI-assisted design workflows to improve efficiency.
  • Create storyboards, mockups, UI concepts, and campaign visuals using AI-assisted design techniques.

Requirements

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, Multimedia Design, Digital Arts, Fine Arts, Animation, UI/UX Design, or a related creative field.
  • 10+ years of professional graphic design or digital creative experience.
  • Demonstrated experience using Generative AI tools for commercial creative work.
  • Strong portfolio showcasing AI-assisted and traditional design projects.

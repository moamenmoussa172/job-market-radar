Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

Ideal Candidate:

Everpure's Senior Systems Engineers drive customer-facing technical engagements, delivering innovative solutions across Data, AI, Cloud, and Modern Infrastructure.

THE ROLE

Everpure's Senior Systems Engineer can drive customer-facing technical engagements and deliver innovative solutions across Data, AI, Cloud, and Modern Infrastructure.

You will work closely with Account Executives, customers, and partners to understand business and technical requirements, develop solution architectures, and lead technical engagements from discovery through POC and deployment.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Partner with Account Executives to develop strategic accounts and identify new opportunities.
  • Lead customer technical discussions, workshops, consulting, presentations, and POCs.
  • Design enterprise storage and data infrastructure solutions.
  • Expand customer engagements into AI, Cloud, Virtualization, and Kubernetes environments.
  • Develop technical proposals, architectures, RFP/RFI responses, and competitive positioning.
  • Act as a trusted technical advisor to enterprise customers.
  • Collaborate with internal and partner teams to deliver successful customer outcomes.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • 10+ years of IT Infrastructure experience.
  • Strong experience in customer-facing Presales / Solution Consulting.
  • Experience in Storage Presales or Engineering.
  • Solid understanding of enterprise infrastructure and data architecture.
  • Experience with one or more of AI, Cloud, Virtualization, or Kubernetes.
  • Excellent communication, presentation, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to translate complex technology into clear customer business value.
  • business-level English communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with AI/ML or GPU infrastructure.
  • Experience with Kubernetes / OpenShift and cloud-native environments.
  • Experience with VMware or other virtualization platforms.
  • Experience with major enterprise storage and infrastructure platforms.
  • Experience working with large enterprise accounts and complex RFP/POC engagements.

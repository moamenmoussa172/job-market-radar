About the role

We’re looking for a Senior Enterprise Customer Success Manager to join our Customer Experience team at Pleo. In this role, you’ll own a portfolio of large, complex, and strategically important enterprise customers, helping them achieve measurable business outcomes, expand adoption, manage change, and build long-term value with Pleo.

You’ll serve as a trusted advisor to senior customer stakeholders while coordinating the broader Pleo relationship across Customer Success, Product, Support, Sales, Finance, and other internal teams.

This is not a reactive account-management role. You’ll be expected to understand each customer’s business priorities, operating model, success criteria, and risk profile. You’ll turn that understanding into clear success plans, executive engagement strategies, adoption programs, renewal plans, and growth opportunities.

The right candidate will bring at least three years of direct experience managing enterprise customers, along with the communication skills, commercial judgment, and operational discipline required to manage high-value relationships in a fast-paced technology environment.

Who you’ll be working with and reporting to

You’ll report to our Manager of Customer Success, Large & High-Growth Customers and work closely with teams across:

  • Customer Support
  • Customer Onboarding
  • Sales and Account Management
  • Product and Product Operations
  • Revenue Operations
  • Finance
  • Data and Analytics
  • Program Management and Operations

You’ll work with customer stakeholders across finance, procurement, operations, technology, transformation, and executive leadership.

You’ll be responsible for creating alignment across these stakeholders, establishing clear governance, and ensuring Pleo remains connected to the customer’s evolving strategic and operational priorities.

What you’ll be doing

As a Senior Enterprise Customer Success Manager, you will:

  • Own a portfolio of enterprise customers with accountability for customer health, value realisation, adoption, retention, renewal readiness, advocacy, and growth.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior and executive stakeholders, positioning yourself as a credible advisor who understands their business priorities and operating environment.
  • Develop detailed customer success plans that connect the customer’s strategic goals to measurable outcomes, adoption milestones, responsibilities, and timelines.
  • Lead executive business reviews and strategic customer meetings that demonstrate value, address risks, align stakeholders, and create clear next steps.
  • Drive product adoption across complex customer organisations, helping customers expand usage across teams, entities, locations, use cases, and workflows.
  • Create and maintain detailed stakeholder maps, identifying decision-makers, champions, influencers, operational contacts, blockers, and executive sponsors.
  • Identify churn risk early using product usage, engagement, stakeholder, support, financial, organisational, and external business signals.
  • Build and execute churn-prevention plans with clear actions, owners, timelines, customer commitments, and executive escalation paths.
  • Maintain detailed and accurate forecasts across renewals, risk, customer health, expected outcomes, growth opportunities, and major account milestones.
  • Lead renewal-readiness activities by ensuring customer value is documented, risks are addressed, stakeholders are aligned, and commercial partners have accurate account intelligence.
  • Identify and support expansion opportunities by connecting additional Pleo capabilities, products, or use cases to the customer’s evolving business needs.
  • Coordinate complex customer escalations across Support, Product, Engineering, Finance, and other teams while maintaining clear and confident customer communication.
  • Act as the customer’s internal advocate, ensuring customer needs, feedback, risks, and opportunities are visible to the right stakeholders at Pleo.
  • Navigate customer change effectively, including leadership transitions, reorganisations, acquisitions, market expansion, platform changes, and evolving business priorities.
  • Track customer commitments and outcomes with strong operational discipline, ensuring actions are documented, owned, and completed.
  • Contribute to the evolution of Pleo’s enterprise Customer Success model by sharing best practices, customer insights, playbooks, and recommendations.
  • Support peer development and knowledge sharing across the Customer Success team without holding direct people-management responsibility.

To put things into context

In this role, you can expect to work on priorities such as:

  • strengthening relationships with Pleo’s largest and most strategic customers
  • improving enterprise customer retention and renewal predictability
  • increasing adoption across complex customer organisations
  • creating stronger executive engagement and governance
  • identifying churn risk before it becomes a renewal issue
  • improving forecast accuracy and account visibility
  • coordinating complex customer needs across multiple internal teams
  • helping customers quantify and communicate the value they receive from Pleo
  • identifying opportunities for expansion and deeper product adoption
  • improving the consistency and quality of enterprise success planning
  • helping customers navigate operational and organisational change
  • turning customer feedback into actionable Product and CX insight

You can expect to work across a technology environment that includes CRM, Customer Success platforms, support systems, product analytics, business intelligence tools, workflow tools, and collaboration platforms.

Examples may include Vitally, Gainsight or similar, Zendesk, Looker, Tableau or Power BI, Jira, Asana, and Slack.

About this Role

We are seeking a Cyber Security Technical Product & Engineering Delivery Lead to provide technical product leadership and end-to-end engineering delivery ownership across the Cyber Front Door platform. This role is ideal for an experienced professional who can translate product requirements, user journeys and architectural designs into an actionable technical product backlog, delivering integrated, testable increments that create business value.

You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Architecture, DevOps, Security, Infrastructure, Data and Operations teams to ensure delivery is coordinated, predictable and aligned to strategic objectives. The role balances platform reuse, architectural integrity and secure delivery practices whilst enabling rapid and incremental delivery of business outcomes.

Location: Pune or Bengaluru

What you’ll do

Lead and challenge technical estimation, delivery sequencing and dependency analysis to ensure realistic, evidence-based planning.Ensure sprint readiness by validating that work items meet agreed entry criteria and that sprint outcomes are measurable and demonstrable.Coordinate the delivery of integrated working solutions through regular demonstrations and feedback cycles.Ensure infrastructure, environments, connectivity, storage, compute, access controls, monitoring and observability capabilities are provisioned ahead of planned delivery milestones.Orchestrate collaboration across Product, Development, DevOps, Cloud, Infrastructure, Security, Architecture, Data, Quality Assurance, Service Operations and supplier teams.Identify, manage and track dependencies, blockers, assumptions, risks and decisions, ensuring timely resolution and escalation where required.Partner with Architecture and Engineering teams to maximise the reuse of shared services, APIs, data models and platform components.Promote adherence to secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC), secure coding standards, code review practices, automated testing, version control, infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD principles.Maintain delivery forecasts with transparent assumptions, confidence levels, capacity considerations, dependencies and mitigation plans.Provide concise and evidence-based reporting on milestones, risks, quality metrics, delivery progress and operational readiness.Drive continuous improvement initiatives through retrospectives, delivery metrics, automation opportunities and value stream optimisation.

Who you are

Significant experience leading the technical delivery of complex digital products or platforms using Agile and Scrum methodologies.Demonstrated ability to decompose complex solutions into incremental, end-to-end deliverables that accelerate feedback and value realisation.Strong expertise in Microsoft Azure, DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub and/or Azure DevOps, API-based integration and modern application architectures.Sound understanding of DevSecOps practices, secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC), automated testing, configuration management and observability.Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills with the ability to engage effectively with engineering teams, architects, product leaders, suppliers and senior stakeholders.Typically 10+ years of technology experience, including substantial exposure to product engineering, technical delivery leadership or DevOps leadership.Proven experience in roles such as Technical Product Owner, Engineering Delivery Lead, DevOps Lead, Platform Lead or similar positions with end-to-end accountability.Experience across at least two of the following areas:

  • Complex product or platform delivery
  • Azure and DevOps engineering leadership
  • Technical backlog ownership
  • Secure release management and operational readiness
  • Cross-team capacity planning and dependency management

Relevant certifications are advantageous, including:

  • Microsoft Certified: DevOps Engineer Expert
  • Azure Solutions Architect Expert
  • Professional Scrum Product Owner (PSPO)
  • Professional Scrum Master (PSM)
  • SAFe Product Owner/Product Manager
  • ITIL 4
  • Recognised cyber security or architecture certifications

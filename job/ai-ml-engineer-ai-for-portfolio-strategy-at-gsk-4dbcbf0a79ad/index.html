About the job

At GSK, we see a world where advanced applications of Machine Learning and AI empower us to drive groundbreaking solutions that further our mission of enabling everyone to do more, feel better, and live longer. This ambitious vision will rely on developing cutting-edge products and solutions in Machine Learning and AI. If this excites you, we'd love to chat.

Our team sits within the AI for Portfolio Strategy group and builds innovative models and solutions that inform GSK’s most consequential decisions: which medicines to develop and how to get them to patients faster. We aim to push the frontier of what AI can do for drug development, evaluating internal assets and analysing external dynamics to optimise strategy at every level across the portfolio

We are seeking ambitious, resourceful engineers who thrive when owning complex problems from inception to delivery. We are looking for both exceptional early-career candidates as well as those with several years of experience. In this role, you will be given broadly defined, high-impact challenges rather than prescriptive specifications, and you will be responsible for shaping the solution, building it, and refining it over time. We are building an entirely new approach to strategic R&D decision-making and value the ability to learn quickly and reason rigorously.

Our AI/ML team operates on principles of ownership, accountability, continuous development, and collaboration. We hire for the long term, and we're committed to creating an excellent working environment. Our leaders are dedicated to your career growth and development from day one.

In this role you will

  • Turn loosely defined business and scientific problems into innovative AI-driven solutions, then iterate relentlessly
  • Help shape the roadmap of features from idea to delivery, balancing an intricate knowledge of how the business works today with an understanding of how to build towards a bigger, more ambitious picture
  • Work both autonomously and collaboratively with minimal direction, proactively communicating any problems, delays, or changes to project leads
  • Engage with spec and code reviews and view them as a learning tool to improve engineering practice, seeking feedback along the way
  • Frequently demo work within the team as well as to non-technical and executive stakeholders across the broader organization

Why you?

Qualifications & Skills:

We are looking for candidates with these required skills to achieve our goals:

  • A master’s degree in a technical field such as applied maths, physics, engineering, computer science, computational biology or equivalent, with a strong academic or professional record
  • Advanced programming expertise in Python and experience applying technical skills to a real-world domain such as biology, healthcare, finance or another science or engineering field.
  • Proven ability to solve complex problems using creative approaches, state-of-the-art tools, and best engineering practice with clear evidence of impact
  • Solid foundations in machine learning, with the judgement to select, evaluate, and critique modelling approaches
  • Hands-on use with AI coding tools, and good judgement about how to ship quickly while maintaining responsibility for quality and correctness
  • A driven team player who is excited by the opportunity to take a high degree of ownership and accountability from the start in a fast-paced, high-impact environment

Preferred Qualifications & Skills:

If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Experience designing and building agentic systems, including tool use and calling, multi-step planning and orchestration, and rigorous evaluation of non-deterministic behaviour
  • Domain knowledge in one or more of the following: portfolio finance, clinical trials, commercial pharma
  • Experience in deploying AI/ML solutions with cloud computing platforms, such as Google Cloud Platform or Azure, and engineering AI/ML pipelines to optimise cloud resources (GPUs, CPUs)

Why GSK?

Uniting science, technology and talent to get ahead of disease together.

GSK is a global biopharma company with a purpose to unite science, technology and talent to get ahead of disease together. We aim to positively impact the health of 2.5 billion people by the end of the decade, as a successful, growing company where people can thrive.

We get ahead of disease by preventing and treating it with innovation in specialty medicines and vaccines. We focus on four therapeutic areas: respiratory, immunology and inflammation; oncology; HIV; and infectious diseases – to impact health at scale.

People and patients around the world count on the medicines and vaccines we make, so we’re committed to creating an environment where our people can thrive and focus on what matters most. Our culture of being ambitious for patients, accountable for impact and doing the right thing is the foundation for how, together, we deliver for patients, shareholders and our people.

About this Role

We are seeking a Network & Security Operations Specialist with 4 to 6 years of experience in the network and security domain to manage day-to-day Office IT network and security operations and support infrastructure implementation.

The role involves network configuration, incident and request resolution, change execution, infrastructure assessment, service continuity, disaster recovery, continual service improvement and operational documentation. You will work across networking and security technologies including Cisco, Nexus, Fortinet, Forcepoint, F5, Cisco ISE, Cisco Wireless, Cisco DNA/Prime, VPN and SolarWinds, while operating within established ITIL processes.

What you’ll do

  • Manage day-to-day Office IT network and security operations and support infrastructure implementation activities.
  • Maintain, manage and assess network infrastructure and associated processes, identifying opportunities to improve business and infrastructure outcomes.
  • Develop and support continual service improvement plans across operations and build activities to increase efficiency and effectiveness.
  • Implement network configurations in accordance with approved designs.
  • Resolve incidents and service requests within agreed Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Execute network and security changes with clear identification of risks and appropriate mitigation plans documented in change records.
  • Support service continuity measures, including backup and restore procedures and disaster recovery planning.
  • Apply best practices and create Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for recurring operational activities.
  • Identify opportunities for automation and continually update knowledge management documentation.
  • Follow shift handover processes, clearly communicating priority tickets and upcoming critical tasks.
  • Actively participate in war rooms during P1/P2 incidents through to resolution.
  • Support ITIL-aligned incident, problem and change management processes.

Who you are

You have 4 to 6 years of experience in network and security technologies, with practical experience across the following areas:

  • Routing: OSPF, BGP, MPLS, VRF Lite and high availability.
  • Switching: Cisco and Nexus, including VSS, VPC, GRE tunnelling, GETVPN and high availability.
  • Firewalls: Cisco ASA, Cisco Firepower and Fortinet.
  • Proxy technologies: Fortinet and Forcepoint.
  • Cisco ISE: AAA, RADIUS and 802.1X authentication.
  • F5 load balancing: Local Traffic Manager (LTM) and Global Traffic Manager (GTM).
  • Wireless networking: Cisco Wireless.
  • Network management: Cisco DNA and Cisco Prime.
  • VPN: Cisco VPN, including multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Monitoring and reporting: SolarWinds.
  • ITIL processes: Change management, problem management and incident management.
  • You can troubleshoot network and security incidents methodically and contribute effectively during high-priority P1/P2 incidents.
  • You can document operational processes through clear SOPs and knowledge articles and identify suitable opportunities for automation and continual service improvement.

What's in it for you

Based on the responsibilities described for this role, you will have the opportunity to:

  • Work across a broad enterprise network and security technology estate covering routing, switching, firewalls, proxies, wireless, VPN, authentication, load balancing and monitoring.
  • Contribute to business-critical network operations, incident resolution and service continuity.
  • Participate in continual service improvement initiatives across operational and infrastructure build activities.
  • Gain exposure to structured ITIL-based incident, problem and change management practices.
  • Build experience in automation, knowledge management and operational standardisation through SOP development.

What skills you will learn

The responsibilities provide opportunities to further develop skills in:

  • Enterprise network and security operations.
  • Advanced troubleshooting across OSPF, BGP, MPLS, switching, firewalls, VPN and wireless technologies.
  • Network authentication and access control using Cisco ISE, AAA, RADIUS and 802.1X.
  • Application delivery and load balancing using F5 LTM and GTM.
  • Network monitoring and reporting using SolarWinds.
  • Incident response and collaboration during P1/P2 service-impacting events.
  • Risk assessment and mitigation for network and security changes.
  • Disaster recovery, service continuity and backup and restore practices.
  • ITIL-aligned incident, problem and change management.
  • Process automation, SOP creation and knowledge management.

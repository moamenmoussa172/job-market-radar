About the job

Amazon's Global Environmental Assurance and Protection team works to make Amazon the global benchmark for environmental excellence across our operations. As the Rest of World Water Program Manager, you own the lifecycle of Amazon's most complex water compliance programs across LATAM, APAC, and MEATR and translate an ambiguous, fast-moving regulatory landscape into program frameworks the field can execute. That scope covers water quality and discharge compliance, stormwater and wastewater management, water stewardship and conservation, and emerging water regulatory obligations across the ROW region. You take partially defined business problems and regulatory requirements and turn them into structured programs with clear objectives, milestones, and success criteria, owning each program through its pilot, prove, and scale phases.
As an individual contributor, you own the strategic definition and lifecycle management of these water programs. You decompose ambiguous challenges into program plans that account for real field constraints, define what "done" looks like, and design the systems, processes, and tools, with measurement built in, that the field organization adopts and sustains without ongoing program-level intervention. You partner with Legal, regulatory affairs, operations, technology, and procurement to keep programs aligned with the operating model, and you contribute to internal operations planning strategy while owning organizational goals within the ROW water program portfolio.
You identify and influence cross-functional investments that solve long-term water compliance requirements, negotiating resources, priorities, and timelines across organizations. You build business cases with quantified risk and ROI, present them to Director and VP audiences, and represent the organization in cross-functional planning cadences so that water compliance requirements are embedded in business decisions. You own program controllership mechanisms, business review cadences, escalation paths, and metric dashboards, that surface risk and drive accountability in real time, and you independently proxy for the Senior Manager in business reviews and cross-functional forums with full ownership.
This role requires independent judgment, a high tolerance for ambiguity, and the ability to find a path forward where solutions are not pre-defined, driving cross-functional alignment without deferring decisions upward. You communicate technical compliance requirements clearly to executive, operational, and legal audiences, and you use data and business intelligence to look around corners, spotting and mitigating risk before it materializes. If you are motivated by solving complex regulatory problems at scale and building the compliance infrastructure to match, this role is for you.
Key job responsibilities
- Translate the Rest of World water compliance vision into functional programs. Integrate them into annual and multi-year planning and shape the budgets that fund them, weighing near-term compliance needs against long-term infrastructure investment.
- Work independently in an ambiguous, high-stakes regulatory environment, finding paths forward where no solution is predefined and driving alignment without pushing decisions upward.
- Own water compliance programs end to end, taking problem definitions through pilot to full rollout, and build the resulting standards into daily operations so field teams sustain them on their own.
- Build the metrics and review system that holds the program accountable, covering KPIs, business reviews, escalation paths, and dashboards that give leaders real-time visibility into performance, milestones, and regulatory standing.
- Report program status, risks, and escalations clearly to director and vice-president audiences across operations, legal, finance, and technology, and stand in for the senior manager in business reviews and cross-functional forums.
- Oversee third-party contractors, consultants, and inspection vendors, holding them to program milestones and quality standards through structured audits.
- Travel approximately 25-50% of the time.
- Experience in compliance, audit or risk management
- Experience in program or project management
- Experience owning program strategy, end-to-end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Bachelor’s degree in the Environmental Sciences, Environmental Engineering, or related field of study, or equivalent level of qualification in line with the European Qualifications Framework (EQF).
- Project Management Professional (PMP) or equivalent certification
- Master's degree in Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) or equivalent level of qualification in line with the European Qualifications Framework (EQF)
- Experience developing and implementing water compliance programs, for example water quality, discharge, stormwater, and wastewater, in accordance with applicable laws
- Professional water credential where applicable, for example a Professional Engineer (PE) with a water or wastewater focus, a Certified Water Professional, or an equivalent professional environmental certification
- Experience with ISO 14001 environmental management system design, implementation, or certification

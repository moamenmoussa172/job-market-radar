A Day in the Life

Our Distribution Center in Alcalá de Henares plays a critical role in the storage, inventory management, and distribution of products across the region. The team is responsible for ensuring inventory accuracy, operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, and high service levels through warehouse operations, shipping and receiving activities, and Value-Added Services (VAS).
This position is based in Alcalá de Henares, Madrid, Spain and is an onsite role. The Warehouse and Inventory Lead partners closely with Supply Chain, Logistics, Quality, and Customer Service teams to support operational performance and business objectives
The Warehouse and Inventory Lead is responsible for supervising warehouse and inventory operations within the Distribution Center, ensuring the efficient, accurate, and compliant flow of materials and products. This role provides people leadership while overseeing inventory control, shipping and receiving processes, logistics operations support, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Lead daily warehouse, inventory, shipping, receiving, and Value-Added Services (VAS) operations.
  • Provide operational support for logistics activities to ensure efficient and compliant product flow across the distribution network.
  • Manage, coach, and develop warehouse team members, including performance management and workload prioritization.
  • Monitor inventory accuracy and material movement through SAP Warehouse Management (WM) systems.
  • Ensure compliance with logistics procedures, transportation requirements, quality standards, and operational policies.
  • Coordinate the receipt, storage, movement, and shipment of materials, products, and supplies.
  • Review and validate shipping documentation, freight records, packing lists, bills of lading, and system transactions.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance productivity, inventory control, and service levels.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Engineer degree with 7+ years of experience in a similar role in Supply Chain, Logistics, Warehouse Operations, or Inventory Management.
  • People management experience leading and developing teams in a warehouse, distribution center, inventory management, or logistics environment.
  • Proven experience in the medical devices or healthcare sector.
  • Experience with SAP Warehouse Management (WM/WMS) systems.
  • Fluent in Spanish and English.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in pharmaceutical or other regulated distribution environments.
  • Experience using Power BI for operational reporting and performance analysis.
  • Experience with Value-Added Services (VAS) operations.
  • Experience leading continuous improvement initiatives in warehouse or logistics operations.
  • Knowledge of transportation management, inventory control, and warehouse performance metrics.

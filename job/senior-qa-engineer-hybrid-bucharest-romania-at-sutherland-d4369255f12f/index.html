About the job

Sutherland is seeking a Senior QA Engineerto join our team in Bucharest, Romania!

This is what you will be contributing to:

● Develop, execute and maintain test plans and test cases to validate software
functionality, performance, and scalability autonomously.
● Conduct thorough functional, regression, integration, and system testing, utilizing both
manual and automated testing tools to identify defects and areas for improvement.
● Perform manual testing to validate edge cases, usability, and user interface elements.
● Generate reports on automated tests.
● Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including developers, product managers,
and designers to identify test requirements and ensure comprehensive test coverage

Experience & skill set you need to succeed:

● Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
● +5 years of QA experience
● Experience using browser developer tools.
● Strong understanding of Angular / React web apps and related tools.
● Experience with iOS and Android native applications testing
● Experience with relational and non-relational databases.
● Proficiency in writing clear, concise, and comprehensive test plans, test cases, and test
scripts.
● Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
● Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively and
autonomously in a team environment.

Nice to have:

● Experience with Playwright.
● Experience with JavaScript/TypeScript and relational databases.
● Experience with Kubernetes deployments and environment configuration.

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.

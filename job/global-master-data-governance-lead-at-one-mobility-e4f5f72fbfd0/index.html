About the job

Join us in driving connectivity forward!

At One Mobility Group, we bridge worlds—combining nearly a century of German automotive engineering precision with the innovation power of the globe's largest electronics and ICT manufacturer. This unique positioning makes us the strategic partner for end-to-end mobility solutions—from initial concept design to large-scale manufacturing. Our comprehensive portfolio spans the full spectrum of electrified mobility, including power distribution systems, high- and low-voltage modules, safety components, and advanced charging solutions. We specialize in integration expertise that seamlessly connects data, power, and sensing – redefining what’s possible in intelligent, connected mobility.

At One Mobility, you will join a vibrant international team of 10,500 people, working together in 17 countries across Europe, Asia, North America, the Middle East, and Africa. Step into a culture where inclusivity is the foundation, where your ideas are trusted and supported, your passions are nurtured, and the spirit of collaboration is encouraged.

We move fast, think differently, and empower our people to deliver breakthrough solutions.

We are looking for a

Global Master Data Governance Lead

at one of our locations inEMEA region - Hungary (Budaörs)

Your tasks:

  • Define the global master data governance vision, framework, and roadmap.
  • Establish policies, standards, ownership models, roles, processes, and decision structures.
  • Coordinate business, IT, data owners, data stewards, process owners, and SAP project stakeholders.
  • Drive global harmonization and standardization of master data.
  • Define data quality rules, KPIs, controls, and issue-resolution processes.
  • Support the implementation of workflows, approvals, validations, and governance controls in SAP S/4HANA.
  • Drive communication, training, change management, and adoption.
  • Report on program progress, risks, success, and business impact.
  • A bachelor’s degree in business administration, economics, information systems, data management, engineering, computer science, or related field.
  • Professional training or education in data governance is an advantage
  • Minimum 3+ years of experience in master data management, data governance, business process management, ERP implementation, or related field.
  • Experience in SAP or ERP transformation projects, ideally SAP S/4HANA.
  • Experience working with cross-functional business stakeholders and IT teams in an international environment.
  • Experience defining or implementing governance processes, data ownership models, standards and data quality controls.
  • Practical master data governance experience:Strong knowledge of master data governance principles, data ownership, stewardship, data lifecycle, data quality rules, issue resolution, and continuous improvement.
  • ERP transformation experience:Strong understanding of SAP S/4HANA or ERP implementation projects, including master data requirements, workflows, validation rules, and migration readiness.
  • Experience with Data Warehouse and Data Lake is an advantage
  • Fluent English (German is nice to have)
  • Willingness to travel (50%)

Our offer:

  • A permanent, full-time position
  • A mixed team of fresh talent and long-standing employees
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • A structured induction program
  • An attractive remuneration package

About the job

  • The Mid-Level EDI Support Analyst is responsible for coordinating and supporting EDI implementations and production operations, acting as a key interface between customers, Sales, SAP consultants, developers, and other internal teams.
  • The role combines EDI technical expertise, customer coordination, mapping development, and advanced support, ensuring solutions follow established EDI and TSIM standards while delivering stable and reliable integrations.
  • Key Responsibilities
  • Act as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for EDI-related topics, coordinating activities between customers, Sales, SAP consultants, developers, and other stakeholders.
  • Coordinate EDI implementation requests according to established processes, standards, and development guidelines.
  • Develop and maintain customer EDI mappings, particularly for two-step mapping scenarios and data transformations.
  • Apply knowledge of ANSI X12, EDIFACT, RND, SAP IDoc, and other EDI formats to ensure data accuracy and consistency.
  • Ensure all implementation documentation is complete and traceable, including mapping specifications, customer requirements, test data, and contact information.
  • Provide guidance on established EDI integration patterns and recommend standard solutions when requirements deviate from defined processes.
  • Act as the first escalation point for EDI topics and as a member of the L3 Support Team, performing advanced incident analysis and supporting problem resolution.
  • Support testing, troubleshooting, go-live activities, and production stabilization.
  • Conduct regular alignment meetings with internal customers, including Sales organizations
  • Support regional management with information and technical input for budget-related discussions.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement, standardization, automation, and service quality.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor’s degree in Systems Analysis, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Fluent English, written and spoken.
  • Fluent Portuguese.
  • Good knowledge of EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) and B2B integration concepts.
  • Experience with EDI mapping and data transformation.
  • Knowledge of XSLT
  • Knowledge of SAP IDoc and system-to-system data integration.
  • Knowledge of EDI standards such as ANSI X12, EDIFACT, and RND.
  • Experience with incident analysis, troubleshooting, and production support.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to coordinate technical topics across multiple stakeholders.
  • Preferred Qualifications
  • Knowledge of Jira.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office, including Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Familiarity with Artificial Intelligence tools, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or similar solutions.
  • Knowledge of SAP R/3 IDoc structures and formats.
  • Knowledge of EDI standards and message formats, including ANSI X12, EDIFACT, ODETTE, and RND.
  • Knowledge of communication and integration protocols, including SFTP, AS2, OFTP2, RESTful APIs, and others.

Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English!

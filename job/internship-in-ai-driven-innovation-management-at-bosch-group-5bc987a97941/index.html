About the job

Take the opportunity to shape AI-driven innovation in Outdoor Power Equipment. Explore, benchmark, and deploy cutting-edge AI tools, from LLM agents and generative design platforms to advanced data pipelines, to uncover next-generation product opportunities and optimize innovation workflows. Connect physical engineering with digital intelligence and help bring innovative ideas to life.

  • During your assignment, you will shape Innovation Management for garden tools using AI tools, from early idea to concept.
  • You will identify innovation opportunities by supporting product discovery and ideation activities and collaborating closely with Engineering and Product Marketing to spot AI-driven features for outdoor and garden equipment.
  • Additionally, you will coordinate, execute, and analyze field tests for garden equipment to validate product concepts and gather real-world performance data.
  • You will co-create AI workflows by contributing to the design, testing, and roll-out of AI-powered tools that optimize internal product management and engineering workflows.
  • Finally, you will enable the team by facilitating internal workshops, drafting user-friendly how-to documentation, and promoting the adoption of productivity-enhancing AI tools across the department.
  • Education: studies in the field of Mechanical Engineering, Data Science, Business Management or comparable
  • Experience and Knowledge: strong familiarity with modern AI paradigms for example, Large Language Models, Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Copilots
  • Personality and Working Practice: you are an analytical thinker who communicates effectively and takes initiative with a self-starter attitude
  • Work Routine: we offer you the opportunity to work in a hybrid setup
  • Languages: fluent in English

Start: according to prior agreement
Duration: 6 months

Requirement for this internship is the enrollment at university. Please attach your CV, transcript of records, enrollment certificate, examination regulations and if indicated a valid work and residence permit.

As a global company, we are legally required to comply with applicable EU and U.S. (re-)export control and sanctions regulations. This means that for certain, particularly sensitive positions, we may only hire individuals who have undergone an additional legal screening. This position is one such sensitive role. We will therefore ask you for additional information required for this screening as the application process continues. You can find information about the processing of your data in our Privacy Notice for the application process.

Diversity and inclusion are not just trends for us but are firmly anchored in our corporate culture. Therefore, we welcome all applications, regardless of gender, age, disability, religion, ethnic origin or sexual identity.

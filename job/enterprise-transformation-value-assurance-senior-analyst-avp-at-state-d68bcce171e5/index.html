Enterprise Transformation (Value Assurance), Senior Analyst - AVP

Position Overview

As part of State Street's transformation journey, we are implementing a product and platform operating model to simplify how the firm operates, modernize platforms and processes, accelerate technology and AI adoption, strengthen resiliency and controls, and improve outcomes for clients, employees and shareholders. Transformation is business-led and enterprise-enabled, with the Transformation Management Office providing enterprise coordination, governance, methodology, execution support and value-realization oversight.

The Senior Analyst, Impact Assurance is a hands-on analytical role within the Transformation Management Office responsible for supporting consistent measurement, validation and reporting of transformation outcomes. Reporting to the Head of Impact Assurance, the role executes key components of the impact-assurance framework, including baseline analysis, business-case review, benefit tracking, outcome validation and preparation of decision-ready insights.

The Senior Analyst works across Finance, Business Units, Technology, Risk and Program Delivery to evaluate evidence, challenge assumptions and maintain transparent views of committed, forecast, realized and at-risk transformation value. The role provides evidence-based recommendations and escalates material inconsistencies, risks and data-quality issues through established governance channels.

This is an individual-contributor or coordinating role requiring strong analytical judgment, attention to detail and the ability to translate complex financial and operational data into clear conclusions. The Senior Analyst operates within established Impact Assurance standards and may coordinate work performed by analysts, initiative teams and data owners.

Primary Responsibilities

Baseline, Target & Measurement Analysis

  • Execute baseline analyses for financial and non-financial transformation outcomes using approved methodologies and source data.
  • Assess the completeness and consistency of targets, measures, attribution, timing and supporting evidence.
  • Document assumptions, data sources, calculation methods and limitations to enable transparent and repeatable measurement.
  • Maintain structured records of approved baselines, targets and validation requirements.
  • Recommend refinements where proposed measures are unclear, inconsistent or insufficient for decision-making.

Business Case Review & Analytical Challenge

  • Analyze transformation business cases, including benefit drivers, implementation costs, timing, dependencies, sensitivities and risks.
  • Test calculations and assumptions against available evidence, financial plans and approved enterprise definitions.
  • Identify potential double-counting, overlapping benefit claims, attribution gaps and inconsistencies across initiatives.
  • Prepare clear findings and recommendations for the Head of Impact Assurance, Finance and accountable business leaders.
  • Support scenario and sensitivity analysis used in prioritization, sequencing and investment decisions.

Benefits Tracking & Outcome Validation

  • Maintain initiative and portfolio-level benefit tracking from initial commitment through implementation and realization.
  • Validate financial, client, operational, productivity, adoption and risk outcomes against approved baselines and targets.
  • Reconcile reported outcomes with source systems, Finance records and supporting evidence.
  • Identify value leakage, forecast deterioration, implementation gaps and delayed or unsupported benefits.
  • Recommend corrective actions and monitor agreed follow-up items through completion.

Portfolio Analytics & Executive Materials

  • Produce timely analyses of realized, forecast and at-risk transformation value.
  • Prepare concise, decision-oriented materials for Quarterly Business Reviews, portfolio reviews and governance forums.
  • Translate complex data into clear trends, drivers, risks and implications for senior stakeholders.
  • Maintain analytical models, dashboards, supporting schedules and traceable evidence for reported outcomes.
  • Partner with Governance and Reporting to ensure validated outcomes are reflected consistently in enterprise transformation reporting.

Data Quality, Controls & Methodology

  • Perform data-quality checks, reconciliations and controls across Impact Assurance inputs and outputs.
  • Maintain version control, review trails and documentation for business cases, baselines, benefit forecasts and validation decisions.
  • Apply Impact Assurance standards consistently and escalate exceptions or methodology questions.
  • Support continuous improvement of templates, analytical tools, validation procedures and reusable playbooks.
  • Use analytics, automation and approved AI-enabled tools to improve efficiency, accuracy and insight while maintaining appropriate controls.

Stakeholder Coordination & Capability Support

  • Partner with Transformation Directors, Program Leads, Finance, business owners and data owners to obtain information and resolve analytical questions.
  • Communicate findings objectively, distinguishing validated evidence from assumptions or unsupported claims.
  • Provide practical guidance to initiative teams on baseline, target and benefits-tracking requirements.
  • Support working sessions focused on business cases, outcome measurement, benefits realization and corrective actions.
  • Share lessons learned and contribute to stronger value-management capability across the transformation portfolio.

Experience, Skills & Leadership Capabilities

  • Relevant experience in financial analysis, business-case development, benefits realization, transformation analytics, FP&A, portfolio reporting, enterprise performance management or management consulting within financial services or another complex regulated industry.
  • Strong quantitative and analytical skills with demonstrated ability to challenge assumptions, reconcile data, identify inconsistencies and draw evidence-based conclusions.
  • Working knowledge of financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, productivity measurement and performance-management concepts.
  • Experience developing analytical models, management information, dashboards or executive materials using Excel, PowerPoint and relevant analytics or visualization tools.
  • Ability to operate effectively across Business, Finance, Technology, Risk and transformation teams with appropriate independence and judgment.
  • Strong attention to detail, documentation discipline and commitment to data quality, transparency and control effectiveness.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain analytical findings and recommendations to senior and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Objective and curious mindset with the confidence to constructively challenge assumptions and escalate material concerns.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and adapt as portfolio requirements change.
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant experience required; degree in finance, economics, accounting, business, analytics, mathematics or a related field preferred. Relevant finance, analytics, portfolio-management or transformation credentials are advantageous.

Success Measures

Success in this role will be measured through:

  • Accuracy, completeness and timeliness of baseline, business-case, benefit-tracking and validation analyses.
  • Quality and clarity of evidence, assumptions, analytical conclusions and recommendations.
  • Early identification and escalation of double-counting, unsupported claims, value leakage and data-quality issues.
  • Reliable maintenance of realized, forecast and at-risk benefit information.
  • Quality and timeliness of Quarterly Business Review and governance materials.
  • Adherence to Impact Assurance standards, documentation requirements and control procedures.
  • Stakeholder responsiveness and effective resolution of analytical questions and data gaps.
  • Contribution to reusable analytical tools, playbooks and continuous improvement of the Impact Assurance capability.

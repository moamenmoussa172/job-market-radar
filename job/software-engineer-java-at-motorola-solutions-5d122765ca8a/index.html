Department Overview

The Pronto Platform Team is at the heart of our mission of "Solving for safer" across the United Kingdom. As the UK’s leading provider of mobile policing applications, Pronto supports 50% of the country's 45 police forces and over 70,000 active officers every single day. Our dedicated group keeps Pronto reliable, secure, fast, and easy to evolve—working closely with project teams to deliver vital improvements and new features that help frontline officers be their best in the moments that matter.


Job Description

This role is primarily remote, with occasional travel to offices.

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We are a global, close-knit, and inclusive community united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. As a Software Engineer on the Pronto Platform Team, you will help build and connect the technologies that protect communities, schools, hospitals, and businesses across the UK.

In this role, you will bring an analytical mindset and strong collaborative spirit to a large, long-lived codebase, partnering closely with project teams to share knowledge, review code, and continuously enhance our internal workflows. While a university qualification in Computing Science or Software Engineering provides a strong foundation, we deeply value diverse paths into technology and welcome candidates with equivalent practical experience who are ready to make an immediate, meaningful impact on public safety.

Key Responsibilities

Platform Reliability & Incident Resolution

  • Respond to and analyze P1 incidents to uncover root causes, deliver lasting fixes, and prevent future recurrences
  • Resolve software bugs, address security vulnerabilities, and deliver user experience enhancements backed by automated testing
  • Profile client, server, and database performance to implement measurable speed and efficiency gains across the platform

Release Engineering & System Maintenance

  • Prepare, build, verify, and ship software releases—including backports—while maintaining release pipelines and accurate release notes
  • Execute routine dependency, platform, and tooling updates alongside proactive technical debt management
  • Enhance developer experience by continuously improving internal tools, build systems, and engineering workflows

Cross-Team Collaboration & Feature Delivery

  • Partner closely with project teams to co-deliver platform improvements and new product features
  • Conduct thorough, constructive code reviews to uphold engineering standards across our codebase
  • Share technical insights and domain knowledge across the Pronto Platform Team to foster an inclusive, high-performing engineering culture


Basic Requirements

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computing Science or Software Engineering
  • Solid hands-on software engineering experience developing and maintaining production applications
  • Must be able to attain Non-Police-Personnel-Vetting Level 3 (NPPV3), which requires a minimum of 3 years UK residency

Technical Expertise

  • Java and Kotlin to develop, maintain, and optimize scalable platform components across a large codebase
  • SQL to write, analyze, and optimize complex database queries and backend data structures
  • Analytical Problem-Solving to diagnose issues and navigate complex, long-lived software architectures
  • English Language Proficiency with an excellent command of written and spoken English to collaborate effectively across remote engineering teams

Professional Experience

  • Software Maintenance & Debugging to troubleshoot production issues, resolve vulnerabilities, and improve application stability
  • Collaborative Development to participate in code reviews, communicate technical concepts clearly, and deliver software alongside cross-functional project teams

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Android development experience for mobile application support and enhancement
  • Java Swing and legacy web frameworks including Jetty, Struts, JSP, and GWT
  • Vue and TypeScript for modern frontend interface development
  • Firebase (specifically Analytics and Messaging)
  • Gradle build automation and CI/CD pipelines utilizing GitHub Actions
  • XML processing and XSLT transformations

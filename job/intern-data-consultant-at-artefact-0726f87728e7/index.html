  • Undergraduates and bachelor’s degree holder

  • Excellent communication skills
  • Familiar with Microsoft Office applications especially Excel and Powerpoint
  • Can work 3 days per week for 2 months at least(mini 2 days onsite)

  • Solution-oriented mindset

Product Manager, Finance Engineering

Deliveroo · London, United Kingdom

Onsite · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 1 October 2026

About the job

Our Global Structure

Deliveroo is now part of DoorDash, bringing together teams with even greater reach, scale, and ambition. Depending on your role, you may collaborate with teammates, systems, and leaders across DoorDash and Wolt. Together, we’re unlocking new possibilities as one global team.

Join us to redefine the future of on-demand delivery. To do this, we're building a world-class Finance Engineering (FinEng) organization across all global markets we support at DoorDash, Deliveroo and Wolt. in which each of our product managers plays a critical role in helping to define and execute our vision to move money accurately and at scale across every market we operate in and ensure finance and accounting compliance. As we grow, billions of dollars in money movements flow through our platform every year and our team makes sure every one of them is understood, trusted, and correctly reflected in the financial books.

About the Role

Product Managers on the Finance Engineering team need a systems-oriented mindset, platform thinking, and strong cross-functional collaboration. In this role, you will define the strategy and vision for systems that track billions of dollars in company-wide money movement, translate transactions into accounting entries, and accelerate the financial close. You will build scalable, audit-ready automation for complex, high-volume financial activity.

This is an opportunity to reshape modern accounting by applying AI to reconciliation, investigations, and incident detection and mitigation. Your products will form a critical part of the company’s financial infrastructure and help earn the confidence of leadership, auditors, and regulators.

You're excited about this opportunity because you will…

  • Drive the product definition, strategy, and long-term vision for accounting automation. You own the roadmap
  • Build systems that track billion-dollar-level money movements across the entire company and automatically convert them into accurate accounting entries
  • Reinvent and accelerate the accounting close process, reducing manual effort and shortening close timelines
  • Champion AI-driven approaches to transform close reconciliation, financial investigations, and incident detection and mitigation
  • Partner with FinEng, Accounting, and Finance leaders to translate complex financial and regulatory requirements into scalable, automated products
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams of engineers, designers, data scientists, accountants, and operators
  • Communicate product plans, benefits, and results to key stakeholders including the leadership team

We're excited about you because…

  • You have a track record in product management, including driving product strategy, vision, and roadmap alignment
  • You have built complex, back-end, data-intensive, or platform products. Ideally in fintech, payments, accounting, or financial systems
  • You have a strong grasp of accounting concepts (e.g. double-entry, ledgers, reconciliations, the close process) and how financial data flows through complex system
  • You do your best work with range: global platform thinking alongside the accounting reality of a specific brand or market
  • You're an execution power-house who thrives in ambiguity and operates at the lowest level of detai
  • You are excited about applying AI/ML to real-world financial problems such as reconciliation, investigation, and incident mitigation
  • You have experience presenting business reviews to senior executives
  • You have empathy for the users you build for, including accountants, controllers, and finance operators
  • You are motivated by building financial infrastructure that works across DoorDash, Deliveroo, and Wolt

Why Join Us?

At Deliveroo, you’ll do work that matters, solving real-world problems in a three-sided marketplace that’s constantly evolving. We’re food lovers, problem solvers, community builders and more, brought together by a shared drive to make things better. Working here you can expect to:

🔧 Solve meaningful problems at real scale Work on a complex, always-on marketplace that impacts millions every day.

🌱 See your impact, fast Ship, test and improve ideas quickly in a low-hierarchy, high-ownership environment.

🧠 Grow through challenge and ownership Take on big, ambiguous problems and accelerate your career with strong support.

🌎 A culture built for builders High standards, collaboration, flexible working and continuous learning. 💰 Share in the success you help create Competitive salary and equity options, so you’re rewarded for the impact you make.

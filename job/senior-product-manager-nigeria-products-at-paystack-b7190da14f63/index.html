About the job

About Paystack

Paystack’s mission is to power African ambition. Over 300K businesses across Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and Kenya use Paystack’s modern payments gateway, including Qatar Airways, MTN, Burger King, UPS, Africa World Airlines, AXA Mansard Insurance, FairMoney, PiggyVest, Crocs, Under Armour, Richemont Lifestyle Group, and many others.

Over the last 10 years, we’ve built products that have helped shape online payments in Africa, from automated recurring payments to direct bank payments and automated chargebacks. Today, Paystack is part of The Stack Group (TSG), a family of technology brands building modern infrastructure across payments, banking, consumer products, and emerging technologies.

At Paystack, we hire talented people, treat them with genuine respect, and give them the space, resources, and support to do the best work of their lives. We’d love your help.

About the Senior Product Manager role

Our team is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own payments in Nigeria, Paystack's largest market.

Paystack's central teams build the core: core payments, financial systems, trust and safety, merchant experience and onboarding, and internal automation. This role owns the last mile in Nigeria: the local rails, payment methods, rules and integrations that turn the core into products Nigerian merchants and their customers use every day.

You will own processing in Nigeria, from connecting to local banks, switches and fintechs to making sure every transaction is finalised correctly, shows up accurately in the merchant's dashboard and is ready to settle.

You will own Nigeria's local and alternative payment methods and the local rules they must follow.

You will work closely with Nigeria's go-to-market team, so the product is ready for the merchants Paystack needs to win in its largest market.

The role reports to the Head of Product. The role is based in Lagos, Nigeria, and the candidate must be based in Lagos.

We’ll trust you to

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for payments in Nigeria.
  • Own processing in Nigeria: integrations with local banks, switches, fintechs and other rails, built on the core services the Core Payments team provides.
  • Add direct connections to banks and fintechs, and reduce dependence on any single switch or partner.
  • Own Nigeria's local and alternative payment methods, including bank transfers, direct debit, dedicated virtual accounts and USSD, and decide which ones to add next.
  • Make sure every transaction is finalised correctly, with the right final status, shown accurately in the merchant's dashboard and ready for settlement.
  • Own the last mile of settlement in Nigeria, working with Financial Systems on local banks, payout rails, timings and rules.
  • Shape the Nigeria-specific parts of checkout with Core Payments, so customers see the payment methods that work best for them.
  • Make sure Nigeria's payment products meet local rules, including CBN and card scheme requirements, working with Compliance.
  • Deliver the Nigeria last mile for work coming out of central teams, with Trust & Safety, Merchant Experience & Onboarding and Internal Automation, so it fits Nigerian merchants.
  • Work closely with Nigeria's go-to-market team: join conversations with large merchants, shape what product can offer in a deal, and turn what you hear into priorities.
  • Improve success rates, uptime and latency on Nigerian rails, and hold local partners to performance standards.
  • Use transaction data to find where Nigerian payments fail, stall or slow down, and fix the biggest leaks first.
  • Define and track the metrics: success rate by channel, partner uptime, time to finalise a transaction, and adoption of local payment methods.

You’ll thrive in this role if you

  • 7+ years of product management experience, including shipping complex, technical payments products
  • Deep experience in Nigerian payments, such as banks, switches, card schemes, transfers, direct debit or virtual accounts
  • Experience building and managing integrations with banks, switches, processors or fintechs, and holding them to a performance standard
  • A track record of improving success rate, uptime or transaction completion, with the numbers to show it
  • Experience with transaction lifecycle problems such as pending, reversed or duplicate transactions
  • A working understanding of CBN, NIBSS and card scheme rules as they apply to payment companies
  • Experience working directly with sales or go-to-market teams on large merchant deals
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort working with transaction data
  • Technical fluency to make integration and reliability trade-offs with engineers
  • Experience working between central platform teams and a local market team
  • Strong stakeholder management across partners, commercial teams and engineering
  • Prior experience at a PSP, bank or switch in Nigeria, which is a strong asset

About the job

Job posting may be removed earlier if the position is filled or if a sufficient number of applications are received.

Meet the Team

Optical engineers at Cisco design and build the next generation of high-capacity optical communication systems, subsystems, and transmission components. By delivering ground breaking, power-efficient optical transport platforms, our team builds the essential network backbone connecting modern cloud data centers and enterprise infrastructure worldwide. Operating as a dedicated Center of Excellence, our agile engineering team blends deep technical mastery with an open, high-trust, and collaborative environment focused on continuous photonic innovation. What makes our team exciting is having direct access to innovative optical laboratories, working with industry-leading partners to turn breakthrough optical designs into deployed, world-class products.

Your Impact

Manage sophisticated hardware product development programs and sustaining lifecycles independently with minimal mentorship to drive predictable execution across our product portfolio. Prepare program plans, schedules, and budgets by working closely with teams from Engineering, Supply Chain, and support functions to align all project work. Conduct program reviews to provide and present accurate program status and progress reports directly to Program Core Teams and Senior Management. Identify program risks early and devise proactive mitigation plans to avoid or minimize schedule, quality, and budget impacts, ensuring all committed results are met. Direct accurate budget planning and spend management while ensuring every program rigorously adheres to Cisco's governance processes.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in project management for hardware-based product development programs.
  • Demonstrated track record of managing end-to-end hardware product lifecycles, from concept gate contribute through prototyping and manufacturing release to First Customer Shipment.
  • Experience developing and managing critical-path project schedules, multi-phase development, program budgets, and formal risk registers for complex hardware systems.
  • Professional working proficiency in English (written and spoken) for international technical collaboration

Preferred Qualifications

  • Project Management Professional (PMP) certification or any other formal program management credential.
  • Direct technical program management or engineering background within the Telecom, Optical Networking, or High-Speed Datacenter Systems industry (e.g., DWDM, transceivers, linecards, routers/switches) is desired.
  • Knowledge about Cisco/ISO/TL9000 processes.
  • Experience influencing leadership, aligning partner priorities across time zones, and building consensus without authority.

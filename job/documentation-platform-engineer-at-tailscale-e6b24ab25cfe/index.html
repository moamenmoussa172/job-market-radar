About Tailscale

Tailscale is making safe connection effortless by delivering software that makes it easy to securely interconnect people and their devices, no matter where they are. From hobbyists to multinational corporations, teams of every size use Tailscale each day to protect their networks, share access to internal tools, and more. We're building a future for the Internet that's easy, sensible, and safe, like it used to be. Founded in 2019 and fully distributed, we're backed by Accel, CRV, Insight, Heavybit, and Uncork Capital.

About the Role

We're looking for an experienced full-stack Documentation Platform Engineer to evolve our documentation site into a platform that delivers an exceptional experience for Tailscale's prospective and current customers.

You'll build, maintain, evolve, and support the platform and tools that internal teams and external contributors use to showcase Tailscale's features and products. This includes the documentation platform itself, our content pipelines, API documentation, and search. You'll work on a highly-visible customer-facing documentation site and you'll influence how documentation gets created, published, and consumed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Continuously improve and evolve our documentation site to create a helpful, delightful, and educational experience that helps homelabbers, developers, and enterprises (and their agents) get the most of out of Tailscale's products and features.
  • Maintain and expand our docs-as-code tooling and content publishing pipelines, ensuring a smooth experience for both internal and external contributors to publish error-free content quickly.
  • Work cross-functionally with other engineering and product teams to integrate documentation with the Tailscale platform.
  • Partner with designers, user research, and growth teams to implement designs and features as they move from experimentation to production.
  • Help shape and tune a modern search and discovery infrastructure to help visitors, and agents, discover answers quickly.
  • Work with open source contributors through GitHub discussions, issues, and pull requests.
  • Monitor the platform's performance and maintain scalability for millions of visitors per month.
  • Stay ahead of documentation trends through research, experimentation, and measuring results.

What We Are Looking For

  • You have a proven track record of building, extending, maintaining, and deploying documentation sites using static site generators like Hugo or Docusaurus, or documentation platforms built on NextJS, Astro, or other similar tools.
  • You are familiar with a docs-as-code publication workflow, and you understand the impact that a good documentation experience has on user awareness, adoption, and growth.
  • You are fluent in TypeScript / JavaScript and React, and are comfortable with Python, command-line interfaces, shell scripting, and Git,
  • You are comfortable with modern HTML and CSS and can implement designs provided by others.
  • You have experience with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and automation for content or documentation workflows to reduce repetitive tasks and provide real-time feedback to authors.
  • You can explain the impact and implications of LLMs as both tools for application development, and as consumers of content that documentation sites publish.

Nice to Have

  • You have open source experience and are comfortable engaging with contributors on GitHub issues and pull requests.
  • You understand how LLMs consume and interact with documentation.
  • You understand multi-format content output (HTML, JSON, XML, raw Markdown, PDF, ePub

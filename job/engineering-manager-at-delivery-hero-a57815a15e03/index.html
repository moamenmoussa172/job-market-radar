About the job

A little bit about the role:
We are seeking an Engineering Manager for the Core Services Backend Domain. The focus of the Vendor Growth product line is to enhance vendor onboarding processes and availability tracking, product catalog integrations and Point of Sale systems, and, order transmission between logistics networks and business systems. This role is part of the Core Platforms tribe, which drives the development of our core platforms to ensure a coherent and seamless experience.

Role summary
● Alongside your team counterparts (product, design, data), understand your
customer/business needs/desires/problems to drive desired outcomes and create a strategy
to drive desired outcomes
● Lead, inspire and empower a talented group of engineers to self-organize and discover,
innovate, implement, deploy and own end-to-end complex software solutions
● Develop and grow engineering talent through effective mentoring, coaching, and retention
strategies in a hyper-growing, highly competitive economic environment
● Guide your team in creation of software as a solution to an end-to-end problem in your
business domain and deliver value in small batches, taking into account non-functional
requirements such as availability, scalability, latency and efficiency of talabat’s services in line with our internal engineering standards.
● Guide your team’s discovery, planning and execution to successfully deliver value to our customers in small batches

The following experience is relevant to us

● 8+ years of software engineering experience with 2+ years in technical leadership and people management

● Proven ability to build, lead, and empower cross-functional, self-organized teams of multidisciplinary engineers

● Strong product sense and ability to balance continuous discovery with delivery of value in small batches

● Demonstrated talent for mentoring, coaching, and developing engineering talent in competitive environments

● Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills with ability to create compelling narratives

● Solid understanding of lean/agile principles and practices

● Technical expertise in Distributed Systems Architecture (Microservices, Event-Driven, RESTful Services) and full mobile development lifecycle

● Hands-on experience with Trunk Based Development, Continuous Delivery, and automated testing practices

● Strong proficiency in designing, implementing, and scaling web applications and APIs on cloud platforms (preferably AWS)

● Deep understanding of quality, performance, monitoring, alerting, and security standards

● Experience integrating AI tools and LLM-based solutions to enhance team productivity and code quality

● Humble, curious, and growth-minded with openness to continuous feedback

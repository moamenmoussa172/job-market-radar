About the job

Business Solution Architect, Logistics and Trade Compliance.

Description -

  • We are looking for a Business Solution Architect, Logistics and Trade Complianceto lead the transformation of logistics, customs & compliance processes across HP’s Service Supply Chain as part of a multi-year SAP S/4 journey.

This is a high-impact, global role where you will:

  • Shape how logistics processes operate end-to-end across regions
  • Influence system design and business decisions in SAP S/4
  • Drive cross-functional integration, standardization and simplification at scale
  • Define Transformation vision aligned to strategy

If you are looking to step into a strategic, transformation-focused role with global exposure, this is your opportunity.

What you will be accountable for

You will act as the Subject Matter Expert on the global process, bridging business, technology, and operations.

1. Logistics Business Solution Architecture

  • Lead solution architecture for logistics capabilities and transformation initiatives, translating business problems and requirements into end-to-end solution designs.
  • Assess current processes, systems and integration points, identifying opportunities for simplification, standardization and automation.
  • Partner with process owners, Operations and Digital Technology teams to define business capabilities, solution requirements and implementation roadmaps.
  • Ensure solutions consider the complete business process, including upstream/downstream dependencies, data, controls and operational impacts.
  • Provide solution architecture guidance across initiatives such as ERP/SAP transformation, logistics automation, returns, transportation, warehousing and trade-related capabilities.
  • undefined

2. Customs & Compliance Expertise

  • Serve as the functional expert and key engagement point for Customs & Compliance across logistics transformation and technology initiatives.
  • Translate customs, import/export and trade-compliance requirements into business processes, controls, data requirements and system capabilities.
  • Advise project teams on areas including customs declarations, classification, valuation, country of origin, export/import controls, documentation and auditability.
  • Identify compliance risks and required controls early during solution design rather than addressing them downstream during deployment.
  • Partner with Customs & Compliance stakeholders to ensure changing regulatory requirements are reflected appropriately in process and solution roadmaps.
  • undefined

3. Solution Governance & Design

  • Define and document business solution architecture, including process flows, capability requirements, business rules, system interactions and control points.
  • Challenge unnecessary complexity and customization, promoting standardized and sustainable solutions wherever possible.
  • Ensure business requirements, solution design and implementation remain aligned throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Support architecture and design reviews, providing recommendations and highlighting business or compliance implications of proposed solutions.
  • Maintain clear traceability between business requirements, compliance requirements and implemented solutions.
  • undefined

4. Cross-functional Engagement

  • Act as the connection point across Logistics, Customs & Compliance, Finance/Tax, Operations and Digital Technology.
  • Facilitate discussions where business requirements, regulatory obligations and system constraints need to be reconciled.
  • Engage regional and country stakeholders to understand local regulatory requirements while driving global standardization where feasible.
  • Provide subject-matter guidance to programs and projects requiring Customs & Compliance expertise.
  • Influence stakeholders toward solutions that balance compliance, customer experience, operational efficiency, cost and technology sustainability.

Key Deliverables

The role would typically be accountable for:

  • End-to-end logistics solution designs
  • Business capability and process architecture
  • Customs & Compliance solution requirements
  • Business requirements and functional design inputs
  • Process/control requirements and traceability
  • Architecture/design assessments
  • Gap and impact assessments
  • Solution options and recommendations
  • Transformation roadmaps
  • Business-to-DT alignment through design and implementation

Who should apply

  • Experienced Business Solution Architects, Logistics/Trade Transformation professionals, or Customs & Compliance leaders with strong technology and process-design experience who can bridge business, regulatory and digital requirements to design scalable end-to-end logistics solutions.

What you bring

  • 8+ years of experience across logistics, supply chain, solution architecture, process transformation or trade compliance, preferably in a multinational environment.
  • Strong domain knowledge across logistics processes such as transportation, warehousing, fulfillment, returns and import/export.
  • Demonstrated expertise in Customs & Compliance, including practical understanding of import/export processes, customs documentation, classification, valuation, origin and regulatory controls.
  • Experience translating business requirements into technology-enabled solutions.
  • Experience working with ERP and logistics platforms. SAP/S4 experience would be highly desirable given the transformation environment.
  • Demonstrated ability to work across Business, Operations and Digital Technology organizations.
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to identify process, system, data and control implications across an end-to-end solution.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills, including the ability to communicate complex compliance or technical issues clearly to business leaders.

Must have

  • Able to work cross time-zone, especially in Europe and Americas time-zone.
  • Business Solution Architecture: Demonstrated ability to translate business needs into end-to-end process, capability, data, controls and technology solutions, not simply gather requirements.
  • Logistics domain expertise: Strong understanding of end-to-end logistics, including transportation, warehousing/fulfillment, returns and cross-border import/export flows.
  • Customs & Compliance knowledge: Practical experience with key areas such as HS classification, customs valuation, country of origin, customs documentation and import/export controls.
  • Transformation experience: Experience leading or contributing to significant process and technology transformation, including solution design through implementation.
  • Business-to-technology translation: Able to work effectively between Operations/Business, Customs & Compliance and Digital Technology teams.
  • Stakeholder engagement: Able to act as the primary Customs & Compliance engagement point, challenge requirements and influence solution decisions across functions and regions.
  • Global/regional experience: Experience working in a multinational environment where global process standards need to accommodate country-specific regulatory requirements.
  • undefined

Nice to have

  • SAP S/4HANA logistics transformation experience.
  • SAP GTS or another Global Trade Management platform.
  • Experience with logistics applications covering TMS, WMS, returns or trade-compliance automation.
  • Experience integrating with customs brokers, freight forwarders, carriers and 3PLs.
  • Deeper expertise in areas such as rules of origin, Free Trade Agreements, denied-party screening, export controls and customs audit requirements.
  • Experience designing automated compliance controls rather than relying on manual controls.
  • Experience in process architecture / capability modeling / Clean Core principles.
  • Experience leading global standardization while managing legitimate localization requirements.

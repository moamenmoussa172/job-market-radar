About the job

Meet the Team

Join Cisco's Cloud and Data Center Engineering team - a collaborative group of engineers, architects, and operations specialists dedicated to designing, implementing, and optimizing Cisco's cloud-based and hybrid infrastructure with a strong focus on network engineering and data center operations. Our team partners with product managers, security, and IT operations to deliver secure, scalable, and reliable solutions, while staying aligned with Cisco's security standards and industry best practices. We thrive on cross-functional collaboration, continuous learning, and delivering high-impact solutions that power Cisco's business.

Your Impact

  • Design, develop, and implement cloud-based and data center network solutions, combining cutting-edge technologies to optimize architecture in line with Cisco's security standards and policies.
  • Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and security teams to develop detailed specifications for new cloud-based products, services, and applications, focusing on network security optimization, scalability, and performance.
  • Plan and execute compute and storage migration activities for data center relocation or consolidation projects, including assessing environments, creating migration strategies, and mitigating risks.
  • Deploy, configure, and maintain virtualization platforms (e.g., VMware, OpenStack), performing both live and cold VM migrations and decommissioning with minimal service disruption.
  • Build and maintain enterprise storage systems (NAS/SAN), including volume/LUN creation, host mapping, multipathing, and data replication.

Minimum Qualifications

  • A completed Degree in Computer Science, or Engineering, combined with 8+ years of experience such as network engineering or data center operations
  • Experience in deploying, administering, and operating virtualization platforms (VMware, OpenStack, Hyper-V).
  • Experience in building, operating, tuning, and troubleshooting Linux and Windows Server environments for high availability and performance.
  • Experience in enterprise storage systems (e.g., NetApp, Dell EMC) and advanced storage architectures (NAS, SAN), including volume/LUN management, host mapping, multipathing, data replication, and snapshots.
  • Experience in Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible, GitOps).
  • Experience with IPv4/IPv6 networking and experience with cloud network architecture and automation.
  • Experience with scripting languages (Python, Bash, PowerShell) for infrastructure automation.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in data center operations, including hardware installation, rack management, cabling, power management, and environmental monitoring.
  • Experience with container technologies (Docker, Kubernetes, etc.).
  • Experience in automation and modern DevOps practices.
  • Experience with Source Code Control Management and CI/CD tools (Git, Jenkins, Argo, Harness, etc.).
  • Experience with Cloud Platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
  • Experience in development of next gen agentic AI ops agents

About this role

We are seeking a Telecom Billing Operations Engineer to provide hands-on L2 operational ownership of telecom billing platforms and billing-cycle activities. This role is responsible for ensuring the successful execution of bill runs, invoice processing, call/CDR handling, reconciliation activities, and exception management while maintaining SLA and KPI commitments. The individual will collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve billing, rating, mediation and integration issues, contribute to automation and AI-enabled operational improvements, and support governance, compliance and continual service improvement initiatives. This opportunity is ideal for professionals with 2 to 5 years of Telecom IT, BSS or enterprise billing application operations experience and strong production-support expertise

What you will do

Billing Operations & Bill Run Processing
•Own the day-to-day operational health and technical support of billing processes within the assigned service scope.
•Execute and monitor pre-bill checks, bill preparation, bill production, bill generation, customer notifications and bill-cycle closure activities.
•Diagnose and resolve bill-run failures, rejected accounts, long-running jobs, capacity constraints, database issues, scheduler failures and billing exceptions.
•Perform post-bill validations, billed-account reviews, amount reconciliations and exception tracking to support accurate and timely cycle closure.
Invoice Posting & Downstream Processing
•Monitor invoice generation, validation, posting, extraction and delivery to document management, printing and reporting platforms.
•Investigate invoice rejections, posting failures, missing outputs, balance mismatches and data-quality issues, coordinating service restoration within agreed timelines.
Call / CDR Processing
•Monitor daily call, event and CDR processing, including rating and re-rating activities, suspended or rejected records, processing backlogs and delayed usage records.
•Troubleshoot file validation, processing-chain, sequencing, capacity and data-quality issues affecting throughput and performance.
•Support record recycling and reprocessing activities to maintain processing accuracy and service continuity.
Discrepancy Management & Billing Assurance
•Analyse discrepancies across customer, plan, discount, billing profile, usage, invoice, payment and accounts receivable data.
•Coordinate reconciliations across CRM, mediation, billing, invoicing and downstream systems, ensuring timely resolution of exceptions.
•Identify recurring mismatch patterns and support actions to reduce billing and revenue risks.
Incident, Problem, SLA & Service Management
•Lead L2 technical triage and restoration activities for priority billing incidents, engaging specialist support teams and vendors where required.
•Complete root cause analysis, maintain known-error records and implement corrective actions to reduce recurring issues.
•Monitor SLA and KPI performance, MTTR, backlog levels, operational risks and incident ageing, escalating concerns appropriately.
Automation, AI & Continual Improvement
•Develop and enhance automation for health checks, validations, reconciliations, batch monitoring, alerting and operational reporting using Shell scripting, SQL/PLSQL and approved automation platforms.
•Improve monitoring dashboards, proactive alerting capabilities and operational visibility across billing processes.
•Apply approved AI and AIOps capabilities to support anomaly detection, event correlation, incident triage, knowledge retrieval and root cause investigations with appropriate validation controls.
•Contribute to continual improvement initiatives focused on billing accuracy, operational efficiency, reduced manual effort and faster issue resolution.
Operational Governance & Knowledge Management
•Provide concise operational status updates covering SLA/KPI performance, bill-cycle status, incidents, discrepancy trends and improvement activities.
•Maintain runbooks, billing calendars, operating procedures, reconciliation controls, recovery processes, known-error documentation and audit evidence.

Who you are

2+ years of Telecom IT, BSS or enterprise billing application operations experience with strong production-support responsibility.
•Hands-on experience with telecom billing platforms such as Oracle BRM, Amdocs, Kenan, Geneva/SingleView, BSCS or equivalent billing solutions.
•Proven experience in bill-run processing, invoice generation and posting, call/CDR processing, billing assurance and discrepancy management.
•Strong Unix/Linux administration and Shell scripting capabilities.
•Advanced SQL and PL/SQL knowledge for troubleshooting, data validation, reconciliation and operational automation.
•Experience working with application logs, batch processing, schedulers, file-processing systems and database troubleshooting.
•Understanding of integrations between CRM, mediation, billing, invoicing, payment, reporting and document management systems.
•Exposure to monitoring and observability tools such as Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Kibana, Splunk, Datadog, Grafana, Zabbix or similar platforms.
•Knowledge of ITIL-based Incident, Problem, Change and Service Request processes.
•Familiarity with billing assurance, reconciliation controls, audit readiness and operational governance.
•Strong analytical thinking, communication and stakeholder management skills.
•Bachelor’s degree or equivalent relevant professional experience.
•ITIL certification or billing-platform certifications are desirable.
•Exposure to SRE practices, cloud technologies, containerised services and AIOps capabilities would be advantageous.

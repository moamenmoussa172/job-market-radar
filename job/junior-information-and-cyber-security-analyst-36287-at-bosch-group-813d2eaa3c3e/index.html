About the job

Bosch (RB) has acquired the Residential & Light Commercial (R&LC) business from Johnson Controls
(JCI) and Johnson Controls-Hitachi (JCH). The integration of the acquired IT environment into the
Bosch IT network is planned for completion by December 2029. Throughout this transition, it is
crucial to maintain business continuity while also preparing for the Bosch organizational and
network integration.

We are seeking a motivated and eager-to-learn Junior Information & Cyber Security Analyst to contribute to the successful integration of our newly acquired R&LC business.

Key Responsibilities:

- Information & Cyber Security:
- Representing the role of the Cyber Security Manager for assigned projects
- Main contact for the assigned sites or IT applications for the deployment of Information &
Cyber Security standards.
- Planning and performance of Cyber Security Assessments (e.g. Security Audits, Risk Analysis).
- Delivery of standardized Cyber Security & IT Compliance services (e.g. Vulnerability
Management, review of Operator Self-Checks, coordination of PenTests, Awareness Sessions, training,
templates) for IT compliance partners, IT owners, managers and associates.
- Support in preparation of security reports for stakeholders (e.g. Plant Managers, IT owners).
- IT Risk & Compliance Management:
- Consulting and support in the implementation of sustainable and effective measures for risk
mitigation and IT Compliance.
- Responsible for the inventory and appropriate follow-up of risks and respective measures to
ensure timely closure of agreed-upon measures.
- Support in the establishment of a positive IT risk-aware culture.

#LI-MM1

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Business, or related discipline.
- in Information Security, Cyber Security for Enterprise IT, IT Risk Analysis, or Audits.
- Experience participating in comprehensive Cyber Security assessments or IT audits.
- Understanding of common IT control frameworks (e.g. ISO 27001, CIS) or Bosch IT regulatory requirements (e.g. CD09000, CD02900).
- Stakeholder-driven attitude to consult and support IT owners and head of departments.
- Systematic approach as well as strong analytical and problem-solving skills with meticulous attention to detail.
- Written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to effectively articulate technical concepts to diverse audiences.
- Motivation to work as part of a global team in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
- Presentation and training delivery skills.

- Fluent English.

Preferred:
• Exposure to or interest in pursuing relevant industry certifications such as ISO 27001 Lead Auditor, CRISC, CISSP, CISM, CISA, or equivalent.

***Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English.

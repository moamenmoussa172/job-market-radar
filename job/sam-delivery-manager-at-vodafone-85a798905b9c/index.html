About the role:

Location:Newbury (Hybrid)

The SAM Delivery Manager is responsible for the successful delivery of Software Asset Management (SAM) services to assigned customers across the globe, ensuring operational excellence, customer satisfaction, commercial performance, and business growth.
The role acts as the primary customer-facing delivery lead, accountable for managing customer relationships, service performance, onboarding activities, financial governance, and support for new business opportunities. The SAM Delivery Manager works closely with customers, delivery teams, sales, procurement, finance, and service management stakeholders to drive value realization, strengthen customer partnerships, and support the expansion of SAM services both within and outside of Vodafone.

Key Responsibilities:
• Own the end-to-end delivery of Software Asset Management services for assigned customers
• Act as the primary delivery contact and trusted advisor for customer stakeholders
• Ensure services are delivered in accordance with contractual commitments, SLAs, KPIs, and governance requirements.
• Lead regular service reviews, operational meetings, and executive business reviews.
• Drive continual service improvement initiatives that enhance customer outcomes and operational efficiency.
• Support development and review of business cases, pricing models, and commercial proposals.
• Ensure accurate tracking of customer consumption, chargeback and contractual commitments.
• Lead customer onboarding activities for new SAM engagements.
• Facilitate customer understanding and adoption of SAM processes, tooling, and governance frameworks
• Support qualification, shaping, and development of new business opportunities.
• Contribute to RFPs, RFIs, proposals, and customer presentations.
• Identify expansion opportunities within existing customer accounts.
• Foster a customer-centric culture focused on service quality and business value.
• Actively gather customer feedback and translate insights into service improvements.
• Ensure customers achieve value realization objectives and agreed implementation outcomes.
• Talk with knowledge and confidence regarding SAM delivery, including license compliance metrics, software risk profiles and software tooling capabilities.

What you’ll do

• Ensure contractual commitments, SLAs, KPIs, and governance obligations are consistently achieved.
• Own operational escalations, risk management, and service improvement plans
• Represent the voice of the customer within the SAMaaS organisation
• Identify opportunities for cost optimisation, efficiency improvements, and revenue growth.
• Ensure financial commitments and contractual obligations are accurately tracked and managed
• Ensure customers adopt agreed SAM processes, tools, and reporting capabilities
• Support the development of new business opportunities within existing and prospective customers
• Identify opportunities to expand service adoption and increase customer value.
• Manage risks, dependencies, actions, and improvement plans across customer portfolios

Who you are

• 5+ years of experience in in Software Asset Management, IT Service Management, Managed Services, or similar customer-facing delivery roles.
• Proven experience managing customer relationships within large enterprise environments.
• Experience leading customer onboarding and service transition activities.
• Experience supporting bids, proposals, and pre-sales activities.
• Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills.
• Understanding of ITIL Service Management Principles
• Experience of working as a provider of outsourced or managed service environments
• Demonstrable knowledge of Asset Lifecycle Processes (Acquire, Deploy, Manage, Retire) ensuing processes are executed in line with organisational objectives
• Experience in supporting different licensing models across multiple asset types and business units.
• Ability to work across cross-functional and cultural boundaries to deliver service and change.
• Strong communication and decision-making skills with the ability to balance conflicting interests between team members, customers and business requirements
• Experience in financial planning, cost modelling, and chargeback models, showing ability to interpret and present actionable insights.
• Excellent problem-solving skills, with ability to manage interdependencies and minimize overhead tasks.
• Ability to contribute best practices around quality standards and continuous improvement to enhance organizational capability.
• Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with ability to craft compelling narratives for strategic communications and bid support.

Must have technical / professional qualifications:
• ITIL Foundation certification (preferred)
• Certification in an Asset Management qualification such as CHAMP/CSAM/ ISEB/MCP/PITAM/ITIL (preferred)
• Prince2, PMP, Agile, or equivalent project management qualification (preferred)
• Hands on experience of using of ITAM toolsets, preferably SNOW/Atlas Software, BMC Helix, SaaS Cloud Portals.
• Familiarity with financial planning and chargeback models.
• Knowledge of SAM adjacent functions such as Cyber, Procurement, Finance, Compliance and Legal are desirable

What's in it for you

This role offers the opportunity to work across global customer environments and build experience at the intersection of Software Asset Management, service delivery, customer engagement and commercial management. You will contribute to customer value realisation, continual service improvement and the growth of SAM services while collaborating across multiple business functions and cultures.

What skills you will learn

You will have opportunities to deepen your expertise in enterprise SAM delivery, customer onboarding, service governance, licence compliance, software risk management and ITAM tooling. The scope also provides exposure to commercial proposal development, financial and chargeback models, customer value realisation, executive service reviews and cross-functional collaboration across Cyber, Procurement, Finance, Compliance and Legal.

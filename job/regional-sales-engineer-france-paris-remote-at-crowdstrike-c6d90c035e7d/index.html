About the Role:

Are you ready for your next real security challenge? Are you passionate about helping organizations stop breaches using a platform that just works? If so, CrowdStrike is seeking a Sales Engineer with significant security expertise. You should be able to articulate how current security solutions are designed and deployed, and why they are failing. You will be part of a team responsible for changing what the security market and customers believe is possible with the AI-native CrowdStrike Falcon platform, spanning endpoint, cloud, identity, exposure management and next-gen SIEM.

You should be comfortable working in a fast-paced environment on multiple engagements.

This role is remote-based in or around the Paris area.

What You'll Do:

  • Understand customer needs: lead technical discovery to understand customer pain points, goals and risk exposure, and qualify opportunities with MEDDPICC, from the first engagement through expansion.
  • Be a trusted advisor: help customers understand their risk exposure and how CrowdStrike is uniquely positioned to reduce it, building long-term relationships with technical and executive stakeholders.
  • Present and demonstrate: deliver compelling presentations, whiteboards and tailored demos of the Falcon platform across AI, endpoint, cloud, identity, exposure management and next-gen SIEM, from SOC analysts to C-level executives.
  • Deliver Proofs of Value: design and run POVs tied to measurable customer objectives (risk reduction, improved efficiency, cost savings) and turn them into technical wins.
  • Answer RFPs: own the technical response to customer RFPs and security questionnaires.
  • Work as one team: act as the technical bridge between CrowdStrike and customers, aligning with Sales on account strategy and working with specialists (CTEM, AI, SecOps, Identity), Product, Marketing and Customer Success.
  • Share and continuously improve: share projects, scripts and best practices with the team, use customer feedback to improve the pre-sales process, and build technical champions in your accounts.

What You'll Need:

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in a relevant field
  • Sales engineering background or 5+ years of relevant work experience
  • Broad cybersecurity background across multiple domains – endpoint (EDR/XDR), security operations (SIEM/SOAR, incident response, forensics, threat hunting), cloud security, identity protection and exposure/vulnerability management – with a clear understanding of how they come together in a modern security architecture
  • Strong understanding of Windows, macOS and Linux operating systems
  • Strong self-starter, able to work independently and manage multiple engagements at once
  • Ability to travel as required
  • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to present to a variety of external audiences, including C-level executives
  • Extensive contacts in the information security field and strong references
  • English and French fluency

Preferred Skills:

  • CTEM (Continuous Threat Exposure Management): exposure and vulnerability management, internal and external attack surface management, asset discovery and risk-based prioritization of remediation
  • AI: AI-driven SOC workflows and agentic automation, securing AI/LLM workloads (AI-SPM, prompt injection, data leakage), and positioning AI-native security to technical and business audiences
  • SecOps / Next-Gen SIEM: SIEM and log management, SOAR and workflow automation, SOC modernization and legacy SIEM displacement (e.g. Splunk, QRadar, Sentinel), detection engineering and threat hunting
  • Identity: Active Directory and Entra ID security, Identity Threat Detection and Response (ITDR), credential-based attacks and lateral movement, Zero Trust and IdP integrations (Okta, Ping)

Bonus Points:

  • Experience with malware, threat intelligence, and/or sandbox analysis
  • Experience with programming or scripting languages such as PowerShell, Python, and Bash
  • Experience with virtualization/VDI technologies
  • Experience with large scale cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, GCP, etc.
  • Understanding of the OSI model
  • Certifications such as CEH, CISSP, OSCP, and SANS

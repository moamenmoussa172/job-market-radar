About the job

We are seeking a skilled and experienced Product Manager to join our team at Nawy Real Estate. As a Product Manager, you will be responsible for driving the success of our real estate platform by defining and executing the product strategy. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, stakeholders, and customers to identify product enhancements, prioritize features, and ensure a seamless user experience throughout the platform. If you have a passion for real estate and a proven track record in product management, we would love to hear from you!

Requirements

Bachelor's Degree required or equivalent in education and experience 3-6 years of experience as a Product Owner/Product Manager with software for mission-critical products, working with Agile methodology.

- Background in engineering, technology, or UX preferred

- Proven experience in customer development, product definition, feature scoping, and balancing tradeoffs for industry

-leading business software products.

- Experience with creating/managing/delivering

- Strong organizational skills: ability to track and prioritize multiple issues.

- Ability to adapt to changing situations and requirements.

- Excellent oral, written communication, and presentation skills required.

- Must be a strong collaborator, team player, and individual contributor.

- Willing to learn new product management skills and techniques.

- Experience working with agile teams.

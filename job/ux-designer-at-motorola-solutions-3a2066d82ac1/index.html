Job Description

As an Associate UX Designer, you will be a key contributor to our design team in Edinburgh. This role is a hands-on blend of user-centered design, prototyping, and foundational user research. It's a perfect opportunity for an early-career design professional or graduate who is passionate about creating intuitive experiences, learning from senior mentors, and growing their skill set within a dynamic product design environment.

What You'll Do

  • Deliver User-Centered Designs: Work closely with developers, Product Managers, and senior designers across teams to deliver user-friendly wireframes, user flows, and interface designs based on UX research.
  • Support Design Strategy: Assist in translating user feedback and project goals into actionable design solutions that address user needs.
  • Participate in Discovery: Support user research sessions, usability testing, and workshops to gather user insights and help analyze feedback.
  • Prototype & Iterate: Rapidly turn concepts into interactive prototypes using modern design tools, iterating based on feedback from team members and users.
  • Collaborate Across Disciplines: Partner with cross-functional team members in Product, Engineering, and Research to understand technical constraints and user needs.
  • Communicate Design Rationale: Clearly present your design concepts and underlying decisions during team reviews and design critiques.
  • Elevate Our Practice: Continuously refine and improve our team's design processes, methodologies, and collaborative practices.


Basic Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in HCI, Design, Psychology, Computer Science (with a focus on AI/HCI), or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Previous experience in UX/UI design, product design, or a related field (internship experience included).
  • A portfolio demonstrating user-centered design processes, wireframes, visual design, and problem-solving skills.
  • Familiarity with design tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, or similar prototyping applications.
  • Understanding of core user-centered design principles, wireframing, and basic usability research methods.
  • Exceptional communication and storytelling skills (verbal, written, and visual), with the ability to distill complex information into clear, compelling narratives.
  • Proficiency with modern design and prototyping tools to rapidly visualize and test ideas.

What Sets You Apart (Preferred Qualifications)

  • A foundational understanding of core AI concepts (e.g., Machine Learning, NLP, Generative AI), including their capabilities, limitations, and ethical considerations.
  • Experience using AI tools to rapidly prototype experiences or visualize complex data.
  • A passion for speculative design and exploring novel applications of emerging AI to envision future user experiences.
  • Comfort navigating ambiguity and a proven ability to thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

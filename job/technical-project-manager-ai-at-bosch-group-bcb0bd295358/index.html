About the job

Job Description:

  • Expected Around 10+ - 12Yrs As a Project Manager, you will be responsible for planning, organizing, and executing data-led technology projects for automation (Generative AI/AI/ML/NLP/Computer Vision).
  • You will work across data science, engineering, sales teams, and domain experts throughout the lifecycle of projects: from proposal to closure.

Your key responsibilities include:·

  • Working with business customers to identify new opportunities with Automation, AI/ML, NLP, Computer Vision, etc. that aligns with department goal·
  • Lead and grow the team on Data scientists and AI engineers. Provide tactical leadership and manage AI/ML teams with diverse talents and responsibilities.·
  • Manages and leads team, communicates goals and objectives, provides iterative feedback and monitors performance of team members.·
  • Contributes to the growth of the organization in acquiring new customers, hiring, training, and mentoring teams for growth and competency development.·
  • Manage the continuous improvement of data science and machine learning led initiatives by following industry best practices and staying up to date with and extending the state-of-the-art in data science research.·
  • Lead teams building new assets and products by supporting the team and assisting to clear QG stages·
  • Organizing programs and activities in accordance with the mission and goals of the department and the organization.·
  • Developing new programs to support the strategic direction of the department. Identify cross-team dependencies to incorporate them into the program planning process.·
  • Developing a budget, operating plan for the program, evaluation method to assess program strengths and identify areas for improvement.·
  • Managing schedule, planning, audit, PSR, PQSR, Management review meeting for projects that are being handled·
  • Writing funding proposals to guarantee uninterrupted delivery of services.·
  • Ensuring project goals are met in areas including customer satisfaction, safety, quality, and team member performance.·
  • Meeting with stakeholders to make communication easy and transparent regarding project issues and decisions on services to senior management and cross-functional stakeholders·
  • Producing accurate and timely reporting of project status throughout its life cycle. Anticipating risks and mitigation plans.

Requirements:·

  • 10+ years of experience in software development lifecycle, 5+ years of experience on AI/ML delivery management·
  • Prior experience with accessibility solutions and their technical aspects is a plus.·
  • An ability to identify customer needs and translate them into technical and product requirements is a must.· You need to have a customer-first mindset to determine what the customer wants, how they want it, and in what capacity.·
  • Prior experience in planning and conducting usability studies among various groups of customers is a preference.
  • Experience in conducting usability studies based on accessibility solutions is a plus.·
  • Willingness to keep up to date w.r.t. new AI/ML and other niche technologies.·
  • Research mindset to understand upcoming trends and technologies and challenge the status quo.·
  • Proven experience delivering solutions to customers and managing teams.·
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills·
  • A good understanding of business administration and management·
  • Experience with SDLC methodologies such as Agile, Waterfall, SAFe etc.·
  • Domain expertise in mobility, healthcare, industry 4.0 would be a bonus.

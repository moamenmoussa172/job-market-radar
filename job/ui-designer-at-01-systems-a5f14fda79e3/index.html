About the job

We are looking for a seasoned and detail-oriented UX/UI designer who deeply understands real-world user experience challenges and knows how to apply modern AI tools to solve them effectively. The ideal candidate combines strong design intuition with a strong focus on delivering responsive, polished, and user-friendly interfaces for web and mobile applications.

This role demands more than just visual polish—it requires a designer who thinks in terms of user needs, adapts to constraints, and delivers practical, user-focused solutions that bridge design and implementation. This role emphasizes UI design execution, cross-functional collaboration, and production-ready visuals, with an additional requirement for form design, RTL interface support, and vector asset creation.

Success in this role hinges on your ability to translate complex requirements into user-centred interfaces that are both functional and visually refined, all while leveraging the latest design tools and AI-enhanced workflows to accelerate and elevate outcomes.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Design intuitive, accessible, and visually engaging user interfaces for web and mobile platforms.
  • Build and manage component libraries and design systems in Figma.
  • Design User Interfaces that not only address current requirements but also account for potential future requirements, elegantly and with scale.
  • Create rapid prototypes to validate the design approaches.
  • Design for Arabic (RTL) interfaces, ensuring accurate mirroring, alignment, and cultural nuances.
  • Create vector-based assets such as icons, graphical elements using Adobe Illustrator.
  • Design structured, fillable PDF and web-based forms with a focus on usability, clarity, and visual flow.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless design-to-development handoff.
  • Clearly present and explain design concepts and rationale in team and stakeholder reviews.
  • Ensure designs meet responsiveness, accessibility and performance standards.
  • Stay informed on the latest design trends, tools, and design system practices—including AI-enhanced workflows.
  • 3+ years of professional UI design experience, including mobile, web, and form-based UI.
  • Expert-level skills in Figma, including auto-layout, variants, design libraries, and prototyping.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop with strong vector asset creation experience.
  • Experience designing RTL (Arabic) user interfaces with accurate mirroring and typographic alignment.
  • Ability to understand product strategy and convert it into actionable design tasks
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating modern, responsive, and accessible UI design.
  • Solid understanding of responsive design, accessibility principles, and basic front-end structure (HTML/CSS).
  • Highly initiative, along with the ability to work with minimal oversight, and capability to multitask.
  • Strong communication skills and an ability to work effectively with developers, product teams, and stakeholders are essential.
  • Ability to design both PDF and web-based forms.

Skills and Experience

The ideal candidate brings:

  • Expert-level proficiency in Figma.
  • Hands-on Adobe Creative Suite skills.
  • Experience designing for English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) and bilingual interfaces.
  • Strong mobile and responsive UI work.
  • Creation of vector-based assets (e.g., icons, illustrations).
  • Ability to design both PDF and web-based forms.

Desirable skills:

  • Experience with Adobe After Effects, Lottie, or similar tools for animated UI illustrations and micro-interactions.
  • Familiarity with Adobe XD, Sketch, or animation plugins for Figma.
  • Knowledge of front-end frameworks such as React, Vue, or Angular for improved developer collaboration.
  • Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git) and coding environments (e.g., VS Code)
  • A collaborative team player willing to assist colleagues through brainstorming, reviews, and knowledge sharing.
  • Ability to analyse complex workflows and simplify them into intuitive experiences.
  • Familiarity in front-end languages including HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Familiarity with AI-enhanced design processes (e.g., generative AI for ideation and UI drafts)
  • Experience working in an agile development environment.
  • Experience with animation or rich visuals is a bonus.

Department Overview

The Customer Success Team within Systems Integration International is dedicated to managing the in-life and post-implementation journey of software customer accounts utilising Motorola Solutions' critical technologies across international markets. Serving as the primary point of operational contact, the team ensures a seamless experience across our Guardian portfolio (including CAD and ICCS), Kodiak Broadband Push-To-Talk (PTT) solutions, and Wave On-Premise systems. We engage with in-life customers to drive user adoption and satisfaction, forge highly effective customer partnerships, and establish a trusted strategic advisor relationship with target accounts. We foster collaboration across all international regions, public safety agencies, commercial enterprises, and telecommunications ecosystem partners to maximize the value and stability of their safety and security technology investments, ultimately "Solving for safer".


Job Description

As a Customer Success Lead Manager, you will manage and grow a dedicated team owning long-term relationship health, operational governance, and service performance for international accounts across our Guardian, Kodiak, and Wave On-Prem product lines. In this role, you will bridge the gap between regional teams, international customers, internal engineering teams, hosting operations, and external vendors to help ensure high solution availability, smooth software life cycles, and high customer satisfaction. At Motorola Solutions, everything starts with our people. We offer a people-first culture where you are empowered to bring your authentic self, drive proactive operational excellence, and help deliver on the promise of a safer world.

Key Responsibilities

Customer Relationship & Account Governance

  • Serve as the single point of contact for assigned international accounts across Guardian, Kodiak, and Wave On-Prem deployments, managing their ongoing in-life journey and account health
  • Author and distribute monthly Service Pack reports, and schedule, prepare, and deliver quarterly Service Reviews to evaluate performance metrics, SLAs, and expansion opportunities
  • Oversee account financial reporting both internally and externally, leading quarterly alignment with cross-functional customer success and commercial teams to maintain a unified international account strategy

Incident, Problem, & DevOps Governance

  • Co-lead regular customer Incident and Problem review meetings alongside Services teams to drive systemic issue resolution, bug fixes, and continuous service improvement
  • Run customer DevOps Backlog Review meetings to prioritize user stories, software patches, and feature enhancement requests across Guardian control room tools, Kodiak, and Wave On-Prem platforms
  • Administer Azure DevOps access, permissions, board configurations, and operational workflows for external customer stakeholders and internal teams

Software Life Cycle & Technical Coordination

  • Schedule, plan, and execute multi-product software upgrade cycles and patching through Staging, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and live Production deployment
  • Raise, document, and track Change Requests (CRs) while coordinating technical resourcing across Engineering, Hosting, Telecom/Carriers, and third-party vendors
  • Draft and dispatch clear technical customer advisories, collaborate with Regional Services teams to host live Technical Bridge Meetings during scheduled maintenance windows, and help to ensure proactive communication across high-availability mission-critical networks


Basic Requirements

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Business, Information Technology, Engineering, or a technical field
  • Experience in Customer Success, Technical Project Management, or IT Service Management (ITSM) supporting enterprise or mission-critical software solutions

Job Requirements

Technical Expertise

  • ServiceNow & Azure DevOps: Demonstrable proficiency with ServiceNow Change Management, Jira, and Azure DevOps board administration and workflows
  • Software Release Management: Strong understanding of end-to-end software release lifecycles across Staging, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and Production phases, infrastructure patching, and environment dependency management
  • Technical Communication: Excellent written and spoken English communication skills with a demonstrated ability to translate complex technical issues into clear customer advisories and command live technical bridge calls during service windows. A second language is an asset

Professional Experience

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Proven ability to align internal teams (Business Analysts, Hosting Engineers, Systems Test Engineers, Product Management) alongside external telecommunications partners and third-party vendors
  • Operational Governance: Demonstrated track record running incident/problem reviews, account financial tracking, and executive quarterly service reviews

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting SaaS, broadband PTT (Kodiak), mission-critical dispatch/CAD systems, or enterprise communications (Wave On-Prem)
  • Familiarity with international public safety environments, TETRA/LMR integration, control room operations, or carrier-grade telecommunications networks

About the job

Job Description:

A position of Industrialization Test Area Engineer is now open at Airbus CRISA (Airbus Defense & Space) in Tres Cantos (Madrid area). In our industrialization department, our mission is guarantee the technical / digital means needed to execute manufacturing and tests processes for Space Electronic equipment with excellent performance. Our way of working is built upon trust, based on the principles of collaboration & cooperation, autonomy / accountability and flexibility. Our activities are split into missions whose set-up are integrated in different Space business: Launchers, Earth Exploration (Copernicus), Space Exploration (Artemis), Commercial (Telecommunications Satellites).

This is an opportunity to join a diverse and dynamic Technical Team focused on:

  • Shape the future based on innovation of our products, new technologies and way of working,
  • State of the art product consolidation,
  • Continuous Performance improvement.

Be part of our diversity and teamwork culture that propel us to accomplish the extraordinary - on the ground, in the sky and in space. Definitely, we are moved by our passion to make it fly!

Task and accountabilities

We are looking for candidates who are sharing our values and want to make a true impact at Space Electronic business moving things faster, gaining agility, and being Innovative.

Your main tasks and responsibilities will be:

  • Define, specify, buy, deploy and support for new Industrial Test means (Climatic/ vacuum test chambers and shakers) according to customers requirement and ergonomics standards.
  • Lead continuous improvement of the existing Test means, implementing best practices according to Quality, Cost and Time, mainly increasing its automatization approach.
  • Managing dedicated software (f.i. LabView),
  • Managing automatisms, PLCs programming, HMIs, distributed systems, I/O controls and different communication protocols (TCP/UDP/IP, MOD-BUS, EtherCAT, etc.),.
  • Programming autonomous controls (programmed relays, thermal control modules,etc.).
  • Programming remote controls.
  • Support and troubleshooting in the shop floor
  • Define and execute Preventive and Corrective maintenance plan of Test equipment.
  • Provide support to customer (projects) during Vibration, Vacuum and Thermal test campaigns, including technical assessment and contributing actively to find adequate solutions.

Required skills and experience

We are looking for candidates with the following skills and experience:

  • Higher Education (Grade or Master) related to Industrial, Electronic, or Aeronautic Engineering
  • At least 5 years experienced leading Industrialization / Improvement Projects in Test Areas (Production and Test Sytems, Lean, MES..)
  • Previous experience with Vacuum systems, Climatic, Thermal chambers and Shakers maintenance and control.
  • Digital mindset (automation of tasks, scripting, use of digital design tools and simulators)
  • Electronic manufacturing and Test processes knowledge will be valued positively
  • English Language proficiency
  • Proactive, well organized, autonomous and with clear Team Work value
  • Continuous improvement orientation
  • Customer support approach, including Openness and Flexibility
  • Leader and booster for change management
  • Keen interest in Space Business

Feel like you can't tick some boxes above? If you have most of the skills and experience that we're looking for and are willing to use your talent to learn the rest, we encourage you to apply!

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Technology (CA)
Title: Mid-level Platform Engineer (Cloud & Infrastructure) - Indaiatuba/SP - 125049

We are looking for a Mid-level Platform Engineer to join our team at the Regional Office in Indaiatuba, SP. You will act as a key technical enabler, maintaining cloud infrastructure, enterprise databases, and core hosting platforms. Reporting locally while maintaining a dotted-line alignment with international platform leadership, you will partner with a global team. You will play an essential role in driving operational excellence, infrastructure as code automation, and mission-critical system reliability.

Main Responsibilities

Platform Infrastructure

  • Support enterprise hosting environments;
  • Manage server lifecycle activities;
  • Perform platform maintenance and upgrades;
  • Improve platform reliability and stability.

Cloud Operations

  • Support AWS-based infrastructure services;
  • Provision and manage cloud resources;
  • Participate in cloud governance and optimization efforts;
  • Support operational excellence initiatives.

Database Services

  • Support enterprise databases;
  • Execute monitoring and maintenance activities;
  • Perform troubleshooting and performance analysis;
  • Support backup and recovery processes.

Security & Access Management

  • Support password management solutions;
  • Manage privileged access controls;
  • Support security patching activities;
  • Remediate vulnerabilities and compliance findings.

Automation & Reliability

  • Develop automation scripts and tools;
  • Reduce manual operational activities;
  • Improve monitoring, alerting, and observability capabilities;
  • Support disaster recovery and resiliency initiatives.

AI & Modern Operations

  • Utilize AI-assisted operational tools;
  • Contribute to intelligent automation opportunities;
  • Support adoption of AIOps capabilities and operational copilots.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or
    equivalent discipline;
  • Advanced English;
  • Experience in platform engineering, including hands-on work with a infrastructure as code
    language such Terraform;
  • Experience working with relational or non-relational databases administration;
  • Working knowledge of cloud services and automated deployment pipelines;
  • Working knowledge of unit testing, secure coding, version control, and CI/CD practices;
  • Foundational understanding of AI concepts and experience using AI-enabled development;
    tools or integrating an AI service into an application;
  • Ability to break down defined problems, communicate progress and blockers, and collaborate
    with stakeholders and engineers;
  • Working knowledge of security by design principles;
  • Working knowledge of server OS administration (Linux, Windows Server);
  • Curiosity, learning agility, and a disciplined approach to experimentation;
  • Availability for a flexible schedule and participation in a defined on-call/maintenance rotation
    system that includes weekends;
  • Availability to work in a hybrid model (3 days per week on-site in Indaiatuba/SP).

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with workflow automation, low-code orchestration, or enterprise system
    integrations;
  • Experience with PL/SQL development or foundational understanding of this language;
  • Experience with prompt design, retrieval-augmented generation, AI evaluation, or responsible
  • AI practices;
  • Understanding of Product Management, discovery techniques, or service design;
  • Experience with test automation and observability.

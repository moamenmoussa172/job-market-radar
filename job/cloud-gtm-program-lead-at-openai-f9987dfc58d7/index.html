About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the role

OpenAI is looking for a Cloud GTM Program Lead to own end-to-end delivery of cross-functional launches for our cloud offerings, define business requirements and operating workflows, and drive execution across teams. You will bring new offerings, model releases and commercial changes into operation, from defining what needs to work through implementation, validation, rollout and operational handoff.

You will work with cloud partnership and strategy and operations leaders, Product, Engineering, IT, Product Marketing, Communications, GTM Enablement, Finance, Legal, operational teams and external cloud partners to translate business priorities into actionable requirements, align delivery owners and resolve dependencies.

In this role, you will

  • Define business requirements and design operating workflows for cloud GTM programs. Translate agreed partnership commitments and business needs into clear requirements and readiness criteria, and drive cross-functional implementation, validation, rollout and operational handoff.
  • Lead cross-functional launch and operational readiness for new model releases, pricing changes, and new marketplace or cloud launches. Own the integrated launch plan and drive execution with functional owners across technical delivery, field and partner enablement, and launch communications, with clear owners, milestones, and readiness criteria for each workstream. Coordinate listing readiness, implementation of approved pricing, dimension testing, and cloud-partner dependencies.
  • Translate seller, customer, and partner feedback into actionable requirements; distinguish approved commercial or policy decisions from the system changes needed to implement them.
  • Build and prioritize a systems improvement backlog with IT, Product, Finance, and Partner stakeholders.
  • Map how marketplace requests, offers, customer records, and downstream fulfillment or billing information move across systems; identify gaps, manual work, and unreliable handoffs.
  • Coordinate user acceptance testing and validation of operational workflows, track defects and launch risks, and ensure teams understand how to use changes.
  • Transition delivered capabilities and workflows to the relevant operational owners with clear responsibilities, documentation, training and escalation paths. Ensure teams are prepared to run the ongoing processes and use operational feedback to prioritize further improvements.
  • Track program progress and operational impact, communicate decisions and blockers clearly, and use recurring field issues to guide the next round of improvements.

You’ll bring

  • Experience managing systems, business operations, or technical programs that require coordinated delivery across commercial and technical teams.
  • The ability to turn ambiguous business needs into clear requirements, process maps, acceptance criteria, and executable plans.
  • Working knowledge of enterprise software transaction workflows, such as CRM, quoting, order management, provisioning, or billing, and how changes affect connected systems.
  • Strong program management judgment: you can set priorities, make delivery tradeoffs, manage dependencies, surface risks early, resolve ownership gaps, know when to escalate, and drive work through completion.
  • Clear written communication and the ability to work effectively with systems teams, product owners, finance partners, and customer-facing colleagues.
  • Experience leading cross-functional launch programs, including business-systems or operational delivery from requirements through UAT and handoff, field and partner enablement, and launch communications.
  • Experience with cloud co-sell programs, cloud marketplaces, private offers, partner portals, and marketplace integration platforms; Amazon Bedrock experience strongly preferred.

You might also have

  • Program management experience in a usage-based or consumption-based business.
  • Experience using data and automation to reduce repetitive operational work and improve the reliability of transaction workflows.

Success in this role

  • Launches meet agreed technical, operational, field and partner readiness criteria, with launch communications delivered as planned, dependencies resolved and operational owners prepared to support the release.
  • Delivered system and process changes reduce recurring transaction issues, manual steps, or avoidable delays against an established baseline.
  • Field feedback becomes a prioritized backlog with clear decisions, accountable owners, and visible delivery progress.

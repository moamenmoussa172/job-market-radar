About the job

CSQ427R351

At Databricks, we aim to empower our customers to solve the world's most challenging problems using the Data + AI Platform while putting trusted data and AI in the hands of every business user. As a Senior Scale Solution Engineer, you are a technical anchor for our scaled customer motion, advising customers across many stages of their lifecycle: from pre-sales evaluation through onboarding and into production. You independently take on complex engagements and build and improve AI-augmented workflows that enable the whole team to deliver high-quality guidance at scale.

The impact you will have:

  • You will ensure customers have an excellent experience by independently owning engagements across their lifecycle.
  • You will be the go-to expert on the Databricks Data + AI Platform, owning technical direction and building reusable AI-augmented workflows.
  • You will work directly with multiple customers concurrently and set the quality bar for the team.

What we look for:

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to assess customer needs, foster relationships, address objections, and effectively showcase the value of the Databricks platform.
  • Undergraduate degree or higher in Computer Science, Information Systems, or similar relevant experience.
  • 4+ years of experience in a technical role, preferably in the Data, AI, or Cloud field.
  • Deep knowledge of at least one of the following cloud platforms, AWS, Azure, or GCP; familiarity with more than one is a plus.
  • Knowledge of AI-driven development with programming languages like Python or SQL, including building and extending AI-augmented workflows.
  • Understanding of the end-to-end data analytics and data engineering workflow, and their trade-offs.
  • Excellent time management and prioritization skills.
  • Bonus: Experience building AI tooling and automation, consulting or pre-sales, or with agentic solutions.

About Databricks

Databricks is the Data and AI company. More than 20,000 organizations worldwide — including adidas, AT&T, Bayer, Block, Mastercard, Rivian, Unilever, and 70% of the Fortune 500 — rely on the Databricks Data + AI Platform to build and scale data and AI apps, analytics and agents. Headquartered in San Francisco with 30+ offices around the globe, Databricks offers a unified platform that includes Genie, Lakebase, Agent Bricks, Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Unity Catalog. To learn more, follow Databricks on LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and Instagram.

About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

What You Will Do

  • Develop & Maintain Test Packages: Assist in creating, updating, and maintaining automated test packages in ECU-TEST for smoke testing and ECU flashing procedures.
  • Support Standard Generators: Help build and maintain standard test generators that streamline the creation of test cases for the wider engineering team.
  • Execute Mapping Checks: Implement automated routines to verify signal mapping and configurations across different ECU software builds.
  • Troubleshoot & Debug: Analyze test execution logs, identify the root causes of failed tests, and collaborate with senior engineers to fix automation scripts.
  • Continuous Integration: Work with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins) to ensure test packages run smoothly in our automated build and release pipelines.
  • Learn & Grow: Actively learn automotive communication protocols and advanced ECU-TEST functionalities under the guidance of senior automation experts.

What We Are Looking For (Required Qualifications)

  • 1-2 years of experience in Testing
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, communication Engineering, Mechatronics, Automotive Engineering, or a related field.
  • Programming Skills: Solid foundational knowledge of at least one scripting language (Python is highly preferred, as it integrates heavily with automotive testing).
  • Basic Knowledge for software testing methodologies (ISTQB foundation is a plus).
  • Basic understanding of automotive bus systems and protocols (CAN, LIN, Ethernet, UDS, XCP/CCP).
  • Good Knowledge about TraceTronic ECU-TEST, Vector tools (CANoe/CANalyzer), or HIL/SIL environments.
  • Basic knowledge of version control systems (e.g., Git) and CI/CD concepts.
  • Analytical Mindset: Strong problem-solving skills and a meticulous approach to troubleshooting logic and configuration errors.
  • Team Player: Excellent communication skills and a willingness to ask questions, learn rapidly, and collaborate in a technical team environment

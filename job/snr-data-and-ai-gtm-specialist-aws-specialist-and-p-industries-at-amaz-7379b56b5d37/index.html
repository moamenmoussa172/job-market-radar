About the job

Are you a customer-obsessed leader with deep expertise in Data, Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence?
Do you have the strategic vision and business acumen to shape multi-year AI transformation programs for the world's largest enterprises?
Do you thrive at the intersection of technology and complex business challenges?
Join AWS Specialists & Partners for Industries (ASPI) organization within the Data & AI Specialists team for Automotive & Manufacturing as a Senior Data & AI Go To Market Specialist, requiring exceptional non-formal leadership, cross-functional orchestration, and strategic impact at the most senior customer and AWS leadership levels.
The ASPI organization is responsible for driving revenue and accelerating adoption of cloud and partner services across our most strategic global customers. We work backwards from our customers’ most complex and business-critical challenges to develop and execute go-to-market plans that transform ideas into scalable, high-impact businesses. ASPI teams include business development specialists and specialized solution architects.
As part of ASPI, you will contribute across the full lifecycle of AWS customer initiatives – from shaping new service and solution concepts to accelerating adoption of established offerings. We pride
ourselves on thinking big, delivering exceptional customer outcomes, and collaborating seamlessly across AWS as #OneTeam
Key job responsibilities
In this role, you will define and drive the Data & AI go-to-market strategy for the most complex and strategically significant customers in the Automotive & Manufacturing industry.
You will work across the full AWS Analytics and Generative AI & ML services portfolio and partner ecosystem solutions to develop multi-year transformation roadmaps that deliver measurable business outcomes at enterprise scale.
You will engage directly with C-suite executives, Chief Data Officers, Chief AI Officers, and senior business leaders at strategic enterprise customers to influence their data and AI strategy.
As a Principal-level individual contributor, you will operate with significant autonomy, set direction for cross-functional teams without formal authority, and represent AWS in the most critical customer and partner discussions.
As the ideal candidate, you bring an exceptional combination of deep domain expertise in Data & AI technologies and world-class business development skills. You have a proven track record of building and executing complex, multi-year strategic programs that generate
significant pipeline and revenue.
You excel at leading through influence in highly matrixed
environments, orchestrating diverse teams of specialists, solution architects, account managers, partner organizations, and AWS leadership to deliver outcomes that no single individual could achieve alone.

About the job

Location: Chennai, India (offshore delivery for a UAE based client)

Experience: 4+ years of hands-on Sitecore development

Domain: Banking, Wealth Management, Fintech

About the Role

We are hiring a Sitecore Engineer to join the digital delivery team of a leading UAE bank, working from Chennai. You will build, customize and scale the bank's customer-facing web platforms, focusing on banking and wealth journeys. You will also help the team adopt Sitecore's AI features (Sitecore AI, formerly XM Cloud, and Sitecore Stream) to deliver content faster and personalize it better.

This role suits someone who is highly motivated, eager to learn, quick to adapt to changing requirements, and comfortable working in a cross-functional Scrum team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop and maintain Sitecore websites and components using ASP.NET, C#, MVC and SQL
  • Define and build Sitecore templates, page layouts, renderings and components following Helix architecture principles
  • Implement custom rules, personalization and content targeting for retail banking and wealth customer journeys
  • Customize and extend Sitecore content APIs, including GraphQL / Experience Edge, used by other applications such as mobile apps and partner platforms
  • Build and support headless implementations using Sitecore JSS / Next.js where applicable
  • Configure and integrate Sitecore AI features: AI assisted content generation and optimization, Brand Kits, and AI driven personalization through Sitecore Personalize and CDP
  • Use AI coding assistants such as GitHub Copilot responsibly to improve delivery speed, code quality and test coverage
  • Deploy and support Sitecore in scaled, multi environment setups (Azure, containers, CI/CD pipelines)
  • Follow banking grade standards for secure coding, unit testing, code review and documentation
  • Take an active part in Scrum ceremonies with the UAE team: sprint planning, daily stand ups, reviews and retrospectives
  • Research and benchmark new technologies, and share knowledge to raise the productivity of the wider team
  • Present technical ideas and solution options clearly to senior and executive stakeholders

Must Have

  • Bachelor's or postgraduate degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent industry experience
  • 4+ years of development and design experience on Sitecore
  • Sitecore Certified Developer
  • Strong skills in ASP.NET, C#, MVC and SQL
  • Solid knowledge of Helix architecture and Sitecore development best practices
  • Proven experience creating templates, pages and custom personalization rules
  • Experience customizing Sitecore content APIs used by other applications
  • Experience deploying and supporting Sitecore in a scaled enterprise environment
  • Working experience in an Agile / Scrum team

Good to Have

  • Exposure to Sitecore AI (XM Cloud), Sitecore Stream, Agentic Studio or Sitecore AI Search
  • Hands-on experience with Sitecore Personalize, CDP or A/B testing for customer journeys
  • Experience integrating LLM services such as Azure Open AI into web platforms, with awareness of data privacy and responsible AI controls in a regulated environment
  • Headless experience with Sitecore JSS, Next.js and GraphQL
  • Banking, financial services or fintech project experience in an enterprise setting
  • Wealth management product knowledge: investments, mutual funds, bonds, structured products, priority and private banking propositions
  • Familiarity with UAE banking regulatory expectations around security, accessibility and data handling

Soft Skills

  • Self motivated and self starting; owns tasks end to end with minimal supervision
  • Eager to learn and quick to adapt when requirements change
  • Strong team player with an agile mindset and a drive for continuous improvement
  • Able to influence teams on technical decisions by sharing deep knowledge
  • Clear communicator who can explain technical ideas to senior and executive management

Why Join

  • Work on high-visibility digital platforms for one of the region's leading banks
  • Get hands-on with Sitecore's latest AI and composable capabilities
  • Grow within a collaborative, Scrum-based engineering culture working with a UAE client team

THE ROLE

At Everpure™, we empower innovators by simplifying how the world interacts with data. As a Sr. SAP CPI Developer on our expanding enterprise applications team, you will architect, deploy, and optimize high-impact SAP Cloud Platform Integration (CPI) solutions that connect Everpure’s core operations across hybrid environments. Serving as a technical bridge between business strategy and modern SAP integration architecture, you will partner with enterprise architects, cross-functional business stakeholders, and engineering squads. Your work directly fuels operational scale, powering real-time data flows and generative AI workflows that keep our business-critical systems agile and resilient.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Architect Enterprise Integration Flows: Own the end-to-end design and deployment of resilient SAP CPI middleware connecting SAP and non-SAP platforms (via REST, SOAP, OData, IDOC, and SFTP) to accelerate data exchange for global business teams.
  • Scale Event-Driven & API Ecosystems: Partner with enterprise architects to establish integration governance, security standards, event-driven messaging (Advanced Event Mesh), and API management strategies that future-proof Everpure’s digital ecosystem.
  • Streamline Global B2B Connectivity: Lead the delivery of automated B2B and EDI integrations (ANSI X12, EDIFACT) using SAP Integration Suite, enabling seamless digital transactions with external trading partners.
  • Pioneer SAP Business AI & Automation: Collaborate with cross-functional engineering squads to embed generative AI, machine learning, and autonomous agent workflows across SAP S/4HANA and BTP, unlocking intelligent automation across the enterprise.
  • Elevate Engineering Excellence & Reliability: Guide onshore and offshore engineering partners through architectural reviews and agile delivery while driving proactive platform monitoring to safeguard mission-critical SLAs.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Full-Lifecycle SAP Integration Mastery: Proven capability architecting, building, and supporting complex SAP CPI/BTP integration solutions from initial design through production optimization across distributed delivery teams.
  • Deep Technical & Scripting Fluency: Hands-on command of building integration flows using Groovy, Java, or Python alongside modern messaging protocols (REST, SOAP, IDOC, RFC, BAPI, OData, SFTP, and Solace/Event Mesh).
  • API Management & Event Mesh Expertise: Demonstrated ability to configure and govern event brokers, topics, queues, and API proxies/policies within SAP Event Mesh, Advanced Event Mesh, and SAP API Management.
  • B2B/EDI & Enterprise Security Execution: Practical expertise onboarding external trading partners via SAP Integration Suite (Trading Partner Management, Integration Advisor, AS2) while enforcing enterprise security frameworks (OAuth, SAML, SSL/TLS, XSUAA) across SAP BTP and Cloud Foundry.
  • Boomi Familiarity: Understanding of the Boomi integration platform is a plus.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, US office in compliance with Everpure's policies, unless you are on PTO, work travel, or other approved leave.

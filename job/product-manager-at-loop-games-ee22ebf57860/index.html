About the job

Who are Loop Games?

Loop Games is a leader in the mobile gaming industry, known for pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation. As the pioneers behind the Match 3D genre, we’ve captivated millions of players worldwide. Our success is reflected in our accomplishments:

Top-Grossing Hits: Our games consistently dominate global charts.

100+ Million Downloads: Connecting with a vast, international audience.

$100+ Million Revenue: Exceptional success driven by in-app purchases.

At Loop Games, we blend creativity with cutting-edge technology to foster a dynamic, collaborative culture. We are looking for passionate, innovative individuals to join us in shaping the future of gaming. If you're excited about crafting the next big hit, we want to hear from you!

A Bit About The Role?

As a Product Manager, you will define innovative product strategies, oversee the complete lifecycle of mobile games, and utilize player insights to create compelling gaming experiences.

This role requires a deep understanding of the mobile gaming industry, strong analytical skills to interpret player behavior, and the ability to lead products to success. You will drive development, navigate challenges with creativity, and ensure projects are delivered seamlessly.

We’re looking for someone who flourishes entertainment space, understands the dynamics of the gaming industry, and can merge strategic thinking with a passion for creating memorable experiences.

If you're excited about playing games and shaping the future of the gaming world, we’d love to hear from you.

Join us in building the next big hit!

Now a Bit About You...

If trust and harmony are at the core of how you work, you’ll feel right at home with us.

You’re passionate about games, not just as a creator but as a fan of what you build. Innovation excites you, and you’re always eager to explore the latest trends and bring fresh ideas to life. You take ownership of your work while valuing collaboration, knowing that the best results come from working together.

If you’re driven by creativity, synergy, and a sense of purpose, we’d love to have you on our team!

Key Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including art, development to deliver high-quality games that align with Loop Games’ goals.
  • Analyze player behavior, market trends, and competitor insights to shape product roadmaps and drive player engagement.
  • Use player feedback and data to prioritize innovative features, design updates, and improve the overall gaming experience.
  • Oversee the complete lifecycle of game development from concept and launch to ongoing improvement.
  • Partner with artists and engineers to ensure timely delivery and the continuous enhancement of game features.
  • Foster a culture of accountability and ownership across teams, ensuring projects align with the strategic vision.
  • Merge data-driven insights with creative thinking to deliver games that are both fun and engaging.
  • Brainstorm and develop new game ideas with the team, pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in mobile gaming.
  • Our ideal candidate has...
  • Minimum 3 years experience as a Product Manager.
  • Background in the gaming industry.
  • Creative and analytical thinker with a strong focus on data-driven decision-making.
  • Expertise in leveraging data optimization techniques to enhance product performance, user engagement, and overall outcomes.
  • Collaborative team player with experience working alongside cross-functional teams.
  • Exceptional project management skills with keen attention to detail.
  • Growth mindset with an entrepreneurial spirit.
  • Strong passion for continuous learning and development.
  • Fluent in both written and spoken English.

About the job

About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

Corporate Network Engineering designs, builds, and operates the networks in Anthropic's offices around the world: campus switching, Wi-Fi, and edge. We think a good office network is no more complicated than its requirements demand, fast and reliable enough that users never think about it, secure by default, and tolerant of failures to enable a lean team to operate a global portfolio of offices.

Anthropic is growing quickly, and our team is small and runs with high autonomy, so we care a great deal about how the work gets done: configuration kept in version control and shipped through review and staged rollouts, problems that happen more than once fixed at the source, and incidents closed only once the cause is fixed. We use Claude heavily in this work and expect you to as well.

We're hiring an experienced network engineer who works this way already: someone who has designed, built, and operated campus networks at scale, wired and wireless, and has also built the code and tooling to run them. As a network engineer here you'll work across all of it: network design and standards, new office buildouts, infrastructure as code, monitoring and incident response, and network security, where Corporate Security and Detection & Response are close partners. This is a hands-on individual contributor role.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and operate campus networks for offices of every size: switching, Wi-Fi, edge, carrier, and out-of-band connectivity
  • Contribute to the technical design standards
  • Build and maintain network infrastructure as code (source of truth, generated configuration, review, CI, staged rollout), and automate recurring manual work away
  • Build monitoring and alerting that catches network problems early, respond to network events, and run incidents through to a fix for the cause
  • Help establish the team's engineering practices, including change management, incident response, and post-incident review
  • Partner with Corporate Security, Detection & Response, and Endpoint on network authentication, segmentation, and the network as a detection source
  • Deliver the network for new offices end to end, from landlord and contractor requirements through carriers, cabling, staging, and cutover, alongside Buildouts, Workplace, and integrators
  • Share the team's on-call rotation and travel to sites for buildouts, cutovers, and incidents (up to 25%)

You may be a good fit if you

  • Have 8+ years designing, building, and operating enterprise campus networks, wired and wireless, for corporate offices across many sites
  • Have deployed and operated Wi-Fi at scale that users never had to think about, including high-density spaces, and can trace a user complaint to its cause
  • Treat network configuration as code, have written a substantial part of the pipeline that ships it, and have operated a network from it
  • Write code well (Python or similar), build automation and tooling as a normal part of the job, and make a habit of automating yourself out of work
  • Have built monitoring for a network, carried the pager for it, run incidents through to the fix, and helped a team adopt change management and post-incident review as a peer
  • Have delivered the network for new offices end to end, with real estate, construction, carriers, and integrators
  • Prefer the simplest design that meets the requirement, know what you gave up to get it, and write it down clearly enough for others to build from
  • Hold a high bar for security, and have designed or deployed 802.1X/EAP-TLS and segmentation in partnership with a security team
  • Already use AI tools as a core part of how you work, and know which work to hand to a model and which to keep

Strong candidates may also

  • Have served as incident commander for incidents that spanned several teams, or built a team's on-call and incident process
  • Have migrated a network between platforms or vendors across live sites
  • Have designed segmentation for devices that cannot run an agent, such as AV, IoT, and physical security systems

Technical skills

  • Campus switching and high-density Wi-Fi: L2/L3 design, Wi-Fi design for dense spaces, 6 GHz, roaming
  • Access control and edge: 802.1X/EAP-TLS, RADIUS, segmentation, firewalls, multi-carrier BGP, out-of-band access
  • Automation: Python, Ansible or Nornir, Terraform, vendor APIs, GitOps and CI
  • Observability: streaming telemetry, flow and log pipelines, alerting
  • Buildout infrastructure: MDF/IDF design, structured cabling, power and cooling, carrier diversity
  • Any vendor: Arista, Cisco, Juniper, Aruba, Palo Alto, Fortinet, or equivalent

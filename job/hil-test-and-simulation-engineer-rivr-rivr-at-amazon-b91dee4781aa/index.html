About the job

RIVR, an Amazon company, is building Physical AI by deploying autonomous robots for real-world doorstep delivery. Operating daily in diverse urban environments, RIVR's robots continuously learn from and navigate the millions of scenarios encountered during deliveries. By owning the full stack from software to hardware, RIVR is purpose-built for safety, reliability, and the customer from day one.
As a HIL Test & Simulation Engineer, you will build and operate the test infrastructure that verifies the embedded firmware running across our robot's subsystems, starting with motor drives. You are the link between the firmware architect who specifies what the software must do and the structured evidence that proves it does correctly, safely, and repeatably.
Your core mission is developing HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) test benches, including the system models that simulate the physical behavior of the system under test (e.g., actuators, power systems, sensors), specifying and automating test sequences, and integrating everything into a continuous integration and deployment pipeline. You will work closely with the firmware architect who defines the test cases, firmware engineers developing the firmware, and the hardware engineers who validate results on physical benches. Your job is to build the simulation environment, turn test specifications into automated execution, and produce the evidence for certification.
This role is central to our functional safety certification effort. You will produce the test evidence and requirement traceability matrices that demonstrate firmware compliance with IEC 61508.
Key job responsibilities
Hands-On Testing & Engineering
• Develop and maintain HIL test benches for embedded firmware across robot subsystems (actuators, power management, sensors) with automated sequences and fault injection
• Build, tune, and validate physical system models (motor dynamics, power electronics, battery/sensor behavior) ensuring simulation fidelity across operating conditions
• Specify and execute firmware test cases covering control modes, protocols, state machines, and edge cases in collaboration with firmware architect
Functional Safety & Documentation
• Define firmware test coverage matrices tracking tested items, levels, and gaps to meet functional safety certification requirements
• Develop and execute IEC 61508 safety test cases: safe states, fault reactions, watchdog behavior, and protection functions
• Produce test evidence and traceability matrices linking results to requirements for safety audits and certification
Team & Collaboration
• Collaborate with electrical, firmware, and systems engineers to align test strategies with product and safety requirements
• Build and operate CI/CD pipelines for firmware: automated build, flash, HIL test execution, and regression reporting
• Specify, procure, commission, and operate professional HIL systems with external vendors
- • Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Control Engineering, or a closely related field;
- • 3–5 years of experience in embedded firmware development, testing or validation, preferably in mechatronic systems, power electronics, advanced sensors, or motor drives;
- • Hands-on experience with HIL test systems (dSPACE, NI, Speedgoat, or similar): configuring real-time targets, building test sequences, and automating test execution
- • Experience developing or tuning electromechanical system models for real-time simulation: e.g., electrical machines, power electronics, sensors, batteries, or other physical system models (Simulink, Simscape, or similar tools);
- • Understanding of embedded real-time control firmware: control loops, peripheral interaction (PWM, ADC, timers), and fault reaction sequences.
- • Experience with firmware test coverage and continuous integration: you have defined or maintained test coverage matrices for embedded control software, integrated automated tests into CI pipelines, and understand what it takes to catch regressions in real-time control firmware before they reach hardware.
- • Experience with CAN/CANFD bus: protocol analysis, message definition, and test automation.
- • Strong Python skills for test automation, data acquisition scripting, and analysis.
- • Structured approach to test documentation: writing test plans, traceability records, and reports that others (including auditors and certification bodies) can follow.
- • Exposure to functional safety processes (IEC 61508 or ISO 26262): understanding what V&V evidence looks like and how test data is used in certification
- • Experience with model-based development workflows — model-in-the-loop (MIL), software-in-the-loop (SIL), hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) V-cycle.
- • Experience producing requirement traceability matrices and test evidence packages for safety-critical systems.
- • Experience with real-time microcontrollers: understanding ISR timing, ADC sequencing, and peripheral configuration (e.g., PWM) helps you write better tests.

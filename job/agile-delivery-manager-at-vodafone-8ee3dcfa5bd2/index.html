About the job

Who we are

As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. About this role

Location:Newbury (Hybrid)

We are seeking an Agile Delivery Manager to join Vodafone Business IT with accountability for the successful delivery of one or more technology Value Streams.

You will lead complex, cross-functional technology delivery across Vodafone Business IT, ensuring outcomes are delivered to agreed time, cost, quality, risk and benefit expectations. You will use SAFe and Agile delivery practices to shape delivery and operating models, coordinate multidisciplinary teams and drive continuous improvement across Agile and DevOps ways of working.

The role combines hands-on delivery leadership with strategic stakeholder management, technology transformation, supplier management and responsibility for budgets of up to €10 million. You will work across multiple geographies and time zones, collaborating with Vodafone Business, Technology, VOIS and external partners.

What you’ll do

  • Take accountability for technology delivery across one or more Value Streams, including critical paths, contingencies, scope changes, risks and budgets.Use SAFe methodology to design and implement delivery and operating models that support agreed business outcomes.Lead cross-functional teams in delivering Value Stream roadmaps.
  • Translate business priorities into prioritised delivery requirements and solutions that contribute directly to business benefits.
  • Lead project teams to deliver new and enhanced business capabilities on time and within budget.
  • Support the Vodafone Business IT transformation agenda by managing substantial programmes of work and coordinating channel-specific delivery teams.Influence strategic technology roadmaps in collaboration with Platform and Enterprise Architects, Vodafone Business Commercial & Operations, Digital Marketing and Transformation teams.
  • Represent Technology within delivery discussions, supporting sustainable delivery practices including technical debt reduction and platform improvements.
  • Shape continuous improvement across Agile and DevOps delivery practices.Develop and lead high-performing teams across Technology, VOIS and external partners.
  • Support the insourcing agenda for T2025 skills within Vodafone Business IT.
  • Adapt to evolving Vodafone Business IT domain and operating model structures, taking ownership of different platforms and technologies where required.
  • Coordinate with local markets to support delivery and operational excellence across Vodafone Business.Manage suppliers and budgets of up to €10 million.
  • Lead culturally diverse teams across multiple geographies and time zones through a matrix organisation comprising permanent employees, contractors and suppliers.

Who you are

  • An experienced technology delivery professional with a strong track record of delivering complex IT projects and programmes, using Agile and waterfall delivery approaches.
  • An expert Agile practitioner with practical experience of scaled Agile frameworks such as SAFe, Tribes and Squads.
  • Experienced in building, coaching and developing high-performing, cross-functional teams.
  • Confident working with and influencing senior leadership teams and stakeholders across complex organisations.
  • Experienced in technology transformation, delivery governance and strategic technology roadmaps.
  • Comfortable managing suppliers, significant budgets and geographically distributed delivery teams.
  • Able to communicate complex technical and organisational concepts clearly and adapt the message to different audiences.
  • Able to rapidly develop and articulate technology organisational strategy.
  • Experienced in workforce planning, including matching demand and supply and making objective resourcing decisions that support Value Stream delivery.Comfortable operating within matrix organisations and collaborating across internal teams, contractors, suppliers and external partners.
  • Adaptable in changing environments, with a growth mindset and an ability to bring new perspectives to delivery challenges.

What's in it for you

  • The opportunity to lead complex, high-impact technology delivery across Vodafone Business IT.
  • Exposure to a broad technology landscape spanning Digital, OSS, BSS, AI, Big Data and sales enablement.
  • Opportunities to work with technology areas supporting Vodafone Business products and services, including IoT, Mobile Private Networks and Software Defined Networks.
  • Collaboration with international teams across Vodafone Business, Technology, VOIS, local markets and external partners.
  • The opportunity to influence strategic technology roadmaps, Agile transformation and improvements to delivery and operating models.Experience leading substantial programmes and managing budgets of up to €10 million.

What skills you will learn

This role provides opportunities to further develop:

  • Enterprise-scale Agile and SAFe delivery leadership.
  • Agile and DevOps continuous-improvement practices.
  • Strategic technology roadmap development and delivery.
  • Leadership of globally distributed, cross-functional technology teams.
  • Supplier and large-scale budget management.
  • Delivery across diverse technology domains including Digital, OSS, BSS, AI and Big Data.
  • Strategic workforce and capacity planning within a large technology organisation.
  • Senior-level stakeholder management and influencing across complex matrix environments.

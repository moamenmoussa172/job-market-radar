About the job

We are looking for a Specialist to build a new technology capability in Technology & Innovation at Bosch Global Software Technologies Vietnam, from zero to first real deployments. This role is ideal for someone who wants to own a system end-to-end, work directly with real users, and shape how a small team engineers. You will work hands-on with latest-generation AI hardware platforms, from manipulation to mobile systems.

You will report directly to the Head of Technology & Innovation and work closely with a partner engineering team on cross-functional prototypes and pilots.

Key Responsibilities

Technical Ownership & Architecture

  • Own the architecture of systems combining AI, software and physical devices.
  • Make build, buy and reuse decisions, including technical assessments of vendors and platforms.
  • Set up the lab, toolchain and simulation environment for the team.

Delivery with Users & Partners

  • Turn validated user problems into working pilots and measure whether they are actually used.
  • Integrate edge devices with enterprise applications and data (e.g., identity, scheduling, visitor and facility systems).
  • Deliver within the security, safety and quality constraints of real environments.

Team & Knowledge Building

  • Guide and review the work of other engineers, including part-time contributors from partner teams.
  • Define engineering practices and quality gates for the team.
  • Capture reusable components, documented know-how and invention reports so each project speeds up the next.
  • Ownership of at least one feature or component end-to-end.
  • Solid C++ and/or Python on Linux.
  • Hands-on experience with at least one of: ROS 2, computer vision, sensors, embedded Linux, edge AI.
  • Fluent with AI coding tools.
  • Comfortable with ambiguity and with hardware that does not behave as expected.
  • Good communication skills (English + Vietnamese).

Nice to Have

  • Simulation tools (e.g., Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, Gazebo).
  • Experience with NVIDIA Jetson or similar edge platforms.
  • Automotive or safety-critical background (e.g., ASPICE, ISO 26262).
  • Patents, invention reports or published reusable components.

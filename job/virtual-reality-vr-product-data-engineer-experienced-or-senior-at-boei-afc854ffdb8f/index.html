About the job

Virtual Reality (VR) Product Data Engineer (Experienced or Senior)

Company:

Boeing Poland sp. z o.o. EFS

In this role you will support engineering decisions across product design, production system design, manufacturing, maintenance, and human safety by developing immersive Virtual Reality (VR) scenes and scenarios. You will plan and facilitate collaborative VR design-integration reviews, create and deliver concise report-out materials for engineering and leadership, and serve as the focal for the advanced visualization (Innovation) Lab — maintaining the hardware and software required to run effective VR reviews.

Mission: Airplane-level collaborative digital design integration and analysis to verify and validate that the product is integrated, interference-free, producible, maintainable, serviceable, and safe prior to design release — enabling First Pass Quality.

This role combines hands-on systems administration of the advanced visualization (Innovation) Lab with engineering knowledge to translate use cases into effective VR experiences, prepare and optimize CAD/CATIA and IVT data for real-time review, maintain the hardware and software environment, and deliver user training and knowledge transfer. The ideal candidate understands design engineering constraints and manufacturing processes and has practical experience integrating VR into engineering and manufacturing workflows. We are seeking excellent verbal and written English communication skills and a demonstrated ability to collaborate across multidisciplinary teams.

Hiring details:

  • This position is expected to be 100% onsite. The selected candidate will be required to work onsite
  • Employer will not sponsor applicants for employment visa status
  • Relocation: This position does not offer relocation assistance

Primary Responsibilities:

  • Translate engineering, manufacturing, and maintainability use cases into immersive VR scenes and scenarios for collaborative design-integration review.
  • Prepare, optimize, and validate CAD/CATIA geometry and IVT datasets for real-time VR review (interference, clearance, producibility, serviceability, human-factors/reach).
  • Plan and facilitate collaborative, airplane-level VR design-integration reviews with design, manufacturing, and quality teams to verify the product is integrated, interference-free, producible, maintainable, serviceable, and safe prior to design release.
  • Serve as focal for the advanced visualization / Innovation Lab: administer and maintain VR hardware (headsets, workstations, GPUs, tracking) and the software stack; troubleshoot and keep the environment review ready.
  • Create and deliver concise report-out materials and findings for engineering and leadership.
  • Deliver user training, onboarding, and knowledge transfer to grow VR review adoption across teams.
  • Provide ongoing technical and operational support to VR integrators and cross-functional teams during development, testing, and deployment.
  • Collaborate across multidisciplinary teams to continuously improve VR review processes, tools, and best practices. Basic Qualifications (Required Skills/ Experience):

  • Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Engineering Technology (including Manufacturing Technology), Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or non-US equivalent qualifications directly related to the work statement
  • Strong CAD interoperability skills and experience
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to produce concise report-outs for multi-function leadership
  • Existing rights to work in Poland

Preferred Qualifications (Desired Skills/Experience):

  • LVL 3 (Experienced): 5+ years of experience working in engineering with cross-functional or cross-organizational teams
  • LVL 4 (Senior): 9+ years of experience working in engineering with cross-functional or cross-organizational teams
  • Formal training or experience in human factors, ergonomics, or safety engineering
  • Strong CATIA and IVT interoperability skills and experience
  • Familiarity with MBOM/EBOM concepts and manufacturing/assembly process visualization
  • Certifications or coursework in VR/AR development, simulation, or continuous improvement methodologies
  • Hands-on experience with real-time engines and VR development tools such as Unity or Unreal Engine

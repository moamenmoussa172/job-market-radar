Job Description

We’re looking for a QA Engineer to join our Data & Analytics Platforms RUBIK engineering team. We are searching for self-motivated candidates who demonstrate modern Agile and DevOps practices and can own the design and development of IT systems and applications, delivering global projects in multinational teams.

Responsibilities

  • Lead testing activities for applications throughout their entire lifecycle, from development and deployment to upgrade and replacement/termination
  • Write test scenarios and automation code for Data & Analytics applications, and support end-to-end cloud-native solutions on Azure
  • Perform frontend, backend, and data layer testing, covering React-based UI applications, Java-based backend services, and data validation across Databricks and SQL environments
  • Ensure development and architecture adhere to established standards, including modern software engineering practices such as CI/CD, Agile, and DevOps
  • Collaborate with internal technical specialists and vendors to develop final products that improve overall performance, efficiency, and support new business processes

Job Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience
  • 5+ years of experience working as a Test Manager or Automated Test Engineer
  • Experience with Selenium and performance testing
  • Hands-on experience working with Robot Framework and Python
  • At least 2 years of hands-on experience with Databricks and SQL, including testing, validating, and analyzing data in Data & Analytics environments
  • Experience developing test scenarios and strategies for business-critical applications
  • Proven understanding of Docker and CI/CD pipelines
  • Strong verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills
  • A strong desire to produce high-quality software through cross-functional teamwork, testing, code reviews, and standard engineering best practices

We offer

  • P&G-sized projects and access to world leading IT partners and technologies from Day 1.
  • Wide range of self-development possibilities (training and certifications paths).
  • Competitive starting salary and benefits program (private health care, P&G stock, saving plans, sport cards).
  • Regular salary increases and possible promotions - in line with your results and performance.
  • Opportunity to change role every few years to be in the best place for you and best for P&G.

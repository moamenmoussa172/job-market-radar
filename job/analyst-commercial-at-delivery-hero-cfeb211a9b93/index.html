About the job

We’re looking for a highly driven and motivated “Analyst, Commercial” for our team in Pakistan. If you are looking for a place where you can gain hands-on exposure and have a direct impact, then this may be the place for you!

The ideal candidate will have a track record as a significant individual contributor – comfortable working independently, whilst working closely with the management team.

Responsibilities:

  • Leverage analytical tools to provide insights and recommendations for the growth of commercial business nationwide
  • Conduct meaningful data analysis to provide visibility and transparency to commercial stakeholders which can unlock growth opportunities
  • Identify & track performance KPIs against daily, weekly and monthly targets
  • Develop KPI dashboards, reports, and presentations to set up monthly targets to achieve MoM business goals
  • Identify opportunities to develop and improve solutions and strengthen the overall customer experience
  • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders to understand business challenges and opportunities. Recommend solutions across complex range of problems
  • Develop, maintain, and update comprehensive project plans from initial idea to launch and growth
  • Support the development and execution of a robust portfolio strategy

Requirements:

  • Bachelor’s/Master’s Degree in Business, Data Science or related disciplines
  • 2-4 years of experience in strategic analysis, planning, and execution
  • Thorough understanding of and knowledge of relevant tools and systems (SQL, Tableau, G sheets). SQL proficiency is preferred
  • Must have excellent analytical skills and business acumen to build strong business cases
  • Commercially focused and consumer centric. Demonstrated ability to convert information into clear ideas
  • Strong verbal and written communication
  • Experience working within the technology industry is a plus

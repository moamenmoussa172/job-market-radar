What you’ll do
You’ll join at a defining moment as we bring together our collective strengths to create the UK’s biggest mobile network provider. In this role, you’ll sit at the heart of our Customer Experience team, helping us understand why customer-impacting issues happen and turning complex data into clear insight and lasting improvements. You’ll combine curiosity and collaboration with confident decision-making, using AI, analytics and robust governance to connect the dots across Credit File, Credit Vetting, Payments, Collections and Billing Assurance. Your work will help us create better experiences for our customers while building operational standards we’ll be proud of.

  • Drive robust governance across RCA activity establishing clear ownership and milestones, holding stakeholders accountable for delivery, proactively managing dependencies and blockers, and escalating risks or issues promptly to maintain momentum and achieve sustainable customer outcomes.
  • You’ll lead first-line root cause analysis (RCA) under a hub-and-spoke model, investigating operational incidents, process failures and customer-impacting issues.
  • You’ll use tools such as Copilot, SAS and other data analytics platforms to explore operational data, incident records, case histories and process outputs, identifying patterns and potential causes.
  • You’ll develop clear RCA hypotheses and structured summaries, bringing together potential root causes, contributing factors, customer impact and affected processes.
  • You’ll act as the central coordination hub for RCA activity, bringing different teams and RCA SPOCs together to validate findings and reach well-evidenced conclusions.
  • You’ll drive governance with clear ownership and milestones, confidently following up on actions, constructively challenging blockers and escalating risks when needed.
  • You’ll facilitate RCA reviews and stakeholder discussions, creating an environment where people can challenge ideas, share perspectives and work together towards sustainable solutions.
  • You’ll track outcomes beyond the immediate fix, using insight to help reduce repeat issues and support measurable improvements in customer and operational performance.
  • You’ll help turn RCA findings into remediation initiatives while strengthening documentation, templates and best practices so that our approach continues to evolve.

Who you are

  • You’ll bring experience from customer experience (CX), complaints, operations, performance improvement or a related environment, along with an understanding of service journeys and their impact on customers.
  • Experience in governance activities, including action tracking, progress reporting and timely escalation of risks and issues.
  • Proven ability to lead governance forums, drive progress and hold action owners accountable for delivery.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence, constructively challenge and resolve blockers across teams.
  • You’ll be comfortable with root cause analysis (RCA) techniques and will know how to take an evidence-led, curious approach to finding the underlying cause of an issue rather than simply treating the symptoms.
  • You’ll bring governance experience, confidently leading forums, tracking actions and progress, managing dependencies and ensuring risks and issues are escalated at the right time.
  • You’ll be skilled in analysing and interpreting complex or large datasets, using tools such as Excel, Power BI or similar analytics and reporting platforms to uncover meaningful insights.
  • You’ll communicate complex analysis in a clear, concise and compelling way, adapting your approach so that different audiences can understand the issue, evidence and action required.
  • You’ll build collaborative relationships across multiple teams while being comfortable influencing, constructively challenging and holding stakeholders accountable for agreed outcomes.
  • You’ll bring a willingness to learn and experiment with new technology; working knowledge of SAS and SQL scripting will be an advantage rather than a barrier to applying.

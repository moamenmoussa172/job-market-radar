Digital Verification Engineer Intern

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, and software technologies into solutions that help drive advancements in digitized factories, mobility, and digital healthcare, combat climate change, and reliably connect humans and the world.

The Digital Design & Functional Verification team is seeking a motivated Digital Verification Engineer candidate to provide support to our Industrial BU located at ADI’s Milan design center within the High-Performance Power Group.

This opportunity will allow the candidate to work within a young and proactive team (11 team members located in Milan DC). The candidate will work as an individual contributor on a wide range of different types of products.

Responsibilities include, but not limited to:

  • Interact with product definers and business managers during the preliminary phase of the design to consolidate and fully understand the requirements.
  • Creation of verification plans.
  • Functional verification according to the Verification Plan established, this includes writing tests, systemVerilog assertions, covergroups.
  • Deep understanding of internal Verification IPs and their utilization inside a UVM environment.
  • Run regression to close structural and functional coverage.

Minimum qualifications:

  • Electronic Engineer Bachelor degree
  • Being familiar Windows and Linux OS
  • Verilog/SystemVerilog knowledge
  • CMOS logic and more in general VLSI
  • Basic concepts of UVM
  • Object oriented programming knowledge

Preferred qualifications

  • Basic analog circuits knowledge
  • Synopsys/Cadence tool skills

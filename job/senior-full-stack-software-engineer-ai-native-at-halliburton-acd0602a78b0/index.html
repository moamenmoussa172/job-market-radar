About the job

We are looking for the right people — people who want to innovate, achieve, grow and lead. We attract and retain the best talent by investing in our employees and empowering them to develop themselves and their careers. Experience the challenges, rewards and opportunity of working for one of the world’s largest providers of products and services to the global energy industry.

Job Duties

Landmark, a Halliburton company, builds the software and data platforms that help the global energy industry make better decisions. Our products span subsurface interpretation, well construction planning, reservoir simulation, and digital operations — tools used daily by engineers and scientists at the world’s largest energy companies. We are investing significantly in our engineering capability in Oslo, and these roles are part of building a permanent, locally-owned engineering organization to take long-term ownership of the platform.

Role Overview

Design, develop, and support scalable cloud-native SaaS applications using Angular, Node.js and/or Python, deployed on Azure and AWS. The ideal candidate embraces AI-assisted development to improve productivity, quality, testing, and delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain full stack applications using Angular, Node.js, and/or Python.
  • Build responsive web applications, REST APIs, and microservices.
  • Design, deploy, and support cloud-native solutions on Azure and AWS.
  • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines using Docker, Kubernetes, GitLab, and Jenkins.
  • Participate in architecture reviews, code reviews, and technical design discussions.
  • Use AI-powered development tools to improve coding, testing, debugging, and documentation.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams and mentor junior engineers.

Qualifications

  • 8+ years of software development experience.
  • 3+ years with Angular, TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS/SASS.
  • Strong experience with Node.js and/or Python.
  • Experience building REST APIs, microservices, and distributed systems.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Azure and/or AWS.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, or similar databases.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience using GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf, or similar AI tools.
  • Experience validating AI-generated code for security, performance, and maintainability.
  • Knowledge of LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, RAG, Semantic Kernel, or LLM APIs.
  • Experience with vector databases and AI-enabled applications.

Technical Skills

  • Frontend: Angular, TypeScript, HTML5, CSS/SASS
  • Backend: Node.js, Python, Express, FastAPI, REST APIs, Microservices
  • Cloud & DevOps: Azure, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, GitLab, Jenkins, CI/CD
  • Databases: PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis
  • Testing: TDD, Unit, Integration and API Testing, Jest, Cypress, Playwright

Nice to Have

  • Oil & Gas industry experience.
  • Experience building AI-powered applications, agents, RAG solutions, or MCP integrations.

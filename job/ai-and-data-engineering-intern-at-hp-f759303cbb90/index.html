About the job

AI & Data Engineering Intern

Description -

Before applying please note that, to be eligible for this position, you must be currently enrolled at a university in Spain, remain enrolled until the end of the internship, and be eligible to sign an internship agreement with your university. You must also be available to start the internship in February 2027 and continue participating in the program until June–July 2027.

Job Summary

We are looking for a Data Engineer Intern to join our Data & AI team and contribute to the development of modern data and AI solutions.

You will work in a dynamic environment where data engineering meets Artificial Intelligence, gaining hands-on experience building solutions on Databricks and working with real business challenges.

You will collaborate with data engineers, architects, analysts and business teams, learning how enterprise data is transformed into trusted data products that power analytics, AI and intelligent applications.

What you'll do

  • Develop and maintain data pipelines and transformations in Databricks.
  • Work with Python and SQL to transform and prepare enterprise data.
  • Help build reliable, reusable and business-ready datasets.
  • Implement data quality checks and support data governance and documentation.
  • Explore how data can enable AI-powered solutions and intelligent applications.
  • Collaborate with Data Engineers, Data Architects and business teams to translate business needs into technical solutions.
  • Experiment with new technologies and identify opportunities to innovate and automate.
  • Learn and apply modern Data & AI engineering practices in a global enterprise environment.

This scope is consistent with the team's existing Databricks operating model, where Data Engineering covers pipelines, data quality, governed datasets and implementation in Unity Catalog.

What we're looking for

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in:

  • Computer Engineering
  • Data Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence

Technical skills

  • Databricks
  • Python
  • SQL

We don't expect you to know everything. We are looking for someone with solid fundamentals and, most importantly, the motivation to learn by doing.

Who you are

  • Proactive: you take initiative and look for ways to move things forward.
  • Innovative: you enjoy exploring new ideas, technologies and better ways of doing things.
  • Curious: you ask questions, challenge assumptions and are eager to learn.

What you'll gain

You'll have the opportunity to gain hands-on experience across the modern Data & AI ecosystem:

Databricks • Python • SQL • Data Engineering • Data Quality • Data Governance • AI • Generative AI • Intelligent Applications

Most importantly, you'll work on real Data & AI solutions with real business impact, alongside a team that will give you the space to learn, experiment and grow.

Experience our benefits

  • Paid internship within a global, technology-driven company.
  • International work environment with opportunities for learning, professional development, and networking across teams.
  • Flexible working schedule, with part-time (4 hours/day) or full-time (8 hours/day) agreements. Internship duration ranges from 4 to 12 months.
  • Access to our intern community, including career development workshops, learning sessions, and social activities.
  • Dedicated mentor throughout your internship.
  • Opportunity to join the HP Graduate Program following the placement.
  • Lunch at reduced prices at our on-site canteen.
  • Access to our on-site sports center, including: Gym facilities. Squash, tennis, and basketball courts. 25+ organized sports and wellness activities, such as HIIT training, squash, basketball, and yoga.
  • Participation in the NextGen Employee Network, with regular events and opportunities to connect with other early-career professionals.
  • On-site medical services, including access to a doctor, nutrition support, physiotherapy, and general healthcare.
  • Free printing access and hands-on workshops using the latest HP technologies, from large-format prints to 3D-printed models.
  • Easy commuting, with free parking and convenient access via nearby train services.
  • Access to Women Network initiatives, including networking events, STEM promotion, business acumen talks, work-life balance sessions, and future-skills development.

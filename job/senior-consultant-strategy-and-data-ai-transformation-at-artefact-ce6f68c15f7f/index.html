About the job

Join Artefact Belgium: Help our clients in their AI transformations

Are you an analytically minded consultant with a passion for data, AI, and business transformation? Artefact Belgium is looking for a Senior Consultant – Strategy & Data/AI to help our clients navigate their most complex strategic challenges at the intersection of business and technology. If you have 2-4 years of consulting or related experience, combined with a dual affinity for strategy and data, we'd love to hear from you.

Who We Are

Artefact is a next-generation consulting firm dedicated to transforming organizations through data and AI. We combine the rigor of top-tier strategy consulting with deep expertise in AI applications to help clients achieve tangible business impact. Our Belgium office advises clients on their most pressing strategic challenges : from AI strategy and governance to digital transformation roadmaps and new business model design. Our inclusive and forward-thinking culture encourages creativity, collaboration, and professional growth.

Who You Are

  • Strategic thinker: You break down complex business problems into clear, structured recommendations. You are comfortable operating at the C-level and translating ambiguity into actionable insights.
  • Data-savvy consultant: You don't need to be a programmer, but you are genuinely curious about data and AI technologies and eager to deepen your expertise in this space.
  • Project & Engagement Leader: You have a proven capacity to lead projects client side. You excel at managing expectations, defining project scope, and providing strategic guidance to technical teams to ensure delivery aligns with business objectives.
  • Client-facing communicator: You build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders, present ideas with clarity, and adapt your message to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Collaborative doer: You thrive in multidisciplinary teams, move fast, and take ownership of your work from day one.
  • Ambitious and driven: You are motivated by impact, eager to grow, and excited by the opportunity to help shape a fast-growing office.

What You Will Do

  • Drive strategy development: Work with client leadership teams to address high-stakes questions — How can AI reshape our business model? How should we govern and organize for data? Which strategic programs deliver the most impact?
  • Solve complex problems: Break down ambiguous challenges into clear, actionable recommendations using structured frameworks, enriched by Artefact's data and AI expertise.
  • Deliver end-to-end engagements: Support projects from hypothesis framing and market/competitor analysis to client workshops, governance design, and implementation roadmaps with the technical team.
  • Engage senior stakeholders: Collaborate closely with C-level executives and business unit leaders, building trusted relationships and ensuring strategic alignment throughout engagements.
  • Build knowledge and offerings: Contribute to the growth of Artefact's Belgium office by shaping new service offerings, creating intellectual capital (frameworks, thought pieces, benchmarks), and supporting business development efforts.
  • Contribute to thought leadership: Publish insights, participate in industry events, and help position Artefact Belgium as a leading voice on AI and data transformation.

What You Bring

  • A Bachelor's and Master's degree (preferred) in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Economics, or a related field; an excellent academic track record is highly valued.
  • Approximately 2-4 years of experience in strategy consulting, data/digital consulting, or a related field (including internships or business analyst roles).
  • A dual affinity for business and technology — you enjoy solving complex problems and can approach challenges in a structured, strategic way.
  • Familiarity with data and digital technologies (e.g. analytics, AI, machine learning concepts, digital marketing, cloud platforms); you don't need to be a programmer, but you should be tech-savvy and eager to grow in the data/AI domain.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience creating impactful presentations and reports for senior audiences.
  • Proficiency in English and French or Dutch is required; additional languages are a plus.
  • You must be eligible to work in Belgium.

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation aligned with your expertise and experience.
  • A unique positioning at the crossroads of boardroom-level strategy and cutting-edge AI — not a traditional consultancy, not a pure tech player.
  • Visibility and impact from day one as part of a growing Belgian team.
  • Professional growth through regular training in both strategy consulting methodologies and AI/data capabilities, combined with international exposure across Artefact's global network.
  • A collaborative, entrepreneurial culture of doers — innovative, fast-moving, and committed to delivering impact while supporting each other's growth.

This position is based in Brussels, with opportunities to collaborate on projects worldwide.

At Artefact Belgium, we value motivation and potential above all else. Don't worry if you don't meet every criterion — if you're passionate about leveraging data and AI to drive real business impact, we'd love to hear from you.

About the job

About ASAL:

ASAL Technologies was established in 2000 and has grown to become a leading technology company across Jordan, KSA, UAE, and Palestine. With a workforce of over 450 engineers, ASAL offers a wide range of AI agents, services and products, Software and Digital Transformation services, Engineering and R&D services. ASAL Technologies is assembling a team of PD Engineers for a high-end multinational company. The team will be working with top notch engineers in the semiconductor industry from all over the world.

What you will do:

  • Monitor and triage security alerts for our clients in our own triage console.
  • Investigate suspicious activity across firewall, endpoint and authentication logs.
  • Escalate real incidents, manage cases and keep clear timelines.
  • Run threat hunts and write the monthly client security report.
  • Tell our engineers about every false alarm and missed attack, and help write the runbooks.
  • Take part in the on-call rota.
  • Hunt for threats, including AI-assisted attacks, and use AI tools critically to investigate faster.
  • 1-3 years in a SOC, NOC or IT security role, or a strong graduate with hands-on lab experience.
  • Good understanding of networking, Windows and Linux.
  • Knowledge of common attack techniques (MITRE ATT&CK).
  • Knowledge of threat hunting, common vulnerabilities, defence methods and attack techniques.
  • Awareness of red-teaming tools and of how attackers use AI (for example AI-written phishing, deepfakes and AI-assisted red teaming), and how to use AI tools critically in investigations.
  • Strong written and verbal English skills.

Nice to have

  • Experience with a SIEM (Wazuh, Elastic, Splunk, QRadar or similar).
  • Security+ / CySA+ / BTL1 or similar.
  • CTF, TryHackMe or HackTheBox SOC-path experience.

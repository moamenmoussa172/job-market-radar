About the job

Major Purpose

Implements and supports logistic processes for Deere units, suppliers and dealers/customers for mutual sustainable benefits. This job applies to either inbound and/or outbound logistical processes.

Major Duties

  • Support material consolidation of packing and shipping for JDB and Ramos which including JDB’s BCC project, 544G/644G T3 project and Ramos 644G/544G FT4 project and Mid SSL project.
  • Support TCW factory daily mass production, not limited as below:
  • All materials delivery on time
  • Material cycle count of both TCW and Warehouse
  • PDP masa material management
  • Service parts management
  • Bonded materials management
  • Trouble shouting on all issue not limited to materials issues and operation issues

Required

  • Familiar with material flow process
  • Be good at SAP
  • Good communication skills

Desired

  • 3 + years working experience of warehouse management or logistics
  • Be good at MM module
  • Simple communication in English

Education

  • Bachelor's degree or above

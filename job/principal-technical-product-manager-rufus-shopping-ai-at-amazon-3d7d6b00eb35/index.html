Principal Technical Product Manager - Rufus Shopping AI

About the job

We are seeking a Principal Product Manager - Technical (PM-T) to build agentic shopping experiences on Amazon. Rufus is Amazon's Shopping AI, a high-visibility generative AI investment within the Stores business that leverages large language models (LLMs). As we evolve Rufus from a conversational assistant into a personal shopping concierge, we are laying the foundation for AI that works seamlessly behind the scenes to act on behalf of our customers.
By shifting the paradigm from answering questions to performing meaningful, automated tasks, your work will ensure Rufus becomes an essential part of the customer's daily routine. If you are passionate about reinventing e-commerce and empowering customers to offload tedious shopping tasks through smart, autonomous AI, this is a rare opportunity to define the future of agentic commerce at Amazon.
In this role, you will own the vision, strategy, and roadmap for Rufus’s automation and agentic capabilities. You will invent intuitive ways for customers to initiate complex, multi-step tasks using natural language, and develop intelligent systems where Rufus anticipates needs. Success means simplifying everyday shopping, establishing deep customer trust in autonomous actions, and driving a step-change in engagement.
To achieve this, you will shape the priorities of Rufus Engineering and Science teams, balancing creative vision with rigorous technical execution. You will work directly with multiple Applied Science and Engineering organizations to push new frontiers, building applications that leverage state-of-the-art models to invent experiences that are only now possible. Bringing high judgment and high standards to the table, you will make data-driven decisions to relentlessly improve the user experience, inventing novel LLM-driven interactions that balance technical feasibility with true customer delight.
- Bachelor's degree
- Experience in technical product management, program management or engineering
- Experience leading cross-organizational programs and setting and holding teams and individuals to timelines and deliverables without explicit authority
- Experience as a strong leader who can prioritize well, communicate clearly and effectively influence across cross-functional teams
- Experience contributing to engineering discussions around technology decisions and strategy related to a product
- Experience developing, deploying and managing AI products at scale
- Proven ability to thrive in highly ambiguous environments by translating high-level vision into clear, actionable requirements and scalable processes.
- A history of successfully identifying technology trends and inventing disruptive, customer-obsessed solutions that establish new paradigms in their respective industries.
- Experience working directly with Engineers on product enhancements
- Experience driving technical products built on ML/AI/LLMs directly with Applied Science and Engineering partners.

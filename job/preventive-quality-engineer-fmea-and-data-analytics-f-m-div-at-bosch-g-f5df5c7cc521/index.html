About the job

Your contribution to something big:

As our Preventive Quality & Data Analytics Engineer, you will be the vital link between product development and field reliability. You will lead and moderate Design FMEAs (D-FMEA) for our smart thermotechnology and heat pump products, while simultaneously building data-driven Early Warning systems to prevent failures before they can impact our customers:

  • Plan, coordinate, and moderate Design FMEAs (D-FMEA) within active product development projects, ensuring methodical correctness and alignment with quality guidelines.
  • Guide and mentor multidisciplinary engineering teams in identifying technical risks, failure modes, and establishing robust preventive/detection actions.
  • Analyze data streams, including field, connectivity, laboratory test, and service engineering data—to identify emerging quality and reliability trends.
  • Support Early Warning and root-cause analysis with robust, data-based evidence to drive rapid problem-solving.
  • Apply analytical and statistical methods to detect patterns, anomalies, and potential weak points in our technical systems.
  • Translate complex data insights and field learnings into actionable, practical engineering recommendations to continuously optimize product design.
  • Feed analytical field insights back into FMEA structures, laboratory validation procedures, and active development lifecycles.

What distinguishes you:

Education

  • University degree in Engineering, Natural Sciences, or a related technical discipline.

Experience and Know-how

  • Quality & Risk Analysis: Proven experience in technical product development, reliability engineering, or quality management.
  • FMEA Methodology: Solid, practical understanding of FMEA methodology; hands-on experience in D-FMEA moderation and familiarity with dedicated FMEA software (preferably APIS IQ-RM) is highly valued.
  • Data Analytics Tech Stack: Experience or strong interest in data processing, statistics, and visualization. Practical experience with Python, SQL, Databricks, or comparable analytics tools is a strong advantage.
  • Domain Affinity: Familiarity or interest in thermodynamic systems, household appliances, HVAC, or connected IoT platforms is a plus.

Languages

  • Excelent communication skills in written and spoken English are required.

Working Style and Methods

  • Outstanding moderation, facilitation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to lead interdisciplinary and international workshops.
  • An analytical, structured, and systems-oriented thinker with a proactive, self-driven work ethic.
  • Strong problem-solving mindset and a high degree of technical curiosity.

Personality

  • Strong collaboration skills in interdisciplinary and international teams

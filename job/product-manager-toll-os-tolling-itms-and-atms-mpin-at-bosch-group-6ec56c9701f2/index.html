About the job

What the role owns

Product strategy and roadmap - Own and evolve the roadmap across tolling (multi-lane free flow and plaza software), intelligent traffic management and enforcement, and integration with highway ATMS. Sequence work by customer need, economics, certification effort and regulatory readiness.

Field learning into requirements - Work from live deployments: recognition accuracy, manual validation rate, reconciliation gaps, notice and dispute failures, incident root causes. Turn each pattern into a prioritised backlog item with an owner.

Recognition and sensor-fusion quality - Define accuracy and confidence targets across RFID, number-plate recognition, vehicle classification and other sensors. Work with engineering and AI teams on model, camera and edge decisions, and lead make-or-buy choices on components.

Interoperability, compliance and security - Ensure the product meets national interoperability specifications for electronic toll collection, RFID and equipment standards, certification requirements, data protection, audit logging and retention. Work with Legal and Compliance on regulatory change.

Tender and bid support - Own the technical content of responses to highway authority and concessionaire tenders: compliance matrices, equivalence of components, architecture and service-level proposals. Work with Finance on bid-versus-investment discipline.

Deployment and operations readiness - Define site readiness, integration with acquirer banks and payment networks, settlement and reconciliation, service-level design and acceptance criteria. Hand over cleanly to operations and keep the live KPIs visible.

Unit economics - Own cost per passage and cost per plaza, and shape transaction-fee and service models with Finance, so the product stays competitive in price-led tenders.

Ecosystem and partners - Work with highway authorities, concessionaires, acquirer banks, system integrators and hardware vendors. Work with the Global Partner Manager on technology and supplier partnerships.

Platform and data integration - Publish passage and vehicle events into the Mobility Prism platform so the same vehicle can be understood across tolling, traffic and other services, within consent and data protection rules.

Team and ways of working - Guide the product owners and engineering squads working on Toll.OS using agile (SAFe) practices, and build the product-management discipline of the team through coaching and clear decision records

How the role is measured

The role is accountable for business outcomes, not only for product documentation. Effectiveness is assessed on:

  • Recognition accuracy and share of passages verified without manual review
  • Time from field issue to product fix
  • Cost per passage and cost per plaza trend
  • Technical compliance rate and win rate on tenders the role supported
  • Deployment lead time from contract to go-live
  • Reconciliation and settlement accuracy with acquirer banks
  • Number of corridors and plazas running on the standard product
  • Quality of field feedback captured into the roadmap

Who we are looking for

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in engineering (electronics, electrical, computer science, civil or transportation); a master's degree or MBA is an advantage.

Experience

  • 8 to 12+ years in product management, systems engineering or programme leadership in intelligent transport systems, electronic toll collection, smart-city or transport technology, payments infrastructure or computer-vision based systems.
  • Has owned a product or system through design, certification, field deployment and operation, and has seen how it behaves when real traffic arrives.
  • Experience in public-sector or regulated procurement (tenders, compliance matrices, service-level and performance guarantees) is strongly preferred.

Domain and technical knowledge

  • Electronic toll collection: FASTag and national electronic toll collection specifications, RFID standards, acquirer and issuer roles, settlement and reconciliation.
  • Number-plate recognition, computer vision and sensor fusion; understanding of accuracy metrics, confidence cascades and model monitoring.
  • Edge computing, plaza and back-office systems, and integration through APIs; familiarity with traffic management and enforcement systems is an advantage.
  • Certification, equipment standards, cybersecurity and data protection as they apply to roadside systems.
  • Able to read an architecture, challenge a design and translate field problems into engineering requirements.

Commercial skills

  • Unit economics, pricing and service-level commercials; comfortable with a P&L view of a product.
  • Negotiation and relationship skills with authorities, banks and vendors.

Personal attributes

  • Strong ownership and bias to action; comfortable operating with ambiguity in a new business.
  • Field-minded: willing to spend time at sites and with operations teams, with regular domestic travel.
  • Clear structured communicator, in writing and in the room, who can explain a technical trade-off to a senior stakeholder.
  • Integrity and discretion when working with public authorities and sensitive data.
  • Collaborative leader who influences without formal authority across engineering, business and partners.

Who the role works with

  • Product Area Leader for the UtilizationStack (MPS/PAZ)
  • Engineering, AI and DevSecOps teams
  • Delivery, site and operations teams
  • Bid and tender team, and the Global Partner Manager
  • Finance, Legal and Compliance
  • Regional business leadership (MPS/RBR)
  • Highway authorities, concessionaires, acquirer banks, system integrators and vendors
  • Product owners for Mobility Prism and for financial services built on toll data

How to read this role

This is a product-ownership role with business accountability, not a project management or pure engineering role. The ideal candidate combines systems depth, field experience, commercial judgement and the ability to win trust with authorities and partners.

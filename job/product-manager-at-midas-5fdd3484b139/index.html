About the job

At Midas, we are working on real-life user needs to transform the world of finance.

We’ve transformed investing in Turkey by delivering a seamless experience for everyday investors.

Today, 4.5 million users invest with Midas. Backed by an $80M Series B, the largest fintech investment ever in Turkey, we are scaling faster than ever.

Product Manager at Midas, owns the development and delivery of specific features or products, ensuring alignment with the broader roadmap and customer needs. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to execute on priorities and deliver value to users.

What You’ll Ship

  • Prioritize “What is the next killing feature?”, collaborate on and execute the product roadmap, delivering features aligned with customer and business needs.
  • Own the strategy, positioning, planning, and competitive analysis activities of Midas’s specific product areas or features, ensuring timely delivery and quality outcomes.
  • Conduct market research and gather customer feedback to inform product improvements.
  • Focus on tactical decision-making to address immediate product challenges and deliverables.
  • What You’ll Bring
  • Ability to drive end-to-end strategy and execution for a key product area, with responsibility for shaping the long-term product roadmap.
  • Work closely with leadership to influence business decisions and deliver impactful results across the organization.
  • Experience in collaboration with cross-functional teams, including engineering, design, brokerage and marketing, fostering a high-performance product team culture.
  • Strong understanding of the development process, from the user’s request and finishing with rolling out into production, with knowledge of the roles various systems play within this process.
  • Interest in crypto/fintech/trading products is a plus.

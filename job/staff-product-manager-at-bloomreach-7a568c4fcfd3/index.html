About the job

Bloomreach is building the world’s premier agentic platform for personalization.We’re revolutionizing how businesses connect with their customers, building and deploying AI agents to personalize the entire customer journey.

  • We're taking autonomous search mainstream, making product discovery more intuitive and conversational for customers, and more profitable for businesses.
  • We’re making conversational shopping a reality, connecting every shopper with tailored guidance and product expertise — available on demand, at every touchpoint in their journey.
  • We're designing the future of autonomous marketing, taking the work out of workflows, and reclaiming the creative, strategic, and customer-first work marketers were always meant to do.

And we're building all of that on the intelligence of a single AI engine — Loomi — so that personalization isn't only autonomous…it's also consistent.From retail to financial services, hospitality to gaming, businesses use Bloomreach to drive higher growth and lasting loyalty. We power personalization for more than 1,400 global brands, including American Eagle, Sonepar, and Pandora.

We are looking for a Staff Product Manager to own the AI models behind Loomi for Bloomreach Marketing. You will set the direction for our predictive, decisioning, clustering and generative models, and make sure they deliver measurable results for the brands that use them.

About the Team / Product Area

The Loomi Intelligence team builds the AI behind Bloomreach Marketing. For every customer, our models decide who to reach, what to show them, where to reach them and when. They run on each brand's first-party data, across all channels. The team owns four types of models:

  • Predictive and uplift models: purchase and churn propensity, custom predictions, and uplift estimates of the incremental effect of reaching each customer
  • AI decisioning (reinforcement learning): contextual bandits that choose the best variant, offer, channel or timing for each customer
  • Clustering and embeddings: audience sub-clustering and content embeddings that give every model a shared view of customers and content
  • Generative models: LLM reasoning layered on these models, to give them a head start on new campaigns and explain each decision in plain language

Responsibilities

  • Own the roadmap. Decide which models to build, improve, change or retire, based on what will deliver the most impact for customers.
  • Start from the business problem. Work with marketers to find the outcomes that matter, then pick the right approach: predictive, decisioning, generative or rules-based, built or partnered.
  • Drive adoption. Find where customers get stuck with each model, remove the friction, and grow the number of brands running it in live campaigns.
  • Prove the value. Stay close to customers and judge every model by the business outcomes it drives for them, such as incremental revenue, conversion and retention.
  • Take models to market. Partner with Product Marketing, Sales and Customer Success on launches and enablement, and join enterprise deals and RFPs to show where Loomi's models win
  • Make models easy to set up. Design how marketers configure each model, so they can put it to work quickly and without specialist help.
  • Raise model quality. Define the quality bar for each model, covering accuracy, reliability and freshness, and partner with data science and engineering to meet and raise it.
  • Make every decision explainable. Give marketers and their stakeholders a clear view of why a model made each decision and what it delivered, in language they can act on.

What We Expect of the Candidate

Requirements

  • 5+ years of product management in B2B SaaS, including 3+ years owning ML or AI products in production, such as predictive models, reinforcement learning, ranking or generative AI.
  • Hands-on experience proving a model's impact in production, including how you set up the test and what you changed based on the results.
  • Experience as the product lead on a team that includes data scientists or ML engineers.

Skills & Capabilities

  • Experimentation: you choose the right test for the question, from A/B tests to holdouts, and understand the trade-offs between model approaches.
  • ML fluency: you know how predictive, deep learning and generative models are trained, evaluated, served and monitored, including their data and feature requirements.
  • Data-driven decisions: comfortable with SQL and analyzing model and product performance yourself.
  • Customer focus: you start from the marketer's problem and the business outcome, not the algorithm.
  • Product sense for AI: you design experiences that make complex models feel simple, so marketers know what a model did and why.
  • Comfort with complex data: you are at home with customer, event and content data, and know which signals a model needs.
  • Communication: clear storytelling for customers, executives and sales teams, in fluent English.
  • Ownership: you take on ambiguous problems, make trade-offs and follow through to results.

Nice to Have

  • Martech, CDP, ecommerce or lifecycle marketing experience
  • Experience with AI decisioning, reinforcement learning (including contextual bandits) or recommendation systems
  • Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol) or building AI capabilities that agents and external tools can call

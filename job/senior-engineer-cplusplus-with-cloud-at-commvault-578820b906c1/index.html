Recruitment Fraud Alert

We’ve learned that scammers are impersonating Commvault team members—including HR and leadership—via email or text. These bad actors may conduct fake interviews and ask for personal information, such as your social security number.

What to know:

  • Commvault does not conduct interviews by email or text.
  • We will never ask you to submit sensitive documents (including banking information, SSN, etc) before your first day.
  • Commvault recruiters may contact prospective candidates through LinkedIn and other professional networking platforms regarding career opportunities. If you are unsure whether a recruiting communication is legitimate, review the profile to ensure they have a badge verifying them as a Commvault employee.

If you suspect a recruiting scam, please contact us at wwrecruitingteam@commvault.com

Senior Engineer- C++ (with Cloud)

The Opportunity

We have an outstanding career opportunity for Senior Engineer- C++ (with Cloud) at Bangalore location. As a Senior Engineer, you will design, develop, and maintain cyber resiliency modules for cloud hyperscale workloads.

What you’ll do…

  • Build scalable, high-performance enterprise software.
  • Participate in architecture discussions and technical design reviews.
  • Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
  • Debug complex production issues and improve system performance.
  • Collaborate with Product Management, QA, DevOps, and cross-functional engineering teams.
  • Mentor junior engineers and conduct code reviews.
  • Drive innovation while maintaining product quality and reliability.
  • Follow the Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SSDLC) by incorporating security throughout the software development process.

Who you are…

  • Bachelor's degree required.
  • 4 or more years of software development experience
  • Strong experience in C++, Object oriented design.
  • Java, C#, Dotnet knowledge is preferable.
  • Good knowledge of Operating Systems (Windows/Linux), Multithreading and concurrency, Data structures and algorithms, Networking concepts, Distributed systems.
  • Experience with debugging and performance optimization.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to work in Agile development environments.
  • Security and SaaS experience strongly preferred.

You’ll love working here because:

  • Employee stock purchase plan (ESPP)
  • Continuous professional development, product training, and career pathing
  • Annual health check-ups, Car lease Program, and Tuition Reimbursement
  • An inclusive company culture, an opportunity to join our Community Guilds
  • Personal accident cover and Term life cover

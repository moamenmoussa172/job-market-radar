About the job

About the Team

The Identity team builds the foundational infrastructure that powers how people access and interact with OpenAI’s products. With more than one billion weekly active users across our products, Identity is one of the most critical platform layers at OpenAI, enabling secure, reliable, and seamless access to ChatGPT, Codex, and the broader OpenAI ecosystem.

We operate at the intersection of large-scale distributed systems, security, and consumer product engineering. Our systems must support unprecedented user growth, increasingly personalized experiences, and an expanding ecosystem of AI-powered products and agents. Our mission is to build an identity platform that scales with OpenAI’s ambitions while maintaining the highest standards of reliability, performance, security, and user trust.

About the Role

As a Software Engineer on the Identity Platform team, you will design, build, and scale the mission-critical distributed systems that power identity across OpenAI. You will own foundational infrastructure spanning authentication, authorization, session management, account lifecycle, and access control for one of the largest and fastest-growing consumer technology platforms in the world.

You’ll tackle complex engineering challenges across high-throughput services, globally distributed data systems, low-latency request paths, and fault-tolerant architectures. You will help evolve our existing infrastructure to support the next billion users while building the foundational capabilities that enable entirely new products and experiences.

This is a highly technical, hands-on role with significant architectural ownership, broad cross-functional impact, and the opportunity to shape the long-term direction of Identity at OpenAI.

In this role, you will:

  • Architect, build, and operate highly available, low-latency distributed systems that power identity and account infrastructure at global consumer internet scale.
  • Evolve our core authentication, authorization, session management, and account lifecycle services to support continued hypergrowth and an expanding product ecosystem.
  • Solve complex infrastructure challenges involving distributed data consistency, caching, fault tolerance, capacity planning, and performance optimization.
  • Drive foundational architectural investments and large-scale migrations while maintaining reliability and continuity for over a billion users.
  • Build reusable platform primitives, APIs, and tooling that enable engineering teams across OpenAI to develop and launch new products securely and efficiently.
  • Establish and elevate engineering standards around system reliability, observability, operational excellence, and security.
  • Partner closely with engineering teams across Infrastructure, Security, Integrity, and Product to solve cross-cutting technical challenges.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentor engineers, and influence the long-term architectural direction of OpenAI’s identity platform.

You might thrive in this role if you:

  • Have 7+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong track record of designing, building, and operating large-scale backend or infrastructure systems.
  • Have deep expertise in distributed systems, including service-oriented architectures, distributed databases, caching, consistency models, and fault tolerance.
  • Have experience scaling mission-critical, high-throughput services in environments with demanding availability, latency, and correctness requirements.
  • Have led significant architectural initiatives, infrastructure migrations, or platform modernization efforts in complex production environments.
  • Care deeply about engineering fundamentals, operational excellence, security, and building systems that other engineers can depend on.
  • Demonstrate strong technical judgment and the ability to balance near-term product needs with long-term architectural investments.
  • Take ownership end-to-end and thrive in fast-moving, ambiguous environments where the technical challenges evolve alongside the business.
  • Have experience with or an interest in authentication, authorization, identity management, or security infrastructure. Prior Identity domain experience is not required.

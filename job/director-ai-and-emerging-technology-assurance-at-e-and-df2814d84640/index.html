About the job

Lead the assurance activities within the Risk & Assurance department, focusing on digital applications, infrastructure, services such as AI, Data, IoT, Cloud, Blockchain, Fintech, SaaS, Web & Mobile Applications, Cybersecurity and Emerging Technologies. This role is pivotal in safeguarding the interests of e& group and its verticals by ensuring corporate strategies, values and business goals are met through comprehensive assurance over digital applications, infrastructure, and services. The incumbent will drive the development and execution of the annual assurance plan, collaborate with key stakeholders, and provide expert guidance on digital transformation, cloud strategy and emerging technologies. By identifying and mitigating risks, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement, the job role will enhance the organization’s AI & emerging technologies assurance.

About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Define and maintain cloud architecture for AI4ALL platforms and applications.
  • Design secure, scalable, highly available, and cost-efficient infrastructure for AI solutions on GCP.
  • Support hybrid and cross-cloud integration with other cloud platforms when needed.
  • Establish reusable cloud patterns, standards, and reference architectures.
  • Define and govern Infrastructure as Code practices, preferably using Terraform.
  • Build CI/CD pipelines and GitHub Actions workflows for development teams.
  • Implement automated testing, code-quality checks, security scanning, and deployment automation.
  • Work with cybersecurity teams on IAM, networking, secrets management, compliance, and cloud governance.
  • Design infrastructure supporting AI, data, and generative-AI workloads.
  • Define standards for monitoring, logging, resilience, disaster recovery, and cost optimization.
  • Lead architecture reviews, mentor engineers, and collaborate with cross-functional Agile teams.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 9+ years of experience in cloud engineering, infrastructure, DevOps, platform engineering, or solution architecture.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Google Cloud Platform.
  • Experience working with hybrid or cross-cloud environments, such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.
  • Hands-on experience working with agentic frameworks and AI orchestration tools (e.g., LangGraph, Google GenAI SDK/Agent Builder, Google ADK, AutoGen) to deploy autonomous AI agents
  • Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code, preferably Terraform.
  • Experience building CI/CD pipelines and GitHub Actions workflows.
  • Knowledge of automated testing, code quality, security scanning, and deployment automation.
  • Strong knowledge of cloud networking, IAM, security, observability, and cost management.
  • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, GKE, or Cloud Run.
  • Strong leadership, communication, mentoring, and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in Agile and cross-functional teams.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect certification.
  • Experience with GCP landing zones, Shared VPC, organization policies, and multi-project governance.
  • Familiarity with Vertex AI, GitOps, policy as code, SRE, FinOps, or OpenTelemetry.
  • Experience in automotive, industrial, or regulated environments.

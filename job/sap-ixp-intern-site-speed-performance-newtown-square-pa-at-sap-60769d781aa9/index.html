Position Title: SAP iXp Intern - Site Speed Performance

Location: Newtown Square, PA

Anticipated Start Date: October 2026

Contract Duration: 6 to 12 months

Work Hours: Full-time internship – 40 hours per week

The SAP.com Digital Platforms Team is seeking a Site Speed Performance Intern to support fast, seamless experiences across desktop, mobile, and automated crawler environments. Reporting to the Product Owner for Site Speed Performance and Optimization, you will analyze performance data, investigate front-end and rendering bottlenecks, support synthetic and real-user monitoring, and help identify measurable optimization opportunities for both human users and AI-powered search and discovery systems.

You will also help advance AI-assisted performance workflows by using large language models and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations to streamline testing, diagnostics, reporting, and regression analysis. This is a technical analysis and liaison role connecting SAP IT Development with business stakeholders across SAP Global Marketing. Hands-on production development is not required, but curiosity about front-end technology, web performance, bot rendering, crawler optimization, and AI-enabled automation is essential.

Core Responsibilities

Performance Testing and Analysis

  • Conduct synthetic and real-user performance testing using platforms such as Catchpoint and Akamai mPulse, and help evaluate future RUM capabilities.
  • Analyze Core Web Vitals and related performance data using Chrome UX Report data and SAP.com Grafana dashboards to identify trends and regressions.
  • Investigate front-end and rendering bottlenecks using Chrome DevTools and software-as-a-service (SaaS) performance platforms.
  • Document findings and recommend optimization opportunities to product and development teams.

Bot and Crawler Performance

  • Monitor Prerender.io bot traffic, rendering usage, and crawler performance for SAP.com and SAP.cn.
  • Help analyze crawler errors, CDN routing rules, robots.txt directives, and custom user-agent traffic, and identify opportunities to reduce 404 responses.
  • Support efforts to improve bot rendering efficiency and content accessibility.
  • Support proof-of-concept projects designed to improve the performance of SAP crawlers and custom user-agent traffic.

AI-Assisted Workflows

  • Explore SAP-approved generative AI and MCP-enabled tools for test-script generation, diagnostic analysis, and reporting.
  • Identify opportunities to replace repetitive manual analysis with scalable, AI-assisted workflows.
  • Review AI-generated findings and validate them against performance data.

Collaboration and Documentation

  • Collaborate with product owners, developers, UX teams, analysts, and external partners.
  • Maintain performance documentation, dashboards, and internal knowledge resources.
  • Stay informed about web performance standards, tools, and emerging technologies.
  • Support the creation and publication of internal customer success stories on the team’s SharePoint site.

What You’ll Bring

A curious and agile mindset, with a passion for learning and the resilience to adapt in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Emotional intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and thoughtful stakeholder engagement. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies — especially artificial intelligence — paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take initiative.

Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, or recently graduated (start date must be within 6 months of graduation date) from a coding academy/bootcamp, apprenticeship, associate, bachelor’s, master’s or JD/PhD program.

Skills and Qualifications

Web Foundations

  • Basic understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and how modern websites are delivered and rendered.
  • Familiarity with browser developer tools, particularly Chrome DevTools, or a strong interest in learning web performance analysis.
  • Awareness of web performance concepts such as Core Web Vitals, accessibility, front-end rendering, and asset optimization.

AI and Emerging Technology Literacy

  • Curiosity about AI-assisted workflows, automated crawlers, search technologies, and their relationship to web performance.
  • Strong interest in learning how large language models, retrieval systems, vector databases, AI agents, and agentic orchestration work together.
  • Interest in using generative AI and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations to support testing, diagnostics, analysis, and workflow automation.
  • Ability to evaluate AI-generated findings critically and validate them against technical data.

UX and Visual Design Fundamentals

  • Familiarity with Figma for reviewing prototypes, design specifications, and AI-assisted UX concepts.
  • Basic understanding of responsive design, usability, accessibility, and visual design principles.
  • Familiarity with Adobe Creative Cloud applications used to create and optimize bitmap, vector, and generative visual assets, such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and Firefly.

Enterprise Platform Competency

  • Familiarity with Adobe Experience Cloud technologies—such as Adobe Experience Manager, Adobe Experience Platform, Adobe Target, or Adobe Analytics—or comparable enterprise content management, personalization, and analytics platforms.
  • General understanding of how content management systems, analytics platforms, content delivery networks, and third-party services contribute to a digital experience.

Collaboration and Workflow Tools

  • Familiarity with Jira and Confluence—or comparable platforms—for managing work, documenting requirements, and supporting Agile collaboration.
  • Ability to review technical findings, maintain clear documentation, and contribute to shared knowledge resources.

Education

  • Currently pursuing a degree – or possessing equivalent practical experience – in computer science, web development, data analytics, UX design, information technology, marketing technology, or a related field.

Communication and Problem-Solving

  • Ability to translate business needs into clear, actionable technical requirements.
  • Ability to communicate effectively and collaborate with both technical and nontechnical stakeholders.
  • Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, curiosity, and willingness to learn unfamiliar technologies.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Familiarity with Core Web Vitals, Chrome DevTools, Lighthouse, or WebPageTest.
  • Exposure to analytics, synthetic monitoring, real-user monitoring, CDNs, content management systems, or Agile tools such as Jira and Confluence.
  • Interest or experience with generative AI, MCP integrations, dynamic rendering, or crawler management.
  • Familiarity with responsive design, visual asset optimization (e.g., images, animation, and video), or modern JavaScript frameworks.

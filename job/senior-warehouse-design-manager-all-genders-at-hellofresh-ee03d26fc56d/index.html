Senior Warehouse Design Manager (all genders)

HelloFresh · Magdeburg, Germany

Onsite · Senior · Other tech role · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on HelloFresh's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

About the role: What's in the Box

Are you passionate about turning operational challenges into practical technical solutions? Do you enjoy working across different locations, bringing people together and taking projects from initial concept through to on-site implementation?

As Senior Warehouse Design Manager (all genders), you will play a key role in improving and developing the infrastructure and processes across our European Fulfilment Centre network. Rather than focusing on large-scale factory launches, you will lead targeted engineering and operational improvement projects, translating business needs into practical solutions that make our operations more efficient, scalable and reliable.

Working closely with local Operations teams, Finance, Procurement, external suppliers and our Automation specialists, you will take ownership of projects from initial assessment and solution design through procurement, coordination and on-site implementation.

This is an international, hands-on role with a strong operational focus. You will regularly travel to our Fulfilment Centres across Europe, including locations in Germany, the Netherlands and the Nordics, to understand local challenges, coordinate implementation activities and ensure that solutions work in practice.

What you’ll do: The Recipe

  • Lead engineering and improvement projects: Own targeted projects from initial scoping and requirements gathering through solution development, implementation and handover to local operations.
  • Design practical solutions: Develop and evaluate concepts for material flow, warehouse layouts, equipment and operational infrastructure, ensuring solutions meet local business needs.
  • Drive procurement and implementation: Coordinate direct and indirect procurement activities, including CAPEX projects, in close collaboration with local Finance teams, Procurement and our Amsterdam-based colleagues.
  • Make things happen on-site: Coordinate suppliers, contractors and local stakeholders to manage equipment delivery, installation, commissioning and implementation at our Fulfilment Centres.
  • Collaborate across functions: Work closely with Operations, Automation, IT Infrastructure, Tech, FSQA and Health & Safety to ensure smooth project execution.
  • Improve existing operations: Identify opportunities to optimize existing processes, equipment and material flows, translating operational challenges into tangible improvements.
  • Manage project delivery: Define timelines, milestones and deliverables, manage dependencies and proactively address risks and resource constraints.
  • Connect our international network: Build relationships across different Fulfilment Centres, understand local requirements and ensure knowledge sharing and consistency across locations.
  • Support operational teams: Provide hands-on support during implementation, troubleshooting and post-launch activities where needed.

What you’ll bring: The Ingredients

  • Experience in technical project management, engineering, operations, logistics, warehouse design or a comparable field.
  • A solid understanding of material flow, operational processes, industrial hardware or warehouse infrastructure.
  • Proven ability to independently manage projects, coordinate different stakeholders and drive implementation from concept to completion.
  • Experience working with suppliers, contractors or external service providers.
  • Strong organizational and communication skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts into practical solutions.
  • A hands-on, pragmatic mindset and the confidence to take ownership and make things happen in a fast-paced operational environment.
  • Willingness to travel internationally for up to 50% of your working time. Regular on-site presence at our European Fulfilment Centres is an essential part of the role.

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Save on meals with discounts on HelloFresh boxes delivered to your door.
  • Prepare for the future with our company pension scheme.
  • Receive discounted memberships at Urban Sports Club and John Reed, along with mental health support via Headspace and Spill.
  • Benefit from 30 days of paid vacation each year to rest and recharge.
  • Travel sustainably and affordably with a monthly Deutschlandticket subsidy.
  • Grow your skills through internal trainings, team-specific L&D resources, and a structured onboarding program.
  • Work in a modern environment with friendly, supportive co-workers, plus perks like a cafeteria, a Selgros wholesale card, and provided safety equipment.

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

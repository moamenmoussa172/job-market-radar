What you will do

SAP Chile Next Gen roles are a global-minded, strategic positions that provides recent university graduates with opportunities to find purpose in their careers. This is the foundation for a career built on connection, creativity, and impact.

Position Title: Sales Operations – SAP Next Gen

Location: Santiago, Chile

Duration: Temporary 6 months up to 2 years

General role responsibilities are:

  • Administrative tasks and daily office functions
  • Monitoring the sales operations process and initiatives
  • Proactively give qualitative and quantitative feedback using standard procedures and reports
  • Data entry in CRM
  • Document all the campaign execution using the adequate systems correctly (work together with manager/expert as necessary)
  • Contract tracking
  • Central contact and all other ad-hoc support as needed by manager and his/her team
  • Managing workflows and monitoring items like purchase orders, internal orders, distribution lists, inventory etc.
  • Preparation and follow up of meetings and larger external events for Managers

What You’ll Bring

A curious and agile mindset, with a passion for learning and the resilience to adapt in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Emotional intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and thoughtful stakeholder engagement. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies, especially artificial intelligence, paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take initiative.

Education and Experience:

  • Must be a recent University graduate of Bachelor's degree
  • Preferably a graduate of Business Administration and/or related studies
  • 0–2 years of professional experience (not including internships)

Requirements and Competencies:

  • English: Fluent (able to fluently understand and communicate verbally)
  • Microsoft Excel Knowledge (including macros)
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to network within a client´s organization
  • Strong in teamwork and ability to learn and adapt quickly
  • Clear interest to develop a sales career when graduating
  • Energetic and goal oriented
  • Time management to arrange a weekly and monthly time schedule
  • AI Skills:
  • Ability to apply generative AI tools (Claude, GPT) to streamline, automate, and scale operational tasks
  • Understand machine learning, generative AI, data ethics, and AI-driven decision-making.
  • Ability to integrate AI tools into daily workflows, craft strategic prompts to drive innovation, and apply best practices to ensure ethical and effective outcomes.
  • Ability in the use of AI coding assistants as everyday development tools — and develop the judgment to know when to trust, verify, and override their output
  • Ability in using AI-driven automation solutions to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the processes
  • Staying curious — track emerging developments in AI tooling, foundation models, and enterprise software

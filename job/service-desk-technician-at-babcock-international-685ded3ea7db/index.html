About the Role


If you're passionate about technology and thrive on helping others, this is your opportunity to join a high-performing IT team supporting users across Australia, New Zealand and the UK. As a Service Desk Technician, you'll be the first point of contact for IT support, providing technical assistance, resolving issues, and delivering an exceptional customer experience in a fast-paced and collaborative environment.

Working within a modern and dynamic IT function, you'll support a broad range of technologies including desktops, laptops, networks, telecommunications, and collaboration tools. You'll play a key role in keeping our people connected and productive, while building your technical expertise and developing your career alongside experienced IT professionals.

This role operates on 4 days on, 4 days off roster with 10-hour shifts, including weekends, providing national support coverage. Shifts rotate between 5:00am-3:00pm and 7:00am-5:00pm throughout the year to meet business requirements across multiple time zones.

We're looking for a customer-focused problem solver who enjoys troubleshooting, working as part of a team, and continuously improving the way IT services are delivered. In return, you'll enjoy a supportive environment, exposure to a diverse range of technologies, and genuine opportunities for professional growth and development.

To be successful, you will bring

  • Australian Citizenship with a Defence security clearance (or the ability to obtain).
  • Prior experience in Customer Service and or a similar IT focused role.
  • IT service management knowledge.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills across IT environments.
  • Ability to prioritise and manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.

Your future at Babcock – what we offer you

  • Our agile working policies offer more choice and autonomy. For you, this could mean flexible start and finish times, a mix of office and home working, or even condensed working weeks.
  • Our work life integration policy offers attractive leave entitlements including (not limited to) wellbeing leave, volunteer leave, paid parental leave, defence reservist leave, and we are proud to be a Work180 endorsed employer.
  • Babcock’s supportive and engaging environment, coupled with our capability framework, aims to advance your career with internal development programs, self-led learning modules through LinkedIn Learning and access to study leave.
  • Our beRewarded platform offers endless discounts across retail, health, entertainment, travel and much more, plus novated leasing options and corporate shares plan.
  • As a principles driven organisation, our weRecognise program aims to recognise the outstanding work our people do each and every day in line with our Principles which are Be kind, Collaborate, Be Curious, Think Outcomes, Be Courageous and Own & Deliver.

Our team of 1,800+ experienced staff ensure critical services and assets are readily available, affordable, and future proof.

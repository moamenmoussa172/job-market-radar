Department Overview

The UK Software Enterprise Support team maintains and supports IAAS, Pronto Hosted Services and the Guardian Public Safety Solution.
The Pronto Hosted Services ecosystem empowers operational personnel with real-time access to core databases via mobile platforms. By digitizing workflows directly at the point of contact, teams streamline data entry and maximize operational efficiency. Supporting everything from field enforcement to administrative workflows, our software suite transforms frontline processes into a seamless, fully paperless ecosystem.
Our Guardian Public Safety Solution serves as an enterprise-grade control room system, integrating multi-channel voice, data, computer-aided dispatch, and multimedia communications. Delivered through a scalable software-as-a-service model, it simplifies infrastructure maintenance while enhancing reliability and capability across public safety operations.


Job Description

This position focuses specifically on the Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) product.

Key responsibilities include performing comprehensive diagnostics as well as leading, guiding, and training the broader support team. The primary objective is to cultivate specialized product expertise within CAD, thereby reducing reliance on Engineering and empowering the support organization to resolve issues independently in the future.

Technical Escalations: Take ownership of high-priority technical escalations within the CAD product area, acting as the definitive point of expertise for the CAD.

​

Team Lead: As the team lead for CAD you will be required to use your knowledge to guide, mentor and train the rest of the support team.

​

Product Engineering Bridge: Act as the primary liaison between the SES team and 3rd line support teams.

​

Major Incident Response: Asssit in driving the response to major incidents, ensuring rapid service restoration within agreed SLAs.

Technical Documentation: Capture and publish diagnostic findings, workarounds, and resolution paths to improve front-line issue resolution.

​

Product Specialisation & Continuous Learning: Stay current with product updates, releases, and changes to maintain deep expertise in the CAD product area and remain the team's go-to authority on emerging issues.

​

Technical On-Call: Participate in a support on-call rota to provide after-hours emergency support for critical production issues.


Basic Requirements

Person Specification & Requirements

Technical Expertise: Demonstrated ability to independently investigate and resolve complex enterprise software escalations.

​​

Advanced Diagnostic Capabilities: Demonstrates robust troubleshooting skills with a primary focus on expeditious incident recovery

Frameworks & Methodologies: Working knowledge of ITIL methodologies, specifically Incident, Problem, and Change Management.

​

Technical Communication: Able to translate complex technical findings and root causes clearly for both technical and non-technical internal stakeholders.

​

Cross-Functional Collaboration: Possesses a proven ability to collaborate effectively across all support functions and Tier 3 engineering teams.

​​

Crisis Management: Composed and decisive during high-stakes after-hours emergencies or critical system outages.

​

Security Clearance: Must be able to attain NPPV3 and SC clearance (requires a minimum of 3 years UK residency)

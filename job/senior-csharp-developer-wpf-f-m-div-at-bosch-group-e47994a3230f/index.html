About the job

We are seeking an experienced Senior C# Developer to join our dynamic team. As a Senior C# Developer, you will play a crucial role in designing, developing, and maintaining robust desktop applications using C# and related technologies. You’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams to create intuitive user interfaces and ensure code quality.

Responsibilities:

  • Application Development: Design, develop, and maintain complex desktop applications using C#.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with cross-functional teams to define and implement new features/requirements.
  • Code Quality: Ensure software meets all requirements of quality, security, extensibility, efficiency, and maintainability.
  • Problem-Solving: Analyze system requirements and provide technical solutions.
  • Modelling: Use modelling tools to create software designs
  • Experience: 5 to 8 years of professional experience in C# software development.
  • C# Proficiency: Strong understanding of C# programming.
  • Object-Oriented Programming: Solid grasp of object-oriented programming principles.
  • UML: Excellent knowledge of software design and UML
  • Communication Skills: Excellent communication and teamwork abilities, fluency in English, both written and spoken.
  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or a related field.

Preferred Skills (Not Mandatory):

  • Experience with WPF.
  • Knowledge of other .NET technologies and frameworks.
  • Familiarity with agile development methodologies.
  • Exposure to CI/CD pipelines.

Additional Information:

  • Passionate about clean code and continuous improvement.
  • Excited to contribute innovative ideas to the tech world

Department Overview

The Transmission Operational Readiness and Support department serves as the critical gateway for integrating new transmission systems and equipment into one of the world’s largest Emergency Service TETRA networks. By guiding cutting-edge technologies from initial design concepts through to live network acceptance, we directly advance Motorola Solutions' mission of "Solving for safer." Our team acts as the organization's subject matter experts, providing advanced Tier 3 engineering and major incident support to maintain seamless connectivity when communities need it most.


Job Description

The Role

Based primarily at our Rugby, UK offices, this role places you at the intersection of complex network engineering, operational readiness, and mission-critical public safety. You will partner closely with project, design, and regional teams to validate that new transmission deliverables meet strict operational standards while minimizing service interruptions. We value inclusive collaboration and creative problem-solving, welcoming professionals with equivalent practical experience who thrive on taking ownership of complex technical challenges, mentoring 1st and 2nd line engineers, and participating in a 1 in 4 on-call rota.

Key Responsibilities

  • Oversee the survey, installation, commissioning, and operational acceptance testing of new and replacement transmission equipment to help provide full network availability and functionality.
  • Deliver Tier 3 engineering and major incident support to Network Operations, investigating root causes of complex network faults and implementing permanent preventative solutions.
  • Collaborate with project and design teams to translate strict contractual and operational requirements into seamless live network integrations.
  • Develop and execute comprehensive test strategies and plans for new transmission products, services, and self-provide networks utilizing microwave radio and leased services.
  • Provide technical expertise to commercial contract teams, supplier managers, and customers during pre-sales, service reviews, and complex fault investigations.
  • Proactively analyze existing network performance to identify optimization opportunities that enhance reliability and resilience while reducing operational costs.
  • Participate in a 1 in 4 out-of-hours on-call rota and travel occasionally across the UK to support major project rollouts and critical incident resolution.


Basic Requirements

Basic Requirements (Must-Haves)

  • Bachelor’s Degree, HND, or HNC in Telecommunications Engineering.
  • Solid experience within a telecommunications network problem-solving, engineering, and operations environment.
  • Eligibility for UK Security Clearance, requiring a minimum of three years of continuous residency in the UK.
  • Full professional fluency in English (written and spoken).
  • Remote Network Management Tools (including iNMS, ProVision, SOX/SOA, and CLI interfaces) to oversee, configure, and troubleshoot transmission infrastructure.
  • Layer 1, 2, and 3 Networking Protocols (including Ethernet, IP, IP-MPLS, MPLS-TP, and Cisco/Juniper platforms) to design and support packet-based transmission networks.
  • Microwave Radio & Multiplexing Systems (including Hybrid TDM-Ethernet, SDH, and PDH) to manage band selection, capacity multiplication, and legacy-to-modern integration.
  • Optical & Software-Defined Networks (including SDN, SON, and optical vs. electrical multiplexing techniques) to support route manipulation and high-capacity transport.
  • Mobile & Mission-Critical Communications (including TETRA and cellular network architectures) to support emergency service network reliability.
  • Operational Fault Rectification & Commissioning to execute installation, maintenance, and major transmission project rollouts.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Basic project management skills to coordinate multi-stakeholder transmission rollouts.
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and decision-making abilities with high attention to detail.
  • Ability to take proactive ownership of issues, work under pressure to tight timescales, and prioritize inbound calls, live chats, and case management queues.
  • Strong interpersonal, presentation, and negotiation skills to influence internal teams, customers, and external suppliers.
  • Adaptability and a flexible working approach to rapidly learn emerging telecommunications products and technologies.

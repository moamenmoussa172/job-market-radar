About the job

We are looking for a Local Product Owner to join our team and contribute to our product management activities, from market analysis and product strategy to successful product launches.

  • Define and drive product vision and strategy aligned with company goals and market opportunities
  • Plan and execute product launches with whole organization to ensure market adoption
  • Conduct market, competition, and user research; translate insights into product requirements and user stories
  • Monitor market trends, legal requirements, and regulations
  • Represent the company in technical and sectoral committees
  • Develop product-based pricing strategies, market segmentation, and profitability analysis
  • Support internal and external stakeholders (e.g. SEO content, catalogs, product tools)
  • Maintain close contact with the field and lead product-related UX activities
  • Review and release technical documentation
  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant engineering field (Mechanical or Electrical/Electronic preferred); Master’s is a plus
  • 3+ years of experience in Product Management and/or Business Development, preferably in HVAC
  • Knowledge of boiler, heat pumps and air conditioner technologies are a plus
  • Proficiency in market research, competitive analysis, and market trend assessment.
  • Knowledge of local and regional regulatory requirements.
  • Strong analytical and reporting skills (MS Excel, PowerPoint; Power BI is a plus)
  • Excellent communication, presentation, and stakeholder management skills
  • Customer- and results-oriented, self-motivated, and resilient
  • Ability to work effectively in cross-functional and regional teams
  • Ability to integrate AI into daily business
  • Excellent command of English
  • Willingness to travel domestically and internationally

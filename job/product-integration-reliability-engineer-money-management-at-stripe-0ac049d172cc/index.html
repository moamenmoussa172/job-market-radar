About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Stripe's Money Management products (Financial Accounts, Treasury, Global Payouts, Issuing, and Capital) let businesses and platforms hold, move, spend, and earn on their money using the same infrastructure they already use for payments. The Money Management Product Integration Reliability Engineering (IRE) team is responsible for scaling the core technical operations that enable product expansion across Stripe’s Money Management products.

This role will focus primarily on Stripe’s Issuing product. Stripe Issuing provides the card-issuing infrastructure that lets businesses create, manage, and control virtual and physical card programs.

What you’ll do

As a Money Management Product Integration Reliability Engineer (IRE), you’ll work with product engineers, product managers, and go-to-market teams to keep Stripe Issuing’s card programs, APIs, and integrations with financial partners and card networks operating reliably and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Become a domain expert in Stripe’s Issuing stack
  • Identify inefficient or unnecessary manual processes and automate or eliminate them through scoped code changes, changes to partner agreements, or coordination of more complex engineering work across Stripe
  • Design anomaly detection systems and tools to identify discrepancies in data flowing through partner and internal systems.
  • Organize and direct complex technical changes and projects between Stripe and our partners to support product expansion
  • Engage with Stripe's financial partners to drive alignment on product usage, troubleshoot and solve technical problems or breakages
  • Handle inquiries from internal Stripe teams and external partners’ technical teams, often requiring detailed investigation and sometimes code changes
  • Collaborate across the company, including engineering, accounting, financial partnerships and product, to trace and account for billions of dollars moving through the Stripe platform

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement. Integration Reliability Engineering roles are one part coding and one part managing projects. The ideal candidate will have experience in both, but successful candidates may index more highly in one or the other.

Minimum requirements

  • Ability to write high-quality, maintainable code, read and navigate an unfamiliar codebase, and make scoped changes safely; familiarity with developer tools such as Git
  • We work mostly in Ruby and Java. However, languages can be learned: we care much more about your general coding skill than knowledge of a particular language or framework
  • Comfort using SQL to navigate Stripe's data and diagnose the scope and urgency of issues
  • Great technical problem-solving skills, with an ability to develop a working understanding of a technical system using a variety of methods. These include (but aren't limited to) reading/writing code, reading documentation or partner specifications, running debugging programs, reading logs, asking well-formed questions to domain experts, or digging into the raw data through various interfaces.
  • Great communication skills, with an ability to frame messages appropriately for technical and non-technical audiences
  • The will to deliver results, with an ability to establish priorities and reliably execute on solutions (often with hard external deadlines)
  • Experience producing work that increases the impact of others and helps us scale and grow (e.g., automation, tools, documentation)

Preferred qualifications

  • Knowledge of payments or money movement technologies and ecosystems, such as bank transfers, payouts, or card issuing
  • Experience designing or operating agentic or Large language model (LLM) powered workflows in a production environment

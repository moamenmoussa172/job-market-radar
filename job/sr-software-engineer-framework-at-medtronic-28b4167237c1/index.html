A Day in the Life

At Medtronic Patient Care Systems (PCS), we power the digital heartbeat behind life-saving cardiac therapies. We build the connected ecosystem that enables clinicians to monitor, manage, and protect millions of patients living with implantable cardiac devices around the world.

From secure device connectivity and real-time data platforms to intelligent clinical tools and patient-facing experiences, PCS transforms complex cardiac data into clarity — helping physicians make faster decisions, improving outcomes, and giving patients greater confidence in their care.

Every line of code we write supports someone’s parent, spouse, child, or friend. Our work ensures that life-saving therapies are not only delivered safely and reliably, but continuously improved through innovation, insight, and global scale.

If you want to build technology that truly matters — where uptime means peace of mind and innovation means more birthdays — join us. Together, we are shaping the future of connected cardiac care and changing lives every single day

A Day in The Life Of:

In general, the following responsibilities apply for the Senior Software Engineer role. This includes, but is not limited to the following:

  • Design, develop, and test innovative software solutions that power life-changing medical technologies, ensuring compliance with software development standards and best practices.
  • Collaborate with product engineers, technology architects, software developers, and test engineers to define technical solutions, plan project deliverables, and drive successful execution from concept to release.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentorship, and guidance to software engineers, fostering a culture of collaboration, continuous learning, and engineering excellence.
  • Participate in technical reviews of requirements, architecture, designs, code, and other engineering artifacts, ensuring high-quality outcomes and timely delivery of project commitments.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities to enhance development processes, quality practices, and team effectiveness.
  • Design and implement reusable frameworks, components, and engineering patterns that accelerate development, improve maintainability, and increase team productivity.
  • Drive innovation by exploring new technologies, tools, and approaches that enhance software quality, performance, and user experience.
  • Partner closely with cross-functional teams and global stakeholders to deliver high-quality software solutions that make a meaningful impact on patients' lives.

Key Skills & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • Minimum of 4 years of relevant software engineering experience, or an advanced degree with 2 years of relevant technical experience.
  • Passion for building high-quality, scalable, and maintainable software solutions, supported by strong documentation practices.
  • Experience developing frameworks, reusable libraries, platforms, or shared software components that enable efficient product development.
  • Strong leadership, mentoring, and collaboration skills with a desire to help teams grow and succeed.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a passion for tackling complex technical challenges.
  • Proven ability to thrive in an Agile, fast-paced, and highly collaborative environment.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to influence technical decisions across teams.
  • Experience developing software within regulated environments such as medical devices, healthcare, automotive, aerospace, or other quality-focused industries is an advantage.
  • Experience with Agile methodologies, Test-Driven Development (TDD), and modern software engineering best practices.
  • Proficiency in two or more of the following technologies: JavaScript, HTML, C#, .NET MAUI, iPadOS.
  • Experience with application architecture, software frameworks, design patterns, and reusable component development is highly desirable.

Recruitment Fraud Alert

We are aware of phishing scams targeting job seekers. Please keep the following in mind:

Apply only through official Medtronic channels. All legitimate Medtronic recruiting communications come from approved Medtronic platforms and official @medtronic.com email addresses.

Medtronic will never ask for payment or sensitive personal information (such as bank account or Social Security details) during early stages of the hiring process. Any such requests are not legitimate.

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from Medtronic, do not respond, click links, or open attachments.

If you have any questions, concerns regarding the authenticity of a communication alleged to have been made by or on behalf of Medtronic, please contact us immediately at AskHR@medtronic.com.

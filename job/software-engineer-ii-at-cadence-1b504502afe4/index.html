Job Title: Software Engineer II

Location: Edinburgh, United Kingdom

Reports to: Senior Software Engineering Manager

Job Overview:

Join the team working on Cadence’s Virtuoso Analog Design Environment (ADE) and help build new features and interfaces in the Cadence Virtuoso AI Studio. You will design and implement software that meets the needs of customers designing their most advanced integrated circuits (ICs). The work spans algorithms, APIs for other tools and AI agents, and GUI support, as part of a global engineering team.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Implement internal algorithms, develop APIs for other tools and AI agents, and build GUI support for end users.
  • Select and design efficient data structures to handle large data sets.
  • Write automated tests to validate your implementation.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams in the US, China, UK and India, across time zones, to deliver production-quality software.

Job Qualifications:

  • MS, or Bachelor’s degree with at least 2 years of experience, in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent.
  • C/C++, Python software development experience in Linux environment.
  • Solid understanding and usage of data structures and algorithms.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good understanding of software debugging and testing principles.
  • Clear written and spoken communication in English, to work with colleagues across global teams.

Additional Skills/Preferences:

  • Experience in the use of AI/ML for Electronic Design Automation (EDA) is a strong plus.
  • Experience with SKILL (Cadence programming language).
  • Software quality practices: experience with unit testing and code review processes.
  • AI-assisted development workflows: ability to integrate AI tools into engineering workflows to enhance productivity and code quality.
  • Familiarity with the Virtuoso platform.

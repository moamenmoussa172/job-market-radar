About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

About The Role

As our CX Systems & Automation Lead, you'll own the systems that power Notion's global support organization. Reporting to the Head of AMER & EMEA Direct Support, you'll administer and evolve our CX tools (Zendesk, Decagon, and Assembled) as Notion scales, and help decide what our stack looks like next. This is a hands-on individual contributor role: you'll work closely with CX leadership to shape our systems strategy, then design, build, and ship it yourself.

This role is based in our San Francisco office. We work from our offices on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays (our Anchor Days) because we do our best thinking and building together in person. We’re looking for someone who’s excited to work alongside the team during those days.

What You'll Achieve

  • Own administration and architecture of our Helpdesk and CX platforms, design and build routing, automations, macros, SLAs, permissions, customizations, and third-party integrations, and set best practices for how they're used
  • Partner with CX leadership to shape our CX systems strategy and roadmap, then lead delivery from design through implementation, including running system RFPs when needed and advising on the impact of changes on teams, workflows, and customers
  • Partner with our AI team to configure, maintain, and improve our AI support agents, including knowledge inputs, guardrails, and performance tracking, and help CX leaders decide where automation should and shouldn't handle customer conversations
  • Own change management for CX systems, from prioritizing requests to sandbox testing, release planning, access controls, data privacy (e.g., GDPR, PII handling), and communicating changes to global teams
  • Partner with our global (AMER, EMEA, APAC, & Vendor) CX team to be the point of contact for our CX tool stack
  • Partner with CX leaders to drive tool-related reporting and data visibility
  • Identify, diagnose, and resolve issues that arise with CX tooling systems
  • Maintain up-to-date documentation for our CX tools, including key decisions, major changes, and the business reasons behind routing and automation choices

Skills You'll Bring

  • You have 7+ years of relevant experience, ideally in SaaS or AI, with deep hands-on administration of an enterprise helpdesk or CRM (e.g., Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Fin); experience running or migrating between platforms is a plus
  • You're comfortable with APIs, webhooks, and SQL, have built custom integrations, and have partnered with technical teams to deploy AI agents in a support environment; scripting (JavaScript or Python) or integration tools (e.g., Workato, Zapier) are a plus
  • You're as comfortable in a strategy discussion as in the admin console, and you've personally built the configurations and integrations you've recommended
  • You have experience supporting cross-functional programs that relate to CX, operations, engineering, and enablement
  • You can translate business needs and strategy into technical deliverables and clear roadmaps, and keep leaders and stakeholders aligned on status, milestones, and risks
  • You have a metrics mindset: you measure success by outcomes and have built dashboards that tell a clear story with KPIs
  • You have a scaled approach to tool management & implementation
  • You thrive in ambiguity and can manage technical and administrative work independently as priorities change

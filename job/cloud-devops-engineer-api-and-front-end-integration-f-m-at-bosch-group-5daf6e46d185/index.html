About the job

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to join our platform development team within the Bosch Development Cloud (BDC) in Microsoft Azure. In this role, you will help expand our platform ecosystem by contributing to both backend integrations and the user experience. Since we are looking for a balanced profile, you will have the opportunity to focus on building robust APIs, developing modern portal front-end features, or driving a combination of both depending on your strengths.

  • You will design, build, and maintain scalable RESTful APIs and backend services.
  • You will develop and enhance user-facing features on our BDC Developer Portal.
  • You will connect and automate internal software engineering tools with the cloud platform.
  • You will manage and deploy cloud infrastructure using Terraform in Microsoft Azure.
  • You will automate testing and deployment processes using Azure DevOps pipelines.
  • You will leverage modern AI-assisted development tools, such as GitHub Copilot, to write, optimize, and document code efficiently.
  • You will participate actively in an agile Scrum team to translate user needs into clean, working code.
  • You have solid software development experience, are familiar with the OpenAPI specification standard, and have a strong interest in either API/backend systems, modern front-end development, or a mix of both.
  • You possess strong communication skills and the ability to work independently, which is essential for regularly engaging with internal customers to understand their requirements, planning and implementing concrete solutions based on those needs, and presenting your work during technical reviews with other team members.
  • You possess proficiency in programming or scripting languages such as TypeScript, JavaScript, PowerShell, or Python.
  • You bring familiarity with modern web frameworks such as React or Angular for portal development.
  • You have practical experience with Microsoft Azure services and infrastructure automation using Terraform.
  • You have hands-on experience or a strong interest in working with AI-powered development assistants like GitHub Copilot to boost productivity.
  • You understand version control systems with Git and automated CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps.
  • You communicate fluently in English, both written and spoken, within an international environment.

Bosch’s culture of innovation and digital transformation offers you a fantastic platform to grow your skills and enhance your network. We are dedicated to building a warm, open, transparent, and inclusive work environment for all.

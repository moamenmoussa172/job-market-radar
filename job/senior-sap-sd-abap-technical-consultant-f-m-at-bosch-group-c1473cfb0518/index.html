Senior SAP SD/ABAP Technical Consultant F/M

Bosch Group · Warszawa, Poland

Hybrid · Senior · ERP / CRM Developer · Posted 2 November 2026

ABAPSAP S/4HANA

About the job

Technical solution and delivery:

• Analyse requirements with process experts and turn them into functional specifications and technical concepts in SAP SD.

• Design and implement solutions in S/4HANA, combining configuration with enhancements (BAdIs, user exits, Enhancement Framework); develop ABAP solutions yourself as a regular part of the role.

• Act as the technical escalation point for defects and incidents: debugging, error analysis, transport issues.

• Develop interfaces between S/4HANA and external systems (logistics, e-commerce, EDI) using IDoc/ALE, RFC and Web APIs.

• Work with international teams and architects; provide consulting, knowledge transfer and mentoring.

• Support functional and technical testing and deployment to production.

Coordination of FIS third party product (we will provide training/onboarding):

• Lead regular technical alignment with FIS: system prerequisites, software updates, SAP upgrades, customizing and design reviews.

• Ensure FIS/wws is integrated into the end-to-end O2C flow, with Source-to-Pay (S2P) interfaces where needed.

• Analyse FIS-related defects together with test teams and FIS.

• Bridge communication between developers (internal and FIS) and non-technical business users

• Hands-on ABAP development experience: you write production code yourself (reports, enhancements, BAdIs, user exits, interfaces via RFC/IDoc/Web APIs) and are fluent in debugging and analysing existing code.

• Experience in S/4HANA implementation projects and understanding of the S/4HANA extensibility model.

About this Role

We are seeking a Technical Product Owner to coordinate a cross-functional technical team of up to 10 direct reports, working across Functional Analysis, Front-End Development, Back-End Development, Database Development and Full-Stack Development.

This role combines end-to-end Product Owner ownership with technical coordination. You will act as a key link between business stakeholders and development teams, translating business and user needs into clear requirements, priorities and actionable user stories.

You will own and continuously refine the product backlog, align the product roadmap with business objectives and technical direction, and help the team deliver scalable, maintainable solutions that create measurable value.

What you’ll do

  • Own and communicate the product vision, strategy and roadmap, ensuring alignment with business objectives and customer needs.
  • Act as a primary point of contact between business stakeholders and the development team.
  • Gather and translate business and user requirements into clear epics, features, user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Own, manage and prioritise the product backlog based on business value, user impact, delivery effort, risk and technical dependencies.
  • Prioritise the product roadmap and backlog using SAFe Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) principles.
  • Ensure development teams have clear priorities and well-defined requirements.
  • Coordinate development activities and dependencies across multiple technical disciplines.
  • Validate delivered features against agreed requirements and acceptance criteria and provide final business approval.
  • Monitor product performance, adoption and user feedback to identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Coordinate product releases and operational readiness, including stakeholder communication, training and support arrangements.
  • Ensure product development aligns with relevant security, privacy, architecture and governance requirements.
  • Monitor product ROI against agreed values.
  • Lead, coach and develop up to 10 direct reports, supporting both individual and collective performance.
  • Conduct periodic reviews of workplace health and safety conditions and ensure relevant policies, procedures and controls are implemented in line with applicable requirements.

Who you are

  • You have at least five years of experience as a Product Owner, Business Analyst or in a comparable digital product role.
  • You have a strong understanding of Agile product development, particularly Scrum and iterative delivery, with experience using tools such as Azure DevOps or Jira.
  • You can understand complex business processes and translate them into clear, actionable requirements for technical teams.
  • You have experience managing digital products or enterprise web and mobile applications throughout their lifecycle.
  • You understand software development processes, system integrations, APIs, data flows and non-functional requirements.
  • You have strong analytical, problem-solving and decision-making capabilities and can balance business priorities with technical considerations.
  • You are confident in stakeholder management, negotiation and communication across business and technology functions.
  • You can create alignment and make informed decisions within complex, cross-functional environments.
  • You take a customer-focused approach and can use feedback, usage data and performance indicators to guide product evolution.
  • You can work autonomously, manage multiple priorities and adapt effectively to a fast-changing environment.
  • You hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related technical field, or have equivalent professional experience.
  • You are fluent in written and spoken English at C1 level.

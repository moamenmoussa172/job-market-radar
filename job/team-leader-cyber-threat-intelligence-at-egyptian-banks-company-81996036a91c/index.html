Team Leader, Cyber Threat Intelligence

Egyptian Banks Company · New Cairo City, Egypt

Onsite · Senior · SOC / Security Analyst · Posted 25 November 2026

SIEM / SOC

Apply on Egyptian Banks Company's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Collect and analyze threat data from various sources (open-source, dark web, closed sources).
  • Monitor cybersecurity threat feeds, forums, social media, and other platforms for emerging threats.
  • Analyze malware, tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) used by attackers to inform security defenses.
  • Support incident response teams with intelligence on threat actor techniques.
  • Document threat intelligence processes and keep intelligence databases updated.
  • Prepare detailed reports on emerging threats and current vulnerabilities.
  • Develop and distribute situational activity reports on existing and newly discovered vulnerability ties within and without the organization, as applicable.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or Information Systems.
  • Proven experience in Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) tools and platforms.
  • Experience in malware analysis, reverse engineering, and incident response processes.
  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or Information Systems.
  • CTIA certification is preferred; CISSP is a plus.

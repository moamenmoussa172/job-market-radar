Senior Product Manager, Application Platform

HelloFresh · Warszawa, Poland

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 24 November 2026

Apply on HelloFresh's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

About the role: What's in the Box

Foundations Alliance's mission is to accelerate the delivery of high-quality, scalable, and highly available digital products at HelloFresh. We do this by providing services and platforms that improve our internal builders' productivity and the reliability of our applications and our data.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the vision, strategy, and execution for our Application Platform — the runtime that every HelloFresh service is deployed to and runs on. You'll own a high-leverage layer of our engineering ecosystem: one standard way to deploy, a platform that decides where workloads run and how traffic reaches them, and the guarantees around resilience, security and cost that product teams shouldn't have to think about. You'll define the roadmap for your squad, partner closely with the Engineering Lead for Application Platform, and work with the product teams who deploy on the platform and with neighbouring core platform squads.

You join during an active phase of migrating services onto our modernised application platform. You'll drive adoption across engineering teams while advancing an architecture built for high resilience and performance for our global customer base.

What you’ll do: The Recipe

  • Own the product vision and roadmap for the Application Platform — the standard deployment path, platform-owned traffic routing, and the runtime services behind them — partnering closely with the Engineering Lead for Application Platform.
  • Drive adoption of the standard deployment path across our service landscape and measure progress by services actually running on the platform, not capabilities delivered.
  • Shape and scale automated, agentic migration and work with engineering leadership to establish the review-and-merge expectations that make it succeed.
  • Own the resilience of the platform: setting failover and availability targets, partnering with engineers on the architecture for highly available runtime, and feeding incident learnings back into the roadmap.
  • Drive the platform's cost efficiency as a core constraint on every roadmap decision.
  • Be the primary interface between the Application Platform and the product teams that deploy on it, translating their needs into roadmap priorities and negotiating clear ownership boundaries.
  • Define, track, and report your squad's KPIs against the defined goals.

What you’ll bring: The Ingredients

  • Experience as a Product Manager working on infrastructure, cloud, developer platforms or backend systems, comfortable with concepts like containers, orchestration, deployment pipelines and service networking.
  • Working familiarity with a modern cloud runtime stack — enough to partner credibly with engineers on technical trade-offs around reliability, security, cost and “thinnest useful platform”.
  • Advanced systems-thinking mindset, with deep focus on managing dependencies, anticipating failure modes, and minimizing blast radius.
  • A track record of owning the roadmap and KPIs for complex B2B or internal platforms where engineers are your customers, defining metrics such as adoption, reliability, latency or cost and driving them to target.
  • Comfort using data to inform prioritisation and roadmap decisions, and building clear KPI trees that connect platform work to outcomes.
  • Strong collaboration skills — you build trust and alignment with engineering leads and stakeholders across teams, backed by clear written and verbal communication, and you can drive change across teams that don't report to you.
  • A pragmatic, execution-oriented approach — you drive day-to-day delivery with minimal guidance, and you're comfortable navigating reliability, cost and tech-debt trade-offs in a live production environment.
  • Experience leading a larger realignment or multi-team migration, and comfortable building relationships up to senior-director level.

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at global scale, supporting 6+ millions of customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to workspace at Warsaw centre. The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

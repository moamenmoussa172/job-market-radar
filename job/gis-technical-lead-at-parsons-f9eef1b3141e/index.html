Job Description:

GIS Technical Lead

Dubai, UAE

Parsons is seeking an experienced GIS Technical Lead to lead the technical design and delivery of enterprise geospatial solutions for complex smart infrastructure and public-sector programmes in the Middle East.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Lead the technical design and implementation of enterprise GIS solutions, ensuring alignment with the overall solution architecture and project requirements.
  • Define detailed GIS solution designs covering enterprise geodatabases, spatial data models, GIS services, web maps, web scenes, dashboards, mobile/field applications, and spatial analytics.
  • Oversee implementation using the Esri ArcGIS ecosystem, including ArcGIS Enterprise, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Pro, Experience Builder, Dashboards, Field Maps, and related components as appropriate.
  • Design and govern spatial data models for assets, networks, environmental/landscape information, operational devices, imagery, analytical outputs, and historical records.
  • Dierct configuration and development of 2D and 3D GIS experiences, including asset visualization, network representation, terrain/elevation, imagery, interactive filtering, pop-ups, dashboards, and operational views.
  • Define GIS approaches for imagery and remote sensing, including satellite/aerial imagery, LiDAR where applicable, change detection, vegetation/condition analysis, and integration of derived analytical products.
  • Work with the Integration Lead to expose and consume GIS information through feature services, map services, ArcGIS REST APIs, OGC services, and other enterprise interfaces.
  • Support integration of IoT, telemetry, SCADA/OT, EAM/CMMS, workflow, and enterprise data into geospatial applications while maintaining appropriate system-of-record boundaries.
  • Enable GIS consumption and visualization of AI/ML and advanced analytics outputs, including classification, anomaly detection, computer-vision results, predictions, recommendations, and spatial decision-support information.
  • Define GIS rules for symbology, filtering, clustering, scale-dependent visibility, labeling, pop-ups, layer organization, temporal visualization, and 3D representation to ensure clear and performant user experiences.
  • Establish technical standards for coordinate systems, topology, spatial accuracy, geometry validation, metadata, data quality, versioning, and geospatial interoperability.
  • Lead GIS development/configuration activities and provide technical direction to GIS developers and analysts, including technical reviews and quality assurance.
  • Define GIS performance and scalability requirements covering service configuration, caching, spatial queries, large datasets, imagery, concurrency, and application responsiveness.
  • Support environment configuration and deployment across DEV, SIT/TEST, UAT, PROD, and DR, including publishing, service configuration, security, migration, and release management.
  • Work with infrastructure and cybersecurity teams to implement appropriate GIS authentication, authorization, SSO, role-based access, service accounts, network security, and secure sharing models.
  • Manage technical troubleshooting across GIS applications, databases, services, APIs, browsers, mobile applications, and infrastructure.
  • Support SIT, UAT, performance testing, production readiness, deployment, go-live, and post-deployment stabilization.
  • Maintain detailed solution documentation, configuration records, deployment procedures, GIS standards, administrator guides, and technical handover material.
  • Support training and knowledge transfer to technical administrators, GIS users, analysts, and operational teams.

What Required Skills You’ll Bring

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in GIS, Geomatics, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related discipline from an accredited university.
  • Over 10+ years of GIS / geospatial technology experience, including substantial experience with enterprise GIS design and implementation.
  • Extensive hands-on experience with the Esri ArcGIS platform including web applications and the broader Esri product portfolio including Velocity.
  • Proven experience designing and implementing enterprise geodatabases and complex spatial data models.
  • In-depth knowledge of PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Oracle Spatial, SQL Server spatial, or comparable enterprise spatial database technologies.
  • Strong understanding of GIS services including FeatureServer, MapServer, imagery services, REST APIs, and common geospatial interoperability patterns.
  • Proficiency in delivering web GIS and mobile GIS applications, preferably using Experience Builder, Dashboards, Field Maps, Survey123, or comparable Esri technologies.
  • Thorough understanding of 2D and 3D geospatial visualization, including web scenes, elevation, 3D symbology, terrain, underground/subsurface assets, and interactive mapping.
  • Experience working with satellite/aerial imagery, raster data, remote sensing, and spatial analytical workflows.
  • Good understanding of geospatial data quality, topology, coordinate reference systems, metadata, geometric relationships, and spatial analysis.
  • Experience integrating GIS with enterprise applications, asset-management systems, IoT/telemetry, operational systems, and external data services.
  • Working knowledge of consuming and presenting AI/ML and analytical outputs within GIS applications and spatial workflows.
  • Expertise with GIS automation and scripting using Python/ArcPy, ArcGIS API for Python, or comparable geospatial automation tools.
  • Exposure with GIS performance optimization, troubleshooting, service management, security, and production operations.
  • Proven experience leading GIS delivery through design, development, integration testing, UAT, deployment, and go-live.
  • Ability to provide technical leadership, review implementation work, resolve complex issues, and coordinate effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
  • Strong technical documentation and stakeholder communication skills.
  • Fluency in spoken and written English.

What Desired Skills You’ll Bring

  • Experience delivering GIS solutions for smart cities, smart infrastructure, utilities, landscape/environmental management, irrigation, municipalities, or asset-intensive organisations.
  • Advanced experience with ArcGIS Enterprise architecture, including Portal for ArcGIS, ArcGIS Server, enterprise geodatabases, hosting servers, federated services, and distributed deployments.
  • Background developing or extending Esri applications using the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript, ArcGIS REST APIs, or related development frameworks.
  • Expertise with ArcGIS Image Server, imagery analysis, GeoAI, deep-learning workflows, or remote-sensing toolchains.
  • Knowledge of 3D GIS, digital twins, GIS integration, scene layers, LiDAR/point clouds, and subsurface utility visualization.
  • Proficiency integrating GIS with EAM/CMMS, SCADA, IoT, telemetry, and time-series platforms.
  • Track record in spatial/network modelling for utility, irrigation, transportation, landscape, or environmental assets.
  • Familiarity with OGC standards, ISO 19115 metadata, GeoJSON, GeoPackage, WMS/WFS/WMTS, and other geospatial interoperability standards.
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure, AWS, or comparable cloud environments supporting enterprise GIS deployments.
  • Familiarity with DevOps, source control, CI/CD, automated testing, containerization, and infrastructure-as-code practices.
  • Relevant Esri technical certifications or cloud certifications are advantageous.

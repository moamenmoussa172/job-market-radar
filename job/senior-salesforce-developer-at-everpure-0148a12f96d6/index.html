About the job

Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Drive the strategic evolution of Everpure’s enterprise quote-to-cash ecosystem as a Senior Salesforce Developer. In this role, you will architect, build, and optimize complex Salesforce and Zuora Billing/CPQ solutions that accelerate our revenue operations and eliminate business bottlenecks. You will solve variable technical challenges, design robust APIs, and partner directly with cross-functional leaders in Finance, Sales Operations, and IT to deliver seamless subscription and billing processes.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Architect Scalable Quote-to-Cash Workflows: Design, code, and deploy custom Apex, Lightning Web Components, and automated flows on the Everpure Platform to optimize quote creation, product rate plans, subscriptions, and billing renewals.
  • Drive Strategic Enterprise Integrations: Build and maintain high-performance REST/SOAP API integrations and AppExchange connections between Salesforce, Zuora, and enterprise systems to ensure fault-tolerant data synchronization and security.
  • Optimize Operational Efficiency & System Health: Identify process bottlenecks, perform root-cause debugging, monitor performance metrics, and participate in production support to ensure high availability and seamless data integrity across ecosystem solutions.
  • Lead Modern Engineering Practices: Establish best practices for change management, GitHub version control, deployment pipelines, and the utilization of AI-assisted coding tools to elevate engineering quality across the team.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Advanced Technical Mastery: Extensive expertise in Salesforce architecture and programmatic development (Apex, LWC, Visualforce, Web Services/oAuth) paired with deep hands-on experience configuring Zuora Billing, Zuora Quotes/CPQ, and enterprise quote-to-cash business logic.
  • Complex Problem-Solving & Innovation: Ability to evaluate complex architectural requirements, select effective declarative or programmatic methods, and translate strategic business goals into secure, scalable technical designs.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership & Collaboration: Strong analytical, communication, and consultative skills with a track record of partnering with cross-functional stakeholders, driving consensus, and guiding technical execution.
  • Modern Development Tooling Proficiency: Direct experience utilizing version control systems (such as GitHub), CI/CD release management practices, and modern AI-assisted coding tools to deliver quality software efficiently.
  • Location Expectation: This role is based on-site at our Santa Clara office in accordance with Everpure’s in-office policy, with no remote or relocation options available. As such, we are currently considering applications exclusively from local candidates residing within commuting distance of the Bay Area.

About the job

P-1343

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

The Runtime Observability team builds and maintains the telemetry, data pipelines, and debugging tools that provide insight into the behavior of the Databricks Spark Engine. The Spark Engine runs millions of workloads scheduled every day by Databricks customers and is a highly critical component of the Databricks product. The team's mission is to provide reliable, fresh, product-centric runtime insights that enable faster detection of regressions, easier troubleshooting of failures and performance issues, and data-driven product decisions.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Design and implement critical backend infrastructure and data pipelines that provide insight into the performance and stability of the Runtime Engine at very large scale.
  • Build telemetry and debugging infrastructure that enables Runtime Engine teams to understand system behavior and troubleshoot issues effectively.
  • Optimize ingestion of very large datasets to deliver metrics and insights as quickly as possible, enabling early detection of issues and faster debugging.
  • Take ownership of complex technical projects from design through delivery, ensuring high quality and reliability.
  • Collaborate with Runtime Engine teams to understand their use cases and translate requirements into scalable observability solutions.

What We Look For

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience.
  • Experience designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems in production environments.
  • Experience with one or more programming languages including Scala, Rust, Java, or Go.
  • Strong understanding of data pipelines, telemetry systems, and observability infrastructure.
  • Ability to independently own complex technical domains and drive projects from conception to production.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and ability to diagnose performance bottlenecks in distributed systems.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively across teams.
  • B.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

About the job

Shape the future of data-driven reporting and decision-making

We are looking for a Power BI Reporting Developer & Consultant who combines strong technical expertise with a business-oriented mindset.

In this role, you will design and develop professional reporting solutions while working closely with business stakeholders to translate their requirements into scalable, intuitive, and value-adding analytics products.

You will act as the bridge between business requirements, data, and technology, helping us further develop our reporting landscape and enable efficient, data-driven decision-making.

Support the transition from manual or Excel-based reporting toward automated and standardized Power BI solutions.

Identify opportunities to further improve the reporting landscape through automation, standardization, and self-service analytics

Your responsiblities:

•Develop and enhance Power BI reports and dashboards for management and operational reporting.

•Translate business requirements into clear reporting concepts, KPIs, data models, and visualizations.

•Consult business stakeholders on the optimal use of Power BI and modern reporting solutions.

•Design efficient data models, measures, calculations, and reporting structures using Power Query and DAX. Connect and integrate data from MS SQL Server, APIs, Power Bi Semantic models and other data sources.

•Explore and adopt AI technologies to enhance dashboard development and analytics.

•Connect and integrate relevant data sources and ensure reliable data flows into reporting solutions.

•Adopt creative UX/UI and visualization designs tailored to different dashboard requirements.

•Improve existing reports regarding performance, usability, standardization, and maintainability.

•Work closely with stakeholders (within + outside of finance community) to establish consistent reporting and KPI definitions.

•Create documentation and support knowledge sharing and reporting best practices.

Educational qualification:

Degree in Computer Science or business administration or a comparable qualification

Experience :

At least 8-10 years experience in Power bi dashboard development, preferably in Finance & Controlling or management reporting area. Experience working in international and cross-functional environments.

Mandatory/requires Skills :

  • Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service,,
  • -Power Query
  • -DAX
  • -Microsoft Fabric
  • -Data modeling
  • -And other related platforms
  • -Hands-on experience data visualization and dashboard design
  • Good understanding of relational data structures and SQL.
  • Working style & Personality: Ability to understand business processes and translate business needs into technical solutions. Structured, independent, and customer-oriented way of working. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Language & Communication: Confidentcommunicationwithbothbusiness and technicalstakeholders. Very good English communicationskills

Shape the future of energy performance and sustainability

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as an Energy Analyst at our Devonport Royal Dockyard site.

The role

As an Energy Analyst, you’ll be at the forefront of driving Devonport’s journey to net zero. This is an exciting opportunity to combine data analysis, energy management and sustainability expertise to deliver meaningful environmental improvements across one of the UK's most complex operational estates.

Day-to-day, you’ll use data, technology and innovation to unlock energy savings, optimise building performance and support sustainability projects that have a real impact on both business operations and the environment. Your work will directly contribute to reducing carbon emissions, improving efficiency and helping shape a more sustainable future.

  • Analyse energy consumption and identify efficiency and cost-saving opportunities across the estate.
  • Monitor and optimise Building Management Systems (BMS), utility usage, and metering performance.
  • Support the delivery of energy, sustainability, carbon reduction, and net-zero initiatives.
  • Produce reports, dashboards, and maintain energy performance data to support compliance and decision-making.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders, maintenance teams, and contractors to drive energy awareness, operational improvements, and corrective actions.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements.

Essential experience of the Energy Analyst

  • Experience working with energy, utilities, facilities management, project management, building services, or asset management data.
  • Experience in, and willingness to develop in the following key areas: Energy monitoring and Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS); supporting energy efficiency, sustainability and carbon reduction; Energy legislation and reporting requirements.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving skills and experience analysing large data sets and producing meaningful performance reports.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, inc. Excel, including data analysis, pivot tables, and reporting functions
  • Ability to communicate technical information to a range of stakeholders.

Qualifications for the Energy Analyst

  • HNC/HND, Degree, or equivalent experience in Energy Management, Building Services, Engineering, Environmental Management, Data Analysis, Facilities Management, or a related discipline.
  • Membership of, or working towards membership of, a relevant professional body (e.g. Energy Institute (EI), Institute of Engineering and Technology (IET), Chartered Institution of Building Services Engineers (CIBSE).

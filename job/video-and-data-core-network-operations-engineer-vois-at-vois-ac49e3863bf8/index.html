About this Role

We are seeking a skilled Video & Data Core Network Operations Engineer to join our 24x7 Integrated Network Operations Centre (INOC) in Bangalore. This role is responsible for first-line monitoring, incident detection, fault analysis, troubleshooting, escalation and service restoration activities across Video, Data Core, Core Services and associated telecom network platforms. The successful candidate will play a key role in maintaining network availability, supporting service continuity and ensuring operational excellence through effective alarm monitoring, incident management and stakeholder coordination in a fast-paced telecom operations environment.

What you will do

  • Monitor network alarms, events and service-impacting incidents across Video and Data Core platforms, ensuring timely detection in line with operational procedures and service level commitments.
  • Detect, validate, register, update and track incidents accurately within approved ticketing systems.
  • Perform first-level fault analysis, diagnosis and remote troubleshooting to support rapid service restoration and first-time resolution where possible.
  • Correlate alarms and events to determine service impact, incident priority and the appropriate escalation path.
  • Manage the incident lifecycle from detection through to closure, ensuring timely communication, documentation and ownership tracking.
  • Escalate critical or unresolved incidents to resolver groups, vendors, suppliers or field support teams in accordance with established escalation procedures.
  • Coordinate with internal teams, external partners and support functions to support investigation, troubleshooting and restoration activities.
  • Monitor and support Voice, Data Core, Charging, SMS and Core Services platforms across 2G, 3G, LTE, 5G and 5G Standalone network environments.
  • Support technologies including SMSC, Voicemail, SGSN, MME, S/PGW, ePDG, PCRF, AMF, SMF, PCF, NRF, UDM, AUSF, EIR, HLR/HSS, STP, MSC, MGW, CSCF, MTAS and USSD.
  • Participate in planned maintenance activities, alarm testing, health checks and post-change monitoring.
  • Maintain accurate shift reports, operational updates and handover documentation covering critical incidents and pending actions.
  • Ensure compliance with operational procedures, security requirements and business continuity standards.
  • Support network inventory verification activities and raise discrepancies where identified.
  • Work in rotational shifts, including weekends, public holidays and night shifts, to support continuous network operations.

Who you are

  • Bachelor's degree in Telecommunications, Electronics, Computer Science, Information Technology or a related technical discipline.
  • BE/B.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunications preferred.
  • 4-7 years of experience within telecommunications operations, network operations centres or service operations environments.
  • Good understanding of GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5G and Core Network architectures.
  • Knowledge of SMS, Voice, Charging, Data Core and Core Services technologies and platforms.
  • Experience in alarm monitoring, event correlation, incident handling and first-level troubleshooting.
  • Understanding of ITIL Incident Management practices, including incident logging, prioritisation, escalation, communication and closure.
  • Ability to assess service impact and make informed decisions in time-sensitive operational situations.
  • Effective communication skills with the ability to engage technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Ability to collaborate with resolver teams, suppliers, vendors and operational stakeholders.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accurate incident documentation.
  • Good written and spoken English communication skills.
  • Ability to perform effectively in a shift-based operational environment.

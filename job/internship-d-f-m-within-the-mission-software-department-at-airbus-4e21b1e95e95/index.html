About the job

Job Description:

In order to support the Mission Functions department, Airbus Helicopters is looking for an

Internship (d/f/m) within the mission software department

Are you looking for an internship and want to get to know the work of an Embedded Software Engineer (d/f/m)? Then apply now! We look forward to you supporting us in the Mission Functions department as an intern (d/f/m)!

  • Location: Donauwörth
  • Start: February 2027
  • Duration: 6 months

Your location

At our Donauwörth site, located on the banks of the river Danube, we develop helicopters from the initial idea to the airworthy product. Watch them take off and touch down on our own on-site test airfield.

Your benefits

  • Attractive salary and work-life balance with a 35-hour week (flexitime).
  • International environment with the opportunity to network globally.
  • A final thesis is possible after consultation with the department.
  • Traveling within Europe (team events) is possible after consultation and agreement from the department.
  • Work with modern/diversified technologies
  • At Airbus, we see you as a valuable team member and in close contact with the interfaces and are part of our weekly team meetings
  • Opportunity to participate in the Generation Airbus Community to expand your own network.

Your tasks and responsibilities

  • Collaborate with the transversal team to design, code software in programming languages like C, ADA, etc.
  • Work on embedded systems to integrate and verify software with hardware components.
  • Debug and troubleshoot existing software to improve reliability.
  • Assist in requirements analysis for implementing them in the software.
  • Participate in testing and validation of the embedded software.
  • Collaborate with team members to align software solutions.
  • Contribute to the team’s innovation by suggesting improvements or new ideas for the software development process.
  • Write and maintain Python scripts for DevOps tasks, for continuous integration and improve the processes.

Desired skills and qualifications

  • Enrolled full-time student (d/f/m) in the area of IT, computer Science or an equivalent field of study.
  • Knowledge in programming languages (Python, C, C++, .).
  • Basic experience in agile software development methods would be a plus.
  • Ability to work in teams and solve complex technical problems.
  • Ability to manage time effectively, meet deadlines, and work on multiple tasks simultaneously, especially in a dynamic environment.
  • Fluent in English.

Please upload the following documents: cover letter, CV, relevant transcripts, enrollment certificate.

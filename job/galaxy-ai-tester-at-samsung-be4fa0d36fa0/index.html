About the job

Position Summary

We are seeking junior members to join our Mobile Quality Assurance team at Samsung for a 4-month project starting from December. The primary focus of this team is to ensure the high quality of mobile products across European markets. We are actively looking for language testers with a technical flair to strengthen our language verification team. This role offers a pivotal and exciting opportunity to contribute to a one-of-a-kind AI initiative in Hungary, focusing on Samsung Galaxy AI solutions across Samsung devices (smartphones, tablets, wearables, etc.).

Role and Responsibilities

Roles and Responsibilities

Key objectives

The Galaxy AI Tester will serve as a language tester, contributing to the enhancement of our language verification team, specifically focusing on Samsung Galaxy AI solutions integrated into Samsung devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearables.

Main responsibilities

  • Verify and evaluate performance of Galaxy AI functions.
  • Prepare language datasets for automatic translation.
  • Generate and validate test cases to check new product releases.
  • Register defects and create reports.
  • Discuss with other test members and R&D to ensure that proper solutions are implemented and checked, considering potential impact on the market.
  • Run automated AI tests.
  • Propose new solutions in language testing area.
  • Actively participate in project meetings.

Skills and Qualifications

Requirements

  • Excellent Hungarian and English knowledge (written and verbal)
  • University degree in IT area or equivalent knowledge/experience
  • Fluent at operating MS Excel and main GenAI tools
  • Genuine technical aptitude, interest in technology
  • Quality-focus and attention to details
  • Great communication skills, positive attitude and team-work approach

Nice to have skills

  • Experience in Testing/Quality Assurance process
  • Language translations experience
  • Familiarity with Android system and Python scripting

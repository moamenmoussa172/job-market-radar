Deputy Manager_eAxle System engineer_Thermal system

Bosch Group · Bengaluru, India

Onsite · Cloud / Infrastructure Engineer · Posted 23 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

**Responsibilities:**

  • Responsible for eAxle thermal-system concept, modeling, measurement, correlation and release to ensure component temperatures and continuous performance meet customer operating conditions
  • Assess customer ambient, coolant and duty-cycle requirements and translate them into thermal design specifications
  • Define and optimize eAxle thermal architecture and interfaces across multiple vehicle platforms and operating scenarios
  • Develop and maintain thermal models using GT-SUITE, Ansys, and MATLAB, ensuring accuracy and traceability
  • Plan, execute and analyze thermal characterization tests, including bench testing and vehicle-level validation
  • Correlate models with experimental data and release thermal predictions for design and manufacturing teams
  • Define component temperature limits and derating recommendations based on material properties and reliability requirements
  • Support design reviews, risk assessments and customer discussions to address thermal performance concerns
  • Lead and mentor junior engineers and technical staff on thermal analysis methodologies and best practices
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including electrical, mechanical, and controls engineering to integrate thermal solutions
  • Manage supplier and vendor relationships for thermal components, ensuring compliance with specifications and timelines
  • Monitor thermal system performance in production vehicles and implement continuous improvement initiatives
  • Prepare technical documentation, reports, and presentations for internal stakeholders and customer communications
  • Manage thermal engineering budgets and resource allocation to support project timelines and deliverables

**Qualifications:**

  • Master's or Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Thermal, Automotive, or Mechatronics Engineering
  • More than 8 years of experience in thermal system development, preferably in electric drive, inverter, motor, or transmission cooling applications
  • Proficiency with thermal modeling software including GT-SUITE, ANSYS, and MATLAB
  • Demonstrated experience with thermal characterization testing, bench testing, and vehicle-level validation
  • Strong knowledge of electric vehicle thermal management systems and eAxle architecture
  • Experience defining component temperature limits and derating strategies based on material properties and reliability requirements

**Preferred:**

  • Advanced degree (Master's or PhD) in Thermal Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or related field

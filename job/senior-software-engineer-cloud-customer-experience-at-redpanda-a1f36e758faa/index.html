About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Redpanda is the first runtime and control plane for agent-data interaction — a unified platform that combines streaming, SQL analytics, and intelligent connectivity with the governance layer enterprise AI agents need in production.

Built on infrastructure already trusted by Fortune 500 companies and fast-growing startups, Redpanda enforces governance entirely outside the agent's data path, controlling what agents see, limiting what they do, and capturing a tamper-proof record of everything they touch, so organizations never have to choose between innovation and control.

About the Role:

We're looking for an experienced senior software engineer to drive Redpanda Cloud's billing and payments services and workflows end-to-end, as part of a new Warsaw-based team. You'll take ownership of usage-based billing via Metronome, and credit-card management via Stripe, coming up to speed with our current implementation, stabilising it, and then driving the roadmap to keep metering, invoicing, and payments accurate, transparent, and trustworthy as we expand our products and services with the Agentic Data Plane. You'll partner closely with finance, product, and the wider engineering team across the wider customer-experience journey.

This is a hybrid role based in our central Warsaw office, with a minimum of 3 days a week on-site.

The Team

You'll join a newly-formed small, cross-functional Cloud team based in our new Warsaw office that owns the services powering the customer-facing journeys at the heart of Redpanda Cloud. This is how every customer registers, manages their account, and pays for what they use. The team owns these critical workstreams:

  • Register / Sign-up / Sign-in
  • Account management
  • Usage-based billing (UBB) via Metronome
  • Credit-card management for self-service customers via Stripe
  • Cloud marketplace integrations (AWS, GCP, and Azure with more to come) for UBB, public offers, and private offers

The team is adopting ownership of these areas from the teams that built them, will come up to speed on what we have today, and then drive a product and technical roadmap that gives our customers confidence whenever they interact with our services. It's cross-functional and works closely with design, product, and finance. We hire for the skill mix across the team, not every skill in every person.

Everybody at Redpanda is reinventing how they work using the latest AI tooling and workflows.

You Will:

  • Own the technical implementation of UBB (using Metronome) and payments (using Stripe) end-to-end, design, building, operating, and evolving the architecture and services
  • Build and operate correctness-critical metering, billing, and payment workflows with strong idempotency, reconciliation, and auditability
  • Adopt the existing metering, billing, and payments systems from other teams, learn them deeply, and drive them forward to meet future requirements
  • Turn product and commercial requirements into clear technical designs and shipped workstreams
  • Partner with finance and customer success teams on invoicing accuracy, reconciliation, and revenue correctness, and with product and engineering teams on the technical roadmap
  • Uphold high standards for security and privacy of PII
  • Participate in on-call once you’ve ramped up

You Have:

  • Solid software engineering fundamentals and a track record of owning development of features and components and delivering them reliably
  • Experience developing, operating, and supporting large scale distributed systems
  • Working backend proficiency in Go (or a comparable systems language and are willing to ramp up) and a foundation in public cloud infrastructure (at least one of AWS/Azure/GCP)
  • Frontend familiarity - while not a specialist, you are comfortable maintaining and adapting customer-facing web UIs (React / TypeScript or similar) with support from UX designers and the latest AI tools
  • Production experience using AI-assisted software development workflows (e.g. Claude Code, Codex, etc) to accelerate prototyping, delivery, and operations
  • Good written and verbal communication in English and can work effectively in a globally distributed, async environment, collaborating with product management, design, finance, and customer-facing stakeholders

Strong Plus:

  • Cloud marketplace integration experience (AWS, GCP, and/or Azure)
  • Frontend depth (React / TypeScript) for customer-facing UIs
  • Experience developing or owning signup and login flows and identity provider integration
  • Exposure to billing and payment services like Metronome and Stripe

Nice to Have:

  • Experience building or operating large-scale production SaaS platforms
  • Experience with streaming platforms as a user or provider

This is a B2B contract role in Warsaw. The rate is 387,000–460,000 PLN per year (net + VAT). Your final rate will depend on your skills, experience, and interview performance. Your recruiter can share more details about the full package.

Join Redpanda if you’d enjoy being part of a fast-moving, diverse, people-first organization with team members around the globe and a culture based on trust, transparency, communication, and kindness. You'll dive into a nimble, high-impact team with the latest AI tools — and the budget to actually use them.

About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

As an intern on the IT team, you will provide advanced end-user technical support as part of Site Support and IT Operations. Serve as a technical escalation resource for complex endpoint, application, authentication, and connectivity issues. Coordinate with specialist teams and vendors to restore service, communicate clearly with users, and document repeatable solutions that reduce recurring incidents.

Role and Responsibilities

  • Own complex incidents escalated from front-line support, including diagnosis, resolution, user communication, and complete ticket documentation.
  • Troubleshoot Windows and macOS endpoints, enterprise applications, peripherals, VPN, Wi-Fi, and network connectivity issues.
  • Resolve access and authentication issues involving Okta, Active Directory, and supported corporate accounts; coordinate authorization changes with the appropriate teams.
  • Install, configure, and troubleshoot approved software; support endpoint updates and security patches through established processes.
  • Perform advanced hardware diagnostics and coordinate repairs, replacements, or vendor support within approved scope.
  • Identify recurring issues, document root causes and workarounds, and partner with endpoint engineering, networking, application, and security teams on durable fixes.
  • Create and maintain knowledge articles, troubleshooting procedures, and escalation documentation; share technical guidance with front-line support staff.
  • Prioritize support work by business impact and urgency, maintaining clear handoffs and timely status updates.

Must Have Skills

  • Advanced endpoint, application, and hardware troubleshooting
  • Windows and macOS support
  • Microsoft 365
  • Okta and Active Directory account troubleshooting
  • VPN, Wi-Fi, DNS, DHCP, and TCP/IP fundamentals
  • Ticketing-system proficiency
  • Clear incident documentation
  • Strong customer service, prioritization, and communication skills. Demonstrated ability to resolve complex escalations and collaborate across support teams.

Nice to Have Skills

  • Serval experience
  • Microsoft Intune, SCCM, or Jamf familiarity
  • PowerShell or other administrative scripting
  • Linux troubleshooting
  • Mobile-device support
  • Knowledge-base development
  • Experience supporting manufacturing or laboratory environments

Education

  • Field(s) of study: Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related technical field; equivalent practical experience considered.
  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree program at an accredited university
  • Proof of enrollment in current or into next program (MS, PhD)

Program Information:

  • Timing: May- August, or June-September pending class schedules.
  • Duration of Internship: Our internship program is 12 weeks; the end date is flexible based on individual needs.
  • Work Location & Environment: This position will be in Casa Grande, AZ, and is fully in-person. You must be prepared to start onsite for your first day of employment.
  • Compensation: Student compensation is based on location and current degree level, non-negotiable ranging from $34 to $48 (subject to changes).
  • Relocation: May be provided on a case-to-case basis.
  • Housing/Transportation: You must provide your own living arrangements and transportation in the local area.
  • Background Check: All offers of employment are contingent on clearing a background check.
  • Drug Screening: All offers of employment are contingent on passing a drug screen.
  • Medical benefits: Dental, vision, and medical on day 1 for all interns working full-time.
  • Visa Sponsorship: Lucid may but is not obligated to sponsor foreign national employees requiring company sponsorship for U.S employment authorization.

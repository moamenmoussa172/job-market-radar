About this role

We are seeking an experienced Senior UX/UI Product Designer to join our Digital Sales & Marketing organisation in Budapest, with a strong product focus on the Travel eSIM digital experience.

You will support the evolution of the Travel eSIM app and website, helping shape a more comprehensive digital travel experience that extends beyond eSIM purchasing. Combining senior UX/UI expertise with a product-focused mindset, you will contribute to product evolution, challenge design decisions where appropriate and help create a cohesive experience across app and web.

The role operates in an international environment and may require business travel within the EU, up to 5% of working time. You will also collaborate with cross-functional teams across different locations.

What you’ll do

  • App Experience & Product Evolution: Support the redesign and evolution of the Travel eSIM app, helping shape a broader digital travel experience that goes beyond the existing eSIM purchasing journey.
  • Experience Redesign: Support the restructuring and redesign of the existing Travel eSIM website, creating a more cohesive, scalable and user-friendly experience rather than expanding the existing structure page by page.
  • Design Collaboration & Challenge: Provide senior design input and guidance throughout the product development process, reviewing proposed solutions, constructively challenging design decisions where appropriate and ensuring the resulting experience meets Travel eSIM product and customer requirements.
  • Design System Alignment: Support the effective adoption and application of the design system, assessing where standard components meet product requirements and where specific components or adaptations are needed.
  • Cross-Platform Design Consistency: Provide design oversight across app and web initiatives to create a consistent user experience, coherent design principles and alignment across the wider Travel eSIM digital ecosystem.
  • Translate research, product data and customer insights into user journeys, flows, wireframes, prototypes and high-quality UI designs.
  • Contribute to UX/UI research and turn findings into actionable product and design decisions.
  • Present design concepts and recommendations clearly to stakeholders and development teams, while providing constructive feedback and mentoring support to other designers.

Who you are

  • You have at least 5 years of experience in UX, UI or Product Design, ideally within a commercial product or service development environment.
  • You combine strong UX/UI expertise with a product-oriented mindset, considering not only individual screens and journeys but also how the overall digital product should evolve.
  • You can independently take design work from research and initial concepts through wireframes, user journeys and high-fidelity prototypes.
  • You have experience designing across web and mobile platforms and understand how to create consistent experiences across different digital touchpoints.
  • You are proficient in Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud and have an excellent portfolio demonstrating your UX/UI design capabilities.
  • You can develop, maintain and evolve design systems and understand when existing components should be used or adapted to meet product requirements.
  • You have a strong understanding of accessibility guidelines and responsive design principles.
  • You are comfortable constructively challenging proposed solutions and can clearly explain design decisions and product trade-offs to stakeholders.
  • You can work effectively in Agile, cross-functional and geographically distributed teams.

What are your contributions to the team?

Proposal Governance & Gatekeeping

  • Functions as the governance gatekeeper, confirming that every proposal phase adheres to defined requirements prior to approval
  • Maintain the Capture & Proposal governance framework, including workflows, templates, approval points, and quality standards.
  • Track adherence to the defined lifecycle, ensuring all documentation and validations are complete.
  • Provide early warnings to leadership when proposal teams deviate from standard processes, schedules, or requirements.
  • Prepare, consolidate, and communicate proposal campaigns status updates and progress reports for internal and external stakeholders to support effective planning, decision-making, and coordination.

Transformation Sustainment

  • Ensure transformation-related improvements to the C&P workflow remain embedded and sustainable.
  • Perform periodic audits to confirm ongoing compliance with proposal governance and identify opportunities to refine or strengthen processes.
  • Maintain up-to-date standards across all capture and proposal documentation, tools, and dashboards.
  • Coordinate with Defense and supporting functions leaders to keep the process synchronized across all stakeholders.
  • Maintain governance processes and proactively resolve governance-related issues to ensure the accuracy, reliability, and continuous operation of reporting dashboards and performance metrics.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives across the C&P team, including the development, implementation, and monitoring of key performance indicators (KPIs), reporting frameworks, and dashboard/reporting solutions.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You have a degree in business, project management, or a related field.
  • 2+ years of experience in capture management, proposal development, business development, or defense/aerospace contracting. (continuous improvement, six sigma / quality assurance / operational excellence)
  • You have a strong understanding of capture & proposal lifecycle processes, gate reviews, and compliance frameworks.
  • You are skilled at enforcing standards and acting as an unbiased gatekeeper for governance and quality criteria.
  • Proven ability to manage & coordinate cross-functional teams and ensure timely and disciplined process execution.
  • Demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities in data analytics and dashboard development, with proficiency in data manipulation and visualization tools.
  • Advanced knowledge of the Microsoft Power Platform, including Power BI, Power Apps, and Power Automate, as well as the broader Microsoft 365 suite, is required.

Mandatory

  • You are eligible for Canadian Controlled Goods Program clearance (where appropriate) and/or US International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) clearance
  • You are eligible for security clearance at SECRET or above (where appropriate) in either Canada or US
  • You are able to fluently communicate in both English and French (verbal & written) (Asset)

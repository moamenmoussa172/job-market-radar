About the job

Your new team
This position supports Alternative Investment Solutions (AIS) and reports to the AIS Tech team supporting various strategic programs and initiatives across AIS.

Teams build tactical solutions (e.g., Python, VBA) and deliver global initiatives that enhance client experience, increase operational efficiency, and meet regulatory requirements.
Who are we looking for?

We are seeking a highly skilled detailed-oriented Python developer to join ourAlternative Investment Solutions Technology team. The role involves developing, automating, and maintaining tools and processes that support critical financial workflows including reporting, reconciliation, and operational risk management. The Ideal candidate will have strong technical capabilities combined with an understanding of financial products, systems, and operational processes.

A talented and self-driven individual able to leverage strong IT experience (Python development) to manage a growing automation demand. The successful candidate will collaborate closely with global business stakeholders to design, develop, and implement high-quality automation solutions that align with business requirements while adhering to development best practices, organizational standards, and industry guidelines.

The role requires effective management of a development pipeline, including effort estimation, and delivery planning. The individual will also be responsible for performing thorough testing and quality assurance activities to ensure the reliability, scalability, and effectiveness of implemented solutions.

The ideal candidate demonstrates a proactive approach, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, highest development standards and a continuous learning mindset. A willingness to adopt emerging technologies, development standards, and best practices is essential for success in this role.

Your new role
As a Project Manager with Python, Officer you will:

  • Design, develop, and implement scalable automation solutions and applications (Python) based on business and technical requirements
  • Identify opportunities for automation, process optimization, and technology-driven improvements across multiple business functions
  • Create new tools that aim to save time, risk and improve quality Perform Project Analysis of requirements received for each development effort
  • Manage the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, solution design, development, testing, deployment, and post-production support
  • Engage the existing development resources to ensure best practices are being applied and/or promote new best practices
  • Manage/maintain oversight of the promotion and demonstration of strategic initiatives
  • Collaborate with global stakeholders, business analysts, and development teams to deliver high-quality solutions aligned with strategic objectives
  • Continually review and redefine development processes e.g. learn new methods
  • Prepare and maintain technical documentation, solution designs, and support materials in line with organizational standards
  • Provide regular status reporting to the project/program leads and management
  • Monitor project progress, communicate development status, and proactively manage technical risks, issues, and dependencies
  • Ensure timely delivery of technology solutions while maintaining high standards of quality and maintainability
  • Ensure compliance with internal control frameworks, audit standards, and data security policies
  • Conduct ad hoc reports, administration, projects and tasks as required

What we value

Qualifications and Previous Experience needed to succeed in the role:

  • A University Degree in banking/finance, technology/IT or related field
  • 3-plus year experience with proven process automation using Python, data manipulation (Pandas, NumPy), and API integration.
  • 5-plus years of experience in financial operations or a similar environment
  • Familiarity with object oriented programming
  • Further qualifications in IT related field would be an asset
  • Any other programming language knowledge would be a plus
  • Familiarity with Agile development methods would be an advantage
  • Solid understanding of financial products (equities, fixed income, derivatives) and trade lifecycle will be beneficial
  • Advanced command of English language

Preferred Skills and Competencies:

  • Analytical thinking and problem solving skills
  • Demonstrated excellence in organizational, time management skills
  • Be comfortable in giving trainings and presentations
  • Be a team player by working with other team members to deliver work within agreed deadlines
  • Communicate effectively with others and seek clarifications where required
  • Conduct research or seek help when required
  • Proactively resolve inquiries and exceptions on a case-by-case basis

Work organization requirement:

  • Hybrid work model: 3 days of office work per week

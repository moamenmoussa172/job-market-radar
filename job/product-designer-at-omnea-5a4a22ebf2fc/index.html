About the Role

We’re now looking for a Product Designer to help shape the next phase of Omnea’s product - turning complex, high-stakes enterprise workflows into experiences that feel clear, intuitive, and genuinely good to use for a diverse set of users. You’ll join at a pivotal moment, with real ownership, close customer exposure, and the chance to raise the design bar as we scale the category of AI Supplier Relationship Management.

Your day-to-day

  • You’ll spend a meaningful amount of time talking directly to customers - procurement leaders, finance teams, and security stakeholders - at companies like Spotify, MongoDB, Monzo, and Albertsons, to understand how buying decisions actually get made inside large organisations.
  • You’ll work end-to-end on product areas, from early problem definition through to high-quality shipped work - often starting from a blank page rather than iterating on tired, legacy UX.
  • You’ll collaborate closely with Product and Engineering (often in the same room). At ~80 people, there’s very little hand-off - decisions are made quickly, and design has a real seat at the table.
  • You’ll design for complex, high-stakes workflows involving multiple personas, approvals, and real organisational risk - and make them feel clear, intuitive, and usable.
  • You’ll help shape patterns and systems as we scale, contributing to a design language that’s still being actively defined, not just maintained.

You’ll love this role if…

  • You like being close to the problem - speaking to customers, pressure-testing ideas, and iterating quickly based on real feedback.
  • You’d rather design new product surfaces than rework mature features that might have existed for years.
  • You enjoy working in high-growth environments where things move fast, priorities evolve, and design plays a meaningful role in shaping what gets built.
  • You’re comfortable navigating ambiguity and helping bring clarity - whether that’s shaping a fuzzy problem, aligning stakeholders, or making a strong design call.
  • You care deeply about craft, but you’re pragmatic about shipping - you know when to push for polish, and when momentum matters more.

Why this role is a career accelerator ⭐

  • Design isn’t a service function at Omnea - we see UX as central to how we compete and win in the market.
  • You’ll work on a product that sits at the intersection of finance, legal, security, and operations, where good design has real commercial impact.
  • The problems are genuinely hard, the customers are demanding, and the bar is high — but if we get it right, the product becomes deeply embedded and hard to replace.

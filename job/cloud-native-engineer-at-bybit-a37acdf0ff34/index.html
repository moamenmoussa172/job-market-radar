About the Role
You will develop and operate the company’s container infrastructure platform, ensuring that business applications run reliably on public cloud container platforms. We are looking for someone with both container infrastructure development capabilities and hands-on experience operating Kubernetes clusters in public cloud environments. You should be able to resolve production issues and continuously improve platform reliability, while using AI tools effectively to enhance development and operational efficiency and taking ownership of delivery quality.
Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain container infrastructure capabilities for public cloud environments, including cluster management, application delivery, resource management, and operational tooling.
  • Manage the day-to-day operations of public cloud container clusters and core infrastructure components, including version upgrades, capacity planning, configuration management, troubleshooting, and reliability assurance.
  • Establish and improve monitoring, alerting, logging, incident response, and post-incident review processes for container clusters to continuously enhance platform availability and operational efficiency.
  • Collaborate with application development teams to resolve issues related to application deployment, resource utilization, security, networking, and storage.
  • Drive AI-assisted development and operations practices by building reusable tools for coding, log analysis, alert assessment, troubleshooting, and knowledge capture, and validate their effectiveness in practice.

Requirements

  • Strong knowledge of Kubernetes architecture and core components, with in-depth expertise in container technologies and underlying principles. Ability to diagnose issues involving container runtimes, images, networking, and resource isolation.
  • Proficiency in at least one backend programming language, Go or Java, with the ability to independently design, develop, and maintain production services or tools.
  • Hands-on experience operating AWS EKS, Tencent Cloud TKE, or other public cloud Kubernetes clusters. Experience deploying, upgrading, configuring, and troubleshooting core infrastructure components, resolving production incidents, and maintaining day-to-day reliability.
  • Solid knowledge of Linux, networking, and common cloud infrastructure, with strong troubleshooting and engineering collaboration skills.
  • Practical experience using AI coding tools such as Claude Code or Codex in production projects. Ability to independently evaluate generated outputs and ensure delivery quality through code reviews, testing, and security checks.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience deploying and operating AI applications or model inference services on Kubernetes, with familiarity in GPU resource management, autoscaling, monitoring and alerting, or cost optimization.
  • Proven experience applying AI to alert noise reduction, log analysis, incident knowledge bases, or automated remediation.
  • Experience operating large-scale clusters, automating operations, strengthening security, or building reliability frameworks.

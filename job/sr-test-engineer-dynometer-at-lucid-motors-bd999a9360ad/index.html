Position Overview

The Sr Test Engineer, Dynamometer owns dynamometer test operations at AMP-2 —dynameter, efficiency mapping, and performance/durability validation. Joining a greenfield facility, this engineer builds dyno infrastructure from first commissioning through volume ramp to SOP. The role is accountable for dyno test integrity, correlation, and productivity. The role operates through disciplined change control, objective launch evidence, cross-functional problem solving.

Key Responsibilities

• Dyno Operations & Test Cycles

• Own dyno stations operations

• Maintain dyno stations readiness and safety compliance

Performance & Efficiency Mapping

• Own efficiency mapping across torque/speed operating envelope

• Execute steady-state and transient performance validation

Torque, Speed & Power Measurement

• Own torque transducer standards, correction factors, and correlation

• Ensure speed and power measurement integrity

Test Cell Instrumentation

• Own instrumentation for temperature, current, voltage, and position

• Maintain calibration and traceability of test cell instruments

High-Speed Data Acquisition

• Own DAQ standards, calibration, and sampling strategies

• Ensure data integrity for validation and analytics

TPM & Autonomous Maintenance Support

• Support TPM deployment across Autonomous Maintenance, Planned Maintenance, and Focused Improvement pillars

• Contribute to equipment OEE improvement, MTBF/MTTR reduction, and predictive maintenance strategies

• Leverage MES, IIoT, and historian analytics to identify and eliminate losses

HV Safety & Compliance

• Enforce HV LOTO, HVIL continuity, and mandatory HV PPE for all work on or near EV Powertrain equipment

• Comply with SASO, Saudi MOL, IEC 60204, and functional safety standards (ISO 13849 / IEC 62061)

• Support HV emergency response including thermal runaway isolation and battery-related incident procedures

• Achieve and maintain HV safety competency appropriate to scope of work

Launch Governance & Ramp-Up

• Support AMP-2 Safe Launch activities, run-at-rate events, and capacity verification within scope

• Contribute to Launch Readiness Reviews, Layered Process Audits, and Process Confirmation routines

• Participate in QRQC / RCCA escalations during launch and ramp-up phases

Dynamometer Engineering & Test Integrity

• Commission and sustain dyno controls, inverter/battery simulators, cooling, oil conditioning, torque/speed measurement, DAQ, safety systems and facility interfaces.

• Develop automated test sequences and, where applicable, LabVIEW or equivalent softwares; manage recipes, configuration and software release.

• Correlate torque, speed, power, temperature, voltage/current and efficiency results across cells using reference units and uncertainty analysis.

• Own cell availability, preventive maintenance, instrumentation health, test throughput and safe response to HV, overspeed, thermal, fluid and rotating-equipment hazards.

Launch Governance, Change Control & Operational Readiness

• Support FAT/SAT, commissioning, equipment acceptance, Safe Launch, run-at-rate and capacity verification with objective evidence and disciplined punch-list closure.

• Control production, process, software and test changes through documented risk assessment, approvals, validation, revision history and rollback or containment planning.

• Participate in readiness reviews, Layered Process Audits, Process Confirmation and QRQC/RCCA; close assigned actions with verified effectiveness.

Safety, Quality, Data Integrity & Compliance

• Apply role-specific HV LOTO/HVIL, machine safety, stored-energy isolation, electrical safe-work and PPE requirements; never bypass safety devices or product protections.

• Protect product genealogy, calibrated-tool status, test/measurement integrity, document control and audit-ready objective evidence.

• Comply with applicable AMP-2, SASO, Saudi labor/EHS, IATF 16949, IEC 60204 and functional-safety requirements within assigned scope.

Saudization, Capability Transfer & Collaboration

• Build Saudi national capability through structured OJT, ILU/skills matrices, practical assessment, certification, cross-training and documented knowledge transfer.

• Create technical standards, troubleshooting guides and training material; support interviews and KSA university/TVTC engagement where requested.

• Collaborate across Production, Quality, Maintenance, Automation/Robotics, Manufacturing Engineering, EHS, IT/OT, MES, Supply Chain, Product Engineering and suppliers.

Skills

• Deep dynamometer test expertise

• Torque/speed/power measurement expertise

• Data-analytics capability

• Effective cross-functional communicator

• Technical standards, structured problem solving, risk-based decision making and controlled documentation

Preferred Qualifications

• Ability to support site, shift, weekend, shutdown or emergency activities when operationally required

• Preferred: recognized discipline certification, Lean/Six Sigma, greenfield EV/automotive launch and KSA workforce-development experience

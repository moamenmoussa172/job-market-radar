A Day in the Life

The NextGen Academy is a 12-month immersive program designed to launch your career in medical technology, offering hands-on experience and collaboration with diverse teams to support therapies and introduce innovative products across the region. The program is designed to develop you into a confident and capable future Market Intelligence Analyst, aligned with Medtronic's mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life.
The Eurasia Business Operations - Sales Enablement organization supports commercial teams across Region Led Markets by improving the quality, consistency and accessibility of market, performance and operational information. Through collaboration with Sales Enablement, Data & Analytics and regional stakeholders, the function helps strengthen decision-making, reporting standards, commercial effectiveness and data-driven ways of working.This role is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates and operates in a hybrid work model. The position partners with colleagues across multiple countries and functions to support market intelligence, performance management, reporting and business process improvement. Travel is expected to be minimal and based on business needs.

The Market Intelligence Analyst supports market intelligence and performance management activities across Eurasia Region Led Markets. As part of the NextGen Academy, the role provides practical experience in data analytics, reporting, process improvement and automation, contributing to business insights and operational effectiveness. Working closely with Sales Enablement, Data & Analytics and business stakeholders, the analyst helps improve data readiness, reporting consistency and commercial visibility.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Collect and document performance management requirements, objectives, key results (OKRs) and reporting needs from business stakeholders.
  • Consolidate and reconcile internal and external data sources, documenting definitions, assumptions and validation checks.
  • Maintain product mappings across platforms and datasets, identifying inconsistencies, missing matches and data-quality issues.
  • Prepare recurring performance reports, analyze variances and trends, and summarize findings for business reviews.
  • Standardize key performance indicator (KPI) definitions, reporting templates and governance practices with business and analytics teams.
  • Develop and maintain Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Power BI reports, dashboards and visualizations aligned to business requirements.
  • Build, test and support workflow automation using Microsoft Power Automate or equivalent tools to improve reporting efficiency.
  • Document process improvements, collect user feedback and measure adoption, reporting effort, data readiness and reporting accuracy.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Engineering, Information Systems, Analytics or related field and 1-3 years of professional experience, including internships, graduate programs or business placements.
  • Experience gathering business requirements, documenting processes and supporting project delivery with milestones, dependencies and risk tracking.
  • Intermediate Microsoft Excel skills including lookup functions, pivot tables, data cleansing, validation and reporting.
  • Intermediate Microsoft Power BI skills including data transformation, data modeling, measures and interactive dashboard development.
  • Experience using Microsoft Power Automate or comparable automation tools to build, test and troubleshoot workflows.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with market research, commercial analytics, product classification or multi-source data reconciliation.
  • Experience using artificial intelligence tools for information synthesis, data matching or insight generation with source validation.
  • Knowledge of SQL, product hierarchies, market share analysis or data-quality management techniques.
  • Experience simplifying recurring reporting processes or developing analytical dashboards.
  • Exposure to the healthcare, medical technology or life sciences industry.

Sr. Field Application Engineer

Bosch Group · Sunnyvale, United States

Hybrid · Senior · Software Engineer · Posted 12 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Key Responsibilites:

  • Responsible for technical customer support and coordination. First point of technical contact for customers - provide technical feedback and solutions per product training material and published customer approved application notes and datasheets (TCDs).
  • Where necessary, work with Product Area Sensors (PAS), Product Area ICs (PAI), or Product Area Power Semiconductors (PAP) teams to request the appropriate information and communicate to customer.
  • Using application knowledge, assists customer in correctly designing in Bosch products.
  • Coordinates technical customer meetings with the appropriate Bosch team members, taking the lead to document the OPL when customer development lead is in the region.
  • Obtain customer's requirements and communicates them to the Bosch teams including Product Management.
  • Responsible for supporting customer quality issues related to samples provided during their development phase, prior to SOP.
  • Establish customer relationships by working closely with engineering teams.
  • Assist in pre-selling activities of components at customers by leading technical presentations, providing information and support.
  • Provide information on application specific customer trends, innovation, market, and competition.
  • Support development of local customer communication information (verbal and written) in native language.
  • Customer PPAP coordination and support as required
  • Assure customer technical satisfaction from the first engagement, through design-in phase and finally series production
  • Support sample warehouse coordination for components and development kits including testing and packaging of customer kits prior to delivery.

Basic Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering, or equivalent.
  • 5+ years of experience working in a design and/or application engineering role.
  • Strong communication skills verbally and written. Must be able to build relationships quickly with internal and external stakeholders with the ability to work both independently and as part of a global team.
  • 5+ years of design and/or application engineering experience with semiconductors, MEMS devices, and/or Electronic Control Modules with an understanding of the automotive electronics market.
  • Knowledge of vehicle systems including, navigation and mobility, ADAS, and safety systems including airbag systems.
  • Ability to travel nationally/internationally as required for customer meetings, training, and customer support. May be required to spend 2 weeks in Germany for initial technical training.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Knowledge of robotics in environments of manufacturing, humanoid and others.
  • Experienced with electronic engineering tools such as oscilliscopes, that are required for product development.
  • Strong entrepreneurial mindset with a clear understanding of the business environment as a supplier to Automotive Tier 1’s and/or OEM‘s.

Candidates must live within commutable distance from the Bosch Sunnyvale, CA facility and be available to come into the office weekly (or as required).

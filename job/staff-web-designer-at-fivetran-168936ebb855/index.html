About the job

From Fivetran’s founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven.

About Us

Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data.
Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on.
Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. Together, we support thousands of organizations as they build a trusted foundation for analytics, AI, and better business decisions.
As we bring our teams and technology together, we’re building on the strengths of both companies while continuing to deliver the products and experiences our customers know and trust. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact.
During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites.

About the Role

Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies.

We're looking for a strategic, hands-on Staff Web Designer who loves solving complex UX challenges, building scalable systems, and turning ideas into real experiences. This role is ideal for a scrappy, systems-minded designer who thrives on ownership, enjoys getting into the details, and is excited to be the primary designer shaping Fivetran's web experiences.

You will own the design strategy and hands-on execution for fivetran.com and getdbt.com, building scalable design systems that balance consistency with flexibility. You'll choose when to use existing patterns, when to evolve them, and when a unique experience calls for something new. To bring your projects to life, you will partner closely with Web Strategy, Web Engineering, Brand, Growth Marketing, Product Marketing, and Product teams.

Success in this role requires balancing big-picture systems thinking with a willingness to roll up your sleeves. You should be comfortable moving between strategy, wireframes, prototypes, and design systems, making thoughtful trade-offs to ship high-quality work quickly. You will champion AI-powered tools and new ways of working, create scalable processes that reduce repetitive work, and help shape how web design operates as the business grows.

We're looking for someone resourceful, opinionated, curious, and energized by building and thinking in systems. You will build the foundations that make great web experiences easier to create, evolve, and scale.

This is a full-time position based out of our Oakland office. Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration, including two days in the office each week to connect and build as a team.

What You’ll Do

  • Own the end-to-end design strategy and execution for Fivetran and dbt, translating complex web strategy into scalable design systems from concept through launch.
  • Operate as the sole web designer, independently driving high-impact initiatives with a scrappy, pragmatic mindset that balances speed, quality, and technical feasibility.
  • Shape and evolve the design systems that power our website, including reusable components, design standards, and interaction patterns that improve consistency and accelerate time to market.
  • Lead AI innovation for web design, experimenting with emerging tools and building repeatable workflows that accelerate prototyping, production, and implementation.
  • Partner closely with web strategy to connect designs to business outcomes and with web engineering to translate designs into real experiences.
  • Champion user-centered design, leveraging user research and competitive insights to identify opportunities and influence web strategy.
  • Serve as a trusted design partner across Growth Marketing, Product Marketing, Brand, Product, and Engineering, challenging assumptions and building alignment around user-centered solutions.
  • Establish high standards for usability, interaction, accessibility, and responsive design, creating reusable guidance and documentation that enable quality at scale.
  • Proactively improve how the web team operates by anticipating risks, removing bottlenecks, and introducing lightweight processes that make execution faster and more effective.
  • Communicate design strategy and outcomes with clarity to influence stakeholders and executive leadership.

Skills We’re Looking For

  • Extensive experience in web, product, or UX design, with a proven track record of independently leading high-impact digital experiences from strategy through launch.
  • Exceptional systems thinking, with the ability to simplify complex problems, design scalable information architecture, and create reusable patterns that work across multiple experiences and use cases. Proven experience building and evolving design systems, component libraries, and scalable design frameworks.
  • Deep expertise in UX and interaction design, including user flows, navigation, responsive design, accessibility, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping.
  • Experience leveraging AI-powered tools for design, prototyping, design-to-code workflows, or workflow automation, with a strong interest in building more efficient and scalable ways of working.
  • Strong technical fluency and understanding of modern web development, with the ability to collaborate closely with engineers, evaluate implementation trade-offs, and contribute hands-on to bringing designs to life.
  • A resourceful execution mindset with demonstrated success operating independently, navigating ambiguity, prioritizing effectively, and shipping high-quality work in fast-moving environments.
  • Exceptional cross-functional communication and influence, with the confidence to challenge assumptions, articulate design decisions, and align stakeholders.

Bonus Skills​

  • Experience designing for B2B SaaS, enterprise software, developer tools, or data products.
  • Experience working as a solo designer, building design practices and systems from the ground up.
  • Curiosity about emerging web technologies, technical product experiences, and new approaches to designing and building for the web.
  • Strong business acumen, with the ability to leverage behavioral analytics, user research, and experimentation to inform decisions and improve user journeys.
  • Familiarity with experimentation, conversion rate optimization, and behavioral analytics tools such as Optimizely, GA4, Amplitude, or Microsoft Clarity.

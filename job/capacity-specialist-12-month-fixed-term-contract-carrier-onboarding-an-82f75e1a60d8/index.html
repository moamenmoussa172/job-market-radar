Capacity Specialist (12-month Fixed-Term Contract), Carrier Onboarding & Attrition

Amazon · London, United Kingdom

Onsite · Other tech role · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Amazon's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fixed-term contract for a period of 12 months.
If you are excited to work in a high paced operational team, manage a team of high performing individuals and grow and scale the Amazon middle-mile transportation business while maintaining a high bar for quality and performance, this is the team for you!
At Amazon, we're committed to being Earth's most customer-centric company, and that commitment extends to every aspect of our Transportation network.
Our Capacity Specialists are instrumental in orchestrating one of the world's largest and most sophisticated supply chain operations, ensuring seamless delivery experiences for millions of customers worldwide. As a Capacity Specialist, you will collaborate with diverse stakeholders across Amazon to optimize our Relay Carrier Supply Base within the Middle Mile Network (MMN). You will analyze critical performance and compliance metrics to ensure we maintain a high bar for quality in the network. Through data-driven insights and strategic relationship management, you'll develop and implement solutions using AI, improve network reliability, and drive operational excellence. You'll be at the forefront of innovation, helping us create more efficient, sustainable, and customer-focused supply chain solutions while optimizing costs and reducing environmental impact.
Key job responsibilities
This role focuses on maintaining day to day operations along with delivering specific long-term strategic programs within the Amazon Network. The primary responsibilities include:
- Sourcing carriers in order to increase our partners portfolio
- Identify gaps in carrier liquidity and build/implement action plan to increase capacity
- Manage carriers during on-boarding period
- Maintain high operational standard with new carriers on safety, performance, process compliance
- Continuous Improvement Management with carriers, development of SOPs
- Be innovative and support new features/ideas development with tech teams on Relay platform to improve carrier experience or process improvement
- Project support ensuring Amazon requirements are communicated & implemented by Line Haul carriers
- Work across the EU in a cross-functional environment
About the team
The Carrier Onboarding team manages end-to-end carrier lifecycle from onboarding a carrier, growth/engagement within Amazon Relay and ensuring compliance with Amazon and EU operating policies.
- Bachelor's degree or Advanced Degree
- Experience working cross functionally with tech and non-tech teams
- Experience in cross functional project delivery
- Experience in program or project management
- Experience with operations/supply chain
- Experience in program requirements definition, together with data and metrics leveraging to drive improvements
- Knowledge of Excel at a basic level (e.g., UX navigation, math & logical functions, lookup functions, etc.)
- Experience managing, analyzing and communicating results to senior management
- Experience in one or more of the following domains: access- control system and methodology, network security, application- and system-development security, security architecture and models, cryptography, and operations security
- Experience in logistics, or experience in an operational role
- Experience leading process improvements
- Experience in data analysis and reporting
- Experience in creating process improvements with automation and analysis, or experience communicating complex information and solutions to senior stakeholders and influencing decisions

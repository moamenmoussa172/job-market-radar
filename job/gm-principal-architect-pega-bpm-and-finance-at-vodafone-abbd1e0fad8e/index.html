About the job

Who we are

VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.

As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. This partnership is a unique, industry-first model which brings together the best of in-house and 3rd party capability.

We work with customers across 28 countries from 10 VOIS locations: Albania, Egypt, Hungary, India, Romania, Spain, Turkey, UK, Germany, Ireland, and with a network of teams in Czech Republic, Italy, Greece, and Portugal.

About this Role

We are seeking an experienced enterprise architect to define and govern enterprise-wide architecture for workflow automation, case management, decisioning, and financial business systems across VOIS. This role combines deep expertise in Pega architecture, business process management, enterprise integration, low-code platforms, intelligent automation, and finance transformation. The successful candidate will provide architectural leadership for business-critical platforms, establish governance standards, and drive scalable, secure, reusable, and AI-enabled solutions that support Vodafone’s digital transformation objectives.

What you’ll do

  • Lead end-to-end architecture, design, and delivery of enterprise Finance, HR, Workflow, and Automation platforms.
  • Define technology roadmaps, architecture standards, governance frameworks, and solution strategies aligned to business objectives.
  • Act as the design authority for complex enterprise applications, digital transformation initiatives, and cross-functional integrations.
  • Lead architecture and governance of enterprise-scale Pega and BPM solutions, including workflow orchestration, case management, business rules, and decisioning capabilities.
  • Drive adoption of intelligent automation, AI, GenAI, and workflow technologies to improve operational efficiency and user experience.
  • Partner with Finance, HR, and Business stakeholders to simplify, standardize, digitize, and automate critical business processes.
  • Define and govern enterprise integration architecture across Pega, SAP, SuccessFactors, ServiceNow, Power Platform, Azure, and Data & Analytics platforms.
  • Drive API-first, event-driven, and reusable integration patterns while ensuring enterprise data governance and system interoperability.
  • Influence platform strategy, architecture decisions, technology investments, and transformation priorities through effective governance and leadership.
  • Lead architecture reviews, governance forums, and executive stakeholder discussions to ensure strategic alignment and solution quality.
  • Strengthen enterprise capabilities across architecture, BPM, integrations, financial systems, automation, and emerging technologies.

Who you are

  • Extensive experience in enterprise application delivery and architecture, with 12+ years of relevant experience.
  • Pega Lead System Architect (LSA) certification, Infinity 26 certification, and TOGAF certification.
  • Proven expertise in Business Process Management (BPM) and workflow orchestration.
  • Strong experience designing enterprise integrations and API-led architectures.
  • Strong understanding of finance and shared services business processes.
  • Experience leading complex global transformation programmes.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and executive communication capabilities.
  • Telecom and finance domain experience.
  • Experience integrating SAP Finance and SuccessFactors solutions.
  • Knowledge of Workato, ServiceNow, Power Platform, Camunda, and Azure Integration Services.
  • Experience with AI, GenAI, and intelligent automation solutions.
  • Demonstrated ability to shape architecture strategy, governance, and enterprise-scale transformation outcomes.

What skills you will learn

  • Enterprise-scale AI, GenAI, and intelligent automation implementation practices.
  • Financial systems transformation and digital process optimisation.

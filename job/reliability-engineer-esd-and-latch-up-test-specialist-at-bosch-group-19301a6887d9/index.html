Reliability Engineer – ESD & Latch-Up Test Specialist

Bosch Group · Batu Kawan, Malaysia

Onsite · QA / Test Engineer · Posted 11 November 2026

Test automation

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Technical Moderation & Alignment:

  • Consult global R&D on ESD / Latch-Up test setups, layouts & specifications.
  • Own and drive internal Test Request Form.
  • Close knowledge gaps from R&D/Engineering regarding test inputs; secure required pin-lists & design data to perform ESD / Latch-Up tests.
  • Align test limits between design constraints and lab capabilities.

Project Ownership for Reliability Engineers:

  • Local owner for ESD / Latch-Up sub-work-packages in global qualification projects.
  • Manage full loop: requirements alignment, capacity scheduling, test execution, result evaluation, and final reporting.

Lab Operations & Execution (Penang Plant 5):

  • Set up, operate, and maintain ESD & Latch-Up test equipment in Penang.
  • Execute product-level ESD (HBM, CDM) & Latch-Up tests per AEC-Q100/AEC-Q101 & JEDEC.

ESD Control Representative:

  • Local ESD Representative for reliability lab in Penang; align with Reutlingen coordinators.
  • Certify & validate ESD Protected Area (EPA)
  • Perform control walks, host audits, conduct annual ESD safety training, issue corrective instructions.

Troubleshooting & DOEs:

  • Moderate failure analysis and problem solving teams, e.g. with designers, reliability engineers, project leads during ESD / Latch-Up test failures.
  • Define and set up DOEs to isolate ESD / Latch-Up failure root causes and align on design workarounds.
  • Master’s in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Physics, or equivalent.
  • Minimum 5 years in semiconductor reliability testing, quality assurance, or product characterization (automotive preferred).
  • Experience executing ESD / Latch-Up test

Professional competence

  • Semiconductor Technologies: Deep understanding of semiconductor technologies, e.g. CMOS, Mixed-Signal (BCD, LBC), and Bipolar (HVS).
  • ASIC Design & Layout: Evaluate design changes to assess ESD / Latch-Up risks and determine re-qualification scope. Understanding of parasitic structures triggering Latch-Up.
  • Standards & Lab: Working knowledge of AEC-Q100 & AEC-Q101 & JEDEC. Experience with automated test systems (MK2, Orion).
  • Certification: Certified ESD Representative (or willingness to complete basic + advanced training per IEC 61340).
  • Analytical & Systematic Mindset: A systematic and analytical approach to work, with a proven track record of effective problem-solving.
  • Benefits: Company Insurances, Leave Entitlement, Transport Allowance, Flexi Work hours, Meal Subsidy, competative employer EPF contribution, Performance Bonus, Contractual Bonus etc.
  • Work in a fast-growing semiconductor environment.
  • Take ownership of impactful tasks and profit from Career development opportunities
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams on a global scale.
  • Be part of a strong, intercultural team with high team spirit

What We Offer:

  • A challenging and rewarding role in a leading global technology company.
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development.
  • A collaborative, innovative, international and intercultural work environment.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.

If you are a passionate professional looking to make an impact in a fast-paced environment, we would love to hear from you. Apply now to join our team!

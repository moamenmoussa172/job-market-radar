About the job

About Mirakl:

Founded in 2012, Mirakl has been at the forefront of marketplace innovation, empowering every business to compete in the platform economy.
Today, Mirakl’s operating system combines an enterprise marketplace solution (Mirakl Platform) that enables retailers and B2B organizations to launch, scale, and operate marketplaces and dropship, AI-powered multichannel selling (Mirakl Connect), retail media (Mirakl Ads) and an agentic commerce infrastructure (Mirakl Nexus).
With dual headquarters in Boston and Paris, Mirakl helps a global ecosystem of 450+ marketplaces (B2C and B2B) and a network of over 100k third-party marketplace sellers. Brands like Macy’s, Decathlon, Carrefour, Asos, and Airbus Helicopters use Mirakl to grow their businesses in new and remarkable ways.

For more information, visit www.mirakl.com.

Mirakl in Numbers:

  • 👥 750+ employees in 9 offices worldwide: Paris, Barcelona, Bordeaux, Boston, London, Munich, New York, Sydney, Tokyo

The Team You'll Join

You'll be part of our Revenue Operations team led by Julien Amoureaux, Team Lead, Revenue Operations. The team consists of 10 members working across all of Mirakl's suite of products. Together, you'll be driving the efficiency and scalability of Mirakl's sales cycle by ensuring systems, processes, and automation run seamlessly across the organisation.

Your Impact

Revenue Systems Support

  • Maintain and improve the tools supporting prospecting, sales execution, quoting, and revenue recognition across all of Mirakl's business units
  • Administer Salesforce — managing users, permissions, workflows, approvals, and data quality — to keep the sales organization running smoothly
  • Collaborate with RevOps Business Analysts to understand business needs and translate them into system or process improvements
  • Partner with the RevOps Tech Lead on system architecture, integrations, data quality, and prioritization
  • Document solutions, recurring issues, and standard operating procedures to build a reliable, scalable knowledge base for the team

AI & Automation

  • Design, deploy, and maintain AI-agent processes and n8n workflows that automate recurring operational tasks across Revenue Operations
  • Support business users and RevOps analysts in identifying automation opportunities and translating requirements into reliable, scalable solutions
  • Monitor automated processes, troubleshoot failures, and continuously improve their performance and usability
  • Contribute to building a robust automation framework with appropriate documentation, controls, and support processes

CPQ Transformation :

  • Support the rollout of Mirakl's new AI-enabled CPQ operating model, coordinating requirements, testing, documentation, and rollout activities
  • Work cross-functionally with Sales, Finance, RevOps, and technical stakeholders to ensure new CPQ processes meet business needs

What You'll Bring to the Role

Experience:

  • 1 to 2+ years of experience supporting business applications such as Salesforce, CRM, sales engagement tools, or related Revenue Operations platforms
  • Demonstrated ability to manage complex, cross-functional projects with multiple stakeholders
  • Hands-on experience with or strong interest in AI agents, workflow automation, or tools such as n8n
  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, or a related field — or equivalent experience

Skills:

  • Analytical problem-solving with strong attention to detail
  • Ability to translate business requirements into system or automation solutions
  • Clear communication and cross-functional collaboration
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize multiple requests
  • Adaptability and eagerness to learn in a fast-moving environment

Tools Used

  • CRM: Salesforce
  • CPQ: DealHub or equivalent CPQ platform
  • Automation: n8n, Dust, AI agents
  • Data Managerment : Databricks
  • Sales Engagement: Prospecting and outbounding tools (e.g. Gong, Outreach)
  • Productivity & Documentation: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, Confluence or Notion

Languages

  • English: C2 (Full professional proficiency)
  • French: C2 (Full professional proficiency)

1. About the Opportunity

MoonPay is looking for an IT Engineer to join our IT Engineering team. In this role, you will be responsible for developing automations and systems integrations across a variety of Core Business platforms.

2. What you will do

  • Build, support and monitor applications within our Corporate Services application portfolio.
  • Build and maintain custom applications in our iPaas platform to support the automation of workstreams.
  • Responsible for Authentication services (Okta), Collaboration Services (GSuite, Slack, Zoom)and iPaaS (Workato, Fivetrans)
  • Develop IT Engineering best practices and policies for the organization.
  • Prepare and document standard operating procedures.
  • Respond to and lead IT responses for incidents.
    Bonus:
  • Evangelize the adoption of new tools.
  • Escalation point for members of the IT Organization.
  • Test, implement and improve/maintain IT systems and controls.
  • Engineer, implement and monitor IT systems and controls.
  • Identify and define IT system requirements.
  • Prepare and document standard operating procedures and playbooks.

3. About You

Skills & Experience:

  • Ability to prioritize projects
  • An understanding of IT Engineering best practices and how to implement them.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment
  • Excellent communication skills
  • 5+ years of JAMF, Okta & GSuite Administration or similar systems.
  • Experience with FIvetrans, Workato or similar platforms.
  • Experience with Scripting (i.e. Bash, Python, Powershell).
  • Proficient in SQLand understand the basics of data warehousing concepts
  • Experience with Cloud (GCP / AWS) and IaC (Terraform).
  • Excellent troubleshooting skills with the ability to think outside the box when complex problems arise.
  • Experience with SIEM platforms such as Datadog, Splunk or Google Chronicle.
  • Critical thinking skills and the ability to solve problems as they arise
  • Basic coding skills, such as HTML, CSS and other languages.
  • Familiarity with web related technologies and network/web related protocols.
  • Domestic and International travel may be possible.Ability to travel. (do not have to travel but may have to travel for certain work purposes)

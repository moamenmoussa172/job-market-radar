About the role...

This person will be part of our Growth Data Science team, collaborating with peers and cross-functional partners in Product, Finance, and Strategy to continuously improve our forecasting capabilities. They will drive methodological advances and deliver trustworthy business-critical quarterly forecasts, built on a deep understanding of the dynamics of user growth across Duolingo’s product portfolio.

🧠 You will...

  • Join a small team of data scientists focused on User Growth forecasting. Collaborate with peers and mentor junior data scientists to ensure that our forecasts remain trustworthy and reliably delivered.
  • Work with diverse stakeholders to develop forecasts that reflect a deep understanding of our product development, big bets, and evolving business strategy.
  • Translate complex analytical findings into clear narratives for senior leadership and cross-functional partners. Influence strategic decisions through a combination of technical depth and effective communication.
  • Develop and validate methodological advances to our forecasts, aligning methodological strengths with business priorities.
  • Build systems and processes that ensure our forecasting process is robust and repeatable.

✅ You have...

  • Applied industry experience building forecasts in messy real world settings where details, confounds, and the validity of assumptions matter.
  • A graduate degree in Data Science, Statistics, Economics, or a related quantitative field, or equivalent demonstrated expertise in formal analytical techniques.
  • Demonstrated ability to connect technical results to real-world recommendations, and to translate product and business changes into technical requirements.
  • Strong command of SQL, Python, AI analysis tools, and data/analytics engineering ecosystems.
  • Exceptional communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to distill complex quantitative work into crisp, actionable insights for diverse audiences.

⭐ Exceptional candidates will have...

  • Experience operating at a high-growth consumer technology company, including experience with user growth forecasting specifically.
  • Experience at a public company, including the analytical demands that come with investor-grade reporting and forecasting.
  • An impressive Duolingo streak.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Lead the technical strategy for Everpure™ Cloud across the sales cycle, turning complex cloud and hybrid-storage challenges into validated solutions that win customer trust and revenue. Bridge Cloud Sales Specialists, account teams, Product, Engineering, hyperscaler partners, and customer architects to shape the right path across Everpure™ Cloud Dedicated and Everpure™ Cloud Azure Native. Use expert judgment to connect technical differentiation to measurable outcomes such as lower total cost, stronger disaster recovery, faster migration, and confident cloud adoption. As a recognized technical voice, influence pooled-account strategy, elevate field engagement, and help define how Everpure™ Cloud wins in a competitive market.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Lead technical strategy for pooled Everpure™ Cloud opportunities from discovery through Closed Won, aligning Cloud Sales Specialists, account teams, sales engineering leaders, and partners to improve technical win rates and TCV outcomes.
  • Translate customer success criteria into validated deployment architectures for Everpure™ Cloud Dedicated or Everpure™ Cloud Azure Native, making sizing, TCO, Marketplace and committed-spend paths, risks, and decision criteria clear before stage progression.
  • Guide customer architects and engineers through tailored demonstrations, design workshops, and PoCs/PoVs that validate disaster recovery, migration, performance, data reduction, and cost outcomes—and convert evidence into technical wins.
  • Build demand and technical thought leadership through content, industry and partner events, hands-on labs, and community engagement that grows Everpure™ Cloud pipeline and clarifies its value against native cloud and competitive offerings.
  • Own the technical bridge from Closed Won into customer success by documenting architectures, integrations, dependencies, and success checkpoints, then partnering with CX, Product, Engineering, and customers to drive adoption, expansion, actionable feedback, and reference opportunities.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Certification-level fluency in AWS, Azure, or GCP, with the ability to design and defend cloud and hybrid architectures spanning block storage, data protection, and disaster recovery.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead discovery, technical qualification, solution validation, and technical close with architects, engineers, business stakeholders, and partners in complex environments.
  • Working knowledge of Kubernetes, VMware migration including AVS and EVS, infrastructure as code, automation, networking, identity, observability, and security controls.
  • Ability to connect technical differentiation to business outcomes, TCO, ROI, BVS, and cloud commercial models including AWS and Azure Marketplaces and committed-spend agreements.

About the job

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is seeking a Senior Solutions Architect (Polish Speaker) to join our thriving technical community and growing business across Central Eastern Europe. You will be part of the Financial Services Enterprise team, leading technical strategy and innovation across the region. As a senior member of our Solutions Architecture team, you'll drive transformational innovation for large enterprise financial services customers by architecting and implementing cloud solutions. You'll be at the forefront of emerging technologies, guiding customers through their journey to adopt agentic AI workloads, autonomous systems, and intelligent automation that reshape how financial institutions operate and serve their customers.
In this role, you'll leverage the full breadth of AWS cloud technologies to design scalable, secure, and compliant architectures that accelerate digital transformation across multiple markets. You'll champion innovation by identifying opportunities to apply agentic architecture patterns, AI-SDLC methodologies, and modern application designs that deliver measurable business outcomes. Working closely with C-level executives and technical leaders across the region, you'll translate complex business challenges into agentic, cloud-native solutions that drive competitive advantage in the rapidly evolving financial services landscape.
AWS Global Sales drives adoption of the AWS cloud worldwide, enabling customers of all sizes to innovate and expand in the cloud. Our team empowers every customer to grow by providing tailored service, unmatched technology, and unwavering support. We dive deep to understand each customer's unique challenges, then craft innovative solutions that accelerate their success. This customer-first approach is how we built the world's most adopted cloud. Join us and help us grow.
Key job responsibilities
As a Senior Solutions Architect, you'll provide technical leadership across Europe Central, taking ownership of strategic customer relationships and driving architectural excellence. You'll deliver expert recommendations on solution design, migration strategies, security, and best-practice architectures - with a focus on agentic architecture patterns, AI-SDLC, and intelligent automation systems. Your influence extends beyond customer interactions; you'll architect solutions leveraging agentic workloads, autonomous systems, and AI-native application designs to deliver measurable business outcomes.
You'll serve as a technical thought leader, mentoring Solutions Architects across the region and elevating team capabilities. As an internal advocate, you'll drive continuous improvements to the AWS platform based on customer needs, particularly in agentic AI and AI-SDLC transformation. You'll contribute to the AWS knowledge base by creating best practices, reference architectures, and reusable artifacts that advance cloud adoption in financial services (white papers, code samples, blog posts on agentic architecture patterns).
You'll educate and evangelize AWS technology through workshops, conferences, and public speaking - expanding our reach across Europe Central. You'll champion agentic AI systems and autonomous workload architectures that enable financial institutions to innovate at scale.
You are an experienced technology leader with strong business acumen and a builder mindset. You excel at translating complex business challenges into agentic, cloud-native solutions with proven track record implementing agentic AI workloads and AI-SDLC practices in enterprise contexts.

About the job

Responsible for managing customer contacts, requests and disputes from customers. Registers the cases in systems and classifies them according to the classification matrix. Performs a first analysis to analyze and resolve within fixed service level target time.

Coordinates with other neighboring functions (e.g Supply Chain, Commercial, Quality) to close the case by gathering information and investigating. The objective is to reach the highest first call resolution rate and provide a positive customer experience framed by a target lead time determined by priorities.

This role requires a detailed understanding of Order to Cash activities passionated by customer satisfaction and problem resolution.

Main responsibilities:

  • Intake — Collect and register inquiries, requests, claims, and returns from internal and external customers promptly, by priority, aiming for first call resolution on requests.
  • Customer interaction — Answer customer calls, manage expectations, and positively influence Customer Experience at Sanofi.
  • Case ownership — As case owner, run claims/returns policy checks, escalate non-compliant cases to the accountable business contact, and follow up pending cases with neighboring functions (Trade, Quality, Distribution, External 3PL).
  • Investigation — Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to investigate cases, and liaise with third parties or sites on refusals, damage, overages, DC errors, or shortages.
  • Communication & classification — Inform internal stakeholders of decisions when relevant, and apply request/claim classification consistently to support root cause analysis.
  • Resolution — Select the right resolution process, manage credit/debit memos and returns/refusal orders, follow approval workflows and internal controls, and monitor reports to secure resolution and customer communication.
  • Process standardization — Document local specifics in the DTP, escalate exceptions to TL/Key users, and support Core Model adherence initiatives.

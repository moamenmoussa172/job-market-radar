About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Germany.

Who we are

Aleph Alpha’s mission is to deliver sovereign, human-centric AI to governmental institutions and large enterprises. To that end, we deliver category-defining AI innovation that enables open, accessible, and trustworthy deployment of GenAI in industrial applications. Our organization develops foundational models and next-generation methods that make it easy and affordable for Aleph Alpha’s customers to increase productivity in development, engineering, logistics, and manufacturing processes.

About the role

We are looking for a (Senior) AI Researcher to join our hallucination reduction capability team to push the boundaries in LLM post-training (SFT and RL).

We're one of the few places in Europe training foundation models from scratch. You will be part of a team of (senior) researchers and PhD interns doing cutting-edge research across pre-, mid- and post-training, grounding and specialized models with hundreds of GPUs at our disposal.

What will you do

  • Core Research & Development: Design, execute, and scale post-training methodologies (SFT, RL) specifically aimed at eliminating model hallucinations and making them grounded in evidence.
  • Uncertainty & Reliability: Research and implement techniques in uncertainty estimation, calibration, self-consistency, and self-correction mechanisms to enable models to identify and signal when they “don't know”.
  • Post-Training Pipelines: Build and optimize high-throughput, distributed fine-tuning and training pipelines across multi-node GPU clusters.
  • Evaluation & Benchmarking: Design and implement rigorous evaluation methods to benchmark hallucination rates, truthfulness, and reasoning accuracy.
  • Technical Ownership: Drive research projects independently from initial hypothesis to scalable production implementation and technical reports.

Qualifications

  • Hands-on experience fine-tuning or post-training LLMs using modern distributed training frameworks.
  • Track record of research or implementation in at least one of the following domains:
  • Hallucination mitigation / truthfulness alignment / grounding in RAG
  • Uncertainty estimation, or out-of-distribution detection
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation evaluation / alignment, refusal behavior calibration, or claim verification
  • Strong programming skills (Python & PyTorch).

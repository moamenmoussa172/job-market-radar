About the job

This Product Manager will drive customer pricing for Amazon South Africa. This is a product role that will require the incumbent to build and execute roadmaps for Pricing and Price Perception. It also has a critical analytics component to it given the need to work with and build data-driven models that inform our pricing and deals strategy.
Key job responsibilities
As part of this role, the PM will be responsible for
Building a country-level product and program road map working with (and spanning) 3P Domestic, 3P OOC, Retail and Global Store with the main aim of making Amazon the lowest priced retailer for our customers,
Rolling out WW best practices and initiatives that help Price Perception for Amazon in South Africa.
Work with Marketing/Merchandizing to create the right price perception build-up strategy on our key website assets,
Create the right offline price perception management strategy – as offline is a key part of how SIC drives price perception in our marketplaces, and finally
lead the pricing product portfolio across products that automate pricing, or build trust with customers. This role will lead pricing product and program for Amazon in South Africa.
- 3+ years of product management experience
- Experience managing technical products or online services
- Experience in product, program, or project management in leading cross-functional teams in delivery of major new products or services
- Knowledge of SQL and VBA at an advanced level
- Experience building and analyzing cost/benefit scenarios and business cases and communicating results throughout the organization

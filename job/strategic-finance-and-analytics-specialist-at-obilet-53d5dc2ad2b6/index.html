Strategic Finance and Analytics Specialist

Obilet · Istanbul, Turkey

Hybrid · Mid · Data Analyst · Posted 24 October 2026

Apply on Obilet's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Founded in 2012, Obilet.com is a comprehensive travel and tourism platform offering bus, flight, and ferry ticketing, along with hotel bookings and car rentals.

With its advanced SaaS Inventory Management System (IMS) supporting bus carriers, over 25 million monthly visitors, and more than 15 million app downloads, Obilet is the leading player in both B2B and B2C markets in Türkiye and the Eastern European region.

Obilet has reached an agreement with BlaBlaCar for a strategic investment in exchange for a majority share in 2024. With more than 50 million transactions projected for 2026, Obilet continues its international expansion with the ambition of becoming one of the world's leading travel tech companies.

Main responsibilities will include:

  • Process, validate, and reconcile monthly financial data received from Finance team, investigate discrepancies, errors, and missing information with the relevant business units
  • Design and implement control mechanisms to ensure data accuracy, consistency, and completeness
  • Verify the integrity of data submitted to Group systems and provide clear, timely responses to Group-level reporting requests
  • Provide detailed, clear, and timely responses to inquiries and requests received from the shareholders
  • Prepare executive-level analyses, financial projections, and presentations for senior management and the Board
  • Incorporate monthly financial results into budgeting and forecasting models
  • Contribute to the enhancement, optimization, and continuous development of budgeting models
  • Develop Long-Term Business Plans (LTBP) and conduct scenario and sensitivity analyses to support strategic decision-making
  • Analyze financial and operational data to support business performance evaluation and provide actionable insights to relevant stakeholders
  • Create and maintain data tables and supporting materials for quarterly Board presentations
  • Monitor, analyze, and maintain assigned KPI data, ensuring accurate and timely data entry

To be successful in this role, you need to have:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering, Business Administration, Economics, or another relevant multi-disciplinary field
  • 3-4 years of professional work experience in strategy, management consulting, audit or a relevant field
  • FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis) experience, as a plus
  • Advanced knowledge of Google Sheets and Microsoft Office applications
  • Exceptional attention to detail and commitment to quality
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously with a proactive mindset and strong ownership
  • Excellent command of Turkish and English, including writing and grammar skills

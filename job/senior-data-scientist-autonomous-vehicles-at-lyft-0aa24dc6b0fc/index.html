About the job

Senior Data Scientist, Decisions – Autonomous Vehicles

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive.

The future of Lyft is a hybrid network, where autonomous vehicles and human drivers serve riders side by side on one platform. That future has already started. In September 2026, Lyft riders in Nashville were matched with Waymo autonomous vehicles on the Lyft app for the first time. Our partnerships now span Waymo in Nashville, May Mobility in Atlanta and Baidu in Europe, with more on the way.

The Global Growth organization is charting the future of Lyft’s growth beyond North American rideshare’s core products, including bringing Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) to riders domestically and abroad. As a Senior Data Scientist, Decisions for AV, you will be the science partner for the AV product team. You’ll use experimentation and data to decide what we build, how we launch it, and how we know it’s working. You will work closely with product managers, engineers, designers, operations and partnership leaders, and present your recommendations to senior leadership and AV partners.

In this role, you will help us answer questions such as:

  • Rider adoption and trust: What makes a rider choose, accept or opt out of an autonomous ride, and which product changes increase adoption?
  • The AV product experience: How should we design and test the experience from request to pickup to post-ride, including flows like airport pickups?
  • Launch decisions: Which features are ready to scale, and how do we measure their impact before and after launch?
  • Product health: What metrics define a great AV experience for riders, and how do we monitor them across partners and markets?
  • Partnership performance: What shared product metrics and quality guardrails should define success with each AV partner?

Responsibilities:

  • Help design, run and analyze A/B tests and other online experiments on AV products, and turn results into clear launch decisions
  • Help define the metrics framework for AV product success, including adoption, retention, trust and rider experience
  • Partner with product managers, engineers and designers to identify opportunities and shape the AV product roadmap
  • Build dashboards and analytical frameworks to monitor AV product and business performance
  • Present data-driven recommendations to senior leadership and AV partners
  • Partner with central science teams on experimentation best practices for a hybrid network
  • Mentor data scientists and analysts across Global Growth

Experience:

  • Degree in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field, or equivalent work experience; advanced degree preferred
  • 5+ years in product data science or analytics, with a track record of work that changed product direction
  • Deep hands-on experience designing and analyzing online experiments at scale, including metric selection, power analysis and interpreting results
  • Strong SQL on large datasets, and Python or R for analysis and visualization
  • Experience presenting to senior stakeholders and influencing product decisions without formal authority
  • Nice to have: experience in AVs, mobility, rideshare, delivery or other two-sided marketplaces; experimentation in marketplace settings; working with partner or third-party data

Skills:

  • Ability to turn ambiguous product questions into clear analyses and recommendations
  • Ability to choose the right experiment design for the decision, and explain the trade-off between rigor and speed
  • Ability to craft a compelling story with data for technical and non-technical audiences
  • Strong product sense and real curiosity about how riders experience a new technology
  • Entrepreneurial self-starter who takes ownership in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment

Please submit your resume in English.

About the job

GSK Saudi Arabia is committed to supporting Vision 2030 and the Saudi Arabian National Agenda including Saudization in line with the hiring and the training and development of Saudi National capability.

GSK remains committed to achieving bold commercial ambitions for the future. By 2031, we aim to deliver £40 billion in annual sales, leveraging our existing strong performance momentum to significantly increase our positive impact on the health of billions of patients globally. Our Ahead Together strategy is centred on early intervention to prevent and alter the course of disease, thereby protecting people and supporting healthcare systems.

Job title: Associate Product Manager Oncology

Location: Riyadh

Closing date for applications: 10 December 2026

At GSK we unite science, technology, and talent to get ahead of diseases together.

Are you looking for an opportunity to manage the integration of pharmaceutical brands and execute the marketing campaign to optimize customer experience? If so, this is the role for you.

An exciting opportunity as presented for a dynamic Associate Product Manager to bring value to our newly established Oncology franchise.

This role is a driving force for the organization and will be accountable for jointly creating and implementing the operational plan of new launches.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams to launch our new brands, build our advocacy and achieve our ambition for patients.

Job Purpose

The objective of the role is to lead the execution and delivery of solid tumors portfolio marketing strategy and operational plans.

Core Responsibilities

  • Deliver on the execution of the brand operational plan with speed and quality.
  • Prepare and execute the marketing P2P educational programs, ensuring brand message delivery and understanding of best candidate patients for our medications.
  • Manage GSK third-party sponsorship events, work with the different healthcare societies to build GSK advocacy and reputation.
  • Develop marketing campaigns and communication plans and secure approvals of promotional materials in line with the local SOPs.
  • Orchestrate all activities that have been implemented at the customer level to ensure end-to-end brand management and best customer experience.
  • Ensure resources allocation is in line with the decided priorities.
  • Work closely with sales team to ensure training of field force on the brand key message and promotional materials.
  • Spend up to 50% of their time in the field connecting customers, supporting the sales team and ensuring a deep understanding of the market insights and dynamics.

Knowledge and Application:

  • Coordinate the local approval process for promotional campaigns, ensuring adherence to monthly cycle deadlines and conducting business in accordance with GSK internal policies and country-speciﬁc regulations.
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of marketing techniques, selling skills, and compliance frameworks to effectively support the measurement of tools and gain approvals within the appropriate governance framework.
  • Possess knowledge of internal metrics and stakeholders to identify the appropriate individuals involved in decision-making processes for delivering operational plan.

Problem solving and Innovation:

  • Effective problem-solving skills to overcome business challenges and achieve business objectives.
  • Create innovative solution to engage and influence customers behaviour.

Interaction:

  • Adhere to GSK policies and compliance standards in all business activities.
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders such as sales, medical, regulatory, and others to lead and inﬂuence efforts aimed at gathering customer insights, ensuring a mutually beneﬁcial outcome for customers, patients, and GSK.

Why you?

Basic Qualifications & Skills

We are looking for professionals with these required skills to achieve our goals:

  • Saudi National
  • A pharmacist with a valid SCHS.
  • 3 or more years of experience in Saudi pharmaceutical industry.
  • Proven track record of performance.
  • Oncology experience is a must

Preferred qualifications:

· Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

· Experience in GI solid tumors.

· Excellent organization and project management skills.

· Strong commercial acumen and analytical skills.

· Fast-paced, with ability to deliver against stretched timelines.

· Strong MS office skills (excel and PowerPoint).

· Marketing experience is a plus.

· Knowledge of the Oncology/Hematology Key External Experts is a plus.

· Ability to travel extensively (up to 50% of the time).

Please note that relocation support cannot be provided; therefore, only candidates who are already based in the indicated territory can be considered.

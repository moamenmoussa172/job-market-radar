Summary

As a Senior Manager, Executive and Strategic Communications – Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Deere & Company (located in Urbandale, IA or Moline, IL), you will be the senior communications partner to the CTO and the leaders of the CTO organization, including IT, Global Information Security (GSEC), and Intelligent Solutions Group (ISG). You will serve as the strategic communicator and liaison between CTO leadership and enterprise audiences, and you will lead the communicators who support the organization's leaders and unit communications. This role is critical to inspiring and engaging key audiences, protecting brand sentiment, and connecting CTO initiatives to enterprise-wide understanding.

Your Responsibilities

  • Serve as the strategic communications advisor to the CTO leader, shaping internal communication plans that align with CTO and enterprise goals. Develop the messaging, tactics and channels that meet business needs.
  • Act as the primary liaison between CTO and the enterprise audience. Identify and lead strategic content and campaigns that build enterprise understanding of CTO priorities and support business and customer success.
  • Develop the strategy for the CTO leader’s external and internal speaking engagements, shaping key messages, audience priorities and talking points, and support their execution.
  • Coordinate with communications leaders across the CTO organization (IT, GSEC, and ISG) so that key messages affecting the broader organization are aligned and consistent.
  • Lead and develop the ISG communicator team, which supports further leadership and unit communications.
  • Define clear outcomes and use engagement metrics and sentiment analysis to inform strategy, refine messaging and improve content visibility.
  • Continuously innovate communication channels, tactics and methods to improve reach, engagement and impact. Stay ahead of communication trends and apply best practices to drive audience-centric strategies.
  • Identify stakeholder needs and concerns, facilitate meetings and events, and develop materials that foster understanding and alignment.
  • Invest in personal growth and technological fluency to enhance communication quality and productivity.
  • Edit and prepare content in accordance with organizational style guidelines for publication across internal and external channels.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Brazil.

Our Client is one of the United States’ largest insurers, providing a wide range of insurance and financial services products with gross written premiums well over US$25 Billion (P&C). They proudly serve more than 10 million U.S. households with more than 19 million individual policies across all 50 states through the efforts of over 48,000 exclusive and independent agents and nearly 18,500 employees. Finally, our Client is part of one the largest Insurance Groups in the world.

Job Summary

The SAP Finance team supports data, analytics, and financial reporting across the Farmers Finance organization. The team is responsible for developing, supporting, and enhancing the Power BI reporting platform used by Finance stakeholders to support reporting, analytics, and business decision-making.

The Application Subject Matter Expert III will serve as a key contributor to the SAP Finance reporting ecosystem, supporting the design, development, maintenance, and enhancement of Power BI reporting solutions. This role partners directly with Finance leaders and business stakeholders to deliver impactful analytics and reporting capabilities while helping modernize the platform through automation, AI, and Microsoft Copilot integration.

Key Responsibilities

· Design, develop, and maintain Power BI dashboards and reporting solutions.

· Develop Financial Reporting, Executive Dashboards, KPI Dashboards, and Operational Reporting solutions.

· Partner directly with Finance leadership and business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business needs into reporting solutions.

· Connect, transform, and model data from SAP and other database environments.

· Support and maintain production reporting environments.

· Troubleshoot reporting, data quality, and system issues.

· Enhance reporting capabilities through automation, AI, and Microsoft Copilot technologies.

· Drive continuous improvements to reporting processes and platform functionality.

Requirements

· 4-6 years of experience in Business Intelligence, Reporting, Analytics, Application Development, or a related field.

· Fluent English, both written and verbal.

· Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Analytics, Finance, Accounting, or a related discipline preferred.

Technical Skills

· 4-6 years of hands-on experience with Power BI development, including:

  • Data Modeling
  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Dashboard and Report Development
  • Data Visualization Best Practices

· Experience working with reporting environments and database platforms; SAP experience is preferred but not required.

· Knowledge of enterprise reporting architectures and data integration concepts.

· Familiarity with Microsoft Copilot and AI-enabled productivity solutions.

· Experience with Microsoft Power Automate preferred.

· Exposure to GitHub or source control tools preferred.

Business Knowledge

· Ability to work directly with stakeholders across multiple levels of the organization.

· Understanding of Finance and Accounting principles and reporting requirements.

· Familiarity with financial reporting, P&L analysis, and business performance metrics.

· Insurance industry knowledge is a plus.

· Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

· Ability to translate complex business requirements into technical reporting solutions.

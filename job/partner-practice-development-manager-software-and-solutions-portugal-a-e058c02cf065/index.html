About the job

Partner Practice Development Manager Software & Solutions Portugal

Description -

Job Summary
Leads highly complex, high-value, multi-market, or strategically important partner-development portfolios and growth motions requiring advanced commercial judgment and cross-functional orchestration. Shapes partner growth strategies, strengthens partner capability, and mobilizes specialized resources to address material business opportunities and risks. Serves as a recognized expert in partner development and influences practices beyond assigned partners.
Responsibilities
• Set and lead comprehensive partner-development strategies aligned with profitable growth, engagement, share of wallet, pipeline, conversion, portfolio adoption, and partner capability objectives.
• Build and maintain senior partner relationships and coordinate engagement across management, sales, business development, practice, technical, marketing, and operational stakeholders.
• Shape integrated partner plans covering market opportunities, customer targeting, portfolio focus, enablement, campaigns, programs, pipeline, and opportunity execution.
• Lead complex partner-led opportunity strategies, co-selling motions, capability development, program adoption, and recovery actions.
• Direct cross-functional coordination across Channel, Category, End-User Sales, Technical, Marketing, Business Planning, Operations, Finance, Legal, and specialist teams.
• Anticipate material partner, market, portfolio, commercial, capability, operational, and channel risks and establish mitigation or escalation plans.
• Influence partner focus, resource deployment, program support, campaign investment, specialist engagement, and management attention for priority growth opportunities.
• Use advanced partner, customer, performance, competitive, and market insight to identify structural growth opportunities and recurring execution barriers.
• Provide expert guidance on partner strategy, commercial judgment, capability development, opportunity recovery, program engagement, and cross-functional decision-making.
• Develop reusable methods, standards, dashboards, and practices that improve partner development across the organization.
Education & Experience Recommended
• Four-year or Graduate Degree in Sales, Marketing, Business Administration, or any other related discipline or commensurate work experience or demonstrated competence.
• Typically has 7-10 years of work experience, preferably in enterprise selling, channel & alliance, or a related field.

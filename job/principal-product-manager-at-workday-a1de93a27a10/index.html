About the Team

The Deployment Technology team is transforming how Workday is deployed and managed. Our mission is to reduce deployment time, cost and complexity while helping customers realize value faster.
We build products that help Workday implementation teams and partners get customers live faster by making it easier to discover proven setup approaches, reuse configuration, automate repetitive work and use AI-powered tools to guide and complete complex implementation tasks.
We also help customers become more self-sufficient after deployment, making it easier to manage and maintain Workday, adopt new products and features, and stay on top of ongoing change through intuitive, AI-enabled experiences.
Working closely with customers, partners and product teams across Workday, we’re shaping the next generation of deployment technology across the full customer lifecycle.

About the Role

As a Principal Product Manager in Deployment Technology, you will shape the vision, strategy and adoption of products that make Workday deployments faster, simpler and more scalable. You will lead strategic initiatives across deployment capabilities and agentic experiences, turning complex customer and partner needs into compelling products and measurable outcomes.

This role combines hands-on product ownership with broader leadership and influence. You will raise the quality of product decisions and ways of working, mentor other product managers, and build strong relationships across engineering, design and business teams. You will also represent our products with customers and partners, shape go-to-market and commercial strategies, and help grow adoption across the Workday ecosystem.

We are looking for someone who thinks big, moves quickly through ambiguity and brings others with them, while staying close to the product, the technology and the people using it.

Responsibilities:

  • Set product vision and strategy. Define and drive strategy and roadmaps across strategic areas of Deployment Technology, connecting customer needs, market opportunities and emerging technologies with Workday’s broader business direction.
  • Lead through ambiguity. Turn emerging opportunities and complex, loosely defined problems into clear priorities, decisions and actionable plans. Balance long-term ambition with focused, incremental delivery.
  • Own outcomes from discovery through adoption. Lead discovery, prioritisation, delivery, launch and iteration, remaining accountable for customer value, adoption and business impact.
  • Partner deeply with engineering and design. Understand product architecture, integrations and technical constraints sufficiently to challenge assumptions, shape user experiences and make informed trade-offs.
  • Advance agentic experiences. Apply practical experience with AI agents to shape capabilities including Skills, MCP-based tools, automation and agentic workflows, with attention to usability, reliability, evaluation and appropriate user control.
  • Strengthen product operations. Establish effective, lightweight practices for discovery, prioritisation, roadmap planning, decision-making and outcome measurement that help the team work consistently and move faster.
  • Mentor and influence other product managers. Coach product managers and senior product managers, strengthening strategic thinking, customer discovery, execution and stakeholder communication through feedback and example.
  • Drive go-to-market and ecosystem adoption. Partner with sales, services, marketing and partner teams on positioning, launch readiness, enablement and awareness. Identify barriers to adoption and lead initiatives that help customers and partners realise value.
  • Shape commercial strategy. Assess market opportunities and contribute to pricing, packaging and routes to market, balancing customer value, adoption and business objectives.
  • Build customer and partner relationships. Lead discovery conversations, product demonstrations and strategic discussions with confidence. Translate feedback into scalable solutions and communicate product direction credibly.
  • Align stakeholders and measure results. Build alignment across product, engineering, design, sales, services, marketing, legal and compliance. Use product data, customer insight and market evidence to guide investment decisions and continuously improve outcomes.

About You

Basic Qualifications:

  • 10+ years of product management experience delivering enterprise software, SaaS or platform products, with a track record of increasing scope and strategic impact.
  • Demonstrated experience setting product vision and strategy, leading complex initiatives across teams and delivering measurable customer and business outcomes.
  • Proven experience developing and delivering AI agent or agentic workflow capabilities, with a practical understanding of their opportunities, limitations and operational requirements.
  • Strong go-to-market experience, including product positioning, launch planning, customer or partner enablement, and driving adoption.
  • Experience working closely with software engineering and design teams, with sufficient technical depth to engage in architecture, integration and feasibility discussions.
  • Demonstrated ability to improve product practices and mentor other product managers, including senior product managers.
  • Strong communication and influencing skills, with experience representing products to customers, partners and senior stakeholders.
  • Proven ability to make sound decisions and sustain delivery in fast-moving, ambiguous environments.

Other Qualifications:

  • Experience shaping pricing, packaging or commercial strategy for enterprise products.
  • Experience building adoption through partner ecosystems, implementation partners or professional services organisations.
  • Familiarity with APIs, MCP, configuration management, deployment technologies or enterprise workflow automation.
  • Experience evaluating AI quality, reliability, permissions and human oversight in enterprise environments.
  • Strong customer empathy, curiosity and collaborative judgement, with the ability to challenge constructively and build trust across disciplines.
  • Ability to recognise common needs across customers and translate them into scalable, reusable capabilities.
  • Familiarity with Workday products or its customer and partner ecosystem is an advantage, but is not required.

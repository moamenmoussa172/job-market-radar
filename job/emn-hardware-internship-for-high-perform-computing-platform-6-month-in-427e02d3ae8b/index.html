About the job

Hardware Engineering Intern - Automotive Chiplet System

Join our Automotive Chiplet System team and begin your learning journey in advanced hardware engineering.

During the internship, you will receive structured technical training, mentoring and continuous feedback from experienced hardware and system engineers. You will apply what you learn through practical assignments connected to the Automotive Chiplet System project and contribute to reusable engineering assets for the team.

The program is designed for candidates who want to develop their capabilities in hardware design, automotive development, advanced packaging and chiplet technologies.

What You Will Learn

  • Hardware and electronics fundamentals
  • Automotive hardware development methods and workflows
  • Engineering discipline and professional working practices
  • High-speed and high-power hardware design principles
  • PCB design, simulation and design review processes
  • Component library development and manufacturing considerations
  • Practical applications of AI in hardware design, testing and verification
  • Technical documentation and configuration management

Throughout the program, you will receive technical guidance, design reviews and regular feedback from experienced engineers.

Your Responsibilities

Depending on your interests and technical development, you will contribute to one or more of the following assignments:

1. Hardware Design Toolchain and Workflow

  • Support the setup and evaluation of Cadence System Capture, PCB Editor, simulation tools and design-control workflows.
  • Study automotive hardware development practices and configuration discipline.
  • Contribute to automotive, high-speed and high-power design guidelines.
  • Develop reference design examples and supporting documentation.
  • Document toolchain configurations, workflows and engineering recommendations.

2. Component Library and Manufacturing

  • Research electronic component standards, qualification requirements and manufacturing constraints.
  • Support the creation and management of schematic symbols, PCB footprints, metadata and component lifecycle information.
  • Contribute to library review, approval, release and change-control processes.
  • Prepare component library guidelines and manufacturing checklists.
  • Identify opportunities to improve component library governance and workflow efficiency.

3. AI in Hardware Design and Testing

  • Explore AI applications for schematic design, PCB layout and component library development.
  • Investigate AI-supported preparation and analysis of Signal Integrity and Power Integrity models.
  • Develop concepts or prototypes for AI-assisted schematic, layout and release reviews.
  • Explore AI applications in test planning, automation, log analysis and verification evidence.
  • Document practical use cases, limitations, workflows and quality considerations.

The expected outputs may include workflows, guidelines, reference implementations, library models, manufacturing checklists, AI prototypes and documented engineering evidence.

Education and Technical foundation:

  • Final-year student in: Electronics Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, or another related technical field
  • University recommendation/introduction letter for internship is mandatory
  • Good English communication and interpersonal skills
  • Basic understanding of electronic circuits and hardware design.
  • Familiarity with schematic design, PCB layout, simulation or embedded hardware is an advantage.
  • Interest in high-speed interfaces, automotive electronics, semiconductor packaging or chiplet systems.
  • Basic programming, scripting or AI-tool experience is beneficial but not mandatory.

Personal Attributes

  • Strong curiosity and willingness to learn unfamiliar technologies.
  • Proactive and structured approach to problem solving.
  • Persistent when working on complex technical topics.
  • Open to technical feedback and continuous improvement.
  • Responsible and committed to delivering high-quality engineering outputs.

We value potential, learning attitude and commitment to grow. You are not expected to know every engineering tool from the first day

Join the team building an AI-first company 🚀

AI is changing how we build products, support customers, work together, and solve problems. It helps us move faster, spend less time on repetitive work, and focus on bigger ideas.

Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles.

Your role at Hostinger

As Senior Product Manager on the Web Hosting team, you’ll own core parts of the platform that millions of websites, apps, and developers run on every day. This is a technical role: you’ll be just as comfortable talking about PHP versions, Node.js deployments, Git workflows, caching, and server performance as you are about conversion funnels and retention. Developers and vibe-coders are increasingly building with AI coding tools, and we want Hostinger to be the place where that code goes live. You’ll shape how people go from an idea or a local project to a fast, secure, production-ready site, and you’ll help define the strategy and roadmap rather than just execute it.

Curious to learn more? Meet your future Team Lead: Mario Bañares Colastra

Your day-to-day

  • Own key parts of Hostinger’s hosting and developer products end to end, from discovery and technical scoping to launch and growth.
  • Define the product strategy and roadmap for developer workflows: deployments, Git integration, staging and previews, databases, APIs and CLI tooling, and AI coding integrations.
  • Translate customer needs and technical constraints into clear, well-scoped requirements, and make informed trade-offs together with engineering on architecture, performance, and reliability.
  • Get hands-on: build prototypes, deploy test projects, use the product the way developers do, and test competitor products yourself.
  • Conduct client interviews and analyze product data to understand developer behaviour, activation, and business impact.
  • Work closely with infrastructure, engineering, design, and data teams to ship intuitive, reliable, and powerful experiences at scale.
  • Monitor customer feedback, developer communities, and the competitive landscape (cloud platforms, PaaS, edge, and AI-native builders) to identify opportunities.
  • Stay up to date with AI, developer tooling, and web infrastructure trends to keep Hostinger at the frontier.

Your expertise and experience

  • 5+ years of product experience, with a strong track record of owning technical or developer-facing products end to end.
  • Solid technical foundation: you understand how the web works (DNS, HTTP, SSL, web servers, databases, CDN and caching) and can hold your own in architecture discussions. An engineering background is a big plus.
  • Hands-on familiarity with modern developer workflows: any of Git, CI/CD, frameworks like Next.js or Laravel, WordPress, containers, and/or AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, or similar.
  • Strong data mindset: comfortable working with analytics, SQL, and experiments to drive decisions.
  • Willingness to try and test new tools and approaches in the industry to maintain a good understanding of the market.
  • Strong sense of ownership: you drive things forward proactively rather than waiting to be guided, and you raise the bar for the people around you.
  • Thrive in a dynamic, always-changing environment and navigate ambiguity comfortably.
  • Comfortable working with international teams and users; experience with products serving global markets is a big plus.
  • Experience building applied AI or developer platform products would be an advantage.
  • Fluent English language skills.

Meet your team

The Web Hosting team runs one of Hostinger’s core products, the platform behind millions of websites and the foundation that many of our other products build on. You’ll work with engineers, designers, and product managers on the team, and collaborate closely with infrastructure, AI, and other product teams to turn technical capabilities into products customers love..

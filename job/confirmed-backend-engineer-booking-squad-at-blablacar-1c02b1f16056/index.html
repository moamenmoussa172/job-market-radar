About the job

About BlaBlaCar
BlaBlaCar is the world’s leading community-based travel app enabling 40 million active members to share rides across 41 countries each year. Its technology matches drivers with empty seats with passengers heading the same way, so they can share the cost of the trip.
BlaBlaCar’s mission is to be the go-to marketplace for shared travel, bringing carpooling, buses, and trains together in one place. Globally, the BlaBlaCar Group also includes Obilet, the leading online bus ticketing platform in Turkey. By partnering with over 20,000 operators, the Group offers a wide choice of affordable and sustainable transport solutions.
In 2025, the BlaBlaCar community connected 2.7 million meeting points worldwide and enabled 138 million connections between travelers. Drivers saved 568 million euros by carpooling, and all of the BlaBlaCar Group’s mobility services contributed to avoiding 4 million tonnes of CO2 emissions.

Your Mission

We are looking for a confirmed Backend Engineer to join the Booking team at BlaBlaCar. Their mission is to optimize our multimodal (Carpool, Bus, and Train supplies) booking and checkout experience for passengers, by integrating and unifying multiple heterogeneous inventory sources from external partners.

As confirmed Backend Engineer, your role will be to work on technical challenges and product feature developments from early-stage conception, to development, testing strategy, deployment in production and monitoring. You will be working with a full-stack team of 10 developers.

Your responsibilities

  • Lead the development of our new impactful features, from the early technical design to the actual rollout
  • Take part in the design definition of the services and their APIs to ensure they are scalable, robust and easy to use by Frontend developers
  • Interact with multiple business, product and engineering stakeholders from different roles, backgrounds and culture
  • You’re definitely a team player, being eager to share and learn from your teammates and to grow altogether as a team

Your qualifications

  • You have 5+ years of experience from companies with a strong engineering culture, within a growth context
  • You are fluent in Java and know frameworks such as Spring, Spring Boot, JUnit, and Mockito
  • You have hands-on experience with Git, Docker, and Kubernetes, and monitoring solutions (Datadog)
  • You know what service-oriented architecture looks like, and you can determine when to apply a given architectural pattern
  • You have good communication skills, you’re humble and you're a team player
  • You are able to take ownership, aligned with business priorities
  • Full working proficiency in English
  • Fit with our BlaBlaPrinciples

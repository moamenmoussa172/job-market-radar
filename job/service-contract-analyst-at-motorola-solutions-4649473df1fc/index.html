Department Overview

Service Contracts & Subscriptions Operations (SCSO) organization provides financial and operational support for the North American Managed & Support Services business.


Job Description

· Assist with the administration of assigned contracts and related submittals

· Coordinating preparation of assigned documentation through internal approval process

· Monitoring contract submittals to ensure timely and coordinated responses and compliance with terms and conditions

· Contract loading, Contract corrections and changes, database management.

· Serving as a liaison for Field Service, Finance, Credit, Order Management, CMSO, and Product Management departments

· Communicating across MSI functional organization and Service Partners to resolve any contract.

· Maintaining efficient cycle time of processing contracts through measured results and case management

· Communicating with NA team throughout contract process


Basic Requirements

· Bachelor degree in Finance, Administration or related field

· English B2 level

· Keen attention to detail and problem solving abilities

· Team player with strong communications skills

Preferred Skills:

· 1+ years of contract administration, help desk or related experience

· Experience with common computer applications (Microsoft Office, Google Work Suite, Windows)

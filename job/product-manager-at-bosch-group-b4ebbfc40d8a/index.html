About the job

Drive and enable go to market strategy to cover local market specifics based on existing products. Bridge between local market needs to get the “right” product/value proposition and bring product to the market to realize revenue, volume and profitability. This position is responsible for all aspects of product management including product roadmap, product portfolio, suggested prices, margins, discount structure, influence of manufacturing site and life cycle management.

Product Strategy

  • Collect market needs and requirements, customer feedback about existing products, conduct market and competitor analysis, observe future developments and trends in market (customers, competitors, technology, …) as base for portfolio development.
  • Get confirmation from sales on sales figures for business case calculation and pilot customer selection
  • Bridge market needs to define product requirements and deliverables (performance targets, target market, target pricing, quality standard, ROI valuations for new projects/products.
  • Manage price list, discount structure, profitability of all current products, pricing of new products, costing of products, market competitiveness, etc.

Drive products launch

  • Drive launch of new products
  • Drive and enable sales management to exploit opportunities with new products
  • Advice on sales management on price positioning
  • Define product development timing and delivery of validated products to commercialization. Communicate status, keep projects on time and budget.
  • Support of sales initiatives by identifying optimal solutions and proposing pricing policies

Product Processes & Tools

  • Work to enhance processes and tools to improve speed and step up beyond average market requirement to gain a competitive advantage
  • Input on assortment / lead time requirements to support assortment management
  • In collaboration with Marketing drive promotion & marketing of product portfolio (support exhibitions, customer events, …)
  • Bachelor's degree in engineering or related field
  • Over 5 years experience in the hydraulics industry and market knowledge
  • Advanced knowledge of the product portfolio
  • Strong leadership skills with the ability to motivate and drive accomplishment
  • Excellent interpersonal skills
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills to communicate up and down the organization
  • Ability to prioritize and accomplish tasks
  • Advanced MS Office Suite skills
  • Knowledge about product strategy development and management
  • Capability of defining the product target portfolio

Ability to travel domestic and internationally, up to 25%.

Salary range is $115,000 to $130,000 annually, plus bonus.

Indefinite U.S. work authorized individuals only. Future sponsorship for work authorization unavailable.

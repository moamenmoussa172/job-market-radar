Job Description:

The opportunity

We are looking for a professional Network Manager Trainer – SCADA and Data Engineering to deliver high-quality technical training and contribute to the development of training capabilities within Network Control. The goal is to ensure that customers and internal engineers acquire the knowledge, skills, and competencies required to effectively understand, configure, and use Network Manager SCADA and Data Engineering functionalities.

How you´ll make an impact

  • Deliver and adapt engaging customer training on Network Manager SCADA and Data Engineering to meet varying audience needs, skill levels, and learning objectives.
  • Organize and deliver internal training and knowledge-sharing sessions in collaboration with Technical Practice Leaders and subject matter experts, identifying training needs and relevant technical topics.
  • Collaborate with instructional designers and technical experts to continuously improve training materials, course structure, practical exercises, and learning methods.
  • Provide technical expertise to ensure training content remains accurate, relevant, and aligned with Network Manager functionality and engineering best practices.
  • Develop, maintain, and enhance practical exercises, demonstrations, documentation, and supporting course materials that strengthen the learning experience.
  • Coach and support engineers in developing their training and presentation capabilities, while promoting knowledge sharing and lessons learned across Network Control.
  • Drive continuous improvement of training standards, methodologies, and best practices based on experience, learner feedback, and organizational needs.

Your background

  • Strong technical knowledge and hands-on experience with Network Manager SCADA and/or Data Engineering, including a solid understanding of SCADA concepts, data engineering processes, and their practical application.
  • Proven experience delivering technical training, workshops, and knowledge-sharing sessions, with the ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly and effectively.
  • Ability to adapt training content, learning methods, and presentation styles to different audiences, skill levels, and learning objectives.
  • Experience developing training materials, practical exercises, and realistic Network Manager use cases, with Microsoft PowerPoint as a primary training support tool.
  • Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with Technical Practice Leaders, subject matter experts, instructional designers, and engineering teams.
  • Continuous improvement mindset, combined with the ability to coach and develop other engineers’ training capabilities and contribute effectively in an international English-speaking environment.

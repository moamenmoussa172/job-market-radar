Your Responsibilities

As a Part Time Student - Software Engineer for John Deere Intelligent Solutions located in Waterloo, IA or Cary, NC you will:

  • Support the development of tools & scripts for increasing efficiency and user experience in John Deere's OpenRTOS based embedded software development.
  • Participate in innovation efforts to define the next generation of tools used by the entire embedded development community at Deere.
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders

This position is NOT available to students on immigration visas

What Skills You Need

  • Currently enrolled in, or working toward completion of, a degree in Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering. Computer Science, Engineering, Agricultural Engineering, or equivalent embedded systems experience.
  • Must be registered as a full-time student at a U.S. local accredited college/university.
  • Expertise in embedded software development workflow.
  • Object-Oriented skills with languages like C# or Java.
  • Knowledgeable in Embedded C and scripting languages.
  • Graduation date of Spring 2028 or later.
  • Cumulative GPA of 2.8 or above.
  • Available to work during the academic year 16-20 hours/weekly.
  • Available to work during the summer semester 30-40 hours/weekly.
  • Must be able to commute to the work location in Waterloo, IA or Cary, NC on a daily basis year-round.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with C# and/or DevOps practices and tools
  • Ability to grogram using Python, Perl, and JavaScript
  • Working toward advanced degree in an engineering discipline or computer
  • Experience with Artificial Intelligence skills, agents or MCP

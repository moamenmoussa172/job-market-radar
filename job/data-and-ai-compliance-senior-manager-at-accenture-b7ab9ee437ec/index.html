Data & AI Compliance Senior Manager

About the job

WHO WE ARE:

Accenture is a leading global professional services company that helps the world’s leading businesses, governments, and other organizations build their digital core, optimize their operations, accelerate revenue growth, and enhance citizen services. We offer solutions and assets across Strategy & Consulting, Technology, Operations, Industry X, and Accenture Song.

THE WORK:

As Senior Manager, Data & AI Compliance, you will play a key role in shaping and leading Accenture’s Data & Artificial Intelligence Compliance Program, working closely with the Associate Director, Data & AI Compliance.

  • Maintain and continuously enhance a global compliance program covering data governance and artificial intelligence, aligned with Accenture’s compliance framework.
  • Translate evolving legal, regulatory, policy and voluntary requirements into practical policies, standards and controls, while staying forward-thinking and growth-driven.
  • Develop and conduct compliance reviews and assessments, with particular focus on higher-risk areas, while supporting risk, quality management and post-market monitoring activities.
  • Partner with Legal, Responsible AI, Information Security, risk and other teams to integrate Data & AI compliance into new services, products and initiatives.
  • Support client-facing activities, including compliance assessments and contractual matters, providing pragmatic and business-focused guidance.
  • Lead and collaborate with a global team of professionals, creating opportunities for continuous learning and knowledge sharing.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior business stakeholders, legal teams, external experts and clients, and represent the function in selected committees and working groups.
  • Monitor developments in technology and Data & AI governance and turn emerging insights into guidance, training and practical action, supporting ongoing improvement across the organization.

WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU?

  • Take a central role in a global Data & AI compliance program and expand your expertise at the intersection of technology, governance, regulation and responsible AI.
  • Collaborate with senior leaders and multidisciplinary teams across Legal, Responsible AI, Information Security, risk and business functions, gaining broad exposure to complex global compliance matters.
  • Deepen your knowledge of evolving Data & AI regulation and technology while continuously developing your leadership and governance skills.

HERE’S WHAT YOU’LL NEED:

  • 6+ years of relevant experience in compliance, ethics or governance, ideally gained within a multinational organization and/or major law firm.
  • Strong knowledge of data governance and data management principles, together with practical understanding of AI-related policy, legal and regulatory requirements.
  • 3+ years of team leadership experience, with the ability to develop and operationalize strategies in a global environment.
  • Experience monitoring regulatory and technology developments and translating complex requirements into practical recommendations, policies and controls.
  • Strong stakeholder management, communication and decision-making skills, including the ability to work effectively with senior stakeholders and global, cross-cultural teams.
  • Fluent English.

Research indicates that some candidates, especially the most diverse ones, may hesitate to apply for positions if they don't meet all requirements. If you believe you possess the necessary skills, even if not meeting every requirement, we wholeheartedly encourage you to submit your application.

BONUS POINTS IF YOU HAVE:

  • A JD or equivalent law degree, or an M.Sc. or comparable degree in business informatics, data science, information management or a related field.
  • Relevant certifications in areas such as data privacy, data governance, audit, artificial intelligence or information security.
  • Strong project management and organizational experience, together with an established professional network of experts and/or regulators.

WHAT WE OFFER?

  • Permanent employment contract.
  • Individual support of a People Lead and a specific path of professional development, as well as the possibility of a session with a Coach.
  • A wide training package including soft, technical and language training, access to e-learning platforms, Gallup test, GenAI training, and the possibility of co-financing courses and certifications.
  • Employee Assistance Program including legal, financial and psychological consultations.
  • Accenture employees eligible for the Employee Share Purchase Plan automatically become eligible for quarterly dividends if they own company shares.
  • Paid employee referral program.
  • Private medical care and life insurance.
  • Access to the MyBenefit platform, including the possibility of using a wide range of products and services such as the Multisport card.
  • Company car.

About the job

As a Network Planning Analyst, you'll bring energy and fresh thinking to how we plan and deliver within our network. Working closely with your team and planning partners, you'll tackle defined challenges with creativity by conducting research and analysis that drives real improvements. This is your opportunity to make a meaningful impact on our supply chain, helping deliver a better customer experience while improving cost and energy efficiency.
Key job responsibilities
• Drive creative solutions to defined team challenges that improve efficiency, quality, or customer experience.
• Build strong relationships with planning partners, actively contributing to cross-functional coordination.
• Drive execution of improvement initiatives, proactively tracking milestones, surfacing insights, and recommending adjustments to accelerate outcomes.
• Provide standard work process execution and functionality support to routing and planning execution teams.
A day in the life
Being a Network Planning Analyst for Amazon involves solving lots of complex challenges. You’ll spend time resolving temporary issues and looking at the bigger picture to drive Amazon towards achieving new objectives. This role is dynamic, and you’ll have the opportunity to help forecast and plan for new projects. As a Network Planning Analyst, no two days will be the same. Having so much variety in your role means you’ll be able to learn something new every day.
About the team
Global Logistics International Crossborder Operations Network (ICON) is a team of highly experienced logistics professionals, carrier specialists, and program managers focused on improving the speed and reliability of Amazon’s cross-border logistics capabilities.
The team is responsible for executing the timely movement of freight from global manufacturing hubs cross-border into Amazon’s international marketplaces for Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) sellers and Retail direct import vendors. Leveraging the capabilities of world-class logistics providers and internal Amazon supply chain solutions, Global Logistics increases selection and lowers cost for our customers.
Work hard, have fun, and make history at Global Logistics!

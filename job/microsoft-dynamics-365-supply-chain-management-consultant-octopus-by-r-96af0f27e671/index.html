About the job

About Octopus by RTG

Octopus by RTG is the tech hiring and outsourcing arm of Robusta Technology Group, dedicated to connecting exceptional tech talent with top-tier organizations across the MENA, GCC, Europe, the US, and Canada. We specialize in building strong, long-term partnerships between skilled professionals and innovative companies. Our mission is to empower growth, innovation, and excellence by matching the right talent with the right opportunities.

Currently, we are hiring a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Consultant for one of our partner organizations in KSA, offering the opportunity to contribute to exciting projects within a dynamic and forward-thinking environment.

The Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant is responsible for implementing, configuring, and optimizing Inventory Management and Procurement & Sourcing modules within Microsoft Dynamics 365. This role ensures that inventory and procurement processes are efficient, compliant, and fully aligned with business objectives, while delivering scalable and sustainable supply chain solutions.

Main Responsibilities

  • Requirements Gathering: Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand and document requirements related to inventory, procurement, and supply chain operations.
  • Solution Design & Configuration: Configure and customize Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management modules, including Inventory Management and Procurement & Sourcing, to meet business needs.
  • Process Optimization: Analyze current inventory flows, procurement cycles, and vendor management processes, and recommend improvements and best practices.
  • Implementation & Deployment: Lead or support end-to-end implementation activities, including system setup, data migration, integrations, and cutover planning.
  • User Training & Support: Deliver end-user training sessions, develop user guides and documentation, and provide ongoing functional support.
  • Testing & Quality Assurance: Prepare test scenarios, execute functional testing, support user acceptance testing (UAT), and ensure solution quality and readiness.
  • Continuous Improvement: Stay up to date with Dynamics 365 enhancements, recommend system improvements, and support post–go-live optimization initiatives.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365FO), specifically Supply Chain Management.
  • Strong functional expertise in Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, with a focus on Inventory Management and Procurement & Sourcing modules.
  • Microsoft certifications such as Dynamics 365 Functional Consultant or Solution Architect are preferred.
  • Proven experience configuring workflows, purchasing policies, vendor catalogs, inventory controls, and approval processes.
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to engage and manage stakeholders effectively.
  • Fluency in both Arabic and English is required.

Sr. Product Manager - Foodics ERP

Foodics · Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 13 September 2026

Apply on Foodics's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Who Are We❓

We Are Foodics! a leading restaurant management ecosystem and payment tech provider. Founded in 2014 with headquarters in Riyadh and offices across 5 countries, including UAE, Egypt, Jordan and Kuwait. We are currently serving customers and partners in over 35 different countries worldwide. Our innovative products have successfully processed over 6 billion (yes, billion with a B) orders so far! making Foodics one of the most rapidly evolving SaaS companies to ever emerge from the MENA region. Also, Foodics has achieved three rounds of funding, with the latest raising $170 million in the largest SaaS funding round in MENA, boosting its innovation capabilities to better serve business owners.

What Will You Do❓

  • Define and execute the product strategy for Foodics ERP across the Middle East and Egypt.
  • Build an AI Native ERP platform deeply integrated with the Foodics Financial Services ecosystem.
  • Ensure the product supports end-to-end financial workflows for restaurants, from transaction capture to financial reporting.
  • Design ERP workflows compliant with regional tax authorities, including:
  • ZATCA (Saudi Arabia)
  • UAE VAT regulations
  • Egyptian Tax Authority
  • Other regional compliance frameworks.
  • Ensure the system supports VAT, e-invoicing, and regulatory reporting requirements across markets.
  • Develop AI-driven automation rules for bookkeeping and reconciliation to minimize manual finance tasks.
  • Enable AI-powered categorization of transactions from POS, payments, and expenses.
  • Design smart, AI Native ERP workflows tailored for restaurant operations.
  • Ensure seamless integration with:
  • Foodics POS
  • Foodics Payments
  • Inventory & purchasing
  • Payroll and expenses
  • Banking integrations
  • Build APIs and integrations with external financial systems where necessary.
  • Translate product vision into roadmaps, PRDs, and execution plans.
  • Work closely with engineering, design, compliance, and financial experts.
  • Prioritize features based on customer value, regulatory requirements, and scalability.
  • Conduct market research on regional ERP solutions and regulatory environments.
  • Gather feedback from accountants, restaurant operators, and finance teams.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify financial workflows for SMEs and enterprise restaurant groups.

What Are We Looking For❓

  • 5-6 years of experience in Product Management; prior ERP experience is a must, and Fintech experience is a plus.
  • Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial reporting.
  • Experience building products with tax compliance or regulatory frameworks.
  • Ability to work with complex financial workflows and automation systems.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience working with cross-functional teams including engineering, compliance, and finance.

Benefits

What We Offer You❗

We believe you will love working at Foodics!

  • We offer highly competitive compensation packages, including bonuses and the potential for shares.
  • We prioritize personal development and offer regular training and an annual learning stipend to tackle new challenges and grow your career in a hyper-growth environment.
  • Join a talented team of over 30 nationalities working in 14 countries, and gain valuable experience in an exciting industry.
  • We offer autonomy, mentoring, and challenging goals that create incredible opportunities for both you and the company.

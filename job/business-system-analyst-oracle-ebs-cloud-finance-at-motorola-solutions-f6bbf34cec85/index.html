Job Description

Motorola Solutions is in the process of rolling out Coupa for Indirect Procurement globally. Coupa will be used for Supplier Onboarding, Risk Assess and Procure to Pay.

Business Systems Analyst with hands-on Coupa expertise will work in Motorola Solution’s Enterprise Core Technology to support Procurement and Finance Organizations.

Enterprise Core Technology team is accountable for the transformation, maintenance, and enhancement of Coupa platform to support Indirect Procurement. The team works closely with Procurement and Finance business groups globally to implement and support Procure to Pay, Supplier onboarding and Risk assessment processes, and adopts industry best practices to simplify user experience for internal employees and suppliers.

As a Business System Analyst, you will have the opportunity to play a key role in understanding business requirements, work with cross functional IT and business teams, and apply Coupa expertise/ industry knowledge to provide robust, configurable and scalable solutions.

Responsibilities:

Perform functional, technical and maintenance functions to include, but not be limited to the following:

  • Own and maintain Coupa Procure to Pay, SIM, Core and CRA processes (80% functional, 20% technical)
  • Manage key processes like Catalog management, Project and non project based procurement, Approval workflows, Accruals, Supplier collaboration through CSP, cXML, SAN
  • Manage Coupa integrations with Oracle ERP, Vertex, Workday, Servicenow and other boundary applications
  • Must be able to work with internal IT and lead external IT teams to adopt best practices to simplify user experience
  • Ability to debug the system for issues and provide management summary in business speaks for the issues, root cause, next steps in business terms
  • Should be able to work in fast pace, Agile environment and multiple projects simultaneously
  • Collaborate with business and IT stakeholders in US and EMEA time zones.
    In return for your expertise, we’ll support you in this new challenge with coaching & development every step of the way. Also, to reward your work, you’ll get the following:

  • Flexible working hours (usually the day starts between 7-10),
  • 8 hours working day (30 minutes lunch break included).
  • Hybrid work mode (2 days per week from KRK ofice)

  • A bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Finance or a related discipline
  • 5+ years of hands-on experience with Coupa P2P, SIM, Core, CRA modules with integration touchpoints with Oracle ERP
  • Excellent Interpersonal & communication skills, detail oriented and a self-starter

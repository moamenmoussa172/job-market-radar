Operations Execution Analyst II

Capgemini · Remote

Fully remote · Data Analyst · Posted 28 October 2026

Apply on Capgemini's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Mexico.

Job Summary

The Operations Execution Analyst will support the Business Operations Strategy & Solutions (BOSS) team in the execution of strategic initiatives and operational improvements across Service Operations. This role will partner closely with Project Managers, executive sponsors, IT, Workforce, and other cross-functional stakeholders to ensure key initiatives remain aligned, on track, and successfully delivered.

The ideal candidate is a highly organized and proactive professional with strong leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills. This individual should be comfortable managing multiple high-priority projects, driving timelines and deliverables, facilitating meetings with stakeholders at different levels of the organization, and developing and delivering effective presentations. The role will also contribute to process improvement, change management, and the integration of AI tools into workflows.

Requirements

Lead and support medium to large-scale strategic and operational initiatives from planning through execution.

Manage project timelines, deliverables, dependencies, risks, and stakeholder responsibilities across multiple concurrent initiatives.

Partner closely with Project Managers, IT, Workforce, and other cross-functional teams to support successful project delivery.

Facilitate meetings with executive sponsors, business leaders, and cross-functional stakeholders, ensuring alignment and clear follow-up on actions and deliverables.

Develop and deliver clear, visually effective presentations and executive-level communications.

Support process improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities, analyzing business needs, and recommending practical solutions.

Support change management activities, including stakeholder engagement, impact analysis, readiness, communication, and implementation.

Explore and support the integration of AI tools, including Microsoft Copilot, into workflows and operational processes.

Proactively identify potential issues, risks, and dependencies and escalate them appropriately.

Share best practices, provide guidance to team members, and contribute to continuous improvement across the organization.

Build strong relationships across different levels of the organization and act as a connector between business stakeholders and delivery teams.

Qualifications

7–9 years of total professional experience required; 9+ years preferred.

4–6 years of relevant experience in project management, change management, strategic support, business operations, or related areas; 7–9 years preferred.

Experience managing multiple projects, timelines, deliverables, stakeholders, risks, and dependencies.

Advanced stakeholder management and executive-level communication skills.

Strong meeting facilitation and presentation skills, including experience presenting to senior-level stakeholders.

Strong organizational, strategic thinking, and problem-solving capabilities.

Demonstrated leadership skills and the ability to drive initiatives without requiring direct authority over stakeholders.

Strong curiosity and willingness to ask questions, challenge existing processes, and identify opportunities for improvement.

Experience supporting process improvement and organizational change initiatives.

Intermediate proficiency with Microsoft Office tools.

Familiarity with AI productivity tools, particularly Microsoft Copilot, is preferred.

Fluent English required.

Bachelor's degree preferred; no specific degree is required.

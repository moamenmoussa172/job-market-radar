About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

You will join the Digital Commerce Acceleration team, which builds the platform that automates important commerce and revenue processes across Pure. DCA connects systems such as Salesforce, Pure1, Azure Marketplace, and other internal and external services to turn business events into reliable, repeatable workflows.

You will work on an event-driven, Java-based platform built from Spring Boot services and cloud infrastructure. The platform processes signals, applies business rules and decisioning, and executes actions across enterprise systems. Your work will contribute directly to capabilities such as subscription lifecycle automation, quoting, partner commerce, revenue recognition, and AI-enabled commerce workflows.

This is an opportunity to work on meaningful business problems while developing your skills across distributed systems, integrations, cloud platforms, automation, AI-assisted development, AI agents, and production software engineering.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Dsign, develop, test, and maintain production-quality backend services and integrations.
  • Design, prototype, test, and improve agentic AI workflows that use tools, APIs, and enterprise data to automate engineering or commerce tasks, with appropriate guardrails, permissions, observability, and human-in-the-loop controls.
  • Deliver well-scoped features, fixes, and improvements through the full software development lifecycle.
  • Implement event-driven workflows using APIs, messaging, and asynchronous processing patterns.
  • Build and maintain integrations with enterprise systems such as Salesforce, Pure1, and Azure Marketplace.
  • Work with Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Kafka, relational databases, and cloud-native services.
  • Contribute to orchestration logic that manages business journeys, retries, error handling, and reliable state transitions.
  • Write unit, integration, and end-to-end tests and use automation to protect product quality.
  • Participate in technical design discussions, code reviews, backlog refinement, and sprint planning.
  • Investigate defects and production issues, using logs, metrics, traces, and runbooks to identify and resolve problems.
  • Contribute to CI/CD, deployment, observability, and operational improvements.
  • Document technical decisions, service behavior, and operational procedures.
  • Use Claude Code or similar AI-assisted engineering tools responsibly to explore designs, implement well-scoped changes, generate and improve tests, troubleshoot issues, and maintain code quality, while validating outputs through human review, tests, and security checks.
  • Collaborate with product managers, architects, engineers, and partner teams across Pure.
  • Learn the business domain and progressively take ownership of components and features with appropriate team support.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Professional experience developing and delivering software in a production environment.
  • Strong programming experience in Java or a comparable backend language, with a willingness to work primarily in Java.
  • Experience designing, implementing, and testing REST APIs or service integrations.
  • Understanding of asynchronous processing, messaging, distributed systems, or event-driven architecture.
  • Working knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
  • Experience with Git, pull requests, code reviews, and automated build or deployment pipelines.
  • A practical approach to testing, debugging, code quality, and maintainability.
  • Hands-on interest or experience using Claude Code or similar AI coding assistants to plan, implement, test, and debug software, with sound judgment about verification, security, data handling, and human review.
  • Experience working in an Agile or similarly collaborative software development environment.
  • Ability to take ownership of clearly defined work, make progress with reasonable oversight, and communicate risks early.
  • Willingness to receive feedback, learn new technologies, and contribute positively to the team.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills in an international environment.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Prague, Czech Republic office in compliance with Everpure's policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

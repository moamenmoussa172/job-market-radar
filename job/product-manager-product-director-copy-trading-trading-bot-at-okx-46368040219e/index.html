About the job

OKX will be prioritising applicants who have a current right to work in Singapore, and do not require OKX's sponsorship of a visa.

Who We Are

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er.

About The Opportunity

The trading bot domain has emerged as one of OKX's most critical and rapidly growing sectors over the past two years. It constitutes a significant share of our platform's daily active traders and trading volume. With this swift growth, escalating user demands, and a promising future for this business, we are committed to allocating more resources to this sector. Our aim is to build top-tier products and ultimately grow this line into a billion-dollar enterprise.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Develop strategies to boost trading bot volume and user acquisition. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including design, engineering, and data analysis.
  • Based on trading users' needs, design trading bots efficient for particular market situations.
  • Conduct user research and competitor assessments to identify potential opportunities for product innovation and optimization, enhancing user conversion and retention.
  • Advocate for robust engineering solutions and scalable product/system architecture designs, ensuring efficient new product development.
  • Manage projects with efficient scope, timeline, and stakeholder coordination, prioritizing quick iterations and impactful outcomes.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor's degree in a relevant field or equivalent practical experience, preferably in Financial Engineering, Mathematics or Engineering.
  • 5+ years of product management experience, particularly within the crypto or internet industry.
  • Strong grasp of strategy design and backend logic.
  • Passion for problem-solving and delivering exceptional user experiences.
  • Self-motivated, independent, persistent, objective, with strong logical thinking, capable of project ownership.
  • Curious with a strong ability for self-learning, open to exploring innovative projects for driving product growth.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to influence and present ideas effectively.

Nice-To-Haves

  • Experience in Crypto/FX/Stocks trading, including quant trading, bot trading, or technical analysis.
  • Strong understanding of the industry and market dynamics.
  • Fluency in Mandarin will be a plus to work with cross tech teams based in Greater China Region.

Perks & Benefits

  • Competitive total compensation package
  • L&D programs and Education subsidy for employees' growth and development
  • Various team building programs and company events
  • Wellness and meal allowances
  • Comprehensive healthcare schemes for employees and dependants
  • More that we love to tell you along the process!

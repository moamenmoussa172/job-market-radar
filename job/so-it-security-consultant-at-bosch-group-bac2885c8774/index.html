About the job

We are looking for a Cybersecurity expert to strengthen our IT Security Governance function, with a focus on ensuring compliance with internal regulations and industry standards while enabling secure and resilient business operations.

What You’ll do:​

  • Implement, and maintain the organization’s cybersecurity strategy, policies, and procedures.​
  • Strengthen the enterprise’s security posture by safeguarding applications.​
  • Identify and mitigate security risks, addressing emerging threats.​
  • Support vulnerability management, penetration testing, and continuous monitoring programs for IT applications.​
  • Ensure compliance with relevant internal and external regulations and frameworks.​
  • Collaborate with IT, legal, ISP, software development teams to create documentation in accordance with internal regulations.​

Technical​

  • Education: Degree in Information Security, Computer Science, or related field.​
  • Minimum working experience: 3 years​
  • Proven experience in IT security management or cybersecurity role such as ISP internal Audit, IT Operations, Compliance, IT Security​, Network Security, Cloud Security
  • Familiar with cybersecurity work products such as SoTA, TARA, SBOM, etc.​
  • Knowledge of security frameworks, risk management, and compliance requirements.​
  • Language: Advanced English (Good communication, writing)​
  • Relevant certifications such as: CISM, ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer / Lead Auditor, ISO 31000 Risk Management

Soft-skill:​

  • Customer Service Mindset​
  • Self-motivation​
  • A proactive mindset and passion for cybersecurity​
  • Creativity​
  • Detail orientation thinking​
  • Ability to focus as well as multi-task handling​
  • Assertive and objective communication​

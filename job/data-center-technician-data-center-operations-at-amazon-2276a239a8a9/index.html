Data Center Technician, Data Center Operations

Amazon · Zaragoza, Spain

Onsite · Other data role · Posted 20 November 2026

About the job

Are you passionate about keeping the digital world running smoothly? Join our AWS Infrastructure Services team and become a critical guardian of cloud technology! You'll be at the heart of maintaining the infrastructure that powers global innovation, solving complex technical challenges, and ensuring uninterrupted service for millions of customers worldwide.
Technicians in our Data Center Operations team perform hands-on hardware maintenance, including server installation, replacement, and troubleshooting of failed components like hard drives, memory modules, and network cards.
*Please note that this role involves working on a shift rotation that includes working early mornings, nights, weekends, and public holidays.
Our sites are often in areas without public transportation links and would require access to your own transportation to the work location.
Key job responsibilities
- Diagnose and resolve complex hardware and network infrastructure issues with precision and efficiency
- Conduct thorough root cause analysis for equipment failures and implement preventative solutions
- Maintain rigorous safety standards and contribute to a proactive safety culture
- Perform physical repairs and equipment maintenance across data center environments
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive comprehensive technical solutions
A day in the life
Your day will be dynamic and impactful, diving into the intricate world of data center operations. You'll navigate through hardware diagnostics, network troubleshooting, and collaborative problem-solving, working alongside a diverse team of technical experts to maintain the robust infrastructure that keeps AWS running seamlessly.
About the team
We are a collaborative and innovative group dedicated to maintaining the backbone of cloud computing. Our team represents a diverse mix of technical professionals who share a common goal: delivering reliable, secure, and efficient infrastructure that supports global technological advancement. We value creativity, teamwork, and continuous learning.
- High school or equivalent diploma
- Knowledge of server hardware and components
- Experience with computer hardware troubleshooting and repair
- Experience dealing effectively with customers during problem resolution and operating efficiently under pressure

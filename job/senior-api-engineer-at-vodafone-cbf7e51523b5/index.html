What you’ll do

As a Senior API Engineer, you will work in a team of REST API engineers, closely with the Engineering Manager, to design and implement Vodafone’s high-volume, high-impact microservices. You will apply an agile mindset and iterative ways of working within the engineering team. You will bring advanced, hands-on AI engineering skills to modernise applications through AI-enabled analysis, design, development and testing, while maintaining technical quality, security and human oversight.

More specifically you will:
• Take technical ownership of REST API and microservice solutions guiding iterative delivery, resolving dependencies and ensuring scalable, reliable outcomes.
• Translate requirements into low-level technical designs, providing clear direction for implementation and testing.
• Apply advanced generative AI and agentic engineering workflows to understand, redesign and rebuild core IT applications, validating generated code and preserving functional integrity.
• Coach fellow API engineers through design and code reviews, sharing expertise and strengthening technical judgement and engineering practices.
• Partner with the Engineering Manager to uphold technical standards, security and delivery quality, surfacing risks and ensuring appropriate oversight of AI-enabled development.
• Help shape the domain’s technical roadmap by identifying modernisation opportunities and improvements to architecture, platforms and tooling that build long-term engineering capability.

Who you are

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Engineering or other related field.
  • 4-6 years of experience in design and development of RESTful APIs using cloud-friendly technologies like Quarkus, Spring Boot, or any other JVM-based technology.
  • Demonstrated ability to use AI-enabled workflows to understand existing codebases, produce technical specifications, rebuild or refactor applications, and generate and validate automated tests.
  • Ability to design and integrate AI-assisted or agentic engineering workflows into the software development lifecycle, with clear evaluation criteria, human review and secure handling of code and data.
  • Strong understanding of secure software delivery, cloud-native architectures and engineering quality controls, with the judgement to identify and correct inaccurate or unsafe AI-generated outputs.
  • Deep understanding of RESTful principles (WSDL understanding is a plus but not required).
  • Familiarity with tools like PostgreSQL, Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, Drools, Apache Camel will be highly appreciated
  • Experience applying an agile mindset and iterative ways of working within an engineering team, including incremental delivery, feedback, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, in both Greek & English and with both technical and business audiences
  • Fulfilled military services (for male candidates)

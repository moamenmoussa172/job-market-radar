About the job

Join Us

When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact.

What you’ll do

Being part of an agile team, the SR API Engineer contributes to designing and implementing Vodafone’s high volume and high impact microservices for mobile/web applications.

More specifically you will:

  • Work as part of a team and develop highly performant microservices based on the JVM
  • Develop scalable solutions - use the latest in Tech to handle large traffic spikes and millions of REST calls per microservice per day
  • Improve the core Vodafone infrastructure (Docker, Kubernetes, Java) to deliver value to technical business and improve the Cluster Health
  • Contribute, as member of an agile team, in the research, analysis, design development and testing of solutions that best suit business and technology strategy
  • Contribute to the development of in-house tools to facilitate development and maximize developer happiness
  • Ensure technical integrity and quality control consistency throughout the development life cycle
  • Participate in code reviews and promulgate knowledge
  • Transform functional requirements to technical user stories and assign business value
  • Collaborate with mobile and web development teams to develop features from conception to launch
  • Help in removing technical impediments raised by team

Who you are

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Engineering or other related field
  • At least 3-5 years of experience in design and development of RESTful APIs using a cloud-friendly technology like Quarkus, Spring Boot, or any other JVM-based technology
  • Have a deep understanding of RESTful principles (WSDL understanding is a plus, but not required)
  • Familiarity with Document Storages (like MongoDB) will be highly appreciated
  • Have a grasp of Service-Oriented Architectures (SOA), Microservices, and distributed systems
  • Ability to grasp Software Engineering concepts at scale
  • Be able to work in a fast-moving team which values communication & team spirit
  • Demonstrated experience in problem solving, self-motivated and self-managing with strong interpersonal skills
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, in both Greek & English and with both technical and business audiences
  • Open-source contributions will be considered a plus
  • Working experience in agile environments/teams will be highly appreciated

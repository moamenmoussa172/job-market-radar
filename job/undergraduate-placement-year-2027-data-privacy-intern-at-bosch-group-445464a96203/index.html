Undergraduate Placement Year 2027 – Data Privacy Intern

Bosch Group · Worcester, United Kingdom

Onsite · Intern / Student · Data Governance / Privacy · Posted 4 November 2026

About the job

Join us for your placement year in summer 2027! Our internships are designed to give you an insight into what it's like to work for a world-renowned brand with a culture of support and community.

Are you ready for a new challenge?

We are seeking an ambitious new member to join our Data Security Team - Is it You?

The Data Security Team support and advise the business on Information Security and Data Protection topics whilst ensuring that the business remain compliant with European and National Laws.

Do you want to help our business maintain compliance with GDPR?.

Do you have the ability to manage your own caseload of Data Subject Requests for Bosch in the UK?.

Do you like researching and consulting with others on data privacy issues?

In this role, you will be responsible for:

  • Managing the Data Privacy mailbox.
  • Actioning requests through the Data Security reporting tool.
  • Managing Data Subject Requests.
  • Carrying out Office inspections in support of the company Clear Desk Policy.
  • Supporting the ISP Manager with related project work

Interested? Upload your CV and a Cover Letter today!

Please note that to be eligible for this role, you must be an undergraduate student seeking a placement year as part of your degree - if you do not meet these criteria please consider applying for other positions.

Our ideal candidate must be:

  • Studying for a degree in Law or an Information Technology related subject and have an interest in Data Privacy.
  • A confident and enthusiastic individual who wants to challenge themselves.
  • Attentive to detail and have clear methodical ways of working.

Our ideal candidate should be:

  • Able to work to deadlines and be flexible whilst being self-motivated and can work well as part of a team
  • Able to create and deliver awareness content on-line or in-person to groups across the business.

Right To Work:please ensure you have a valid Right To Work in the UK status for the entirety of the placement year upon submitting an application.

Deadline for Applications: 27/11/2026

Please note, we reserve the right to close this vacancy at any time so we encourage candidates to submit their applications as soon as possible.

Expected Start Date: July 2027

Please note that this date is advisory and can be flexible within reason.

Contract Duration: 1 year

Working Hours: 39 hours per week – standard working pattern of 08:00 – 17:00 Monday to Thursday and 08:00 – 16:00 Friday with a 1-hour lunchbreak

Working with us comes with the following benefits:

  • 20 days annual leave per year plus bank holidays
  • Access to the Perkbox Benefits platform
  • Discounts on products from across the Bosch Group
  • Enhanced pension contributions
  • Access to Bosch Social Club membership offering you and up to 5 friends and family discounts on excursions and events
  • Volunteering days
  • Policy to support growing families
  • Health & wellbeing support including: over 200 trained mental health first aid champions, free of charge employee assistance programme
  • And more!

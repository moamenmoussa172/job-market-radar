About the job

With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven.

Customer Solutions Architect

About the role

The Customer Solutions Architect (CSA) is the primary post-sales technical owner for our existing customers, helping them get the most value from the combined Fivetran and dbt platform. Working alongside Account Executives (AEs) and Solutions Engineers (SEs), the CSA makes sure customers not only deploy successfully, but keep scaling and expanding their usage over time.

This role operates as a technical account manager and strategic advisor, bridging the gap between onboarding success and long-term growth. The CSA owns the technical narrative after go-live: monitoring an existing book of business, leading technical discussions, uncovering new data challenges, and guiding customers through architecture evolution, adoption, and expansion through live demos and technical workshops.

Unlike implementation-focused roles, the CSA is accountable for both retention and growth, and is measured on incremental ARR, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR). You will act as a trusted advisor, advocate, and accelerator, ensuring customers derive maximum value from their data investments while aligning their technical roadmap with the platform's capabilities.

Location: New York City, NY (hybrid). This is a full-time position, and candidates must be located in the greater NYC area and able to work in the office two days a week (Tuesday and Thursday).

What you'll do

Product Adoption and Customer Value Realization

  • Own post-sales technical engagement across a portfolio of assigned Commercial and Enterprise accounts
  • Drive adoption, optimization, and ongoing usage of the platform, moving customers after initial deployment to sustained, scalable success
  • Educate customers on new products and features as they launch, and on existing capabilities they may not be using
  • Deliver enablement sessions, workshops, and best-practice guidance
  • Monitor account health signals (usage, performance, support trends) and proactively intervene to address churn or contraction risks, including running implementation reviews and healthcheck sessions

Expansion and Growth

  • Identify, validate, and execute expansion opportunities across business units, regions, and use cases, including opportunities to bring both Fivetran and dbt into an account
  • Translate customer initiatives into scalable data workloads and technical solutions
  • Partner with account teams to drive incremental ARR through expansion plays.
  • Replicate successful use cases across the organization to accelerate growth
  • Provide strategic input on the customer lifecycle and renewals process

Technical Strategy and Architecture

  • Define and maintain Enterprise Reference Architectures aligned to customer environments, ensuring architectural consistency and scalability across implementations
  • Guide customers on best practices for data pipelines, transformation, automation, and platform adoption
  • Maintain technical artifacts such as architecture documentation, design patterns, and knowledge bases

Stakeholder Engagement and Account Planning

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with technical, operational, and executive stakeholders, using stakeholder maps and multi-threaded engagement strategies
  • Run regular technical cadence calls, business reviews, and roadmap sessions
  • Contribute to Mutual Account Plans and own Mutual Success Plans, mapping whitespace opportunities across business units, subsidiaries, and use cases
  • Align customer technical strategy with business objectives and commercial plans

Internal and Partner Collaboration

  • Act as the technical orchestrator across Sales, Professional Services, Product, and Engineering, keeping delivery teams aligned with long-term expansion strategy
  • Advocate for customer needs internally, influencing the product roadmap and priorities
  • Collaborate with technology partners (e.g., Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, GCP) to drive joint value
  • Help improve team processes, refine assets, and enable teammates through our knowledge-sharing loop

Thought Leadership

  • Contribute to and deliver external-facing content through live events, speaking engagements, blog posts, recorded tutorials, or other educational resources
  • Work closely with Regional and Product Marketing teams to support field and industry events
  • Support senior leadership in aligning field and industry requirements with the product roadmap, collaborating with Product and Product Marketing under their guidance.

What you bring

  • 2+ years of experience in a post-sales, customer-facing technical role, such as Solution Architect, Technical Account Manager, or Customer Success Engineer
  • Strong ability to own and drive customer outcomes after the sale, including adoption, renewal, and expansion
  • Solid technical background, with experience designing scalable data architectures, a firm understanding of modern data warehousing and the analytics stack, and SQL proficiency
  • Ability to translate business objectives into technical solutions and measurable outcomes
  • Strong stakeholder management skills across technical and non-technical audiences, and a high degree of comfort presenting to diverse audiences
  • A proactive, self-sufficient, and highly organized approach to managing multiple accounts
  • Comfort operating strategically while also engaging in deep technical discussions
  • Ability to operate in an ambiguous, fast-paced environment and think on your feet in customer conversations
  • A team-player mindset, both within the CSA team as we evolve our ways of working and in daily collaboration with AEs and SEs
  • Openness to travel: we value in-person relationship building, both internally and externally, and expect willingness to travel to events as needed

What Will Make You Stand Out

  • Hands-on experience with dbt and/or Fivetran; dbt certification is a bonus
  • SQL knowledge, with a working understanding of AI architecture, including LLMs, Knowledge Graphs, and RAG
  • Experience with cloud data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Background in data integration, ETL/ELT, or replication technologies, plus experience with ancillary tools, APIs, and managing data infrastructure
  • Experience with workload-based or consumption-based sales models
  • Familiarity with enterprise architecture frameworks and design patterns
  • Experience working with system integrators or technology partners

Technologies You’ll Use

  • Databases: SAP HANA, Oracle, SQL Server, Postgres
  • Data platforms: Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, Redshift
  • Products: Fivetran, dbt platform
  • Languages: SQL, Python, bash or PowerShell
  • Networking: SSH tunneling, VPNs, PrivateLink
  • Modern data stack tools and ecosystem technologies

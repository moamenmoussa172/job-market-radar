About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

The Global Supplier Industrialization group is seeking a Senior Program Manager who will play a key role in supporting the supplier industrialization team. This position is responsible for leading supplier deliverables and ensuring these results are closely aligned with the objectives and timelines set by the vehicle program management group. The Senior Program Manager will respond proactively to any contingencies that arise, providing guidance and support to ensure that the group consistently delivers high-quality products on schedule.

YOU WILL:

  • Lead cross‑functional teams to achieve program objectives, proactively managing risks and resolving issues that could impact program launch timelines.
  • Develop, maintain, and optimize ETL pipelines and KPI dashboards, ensuring clear data visualization and compelling storytelling that enable executive‑level decision‑making.
  • Conduct regular data compliance audits and drive disciplined follow‑up with Supplier Industrialization Engineering (SIE) and key stakeholders to ensure data accuracy and timeliness.
  • Provide hands‑on training, coaching, and continuous support to stakeholders on KPI dashboards, metrics usage, and data interpretation.
  • Contribute to both strategic and tactical planning discussions, translating business objectives into executable program plans.
  • Track and drive execution against key milestones and deliverables to ensure on‑time, high‑quality program launches.
  • Engage directly with suppliers to review program timelines, identify gaps and inefficiencies, and challenge processes, assumptions, and delivery plans as needed.
  • Exercise sound judgment to determine when issues can be resolved within the working team versus when escalation is required, ensuring timely and effective resolution.
  • Influence cross‑functional partners to identify data‑driven improvement opportunities that drive measurable business impact.
  • Oversee the end‑to‑end planning, execution, and delivery of Supplier Industrialization Engineering (SIE) programs, ensuring adherence to scope, schedule, cost, and quality requirements.
  • Stay current on industry trends, emerging tools, and regulatory considerations related to program management and supplier industrialization.
  • Travel domestically and internationally, including short‑notice and extended assignments, to support manufacturing and supplier readiness activities.

YOU BRING:

  • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, industrial engineering or related field.
  • 5 - 7 years of automotive program management experience in any of these commodities:
  • Stamping
  • Casting
  • Injection molding
  • Chassis components
  • Interiors (Interior cabin, passive safety, seats)
  • Exceptional in communication, collaboration, technical writing and presentation
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret complex data and identify actionable insights.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
  • Experience working in a dynamic/start-up environment is a plus.
  • Flexibility in work schedule is required (to include evenings and weekends, as needed)
  • Advanced skills in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, Project. Working knowledge of Smartsheet, SAP, JIRA, Tableau, and any costing software is a plus.

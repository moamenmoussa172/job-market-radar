About the job

The Software Quality Assurance Engineer will work in a mobile application developed by Bosch that allows users to remotely control their compatible heating, air conditioning, and hot water systems. Designed for convenience and energy efficiency, the app provides a simple interface to manage home climate from a smartphone or tablet.

Your contribution to something big:

  • Analyze software requirements, assess their testability and derive test cases from there;
  • Create test plans, define test objectives and specify the best approaches to reach those goals;
  • Design and implement test cases in order to validate previous software requirements;
  • Execute test cases and create test reports in order to give feedback to stakeholders about the
  • quality of the software;
  • Contribute for a state-of-the-art test automation process;
  • Maintain and improve the Continuous Integration and Deployment pipeline;
  • Promote the adoption of quality best practices within the team.

What distinguishes you:

  • Academic degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or similar;
  • Working experience in software development and software quality assurance;
  • Deep understanding on software testing principles, tools and processes;
  • ISTQB certification is a plus (Foundation, Agile, …);
  • Knowledge on Android and iOS mobile app development and related programming languages,
  • like Swift, Kotlin or Java;
  • Familiar with mobile automated testing frameworks like Appium, XCTest, Espresso…
  • Knowledge on Robot Framework and Python scripting language;
  • Experience with concepts and frameworks related to a Continuous Integration and Deployment pipeline;
  • Experience with Agile Development process;
  • Good command of English, both spoken and written.

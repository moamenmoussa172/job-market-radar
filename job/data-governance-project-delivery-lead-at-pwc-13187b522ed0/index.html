About the job

Job Description & Summary

About the team

Our Data and AI team is a dynamic group of Data and AI experts, leading the way in delivering AI trust, governance, design, strategy, and development services. We operate in a flat culture with a start-up mindset, fostering open and transparent communication. We serve both the Czech market and cross-border clients, making us a truly international team.

About your manager

Lukáš is a manager specializing in data management and governance. He gained his focus on data management while working at Ataccama, a leading provider of software for data quality, data cataloging, and master data management. After completing his studies at the Czech Technical University in Prague, he moved to PwC, where he uses his expertise to help meet client's needs in the field of data management.

Job description & summary

  • Lead end-to-end delivery of data governance projects, ensuring timely and successful implementation across PwC and client teams.
  • Configure and implement industry-standard data governance platforms such as Ataccama, Informatica, Collibra, Purview, Alation, Dawiso, or similar; hands-on configuration experience is essential.
  • Define project tasks, delegate responsibilities to delivery teams both within PwC and at client sites, and proactively manage project budget and timelines.
  • Act as the primary client liaison to lead strategic discussions, provide expert guidance, and maintain strong stakeholder relationships.
  • Own the data governance solution throughout the project lifecycle by identifying opportunities, proposing actionable next steps, and developing both short-term and long-term roadmaps tailored to client needs.
  • Stay abreast of the latest trends, best practices, and emerging technologies in data governance, with a focus on metadata management, and integrate these insights into project delivery.

What matters to us

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • At least 5–8 years of professional experience in data governance, data management, or a related discipline, including successful end-to-end delivery of data governance projects in large, complex environments.
  • Hands-on experience configuring and implementing one or more data governance platforms such as Ataccama, Informatica, Collibra, Microsoft Purview, Alation, Dawiso, or similar solutions.
  • Strong understanding of data governance frameworks, metadata management, data quality, data cataloging, and data stewardship best practices.
  • Proven experience managing cross-functional teams, project budgets, schedules, and resources, combined with strong client and stakeholder management skills.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, with a communicative level of both English and Czech..

What will help you stand out

  • Master’s degree or relevant professional certifications such as CDMP or PMP.
  • Strong knowledge of data governance technologies, tools, and methodologies beyond a single platform.
  • Experience with project management tools such as MS Project, JIRA, and Microsoft Teams.
  • Strong leadership skills and the ability to motivate and guide cross-functional teams.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive approach to identifying and mitigating risks.
  • A strong interest in emerging data governance and metadata management technologies and best practices.

Why you’ll enjoy working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.
  • A flexible benefits programme with 55,000 points to spend on what matters most to you.

  • An opportunity to give back to the community with one paid volunteering day every year.

  • We support your learning and development journey: Benefit from training and certifications in business and digital skills aligned with your area of expertise, as well as soft-skills development covering presentation skills, coaching, effective communication, and more. We cover the costs of professional certifications.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.
  • Social events and Away Days.

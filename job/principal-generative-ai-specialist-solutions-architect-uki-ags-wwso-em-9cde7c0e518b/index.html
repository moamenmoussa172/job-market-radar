About the job

Help the UK and Ireland's biggest organisations take generative AI out of the lab and into production. You'll be the senior technical expert customers turn to when they're building AI agents, customising foundation models and working out how to run GenAI safely at scale.
This is a hands-on role for a builder who enjoys the hardest problems. You'll work with CTOs, Chief Data and AI Officers and engineering leaders to understand what they're really trying to achieve. Then you'll design and prototype the solution with their teams. When something works, you'll turn it into a pattern Solutions Architects across the UK and Ireland can reuse, so your impact reaches far beyond the customers you work with directly.
What you'll do:
- Design agentic AI systems, from single agents to multi-agent workflows connected to real enterprise data and tools
- Advise on model choice, fine-tuning, distillation and inference optimisation, balancing quality, cost and latency
- Help customers get their data ready for AI, so agents can find and act on the right information
- Set the bar for production: evaluation, guardrails, monitoring and responsible AI
- Coach and enable Solutions Architects across AWS so AI expertise reaches every customer
- Take what you learn back to AWS service teams and help shape what we build next
- Share your ideas publicly through blogs, code, talks and events like AWS Summit London and AWS re:Invent
Why you'll love it:
- You'll work on some of the most ambitious generative AI programmes in the UK and Ireland, across industries from financial services to retail and media
- You'll have direct influence on services like Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Amazon SageMaker AI
- You'll join a team of AI and data science specialists who share what they know and care about getting it right
Key job responsibilities
- Be the trusted technical advisor to CTOs, Chief Data and AI Officers and engineering leaders at strategic customers across UKI, turning business goals into AI architectures and delivery plans
- Design agentic AI systems, from single agents to multi-agent workflows, using protocols such as MCP and A2A to connect agents with enterprise data and tools
- Advise on model selection, customisation (fine-tuning, distillation, reinforcement learning) and inference optimisation for quality, cost and latency
- Assess how ready customers' data is for AI and design retrieval architectures that hold up in production
- Define evaluation frameworks and guide customers from prototype to production
- Build prototypes with customer teams to prove patterns quickly
- Turn successful engagements into reusable assets and coach Solutions Architects to deliver AI workloads on their own
- Use customer evidence to influence AWS service teams such as Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Amazon SageMaker AI
- Speak publicly on generative AI at conferences, customer events and industry forums such as AWS Summit London and AWS re:Invent
- Share your expertise externally through blogs, open source and talks at events such as AWS re:Invent
A day in the life
You'll split your time between solving customers' hardest generative AI problems and scaling what you learn across AWS. A typical week might include:
- Designing a multi-agent architecture with a bank's AI platform team
- Finding the data gaps blocking a retailer's customer service agent
- Recommending whether to prompt, fine-tune or distil a model
- Building a prototype agent with a customer's engineers
- Preparing an account team for an executive briefing
- Running a deep dive for Solutions Architects
- Taking customer feedback to AWS service teams
You'll work closely with account teams, specialist sales and partners.
About the team
We're a team of AI and machine learning specialists helping organisations across the UK and Ireland build with generative AI on AWS. Our mission is to bring the deep technical and data science expertise that turns ambitious ideas into production systems customers trust.
We come from engineering, research and data science backgrounds, and we learn from each other every day. We share code, review each other's designs and celebrate what we ship together. We work closely with Solutions Architects and sales account teams across AWS, so what we build reaches every customer.

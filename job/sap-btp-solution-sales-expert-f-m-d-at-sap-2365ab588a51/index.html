About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. All SAP locations in Germany as for example Walldorf, Hamburg, Berlin, Ratingen, Eschborn, Gerlingen, Garching, etc. are possible.

What you'll bring

The Solution Sales Executive (SSE) (f/m/d) builds trust and manages strategic executive relationship with customers’ buying center CXOs to address customers’ business challenges. This quota carrying resource drives demand for the LoB solutions using GTM strategy through direct customer engagements and accelerates deal velocity through transformative sales motions.

  • Account management: Expert-level work experience in customer relationship management, sales and software license and cloud subscription revenue; Successful track record in driving solution adoption at the account, creating and managing roadmap for solutions, enabling value realization and promoting sustained solution-led expansion
  • Functional expertise: Several years of work experience in sales of business software/ IT solutions with proven track record in business application software sales; Demonstrated success with large transactions and lengthy, multidimensional sales processes in a fast-paced, consultative and competitive market
  • SAP specific knowledge: Deep understanding of SAP BAIP and SAP BTP offerings and broad understanding of SAP solution’s portfolio and the business processes they enable are a plus
  • Language proficiency: Fluency in German and English, alongside excellent communication and presentation abilities as part of your core toolkit

We also look for people who think strategically, understand the business, and can bring others along with them — combining influencing skills and emotional intelligence with the ability to work through complex problems confidently.

In addition to the skills and experience specific to this role, all positions at SAP require candidates who are comfortable using AI tools — including agentic AI — in their day-to-day work, from crafting effective inputs and automating routine tasks, to evaluating AI-generated outputs for quality and driving adoption of AI practices within their teams.

What you'll build

SAP’s Business AI Platform (BAIP)represents one of the most strategically significant transformations in SAP’s history. Having successfully established SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) and SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC) as market-leading solutions across Middle & Eastern Europe, SAP is now driving the next evolution, bringing BAIP to market and positioning it as the foundation for the autonomous enterprise.

SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) is an open, modular platform that combines integration, application development, automation, and AI to enable businesses to quickly create and operate innovative, intelligent, and scalable business applications — true to our motto #ThinkPlatform.

  • Build trust and relationship with CXO: Build trust via collaboration and executive relationship and ensure a continued path to success through the Customer Value Journey
  • Position value through solutions: Understand customers‘ business challenges and bring in domain and process expertise to provide strong PoV as well as to position the value
  • Drive demand and revenue via GTM strategy: Focus on demand generation via GTM strategy, accelerate deal velocity and grow strategic footprint for BAIP and BTP solutions

Where you belong

This is a unique opportunity to contribute directly to SAP’s BTP MEE revenue growth agenda and its market establishment journey, while gaining hands-on exposure to strategic decision-making at the highest levels of the organisation. You will be based at one of our offices in Germany.

The SAP Business Technology Platform area offers a solution portfolio focused on Database, Data Management, Intelligent Technologies, Application Development, and Artificial Intelligence—the very foundation for intelligent enterprises and digital transformation. We are a young team that strives for top performance while also valuing physical and mental well-being, as well as fun at work. Humor and pragmatism are part of our culture.

We offer diverse development opportunities, including leadership career paths. In regular development talks, we address your individual aspirations and plan both your short- and long-term career growth. Even though your teammates may work with different customers, we see ourselves as one team. We live a spirit of knowledge sharing, mutual support, and strong collaboration.

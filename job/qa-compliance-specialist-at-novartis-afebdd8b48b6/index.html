Help shape a culture where quality is built into every step. As a QA Compliance Specialist, you will play a key role in strengthening quality and compliance across our operations, partnering with colleagues to maintain robust quality systems and uphold Good Manufacturing Practice standards.
You will contribute to investigations, audits, data integrity and continuous improvement activities, helping ensure our processes consistently meet regulatory expectations. This is an opportunity to combine your quality expertise with meaningful collaboration and make a tangible impact on the reliable delivery of medicines to patients.

Job Description

We offer 1 year contract with the possibility of extension.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Maintain and continuously improve quality systems in compliance with Good Manufacturing Practice and regulatory requirements.
  • Review quality documentation to ensure accuracy, completeness, compliance, and timely approval.
  • Support investigations, deviations, corrective and preventive actions, and implementation of sustainable improvements.
  • Promote data integrity principles and ensure quality records meet established standards.
  • Support internal and external audits and contribute to inspection readiness activities.
  • Partner with cross-functional teams to identify compliance risks and strengthen quality processes.
  • Provide quality guidance and promote a proactive culture of compliance and continuous improvement.

Obligatory Requirements:

  • University degree in Pharmacy or a closely related scientific discipline.
  • At least two years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry.
  • Strong knowledge of Good Manufacturing Practice and pharmaceutical quality standards.
  • Advanced English language skills, with the ability to communicate effectively in a professional environment.
  • Solid understanding of pharmaceutical product and material quality, quality assurance, and regulatory compliance.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and standard digital applications supporting quality activities.

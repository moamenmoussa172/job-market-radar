Job Description:

Flexible on location, this role can be based from any of our Hitachi Rail European offices.

Your new role

We are looking for a Digital Solutions & Systems Lead to join our Governance, Digital & AI team within Internal Audit & Internal Controls.

This is a global role focused on the ownership, development and optimisation of the digital systems that support our Internal Audit and Internal Controls activities. You will take responsibility for key audit systems, including Optro, ensuring they remain reliable, well governed and continue to evolve in line with business requirements.

Working at the intersection of business processes and technology, you will translate user requirements into practical system improvements, support automation and reporting initiatives, and help identify opportunities to reduce manual effort and improve the way our global teams work.

You will work closely with colleagues across the UK, Italy, USA and Japan, acting as a key link between assurance teams, Digital & AI stakeholders, system providers and users.

Some key responsibilities

  • Own the administration, configuration, enhancement and optimisation of Optro and related audit and controls systems.
  • Manage system access, licences, documentation, service continuity, backups and year-end activities.
  • Coordinate system enhancements and new functionality through requirements gathering, testing, UAT, rollout and controlled deployment.
  • Translate business requirements into effective system, workflow and reporting improvements.
  • Identify opportunities to improve productivity through low-code/no-code automation and digital tooling.
  • Support dashboards, integrations and approved digital and AI use cases.
  • Develop user guidance and training materials and provide practical training, onboarding and system support.
  • Work with stakeholders across global regions to understand requirements and successfully deliver digital initiatives.

About you

You will be an experienced systems or digital solutions professional who is comfortable taking ownership of business-critical platforms and working with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Ideally, you will bring:

  • Strong experience administering, supporting and improving enterprise business systems.
  • Experience within Internal Audit, Internal Controls, risk, compliance, finance or a similar assurance environment.
  • Experience of system configuration, access management, change management, testing, UAT and deployment.
  • Practical knowledge of workflow improvement and low-code or no-code automation.
  • Experience supporting reporting solutions, dashboards or system integrations.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to translate business needs into practical technical solutions.
  • Excellent analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.
  • The ability to operate effectively within a cross-cultural, global environment.
  • Fluent written and spoken English and flexibility to travel internationally when required.

Experience with Optro or a comparable audit, controls, risk or compliance platform, alongside knowledge of J-SOX/SOX, Internal Controls or Internal Audit processes, would be particularly beneficial.

Your Responsibilities

As the Remote Sensing & Geospatial Data Science Lead for John Deere Data Solutions Group, you will lead our enterprise geospatial intelligence platform. The platform brings satellite, aerial, drone, weather, terrain, hydrology and soils data together with Deere's machine and field-operations data, one of the largest agricultural ground-truth datasets in the world. You will set the direction for the platform, build and lead the team of remote sensing scientists behind it, and be accountable for the insight it delivers across Deere's products and teams. Additionall, you will:

  • Set the direction. Own the strategy and roadmap for the platform's engineering, science and analytics, balancing near-term product commitments with a multi-year vision.
  • Build and lead the team. Hire, lead and develop a growing team of remote sensing scientists and engineers. Set clear goals, coach for growth, and build a culture of scientific rigor and delivery.
  • Own the science and platform design. Set the technical direction for how imagery and environmental data are processed, modeled, validated, cataloged and served. Stay hands-on with the hardest modeling and
    architecture problems.
  • Deliver insight that products depend on. Lead the development of models that infer field, crop and practice attributes from imagery and environmental data, using Deere's machine and field-operations data as ground truth. Deliver them with the reliability production products require.
  • Build on what exists. Partner with the engineering teams that run Deere's existing geospatial data systems. Extend those systems rather than duplicate them, and protect what already runs in production.
  • Make the platform easy to adopt. Help teams across the company move from one-off imagery work onto shared, reusable services, and make remote sensing outputs usable by builders who are not geospatial specialists.

Your Responsibilities Continued

  • Make model performance visible. Set validation standards and put monitoring in place so teams know how deployed models perform, and feed what you learn back into development.
  • Make smart sourcing decisions. Evaluate commercial and public imagery and environmental data sources, tools and partners on quality, coverage and cost, and help decide what to buy, adopt or build.
  • Lead across teams. Manage dependencies with adjacent data platforms and the product teams that rely on this work. Represent the platform in leadership and planning forums, and report on progress, risks and costs clearly.
  • Communicate clearly. Explain methods, tradeoffs and risks to technical and non-technical audiences, from scientists to senior leaders.
  • Lead with AI. Use AI coding assistants and agents to speed up research and engineering, and build geospatial data and services that AI agents can discover and use, for example through APIs or MCP.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

  • 10 or more years of relevant technical experience in remote sensing, geospatial data science and machine learning.
  • 3 or more years leading teams of data scientists or engineers, including hiring, coaching, setting goals and developing careers.
  • A track record of setting technical direction for, and delivering, a platform or data product used by several teams.
  • Deep hands-on experience building, validating and deploying machine learning or statistical models on satellite, aerial or drone imagery using Python.
  • Strong grounding in remote sensing fundamentals: radiometry, atmospheric correction, spectral indices, sensor characteristics, SAR basics, spatial and temporal resolution tradeoffs, and map projections.
  • Experience with large raster and vector datasets using tools such as GDAL, Rasterio, xarray and GeoPandas, on scalable compute such as Spark, Databricks, Dask or Google Earth Engine.
  • Strong foundation in statistics and validation: sampling design, accuracy assessment, spatial autocorrelation, uncertainty, bias, drift, and making models hold up across regions and seasons.
  • Experience taking models from prototype to production, including testing, documentation, monitoring and support.
  • Ability to turn ambiguous goals into a clear strategy, roadmap and measurable plan, and to adjust as priorities change.
  • Experience managing dependencies and aligning stakeholders across teams you do not directly control.
  • Excellent communication skills, including presenting strategy, tradeoffs and risks to senior leaders.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • You have built a remote sensing or geospatial science team from an early stage and scaled it, ideally in agriculture.
  • You have led a geospatial platform from build into steady-state operation.
  • You have agricultural remote sensing experience, such as crop-type mapping, tillage and cover crop detection, crop condition, yield estimation, soil moisture, or drainage and irrigation mapping.
  • You have combined remote sensing with in-field, sensor or machine data through spatio-temporal modeling, data assimilation or crop models.
  • You have evaluated and managed imagery and data vendors, including commercial constellations alongside public missions such as Sentinel-1/2 and Landsat.
  • You have experience with deep learning for Earth observation and with geospatial foundation models or embeddings (for example, AlphaEarth, TESSERA or Prithvi).
  • You have experience with cloud-native geospatial tools such as STAC, COG, Zarr, H3, Apache Sedona or TorchGeo.
  • You have built analytics customers must be able to trust, explain and defend.
  • You have worked in both early-stage product, R&D or startup settings and large enterprises.
  • You are familiar with John Deere's Precision Tech Stack or other digital agriculture platforms.

Education

Ideally, you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

  • An advanced degree, such as a Master's or Ph.D., in Remote Sensing, Geography/GIScience, Earth or Environmental Science, Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Mathematics or another quantitative field is preferred, or equivalent experience.

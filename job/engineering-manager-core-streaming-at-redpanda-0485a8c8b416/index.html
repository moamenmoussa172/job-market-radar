About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Redpanda is the first runtime and control plane for agent-data interaction — a unified platform that combines streaming, SQL analytics, and intelligent connectivity with the governance layer enterprise AI agents need in production.

Built on infrastructure already trusted by Fortune 500 companies and fast-growing startups, Redpanda enforces governance entirely outside the agent's data path, controlling what agents see, limiting what they do, and capturing a tamper-proof record of everything they touch, so organizations never have to choose between innovation and control.

About the Role:

As Engineering Manager for Core Streaming, you’ll lead the Enterprise team, the group behind most of Redpanda’s customer-facing features. Your team owns the capabilities our largest customers depend on: security and access control, Schema Registry, Kafka API parity, and Shadow Linking, our foundation for disaster recovery and for migrating enterprises off Kafka. You’ll inherit a talented, self-sufficient team that’s already shipping, so your focus is on growing strong engineers, keeping the roadmap predictable, and partnering with Product and the field to turn what customers need into what we ship next.

This is a hybrid role based in our central Warsaw office, with a minimum of 3 days a week on-site.

You Will:

  • Own the roadmap for Core Enterprise: authentication and authorization (SASL/SCRAM, OAuth/OIDC, Kerberos, mTLS), RBAC, audit logging, FIPS compliance, Schema Registry, the HTTP Proxy, and Shadow Linking for enterprise disaster recovery.
  • Drive Kafka API parity and ensure Redpanda remains a drop-in replacement for the most demanding Kafka workloads.
  • Partner with the Cloud, Serverless, and the Agentic Data Plane teams to deliver the core capabilities they depend on.
  • Maintain a high bar for operational excellence: incident response, on-call health, escalations from Support, and post-release quality.
  • Lead end-to-end delivery: scoping, prioritization, execution, and on-time, high-quality releases across Redpanda Cloud and Redpanda self-managed.
  • Surface risks, dependencies, and trade-offs early, and communicate contingency and mitigation plans.
  • Work with Product and the field to turn customer and prospect requirements into clear engineering plans.
  • Provide regular career development, coaching, and actionable feedback, and drive performance reviews for a high-functioning team of talented engineers.
  • Create an environment where the team is empowered, aligned with company goals, and makes decisions collaboratively.
  • Continuously improve how the team works, and measure the results.

You Have:

  • 7+ years in software engineering, including building distributed systems, infrastructure, or data platforms.
  • 3+ years managing engineering teams, with a track record of delivering predictably against committed roadmaps.
  • Strong systems fundamentals and enough depth in C++, Rust, Go, or another systems language to earn the trust of senior engineers and be a useful reviewer of their designs.
  • Experience leading teams across time zones, including at least one team you did not sit with.
  • Experience turning ambiguous product and customer requirements into executable engineering plans.
  • Experience with AI-assisted development workflows (e.g. Claude Code).
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

Nice to Have:

  • Experience with Apache Kafka or other streaming platforms, as a builder or operator.
  • Experience delivering enterprise security or access control features, with knowledge of protocols such as OAuth/OIDC, Kerberos, SASL/SCRAM, LDAP, and TLS.” (SCRAM is a SASL mechanism, so “SASL/SCRAM
  • Experience with FIPS, SOC 2, or other compliance regimes in regulated industries such as financial services.
  • Experience with schema management and serialization formats (Avro, Protobuf, JSON Schema).
  • Experience building replication, disaster recovery, or multi-region systems.
  • Experience with Kubernetes and high-throughput, low-latency production systems.

This is a B2B contract role in Warsaw. The rate is 415,000 - 490,000 PLN per year (net + VAT). Final compensation will depend on your skills, experience, and interview performance. Your recruiter can share more details about the full package.

Join Redpanda if you’d enjoy being part of a fast-moving, diverse, people-first organization with team members around the globe and a culture based on trust, transparency, communication, and kindness. You'll dive into a nimble, high-impact team with the latest AI tools — and the budget to actually use them.

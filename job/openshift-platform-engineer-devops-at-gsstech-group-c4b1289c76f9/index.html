About the job

We are looking for an experienced Senior Platform Engineer with strong expertise in OpenShift Administration, DevOps automation, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and cloud-native technologies.

The ideal candidate will have deep hands-on experience managing large-scale OpenShift environments, automating infrastructure deployments, building CI/CD pipelines, implementing GitOps-driven delivery models, and supporting cloud migration initiatives.

This role requires a strong engineering mindset with expertise across Linux, Kubernetes/OpenShift, automation, platform engineering, and enterprise infrastructure solutions. The candidate will be responsible for designing, implementing, standardizing, and automating technology platforms while ensuring scalability, security, availability, and operational excellence.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities

• Design, install, configure, manage, and automate large-scale OpenShift clusters.

• Develop and implement automation solutions for platform provisioning, configuration management, and deployment processes.

• Drive Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices using Terraform, Ansible, and automation frameworks.

• Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and support modern DevOps delivery practices.

• Implement GitOps-driven configuration management and application deployment approaches.

• Support application migration initiatives from traditional environments to cloud and container platforms.

• Design standardized technology stacks and reusable platform components.

• Develop automation solutions to enable zero-touch and unattended deployments.

• Perform platform capacity planning, performance optimization, and scalability improvements.

• Collaborate with architects, application teams, security teams, and infrastructure teams to deliver enterprise platform solutions.

• Participate in technical design discussions and provide recommendations on platform architecture.

• Troubleshoot complex platform issues related to OpenShift, Kubernetes, networking, security, and integrations.

• Support production stability by improving automation, reliability, monitoring, and operational processes.

• Contribute to engineering best practices, open-source adoption, and continuous platform improvements.

About the job

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Automotive Audio Software Developer with strong hands-on experience in Android Automotive/AOSP and QNX-based embedded platforms.

The candidate will design, develop, integrate, and debug automotive audio software across the complete stack, covering Android Audio Framework, Audio HAL, audio middleware/management, QNX, and underlying hardware interfaces.

The role requires strong Modern C++, embedded-system fundamentals, system-level debugging capability, and a good understanding of Automotive Infotainment audio architecture.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze and refine requirements for automotive audio software components.
  • Design and develop embedded audio software using Modern C/C++.
  • Develop, customize, and debug Android/AOSP Audio Framework and Audio HAL components.
  • Develop and maintain QNX/POSIX-based audio middleware, audio services and audio-management components.
  • Work with POSIX concepts including multithreading, IPC, synchronization, shared memory, message queues and sockets.
  • Integrate audio software with DSP frameworks, OS services, HAL and hardware interfaces.
  • Analyze complex issues across Application Framework/Middleware , HAL, OS, DSP , Hardware layers.
  • Support platforms bring-up, integration, validation, and customer-specific audio configurations.
  • Prepare and review high-level and low-level software designs, including UML-based designs.
  • Perform unit testing using frameworks such as Google Test/GTest and contribute to CI/CD activities.
  • Participate in design and code reviews and drive clean-code and software-quality practices
  • Provide technical guidance to team members and support cross-functional teams in resolving complex audio issues.

What We’re Looking For

  • Must-Have Technical Skills - Embedded C++ 11/14/17 programming skills. Strong understanding of object-oriented design, STL, memory management, multithreading, concurrency and synchronization.
  • QNX Hands-on development experience on QNX-based embedded systems. Experience with QNX-based audio middleware/audio-management components.
  • Understanding QNX Sound Architecture and device-driver concepts is beneficial.
  • Automotive Audio : Good understanding of audio routing and management, Audio streaming, Audio device handling Low latency audioInternal/external amplifiers DSP integration
  • Strong system-level debugging and problem-solving skills.
  • Android Automotive AudioHands-on experience with AOSP/Android Audio Framework.
  • Experience with: AudioFlinger / AudioPolicyManager / AudioPolicyService / AudioManager / AudioService / Car Audio Framework / Audio routing and device management / Audio HAL / AIDL / HIDL
  • Strong Android debugging using logcat, bug reports and Tombstone logs.
  • Familiarity with ALSA/TinyALSA and PCM/audio streaming is advantageous.
  • Good understanding of embedded systems and RTOS concepts.

B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Telecommunication, or a related engineering discipline

5-9 years

  • Experience with Qualcomm automotive audio architecture and DSP frameworks.
  • Experience with Hypervisor-based IVI architecture.
  • Hands-on knowledge of tools such as QACT/QXDM and Qualcomm ACDB.
  • Knowledge of low-level audio interfaces such as I2S
  • Knowledge of audio DSP processing, effects and tuning. Understanding of CAN/vehicle signals influencing audio behavior.
  • Experience with UML/design tools and software design patterns. Familiarity with SAFe/Agile and ASPICE.
  • Knowledge of ISO 26262 and secure software development practices.
  • Exposure to AI-assisted software development tools such as GitHub Copilot; familiarity with LLMs/AI Agents/agentic workflows can be an additional advantage.

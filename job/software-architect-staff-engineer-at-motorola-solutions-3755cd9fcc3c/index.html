About the job

Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

Our still-growing Department is composed of well balanced agile teams with a full range of software development experience and the embedded mindset of DevOps culture and continuous delivery approach. Teams are responsible for end-to-end software development including: design, implementation, testing, delivery and maintenance in a 24/7 support model.


Job Description

Job Description

When every second counts, 99.999% availability isn't just a metric—it’s a lifeline. Join our Emergency Call Handling team in Kraków to evolve the mission-critical tech powering VESTA NXT. Watch to learn more about VNXT Call Handling!

Tech stack:

Kubernetes/Docker, Java, Kotlin, Spring Boot, Quarkus, GitHub, ELK Stack + Prometheus/Grafana, Redis, Azure & AWS, Proxmox, Openshift, SIP/RTP/WebRTC/MSRP , RabbitMQ, Gstreamer, RUST, Asterisk, Postgresql, NGINX, Angular, TypeScript, JavaScript,

What You Will Do

As a member of the Architecture Team you will guide the technical vision and build a mission-critical platform.

  • Design & Build: Architect fault-tolerant, distributed, event-driven systems that process real-time emergency data with zero single points of failure.
  • Code & Implement: Write clean, resilient code as a hands-on leader collaborating with global engineering teams across Poland, North America, Brazil, South Africa, and India.
  • Orchestrate: Drive seamless scalability and zero-downtime deployments using cloud-native technologies.
  • Leverage AI: Partner with product and data engineering teams to embed AI into our SDLC and troubleshooting workflows.
  • Lead & Mentor: Elevate engineering standards by championing fault tolerance, security, and ultra-reliability.


Basic Requirements

Must have:

  • 8 + years experience in building products and driving their architecture
  • Java
  • Kubernetes or alternative
  • Docker or alternative
  • Cloud experience with AWS or Azure or Openshift/Proxmox and distributed systems
  • Automated testing experience
  • Messaging systems
  • Observability and monitoring experience
  • Networking knowledge
  • Angular experience


Could have:

  • SIP
  • RTP
  • GStreamer experience

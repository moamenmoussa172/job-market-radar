About the job

The Kubernetes teams develop software that makes managing MongoDB products in or from Kubernetes frictionless, with the aim of being the de facto choice for the backbone of cloud-native applications. We are enabling building database-as-a-service experiences for MongoDB users on Kubernetes. We are responsible for a collection of Kubernetes Operators that allow our users to deploy fault tolerant, globally distributed MongoDB clusters in minutes, in the cloud or on-premises.

We’re looking for a Software Engineer to join our Kubernetes Foundations team that is responsible for how the Kubernetes operators that we build for Enterprise Advanced and Community users are developed, distributed and integrated in the greater on-prem ecosystem in order to deliver a best-in-class experience of building database-as-a-service platforms that can support the most complex features offered by MongoDB. As part of the team, you will propose, design, build and support product features. We are looking for passionate, intrinsically motivated software engineers who consistently work to grow their skills and want to take their careers to the next level. No prior experience with MongoDB technologies is required! During interviews, you will meet most of our team and have the opportunity to ask questions about working at MongoDB. We pride ourselves on our team’s culture and on being an inclusive and collaborative group that Builds Together.

We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model.

Our ideal candidate

  • Loves coding and building reliable software
  • Although this is not a SRE role, you are an advocate of the DevOps culture and can empathise with system administrators and platform builders, who are our main customers
  • Enjoys collaboration and being part of a team. You will extensively collaborate with engineers, product managers, and other stakeholders
  • Be flexible! You'll be joining a growing team, so you must be willing to take on a wide variety of responsibilities, learning as you go
  • You resonate well with our team’s core engineering values: Context, Predictability, Transparency, Focus and Craftsmanship

Responsibilities

  • Propose, design, build and support product features for our Kubernetes Operators
  • Advocate for clear integration and compatibility with established cloud-native tooling
  • Work in an agile team environment with a close relationship with customers
  • Incrementally ship elements of sophisticated projects
  • Apply our core values to your work
  • Assist our Operations & Support Engineers to troubleshoot operational issues with customers

What is in it for you

  • Generous compensation package (top-range salary, equity, comprehensive benefits)
  • You'll be joining a team that is diverse and highly collaborative, we value shipping fast and clean
  • Opportunities to learn on the job (time to upskill in new technologies)
  • High level of independence in your day to day work (not a strict 9-6 role, focus on results, not the appearance of work)

Requirements

  • Experience in a modern programming language. We mostly use Golang but we believe great engineers are much more than the tools they use
  • Experience working with Kubernetes and Docker. Great plus for candidates who are familiar with the CNCF landscape and can easily discuss the value and trade-offs of adopting these tools
  • Able to write technical documents and describe complex concepts in a concise way
  • Have worked with one or more of the major public cloud providers
  • Good understanding of computer networking and protocols
  • Experience working with and testing distributed systems
  • Experience working with systems in production
  • Strong command line experience
  • Experience working with Linux distributions

Nice to have

  • Kubernetes development experience for custom components and knowledge of Kubernetes internals
  • Kubernetes certifications
  • Experience with performance and security analysis of storage and networking providers in Kubernetes.
  • Experience building Kubernetes Operators OR extending the Kubernetes APIs

About the job

Department Overview

Noggin provides an award-winning, cloud-based platform that unifies 10 core resilience management solutions, including risk, continuity, incident, emergency, and safety operations. Integrated with Motorola solutions, Noggin ensures comprehensive security, response, and preparation.
Our target-driven Sales team drives growth by acquiring new customers and growing existing customers using methodologies like MEDDPICC across the full sales cycle.
The Strategic Account Manager (SAM) is responsible to manage, retain, and expand our highest-value enterprise accounts. Acting as a trusted advisor and long-term business partner, you will align our solutions with each client's strategic goals, drive multi-year retention, and uncover cross-sell and upsell opportunities across complex organizations.


Job Description

Key Responsibilities:

  • Account Growth & Retention: Own the end-to-end commercial relationship for a portfolio of tier-one enterprise accounts, consistently meeting or exceeding retention, net dollar retention (NDR), and expansion revenue targets.
  • Strategic Account Planning: Develop and execute comprehensive, data-driven account plans that map key stakeholders, identify whitespace opportunities, and establish long-term growth roadmaps.
  • Executive Relationship Building: Build and maintain deep, multi-threaded relationships across C-suite, VP, and operational levels within client organizations to ensure broad organizational buy-in and mitigate churn risk.
  • Business Reviews & Value Realization: Lead quarterly and executive business reviews (QBRs/EBRs) to demonstrate measurable ROI, review key performance metrics, and align on upcoming strategic initiatives.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Sales Engineering, Customer Success, Product, and Executive leadership to orchestrate seamless onboarding, resolve escalated issues, and advocate for customer-driven product enhancements.
  • Pipeline & Forecasting Management: Maintain accurate CRM records, build reliable expansion pipelines, and deliver precise quarterly and annual revenue forecasts.


Basic Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or a related field
  • Extensive experience in enterprise SaaS business development and major account management with a proven track record of achieving commercial targets
  • Ability to successfully pass a UK police security clearance check

Personal Attributes:

  • You bring a strategic mindset and a proven sales track record, adept at managing complex sales processes and driving business growth. Your expertise in CRM systems, combined with strong technical skills and a customer-focused approach, will ensure your success in engaging new customers and meeting sales targets. A competitive edge, creativity with solutions, inquisitive nature, and total ownership will be key.

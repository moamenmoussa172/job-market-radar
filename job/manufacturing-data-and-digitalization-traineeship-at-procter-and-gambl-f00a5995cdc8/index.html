About the job

Job Location

BUDAPEST PLANT

Job Description

📋 WHAT YOU WILL DO:

  • Support digitalization and data projects in a manufacturing environment
  • Work with manufacturing data and help develop data collection solutions
  • Create and improve dashboards and data visualizations
  • Support programming, automation, and AI-related projects
  • Collaborate with Engineering, Operations, IT, and other teams

🔑 WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

  • Final year of Engineering, Computer Science, IT, Data Science, or other relevant STEM BSc/MSc studies, or a degree obtained in the last 12 months
  • Availability for 24–40 hours per week
  • Basic programming knowledge, preferably in Python, SQL, .NET, or similar technologies
  • Interest in data, digitalization, automation, and technology
  • Proficient in Hungarian and English
  • Analytical, proactive, and eager to learn

➕ NICE TO HAVE:

  • Experience with programming, data analysis, or visualization through university or personal projects
  • Familiarity with Grafana or similar visualization tools
  • Basic knowledge of PLCs, automation, or industrial systems
  • Interest in AI and Machine Learning
  • Team-oriented and hands-on mindset

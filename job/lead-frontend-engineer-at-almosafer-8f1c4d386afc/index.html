About the job

We at Seera Group are looking for a Lead Frontend Engineer.

  • Lead and manage a team of developers
  • Leading the project activities as well as managing project resources in technical terms
  • Discuss requirements, planning, design, etc.
  • Run teams using Scrum methodology
  • Collaborate with other team members and stakeholders
  • 6+ years of experience including 2 years of experience in leading a team
  • Write clean, solid, scalable and testable code
  • Knowledge of modern JS frameworks (Reactjs is a must)
  • Understanding of all major browsers and the special considerations required for all various quirks
  • Understanding of CSS pre-processing platforms, such as SASS
  • Excellent written and spoken communication skills
  • Able to communicate technical issues with non-technical users
  • Ability to multi-task and run tasks with varying priorities
  • Impeccable organizational skills and the ability to think on your feet and use your initiative
  • The ability to work effectively under deadlines and lead multiple projects simultaneously
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills

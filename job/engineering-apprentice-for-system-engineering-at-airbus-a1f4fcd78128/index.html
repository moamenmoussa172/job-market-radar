About the job

Job Description:

We are seeking a motivated Engineering Apprentice to join our multidisciplinary team. You will gain hands-on experience in Systems Engineering, Verification and Validation (V&V), Integration, and ITCOMs (Information Technology and Communications).

This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to complex technical projects while developing core engineering competencies.

Key Responsibilities:
- Support Systems Engineering activities, including requirements analysis, system design documentation, and interface management.
- Assist in Verification and Validation processes by helping define test cases, executing tests, and documenting results to ensure system compliance.
- Participate in hardware and software Integration efforts, troubleshooting interoperability issues, and supporting system-level testing.
- Contribute to ITCOMs projects, aiding in the deployment, configuration, and maintenance of communication and IT infrastructure.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve technical challenges and ensure project milestones are met.
- Maintain accurate technical documentation and adhere to quality and safety standards.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Excellent English speaking and writing skills, as you will be working in an international environment and producing technical documentation.
- Willingness to travel domestically to support site integrations, testing, or client visits as required.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
- Basic understanding of systems engineering principles, testing methodologies, or IT/network concepts is a plus.
- Proficiency in technical documentation and familiarity with tools like Jira, Confluence, or similar is beneficial.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in a team.
- Eager to learn and adapt in a dynamic engineering environment.

What We Offer:
- Structured mentorship and training in Systems Engineering, V&V, Integration, and ITCOMs.
- Exposure to large-scale, complex engineering projects.
- Collaborative and innovative work culture.
- Potential for a permanent position upon successful completion of the apprenticeship.

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.

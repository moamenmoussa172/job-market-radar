Job description

In our BiMOS Backend plant in Lenzburg, we manufacture efficient and high-quality power semiconductors for use in motor control and inverter systems. Our high-tech products contribute decisively to making industry, public and individual transport more energy-efficient and, on the other hand, our technology enables the production and transport of renewable energies from the production site to the consumer.

Our highly integrated Industry 4.0 fab and state-of-the-art processes provide an exciting and challenging environment for our new Engineering Manager BiMOS Backend, who will lead the Process Engineering, System & Equipment Engineering, and Project Management teams to secure leading manufacturing technology and technical capacity.

Become part of the energy transition, support a high level of production capability and guide the team through the next steps of our journey.

How you will make an impact

  • Lead, develop and empower BiMOS Backend Engineering with a hands-on approach: regular shopfloor presence, supportive involvement in technical problem-solving and removal of execution barriers
  • Balance several time horizons, considering the overall interests of Backend operations: set priorities and resource focus from days to months, deliver CAPEX and improvement projects, and shape long-term factory capacity and technology plans
  • Translate business, product and capacity needs into an aligned engineering strategy, executable roadmaps, technical standards, investment priorities, and team backlogs
  • Ensure stable, scalable and high-yield manufacturing processes by driving process performance, qualification, industrialization, root-cause analysis and sustainable corrective action
  • Ensure long-term equipment reliability and availability by governing equipment concepts and standards, maintenance strategies, lifecycle and obsolescence management, advanced technical support and supplier interfaces
  • Own and steer the engineering project and CAPEX portfolio from concept and specification through implementation, qualification and operational handover, with clear scope, milestones, risks, resources and financial control
  • Enable new product introductions, engineering lots and new manufacturing technologies in close collaboration with Production, Product Engineering, R&D, Quality, Factory Integration, Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain and external partners
  • Lead the continued development of the Engineering operating model, including roles and responsibilities, skills and succession, work prioritization, cross-functional delivery teams, management cadence and effective use of Lean and Agile methods
  • Develop technical and leadership talent, strengthen organizational capabilities and create sustainable knowledge transfer rather than dependency on individual experts

Your background

  • University degree in engineering, physics or a related technical field; an advanced degree is an advantage
  • Substantial leadership experience in semiconductor manufacturing or similar high-tech environments, leading managers and engineers through change
  • Strong systemic thinking, with the ability to connect organization design, people and competencies, processes, technology, data, governance and business priorities into one coherent operating model
  • Practical experience in CAPEX planning and execution, equipment industrialization, production ramp-up, technology transfer, project and portfolio management, and financial control
  • Sound understanding of semiconductor backend manufacturing processes and equipment. Experience with soldering, wire bonding, assembly, electrical testing, automation and equipment integration is an advantage
  • Profound Lean and Agile change-leadership capability, with demonstrated hands-on experience
  • Human-oriented leadership style grounded in trust, openness and transparency, combined with clear expectations, sound decisions and consistent follow-through
  • Strong analytical and data-driven problem-solving skills, including the use of OEE, yield, reliability, capacity and process-performance indicators to set priorities and verify impact
  • Fluency in English and German; minimum C1 level in both languages

Senior Product Manager - Payments (GTXN)

Luno · Cape Town & Johannesburg, South Africa

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 18 November 2026

Apply on Luno's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

About us:

Luno has spent over a decade making digital assets safe to own. Since 2013, we’ve helped millions of people around the world buy, store and invest in crypto, working with regulators in every market and holding all customer crypto 1:1. That discipline built the licences, banking relationships, custody and compliance machinery that the next era of this market will run on
The financial system is moving on-chain. Stablecoins are the only digital asset class operating at institutional scale today, and while 98% of supply is dollar-pegged, roughly half of global payment flows are not. That gap is the opportunity, and it is largest in exactly the markets Luno has spent twelve years building in.

About the team

You will join the Product team building GTXN, Luno’s global settlement business. GTXN focuses on institutional payments: the collections, pay-outs, virtual account and conversion products that let businesses and financial institutions move money in and out of emerging markets through one integration. You will work with dedicated engineering and UX partners to build and run the payment products that GTXN customers depend on.

GTXN has the ambition to operate like a startup so this role needs flexibility and a balance between systems thinking and speed: you will design for the long term and still ship quickly.

The role in a nutshell

You will turn GTXN’s strategy into the payments roadmap, work with Engineering and Payment Partnerships to build the money movement products customers run on, such as collections, pay-outs and virtual accounts, and make sure every payment is fast, reliable, affordable and controlled.

Your mission will be

  • Own the GTXN payments roadmap: Work closely with Commercial and business owners to turn the GTXN and business strategy into the payments roadmap. Lead discovery through to launch and adoption, enabling the payment flows customers need
  • Define what our rails and providers must deliver: Set the product requirements for every new payment flow or corridor
  • Work closely with Payment Partnerships: Lead the product and engineering input to provider selection, and keep the corridor backlog aligned with their provider pipeline
  • Launch new corridors and currencies: Take new corridors from customer need to live product. Turn requirements into an integration scope that Engineering can build from without guesswork, and turn each launch into a playbook that makes the next one faster.
  • Drive product decisions with data: Define the right measures for institutional payments, so you can see where they succeed, stall or cost too much, and decide what we build, fix and prioritise
  • Lead the cross-functional effort to make payments reliable, operable and trusted. Define the product behind routing, reconciliation, failure handling and internal tooling, supporting Operations
  • Shorten the path from signed customer to first live payment: Shape the customer implementation journey. Work hands-on with new customers to plan their integration, find where activation loses customers, and turn what you learn into repeatable integration capabilities.

A little about you

  • You are a senior Product Manager in payments, banking, a regulated fintech or a crypto business.
  • You have built institutional or B2B money movement products, such as collections, pay-outs, account-to-account transfers or virtual accounts, including integrating with and managing banks or payment providers
  • You understand how a payment works end to end: local and cross-border rails, settlement, reconciliation, returns and failures, and what each step costs
  • You are technically comfortable where product meets integration: the transaction lifecycle, ledger and reconciliation basics, API integration design, bank and provider APIs, payment messaging, retries and failure handling, in enough depth to make good trade-offs with engineers
  • You are rigorous with data: success rates, failure reasons, time to settle and cost per payment. You can find where a flow leaks, quantify what it costs, and prioritise accordingly
  • You understand the commercials of payments: pricing, unit economics and how cost flows through to margin
  • You write a clear integration scope, and you communicate precisely with the engineering partners you lead the work with
  • You manage stakeholders as a craft: keeping payment operations, treasury, compliance, banks and customers aligned, communicating risks early
  • You can balance speed with compliant, secure and trusted payments, and hold a position on customer experience when a control or regulatory requirement pulls the other way
  • Experience with payments in emerging markets, in Africa or Southeast Asia, or with multi-currency, cross-border or stablecoin settlement is a strong advantage
  • You use AI as part of how you work and can show where it has changed your output, not just your tooling. Exposure to AI-enabled back-office operations is a plus.

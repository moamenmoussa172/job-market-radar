About the job

Meet the Team

You'll join the High Touch Delivery & Routing Operations team—a friendly, collaborative, and globally connected group. We sit right at the intersection of customer engineering, operations, and modern data platforms, working together to give our engineers and leaders the insights they need to deliver exceptional customer experiences.

Your Impact

We are looking for a Business & Data Analyst to help our global support teams work smarter through data. In this role, you will turn complex operational data into clear Tableau dashboards and actionable insights that help leaders across Customer Experience (CX) balance workloads, spot delivery trends, and improve customer care. Your main responsibilities:

  • Build & Own Dashboards: Design and maintain interactive Tableau dashboards that give teams real-time visibility into support case routing, account health, and delivery metrics.
  • Query & Model Data: Write and optimize SQL queries across Snowflake and Oracle databases to uncover trends, identify bottlenecks, and track team performance.
  • Partner with Stakeholders: Work directly with Technical Assistance Center (TAC) and delivery managers to understand their operational challenges and translate their questions into useful reporting.
  • Tell the Story Behind the Numbers: Turn raw data into clear, visual summaries and insights that help leaders make informed decisions.
  • Automate & Simplify: Find ways to automate manual reporting routines, making data more accessible and saving your team valuable time.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 3+ years of data analytics experience: Hands-on background in business intelligence, operational analytics, or reporting.
  • Strong Tableau skills: Proven ability to build, publish, and maintain interactive dashboards from scratch.
  • Solid SQL proficiency: Comfortable writing, debugging, and optimizing SQL queries across relational databases (e.g., Snowflake, Oracle, or PostgreSQL).
  • Curiosity & communication: You enjoy asking the right questions, gathering requirements from non-technical stakeholders, and clearly explaining insights.
  • Education or equivalent experience: Bachelor’s degree in Business Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Economics, Mathematics, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Language: Fluent in English (written and verbal).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Domain Knowledge: Experience in IT services, technical customer support, or TAC operations.
  • Automation & Cloud Warehouses: Familiarity with data automation tools (Python, Alteryx, VBA) or cloud data platforms like Snowflake.
  • Adaptability: Comfortable managing shifting priorities and delivering insights in a fast-paced environment.

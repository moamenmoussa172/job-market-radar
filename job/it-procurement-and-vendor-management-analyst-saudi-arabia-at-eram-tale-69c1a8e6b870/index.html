IT Procurement & Vendor Management Analyst - Saudi Arabia

About the job

The IT Procurement & Vendor Management Analyst supports Digital Services (DS) by managing and coordinating the end-to-end procurement lifecycle for IT services, applications, technology assets, and related operational requirements, from procurement planning and vendor coordination through delivery verification, invoice review, and payment follow-up. The role contributes to renewal strategies, commercial evaluation, vendor performance, and asset lifecycle planning to achieve value for money, financial accuracy, compliance, and service continuity. It identifies opportunities to optimize spending and strengthen procurement and invoicing processes, working closely with DS service managers, Finance, Procurement, and suppliers.

Scope of Work

DS Procurement Planning & Execution

  • Coordinate DS procurement activities across DS services, software applications, subscriptions, maintenance, and technology assets.
  • Work with service owners to consolidate requirements, plan procurement timelines, and prepare complete, compliant procurement requests and supporting documentation.
  • Track purchase requests, purchase orders, contract milestones, approvals, and delivery commitments; proactively resolve bottlenecks and escalate risks.
  • Support projects with procurement and financial documentation, ensuring contracts, approvals, and milestones align with DS budgets.

Renewal Strategy & Asset Lifecycle Coordination

  • Maintain a forward-looking register of service, software, and support contract renewals, including expiry dates, costs, usage.
  • Support renewal and sourcing strategies by assessing demand, licensing needs, alternatives, consolidation opportunities, and service continuity risks.
  • Coordinate with asset owners on procurement requirements arising from technology refresh, replacement cycles, warranties, and asset management plans.

Value for Money & Vendor Management

  • Analyze procurement spend, quotations, pricing structures, and contract terms to identify cost efficiencies and opportunities for better value.
  • Coordinate vendor engagement, monitor delivery and contractual performance, and follow up on commercial or service issues with responsible teams.
  • Support competitive sourcing, vendor evaluations, and negotiations in coordination with the authorized Procurement function and DS stakeholders.

Governance, Reporting & Continuous Improvement

  • Ensure procurement activities follow Client policies, delegated authorities, contractual requirements, and documentation standards.
  • Prepare procurement dashboards and reports covering commitments, renewals, savings opportunities, vendor performance, and outstanding actions.
  • Identify gaps in procurement workflows, systems, and controls; recommend and implement approved process improvements.
  • Review supplier invoices against purchase orders, contracts, agreed pricing, delivery evidence, and service milestones to verify accuracy and resolve discrepancies before payment.
  • Coordinate with service owners, Finance, Procurement, and vendors to ensure timely invoice approvals, service entry confirmation, and payment processing.
  • Track invoice status, outstanding commitments, and payment issues; maintain reliable procurement-to-payment records and reporting.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain, Finance, Information Systems, or a related discipline.
  • Knowledge of procurement processes, contract renewals, vendor management, and commercial evaluation.
  • Familiarity with IT services, software licensing, and technology asset lifecycles is preferred.
  • Fluent in English
  • Minimum of 3 years’ experience in procurement, sourcing coordination, vendor management, contract renewals, or related business operations.
  • Experience supporting IT procurement is preferred.

About the job

Job Title: Senior Database Administrator

Location: Stoke Gifford, Bristol + Hybrid Working Arrangements

Compensation: £44,600 - £55,800 + Benefits

Role Type: Full time / Permanent

Role ID: SF78459

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Senior Database Administrator at our Babcock Technology Centre site.

The role

As a Senior Database Administrator, you’ll play a vital role in supporting critical digital services that underpin defence capabilities and national security programmes. Reporting to the Head of Service Operations, you'll be responsible for the day-to-day operation, performance, availability, and security of a complex Oracle database and middleware environment supporting key business and customer systems.

Day-to-day, you’ll have an excellent opportunity to develop your expertise within a highly skilled Digital Solutions team while working on complex enterprise-scale systems. You'll contribute to innovative technology solutions, collaborate with industry and Ministry of Defence stakeholders, and help ensure the delivery of reliable services that support important defence outcomes.

  • Managing Oracle database services, ensuring availability, performance, resilience, and compliance with agreed service levels.
  • Installing, configuring and upgrading Oracle databases, middleware, and application services across Linux and Windows environments.
  • Optimising database performance through tuning, capacity planning, automation, and proactive monitoring.
  • Designing and maintaining backup, recovery, and disaster recovery solutions to support business continuity.
  • Collaborating with developers, technical teams, and stakeholders to support deployments, security requirements, and service improvements.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements.

Essential experience of the Senior Database Administrator

  • Experience administering Oracle databases, including Recovery Manager and Flashback technologies.
  • Experience supporting Oracle WebLogic Server and Oracle middleware technologies including SOA and Oracle Application Server environments.
  • Strong SQL scripting skills supporting Oracle database services and performance optimisation.
  • Knowledge of Red Hat Enterprise Linux, Oracle Linux, and Windows Server operating systems.
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and problem-solving skills with the ability to perform effectively under pressure.

Qualifications for the Senior Database Administrator

Essential:

  • Degree qualified or holding equivalent professional experience.
  • Familiarity with ITIL and ISO20000-1 service management frameworks.

Desirable:

  • ITIL Version 4 qualification or higher.

Security Clearance

The successful candidate must be a sole UK National who is able to achieve and maintain Naval Nuclear Propulsion Information (NNPPI) security clearance for this role.

Many of the positions within our company are subject to national security clearance and Trade Control restrictions. This means that your eligibility for certain roles may be affected by your place of birth, nationality, current or former citizenship, and any residency you hold or have held. Further details are available at United Kingdom Security Vetting: clearance levels - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk).

About the job

  • Participate and support strategic planning and optimization of distribution centre network to support business growth and customer satisfaction for Bosch Home Comfort APAC.

Investigate, plan and execute logistics related projects.

  • Own, collect, clean, and analyze large datasets related to transportation, warehousing, inventory, and customer demand.
  • Support the data preparation activities for running Supply Chain Network Design modeling and other logistics related projects.
  • Perform scenario analysis and sensitivity testing to form the business cases.
  • Translate business requirements into analytical models and actionable insights.
  • Create dashboards and reports to visualize key metrics and project outcomes using tools like Power BI, Tableau, or Excel.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across supply chain, operations, procurement, finance, and IT to gather requirements and validate assumptions.
  • Document project findings, methodologies, and recommendations in a clear and structured format.

Stay informed on industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices in logistics and supply chain analytics.

  • Good knowledge of physical logistics, DC operations, and international trade regulations.
  • Expertise in translating business requirements into analytical models and actionable insights

Familiarity with financial and logistics analytics tools.

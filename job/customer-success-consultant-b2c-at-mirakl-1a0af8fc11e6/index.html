About the job

About Mirakl:

Founded in 2012, Mirakl has been at the forefront of marketplace innovation, empowering every business to compete in the platform economy.
Today, Mirakl’s operating system combines an enterprise marketplace solution (Mirakl Platform) that enables retailers and B2B organizations to launch, scale, and operate marketplaces and dropship, AI-powered multichannel selling (Mirakl Connect), retail media (Mirakl Ads) and an agentic commerce infrastructure (Mirakl Nexus).
With dual headquarters in Boston and Paris, Mirakl helps a global ecosystem of 450+ marketplaces (B2C and B2B) and a network of over 100k third-party marketplace sellers. Brands like Macy’s, Decathlon, Carrefour, Asos, and Airbus Helicopters use Mirakl to grow their businesses in new and remarkable ways.

For more information, visit www.mirakl.com.

Customer Success Consultant – B2C | London

1. The Team You'll Join

You’ll be joining our B2C Customer Success team, led by Patrick Luetjens, and working closely with Jessica Winward, our Senior Customer Success Consultant.

The team consists of around 5 Consultants based across Munich and London, covering a portfolio of B2C accounts in the DACH and UK regions. Together, you'll be ensuring each client's marketplace reaches its full performance potential — acting as the strategic backbone behind their growth.

2. Your Impact

  • Serve as the strategic and functional point of reference for B2C clients across both project and run phases — running tailored workshops with C-level stakeholders, shaping marketplace strategy, and keeping projects firmly on track to go-live.
  • Build personalised post-launch action plans with each client, lead data-driven business reviews, and translate KPI analysis into concrete growth recommendations for marketplace teams and senior leadership.
  • Define seller and customer strategies, benchmark against leading global marketplaces, and leverage the latest e-commerce trends and Mirakl product features to continuously drive client performance.
  • Work cross-functionally with Mirakl's Business and IT teams to anticipate risks early, align on priorities, and ensure clients extract maximum value from the platform.
  • Embed AI into your daily workflows — testing agents, providing structured feedback, and applying critical thinking to push the boundaries of how the team operates.

3. What You'll Bring to the Role

Experience:

  • 18 to 24 months of experience in at least one of the following: management consulting, a business role within an e-commerce company or online marketplace (B2C), or Customer Success in B2B Enterprise SaaS — marketplace or digital commerce experience is a strong plus
  • Proven track record of influencing and guiding senior stakeholders, including C-level, in a client-facing environment
  • Master's degree in Business, Management, Finance, or a related field

Skills:

  • Analytical and data-driven thinking: comfortable building business cases, interpreting KPIs, and turning data into actionable recommendations
  • Senior stakeholder management and influence
  • Strategic business advisory and structured problem-solving
  • Strong written, verbal, and presentation communication
  • AI literacy with a critical mindset — you already use AI tools and are curious to go further

4. Tools Used

Salesforce, Gong, Google Workspace, Dust, Excel

  • English: Full professional proficiency (required)

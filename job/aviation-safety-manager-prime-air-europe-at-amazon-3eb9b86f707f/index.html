Amazon Prime Air is seeking an experienced aviation safety professional to be the Safety Manager for our European drone delivery operations. You will own and run the Safety Management System that underpins our CAA operational authorisation, lead safety investigations and Mandatory Occurrence Reporting, act as a flight operations expert for the team, and be the primary safety point of contact for the CAA during inspections and audits. As we grow from our first European site to further sites, you will make sure safety keeps pace with growth, and you will build the safety standards and mechanisms that the rest of our European operations can adopt.
Key job responsibilities
• Act as the nominated Safety Manager for Prime Air’s European operations, and own the Safety Management System, including the safety policy, risk register and safety performance indicators
• Own the safety content of the operations manual for our CAA operational authorisation, and keep Prime Air’s documentation current with changes to CAA UAS regulation, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material, working with the European Commercial Operations Leader
• Lead hazard identification and risk assessment for European operations and for changes to them, including new sites, procedures and system changes
• Lead safety investigations to root cause and corrective action, and discharge Mandatory Occurrence Reporting obligations to the CAA, working with Product Support Engineering on technical root cause
• Run the safety assurance programme, including audits of flight operations, maintenance and site health and safety, with corrective actions tracked to closure
• Design and deliver safety training for remote pilots, flight monitors, ground handlers and maintenance staff, and build a strong reporting culture
• Measure safety performance, identify trends and report risks and findings clearly to European leadership
• Act as the primary point of contact for the CAA during inspections, audits and visits
• Review site designs and launch readiness for new European sites from a safety perspective, working with the Expansion and Commercial Operations teams
- Experience in aviation regulatory compliance
- Experience owning a Safety Management System in a certificated or authorised operation
- Experience as a nominated postholder, or direct deputy to a nominated postholder, with a civil aviation authority
- Knowledge of flight safety, health and safety and flight operations regulations and procedures
- Experience operating unmanned aircraft systems, or extensive general aviation experience
- Experience owning and updating safety manuals in a regulated environment as standards change
- Experience leading safety investigations, audits and corrective action programmes
- Experience designing and delivering safety training programmes
- Experience analysing safety data to identify trends and risks, and reporting findings to senior leadership
- Business fluency in English (C1 or above)
- A Health and Safety NVQ Level 3 or NEBOSH National General Certificate (or equivalent), or NEBOSH (National General Certificate)
- Bachelor's degree in engineering, aviation, safety or a related field
- Experience with CAA and or EU NAA specific category operational authorisations or SORA
- Experience with beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) drone operations
- Experience in human factors or just culture programmes
- Experience working with more than one civil aviation authority

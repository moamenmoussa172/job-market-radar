About the job

Your contribution to something big:

Help shape secure and reliable access management across Bosch Home Comfort's SAP landscape and S/4HANA transformation.

As a SAP S/4HANA Authorization Expert, you will take responsibility for authorization topics across SAP projects and systems, ensuring that role development and authorization concepts are implemented in line with Bosch standards.

You will work at the intersection of business, IT and SAP projects, collaborating with stakeholders around the world and supporting the transition from existing SAP ERP environments towards SAP S/4HANA.

In this role, you will:

  • Ensure the effective implementation of SAP role development and authorization processes in accordance with relevant Bosch standards and requirements.
  • Take responsibility for SAP authorization topics within Bosch Home Comfort SAP projects.
  • Act as a key interface between business departments, project teams and role development teams, ensuring effective coordination and communication.
  • Develop, implement and continuously improve SAP roles and authorization concepts across relevant ERP environments.
  • Support SAP S/4HANA project phases including cutover, migration, go-live and hypercare.
  • Advise project teams and business stakeholders on authorization-related requirements, risks and solutions.
  • Provide technical support and consulting to SAP users worldwide on roles, access and authorization-related issues.
  • Collaborate with international stakeholders to ensure consistent and sustainable authorization solutions across the organization.

What distinguishes you:

Education

  • Degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science or a related field, or a comparable technical qualification and professional background.

Experience

  • Several years of relevant professional experience in SAP authorization management, SAP security or roles and authorizations.
  • Experience designing and implementing authorization and role concepts in SAP ERP environments.
  • Experience supporting SAP projects throughout different implementation phases, such as migration, cutover, go-live or hypercare.
  • Experience with SAP R/3 and exposure to SAP S/4HANA environments.
  • Experience working with international stakeholders and cross-functional project teams is valued.

Know-how

  • Strong knowledge of SAP authorization management and role development.
  • Good understanding of functional processes within SAP ERP environments and their impact on authorization concepts.
  • Broad cross-module SAP knowledge and the ability to understand authorization requirements across different business processes.
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA roles and authorization concepts is a strong advantage.
  • Ability to analyze authorization issues and translate business requirements into secure and practical technical solutions.
  • Understanding of authorization requirements during SAP migration and transformation projects.

Languages

  • Very good command of English, written and spoken, enabling effective collaboration in a global environment.
  • German language skills are an advantage.

Working Style and Methods

  • You approach complex authorization topics in a structured, analytical and solution-oriented way.
  • You work independently while collaborating closely with business, technical and project stakeholders.
  • You communicate complex SAP topics clearly and adapt your communication to different audiences.
  • You are comfortable coordinating multiple topics and stakeholders in an international environment.
  • You combine a strong service mindset with a focus on quality, security and business requirements.

Personality

  • You enjoy collaborating with people from different functions, cultures and locations.
  • You communicate confidently and constructively and enjoy bringing stakeholders together.
  • You are proactive and take ownership of your topics.
  • You are adaptable and open to evolving technologies, processes and ways of working.
  • You enjoy solving complex problems and helping others navigate SAP authorization topics.

Additional Information

  • Willingness to travel internationally.

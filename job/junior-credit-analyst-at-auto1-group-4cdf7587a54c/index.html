Junior Credit Analyst

AUTO1 Group · 10000, Albania

Onsite · Junior · Business / Domain Analyst · Posted 19 October 2026

Apply on AUTO1 Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

As a Junior Credit Analyst, you will support the financing process by reviewing customer applications, verifying financial documents, and assessing whether applications meet the required policies and guidelines. You will work with customer data and credit assessment tools to ensure accurate evaluations and a smooth financing process from application review to contract signature.

Your tasks:

  • Review financing applications to ensure all required information and documents are complete and accurate.
  • Verify customer identification documents and salary information in line with internal policies and guidelines.
  • Enter customer data into the credit scoring system to support the credit assessment process.
  • Use credit agency information and available financial data to support credit evaluations.
  • Calculate and adjust financing offers within the approved guidelines.
  • Coordinate with internal departments to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.
  • Identify missing information or discrepancies and follow up when necessary.
  • Contribute ideas to improve processes and maintain high-quality standards.

Requirements:

  • Completed commercial education or at least one year of relevant professional experience, preferably in banking, finance, or insurance.
  • Strong attention to detail and a structured, independent approach to work.
  • Good numerical and analytical skills.
  • Confidence in using Google Workspace tools, especially Sheets, Gmail, and Calendar.
  • Experience with credit reports or credit assessment tools is an advantage.
  • Good command of English and/or German.
  • Reliability, a sense of responsibility, and the ability to work effectively in a team

The place of work will be at AUTO1 Albania headquarters, Rruga Dritan Hoxha, Kompleksi E88, Tiranë, Albania.

If you are detail-oriented, comfortable working with numbers, and interested in developing your career in credit analysis and financing operations, we would love to hear from you.

Please apply by uploading your CV in English.

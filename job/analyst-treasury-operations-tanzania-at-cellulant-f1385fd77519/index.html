About the job

About Cellulant:

Cellulant is Africa’s leading payments company, providing seamless, secure and innovative solutions that empower businesses, banks, and global brands to thrive in a fast-changing global economy.

With a presence in over 24 countries and 200+ payment methods, including cards, bank transfers, and mobile money, our single API payment platform, Tingg, simplifies collections, disbursements, and reconciliations. It processes over 4.5 million transactions daily for market leaders across
sectors such as
Airlines, Telecoms, E-commerce, Ride-Hailing, Retail, and Remittances.

By simplifying how people pay and get paid, we drive trust, commerce and scale – and connect companies to their ambitions.

Role Overview:

The Analyst:Treasury Operations provides administrative and operational support to the treasury team, ensuring smooth and accurate execution of back-office activities. This role is ideal for individuals looking to grow into treasury operations by supporting analysts in managing daily tasks and documentation while gaining exposure to treasury processes.

What You'll do:

  • KYC & Documentation Support: Assist in gathering and organizing KYC documents for merchants and internal stakeholders. Ensure all required documentation for adding or removing users on banking platforms is complete.
  • New Account Support: Help complete forms and collect board resolutions for new account openings.
  • Payments Support: Assist in sending proofs of payment (POP) to relevant stakeholders once payments are completed.
  • Daily & Weekly Reporting: Update daily and weekly bank and wallet balances, ensuring records are accurate and available for review.
  • Incoming Funds Confirmation: Monitor merchant emails to confirm receipt of incoming payments and escalate discrepancies to analysts.
  • Settlement Assistance: Support the settlement and reconciliation team in tracking daily settlement amounts and recording transactions accurately.
  • Bank Mandate & Compliance Support: Assist in maintaining updated bank mandates and other compliance-related documents.
  • Administrative Support: Provide general administrative support to treasury analysts to ensure efficient operations.

What we are looking for:

  • 5 years of experience in a payments, back-office, or administrative support role within the Payment or Fintech.
  • Familiarity with different payment methods.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Excel.
  • Ability to manage time efficiently and prioritize tasks.
  • Eager to learn, grow, and develop a career in treasury operations.
  • Knowledge of fintech operations or banking experience.
  • B.Com, Economics, Accounting or applicable Business related courses.

Why the role matters:

In a fast-moving, multi-region fintech environment, operational precision and regulatory compliance form the bedrock of our trust. The Analyst: Treasury Operations is the essential engine room that keeps our back-office moving smoothly. By meticulously managing vital documentation from bank mandates and KYC to daily balance tracking and transaction proofs you ensure our compliance remains airtight and our reporting stays flawless. This role bridges administrative execution with core financial operations, directly protecting the business from regulatory risk while freeing up strategic capacity for the wider treasury team. It is a high-growth launchpad designed for a sharp, detail-oriented professional ready to master the fundamentals of fintech treasury.

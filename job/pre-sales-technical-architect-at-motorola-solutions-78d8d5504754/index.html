Department Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we are dedicated to "Solving for safer" by building the mission-critical ecosystem that public safety and enterprise customers rely on as their lifeline. Our Pre-Sales Architectural team plays a pivotal role in this mission by designing compelling, highly supportable applications using consulting-led selling techniques. We partner across functional areas, collaborating with subcontractors and customers to finalize requirements and address complex technical challenges that ultimately help protect communities.
As a Pre-Sales Technical Architect, you will serve as a strategic technical advisor, deeply understanding customer problem spaces to propose and implement appropriate technology infrastructures. You will thrive in an inclusive environment that values diverse perspectives, working alongside multiple bodies within customer organizations to improve business processes and evaluate testing systems to help provide satisfactory functionality. Bringing a strong foundation of equivalent practical experience, you will act as the bridge between complex public safety needs and our innovative technology solutions.


Job Description

  • Lead technical sales activities related to assigned sales opportunities to yield maximum product and services revenue while driving customer satisfaction.
  • Partner closely with the entire bid team to maintain overall customer objectives and strategy throughout the sales cycle.
  • Provide specific solutions, technology, and product consulting, performing in-depth and high-level technical presentations for customers and partners.
  • Define new services and technical solutions deployed in on-premise and multi-tenanted off-premise configurations alongside internal product groups and third-party vendors.
  • Design and implement solutions for user trials and pilots, assisting with the deployment of post-sale architectures where required.
  • Document comprehensive system designs and prepare proposal materials, including system descriptions and bill-of-materials.
  • Support the procurement organisation by evaluating and obtaining quotations from equipment vendors.
  • Extensive experience working as a supplier of solutions to UK public safety agencies (preferably both pre-sale and post-sale)
  • Solid experience in the detailed design and deployment of complex IT solutions to establish credibility with potential customers.

Domain Expertise in at least three of the following to develop technical architectures: Emergency telephony and ICCS, CAD, CCTV/mobile video streaming, Digital Evidence Management, Records Management, Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and location services, Data management/analytics, or Mobile applications.

Full professional fluency in English (written and spoken) to clearly document complex technical solutions to customers.


Basic Requirements

  • Strong understanding of control room business processes used by public safety agencies (police, fire, and health) in Western Europe.
  • Proficiency in transition planning and execution to bring solutions into service.
  • Working knowledge of the latest IT, IP networking technologies, and software integration technologies (e.g., web services and ESBs).
  • Familiarity with the Motorola Solutions product portfolio or relevant experience with key competitor offerings in the specified domains.
  • Experience working with ITIL practices in the delivery of IT service management.
  • Background in interfacing with software development organizations, including requirements specification, use of requirements management tools, and Agile development.
  • Excellent listening, negotiation, and presentation skills.
  • Self-motivated ability to initiate and build solid working relationships and operate with minimal supervision.
  • Strong communications, business/marketing, financial, and computer skills.

Education

Successful candidates are likely to hold degree with a minimum of military experience, OR experience working in an external customer interfacing role specifically in the Telecommunications or IT Industry. Certifications related to system and network architecture such as MCSE, MSNE, CWNA, CCNA/CCDP, MCSE/NE, CISSP would be an advantage.

Location/Travel

The position is home office based, with up to 20% located at Motorola Solutions or customer offices as required for specific projects. (Car Allowance Provided)

The candidate must be eligible to live and work in the UK and documented evidence of the eligibility will be required from candidates as part of the recruitment process.


The candidate will ideally already hold a current UK Security Clearance, or be willing to undergo Security vetting, following a Baseline Personnel Security Screening (BPSS) check.

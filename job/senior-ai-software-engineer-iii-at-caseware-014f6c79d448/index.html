About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Colombia.

Caseware combines over 35 years of industry expertise with AI-powered technology to transform how audit and accounting professionals work. Serving organizations across 130 countries, we bring intelligent automation directly into audit and financial reporting workflows—helping professionals reduce manual work, navigate complexity, and focus on informed decisions. Join our global team and help build technology that strengthens trust in financial information.

📍 Location: This is a fully remote position located in Colombia.
Contact
Maira Russo - Senior Talent Acquisition Partner

What you'll be doing:

• Define and evolve the technical architecture for Caseware’s AI platform,
establishing architectural standards, contracts, and design principles that enable
scalable, secure, reusable, and trustworthy AI capabilities across multiple teams.
• Design, build, and operate AI systems powered by LLMs, retrieval pipelines, agentic
workflows, and AI-enabled services, including multi-agent systems, agent
execution runtimes, workflow orchestration, memory management, and human-inthe-loop AI capabilities.
• Design and implement evaluation, governance, auditability, and traceability
frameworks that ensure AI systems are measurable, explainable, compliant, and
continuously improving.
• Translate advances in AI into practical, maintainable platform capabilities that
solve real customer problems.
• Influence technical direction through architecture reviews, RFCs, design
discussions, and hands-on collaboration.
• Evaluate emerging AI technologies, models, frameworks, and cloud capabilities.
• Lead proof-of-concepts that inform platform strategy and future investments.
• Balance delivery speed, quality, maintainability, and long-term platform health
when making technical decisions.
• Drive reliability, observability, monitoring, alerting, and incident response practices
across AI systems and platforms.
• Ensure systems are resilient, scalable, cost-effective, and operationally efficient in
production environments.
• Partner closely with Product, QA, Security, Domain SMEs, and Engineering teams to
deliver business value.
• Act as a trusted technical leader, helping teams succeed through collaboration,
influence, and shared ownership.
• Mentor engineers across teams and contribute to raising overall engineering
maturity.

What you'll bring:

• 8+ years of professional software engineering experience, with demonstrated
impact beyond a single team
• 2+ years building and operating production AI systems.
• Proven experience designing distributed, SaaS, cloud-native, multi-tenant
platforms at scale.
• Proven experience designing distributed, SaaS, cloud-native, multi-tenant
platforms at scale.
• Experience implementing AI guardrails, governance controls, and safety
mechanisms.
• Strong understanding of AI system tradeoffs including latency, cost, accuracy,
reliability, and scalability.
• Extensive experience building and operating production systems on AWS.
• Experience with Infrastructure as Code using CDK, CloudFormation, or Terraform.
• Experience mentoring engineers and influencing architectural decisions across
teams.
• Strong English language communication and collaboration skills.
• Comfortable operating in fast-moving environments with ambiguity and evolving
requirements.

Nice to Have

• Experience operating systems in regulated or compliance-heavy domains.
• Familiarity with accounting, auditing, or financial workflows.

Tech stack you’ll be working with:

• Backend & Platform: TypeScript, NestJS, Python
• Cloud & Infrastructure: AWS EKS, AWS Lambda, AWS Bedrock, AWS AgentCore
• Search & Retrieval: AWS OpenSearch Serverless
• Document & Data Processing: AWS Textract, DynamoDB, S3
• AI Evaluation & Observability: LangFuse, LangSmith, LangChain, LangGraph
• AI-Assisted Development: GitHub Copilot, AWS Kiro
• Developer Tooling: GitHub, GitHub Actions, Nx Monorepo
• Collaboration: Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Teams, Outlook

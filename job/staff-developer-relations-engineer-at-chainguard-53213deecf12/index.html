About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States and Europe.

Chainguard is the trusted source for open source. By delivering hardened, secure, and production-ready builds of all the open source software engineers and AI agents rely on, Chainguard helps organizations build faster, stay compliant, and eliminate risk.
Our customers include Fortune 500 enterprises and global industry leaders, including Anduril, Canva, Fortinet, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, OpenAI, Snap Inc., and Snowflake.
Chainguard is venture-backed by leading investors, including Amplify, IVP, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Mantis VC, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Spark Capital.

The role in a nutshell:

At Chainguard we're focused on securing the software supply chain, and we're launching the next generation of CI/CD products at the intersection of security and AI. We're hiring a Staff DevRel Engineer to help developers everywhere secure their code and workflows, and ship with confidence.

This is a high-impact, high-visibility role. We're looking for someone developers already know and trust: a builder who ships useful content fast and meets developers where they work. Adoption follows from experience content that solves real problems. Our success is measured by guiding developers from sign up, through first use, and building on our platform.

Reporting to the Head of DevRel, you'll join a highly collaborative, world-class Developer Relations team. You'll be the vital interface between our product and engineering teams and the community of developers adopting our CI/CD products.

What you'll do

  • Drive developer adoption for our CI/CD products, helping developers go from first discovery to secure workflows running in production
  • Ship a steady, high-velocity stream of experience content (code samples, tutorials, workshops, videos and posts) that gets developers from first look to a securing workflows fast
  • Design and run engagement campaigns that bring waves of new developers with intent to build, and help that interest turn into active use
  • Show up where practitioners already are (GitHub, Reddit, LinkedIn, community forums, conferences and meetups) and be a trusted voice there
  • Track activation and sustained usage, and double down on what helps developers succeed
  • Evaluate product updates hands-on, bring feedback from your own experience and the community, and shape messaging that helps developers see the true value of what we're building
  • Collaborate with partners, open-source maintainers and community leaders to extend your reach beyond our own channels
  • Partner across our Marketing org (product marketing, brand/creative, growth) to amplify launches and campaigns
  • Help scale our programs to GTM teams (ABM, Sales Engineers, Customer Success, PS) so they drive growth and success with existing customers
  • Expect up to 50% travel

What we're looking for

  • A proven record of driving developer adoption at scale: content, community work or campaigns that brought thousands of users to a product
  • A portfolio of code and content created to enable or teach developers, and the ability to produce high-quality work quickly
  • 7+ years of technical experience with a true software engineering foundation
  • 5+ years engaging other developers, whether in DevRel, professional services, technical instruction or product engineering
  • Deep, hands-on experience building CI/CD systems and pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, CircleCI), plus familiarity with containers and cloud infrastructure
  • Established influence in the CI/CD and DevOps community, with a strong presence in at least one channel like GitHub, LinkedIn, Reddit or community forums
  • Hands-on experience with AI agents and an understanding of the benefits and challenges they introduce to developer workflows
  • Comfort across the full developer journey, from first awareness to hands-on use and customer success

Nice to have

  • Maintainer or leading contributor to an open-source project, ideally in the CI/CD or DevOps space
  • Experience with our ecosystem partners
  • Familiarity supporting account-based programs alongside a go-to-market team

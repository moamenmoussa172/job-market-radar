About this Role

We are seeking a SAP Datasphere & SQL Developer with 4–6 years of experience in data warehousing or SAP analytics environments. The role requires hands-on experience with SAP Datasphere, strong SQL knowledge and a sound understanding of data modelling, dimensional modelling and data integration. You will independently interpret requirements, develop appropriate technical solutions and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.

What you’ll do

  • Develop data solutions using SAP Datasphere.
  • Design and work with SAP Datasphere views, data flows, transformations and connections.
  • Develop and optimise SQL-based solutions to support data and analytics requirements.
  • Apply data warehousing and dimensional modelling concepts when developing solutions.
  • Support data integration activities across relevant systems and data sources.
  • Independently understand requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Analyse and resolve data and technical challenges using structured problem-solving approaches.
  • Collaborate and communicate effectively with cross-functional teams.

Who you are

  • You have 4–6 years of experience in data warehousing or SAP analytics environments.
  • You have hands-on experience with SAP Datasphere.
  • You understand SAP Datasphere data modelling concepts, including views, data flows, transformations and connections.
  • You have strong hands-on knowledge of SQL.
  • You understand data warehousing, dimensional modelling and data integration concepts.
  • Experience with an ETL or data integration tool would be beneficial.
  • You demonstrate effective analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • You can independently understand business or technical requirements and develop appropriate solutions.
  • You communicate effectively and work collaboratively with cross-functional teams.

What's in it for you

  • Work hands-on with SAP Datasphere and SQL within data and analytics environments.
  • Apply data warehousing, dimensional modelling and data integration knowledge to practical requirements.
  • Work independently on technical solutions while collaborating with cross-functional teams.
  • Broaden your exposure to enterprise data integration and analytics use cases.

What skills you will learn

  • Advanced SAP Datasphere data modelling capabilities.
  • SQL development and optimisation skills.
  • Practical experience with views, data flows, transformations and connections.
  • Data warehousing and dimensional modelling expertise.
  • Data integration and ETL-related capabilities.
  • Requirement analysis, technical solution design and cross-functional collaboration skills.

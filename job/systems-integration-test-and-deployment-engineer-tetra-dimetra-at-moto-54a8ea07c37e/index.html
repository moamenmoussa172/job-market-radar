Department Overview

Join our team and help us build the future of communication!
Motorola Solutions is looking for a talented and motivated Systems Integration, Test and Deployment Engineer to join our Systems Integration team. In this role, you will be responsible for the system-level integration and validation of TETRA/Dimetra systems, as well as their deployment and roll-out directly at customer sites, ensuring they meet the highest standards of quality and functionality.


Job Description

What you will do:

  • Analyze & Design: Analyze system requirements for Dimetra systems operating within the TETRA standard and design comprehensive test scenarios.
  • Test & Debug: Execute functional system-level tests, analyze results, delve into log files, debug applications and interfaces, and troubleshoot protocol stacks.
  • Deploy & Integrate: Travel to customer sites to deploy, configure, and integrate certified Dimetra features, system updates, and infrastructure into live environments.
  • On-site Validation: Conduct final acceptance testing (SAT/FAT) and validation of implemented features directly on the customer's infrastructure.
  • Troubleshoot & Optimize: Provide expert-level troubleshooting and system tuning during the deployment phase, utilizing various tools for debugging and analysis.
  • Environment Preparation: Prepare system configurations for Transport Network, Base Stations, Core Servers, all SW applications, interfaces, and mobile subscribers for both lab and system-level tests.
  • Collaborate: Act as a technical point of contact for clients during the roll-out phase, and collaborate effectively with internal Architects, System Designers, and development teams.
  • Methodology: Leverage your experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies and balance strong manual testing skills with the development of automated tests using AI.

This job requires 4 days a week in the Krakow office.


Basic Requirements

Who you are:

  • Education: You hold a Bachelor's degree in Telecommunications, Information and Communications Technology, Computer Science, Electronics, Automation, or a related field.
  • Technical Knowledge: You possess a solid understanding of IP networks (v4), routing protocols, TCP/IP, and telecommunications around RF signaling.
  • OS Familiarity: You are familiar with operating systems: Linux, Unix, Windows 10/11, Windows Server.
  • Mobility: You are ready and willing to travel to customer locations (domestic and international) for deployment projects (approx. 60-70% of the time).
  • Soft Skills: You have excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to represent the company professionally and work effectively under pressure in dynamic, on-site environments.
  • Language: You are proficient in English (allowing for fluent communication), goal-oriented,

Additional skills that will be an advantage:

  • Experience in building IP networks and configuring network devices.
  • Experience with servers hosting Linux OS.
  • Network traffic analysis skills with Wireshark.
  • Advanced telecommunications knowledge.
  • ISTQB certificate

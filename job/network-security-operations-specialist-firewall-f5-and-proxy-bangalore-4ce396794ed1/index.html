About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Network Security Operations professional to join the VNO-ESOC team supporting Heritage 3 Network Operations. This role is focused on delivering reliable, secure and efficient operational support across enterprise network security infrastructure, including firewalls, proxy platforms, load balancers, VMware environments and Cisco FMC. The successful candidate will play a key role in incident resolution, service restoration, change implementation, risk management and continuous service improvement while ensuring high availability of customer-facing security services.

What you’ll do

Incident, Problem and Service Request Management
•Troubleshoot and resolve incidents across firewall, proxy, load balancer, virtualisation and security management platforms within agreed service levels.
•Investigate complex service-impacting issues across Cisco, F5, Clavister, Fortinet, NetGuard Firewalls, Symantec/Broadcom Blue Coat Proxy, VMware Hypervisors and Cisco FMC.
•Manage L2 and L3 operational escalations including traffic flow analysis, routing issues, NAT issues, VPN connectivity problems, proxy access failures, load balancing issues and performance degradation.
•Maintain accurate incident records, root cause analysis, resolution details and closure documentation in line with ITIL practices.
•Collaborate with customers, vendors, internal teams and third-party providers to ensure timely resolution of critical incidents.
•Identify recurring issues and drive corrective actions to improve service stability and operational efficiency.
•Support hardware replacements, vendor escalations, RMA activities and service restoration efforts.
•Create and maintain operational documentation, standard operating procedures and implementation guides.
Change and Operational Support
•Review and validate change requests for technical accuracy, risk assessment, rollback planning and customer impact.
•Support platform upgrades, firmware updates, patch deployments, certificate renewals, vulnerability remediation and lifecycle management activities.
•Ensure operational resilience and availability of critical application and security services.
Firewall Operations and Security Policy Management
•Support day-to-day firewall operations across Fortinet, Cisco, Clavister and NetGuard platforms.
•Implement and validate firewall rule changes, access policies, NAT configurations, VPN configurations, routing updates and security zoning requirements.
•Participate in policy optimisation, governance and operational best practices.
F5 Load Balancer Operations
•Manage F5 load balancer services including virtual servers, pools, nodes, monitors, SSL profiles, iRules and traffic policies.
•Troubleshoot application availability, persistence, SSL offloading, health monitoring, traffic routing and performance issues.
Proxy and Web Security Operations
•Support enterprise proxy technologies including Symantec/Broadcom Proxy and Blue Coat platforms.
•Resolve URL filtering issues, authentication failures, SSL inspection concerns, policy mismatches and web traffic routing problems.
•Implement approved proxy policy changes, access controls, category exceptions and allow/block list updates.

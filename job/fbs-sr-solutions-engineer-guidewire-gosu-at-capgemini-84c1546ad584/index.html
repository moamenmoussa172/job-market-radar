About the job

FBS – Farmer Business Services is part of Farmers Insurance operations with the purpose of building a global approach to identifying, recruiting, hiring, and retaining top talent. By combining international reach with US expertise, we build diverse and high-performing teams that are equipped to thrive in today’s competitive marketplace.

We believe that the foundation of every successful business lies in having the right people with the right skills. That is where we come in—helping Farmers build a winning team that delivers consistent and sustainable results.

Since we don’t have a local legal entity, we’ve partnered with Capgemini, which acts as the Employer of Record. Capgemini is responsible for managing local payroll and benefits.

What to expect on your journey with us:

  • A solid and innovative company with a strong market presence
  • A dynamic, diverse, and multicultural work environment
  • Leaders with deep market knowledge and strategic vision
  • Continuous learning and development

The Sr Solution Engineer will serve as the senior engineering leader responsible for driving technical delivery, execution governance, and engineering excellence across multiple Guidewire programs and delivery teams. This role acts as the bridge between Architecture, Product, Engineering, and Business stakeholders, ensuring successful delivery of modernization initiatives while maintaining platform stability, scalability, and operational excellence.

💡 Key Responsibilities

  • Lead technical delivery across multiple Guidewire initiatives.
  • Partner with Architecture to translate solution designs into executable implementation plans.
  • Drive delivery predictability through dependency management and risk mitigation.
  • Facilitate technical decision-making, design reviews, and implementation planning.
  • Ensure engineering best practices, governance, and quality standards.
  • Promote automation, reusable frameworks, and engineering accelerators.
  • Lead production readiness, performance optimization, and operational excellence.
  • Coordinate critical production incidents and major release activities.
  • Mentor technical leads and senior engineers.
  • Drive scalability, reliability, and modernization efforts across the platform.
  • Lead Guidewire modernization initiatives leveraging experience from 2–3 full transformation journeys.
  • Apply modernization best practices to mitigate risks and accelerate delivery.
  • Guide teams through complex Guidewire implementation and cloud transformation challenges.

Requirements

  • 15+ years of overall software engineering experience.
  • 9+ years of Guidewire Insurance Suite experience.
  • 7+ years of advance Gosu, Java/Spring Boot, REST APIs,
  • 3Hrs Overlap with US Time zone (Pacific Time) Flexibility
  • 5+ years leading multiple delivery teams or large-scale programs.
  • Strong background in Guidewire PolicyCenter implementations and modernization initiatives.
  • Experience driving cloud transformation and Guidewire Cloud Platform adoption.
  • Proven ability to lead technical strategy and execution across cross-functional teams.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influence without direct authority.
  • Experience mentoring senior engineers and technical leads.
  • Ability to manage technical risks, dependencies, and large-scale enterprise delivery.
  • Strong communication skills with business and technical stakeholders.
  • Fluent English.

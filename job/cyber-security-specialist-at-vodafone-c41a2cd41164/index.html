At Vodafone, Cyber Security plays a critical role in protecting our networks, systems, customers and critical infrastructure from an evolving cyber threat landscape.

We are looking for an experienced Cyber Defence Specialist with at least 5 years of relevant Cyber Security experience to strengthen our Cyber Defence capability.

You will be responsible for detecting, investigating and responding to cyber threats, assessing emerging vulnerabilities and attack techniques, proactively identifying security weaknesses, and continuously improving our monitoring and defensive capabilities.

This is a hands-on technical role for someone who combines strong Cyber Security expertise with an investigative mindset and can translate complex technical findings into clear risk, business impact and remediation priorities.

What you’ll do

  • Monitor, analyse and investigate security events, alerts and suspicious activity across endpoints, networks, identities, cloud environments and applications;
  • Perform proactive threat hunting, using available security telemetry and threat intelligence to identify potentially malicious activity;
  • Develop and continuously improve security monitoring and detection use cases, correlation rules and detection logic;
  • Assess emerging cyber threats, vulnerabilities and exploits to determine potential exposure and relevance to Vodafone;
  • Support the prioritisation of vulnerabilities based on exploitability, threat intelligence, asset criticality and potential business impact;
  • Work with relevant teams to improve security telemetry, visibility and detection coverage;
  • Analyse indicators of compromise including suspicious IP addresses and network activity;
  • Work closely with SOC, Incident Response, Network, Infrastructure, Cloud, Application Security and Secure by Design teams to investigate and reduce cyber risk;
  • Contribute to Red Team, Blue Team and Purple Team activities.

Who you are

  • Strong investigative and analytical mindset; Naturally curious and comfortable working with incomplete or ambiguous information;
  • Distinguishes between a security alert, a technical vulnerability and a material Cyber Security risk;
  • Remains calm and make sound decisions under stress;
  • Takes ownership of an investigation from initial detection through analysis, remediation and closure;
  • Challenge assumptions and follow technical evidence to reach a conclusion;
  • Communicates complex technical issues clearly to both technical teams and senior stakeholders;
  • Works effectively across Cyber Security, Technology, Network and Operational teams;
  • Proactive in identifying opportunities to improve Vodafone’s defensive capabilities;
  • Build strong and effective relationships with Cyber team members, local and global and with other technology and business stakeholders;
  • Fluency in the English language.

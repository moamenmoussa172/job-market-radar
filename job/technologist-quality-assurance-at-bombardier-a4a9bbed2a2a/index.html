About the job

Why join us?

At Bombardier, we design, build and maintain the world’s peak-performing aircraft for the world’s most discerning people and businesses, governments and militaries. We have been successful in setting the highest standards by putting our people at the heart of it all, and defining excellence, together.
Working at Bombardier means operating at the highest level. Every day, you are part of a team that delivers superior experiences and products, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in our industry and beyond. By prioritizing employee growth and development, we empower everyone to reach their full potential on their own terms, because the best work happens when you are free to be yourself and share your unique expertise.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Perform source inspections at supplier sites to validate product conformity
  • Take ownership of production disruptions related to supplier performance
  • Lead structured problem-solving activities (8D, RCCA) across stakeholders
  • Identify root causes and implement robust corrective actions
  • Drive follow-up to ensure actions are completed and effective
  • Coordinate cross-functional efforts to restore operational stability
  • Organize and perform other QA related activities (i.e., project related activity, Monthly Reports, reporting needs as required

How to thrive in this role?

  • Typically 5+ years of experience in a technical field (technology, documentation, technical writing, website development,

Office, etc)

  • Thorough data compilation and program tracking experience
  • Thorough knowledge and experience reporting with MS Word, PowerPoint and Excel
  • Thorough knowledge and experience in organization and delivery of training with high degree of presentation delivery skills
  • Thorough knowledge and experience s of policy / procedure writing including coordination of any project
  • Thorough knowledge and experience in benchmarking and documentation mapping
  • Thorough knowledge and experience of regulatory Quality Systems (TCCA, FAA, etc), Quality Assurance interpretation, methodology and techniques
  • Six Sigma Black Belt preferred

Now that you can see yourself in this role, apply and join the Bombardier Team!

About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Technology (CA)
Title: Mid-Level Software Engineer (Data Solutions) - Indaiatuba/SP - 124942

As a Software Engineer for our IT Human Resources & Labor Relations team at John Deere, you will have the opportunity to work cross global applications over IT HR & LR products to sustain and enhance a diverse set of applications, own processes and servers, serve business and the team, implement global solutions and support local business needs.

Main Responsibilities

  • Develop, enhance, and maintain data pipelines and applications within the Enterprise Data Lake (EDL) and Databricks ecosystem;
  • Design and support analytical and operational integrations for labor relations and workforce management systems (wages, shifts, and time tracking);
  • Build and maintain APIs, ingestion routines, and connections to enterprise relational databases;
  • Actively collaborate within a global agile team, engaging daily with international teams and stakeholders in English;
  • Promote and share best practices in data engineering and software development across the team;
  • Ensure delivery quality through automated testing, monitoring, and CI/CD pipelines.

Skills You Need

  • Graduation on Technology areas, such as Computer Science, System Analysis, or similar;
  • Advanced English skills (read, write and speak);
  • Strong experience as a Software Engineer;
  • Strong experience with EDL / Databricks;
  • Experience with at least one of the following programming languages: Java, Python, JavaScript, Node/React or similar;
  • Previous experience working with Cloud (AWS preferred);
  • Strong experience working with Databases (SQL syntax, SQL Server, Oracle DB, MySQL, and/or PostgreSQL);
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to excel on a cross-functional team;
  • Experience working on Agile Teams;
  • Availability to work in a hybrid model (at least 3 days a week on-site in Indaiatuba, SP).

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Solid Experience working with Data Lake, DevOps, and/or CICD pipelines;
  • Experience integrating systems, designing and coding APIs;
  • Experience with Javascript modern libraries (React, Angular, BootStrap,..);
  • Experience with automation & data analytics;
  • Experience with automated tests, complex pipelines and best programming practices.

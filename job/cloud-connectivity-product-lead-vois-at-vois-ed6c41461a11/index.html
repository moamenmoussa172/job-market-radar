About this Role

We are seeking a highly motivated professional to drive the strategy, roadmap and lifecycle management of Multi Cloud Networks and Cloud Network Services within the Connectivity portfolio. This role will play a key part in enabling standardised, secure and scalable connectivity solutions for cloud-based services by collaborating with technology, architecture, security, engineering and operations teams. The successful candidate will contribute to product strategy, investment prioritisation, stakeholder engagement, adoption planning and delivery execution while supporting agile ways of working across a global environment.

What you’ll do

  • Own and evolve the product strategy, roadmap and lifecycle for Multi Cloud Networks and Cloud Network Services.
  • Translate cloud connectivity requirements into scalable product capabilities and prioritised roadmap initiatives.
  • Support the development of business cases, funding proposals, go-to-market plans and communications strategies.
  • Manage demand and prioritisation across cloud, application and infrastructure stakeholders.
  • Collaborate with network, cloud, architecture, security, engineering and operations teams to enhance connectivity products and services.
  • Promote standardisation and reusability of cloud connectivity patterns to improve efficiency and scalability.
  • Govern product initiatives from concept through implementation, adoption and lifecycle management.
  • Define, monitor and communicate business and technical performance indicators, adoption trends and customer outcomes.
  • Maintain strong customer engagement and continuous feedback mechanisms to support ongoing product improvement.
  • Support Agile and SAFe delivery practices across product and delivery teams.

Who you are

  • Proven experience in Product Management within connectivity, enterprise networking, cloud connectivity or infrastructure services.
  • Experience working within Agile delivery environments and Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) methodologies.
  • Strong expertise in product roadmap management, demand prioritisation, portfolio management and investment planning.
  • Sound understanding of enterprise networking, cloud infrastructure and public cloud connectivity, including Microsoft Azure and cloud network architecture.
  • Ability to translate technical requirements into customer-focused product outcomes while collaborating with cross-functional teams.
  • Excellent stakeholder engagement, communication and influencing skills within international and multi-market environments.

  • Opportunity to shape strategic cloud connectivity products used across a global telecommunications environment.
  • Exposure to cloud networking, enterprise infrastructure and large-scale transformation initiatives.
  • Collaboration with international stakeholders across technology, security, architecture, engineering and operations functions.
  • Opportunity to influence investment decisions, technology direction and product adoption at scale.
  • Experience working within a mature Agile and SAFe product delivery environment.

What skills you will learn

  • Advanced cloud connectivity and network product management practices.
  • Product strategy development, roadmap planning and lifecycle governance.
  • Portfolio prioritisation and investment management techniques.
  • Stakeholder management across complex global organisations.
  • Business case development, go-to-market planning and value realisation measurement.
  • Agile product leadership and SAFe delivery frameworks.
  • Enterprise cloud networking and Microsoft Azure connectivity architecture.

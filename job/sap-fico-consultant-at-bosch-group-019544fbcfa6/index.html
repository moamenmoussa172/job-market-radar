About the job

Strong business knowledge in accounting & finance. Work experience in handling accounting for manufacturing companies’ preferably.

  • Should have SAP experience of 3-4 years as a Consultant.
  • Minimum One full end to end implementation in SAP, 2 roll-out projects and support project experience in FICO. Collect and review country requirements together with key user.
  • Knowledge of General Ledger/Accounts Receivable/Accounts Payable/ Bank transactions/Direct & Indirect Taxes/Fixed Assets.
  • Exposure to country specific requirements/ New GL will be preferred.
  • Knowledge of CO- Cost center Accounting, Profit Center Accounting and Internal order accounting.
  • Knowledge on Product Costing and CO-PA are preferable.
  • Strong Knowledge of FI -integration with MM, SD & PP is a must. HR integration will be an added advantage.
  • Exposure to Cutover strategy, Testing and Migration are mandatory.
  • Knowledge on any sub module of FSCM is preferable.
  • SAP certification will be an added advantage
  • 3-4 years of experience in SAP ECC, S4 HANA Implementation/Rollout experience.
  • SAP experience on functional areas with the Financial Accounting & Controlling modules.
  • Experience full end to end implementation in SAP, roll-out projects and support project experience in FICO.
  • Knowledge of General Ledger/Accounts Receivable/Tax/Accounts Payable/ Bank/ transactions/Direct & Indirect Taxes/Fixed Assets, Electronic Invoicing.
  • Exposure to country specific requirements/ New GL will be preferred.
  • Knowledge of CO- Cost center Accounting, Profit Center Accounting and Internal order accounting.
  • Knowledge on Product Costing and CO-PA are preferable.
  • Knowledge on IDOC and Interfaces.
  • Desirable SAP ECC or S4/HANA certification.

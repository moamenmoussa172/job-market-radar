Email Security Operations Analyst

About the job

Role Summary:
Support email operations, spam/abuse detection, and Trust & Safety workflows to ensure a secure, reliable, and high-quality email ecosystem.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Monitor and investigate inbound/outbound email traffic for spam, phishing, spoofing, and abuse patterns
  • Perform log analysis using Splunk to identify anomalies, suspicious behavior, and emerging threats
  • Execute mitigation actions (blocking, quarantining, escalation) in line with defined SOPs
  • Validate email authentication protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and flag discrepancies
  • Support incident response by documenting findings, maintaining audit trails, and escalating critical risks
  • Adhere to SLAs, quality benchmarks, and compliance standards in a 24x7 operational environment
  • Collaborate with internal teams and external vendors for issue resolution and trend tracking
  • 2+ years of experience in email operations, Trust & Safety, or SOC environments
  • Working knowledge of SMTP, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and email delivery infrastructure
  • Experience in Splunk SIEM and Email security tools like O365 Defender, Proofpoint
  • Hands-on experience with Splunk for log analysis
  • Strong analytical, investigative, and problem-solving skills
  • Must have PDP score of Exceeds Expectations or Outstanding Tenure of at least 18 months
  • No Active DA Interested candidates
  • Must be willing to work onsite in Taguig

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.

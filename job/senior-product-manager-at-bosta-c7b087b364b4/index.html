About the job

About Bosta:

Launched in 2017, Bosta is an overnight delivery provider for E-commerce companies in Egypt. Leveraging technology to empower businesses is what we do best. Our goal is to disrupt the logistics industry by bringing technology and experience together to provide a one-stop-shop for delivery solutions.

We rely on advanced technology to deliver orders to our customers in a fast way. Businesses can track and connect with couriers and follow up on their orders - all in one system.

About the Role:

As a Senior Product Manager, you'll own a full product area end-to-end, setting its direction, roadmap, and strategy. You'll operate with a high degree of autonomy and be a trusted partner to senior stakeholders, while also supporting the growth of other Product Managers.

Job Description:

  • Frame and scope problems independently across your full area of ownership
  • Design and drive solutions end-to-end, inventing new approaches when no existing playbook applies
  • Own the roadmap and strategy for your area
  • Deliver independently and help raise the delivery bar for your wider team
  • Use experimentation and data analysis to guide decisions
  • Take full ownership of outcomes across your area, including resolving open questions about ownership and scope
  • Build lasting trust and partnership with senior stakeholders
  • Formally mentor and support the growth of other Product Managers
  • Reason independently about cost and revenue trade-offs
  • Job Requirements:
  • 4+ years of experience as a Product Manager
  • A demonstrated track record of owning a full product area roadmap, strategy, and outcomes with strong autonomy
  • Experience inventing new approaches to problems when no existing playbook applies
  • Comfort navigating ambiguity, including resolving cross-team ownership questions
  • A history of being trusted by senior leaders as a genuine partner
  • Experience formally mentoring other Product Managers
  • Strong, independent commercial judgment around cost and revenue trade-offs

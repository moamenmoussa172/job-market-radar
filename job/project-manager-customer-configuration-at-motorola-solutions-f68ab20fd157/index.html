Department Overview

Motorola Solutions is a global leader in mission-critical communications and public safety technologies. Within our Defense & Tactical Systems division, we equip armed forces with resilient command, control, and tactical communications systems (TETRA, mission-critical broadband, C4ISR). To ensure long-term operational readiness, material maintenance, and comprehensive through-life support for our communication systems aboard German Navy platforms (frigates, corvettes, submarines, and naval shore stations), we are seeking a qualified Project Manager (Customer/Configuration) (m/f/d).


Job Description

In this critical role, you will take ownership of the end-to-end configuration management lifecycle for our maritime communication systems—from initial system architecture and integration through operational deployment and in-service support:

  • Planning & Governance: Develop, maintain, and execute the Configuration Management Plan (CMP) in accordance with industry standards such as V-Modell XT Bund, ISO 10007.
  • Baseline Management: Establish, control, and audit system baselines (Functional, Allocated, and Product Baselines) to ensure complete alignment across As-Designed, As-Built, and As-Maintained configurations.
  • Change Control & Traceability: Lead and coordinate the Change Control Board (CCB), evaluate Engineering Change Proposals (ECPs) and deviation requests, and maintain rigorous end-to-end traceability across system technical documentation.
  • Audits & Verification: Prepare, conduct, and document Functional Configuration Audits (FCA) and Physical Configuration Audits (PCA) in close collaboration with the German Defence (Federal Office of Bundeswehr Equipment, Information Technology and In-Service Support), naval arsenals, and the military quality assurance service.
  • Tooling & Data Administration: Oversee and enhance configuration data structures within standard PLM, ALM, and requirements management platforms (e.g., Windchill, Polarion, Jira, IBM DOORS).
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as the primary liaison between systems engineering, software development teams, subcontractors, shipyard partners, and project management offices.


Basic Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or an equivalent technical qualification.
  • Professional Experience: Proven track record in technical configuration management or change management within complex systems environments—ideally in the defense, aerospace, or maritime sector.
  • Standards & Methodologies: In-depth working knowledge of configuration management standards such as ISO 10007, EIA-649 / MIL-STD-973, and V-Modell XT (Bund). Relevant industry certifications (e.g., CM2, ITIL, or V-Modell XT) are considered a strong plus.
  • Technical Acumen: Working familiarity with naval platform integration, tactical radio communications (HF/VHF/UHF, TETRA), mission IP networking, or naval cybersecurity standards.
  • Languages: Professional business proficiency in German (for interactions with German authorities and local naval documentation) and fluent English (written and spoken).
  • Due to the specific requirements of the project, candidates interested in this position will be required to undergo a background check in accordance with applicable legal requirements. German or EU citizenship may be required, depending on the applicable requirements.

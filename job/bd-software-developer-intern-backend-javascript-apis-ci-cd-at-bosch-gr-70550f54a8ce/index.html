About the job

Are you looking to kickstart your career in software development with real products and global impact?

At Bosch, you will join our Global Product & Service team to build enterprise applications used by hundreds of thousands of users worldwide. You will work with modern technologies including APIs, JavaScript, CI/CD workflows, and our Low-code platforms (OutSystems) - with full training provided.

Key responsibilities:

  • Learn and develop applications using modern platforms (training provided)
  • Work with APIs, JSON, and system integration
  • Support building features for internal/global products
  • Collaborate with developers, product owners, and global teams
  • Get exposure to Agile/Scrum and CI/CD workflows
  • Support troubleshooting and improving existing systems
  • Senior or final year student pursuing a Bachelor's or Master degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related subject.
  • Can provide the recommendation/introduction letter from the universities or professors to apply internship.
  • Able to join full-time internship for 6 months (Monday to Friday)
  • Experienced in JavaScript or equivalent coding languages development for custom logic implementation.
  • Familiar with REST APIs, JSON, and integration patterns.
  • Good in English communication (speaking, listening, reading, and writing).
  • Possess basic knowledge about requirement gathering/ management.
  • Knowledge of software development process, especially software requirement analysis.
  • Aptitude to learn and understand new and emerging technologies.
  • Pro-activeness (self-starter), good communication and fast learning.
  • Good for logical thinking and problem solving.
  • Monthly Internship Allowance, plus Meal and Parking support.
  • 1 paid leave day + 1 sick leave day for each internship month.
  • 1 birthday leave day if your birthday falls during the internship period.
  • Accident insurance provided throughout the internship.
  • Opportunity to observe and engage in international projects and innovation.
  • Access to our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professionalism.
  • Participation in various company activities such as football, yoga, badminton, and team building.

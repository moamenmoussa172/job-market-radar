About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

Company Overview

Innovative Solutions (IS) is a leading Cybersecurity company established in 2003, with its headquarters in Riyadh and additional offices in Al Khobar, Jeddah, Dubai, and Abu Dhabi. We specialize in delivering Comprehensive Cybersecurity Solutions and Services encompassing Advisory Services, Technical Assurance, Solution Deployment, Professional Services, and Managed Security Services.

Our mission is "Delivering secure and intelligent digital services that empower organizations"

Role Summary

As part of our growing Managed Services operations, we are looking for experienced Senior Network Engineers based in Egypt to provide offshore support for enterprise network environments.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and support enterprise network infrastructure and day-to-day network operations.
  • Operate and troubleshoot Cisco ACI environments.
  • Configure, monitor, and troubleshoot Cisco Firewalls.
  • Support Cisco Nexus Dashboard Insights (NDI).
  • Monitor network availability, performance, and overall health.
  • Troubleshoot network incidents and perform root cause analysis.
  • Handle technical incidents, service requests, and escalations according to agreed SLAs.
  • Perform network changes, configurations, upgrades, and maintenance activities.
  • Coordinate with security, cloud, infrastructure, and other technical teams when required.
  • Maintain network documentation, configurations, and operational records.
  • Participate in shift-based operations and provide support during critical incidents.

Technical Requirements

  • 5-7 years of relevant experience in Network Engineering or Network Operations.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Cisco ACI.
  • Hands-on experience with Cisco Firewalls.
  • Experience with Cisco Nexus Dashboard Insights (NDI).
  • Strong knowledge of routing, switching, TCP/IP, VLANs, BGP, OSPF, and network security.
  • Previous experience in Managed Services, NOC, or shift-based environments.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Experience with cloud infrastructure/networking is an advantage.
  • Cisco certifications such as CCNP Enterprise or CCNP Data Center are preferred.

Working Arrangement

  • Based in Egypt.
  • Remote / Offshore working model.
  • Must be comfortable working in a shift-based environment.
  • Must be willing and available to travel to Saudi Arabia when required.

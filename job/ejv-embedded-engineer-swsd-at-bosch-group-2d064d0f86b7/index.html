About the job

As a part Bosch group, our responsibility is to develop the Software for Engine Control Unit which is supplying for the world-wide OEM (Europe, Japan, Korea, ...). You will contribute to projects with latest Technologies as below:

  • New European vehicle emissions standards – EU7 (new across the world)
  • Fuel cell for Electric Vehicles (EV)
  • Hydrogen Internal Combustion Engine (H2 ICE)

We are looking for a skilled Embedded Software Specialist to design and implement new systems and features, as well as modify and maintain existing systems (Engine Management System Software) for adaptation to business and/or technology changes. The Senior Software Engineer engages directly with Customer, development teams, technical delivery teams and HW system teams to ensure the successful design, development, and delivery of technology-based solutions.

To be successful as a Embedded Software Specialist, you should be able to drive the software change and release management processes, assisting with the overall maturity of the software development lifecycle. Ultimately, a top-notch Senior Software Engineer will ensure that software deliverables comply with quality standards and are completed on time.

  • Design and implement software of embedded devices and systems from requirements to production and commercial deployment
  • Handle requirements engineering: registration, scheduling, ordering and tracking of requirements and their implementation
  • Review code, design and Test results
  • Follow the SW development process (V-Model, ASPICE, SDLC…)
  • Software architectural decisions for the specific package
  • Component design that is aligned to overall software architectural decisions
  • Problem Resolution Management (identification, recording, analysis, implementation, track to closure, monitoring)
  • Responsible for Change Request Management in the customer project
  • Responsible for System and SW requirements analysis, internal request of System and SW components, putting into operation and requirements-based test of new systems

Mandatory:

  • At least 5 Years experience in Embedded Software development
  • Strong in C/C++ language Programming
  • Experience in Automotive Industry with understanding of Network topology (CAN, LIN, FlexRay, Ethernet; or Diagnostic protocols like UDS, OBD)
  • Experience in AUTOSAR (Layer Architecture, Communication Stack)
  • Automotive product development process and quality experience (A-SPICE, CMMI, FMEA, ...)
  • Experience in Real Time Operating System (RTOS)
  • Excellent English language skills (Reading, Writing, Speaking and Listening)
  • Excellent debugging & Problem-solving skills

Nice to have:

  • Experience in debugging tools such as debuggers, oscilloscopes; CAN bus debugging tool like CANoe, CANalyer
  • Scripting languages (python, Perl, VB …)
  • Experience in multi core micro controllers and other hardware components
  • Experience in Emulators, Simulators, debugging and Test Equipment
  • Ability to work independently while leading multiple projects in a dynamic environment
  • Japanese proficiency is an advantage with N4, N3 level.

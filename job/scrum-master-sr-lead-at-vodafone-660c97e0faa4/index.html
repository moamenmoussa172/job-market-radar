About the job

Role Purpose:

The Senior Scrum Master Lead plays a pivotal role in accelerating the COPs Tribe transformation toward an AI-powered, digital-first customer care operating model.

The role acts as an Agile servant leader, delivery enabler, and transformation catalyst, driving high-performing cross-functional squads delivering Agentic AI, GenAI, intelligent automation, digital assistants, voicebots, and conversational AI solutions.

The Senior Scrum Master Lead ensures effective Agile execution, seamless cross-squad collaboration, continuous improvement, and predictable value delivery, while promoting innovation, technical excellence, and customer-centric outcomes.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Lead and coach Agile squads to achieve high performance, delivery excellence, accountability, and continuous improvement.
  • Drive effective adoption of modern Agile methodologies, including Scrum, Kanban, Scrumban, SAFe, Lean Agile, and hybrid delivery approaches based on team and product needs.
  • Facilitate Agile planning, refinement, reviews, retrospectives, and cross-squad synchronization, ensuring outcome-driven execution rather than ceremony-driven delivery.
  • Partner with Product Owners, Engineering Leads, AI teams, and business stakeholders to ensure backlog readiness, clear priorities, effective sprint planning, and alignment with strategic objectives.
  • Proactively identify, manage, and escalate delivery risks, technical dependencies, security requirements, and impediments across multiple squads and technology platforms.
  • Enable seamless coordination across AI development, backend/API integration, testing, cybersecurity, infrastructure, and business teams.
  • Support incremental delivery of AI-powered frontline capabilities through effective release planning, continuous testing, and DevSecOps collaboration.
  • Drive Agile maturity, team empowerment, transparent communication, and evidence-based decision-making.
  • Leverage Agile delivery metrics, flow analytics, and AI-powered productivity tools to improve delivery predictability, quality, efficiency, and time-to-value.
  • Mentor Scrum Masters and squad members, fostering a strong Agile community and a culture of ownership, experimentation, and learning.

Core Competencies, Knowledge, and Experience

  • 1. Advanced Agile Leadership & Modern Delivery Practices
  • Strong practical understanding of Scrum, Kanban, Scrumban, SAFe, Lean, and Agile at scale.
  • Ability to select and adapt Agile practices for complex, multi-squad, fast-paced digital delivery environments.
  • Strong capabilities in Agile coaching, servant leadership, conflict resolution, team facilitation, and stakeholder management.
  • Experience driving cross-functional collaboration, continuous improvement, and self-organizing teams.
  • 2. GenAI, Agentic AI & Digital Frontline Awareness
  • Good understanding of Generative AI, Agentic AI, AI assistants, conversational AI, chatbots, voicebots, and intelligent automation.
  • Familiarity with AI-enabled customer service journeys, digital self-service, and AI-assisted frontline operations.
  • Awareness of LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, orchestration, and human-in-the-loop workflows.
  • Ability to facilitate Agile delivery within AI product teams, balancing experimentation, integration, testing, and production readiness.
  • Awareness of responsible AI principles, AI quality evaluation, governance, and security requirements.
  • 3. Technical Delivery, DevSecOps & Engineering Collaboration
  • Sound understanding of software development lifecycles, API integrations, microservices, cloud-based platforms, and system dependencies.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD, DevSecOps, automated testing, quality assurance, and release management.
  • Understanding of Agile engineering practices such as XP, TDD, BDD, continuous integration, and continuous delivery.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with developers, solution architects, AI engineers, cybersecurity, and infrastructure teams without requiring hands-on development expertise.
  • 4. Agile Tools, AI Productivity & Data-Driven Delivery
  • Hands-on experience with Agile delivery and collaboration tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence, Miro, or equivalent platforms.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted productivity tools supporting backlog refinement, documentation, knowledge management, and team collaboration.
  • Ability to leverage Agile metrics including cycle time, lead time, throughput, work in progress, delivery predictability, and impediment trends.
  • Ability to translate delivery insights into actionable improvements and communicate progress clearly to senior stakeholders.
  • 5. Product Mindset, Customer Experience & Business Value
  • Strong understanding of product-oriented delivery, customer journeys, business capabilities, and value-driven prioritization.
  • Ability to connect Agile execution with measurable business outcomes such as customer experience, containment, first-contact resolution, automation adoption, and operational efficiency.
  • Experience facilitating alignment between business priorities, technical feasibility, customer needs, and delivery capacity.
  • Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and change leadership capabilities.

Professional Qualifications

  • Certified Scrum Master, holding a recognized certification such as Certified Scrum Master (CSM), Professional Scrum Master (PSM I or higher), SAFe Scrum Master (SSM), or equivalent.
  • 2–5 years of hands-on professional experience as a Scrum Master, preferably within Agile software development, digital transformation, or technology delivery environments.
  • Proven experience coaching cross-functional Agile teams and facilitating end-to-end product delivery.
  • Strong working knowledge of Scrum and Kanban, with demonstrated experience applying Agile practices in real delivery environments.
  • Practical experience using Agile lifecycle management tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, or equivalent.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business, or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant professional experience.
  • Experience in GenAI, Agentic AI, conversational AI, digital assistants, intelligent automation, or chatbot/voicebot delivery environments.
  • Experience coordinating multiple Agile squads and managing complex cross-team dependencies.
  • Exposure to customer care, telecommunications, digital channels, or enterprise technology transformations.
  • Additional Agile certifications such as SAFe, Kanban, Agile Coaching, or Agile Leadership.
  • Exposure to AI-assisted development, Agile analytics, automation, and DevSecOps environments

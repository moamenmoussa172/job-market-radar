About the job

Job Description:We are seeking an experienced ADAS Middleware Senior Developer to join ADAS middleware product development team.

This role requires technical leadership, strong debugging skills, and the ability to collaborate across global teams to deliver reliable, safe, and scalable middleware solutions.

Roles & Responsibilities:

Collaborate with the business teams to integrate sub-product Adapt the changes required for new sub-product integration Debug and analyze the issues related to sub-product in customer environment Communicate and work with the different international teams Self-motivated individual to drive the integration and debugging topic Design & Architecture of solutions Define system and software architecture for embedded platforms aligned with ASIL-B safety requirements.

Develop Software Safety Analysis (SSA) and ensure traceability to safety goals and technical safety requirements.

Lead High Level Design (HLD) and Detailed Design (DD) activities.

Perform architecture trade-off analysis for performance, safety, and scalability. Define software partitioning, module boundaries, interfaces, and safety mechanisms.

Mandatory Skills:

Hand’s-on experience in developing embedded SW with C++ & Python using CMake (or Bazel) in Linux & QNX environment C++ 14 & above - Coding, testing, and supporting reliable integration of sub-product with high-quality standards

Analyzing issues in the customer environment and streamline the problem Communicate the problem to sub-product team and provide technical details of issue Experience with Unit Test and Test-Driven Development

Ability to work in Agile teams in real spirit (culture & mindset) Safety-compliant software design across High-Level Design (HLD), Detailed Design (DD), and Software safety Analysis (SSA)

Nice-to-Have Skills:

MISRA-C++ compliance, UML modeling, Enterprise Architect, System and software architecture design Experience with safety-critical automotive software (ASIL-B or higher) Knowledge of Python, Conan, Bazel, Gtest, Robot tests, Pytest, Robot framework Knowledge in JIRA, GIT, Github actions, Docker

8 - 15 Years

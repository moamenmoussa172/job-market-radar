The Role

As AI agents redefine software development, Docker is at the center of that shift, providing the local and cloud sandboxed environments that make interactive and autonomous agentic workflows trustworthy by default. At the heart of our trust model is the fact that user credentials and other sensitive secrets are injected at the sandbox layer and never exposed to an agent. An agent can never leak secrets it never sees.

The Secrets team builds the software that makes that possible, from easy-to-use SDKs to secure cloud services to high-performance network proxies. We operate at the intersection of the developer experience, enterprise governance, and security – delivering core security features that make Docker sandboxes the best way to run agents and other workloads safely.

As a Staff Software Engineer on the Secrets team, you’ll design and build core security systems that power Docker’s agentic platform and AI governance products. Your work will focus on creating scalable, reliable, and secure software that enables developers to safely run agentic workloads while allowing administrators to confidently manage their organization’s security posture.

The successful candidate is a technical, product-minded engineer with deep experience building secure software and scale-out cloud services and a track record of shipping critical infrastructure. They think naturally about isolation and security boundaries, understand the tradeoffs involved in running untrusted or semi-trusted workloads safely, and can reason confidently about a system that spans multiple surface areas.

If you thrive on solving security and usability challenges, are enthusiastic about agentic development but conscious of the risks it entails, and want to help shape the future of Docker’s platform, we’d love to hear from you.

Please note: this role is currently prioritizing candidates currently located in the Western time zone (US or Canada).

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and lead improvements to the secrets management and credential injection systems.
  • Investigate and resolve security, correctness, performance, and stability issues across cloud, macOS, Windows, and Linux.
  • Collaborate with peers, Product, and adjacent teams to evolve the architecture as requirements grow.
  • Mentor engineers on the team and set the bar in design reviews and code reviews.
  • Engage with the open source and agentic development community around sandboxing, MCP, and related ecosystems.
  • Participate in on-call rotations and respond to critical stability issues affecting users.
  • Document your work clearly and share knowledge with the rest of the team.

Qualifications

Required

  • 8+ years of software engineering experience, with demonstrated technical leadership on production systems at scale.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
  • Experience building developer tools, desktop applications, or runtime infrastructure used directly by developers.
  • Familiarity with the agentic software development ecosystem: how coding agents work and how tools like Claude Code, Codex, or similar agents interact with their environment.
  • Ability to reason about isolation and security boundaries: what a sandbox should and shouldn't expose, and how to make safe defaults obvious to users.
  • Experience driving architecture and technical strategy across multiple engineers or teams.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Comfortable working with a high degree of autonomy in a remote-first environment.

Preferred

  • Strong proficiency in Go, Rust, and/or Java
  • Experience with cryptographic key management systems and techniques (key wrapping, HSMs, cloud KMSes, etc.)
  • Familiarity with best practices for protecting credentials and secret material at-rest and in transit
  • Understanding of networking, TLS, HTTP, and the like.
  • Understanding of one or more OS-specific credential management capabilities.

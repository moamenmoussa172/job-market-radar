About the job

Are you ready to lead a team that directly impacts the accuracy and reliability of one of the world's largest fulfillment networks? We are looking for a Manager II, Inventory Control and Quality Assurance (ICQA) to join our fulfillment center operations. In this role, you will oversee inventory control, quality assurance, and problem-solve operations while developing and mentoring a team of associates and team leads. You will use data to identify trends, drive process improvements, and ensure that every product reaches our customers accurately and on time. If you are a collaborative, detail-oriented leader who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is energized by solving complex operational challenges, this is your opportunity to make a meaningful impact at Amazon.
Key job responsibilities
- Lead and manage a team of hourly associates, including team leads and data analysts, responsible for inventory control, quality assurance, and problem-solve operations within the fulfillment center.
- Analyze key performance indicators on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis to identify trends, resolve inventory discrepancies, and implement process improvements that enhance accuracy and the customer experience.
- Champion safety programs and ensure compliance with all policies, procedures, and regulatory standards to maintain a safe and productive work environment for your team.
- Collaborate with operations, supply chain, loss prevention, and process engineering partners to optimize workflows, streamline processes, and drive operational excellence across the fulfillment center.
- Attract, develop, and retain talent by providing actionable coaching and feedback, facilitating career growth conversations, and establishing onboarding plans that accelerate new team members' readiness and skill development.
A day in the life
Your shift begins with a team huddle where you review the previous shift's performance, set priorities, and align your team on daily goals. From there, you move to the floor to observe processes, coach associates on accuracy improvement, and investigate inventory discrepancies using real-time data. You will partner with cross-functional teams to resolve receiving issues and test new ideas for quality improvement. Throughout the shift, you balance hands-on problem solving with strategic planning, and you close out by preparing a thorough handover so the next shift is set up for success.
About the team
Our ICQA team works at the core of Amazon's fulfillment operations, focused on preventing and removing defects so that every order reaches the customer accurately and efficiently. We implement quality standards across receiving, stowing, counting, picking, packing, and shipping processes. Our team values collaboration, continuous learning, and building an inclusive environment where every member feels empowered to contribute ideas and grow their career. We are committed to raising the bar on inventory accuracy and operational quality, and we are looking for leaders who share that commitment.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent
- Experience managing and leading employees
- Experience in English-language communication skills, both written and verbal
- Experience developing and managing a high-performing team
- Experience using the entire MS Office suite, especially Excel
- Work a flexible schedule/shift/work area, including weekends, nights, and/or holidays

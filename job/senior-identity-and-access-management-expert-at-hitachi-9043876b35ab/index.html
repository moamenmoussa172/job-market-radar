Job Description:

The opportunity

From the business unit level help product and application teams implement secure, efficient identity and access controls across the business. As an internal consultant, you will connect central identity and access management teams with product and application teams, turning policies, standards, and proven practices into workable solutions. You will review architectures and implementations, guide teams through complex cross-organizational dependencies, and identify risks and gaps that need resolution. This role offers broad influence across Grid Integration and the opportunity to improve how identities, authentication, authorization, and access lifecycles are managed across a diverse application portfolio.

How you’ll make an impact

  • Act as the primary identity and access management advisor for product and application teams across business units.
  • Guide teams in implementing identity lifecycle, authentication, and authorization controls so access is granted, reviewed, and removed appropriately.
  • Translate central policies, standards, and guidance into practical requirements, implementation instructions, and reference architectures.
  • Review identity and access management architectures, designs, and implementations; document recommendations and identify deviations for resolution.
  • Develop risk-based review criteria using access privileges, data sensitivity, external exposure, and integration complexity to determine assessment and escalation needs.
  • Identify gaps, risks, and improvement opportunities, then recommend updates to identity and access management guidance and practices

Your background

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related field.
  • Extensive experience with identity and access management across diverse product and application portfolios, including architecture and implementation reviews.
  • Strong knowledge of federation, authentication, authorization, and directory technologies, including SAML, OpenID Connect, OAuth, and LDAP.
  • Proven ability to translate central identity and access management policies into workable solutions and coordinate across organizational boundaries.
  • Experience with cloud-based identity and access management solutions and hybrid environments; knowledge of related compliance requirements is preferred.
  • Excellent communication and consulting skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders, build consensus, lead work, and mentor others.

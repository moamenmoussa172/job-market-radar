About the job

We are looking for a Machine Learning Research Intern to join the Doctolib’s Artificial Intelligence teams.

As a Machine Learning Research Intern, your mission will be to contribute to developing AI solutions that transform access to healthcare and improve the experience of healthcare professionals and patients. You will work within a research team developing ethical AI technologies to optimize care pathways for millions of patients across Europe.

Working in the tech team at Doctolib involves building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. We work in feature teams in an agile environment, while collaborating with product, design, and business teams.

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Develop and experiment with machine learning models to solve real-world healthcare challenges
  • Collaborate with ML engineers and researchers on applied research projects
  • Participate in improving medical practice efficiency through AI
  • Explore new model architectures and data processing techniques

We leverage AI ethically across our products to empower patients and health professionals. Discover our AI vision here and learn about our first AI hackathon here!

You can also discover the topic you might work on during your internship here.

Who you are

Before you read on, if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

  • You are a Master's student with practical experience working on ML problems
  • You are a strong programmer (we use Python)
  • You are enthusiastic about collaborative work
  • You are eager to ask questions, admit mistakes, and learn new things
  • You are interested in improving healthcare through technology
  • You are fluent in English and French

What we offer

  • A flexible workplace policy offering both hybrid and office-based mode
  • Lunch voucher with Swile card
  • Work Council subsidy to refund part of sport club membership or creative class
  • Reimbursement of public transportation

The interview process

  • A coding game online test
  • An interview with our Talent Partner (30 minutes)
  • A technical interview (1 hour)
  • An interview with the hiring manager (45 min)

Job details

  • Internship
  • Duration: 6 months
  • Start date: April 2027
  • Location: Levallois-Perret
  • Working mode: Hybrid - 3 days a week at the office

About the job

Description

We are seeking a skilled and experienced Senior Product Manager to join our team at Nawy Real Estate. As a Senior Product Manager, you will be responsible for driving the success of our real estate platform by defining and executing the product strategy. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, stakeholders, and customers to identify product enhancements, prioritize features, and ensure a seamless user experience throughout the platform. If you have a passion for real estate and a proven track record in product management, we would love to hear from you!

﻿

  • Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap that aligns with the company’s business goals.
  • Conduct market research to understand industry trends, competitive landscape, and customer needs.
  • Set product goals and key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure success.
  • Make data-driven decisions to balance short-term goals with long-term product vision.
  • Write clear, detailed product requirements and user stories.
  • Oversee the product from ideation through development, launch, and growth stages.
  • Track product metrics post-launch, identify areas for improvement, and implement necessary changes.
  • Gather feedback through customer interviews, surveys, and usability studies.
  • Create and prioritize product features based on user needs and feedback.
  • Collaborate with UX/UI designers to ensure the product is user-friendly and intuitive.

Requirements

Requirements

  • 5-7 years of experience as a Product Owner/Product Manager with software for mission-critical products, working with Agile methodology.
  • Background in engineering, technology, or UX preferred
  • Proven experience in customer development, product definition, feature scoping, and balancing tradeoffs for industry
  • Leading business software products.
  • Strong organizational skills: ability to track and prioritize multiple issues.
  • Must be a strong collaborator, team player, and ability to manage a team
  • Experience working with agile teams.

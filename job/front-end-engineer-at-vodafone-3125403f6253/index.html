About the job

Join us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers — we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world.

If you want to grow your career whilst finding the right balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunity to help you belong and make a real impact.

Role Purpose:

We are seeking a motivated Front End Engineer to join our team in Dublin, building and maintaining our customer facing web applications, used by millions of customers across Ireland.

The ideal candidate will have around two years of commercial experience, a strong foundation in modern front end development, a keen eye for detail, and the ability to work effectively with cross functional teams. You'll gain exposure to microsites, microservice integration and cloud based architectures as you grow.

You will work as part of a wider Product Team to ensure that our solutions meet the needs and expectations of our customers and internal stakeholders.

What you’ll do

Essence of Role – Key Accountabilities:

  • Develop and maintain responsive, accessible, and performant web interfaces using React and modern JavaScript.
  • Work with design, product and backend teams to turn requirements into working features, flagging technical constraints and feasibility concerns early
  • Build reusable components and contribute to shared libraries used across the wider engineering organisation
  • Participate in code reviews, both giving and receiving feedback, and contribute to our testing and quality practices
  • Help identify and address performance and accessibility issues in the applications you work on
  • Grow your technical depth with support from senior engineers, taking on increasing ownership as you do

Who you are

Required Qualifications:

  • Experience: 2 years of commercial experience in front end development
  • Education: A degree in Computer Science or a related field is welcome but not required.

Technical Skills:

  • HTML/CSS: Solid grasp of semantic, accessible HTML and modern CSS
  • JavaScript/TypeScript: Good working knowledge of modern JavaScript (ES6+) and experience with TypeScript
  • Frameworks/Libraries: Experience with React.js and exposure to Next.js
  • State Management: Familiarity with Redux, Context API or similar
  • Version Control: Comfortable working with Git in a team
  • Responsive Design: Experience building interfaces that work across screen sizes

Preferred Skills:

You are not expected to have all of these. If you have a few and you're interested in the rest, we'd like to hear from you.

  • Testing: Experience with front end testing frameworks and tools
  • Build tooling: Familiarity with modern build pipelines (Webpack, Vite, npm/yarn)
  • Backend integration: Experience consuming RESTful APIs
  • Cloud services: Basic exposure to AWS, Azure or Google Cloud
  • Microservices: Exposure to microservice-based architectures
  • Accessibility: Understanding of web accessibility standards and best practices (WCAG)
  • Performance: Awareness of techniques for optimising web performance
  • UI/UX: Basic understanding of design principles and tools such as Figma
  • Agile: Experience working in Agile or Scrum environments
  • DevOps: Familiarity with CI/CD and DevOps practices
  • AI-assisted development: Comfortable using AI coding tools (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code or similar) as part of your workflow, with good judgement about when to rely on them and when not to

Soft Skills:

  • Excellent problem solving and analytical skills
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Comfortable working with colleagues across different locations and time zones
  • Detail-oriented with a commitment to quality
  • Curious, and willing to ask questions and learn from feedback

Not a perfect fit?

Worried that you don’t meet all the desired criteria exactly? At Vodafone we are passionate about empowering people and creating a workplace where everyone can thrive, whatever their personal or professional background.

If you’re excited about this role but your experience doesn’t align exactly with every part of the job description, we encourage you to still apply — you may be the right candidate for this role or another opportunity.

What’s in it for you?

The flexibility to work how you perform best, with hybrid ways of working and real focus on work-life balance

A competitive package including salary, bonus and a strong pension to support you now and long-term

Generous flexible benefits allowance, plus access to comprehensive health and wellbeing support

Access to our on-site wellness centre and gym, alongside a wider wellbeing programme

Free mobile plan, device offers and exclusive discounts for you, your family and friends

Market-leading family policies and flexibility to support you at every stage of life

Real opportunities for career growth through learning, development and internal progression

Who we are

We are a leading international Telco, serving millions of customers. At Vodafone, we believe that connectivity is a force for good. If we use it for the things that really matter, it can improve people’s lives and the world around us.

Through our technology, we empower people — connecting everyone regardless of who they are or where they live — and we protect the planet, whilst helping our customers do the same.

Belonging at Vodafone isn’t a concept; it’s lived, breathed, and cultivated through everything we do. You’ll be part of a global and diverse community with many different minds, abilities, backgrounds and cultures.

We’re committed to increasing diversity, ensuring equal representation, and making Vodafone a place where everyone feels safe, valued and included.

Vodafone is committed to supporting candidates with disabilities during the recruitment process. If you require any adjustments, please let us know.

Together we can.

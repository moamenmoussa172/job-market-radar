About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Brazil.

Our Client is one of the United States’ largest insurers, providing a wide range of insurance and financial services products with gross written premiums well over US$25 Billion (P&C). They proudly serve more than 10 million U.S. households with more than 19 million individual policies across all 50 states through the efforts of over 48,000 exclusive and independent agents and nearly 18,500 employees. Finally, our Client is part of one the largest Insurance Groups in the world.

About the Role

Join our Distribution Analytics team and help drive business growth through data-driven insights. In this contractor role, you will support initiatives focused on agent acquisition, retention, cross-selling, profitability, and agency development. Working closely with business leaders, you will transform complex data into actionable recommendations, create impactful dashboards and presentations, and contribute to strategic decision-making across the organization.

What You'll Do

  • Support advanced analytics projects focused on Distribution performance and growth.
  • Analyze agent, district, and market data to identify trends, opportunities, and business recommendations.
  • Develop and maintain reports, dashboards, scorecards, and visualizations using Power BI and Excel.
  • Create presentations and insights for senior leadership to support strategic planning and decision-making.
  • Assist with agency segmentation, local market planning, and campaign performance analysis.
  • Monitor key business metrics and identify adverse trends or opportunities for improvement.
  • Partner with cross-functional teams on planning, reporting, and distribution-related projects.
  • Translate complex data findings into clear and compelling business stories for stakeholders.
  • Support the development of trigger-based marketing campaigns through data analysis and actionable recommendations.

Requirements

  • 2+ years of experience in business planning, analytics, marketing, consulting, or a related field.
  • Experience working with large datasets, reporting, and business performance analysis.
  • Advanced Excel skills, including data analysis, reporting, and dashboard creation.
  • Intermediate Power BI skills with experience building visualizations and business reports.
  • Strong storytelling and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate insights effectively.
  • Experience identifying trends, opportunities, and business recommendations through data analysis.
  • Fluent English communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Statistics, Economics, Marketing, Data Analytics, or a related field preferred.
  • Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and work independently in a fast-paced environment.

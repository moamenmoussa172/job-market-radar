Senior Corporate Units Reporting Analyst

stc · SA, Saudi Arabia

Onsite · Senior · BI Analyst / Developer · Posted 23 November 2026

Apply on stc's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Job Purpose

The role holder is responsible for supporting profitability analysis and improvement projects across corporate units (CPM, RA, HR, Tech & Ops, Finance) by conducting exploratory and predictive analyses, developing decision models, and assisting in the creation of action plans and reports. This includes engaging with the units to communicate insights, identify opportunities for profitability improvement, and ensure accurate and timely report maintenance. The role holder shall carry out his duties in accordance with the stipulated business policies and procedure.

Job Responsibility

· Supports exploratory analysis projects for the corporate units (CPM, RA, HR, Tech & Ops, and Finance) to explain and provide insights into the profitability of products, services, segments, units, technologies, etc.

· Assists in predictive analysis projects for corporate units (CPM, RA, HR, Tech & Ops, Finance) by supporting the development of decision models to assist in management decisions (investment, operational, and commercial).

· Engages with the corporate units (CPM, RA, HR, Tech & Ops, Finance) to communicate and explain the findings and recommendations to help steer profitability improvement actions

· Assist in conducting proactive identification of profitability improvement opportunities through regular profitability reviews and benchmarks

Job Responsibility (cont.)

· Supports defining and structuring profitability-improvement action plans with the corporate units (CPM, RA, HR, Tech & Ops, Finance), including agreement on objectives, scope of work, definition of objectives, KPIs, timelines, etc.

· Supports maintaining a calendar of required report publications at monthly and quarterly frequency

· Supports defining, designing, and developing profitability reports as per the requirements of the organization based on scope, format , delivery channel, etc.

· Assists in ensureing the accuracy, regular maintenance, updates, and enhancement of existing profitability reports.

Years of Experience

2 - 4 Years

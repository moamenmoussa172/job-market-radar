Sterrennacht Marathi - Audio Transcription QA

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in India.

Role summary

Review and resolve differences between the two blind pass transcriptions where adjudication is needed, and uphold quality standards for the locale. This role calls for strong judgment and deep familiarity with the conventions.

Project: Sterrennacht

Location: India (Remote)

Engagement Type: Freelance

Rate: USD 6.30/hour

Estimated Hours: 20-25 hours/week

Key responsibilities

  • Compare the two independent transcriptions against the audio and decide the correct final output
  • Judge which differences are real disagreements, such as wording, tagging, speaker attributes, or convention application
  • Check that the final transcript follows the client's conventions end to end
  • Provide clear, constructive feedback to Generalists, and track recurring error patterns
  • Document guideline gaps and ambiguities, especially around disagreement thresholds, and escalate them to the program team
  • Support calibration sessions and help keep decisions consistent across the locale
  • Requirements
  • Native-level fluency and strong written accuracy in the Marathi language
  • Proven experience in transcription QA, linguistic review, or adjudication
  • Thorough knowledge of transcription conventions and the ability to apply them consistently
  • Analytical judgment, objectivity, and the ability to justify decisions
  • Clear written communication for feedback and escalations
  • Previous experience as a lead, reviewer, or senior linguist preferred

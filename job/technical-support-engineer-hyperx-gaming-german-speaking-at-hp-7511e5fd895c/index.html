Technical Support Engineer – HyperX Gaming - German Speaking

Job Summary
Are you fluent in German and English, passionate about technology, and interested in gaming peripherals? Join the HyperX Technical Support team and help customers get the best experience from their headsets, keyboards, mice, microphones, and other gaming products. As a Technical Support Engineer, you will be on the front line of our EMEA customer support: resolving technical issues, answering enquiries and guiding customers on product use. Turkish speakers are strongly encouraged to apply.

Responsibilities

· Respond to technical issues and product questions in German and English (and Turkish where applicable) by phone, email, chat, social media and web request.

· Independently identify, troubleshoot, and resolve customer issues; escalate complex problems according to defined procedures.

· Document and categorise cases accurately in the CRM system, researching and gathering the information needed to resolve issues professionally, courteously and promptly.

· Regularly review and update regional email templates to ensure accurate correspondence with customers.

· Maintain up-to-date knowledge of all HyperX hardware and software products.

· Back up colleagues where necessary, especially for language cover.

· Contribute to team projects and continuous-improvement initiatives.

· Perform other duties as assigned by the Manager/Team Leader.

Education & Experience Recommended
Educated to A Level standard (or equivalent).

A degree in a relevant discipline will be a distinct advantage.
Knowledge & Skills

· Fluent German and English, spoken and written, to business level (essential). Turkish would be a distinct advantage.

· A keen interest in computer gaming and gaming peripherals (headsets, mice, keyboards, etc.).

· Knowledge of the main gaming consoles is an advantage.

· Knowledge of PC architecture.

· At least 1 year’s customer support experience is preferred.

· Excellent communication and organisational skills, with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.

· Excellent time management, with the ability to prioritise your own workload and multi-task.

· A great team player with a positive working attitude and the initiative to research answers to complex questions.

· A technophile who loves to be immersed in the gaming scene and enjoys social media discussions about products and technology.

Role

· Permanent hybrid role

· Office attendance 3 times a week – Reading (RG6 1PT)

· Working hours: 37.5 hours/week

What you can expect

· Structured onboarding and product training

· Exposure to a wide range of HyperX gaming products and technologies

· Opportunities to develop technical, customer-support, and project skills

· Collaboration with an international EMEA support team

· Access to HP employee benefits and development programmes, where applicable.

Your role at Hostinger

Join Hostinger's Internal Tools team and help us build and run the internal products our colleagues rely on every day. As a Software Engineer (Generalist, PHP and Node), you'll build and maintain features across our PHP and Node.js services, own changes from idea to production, and help make internal tools reliable, secure and easy to use. Your work directly speeds up how teams across Hostinger operate.

Curious to learn more? Connect with your team: Dominykas Murauskas, Engineering Manager

Your day-to-day

  • Build with agents, not just autocomplete - you orchestrate agents across specs, code, tests and analysis. You're the builder, and agents write a large share of the code. Reviewing, steering and knowing when to throw their output away is the real skill.
  • Operate with minimal guidance - you don't wait for perfect specs. You ask the right questions, make reasonable assumptions and ship.
  • Own problems, not tickets - take a stakeholder problem from idea to production. You define the solution, you ship it, and you measure whether it worked.
  • Move across the stack - frontend, backend, infra. Wherever the problem lives, that's where you go.
  • Collaborate directly - work with other stakeholders, managers and engineers in small, autonomous squads.
  • Improve the system - spot patterns that slow the team down, propose what to delegate to agents, and implement the changes.

What will help you succeed / Your expertise and experience

  • 2+ years with PHP (Laravel), Node and TypeScript, shipping real products
  • Production experience with MySQL or PostgreSQL databases and ORMs, such as Eloquent and TypeORM
  • Demonstrated experience using AI tools in your daily workflow, such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex. The tooling is provided; you bring the power-user knowledge.
  • Self-starter mentality: you take ownership, ask good questions and aren't afraid to challenge approaches when something feels off
  • Strong communicator: you document your work, push back on a bad idea, and explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders
  • Comfortable with ambiguity: you've worked where the problem was clearer than the solution, and you figured it out
  • Comfortable working in a Google Cloud environment with Kubernetes, Docker and containerized services.
  • Familiarity with observability tooling such as Grafana and Prometheus

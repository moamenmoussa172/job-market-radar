About the job

Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

As a DevOps Engineer, you will specialize in managing, automating, and optimizing our hybrid cloud infrastructure—spanning VMware, OpenStack, multi-cloud platforms, and enterprise Kubernetes. In this role, you will be instrumental in ensuring the high availability, scalability, and performance of critical services. Partnering closely with cross-functional teams, you will architect infrastructure automation solutions that streamline engineering operations, empower developers through self-service lifecycle management, and support business growth.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Architect & Scale Infrastructure: Design, deploy, and maintain scalable multi-cloud and on-premises environments across VMware, OpenStack, and bare-metal Kubernetes clusters to meet organization-wide standards.
  • Automate CI/CD & Workload Lifecycles: Develop infrastructure-as-code and automated CI/CD pipelines to streamline day-to-day operations and enable self-service VM and container lifecycle management for engineering teams.
  • Drive Observability & System Reliability: Establish enterprise observability standards—leveraging monitoring, logging, and metrics frameworks—to maintain high uptime and rapidly diagnose complex infrastructure issues.
  • Collaborate & Modernize Operations: Partner with internal business units to translate technical requirements into robust architectures, share DevOps best practices, and continuously optimize platform efficiency.
  • Ensure Operational Excellence: Provide ongoing infrastructure support across public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP), contribute to technical documentation, and participate in a "follow-the-sun" on-call rotation.

WHAT YOU BRING


Core Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Systems & DevOps Expertise: 5+ years of hands-on experience managing and automating large-scale VMware, OpenStack (Yoga or newer preferred), and enterprise Kubernetes environments on bare metal.
  • Infrastructure Automation & Code: Proficiency in programming/scripting languages (Python, Go, or Java) alongside infrastructure automation tools such as Ansible, Terraform, or Puppet.
  • Observability & Networking: Solid foundation in core networking (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, Open vSwitch) and experience implementing observability stacks using tools like Prometheus, Grafana, Elastic, Logstash, or Fluent-bit/Fluentd.
  • Cloud & Virtualization: Practical experience managing virtualization technologies (KVM, VMware, KubeVirt) and providing automated infrastructure support across major public clouds (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
  • Soft Skills: Exceptional problem-solving abilities, strong attention to detail, and a collaborative mindset suited for cross-functional teamwork.


Preferred Qualifications

  • Hands-on experience running virtualised or containerised infrastructure on Pure Storage products (FlashArray, FlashBlade, Portworx).
  • Professional certifications such as CKA, CKAD, VCP, or COA.
  • Direct experience running production workloads with KubeVirt.
  • Familiarity working in Agile environments and using modern work-tracking tools.

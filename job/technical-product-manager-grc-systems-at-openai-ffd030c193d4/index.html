About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the Team

OpenAI's Governance, Risk, and Compliance team works within Security to connect obligations and customer commitments to effective controls and reliable evidence. We partner across the organization to make security easier to put into practice and easier to understand.

About the Role

We are hiring a Technical Product Manager to own the vision and build the systems behind GRC and Security. You will discover what users need, decide what to build and sequence first, and personally implement data models, integrations, and working workflows. You will carry those products through testing, adoption, and operation.

Much of this work is a data problem: understanding what exists, how it changes, which information can be trusted, and what should happen next. You will apply product design and solution architecture to make security controls part of everyday work and produce reliable evidence through their operation. That evidence should support organizational excellence, audits, customers, and regulatory needs and help our audience understand and use the security information relevant to them.

You should be comfortable moving between implementation detail and consequential decisions. You may spend one part of the day debugging a data flow and another explaining the architecture, evidence, and tradeoffs to leadership or an external audience. You will begin as a hands-on individual contributor, setting direction and helping colleagues build effectively.

In this role, you will:

  • Own the product vision and technical direction for an agreed portfolio of GRC systems, discovering user needs and deciding what to build and sequence first.
  • Design and build scalable data models, integrations, and workflows that connect requirements, systems, people, controls, actions, and evidence.
  • Establish data quality, ownership, history, and freshness practices so changes and missing information produce accurate, useful behavior.
  • Build approved security requirements into usable workflows, automate appropriate checks and actions, and verify that controls operate as intended with the teams that own them.
  • Create clear employee experiences that reduce duplicate requests and make the secure path easy to complete.
  • Develop evidence capabilities for auditors, regulators, and customers, including understandable views and structured outputs with appropriate access, scope, and disclosure boundaries.
  • Carry work through testing, release, adoption, monitoring, and maintenance; improve the system when scale or new requirements expose weaknesses.
  • Explain architecture, security outcomes, evidence, and tradeoffs to technical teams and senior or external audiences; create reusable patterns that help others deliver independently.

You might thrive in this role if you:

  • Have personally designed, built, and scaled products or operational systems and can explain the decisions, failures, and outcomes you owned.
  • Can still build and troubleshoot data models, queries, integrations, or workflows, even if your current role is not software engineering.
  • Understand how data identity, relationships, quality, history, and access affect the correctness of a system and its outputs.
  • Can turn an ambiguous need into a product direction, buildable requirements, a practical first release, and evidence that people benefit from it.
  • Understand security controls and assurance well enough to connect a requirement to implementation, operating evidence, and an appropriately scoped claim; deepen domain knowledge with experts where needed.
  • Communicate complex systems and risk clearly to engineers, executives, and external audiences, preserving the facts and limitations.
  • Take responsibility through operation, learn from feedback, and help others own and extend your work.

Relevant backgrounds include engineering, data architecture, technical product ownership, internal systems, and security or compliance engineering. Prior engineering experience is valuable; a current engineering title, specific degree, certification, management history, or experience with our tools is not required.

About the job

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us!

We’re building the next chapter of AI-native craft and creativity - where craft and technology meet. Our unified platform bridges the gap between AI capabilities and human creativity, and lets professional creatives and designers harness the power of the world’s leading AI models without losing precision, expression, or soul. Together, we’re building tools that help people move faster without sacrificing quality, precision, or craft - a platform where AI supports creativity instead of flattening it.

We’re looking for a scrappy, hands-on AI Product Technical Program Manager to join Figma Weave as the team’s first TPM. You’ll partner with Engineering, Product, and Design to turn ambiguous customer needs and new AI capabilities into reliable creative workflows. You’ll lead programs across the AI model lifecycle, usage economics, and products like the Weave API. You’ll get close to the architecture, help teams make informed technical tradeoffs, and stay accountable through launch and adoption. You’ll also build AI workflows and automation yourself to help the team move faster.

This full-time role can be based in our New York hub or remotely on the East Coast of the United States. You’ll join a team spread across the United States, Europe, and Tel Aviv, with occasional travel to work alongside Figma Weave partners in Tel Aviv.

What you’ll do at Figma Weave:

  • Lead model lifecycle management across image, video, editing, enhancement, audio/lip-sync, 3D, and vector models, using the existing evaluation process to drive new model integration, launch, updates, and deprecation
  • Make tradeoffs around quality, reliability, latency, cost, and capacity clear to the decision owners
  • Track model limitations, integration issues, and provider dependencies through fixes, rollout, and follow-up
  • Work with Engineering, Partnerships, and Finance on provider agreements, quotas, rate limits, and capacity commitments so Weave can support product growth and launch demand. Bring in Product, Legal, and Security to resolve dependencies
  • Manage AI costs, capacity, and unit economics across 10+ providers and 50+ models. Forecast usage and spend, plan provider capacity for launches, and work with engineers on fallback options
  • Build things that make work less work. Improve process design and build AI workflows and automation that help the team move faster. Use APIs, scripts, or data queries to improve program visibility, streamline processes, and remove recurring bottlenecks
  • Lead horizontal product programs that span Weave teams, such as the Weave API. Understand how API contracts, authentication, permissions, asynchronous execution, and usage limits affect delivery. Coordinate dependencies across Weave and Figma, work through technical blockers, and follow the program through testing, release, and adoption
  • Lead cross-functional technical readiness for Config and other major launches. Work with engineering leads on end-to-end workflow testing, provider capacity, monitoring, and rollback plans. Bring together Support, Legal, Marketing, and other launch partners, make risks and go/no-go decisions clear, and follow through on customer issues and incident action items after release

We’d love to hear from you if you have:

  • Scrappiness and adaptability: You get into the details and take practical steps to move work forward. You can investigate a problem, partner with your engineering counterparts on a solution, or build a quick prototype without waiting for a perfect plan. You adjust when priorities change and follow through on what you start
  • Third-party AI model integration: You’ve led programs that bring third-party AI models into a product and keep them working. You can use evaluation findings and usage data to drive decisions across Engineering, Product, and Partnerships, including tradeoffs around quality, reliability, latency, cost, capacity, and unit economics
  • AI-enabled process design and automation: Hands-on experience building lightweight processes by leveraging AI workflows, automation, or internal tools
  • Technical judgment and hands-on problem solving: You can follow a workflow from the user interface through backend services and external model APIs. You reason about latency, throughput, rate limits, retries, and failure modes with engineers. You’ve managed AI cost structures, including usage and spend, pricing negotiations, and capacity planning
  • AI product program delivery and ownership: You’ve led customer-facing technical programs with cross-functional partners, from an ambiguous need through launch and adoption. You judge success by whether the product works for customers and delivers the intended result

While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have:

  • You’ve been an early TPM or worked at a startup, where you helped engineering teams plan and ship without adding more process than they needed
  • You’ve worked with a broad set of model providers, including early-access programs or model migrations
  • AI research or applied AI program experience is preferred. You’ve partnered with researchers and engineers and used their findings to bring new capabilities into a product. Experience with generative image or video products, and how creative users judge output quality, is especially useful

At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go. We believe in hiring smart, curious people who are excited to learn and develop their skills. If you’re excited about this role but your past experience doesn’t align perfectly with the points outlined in the job description, we encourage you to apply anyways. You may be just the right candidate for this or other roles.

About the job

Job Ref ID: JR0089102

Your mission:

As a Data Intern, you will play a pivotal role in empowering product and business teams to make data-driven decisions. Embedded directly within the Core Science team, you will dive into the full data lifecycle—from hands-on exploratory analysis to building machine learning models and shaping user experience on the Glovo app.

Here’s what you’ll do:

  • Data Exploration & Insights: Leverage SQL and Python to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and complex data aggregations that uncover actionable trends.
  • Product Optimization: Stay close to the Glovo product, identifying bottlenecks and optimization opportunities across the user funnel.
  • Experimentation & A/B Testing: Learn and utilize our experimentation framework—designing tests, running Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE) analyses, and evaluating product features.
  • Machine Learning in Practice: Build and onboard ML models from scratch with dedicated mentorship from senior data scientists.
  • Business Impact: Translate data into impactful narratives, communicating insights clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

What You Bring to the Table:

  • Strong SQL Proficiency: Confident writing clean, complex queries from scratch without AI or documentation assistance (expert in multi-table JOIN operations, advanced filtering with WHERE/HAVING, and database schema relationships).
  • Python for Data Science: Solid grasp of core programming, data manipulation, and basic Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) principles.
  • Statistical Foundations: Grounded understanding of core probability, statistics (e.g., percentiles, baseline forecasting), and analytical problem-solving.
  • Technical Agility: Hands-on comfort with command-line interfaces (CLI/terminal), environments like Google Colab, and the curiosity to independently adopt new tools (experience with BI tools like Looker is a plus).
  • STEM Background: Ongoing studies or a degree in a STEM field (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) or equivalent practical experience.
  • Ownership & "Solve-it-First" Mindset: Extreme proactivity and self-sufficiency—you actively look for solutions before escalating issues.
  • Cross-Functional Communication: Exceptional ability to translate technical concepts and data insights into clear business language for non-technical stakeholders.
  • Execution Under Pressure: Strong prioritization skills to handle high-tempo demands and changing priorities without losing focus.
  • Availability & Presence: Ability to commit full-time (40h/week) for at least 6 months, follow our hybrid office rhythm (on-site on Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday), and hold valid local work authorization without visa sponsorship.

About the job

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.
Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

As Senior Research Scientist in our Helsinki Automated Planning team, you will develop advanced algorithms for our radiotherapy treatment planning systems.

Working with Research Scientists and Software Developers, you will cover the full software lifecycle, turning clinical needs into innovative, production-ready software.

Our team works on development projects that make a difference on a global scale, tackling complex and rewarding challenges every day. Our shared purpose is to improve the lives of more than three million patients treated with our products worldwide, while enabling healthcare professionals to deliver care more efficiently and effectively.

We are looking for someone with a strong background in algorithm development and the ability to independently tackle complex technical challenges.

We value diverse experiences and perspectives, so if you are excited about the role, we encourage you to apply even if you do not meet every requirement.

This opportunity could be a great fit if you:

  • Have excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Take ownership and have a strong drive to deliver results.
  • Bring a curious, self-driven, and continuous-learning mindset.
  • Are a team player.

Your role:

  • Design, prototype, implement, validate, and maintain algorithms and software for radiotherapy treatment planning.
  • Take an active role in the full software development lifecycle, from concept and design to implementation, testing, and delivery.
  • Partner with product managers, clinical users, researchers, and other stakeholders to validate algorithm performance and turn their feedback into continuous improvement.
  • Contribute to successful product releases by collaborating across teams and supporting development activities where needed.
  • Drive technical discussions and innovation activities and continuously improve our treatment planning solutions.
  • Apply academic and scientific research to develop innovative algorithmic solutions, while staying current with relevant publications, standards, and industry developments.
  • Mentor colleagues, contribute to technical decision-making, and help shape the future direction of our solutions.


Experience and Qualifications:

  • M.Sc. or Ph.D. degree in computer science, mathematics, physics, engineering, or biomedical sciences, or equivalent experience.
  • Strong programming skills in compiled languages such as C#, C++, and in scripting languages such as Python.
  • Solid software development skills, including unit and integration testing and a disciplined approach to software quality.
  • Experience independently driving technical work from concept through implementation and delivery.
  • Experience with collaborative software development practices such as version control, peer reviews, and shared codebases.
  • Ability to write clear, concise, and accurate documentation for internal and external use.
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken.

Beneficial skills and expertise:

  • Knowledge of radiotherapy treatment planning algorithms and radiation physics.
  • Experience in medical device software development.
  • Experience in scientific research and publishing.

What we offer:

  • A supportive, high-performing team that values collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continuous learning.
  • Meaningful work within the medical domain, where your contributions directly impact the lives of cancer patients worldwide.
  • Opportunities for professional development and career growth.
  • A truly international and inclusive work environment.
  • A modern office in the heart of Helsinki.
  • Culture that promotes work-life balance.
  • A flexible hybrid working model.

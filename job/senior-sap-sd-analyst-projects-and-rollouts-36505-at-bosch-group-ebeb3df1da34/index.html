SENIOR SAP SD ANALYST (PROJECTS & ROLLOUTS) - 36505

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

Hybrid · Senior · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 9 November 2026

ABAPSAP S/4HANA

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

- Responsible for solution design, configuration, testing, and implementation activities, with support from EDL teams whenever required.

- Coordinate and monitor Level 3 (L3) support tickets, ensuring timely resolution of complex incidents and service requests.

- Ensure SAP SD processes remain compliant with legal requirements, regulatory changes, and system upgrade activities.

- Collaborate closely with business stakeholders to define and execute the application roadmap, identifying opportunities for process improvements and innovation.

- Coordinate and oversee new ABAP developments, including requirement gathering, functional specification creation, testing, and deployment.

- Lead and support SAP implementation projects, rollouts, template deployments, and process harmonization initiatives.

- Participate in SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA migration and transformation programs, including fit-gap analysis, process redesign, data migration validation, testing, and business adoption activities.

- Perform impact assessments for business requirements and technical changes.

- Work with cross-functional SAP teams to ensure end-to-end integration across business processes.

- Provide functional expertise and guidance to key users, business representatives, and support teams.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor Degree
  • Hands-on experience with SAP SD configuration, implementation, and support, including sales orders, pricing, shipping, delivery, and billing
  • Experience coordinating SAP Level 3 (L3) support and managing complex incidents
  • Experience with SAP implementation projects and SAP ECC/S/4HANA
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA migration and transformation projects, including fit-gap analysis
  • Experience preparing functional specifications for ABAP development and coordinating with technical teams
  • Knowledge of SAP integration with MM, FI, and CO modules and cross-module business processes
  • Experience with system testing, UAT, and defect management
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills
  • Advanced English, with the ability to communicate with global teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of ABAP development, user exits, BAdIs, and integration technologies, including APIs, IDocs, and BAPIs
  • Experience with large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformation programs and data migration
  • Previous experience with large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformation programs
  • Experience with data migration and validation
  • Knowledge of change management and business adoption activities
  • Experience working in global or cross-functional SAP environments

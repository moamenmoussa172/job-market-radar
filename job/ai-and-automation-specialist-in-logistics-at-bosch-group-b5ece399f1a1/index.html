About the job

The AI & Automation Specialist – Logistics drives the identification, development, implementation and scaling of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and automation solutions across Warehousing, Transportation and Foreign Trade.

1. AI & Automation Strategy and Use-Case Management

  • Identify and prioritize AI, GenAI, Machine Learning, analytics and automation opportunities across logistics.
  • Build and maintain an AI & Automation roadmap based on business value, feasibility, data availability and implementation effort.
  • Translate logistics pain points into clearly defined digital use cases and technical requirements.
  • Develop business cases covering productivity, cost, service, quality, compliance and working-capital impact.
  • Drive solutions from PoC/pilot stage toward stable, scalable production applications.
  • Establish KPIs to measure adoption and realized business benefits.

2. Warehousing

  • Identify and implement AI and automation opportunities within inbound, storage, material movement, picking, packing and outbound processes.
  • Develop solutions forworkload and capacity forecasting, resource planning, inventory optimization, warehouse productivity and exception handling.
  • Apply AI/computer vision/automation where appropriate for document processing, quality checks, material identification and transactional activities.
  • Support warehouse network and material-flow optimization using data analytics and simulation.
  • Enable intelligent decision support for warehouse supervisors and operational teams.

3. Transportation

  • Develop AI-supported solutions for transport planning, network design, routing, consolidation and carrier/LSP selection.
  • Apply predictive analytics for shipment volumes, transport capacity, lead times, freight cost and service performance.
  • Automate shipment tracking, exception management, reporting and freight-related administrative processes.
  • Combine shipment history, rate-card, TMS and operational data to support network and freight-cost optimization.
  • Develop advanced analytics and simulation solutions to identify savings and improve transport performance.

4. Foreign Trade & Customs

  • Identify AI and automation opportunities within import/export, customs clearance, trade agreements and foreign-trade compliance.
  • Automate document extraction, validation and reconciliation for customs and trade documentation.
  • Apply AI to support classification, plausibility checking, exception identification and compliance-related workflows while ensuring appropriate human validation.
  • Develop analytics for customs duties, Free Trade Agreement benefits, preferential treatment and process performance.
  • Improve data quality and interfaces between internal systems, service providers and customs-related processes.

5. Technology & Solution Development

  • Evaluate the most appropriate technology for each use case, including GenAI/LLMs, Machine Learning, predictive analytics, process automation/RPA, OCR/document intelligence, optimization algorithms and AI agents.
  • Work with platforms and systems such as SAP, TMS/WMS solutions, Microsoft Power Platform, Python/data platforms and enterprise AI solutions.
  • Define data requirements and collaborate with IT/data teams to establish reliable data pipelines and interfaces.
  • Ensure solutions comply with IT architecture, cybersecurity, data protection and AI governance requirements.
  • Evaluate make-versus-buy decisions and coordinate external technology partners where appropriate.

6. Implementation & Change Management

  • Lead cross-functional AI and automation projects with Operations, Process Owners, IT, Data/AI teams and external partners.
  • Develop prototypes rapidly, validate solutions with end users and convert successful prototypes into sustainable operational solutions.
  • Establish process ownership and support models after rollout.
  • Train logistics associates and process experts on AI-enabled tools.
  • Promote responsible AI adoption and build AI capabilities within the logistics organization.
  • Build a community of key users and identify opportunities for reuse and scaling across locations and processes.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or Data Science mandatory
  • 2–5 years of relevant professional experience with AI and automation projects in logistics and supply chain management.
  • Strong understanding of at least two of the following areas, with willingness to develop the third:
  • Warehousing / Intralogistics
  • Transportation / Freight Management
  • Foreign Trade / Customs
  • Practical industry experience delivering digitalization, analytics, automation or AI projects.
  • Ability to understand operational processes and translate business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Experience working with structured operational data and enterprise systems.
  • Practical experience in Python and/or SQL. AI / Machine Learning and predictive analytics.

About the job

Are you ready to launch your career at the forefront of software innovation?
At Bosch Vietnam, our Global Product & Service team builds best-in-class digital products using the latest technologies—from Low-Code/No-Code platforms to GenAI solutions. We are seeking passionate and high potential talents for our JavaScript Developer Internship Program, where you will gain real-world experience shaping software that serves haft million users across the globe.

As an intern, you will be immersed in a world-class product environment where you will:

  • Receive structured, in-depth training on complex enterprise systems and modern technology frameworks.
  • Collaborate with talented engineers and product experts in a global team, mastering professional software development practices, agile delivery, and CI/CD workflows.
  • Work on high-impact features that directly contribute to our global platforms.
  • Grow with us through continuous coaching and hands-on learning, with a clear opportunity to transition into a full-time role reserved for top-performing and high potential interns.

If you’re eager to challenge yourself, sharpen your skills every day, and be part of a team that driving innovation, excellence, and global collaboration—we believe you will thrive at Bosch Vietnam.

Key responsibilities:

  • Analyze, design, and develop business workflows application.
  • Implement custom actions and logic using JavaScript where advance feature / logic / validation required.
  • Collaborate with business analysts and stakeholders to ensure workflows meet business requirements.

General Qualification

  • Senior or final-year student pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Available for a 6-month full-time internship (Monday to Friday) or at least 4 working days per week.
  • Able to provide a university recommendation or introduction letter for the internship
  • Basic understanding of JavaScript and HTML.
  • Familiarity with debugging JavaScript applications.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Ability to read, understand, and work with existing codebases.
  • Eagerness to learn and adapt to different coding standards and development practices.
  • Previous experience in web development through coursework, personal projects, or prior internships is an advantage.

Culture-fit requirement

  • Take ownership and follow up.
  • Professional in communication and deliverables.
  • Result and success oriented.
  • High energy and passionate.
  • Teamwork spirit & high result oriented.
  • Ambitious and focus on action.
  • Be curious, wiling to learn and get new assignments or projects.

WHY BOSCH?

Because we do not just follow trends, we create them. Together we turn ideas into reality, working every day to make the world of tomorrow a better place.

Do you set high expectations for your learning journey? So do we. At Bosch, you’ll explore, grow, and challenge yourself in a dynamic and innovative environment.

Internship Benefits & Learning Opportunities at Bosch

  • Monthly Internship Allowance, plus Meal and Parking support.
  • 1 paid leave day + 1 sick leave day for each internship month.
  • 1 birthday leave day if your birthday falls during the internship period.
  • Accident insurance provided throughout the internship.
  • Opportunity to observe and engage in international projects and innovation.
  • Access to our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professionalism.
  • Participation in various company activities such as football, yoga, badminton, and team building.

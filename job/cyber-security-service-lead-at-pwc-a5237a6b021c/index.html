Cyber Security Service Lead

PwC · Sofia, Bulgaria

Onsite · Lead / Principal · Security Engineer · Posted 17 November 2026

About the job

Job Description & Summary

At PwC Bulgaria, we build trust in society and solve important problems. Our Cyber Security & Privacy practice is dedicated to helping organizations across Bulgaria and the broader CEE (Central and Eastern Europe) region defend their critical assets, comply with regulations, and turn risk into opportunity.

We are seeking a Cyber Security Service Lead to spearhead our practice in Bulgaria and lead go-to-market initiatives across CEE and Western Europe. This is a critical leadership role responsible for growing the team, delivering excellence in client service, and expanding our market presence. The ideal candidate combines deep technical cybersecurity expertise with proven leadership and business development success.
Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop the Bulgaria Cyber Security team, fostering a high-performance culture focused on innovation and quality.
  • Drive the go-to-market strategy end to end, from pipeline generation and pricing to delivery oversight.
  • Manage complex projects in cybersecurity governance, regulatory compliance (NIS2, DORA, ISO 27001, GDPR), Zero Trust implementation, Cyber Target Operating Models, M&A cyber due diligence, application security governance, and cyber risk management.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with C-level stakeholders across diverse sectors, both locally and internationally.
  • Mentor and coach team members to develop their capabilities and career paths, empowering them to become top-tier cybersecurity professionals.
  • Work with regional and global teams to harmonize practices and drive innovation.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Minimum 10 years in cybersecurity, preferably within consulting or professional services environment.
  • Proven success in scaling cybersecurity practices (e.g., growing team size and revenue portfolios).
  • Strong experience in pitching, proposal development, and client relationship management.
  • Deep understanding of the cybersecurity landscape and regulatory frameworks (NIS2, DORA, ISO 27001) across Bulgaria, CEE, and Western Europe.
  • Proficiency in risk management, security architecture, compliance solutions, and cyber due diligence.
  • Professional proficiency in written and spoken Bulgarian and English; knowledge of additional CEE or Western European languages is an advantage.
  • Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills; comfortable working at senior executive levels.
  • Relevant degree in ICT, information systems, computer science, cybersecurity, business computing or a related discipline is desirable.
  • Professional certifications such as CISM, CISSP, CISA, CRISC or ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor are an advantage.

What we offer

  • Opportunities to work with leading local and international clients, industry experts, and the global PwC network.
  • Challenging and meaningful projects that help you grow professionally and make impact.
  • Working with leading technology, advanced tools and AI models.
  • A collaborative and inclusive culture.
  • Flexible ways of working, including a hybrid model and a modern office in central Sofia.
  • Competitive reward and benefits package, including 25 days of annual leave, additional health insurance, meal vouchers at the maximum amount, a Multisport card, an annual fringe benefit and wellbeing initiatives.
  • Clear career growth opportunities and support to build a long-term career at PwC.

Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

About this role

We are seeking a Junior Marketing Automation Specialist in Hungary to support the design, execution and optimisation of digital marketing campaigns using marketing automation platforms. You will work alongside experienced Marketing Automation Specialists, developing hands-on expertise in campaign execution, data segmentation, reporting and analysis while helping improve campaign effectiveness and contact-data quality.

What you’ll do

  • Work closely with experienced Marketing Automation Specialists and complete campaign-related activities according to agreed schedules and requirements.
  • Create and execute digital marketing campaigns using marketing automation tools, including landing pages, emails and forms, following established guidelines.
  • Create and run regular activity reports using predefined templates.
  • Where required, create data segmentation filters, groups and distribution lists for campaigns and nurturing programmes.
  • Support post-campaign reporting and analysis to identify opportunities for improvement.

Who you are

  • You have a BA degree in Marketing, Communications or a related field.
  • You have 1–2 years of relevant digital marketing experience; experience in a junior
  • Marketing Automation or Email Marketing role is an advantage.
  • You have basic knowledge or internship experience in delivering digital marketing campaigns using platforms such as Oracle Eloqua, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Pardot or Adobe Marketo, or relevant experience in digital or social media brand communication.
  • You have good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • You can communicate effectively in English and work collaboratively with colleagues.
  • You can organise priorities and manage deadlines effectively.
  • You have basic working knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

What's in it for you

  • Hands-on experience in creating and delivering digital marketing campaigns.
  • Practical exposure to established marketing automation platforms and processes.
  • The opportunity to work alongside experienced Marketing Automation Specialists and learn through structured guidance.
  • Experience with campaign reporting, email performance metrics, audience segmentation and nurturing programmes.
  • An opportunity to develop your marketing automation expertise within an international digital sales and marketing environment.

In return we provide you with

  • A dynamic environment where innovative ideas are always welcome
  • A collaborative community where your professional goals and work are supported by a diverse team
  • Access to internal and external trainings via other providers
  • A supportive internal coaching and mentoring culture
  • Corporate assets including a laptop and mobile phone with One Business Mobile Superior subscription (unlimited voice, text message, and data within the EU)
  • Cafeteria package
  • Private Medical Services
  • Employee Assistance Program to support your wellbeing and mental health
  • Life and accident insurance
  • You can work remotely overseas for 20 days a year
  • 16 weeks fully paid parental leave. This applies to all non-birthing partners who become parents due to birth or adoption
  • You and your family members can enjoy several discounts from our partners
  • You will be working in a hybrid model, which means 3 days working from home with 2 days office visit per week

What skills you will learn

  • End-to-end execution of digital marketing campaigns using marketing automation platforms.
  • Creation of landing pages, emails and forms.
  • Audience segmentation, distribution-list management and nurturing programme support.
  • Campaign reporting and interpretation of email and campaign KPIs.
  • Post-campaign analysis and identification of opportunities to improve campaign effectiveness.
  • Effective application of marketing automation processes and standard procedures.

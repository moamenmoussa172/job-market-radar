About the job

Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

We build and maintain high-quality voice and data communication systems for mission-critical operations and public safety. Working in small, autonomous teams and collaborating across our global offices, we focus on engineering dependable infrastructure where uptime and low latency are vital.


Job Description

The Role

Our users rely heavily on our tools daily, and their feedback loop is tight and demanding. If you have experience shipping production software and want to actively shape our development standards rather than just maintain legacy workflows, this role gives you the space to do it.

To support the ongoing growth of our platforms, we are seeking software engineers to build AI-driven solutions that seamlessly connect with our core communication services. You will contribute across the full lifecycle: designing, building, testing, deploying, and maintaining software. Success in this role requires strong technical craft paired with a genuine commitment to understanding customer workflows. Together, we engineer systems built for high performance, precision, and dependability.

Our Development Culture

  • Dedicated R&D Time: We reserve 10% of your working time for exploratory side projects, tooling improvements, and continuous learning.
  • Modern Developer Tooling: We dogfood our AI systems internally and equip the team with modern AI tooling to accelerate delivery while keeping code quality high.
  • High Autonomy, Small Teams: Engineers own their systems end-to-end and have a direct say in architecture, tech choices, and roadmap priorities.
  • Blameless & Collaborative: We treat mistakes as learning opportunities through blameless post-mortems and prioritize knowledge sharing over gatekeeping.
  • Empathetic Code Reviews: We believe high engineering standards and kind, constructive communication go hand in hand.

Tech Stack

Elixir, TypeScript, Angular, Kubernetes, Docker, Azure, Redis, NATS.io, Azure DevOps, GitHub, Helm.


Basic Requirements

What We’re Looking For

  • A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical production experience.
  • Experience with functional programming. Ideally in Elixir, Erlang, or similar, though we are happy to cross-train engineers with a strong interest in functional design.
  • Solid understanding of core software engineering practices: automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, and clean architecture.
  • Experience with (or strong technical interest in) distributed systems, microservices, and high-availability/low-latency services.
  • Clear, proactive communication in English.
  • A willingness to mentor peers, review code thoughtfully, and receive feedback with an open mind.

Nice to Have

  • Hands-on experience containerizing and orchestrating services using Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Familiarity with high-throughput message brokers (NATS.io, Kafka, RabbitMQ) and efficient serialization/RPC protocols (Protocol Buffers, gRPC).
  • Practical exposure to cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP).
  • Experience building or consuming modern AI/LLM APIs and toolchains in production.

Bring your passion, potential, and talents to Motorola Solutions, and help us usher in a new era in public safety and security.

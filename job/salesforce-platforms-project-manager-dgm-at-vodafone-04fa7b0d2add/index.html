About this Role

We are seeking a Salesforce Platform Project Manager to successfully manage key projects supporting the Ignite platform, Vodafone's global implementation of Salesforce.com. The platform supports approximately 6,000 users across multiple local markets and central Group functions for account and opportunity management.

You will be accountable for delivering projects to agreed time, cost and quality requirements while working across a broad range of senior stakeholders. The projects will vary in size and complexity and contribute to improving platform capability, user experience and operational effectiveness.

What you’ll do

  • Manage key projects impacting the Ignite platform within Sales Operations, including developing project plans and proactively managing risks and issues.
  • Drive programme progress, understand dependencies and communicate status clearly to management and stakeholders.
  • Define release-readiness requirements against expected business outcomes and validate readiness before releases move into production.
  • Act as Product Owner for the DocuSign managed package within the Ignite platform.
  • Support the timely delivery of projects while maintaining agreed cost and quality requirements.
  • Contribute to successful business-change adoption and provide clear, concise reporting to key stakeholders.
  • Collaborate with Salesforce platform sprint delivery teams through dotted-line working relationships.

Who you are

  • Knowledge of Salesforce Sales Cloud, with an understanding of Salesforce releases and its product roadmap.
  • Experience managing global telecommunications or technology projects across global, matrixed teams.
  • An understanding of sales and commercial processes.
  • Excellent planning, analytical and conceptual skills, combined with effective decision-making capabilities.
  • A graduate qualification or equivalent professional experience.
  • PMP or PRINCE2 qualification.

What's in it for you

This role provides the opportunity to work on a large-scale Salesforce platform supporting approximately 6,000 users across multiple local markets and central Group functions. You will gain exposure to global platform delivery, senior stakeholder management, Salesforce release management, sales operations and digital business-change initiatives.

You will work collaboratively with Salesforce platform sprint delivery teams and contribute to projects focused on delivering platform capability, user experience and effective operations.

What skills you will learn

  • Enterprise-scale Salesforce platform project delivery.Salesforce release-readiness and production-release management.
  • Global programme governance, dependency and risk management.
  • Product ownership within a Salesforce environment, particularly for DocuSign integration.
  • Senior stakeholder communication and concise project reporting.
  • Business-change adoption and outcome-focused delivery.
  • Collaboration across global matrixed and agile sprint delivery teams.

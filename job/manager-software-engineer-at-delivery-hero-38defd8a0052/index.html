About the job

The Budget Steering squad builds a holistic approach to budget allocation and optimization for performance marketing — from quarterly planning to monthly optimization down to daily decisions at campaign and ad-group level.

We operate in a highly complex and ambiguous environment where:

  • Ground truth is often unobservable (incrementality vs attribution)
  • Budget decisions must be made continuously, under uncertainty and with imperfect data
  • Every allocation change moves real spend across markets, channels and platforms

Our goal is simple to state and hard to solve: invest each euro where it generates the biggest leverage, across scenarios ranging from volume maximization to payback-based allocation.

We are looking for an Engineering Manager to lead a cross-functional squad of engineers and data scientists, own a large-scale production service the business depends on daily, and turn ambiguous commercial questions into reliable, model-driven decision products. This role requires a strong technical foundation — in backend engineering or in data science / ML — combined with the leadership to grow people and the pragmatism to partner closely with Product and business stakeholders.

In this role you will,

Lead and grow a cross-functional squad

  • Lead engineers and data scientists across the full product lifecycle — from problem framing to production and adoption
  • Grow individuals through coaching, feedback and clear development paths, and hire to close capability gaps
  • Set measurable goals for the team and create the conditions for the squad to hit them
  • Build a culture of ownership, proactiveness and high engineering standards

Own the budgeting lifecycle end to end

  • Take accountability for budget allocation across every dimension and timeline — from strategic quarterly allocation to daily bid and budget changes at campaign level
  • Drive the development of payback-based allocation models, including CLV estimation and constrained optimization
  • Consolidate a fragmented tool stack into a single budgeting platform with coherent interfaces, ownership and roadmap
  • Balance methodological rigor with business constraints — budget caps, pacing and channel dependencies

Guide technical strategy and execution

  • Shape the squad's technical strategy and stay close enough to the work to be a credible partner in most technical conversations at squad and domain level
  • Make architecture and prioritization trade-offs explicit — effort, value, feasibility and pace
  • Set the bar for reliability and data quality: uptime, correctness of allocation, and validated inputs and outputs across every source
  • Ensure solutions are built as decision products, not one-off analyses — tested, monitored, documented and reproducible

Partner with Product and business stakeholders

  • Work with Product Managers to define scope, trade-offs and timelines, and to set common, measurable goals across planning cycles
  • Act as an ally of Product: negotiate constructively, know when and how to say no, and compromise without losing the long-term technical direction
  • Reduce ambiguity by asking the right questions before committing the team to build
  • Navigate a complex stakeholder network spanning marketing, finance, analytics and central platform teams

Drive decision-making under uncertainty

  • Provide clear recommendations despite imperfect measurement, articulating trade-offs and confidence levels
  • Navigate conflicting signals (e.g., attribution vs incrementality vs MMM) and help the team decide what to trust
  • Translate complex modeling and system behaviour into clear narratives for non-technical audiences
  • Build trust in the squad's outputs over time, including when they contradict established assumptions
  • Technical Excellence: Depth in either of the following, with hands-on experience on cross-functional products:
  • Software Engineering: strong backend background, building scalable and reliable applications used in production every day
  • ML / MLOps or Data Science: deep expertise across the ML lifecycle and its tooling ecosystem

Willingness and ability to ramp up on the other side quickly: the squad is cross-functional by design, and you will be expected to engage credibly with both.

  • People Leadership: Proven track record leading and mentoring engineers and/or data scientists, growing individuals and taking accountability for a large-scale production service. Ability to mediate and resolve conflict without taking sides, acknowledging different perspectives and building shared understanding.
  • Building Systems Where Modelling Is the Core: Experience delivering scalable, reliable applications with data science modelling at their centre — where the model output is the product, not a supporting artefact.
  • Comfort with production-grade practices: modular design, testing, CI/CD, monitoring, version control and modern data/ML stacks.
  • Product Collaboration & Business Acumen: Pragmatic and realistic. Evaluates business value against technical feasibility, and turns complex business requirements into bite-sized, deliverable work for the team. Understands which metrics matter and what a marketplace business optimizes for — or can demonstrably follow the train of thought and reach sound conclusions in an unfamiliar domain.
  • Innovation & Ownership: Experience building new solutions from scratch and setting measurable goals for a team. Deep sense of ownership and proactiveness — comfortable operating without a clearly defined problem statement.

Nice to have:

  • MarTech Domain Expertise: Understanding of performance marketing: budget allocation and optimization, bidding, attribution, and the dynamics of incrementality, diminishing returns and cross-channel interactions.
  • Experience building bidders or budget optimization systems is a strong plus.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

At Quest Analytics®, we build technology that helps health plans navigate some of the most complex challenges in healthcare. We’re searching for a Platform Product Manager to own the shared capabilities our product teams build on.

You’ll own the “what and why” for those capabilities, then bring Engineering, Architecture, Data, Regulatory, Legal, Client Services, and Finance together to deliver measurable results. What you build carries into the next product and the next market we enter, allowing us to deliver more efficiently.

The person we’re searching for is curious and self-directed, and thinks in terms of the client journey. You’ll own a capability from the first problem through adoption, learning how health plans work so what we build fits into payer workflows and changes how clients use Quest Analytics®. Healthcare experience is preferred, and we welcome candidates from other domains who bring strong communication, sharp curiosity, and a record of picking up a new industry quickly. You’ll talk comfortably with engineers, lead with a recommendation, bring a bias for action, and own results and misses alike.

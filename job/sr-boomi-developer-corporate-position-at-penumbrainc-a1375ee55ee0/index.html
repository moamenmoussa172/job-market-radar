About the job

Penumbra is a global healthcare company focused on innovative therapies. Penumbra designs, develops, manufactures, and markets innovative products, offering a broad portfolio that addresses complex medical conditions. Penumbra supports healthcare professionals, hospitals, and clinics in more than 100 countries.

General Summary

The Sr Boomi Developer is responsible for defining, designing, implementing, and governing enterprise integration solutions that enable secure, scalable, and reliable data exchange across Penumbra's business applications.

Position Qualifications

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Information Systems or related field.

• 7+ years of progressive experience in enterprise application integration, middleware platforms, or enterprise architecture.

• Demonstrated experience designing, implementing, and supporting enterprise integration solutions within complex, global organizations.

Other Qualifications

• Boomi Professional Developer, Professional Architect, or equivalent Boomi certifications.

• Experience with SAP Integration Suite, Azure Integration Services, or other enterprise integration technologies.

• Experience with AI-assisted integration development, automation, or intelligent mapping capabilities.

• Experience in medical device, pharmaceutical, biotech, or other regulated manufacturing industries.

• Strong hands-on and architectural experience with Boomi AtomSphere and at least one other enterprise integration platform.

• Proven experience designing and implementing integrations between SAP S/4HANA and enterprise applications including Salesforce, UKG, ServiceNow, Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Active Directory, and other business applications.

• Deep understanding of enterprise integration architectures, including API-led connectivity, event-driven architecture, canonical data models, messaging, file-based integrations, and hybrid integration patterns.

• Experience designing scalable, resilient, and secure cloud, on-premises, and hybrid integration solutions.

• Advanced knowledge of Boomi AtomSphere, API Management, Boomi Flow, integration processes, deployment strategies, environment management, and reusable integration components.

• Strong understanding of Atom, Molecule, Atom Cloud, deployment topologies, licensing strategies, and platform administration.

• Experience implementing enterprise error handling, exception management, logging, monitoring, and operational support frameworks.

• Ability to translate business requirements into scalable technical integration architectures and solution designs.

• Strong understanding of API security principles including OAuth 2.0, JWT, API Keys, encryption, authentication, and authorization mechanisms.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to communicate complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

• Demonstrated ability to influence architectural decisions and collaborate effectively with business stakeholders, Enterprise Architecture, Infrastructure, Information Security, application teams, vendors, and project teams.

• Strong analytical, troubleshooting, organizational, and problem-solving skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Join our team and help manufacture medical devices that treat complex diseases in an environment that fosters innovation, teamwork, professional development, and competitive compensation.

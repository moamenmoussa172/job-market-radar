Sr. Vendor Manager Kindle (French speaker) - Fixed Term Contract, Kindle France

Amazon · Luxembourg

Onsite · Senior · Other tech role · Posted 20 November 2026

About the job

Amazon Kindle team is looking for is looking for an innovative, results-oriented, customer-centric team player to help us continue to revolutionize how customers buy and read books. If you are ready to change the world and engage in a rapidly changing industry, this position is right for you. We offer a creative, fast paced, entrepreneurial work environment where you’ll be contributing to Amazon's innovation.
The ideal candidate has a track record of strong negotiation, relationship building, and analytical skills, ability to recommend and execute a vision, and build programs and features from the ground up. You will be able to influence across multiple company organizations, be an exceptionally strong analytical thinker and have a history of delivering results. You will establish and drive new relationships and strengthen existing relationships with publishers. You will be customer focused, entrepreneurial, a proven self-starter, and have the demonstrated ability to invent.
Within this role, you will:
- Be an expert relationship manager and communicator, who works closely with publishers up to C-level to ensure Amazon customers can find any book in our store, download it quickly at a great price and have an excellent experience reading it.
- Be responsible for specific publisher level metrics around selection, subscription participation, and profitability
- Become an ebooks expert who understands industry trends, the competitive environment and readers’ needs - and innovates in response.
- Work closely with leaders across the Kindle organization – locally and globally – to ensure we continue to develop new services and products.
- Identify opportunities for innovation and automation
This position can be based at London.
Key job responsibilities
- Strategic management of key publishers
- Defining category strategy/vision and executing it with stakeholders
- Master's degree
- Minimum 5 years of professional experience in procurement, planning, e-commerce, strategy, consulting, or finance
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with top management (internal and external)
- Ability to influence and negotiate with various stakeholders (internal and external)
- Strong data analysis and P&L management capabilities
- Fluent in French and English
- Experience in digital, e-commerce, and/or logistics

Description

In this role, you will:

  • Review technical drawings and Quality Assurance (QA) documentation
  • Inspect incoming material and confirm conformity to purchase requirements and technical specifications
  • Identify and assess used parts, components, and assemblies to determine fitness for use or repair
  • Interface with internal and external stakeholders to investigate, track and resolve non-conformance issues
  • Update company database programs, recording inspection results and documenting non-conformance issues
  • Report on and effectively communicate material availability and conformance issues to stakeholders
  • Assist warehouse personnel in inventory management as required
  • Participate in other duties as required including the Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) and Emergency Response Team (ERT)

The ideal candidate brings:

  • 2-3 years of technical inspection experience.
  • A technical background in either the marine or aviation sectors.
  • The capacity to review / interpret national and international standards and apply these requirements during product assessments.
  • The ability to understand and interpret technical drawings.
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills.
  • Fully conversant skills in the use of Microsoft Office products, particularly Outlook, Excel, and Word.
  • Experience performing material QA inspections is desirable.
  • Knowledge of Quality Control or QA in manufacturing or in maintenance of mechanical, electronic, submarine, or industrial equipment.
  • An accredited technical college diploma as an asset.
  • Certification through the American Society of Quality as a Certified Quality Inspector/Technician, or similar.
  • Knowledge in material management for major equipment and systems in the Department of National Defence with DRMIS experience. This would be advantageous.
  • Knowledge of ISO 9001 and 18001 standards. This would be advantageous.
  • Available for on call shifts as required by the business.
  • You are eligible for a Canadian Secret Security and ITAR Clearance.

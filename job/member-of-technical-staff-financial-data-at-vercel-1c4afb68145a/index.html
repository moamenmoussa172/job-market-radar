About the job

About Vercel:

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience.

Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

About the role:

As a Member of Technical Staff, Finance Data, you’ll lead the design of the Financial Data Platform that Finance, Accounting, and Revenue Operations rely on to close the books, report revenue, and forecast. You’ll build auditable pipelines on Kafka, ClickHouse, Tinybird, and Snowflake that turn raw usage into revenue, and set the bar for data accuracy and governance where correctness isn’t optional. Your main focus is a multi-phase roadmap that takes the platform to maturity and audit readiness as the business grows. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in San Francisco or New York City.

What you'll do:

  • Design and build the data platform behind revenue recognition, consumption-based billing, and financial reporting, across batch and real-time workloads
  • Set standards for financial data ingestion, transformation, and storage, with auditability, lineage, and data integrity
  • Work with FP&A, Accounting, and RevOps to turn reporting requirements, close-cycle timelines, and forecasting needs into architecture
  • Partner with Security, Compliance, Legal, and Internal Audit to meet SOX and SOC 2 requirements, backed by monitoring, alerting, and incident response
  • Set the technical roadmap, make build-vs-buy calls on ERP, billing, and revenue tools, and mentor engineers as the team grows

What you need:

  • 8+ years of experience in data engineering, analytics engineering, or a closely related field
  • Owned production data pipelines end-to-end on AWS, GCP, or Azure
  • Production experience with Kafka, ClickHouse, Tinybird, and Snowflake
  • Built data pipelines that feed financial reporting, revenue recognition, billing, or consumption-based pricing
  • Supported audit, SOX, or SOC 2 requirements for data systems
  • Translated Finance or Accounting requirements into technical architecture

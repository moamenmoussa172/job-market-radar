Job Description:

Project Director - Infrastructure

Abu Dhabi, UAE

Parsons is looking for a talented Project Director to lead the successful delivery of large-scale infrastructure projects while managing multidisciplinary teams, clients, budgets, schedules, quality, safety, and stakeholder coordination.

What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Lead the planning and execution of large-scale government and commercial building projects.
  • Manage interdisciplinary teams of engineers, architects, consultants, contractors, and subcontractors.
  • Serve as the primary company representative to clients, government agencies, tenants, and stakeholders.
  • Prepare and deliver project reports, presentations, briefings, and correspondence.
  • Develop and manage project budgets, schedules, execution plans, and resource requirements.
  • Coordinate project activities across design, engineering, construction, facilities, and operations teams.
  • Manage projects within occupied facilities while minimizing disruption to building users.
  • Oversee project scope, quality, health and safety, risk, and compliance requirements.
  • Monitor project progress, milestones, costs, schedules, and deliverables.
  • Identify and resolve project risks, delays, cost overruns, and scope changes.
  • Negotiate changes to the scope of work with clients and subcontractors.
  • Maintain accurate and timely change orders and project documentation.
  • Lead regular project status meetings and track action items.
  • Ensure compliance with government procedures, industry standards, contractual requirements, and regulatory approvals.
  • Provide technical and strategic guidance to project teams and remain actively involved in project design and delivery.
  • Build and maintain strong client relationships while identifying opportunities for additional work.

What Required Skills You’ll Bring:

  • Bachelor’s degree in project management, Civil Engineering, or a related technical or business field.
  • Minimum of 18 years of relevant engineering or project management experience on large-scale programs.
  • Broad technical and business knowledge, with a strong understanding of project delivery and management principles.
  • Professional registration or licensure may be required.
  • Proven ability to manage complex projects and lead multidisciplinary teams.
  • Experience interfacing effectively with clients, stakeholders, and senior management.
  • Willingness and ability to remain actively involved in the technical and design aspects of projects.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of applicable industry standards, practices, and procedures.

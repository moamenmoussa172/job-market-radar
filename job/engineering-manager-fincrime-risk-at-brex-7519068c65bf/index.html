About the job

Why join us

Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.

Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career.

What you’ll do

As Engineering Manager for FinCrime, you’ll directly manage a team of software engineers building fraud prevention and financial crime compliance systems at Brex. Your team will protect Brex and our customers while delivering secure, intuitive product experiences.

Reporting to the Senior Engineering Manager for Risk, you’ll own your team’s delivery, technical quality, and engineers’ growth. You’ll partner closely with Product, Fraud, Compliance, and Operations to translate risk priorities and regulatory requirements into reliable systems and effective controls.

This is a first-line management role focused on people leadership, technical judgment, and execution across fraud and compliance.

Where you’ll work

This role will be based in our Vancouver office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop engineers. Coach your direct reports, provide actionable feedback, support career growth, and recruit great talent.
  • Drive team execution. Translate agreed priorities into clear plans, milestones, and deliverables. Manage dependencies, surface risks early, and drive projects to completion.
  • Build fraud and compliance systems. Deliver services, controls, and customer experiences that prevent fraud and support compliance obligations while minimizing unnecessary friction.
  • Provide technical leadership. Guide design and architecture decisions, balancing delivery speed with reliability, maintainability, and evolving requirements.
  • Partner with domain experts. Work with Product, Fraud, Compliance, and Operations to clarify requirements, make trade-offs, and evaluate whether solutions achieve their intended outcomes.
  • Improve operational effectiveness. Build tools and automation that reduce manual effort and make investigations and controls more effective and auditable.
  • Own operational excellence. Hold a high bar for testing, observability, incident response, and safe changes to critical production systems.
  • Contribute to planning. Partner with your manager on team priorities, staffing, and technical investments, bringing insights from day-to-day execution.

Requirements

  • 2+ years of engineering management experience directly managing software engineers.
  • Experience building or leading engineering teams in fraud, financial crime, risk, or a closely related domain.
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals and experience designing, delivering, and operating reliable production systems.
  • A track record of delivering complex projects with cross-functional partners while setting clear expectations and managing dependencies.
  • Experience coaching engineers, supporting career development, and building a collaborative, accountable team.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to translate ambiguous business requirements into practical engineering plans.
  • Sound judgment balancing customer experience, risk reduction, operational needs, and technical quality.
  • A hands-on leadership approach: comfortable engaging in technical details and helping engineers solve difficult problems without becoming a bottleneck.

Bonus points

  • Experience building fraud prevention controls, real-time decisioning systems, or rules engines.
  • Experience with sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, customer due diligence, or other financial crime compliance systems.
  • Experience building investigation tools, case management systems, or auditable operational workflows.
  • Familiarity with Snowflake, Hex, or Retool.
  • Experience working in fintech or another regulated environment.

Compensation

The expected salary range for this role is $240,000 CAD - $300,000 CAD. However, the starting base pay will depend on a number of factors including the candidate’s location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity. Depending on the position offered, equity and other forms of compensation may be provided as part of a total compensation package.

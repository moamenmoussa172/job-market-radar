About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

At Lucid Motors, we’re building the future of mobility, supported by technology that enables our teams to deliver exceptional experiences to customers and partners. We’re looking for an enthusiastic Salesforce Developer Intern to join our team in Europe.

This is a great opportunity for a student who wants to gain hands-on experience with Salesforce and learn how business requirements are translated into scalable technical solutions in a fast-paced, international environment.

What you’ll do

As a Salesforce Developer Intern, you will work alongside experienced Salesforce professionals and contribute to real projects, including:

  • Support the development and configuration of Salesforce solutions
  • Assist with building and testing Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Flows, and other Salesforce functionality
  • Work with business stakeholders to understand requirements and help translate them into technical solutions
  • Help analyze business processes and identify opportunities for improvement or automation
  • Help investigate and resolve technical issues
  • Participate in code reviews, testing, and deployment activities
  • Contribute to integrations between Salesforce and other business systems
  • Help improve technical documentation, development processes, and ways of working

What we’re looking for

We’re looking for someone who is curious, eager to learn, and enjoys combining technology with business problem-solving.

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Business Information Management, Business Analysis, or a related field
  • Basic understanding of programming or software development concepts
  • Interest in understanding business processes and translating business needs into Salesforce solutions
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Fluent in English, written and spoken
  • Good communication skills and comfortable working with both technical and business stakeholders
  • Willingness to learn new technologies and take on new challenges

Nice to have

  • Previous experience or coursework involving Salesforce
  • Familiarity with Apex, Lightning Web Components, SOQL, or Salesforce Flow
  • Familiarity with JavaScript, HTML, CSS, or another programming language
  • Experience with business analysis, requirements gathering, or process modeling
  • Basic understanding of REST APIs and system integrations
  • Experience with Git or other version-control tools
  • Interest in CRM, cloud technologies, or enterprise application development

What you’ll gain

This internship is designed to give you practical experience at the intersection of technology, business processes, and Salesforce development.

  • Hands-on experience developing and configuring solutions on the Salesforce platform
  • Exposure to Apex, LWC, Flow, APIs, integrations, and Salesforce development tools
  • Experience understanding business requirements and turning them into working solutions
  • Exposure to requirements analysis, business processes, testing, and solution design
  • Experience working with Git-based development and deployment processes
  • Understanding of how Salesforce supports real customer, service, and business processes
  • Coaching and support from experienced Salesforce professionals

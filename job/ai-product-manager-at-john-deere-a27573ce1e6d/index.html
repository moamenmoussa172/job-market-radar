Purpose

Digital Engagement & Commerce requires a dedicated Product Manager to build and scale AI-powered parts experiences across Shop.Deere.com, Deere.com, and Unified Parts Experience.

This role will lead a dedicated development team and own the full product lifecycle for Parts AI capabilities: customer and dealer discovery, product direction, roadmap, backlog, requirements, delivery, release readiness, adoption, quality, and measurable business outcomes. The role will also orchestrate work across Digital Engagement & Commerce so that capabilities are reusable and consistent across platforms, rather than developed as disconnected point solutions as well lead the Internal Transformation for A&CS employees by supporting high-impact AI opportunities, cross-functional delivery, everyday AI fluency, and a clear path from idea intake through validation, governance, execution, and recognition.

Your Responsibilities

  • Own the product and outcomes: Define the AI agents needed, value proposition, objectives, roadmap, and measurable customer, dealer, and business outcomes for Parts AI.
  • Lead a dedicated development team: Provide day-to-day product direction, prioritize the backlog, make scope tradeoffs, remove ambiguity, and move capabilities from discovery through production.
  • Discover and validate user problems: Conduct customer and dealer discovery, identify high-value workflows, test concepts, and validate that AI is solving meaningful problems.
  • Translate needs into buildable work: Define journeys, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, data needs, guardrails, and release decisions in partnership with UX, Engineering, Architecture, Data, and Content.
  • Deliver and operate AI products: Lead pilots, phased releases, production readiness, monitoring, issue prioritization, and continuous improvement.
  • Own AI product quality: Establish evaluation criteria for accuracy, relevance, task completion, safety, and user trust; use findings to prioritize improvements.
  • Drive adoption and business impact: Partner with product, marketing, dealer, regional, and support teams to embed capabilities in workflows and measure utilization and value.
  • Orchestrate across DEC: Coordinate shared skills, tools, data sources, and interaction patterns so each platform can consume common Parts AI capabilities without duplicating development.
  • Prioritize AI work with teams using business value, demand, and support capacity.
  • Share company and industry AI insights to encourage reuse of proven work and reduce duplication.
  • Provide agent-building guidance, including instructions, templates, and reusable patterns for designing, testing, and deploying AI agents.
  • Coach teams through use-case testing, alongside AI Champions, and help review results before expansion.
  • Own program metrics, agree measures and baselines with teams, and review project status and before-and-after results; Armaan Arora supports reporting.
  • Review evidence with team leads to inform continuation, adaptation, or stopping; the leadership sponsor approves the next phase.

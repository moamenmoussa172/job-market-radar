About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. Location: Open across US

You bring expertise in SAP service delivery and a track record of driving real customer value. This role is for you.

What you'll bring

You bring 8 or more years managing SAP service engagements end-to-end, building trust with stakeholders at every level, including the C-suite. Technical progress translates into clear business language; business value tracked with precision. Experience spans time-boxed 90-day delivery cycles, structured governance, and accountability frameworks. Confident in technical conversations about SAP's integration platform and cross-system architecture. Applied AI skills across agentic AI, context engineering, and AI-assisted automation, plus emotional intelligence and problem-solving to navigate complex delivery challenges.

What you'll build

You own a portfolio of SAP service engagements end-to-end, developing strategies aligned to customer goals and business outcomes. Governance models drive accountability, momentum, and issue resolution. Structured 90-day cycles with fixed scope and clear milestones keep delivery on track. Value realization is tracked and communicated — helping customers articulate the full impact of their SAP investment. AI tools are integrated into daily workflow to accelerate outcomes.

Where you belong

You belong at SAP, where individual contribution matters and specialists own real customer relationships. Programs run with genuine autonomy, backed by global support and tools to do the best work. The impact is real — and it's yours to own.

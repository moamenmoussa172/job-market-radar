About the job

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Role Overview

As a Product Manager within our Trading Experience & Institutional team, you will own the end-to-end strategy, delivery, and optimization of our institutional client service platforms and core financial trading products. You will bridge institutional-grade platform requirements with cutting-edge crypto trading infrastructure—driving product strategy across margin/derivatives trading, institutional dashboards, BI analytics, and client onboarding workflows.

Key Responsibilities

  • Institutional Platform Ownership: Lead the end-to-end development and optimization of the institutional client service platform. Define client dashboard requirements, business needs, and data/analytics reporting for institutional, corporate, and professional traders.
  • Financial Product Innovation: Own the continuous delivery and optimization of financial trading products across crypto derivatives, spot, margin, and structured products. Continually monitor market evolution and operational data to drive innovation in platform logic and trading workflows.
  • Client & Market Research: Conduct deep competitive research and user analysis to identify critical feature highlights that elevate the trading experience for professional clients, funds, and quantitative market makers.
  • Cross-Functional Delivery: Partner closely with Engineering, QA, Risk, Compliance, and Business Development teams to translate business and market requirements into seamless PRDs, ensuring initiatives stay on schedule and adhere to high quality standards.
  • Backlog & Issue Prioritization: Collect feedback, operational challenges, and strategic feature requests from institutional clients and internal stakeholder teams; evaluate, prioritize, and drive efficient resolution.

Requirements

  • Experience: 5+ years of relevant product management experience in internet-based B2B platforms, financial trading products, or B2B data/BI products (or 2+ years with a Master’s degree).
  • Track Record: Proven ability to independently drive medium-to-large business initiatives, with at least 1 year of tenure on a successful live product or a demonstrated record of launching a trading/B2B product from scratch.
  • Domain Expertise: Deep logical understanding of financial trading concepts (stocks, futures, forex, or digital currency derivatives) combined with personal trading interest or professional experience.
  • Analytical & Technical Rigor: Exceptional multi-dimensional data analysis skills, strong logical thinking, and high-quality product documentation capabilities (PRDs, workflows, data mapping).
  • Leadership & Execution: Self-motivated, highly accountable team player with outstanding cross-team communication, coordination, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Language: Fluent/Professional English proficiency (spoken and written) as a primary working language.

Preferred Qualifications / Nice to Have

  • Prior product management experience directly at a tier-1 cryptocurrency exchange or fintech trading platform.
  • Experience in quantitative trading products, API product development (REST/WebSocket), or blockchain on/off-ramp mechanisms.
  • Familiarity with broader traditional finance instruments including securities, funds, bonds, and institutional compliance frameworks (e.g., AML, Travel Rule, KYC pipelines).

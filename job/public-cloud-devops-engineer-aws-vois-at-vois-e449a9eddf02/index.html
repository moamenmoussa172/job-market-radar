About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Public Cloud DevOps Engineer with strong AWS expertise to design, build, implement and operate business-critical cloud infrastructure services supporting Vodafone's internal applications and customers across Europe. This role focuses on developing cloud-native solutions, driving infrastructure automation, enabling cloud transformation initiatives, and ensuring high availability and operational excellence across AWS environments. The successful candidate will play a key role in cloud adoption, optimisation, platform engineering, and DevOps culture enablement.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build, implement and operate AWS-based cloud infrastructure services for business-critical applications.
  • Manage project-driven cloud solution integrations and daily platform administration activities.
  • Develop prototypes, modules and cloud platform solutions using Agile delivery practices.
  • Maintain, enhance and optimise cloud services to deliver positive business outcomes.
  • Conduct peer reviews and promote engineering best practices and coding standards.
  • Drive cloud automation and workload integration activities across AWS environments.
  • Support cloud cost optimisation initiatives and FinOps practices.
  • Design and deploy direct connectivity between AWS and on-premise data centres.
  • Troubleshoot cloud networking, storage, performance and platform-related issues.
  • Support incident and problem management activities, including Level 2 and Level 3 operational support
  • Contribute to the adoption and growth of DevOps methodologies and cloud engineering capabilities across the organisation.
  • Share knowledge and help strengthen AWS expertise within teams.

Who you are

  • Experience working with AWS cloud services including VPC, EC2, EKS, S3, EBS, Glacier, ELB and Elastic IPs.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS Lambda and cloud-native architectures.
  • Strong knowledge of cloud networking, security and platform engineering principles.
  • Experience with infrastructure automation using CloudFormation, Ansible and Python.
  • Working knowledge of DevOps tools and technologies such as Terraform, Kubernetes and ArgoCD.
  • Experience managing AWS Organisations and multi-account environments.
  • Familiarity with JSON, YAML and Python scripting.
  • Strong troubleshooting, analytical, documentation and communication skills.
  • Understanding of ITIL processes, SOX compliance and security regulations.
  • Experience working in Agile delivery environments.
  • Experience supporting highly business-critical environments.
  • Degree qualification in Information Technology or a related discipline.
  • AWS certification at Associate level or higher is advantageous.
  • Experience within telecommunications environments is beneficial.

About the job

Location: Customer Site - Leiston, Suffolk + Location Independent Worker
Salary: Excellent basic salary plus bonus and Vodafone benefits
Working hours: Full time 37.5 hours per week - Monday to Friday, with flexibility to work between 8am-6pm each day and provide remote Standby Support as & when required
Security Clearance: This position requires the ability & willingness to undergo and obtain a Secure Vetting (SC) clearance.
Location Independent Worker
As a Location Independent Worker, for operational reasons you may be required to perform your duties, either temporarily or permanently, from any of our offices or customer sites within a reasonable geographic area of your home address. Our “Office in a Box” home working kit will provide you with everything you need, no matter where you are.

What you’ll do

Working in a team of like-minded and highly skilled Network Engineers, the primary focus for this role is delivery of reliable LAN, WLAN and WAN solutions using a wide range of products from data vendors including: Cisco and Vodafone for one of VodafoneThree’s largest Enterprise and Global customers.

The role will be predominantly based on our customer site in Leiston, Suffolk.

Role Responsibilities:

  • Plan, Design and Implement network solutions to meet customer business requirements.
  • Technical Lead for delivery of customer network projects.
  • Ensure Change Control and other mandatory processes are adhered to.
  • Ensure all work is risk-assessed with the production and maintenance of H&S Compliant documentation (RAMs and Point of Works Risk Assessment).
  • Where required undertake hands-on build activity to stage, commission and install new network equipment.
  • Production of customer site and solution network documentation.
  • Monitor progress of activities within each project; providing regular updates to management and key stakeholders.
  • Management and coordination of 3rd party suppliers.
  • Presentation of solutions delivered to team members and colleagues as required.
  • Provide technical support and assistance to pre-sales activity.
  • Develop and maintain departmental procedures and customer best practices standards.
  • Ensure technical knowledge is kept up to date and make recommendations to changes in customer technical standards.
  • Work co-operatively with team members and colleagues, contributing positively and constructively to the achievement of team and organisational objectives.

Who you are

  • Extensive experience working with (configuring and implementing) predominantly Cisco LAN, WLAN and WAN solutions in an enterprise environment.
  • Extensive field experience including installation of networking equipment.
  • Self-motivating individual with the ability to develop strong customer relationships as well as functioning in a team environment.
  • Full SC Security Clearance or the ability to obtain.
  • Able to work a flexible shift pattern between the hours of 8am-6pm, with also being on standby for out of hours remote support as & when required.
  • Project Management experience.
  • Experience of working in a construction environment is advantageous.

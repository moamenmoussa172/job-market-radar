About the job

Our Global Structure

Deliveroo is now part of DoorDash, bringing together teams with even greater reach, scale, and ambition. Depending on your role, you may collaborate with teammates, systems, and leaders across DoorDash and Wolt. Together, we’re unlocking new possibilities as one global team.

Join us in shaping the experience millions of customers worldwide land on every time they open DoorDash, Wolt or Deliveroo.

You’ll be embedded in our global Discovery team, part of the Core Consumer organisation, working side by side with US product managers on a shared global tech stack. Together, we own how customers search, explore and discover everything we have to offer, from their favourite local spot to something they didn’t know they wanted.

Your roadmap will be a mix of consumer problems felt most in EMEA but worth solving globally, global capabilities we’re accelerating into EMEA, and EMEA-specific problems where speed matters.

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager who loves consumer products end to end, from spotting the customer problem to shaping the experience, shipping it and measuring the impact.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Own vision and strategy: Define and drive the roadmap and OKRs for your area of discovery, from early problem discovery through to scaled impact.
  • Build end-to-end experiences: Shape how customers find everything on DoorDash, Wolt and Deliveroo, partnering closely with Design and Ux Research and holding a high bar on the details.
  • Shape AI-powered discovery: Work with Engineering, Data Science and ML on ranking, personalisation and recommendations, and define how GenAI and agentic experiences come to life for customers.
  • Customer first, business-aware: Start with what’s right for the customer, and find solutions that work for the business too, so wins don’t come at the customer’s expense.
  • Build for scale: Understand customer problems across EMEA markets and beyond, spot the patterns, and build solutions that scale globally rather than one-off fixes.
  • Combine data with strong judgment: Lead experiment-driven development, make clear ship decisions, and know when to optimise incrementally versus rethink an approach entirely.
  • Align stakeholder: Communicate strategy, trade-offs and learnings clearly to build conviction across the wider team and senior leadership.

What You'll Need to Thrive

Essential Requirements

  • Customer-first thinking: You start with the customer, always. You seek out customer insight, empathise deeply with real needs and frustrations, and spot opportunities others miss.
  • Consumer product ownership: A proven track record of delivering measurable outcomes on B2C, consumer-facing products at scale.
  • End-to-end generalist: You care as much about the UX and UI customers see as about the systems underneath: the data, ML models and platform capabilities that power the experience. You grasp technical concepts quickly and partner effectively with Engineering and ML.
  • Analytical and commercial rigour: Strong quantitative skills, comfort with experimentation, and sound business instincts to make consequential calls.
  • High-velocity execution: Demonstrated ownership of shipping at pace, with a sharp eye for detail.
  • Stakeholder leadership: You build trust with people whose priorities differ from yours, and align senior stakeholders on difficult trade-offs: collaborative, with a clear point of view.
  • Resilience in ambiguity: You thrive in a high-ownership environment where the problems are big and the industry is changing fast.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with discovery, homepage, feed, ranking or recommendation products.
  • Hands-on experience building GenAI or LLM-powered consumer experiences.
  • Experience in a multi-sided marketplace or a global/regional product setup.

Why Join Us?

At Deliveroo, Wolt and DoorDash, you'll do work that matters, solving real-world problems in a three-sided marketplace that's constantly evolving. We're food lovers, problem solvers, community builders and more, brought together by a shared drive to make things better. Working here you can expect to:

  • Solve meaningful problems at real scale: Work on a complex, always-on marketplace that impacts millions every day.
  • See your impact, fast: Ship, test and improve ideas quickly in a low-hierarchy, high-ownership environment.
  • Grow through challenge and ownership: Take on big, ambiguous problems and accelerate your career with strong support.
  • A culture built for builders: High standards, collaboration, flexible working and continuous learning.
  • Share in the success you help create: Competitive salary and equity options, so you're rewarded for the impact you make.

We aim to create a fair process that lets your skills shine. Our interview typically includes 3-4 stages.

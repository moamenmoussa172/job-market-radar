About the job

As a hands on Senior Technical Architect, you will be responsible for end-to-end architecture, design, and implementation of mission-critical systems. You will define both macro and micro level architecture and implement with engineering teams to modernize a VC++ platform into Reacts JS/Java based event based high throughput, low latency micro services based platform, ensuring alignment with cloud-native principles.

Key Responsibilities

Architecture & Platform Design

  • Define and evolve the architecture for a distributed, cloud-native Investment Accounting platform, aligned with enterprise principles (scalability, resiliency, availability).
  • Design modular microservices architectures, ensuring clear service boundaries, domain-driven design, and reusability across business capabilities.
  • Establish event-driven architecture patterns for real-time processing, enabling decoupled and scalable workflows.
  • Define data architecture and integration patterns, including unified data models, event contracts, and consistency strategies across IBOR/ABOR domains.
  • Hands on experience and capability to walk the talk is critical. This is not architecture only role.

Modernization Leadership

  • Lead the modernization of legacy VC++ systems into Java-based, distributed services
  • Define migration patterns, decomposition strategies, and phased transformation roadmaps
  • Ensure functional equivalence where required, while enabling architectural evolution beyond legacy constraints
  • Guide teams on translating low-level C++ constructs into scalable Java service patterns (stateless services, async processing, etc.)

Performance, Throughput & Scalability

  • Architect systems for high-throughput transaction processing and near real-time accounting workflows
  • Define cache-driven design strategies (distributed caching, in-memory processing, read/write optimization)
  • Establish performance benchmarks, scalability patterns, and capacity planning principles

Cloud-Native & Engineering

  • Design and implement for cloud-native (containerization, orchestration, DevSecOps).
  • Establish cross-cutting capabilities:
  • Observability (logging, metrics, tracing) for operational intelligence
  • Resiliency patterns (retry, circuit breaker, idempotency)
  • Security and compliance controls
  • Ensure API-first and contract-first design principles are adhered to.
  • Required Qualifications
  • Extensive experience in software architecture and distributed systems design
  • Strong programming background in VC++ and Java, with proven experience in system modernization.
  • Rich experience with AWS/Azure along with modern CICD platforms and pipeline as code.
  • Experience building large-scale distributed systems with low latency and high throughput
  • Strong understanding of data consistency, transaction processing, and accounting-like systems

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in Investment Accounting / Financial Services platforms (transactions, valuations, positions, multi-currency processing)
  • Familiarity with Observability and operational intelligence frameworks,Unified data models and ledger-based architectures
  • Experience working in regulatory and audit-heavy environments.

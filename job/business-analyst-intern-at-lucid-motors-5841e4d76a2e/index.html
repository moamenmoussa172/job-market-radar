About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

At Lucid Motors, we’re building the future of mobility, and our After-Sales organization plays a key role in delivering a premium ownership and service experience across Europe. We’re looking for an enthusiastic Business Analyst Intern to join our European After-Sales team in Amsterdam.

This is a great opportunity for a student who enjoys working with data, systems, and people and wants to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced, international automotive environment. You will support the development of reporting and management tools that help us better understand our operations, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

What you’ll do

As a Business Analyst Intern, you will work across different areas of After-Sales to improve our reporting capabilities and management visibility, including:

  • Review existing reports, data sources, and KPIs and identify opportunities to improve the quality and usability of our reporting.
  • Develop reports and dashboards covering areas such as Warranty, Technical Support, Workshop Efficiency & Productivity, and other After-Sales activities.
  • Engage with relevant stakeholders to understand where data is available, how it can be accessed, and how it should be interpreted.
  • Consolidate data from different systems and translate it into clear, meaningful business insights.
  • Support the development of practical management reporting tools and dashboards to track performance, trends, and operational KPIs.
  • Work with tools and platforms such as Salesforce, Jira, Tableau, and Excel.

What we’re looking for

We’re looking for someone analytical, curious, and comfortable collaborating with different teams and stakeholders.

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Administration, Engineering, Economics, or a related field.
  • Strong Excel skills and confidence working with data, including organizing, analyzing, and presenting information.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset with an ability to turn data into clear insights.
  • Strong team and collaboration spirit, with the patience to understand different processes, systems, and stakeholder needs.
  • Adaptable, structured, detail-oriented, and eager to learn.

Nice to have

  • Familiarity with Salesforce, Jira, Tableau, or similar reporting and business intelligence tools.
  • Previous experience with dashboards, data visualization, KPI reporting, or data analysis.
  • Interest in the automotive industry, After-Sales, service operations, or customer experience.
  • Basic understanding of operational performance metrics or business process analysis.

What you’ll gain

This internship offers practical exposure to how data is used to manage and improve After-Sales operations within an international automotive organization.

  • Hands-on experience building operational reports, dashboards, and management tools.
  • Exposure to key After-Sales areas including Warranty, Technical Support, and Workshop Performance.
  • Practical experience working with data across Salesforce, Jira, Tableau, and Excel.
  • Experience collaborating with stakeholders across different functions and markets.
  • The opportunity to develop reporting solutions that are actively used to support European After-Sales management and decision-making.

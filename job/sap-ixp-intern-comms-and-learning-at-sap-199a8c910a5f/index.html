About the SAP Internship Experience Program:
The SAP Internship Experience Program is SAP’s global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

Three reasons to intern at SAP:
1. Culture of collaboration: meet with mentors, make new friends across the globe and create a thriving personal network.
2. Project-driven experience: gain cross-functional skills from our virtual and in-person learning sessions, diverse subject matter experts, and project deliverables.
3. Gain visibility: with Internship Experience Project in your title, you’ll have a global network of SAP leaders, entrepreneurs and career development opportunities at your fingertips.
What you’ll do:
Position title: SAP iXp Intern – Comms & Learning
Location: São Leopoldo, RS, Brazil - hybrid

  • Support Communication Strategies: Assist in the development and execution of internal communication strategies to enhance employee engagement and knowledge sharing.
  • Contribute to Branding and Design Initiatives: Support the maintenance of brand identity, assist with design tasks, and ensure consistency across various materials.
  • Content Creation: Help produce engaging content for internal communications, learning materials, and social media platforms, including articles, newsletters, and presentations.
  • Learning and Development Support: Aid in the organization and implementation of learning programs, including coordination, material preparation, and feedback collection.
  • Information Management Assistance: Support the upkeep of information management systems like SharePoint and MS Teams, ensuring content is up-to-date and accessible.
  • Project Management Support: Assist the Procurement Business Partners with administrative tasks such as meeting preparation, documentation management, reporting, survey feedback data review, and more.
  • Reporting & Analytics: Contribute to reporting and analytics tasks, helping to derive actionable insights from various data sources.

What you bring:
We’re looking for someone who takes initiative, perseveres, and stay curious. You like to work on meaningful, innovative projects and are energized by lifelong learning.

  • Undergraduate student from the 2nd semester or to be graduated in approximately 24 months
  • Preferred Fields of Study: Communications, Learning & Development, Marketing, Business Administration, Business Informatics, or related fields.
  • Computer Skills: Proficiency in MS Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel); familiarity with design tools like Adobe Creative Suite is a plus. Advanced knowledge in Excel and database skills (Access) are advantageous.
  • Language Skills: Fluent in English; Additional language skills are beneficial.
  • Soft Skills: Effective communication skills, initiative-taking attitude, ability to work independently, and creativity in problem-solving. High service attitude, result-driven, and able to manage complex and dynamic environments.
  • Other: Prior experience in content creation, branding, project management, employee communications or procurement is beneficial. A keen interest in technology and continuous improvement is key.

Meet your team:
The Communications and Learning team within SAP's Global Procurement Organization (GPO) plays a critical role in enhancing communication, fostering learning, and building strong partnerships across the organization. This team is responsible for providing strategic communication support, developing and executing communication strategies that drive transparency, standardizing learning programs, and supporting leadership development. By joining this team, you'll contribute to regular operational tasks and initiatives that ensure continuous improvement and effective stakeholder engagement, all while gaining valuable experience in a collaborative and innovative environment.*Please, add your CV in English

What you’ll do

Role purpose: Data engineers that transform various pools of data sources into usable and readable information that can be easily analysed. Develop, construct, test and maintain data pipeline architectures as well as optimize data flows and collections for cross functional teams. Once continuous pipelines are installed to – and from – these huge “pools” of filtered information, data scientists can pull relevant data sets for their analyses.

Job Responsibilities

Key accountabilities and decision ownership:

  • Design, construct, install, test and maintain highly scalable data management systems
  • Ensure systems meet business requirements and industry practices
  • Build high-performance algorithms, prototypes, predictive models and proof of concepts
  • Research opportunities for data acquisition and new uses for existing data
  • Develop data set processes for data modelling, mining and production
  • Integrate new data management technologies and software engineering tools into existing structures
  • Recommend ways to improve data reliability, efficiency and quality
  • Collaborate with IT and Business team members on project goals
  • Support operations team in analysing issues reported in live environment

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Strong RDBMS knowledge, OLAP and OLTP databases
  • Experience with Big Data Technologies (Google Cloud) is a plus
  • Knowledge of programming languages ( Python , Java) is a plus
  • Experience with Oracle ODI and Oracle software stack in general
  • Comfortable with Unix environment and shell scripting

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

  • Hold a relevant degree on Computer Science, Engineering or IT
  • Excellent knowledge of English and Albanian (speaking and writing)

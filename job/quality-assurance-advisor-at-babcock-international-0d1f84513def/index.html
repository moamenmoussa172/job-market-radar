About the Role

Join Babcock and contribute to the delivery of a critical Defence capability as a Quality Assurance Advisor within the JP9101 High Frequency Communications Program based in Abbotsford, Victoria. This is an exciting opportunity to play a key role in ensuring the delivery of high-quality, compliant outcomes that support the Australian Defence Force's communications capability.

Working closely with project teams, suppliers and quality leaders, you will support the implementation and continuous improvement of quality assurance processes across the program. From conducting audits and reviewing Objective Quality Evidence (OQE) to maintaining ISO 9001 certification requirements and supporting supplier quality activities, you'll help ensure the highest standards of quality, compliance and operational excellence are achieved.

You'll be a trusted advisor to stakeholders, providing guidance on quality-related matters, supporting corrective and preventive actions, and contributing to process improvements that drive better outcomes for both the customer and the business. You'll also have the opportunity to participate in program reviews, Integrated Product Teams, supplier engagements and continuous improvement initiatives that make a real impact.

This role is ideal for a quality professional who enjoys working in a collaborative, fast-paced environment and is passionate about driving compliance, continuous improvement and quality excellence within a highly regarded Defence program. In return, you'll join a global organisation with strong values, career development opportunities and the chance to support projects that matter.

To be successful, you will bring:

  • Australian Citizen with a Defence security clearance (or the ability to obtain one).
  • Tertiary qualification in Quality, Business Management related field or equivalent qualification associated with an engineering, technical or maintenance environment.
  • Experience in a role applicable to Quality Assurance role.
  • Experience in Process Improvement applying Lean/Six Sigma concepts.
  • A sound knowledge of Business Management System auditing techniques.
  • ISO 9001:2015 Quality Auditor training.

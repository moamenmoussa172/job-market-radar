About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

ClickHouse is powering the next generation of real-time analytics and AI infrastructure. We recently raised a $400M Series D at a $15B valuation, and we're growing fast: expanding our customer base, scaling our cloud business, and widening our product line through acquisitions and net-new Tier-1 launches into new product categories.

We're looking for a Principal Technical Program Manager, GTM Integration & Launch, to own the commercial side of how those products get to market. An acquired product isn't sellable on day one, and neither is a Tier-1 launch. Someone has to decide who we sell it to first, how it's priced, packaged, quoted and approved, how reps get paid for it, and how we'll know whether it's working. This role bridges product strategy and getting deals closed.

Reporting to the Sr. Director of Global Revenue Operations, you'll partner with our NPI program team on every acquisition and Tier-1 launch and own the commercial workstream: GTM strategy, commercial diligence, partnering with Commercial leadership on pricing and approvals, compensation alignment with Sales leadership, and contract migration for acquired customers. You'll also report monthly to leadership on how our emerging products are actually selling. Beyond any single launch, you'll turn commercial integration into a repeatable playbook. This is a senior individual contributor role and the commercial owner for every acquisition and Tier-1 launch.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Shape GTM strategy for new and acquired products. Define the motion for newly acquired products (e.g., Langfuse) and net-new Tier-1 launches (e.g., Postgres): who we sell to first and why, how it fits our existing selling motions, and what new roles or coverage it needs. Partner with Sales Leadership and Enablement, and build target account lists from usage signals and account intelligence.
  • Own compensation alignment. Work with Sales Leadership and Comp/Planning so reps are paid in a way that drives the motion we want for each new product.
  • Own monthly GTM reporting. Define how we measure launch performance, report wins, gaps, and what broke to leadership, and feed it back so the next launch goes faster.
  • Run commercial diligence and pre-close planning. Assess the target's pricing model, contract paper, billing stack, metering, and revenue recognition, and land at close with a sequenced commercial integration plan rather than a blank page.
  • Partner on commercial design. Work with Commercial leadership on pricing setup, quoting logic, discount and approval rules, and the controls that govern how the product gets sold. Bring the requirements from the acquired business or new product, and make sure the design supports the GTM motion.
  • Own contract and customer migration. Map the acquired base's entitlements to ClickHouse SKUs, move customers onto ClickHouse paper at renewal, and protect revenue through the transition.
  • Own commercial readiness within the NPI program. Drive Finance, Legal, Sales, and the acquired team to commercial readiness, define commercial acceptance criteria for quote-to-cash, sign off on them in NPI's UAT, and provide commercial readiness as an input to the go/no-go.
  • Build the playbook. Turn each integration into a repeatable commercial path, and find and fix what's manual, slow, or error-prone in the commercial system along the way.

What You Bring

  • 10+ years across M&A integration, strategy or deals consulting, corporate development, GTM or business operations, revenue operations, or commercial systems, with a track record of owning the commercial side of launches or acquisitions.
  • Direct M&A integration experience, ideally on the GTM side (sales motion, compensation, quote-to-cash).
  • Direct experience building GTM strategy for a new or unproven product.
  • Experience with usage-based or consumption-based business models. You understand how metering, commits, and overages create dependencies across billing, contracts, revenue recognition, and the commercial system.
  • Working knowledge of the systems a commercial integration depends on: Salesforce and CPQ, financial and billing systems, product rating, and telemetry. You can translate GTM needs into clear requirements for quoting, approval, and control logic.
  • Sales compensation design experience, or direct partnership with Sales/Comp teams on incentive structures.
  • Cross-functional leadership. You drive commercial decisions across Sales, Finance, Legal, and Product and present them to senior stakeholders.
  • Partnership with enablement teams to build training programs and measure them against outcome metrics.
  • Strong analytical skills, including SQL, to build target lists, measure pilot results, and report on launch effectiveness.
  • Strong written and verbal communication.
  • Bachelor's degree; quantitative or technical field preferred. MBA a plus.

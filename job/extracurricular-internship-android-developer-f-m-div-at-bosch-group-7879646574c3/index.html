About the job

Your contribution to something big:

During your internship, your main goal will be the research and development of Android applications for connected IoT appliances, as part of a multidisciplinary mobile team working with agile methodologies (Scrum). You will contribute to Dashboard App, the codebase behind the HomeCom Easy (Bosch), MyBuderus (Buderus), IVT Anywhere II (IVT) and Vulcano Connect (Vulcano) apps. These apps won the Red Dot Design Award and are available across Europe. Your main tasks will be:

• Feature Development: Implement new features in Kotlin, from requirements analysis to delivery, in collaboration with Product Owners, UX, QA and the iOS team.

• Improvements and Fixes: Develop improvements and fix bugs, contributing to the stability and quality of Google Play releases.

• Testing: Develop tests for the developed code.

• Software Quality: Gain knowledge of the software quality processes of our products (code review, continuous integration, static analysis).

• Technology Research: Analyse and evaluate new mobile development frameworks and tools.

• Cross-functional Collaboration: Work in a multidisciplinary team (Android, iOS, Backend, UX and Testing), supporting and contributing to the mobile development team.

What distinguishes you:

• Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Informatics Engineering or a similar field.

• Kotlin: Good knowledge of the Kotlin programming language.

• Android Development: Familiarity with Android Studio, the Android SDK, Jetpack Compose and/or Views, and the main Android architecture patterns (MVVM, MVI or other).

• Reactive Programming and Concurrency: Knowledge of reactive programming concepts (Kotlin Flow, RxJava or other) and concurrency (Kotlin coroutines).

• Continuous Integration and Quality: Knowledge of continuous integration environments (for example, Jenkins), quality tools (for example, Sonar) and Git.

• Testing: Basic understanding of software testing, including automated and manual testing, test case execution, and defect reporting using JIRA.

• Databases: Basic knowledge of SQL, relational databases, and database operations (CRUD). Familiarity with SQLite and Android Room persistence library is a plus.

• Nice to have: Experience with REST APIs or publishing apps on Google Play. Interest in Artificial Intelligence and basic experience with AI coding assistants (for example, GitHub Copilot).

• Languages: Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Portuguese and English.

• Attitude: Willingness to learn and share knowledge about mobile apps and Android, proactivity, attention to detail and team spirit.

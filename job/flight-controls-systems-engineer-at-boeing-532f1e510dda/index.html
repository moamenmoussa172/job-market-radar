About the job

Flight Controls Systems Engineer

Company:

Boeing Poland sp. z o.o. EFS

Boeing Poland is seeking a highly skilled Flight Controls Systems Engineer to support the design, integration, verification, and safety assessment of aircraft Flight Control and Autoflight systems.

This role offers the opportunity to work on complex aerospace systems across the full product lifecycle, from early concept development through integration, test, certification support, and in-service issue resolution.

The ideal candidate brings strong systems engineering fundamentals, hands-on experience in Flight Controls or Autoflight systems, and the ability to collaborate effectively across engineering disciplines to deliver safe, reliable, and high-performing aircraft systems.

Position Responsibilities:

  • Develop, analyze, and manage system requirements to support robust system design, traceability, and compliance in DOORS.
  • Lead or support verification and validation activities, including test planning, test execution, issue resolution, and documentation of results.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to define, document, and manage system interfaces across hardware, software, safety, integration, and test functions.
  • Create and maintain high-quality system design documentation, including specifications, interface definitions, and technical reports.
  • Perform root cause analysis and support disposition development for complex design nonconformances and technical findings.
  • Investigate technical, operational, and quality issues identified in service and drive resolution through engineering analysis.
  • Support the development of complex systems from conceptual design through product introduction and lifecycle sustainment.
  • Evaluate potential safety implications associated with Autoflight and Flight Control system functions.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement in systems engineering processes, methods, and technical rigor.

Basic Qualifications:

  • Bachelor of Science degree from an accredited course of study in engineering, engineering technology (includes manufacturing engineering technology), chemistry, physics, mathematics, data science, or computer science.
  • Experience in systems engineering and technical disciplines related to aircraft Flight Control systems.
  • Strong understanding of systems engineering principles, including requirements development, interface management, and verification.
  • Upper-intermediate English proficiency in both written and verbal communication.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Direct design or integration experience with aircraft Flight Controls or Autoflight systems.
  • Experience performing engineering analysis of Fight Control or Autoflight systems using guidance, navigation, and control theory and related analytical methods.
  • Familiarity with system safety practices and product safety assessment processes.
  • Working knowledge of relevant government regulations and certification requirements impacting aerospace systems.
  • Experience with MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Experience with DOORS and model-based or requirements-driven development environments.
  • Ability to influence technical decisions in a collaborative, cross-functional environment.

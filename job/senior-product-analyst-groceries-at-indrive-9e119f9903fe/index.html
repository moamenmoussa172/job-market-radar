About the job

We’re looking for a Senior Product Analyst to join our Groceries team and help build a profitable, fast-growing grocery delivery business. In this role, you’ll partner closely with Product, Category/Commercial, Operations, and Marketing teams. You’ll use data and experimentation to grow orders and basket size, improve unit economics, and make sure customers get the right assortment, delivered fast and reliably.

  • Own end-to-end grocery performance and unit economics: monitor core metrics (orders, GMV, AOV, conversion, retention, contribution margin, availability), validate targets, and help drive financial planning for the business unit
  • Lead executive-level reporting and insights (e.g., MBRs), turning complex data into clear narratives and actionable recommendations for senior stakeholders
  • Drive growth by identifying, sizing, and prioritizing high-impact opportunities across the customer funnel, assortment and pricing, promotions, and new markets
  • Establish and monitor guardrails for profitability and customer experience (out-of-stocks, delivery time, cancellations); lead incident response for drops and anomalies
  • Co-pilot product and commercial strategy in close partnership with Product, Commercial, Operations, and Marketing teams, including experimentation (A/B testing), initiative prioritization, and roadmap validation
  • Own data foundations: define the metrics hierarchy, build scalable dashboards and self-serve BI tools, and partner with engineering on data architecture, logging, and customer segmentation
  • 5+ years in data analytics or product analytics
  • Strong analytical mindset with a balance between business impact and resource efficiency
  • Proficiency in Python, SQL, and BI tools (e.g., Tableau, Redash)
  • Solid mathematical background, including statistics, optimization, and causal inference
  • Experience in analyzing large datasets and designing data marts for product needs
  • English: Professional Working Proficiency
  • Experience in q-commerce, e-commerce, or retail analytics is a plus

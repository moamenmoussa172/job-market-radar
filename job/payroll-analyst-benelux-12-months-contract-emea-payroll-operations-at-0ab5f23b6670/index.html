About the job

Transform Employee Experience Through Payroll Excellence!
Are you passionate about delivering flawless payroll services that make a real difference in employees' lives? We're looking for a customer-obsessed Payroll Analyst who'll go beyond just processing numbers to create exceptional experiences. Join us to lead end-to-end payroll operations where your expertise will directly impact thousands of employees across multiple countries.
This role is offered as a 12-month fixed-term contract, based full-time in the office in Bratislava or Prague
Key job responsibilities
• Deliver end-to-end payroll processing for multiple countries/entities
• Execute and enhance payroll controls (Source to Gross, Gross to Net)
• Complete standard accounting reconciliations
• Resolve payroll queries
• Support vendor relationships and performance management
• Partner with Benefits, Compensation, Stock Options, HR, and Finance teams
• Suggest process improvements using Six Sigma methodology
• Support payroll components of strategic initiatives
• Deliver on payroll part of country expansion projects
• Contribute innovative solutions to enhance operational efficiency
• Upkeep of payroll processes and inventory (SOPs, process maps etc.)
• Raise blockers and issues to senior analysts and managers
A day in the life
Your day begins by reviewing incoming payroll tickets from employees and stakeholders, prioritizing urgent matters related to upcoming payroll cycles. You'll spend time analyzing payroll inputs and outputs, performing necessary controls, and investigating any discrepancies that require attention. Throughout the day, you'll collaborate with various teams like HR, Benefits, and Data Management to address specific employee situations and ensure accurate processing. You'll also participate in regular meetings with the payroll team to discuss process improvements and upcoming deadlines.
About the team
Our BeNeLux team consists of 12 dedicated professionals who rotate responsibilities to ensure comprehensive coverage and knowledge sharing. We work collaboratively to deliver accurate payroll services that directly impact employee satisfaction across the organization. Our team is focused on continuous improvement, finding ways to enhance processes, implement automations, and transform traditional payroll operations for greater efficiency and accuracy.

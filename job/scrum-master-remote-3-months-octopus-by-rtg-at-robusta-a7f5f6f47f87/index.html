About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in India.

We are seeking a proactive and collaborative Scrum Master to support Agile teams in delivering high-quality products and solutions. The ideal candidate will have solid experience with Scrum methodologies, strong facilitation skills, and the ability to foster continuous improvement within cross-functional teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Facilitate Scrum ceremonies, including Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives.
  • Coach development teams on Agile principles, Scrum practices, and continuous improvement.
  • Identify and help remove impediments affecting team productivity and delivery.
  • Collaborate with Product Owners to ensure effective backlog management and sprint planning.
  • Monitor team performance using Agile metrics such as velocity, burndown charts, and sprint progress.
  • Promote effective communication and collaboration between development teams and stakeholders.
  • Support teams in maintaining transparency, accountability, and continuous delivery.
  • Identify opportunities to improve Agile processes and team efficiency.
  • Facilitate collaboration across distributed and remote teams.

Requirements

  • 3–5 years of experience in Scrum Master, Agile delivery, or related roles.
  • Strong understanding of Agile methodologies, particularly Scrum and Kanban.
  • Proven experience facilitating Scrum ceremonies and supporting Agile teams.
  • Familiarity with Agile project management tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, or Trello.
  • Strong communication, facilitation, problem-solving, and conflict-resolution skills.
  • Experience working with cross-functional software development teams.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and support team collaboration in a remote environment.
  • Good understanding of the software development lifecycle (SDLC).

About the job

Bitwarden promotes better internet security and safety with the leading open source password management solution for individuals, teams, and business organizations. Our philosophy on security is that “we are all in this together” – we continue to offer a full-featured free version of Bitwarden so that everyone can be protected with strong password management. We also provide enterprise-grade solutions to some of the largest companies in the world, and will continue to innovate in the identity and authentication market as the world starts to grow beyond passwords. Read more on the Bitwarden blog.

As a Senior TypeScript/Electron Engineer on the Desktop Native team, you will build secure, reliable desktop experiences that connect the Bitwarden Electron application with native OS capabilities. You will focus on the TypeScript and Electron side of features such as Autotype, the SSH agent, and the DuckDuckGo integration, collaborating closely with Rust engineers and teams working on the vault and desktop platform. This role is primarily for someone strong in TypeScript, Electron, and modern application development who enjoys solving cross-platform challenges in a zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted product. Rust expertise is a plus, not a requirement.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Design, build, and maintain TypeScript/Electron components for Desktop Native features, including Autotype, the SSH agent, and the DuckDuckGo integration.
  • Develop end-to-end desktop workflows across Angular/TypeScript UI, Electron main and preload processes, and Node.js integrations.
  • Work with Rust engineers to integrate native modules, well-defined application interfaces, and OS-level capabilities on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • Design and maintain secure IPC and native integration boundaries, with particular care for sensitive credential data.
  • Partner with product, design, QA, Platform, and Vault teams to deliver reliable, user-friendly experiences across the desktop application.
  • Improve performance, reliability, accessibility, and maintainability through automated tests and cross-platform debugging.
  • Review pull requests and help raise the quality of TypeScript/Electron engineering, including community contributions on GitHub.
  • Identify technical debt and contribute to pragmatic refactoring, architecture improvements, and technical documentation.
  • Participate in technical design discussions, code reviews, and sharing knowledge across the team.
  • Independently plan, estimate, and deliver new feature work and bug fixes in a collaborative, distributed engineering environment.

WHAT YOU BRING TO BITWARDEN

  • Strong production TypeScript/JavaScript experience — 4–6+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Hands-on experience developing Electron desktop applications, including main and renderer processes, preload scripts, and IPC.
  • Experience with Angular or a comparable modern front-end framework, and building maintainable user-facing applications.
  • Strong understanding of Node.js, asynchronous application code, and secure handling of sensitive data across process boundaries.
  • Experience testing, debugging, and shipping applications across desktop operating systems such as Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • Experience using AI tooling in your development and testing workflow (Anthropic Claude preferred).
  • Familiarity with Rust, native Node.js modules (N-API), or OS-level integrations is a plus, not a requirement.
  • Collaborative and adaptable mindset.
  • Openness and authenticity combined with excellent communication skills.
  • Excitement and enthusiasm for open source and for better internet security.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills – you might not know all the answers, but you know how to find and communicate the solution.

WHY WORK WITH US

  • Our user community loves us and we love them. Come to work each day with a sense of purpose as we bring a more secure internet experience to everyone from our friends and family to the world’s largest organizations.
  • Become an expert. You’ll get immersed in the prominent technology markets of security and open source software.
  • We are dedicated to building a diverse and talented team. Work remotely with motivated and supportive team members across the world.
  • Learn and grow. Take on new challenges with the support of your team, and join our #growth-club to continue personal and professional development.
  • At Bitwarden, we are passionate about what we do, but not necessarily everything we do. Our engineering team makes it a policy to carve out some time for personal growth, learning, skill building and pursuing personal passions that positively impact the team and company. To ensure that everyone has time to reaffirm their purpose and grow, you are encouraged to self-direct up to 15% of your week to what you feel is important for the company and for yourself.

Equal Opportunity Employer

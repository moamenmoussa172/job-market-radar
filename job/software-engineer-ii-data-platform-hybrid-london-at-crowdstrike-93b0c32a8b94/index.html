About the Role:

Join CrowdStrike’s Data Engineering team as a Software Engineer II - Data Platform. You’ll help design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines and backend services that ingest and process massive volumes of security data. Working with senior engineers, you’ll contribute to robust data architecture, improve query performance, and help ensure the reliability of distributed systems that support our cybersecurity platform.

What You’ll Do:

  • Develop, test, and maintain high-throughput data processing pipelines using Python, Java, or Scala.
  • Build and maintain backend microservices and REST or gRPC APIs in collaboration with senior engineers.
  • Contribute to real-time data streaming systems using distributed messaging platforms such as Kafka.
  • Support data modeling and storage optimization across relational, NoSQL, and columnar databases, such as PostgreSQL, Cassandra, Elasticsearch, or ClickHouse.
  • Write unit, integration, and performance tests to support system stability and data accuracy.
  • Participate in code reviews, CI/CD deployment workflows, and operational monitoring using tools such as Grafana and Prometheus.

What You’ll Need:

  • 3–5 years of professional software development experience with a bachelor’s degree, or 2+ years with a master’s degree; relevant experience in data infrastructure or backend systems.
  • Proficiency in at least one modern programming language, such as Python, Java, Scala, or C++.
  • Familiarity with distributed data technologies in the Apache ecosystem, such as Spark, Kafka, Flink, Iceberg, Delta Lake, Hive, Presto, or Trino.
  • Hands-on experience with distributed systems, streaming data architectures, and cloud services such as AWS or GCP.
  • Understanding of relational and non-relational data stores, data modeling, and query optimization fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with containerized deployments, including Docker and Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code principles.
  • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.

Bonus Points:

  • Experience with Go.
  • Experience with Jenkins, Parquet, or Protocol Buffers.
  • Experience building or operating gRPC services.

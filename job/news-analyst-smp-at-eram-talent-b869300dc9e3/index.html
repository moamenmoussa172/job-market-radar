News Analyst - SMP

Eram Talent · Dhahran, Saudi Arabia

Onsite · Senior · Data Analyst · Posted 13 October 2026 · Arabic required

About the job

Eram Talent is seeking a detail-oriented and insightful News Analyst focused on Saudi Arabia to join our team. In this role, you will monitor, analyze, and interpret news and media content related to Saudi Arabia, providing valuable insights and reports that help inform strategic decision-making for our clients and stakeholders.

The ideal candidate will possess a deep understanding of the political, economic, and social landscape of Saudi Arabia, with the ability to critically evaluate news sources and trends.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Monitor all media channels across online, social and broadcast.
  • Flag and post critical media news on timely manner and according to predefined criteria.
  • Respond to incoming media inquiries with accuracy and ensure proper tracking.
  • Utilize media monitoring software and tools to monitor news.
  • Coordinate daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly media impact reports summarizing media mentions and analysis and ensure proper distribution internally.
  • Work closely with Media relations and other teams to ensure timely responses.
  • Actively support in crisis situations following the corporate crisis communications manual.

Requirements

  • A Bachelor’s degree in the fields Mass Communications, Corporate Communications, Media, Media Relations, Crisis Communications, Media Monitoring, Public Relations, or Journalism.
  • Candidate should have at least 1 year of professional experience.
  • Candidate should have ability to monitor and analyze media news
  • Candidate should have good English and Arabic language capabilities
  • Candidate should be ready to work in shifts schedule
  • Experience in news analysis, media monitoring, or related roles focusing on Saudi Arabia or the Middle East.
  • Strong analytical and critical thinking skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work under tight deadlines and handle multiple priorities.
  • Proficiency in Arabic and English languages.
  • Familiarity with news databases and media monitoring tools is a plus.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.

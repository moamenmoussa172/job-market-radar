About the job

At Bosch Global Software Technologies Vietnam (BGSW VN), we are continuously expanding our global SAP capabilities to support large-scale digital transformation programs across Bosch entities worldwide.

To stay connected with talented SAP professionals, we are building the BGSW VN SAP Talent Community for individuals who are interested in exploring future career opportunities within a global, innovation-driven, and technology-focused environment.

By joining our SAP Talent Community, you will stay connected with Bosch and allow our Talent Acquisition team to proactively reach out when suitable opportunities aligned with your skills, experience, and career aspirations become available.

We welcome professionals who are passionate about SAP technologies, enterprise transformation, and delivering impactful digital solutions in multinational environments.

Typical opportunities may include:

  • SAP Functional Consultants (FI/CO, MM, SD, PP, WM, EWM, etc.)
  • SAP Technical Consultants / Developers (ABAP, Fiori, BTP)
  • SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)
  • SAP Project Managers

We welcome professionals with experience in:

  • SAP implementation, rollout, or support projects
  • SAP S/4HANA transformation programs
  • SAP ABAP / Fiori / BTP development
  • SAP functional consulting across key modules
  • Experience working in global delivery environments
  • English communication skills for international projects and stakeholder collaboration
  • This is a Talent Community, not an active opening. Our Talent Acquisition team will contact you when a suitable opportunity arises.
  • You are also encouraged to explore our latest career opportunities in our Monthly Career Opportunities published on BGSW VN’s Facebook (Facebook) and LinkedIn pages (Bosch Global Software Technologies Vietnam: Overview | LinkedIn)
  • If you have any questions about the recruitment process, feel free to contact our Talent Acquisition team at career.BGSV@vn.bosch.com

By joining the BGSW VN SAP Talent Community, you will gain visibility into future SAP career opportunities, global digital transformation initiatives, and long-term professional growth opportunities within Bosch’s worldwide network.

Join our SAP Talent Community today and explore future career opportunities with Bosch Global Software Technologies Vietnam (BGSW VN).

About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Own the end-to-end solution architecture for ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management (HAM), focused on the physical hardware asset lifecycle from request and procurement handoff through receiving, deployment, transfer, maintenance, retirement and disposal. Define scalable HAM processes, asset and CMDB data flows, secure enterprise integrations and upgrade-safe platform standards. Provide architectural direction and governance for development teams and business stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities

HAM Solution Architecture & Lifecycle Design

· Lifecycle Architecture: Define target-state workflows and architecture for hardware request, receipt, assignment, transfer, refresh, return, retirement and disposal.

· Stockroom & Asset Design: Design stockroom, inventory, asset model, fulfilment and governance patterns to support traceability, auditability and scale.

· Solution Blueprints: Produce architecture designs, data flows, non-functional requirements, design decisions and phased HAM implementation roadmaps.

Integrations, CMDB & Platform Architecture

· Asset–CI Architecture: Define asset-to-CI relationships, Discovery data flows, system-of-record ownership, reconciliation and data-quality controls with CMDB teams.

· Enterprise Integration Design: Architect secure integrations with procurement, inventory, endpoint management and Discovery via REST APIs, IntegrationHub and import frameworks.

· Platform Standards: Define secure, reusable and upgrade-safe patterns for Business Rules, Script Includes, Glide APIs, ACLs and Workflow Studio/Flow Designer.

Architecture Governance & Technical Direction

· Design Assurance: Review solution designs and implementations, resolve cross-domain dependencies and govern configuration-versus-customization decisions.

· Quality & Operability: Set standards for performance, security, access controls, testing/ATF, observability, release management and production supportability.

· Technical Leadership: Guide leads and developers; align architectural decisions with HAM, ITSM, ITOM, CMDB/CSDM and enterprise architecture stakeholders.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications

· Experience: 17+ years in enterprise software/platform engineering, including extensive ServiceNow solution architecture and large-scale delivery.

· HAM Architecture: Proven design of enterprise HAM lifecycle, stockroom, inventory, asset governance and hardware fulfilment solutions.

· ServiceNow Architecture: Deep expertise in platform scripting, Glide APIs, Workflow Studio/Flow Designer, security/ACLs and upgrade-safe solution patterns.

· CMDB & Data Architecture: Strong knowledge of hardware asset models, asset-to-CI relationships, Discovery, CMDB/CSDM, data ownership and quality.

· Enterprise Integrations: Architecture of REST/JSON APIs, Integration Hub, import sets, transform maps, authentication and resilient integrations.

· Architecture Governance: Experience in solution blueprints, technical design reviews, cross-team governance, release strategy and implementation oversight.

Additional / Preferred Experience

· HAM Transformation: Experience defining HAM target-state architecture, operating models, asset controls and platform modernization roadmaps.

· Cross-Domain Expertise: Exposure to ITSM, ITOM Discovery, Service Catalog, CMDB/CSDM and Service Graph integration patterns.

· Engineering & Automation: Knowledge of ATF, CI/CD, scripting automation, monitoring and maintainability of enterprise ServiceNow solutions.

Certifications

· Mandatory: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) – Data Foundations (CMDB & CSDM).

· Desirable: ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Hardware Asset Management (CIS-HAM); ServiceNow advanced architecture training/certification.

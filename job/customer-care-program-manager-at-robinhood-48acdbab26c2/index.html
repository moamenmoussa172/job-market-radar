About the team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Customer Care, Government Products team supports industry-changing products in collaboration with the U.S. Government, expanding financial access for millions of Americans. This role leads end-to-end Customer Care workstream management for Trump Accounts, developed and managed on behalf of the U.S. Department of the Treasury in partnership with BNY. The Program Manager is the single point of accountability for keeping the program's contact center obligations — to BNY, the U.S. Treasury, and Robinhood's own Legal, Compliance, Operations, Finance, Security, and Engineering teams — running on schedule and in good standing.

This role is based in our Denver, CO, Westlake, TX, or Chicago, IL office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

  • Lead end-to-end Contact Center program management for complex, cross-functional initiatives — scope, milestones, success metrics, governance structures from kickoff through the program lifetime
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, and Risk to ensure CX initiatives meet regulatory requirements (FINRA, SEC, state securities regulations)
  • Run governance cadences — steering committees, status reporting, executive updates
  • Own risk identification and mitigation, maintaining RAID logs and escalating regulatory/operational/tech risks
  • Manage external vendor and financial-services partner relationships — contract deliverables, SLAs, escalation paths
  • Own change management for production changes — testing, sign-off, rollback plans
  • Support audits, regulatory exams, and inquiries — documentation, stakeholder responses, evidence gathering
  • Build and maintain KPI/metrics reporting for program health, adoption, business outcomes
  • Serve as BNY Contact Center Engagement Owner during Trump Account incidents — single point of contact for RH's CX team, alongside the incident ATC
  • Lead incident management and business continuity for production issues affecting customer-facing brokerage systems
  • Manage the program's U.S. Treasury waiver and compliance commitments — deadline tracking, renewal reporting

What you bring

  • Experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder programs with external financial-services partners, vendors, or regulators
  • Familiarity with financial agency relationships, trustee/custodial structures, or public-sector fintech — or comparable experience in a heavily contracted, multi-party environment
  • Incident management and crisis communication experience — formal severity/on-call processes, cross-company coordination
  • Comfort in ambiguity — building governance/SOPs for a first-of-its-kind program vs. an established playbook
  • Excellent written/verbal communication, executive-level reporting for internal and external audiences
  • Compliance-adjacent experience — regulated-data handling, contractual waiver management, third-party vendor governance
  • Cross-functional leadership across Legal, Compliance, Security, Engineering, CX, and BPO oversight
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; PMP or similar a plus

This position is restricted to US citizens or lawful permanent residents due to legal requirements.

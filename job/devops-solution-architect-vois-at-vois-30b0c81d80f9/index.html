About this role

We are seeking a DevOps Solutions Architect to design and support scalable cloud infrastructure, continuous integration and continuous delivery practices, service monitoring, and automation solutions. The role will help improve the reliability, efficiency, and maintainability of technology services by translating technical and operational requirements into effective DevOps architectures.

The successful candidate will collaborate with relevant technology and delivery teams to establish practical architecture patterns, automate repeatable processes, and support dependable service operations.

What you’ll do

  • Design scalable, resilient, and maintainable DevOps and cloud infrastructure solutions.
  • Define architecture patterns that support continuous integration and continuous delivery.
  • Develop and improve automation across infrastructure provisioning, deployment, and service operations.
  • Establish monitoring approaches that provide visibility into service availability, performance, and operational health.
  • Work collaboratively with engineering, infrastructure, operations, and delivery teams to understand requirements and recommend suitable solutions.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify delivery processes, reduce manual effort, and improve service reliability.
  • Support the implementation and continuous improvement of DevOps practices.
  • Produce and maintain clear solution architecture and technical documentation.

Who you are

  • You have experience designing DevOps, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, service-monitoring, or automation solutions.
  • You can translate business and technical requirements into practical solution architectures.
  • You understand the principles of scalable infrastructure, automated delivery, observability, and reliable service operation.
  • You communicate technical concepts clearly to stakeholders with different levels of technical knowledge.
  • You work effectively across multidisciplinary teams and contribute to collaborative decision-making.
  • You apply structured problem-solving and consider security, resilience, maintainability, and operational efficiency when developing solutions.
  • You can create clear technical documentation and support architecture governance.

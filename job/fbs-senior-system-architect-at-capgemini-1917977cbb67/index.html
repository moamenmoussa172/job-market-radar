About the job

Our Client is one of the United States’ largest insurers, providing a wide range of insurance and financial services products with gross written premium well over US$25 Billion (P&C). They proudly serve more than 10 million U.S. households with more than 19 million individual policies across all 50 states through the efforts of over 48,000 exclusive and independent agents and nearly 18,500 employees. Finally, our Client is part of one the largest Insurance Groups in the world

Role Description:

This position is for a Senior System Architect to contribute to the continued evolution and maintenance of the Alta Quoting Platform.

Key Responsibilities and Focus Areas:

As a Senior System Architect, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the technical direction and ensuring the architectural integrity of the Alta platform, focusing on our Angular (frontend) and Java (backend) tech stack. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Architectural Design & Oversight: Designing and evolving scalable, resilient, and performant architectural solutions for new and existing Alta functionalities, with a strong emphasis on Angular and Java best practices.
  • System Integration & Optimization: Driving the seamless integration of Alta with diverse backend systems, ensuring robust data flow and optimal performance. This includes designing APIs and data structures that support efficient multi-product quoting and complex business logic.
  • Technical Leadership & Guidance: Providing architectural leadership, technical guidance, and mentorship to development teams, ensuring adherence to architectural standards and high-quality code delivery.
  • Performance & Scalability: Identifying and implementing architectural improvements to enhance the platform's performance, scalability, and maintainability, particularly as we expand functionality.
  • Problem Solving & Innovation: Leading complex technical problem-solving efforts and driving innovation within the platform's architecture.
  • Phase 2 Expansion & Run and Maintain: Playing a critical role in defining and implementing the architecture for Phase 2 functionalities, and contributing to the robust "run and maintain" strategy to ensure the platform's long-term stability and value.

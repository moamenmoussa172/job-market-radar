Job Description & Summary

Pwc Luxembourg is hiring an Cloud and Devops Specialist . What if it was you?

Meet your future team

You will integrate a project team into IT Infrastructure department to provide expertise around Web Services. From the load balancer to the webserver in the backend, you will cover the full scope of web traffic management (SSL encryption, high availability management, disaster recovery strategy, patch management, continuous deployment …).

What your role will look like

Our role is to support our clients – IT Projects Managers, Functional architects or IT Developers – to design and implement innovative solutions into our IT Infrastructure landscape.

This role may require participation in an on-call duty rotation to ensure 24/7 availability for critical incidents or systems maintenance. The person should be prepared to respond to urgent issues outside of standard working hours when necessary.

  • Design IT Infrastructures based on functional requirements.
  • Implement the solution under Infra as Code guidance and CICD pipelines.
  • Support projects by providing expertise and guidance in your technical scope.
  • Monitor and explore new possibilities to improve our web services platforms.

The skills you bring

  • Expertise in Web Administration, containers and load balancing concepts.
  • Experience in PowerShell and scripting tools are highly recommended.
  • Experience in Azure DevOps pipelines and CICD concepts are recommended.
  • Dynamic, proactive, open mindset and solution oriented
  • Team player, collaborate across teams and open to feedback.
  • Creative and not afraid to go outside your comfort zone.
  • Fluent in English

​

Take your next step

Contribute to the reliability, security and performance of our web platforms while supporting the evolution of our cloud-based infrastructure. Work alongside experts who value collaboration, precision and continuous improvement to keep our digital services running seamlessly and ready for tomorrow.

Grow here. Go further.

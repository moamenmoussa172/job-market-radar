What you’ll do

This role sits within the Geopolitical Risk & Intelligence team, which has responsibilities across geopolitical risk, intelligence, and security and resilience topics.

The successful candidate will contribute to informing Vodafone’s awareness of how geopolitical developments shape, influence, and create risks for the company’s operations and operating environment. The candidate will analyse political, reputational, and geopolitical risks across a range of topics and for different internal stakeholders.

Key accountabilities and decision ownership

  • Due diligence reporting: Use research and analysis skills, alongside background in political risk, to act as the key author of open-source due diligence reports. Produce comprehensively researched, well-written reports that identify relevant political and reputational risks relating to potential commercial activities. Manage internal demand and relationships with internal stakeholders, communicating clearly on risk assessments.
  • Geopolitical risk analysis: Conduct detailed research and analysis on geopolitical developments in line with the team’s intelligence collection plan. Deliver written and verbal outputs as part of team’s offering to internal customers to a consistently high-quality. Engage with external providers to build out analysis.
  • Africa: Hold specific responsibility for analysis on political risks relating to Vodafone’s African footprint. Produce high-quality written research and deliver clear presentations on a regular basis, tailored to audience and internal demand, focused on business implications.
  • Defence & resilience: Contribute to Vodafone’s internal and external positioning on defence, security, and resilience topics, via coordination with internal stakeholders across the business and a strong understanding of Europe’s security landscape. Support engagement with key government stakeholders, including NATO and US.

Key performance indicators

  • Deliver targeted intelligence products (due diligence reports) and manage a pipeline of demand effectively to meet internal customer requirements.
  • Produce regular, business-focused political risk reporting on African markets in line with intelligence requirements.
  • Regularly present written and verbal briefings on geopolitical risks to the company.

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Strong background in political risk and international affairs. Desirable to have experience in assessing the commercial implications of geopolitical developments for a multinational corporate.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a high level of attention to detail and ability to tailor outputs to different audiences.
  • Collaborative approach to working across diverse topics and teams, with the ability to manage multiple stakeholders, coordinate cross-functional work, and influence outcomes as appropriate.
  • Willingness to broaden knowledge, learn quickly, and question assumptions across geopolitical and telecom issues.
  • Desirable to have specific background knowledge of African political risk.

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

  • Experience in a relevant field such as political risk, government, geopolitical advisory, intelligence.
  • Experience producing high-quality written analysis.
  • Relevant undergraduate degree.

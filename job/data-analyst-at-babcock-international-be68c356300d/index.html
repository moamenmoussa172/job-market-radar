The Role

As a Data Analyst, you'll play an important role within our Digital Solutions team, transforming complex engineering and operational data into meaningful insights that support better decision-making across the business. Working alongside customers, stakeholders and the wider Data Exploitation Team, you'll help identify trends, solve business challenges and develop data-driven solutions that improve performance.

This is an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of data, engineering and technology, supporting programmes that contribute to the UK’s defence and engineering capabilities. You'll gain exposure to a diverse range of projects, develop your technical expertise and collaborate with experienced professionals in a business committed to innovation and continuous improvement.

What you’ll do:

  • Gathering, analysing and interpreting data to identify trends, patterns and opportunities for improvement.
  • Delivering analytical support and insights for projects undertaken by the Data Exploitation Team.
  • Creating reports, dashboards and data visualisations that clearly communicate findings to stakeholders.
  • Collaborating with customers and business stakeholders to understand requirements and translate them into analytical solutions.
  • Supporting data quality, validation activities and continuous improvement of analytical processes and tools.

What you’ll need:

  • Experience working in a Data Analyst or similar analytical role.
  • Strong coding capability using Python and/or SQL.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work with complex datasets.
  • Experience using data to identify trends and support business decision-making.
  • Ability to communicate analytical outputs clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Degree in Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Information Management, Statistics, or a related discipline.

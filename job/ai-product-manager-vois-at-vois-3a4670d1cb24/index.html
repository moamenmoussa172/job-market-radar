About this Role

We are seeking an experienced AI and automation product professional to define and drive the vision, strategy and roadmap for AI, GenAI, automation and digital products. Based in Egypt within Digital Delivery Excellence, you will collaborate with engineering, design, customer and business teams to prioritise requirements, shape portfolio initiatives and maximise value across multiple markets.

You will bring strategic thinking, effective communication and a strong interest in digital transformation. The role requires you to monitor portfolio performance, business outcomes, customer adoption and stakeholder alignment while supporting less experienced Product Owners and designers across complex onshore and offshore environments

What you’ll do

  • Define and communicate the AI and automation portfolio strategy, product vision and roadmap.
  • Manage and prioritise the flow of work across allocated squads and portfolio initiatives.
  • Define Programme Increment scope and approve clear, measurable PI objectives.
  • Gather and prioritise customer and business requirements, identifying opportunities to refine requirements and enhance value.
  • Partner with UI/UX teams to design accessible, customer-focused experiences.
  • Act as a subject-matter expert for AI products, providing clear insights and guidance to stakeholders.
  • Guide 10–15 less experienced Product Owners and designers across one or more projects.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders and senior leaders to identify innovative uses of technology that support growth, profitability and operational efficiency.
  • Onboard new workstreams and business areas while supporting digital transformation across a range of project types.
  • Track customer reviews, product adoption, behaviour and performance after launch.
  • Ensure solutions support customer experience, operational efficiency, service stability and the security and integrity of customer data.

Who you are

  • You have approximately eight or more years of relevant experience, supported by a qualification in Computer Science, Engineering or an equivalent combination of education and professional experience.
  • You have previous experience in product ownership, product management, programme management or portfolio management.
  • You have practical experience managing digital, AI, GenAI, automation or IT products and initiatives.
  • You understand AI, GenAI, automation and digital product lifecycles.
  • You are familiar with Agile methodologies; Agile or SAFe practitioner experience is preferred.
  • You understand UI/UX processes and can collaborate effectively with design teams.
  • You bring strong research, analytical, communication, leadership and people-management capabilities.
  • You can communicate complex technical subjects clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • You demonstrate systems thinking, commercial awareness and sound judgement within complex, multi-stakeholder environments.
  • You are collaborative, receptive to peer review and skilled in facilitation, negotiation, prioritisation and structured problem-solving.
  • Knowledge of telecommunications IT or omnichannel IT would be beneficial.

What's in it for you

  • The opportunity to shape AI and automation strategy and roadmaps across multiple local markets.
  • Exposure to senior leadership, Local Market Technology and Group Commercial teams.
  • The opportunity to influence digital transformation, customer experience and operational efficiency.
  • Experience working within cross-functional, onshore and offshore teams.
  • The opportunity to guide Product Owners and designers while strengthening your portfolio leadership experience.
  • Involvement in innovative AI, GenAI, automation and digital initiatives with measurable business and customer outcomes.

What skills you will learn

  • Strategic AI and automation portfolio planning.
  • Roadmap development and portfolio prioritisation.
  • Programme Increment planning and objective setting within Agile or SAFe environments.
  • AI and GenAI product lifecycle management.
  • Customer-centred product discovery and UI/UX collaboration.
  • Product adoption, performance and value-realisation measurement.
  • Stakeholder alignment across markets and organisational levels.
  • Leadership of multidisciplinary product and design teams.
  • Communication of complex technical concepts to non-technical audiences.

About this role

We are seeking a Credit to Collections Analyst to join our FSSC Credit to Collections team in Pune. The role focuses on end-to-end Credit to Collections activities, particularly cash application, collections support, account reconciliation, accounts receivable reporting and customer query resolution.

You will work closely with local markets and internal teams to ensure accurate receipt clearing, timely reporting and effective resolution of customer and stakeholder queries. The role requires good accounting knowledge, attention to detail and the ability to manage high transaction volumes within agreed timelines.

What you’ll do

Perform high-volume cash application and daily receipt-clearing activities accurately and within agreed timelines.Support collections activities through customer follow-ups via telephone and email.Prepare and provide Statements of Account and account reconciliations.Support daily cash-position reporting, invoicing/billing volumes and debtor ageing reports.Prepare and support month-end Accounts Receivable (AR) reporting.Monitor AR debt positions and support bad-debt reporting.Perform relevant balance-sheet reconciliations.Work with Collections teams to resolve outstanding customer issues and queries promptly.Collaborate with internal teams including Treasury, Accounts Payable (AP) and Payment teams, as well as local markets.Support internal and external audit requirements and maintain appropriate process compliance.Compile, deliver and analyse reports, identifying issues and proposing corrective actions where required.Contribute towards reducing Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and improving cash flow through effective Credit to Collections processes.Support initiatives aimed at preventing revenue leakage and minimising bad debt.Contribute to process improvements through automation, Lean/Six Sigma approaches and optimised operating practices.Maintain effective communication and engagement with key stakeholders across shared services and local markets.

Who you are

A graduate in Commerce (B.Com) or an individual with a Master's degree or diploma in Finance.Knowledgeable in the end-to-end Order to Cash (O2C)/Credit to Collections process, particularly cash application, collections and Accounts Receivable.Experienced in cash application and supporting collection activities.Familiar with basic accounting principles and journal entries.Experienced in account reconciliation, receivables reporting and balance-sheet reconciliation.Proficient in Microsoft Excel, particularly for reconciliations and receivables reporting.Familiar with ERP systems, preferably SAP or Oracle.Able to analyse and troubleshoot stakeholder and customer queries.Organised and detail-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and high transaction volumes within tight deadlines.An effective written and verbal communicator who can collaborate with internal teams, customers and key stakeholders.Familiarity with B2B customers, debt collection processes, AR processes and cash-flow forecasting is desirable.Understanding of the telecommunications industry is desirable.

What's in it for you

Opportunity to develop practical expertise across the Credit to Collections and Order to Cash lifecycle.Exposure to Accounts Receivable, cash application, collections, reconciliation and financial reporting activities.Collaboration with teams including Treasury, AP, Payments, local markets and shared services leadership.Experience in supporting audit requirements and working within a global shared-services environment.Opportunity to contribute to process optimisation, automation and continuous-improvement initiatives.

What skills you will learn

End-to-end Credit to Collections and Order to Cash process management.Advanced cash application and customer-account reconciliation practices.Accounts Receivable reporting, debtor ageing and bad-debt monitoring.Cash-flow and DSO management principles.Cross-functional stakeholder and customer-query management.Practical exposure to ERP-enabled finance processes using SAP and/or Oracle.Audit support and financial process controls.Process improvement approaches, including automation and Lean/Six Sigma techniques.

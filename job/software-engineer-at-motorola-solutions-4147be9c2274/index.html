About the job

Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

The Motorola Solutions 'International Mobile Applications’ organisation is responsible for creating the mission critical software solutions which serve emergency first responders across public safety agencies worldwide. The solutions created by this organisation are used by hundreds of thousands of police officers, firefighters and paramedics every day and aim to improve the effectiveness of our emergency services response.
The various applications that we build for first responders are deployed across a wide range of different device types and are sophisticated solutions comprising client applications, server applications, web applications and databases.
Within the organisation, we are organised into different product groups focusing on different sectors and territories across the world. This specific role is focused on our solution which serves UK Police agencies.
Our ‘Pronto’ team is the largest supplier of mobile police apps in the UK, providing software to half of the UK’s 45 police forces - used by around 70,000 police officers on a daily basis. Based in the West End of Glasgow, we currently have around 80 employees. Our team started as Kelvin Connect in 2002, a spin-out company from the University of Glasgow. Now, as part of Motorola Solutions, we continue to expand with future opportunities in the UK and beyond.


Job Description

This role will involve working as part of an agile R&D team of software engineers to architect and implement complex systems for first responders. We are looking for candidates with a strong full-stack software development background who are capable of delivering robust software and developing innovative new features to join our growing engineering team.

You will be responsible for helping to contribute to the entire software development lifecycle, from conceptualisation and design to implementation across platforms. You will work alongside our product owner and designers to turn complex problems into seamless, performant experiences.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop: Build clean, interactive, and responsive user experiences using modern frontend frameworks such as Vue and Compose.
  • Integrate: Navigate and build upon our established codebase, ensuring new functionality aligns with current architectural patterns while identifying opportunities for modernisation or refactoring.
  • Design: Design technical solutions from inception to deployment, balancing architectural best practices with great user experiences.
  • Collaborate: Foster a collaborative engineering culture by participating in code reviews and sharing knowledge with teammates to solve complex technical challenges.
  • Contribute: Contribute to the development of new features and support architecture design as well as implementation.

What We’re Looking For

  • Technical Depth: Ability to adapt quickly to a legacy codebase, with the patience and problem-solving skills needed to understand and improve upon those systems.
  • Innovation: A desire to challenge established design patterns and build upon those systems.
  • Operational Excellence: Experience with collaborating with a team employing Agile/Scrum methodologies.


Basic Requirements

  • A strong understanding of software design patterns used for architecting and developing high performing, scalable software solutions

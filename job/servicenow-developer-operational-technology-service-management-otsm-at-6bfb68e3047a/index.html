About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Design, develop and enhance ServiceNow Operational Technology Service Management (OTSM) solutions for industrial and production environments. Build OT incident, change and service workflows, strengthen OT asset visibility through CMDB integration and connect approved OT information sources to enterprise service processes. Deliver secure, auditable automation with a focus on safety, controlled changes and minimal disruption to plant operations.

Key Responsibilities

OT Service Workflows & Asset Visibility

  • OT Incident Management: Configure and develop incident workflows for industrial assets, including classification, routing, escalation, collaboration and resolution tracking.
  • OT Change Management: Build risk-based change workflows with approvals, maintenance-window considerations, traceability and checks appropriate for production operations.
  • OT Asset Visibility: Develop OT asset records, classifications and relationships to enable trustworthy visibility across industrial sites and equipment.
  • CMDB Integration: Map OT asset information to appropriate CMDB configuration items and relationships, applying identification and data quality controls.

IT/OT Integration & ServiceNow Engineering

  • Secure IT/OT Integrations: Integrate approved OT inventory, monitoring or industrial data sources through secure REST APIs, IntegrationHub, MID Server or other authorized interfaces.
  • Workflow Automation: Implement flows, subflows, approvals, notifications and controlled automation using Workflow Studio/Flow Designer.
  • Platform Development: Build reusable JavaScript/Glide solutions, Business Rules, Script Includes and ACLs using maintainable and upgrade-safe patterns.

Governance, Quality & Production Support

  • OT Security & Governance: Apply role-based access, audit trails, error handling and non-disruptive integration patterns in line with industrial operational requirements.
  • Validation & Support: Support testing and releases, troubleshoot L2/L3 issues, and coordinate fixes with OT and ITSM stakeholders.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications

  • Experience: 7–12 years in software/platform engineering, including substantial hands-on ServiceNow development.
  • OT & CMDB: Strong Understanding of industrial asset records, OT incident/change processes, CMDB data relationships, data quality and controlled operations.
  • OTSM Specialization: Hands-on delivery of OT service workflows, OT asset visibility or IT/OT service integration in a ServiceNow environment.
  • ServiceNow Development: Strong JavaScript, Glide APIs, Business Rules, Script Includes, ACLs and Flow Designer/Workflow Studio experience.
  • Integrations: Experience with REST/JSON APIs, IntegrationHub, import sets/transform maps and secure enterprise integration approaches.
  • OT & CMDB: Understanding of industrial asset records, OT incident/change processes, CMDB data relationships, data quality and controlled operations.
  • Engineering Delivery: Experience in technical design, debugging, ATF/functional testing, release management, documentation and L2/L3 support.

Additional / Preferred Experience

  • Industrial Operations: Exposure to manufacturing or Industrial OT environments and maintenance processes
  • Monitoring & Asset Discovery: Integration with OT asset inventory, monitoring or event sources and familiarity with MID Server, ITOM and CMDB/CSDM.
  • Security & Automation: Familiarity with OT cyber-security principles, network segmentation, Python/PowerShell, Git/CI-CD and controlled workflow automation.

Certifications

  • Mandatory: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) – Data Foundations (CMDB & CSDM).
  • Desirable: ServiceNow OTSM product-specific training or credentials in industrial/OT security and operational change governance.

About the job

Yassir is the leading super App in the Maghreb region set to changing the way daily services are provided. It currently operates in 45 cities across Algeria, Morocco and Tunisia with recent expansions into France, Canada and Sub-Saharan Africa. It is backed (~$200M in funding) by VCs from Silicon Valley, Europe and other parts of the world.

We offer on-demand services such as ride-hailing and last-mile delivery. Building on this infrastructure, we are now introducing financial services to help our users pay, save and borrow digitally.

Helping usher the continent into a digital economy era. We’re not just about serving people - we’re about creating a marketplace to bring people what they need while infusing social values.

What we're looking for

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Senior CloudOps Engineer to join our Mobility team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in cloud infrastructure and operations, with a passion for helping engineering squads ship reliable, scalable systems. You will work daily across different squads, providing hands-on infrastructure support, proposing architecture and tooling improvements, and helping define the best approach to build and maintain our infrastructure on GCP.

Responsibilities

  • Work daily with different engineering squads to support, troubleshoot, and evolve their infrastructure needs.
  • Design, propose, and implement scalable, secure, and reliable infrastructure on GCP.
  • Advise squads on infrastructure architecture and best practices, helping them choose the right setup for their projects.
  • Monitor and troubleshoot production systems, identifying and resolving issues in a timely manner.
  • Automate provisioning, deployment, and scaling processes to minimize downtime and manual effort.
  • Continuously improve infrastructure, tooling, and processes to increase efficiency and reduce costs.
  • Contribute to infrastructure standards and documentation shared across teams.
  • Qualifications
  • Strong experience with Linux administration and scripting languages such as Bash, Python, and TypeScript.
  • Proven experience operating and building infrastructure on GCP (AWS or Azure experience is a plus).
  • Solid knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Experience with configuration management / IaC tools such as Terraform, CDK for Terraform, or Pulumi.
  • Strong understanding of networking, monitoring, and infrastructure reliability practices.
  • Comfortable working cross-functionally with multiple engineering squads and communicating infrastructure trade-offs clearly.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, with a proactive, advisory mindset.

SR SAP SUPPORT ANALYST EWM / WM - 36498 / 36499

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

Hybrid · Senior · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 13 November 2026

ABAPSAP S/4HANA

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Provide 2nd and 3rd level SAP support to Bosch's global users, focusing on the SAP EWM (Extended Warehouse Management) module within the Logistics Execution area.
  • Provide technical and functional support for SAP systems involved in warehouse and logistics execution processes.
  • Support the implementation, enhancement, and maintenance of SAP projects.
  • Manage and monitor problems, incidents, system capacity, availability, and service levels.
  • Monitor system performance, identify failures, and propose solutions and improvements.
  • Develop, update, and maintain technical and functional project documentation, as well as operational manuals.
  • Work closely with global teams and business areas to identify and resolve issues.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree completed.
  • Advanced English proficiency, with the ability to communicate effectively in a global environment.
  • Knowledge of SAP EWM (Extended Warehouse Management).
  • Knowledge of ABAP.
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA.

Desirable Requirements

  • Previous experience in 2nd and 3rd level SAP support.
  • Experience in Logistics Execution, warehouse management, or supply chain processes.
  • Experience in SAP project implementation, support, and maintenance.
  • Experience working in global SAP environments and supporting users across different locations.
  • Knowledge of incident and problem management, as well as Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Experience in system performance monitoring and troubleshooting in SAP environments.

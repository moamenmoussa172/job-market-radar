About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Airbyte:

We’re on a mission to make data available and actionable to everyone, everywhere. With 700+ connectors, our platform powers analytics and gives AI agents the business context they need to do real work. More than 25,000 companies, including 20% of the Fortune 500, rely on Airbyte to put their data to work.

We’re built on open source because we believe companies should have full control over their data. A community of 27K members helps shape what we build, and we’re backed by $181M from investors including Benchmark, Accel, Altimeter, Coatue, and Y Combinator.

Models keep getting better. What holds AI back is the data informing and shaping its decisions, and that's the layer we're building.

We’re just getting started. If you’re excited by big challenges, meaningful ownership, and the chance to help build what’s next, we’d love to meet you.

The Role:

Every great product is built on a great platform. At Airbyte, the Platform team builds the systems that power every product we ship, including Airbyte Cloud, Airbyte Flex, and Airbyte Agents.

We create the infrastructure, internal tooling, developer platforms, and user-facing product surfaces that allow engineers to build quickly, deploy safely, and operate reliably at scale. Our work spans cloud infrastructure, deployment automation, observability, engineering productivity, and the product experiences that our customers and engineers rely on every day.

As a Senior Fullstack Engineer on Platform, you'll work across the full stack to build both the internal tools that accelerate engineering and the user-facing features that customers interact with directly. You'll move fluidly between building an internal deployment dashboard and shipping a product feature in Airbyte Cloud.

This role is ideal for engineers who care about craft across the entire stack, whether the user is an internal engineer or an external customer, and who believe great platform work makes everything around it better.

What You’ll Do:

Build Internal Platform Products

  • Design and build applications that power Airbyte's engineering platform and product surfaces.
  • Develop internal dashboards for deployments, infrastructure health, connector operations, and platform performance.
  • Build and improve user-facing features in Airbyte Cloud and Flex, working closely with product and design.
  • Create self-service tools that enable engineers to provision infrastructure, manage services, and troubleshoot production systems.

Build Across the Stack

  • Build modern React and TypeScript applications backed by scalable backend services.
  • Design APIs and services that support both internal engineering workflows and customer-facing product features.
  • Collaborate with Platform Engineers, SREs, Product Engineering, Product Managers, and Designers to ship high-quality software.
  • Improve the usability and reliability of the systems that power Airbyte's products, for both internal and external users.

Improve Developer Experience

  • Build tooling that automates repetitive engineering work and reduces friction across the org.
  • Improve release workflows, deployment visibility, and operational dashboards.
  • Contribute to the quality of user-facing product surfaces: performance, accessibility, and reliability.
  • Help establish engineering standards and reusable platform components.

Drive Operational Excellence

  • Build interfaces that improve observability, incident response, and production operations.
  • Create tools that help engineers and customers understand system health, deployment status, and service reliability.
  • Improve visibility into platform performance through dashboards, metrics, and reporting.
  • Continuously identify opportunities to reduce operational toil through automation.

Technical Leadership

  • Mentor engineers through design reviews, architecture discussions, and technical leadership.
  • Raise the quality bar for frontend architecture, testing, accessibility, and maintainability.
  • Contribute to platform-wide engineering standards and best practices.
  • Help shape the future of Airbyte's platform and product engineering.

What You’ll Need:

  • 7+ years of experience building production fullstack applications.
  • Deep expertise with React and TypeScript.
  • Strong backend engineering experience building APIs and distributed systems.
  • Experience shipping user-facing SaaS products alongside internal tools, developer platforms, or operational dashboards.
  • Experience working with product managers and designers to ship customer-facing features.
  • Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and modern deployment workflows.
  • Strong product instincts and customer empathy, whether your user is an engineer or an external customer.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • A bias toward ownership, automation, and shipping.

Nice To Have:

  • Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, or cloud infrastructure.
  • Experience with GitHub Actions, CI/CD pipelines, or developer productivity tooling.
  • Familiarity with observability platforms such as Grafana, Datadog, or Prometheus.
  • Experience with Java or Kotlin.
  • Experience with distributed systems and cloud-native architectures.
  • Experience building internal engineering tools or developer platforms.
  • Familiarity with data infrastructure or large-scale SaaS platforms.

What Success Looks Like:

Success means the software you build makes both engineers and customers more effective.

Engineers deploy with confidence.

Infrastructure is easier to understand.

Production issues are easier to diagnose.

Operational workflows become self-service.

Development becomes faster, safer, and more reliable.

You'll build software that spans the full spectrum, from internal platforms that multiply engineering velocity to product features that customers use every day.

Why This Role Matters:

Platform Engineering is the foundation beneath Airbyte Cloud, Airbyte Flex, and Airbyte Agents. Our mission is to build the infrastructure, tooling, and developer experience that allows every engineering team to deliver reliable software at scale.

When Platform succeeds, engineers move faster and customers get a better product. You'll help build the systems that let Airbyte focus on what matters most: building the future of data movement and AI.

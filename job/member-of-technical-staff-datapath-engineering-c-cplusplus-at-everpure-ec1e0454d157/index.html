About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

From redesigning customer experiences with Modern Data platforms and services to evolving internal tools and workflows, we are making deliberate investments to innovate in critical areas and outdistance competitors. Everpure is looking for Software Engineers to act as pure individual contributors driving this digital transformation. In this role, you will collaborate directly with Architects, Engineering Leaders, and Customer Escalation Engineers on new requirements, roadmap development, and complex escalations. You will gain a deep understanding of block storage, asynchronous and synchronous replication, and disaster recovery while building new products from scratch and working extensively within the Kubernetes storage ecosystem.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Designing and implementing creative new algorithms and technologies for high-performance, highly scalable, and highly reliable systems (think six 9’s).
  • Owning and delivering innovation end-to-end, from concept to shipped product.
  • Handling multiple live production environments and resolving complex customer issues within large distributed systems.
  • Working collaboratively with peers, Architects, and Engineering Leaders, while successfully juggling parallel tasks based on priority.
  • Analyzing and solving challenging problems through persistence, working independently with minimal supervision.
  • Making customers genuinely happy by continuously improving the platform and delivering top-tier solutions.
  • Learning extensively, whether you already know a lot or are building your knowledge in system software.
  • Working primarily from the Bangalore office in an in-office environment in compliance with Pure’s policies, barring PTO, work travel, or other approved leave.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • 8+ years of strong expertise in C and C++ and systems-level programming.
  • Extensive experience with large-scale distributed systems, including a track record of significant contributions to systems currently in use by customers.
  • Strong understanding of concurrency and extensive exposure to Kernel basics.
  • Experience in related systems software projects (e.g., distributed systems, operating systems, Linux Kernel, file systems, database internals, hypervisors, containers, compiler optimization).
  • Storage domain experience is highly preferred.
  • Proven design sensibility and a collaborative approach to team-oriented environments.
  • Ability to handle multiple tasks in parallel and the discipline to work independently.
  • A BS degree in Computer Science; advanced degrees are a bonus.
  • Willingness and ability to work in an open-office environment.

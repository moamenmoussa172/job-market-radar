About the job

Amazon Global Media and Entertainment (GME) Content Security is seeking a Content Security Specialist based in London, UK. This broad role protects content, IP, and brand, working closely with internal business teams and external content creators. Primary responsibilities include security policies, standard operating procedures, 3P assessments, security awareness training, screening events, leak and incident response, and security across the content production lifecycle. The role requires subject matter expertise in the content creation process and content-specific security frameworks, so the person can balance security outcomes with business enablement. The role also supports program management for the team, including tracking initiatives and milestones, coordinating across stakeholders, and reporting progress, and contributes to program-level strategy that incorporates emerging threats and industry trends. The role helps raise the bar for content security standards within Amazon and across the Media and Entertainment industry.
A day in the life
The work follows the content slate, not a fixed schedule. We support many titles at once, each at a different stage. One day can include facilitating training for film crews, assessing a facility, auditing a vendor, and preparing screenings and embargo readiness for a title near release. Another day, you step back, look across requests for common themes, and build the standard operating procedures and mechanisms that scale the program. You also help run the program itself: tracking initiatives and milestones across titles, coordinating with Studios, Marketing, and production partners, and preparing status updates for leadership.
- * 5+ years experience in publishing content security policy and security awareness/communication
- * 5+ years experience in performing assessments and audits for content handling environment using content specific security controls (e.g. watermarking, fingerprinting, MPA Best Practices, etc.)
- * 5+ years experience in managing secure screening involving Digital Cinema Package (DCP) and Key Delivery Message (KDM)
- * 5+ years experience in documenting content producing, processing, and handling workflows to identify risks
- Experience in content processing through the industry leading content management tools and systems such as PIX, Box, Flow Capture, Scenechronize, Asset Hub, Movie Magic, SyncOnSet, Aspera, and Signiant.
- Hands on operational experience in film development, production, and post-production (e.g., writers room, DI, editing, visual/audio effects, encoding, dubbing)
- Preferred designation: CISSP, CCSP, CCNA, CISA, AWS Security, Security+, CEH

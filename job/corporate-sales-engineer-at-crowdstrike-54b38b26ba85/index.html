About the Role:

CrowdStrike is seeking a dynamic Corporate Sales Engineer to join our expanding team. This role demands versatility, quick thinking, and a passion for cybersecurity. You'll alternate between hands-on technical work and strategic projects, supporting team members and driving innovation.

What You'll Do:

  • Contribute to team growth by participating in hiring and onboarding processes
  • Support the Value Selling Sales play, including TDDs, Success Criteria, POVs, RFIs, RFPs, and Objection Handling
  • Conduct competitive research and effectively address customer objections while contributing to product roadmap discussions, providing valuable field insights to the product team
  • Drive innovation in processes, tools, and approaches to improve efficiency and effectiveness
  • Deliver enablement sessions for internal teams, channel resellers, prospects, and customers
  • Actively participate in team meetings, sharing insights, best practices, and lessons learned
  • Help improve win rates and opportunity creation within the Sales Engineering team
  • Align with cross-functional teams to drive sales objectives e.g, Collaborate with Marketing and Product Management on feature tracking and enhancements
  • Architect and demonstrate complex technical solutions tailored to customer needs and environments
  • Develop and maintain technical documentation, including POV Frameworks, Success Criteria documentation, demo scripts, and best practices. I.e. Contribute to SE WIKI
  • Stay ahead of emerging cybersecurity threats and trends, incorporating this knowledge into sales strategies and customer recommendations
  • Assist in developing and refining Corporate sales engineering methodologies and processes to improve team effectiveness
  • Mentor junior Sales Engineers, fostering their technical and soft skills development
  • Conduct technical win/loss analysis to identify areas for improvement in the sales engineering process and product offering
  • Demonstrates excellent communication skills, both verbal and written, with the ability to convey complex information clearly and concisely to diverse audiences. Must be able to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams, present ideas persuasively, and adapt communication style as needed in various professional settings.

What You'll Need:

  • Strong technical knowledge of the Falcon Platform and broader security landscape
  • Solid understanding of incident response, malware analysis, forensics, or security analytics
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Resilience and resourcefulness in problem-solving
  • Ability to work independently and adapt quickly
  • Proactive and self-sufficient work ethic
  • Organised and analytical mindset
  • Creative approach to overcoming sales obstacles
  • Commitment to continuous learning and staying updated on security trends
  • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
  • Bonus Points:
  • We're looking for more than just technical expertise. The ideal candidate is a true team player, recognised as the go-to person within the corporate function for creative problem-solving and adaptive approaches.

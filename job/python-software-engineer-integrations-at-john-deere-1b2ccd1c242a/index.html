About the job

Due to the development of John Deere Business Service Center, we are currently seeking candidates for the position of:

Python Software Engineer - IT Payroll Integrations

Poznań, Poland

Your responsibilities:

  • Design and implement solutions to support our payroll systems that adhere to technical standards.
  • Drive integration and software development activities around ADP Global View and SuccessFactors.
  • Participate in and lead code reviews.
  • Resolve system issues through analysis and its impact on hardware and software.
  • Participate in discussions with the Scrum teams to develop technical solutions to satisfy business requirements related to HR/Payroll using SAP HCM technology.
  • Support and guide our business partners in finding and implementing the E2E integration setup between SuccessFactors and ADP Global View Payroll.
  • Configure and test SaaS system extensions as well as internally developed programs and integrations.
  • Evaluate and implement solutions to improve cost, quality, and performance of software applications.
  • Follow Deere Agile methodology for development and documentation.
  • Collaborate with relevant stakeholders and team members to ensure that features meet business needs.

Our requirements:

  • 4+ years’ experience with integration technologies such as Python, CPI, GitHub, AWS tools, Databricks, container etc.
  • Experience in design, integration, development and testing mid-complex software solutions.
  • Develop, code, configure, and test SaaS system extensions as well as internally developed programs and integrations.
  • Good understanding of application development, databases like EDL, data management and infrastructure capabilities.
  • Ability to apply engineering principles and concepts for designs.
  • Good understanding in HR/LR, Time- and Payroll processes.
  • Good inter-personal and communication skills in English.
  • Openness to work with global Product teams.

Nice to have:

  • Working knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors global platform.
  • Proven experience in integration between SAP HCM, SAP SuccessFactors and non-SAP Applications.
  • Strong Interpersonal communication, negotiation, and conflict resolution skills.
  • Experience collaborating with multiple business stakeholders to provide information technology solutions.
  • Experience configuring and implementing system execution/quality monitoring tools.
  • Proven experience in adapting to the change management principles like Lean Service management.
  • Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, Computer Engineering or equivalent experience in a related career field.

What we offer:

  • Employment contract.
  • Strong support for work-life balance, including flexible working hours (start time between 7:00 and 10:00 AM) and a hybrid work model at Business Garden in Poznan.
  • Opportunities for professional development within the company, with strong support for internal career growth.
  • Employee referral program.
  • Additional vacation days based on length of employment.
  • Holiday allowance when taking at least 2 weeks off.
  • Daily use of a foreign language in a friendly international working environment.
  • Private medical care, life insurance, a sports card, meal allowance, language courses, a social fund budget, and various additional fun events in our office.
  • Free access to our e-learning platform, featuring over 1,000 courses, including offerings from LinkedIn Learning and Coursera.

Please attach an English version of your CV.

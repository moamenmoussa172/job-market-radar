About the job

Join Us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers – we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact.

What you’ll do

  • Maintain Telco Cloud critical environment for Vodafone.
  • Perform troubleshooting, fault handling and implement temporary WA and permanent corrective actions. Engage with 3rd party support (hardware or software vendors) to ensure problems are solved within the expected time frames.
  • Apply daily routine monitoring and health checks on the systems. Implement automated incident detection and optimize the system monitoring.
  • Participate in new projects activities, by providing input and solution delivery acceptance, implementing or verifying improvements in processes and work instructions, verifying configuration changes.
  • Interact with customer technical staff, NOC engineers, operation managers and other groups within Service Delivery organization.

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience [max 5]:

• General knowhow of Telco Cloud, CaaS/PaaS/IaaS technologies.

Appreciated knowledge of RedHat Linux, Openshift and VMWare.

• Knowledge of Compute, Storage, SANs, Network and their maintenance.

• Knowledge of containerized functions, K8s cluster management.

• Knowledge of automation solutions and scripting/programming language (Ansible, Git, Python, Grafana).

• Preferred experience working in enterprise environment for problem solving, troubleshooting and analysis processes.

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

Professional Qualifications:

• Good team player

• Problem Solver
• Strong attitude for learning new technologies and methodologies

• Ability to perform in stressing and emergency conditions
• Fluency in English

About the job

About the role
We are looking for a hands-on Endpoint Automation Engineer to design, build and continuously improve the automation that powers our global endpoint environment. This is a cross-platform engineering role focused on making endpoint services scalable, secure, reliable and consistent across Windows, macOS, Linux and mobile devices.
You will replace repetitive administration with reusable code, integrations and event-driven workflows. Working across endpoint management, identity, security, application delivery, observability and device lifecycle services, you will use the Microsoft technology stack as core platforms while creating automation that operates across a broader endpoint technology ecosystem.

Engineering mindset
Automation is the product, not an afterthought. We are looking for someone who sees a repetitive task, fragile hand-off or one-off script and asks, “How can we make this reliable, reusable and self-service?”
You should be comfortable investigating unfamiliar systems, working from API documentation and turning operational requirements into maintainable automation. Your designs account for failure, test before release, instrument what you build and favour reusable services over isolated scripts. You take ownership from design through operation, document your solutions and balance long-term engineering improvements with immediate business needs.
What success looks like
You will measurably reduce manual work, deployment lead time and configuration inconsistency across the endpoint estate. Provisioning, configuration, software delivery, patching, compliance and lifecycle processes will become increasingly automated, observable, auditable and recoverable.
Over time, you will help establish a shared Endpoint Automation model in which cross-platform services are delivered through reusable code, APIs, pipelines and common engineering standards rather than only platform-specific administration.

What you'll do

Endpoint Platforms & Automation

  • Design and maintain automation frameworks for endpoint enrollment, provisioning, configuration, application delivery and retirement.
  • Build reusable tools, services and workflows using PowerShell, APIs and other appropriate programming languages.
  • Integrate endpoint platforms with identity, security, infrastructure, asset and service-management systems.
  • Apply Git, CI/CD, automated testing and configuration-as-code practices to make endpoint changes controlled, repeatable and auditable.
  • Identify manual, error-prone or fragmented processes and redesign them as resilient, self-service automation.

Security, Compliance & Observability

  • Translate endpoint security and compliance requirements into policy-as-code, automated controls and measurable outcomes.
  • Automate operating-system and third-party application patching, vulnerability remediation and exception handling.
  • Build automated guardrails for encryption, endpoint protection, authentication and device compliance.
  • Create telemetry, dashboards and alerting that provide actionable visibility into automation performance, device health and risk.
  • Partner with Security teams to automate evidence collection, audit reporting and remediation workflows.

Engineering, Lifecycle & Support

  • Define endpoint automation architecture, engineering standards and a roadmap that reduces operational effort and platform fragmentation.
  • Engineer automated application packaging, deployment, update and rollback pipelines.
  • Evaluate platform capabilities, APIs and emerging technologies for opportunities to simplify or extend endpoint automation.
  • Troubleshoot complex failures across automation, endpoints, identity, networking, security and device-management services.
  • Convert recurring incidents and support escalations into automated detection, remediation or prevention.
  • Document automation designs, operating models and support procedures, and enable teams to use them effectively.

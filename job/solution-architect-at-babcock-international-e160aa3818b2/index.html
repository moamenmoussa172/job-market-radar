About the job

Shape the digital solutions that support the UK’s critical defence infrastructure

At Babcock, we're working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a Solution Architect at Babcock Technology Centre, Devonport, or Leicester, near Bristol, Plymouth or Leicester.

This is an exciting opportunity to join our growing Digital Solutions team and help deliver innovative technology solutions that support one of the UK's most significant defence infrastructure programmes. Working at the forefront of defence, engineering and digital transformation, you'll collaborate with customers, programme partners and technical specialists to design secure, scalable solutions that support the maintenance and operation of the UK's submarine fleet. Your work will contribute directly to national security while developing your expertise within a complex and highly rewarding environment.

The role

As a Solution Architect, you'll play a key role in shaping technical solutions from concept through to deployment. Working within our architecture team, you'll translate business and technical requirements into robust solution designs, ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture standards, security requirements and operational needs.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Defining solution architectures, system components, integration points and data flows for complex digital programmes.
  • Translating functional and non-functional requirements into high-level technical designs and architecture artefacts.
  • Presenting solution options and recommendations through stakeholder workshops, proposals and architecture reviews.
  • Leading technical assurance activities, managing risks and supporting delivery through implementation and deployment.
  • Collaborating with customers, software partners and internal teams to resolve complex technical challenges and ensure alignment with architecture standards.

This role is 37 hours per week and can be based at Devonport Royal Dockyard, Plymouth, Babcock Technology Centre, Bristol, or Leicester, with hybrid working patterns available and an expectation of approximately two days per week onsite.

Essential experience of the Solution Architect:

  • Proven experience in technical architecture and solution design using tools such as Sparx Enterprise Architect and architecture such as ArchiMate or the C4 notation..
  • Strong understanding of information security principles, governance and architecture best practices.
  • Demonstrable experience designing and delivering complex systems integration solutions.
  • Ability to communicate effectively and influence stakeholders at all levels.
  • Experience in systems modelling and architecture documentation.

Qualifications for the Solution Architect:

Essential:

  • Relevant qualification in technical architecture and solution design, such as the BCS Practitioner Certificate in Enterprise and Solutions Architecture.

Advantageous:

  • Additional certifications in enterprise architecture, cyber security or enterprise systems

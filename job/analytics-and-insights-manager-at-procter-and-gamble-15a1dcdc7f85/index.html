Job Description

Unlock the Power of Data – Join Our Consumer & Market Knowledge Team!

Are you a budding analytical talent eager to dive into the world of consumer insights and shape the future of beloved brands? Do you thrive on transforming data into actionable strategies that make a real-world impact? If so, we want to hear from you!

We're looking for a passionate and curious Entry-Level Data Analyst to join our dynamic Consumer & Market Knowledge (CMK) team. This isn't just about crunching numbers; it's about understanding people, driving growth, and helping P&G stay at the forefront of consumer innovation. This role offers an unparalleled opportunity to launch your career, providing hands-on experience and direct mentorship as you contribute to projects that directly influence brand strategies and market execution.

As an Entry-Level Data Analyst within our CMK team, you will play a crucial supporting role in executing strategies and participating in the development and implementation of new ideas, techniques, and procedures. You will focus on clearly defined objectives, helping to translate complex data into actionable insights that fuel our business.

In this role, you will:

  • Shape Growth Strategies: Support the identification of retailer and shopper insights that drive growth into our brands and joint business plans.
  • Activate Insights: Help translate cross-category learnings into pragmatic in-store, CRM/SBD, and media activations.
  • Build Partnerships: Contribute to data-based thought leadership, helping to position P&G as a preferred partner with customers and retailers.
  • Uncover Opportunities: Provide market-level insights to build consumer understanding and enable effective brand plan activation across markets, regions, and globally.
  • Drive Execution:
  • Assist in shaping integrated brand plans and execution choices for key customers/channels, ensuring alignment with market-specific requirements.
  • Support the optimization of key business drivers, track in-market innovation, and contribute to consumer foundations work.
  • Summarize local consumer/shopper insights and help monitor and optimize in-store execution using corporate tools and regional strategies.
  • Fuel Innovation:
  • Combine analytics and consumer understanding to identify key business drivers and unmet needs.
  • Support the development and execution of category innovation learning plans, translating insights into upstream innovation concepts.

Execute learning plans for key markets and contribute insights that inform our integrated brand plans and innovation focus.

Job Qualifications

To thrive in this role, you will bring:

  • Education: A Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Economics, Statistics, Data Science, or a related analytical field.
  • Experience: 2-3 years maximum of prior experience in market research, consumer insights, data analysis, or a related analytical role is required.
  • Analytical Prowess: Strong analytical skills with a proven ability to collect, organize, and analyze significant amounts of information and data with exceptional attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Technical Skills: Proficiency in data analysis tools and software such as SQL, Power BI, Data Modeling, Machine Learning, and Python is a plus.
  • Communication & Collaboration: Excellent English communication skills (written and verbal) and a proven ability to work effectively and collaboratively within a team.
  • Learning Agility: Able to work effectively under direct supervision, follow well-defined precedents and policies, and demonstrate a proactive desire to develop new knowledge and skills for current and future job requirements.
  • Curiosity: A genuine passion for understanding consumer behavior and market trendsWhat We Offer:
  • Accelerated Learning: A dynamic entry-level role with significant opportunities for hands-on experience and professional development.
  • Real Impact: Contribute to projects that directly shape the strategies and success of world-renowned brands.
  • Diverse Exposure: Gain experience across a wide range of consumer data, analytical techniques, and market categories.
  • Collaborative Environment: Work within a supportive and innovative team that values fresh perspectives.
  • Mentorship & Growth: Benefit from guidance and mentorship from experienced professionals in the CMK field, setting a strong foundation for your career.

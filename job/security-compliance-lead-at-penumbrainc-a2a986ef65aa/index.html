About the job

The Security Compliance Lead owns the day-to-day operation of Penumbra’s IT and security compliance program.

This role keeps standard operating procedures current, drives recurring IT and security control activities such as user access reviews through to completion, partners with IT and the SOX compliance function to close gaps, and reports on program health to leadership.

The Security Compliance Lead provides day-to-day direction to compliance team members and serves as the connecting point between Security, IT, internal audit, and external auditors.

Specific Duties and Responsibilities
• Own and operate the IT and security compliance program, including the calendar of recurring control activities (e.g., user access reviews, privileged access reviews, joiner/mover/leaver checks, backup and restore verification, vulnerability remediation reviews, and policy attestations).
• Complete security-owned periodic reviews, including user access reviews (UARs) for security tools and systems, and coordinate with IT system owners to complete, evidence, and remediate UARs for in-scope applications and infrastructure.
• Author, maintain, and version-control standard operating procedures (SOPs), runbooks, and work instructions for IT and security controls, ensuring documented procedures reflect how controls actually operate.
• Track follow-up items from periodic reviews, assessments, and audits through to closure; escalate overdue or at-risk items and help IT teams develop practical, sustainable remediation.
• Partner closely with the SOX and Product compliance functions to align IT General Controls (ITGCs), testing schedules, evidence requests, and remediation, minimizing duplicate effort for control owners.
• Conduct ongoing control assessments and gap analyses across IT domains using the ITGC framework and applicable standards (e.g., SOX, HIPAA, NIST CSF, ISO 27001) and recommend control improvements.
• Report on compliance program status to management, including metrics and KPIs such as control completion rates, overdue actions, exceptions, and audit findings.
• Provide day-to-day direction, work prioritization, and mentorship to information security team members, and review work product quality and completeness.
• Coordinate internal and external audit readiness, including evidence collection, control walkthroughs, and responses to auditor requests.
• Support the risk and exception register for IT and security controls, including documented risk acceptances and compensating controls.
• Manage the security policy lifecycle, including annual review, updates, approvals, and employee acknowledgments.
• Identify opportunities to automate evidence collection and control monitoring through GRC tooling, scripting, or integrations with identity and IT service management platforms.
• Educate IT and business stakeholders on control requirements and their responsibilities in maintaining compliance.
• Adhere to the Company’s Quality Management System (QMS) as well as domestic and global quality system regulations, standards, and procedures.
• Understand relevant security, privacy, and compliance principles and adhere to the regulations, standards, and procedures that are applicable to the Company.
• Ensure other members of the department follow the QMS, regulations, standards, and procedures.
• Perform other work-related duties as assigned.

Position Qualifications
• Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Security, Business, or related field with 7+ years of experience, or equivalent combination of education and experience..
• Preferred certifications in any of the following: Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA), Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC), Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP), or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor/Implementer.
• 7+ years of experience in IT audit, IT compliance, security governance, or GRC roles, including 2+ years leading programs or directing the work of others.
• Hands-on experience operating ITGCs, including user access reviews, logical access, change management, and IT operations controls.
• Working knowledge of SOX ITGC requirements and experience partnering with SOX, internal audit, and external audit teams.
• Solid understanding of compliance frameworks and regulations (e.g., SOX, HIPAA, NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR).
• Demonstrated ability to write clear, usable SOPs, procedures, and control narratives.
• Familiarity with identity and access management systems (e.g., Active Directory/Entra ID, Okta) and how access review data is produced from them.
• Proficient in using GRC platforms and ticketing or workflow tools (e.g., ServiceNow, Jira) to track control activities and remediation.
• Proven ability to drive accountability with peer teams without direct authority, and to follow up persistently and diplomatically.
• Ability to translate control and audit requirements into practical, actionable steps for technical teams.
• Experience in a regulated industry (e.g., medical devices, life sciences, healthcare) is a plus.
• Strong oral, written, and interpersonal communication skills.
• Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
• Excellent organizational skills with the ability to prioritize assignments while handling various projects simultaneously.

About the job

Nabeh a subsidiary of Master Works, delivers AI-powered solutions that help organizations automate operations, boost efficiency, and drive innovation. Its expertise spans computer vision, LLM applications, speech-to-text, data intelligence, and low-code AI solutions.

Nabeh is looking for an AI Business, Analyst to bridge enterprise business needs and AI implementations. The role involves leading requirements elicitation, analyzing business processes, translating client needs into actionable AI specifications, and ensuring alignment between stakeholders, AI engineering teams, and QA throughout the project delivery lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan and lead requirements-gathering workshops and interviews with business stakeholders, subject matter experts, and executive leadership.
  • Draft, review, and maintain Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), Software Requirements Specifications (SRS), and functional and non-functional specifications.
  • Translate business goals into epics, use cases, user stories, workflows, process diagrams, and acceptance criteria.
  • Maintain the Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) to track scope, dependencies, gaps, and change requests.
  • Collaborate with AI Engineers, Technical Leads, and Solution Architects to assess technical feasibility, model constraints, data availability, and system integration requirements.
  • Define business rules, data requirements, expected model outputs, KPIs, and operational success criteria for AI initiatives, including accuracy thresholds, processing time, and ROI.
  • Review UI/UX designs to ensure wireframes align with functional requirements, and coordinate with QA teams on test planning and scenario validation.
  • Develop User Acceptance Testing (UAT) plans, support clients during test execution, resolve requirement discrepancies, and obtain formal project sign-offs.
  • Manage end-to-end requirements throughout the project lifecycle, including gap analysis, validation, and change management.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Information Systems, Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • 3–5 years of business analysis experience, with direct exposure to AI, custom software development, or digital transformation projects.
  • Proficiency in Agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban), Waterfall, and hybrid project delivery frameworks.
  • Working knowledge of software architecture, REST APIs, enterprise system integrations, data flow modeling, and database concepts.
  • Experience in requirements lifecycle management, gap analysis, risk management, and UAT execution.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and process-mapping skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, stakeholder management, and presentation skills.
  • Professional fluency in English is required; professional proficiency in Arabic is strongly preferred.

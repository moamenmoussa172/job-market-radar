About the job

Sell what you love. For us and millions of users across the globe, that’s Spotify. Join the Sales team and you’ll build the relationships that help grow our business in existing markets and beyond. We don’t just sell creative solutions to our clients and partners, we help to shape them; using our expert knowledge of ad products, sales channels and the industry to impact the way the world experiences music and podcasts.

The Sales Strategy & Operations team is a strategic partner to Sales leadership, turning business priorities into clear, actionable plans that accelerate growth across our Americas Ads business. As Senior Analyst, Sales Strategy, you'll deliver the analysis, insights and tools behind our North America Enterprise strategy, with a primary focus on the U.S. This role reports to the Senior Manager, Sales Strategy - Americas Enterprise.This is an individual contributor role that reports to the Senior Manager, Sales Strategy - Americas Enterprise.

What You’ll Do

  • Support U.S. Enterprise Sales leaders with the analysis and insights they need to run their business, partnering closely with the Manager and Senior Manager, Sales Strategy.
  • Help build and maintain sales playbooks by client health and maturity, pulling the data that shows which clients belong in each stage and what sellers should do next.
  • Support revenue acceleration programs, such as win-back, share-of-wallet expansion, multi-format adoption and SPIFFs, by building target account lists, seller materials and results tracking.
  • Analyze account, product, category and market trends using first- and third-party data, such as competitive ad spend, to spot growth opportunities and risks.
  • Build and maintain dashboards, reporting and AI-powered apps for Sales and CSMs, such as weekly business reviews, seller activity analysis, account health, category deep dives and account planning tools, turning data into clear stories that drive action.
  • Support coverage planning by preparing account segmentation, named account list and book data, and processing account moves.
  • Use AI tools, including LLM tools, to automate recurring analysis and reporting and to build simple tools that help sellers find answers on their own.
  • Collaborate with Finance, Customer Success, Business Operations and Sales Comp on data, processes and program rollouts.
  • Who You Are
  • You have 3+ years of experience in sales strategy, sales operations, business analytics, or a similar analytical role, ideally in digital media, advertising technology or ad-supported platforms.
  • You have strong analytical and quantitative skills, can spot trends in large datasets, and turn findings into clear takeaways.
  • You have experience building reports and dashboards in a BI tool such as Tableau or Looker. Experience with SQL, BigQuery and Salesforce is valuable.
  • You have hands-on experience using AI tools (for example Claude or ChatGPT) to speed up analysis or automate reporting, and you are curious to keep learning.
  • You are inquisitive, challenge how things are done today, and look for ways to make processes simpler.
  • You collaborate well across teams and communicate clearly in writing and in presentations.
  • You are organized and detail-oriented, and can juggle several projects at once in a fast-moving environment.
  • Where You’ll Be
  • This role is based in New York, NY
  • We ask that you come into the New York office 1-2 times per week.
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.
  • The United States base range for this position is $92,292 - $131,847, plus equity. The benefits available for this position include health insurance, six-month paid parental leave, 401(k) retirement plan, monthly meal allowance, 23 paid days off, 13 paid flexible holidays, paid sick leave.

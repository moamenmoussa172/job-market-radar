About the job

We are looking for a motivated Working Student in UX/UI Design to support the creation of intuitive, user-centered digital solutions. In this role, you will work closely with software engineers, product owners, business stakeholders, and users to help shape engaging user experiences across web, mobile, and internal applications.
This is an excellent opportunity for students who want to gain hands-on experience in user research, interaction design, prototyping, and design collaboration within a real engineering environment. For ease in assessment during the recruitment process, please submit a link to your portfolio, alongside your resume, or attach it in PDF format.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the design and improvement of digital products and services from a user experience perspective.
  • Create wireframes, mockups, user flows, and interactive prototypes.
  • Contribute to user research activities, interviews, usability tests, and feedback analy-sis.
  • Contribute to maintaining and applying existing design guidelines to ensure con-sistent user experiences.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand user and business needs.
  • Help define and maintain design guidelines and design system components.
  • Translate requirements into intuitive and user-friendly interface concepts.
  • Support the preparation and facilitation of workshops, ideation sessions, and design reviews.
  • Analyze user feedback and identify opportunities for UX improvements.
  • Ensure consistency, usability, and accessibility across solutions.
  • Ongoing studies in UX Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Product Design, Graphic Design, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Basic understanding of UX principles, user-centered design, and design thinking.
  • Experience with design and prototyping tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or similar.
  • Ability to create wireframes, user journeys, and interactive prototypes.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • Proactive mindset with a willingness to take initiative and ownership of tasks.
  • Analytical thinking and willingness to learn.
  • Fluent English skills.

Nice to have

  • Experience with usability testing or user research methods.
  • Interest in user-centered design and creating intuitive user experiences.
  • Basic knowledge of front-end technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
  • Portfolio showcasing academic or personal UX/UI projects

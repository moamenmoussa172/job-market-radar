About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Docker

Docker has been one of the most loved brands in developer tooling, trusted by more than 20 million monthly users and over 20 billion container image pulls. From solo founders to the world's largest companies, developers rely on Docker to build, share, and run their applications across our suite of products including Docker Desktop, Docker Hub, and Docker Scout.
We are a globally distributed, remote-first team building the tools that define how software gets built and delivered. As AI agents redefine software development, Docker is at the center of that shift, providing the sandboxed environments, verified images, and secure infrastructure that make autonomous workflows trustworthy by default.

_______________________________________________________________________

The Role

Docker AI Gateway is our answer to the complexity of taking AI agents from prototype to production. It’s a powerful, intelligent, and secure control point that eliminates the toil of model orchestration, tool management, observability, and governance - so developers can focus on building incredible AI agents, not gluing together infrastructure.

The Gateway sits at the center of modern AI applications, offering:

  • A model and tool routing layer with built-in security and cost optimization
  • A familiar OpenAI-compatible interface and MCP server
  • Unified observability and policy enforcement
  • Auto-RAG, tool injection, session summarization, and more

We’re just getting started and we need exceptional engineers to help us build the backbone of the future of agent-based development.

_______________________________________________________________________

What You’ll Do

  • Set technical direction for core systems powering the AI Gateway 0 the model router, MCP gateway, and control plane - and make sure architectural decisions hold up as usage scales by 10x
  • Consistently identify gaps in our infrastructure, process, or tooling before they become blockers, and drive the fix from idea to production
  • Take a significant, hands-on role in defining, delivering, and implementing the Gateway's technical strategy, in close partnership with engineering leadership
  • Own the hardest, highest-ambiguity problems in the system: dynamic model selection, auto-failover, cost-based routing, and policy enforcement that has to work end-to-end
  • Act as the go-to expert for secure credential storage, session summarization, caching, and rate limiting - the capabilities everything else depends on
  • Build the infrastructure that lets us, and our customers, evaluate, replay, and backtest agent and LLM behavior with confidence
  • Mentor senior and mid-level engineers, and raise the technical bar for the team through code, design review, and example
  • Partner with product, design, and leadership to shape not just what the control plane UI does, but where the product goes next

Key Problems You’ll Help Solve

  • Define the unified abstraction layer across diverse model and tool providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, AWS Bedrock) that the rest of the org builds on
  • Architect secure, scalable identity and credential vaulting for tool and model access, trusted enough to run at enterprise scale
  • Build the infrastructure for real-time and historical analytics of AI agent behavior that leadership and customers both rely on
  • Guarantee that policy enforcement and logging work end-to-end - from prompt to tool to response - with no gaps an auditor (or attacker) could find
  • Set the bar for developer experience through intuitive APIs and first-class observability
  • Take part in on-call rotation for your team, respond to incidents, debug production issues, and drive continuous improvement of system reliability

Who You Are

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of backend engineering experience, with a track record of owning production-grade systems at significant scale
  • Deep specialization in distributed systems, cloud-native infrastructure, and API design - recognized by peers and cross-functional partners as a go-to expert, not just experienced
  • Experience building secure, high-throughput services (gateways, proxies, load balancers, policy engines), and a history of making judgment calls other engineers rely on
  • Fluency in Go and/or Rust
  • A track record of identifying systemic gaps - in architecture, process, or tooling-— and driving the fix across team boundaries, not just within your own
  • A strong product mindset, with real experience shaping technical direction rather than only executing against it
  • Comfortable operating with wide latitude: given a problem and significant autonomy in how to solve it, including when and who to pull in
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

Bonus Qualifications

  • Prior experience with OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar LLM APIs
  • Familiarity with RAG architectures, vector databases, or agent frameworks (e.g., LangChain, AutoGPT, CrewAI)
  • Experience with policy engines (e.g., OPA), observability frameworks (e.g., OpenTelemetry), or API gateways
  • Understanding of OAuth 2.1, secret management, and cloud IAM systems
  • Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and microservices architecture
  • A track record of setting technical standards that other teams or engineers went on to adopt

About the job

To manage and oversee the activities of the Quality Assurance Team, ensuring the implementation of quality standards and specifications across the programs, systems, applications, reports, and other deliverables of the Automated Systems Department. The role is also responsible for overseeing the acceptance of newly developed and modified programs and systems, assessing the technical efficiency of their outputs, and ensuring compliance with the Bank's approved policies, standards, and requirements.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Establish the Quality Assurance Team's objectives and work plans, allocate tasks and priorities, and monitor team members' performance and deliverable quality to ensure that activities are completed in accordance with approved timelines and procedures.
  • Oversee the review of system and application requirements from the early stages, ensuring that they are clear, testable, and supported by well-defined acceptance criteria to verify that business requirements have been met.
  • Supervise the development and execution of testing plans and methodologies for systems, applications, and digital services, ensuring alignment with the nature and requirements of each project and the approved quality standards.
  • Ensure the readiness of testing environments, data, and other prerequisites; oversee test execution and monitor results; and ensure that all necessary requirements and approvals are fulfilled before systems are moved to the production environment.
  • Oversee the management of test cases, scenarios, defects, observations, and testing results, following up on their resolution, retesting, and regression testing where necessary to ensure deliverable quality and the closure of outstanding issues.
  • Develop and update quality assurance and testing methodologies, standards, and procedures, while continuously enhancing testing tools and techniques and promoting test automation to improve the team's efficiency and the overall quality of its deliverables.
  • Monitor the implementation of quality practices throughout the systems and software development lifecycle, ensuring the involvement of the Quality Assurance Team at appropriate stages of analysis, design, and development to identify and address potential risks and issues at an early stage.
  • Monitor the quality, performance, and stability of systems and applications from a quality assurance perspective; analyze testing results and recurring issues; and contribute to identifying root causes and developing appropriate corrective and preventive recommendations and actions.
  • Coordinate with Development, DevOps, Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Project Management teams, and business stakeholders to ensure clarity of requirements, the effectiveness of testing processes, and the timely resolution of defects and issues.
  • Ensure compliance with the policies, procedures, standards, and controls governing quality assurance activities. Follow up on audit, control, documentation, and protection requirements relating to the Bank's software assets, and prepare and submit relevant quality reports and performance indicators.
  • Serve as a primary or alternate member of the Business Continuity Plan and formally designated emergency response teams, carrying out all assigned responsibilities under the plan to minimize the impact of crises and restore operations as quickly as possible.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology or any other relevant field.
  • Minimum of eight (8) years of practical experience in Quality Assurance and systems and software testing.
  • ISTQB certification or another specialized professional certification in software testing and quality assurance is preferred.
  • Good knowledge of test management and defect-tracking tools; familiarity with test automation tools is preferred.
  • Excellent command of the English language.
  • Good knowledge of the policies and procedures governing the work, including their internal and external updates.
  • Strong planning and organizational skills.
  • Effective verbal communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong analytical skills.
  • Supervisory and managerial skills.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Ability to motivate others and work effectively as part of a team.

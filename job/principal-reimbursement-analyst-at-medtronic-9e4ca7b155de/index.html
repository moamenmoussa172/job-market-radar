A Day in the Life

The Pelvic Health (PH) business within Medtronic is focused on expanding patient access to therapies that address overactive bladder (urinary incontinence) and fecal incontinence. Medtronic has been the leader in this space with sacral nerve stimulators and with our newest portfolio in the implantable tibial neurostimulator technology space. The Reimbursement function partners with commercial, clinical, health economics, government affairs, and market access teams to secure reimbursement, funding, and adoption pathways across Europe, supporting the establishment of innovative therapies as standards of care.
The Principal Reimbursement Analyst leads the development and execution of reimbursement and health economic strategies that support market access, funding, and adoption of Pelvic Health therapies across Europe. In Pelvic Health, there is a unique experience in bringing a first-in-kind technology with the Medtronic implantable tibial neurostimulator technology and provide greater value positioning and adoption for sacral nerve stimulation. This role partners with regional and global stakeholders to generate evidence, shape value propositions, support Health Technology Assessment (HTA) submissions, and advance reimbursement pathways for innovative medical technologies. This remote role may be based in the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy, or other appropriate Western European locations. Expected travel is approximately 20% of working time across the region.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Lead reimbursement and market access strategies for Pelvic Health (PH) therapies across European markets.
  • Shape evidence generation plans by providing reimbursement and health economics input into clinical study design, healthcare utilization measures, and patient-reported outcomes.
  • Develop health economic analyses, including cost-effectiveness, cost-utility, cost-of-illness, and budget impact models supporting reimbursement and funding submissions.
  • Create regional value dossiers, value propositions, and economic tools supporting Health Technology Assessment (HTA), Diagnosis-Related Group (DRG), and payer funding requirements.
  • Partner with marketing, clinical, pricing, government affairs, and market access teams to align evidence-generation and reimbursement priorities.
  • Define and communicate clinical and economic evidence to internal and external stakeholders through reimbursement submissions, scientific publications, and educational initiatives.
  • Monitor reimbursement and healthcare policy developments across Europe, identifying market access risks and opportunities that inform business strategy.

Required Qualifications

  • Experience developing and executing reimbursement, market access, health economics, Health Technology Assessment (HTA), or healthcare funding strategies within the medical device or pharmaceutical industry.
  • Experience developing reimbursement submissions, funding applications, or market access strategies for healthcare technologies.
  • Experience conducting health economic analyses, outcomes research, or economic model development.
  • Knowledge of European reimbursement, healthcare funding, and Health Technology Assessment (HTA) systems.
  • Fluency in English.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's degree, Master of Public Health (MPH), Master of Public Policy (MPP), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), or Medical Doctor (MD).
  • Experience supporting medical device and implantable device therapies and/or diligence/submission in reimbursement strategies for new technologies.
  • Additional European language proficiency, peer-reviewed publications, or experience developing multi-country reimbursement strategies within Europe.

About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :

Architecture & Design

  • Design and architect resilient, highly available, and secure cloud solutions across Azure and AWS.
  • Lead system design discussions and adopt cloud-native and hybrid architectures aligned to Cloud Adoption and Well-Architected Frameworks.
  • Define reference architectures for data pipelines, microservices, service mesh, and enterprise workloads.
  • Provide consulting expertise to internal and external stakeholders, aligning cloud strategy with business outcomes.
  • Migration & Modernization
  • Lead workload migration from on-premises to cloud (Azure & AWS), including lift-and-shift, re-platforming, and modernization.
  • Provide end-to-end guidance on cloud transformation strategies tailored for automotive and manufacturing industries.
  • Infrastructure & Platform Engineering
  • Architect and implement containerized workloads using Kubernetes, AKS, EKS, and service mesh frameworks.
  • Apply network design and security best practices for multi-cloud environments.
  • Define and enforce DevOps and GitOps practices for CI/CD, release management, and operations automation.

Technical Leadership

  • Provide hands-on coding and troubleshooting expertise in Terraform, Helm, Python, and TypeScript.
  • Drive adoption of open-source tools and best practices for infrastructure, monitoring, and observability.
  • Mentor and guide engineering teams, fostering collaboration across cross-functional groups to deliver results.

Educational qualification:

  • B.E/ B. Tech / MCA / MSc / M.E

Experience:

  • 6-10 years

Mandatory/requires Skills:

  • Cloud Platforms: Strong expertise in Azure and AWS cloud architecture and services.
  • Operating Systems: Proficiency in Linux and Windows administration, troubleshooting, and automation.
  • Containerization: Expertise in Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, and orchestration platforms.
  • Networking & Security: Strong knowledge of network architecture, firewalls, VPNs, and cloud-native security controls.
  • Automation & Coding: Advanced coding skills in Terraform, Helm, Python, and TypeScript.
  • Architectural Patterns: Proven experience in designing data pipelines, microservices, service mesh, and distributed systems.
  • Migration Experience: Hands-on experience in migrating workloads from on-premises to Azure, AWS and GCP.
  • DevOps/GitOps: Strong understanding and application of DevOps, GitOps, and infrastructure-as-code practices.
  • Open-Source Tools: Practical knowledge in leveraging open-source tools for enterprise-scale solutions.
  • Domain Expertise: Knowledge of automotive and manufacturing processes, systems, and standards.
  • Leadership: Excellent communication, consulting, team building, and stakeholder management skills.

About the job

Cybersecurity Sales & Business - Part time Intern

This role has been designed as 'Hybrid' with a requirement that you will work on average 2 days per week from an HPE office.

Job Description:

Hewlett Packard Enterprise advances the way people live and work. We bring together the brightest minds to create breakthrough technology solutions, helping our customers make their mark on the world.

At HPE we believe in bold moves, the power of “Yes we can”, and being a force for good. Our work thrives on differences, fueling creativity to give our customers all they deserve. But our work is far from over. In fact, we continuously push for better. We empower our team members to make an impact on our business and in the world while fostering a culture that is unconditionally inclusive. Whether you’re looking for an internship or to start your professional career, at HPE you’ll be able to create and collaborate on projects that challenge and inspire you.

Are you interested in technology, cybersecurity, business and AI, and curious about how they come together in the real world?

We are looking for a motivated and curious student to join our SASE & Security Sales team in Madrid. This internship is designed for someone who wants to learn how a global technology company takes cybersecurity solutions to market, works with customers and partners, and turns technology into real business outcomes.

You don't need to be a cybersecurity expert. We are looking for someone who is curious, proactive, comfortable with technology and eager to learn.

You could be studying engineering or another technical degree and be interested in the business side of technology. Or you could be studying business, economics, marketing or a related field and have a strong passion for technology.

Sound like you? Apply today and join one of the most impactful tech companies in the world!

What you'll do

Working alongside experienced cybersecurity sales specialists, you will get hands-on exposure to different parts of the business, including:

  • Sales & business development: Support the team with account research, opportunity preparation, prospecting and sales initiatives.
  • Sales operations: Help us improve sales processes, reporting, data quality and team workflows.
  • AI & automation: Explore how tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Claude and other AI platforms can make our sales organization smarter and more efficient.
  • Cybersecurity: Learn about areas such as secure networking, Zero Trust, SASE, network access control and firewalls, and understand how companies use these technologies to address business and security challenges.
  • Customer & partner initiatives: Help prepare customer meetings, presentations, campaigns and follow-up activities.
  • Marketing & go-to-market: Collaborate on campaigns, events, communications and initiatives that help us reach customers and generate new opportunities.
  • Research & insights: Research customers, industries, competitors and market trends to help the team identify new opportunities.
  • Problem solving: Work on real business challenges where we will encourage you to bring new ideas, experiment and find better ways of doing things.

Who we're looking for

We're less interested in finding someone who already knows everything and more interested in finding someone with the right mindset and potential.

You might be a great fit if you are:

  • Currently enrolled full-time in a Bachelor’s program in engineering, computer science, telecommunications or another technical degree and interested in business and sales; or
  • Studying business, economics, marketing, management or a related degree and passionate about technology.
  • Curious about cybersecurity and emerging technologies.
  • A natural problem solver who enjoys figuring out how things work and how they can be improved.
  • Proactive and comfortable taking ownership of small projects.
  • Comfortable learning and experimenting with AI tools. Experience with Copilot, Claude or similar tools is a plus.
  • Analytical and comfortable working with data, presentations and business tools.
  • A strong communicator and team player.
  • Comfortable working in an international and multicultural environment.
  • Fluent or highly proficient in Spanish and English.

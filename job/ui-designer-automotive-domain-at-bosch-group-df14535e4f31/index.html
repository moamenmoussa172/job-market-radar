About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
JOB DESCRIPTION:

The UI Designer will work hand in hand with a dedicated User Researcher, translating validated user needs and business requirements into intuitive, scalable, and high-quality user interfaces. The collaboration spans the entire design process from research and ideation to implementation and quality assurance.

MANDATORY SKILLSET:

Automotive or Manufacturing domain experience is mandatory.

General Skills:
-> Collaborative working style with the ability to work effectively in distributed international teams
-> Strong ability to work independently and asynchronously
-> Proactive communication and willingness to raise questions or concerns when requirements are unclear
->Experience collaborating closely with development teams, including design handovers and implementation reviews
-> Strategic mindset with the ability to provide and receive constructive feedback
-> Ability to identify patterns across complex applications and translate them into scalable solutions
-> Experience designing modular applications that can be extended and maintained over time
-> Excellent English communication skills (written and spoken); German is considered a plus
-> Flexible and adaptable mindset in a fast-paced environment with changing priorities
UI/UX Design Skills:
-> Deep understanding of UI and UX principles, patterns, and best practices
-> Ability to align new concepts with existing design patterns and user journeys
-> Creation of pixel-perfect user interfaces with high attention to detail
-> Ability to apply and maintain a consistent, brand-compliant design language
-> Strong prototyping, documentation, and design specification skills
-> User-centered conceptualization based on business and user requirements
-> Experience designing complex enterprise (B2B) applications is highly desirable
Tooling & Design Operations:
-> Figma (Must Have)
-> Expert-level proficiency in Figma
Strong experience with:
-> Auto Layout
->Components
-> Variants
->Shared Libraries
-> Variables & Styles
-> Layout Grids
-> Naming Conventions
-> Creation of clean, reusable, and scalable component structures
-> Consistent use of Auto Layout is mandatory
-> Experience using AI-supported design tools such as Figma Make
-> Ability to work from existing screen descriptions, design systems, and application guidelines
Additional Tools:
-> Openness to learning and using new tools (e.g., Miro, Draw.io, Claude, AI-supported design tools)
-> Experience with Jira, Confluence, or comparable collaboration and project management platforms
Design System Experience:
-> Strong understanding of design systems as a foundation for scalable product development
-> Experience working with shared design libraries and coded web components
-> Proven experience ensuring consistency across multiple products and teams
Agile Working Methods:
-> Experience working in agile product would be a plus.
To better understand your design approach and working style, we expect candidates to provide:
-> Examples of previous projects (preferably enterprise or B2B applications)
-> Insights into their Figma file organization and component structure
-> A walkthrough of their design process from concept to delivery
Experience Required - 5 to 9 years

Join us as a Fraud Analyst to detect and prevent fraud and protect Qonto’s French market customers from scams. You’ll join our specialised Fraud team of about 7 people, where your work protects customers and Qonto from fraud-related risks and supports compliance as we grow. You’ll report to Anamaria Codorean-Termure, our Senior Team Lead Fraud.

➡️ What you’ll do

  • Monitor transactions: You’ll analyse transactions to detect fraud patterns, focusing on scams, phishing and account takeover.
  • Investigate and prevent: You’ll independently investigate complex fraud cases and put preventive measures in place across financing and payment products.
  • Assess risk early: You’ll run risk assessments on new products and features and keep onboarding fraud prevention robust.
  • Own projects: You’ll own medium-scale projects end to end, in line with the objectives set by your Lead.
  • Strengthen detection with Risk and Security: You’ll work with both teams to improve fraud detection and align strategies.

➡️ What we’re looking for

  • Fraud or AML experience: You’ve independently handled complex fraud or AML cases at senior level.
  • Investigative mindset: You use evidence-based and creative thinking to detect patterns and solve complex cases.
  • Clear communication: You’re confident talking with customers and counterparties, including in sensitive situations.
  • Languages: You’re fluent in English and French, and Portuguese is a plus.
  • AI-native learning mindset: You welcome feedback and use AI tools to keep sharpening how you work.

➡️ What we can offer you

  • An established, specialised team: You’ll join a Fraud team with defined levels (L1–L3), documented processes and real operating rhythms.
  • Real ownership: You own your caseload and your medium-scale projects.
  • Breadth of casework: You’ll handle the full range of fraud, from private scams to social engineering and financing-product fraud.
  • A say in how we scale: You’ll help shape our detection processes and tools as the team grows.

➡️ Your future manager

You’ll report to Anamaria Codorean-Termure, our Senior Team Lead Fraud. Anamaria has spent nearly 7 years at Qonto, growing from AML-CFT Analyst to leading the Fraud team across France, Spain and Portugal. She values agility, collaboration and a proactive approach to emerging threats, and she’ll support your ramp-up closely while trusting you to own your cases.

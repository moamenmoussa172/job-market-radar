About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Lead the design, development and delivery of ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management (HAM) solutions covering the physical hardware asset lifecycle from request and procurement handoff through receiving, deployment, transfer, maintenance, retirement and disposal. Own technical delivery, reusable automation, asset-to-CI integration, code quality and production reliability while guiding developers and collaborating with HAM stakeholders and architects.

Key Responsibilities

HAM Solution Development & Delivery

· Lifecycle Engineering: Lead implementation of hardware asset workflows for request, receiving, assignment, transfer, refresh, return, retirement and disposal.

· Stockrooms & Fulfilment: Develop stockroom, inventory, transfer and asset audit processes; automate service catalog fulfilment and approvals using Workflow Studio/Flow Designer.

· Technical Delivery: Translate requirements into technical designs and user stories, estimate work, coordinate development and own technical acceptance of delivered features.

Integrations, CMDB & Platform Engineering

· Asset–CI Data Integrity: Build reliable asset-to-CI relationships, Discovery data synchronization, lifecycle validation and data-quality controls with CMDB stakeholders.

· Enterprise Integrations: Lead procurement, inventory, endpoint management and Discovery integrations using REST APIs, IntegrationHub, import sets and transform maps.

· ServiceNow Engineering: Develop and review Business Rules, Script Includes, Glide APIs, ACLs and reusable automation using secure, upgrade-safe practices.

Quality, Production & Team Leadership

· Engineering Quality: Own peer reviews, automated/functional testing including ATF, defect prevention, deployment readiness and controlled releases.

· Production Ownership: Troubleshoot complex L2/L3 issues, drive root-cause analysis and improve logging, documentation, monitoring and sustainable fixes.

· Developer Enablement: Mentor developers, establish coding practices and coordinate delivery dependencies with architects, product owners and operations teams.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications

· Experience: 13–16 years in software/platform engineering, including extensive hands-on ServiceNow development and technical delivery leadership.

· HAM Specialization: Proven experience implementing ServiceNow HAM, stockrooms, inventory controls and end-to-end hardware asset lifecycle workflows.

· Platform Development: Strong JavaScript, Glide APIs, Business Rules, Script Includes, ACLs and Workflow Studio/Flow Designer expertise.

· CMDB & Integrations: Strong asset-to-CI relationships, Discovery interfaces, REST/JSON APIs, Integration Hub, import sets, transform maps and data quality.

· Engineering Leadership: Experience with solution design, effort estimation, peer reviews, mentoring, ATF, version control, releases and L2/L3 support.

Additional / Preferred Experience

· HAM Process Optimization: Experience improving stockroom operations, asset governance, auditability and procurement-to-fulfilment workflows.

· Adjacent ServiceNow Capabilities: Familiarity with Service Catalog, ITSM, ITOM Discovery, CMDB/CSDM and Service Graph data sources.

· Automation & DevOps: Exposure to Python/PowerShell, CI/CD, monitoring and test automation in ServiceNow delivery.

Certifications

· Mandatory: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) – Data Foundations (CMDB & CSDM).

· Desirable: ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Hardware Asset Management (CIS-HAM).

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Albania, Austria, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark and 12 more countries.

The AI orchestration of your wildest imagination.

n8n is the open workflow orchestration platform built for the new era of AI. We give technical teams the freedom of code with the speed of no-code, so they can automate faster, smarter, and without limits. Backed by a fiercely inventive community and 500+ builder-approved integrations, we’re changing the way people bring systems together and scale ideas for impact.

Since our founding in 2019, we’ve grown into a diverse team of over 260 - working across Europe and the US, connected by a shared builder spirit and with our centre of gravity in Berlin. Along the way, we’ve:

  • Cultivated a community of more than 650,000 active developers and builders
  • Earned 190K+ GitHub stars, making us one of the world’s Top 40 most popular projects
  • Backed by top investors, from Sequoia’s first German seed to our SAP's recent strategic investment - bringing us to a $5.2bn valuation

That’s the company we’ve built. Now we’d love to see what you can build. If you’re applying, try n8n out - whether you’re technical or not - and share a screenshot of your first workflow with us. The easiest place to start is here: app.n8n.cloud/register.

We’re in a defining moment of an incredible journey. Come and build with us.

Your main goal will be to lead n8n's AI Experience group: the teams that shape how people build agents and workflows with AI, how they come to trust what they've automated, and how n8n grows into a self-healing, self-improving platform.

You'll lead the PMs behind the AI Assistant, Agents and AI Trust across four areas:

Lead and grow a team of PMs

  • Lead the PMs of the AI Experience group (today AI Assistant, Agents and AI Trust), owning hiring, performance and career growth.
  • Build PMs who make strong decisions on their own, so the group doesn't depend on you or other leaders to decide.
  • Raise the bar for product craft across the group as the team and its scope grow.

Set the direction for AI Experience

  • Set a clear strategy across three themes: building great agents and workflows with AI, helping users trust their automations, and evolving n8n into a self-healing, self-improving platform.
  • Make considered bets, decide what not to do, and keep the roadmap ambitious but achievable with Engineering and Design leadership.
  • Give your teams clear missions that fit the bigger picture of n8n, and stay close to the other product groups (Capabilities, Enterprise and later Platform) as priorities shift.

Stay hands-on

  • Use the product every day, talk to the people building with it, and form your own view.
  • Co-shape work with your PMs: probe their thinking, challenge assumptions and course-correct where needed.
  • Partner with Engineering, Design and Data so the group delivers its outcomes consistently.

Improve how n8n builds products

  • Shape how PMs, designers and engineers work together in an AI-native org where engineers take on more decisions.
  • Improve product and engineering ways of working across n8n, starting with your own group.
  • Bring in go-to-market, Security, Legal, Finance, Developer Relations and model providers where decisions cross over.

REQUIREMENTS

Must-haves

  • PM leadership: You've managed at least two PMs and stayed hands-on with their work, and you can point to people who got better under you.
  • Product craft: You have sharp product sense, think in systems and can turn a vague problem into something concrete.
  • AI fluency: You use AI heavily in your own work, have built something with agents (a prototype counts) and know evals and harnesses well enough to probe your PMs on them.
  • Group-level strategy: You've set direction for a product group spanning several products and teams.
  • Process mindset: You improve how product and engineering work together, and you're comfortable with engineers owning more decisions.
  • Influence without authority: You align Engineering, Design and peer groups through clarity and trust.

Nice-to-haves

  • AI products in production: You've shipped AI or agent products to real users, ideally with evals running in production.
  • Technical users: You've built for people who write code or workflows, or worked in an open-source ecosystem.
  • Startup pace: You've worked in a fast-growing, remote-first startup and enjoy the entrepreneurial side of it.
  • Org change: You've led a product team through scaling or restructuring.
  • Technical skills: You can read code, query data with SQL or prototype with agents.

Why join us?

You'll lead our AI Experience group as its first dedicated GPM, and build it from here: the PMs, the strategy and how the teams work. We're hiring you to make the calls, so most product decisions in the group will sit with you.

The problems are hard and mostly unsolved. Helping people build agents they can rely on, getting them to trust automations that run without anyone watching, and turning n8n into a platform that spots and fixes its own failures are all open questions. You'll get fast, honest feedback on every bet from a fair-code community of 115k+ members and 207k+ GitHub stars.

You'll also help work out what product leadership looks like when engineers take on more of the decisions. That's still taking shape at n8n, and you'll have a real hand in it.

We're growing fast. We hit $100M ARR in 2026, and SAP's strategic investment in May valued n8n at $5.2B, more than double our Series C valuation seven months earlier. We're aiming to build a trillion-dollar company, and AI Experience is central to getting there.

We're remote-first and async by default, and you'll work with people who care a lot about what they build and are easy to work with.

If you want to lead the AI core of n8n, we'd like to hear from you!

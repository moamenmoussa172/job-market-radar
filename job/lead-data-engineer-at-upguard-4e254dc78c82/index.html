About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Australia.

Who are we?

At UpGuard, we are replacing manual security bottlenecks with AI-driven precision. Fresh off a US$75M Series C, we are scaling our infrastructure to process around 10 billion risk signals daily. This isn’t just growth; it’s a total reimagining of how the world manages cyber risk.

We build the Risk Operations Center, the AI platform where lean security teams run cyber risk end to end. It combines security ratings, threat intelligence and AI agents to give teams one view of risk across their vendors, attack surface and workforce.

We aren’t just building another tool; we’re defining a category. We provide the autonomy to ship world-class technology and the resources to do it at a global scale.

Where does this role fit in?

UpGuard has recently replatformed its analytics stack onto a greenfield foundation built on BigQuery, dbt, infrastructure as code in OpenTofu, CI/CD through GitHub, and a governed semantic layer that both people and AI agents query. We are seeking an experienced data engineer to help take that platform to the next level.

Reporting to the Director of Data Engineering, this role is the strong technical lead within the data engineering team. You will work under the Director's guidance to evolve the platform and raise its engineering standards, and you will set the example for how the team builds, reviews and ships.

The platform has a broad contributor and consumer base. Analysts, data scientists and business teams build on it, query it and increasingly reach it through AI agents. Keeping those stakeholders well served and satisfied is a central measure of success in this role.

What will you do?

  • Technical Leadership: Act as the senior technical voice within the data engineering team by guiding design decisions, reviewing code, mentoring engineers and contributing to the platform roadmap set by the team.
  • Platform Evolution: Help take UpGuard's recently built GCP data platform to its next stage, proposing and delivering improvements to scale, reliability, performance, cost and AI workflows.
  • Engineering Standards & CI/CD: Raise engineering standards across the platform, and build and maintain the CI/CD pipelines, PR checks, BI model validation and developer environments that keep changes safe to ship.
  • Stakeholder Engagement & Satisfaction: Treat the analysts, data scientists, and business teams who depend on the platform as customers. Understand their needs, scope and prioritise requests with them, set clear expectations, follow through on commitments, and track how well the platform serves them.
  • Contributor Enablement: Support the growing group of analysts, data scientists and business users who contribute dbt code, through onboarding, documentation, AI agents, review practices and developer tooling that make good contributions the easy path.
  • Data Integration: Design, build and maintain reliable pipelines that bring data from internal systems (CRM, billing, support, marketing automation and product events) and third-party sources into BigQuery using managed connectors, event streams and vendor APIs, and write data back to operational systems where needed.
  • Platform Operations & Maintenance: Manage infrastructure as code in OpenTofu, run containerised workloads on Docker and Kubernetes, maintain build scheduling and deployment pipelines, and keep the platform monitored, patched and well documented.
  • dbt Development: Author and review dbt models across the staging, source-level and business-level layers, and uphold the team's conventions for modelling, grain testing and documentation.
  • Data Quality: Implement and maintain data tests, source freshness checks, observability and alerting, and take part in incident response so that problems are caught and fixed before stakeholders find them.
  • Data Governance & Privacy: Implement access controls as code, process access requests, and apply data classification, PII masking, audit logging and data subject request handling in line with the team's governance policies.
  • AI Enablement & Conversational Analytics: Build and operate the data, semantic layer and tooling that power conversational analytics and AI agents, including MCP access, context and evaluation that let LLMs query structured and unstructured business data accurately and safely.

What will you bring?

  • Proven Experience: 5+ years in data engineering, including significant hands-on work on a production cloud data platform.
  • Technical Leadership: Experience as a tech lead or senior engineer guiding the work of others through design review, code review and mentoring.
  • AI Fluency: Fluency using AI/LLM tools to accelerate development, review and documentation.
  • Stakeholder & Consulting Skills: A track record of engaging stakeholders, scoping ambiguous requests, managing expectations and communicating clearly with technical and non-technical audiences.
  • dbt Expertise: Very strong, hands-on dbt development covering modular modelling, testing, macros, packages, snapshots, documentation and performance tuning at scale. Experience with a semantic layer such as MetricFlow, Omni’s YAML or LookML is valued.
  • Infrastructure as Code: Experience managing cloud infrastructure with OpenTofu, CDK or Terraform.
  • Containers & Orchestration: Confidence running workloads on Docker and Kubernetes, ideally GKE.
  • Experience with Dimensional Data Modeling techniques and complexities
  • Data Integration: Experience with managed ingestion tools such as Fivetran or Airbyte, event pipelines such as Segment or Pub/Sub, and REST APIs.
  • Data Quality & Governance: Practical experience implementing data testing, observability tooling such as Elementary, access controls and data masking.
  • BigQuery: Deep working knowledge of BigQuery, including partitioning, clustering, cost and quota management, IAM and complex SQL.
  • Proven ability to implement PII detection, masking, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated data lifecycle policies in modern cloud data platforms.

What will give you an edge?

  • Conversational Analytics & AI Management: Experience building or operating conversational analytics, MCP servers, retrieval or tool-calling layers for LLM agents, or evaluation frameworks that measure the accuracy of AI answers on business data.
  • B2B SaaS Experience: Experience working within a software organisation, ideally a B2B SaaS business, and familiarity with metrics such as ARR, NRR/GRR, pipeline and churn.
  • CRM Systems: A firm understanding of CRM data models such as HubSpot or Salesforce, including deals, pipelines, lifecycle stages and property history, and how that data flows into analytics.
  • Modern BI: Familiarity with Looker, Omni or a comparable modern BI tool, and an understanding of how semantic models and BI layers consume warehouse data.
  • Data governance experience in heavily regulated industries or access control around highly sensitive data
  • Hands-on experience designing data architectures compliant with GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, or similar global privacy regulations (e.g., CCPA/CPRA).

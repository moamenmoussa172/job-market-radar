About the job

At Bosch Home Comfort, we are creating sustainable heating, cooling, and ventilation solutions that enable the energy transition across Europe and beyond.

As Application Engineer (m/f/d) within the EMEA International Key Account Management team, you will be the technical expert supporting multinational customers and strategic projects across Europe. Working closely with International Key Account Managers, country organizations, product management, and engineering teams, you will develop innovative HVAC solutions that combine customer value, technical excellence, and commercial success.

You will operate at the intersection of engineering, system design, and business development, helping Bosch Home Comfort strengthen its position with key international customers.

Your contribution to something big

As an Application Engineer within the EMEA IKAM organization, you will:

  • Analyze customer requirements, specifications, tender documents, and project designs to develop optimized HVAC solutions.
  • Select and configure Bosch Home Comfort products and systems for international projects.
  • Support International Key Account Managers during customer discussions, tenders, and strategic account development activities.
  • Develop technical concepts, system recommendations, and application designs that meet customer and project requirements.
  • Evaluate HVAC system designs and ensure proper integration of Bosch solutions into overall project concepts.
  • Provide technical expertise regarding installation, commissioning, serviceability, and lifecycle performance.
  • Act as technical focal point for multinational key accounts and cross-border project opportunities.
  • Coordinate with local application engineers, specification teams, product management, factories, and service organizations across Europe.
  • Identify alternative technical approaches and innovative solutions when customer requirements exceed standard portfolio capabilities.
  • Contribute to best-practice sharing and technical knowledge transfer across the EMEA region.
  • Support profitable growth through technical leadership, customer consulting, and solution-oriented collaboration.

What makes this role impactful

Your expertise directly contributes to:

  • Winning strategic international HVAC projects.
  • Increasing specification success and project conversion rates.
  • Strengthening relationships with multinational customers.
  • Ensuring technically robust and commercially competitive solutions.
  • Supporting the successful implementation of complex cross-country projects.
  • Accelerating Bosch Home Comfort's International Key Account strategy.

What distinguishes you

Education

  • University degree in Mechanical Engineering, HVAC Engineering, Building Services Engineering, Energy Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a comparable technical discipline.

Experience

  • Several years of professional experience in application engineering, HVAC system design, consulting engineering, technical sales support, or a comparable role.
  • Experience with commercial HVAC applications and project business.
  • Exposure to specification-driven sales processes and international projects is an advantage.

Technical competencies

You bring strong knowledge of:

  • Commercial HVAC systems and applications.
  • Heating, cooling, ventilation, and air-conditioning technologies.
  • System design principles and HVAC engineering fundamentals.
  • Product selection, sizing, and application engineering tools.
  • Technical specifications, standards, and industry regulations.
  • System integration and controls concepts.
  • Bosch Home Comfort products, or the ability to rapidly build product expertise.

Personal competencies

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to translate complex technical requirements into practical customer solutions.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Customer-oriented and consultative mindset.
  • Ability to influence technical decisions and build trust with customers and colleagues.
  • Strong collaboration skills within international and cross-functional teams.
  • Fluent in English

Working internationally

As part of the EMEA International Key Account Management team, you will work with colleagues, customers, consultants, and partners across multiple countries and cultures.

Success in this role requires:

  • Intercultural awareness and global collaboration skills.
  • Willingness to work in an international matrix organization with regular travels.
  • Ability to coordinate technical stakeholders across national organizations.
  • Flexibility to support multinational customer opportunities across EMEA.

Location

Position can be based in France or Spain.

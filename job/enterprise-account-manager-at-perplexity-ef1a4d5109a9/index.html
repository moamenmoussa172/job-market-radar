About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Perplexity is seeking a highly motivated Account Manager to grow our largest Enterprise customers. You'll own a focused book of strategic accounts that already use Perplexity Enterprise, and you'll be responsible for expanding them by landing new teams, business units and use cases, growing seats and bringing in new products. This role is ideal for commercially minded sellers who love going deep in a small number of complex accounts, build trusted executive relationships and want to help shape how the world's largest organizations adopt AI.

Responsibilities

  • Own and grow a book of Perplexity's largest Enterprise customers
  • Build multi-year account plans that map org structure, budget owners, priorities and white space across each customer
  • Drive expansion by landing new departments, business units and regions, growing seats and cross-selling new products
  • Build and nurture senior-level relationships with executives, economic buyers and champions, and multi-thread across each account
  • Lead the full expansion cycle, from discovery and business case through security review, procurement, negotiation and close
  • Own renewals and protect the base
  • Partner closely with Customer Success to drive adoption and quantify the value delivered, and turn usage into expansion
  • Work with Product, Marketing and Solutions to bring customer feedback back to the team and shape the roadmap
  • Run executive business reviews that tie Perplexity to each customer's strategic priorities
  • Meet or exceed expansion and retention targets on a quarterly and annual basis
  • Keep accurate account, pipeline and forecast records in Salesforce

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of account management or full-cycle SaaS sales experience with large enterprise customers
  • Proven record of meeting or exceeding expansion or revenue targets in a high-growth environment
  • Experience growing strategic accounts from an initial land into large, multi-department deployments
  • Skilled at complex, multi-stakeholder deals and navigating procurement, legal and security reviews at large organizations
  • Comfortable building business cases and presenting to C-suite executives
  • Strong account planning, forecasting and pipeline discipline
  • Ability to collaborate across Customer Success, Product and technical stakeholders
  • Experience with CRM systems (e.g. Salesforce)
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience

Nice to have

  • Experience selling AI, search, knowledge management or productivity software
  • Experience growing usage-based or product-led accounts upmarket
  • Experience at an early-stage or high-growth company building the account management motion from the ground up

Warehouse Materials Coordinator

Bosch Group · Summerville, United States

Onsite · Other tech role · Posted 26 October 2026

SAP S/4HANA

About the job

Strong customer service oriented professional that is responsible for developing and maintaining excellent customer relationships with both internal and external customers. Possess flexibility within a team environment or individually with minimal supervision in a fast paced culture.
• Professional etiquette dealing both in face-to-face communication as well as via telephone and email
• Support launch of customer delivery creation at warehouse
• Communication with both internal and external customers
• Heavy telephone and email communication to respond to customer inquiries in a timely manner and update when deviations occur
• Daily customer order pick creation
• Entry level material planning
• Drive to seek resolution to variances by going to the source
• Transportation scheduling for both daily and expedite services utilizing customer websites and freight forwarding websites
• Office duties: filing, faxing, scanning
• Maintain and abide by records retention procedures per Central Directives
• Knowledge of what an escalation processes is and reasons
• Ability to problem solve utilizing the 5 Why's or 8D formats
• A team player that can effectively back up others as needed
• Other duties as assigned

• High School Diploma or related discipline preferred
• 3 - 5 years of experience in a warehouse environment required
• Excellent computer skills and working familiarity with multiple software packages, preferably MS Office and SAP ERP software packages
• Must have strong verbal and written communication skills, professional telephone etiquette, and the ability to handle stressful situations in an efficient, professional manner
• Must have a sense of team response to satisfy sales goals and assure 100% customer satisfaction

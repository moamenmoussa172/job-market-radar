About the job

Multilingual Payment Coordinator – Clinical Research Finance

About the Role

We are seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented professional to join our team as a Multilingual Payment Coordinator. This role focuses on supporting investigator and site payment activities, ensuring timely and accurate financial coordination.

This opportunity is ideal for candidates with strong language skills, a passion for finance and administration, and an interest in supporting the operational side of clinical research.

What We're Looking For

Languages

Fluency in English and one or more of the following languages is required:

  • Dutch
  • Spanish
  • Portuguese

Additional European language skills would be considered an advantage.

Skills & Experience

  • Strong interest in finance, payment operations, or financial administration.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • High attention to detail and commitment to accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Ability to work effectively within international and cross-functional teams.

Ideal Candidate

The successful candidate will be a multilingual professional who enjoys working with financial processes, data, and stakeholder communication. You are naturally detail-oriented, enjoy solving problems, and have a genuine interest in contributing to the operational and financial aspects of clinical research while supporting global healthcare innovation.

IQVIA is a leading global provider of clinical research services, commercial insights and healthcare intelligence to the life sciences and healthcare industries. We create intelligent connections to accelerate the development and commercialization of innovative medical treatments to help improve patient outcomes and population health worldwide. Learn more at https://jobs.iqvia.com

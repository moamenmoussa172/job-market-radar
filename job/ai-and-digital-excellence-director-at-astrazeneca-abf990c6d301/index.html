About the job

AI & Digital Excellence Director

ABOUT ASTRAZENECA

AstraZeneca is a global, science-led, patient-focused biopharmaceutical company that focuses on the discovery, development, and commercialisation of prescription medicines for some of the world’s most serious diseases. But we’re more than one of the world’s leading pharmaceutical companies.

Join a high-performing team, empowered to cut through the noise and drive real change. Our agility and pace, coupled with scientific focus, powers us to spot opportunities in the market that keep us moving forward and into new spaces.


SITE DESCRIPTION

AstraZeneca is experiencing a significant transformation in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, driven by a commitment to advancing healthcare, innovation, and sustainable growth. Building on our global legacy, AZ KSA is rapidly evolving its operations to better serve patients, collaborate with healthcare stakeholders, and contribute to the ambitious goals outlined in the Saudi Vision 2030. We are investing in talent, digital solutions, and new ways of working; all designed to enhance our impact on patient outcomes and reinforce our leadership in therapeutic areas. This dynamic environment calls for visionary, agile professionals who thrive in change and are motivated to help shape the future of healthcare in KSA.

ROLE SUMMARY

Leads AstraZeneca Saudi Arabia's transformation into a more customer-centric, data-driven and AI-enabled organization. Provides strategic direction and integration across Customer Engagement, Business Excellence, Launch Excellence, and Data, Insights & Analytics, ensuring digital capabilities, insights and new ways of working translate into measurable customer and business impact.

What you’ll do

  • Set the market AI and digital excellence strategy, priorities and governance, aligned with country business objectives and enterprise direction.
  • Build organizational AI literacy, data fluency and digital ways of working while ensuring responsible and compliant adoption.
  • Lead, develop and integrate the Customer Engagement, Business Excellence, and Data, Insights & Analytics capabilities as a connected ecosystem.
  • Partner across Commercial & medical functions to embed transformation into strategy and execution.
  • Partner with Customer Engagement to lead the evolution of function evolution and go-to-market model, strengthening omnichannel orchestration, personalization and connected customer experiences, with customer journeys, channel strategies, CRM-enabled engagement, campaign orchestration and next-best-action capabilities informed by strong data and analytics.
  • Ensure Digital Solutions translates customer and business priorities into scalable, compliant and measurable digital experiences and solutions.
  • Provide strategic direction for Business Excellence, including segmentation and targeting, sales-force effectiveness, performance management, incentives, field productivity and resource optimization.
  • Champion launch and execution excellence across therapy areas, ensuring disciplined cross-functional readiness and measurable performance.
  • Build Data, Insights & Analytics as a strategic business capability, advancing predictive, actionable and AI-enabled decision-making.
  • Identify and prioritize high-value AI, automation and process-transformation opportunities and drive adoption, scale and measurable value realization.

Essential for the role

AstraZeneca embraces diversity and equality of opportunity. We are committed to building an inclusive and diverse team representing all backgrounds, with as wide a range of perspectives as possible, and harnessing industry-leading skills. We believe that the more inclusive we are, the better our work will be.

Essential Capabilities:

  • 10+ years of relevant experience within pharmaceuticals, biotech, healthcare or a related industry, including significant leadership experience.
  • Strong understanding of the end-to-end commercial model and customer engagement landscape.
  • Demonstrated leadership across Commercial Excellence, Customer Engagement, Digital, Data & Analytics, AI and/or Business Transformation, with a proven track record of leading cross-functional transformation, capability building and organisational change that delivers measurable business impact.
  • Strong strategic, commercial and analytical capabilities with a data-driven approach to decision-making.
  • Strong people leadership and ability to influence senior stakeholders within a complex matrix organisation.

Desirable Capabilities:

  • 5+ years of experience as Country or regional leadership team experience.

  • 8+ years of experience as Leadership of multiple specialist capabilities or teams.

  • Experience in launch excellence, omnichannel transformation, predictive analytics or AI-enabled customer engagement.

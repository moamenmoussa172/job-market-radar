About the job

About the role

We are hiring Senior Frontend Engineers for HelloFresh's consumer products. The core work is building customer-facing TypeScript and React applications, mostly React Native apps for iOS and Android, with some Next.js web work.

You will own features end to end, from problem definition to production, and measure whether they work. You will work in autonomous, cross-functional teams with a high degree of ownership.

What you will do

  • Build and evolve customer-facing apps in React Native (iOS and Android) and React / Next.js web, in TypeScript.
  • Own features end to end: RFC, delivery, feature-flagged rollout and measurement.
  • Shape frontend architecture: navigation, state management, module boundaries and platform scalability. Lead medium to large initiatives.
  • Integrate LLM and GenAI features into customer experiences, designing for latency, cost, non-determinism and graceful degradation.
  • Consume and help shape GraphQL (Apollo Federation) and REST APIs, working with backend engineers on schemas and contracts.
  • Own reliability and observability: crash reporting, performance monitoring, tracing, alerts and SLOs. Take part in incident response and post-mortems.
  • Run experiments: define hypotheses, success metrics and tracking events, and support A/B tests and gradual rollouts.
  • Raise the bar through code reviews, testing (unit, integration, E2E), mentoring and knowledge sharing. Champion AI-assisted engineering workflows.
  • Work with product, design, backend, AI and web engineers to deliver seamless experiences.

What you bring

  • Strong React and TypeScript: production-grade frontend apps used by many people, with architecture and performance work behind them. Experience with React Native is ideal; strong React web engineers who want to move into React Native are welcome.
  • Senior ownership: several years of frontend engineering (7+ years is the target for the most senior profile). You take loosely defined goals and turn them into a plan, and can name decisions you personally drove, the alternatives and the trade-offs.
  • Product mindset: you start from the customer and business outcome, not from the ticket.
  • Production and observability: you monitor what you ship with dashboards, alerts and tracing, and you check whether a fix worked.
  • Measurement: you can design an A/B test, define success metrics and tell whether a change caused an improvement.
  • Architecture and state management: state management, navigation, modularisation and offline support, plus performance profiling on mobile or web.
  • AI-native workflow: daily use of AI coding tools (such as Claude Code, Cursor or Copilot) beyond autocomplete. You plan, give context, validate output and know when not to use AI.
  • Quality ownership: you test your own work (unit, integration, E2E), review code and do pre-release checks. There is no separate QA team.
  • Communication and influence: you explain technical decisions and their business reason clearly, and you can challenge other teams constructively.

We also value backend skills (Node.js, GraphQL, REST, serverless) and product or design judgment. If you do not tick every box but think you would thrive here, we want to hear from you.

Nice to have

  • Growth, conversion, monetisation, acquisition or retention work, with hypotheses and measured outcomes.
  • Native iOS (Swift) or Android (Kotlin) experience, or React Native native module integration.
  • Personalisation, recommendation or search systems, or LLM integration in production.
  • Experience with multi-brand or multi-market platforms, feature flags and A/B testing tools (such as Optimizely or Statsig).
  • Next.js and Vercel, edge caching, or CMS-driven frontend platforms.
  • Experience with high-traffic consumer, e-commerce or subscription products.
  • Work on a frontend platform or engineering standards that other teams adopted.

Teams

  • Teams: Engagement (app engagement), Shopping AI & Search (AI assistant and search), Conversions (web and experimentation) and The Pets Table (web, New Ventures)
  • Location: Warsaw

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at a global scale, supporting 6+ million customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to the workspace at Warsaw Centre Point. The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

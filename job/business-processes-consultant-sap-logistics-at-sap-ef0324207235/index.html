About the job

We help the world run better

YOUR FUTURE ROLE
As Senior Support Engineer (f/m/d) your tasks are:

  • Support our customers on their transformation to SAP S/4HANA / Intelligent Enterprise
  • Delivery of remote and onsite services together with our experts for global customers with focus on system optimization, conceptual planning as well as empowering the customers with newest best practice knowledge.
  • Analysis of highly complex business processes including root cause analysis and optimization in heterogenous system landscapes with focus on the automotive processes.
  • Continuous development of your functional and technical skills in S/4HANA, solutions of Industry Cloud Automotive and BTP.
  • Being a team player, building up strong networks within SAP and working collaboratively and passionately with others (this is one of the key cornerstones for our success).
  • Being a team lead for service delivery teams and escalation teams.
  • Mentoring and training the Early Talents in our team.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Expert skills in applications within SAP S/4HANA:
  • SAP Materials Management
  • SAP Sales and Distribution
  • SAP Logistics Execution
  • Available-to-Promise, Advanced Available-to-Promise
  • Minimum of 2-4 years of functional experience in Support or Consulting in the expertise areas mentioned above. Good technical skills make you a perfect candidate.
  • Practical experience in managing customers, SAP implementation projects, service deliveries and/or project management.
  • Good communication skills with a convincing and friendly appearance.
  • High-quality service mindset and the capability to perform well under pressure in a dynamic environment.
  • Generic programming Skills in ABAP is a plus.
  • Fluent English is a must, German is a plus.

ABOUT THE TEAM
Customer Success drives software adoption, ensuring customers implement and run what they buy whilst safeguarding maintenance and subscription. Using our unparalleled expertise, we guide our customers throughout the entire lifecycle to make the benefits of digital transformation a reality, end to end.
The strength of our Centers of Expertise (CoE), as part of Customer Success, is to analyze customer solutions with 360° view with service delivery teams combining experts for all relevant software components including application, database and technology, as well as engaging a strong back office support.
As member of the CoE Supply Chaing Management Team 2 in Budapest, Hungary you will work with SAP’s premium customers. Our team primarily focuses on and planning the digital transformation, assessing SAP solutions incl. functional and technical design, performance critical processes, assessment of implementation and upgrades, monitoring during testing and GoLive. You are a part of a highly motivated and diverse team with colleagues from 10 nations.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Caseware combines over 35 years of industry expertise with AI-powered technology to transform how audit and accounting professionals work. Serving organizations across 130 countries, we bring intelligent automation directly into audit and financial reporting workflows—helping professionals reduce manual work, navigate complexity, and focus on informed decisions. Join our global team and help build technology that strengthens trust in financial information.

Join us to build the internal platform that powers fast, safe, and scalable delivery across a large Nx monorepo with 1,000+ projects.

You will shape the developer experience for teams shipping TypeScript, Angular, NestJS, Java services, serverless workloads, and AWS cloud infrastructure.

This role is for engineers who love monorepos, automation, architecture, and solving real platform complexity at scale.

❗ This is a full-time permanent position

❗ This is an existing vacancy

📍 Location:

This is a remote location open to candidates legally authorized to work in Canada.

What you will be doing:

  • Design and evolve high-signal GitHub Actions pipelines for build, test, release, and deployment workflows.
  • Optimize pipeline performance, reliability, and feedback loops across multi-language projects (TypeScript, Java, and Python).
  • Build and maintain reusable CI/CD building blocks that product teams can adopt with minimal friction.
  • Improve release safety with stronger validation gates, rollout strategies, and automation around quality checks.
  • Drive Nx monorepo scalability through task orchestration, cache strategy, affected project workflows, and developer productivity improvements.
  • Build internal devkit tooling and generators to standardize platform patterns and reduce repetitive engineering work.
  • Implement and maintain infrastructure-as-code using AWS CDK, Helm, and GitOps patterns (Flux).
  • Support Kubernetes-based workloads and deployment architecture across environments.
  • Partner with engineering teams on service hardening including observability, resilience, scaling, and operational readiness.
  • Improve platform security posture through guardrails, policy automation, and least-privilege patterns.
  • Reliability and Developer Experience
  • Raise service and pipeline reliability through strong monitoring, alerting, and incident-ready runbooks.
  • Improve operability with actionable logs, traces, metrics, and dashboards.
  • Reduce cognitive load for feature teams by making the golden path fast and intuitive.
  • Coach teams on delivery standards, DevOps maturity, and production best practices.
  • What you bring:
  • 4+ years in DevOps, Platform Engineering, SRE, or software engineering roles with strong operational ownership.
  • Strong TypeScript proficiency for platform tooling, CI/CD automation, and infrastructure code.
  • Production experience with AWS and infrastructure-as-code, with AWS CDK as a strong plus.
  • Deep experience designing and operating GitHub Actions pipelines in medium-to-large codebases.
  • Strong understanding of build systems, dependency management, and monorepo-scale engineering.
  • Experience implementing secure CI/CD practices, release controls, and environment promotion strategies.
  • Solid Linux and Docker debugging skills in cloud-native environments.
  • Nx monorepo expertise, including task graph optimization, affected workflows, and caching strategy.
  • Hands-on Kubernetes experience and Helm chart management in real delivery environments.
  • Experience with GitOps delivery models such as Flux.
  • Familiarity with mixed-stack monorepos (Angular, NestJS, Java, and serverless workloads).
  • Experience with observability platforms (metrics, logs, traces, alerting) and production incident response.
  • Salary Range: The annual base salary for this position is between $125,000 CAD and $140,000 CAD per year.

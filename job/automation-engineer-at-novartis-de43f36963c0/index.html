About the Role:
As an Automation Engineer in Puurs, you will play a key role in supporting a highly automated pharmaceutical manufacturing environment that delivers medicines to patients worldwide. You will contribute to investment and digitalization projects, helping deliver them on time and within budget, while ensuring reliable and high-performing production systems. Working across technologies such as PLC, SCADA/HMI, MES, data historians and OT infrastructure, you will support the full automation system lifecycle, from daily troubleshooting and continuous improvement to hardware and software upgrades, patching, equipment integration and commissioning. This role offers broad technical exposure, opportunities to grow your expertise and the chance to make a direct impact on production reliability, product quality and patient supply.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities:

  • Executing and following up on automation projects across the entire production site
  • Managing various automation systems (production, buildings, and utilities) and contributing to automation planning
  • Developing projects, including: preparing technical specifications, creating detailed project plans, performing risk analyses and ensuring proper documentation, coordinating and supervising installation and software development in line with specifications, transferring completed projects to end users (production and maintenance);
  • Collaborating closely with different stakeholders (production, engineering, IT)
  • Delivering projects for operational validation with complete documentation, managing project documentation and software backups (software archive)
  • Supporting validation activities
  • Troubleshooting automation system issues (during and outside office hours)
  • Organizing internal training sessions and supporting onboarding of new automation colleagues
  • Aligning and maintaining up-to-date automation procedures
  • Recommending improvements and upgrades to existing equipment to enhance efficiency

Essential Requirements:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a technical field (e.g., Industrial Engineering with specialization in automation, IT, Electromechanics, etc.)
  • 6+ years’ experience in automation within an industrial environment
  • Strong interest and experience in PLC development (Schneider, Siemens), HMI, SCADA systems (iFix), and databases
  • Analytical and detail-oriented, with strong communication skills and a team-oriented mindset, with a strong passion for learning new technologies
  • Excellent knowledge of Dutch and English, both spoken and written

Desirable requirements:

  • Project management skills
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical industry

Please note this role will require on call support on a rotational basis.

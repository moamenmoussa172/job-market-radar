About the job

P-1360

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

The Runtime Observability team builds and maintains the telemetry, data pipelines, and debugging tools that provide insight into the behavior of the Databricks Spark Engine. The Spark Engine runs millions of workloads scheduled every day by Databricks customers and is a highly critical component of the Databricks product. The team's mission is to provide reliable, fresh, product-centric runtime insights that enable faster detection of regressions, easier troubleshooting of failures and performance issues, and data-driven product decisions.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Design and implement backend infrastructure and data pipelines that provide insight into the performance and stability of the Runtime Engine at scale.
  • Collaborate with Runtime Engine teams to understand their use cases and translate requirements into effective telemetry and debugging infrastructure.
  • Optimize ingestion of large datasets to deliver metrics and insights quickly, enabling early detection of issues and faster debugging.
  • Write clean, tested, and well-documented code in Scala, Rust, and SQL.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering best practices within the team.

What We Look For

  • 2+ years of software engineering experience.
  • Experience with one or more programming languages including Scala, Rust, Java, or Python.
  • Understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design principles.
  • Experience building data pipelines or working with large-scale data processing systems.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
  • B.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

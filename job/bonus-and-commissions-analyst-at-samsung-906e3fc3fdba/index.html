About the job

Position Summary

Why join our team?
This role supports the Head of Performance Based Pay with day-to-day operations and offers an overview of the entire business, with significant and regular interaction with colleagues across the business.

Role and Responsibilities

  • Administer, report on and analyse bonus payments across Samsung Electronics UK, Europe Office and RPMs.
  • Manage calculations for over 1,800 colleagues across the UK and Europe receiving Performance Based Pay / MBO payments.
  • Act as Senior System Administrator for Vulki.
  • Drive the quarterly collection of sales targets with Commercial Finance for all sales divisions across SEUK.
  • Drive product and account mapping changes with Commercial Finance.
  • Manage the core data collection process, ensuring accurate entry of salaries, reporting lines, starters, leavers, cost centre changes, promotions, grade changes and other personal data changes.
  • Reconcile data between Vulki and Workday.
  • Review and analyse the quality of objectives being set.
  • Work to strict deadlines to submit payments to payroll on time in the UK and 10 other Samsung subsidiaries across Europe.
  • Reconcile payments.
  • Explain policies and the PBP scheme to colleagues and stakeholders.
  • Manage and respond to queries promptly and professionally using the Jira ticketing system.
  • Prepare monthly accruals for Samsung employees, including Partner Colleagues, in sales and non-sales roles.
  • Produce scheme exception cost simulations when requested.
  • Respond to ad hoc requests from the Head of PBP and People Team in a timely manner.

Skills and Qualifications

To be successful, you will possess the following skills and attributes:

  • High attention to detail and strong organisational skills.
  • Ability to relate to people at all levels of the organisation.
  • Great communication and relationship-building skills, with a passion for explaining complex schemes clearly and concisely to employees.
  • A discreet team player who handles sensitive and confidential data.
  • Hard-working, enthusiastic, personable and flexible, with a commercial outlook and understanding and a proactive approach.
  • Advanced Excel user with modelling ability, including large data sets, VLOOKUP and pivot tables.
  • Experience of commission and bonus software, specifically Vulki
  • Prior experience of business modelling and/or data management.

Benefits of working at Samsung include

  • Hybrid working
  • Bonus scheme linked to individual, team and company performance
  • Car allowance
  • Pension contribution
  • Three volunteering days each year
  • Holiday - 25 days plus bank holidays and an additional day off for your birthday
  • Access to discounts on a wide range of Samsung products
  • Access to a discount shopping portal
  • Partner Colleagues are not eligible for Samsung Enhanced Paid Sick Leave but may be eligible for statutory payments from their payroll agency
  • Up to 20 (pro-rata) Partner Absence days per calendar year to be used in times of need

Please note this is a Partner Colleague role where you will be engaged via one of our recruitment agency partners.

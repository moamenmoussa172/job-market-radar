Digital Hardware & Firmware Engineer

About the job

Job Description:

As part of the Digital Technologies team working on space telecommunications systems, you will:

· Digital Board Design: Design complex digital electronic boards based on FPGAs and microcontrollers, integrating high-speed digital interfaces, fast ADCs/DACs, and RF signal conditioning stages.

· Layout & Simulation: Perform pre- and post-layout Signal Integrity (SI) and Power Integrity (PI) simulations, supporting rigid and flex-rigid PCB design.

· Firmware & Embedded Software (Test): Develop VHDL firmware (for DSP and control functions on FPGA/ASIC) and embedded C/C++/C# software (for microcontrollers or soft processors) specifically aimed at hardware verification and testing.

· Laboratory Verification: Execute hardware integration, debugging, and functional testing in the laboratory.

· Documentation & Reporting: Draft technical project documentation (Technical Data Packages, Test Requirements, Test Procedures) and present technical results to internal stakeholders and international customers.

· Preparation of project documentation (Technical Data Package, Test Requirements and Test Procedures) and execution of oral presentations towards international customers

Education (Essential):

Master’s Degree in Electronic Engineering (preferably with a specialization in Telecommunications or Digital Systems).

Desirable: Post-graduate master's degree or PhD in Electronic Engineering.

Experience:

Essential: 1 to 3 years of experience in Digital Hardware and/or FPGA Firmware design.

Desirable: Previous experience in the Aerospace, Defence, or Space sectors.

Technical Knowledge (Desirable / Plus):

Familiarity with regulatory and qualification standards for aerospace/defence electronics: RMT (MIL-HDBK-217), EMI/EMC (DO-160, MIL-STD-461), Environmental (DO-160, MIL-STD-810), or Space parts qualification.

Basic knowledge of RF hardware design (baseband up to L-band) and telecom system concepts (digital modulations, link budgets).

Familiarity with Linux OS for embedded systems.

Tools & Software:

Essential: Schematic capture tools (e.g., OrCAD), VHDL simulators (e.g., ModelSim/Questa), FPGA synthesis tools (Xilinx/AMD, Intel/Altera, or Microchip/Microsemi), circuit simulators (SPICE/LTspice), and MS Office suite.

Desirable: Board-level SI/PI simulators (e.g., Mentor HyperLynx) and modeling environments (MATLAB/Simulink, C/C++).

Languages:

English: Fluent (written and spoken, B2/C1 level) for technical documentation and international customer meetings.

Italian: Fluent (written and spoken) for daily operational coordination with the local engineering team and suppliers.

Core Competencies:

Analytical and structured problem-solving approach.

Ability to manage assigned tasks and deadlines within a collaborative, multidisciplinary team.

Clear technical communication in international contexts.

Availability for occasional business travel in Italy and abroad.

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.

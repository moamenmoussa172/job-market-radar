About the job

Job Description

Are you a curious and rigorous researcher who wants to push the frontier of how large language models (LLMs) learn to act? Thomson Reuters Labs is seeking a Lead Research Scientist to drive research on agentic reinforcement learning (RL): finding new, efficient ways to obtain reliable training signals for complex, multi-step tasks in professional domains such as legal, tax and compliance, where answers must be grounded, cited and correct.

Thomson Reuters Labs trains the company’s own frontier-scale LLMs. Our research centres on post-training for agentic behaviour: models that plan, reason over long contexts, call tools and complete long-horizon tasks inside real agent harnesses (the software environments in which models take actions). Our focus areas include multi-turn RL and policy optimisation, reward modelling and training-signal design, harness-aware training, preference optimisation, reasoning control and efficiency, and evaluation with domain experts in the loop.

You will work with product and engineering teams across Thomson Reuters, with academic partners at world-leading research institutions, and with subject matter experts who have decades of experience in data collection, testing and evaluation of trained models. You will also work hand in hand with Research Engineers on our large-scale asynchronous training infrastructure.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead research direction: Own a research agenda in agentic RL and reward design, identify which emerging methods are worth pursuing, and turn them into a roadmap for our models.
  • Innovate: Invent dense, reliable and cost-efficient training signals for real-world tasks where ground truth is scarce, delayed or partly subjective.
  • Experiment rigorously: Design ablations (controlled experiments that remove or vary one component) and diagnostics that separate algorithmic effects from data, reward and system artefacts, then carry results into production models.
  • Publish: Publish regularly as lead or senior author at top-tier venues and represent Thomson Reuters Labs in the research community.
  • Mentor and collaborate: Guide scientists and engineers, and partner with Research Engineers to turn research ideas into trained models at scale.
  • Communicate: Share findings through seminars, lectures, conferences, publications and released technical assets.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD in machine learning, computer science or a related discipline.
  • 3+ years of research experience after the PhD, in industry or academia (for example, an industry research lab or postdoctoral role).
  • Strong publication record at top-tier venues (for example NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLM) on RL for LLMs, reward modelling, reasoning, agents and tool use, or preference optimisation.
  • Hands-on experience post-training LLMs with RL or preference optimisation, using PyTorch (Advanced).
  • Proven experience mentoring or supervising researchers, engineers or students, and guiding their work to published or deployed results.
  • Core competencies:
  • Research leadership: Ability to lead a research agenda from idea to published, reproducible results.
  • Experimental judgement: Designing controlled ablations and diagnosing reward, data and optimisation failures.
  • Mentorship: Developing the skills of the scientists and engineers around you.
  • Communication: Excellent written and oral communication.
  • Self-direction: A self-driven attitude and comfort with ambiguity in fast-paced environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • High-impact publications or recognised community roles (for example area chair, or widely adopted methods or benchmarks).
  • Experience with RL post-training frameworks such as NeMo-RL, verl, OpenRLHF or TRL, and with multi-turn tool environments (for example NeMo-Gym-style environments).
  • Experience designing LLM-as-a-judge or rubric-based reward pipelines and measuring their reliability.
  • Experience with Mixture-of-Experts or long-context models in RL settings.
  • Experience with knowledge-intensive or professional-domain tasks (legal, financial, scientific), particularly grounding and citation.

About The Opportunity

We are seeking a hands-on Director of AI Enablement to identify high-value business problems, deliver AI solutions in real operational environments, and turn proven approaches into reusable enterprise capabilities.

The role combines the business immersion and problem discovery of a Deployment Strategist, the technical delivery capability of a Forward-Deployed Engineer, and the product judgment required to standardize successful solutions. You will work directly with teams such as Compliance, Legal, Risk, Customer Service, Operations, and GBS, in partnership with Product, Engineering, Data, Security, and Infrastructure.

Success is measured by business impact and sustained adoption—not the number of pilots launched.

What You’ll Be Doing

1. Find and Prioritize High-Value Problems

  • Embed with business teams and frontline users to understand their workflows, decisions, data, constraints, and pain points.
  • Separate root causes from symptoms and determine whether the right answer is process redesign, better data, automation, AI, or a combination of these.
  • Prioritize opportunities based on business value, user reach, risk, feasibility, time-to-value, and reuse potential.
  • Translate ambiguous needs into clear problem statements, target outcomes, success metrics, and executable plans.

2. Build and Deliver Solutions

  • Define the minimum valuable product and rapidly validate it with real users and data.
  • Personally prototype solutions such as copilots, agents, workflow automations, and internal tools.
  • For larger or higher-risk initiatives, partner with Engineering and Data teams on architecture, integrations, security, reliability, evaluation, and production readiness.
  • Own delivery from discovery and prototype through launch, adoption, measurement, iteration, and handover.

3. Drive Adoption and Business Impact

  • Design solutions with users and adapt workflows based on real operational behavior.
  • Lead pilots, enablement, training, and change management required for sustained adoption.
  • Track outcomes such as time saved, cycle-time reduction, quality improvement, risk reduction, cost avoided, and active usage.
  • Ensure every initiative has a business owner, an adoption plan, and a sustainable operating model.

4. Productize and Scale What Works

  • Identify recurring workflows, data needs, integrations, and governance requirements across teams.
  • Convert successful deployments into reusable components, templates, reference architectures, or configurable products.
  • Decide when a need should remain bespoke, use shared components, or become a standardized product or platform capability.
  • Build practical playbooks and standards for opportunity assessment, prototyping, evaluation, productionization, and impact measurement.
  • Feed field learnings into shared Product and Engineering roadmaps, reducing duplicated and one-off solutions.

What We Look For In You

  • 4+ years of experience across product management, technical delivery, solutions engineering, enterprise transformation, consulting, or software/data engineering.
  • Demonstrated ability to uncover high-value problems through workflow observation, data analysis, and stakeholder engagement.
  • Hands-on experience building and shipping AI, data, or automation-enabled solutions used in real operational workflows.
  • Strong product judgment, including prioritization, MVP definition, trade-off decisions, rapid validation, and avoidance of over-engineering.
  • Sufficient technical depth to prototype independently and work effectively with Engineering on data, integrations, architecture, security, evaluation, and reliability.
  • Working knowledge of LLMs, RAG, agents, tool use, prompt design, evaluation, observability, and human-in-the-loop systems.
  • Experience turning bespoke business needs into reusable products, shared services, or operating standards.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills across frontline users, technical teams, and senior executives.
  • High ownership and comfort operating in ambiguous, fast-changing environments.

Nice-To-Haves

  • Experience in fintech, crypto/blockchain, financial services, or another regulated industry.
  • Experience working with Compliance, Legal, Risk, Customer Service, Operations, or other non-engineering functions.
  • Experience with enterprise data platforms, knowledge systems, agent frameworks, workflow orchestration, or MCP-based integrations.
  • Experience leading an AI enablement function, forward-deployed team, internal product incubator, or multidisciplinary delivery team.
  • Familiarity with Lark/Feishu and enterprise internal-tooling ecosystems.

Expected Outcomes

Within the first 6–12 months, the successful candidate will be expected to:

  • Establish a repeatable approach for identifying and prioritizing high-value AI opportunities.
  • Deliver production use cases with measurable operational impact and sustained adoption.
  • Create clear ownership and decision paths from prototype to production, scale, handover, or stop.
  • Turn selected deployments into reusable capabilities adopted by multiple teams.
  • Build a network of business champions and technical partners capable of scaling AI adoption across the organization.

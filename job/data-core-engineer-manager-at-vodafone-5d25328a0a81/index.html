About this Role

We are seeking a Data Core Engineer to join our Managed Services team within Vodafone Network Operations in Bangalore, India. This role focuses on the operation, troubleshooting, configuration and optimisation of complex 4G LTE EPC and 5G Core networks supporting mission-critical telecommunications services.

You will work across technologies including MME, SGW, PGW, PCRF, HSS, AMF, SMF, UPF, UDM and PCF, resolving complex network incidents and supporting network evolution, integration and service continuity.

The role requires strong knowledge of 4G/5G signalling and call flows, telecom protocols, Linux and cloud-native environments, combined with hands-on troubleshooting and automation capabilities. You will collaborate with Operations, Planning, Delivery, vendors and Vodafone operating companies to maintain network performance and meet agreed SLAs and KPIs.

The position operates within a 24x7 telecom environment and may involve weekend, night and overtime working depending on operational requirements.

What you’ll do

What you will do

  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex 4G LTE EPC and 5G Core network incidents within agreed SLAs, restoring service quality and continuity.
  • Investigate high-complexity technical issues escalated by first-line and other operational teams and provide appropriate remediation.
  • Analyse attach failures, registration failures, bearer issues, data-session problems, roaming incidents and signalling faults.
  • Perform packet and protocol trace analysis using tools including Wireshark and tcpdump.
  • Configure, integrate and optimise network software parameters in line with defined quality and operational requirements.
  • Support network functions including MME, SGW, PGW, PCRF, HSS, AMF, SMF, UPF, UDM and PCF.
  • Analyse 4G/5G signalling and procedures including LTE attach, 5G registration, PDU session establishment, handovers and roaming.
  • Troubleshoot protocols including Diameter, GTPv2-C, GTP-U, PFCP, SIP, SCTP, DNS, HTTP/2, MAP and TCP/IP.
  • Execute preventive and corrective maintenance to reduce recurring faults and maintain network performance.
  • Conduct periodic preventive system checks to identify latent software and hardware deficiencies.
  • Support parameter configuration, controlled network changes, rollouts and integration activities aligned with network development plans.
  • Evaluate network changes to maintain quality of service and minimise regression risks.
  • Support Linux, VMware and Kubernetes-based environments, including system health monitoring, logging and performance management.
  • Develop and adapt automation and operational solutions using Python, Shell scripting, Ansible, REST APIs and CI/CD practices.
  • Introduce and improve operational processes using automation and AI to increase efficiency and consistency.
  • Support High Availability, Geo-Redundancy, Disaster Recovery, NFV and cloud-native network deployments.
  • Participate in Business Continuity Plan execution and identify practical workarounds or alternative solutions during critical interventions and disaster-recovery scenarios.
  • Escalate complex technical problems to relevant vendors and collaborate through resolution.
  • Engage with Major Incident Management during critical outages in accordance with established escalation procedures.
  • Provide accurate and timely incident communications to agreed stakeholder groups.
  • Coordinate with vendors, other departments and Vodafone operating companies to meet agreed service levels.
  • Support field engineers and Planning, Integration and Deployment teams during complex interventions.
  • Maintain operational procedures and network documentation.
  • Share technical knowledge and troubleshooting practices with teams supporting network deployment and operations.
  • Contribute to Incident, Problem, Change and Major Incident Management processes within a 24x7 operational environment.

Who you are

You have:

  • A BE/B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunications.
  • 6–8 years of experience in Mobile Network Operations.
  • Strong expertise in 4G LTE EPC and 5G Core architectures, including network functions, interfaces, operational processes and solution design.
  • Hands-on experience with MME, SGW, PGW, PCRF, HSS, AMF, SMF, UPF, UDM and PCF.
  • Strong understanding of subscriber mobility, session management, bearer management, policy control, charging, roaming, VoLTE and VoNR.
  • Detailed understanding of 4G/5G signalling and call flows, including attach, registration, PDU session establishment, handovers and roaming procedures.
  • Practical expertise in Diameter, GTPv2-C, GTP-U, PFCP, SIP, SCTP, DNS, HTTP/2, MAP and TCP/IP.
  • Proven experience troubleshooting attach, bearer, data-session, roaming and signalling-related problems.
  • Experience analysing packet and protocol traces using Wireshark and tcpdump.
  • Hands-on knowledge of Linux administration, VMware and Kubernetes-based orchestration.
  • Experience with system health monitoring, log analysis and performance management.
  • Understanding of HA, Geo-Redundancy, Disaster Recovery, NFV and cloud-native network deployments.
  • Experience with Incident, Problem, Change and Major Incident Management in a 24x7 telecom operations environment.
  • Working proficiency in Python, Shell scripting, Ansible, REST APIs, automation frameworks and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Effective analytical, problem-solving, stakeholder management, communication and documentation capabilities.

Desirable experience and qualifications include:

  • Cisco CCNA and/or CCNP certification.
  • Knowledge of cloud fundamentals.
  • ITIL v4 certification.
  • Experience working across multiple telecommunications vendors.
  • Knowledge of scripting, command-line environments and Windows/Unix administration.
  • Operational experience supporting complex, mission-critical IT or telecommunications systems.

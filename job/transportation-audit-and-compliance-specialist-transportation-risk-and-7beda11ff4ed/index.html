About the job

Amazon is the most customer-centric company on Earth. We need exceptionally talented, customer-centric, bright, and driven people. We are looking for an organized self-starter to join the Transportation Risk and Compliance (TRC) team at Amazon. This position will be based out of an Amazon corporate office, and requires the ability to travel periodically.
Amazon Transportation Services have built world-class Middle Mile operations, which rely on an exceptional pool of carriers who work relentlessly to meet our customers’ expectations. TRC is looking for an detail-oriented professional to join us as an Audit & Compliance Specialist to assess carriers’ compliance against contractual and legal requirements, and drive a compliance culture both internally and externally.
We are looking for an individual with audit experience, preferably in transport, who can apply their expertise to 1) conduct and manage thorough carrier assessments, 2) identify legal, financial, and reputational risks at carrier level, and propose risk-mitigation solutions, and 3) deliver detailed finding and recommendation reports for both carriers and business teams (e.g., carrier management teams). The successful candidate will have excellent investigation and attention to details aptitudes, as well as data-driven mindset and capabilities, but also good soft skills for building trustworthy relationships with peers, and carriers. Finally, the successful candidate will display good team spirit and autonomy.
If you wish to innovate on behalf of customers, are passionate about driving compliance, reducing risks, and making an impact on society, come join our team and make history!
Key job responsibilities
• Audit carriers to verify that they operate compliantly with contractual, legal, and regulatory requirements
• Provide precise audit reports to carriers, with clear finding details, and actionable short/mid/long-term corrective and preventative action items, thus helping them achieve and maintain compliance requirements
• Provide thorough findings and recommendation reports to internal management
• Meet individual and team goals with the highest standards and diligence
• Achieve audit productivity and quality standards
• Lead and/or participate in special projects and/or assignments as necessary
A day in the life
There are no two days completely alike in this role, but most will include:
1. Conducting and/or managing audits, working collaboratively with e.g., Global Solutions Risk and Compliance, and Carrier Operations
2. Delivering audit reports, inclusive of compliance-driving recommendations
3. Attending team and stakeholder meetings to drive a compliance culture in and out of the organization
4. Supporting carriers with their compliance questions or doubts
5. Working on special projects and/or initiatives to attain team/individual goals
About the team
TRC’s mission is to empower Amazon’s transportation business and its partners to do the right thing for the community at large, with a vision to inform decision-making that drives business growth through world-class scalable risk management solutions. Through ongoing compliance monitoring and risk management activities, we provide transportation leaders with the data they need to make informed risk management decisions.
- Proficiency in both spoken and written Italian
- Proficiency in both spoken and written French
- Proficiency in both spoken and written English
- Experience in compliance program management, audit, legal, governance, risk management, or loss prevention
- Experience in SQL data manipulation
- Experience with Lean or Six Sigma analytical techniques
- Experience working in risk/fraud analysis or in compliance organizations

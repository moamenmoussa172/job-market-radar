About the role

Anthropic is seeking a Financial Data and Controls Lead to join our Billing Platform team. Billing Platform builds the systems customers use to transact with Anthropic: subscriptions, usage billing, quote to cash, invoicing, and payments. You'll be the dedicated owner of billing accuracy, reconciliation, and financial controls across those systems, making sure the right checks run, the right controls are in place, and the audit evidence exists.

Our billing systems and pricing change often, and each flow needs to meet SOX requirements. Accurate billing is what lets us launch products and pricing quickly and still close the books on time. The hard problems today include validating negotiated pricing from contract to invoice, handling the many forms of credits, app store billing, and keeping controls current while the systems under them are replaced.

This is a foundational role and the first hire of its kind on the team. You'll lead a working group of the reconciliation and controls owners from each billing engineering team, and you'll set the approach they follow. You'll work closely with Finance Systems, Accounting, Internal Audit, and Data Engineering.

The role sits in Engineering and works with Finance every day. Our Data organization builds the pipelines and owns the warehouse, and you'll use that data and partner with that team. We're looking for an engineer or data practitioner with strong accounting domain knowledge, who can talk to engineers about systems and to Finance and auditors about risk and materiality.

Key responsibilities

  • Own reconciliation and accuracy checks for critical billing data (quantity, price, discounts, and invoices) across our product platform, third-party billing and payment providers, app stores, cloud marketplaces, and in-house systems
  • Design and run detective controls that catch billing errors before they reach the accounting books
  • Partner with billing engineering teams to turn repeat checks into automated verifiers
  • Review billing changes that have financial impact, flag the ones that touch SOX or IT application controls (ITACs), and make sure functional test evidence is in place
  • Make SOX review a standard, lightweight step in how billing changes ship, treated like a security review, so launches are not delayed by it
  • Own the end-to-end test strategy and audit evidence for those changes, working with Finance Systems, Data Engineering, Accounting, and Internal Audit
  • Triage billing errors, prioritize backfills and fixes with the owning teams, and track materiality over time
  • Define and maintain the data contracts between billing and finance systems, so downstream teams can find and trust the data they need
  • Lead the working group of reconciliation and controls owners across billing engineering teams, through influence and not reporting lines
  • Act as the Billing owner, alongside Accounting and Finance Systems, for translating the product and pricing roadmap into financial impact, test strategy, and process updates

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience building or owning a financial ledger, billing reconciliation, or cash reconciliation system end to end, one that Accounting relied on to close the books
  • Hands-on SQL skills strong enough to take two messy datasets (for example usage events and invoices), match them, find the breaks, and explain the root cause
  • Accounting domain knowledge (order to cash, month-end close, and materiality) sufficient to tell which billing changes have financial impact
  • Experience working with SOX and Internal Audit teams on IT application controls, detective controls, transaction monitoring, functional test evidence, and auditor walkthroughs
  • Experience defining data contracts between billing or product systems and finance systems such as the ERP, general ledger, or revenue recognition
  • Track record of driving work across Engineering, Finance, Accounting, and Audit without direct authority
  • Ability to explain technical issues to Finance and auditors in their terms, and financial risk to engineers in theirs
  • Interest in AI and a commitment to Anthropic's mission of building safe, beneficial AI systems

Preferred qualifications

  • Background as a backend, data, or analytics engineer who built a financial ledger or cash reconciliation system from scratch
  • Experience running these systems under public company reporting requirements, and scaling them as the company grew
  • Experience with usage-based or consumption billing
  • Experience with multi-party billing, such as marketplaces, or app stores
  • Experience turning manual checks into automated verifiers with engineering teams
  • Experience remediating a control deficiency, or standing up a controls program while the underlying systems were changing
  • A track record of using Claude or other AI tools to automate reconciliation, testing, or evidence collection, with checks that hold up to review
  • Experience with billing and finance tools such as Stripe, Metronome, Zuora, NetSuite, or Workday Financials
  • Python for data analysis or automation

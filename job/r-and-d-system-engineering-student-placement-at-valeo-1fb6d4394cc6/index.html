About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Mission

Our mission is to be the World Leader for Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) within the automotive industry by developing innovative systems, and providing situational awareness solutions using cutting edge technology.

Your mission as a System Engineering Student is to:

  • Support the project team deliverables.
  • Ensure system engineering scope and enhancement using AI tools.
  • Support on-time delivery of system engineering targets through the project life cycle.

Overview

Join on-going OEM development projects for Automotive Vision Systems and groundbreaking ADAS technologies. Contribute to the development of the next generation of automotive autonomous driving technology.

What does the student gain?

  • Applying theoretical AI knowledge to real-world automotive safety standards.
  • Learning how to bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI and rigorous ASPICE compliance.
  • Working with specialized tools like Code Beamer and custom Leah/EMI workflows.

Responsibilities

- Transfer the customer specifications to the internal tools using AI agents

- Generate the KPIs and tailor to the business demands

- Automation of system engineering with AI tools

- System Engineering for the ADAS functions

- Analyze the defects and work with the global team for the resolution

- Deliver the scope of system engineering for the complex ADAS projects

- Working closely with the Functional Safety and Cyber Safety teams to facilitate the overall system engineering scope.

Expected Skillset

1. AI and Data Literacy

  • Prompt Engineering: Basic understanding of how to structure prompts for LLMs (like Gemini or ChatGPT) to get consistent, high-quality results.
  • LLM Fundamentals: Awareness of how Large Language Models work, including their strengths and limitations (e.g., understanding "hallucinations").
  • Data Handling: Proficiency in managing data within Excel or Google Sheets, as much of the AI output currently integrates with these platforms.

2. Technical & Programming Skills

  • Python Basics (bonus skill): Python is the standard language for AI integration. Even basic scripting skills will help them automate the "parsers" and "connectors" between AI tools and engineering documents.
  • API Awareness (bonus skill) : An understanding of how different software tools talk to each other (APIs) is helpful for future tool integration.
  • Document Structuring: Familiarity with structured data formats like JSON, XML, or Markdown, which are often used in AI workflows.

3. Systems Engineering Interest

  • Requirement Basics: A foundational understanding of what a "requirement" is and why traceability matters in a complex project (like automotive).
  • Logical Thinking: The ability to break down a complex system into smaller functions (Functional Breakdown Structure).
  • Process Awareness: Willingness to learn industry standards like ASPICE or the V-Model.

4. Behavioral & Soft Skills

  • Analytical Mindset: The ability to critically review AI-generated content rather than accepting it at face value (maintaining the "critical thinking" you emphasized earlier).
  • Agility & Fast Learning: The AI field moves quickly; the student needs to be comfortable with tools that change or update frequently.
  • Communication: The ability to document their workflows so that other engineers can replicate their results.

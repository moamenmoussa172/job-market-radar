About the job

We're part of the AWS Observability organization. We build the tools customers use to understand how their applications and infrastructure are performing, and to fix problems fast. Our data platform collects operational signals — metrics, logs, and traces — from systems running at enormous scale, and turns them into the analytics, dashboards, and insights customers act on. We run this for Amazon itself and for millions of customers worldwide.
Two things shape our roadmap. We build on open standards like OpenTelemetry and Prometheus so customers aren't locked into a single vendor, with a particular focus on Kubernetes and the open-source observability community. And AI runs through both what we build and how we build it: we use AI to surface insights, let customers interrogate their data conversationally, and investigate root cause, while also building the observability layer for AI itself, covering the agents, inference endpoints, and training jobs customers now run in production.
We work differently from many product teams, largely because AI has changed what a PM can do directly. Our PMs design their own products rather than handing off to designers, build their own prototypes rather than specifying them in documents, and pull their own data rather than queuing for analyst time. That shortens the loop between an idea and customer feedback considerably. As an intern on a small team, you'll see firsthand how the role is evolving, and you'll own a problem end to end.
Key job responsibilities
As a Sr. PMT MBA intern, you will own multiple projects in the AWS observability space from start to finish. You will:
Find and define customer problems in how teams monitor, troubleshoot and operate cloud-native applications
Design and build working prototypes yourself, and use them to test ideas with customers
Work with engineers to turn what your ideas into a product vision, requirements and a roadmap
Work with the open-source community (for example, projects like OpenTelemetry and Prometheus) to understand where it is going and find ways for AWS to contribute
Use AI and data to make decisions and measure customer and business impact
Present your recommendations to senior leaders at the end of the internship
A day in the life
This role suits someone highly autonomous and self-driven. You decide what matters most, set your own direction, and move things forward. AI is how you'll do most of it: synthesizing customer interviews, querying telemetry data, drafting the PRD, building the prototype. One day you might interview a customer running Kubernetes at scale in the morning, have a working prototype by the afternoon, and review it with engineers before you log off — work that used to take a quarter and three other people. Experienced Principal PMs will mentor you, but you own your work and its outcomes from day one.
The team is growing, so strong performers may be considered for a full-time role in 2028.

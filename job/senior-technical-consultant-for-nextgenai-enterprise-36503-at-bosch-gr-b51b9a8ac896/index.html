About the job

The Technical Consultant for NextGenAI Enterprise serves as the technical authority and trusted advisor for enterprise AI solutions, including ChatGPT Enterprise and the broader NextGenAI ecosystem.

The role focuses on translating business requirements into secure, scalable, and compliant AI solutions, supporting the successful adoption of Generative AI technologies across Bosch.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Translate business requirements and use cases into viable technical AI solutions.
  • Provide technical consulting and solution design guidance for Generative AI initiatives.
  • Support the implementation, integration, and adoption of ChatGPT Enterprise and other NextGenAI solutions.
  • Advise stakeholders on solution architecture, technical feasibility, security, scalability, and compliance requirements.
  • Collaborate closely with Product & Service Managers, architects, platform engineers, security specialists, compliance teams, and business stakeholders.
  • Provide technical expertise for integrations between AI solutions, APIs, cloud platforms, and enterprise systems.
  • Support the design and implementation of AI Agents and agentic workflows.
  • Ensure technical solutions are aligned with AI governance, Responsible AI, information security, privacy, and data protection requirements.
  • Act as a technical advisor throughout the Product & Service lifecycle.

While the IT Product & Service Manager owns strategy, lifecycle, budget, and governance, the Technical Consultant is responsible for providing deep technical expertise, solution design guidance, integration consulting, and implementation support.

We are looking for a professional who combines Generative AI expertise, enterprise architecture knowledge, technical consulting skills, and strong stakeholder management capabilities.

AI & Generative AI

  • Experience with ChatGPT Enterprise and enterprise Generative AI use cases.
  • Strong understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Experience with Prompt Engineering.
  • Knowledge of AI Agents and agentic workflows.
  • Understanding of AI Governance and Responsible AI principles.

Enterprise IT

  • Experience with cloud and SaaS platforms.
  • Knowledge of APIs and enterprise system integrations.
  • Understanding of Identity & Access Management (IAM).
  • Knowledge of enterprise architecture fundamentals.
  • Understanding of information security and data protection principles.

Bosch Environment

  • Knowledge of or ability to quickly develop expertise in Bosch AI Governance.
  • Understanding of Bosch privacy and compliance requirements.
  • Familiarity with the Bosch Digital platform landscape.
  • Understanding of Product & Service lifecycle processes.

Professional Competencies

  • Strong technical consulting and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Ability to translate business needs into technical solutions.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Ability to work across multidisciplinary and international teams.
    Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English

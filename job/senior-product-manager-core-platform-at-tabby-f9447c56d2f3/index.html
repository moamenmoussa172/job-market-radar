About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Serbia, United Arab Emirates.

Tabby creates financial freedom in the way people shop, earn and save by reshaping their relationship with money. Over 25 million users choose Tabby to stay in control of their spending and make the most out of their money.
The company’s flagship offering allows shoppers to split their payments online and in-store with no interest or fees. Over 70,000 global brands and small businesses, including Amazon, Noon, IKEA, and SHEIN use Tabby to accelerate growth and gain loyal customers by offering easy and flexible payments online and in stores.
Tabby generates over $18 billion in annual transaction volume for its partner brands and is the highest-rated, most-reviewed, largest, and fastest-growing FinTech in the GCC region.
Tabby launched in 2019 and has since raised +$1 billion in equity and debt funding from global and regional investors, and is now valued at $6,5 billion.
We’re looking for a Senior Technical Product Manager with a strong system analysis background to join Tabby’s Financial Platform domain. Every product Tabby launches passes through this domain. We define the financial logic behind each product: how it charges, settles, accounts, and reports. We make sure every launch meets regulatory and financial audit requirements, and we turn one-off needs into reusable platform solutions that the rest of the company builds on.

This is a cross-functional role spanning product, system design, financial operations, and platform architecture. It suits you if you want to shape how the financial side of every product is designed, and to see those designs through to robust, best-in-class implementation.

  • Turn business, product, finance, and regulatory needs into clear system requirements and implementation-ready specifications
  • Define how money and financial data flow end to end across products, partners, and internal systems, from transaction to accounting record
  • Contribute to platform architecture and service interaction design across product services and CoreBank components
  • Work with PMs, engineers, Finance, Compliance, and adjacent teams to define scope, dependencies, and delivery phases
  • Identify the services, integrations, and teams affected by each change
  • Shape and prioritize the team backlog with the product lead
  • Support engineering and QA through implementation, covering edge cases, expected behavior, and incident analysis
  • Keep technical and functional documentation current and usable for engineers and business stakeholders alike
  • 3+ years as a Technical PM, System Analyst, or Business Analyst
  • Upper-Intermediate English or higher
  • Ability to break down large business processes into clear technical flows
  • Experience with distributed, service-based architectures
  • Solid API and integration design knowledge (REST, SOAP, gRPC)
  • Strong documentation skills: functional specs, sequence diagrams, state models, API contracts, data models
  • Knowledge of UML, BPMN, or similar notations

Strong plus:

  • Fintech, payments, banking, wallets, cards, lending, or ledger/accounting experience
  • Hands-on work with financial transactions, reconciliation, or money movement systems
  • Exposure to regulated environments, such as central bank requirements or e-invoicing
  • ERP integration experience (NetSuite or similar)
  • Product mindset and appetite for broader ownership over time

What success looks like:

  • Ambiguous financial, product, or regulatory requirements become clear, scalable platform designs
  • Teams ship changes safely in critical money movement systems
  • Reusable platform capabilities improve consistency across products
  • You grow from owning requirements into shaping the direction of the platform

Regional Product Manager – Residential Air Conditioning, Hot Water and Well Being products

About the job

The Regional Product Manager is responsible for driving the strategy and commercial performance of the Residential Air Conditioning, Hot Water and Well Being portfolio across South-Eastern Europe. The role translates market needs, customer requirements and competitive insights into portfolio priorities, successful product launches and profitable growth. Acting as the key link between SEE markets and central business units, the position ensures a competitive and future-oriented regional product portfolio.

Your tasks:
• Take ownership of the complete lifecycle of the regional Residential Air Conditioning Bosch/Hitachi/Buderus, Residential Water Heaters and Well Being (Air Purifiers, Convectors, Dehumidifiers etc.) portfolio
• Develop and maintain the portfolio roadmap, phase-in, phase-out, successor planning and complexity reduction.
• Steer country market launches and ensure that product data, documentation, pricing, training and commercial readiness are completed on time.
• Analyse customer needs, legislation, market trends and competitor activities and translate findings into portfolio recommendations.
• Prepare business cases and business plans for new products, portfolio extensions, market segments and distribution opportunities.
• Act as the link between SEE sales teams and central business units, communicating market needs, portfolio gaps and regional priorities.
• Provide product and price benchmarks, competitive positioning, sales arguments and clear product USPs.
• Monitor turnover, profitability, margins, price development reports and product performance and initiate actions to achieve targets.
• Cooperate with Sales, Brand Communication, Controlling, Training, Logistics, Service and central Product Management and International Business Unit.
• Participate in market visits and support sales teams, distributors and installers with product-related topics.
Required Professional Experience
• Minimum 3 years of Product Management/Category management/Sales experience in Residential HVAC products or relevant to HVAC field area.
• Experience managing the complete product lifecycle, from market requirements and portfolio selection through launch, pricing, phase-in, phase-out and replacement planning.
• Experience transferring product knowledge and aligning product data, documentation, pricing, training and commercial processes between organizations or business units.
• Strong experience in competitor benchmarking, market segmentation, customer-needs analysis and regional go-to-market development.
• Experience adapting European or global Residential HVAC portfolios to country-specific requirements; knowledge of South-Eastern European markets is an advantage.
• Developing business cases and managing product profitability, margin development and sales-performance analysis.

• Bachelor's degree or higher in HVAC, Mechanical Engineering, Economics, Environmental Engineering or a comparable field.
• Relevant professional certifications are an advantage.
• Advanced Microsoft Excel, Power Point skills
• Experience with SharePoint, KPI reporting, master-data management and variance analysis.
• Strategic thinking combined with strong analytical and commercial skills.
• Ability to convert product and market information into clear recommendations and actions.
• High ownership, a structured working style and a proactive approach to continuous improvement.
• Strong cross-functional collaboration, influencing, communication and stakeholder-management skills.
• Comfort working across cultures and willingness to travel internationally.
• Fluent English, written and spoken.
• An additional South-Eastern European language is an advantage.

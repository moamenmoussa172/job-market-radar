About the job

Job Title

Modern Workplace/IAM Architect

Job Description

Job description

Are you passionate about shaping the future of the digital workplace and enterprise identity? We are looking for a Product Architect Workplace & Identity to define the architectural direction for two key product domains within our ICT landscape: Workplace and Identity.

The Workplace domain covers the modern Windows 11 workplace, mobile devices, endpoint management through Microsoft Intune, virtual Windows workplaces for specific use cases, and endpoint security using the Microsoft Defender suite.

The Identity domain covers Identity & Access Management (IAM), Microsoft Entra ID, Active Directory, authentication, authorization.

As Product Architect, you will focus on the bigger picture: defining the target architecture, setting architectural principles, developing roadmaps and ensuring that both domains remain secure, scalable and aligned with our broader ICT strategy.

You will work closely with experienced technical teams who are responsible for the engineering and operation of these services.

Your responsibilities

You will:

  • Own and maintain the architecture for the Workplace and Identity product domains.
  • Define the current state, target architecture and architectural roadmap.
  • Establish architectural principles, standards and key design decisions.
  • Ensure Workplace and Identity developments align with business, security and enterprise architecture requirements.
  • Drive simplification, standardization and modernization across both domains.
  • Manage architectural dependencies with areas such as cloud, infrastructure, network, security and applications.
  • Evaluate new technologies and developments and determine their potential value within the product domains.
  • Identify architectural risks, technical debt and lifecycle challenges.
  • Work with product owners and technical teams to translate strategic developments into product roadmaps.
  • Align with stakeholders, other architects and strategic technology partners such as Microsoft.
  • Ensure Workplace and Identity work together effectively where they intersect, for example through identity, device trust and Zero Trust principles.

Your profile

You are an experienced architect with a broad understanding of modern workplace and identity technologies. You are able to look beyond individual technologies and create a clear architectural direction for complex and evolving environments.

You combine strategic thinking with enough technical understanding to evaluate solutions, identify dependencies and make well-founded architectural decisions. You are comfortable working with experienced technical teams and allow them the freedom to determine the best implementation within the agreed architectural direction.

You have experience with topics such as:

Workplace

  • Windows 11 and modern workplace architecture
  • Microsoft Intune and device management
  • Mobile devices
  • Virtual Windows workplaces
  • Endpoint security and Microsoft Defender

Identity

  • Identity & Access Management (IAM)
  • Microsoft Entra ID and Active Directory
  • Authentication and authorization
  • Identity governance and lifecycle management
  • Privileged and external identities

Architecture

  • Target architectures and technology roadmaps
  • Architecture principles and decision-making
  • Security and Zero Trust
  • Technology lifecycle and technical debt
  • Stakeholder and supplier alignment

What you bring

You are a strategic thinker who can turn technology and business developments into a clear architectural direction. You communicate effectively with technical teams, product owners, architects, management and suppliers.

You are pragmatic, able to balance security, user experience, manageability and cost, and comfortable making architectural decisions in a complex international environment.

Most importantly, you see Workplace and Identity as two distinct but closely connected product domains and can shape their evolution together as part of a modern ICT landscape.

Make a real impact

In this dynamic environment, we are looking for an ambitious and self-driven Business Analyst – Senior Associate to join our Delivery Excellence and Innovation team in Athens.

In this role, you will act as a key link between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring that integration and interoperability requirements are clearly defined, documented, and successfully delivered.

  • Lead the analysis of system integrations and interoperability requirements across complex IT environments
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, external partners, and technical teams to gather and clarify requirements
  • Analyze data flows, system dependencies, and integration points, ensuring alignment with business processes
  • Translate business requirements into clear functional and technical specifications
  • Document and maintain interfaces, APIs, data mappings, use cases, and process flows
  • Work closely with developers, architects, testers, and project managers to ensure successful implementation
  • Support testing activitie, interoperability validation, and UAT processes
  • Provide guidance on integration technologies (APIs, REST/SOAP, XML/JSON, authentication, security)
  • Identify opportunities to streamline integrations, improve data consistency, and enhance automation
  • Contribute to client engagements by supporting solution design and delivery in integration-focused projects

What you bring matters

  • University degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field
  • Strong experience in roles such as Business Analyst, Technical Analyst, or Systems Analyst within IT projects
  • Proven experience in system integrations and interoperability analysis and documentation
  • Solid understanding of system integration principles and technologies (APIs, REST/SOAP services, XML/JSON, data mappings, messaging)
  • Experience in writing functional and technical specifications
  • Ability to analyze complex business processes and translate them into structured deliverables
  • Familiarity with AI tools to enhance productivity and quality of deliverables
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to bridge business and technical teams
  • Experience working in multi-stakeholder, cross-functional environments
  • Excellent command of Greek and English (spoken and written)

About the job

  • Analyze HMI requirements, UI specifications, and system behaviors.
  • Design and execute manual and automated test cases for automotive HMI features.
  • Validate functionality of infotainment, cluster, HVAC, navigation, media, and ADAS visualization.
  • Perform system integration testing with CAN/Ethernet communication.
  • Verify touch, hard key, steering wheel control, and voice interaction behaviors.
  • Conduct regression, smoke, functional, and performance testing.
  • Simulate vehicle signals using CAN tools and validate HMI response.
  • Analyze logs, reproduce issues, and support root cause investigation.
  • Report, track, and verify defects using defect management tools.
  • Collaborate with developers, system engineers, UX teams, and OEM customers.
  • Support CI/CD and automated testing activities.
  • 3rd or final year student majoring in Computer Science, Electronics, Automotive Engineering, or related field.
  • Official university recommendation letter with the university stamp (mandatory). This document will be required after you successfully pass Bosch's interview process.
  • Internship Model: Full-time or at least 4 days/week
  • Good knowledge in HMI testing, infotainment, digital cockpit, or embedded systems.
  • Knowledge of automotive communication protocols: CAN, LIN, Ethernet / SOME-IP
  • Good knowledge in Vector CANoe / CANalyzer; ADB / Android logcat/ Linux or Android-based systems.
  • Understanding of software testing methodologies and defect lifecycle.
  • Ability to analyze logs and debug system issues.
  • Basic scripting/programming knowledge with Python

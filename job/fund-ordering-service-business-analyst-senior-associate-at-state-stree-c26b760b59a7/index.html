Fund Ordering Service Business Analyst, Senior Associate

State Street · Krakow, Poland

Onsite · Senior · Business / Domain Analyst · Posted 5 November 2026

Apply on State Street's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Who we are looking for

This is an exciting opportunity to be part of a growing team, and the incumbent will contribute to key strategic projects in addition to delivering services for our clients. This Senior Associate position plays a key role within our mutual fund trading team. The role will be responsible for onboarding new key clients to our Fund Ordering Service and will contribute to testing our new mutual fund trading platform and will contribute to building our future state model.

Why this role is important to us

The team you will be joining plays an important role in the overall success of the organization. Across the globe, institutional investors rely on us to help them manage risk, respond to challenges, and drive performance and profitability. To make that happen we need teams like yours to help navigate employees and the organization. In your role you will strive for cutting-edge solutions, that are straightforward and scalable. You will help us build resilience and execute day to day deliverables at our best.

Join us if making your mark in the financial services industry from day one is a challenge you are up for.

Your new role

As Senior Business Analyst you will:

  • Develop a deep understanding of existing mutual fund trading infrastructure and business processes
  • Assist with client conversions/start-ups of new client relationships
  • Work autonomously with confidence in a fast-moving, complex technical environment
  • Build relationships with colleagues across multiple functional groups
  • Assist with business requirements, testing, and implementing new functionality on our trading application in coordination with management team, clients, IT, and the vendor
  • Research and resolve inquiries for clients, trading partners, and internal stakeholders
  • Ensure all team deliverables are met within assigned deadlines daily, document and update procedures and team checklists, ensure adherence to standard operating procedures
  • Assist with client reporting and client projects
  • Analyse internal processes and look to automate, reduce risk, and streamline workflows

What we value

These skills will help you succeed in this role:

  • Analytical mindset and strong critical thinking
  • Problem solving and decision-making skills
  • Confident & clear communicator with ability to articulate complex concepts to any audience
  • Highly motivated with exceptional integrity
  • Ability to understand and contribute to documented business and technical system requirements

Education & Preferred Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent
  • 3+ years’ experience in financial services industry
  • Technically confident

What we offer

  • Permanent contract of employment from day one
  • Additional holidays (Birthday Day Off, 3rd and 5th year anniversary Day Off)
  • Gold medical package for employees and their families (partner and children)
  • Premium life insurance package and private pension plan
  • Employee savings plan
  • Kafeteria benefits system: Multisport Card
  • Wide range of soft skills training, technical workshops, language classes and development programs
  • Opportunities to volunteer your time to company-driven initiatives, employee networks or organizations of your choice
  • Variety of well-being programs
  • Technical or leadership career pathway

Work requirements

  • Hybrid role model - 3 days of work from the office required

About the job

Are you passionate about helping enterprises transform their businesses through cloud adoption? Do you thrive at the intersection of deep technical expertise and strategic industry knowledge?
AWS Professional Services is seeking a Sr. Cloud Architect to join our team in UK. In this role, you will work directly with customers across Financial Services (FS) to design and implement transformative cloud solutions on AWS. You will serve as a trusted technical advisor, leading complex cloud architecture engagements from strategy through delivery, while mentoring teams and shaping best practices across the region.
Key job responsibilities
- Lead the design and implementation of complex, enterprise-scale cloud architectures for customers in FS industry
- Serve as a trusted technical advisor to C-level executives and senior technical stakeholders, translating business requirements into scalable cloud solutions
- Drive cloud adoption strategies including migration, modernization, data analytics, AI/ML, and hybrid architectures tailored to industry-specific needs
- Develop and deliver technical architectures, reference implementations, and proof-of-concepts that accelerate customer outcomes
- Mentor and guide junior architects, contributing to team development and raising the technical bar across engagements
- Collaborate with AWS service teams to influence product roadmaps based on customer feedback and industry trends
- Contribute to reusable assets, frameworks, and methodologies that scale ProServe's impact across the region
- Navigate regulatory, compliance, and data sovereignty requirements specific to financial services landscape
About the team
At AWS ProServe, you won't just build architectures — you'll shape the cloud journeys of most innovative companies in financial services. Your work will have direct, measurable impact on organizations that drive the region's economy.
AWS Professional Services works with enterprise customers and partners to help them realize their desired business outcomes through cloud adoption. Our team assists customers in achieving the greatest value from AWS by providing guidance, best practices, and hands-on delivery support throughout their cloud journey.
- Bachelor's degree, or experience in a professional field or military
- Experience as technical specialist in design and architecture
- Experience in cloud based solution (AWS or equivalent), system, network and operating system
- Strong proficiency in infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD pipelines, Data, containerization, serverless architectures, and modern application development patterns
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to present complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences
- Experience with data analytics and AI/ML architectures, including data lakes, real-time streaming, and generative AI solutions on AWS
- Proven track record of building and maintaining executive-level relationships (CTO/CIO) and influencing technology strategy at the organizational level
- Experience contributing to pre-sales activities including proposals, statements of work (SOW), and business development efforts within a professional services or consulting organization

About the job

Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact.

A Day in the Life

As Principal Financial Analyst in our Global Supply Chain Finance CoE you’ll be supporting our financial processes and analysis on manufacturing Freight & Duties. You’ll not only work with Finance leaders but also work closely with Transportation, Supply Chain, Analytics & Insights to drive financial performance, forecasting, reporting, governance, and decision support. With your strong financial and analytical skills and the ability to influence stakeholders, lead initiatives, drive accountability, and deliver results in a complex global environment, you’ll help Medtronic to keep our costs under control.
You’ll work in a hybrid format, both in our office in Heerlen or Galway (for a minimum of two days a week), and with a possibility to working from home. We expect you to be located +- within one hour of either the Heerlen office or the Galway office.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Financial Planning & Performance Management: Lead forecasting, planning, month end closing and reporting processes for Freight & Duties.
  • Develop financial models and driver-based forecasting methodologies and analyse financial performance, identify risks and opportunities, and provide actionable recommendations.
  • Partner with the Logistics organisation, Site finance teams, Analytics & Insights and other Finance stakeholders to support business decisions, translate operational drivers into meaningful financial insights and provide clear and concise communication to both finance and non-finance audiences. With these teams, drive accountability through strong ownership, follow-up, and execution
  • Drive consistency and accuracy in forecasting, reporting, and financial management processes, while strengthening financial controls, data quality, and governance.
  • Serve as a subject matter expert within Freight & Duties Finance, building trusted partnerships and effectively navigate a complex, matrixed environment and coaching development of less experienced team members where needed
  • Lead cross-functional initiatives and influence decisions across multiple stakeholder groups.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or related field.
  • Minimum 8 years of relevant finance experience.
  • Fully proficient in Excel, able to work with large data sets in a (database) structured way showing strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience in financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and business partnering.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, working independently and managing multiple priorities.
  • Demonstrated ownership and accountability with a track record of delivering commitments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in supply chain, logistics, transportation, freight, duties, or operations finance, along with experience using SAP, OneStream, Power BI, PowerPoint, transportation management systems, or similar platforms, and a proven track record of leading cross-functional initiatives, driving process improvements, and influencing stakeholders at multiple levels of the organization.

Recruitment Fraud Alert

We are aware of phishing scams targeting job seekers. Please keep the following in mind:

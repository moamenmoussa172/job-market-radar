Role overview

The Senior Database Administrator is responsible for the operational stability, technical evolution, and continuous improvement of the enterprise database platforms and engines. The role combines approximately 40% operational responsibilities with 60% project, engineering, and technology evolution activities. Acting as a senior technical authority and subject matter expert, the Senior DBA provides leadership in database architecture, platform standards, performance, availability, security, automation, and lifecycle management across the enterprise.

The role works closely with Infrastructure, Cloud, Application, Security, and Architecture teams to ensure database services are reliable, scalable, secure, cost-effective, and aligned with enterprise technology strategy.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Database Platform Ownership & Evolution: Own the technical lifecycle and evolution of enterprise database platforms, including technology selection, upgrades, modernization, consolidation, migration, and retirement.
  • Architecture & Technical Standards: Define and maintain database architecture, engineering standards, reference architectures, configuration standards, and technology patterns across on-premises and cloud environments.
  • Operational Reliability & Resilience: Provide senior-level operational support covering availability, capacity, performance, patching, backup/recovery, high availability, disaster recovery, and resolution of complex incidents.
  • Performance & Capacity Engineering: Analyze database and workload performance, identify systemic bottlenecks, establish capacity requirements, and implement optimization strategies to improve performance, scalability, and resource utilization.
  • Project & Solution Engineering: Lead or provide senior technical expertise for database-related infrastructure and application projects, including migrations, cloud adoption, modernization, and major technology changes.
  • Automation & DevOps Integration: Develop and promote automation for database provisioning, configuration, maintenance, monitoring, and lifecycle activities, integrating database management with enterprise DevOps and Infrastructure-as-Code practices.
  • Security & Technical Governance: Ensure database platforms meet enterprise requirements for security, access control, encryption, vulnerability remediation,compliance, and business continuity while providing technical guidance to project and operational teams.
  • Technical Leadership & Continuous Improvement: Act as the senior database SME, lead complex root-cause investigations, advise cross-functional teams, identify improvement opportunities, and contribute to the enterprise database technology roadmap.

How to thrive in this role?

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education and professional experience.
  • 8+ years of professional experience in database administration, database engineering, or database platform management, with demonstrated senior-level responsibility.
  • Strong hands-on experience with enterprise database platforms, including installation, configuration, upgrades, patching, performance tuning, backup/recovery, high availability, and troubleshooting.
  • Strong knowledge of at least one major enterprise database platform such as Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle, PostgreSQL, or equivalent, with the ability to work across multiple database technologies.
  • Demonstrated experience with database architecture, platform lifecycle management, migrations, modernization, and technology upgrades.
  • Experience supporting databases across on-premises and multi-cloud environments, including Azure, AWS and GCP.
  • Strong understanding of database security, access management, encryption, disaster recovery, high availability, business continuity, and capacity management.
  • Working knowledge of scripting and automation such as PowerShell, Python, Bash, or equivalent, together with an understanding of DevOps and Infrastructure-as-Code practices.
  • Strong analytical, communication, and collaboration skills, with the ability to independently resolve complex technical problems and act as a senior technical SME and advisor across Infrastructure, Cloud, DevOps, Architecture, Security, and Application teams. professional experience.
  • Fluency in English and French, both written and spoken.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with database automation, CI/CD, Infrastructure-as-Code, configuration management, and enterprise monitoring/observability.
  • Relevant database certifications, such as Microsoft, Oracle, or PostgreSQL certifications.
  • Experience establishing or evolving enterprise database standards, reference architectures, technology roadmaps, preferably within a large, multinational, regulated, or highly available enterprise environment.

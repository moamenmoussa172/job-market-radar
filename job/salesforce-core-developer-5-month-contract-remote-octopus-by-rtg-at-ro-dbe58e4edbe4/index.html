About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Jordan.

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced Salesforce Core Developer to join our team on a 4-month remote contract. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in Salesforce development, Lightning Web Components (LWC), and Salesforce integrations. You will work closely with technical and business teams to design, develop, and support scalable Salesforce solutions while ensuring seamless integration with external systems.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain custom Salesforce solutions using Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Visualforce, and Salesforce configuration tools.
  • Design and implement scalable business logic and custom Salesforce functionality.
  • Build, enhance, and maintain Salesforce integrations using REST APIs and other integration technologies.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve issues across end-to-end integration flows.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Perform code reviews and ensure adherence to Salesforce development best practices.
  • Support testing, deployment, and production releases.
  • Document technical solutions and provide ongoing support throughout the project lifecycle.

Requirements

  • 4+ years of hands-on Salesforce development experience.
  • Strong expertise in Salesforce Apex development.
  • Proven experience developing Lightning Web Components (LWC) (mandatory).
  • Solid experience designing and implementing Salesforce integrations using REST APIs.
  • Experience troubleshooting and supporting complex integration flows.
  • Good understanding of Salesforce platform architecture and development best practices.
  • Experience with Git and CI/CD processes is an advantage.
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently in a fully remote environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with MuleSoft integrations (highly preferred).
  • Salesforce Platform Developer I or II certification is a plus.
  • Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams.

Contract Details

  • Employment Type: Contract
  • Duration: 4 Months
  • Location: Remote
  • Start Date: Immediate or as soon as possi

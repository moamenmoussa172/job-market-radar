What you’ll do

Role Purpose:

As Network and SW Engineer:

  • You will take care of the SW supporting the required IMS products and solutions
  • he/she will write SW development requirements and specifications,
  • he/she will do SW design, implementation, test and deploy of those IMS services and solutions according to those requirements and specifications
  • he/she will ensure HW and SW performance, monitoring, and reporting, using dedicated SW tools to assure traffic needs capacity
  • You will manage IMS Services technology evolution and capacity planning based on traffic needs.
  • You will manage LCM and Security for IMS services and solutions

Key accountabilities and decision ownership:

  • Accountable for writing SW development requirements, specifications, design SW integration plans and perform SW testing for the required products and solutions for IMS.
  • Accountable for designing, implementing, testing and deploying SW and HW requirements for the required IMS services and solutions to cope with specific commercial requirements or technology evolution inside Vodafone.
  • Accountable for managing IMS Services technology evolution and capacity planning based on traffic needs. Ensure HW and SW performance, monitoring, and reporting, using dedicated SW tools.
  • Manage RFQ and vendor selection to identify the best partner for the evolution of IMS service including the option of internal SW development.
  • Manage LCM and Security

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Proficient with designing and deploying solutions in cloud environments
  • Experience in developing in C...
  • Familiar in IMS Networks.
  • Awareness of wider technology aspects in telecommunications (Core Network, Value Added Services, ...) and ICT
  • Understanding of network architectural Patterns & Design, aligning architecture with business goals.
  • Work closely with product owners, business analysts, and cross-functional teams to align architecture with business goals.
  • Familiarity embedding security and compliance into architectural decision

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering/Telco Engineering, or a related field
  • SW Engineering and Cloud Architecture Proficiency
  • Agile and Application life cycle management methodologies
  • DevOps and CI/CD Expertise
  • Security and Compliance Knowledge
  • Awareness of technology aspects in IMS
  • Understanding of network Architecture Patterns
  • Excellent Communication and Leadership Skills

Graduate AI & Automation Engineer

Bosch Group · Nuneaton, United Kingdom

Onsite · Junior · Software Engineer · Posted 2 November 2026

About the job

The Graduate AI & Automation Engineer will support the development and deployment of practical AI and automation solutions across BEG UK. Working with business and engineering teams, the purpose of the role is to help develop solutions that deliver measurable business benefit.

Key responsibilities:

  • Develop proof-of-concepts using AI technologies to address business and engineering challenges.
  • Work with stakeholders to develop concepts into practical solutions used across BEG UK.
  • Support the creation of AI assistants, automation solutions and reusable tools.
  • Create training and communication materials to support solution adoption.
  • Document solutions and engage with users to increase solution utilisation.
  • Support AI training sessions, workshops and knowledge-sharing activities.
  • Degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering, Engineering, Mathematics or a related discipline.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience with programming, automation or data analysis through projects or personal learning.
  • Good communication skills, ability to work independently and across multiple teams.

This is a 24-month graduate role beginning in September 2027. This position is open to recent graduates looking for a graduate level opportunity.

Location: you will be based at the Nuneaton office.

As a Graduate at Bosch, you will participate in an exclusive learning and development programme designed to support you through your graduate journey. It includes in-person sessions on topics including Assertiveness and How to Manage Conflict provided by expert external trainers.

Interview Process and Right to Work Information:

Shortlisted candidates will then be invited to participate in a face-to-face interview by the hiring team and/or an online assessment centre.

You must have the right to live and work in the UK when you start your graduate role and for the full duration of your graduate programme. Please note that we cannot offer visa sponsorship for this role at this current time.

Job description & summary

Are you an experienced data professional who enjoys connecting business needs with technology and designing solutions that work at enterprise scale? Join our team as a Data Architect and take ownership of designing modern data solutions for a variety of clients, including organizations in the Czech Republic and abroad.

As a Data Architect, you’ll work at the intersection of business, data, and technology. You’ll translate complex requirements into robust data architectures, guide engineering teams during implementation, and help clients make the right technology decisions.

You will:

  • Design end-to-end data architectures covering data ingestion, integration, storage, transformation, analytics, and consumption.
  • Translate business and data requirements into scalable, secure, and maintainable solution designs.
  • Design modern data platforms using technologies such as Databricks, Snowflake, MS Fabric, and cloud-native services across Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  • Define data models, integration patterns, data flows, and architectural standards.
  • Work closely with Data Engineers, analytics teams, and business stakeholders to turn architecture into working solutions.
  • Guide technical teams during implementation and provide architectural oversight throughout delivery.
  • Lead architecture and design workshops with clients and communicate technical decisions clearly to senior stakeholders.
  • Support technology selection and evaluate architectural trade-offs, including scalability, security, maintainability, and cost.
  • Contribute to best practices in data governance, security, CI/CD, and Infrastructure as Code.
  • Stay close to delivery and help teams solve complex technical and architectural challenges.

What matters to us

  • Proven experience designing data architectures and delivering complex data solutions, ideally in enterprise environments.
  • Strong understanding of modern data architecture concepts, including data lakes, Lakehouse, data warehousing, data modeling, and integration patterns.
  • Hands-on experience with modern data technologies such as Databricks, Snowflake, MS Fabric, and at least one major cloud platform such as Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  • Good understanding of data governance, security, cloud engineering, and software engineering principles.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into clear architecture and make pragmatic technology decisions.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to lead workshops and explain complex technical topics to both technical and business audiences.

What will help you stand out

  • Experience defining reference architectures or data architecture standards across multiple projects.
  • Relevant cloud, data, or architecture certifications.
  • Experience with large-scale data platform modernization or cloud migration programmes.
  • Knowledge of data mesh, data fabric, metadata management, or modern data governance approaches.
  • Experience with batch and real-time integration architectures and technologies such as Kafka or similar platforms.
  • Experience incorporating AI/LLM capabilities into data architecture, including RAG, vector databases, or AI governance.

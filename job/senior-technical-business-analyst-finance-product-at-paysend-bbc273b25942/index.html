About the job

About The Role:

We are hiring a product-oriented, analytically fluent Senior Technical Business Analyst - Finance Product to help transform our core financial operations, settlement, and reconciliation infrastructure into a unified, automated product ecosystem.

Our Finance Platform sits at the heart of high-volume, multi-currency payment flows - spanning our Settlement & Reconciliation (SAR) engine, Paysend Data Platform and ERP (NetSuite), Treasury balance/FX feeds, Global Profile Service, Tariff Service and external payment partner integrations. Today, parts of these workflows rely on manual ingestion checks, spreadsheet trackers, ad-hoc file-parsing fixes, and fragmented triage across Settlement, Finance, and Engineering. Working closely with Settlement & Reconciliation, Treasury, and Finance, your mission is to step into that operational reality, cut through manual tracker sprawl, diagnose why exceptions happen at the root, and replace manual workarounds with standardised, automated product capabilities - progressively shrinking routine operational overhead while staying hands-on with complex partner integrations and edge cases.


What You'll Do (Responsibilities):
Product & Process Transformation: Re-engineer manual settlement, reconciliation, and partner data-ingestion workflows into linear, automated product capabilities - partnering closely with Settlement & Reconciliation, Treasury, Finance, Paysend Data Platform, and Engineering to consolidate fragmented operational streams into a single, cohesive data universe, including retiring manual trackers, shaping internal reconciliation tooling/UIs, and supporting legacy BI migrations onto the Paysend Data Platform (Databricks).

  • Pragmatic Solutioning & Bidirectional Translation: Review and refine incoming Business Requirements Documents (BRDs) and requests to uncover true underlying needs, or challenge and bypass unnecessary documentation when an existing platform capability simply requires configuration or refinement. Act as a bidirectional translator - turning business intent into crisp technical specifications, field mappings, and acceptance criteria for Engineering, while translating technical constraints and engineering feedback back into clear business options.
  • Partner Onboarding & External Data Integration: Drive the technical and operational onboarding of new banking and acquiring or payout partners - defining report delivery methods (API, SFTP, automated feeds), mapping partner transaction files, Statements of Account (SOA), and FX/balance APIs into SAR, configuring partner profile identifiers, and validating end-to-end transaction matching from test scenarios to live production.
  • Root-Cause Investigation & Operational Triage: Own the investigation of complex settlement exceptions, unmatched transactions, and file-parsing discrepancies - bridging Settlement Operations, Treasury, Finance, DevOps, and Engineering to resolve immediate blockers while designing systemic fixes that prevent recurrence.
  • Data Lineage & Analytical Validation: Interrogate transactional and ledger datasets across analytics platforms (such as Databricks) to trace data flows, validate reconciliation logic, and verify that delivered product changes meet financial control and operational standards through User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

What You’ll Need To Be Successful In This Role:

We are looking for a commercially minded, technically fluent analyst who thrives on turning messy operational realities into clean, repeatable product workflows.

Core Capabilities:

  • Pragmatic Business Analysis & Solution Design: Proven track record leading technical business analysis in fintech, payments, banking, or financial systems - able to dissect existing BRDs, steer solution design, propose practical alternatives, and translate bi-directionally between business teams and Engineering without getting bogged down in heavy documentation for its own sake.
  • Payments, Settlement & Reconciliation Fluency: Solid understanding of payment lifecycles, card-scheme and partner payout rails, multi-currency transaction flows, clearing/settlement, ledger accounting interfaces, and multi-identifier transaction/SOA reconciliations.
  • Hands-On SQL & Data Interrogation: Confident, hands-on fluency in SQL and data/BI platforms (e.g., Databricks/Snowflake, Metabase/Preset/Superset) to independently query large transactional datasets, trace data lineage, and uncover root causes behind anomalies without waiting on Data Engineering.
  • External Partner & Integration Acumen: Practical experience working with external payment/banking partners, APIs, SFTP file feeds, and schema mappings - comfortable communicating directly with external partner teams to resolve file or matching issues.
  • Product-First Problem-Solving: Drive to look beyond immediate ticket triage and build scalable, automated workflows, paired with strong stakeholder alignment across Settlement, Treasury, Finance, Engineering, and DevOps in a flat, fast-moving structure.

Desirable (Not Mandatory):

  • Working familiarity with Python (or similar scripting tools) for exploratory data analysis and reconciliation checks.
  • Experience with ERP/accounting platforms (e.g., NetSuite), Treasury balance/FX automation, or regulatory reporting data pipelines.

What Success Looks Like (First 3-6 Months)

  • Take ownership of core SAR partner-ingestion, reconciliation, and Treasury feed workflows - resolving active bottlenecks while mapping out the automation roadmap.
  • Standardise partner onboarding and file-mapping specifications so new acquiring or payout channels integrate into the SAR Ledger with minimal manual intervention.
  • Steer pragmatic, technically grounded solutions and lead UAT, where required, across priority Finance Product initiatives, measurably reducing recurring manual triage between Settlement, Treasury, and Engineering.

About the job

Come join and lead the Workload Resilience Team! The Workload Resilience team is focused on improving the availability of MongoDB through solutions in the data plane. We build out the core server workload management infrastructure so that MongoDB can properly queue, load shed, and prioritize operations to stabilize performance during situations where system resources are beginning to become constrained. Workload Resilience is the team behind the recent release of Intelligent Workload Management, a new product feature that introduces dynamic rate limiting and operation prioritizations when the database is overloaded.

Our team champions a strong culture of inclusivity, diversity, and collaboration, and lives MongoDB cultural values every day – we value intellectual curiosity and honesty, building together in an environment that prioritizes collaboration over competition, and working urgently to deliver value to our customers each and every day. If you want to lead a fast-moving team that applies distributed systems, operating systems, operational excellence, performance tuning, and queueing theory to maximize the resilience of MongoDB at scale, join us!

This role is based in North America.

Candidate Profile

  • 3+ years of experience managing a team of software engineers, including hiring, performance and growth management, compensation planning, and mentoring.
  • You have 8+ years of experience building and operating mission-critical low-level and high performance distributed systems
  • Bonus points for experience with performance profiling, codebases in Rust or C++, testing resiliency of distributed systems, queueing theory, and/or managing platforms with both internal and external stakeholders
  • You have experience owning the operations of a projection service
  • You have strong technical judgment that you use to effectively guide engineering decisions that balance tradeoffs in performance, availability, and customer experience
  • You embody customer obsession and don't hesitate to cross team boundaries in search of the right solution
  • You have delivered complex, cross-functional projects to customers that required coordination across many teams, under tight timelines
  • You enjoy coaching, career development, and creating growth opportunities to help your team reach their full potential
  • You have experience building a diverse, inclusive team where engineers can do their best work, operating greater than the sum of its parts

Position Expectations

  • Lead a team of engineers, managing all aspects of people management including hiring, growth, compensation, and performance management.
  • Be directly responsible for the results your team delivers
  • Lead the design, implementation, and operation of:
  • A service in Rust that operates in production on MongoDB Atlas to protect the availability of the database, with a focus on operational excellence
  • Production database components that protect the availability of the database, manage customer workloads, and help customers and MongoDB diagnosticians observe the behavior of the database
  • Systems for preventing the introduction of availability regressions into the Server codebase
  • Own the definition and execution of the team's 12-month roadmap for the Intelligent Workload Management (IWM) tuning platform, the Server features at the core of IWM, and the availability testing systems
  • Work with Product Management, Program Management, and Engineering leadership to specify, prioritize, and deliver new features
  • Handle (or lead the effort to handle) time-sensitive availability incidents and customer escalations
  • Estimate task complexity, communicate delivery timelines, and manage risks for projects executed by the team
  • Ensure the team is running smoothly by eliminating technical barriers, coordinating cross-team dependencies, and focusing on the overall health and happiness of the team
  • Contribute to planning for organizational growth, including allocation of engineering resources, future hiring plans, and assignment of projects

Success Measures

  • In the first month: Understand the high-level architecture of the Intelligent Workload Management (IWM) system, how the key components of the Atlas Data Plane fit together, MongoDB's availability testing framework, the team's current projects, and the required day-to-day processes. Begin developing relationships with key stakeholders across the company
  • In three months: Take over full management of the team and begin building trust with your reports. Assume responsibility for day-to-day team operations. Be involved in reviewing designs for new features and helping the team navigate technical challenges. Hire engineers for the team as needed
  • In six months: Establish yourself as a leader in the organization such that team members naturally come to you for guidance. Take over full ownership of the team's software components. Manage the planning and execution of major features supporting the team’s major annual goals
  • In twelve months: Perform a full annual cycle of performance and growth conversations with your team members. Contribute to the long-term vision for the IWM stack and develop a plan to achieve that vision. Increase the capability and velocity of the team in measurable ways

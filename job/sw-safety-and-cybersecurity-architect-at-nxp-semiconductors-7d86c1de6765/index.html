SW Safety & Cybersecurity Architect

NXP Semiconductors · Sibiu, Romania

Onsite · Senior · Security Engineer · Posted 5 November 2026

About the job

Ready to join the future of innovation in IT at NXP? Become part of the startup of a dynamic team that is leading NXP on a digital transformation journey. Your role is to be a critical team member of the global NXP AP SW quality organization. There is a lot of room for new ideas and innovations and you will be supported to have a continuous focus on development, coaching and creating a supportive environment for your team. As Principal SW Safety Architect, you will be responsible to ensure that the NXP software products delivered to the automotive customers are compliant with ISO 26262 and we have all the controls and org setup in place in the BL Automotive SW R&D to achieve this.

As SW Cybersecurity Responsible, you will be contributing to ensure that the NXP software products delivered to the automotive customers are compliant with ISO 21434 and we have all the controls and org setup in place in the Safety Competence Center to serve the BL Automotive to achieve this.

  • Responsible for creation of VID, CS Plan and Cybersecurity Case.
  • Responsible for reviewing and approving all ISO 21434 work products (ISO specific and Security Maturity Process shared deliverables - req, code, test planning)
  • Responsible for the CRA deployment and compliance for the SW products.
  • Proposes process updates in order to comply with existing security standards
  • Responsible for reviewing MSG products.
  • Offers guidance for achieving security deliverables compliance.
  • Interface with the Security assessors to make sure the project timelines are met.

To be successful in this role you have:

  • BS or MS in Electronics or Computer Science or equivalent
  • At least 10 years experience in Automotive domain
  • ISO 21434 knowledge and certificates
  • SW development background
  • Technical leadership experience
  • Basic ASPICE understanding and ability to interpret and apply quality standards
  • Ability to define a software process by defining roles, activities and deliverables
  • Great communication and collaborations skills.

About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Develop and enhance enterprise Knowledge Management, AI Search, and Virtual Agent experiences on ServiceNow. Build secure, intelligent self-service solutions integrating knowledge, conversational workflows, Now Assist, GenAI/RAG capabilities, Flow Designer, and enterprise APIs, with a strong focus on usability, access control, quality, and reliable fulfillment.

Key Responsibilities

Knowledge Management & Search

  • Knowledge Lifecycle: Configure knowledge bases, templates, workflows, approvals, publishing, retirement, metadata, and access controls.
  • Knowledge Development: Build Knowledge Management enhancements and integrations using JavaScript, Flow Designer, and platform APIs.
  • AI Search: Configure and optimize content sources, indexing, relevance, permissions, and knowledge retrieval.

Virtual Agent & Conversational AI

  • Virtual Agent Development: Design and test topics, dialogue flows, prompts, fallback journeys, and live-agent handoffs.
  • Conversational Fulfillment: Integrate Virtual Agent with Service Catalog, knowledge, workflows, and APIs for automated fulfillment.
  • Channel Integration: Enable and support conversational experiences across Microsoft Teams and other supported channels.

AI & Knowledge Enablement

  • Now Assist & GenAI: Integrate Now Assist and AI capabilities into knowledge and conversational experiences where applicable.
  • RAG & Grounding: Support LLM/RAG and knowledge-graph use cases using trusted content, metadata, grounding, provenance, and controlled retrieval.
  • AI Guardrails: Apply privacy, role-based access, human approval, escalation, and responsible AI controls.

Quality & Production

  • Testing: Validate knowledge visibility, search relevance, conversation flows, permissions, integrations, and fulfillment.
  • Performance & Operations: Monitor search quality, Virtual Agent performance, user feedback, fallbacks, and production issues.
  • Support & Documentation: Troubleshoot platform/integration issues and maintain technical documentation and runbooks.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications:

  • Experience: 7–12 years overall, with strong hands-on ServiceNow development experience.
  • Knowledge Management: Strong experience with Knowledge Management, lifecycle, metadata, permissions, and search.
  • Virtual Agent: Hands-on experience with Virtual Agent topics, conversational workflows, testing, and fulfillment.
  • ServiceNow Development: Strong JavaScript, Flow Designer, REST API, integration, and troubleshooting skills.
  • AI Search & Retrieval: Experience with AI Search and working knowledge of NLP, LLM, RAG, and knowledge-grounding concepts.
  • Security & Access: Experience implementing role-based access, privacy, and secure knowledge retrieval.

Additional / Preferred Experience:

  • Now Assist & AI Agents: Exposure to Now Assist, AI Agent Studio, GenAI, and agentic workflows.
  • Enterprise Knowledge Integration: Experience integrating external knowledge/data sources through APIs and enterprise data platforms.

Certifications:

  • ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) – mandatory.
  • ServiceNow Certified Application Developer (CAD) – preferred.
  • Relevant ServiceNow training in Virtual Agent, AI Search, Now Assist, GenAI, or AI-driven Knowledge Management – preferred

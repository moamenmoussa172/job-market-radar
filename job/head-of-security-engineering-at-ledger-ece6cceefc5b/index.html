About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger.

At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries.

The team

You'll join the Security team will lead Ledger's security engineers, and stay hands-on. You lead the Blue team that defends Ledger's corporate, development and production environments. Your team threat-models and risk-assesses every feature, app and service Ledger builds or uses, runs the company's material security controls, and is the expert escalation on critical cyber incidents.

What you’ll be doing

  • Lead the Blue team defending Ledger's corporate, development and production environments: priorities, planning, hiring and reviews, while staying hands-on yourself.
  • Run threat modelling and risk assessment for every new feature, application and service Ledger builds or adopts, internal or third-party, and turn the findings into owned, prioritised engineering work.
  • Own and run the company's material security controls: cloud guardrails and access control, identity, endpoint protection, CI/CD and release integrity (code signing, protected pipelines), change control on production, and secrets management.
  • Bring offensive knowledge to the team's work and partner closely with the Red Team: design controls the way an attacker would approach them, turn Red Team findings into owned engineering work, and have fixes re-tested by the Red Team or external testers.
  • Act as Tier 3 experts on critical cyber incidents with Security Operations: deep technical analysis, containment and recovery.
  • Secure the use of AI coding assistants and agents in engineering, with clear rules on access, data and approvals.
  • Own the engineering roadmap for security tooling and controls, report progress to the CSO, and give Legal the technical facts it needs for regulatory reporting.
  • Coach the team and keep a clear on-call and review rhythm.

What we’re looking for

  • 10+ years in security engineering, including 3+ years leading a team, and still hands-on.
  • Hands-on threat modelling and risk assessment of applications, services and cloud architectures.
  • Offensive security knowledge and techniques (penetration testing, red teaming or exploit development), enough to think like an attacker when designing defences.
  • Hands-on credibility in cloud, application and product security, with strong infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar) and Kubernetes security.
  • Experience owning and operating security controls at company scale: identity, endpoint, CI/CD, secrets and cloud access.
  • Incident response experience as a senior technical escalation on serious incidents.
  • Ability to work with Product, Engineering and Legal; fluent English is a must, French a plus.

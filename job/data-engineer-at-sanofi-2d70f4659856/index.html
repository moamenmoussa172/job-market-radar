About the job

As Data Engineer within our Digital Data team, you'll design and build the cloud-based data solutions that power Sanofi's data and AI teams, and help establish our new data technical delivery center in Budapest. Ready to get started?

Join the digital engine driving Sanofi's transformation, where AI, automation, and bold experimentation power faster science and smarter decisions. Our Digital Data team works from hubs in Paris, Lyon, Barcelona, Cambridge, Bridgewater and Toronto, and is now opening a new office in Budapest. Here you'll help build the first biopharma company powered by AI at scale, with world-class mentorship and the chance to shape a brand-new team within an established organization.

About Sanofi

We're an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people's lives and delivering compelling growth. About the role

  • Lead the technical design and development of cloud-first data pipelines and data engineering solutions, working from business and technical requirements.
  • Work with business, product and analytics colleagues to break down high-level needs into clear technical plans, timelines and priorities.
  • Improve existing pipelines (ETL) so they run faster, more reliably and at a sensible cost, and quickly fix issues that affect performance or timeliness.
  • Review the work of peers to keep code quality high, and define good practices for code management, automated testing and deployment.
  • Lead the effort to modernize and replace legacy data solutions and platforms, reusing or creating standard Sanofi data pipelines that deliver value to business use cases.
  • Prepare clear documentation for knowledge sharing, training and maintenance, and present findings to stakeholders at all levels.
  • Contribute to the Data Engineering community and stay up to date with emerging technologies, using AI-assisted development tools to boost productivity.

About you

  • Experience: Hands-on experience in data engineering, data warehousing or a similar role, including big data integration on cloud platforms such as AWS and Snowflake, and working with large data sets (performance and query optimization).
  • Technical skills: Strong Python and sql scripting, solid grasp of data structures and algorithms, good knowledge of relational databases. Experience with job orchestration is expected; Airflow is a plus.
  • Ways of working: Experience with agile/scrum and with cross-functional teams, and the ability to pick up new technologies quickly and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Soft skills: Pragmatic problem solver with attention to detail, a service-oriented and approachable team player, and clear communicator at every level.
  • Education: Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.
  • Languages: Fluent English (other languages are a plus).

Why choose us?

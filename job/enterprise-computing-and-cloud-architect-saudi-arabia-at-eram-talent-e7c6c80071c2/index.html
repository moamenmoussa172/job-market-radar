About the job

The Enterprise Computing & Cloud Architect is responsible for defining and governing the enterprise architecture for computing and infrastructure platforms across on-premises environments, private cloud, public cloud, and High-Performance Computing (HPC) and AI infrastructure.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own and maintain the enterprise architecture for computing platforms across on-premises, private cloud, public cloud, HPC, and AI environments.
  • Define enterprise target architectures, reference architectures, technology standards, principles, and design patterns for computing and cloud platforms.
  • Develop and maintain strategic technology roadmaps aligned with business objectives, enterprise architecture strategy, and emerging technology trends.
  • Provide architecture leadership and assurance for major programs, transformation initiatives, and complex technology solutions.
  • Evaluate and recommend infrastructure, cloud, computing, virtualization, containerization, HPC, and AI technologies based on enterprise requirements.
  • Establish architectural principles and standards covering scalability, availability, performance, security, resilience, interoperability, and cost optimization.
  • Ensure alignment between solution architectures and the enterprise architecture, technology standards, and strategic direction.
  • Lead architecture reviews and provide technical governance throughout the solution lifecycle.
  • Assess current-state environments and develop transition architectures and migration strategies toward the target state.
  • Define cloud adoption, hybrid cloud, workload placement, and infrastructure modernization strategies.
  • Collaborate with cybersecurity, data, applications, networking, operations, and other architecture domains to ensure integrated enterprise solutions.
  • Provide architectural guidance for high-performance and AI computing platforms, including compute capacity, acceleration technologies, scalability, and integration requirements.
  • Assess technology risks, technical debt, architectural dependencies, and opportunities for modernization.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
  • Master’s degree in a relevant field is preferred.
  • 10+ years of experience in technology, infrastructure, cloud, or enterprise architecture, with significant experience in large-scale enterprise environments.
  • Demonstrated experience designing and governing complex computing and infrastructure platforms.
  • Strong experience across on-premises infrastructure and public/private cloud environments.
  • Experience with HPC and/or AI infrastructure architecture is highly desirable.
  • Strong understanding of cloud architecture, virtualization, containers, compute platforms, storage, networking, and infrastructure automation.
  • Experience developing enterprise architecture standards, reference architectures, technology roadmaps, and governance frameworks.
  • Proven experience providing architectural assurance for major programs and complex technology implementations.
  • Strong understanding of security, resilience, availability, scalability, performance, and infrastructure cost optimization principles.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Certifications

Relevant architecture, cloud, or technology certifications such as:

  • TOGAF or equivalent enterprise architecture certification.
  • AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud architecture certifications.
  • Relevant VMware, Kubernetes, HPC, AI infrastructure, or other specialist technology certifications.

Key Competencies

  • Enterprise & Technology Architecture
  • Cloud & Hybrid Cloud Architecture
  • Computing & Infrastructure Architecture
  • HPC & AI Infrastructure
  • Architecture Governance & Assurance
  • Technology Strategy & Roadmapping
  • Solution Evaluation & Design
  • Technology Risk Management
  • Stakeholder & Vendor Management
  • Strategic Thinking and Innovation

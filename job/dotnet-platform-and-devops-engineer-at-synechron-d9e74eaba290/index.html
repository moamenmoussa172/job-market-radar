About the job

At Synechron, we harness the power of digital transformation to drive business success. As a global consulting firm, we combine creativity with advanced technology to deliver innovative solutions across industries. Recognized with multiple employer awards, we are committed to building strong expert teams and a collaborative, high-impact work environment.

We are hiring a .NET Platform & DevOps Engineer.

Hybrid working model - Novi Sad or Belgrade

Role Description

We are looking for a hands-on .NET Platform Engineer / DevOps Engineer to help improve the tools, platforms, and workflows used by engineering teams. In this role, you will contribute to building scalable developer platforms, automating delivery processes, enhancing cloud-based solutions, and improving the overall developer experience.

You will work closely with engineering teams to streamline software development, testing, deployment, and operational practices, enabling faster and more reliable delivery of high-quality software.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain internal platforms, tooling, and automation solutions that improve developer productivity and software delivery
  • Build and enhance services, integrations, and workflows using .NET technologies, supporting modern cloud-native engineering practices
  • Improve developer experience by streamlining build, test, deployment, and release processes through automation and reusable engineering patterns
  • Contribute to CI/CD platform improvements using GitHub and Azure DevOps, including deployment automation, templates, and operational tooling
  • Implement and enhance application health monitoring, observability, reliability, and operational reporting capabilities
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to improve platform security, scalability, resilience, and overall engineering effectiveness
  • Support adoption of modern development practices, including AI-assisted engineering tools and cloud-based development workflows
  • Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code, while contributing to code reviews, technical documentation, and knowledge sharing

Your Profile

Must Have Requirements

  • Strong hands-on software engineering experience, with solid proficiency in .NET development
  • Experience building backend services, internal tooling, automation solutions, or platform engineering capabilities
  • Strong understanding of software development lifecycle practices, including version control, automated testing, and code reviews
  • Experience working with APIs, system integrations, and distributed applications
  • Hands-on experience with CI/CD platforms such as GitHub Actions and Azure DevOps
  • Experience with containerization technologies such as Docker
  • Ability to troubleshoot complex technical problems and work effectively across multiple engineering teams
  • Strong communication, collaboration, and documentation skills

Additional Experience Considered an Advantage

For this role, candidates should bring experience in several of the areas listed below. The specific combination of skills may vary depending on the team and platform focus.

  • AWS services, including Amazon EKS, DynamoDB, S3, and cloud-based test environment automation
  • Kubernetes platforms (EKS or AKS), Helm, and container orchestration technologies
  • Infrastructure as Code and cloud automation using tools such as Terraform
  • Application modernization and on-premises to cloud migration initiatives
  • Application health checks, deployment health monitoring, observability, and platform reliability improvements
  • AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot and similar engineering productivity solutions
  • Experience working in high-availability, mission-critical, or trading platform environments
  • Knowledge of .NET Aspire, TypeScript or Rust
  • Developer experience and engineering enablement platforms such as Backstage (nice to have)

What’s in it for you:

  • Competitive compensation package based on experience and skills
  • Flexible hours and hybrid work options, allowing you to achieve a work-life balance that suits your needs
  • Private health insurance plan
  • Access to continuous training, accredited certifications, and a range of resources to help you grow professionally
  • Personalized leadership training program
  • FitPass membership to support your well-being and mental health support programs
  • Comprehensive Referral Rewards Program

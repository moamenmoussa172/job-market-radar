Department Overview

Motorola Solutions is a global leader in mission-critical communications and public safety technologies. Within our Defense & Tactical Systems division, we equip armed forces with resilient command, control, and tactical communications systems (TETRA, mission-critical broadband, C4ISR). To ensure long-term operational readiness, material maintenance, and comprehensive through-life support for our communication systems aboard German Navy platforms (frigates, corvettes, submarines, and naval shore stations), we are seeking a qualified Systems Engineer (Configuration/Test) (m/f/d).


Job Description

In this critical cross-functional role, you will take ownership of the formal verification, validation, testing, and quality assurance framework for complex naval communications systems across the entire integration and qualification lifecycle:

  • Test Strategy & Verification Planning: Develop and coordinate test concepts, qualification and commissioning test specifications, and detailed test procedures based on functional and non-functional requirement baselines derived from German Defence specifications.
  • Verification & Validation (V&V): Establish and maintain rigorous end-to-end requirements traceability from technical specifications through to test procedures and acceptance records using ALM and requirements management platforms (e.g., IBM DOORS, Polarion).
  • Acceptance Criteria & Test Design: Define structured, testable acceptance criteria (initial condition, action, expected result) serving as the contractual basis for factory and customer milestone sign-offs.
  • Test Execution & Trials: Plan, coordinate, and execute Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT), Harbor Acceptance Tests (HAT), and Sea Acceptance Trials (SAT) in integration laboratories, shipyard facilities, and aboard naval vessels in close collaboration with defense technical centers (e.g., WTD 71/81).
  • Quality Assurance per NATO Standards: Enforce contractual quality requirements in compliance with NATO AQAP 2110 and maintain the project Quality Management Plan in accordance with AQAP 2105.
  • Government Quality Assurance & Compliance: Support and facilitate official Government Quality Assurance (GQA / Güteprüfung) conducted by the Defence, prepare Certificates of Conformity (CoC), and oversee non-conformance reports, root cause analyses, and corrective action implementations (e.g., 8D process).


Basic Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s, Master’s degree, or Diploma in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Systems Engineering, Computer Science, Mechatronics, or an equivalent technical qualification.
  • Professional Experience: Demonstrable track record in technical verification and validation (V&V), test management, or quality engineering for complex, safety-critical systems—ideally within the defense, naval, or aerospace sector.
  • Standards & Methodological Expertise: Proficient understanding of test and acceptance processes per V-Modell XT Bund, quality management standards according to ISO 9001, and NATO quality assurance requirements (AQAP 2110 / AQAP 2105).
  • Certifications: Recognized credentials in testing (e.g., ISTQB Certified Tester) or quality management (e.g., Six Sigma, Quality Representative / Lead Auditor) are considered a strong advantage.
  • Technical Acumen: Working knowledge of telecommunications architectures, maritime environmental qualifications (shock, vibration, and climatic testing per German VG standards / MIL-STD), and electromagnetic compatibility (EMC / TEMPEST).
  • Languages: Professional business proficiency in German (for direct coordination with Defence officials and government quality inspectors) and fluent English (written and spoken).
  • Due to the specific requirements of the project, candidates interested in this position will be required to undergo a background check in accordance with applicable legal requirements. German or EU citizenship may be required, depending on the applicable requirements.

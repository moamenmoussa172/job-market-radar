About the job

Job Description:

Are you passionate about pushing the boundaries of NLP and ML research? Thomson Reuters Labs in London is seeking a motivated research intern to lead an independent research project at the intersection of cutting-edge AI and real-world applications.
What does Thomson Reuters Labs do? We experiment, we build, we deliver. We support the organization and our customers through applied research and development of new products and technologies. Labs innovates collaboratively across our core segments in Legal, Tax & Accounting, Government, and Risk & Fraud Investigation. We undertake a diverse portfolio of projects today while investing in long-term research for the future.

The Opportunity

As a Research Intern, you’ll take ownership of a research project aligned with Thomson Reuters products, working alongside a diverse, interdisciplinary team of global experts. The internship duration at Thomson Reuters Labs is typically six months with a flexible start date aligned with your schedule.

The Project

This internship sits at the intersection of knowledge graphs, Large Language Models, and neuro-symbolic AI. Broadly, you’ll explore how structured, graph-based representations of knowledge can be combined with modern LLMs, spanning topics such as knowledge representation, information extraction, reasoning over structured data, the use of knowledge graphs to ground and evaluate LLM-based and agentic systems, and the construction of benchmarks for these settings. Several project directions are possible, from building and reasoning over graph-based representations to using knowledge graphs as an external source of truth for verifying and evaluating model outputs. There is scope to shape the specific direction with your supervisor as the work develops, and the project is intended to lead to a publication.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Drive Research: Lead experiments to answer cutting-edge research questions, designing evaluations and analysing results
  • Build: Implement LLM-based pipelines and graph-based representations, and the information extraction needed to populate them
  • Innovate: Explore novel approaches and emerging technologies that advance TR’s product portfolio
  • Collaborate & Disseminate: Work with experienced research scientists and share your findings through internal presentations and external publications

Required Qualifications:

  • Academic Foundation: Graduated / currently enrolled in a PhD program in Computer Science, Computational Linguistics, or a related field, with demonstrated research experience (at least 1 publication required)
  • Technical Expertise: Strong understanding of AI/ML methodologies, including hands-on experience with Large Language ModelsWorking knowledge of knowledge graphs: their construction, representation, and querying (e.g. entity/relation extraction, graph traversal, or related graph-based methods)Proven ability to design, implement, and evaluate ML experimentsProficiency in Python and common data and ML libraries
  • Research Skills: Experience working with both structured and unstructured data, including extracting structure from unstructured sourcesExperience designing evaluations and benchmarks, and a data-driven approach to problem-solving
  • Practical Experience: Familiarity with version control systems (GitHub, GitLab). Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud)
  • Collaboration: Strong communication skills and enthusiasm for teamwork

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience building LLM agents or agentic systems (tool use, multi-step reasoning)
  • Exposure to neuro-symbolic approaches combining structured/symbolic representations with neural models
  • Experience with graph databases or query languages (Neo4j, Cypher, SPARQL, RDF)
  • Interest in information extraction, entity resolution, or multi-hop reasoning
  • Interest in LLM evaluation, factuality, or model output verification

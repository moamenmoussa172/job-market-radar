About SAP NS2

SAP National Security Services (SAP NS2) delivers secure cloud solutions supporting the U.S. Federal Government and organizations operating in highly regulated environments. As SAP NS2 expands its SaaS portfolio, this role will shape secure cloud engineering, operations, and platform enablement by leading the Enterprise SaaS Operating Model & Engineering Enablement.

Executive Mission:

Build and continuously evolve SAP NS2's enterprise SaaS operating model. Success is measured by accelerating secure software delivery, improving operational resilience and customer experience, increasing engineering productivity through platform capabilities and automation, and building a scalable operating model for SaaS growth.

About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Develop and enhance ServiceNow solutions across IT Operations Management (ITOM), CMDB/CSDM and AI-driven operations and automation. The role requires strong ServiceNow development skills with hands-on specialization in ITOM and/or CMDB/CSDM and the ability to apply AI, AIOps and intelligent automation across service operations.

Key Responsibilities

IT Operations Management (ITOM)

  • Discovery & Service Mapping: Develop Discovery patterns, schedules and MID Server integrations; configure Service Mapping, CI dependencies and service-impact visibility.
  • Event Management & AIOps: Integrate monitoring and observability tools; implement event ingestion, alert correlation, deduplication, service-health visibility and automated incident/remediation workflows.
  • ITOM Integrations: Integrate infrastructure, cloud, monitoring and external data sources using Service Graph Connectors, APIs and IntegrationHub.
  • Operational Automation: Develop workflows and automation to improve event handling, root-cause analysis, service impact assessment and remediation.

CMDB / Common Service Data Model (CSDM)

  • CSDM & Service Modeling: Configure CI classes, relationships, application/service models and CSDM-aligned structures supporting IT operations and service visibility.
  • IRE & Data Integration: Develop IRE-aware ingestion, identification and reconciliation solutions for Discovery, Service Graph and external data sources.
  • CMDB Health & Data Quality: Implement and automate completeness, correctness, compliance, staleness and duplicate controls; develop exception handling and remediation workflows.
  • Service-Aware Operations: Integrate CMDB/CSDM data with Discovery, Service Mapping, Event Management and operational workflows to enable end-to-end service visibility.

Experience & Qualifications

  • Experience: 7–12 years overall in software/platform engineering, with strong practical ServiceNow development experience.
  • Primary Specialization: Demonstrated hands-on experience in ITOM and/or CMDB/CSDM, including integration with enterprise infrastructure and operational tooling.
  • ServiceNow Development: Strong JavaScript, Glide APIs, Business Rules, Script Includes, ACLs, Flow Designer/Workflow Studio and upgrade-safe platform development.
  • Integrations: Strong experience with REST/JSON APIs, IntegrationHub, MID Server and enterprise integration patterns.
  • CMDB & Service Modeling: Strong understanding of CI relationships, CSDM, IRE, service models, CMDB Health and data governance.
  • Engineering Delivery: Experience with requirements analysis, technical design, testing/ATF, debugging, peer review, releases and L2/L3 production support.

Additional / Preferred Experience

  • AI & Intelligent Operations: Exposure to Now Assist, GenAI, AI Agents/agentic workflows, AIOps and AI governance for ITOM/CMDB use cases.
  • Automation & Observability: Experience with IntegrationHub, Workflow Studio, REST APIs, Service Graph Connectors, cloud and monitoring/observability tools.
  • Engineering & Cross-Domain: Familiarity with ATF, Git/CI-CD, Python/PowerShell and integration of ITSM, ITAM or OTSM with ITOM and CMDB.

Certifications

  • Mandatory: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) - Data Foundations (CMDB & CSDM)
  • Desirable: Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) certifications (e.g., CIS-Discovery and/or CIS-Event Management certification)

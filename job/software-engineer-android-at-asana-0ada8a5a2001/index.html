About the job

The Mobile team’s mission is to enable teams to work together effortlessly - even when they are away from their desktops. We partner closely with Product Designers, PMs, and other product engineering teams to continuously improve the Asana experience for our customers on-the-go. We work hard to maintain a codebase and a culture that is a joy to work in by combining tried and true mobile best practices with many of the innovative opportunities that our in-house frameworks provide, along with AI-assisted development tools that are part of how we build every day.

Software Engineers at Asana are quickly trusted to own major parts of both the codebase and our processes through the Areas of Responsibility system. We expect you to thrive in defining and solving problems, with mutual support from your peers.

This role is based in our Warsaw office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do, and your recruiter can share more about the in-office requirements.

Our employees in Poland are employed under a contract of employment.

What you’ll achieve

  • Build and ship high-quality features for our global Android user base in collaboration with Product and Design partners.
  • Contribute to and help grow Asana's Android community by sharing knowledge and best practices across teams.
  • Make improvements, and advocate for code quality, test coverage, addressing technical debt, and user experience.
  • Craft well-documented, well-structured, and well-tested code.
  • Help others on the team improve by providing thoughtful feedback in code/design reviews, and mentoring others in areas you are knowledgeable in.
  • Enable Asana’s Mobile teams to leverage the latest practices, tooling and SDKs, including AI-assisted development and agentic workflows.
  • Contribute to Asana-wide cross-platform feature launches.
  • Own and contribute to AoRs (Areas of Responsibility).

About you

  • 3+ years of experience developing, releasing, and maintaining high-quality Android applications. Ideally using Kotlin.
  • Aware of the latest developments in the Android ecosystem, including AI tooling for Android development.
  • Curious about AI-assisted development and comfortable using AI coding tools, while keeping a high bar for the code you ship and review.
  • Committed to leave code better than you found it.
  • Passion for crafting a delightful end-user experience.
  • Demonstrated strength as a communicator and collaborator.
  • Good judgment for balancing scrappiness and long-term code maintainability.

At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission. If you're interested in this role and don't meet every listed requirement, we still encourage you to apply.

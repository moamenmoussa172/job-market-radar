About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France, Spain, United Kingdom.

Dataiku is the Platform for AI Success, the enterprise orchestration layer for building, deploying, and governing AI. In a single environment, teams design and operate analytics, machine learning, and AI agents with the transparency, collaboration, and control enterprises require. Sitting above data platforms, cloud infrastructure, and AI services, Dataiku connects the full enterprise AI stack - empowering organizations to run AI across multi-vendor environments with centralized governance.
The world’s leading companies rely on Dataiku to operationalize AI and run it as a true business performance engine delivering measurable value. For more, visit the Dataiku blog, LinkedIn, X, and YouTube.

Dataiku is looking for a Sr. Data Platform Engineer to join our Enterprise Data and Analytics (EDA) team. As a member of the EDA Team, you will play a central role in delivering data & GenAI solutions to fuel analytics and data-driven insights to various stakeholders and teams within the company. You will be a key technical member contributing to the Data Platform that fuels centralized analytics, embedded analytics teams, and self-service users across the organization.

At this level of seniority, non-technical skills, learning & teaching, and business acumen are critical, along with the expectation of technical excellence, as you will help drive technical decision-making and solutioning of key technical challenges both within the team and across the Analytics Community. The technical day-to-day will primarily be within the Data Platform built using Snowflake, Dataiku & GitHub. The primary development will focus on GenAI Platform Capabilities, Python & SQL (among other languages), DataOps processes built within GitHub Actions & Dataiku, and data platform processes built within Snowflake & Dataiku. Strong software development lifecycle knowledge and DataOps skills are a must! The ideal candidate is naturally curious, has excellent verbal and written communication skills, has a sharp analytical mind, a positive attitude towards work and thrives when collaborating towards a shared goal. This is an internal and non-client facing role.

How you’ll make an impact

  • Be an expert-level engineer across Dataiku (Platform Automation, GenAI Capabilities, Plugin Development, maintenance & troubleshooting) and Snowflake (data engineering, security/governance), including building & maintaining GenAI Platform solutions with a security/governance focus
  • Build & maintain Python & SQL-based platform automation and DataOps processes for SDLC delivery
  • Build & maintain data quality, observability, monitoring, alerting, access management, and platform transparency systems
  • Assist Data Engineering with Python & SQL data replication & pipelines on large, complex datasets
  • Design data models (short- and long-term) to support data warehouse scalability
  • Collaborate with GenAI Engineering & Analytics Engineering to build scalable Data Platform solutions
  • Perform root cause analysis on complex errors to ensure pipeline availability
  • Help drive technical & architectural decisions on the data platform (architecture, engineering processes, data quality, security/governance frameworks, DataOps, consumption models)
  • Test new Dataiku and partner-tool features, provide feedback, and assess value for internal integration
  • Work closely with stakeholders (Infra, embedded analytics, Product, Engineering) on implementation & requirements gathering, while promoting the Data Platform internally through mentoring, innovation initiatives, and contributions to EDA team standards and best practices

Some expected outcomes for this role

  • A more secure, well-governed GenAI layer on top of the Data Platform, with clear standards for engineering delivery
  • Measurable improvements in data quality, observability, and platform reliability (fewer/faster-resolved incidents via RCA)
  • Stronger, more automated DataOps/SDLC processes reducing manual overhead across the team
  • Scalable data models that support the platform’s growth across Analytics, GenAI Engineering, and self-service users
  • Closer technical alignment between Data Platforms, GenAI Engineering, and Analytics Engineering on shared solutions

What you’ll need to be successful

  • Strong understanding of GenAI development, security & governance concepts — a must
  • 5+ years of relevant experience in Data Engineering / Data Platform Engineering / Data Architecture
  • Expert-level SQL & Python; Snowflake experience required
  • Prior experience with DevOps technologies (GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, Jenkins)
  • Strong understanding of data architecture & data modeling concepts
  • Prior experience building and maintaining replication & data pipelines in a cloud data warehouse or data lake environment
  • Experience working with complex stakeholders: dissecting vague asks and defining tangible requirements
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and competing priorities in a high-trust remote environment, wearing multiple hats as needed
  • Excellent analytical, written, and verbal communication skills, with a passion for continuous learning and teaching, and the ability to produce clear, precise documentation

What will make you stand out

  • Dataiku DSS experience
  • Demonstrated track record of mentoring or upskilling other engineers on new technologies or platform practices
  • Experience delivering GenAI architecture guidance directly to business/senior stakeholders

About this Role

We are seeking an IT Service Desk Specialist to provide first-line service desk support for enterprise end users, supporting Vodafone Automotive and Global Catalogue users through a helpdesk-to-helpdesk support model. The role covers request management, incident management, authorisation management, service level management, relationship management, problem management and inventory-related support, while coordinating with internal teams, partners, affiliates and third-party suppliers to deliver timely, quality-focused resolutions.

This role is based in Cairo and sits within the Enterprise function, supporting service desk operations for Vodafone Automotive, a Vodafone Group entity delivering automotive products and services to multinational customers and their end users.

What you’ll do

  • Receive and manage requests and incidents through channels such as M2M, Remedy, VGECO, ServiceNow, email, phone and other integrated portals, with a strong focus on call-based support.
  • Acknowledge requests and incidents within agreed response times and ensure they are processed accurately in the relevant service support systems.
  • Support Vodafone Automotive users and Global Catalogue users by analysing issues, processing tickets and orders, and providing clear updates throughout the ticket lifecycle.
  • Take ownership of incidents, escalations, orders and tickets, including follow-up activities and liaison with partners until resolution.
  • Validate requests and incidents against agreed templates to ensure the information submitted to the next level of support is complete and accurate.
  • Liaise with internal and external partners, including OPAs, Service Managers and Account Managers, to support effective service delivery.
  • Monitor agreed SLAs and proactively follow up with partners by email or phone to support timely resolution.
  • Provide Level 1 support and first-time resolution where possible, with Level 2 support and resolution where applicable.
  • Contribute to Incident Management, Knowledge Management, Quality Management and Problem Management processes.
  • Create and maintain FAQ content, knowledge management materials and contact lists to improve service desk effectiveness.
  • Authorise senders and callers against authorised contact lists using defined validation procedures, and report unauthorised communications to the correct authorised contacts.
  • Submit requests to add, remove or update authorised contacts through the appropriate internal teams.
  • Contribute to service improvement initiatives that enhance the service desk operation and customer experience.

Who you are

  • You have experience working in a customer-facing support function, preferably within IT.
  • You bring good technical and IT knowledge, with the ability to analyse customer issues and support practical resolutions.
  • You have experience working in a multi-partner environment and can collaborate effectively with internal and external stakeholders.
  • You are confident using Microsoft Office applications and service support systems.
  • You communicate clearly in business-level German and English, both written and spoken.
  • You are organised, analytical and able to manage detailed issue investigation while maintaining a helpful and customer-focused approach.
  • You can work effectively in an international and virtual team environment.
  • You are comfortable working in dynamic, changing environments and can adapt your approach as business needs evolve.
  • Knowledge of ITIL processes or an ITIL v3 qualification would be an advantage.
  • Familiarity with Remedy or other support tools, shared service centre experience, Vodafone process knowledge, relevant qualifications or a general understanding of IoT would be beneficial.
  • Additional written and spoken language skills, especially Spanish, French, Italian or Hungarian, would be an advantage

What's in it for you

  • An opportunity to support multinational Vodafone Automotive customers and enterprise end users in a global service environment.
  • Exposure to a broad service desk model covering incident management, request management, authorisation management, service level management, knowledge management and problem management.
  • The chance to collaborate with internal teams, partners, affiliates and third-party suppliers across a multi-partner support ecosystem.
  • Practical experience with service support tools and integrated customer support portals, including Remedy, VGECO, ServiceNow, M2M, email and phone-based support channels.
  • A role that supports continuous service improvement and encourages contribution to improved service desk operations

What skills you will learn

  • End-to-end incident, request, ticket and order lifecycle management within an enterprise service desk environment.
  • SLA monitoring, escalation follow-up and partner coordination across internal and external support teams.
  • Knowledge management practices, including FAQ creation, support collateral maintenance and contact list management.
  • Authorisation management and validation procedures for approved contacts and support communications.
  • Practical application of ITIL-aligned service management processes, including incident, problem, quality and knowledge management.
  • Customer communication, issue analysis and first-time resolution techniques in a multilingual support environment

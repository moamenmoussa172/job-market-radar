About the job

Property Finder is the leading property portal in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, dedicated to shaping an inclusive future for real estate while spearheading the region’s growing tech ecosystem. At its core is a clear and powerful purpose: To change living for good in the region.

Founded on the value of great ambitions, Property Finder connects millions of property seekers with thousands of real estate professionals every day. The platform offers a seamless and enriching experience, empowering both buyers and renters to make informed decisions. Since its inception in 2007, Property Finder has evolved into a trusted partner for developers, brokers, and home seekers. As a lighthouse tech company, it continues to create an environment where people can thrive and contribute meaningfully to the transformation of real estate in MENA.

Role Summary

You will work closely with the CRM team to build and execute campaigns within Braze, ensuring the platform is being used effectively to deliver relevant, personalised and timely communications.

The ideal candidate will have strong technical knowledge of Braze, experience building complex customer journeys and the ability to work independently to get campaigns live.

Key Responsibilities

  • Braze Campaign Setup & Execution Build, configure and launch campaigns directly within Braze.
  • Set up campaigns across Push Notifications, Email, In-App Messages, Content Cards and other supported channels.
  • Configure campaign triggers, scheduling, frequency caps and audience targeting.
  • Build reusable campaign templates and modular content blocks. Implement dynamic personalisation using Liquid and customer attributes.
  • Manage campaign QA, testing and deployment.
  • Customer Journeys & Automation Build automated customer journeys using Braze Canvas.
  • Configure event-triggered, behavioural and lifecycle campaigns.
  • Develop multi-step customer journeys based on user actions and intent.
  • Set up audience entry criteria, exit rules, delays, branching logic and conversion events. Implement re-engagement and retention journeys.
  • Build cross-channel experiences that connect push, email and in-app messaging. Segmentation & Personalisation
  • Build sophisticated audience segments using customer attributes and behavioural events.
  • Set up dynamic content and personalised messaging using Liquid.
  • Create audience cohorts based on property search behaviour, saved properties, enquiries, app activity and user preferences.
  • Technical Implementation & Troubleshooting Work with CRM, Product, Data and Engineering teams to ensure the correct events and attributes are available in Braze.
  • Troubleshoot campaign delivery, audience, trigger and personalisation issues.
  • Support the implementation of best-practice naming conventions, tagging and campaign governance.
  • Analyse open rates, click-through rates, conversion rates and attributed leads.
  • Support A/B testing and experimentation.

Key Experience

  • Must-have: 5+ years of hands-on experience working with Braze.
  • Strong experience building campaigns and journeys using Braze Canvas.
  • Advanced knowledge of segmentation, triggers, audience filters and event-based campaigns.
  • Strong working knowledge of Liquid templating and dynamic personalisation.
  • Experience with Push Notifications, Email and In-App Messaging.
  • Understanding of customer data, event tracking and user attributes. Strong campaign QA and troubleshooting skills. A
  • bility to independently build, test and launch campaigns without requiring significant technical hand-holding.

Nice-to-have

  • Experience working in property portals, marketplaces, e-commerce or high-volume consumer apps.
  • Experience with mobile app CRM and web-to-app conversion. Knowledge of APIs, SDKs and data integrations.
  • Experience with experimentation and conversion optimisation.
  • Experience managing CRM across multiple markets and languages.

What Success Looks Like

  • Audit the existing Braze setup and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Build and launch CRM campaigns across key consumer and B2B audiences.
  • Develop automated lifecycle journeys using Braze Canvas.
  • Establish reusable campaign templates and personalisation frameworks.
  • Improve campaign execution speed and reduce manual campaign setup. Implement robust QA, testing and campaign governance processes.
  • Upskill the internal CRM team to independently manage and execute campaigns.

About this Role

We are seeking a AWS DevOps & Performance Engineer with 6–8 years of experience in CI/CD, Chaos Engineering, cloud infrastructure, automation and performance engineering. You will work with AWS cloud-native environments, containerised platforms and automation frameworks, with responsibility for building reliable CI/CD capabilities and supporting scalable distributed systems.

The role requires hands-on experience with AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Jenkins, GitHub, python/Shell scripting, alongside an understanding of infrastructure, networking, security and Chaos Engineering. You will work across multiple projects and collaborate with stakeholders with varying levels of technical expertise and seniority.

What you’ll do

  • Design, develop, maintain and enhance AWS resilience and performance test automation frameworks.
  • Execute resilience and performance testing for multi-AZ, microservices-based AWS applications, covering VPC, networking, Security Groups, IAM, S3, EC2, EKS, RDS/Aurora and MSK/Kafka.
  • Design and execute AWS FIS/Chaos Engineering scenarios, including pod/microservice failures, Kafka broker failures, AZ failures and database failover, validating RTO, RPO, availability and recovery.
  • Build and maintain automation using AWS Step Functions, GitHub Actions, Terraform, Bash/Shell and Python.
  • Maintain JMeter scripts, test configurations, test data, sanity checks and automated reporting.
  • Monitor test executions and provide BAU troubleshooting for framework, AWS infrastructure, IAM, CI/CD, performance and resilience issues.
  • Support resilience automation onboarding, rollout, KT and execution for application teams.
  • Continuously improve automation, CI/CD and Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) capabilities for resilience and performance engineering.

Who you are

  • You have 6–8 years of relevant experience, including hands-on experience with AWS cloud, Performance and resilience, DevOps and CI/CD pipeline.
  • You understand cloud migration, servers, storage, backup and restore, networking and security.
  • You have experience designing, building and testing CI/CD pipelines.
  • You have scripting experience using Shell scripting or Python, with Bash or Groovy also relevant.
  • You are familiar with tools such as Jira, GitHub, GitHub Actions, Docker, Terraform and Grafana.
  • You have experience delivering automation solutions and working with automation frameworks.
  • You understand Agile and Scrum ways of working.
  • You have strong analytical, problem-solving and troubleshooting capabilities.
  • You can manage multiple priorities and take ownership of deliverables.
  • You communicate effectively with clients, stakeholders and colleagues.
  • You collaborate well within a team and actively share knowledge.
  • You hold a graduate or postgraduate qualification, preferably in a technical discipline such as Engineering.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
  • Experience or knowledge of Chaos Engineering, including AWS FIS, Litmus, Chaos Monkey or comparable open-source tools.
  • Knowledge of technologies including Kafka, Couchbase and RDS databases such as MySQL, Oracle or PostgreSQL.
  • AWS certifications are desirable.

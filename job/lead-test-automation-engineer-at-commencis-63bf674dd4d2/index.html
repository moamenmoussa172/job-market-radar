About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Turkey.

We are looking for a visionary Lead Test Automation Engineer with strong technical leadership and communication skills to drive quality engineering across the software development lifecycle of high-impact products in diverse domains, built with Commencis’ expertise in a fast-paced agile environment.

In this role, you will take ownership of architecting scalable test automation frameworks, defining end-to-end test strategies, and embedding AI-driven testing practices into our workflows. Your deep technical expertise will be essential to balance project constraints, accelerate release cycles, and establish automated quality gates without compromising product excellence.

You will collaborate closely with developers, product owners, and designers to align quality objectives from early design stages through deployment—overseeing automated UI, API, and performance testing (while stepping into critical manual or exploratory testing when needed) to ensure every release is fit for purpose.

Beyond leading the quality and automation efforts of your core product, you will mentor QA and automation engineers, lead code and architecture reviews across multiple projects, and spearhead initiatives to continuously evolve Commencis’ test automation standards and AI-powered testing practices.

If you are curious by nature, adaptive, collaborative, and always one step ahead, then join us and be a Commencer!

Key Roles and Responsibilities

  • Architect, evolve, and maintain scalable, modular, and maintainable test automation frameworks across mobile, web, and API layers.
  • Define and enforce automation coding standards, review guidelines, and architectural best practices across squads.
  • Mentor and coach QA and test automation engineers, leading regular code reviews and technical knowledge-sharing sessions to elevate team capabilities.
  • Lead tool evaluations and Proof of Concept (PoC) initiatives to optimize test execution speed, stability, and overall automation ROI.
  • Architect Text-to-Test and agentic workflows that translate product requirements, user stories, or design specifications into executable automated test scenarios.
  • Establish AI governance, prompt/context standards, and quality guardrails for AI-assisted test generation across the QA and automation teams.
  • Implement intelligent failure analysis and reporting pipelines to accelerate root-cause identification and reduce triage effort.
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to analyze project requirements and establish end-to-end test strategies aligned with business goals.
  • Design and oversee automated UI (mobile/web), API, and performance (load, stress) test suites, stepping in for critical manual, exploratory, or integration testing when needed.
  • Lead the integration of automated test suites into CI/CD pipelines, managing parallel execution infrastructures (e.g., cloud device farms) and enforcing release quality gates.
  • Track quality metrics, assess release risks, and deliver clear, actionable test progress reports to engineering and product leadership.
  • Requirements
  • BSc or MSc in Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • 8+ years of experience in the software industry, with a strong focus on test automation, software development, and modern QA methodologies.
  • Proven leadership experience guiding QA/automation engineers, driving architectural decisions, and establishing engineering best practices.
  • Proven ability to work effectively within multinational, cross-functional teams.
  • Fluent in verbal and written English, paired with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Extensive hands-on experience designing and scaling mobile, web, and API test automation frameworks using tools such as Appium,WebDriverIO,Maestro and REST Assured ,KarateDsl
  • Proficiency in Java or other object-oriented programming (OOP) languages, along with deep expertise in software design patterns and clean code principles.
  • Hands-on experience with performance and load testing tools such as JMeter or Locust.
  • Strong command of version control systems (e.g., Git, Gerrit) and designing CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Jenkins) with parallel execution and automated quality gates.
  • Demonstrated experience authoring comprehensive test automation strategies, roadmaps, and test plans.
  • Proven hands-on experience integrating LLM-based coding assistants (e.g., Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor) and AI agents into the test development lifecycle to accelerate script generation and maintenance.
  • Ability to design structured context files, prompts, and rulesets that enable AI tools to generate standardized, framework-compliant test automation code.
  • Experience evaluating and implementing AI-driven self-healing mechanisms or locator-independent testing approaches (e.g., vision-based or accessibility-tree driven tools) to minimize test flakiness.
  • Ability to evaluate and integrate emerging AI-driven testing technologies based on project requirements, technical constraints, and ROI.
  • Experience architecting sustainable, multi-platform test automation frameworks from scratch is a plus.
  • Familiarity with security testing practices and tools is a plus.
  • An ISTQB certification is nice to have.
  • Active contributions to the open-source community are a plus.

About the job

Accenture CyberSecurity helps organizations prepare, protect, detect, respond and recover across all aspects of the security lifecycle. Cybersecurity challenges are different for every business and industry. Leveraging our global resources and advanced technologies, we create integrated, end‑to‑end solutions tailored to our clients’ needs across their entire value chain, helping them build cyber resilience and grow with confidence.

As an IAM Consultant, you will analyse requirements and design, implement, configure, test, troubleshoot and support Identity & Access Management solutions aligned with business requirements and security policies. You will apply strong hands-on expertise in one or more IAM/IGA/CIAM platforms, including SailPoint, One Identity, Saviynt, ForgeRock, Okta and Ping Identity, with exposure to additional platforms considered advantageous, helping deliver effective identity lifecycle management, access provisioning, authentication, authorization and access governance capabilities.

The role will deliver hands-on configuration, customization and integration of IAM platforms with enterprise target systems and support key capabilities including role mining, policy definition, access certifications, access reviews, SSO, MFA, identity federation and Privileged Access Management.

You will work within global delivery teams to develop and troubleshoot integrations and connectors and help ensure IAM solutions are effectively documented, tested, integrated and supported through go-live.

What You’ll Do

  • Analyse business and technical requirements and translate them into IAM solution configurations and implementation activities
  • Design and implement IAM solutions aligned with business requirements and security policies
  • Configure, customize and troubleshoot IAM platforms such as SailPoint, One Identity, Saviynt, ForgeRock, Okta and Ping Identity
  • Develop, configure, test and troubleshoot integrations and connectors between IAM tools and target systems, including Microsoft Entra ID, LDAP, databases and cloud platforms
  • Implement IAM capabilities including role mining, policy definition, access certifications, access reviews, SSO, MFA, identity federation, IGA, PAM and CIAM
  • Participate in documentation, solution testing, troubleshooting, implementation and go-live activities within global delivery teams

What You’ll Bring

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Engineering or a related field
  • At least 5 years of hands-on experience in IAM projects
  • Strong hands-on expertise in one or more IAM/IGA/CIAM platforms, including SailPoint IdentityNow/IdentityIQ, One Identity Manager, Saviynt, ForgeRock, Ping Identity or Okta, including developing, configuring, customising, integrating, testing and troubleshooting IAM solutions and connectors
  • Strong knowledge of IAM concepts, including user lifecycle management, access provisioning, authentication and authorisation, SSO, MFA, RBAC/ABAC, identity federation and identity governance
  • Hands-on experience with Privileged Access Management (PAM) and Customer Identity and Access Management (CIAM)
  • Hands-on experience with IAM-related protocols and standards, including SAML, OAuth, OpenID Connect, LDAP and SCIM
  • Hands-on experience with directory services, including Microsoft Entra ID
  • Hands-on experience with databases and web services, including REST and SOAP
  • Programming or scripting experience in Java, PowerShell/Bash, JavaScript or SQL
  • Fluency in English, both written and verbal

Legal: Must own a valid work permit or EU/Schengen Space Nationality to legally work in Portugal.

What Will Set You Apart

  • In-depth experience with One Identity Manager, Saviynt, ForgeRock, Ping Identity or Okta, particularly in provisioning, workflows and customisation
  • Experience with PAM tools such as CyberArk or BeyondTrust
  • Cloud IAM experience with AWS IAM or Google Cloud IAM
  • Experience integrating IAM platforms with enterprise applications
  • Programming or scripting knowledge in Java, PowerShell, JavaScript or SQL
  • Relevant IAM or security certifications, such as SailPoint certification(s) or certifications related to Microsoft identity and access, One Identity or Saviynt
  • Fluency in other European languages, such as French, German, or Spanish

Additional Information

  • Flexibility to travel within Europe as required for project activities and stakeholder engagements
  • Strong communication, analytical, organizational and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment
  • Proactive, detail-oriented and results-oriented mindset

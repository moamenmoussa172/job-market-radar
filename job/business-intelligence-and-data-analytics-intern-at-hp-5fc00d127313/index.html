About the job

Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Intern

Before applying please note that, to be eligible for this position, you must be currently enrolled at a university in Spain, remain enrolled until the end of the internship, and be eligible to sign an internship agreement with your university. You must also be available to start the internship in February 2027 and continue participating in the program until June–July 2027.

Job Description

HP’s Graphics Solutions Business (GSB) works to deliver large-scale cloud solutions that enable on-demand manufacturing of personalized goods around the world. Our products lead the market with a strong focus on technology, scalability, and security. We are working to disrupt industries such as publishing by bringing personalized content that today exists digitally into the physical world. As part of this transformation, we help Print Service Providers streamline the manufacture of books, business cards, packaging, photos, posters, signage, and almost anything else that can be printed.

At HP, Business Intelligence plays a critical role in providing the right data and insights to support business decisions and operations management.

We are looking for a Business Intelligence Data Analyst Intern to join and support the WW Industrial Print Services organization (IPCS).

As a BI Developer and Data Analyst Intern, you will have the opportunity to learn and contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of reports and dashboards used to monitor company, product, and business performance and KPIs.

You will work alongside team members and stakeholders from across the world, gaining hands-on experience in developing BI solutions, supporting BI systems, and addressing ongoing business analysis needs.

This includes learning how to understand and identify relevant data sources, extract and process data, and transform it into meaningful insights and visual representations using BI platforms such as Tableau and Power BI.

Job Responsibilities

  • Dashboard Development: Support the creation and improvement of dashboards and data visualizations using Power BI and/or Tableau, helping translate data into clear business insights.
  • Data Gathering and Analysis: Gather, clean, transform, and analyze data from different sources with support from the team.
  • Requirement Gathering: Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business needs and help translate them into dashboard and reporting requirements.
  • Dashboard Maintenance: Support the monitoring and maintenance of existing dashboards, including data validation and quality checks.
  • Documentation: Document data sources, methodologies, and dashboard specifications to ensure transparency and reproducibility.
  • User Support: Assist users in understanding dashboards, reports, and key business metrics.
  • Continuous Learning: Develop your Business Intelligence and analytics skills by learning new technologies, methodologies, and industry trends.

Education and Experience Required

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Statistics, Data Science, Business Analytics, or another related field.
  • Academic or project-based exposure to databases, SQL, and/or Python for data extraction, analysis, or manipulation.
  • Familiarity with Power BI and/or Tableau, gained through coursework, university projects, personal projects, or previous internship experience.
  • Basic understanding of data analysis, visualization, and Business Intelligence concepts.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Excel is a plus.
  • Exposure to data mining, machine learning, or predictive analytics through coursework or projects is a plus.
  • Exposure to AWS or other cloud-based solutions is a plus.
  • Basic understanding of data warehousing and ETL concepts is a plus.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an interest in working with data.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills. Fluent English is required.
  • Strong attention to detail and willingness to deliver high-quality work.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively in an international, cross-functional environment.
  • Curious, proactive, and eager to learn, with the ability to pick up new concepts and technologies quickly.

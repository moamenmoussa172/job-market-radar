About The Role:
​As a UI/UX Designer on Dsquares’ Delivery Team, you will bridge business requirements, customer needs, and technical execution to craft seamless, engaging digital loyalty and rewards experiences. Working closely with Product Owners, Software Engineers, and external enterprise clients, you will own the end-to-end design lifecycle—from discovery and wireframing to high-fidelity interfaces and scalable design systems—ensuring our client-facing web and mobile solutions are intuitive, consistent, and delivered on schedule.
Duties & Responsibilities:

  • End-to-End Product Design: Translate complex client requirements, user stories, and business objectives into user flows, wireframes, interactive prototypes, and polished UI designs web and mobile applications.
  • ​Design System Governance & Evolution: Maintain, expand, and enforce Dsquares’ multi-brand design system, building reusable UI components, tokens, and documentation that accelerate delivery across multiple client implementations.
  • ​Cross-Functional Delivery Collaboration: Partner closely with Product Owners, Business Analysts, and Front-End/Mobile Engineers throughout agile sprints to ensure technical feasibility, accurate handoff, and high-quality visual QA before release.
  • ​Client & Stakeholder Alignment: Present design concepts, user journeys, and rationale clearly to internal stakeholders and external enterprise clients, incorporating feedback effectively while advocating for usability best practices.
  • ​User Research & Iterative Optimization: Conduct usability reviews, competitor benchmarking, and post-launch UX audits—leveraging product analytics and customer feedback to refine onboarding, engagement, and redemption flows.

Requirements

Required qualifications

  • Experience: Minimum of 3+ years of proven hands-on experience as a UI/UX Designer, Product Designer, or similar role, ideally within a fast-paced software delivery, SaaS, fintech, or digital agency environment.
  • ​Design Systems Expertise: Demonstrated experience building, maintaining, or scaling comprehensive Design Systems (component libraries, variables/tokens, responsive grids, and accessibility guidelines) across web and mobile platforms (iOS/Android).
  • ​Tool Proficiency: Advanced mastery of Figma (auto-layout, interactive prototyping, component variants, dev mode) and familiarity with handoff/collaboration tools such as Jira, Confluence, or Zeplin.
  • ​Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills in English and Arabic, with the ability to articulate design decisions to both technical teams and business stakeholders.
  • Nice to have ​Experience in designing gamified experiences, loyalty programs, e-commerce, or high-volume consumer apps.

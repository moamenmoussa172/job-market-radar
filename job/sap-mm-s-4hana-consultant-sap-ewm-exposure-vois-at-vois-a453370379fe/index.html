About this Role

We are seeking an experienced SAP MM / S/4HANA Consultant with strong Procure-to-Pay (PTP) expertise and implementation experience across SAP MM, SAP S/4HANA and SAP EWM environments. This role is suited to individuals who have successfully delivered SAP transformation and implementation projects, including solution design, configuration, integration, testing and data migration activities. The successful candidate will contribute to the delivery of business-critical SAP solutions, support system integrations with legacy platforms, and collaborate with stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

What you’ll do

Contribute to all phases of the SAP solution development and implementation lifecycle.Gather business requirements and prepare functional documentation required for implementation projects.Design, configure and deploy SAP MM functionalities in line with business requirements.Support SAP EWM-related warehouse and inventory management processes.Perform end-to-end testing of newly developed business scenarios.Develop and support integration interfaces with legacy and third-party systems.Prepare functional specifications, technical specifications and test scripts.Support enhancements, reports, interfaces and data migration activities.Participate in SAP S/4HANA implementation and transformation initiatives.Manage data migration activities from legacy applications into SAP S/4HANA environments.Ensure solution designs comply with agreed specifications and quality standards.Investigate emerging technologies and recommend improvements for architectural review.Analyse and resolve complex business and system issues through structured problem-solving approaches.

Who you are

Proven experience of 6-10 years in SAP MM implementation projects.Strong expertise across the end-to-end Procure-to-Pay (PTP) process.Proficiency in SAP MM within SAP S/4HANA environments.Experience in SAP MM implementation, support and enhancement projects.Ability to design, configure and deploy SAP MM solutions successfully.Experience in interface development, SAP MM/EWM enhancements and report development.Knowledge of preparing functional specifications, technical specifications and testing documentation.Experience in pricing procedure design and development aligned to business requirements.Understanding of SAP S/4HANA implementation and transformation methodologies.Knowledge of equipment handling and serialisation procedures.Working knowledge of inventory and stock management processes within SAP EWM.Understanding of delivery processing, goods receipt, goods issue and internal warehouse processes.Basic understanding of master and transactional data migration activities during cutover.Experience designing interfaces with legacy systems.Strong knowledge of EDI and IDOC processing and support.Exposure to API-based integrations, cloud platform integrations or cloud storage integrations is advantageous.Strong analytical, communication and stakeholder management skills.

What's in it for you

Opportunity to work on large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformation and implementation programmes.Exposure to complex enterprise integrations involving legacy systems, APIs and cloud-based solutions.Opportunity to collaborate with global stakeholders across business and technology functions.Hands-on involvement in end-to-end SAP delivery, from requirements gathering through to deployment and support.Experience working on business-critical supply chain, procurement and warehouse management processes.

What skills you will learn

Advanced SAP S/4HANA implementation and transformation practices.SAP EWM process design and warehouse management capabilities.Enterprise integration design using EDI, IDOCs, APIs and third-party platforms.Data migration planning and execution methodologies during cutover activities.Functional solution architecture, testing approaches and deployment best practices.Cross-functional collaboration across procurement, logistics and supply chain domains.

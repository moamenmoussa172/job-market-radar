About the job

Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

About the team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems.We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Prime team's mission is to build Bitstamp's prime brokerage for institutional clients — the platform that lets hedge funds, market makers, and professional trading firms fund, trade, borrow against, and settle positions at scale. We build the systems that move real institutional money: cross-venue settlement, credit against collateral, matched-book lending, and the bridge that brings tokenized securities to our order book. These are correctness-critical systems where a wrong answer can't be cheaply undone — getting them right is the whole job.

We are looking for a Backend Engineer to join our team. As a Backend Engineer, you will lead the backend development process for core components and build services from inception to deployment. This individual will be helping solve correctness, scaling, and reliability challenges on a backend-first, high-performance, and world-class institutional trading platform.

This role is based in our London office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Requires participation in an on-call rotation to support business needs.

What you’ll do

  • Design and ship production services: Implement and operate resilient backend services and subsystems that move institutional money — contributing to flows such as cross-venue settlement, collateral credit, matched-book lending, and order execution.
  • Author focused technical specs: Draft clear technical designs for new features and components, explicitly defining data invariants, error handling, state boundaries, and recovery mechanisms before writing code.
  • Build for failure: Write clean, idiomatic, and thoroughly tested code, pairing production features with rigorous unit/integration tests, sensible monitoring, structured logging, and targeted alerts.
  • Maintain operational health: Actively participate in the team's on-call rotation, debug production anomalies, and contribute to blameless post-mortems and remediation work to harden our platform.
  • Collaborate and review: Participate in architectural discussions and provide thoughtful, constructive code reviews to help maintain high code quality and consistency across the team.

What you bring

  • Proven backend track record: Solid experience independently designing, implementing, and maintaining backend services in a production environment, taking full ownership of what you ship.
  • Correctness-critical mindset: Hands-on experience with financial, transactional, or distributed systems (e.g., payments, ledgers, trading, order management, or inventory engines) where data integrity is paramount and mistakes are costly.
  • Systems fundamentals: Strong understanding of distributed systems and transactional boundaries, including relational data modeling (PostgreSQL), data consistency, idempotency, and asynchronous workflows/message queues.
  • Defensive engineering: Good design judgment and an instinct to test failure paths; you think about race conditions, boundary failures, and network partitions rather than just the happy path.
  • Language fluency: Proficiency in a modern backend language (such as Python or Go), with the ability to quickly get up to speed on our core stack (Python/Django and Go).
  • Clear communication: Strong written and verbal communication skills; you can clearly explain technical trade-offs, document system behaviors, and collaborate effectively with peers and cross-functional partners.

About the job

What You Will Do:

· You will be the go-to expert for internal teams, providing clear advice on how to set up their applications to meet Bosch standards and Central Directives.

· Help design and plan the right IT infrastructure for new applications, ensuring they are secure, stable, and ready for launch.

· Lead the process of bringing new applications onto our systems, from the first planning meeting to the final go-live day.

· Deliver reliable and high-quality IT services that meet our promises to customers. You'll always look for ways to make our services even better.

· Assist in creating plans for new projects, be ready to provide on-call support for urgent issues, and travel to customer sites when your expertise is needed in person.

· Looking for a candidate with a strong technical foundation and a passion for helping others.

Educational qualification:

Graduate

Experience :

4 - 7 yrs

Mandatory/requires Skills :
Linux /Windows Server Administration, Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, Basic DB, Scripting

Preferred Skills :

Expert In:

o Operating Systems: Managing both Linux and Windows servers.

o Modern Applications: Using Docker and Kubernetes to run applications.

o Automation: Writing scripts to make work faster and more reliable (using tools like PowerShell, Python, or Ansible).

o Monitoring Tools: Using tools like Splunk or New Relic to check system health.

Good Knowledge Of:

o Databases: Basic management of databases like SQL Server, MySQL, or MongoDB.

o Cloud Services: Working with cloud platforms like Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud (a certification is a plus).

o DevOps Tools: Using Git, Jenkins, and Ansible to build and deploy software automatically.

o Networking and Security: Understanding how networks, firewalls, and security work.

SYSTEM SAFETY ENGINEER (D/F/M)

Airbus · Hamburg Area, Germany

Onsite · Software Engineer · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Airbus's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Job Description:

This position is part of our Safety Business Unit, which aims to enable safer, greener and more inclusive industries and transportation systems

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Support the establishment of safety analyses for aircraft systems, supporting all the phases during and after certification
  • Generating and update of assessments (Functional Hazard Assessment/System Safety Assessment) of intended design modifications to influence the system architecture according to the required System Safety/System Integrity
  • Determine the particular Safety requirements for system developments or modifications with respect to hardware and software
  • Manage input from and output to interfacing technical domains and technology (processes, maintenance, human error, aircraft performance and quality, etc.)
  • Review, approval and application of supplier data regarding Safety and Reliability. Generating the System Safety Assessment documentation, based on traceable results and evidence, as mandatory proof to acquire the Type Certificate

Required qualification

The work task requires knowledge and skills that are usually acquired through a relevant, completed course of study with a standard period of study of up to 4 years in the area of aeronautical engineering, Electrical Engineering or Mechatronics Engineering or equivalant. The overall knowledge and experience required may have been acquired in other ways

Desired skills and competences

  • Knowledge and experience in the above-mentioned areas of responsibility
  • First experience in the field of System Safety Engineering in the aviation industry
  • First experience with aircraft systems
  • Target-oriented with analytic problem-solving skills
  • Willing to learn new things
  • Able to perform several Systems Safety tasks in parallel
  • Team player, organized and autonomous person with good communication skills
  • Very good written and spoken English and German (B2)
  • Willingness to travel domestically and internationally

Location
You will be working at the largest production site for civil aircraft situated in Hamburg. Its location on the southern banks of the river Elbe includes the option to commute by ferry. Experience the special flair of Hamburg in your spare time where vibrant cosmopolitan culture meets nautic legacy

Your advantages

  • Mobile working and flexible working hours
  • Fair and attractive remuneration and special payments
  • 30 days' holiday and additional days off for special occasions
  • Intensive induction and expert support as part of onboarding in the form of personalized onboarding with a personal mentor
  • Excellent training opportunities and promising development prospects
  • Attractive social benefits and offers, including employer-financed pension scheme, employee share options, discounted car leasing, bike leasing, special conditions for insurance, employee benefits at cooperating companies
  • On-site facilities (depending on location) e. g. canteen and cafeteria, fitness studio, on-site kindergarten, company medical service and other health-related services

About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Drive the evolution of Everpure’s enterprise connectivity by designing and deploying robust integration solutions that power our Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy. Collaborating closely with Digital Transformation Group Architects and cross-functional business partners, you will establish the Integration North Star and build scalable middleware pipelines. In this role, you will eliminate data silos, optimize workflow architectures, and directly accelerate business performance across core commercial platforms.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Architect & Implement Integration Enablers: Design and build enterprise-grade Boomi integration components (API, EDI, and FLOW) to connect critical enterprise endpoints including Salesforce, SAP, Workday, NetSuite, and Kafka.
  • Optimize Integration Workflows: Conduct technical analysis and re-engineer legacy middleware processes to simplify data flows, lower system latency, and enhance output quality across all GTM operations.
  • Drive Strategic Alignment: Collaborate with Digital Transformation Group Architects to translate business vision into scalable, event-driven, and real-time integration patterns using web services, databases, ETL, and messaging streams.
  • Deliver High-Availability Solutions: Establish operational standards and monitor process performance metrics to ensure seamless, reliable data exchange across global business operations.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Expertise in Boomi & Middleware Architecture: Hands-on mastery in building, deploying, and maintaining integration solutions using the Boomi platform (API, EDI, and FLOW) and enterprise middleware architectures.
  • Proficiency in Integration Patterns & Protocols: Deep technical expertise in application and data integration patterns, REST/SOAP APIs, relational databases, ETL processes, and messaging or streaming frameworks such as Kafka and JMS.
  • Ecosystem & Technical Communication Skills: Demonstrated ability to translate complex business requirements into technical features while integrating enterprise platforms such as Salesforce, SAP, Workday, and NetSuite.

Office Alignment: We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Bangalore office in compliance with Everpure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

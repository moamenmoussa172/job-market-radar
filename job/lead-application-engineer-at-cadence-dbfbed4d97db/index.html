Job Title: Lead Application Engineer

Location: Cork

Reports to: AE Director

Job Responsibilities:

  • Engage with customers to understand their design challenges and propose technical solutions, guidance on methodology and best practices.
  • Act as the primary technical interface for customers on schematic capture and PCB front-end flows
  • Collaborate with R&D, Product Engineering and Marketing teams to drive product improvements and increase proliferation of Cadence solutions.
  • Consult and understand the innovative design needs from customers and communicate with internal teams.
  • Conduct technical demos, training sessions and workshops for customers and partners
  • Debug complex schematic design issues, netlisting problems, library inconsistencies and design flows
  • Creating high impact knowledge article content, tool adoption kits and other high-level documents to increase the self-help of customers
  • Establish technical credibility and rapport with the customer and become the go-to expert for all their technical inquiries and support
  • Assume technical leadership roles in small teams as needed.

Job Qualifications:

  • Electrical/Electronic Engineering or related field
  • BE/BTech -7Years+
  • ME/MS/MTech - 5years+
  • Hands-on experience with PCB design/ EDA industry

Requirements and Technical Skills

Minimum:

  • Solid understanding of PCB design flows with Cadence Allegro Design Entry HDL/Orcad Capture
  • 7+ years of relevant industry experience in Hardware Design Engineering, CAD, or application / technical support roles
  • Knowledge about Product Life Management Cycle system
  • Translate customer requirements into EDA workflows, best practices, and automation solutions
  • Ability to quickly analyse design environments and design complexity
  • Verbal and written communication skills
  • Ability to interact effectively with both external customers and R&D teams

Preferred:

  • System-level design and large-scale schematic environments
  • Experience in high-speed and complex PCB design environments
  • Familiarity with complete PCB design cycle and CAD tools involved
  • Exposure with TCL, SKILL, python or equivalent script language
  • Proven experience in customer-facing technical roles

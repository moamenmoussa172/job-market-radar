Your Impact

Hello! I'm Emil Pfrang, Director Information Security at commercetools. Trust is the foundation of modern cloud commerce. My team operates right at the intersection of global privacy law, cloud architecture, and AI innovation—ensuring our multi-tenant SaaS platform remains compliant, resilient, and trusted by the world’s most ambitious enterprises. Rather than treating compliance as a static checkbox exercise, we partner closely with product, engineering, and external clients to navigate evolving global regulations and safely bring new AI capabilities to market

As a Data Protection and Compliance Consultant, you will play a critical role in maintaining and elevating commercetools’ global data privacy and compliance framework. Operating at the intersection of legal requirements, cloud technology, and software engineering, you will advise internal teams, work closely with external clients, and ensure our multi-tenant SaaS platform adheres to international data privacy laws (such as GDPR, and relevant global privacy regulations).

You will:

Data protection and privacy advice

  • Serve as the subject matter expert (SME) on global data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA where applicable, UK GDPR, etc.) for internal product, engineering, and business teams
  • Advise departments on privacy and data protection topics
  • Work with department heads and process owners to collect all required information about each processing activity
  • Check and assess vendors and assets for data protection and privacy as part of the supplier assessments
  • Monitor and handle the privacy related requests from employees, customers, and authorities, e.g. requests for information, requests for deletion, etc.
  • Prepare and review Data Transfer Impact Assessments
  • Check and assess AI tools and features from a privacy perspective
  • Review customer Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
  • Support assessments of AI tools and AI features from a privacy perspective

Audits, strategic compliance and maintenance

  • Monitor global legal developments, court rulings, and regulatory guidance to update commercetools’ internal policies continuously
  • Maintenance of process documentation within data protection
  • Design and manage data protection training together with our external DPO
  • Draft, review, and maintain key privacy documentation, including Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), Privacy Policies, and Privacy Notices
  • Maintain the Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)
  • Support audits from privacy and data protection perspective
  • Provide data protection evidence for internal and external audits
  • Execute internal compliance audits: Support departments on identifying compliance needs
  • Support compliance policy management
  • Maintenance of whistleblower platform

What Sets You Apart

You're a creative problem-solver who is wired to find solutions. You confidently dive into complex challenges and have a talent for making them simple for others. Your curiosity drives you to constantly grow and contribute to an environment of trust and teamwork. Great ideas come from many paths, and your unique perspective matters more than checking every box. What matters most is the mindset you bring to the work.

You bring:

  • Fluent English and German
  • Degree in law, business informatics or a related field, or equivalent experience
  • 2+ years of experience in data protection or privacy compliance, ideally at a SaaS or tech company
  • Solid knowledge of the GDPR (Art. 13–21, 28, 30, 35, 44 ff.). Knowledge of the EU AI Act, CCPA or HIPAA is a plus
  • Experience with privacy management tools, ideally OneTrust
  • Understanding of ISMS frameworks (ISO 27001, SOC 2)
  • Structured, detail-oriented way of working with a strong focus on documentation
  • Professional certifications such as CIPP/E, CIPM, CIPT (IAPP), or CISA are a strong advantage

AI Aptitude: A genuine curiosity for using AI tools to work smarter and more effectively, paired with a drive to learn and put them into practice in your role.

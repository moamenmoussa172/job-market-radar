Primary Location: China (CN) - Tianjin - Tianjin
Function: Product Engineering (CA)
Title: Sr. Application & CI Engineer - 123406
Your Position:

Duties

  • Manage APDP programs to meet partner requirement, including understand vehicle technical requirements, review vehicle layout and tracking prototype project progress , participate in engine application requirement test verifications to meet program schedule and quality.
  • Communicate with global engine engineering team for engineering change request and technical support , ensure the deliverables
  • Communication window with global application/CI engineering to follow global best practices
  • Responsible for the engine new parts development activities for Aisa suppliers
  • Continuous Improvement responsibilities, including warranty claims improvement, VI, etc.
  • NR5 engine program engineering manager responsibilities, cooperate with global PM and represent engineering core team to ensure the PEM deliverables.
  • Other tasks from manager

Required

  • Good Communication and cooperation skills
  • Fluent English Spoken and Written Skills – able to organize meeting with global team
  • Familiar with base engine and after-treatment design - a few systems at least
  • Familiar with new product development processes
  • Strategic mindset across products and a view to the future
  • Process driving and system thinking mindset
  • Self motivated and project management skills
  • Customer/Partner First mindset
  • Vehicle integration experience, be familiar with engine test procedure
  • Family with both engine hardware and software design to enable trouble shooting capability
  • Ability of engineering tools, for example Creo, CAD,etc.

Desired

  • Engine design or application working experience
  • English and communication skills
  • Good problem – solving skill, cross team coordination ability
  • Strong engine engineering experience

Education

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree or above in internal combustion engine, automotive engineering, mechanical engineering or related disciplines

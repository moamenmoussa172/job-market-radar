About the Role: Senior Consultant – Business Analyst

We’re seeking a strategic, AI-savvy Product Leader with strong business and
technical acumen. This person will own the product roadmap, drive vision,
translate complex problems into actionable requirements, lead cross-functional
teams, and deliver high-impact, data-driven solutions aligned with business goals.

Additional Requirements:

• Educational Background: Candidate must have a Computer Science degree
+ MBA.
• Cloud Exposure: Must have been involved in projects built on cloud
platforms, preferably Microsoft Azure.
• Domain Exposure: Healthcare industry background is highly preferred.

Key Responsibilities

• Own and continuously refine the product backlog and roadmap; prioritize
initiatives based on strategic value, risk, dependencies, and measurable
outcomes.
• Drive and communicate product vision, strategy, and success metrics,
ensuring alignment with organizational goals and long-term growth
objectives.
• Lead complex requirements discovery, documentation, and impact analysis
across large-scale initiatives.
• Influence senior stakeholders through clear, confident communication;
articulate trade-offs and drive alignment across business and technology
teams.
• Apply business analysis frameworks, agile product management practices,
and enterprise stakeholder management skills.
• Possess strong working knowledge of Generative AI, Large Language Models,
and AI-driven product features.
• Define sophisticated AI and analytics requirements; interpret insights and
translate business strategy into scalable AI use cases.
• Lead and mentor cross-functional teams including engineering, UX, QA,
data, GenAI/ML, architecture, and business leadership to deliver high
impact outcomes.
• Translate complex business challenges into epics, features, user stories,
and acceptance criteria for efficient and predictable delivery.
• Oversee testing, validation, and release readiness to ensure solutions meet
business objectives, quality standards, and user expectations.

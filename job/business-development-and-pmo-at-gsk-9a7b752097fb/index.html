About the job

Business Development & Project Manager Office (BD & PMO)

Job purpose

As a Business Development & Project Manager Office (BD & PMO), you support BD and Strategic Alliances through project management excellence, coordination of initiatives/alliances and analytical/operational support for growth and partnership execution.

Key responsibilities

BD and alliance project management

  • Act as PMO for partnerships, commercial agreements, expansions and term renewals.
  • Maintain project plans, timelines, risk logs and status reports, tracking milestones and deliverables.
  • Coordinate inputs from Commercial, Medical, Market Access, Finance, Legal, Tech and Supply to ensure on-time, high-quality delivery.

BD and strategic alliances support

  • Support screening and assessment of distribution, co-promotion, licensing and joint-venture opportunities.
  • Prepare market sizing, financial inputs, scenarios, business cases and presentations for decision-making.
  • Coordinate internal reviews and governance documentation across stakeholders.

Performance and relationship management

  • Track alliance KPIs, sales, service levels and compliance, and prepare dashboards for leadership.
  • Organise partner reviews and steering committees, including agendas, minutes and follow-up actions.

Governance, compliance and documentation

  • Ensure adherence to company policies, ABAC, competition law and Egyptian regulatory requirements.
  • Maintain decision logs, approvals and contract references in partnership with Legal and Compliance.
  • Support audit and inspection readiness for BD and alliance activities.

Collaboration and stakeholder engagement

  • Partner across Commercial, Medical, Access, Communications/Government Affairs, Finance, Legal, Tech, Supply and Commercial Excellence.
  • Support internal and external BD communications in collaboration with Communications/Government Affairs, where relevant.

Continuous improvement and PMO excellence

  • Improve PMO processes, tools and templates to enhance efficiency and transparency.
  • Capture and embed lessons learned and best practices across projects and alliances.

Qualifications and experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, economics, pharmacy, engineering or a related field.
  • Postgraduate qualifications such as an MBA is an advantage, particularly in strategy, finance or project management.
  • 5–7 years of experience in business development, strategy, commercial operations or PMO, preferably in pharma/healthcare or FMCG.
  • Proven cross-functional project management experience and exposure to business-case and partnership evaluation.
  • Experience in Egypt or similar markets and in a matrix organization.

Capabilities and skills

  • Strong project planning, tracking, risk management and stakeholder coordination skills.
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills with data, KPIs and financial inputs.
  • Clear communication and presentation skills, with the ability to simplify complex information.
  • Strong collaboration and stakeholder-management capabilities.
  • High integrity, ethics and compliance mindset.
  • Fluency in Arabic and English.

Why GSK?
Uniting science, technology and talent to get ahead of disease together.

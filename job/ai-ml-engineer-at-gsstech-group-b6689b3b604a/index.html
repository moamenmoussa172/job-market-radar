About the job

The AI/ML Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining enterprise-grade artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions. The role translates operational and business requirements into secure, scalable, and measurable AI capabilities, including predictive analytics, intelligent automation, natural language processing, computer vision, and Generative AI. Responsibilities cover the full AI/ML lifecycle, from data preparation and experimentation through production deployment, monitoring, governance, and continuous improvement.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities:
AI/ML Solution Development
- Identify and assess high-value AI/ML use cases in collaboration with business and operational stakeholders.
- Collect, clean, transform, and validate structured and unstructured data for model development.
- Design, train, evaluate, and optimize machine learning and deep learning models for prediction, classification, recommendation, anomaly detection, NLP, and computer vision.
- Develop Generative AI solutions using large language models, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- Build reusable AI services and integrate models with enterprise applications through secure APIs and event-driven interfaces.
MLOps, Deployment & Governance
- Deploy AI/ML models to on-premises or cloud environments using containerization, model-serving, and CI/CD practices.
- Build and maintain automated pipelines for data preparation, training, testing, versioning, approval, deployment, and retraining.
- Monitor model accuracy, drift, bias, latency, availability, security, and infrastructure cost, and implement corrective actions.
- Maintain model documentation, traceability, audit evidence, access controls, and responsible AI controls covering privacy, fairness, explainability, and human oversight.
- Hands-on experience with open-source LLMs, including model optimization, fine-tuning, token and model-size management, and development of secure internal chatbots for enterprise use cases.
- Strong ability to understand business requirements, collaborate with business owners and key stakeholders, and translate business needs into AI solutions supported by appropriate governance, security, and access-control frameworks.

Collaboration & Support
- Work with business teams, data owners, cybersecurity, infrastructure, and application teams to define requirements, success measures, and operating controls.
- Communicate model assumptions, performance, limitations, and risks clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Prepare technical documentation, deployment guides, support procedures, user guidance, and knowledge-transfer materials.

Qualifications:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, or a related field; a master’s degree is an advantage.
- 3+ years of hands-on experience developing and deploying AI/ML solutions in production environments.
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL, with practical experience using scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Solid understanding of data preprocessing, feature engineering, supervised and unsupervised learning, deep learning, model evaluation, and statistical methods.
- Experience with cloud AI/ML services, preferably Microsoft Azure, Oracle OCI and with REST APIs, Git, Docker, CI/CD, and model monitoring.
- Practical exposure to LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, vector databases, NLP, computer vision, or intelligent document processing.
Preferred Skills:
- Knowledge of MLOps tools and practices, including experiment tracking, model registry, data/model versioning, automated testing, deployment, and drift monitoring.
- Familiarity with responsible AI, data privacy, cybersecurity, explainability, governance, and regulatory or audit requirements.
- Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, documentation, and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to work independently and in cross-functional teams.

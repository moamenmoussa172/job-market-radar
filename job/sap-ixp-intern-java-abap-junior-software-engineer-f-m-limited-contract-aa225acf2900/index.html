About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you'll build

Your responsibilities include:

• Develop robust, scalable, and performant applications leveraging cloud-native best practices, patterns and components

• Work with AI-assisted and agentic development tools (e.g., Claude Code), including AI-supported code reviews, testing, and reusable development skills integrated into CI/CD pipelines

• Develop new features and support and maintain the existing Java code base as the primary development focus

• Support and maintain existing ABAP code base (up to 20% on average)

• Collaborate and align with other teams in the unit

• Regularly evaluate the services we develop for performance and cost optimizations

• Identify and suggest learning opportunities, proactively and continuously share knowledge with peers and the team

• Support the product team and development teams in ensuring compliance standards are met

What you bring

• You are currently a Bachelor's student in any IT related-field such as computer science, IT engineering, software engineering, etc.

• Knowledge of Java, ABAP, or another object-oriented programming language

• Willingness to learn ABAP (in case you do not have knowledge of ABAP)

• Interest in or understanding of AI-assisted and agentic software development; hands-on experience is a plus

• A fluent level of English

Where you belong

The SAP Internship Experience Program is SAP's global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers. This is more than an internship; it's the foundation for a career built on connection, creativity and impact.

Our team develops an industry-leading class of Identity and Access Management (IAG) cloud applications. We are focused on microservice architecture, DevOps principles and CI/CD delivery.

We are highly motivated and skilled engineers with locations in Walldorf (Germany), Palo Alto (California, US) and Brno (Czech Republic).

Global team presence and adoption of modern technologies give you a lot of flexibility to follow your developer interests, cooperation with international teams and always the latest technology trends

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Netherlands.

Growth & Marketing Automation Intern

About Fidamy
Fidamy is an embedded insurance operator committed to transforming how insurance is delivered. We partner with leading companies to seamlessly embed innovative non-life insurance products into digital and physical customer journeys, helping our partners generate additional revenue and enhance customer loyalty. Leveraging deep insurance expertise and an API-first infrastructure, we deliver peace of mind for customers and a unique value proposition for partners. Discover more about our mission and culture at Fidamy Jobs.

About the Role
We are seeking an ambitious, entrepreneurial Growth & Marketing Automation Intern to join our Commercial team. Working closely with our Chief Commercial Officer, you'll play a hands-on role in scaling the commercial operations of a fast-growing tech company, gaining exposure to B2B sales, growth, AI, and automation.

What You’ll Learn

  • How modern sales, CRM, AI, and automation tools operate
  • The B2B tech sales cycle, from prospecting to closing
  • How to build scalable outbound growth processes
  • How to craft LinkedIn content that drives engagement and generates leads

What You’ll Do

  • Build target account and prospect lists for the sales team
  • Research companies and key decision-makers in our target markets
  • Support CRM management and sales operations
  • Create and schedule LinkedIn and social content for campaigns
  • Use AI and no-code tools to automate revenue-driving processes (such as CRM-to-Slack workflows, meeting notes, lead enrichment, sales reporting, and marketing)

Who You Are

  • Pursuing or holding a Master's in Business, Entrepreneurship, Marketing, or a related field
  • Curious about startups, technology, sales, AI, and growth
  • Fluent in English (other languages a plus)
  • Proactive, adaptable, and quick to learn
  • Based in Amsterdam and available to work from our office regularly
  • Bonus: Experience with LinkedIn, marketing, content creation, CRM, or automation tools

What We Offer

  • Direct mentorship from our CCO and experienced entrepreneurs
  • Insight into building and executing commercial strategy
  • Hands-on experience with leading sales, CRM, AI, and automation tools
  • Responsibility from day one with real projects and a steep learning curve
  • An entrepreneurial, collaborative team environment with room for fun
  • Internship compensation of €550/month

Practical Details

  • Location: Amsterdam (office attendance required)
  • Duration: 5–6+ months
  • Commitment: Minimum 3 days a week
  • Start date: As soon as possible
  • Clear learning objectives and a dedicated mentor, supported via a formal stageovereenkomst

How to Apply
Apply directly via our Ashby job posting or through LinkedIn. You can also email your CV to careers@fidamy.com and tell us the first process you would automate.

We look forward to hearing from you!

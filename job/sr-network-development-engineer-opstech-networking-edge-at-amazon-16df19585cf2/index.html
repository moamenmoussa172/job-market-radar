About the job

Do you write code to design, deploy and operate large-scale networks, and do you believe the best network change is the one nobody has to make by hand? If yes, please keep reading...
The OpsTech Networking Edge team builds next-generation edge network infrastructure that powers Amazon's global fulfillment and operations network. The team manages WAN connectivity, SDWAN architecture, and edge security for over 3,700 sites worldwide, supporting Amazon Operations and Grocery businesses.
We are seeking a Senior Network Development Engineer (NDE III) to lead the technical direction of our horizontally scalable edge architecture, with network automation at the centre of everything we build. At our scale, manual change is not an option. You will define how the Edge network is designed, deployed, validated and operated through code. You will own the automation strategy behind initiatives that remove current bandwidth constraints by distributing traffic across multiple SDWAN routers, improve availability through SDWAN designs that enable cloud-based firewall integration, and move over 1,000 SDWAN sites to more resilient architectures safely and repeatably.
As a Senior NDE, you will write and review production-quality code in Python or other languages to improve our existing tools and frameworks. You will build automated pre- and post-change validation, deployment workflows and traffic controllers, and you will use data analysis at scale to drive capacity planning and bandwidth forecasting. You will guide software and systems teams on how to operate the network effectively. You will also lead the team's adoption of AI-powered tools, including agentic AWS AI tools such as Kiro, for automation, diagnostics and operational efficiency.
Beyond automation, you will turn high-level business needs into scalable network solutions you can actually implement, working across Cisco SDWAN, AWS Transit Gateway, CloudWAN, Direct Connect, IPSec encryption, application-aware routing and AWS Network Firewalls. We expect you to understand not only how the network works, but why it is designed the way it is. You will challenge existing assumptions and propose major changes to designs and operational processes when the business needs them. Information security and network reliability are fundamental to your approach. You will make pragmatic trade-offs between short-term operational needs and long-term business requirements, and you will own technical decisions that span multiple teams.
You will help set the technical bar for the team. You will lead design and operational reviews, write clear technical documents that explain complex topics to organization-level leadership, mentor NDE I and NDE II engineers, and take an active part in hiring. You will work with customers and stakeholders to shape the roadmap, turning ambiguous problems into multi-quarter project plans. Network reliability is essential to Amazon Operations, so you will drive root cause resolution of the network events most impactful to the business, with automated prevention as the expected outcome.
The OpsTech Networking organization supports multiple business units globally, from delivery stations in emerging markets to advanced automated fulfillment centers in North America, Japan, and Europe. This group works on both tactical improvements and long-term strategic projects. This opportunity will let you shape the long-term technical direction of Amazon's edge network infrastructure and grow the engineers around you.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, a related technical field or equivalent
- Experience in technical leadership of development, testing, and implementation of large-scale, complex technology projects
- Experience writing and publishing technical documents or equivalent
- Experience as a mentor, tech lead or leading an engineering team, or experience managing teams
- Seven or more years of experience designing and operating large-scale production networks
- Three or more years of experience developing network automation in Python or another programming language, including writing, testing and maintaining code used in production
- Five or more years of experience with major routing protocols, specifically BGP and OSPF, including designing routing policy at scale
- Experience with routing and switching hardware from major vendors including Cisco and Juniper
- Experience building network automation frameworks, CI/CD pipelines for network changes, and automated pre- and post-change validation
- Experience applying software engineering practices to network code, including source control, code reviews and automated testing
- Experience with network automation and orchestration tools, and with infrastructure as code approaches for network infrastructure
- Experience with AI-powered tools for network automation, diagnostics, and operational efficiency, including agentic AWS AI tools such as Kiro
- Experience with SDWAN technologies, particularly Cisco SDWAN, including large-scale site migrations
- Experience with AWS networking services including Transit Gateway, CloudWAN, Direct Connect, and AWS Network Firewalls
- Experience developing and delivering large-scale designs in data center or edge networks
- Experience with IPv6 design and implementations
- Proficiency in wide area networking, including SDWAN, MPLS, and Internet connectivity
- Experience with capacity planning and bandwidth forecasting for large-scale networks, including data analysis at scale
- Understanding of information security principles for network infrastructure
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence across organizations

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Why Mozilla?

Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people.

The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms.

About this team and role:

Mozilla Accounts is central to Firefox’s future—a platform that delivers seamless, privacy-respecting authentication and security while unlocking value across our ecosystem of products and services. We are evolving Mozilla Accounts into a frictionless access layer that powers cross-device engagement and growth through Sync, while connecting users to services like VPN, Relay, and emerging AI-powered experiences like Smart Window. Alongside Mozilla Accounts, our subscription platform powers payments across services and is expanding to support entitlements and metering.

We’re seeking a Staff Product Manager to lead key initiatives across authentication, feature access, account recovery, and cross-platform experience, while guiding the evolution of our payments platform. In this role, you will define and drive strategy and execution to make Mozilla Accounts a value-driven growth engine for Firefox, reducing friction and increasing the number of users who are signed in and actively benefiting from our ecosystem.

You’ll work at the intersection of identity, payments, and growth—partnering closely with engineering, design, data science, and Firefox product teams to deliver impactful user experiences and measurable business outcomes.

What you’ll do:

  • Lead end-to-end delivery of key accounts initiatives, including passwordless authentication and improvements to the authentication and account recovery experience across platforms.
  • Manage the Mozilla Accounts and Subscription Platform product roadmap, setting clear priorities aligned to strategic goals and driving focused execution.
  • Guide improvements to Mozilla’s subscription platform across payments, entitlements, and metering, partnering with product teams to support their subscription offerings.
  • Seek out and leverage data and user insights and market research to drive impact through experimentation.
  • Partner closely with engineering, UX, and cross-functional teams to maintain a focused, outcome-driven backlog.
  • Define and monitor key success metrics to measure product performance and course-correct as needed.

What you’ll bring:

  • 6+ years of experience as a product manager with experience on user-facing products and back-end services, serving both internal and external customers.
  • Strong technical acumen and understanding of software architecture.
  • A data-driven approach to identifying issues and opportunities, making decisions, setting goals, and tracking progress. Hands-on experience using data visualizations tools such as SQL, Looker, or Excel.
  • Curiosity and adaptability, comfortable dealing with ambiguity and operating with minimal direction, and a desire to learn and develop.
  • Experience with identity and payment platforms
  • Commitment to Mozilla values:
  • Welcoming differences
  • Being relationship-minded
  • Practicing responsible participation
  • Having grit

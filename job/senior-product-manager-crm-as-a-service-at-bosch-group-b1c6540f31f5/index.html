About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

You are the strategic mind and the face of our CRM portfolio. You don’t just manage backlogs; you position our CRM solutions (with a strong focus on Salesforce Service Cloud as well as future-oriented alternatives) as indispensable value drivers for modern customer service organizations.

You do not convince stakeholders with slides, but with robust, value-based business cases, tangible proof-of-concepts, and collaborative consulting at eye level.

  • Product Strategy & Evolution: You manage the lifecycle of our modular CRM solution. Working closely with our Professional Services organization, you further develop our standardized service blueprints, identify technology trends, and define the roadmap for future-proof service architectures.
  • Hands-on Presales & PoCs: You lead discovery workshops for new internal projects. Together with our Solution Architects, you create rapid, compelling demos and click-dummies to make the operational value-add tangible.
  • Profitability & TCO Excellence: You assume commercial responsibility for your product. Together with IT partners, you conduct objective TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) and TVO (Total Value of Ownership) comparisons to provide sound proof of the strategic benefits of modern CRM systems over grown legacy and in-house solutions.
  • License & Partner Management: You manage the commercial models of our Salesforce ecosystem and unlock new potential for your product.
  • Strong Interdisciplinary Collaboration: You work hand-in-hand with our well-coordinated internal Delivery and Professional Services unit, which is responsible for technical implementation, ensuring a seamless transition from idea to project.
  • Holistic Product Development: You confidently represent your product at and with relevant stakeholders and guide the organizational change during our transition toward Global Services.
  • Experience & Background: At least 5 years of solid professional experience in product management, enterprise solution consulting, or technical presales within the CRM and contact center solution space (ideally in a B2B, BPO, or enterprise IT environment).
  • Technological Understanding: Deep practical insight into CRM architectures (preferably Salesforce Sales or Service Cloud; certifications such as Salesforce Administrator / Consultant are a plus). You are tool-pragmatic and open to alternative platforms (e.g., Microsoft Dynamics, agile CRM tools).
  • Affinity for Prototyping: You enjoy more than just writing down requirements; you can independently or in tandem with architects set up and present a practical PoC.
  • Financial & Negotiation Strength: Excellent skills in business modeling (cost-benefit analyses, SaaS pricing, license controlling).
  • Communicative Confidence: Diplomatic skills, assertiveness in matrix organizations, and the ability to inspire and build enthusiasm.
  • Language Skills: Business-fluent written and spoken English.

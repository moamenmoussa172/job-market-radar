About the job

The Company

Toters is an on-demand e-commerce and delivery platform and operates a service that enables customers to get anything in their city at the highest level of convenience.

At Toters, technology is at the heart of everything we do. We have product teams that are working hard every day to create products that make our customers' lives easier. Our engineers are also continuously creating solutions to make our processes more efficient, all in an effort to get to our customers fast and at the best cost. If you are interested in working in a high growth startup environment, and look to be part of a team that will potentially change the way customers shop in the Middle East, apply now.

Role

The Product Designer is responsible for delivering largely independent UI and UX for our different platforms. As a Product Designer at toters, you will collaborate with teams of designers, researchers, engineers, content strategists, and product managers throughout the product process, setting the bar for creative excellence and providing best-in-class visual design thinking and usability expertise at the start of every project. You will utilize user flows, wireframes, flow diagrams, storyboards, user interface mockups, and/or high-fidelity prototypes to convey the user experience at different phases of the design process. It will be your mission to promote the prioritization of design-centered adjustments, adjustments, and enhancements.

Responsibilities

  • Create user-centered designs by embracing an outside-in approach, bringing the voice of the customers into the organization, gather customer feedback, analyze and synthesize usability findings, develop user journeys.
  • Design intuitive, results driven products and thoughtful user experiences that balance aesthetics and usability.
  • Perform user research, conduct user tests, and design the overall functionality of the product to guarantee an excellent user experience.
  • Be resourceful and creative to overcome obstacles and turn challenges into opportunities.
  • Develop Prototypes to demonstrate and evaluate potential ideas with stakeholders and validate hypotheses by conducting usability testings.
  • Listen and share with colleagues across the company to drive collaboration.
  • Work within an Agile team environment that delivers through iteration.
  • Maintain design consistency throughout our products.
  • Discuss design principles and guidelines with various company stakeholders to gain their support.
  • Be the user-advocate by conducting usability tests on early designs and making implementation recommendations from a user-centered standpoint.
  • Above all, enjoy creating cutting-edge consumer applications for the Web and mobile platforms.

Qualifications

  • You hold a BA/BS degree or equivalent practical experience; an advanced degree is a plus.
  • You have 5+ years of experience in UX & UI design.
  • You’re skilled with Figma, Sketch, or other design tools.
  • You’re familiar with design thinking and human-centered methodologies.
  • You know how to conduct or interpret user research and usability testing.
  • You use data and user insights to guide your decisions.
  • You’re an excellent communicator and a team player.
  • You thrive in a fast-paced, iterative, and remote-friendly environment.
  • You understand the importance of design systems and consistency.
  • You’ve collaborated with PMs, engineers, and stakeholders to bring ideas to life.
  • You’re comfortable designing end-to-end experiences, from research to high-fidelity UI.

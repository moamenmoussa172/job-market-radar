About the job

As an Audit Manager – IT & Cyber Security Audit, you will lead the planning and execution of IT, cybersecurity, and technology audit engagements across Tabby, manage a team of IT audit professionals, and help ensure the technology risk-based annual audit plan is delivered under the direction of the Head of Internal Audit.

  • Lead the planning, execution, and reporting of IT general controls, application controls, and cybersecurity audit engagements, in line with the approved risk-based audit plan.
  • Manage and review the work of IT Audit Leads, Senior Auditors, and Auditors on assigned engagements, ensuring compliance with the Global Internal Audit Standards and internal audit methodology.
  • Contribute to the annual technology and cyber risk assessment and audit universe, identifying and prioritizing audit areas based on risk exposure.
  • Evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of IT general controls (access management, change management, IT operations), application controls, and cybersecurity controls across Tabby's technology environment, including cloud infrastructure.
  • Review audit working papers and technical evidence to ensure quality, completeness, and adequate support for findings and conclusions.
  • Prepare and present audit reports and findings on technology and cyber risk to IT/Engineering leadership and senior management, ensuring recommendations are practical and risk-prioritized.
  • Manage stakeholder relationships with Engineering, Information Security, and Technology leadership, negotiating corrective action plans and remediation timelines.
  • Monitor and track the implementation status of IT and cyber audit recommendations, escalating overdue items to the Head of Internal Audit.
  • Provide advisory input on IT risk, cybersecurity controls, cloud security, and data protection for new systems, products, and vendor relationships.
  • Support the Head of Internal Audit in preparing IT/cyber risk materials for the Audit Committee and external/regulatory audits.
  • Coach, develop, and performance-manage assigned team members, including IT Audit Leads, Senior Auditors, and Auditors.
  • Deputize for the Head of Internal Audit on IT/cyber audit matters as required.
  • Stay current on IT auditing standards, cybersecurity frameworks (e.g., NIST, ISO 27001), the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, and emerging technology risks.
  • 8+ (Senior +10) years of experience in IT audit, information security, or technology risk, including at least 2-3 years in a supervisory or team lead capacity, preferably within banking, fintech, or corporate environments.
  • Strong knowledge of IT auditing standards, cybersecurity frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001), and the SAMA Cyber Security Framework.
  • Proven experience assessing IT general controls, application controls, cybersecurity controls, and cloud environments, and managing full audit engagements.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to engage with Engineering and Information Security leadership, and to present audit findings to the Audit Committee.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and team leadership skills with a detail-oriented approach.
  • Proficiency in IT audit and data analytics tools, and familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP).
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Cybersecurity, or a related field; CISA required, CISSP, CRISC, or CISM highly desirable.

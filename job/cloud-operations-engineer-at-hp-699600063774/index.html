About the job

Cloud Operations Engineer

Description -

As the HP Wolf Security team at HP, our primary focus is developing cyber security solutions to protect our customers' endpoint devices and data. The digital threat landscape is ever-changing, and as the cyber security industry reacts and adapts, so too do malware authors. Our unique micro-virtualization technology helps protect customers from even the most cutting-edge cyber security threats. It forms a key pillar in a wider cyber security suite that we're actively developing. To see what our engineering teams are working on, visit our technical blogs: https://threatresearch.ext.hp.com/blog.

We are looking for an experienced developer to join our Cloud Operations team. This is a new role focused on maintaining and improving our existing cloud services, while working closely with development teams on new cloud-first applications. It's an opportunity for someone with a solid development background who is looking to move into a more cloud-focused role. Flexible hours are available, working in the office or on a hybrid basis. This is an exciting time to join our Cloud Operations team as we continue to expand our cloud footprint.

What you will do as a Cloud Operations Engineer

  • Manage cloud infrastructure and services.
  • Work with engineering teams to design and implement cloud solutions.
  • Design infrastructure with security as a key objective and improve the security of existing services.
  • Automate deployment workflows wherever possible.
  • Ensure applications and services have appropriate availability, reliability and scalability.
  • Help troubleshoot issues and outages.

Technical requirements for the Cloud Operations Engineer:

  • Solid development skills and experience with scripting languages such as PowerShell, Python or Bash.
  • A degree in Computer Science or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience

A great Cloud Operations Engineer will have:

  • Knowledge of cloud technologies and operating cloud platforms, such as AWS or Azure.
  • Experience using the Linux command line and administering Linux systems.
  • Commercial experience in a DevOps role.
  • Experience with Docker and cloud container orchestration technologies such as ECS or Kubernetes.
  • Commercial exposure to Cloudflare services, such as DNS, page rules, firewall or Workers.
  • Experience managing CI/CD systems and automation, such as Jenkins or Azure DevOps.
  • Experience with infrastructure as code, such as Terraform and Terragrunt.
  • Experience using analytics and monitoring systems such as Elastic Stack, New Relic or PagerDuty to monitor systems and diagnose problems.

About HP Wolf Security

HP Wolf Security develops industry-leading cybersecurity solutions that help protect devices, data and users from an ever-evolving threat landscape. Our unique approach combines endpoint security, isolation technology and cloud services to help organisations stay secure against both known and emerging threats. Joining HP Wolf Security means working on products that make a real difference to customers worldwide while collaborating with talented engineers, researchers and security experts.

To see what our engineering teams are working on, check out our technical blogs: HP Threat Research Blog

Our history:

Inspired by the isolation principles of traditional virtualization, our team known then as Bromium, created a game-changing technology called micro-virtualization to protect end users against advanced malware. Every task the user performs, such as opening a document or clicking on a link, is isolated in its own micro-VM, with access to just the resources required for that task, and existing just for the life of the task. Protection is thus provided through isolation, without relying on detection, hence reliably defending the user from polymorphic and even zero-day malware.

Bromium was acquired by HP Inc on 19 September 2019 forming HP Wolf Security.

For more information, visit our website: https://www.hp.com/uk-en/security/endpoint-security-solutions.html

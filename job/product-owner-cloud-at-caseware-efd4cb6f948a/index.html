About the job

Caseware combines over 35 years of industry expertise with AI-powered technology to transform how audit and accounting professionals work. Serving organizations across 130 countries, we bring intelligent automation directly into audit and financial reporting workflows—helping professionals reduce manual work, navigate complexity, and focus on informed decisions. Join our global team and help build technology that strengthens trust in financial information.

As Product Owner for our Cloud solutions, you are responsible for driving the development, maintenance, and continuous improvement of our products. You work closely with a full team—both functional and development—so affinity with both is important. You organize and prioritize the team’s work through a well-managed backlog and sprint board, ensuring delivery of maximum value to our customers.

❗This is a Full time permanent position

📍 Location:

This is a hybrid role requiring the successful candidate to work 3 days a week in our Apeldoorn, Netherlands office, located at Fauststraat 3, 7323 BA Apeldoorn, Netherlands

What you will be doing:

  • Drive the development, maintenance, and continuous improvement of our Cloud products.
  • Collaborate closely with a full team—both functional and development—so affinity with both is important.
  • Organize and prioritize the team’s work through a well-managed backlog and sprint board.
  • Ensure delivery of maximum value to our customers.
  • Define the product roadmap in alignment with strategic goals and market developments.
  • Coach the team to stay aligned with our overall product vision.
  • Focus on financial reporting, audit, and related workflows for the Dutch accountancy market.
  • Act as the final responsible for the success of our Cloud solutions.
  • Understand customer needs and balance various stakeholder demands.
  • Translate insights into clear product decisions and priorities.
  • Share progress regularly via roadmap presentations and other communications.
  • Create clarity and alignment across internal and external stakeholders.
  • What you will bring:
  • Experience or a strong affinity with audit and accounting (hands-on experience in practice is a plus)
  • Possesses deep understanding of the Dutch market and user needs
  • Supports the team in delivering accurate, high-quality, and compliant product content
  • Works effectively both independently and collaboratively, with excellent prioritization skills
  • Excels at translating complex concepts into intuitive, user-friendly solutions
  • Is available full-time and ready to take ownership as a proactive leader within the team
  • What's in it for you:
  • Shape what’s next with AI: Help transform audit and accounting through AI-powered technology. Explore new ways to bring AI into our own workflows, rethink how work gets done, and turn ideas into meaningful improvements for our teams and customers.
  • Make an impact that matters: Contribute to solutions that help professionals navigate complexity, make informed decisions, and strengthen trust in financial information around the world.
  • Bring your voice. Expand your perspective: Collaborate with a diverse global team where your ideas are valued, knowledge is shared, and different perspectives lead to better outcomes.
  • Build a career that grows with you: Develop your skills, take on new challenges, and explore career opportunities as our business and technology evolve.
  • Thrive at work and beyond: Enjoy competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits, recognition programs, and performance bonuses, alongside flexible work options, remote opportunities, and generous time off.
  • Many Voices, One Team: We’re committed to building teams as diverse as our customers through equitable and inclusive hiring. We welcome candidates of all backgrounds.

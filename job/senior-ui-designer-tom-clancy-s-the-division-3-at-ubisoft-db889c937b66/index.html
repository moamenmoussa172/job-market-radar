Senior UI Designer [Tom Clancy's The Division 3]

Ubisoft · Malmö, Sweden

Onsite · Senior · UI / Visual Designer · Posted 27 October 2026

Apply on Ubisoft's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

In 2023, it was announced that Massive Entertainment is leading the development of Tom Clancy's The Division 3. While we cannot unveil any details about the third entry in the series at this time, we are actively recruiting for the project.

We are looking for an experienced UI Designer to join our dedicated UI team. In addition to the UI team, you will also work closely with directors, designers, artists and programmers from other disciplines making sure the game user interface is clear, understandable, and engaging.

What You’ll Do

  • Design UI layouts and user flows that support intuitive player experiences.
  • Create concepts for UI elements, menu screens, icons, animations, and other interface assets.
  • Ensure the UI delivers an immersive experience that aligns with the game's visual direction and creative vision

What You’ll Bring

Your knowledge in the field of UX/UI and games transfers into a clear enthusiasm for what you do. You feel responsible for the player experience and take initiative to bring features forward, ensuring the highest possible quality. To achieve this, you're open to feedback and other perspectives, whilst you're also able to constructively provide feedback of your own to the cross-disciplinary teams around you. You're curious to learn new technologies and are open to challenging yourself in achieving the best end-result.

  • Professional experience of UI development for AAA video games
  • Solid understanding of graphic design, interaction design and information architecture
  • Experience with Snowdrop or other node-based engines is a plus
  • Experience with motion graphics and 3D are a plus

Ubisoft is a global leader in gaming with teams across the world creating original and memorable gaming experiences, from Assassin’s Creed, Rainbow Six to Just Dance and more. We believe diverse perspectives help both players and teams thrive. If you’re passionate about innovation and pushing entertainment boundaries, join our journey and help us create the unknown!

You can also find a summary of the Massive Job Perks here.

Questions? We're more than happy to answer them! Please contact Paula Roese Mesquita, Talent Acquisition Partner for this position.

All your application information will be kept confidential according to EEO & GDPR guidelines.

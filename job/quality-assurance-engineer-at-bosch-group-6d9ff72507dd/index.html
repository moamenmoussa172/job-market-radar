Quality Assurance Engineer

Bosch Group · Ciudad Juarez, Mexico

Onsite · QA / Test Engineer · Posted 10 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Interface to external customers regarding product quality (one voice to customer)
  • Quality assurance for new product launches to achieve product quality targets.
  • Responsible for assurance of product quality (raw material to shipping)
  • Process confirmation to maintain/improve product quality for series production & assure compliance.
  • Responsible of customer claims process and initiate improvement plans

Control of handling of non-conforming material and fast disposition.

Working Experience:

  • >5 years of professional experience in manufacturing or quality, preferably in automotive industry including customer contact.

Key-Skills:

  • Electronics manufacturing process knowledge, APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA, SPC, IATF 16949, VDA.
  • Problem Solving Methods (8D, 5x Why, 6 Sigma, Shainin, etc.)
  • Knowledge of Automotive Customer Requirements & B2B portals.
  • Communication/negotiation skills, high initiative, independent style of working, ability to work under pressure, structured & analytical approach, accountability/ reliability.

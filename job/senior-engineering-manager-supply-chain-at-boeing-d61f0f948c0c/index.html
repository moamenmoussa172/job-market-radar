About the job

Senior Engineering Manager - Supply Chain

Company:

Boeing Poland sp. z o.o. EFS

Boeing Poland is currently looking for a Senior Engineering Manager (Supply Chain) to join our team located in Gdansk, Poland.

Responsibilities:

  • Manages a team of engineering managers, and design and structural analysis engineers.
  • Directs all project phases and acts as primary customer contact for project activities for engineering hardware development projects.
  • Leads safety, productivity and quality-related matters pertaining to program execution, performance, and improvements.
  • Enable strong Investigations and Root Cause Corrective Action.
  • Effectively communicate project expectations to team members, team first line leaders and stakeholders in a timely and clear fashion.
  • Manages employees and first line leaders performing activities within multiple (2 or more) Engineering disciplines.
  • Implements policies and procedures and sets operational goals.
  • Acquires resources for projects and processes, coordinates the strategic and operational aspects of ongoing projects.
  • Develops and maintains relationships and partnerships with customers, stakeholders, peers, partners and direct reports.
  • Identifies and manages actively opportunities, risks and issues to ensure effective resolution or mitigation, and co-ordinate required activities to ensure successful delivery.
  • Delegates tasks and responsibilities to appropriate personnel.
  • Plan, schedule and manage project timelines and milestones using appropriate tools.
  • Drive organizational productivity improvements through the use of the Lean principles
  • Manages, develops and motivates employees for expertise in product line statements of work.
  • Team up with leaders in Poland and Global to support broader business objectives.
  • Champions Boeing Values and Behaviors within his team and in relation with others. Lead by example.

Position Requirements:

  • Bachelor of Science degree in Engineering, Engineering Technology (including Manufacturing Technology), Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or non-US equivalent qualifications directly related to the work statement.
  • 7+ years of related Engineering experience together with people management experience.
  • Fluent English and Polish.
  • Experience of managing complex projects/programs.
  • Experience of Commercial Aerospace and Defense related projects is highly desirable.
  • Experience with Certification projects is highly desirable.
  • Project Liaison experience is highly desirable.
  • Experience working with Regulatory Agencies (FAA or EASA).
  • Extensive Project Management experience, ideally holding a recognized Project Management Certificate.
  • Highly competent in using MS Project & the MS Office products in general.
  • Competent in using Engineering Systems common across aerospace.
  • Competent across engineering disciplines.
  • Experience using tracking and reporting tool.
  • Experience implementing lean or six-sigma principles on a team.
  • Proven track record of successful delivery of complexity and risk in projects across a variety of areas.
  • The individual must be enthusiastic, hands on in their approach and possess excellent written and oral communication skills.
  • They must be comfortable interacting with colleagues at all levels within the organization.

Join us if you want to:

  • Market competitive salary and performance-based incentive program.
  • Have flexible working hours.
  • Enjoy additional paid days off for Christmas break (December 24th-January 1st).
  • Develop professional skills with our virtual learning tools and platforms.
  • Benefit from Learning Together Program and MyBenefit program.
  • Benefit from Employee Recognition Program.
  • Get Medical Plan (Medicover/LuxMed) and life insurance (Warta S.A.) for you and your Family.
  • Participate in Employee Pension Scheme (PPE).
  • Enjoy and create with us friendly and inclusive atmosphere in workplace.
  • Cooperate and exchange knowledge with world class global engineering team.

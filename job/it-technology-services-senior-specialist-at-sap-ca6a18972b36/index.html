Job Title: IT Technology Services Senior Specialist

Location: Dallas, TX

Work Model: Hybrid Work Model.

Purpose and Objective:

SAP America, Inc. seeks an IT Technology Services Senior Specialist at our Dallas, TX location to design and develop systems, applications, products and services to optimize/automate business processes, improve work efficiency.

Expectations and Tasks:

Provide the expert support in operations. Facilitate the service lifecycle and product lifecycle. Deliver outcomes of assigned projects. Enhance complex systems/processes. Represent SAP to customers. Advocate for the end user by having a deep understanding of end user needs, behaviors & usage patterns. Promote the role of User Experience, thorough understanding of contemporary user-centered design methodologies & practices, and integrate those into current and future development efforts. Execute technical feasibility studies / solution reviews. Engage with business stakeholders and other technology teams to deliver solutions to business requirements. Perform complex data and system development along with technology integration through learned technology coding languages. Facilitate the system / service / product lifecycle, and ensure stable, secure, performant and high available operations for and between system and cloud services. Facilitate agile engagement with the business. Revise and update existing programs and provide required documentation.

Education and Occupational Experience:

Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field of study and six (6) years of progressive post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or related occupation. Alternatively, a master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field of study and four (4) years of experience in the job offered or related occupation.

Qualifications/Skills and Competencies Experience:

Experience must include four (4) years involving each of the following:

  • Evaluating SAP architecture, identifying weaknesses, and improving opportunities;
  • Advising customers on technical requirements, constraints, and supporting technical situations throughout lifecycle;
  • Technical planning, implementation, configuration, and integration of SAP solutions;
  • Agile methodologies including Scrum, Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), and blended approaches;
  • Managing and providing hands-on Architect level support of SAP related projects;
  • Collaborating and coordinating with business and internal infrastructure teams to deliver stable, highly available business systems;
  • S/4HANA architecture, conversion, migration path, methodology and tools;
  • SAP S/4 HANA, BW on HANA, SDI, SDA, HA/DR setup, RTO and RPO, HSR, and BW MultiHost; and
  • SAP cloud solutions and integration scenarios of SAP systems with SCP and BTP.

SAP Certified - SAP S/4HANA Conversion and SAP System Upgrade

Travel: N/A.

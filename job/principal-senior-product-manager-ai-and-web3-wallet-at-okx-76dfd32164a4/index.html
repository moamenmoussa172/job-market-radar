Principal/Senior Product Manager, AI & Web3 Wallet

About the job

OKX will be prioritising applicants who have a current right to work in Hong Kong, and do not require OKX's sponsorship of a visa.

Who We Are

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er.

OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

About The Opportunity

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Drive the integration of Agentic Wallet infrastructure across emerging chains, protocols, and AI-native ecosystems, collaborating closely with BD teams to unlock strategic partnerships.
  • Explore and analyze AI Economy trends, identifying opportunities to position wallets as the core execution and value layer for AI Agents. Stay ahead of AI × Web3 convergence trends, contributing to long-term product vision.
  • Build strong connections with the developer community to promote wallet services among public chain developers, increasing integration and usage rates.
  • Develop user growth strategies for Web3 Wallets to enhance user retention and activity levels.
  • Monitor user growth data, analyze user behavior, optimize product experience and growth strategies, and improve conversion rates.
  • Collaborate closely with the R&D team to manage the product iteration process, ensuring it follows the planned schedule.
  • Engage in in-depth discussions about business scenarios and technological trends to ensure the product's applicability and foresight.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor's degree or higher.
  • Over 2 years of experience in Web3 chains or Web3 wallets, along with 2+ years as a product manager in a large internet company.
  • Strong ability to abstract and simplify complex problems, utilizing first-principles thinking; adept at systematic thinking and innovative problem-solving.
  • Excellent project management skills, capable of driving cross-team collaboration and ensuring timely delivery of technical solutions.
  • Strong resilience under pressure, with outstanding communication skills and flexible thinking.
  • A keen interest in Web3, with a sharp awareness of industry trends.

Perks & Benefits

  • Competitive total compensation package
  • L&D programs and education subsidy for employees' growth and development
  • Various team building programs and company events
  • Wellness and meal allowances
  • Comprehensive healthcare schemes for employees and dependants
  • More that we love to tell you along the process!

Please note that Hong Kong is a group-level service hub, and OKX does not carry on a business of operating a virtual asset trading platform in Hong Kong.

About the job

Your contribution to something big:

You will contribute to an industrial research project focused on developing machine learning and AI solutions to predict and optimize energy consumption in residential heating systems. You will work across the full AI lifecycle, from data collection and feature engineering to model development, validation, and deployment. You will also explore Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and AI Agents to develop intelligent applications and automate engineering workflows, collaborating closely with R&D, thermal simulation, controls, and software teams. Your main responsibilities will be:

  • Machine Learning Development: Design, develop, and validate machine learning models to predict residential heating system energy consumption, including hourly consumption, peak loads, and longer-term energy demand.
  • Data Engineering & Feature Development: Work with sensor data, weather information, building characteristics, occupancy data, and other relevant sources to prepare datasets and develop meaningful features.
  • Data Quality & Analysis: Clean and analyse complex sensor and IoT datasets, identifying missing or inconsistent data and addressing seasonal effects and changes in operating conditions.
  • Modeling & Forecasting: Develop and compare different approaches, including regression, time-series forecasting, gradient boosting, neural networks, physics-informed models, and hybrid solutions.
  • Generative AI & AI Agents: Explore and develop AI applications using LLMs, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and AI agents to support data analysis, knowledge extraction, intelligent automation, and engineering workflows.
  • Model Validation & Optimization: Evaluate and continuously improve ML and AI solutions through experimentation, error analysis, performance evaluation, and robustness testing.
  • ML/AI Deployment & Monitoring: Support the integration of ML and AI solutions into production environments, contributing to monitoring, model performance evaluation, and continuous improvement.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with thermal modelling, HVAC, controls, and software engineers to translate complex engineering challenges into data science and AI solutions.
  • Technical Guidance & Documentation: Support junior team members and document modelling approaches, AI architectures, results, assumptions, and technical trade-offs.

What distinguishes you:

  • Experience: Around 3 years of experience in Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
  • Machine Learning: Good knowledge of machine learning, statistical methods, and predictive modelling, including regression, classification, and time-series forecasting.
  • Generative AI & LLMs: Knowledge or practical experience with LLMs, Generative AI applications, prompt engineering, and RAG.
  • AI Agents: Understanding AI agent architectures and the integration of LLMs with external tools, APIs, and data sources is a plus.
  • Programming: Strong Python skills and experience with tools such as scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, TensorFlow, or AI development frameworks.
  • Data Analysis: Experience working with complex datasets, including data cleaning, feature engineering, and analysis. Experience with sensor or IoT data is a plus.
  • ML/AI Deployment: Understanding of model deployment, monitoring, and MLOps/LLMOps concepts.
  • Technical Knowledge: Experience with time-series forecasting, physics-informed ML, HVAC/thermal systems, or IoT applications is considered an advantage.
  • Problem Solving & Collaboration: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, good communication skills, and ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary engineering and software teams.
  • Working Style: Proactive, structured, and comfortable taking ownership of assigned activities and supporting junior team members.

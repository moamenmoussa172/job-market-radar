About this Role

We are seeking a Cyber Security Engineer with 2–7 years of experience in cyber security incident response and threat mitigation to join our DDoS Security team in Bangalore or Pune.

In this role, you will monitor and analyse cyber security alerts and perform real-time threat and DDoS attack mitigation to protect customer and organisational infrastructure. You will use packet and traffic analysis to distinguish malicious activity from legitimate traffic, configure appropriate filters and countermeasures, and support customers during security incidents.

You will collaborate with global teams, markets, customers and technology vendors to optimise threat mitigation strategies and security configurations. The role also involves customer onboarding, platform management, technical consultation and support for pre-sales activities.

As real-time incident response is central to this position, 24x7 flexibility to work in shifts is required.

What you’ll do

  • Monitor and analyse cyber security alerts to identify genuine attacks and initiate appropriate mitigation.
  • Respond to security incidents within defined organisational SLAs.
  • Perform real-time DDoS attack detection and mitigation, including volumetric and application-layer attacks.
  • Conduct deep packet inspection and traffic analysis to identify attack patterns, vectors and malicious traffic.
  • Configure and optimise filters, countermeasures and security controls based on identified attack vectors.
  • Improve mitigation effectiveness through ongoing analysis and optimisation of security configurations.
  • Troubleshoot cyber security incidents with customers and recommend solutions to reduce infrastructure risk.
  • Consult customers on recent threats, attack scenarios and appropriate mitigation strategies.
  • Onboard new customers onto the security platform based on agreed requirements.
  • Provide technical consultation to pre-sales teams supporting prospective customers.
  • Manage the configuration lifecycle of security platforms and implement changes within defined SLAs.
  • Contribute to problem management and major incident management when required.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to share knowledge and implement effective security practices.
  • Engage with vendors to resolve platform issues and obtain threat-related technical consultation.
  • Identify opportunities to automate repetitive operational and security activities.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, local work instructions and operational procedures.

Who you are

You have:

  • 2–7 years of experience in cyber security incident response and threat mitigation.
  • Experience in analysing cyber attacks and taking appropriate mitigation actions.
  • Strong packet analysis skills, with the ability to differentiate DDoS attacks from legitimate network traffic.
  • Hands-on experience in real-time cyber threat mitigation.
  • Knowledge of DDoS attack detection and mitigation.
  • Experience with deep packet inspection.
  • The ability to understand and configure filters and countermeasures for volumetric and application-based attacks.
  • Knowledge of the NIST incident response process and MITRE ATT&CK framework.
  • Experience or knowledge of Radware DefensePro.
  • Experience or knowledge of Radware DefenseFlow.
  • Experience or knowledge of Arbor DDoS protection.
  • Knowledge of application-layer attack mitigation.
  • The ability to collaborate with customers, global teams, internal stakeholders and technology vendors.
  • Flexibility to work in a 24x7 shift environment.

Relevant certifications are desirable, including:

  • Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)
  • ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)
  • CompTIA Security+

DDoS attack mitigation experience would be an added advantage.

About the job

About this Role

We are seeking a skilled and detail-oriented Software Tester with experience in Telecom BSS/OSS environments to support the delivery of high-quality system changes across Fixed Line, Home Broadband, customer care, order management, and provisioning journeys. This role is focused on System Integration Testing (SIT), end-to-end validation, defect management, and quality assurance across complex business and technology landscapes. The successful candidate will collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure solutions are delivered in line with business requirements, quality standards, and release objectives.

What you will do

  • Analyse business and functional requirements, participate in walkthroughs, and assess testing impacts across applications, systems, and customer journeys.
  • Define SIT scope, estimate testing effort, and prepare test plans, schedules, test scenarios, test cases, and requirement traceability artefacts.
  • Execute System Integration Testing, end-to-end testing, integration testing, and regression testing across complex business and technical processes.
  • Validate functional requirements, system integrations, interfaces, data flows, order processing activities, and downstream outcomes.
  • Prepare and maintain suitable test data and ensure supporting test environments are available for planned execution activities.
  • Log, track, retest, and close defects using Microsoft Azure DevOps and actively participate in defect triage discussions.
  • Perform root cause analysis and collaborate with Business, Development, Release Management, and supplier teams to support timely defect resolution.
  • Provide clear updates on test execution, defects, risks, issues, and dependencies, supporting evidence-based Go/No-Go recommendations.
  • Maintain testing metrics, quality dashboards, and test evidence in accordance with SDLC and STLC governance requirements.
  • Apply automation testing knowledge to identify suitable regression scenarios, support automated test execution, analyse results, and collaborate on script maintenance activities.
  • Contribute to reusable test assets, shift-left testing practices, process standardisation, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Coordinate test closure activities, capture lessons learned, and support the implementation of agreed improvements in future deliveries.

Who you are

  • Minimum of 3 years' experience in software testing across the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
  • Proven hands-on experience in System Integration Testing, end-to-end testing, integration testing, regression testing, and defect management.
  • Good understanding of Fixed Line, Home Broadband, telecom customer journeys, order management, and provisioning processes.
  • Experience using testing tools, particularly Microsoft Azure DevOps.
  • Strong knowledge of test planning, requirements traceability, quality metrics, and test reporting.
  • Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, root cause analysis, communication, and stakeholder management capabilities.
  • Ability to work effectively within matrix teams involving onshore, offshore, business, technology, and supplier stakeholders.
  • Telecom, BSS/OSS, or customer care domain experience is preferred.
  • ISTQB or ISEB certification is preferred.
  • Experience working within complex, large-scale release environments is desirable.

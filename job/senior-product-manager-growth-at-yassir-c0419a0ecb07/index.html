About the job

About Yassir and Yassir Cash

Yassir is a super app serving Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia, Senegal, South Africa and other markets with ride-hailing, delivery and financial services.

Yassir Cash is the mobile wallet behind Yassir's financial services. Users pay, send money, top up and pay bills from the app. In Algeria, they also cash in and out through a network of 6,000+ agents.

The role

You will build Yassir Financial Services' growth engines and decide how the business uses them.

Today, incentives are built campaign by campaign, one partner or business line at a time. We want them built once, as configurable engines: loyalty, cashback, referrals, gift cards, subscriptions and customer value management (CVM). Then we want them used deliberately across every Yassir FS product.

The engines won't stay inside FS. You will turn them into internal products, so that other Yassir domains, such as ride-hailing and delivery, can run their own programs on them.

You are the first dedicated growth PM in Yassir Cash. You'll report directly to Karim and work across product, engineering, data, marketing, commercial and finance.

What you'll own

You own six engines end to end, from strategy and specs through launch and economics.

  • Loyalty: One rewards program across Yassir FS: earn rules, tiers, expiry and redemption
  • Cashback: Cashback configurable by merchant, category, segment and business line, with caps, funding source and clawback on refunds
  • Referrals: Referral mechanics with clear reward economics and fraud controls
  • Gift cards: Consumer and corporate gift cards, including restricted balances by category and expiry
  • Subscriptions: Wallet-funded subscription offers and the benefits attached to them
  • CVM: Segmentation, lifecycle triggers and personalised offers, run with CRM and marketing
  • Across the engines, you will:
  • Make them configurable: Operations and marketing should launch a campaign without new engineering work each time.
  • Propose how Yassir FS uses them: For each product and business line (ride-hailing, delivery, merchants, bills and airtime), set which lever applies, who funds it and what it targets.
  • Productise them for Yassir: Package the engines so other Yassir domains can use them on their own. That means admin tools and APIs, onboarding, a funding and cost-sharing model, and service levels.
  • Own the economics: Set incentive cost per incremental active user, payback, budget guardrails and abuse controls.
  • Prove incrementality: Run A/B tests and holdouts, and stop what doesn't pay back.
  • Align the business: Get product, commercial, finance, and legal and compliance behind one set of rules.
  • What success looks like in 12 months Within 12 months, every Yassir FS incentive runs on shared engines, spend is tied to measured incremental activity, and other Yassir domains have started using them.
  • By 3 months: you have mapped every incentive running across Yassir FS: who funds it, what it costs and what it moves. Your engine architecture and governance proposal is agreed with Karim.
  • By 6 months: the first engines are live and configurable without engineering work per campaign. Their impact is measured against holdouts.
  • By 12 months: the remaining engines run on the same rails. FS product teams use them through a playbook you wrote, and at least one other Yassir domain runs a program on them as an internal customer.
  • What you bring
  • 5+ years in product or CVM roles, owning growth, loyalty, rewards or customer value. If your background is CVM or commercial, you owned the platform, the offers and their results, not just the campaign calendar. Backgrounds that carry over well:
  • Telco CVM: campaign management, segmentation, real-time decisioning, offer engines and loyalty modules
  • Mobile money or wallet growth and CVM
  • Bank or card rewards programs
  • Airline, hotel or retail loyalty: tiers, earn and burn, partner economics
  • Growth at consumer marketplaces, delivery or ride-hailing apps: referrals, promo engines
  • You have built an incentive system as a platform, with configurable rules, a rewards ledger and budgets, beyond running campaigns.
  • You command incentive economics: CAC, LTV, payback, breakage, and fraud and abuse patterns.
  • You design A/B tests and holdouts, and you read the results yourself. You are comfortable in SQL.
  • You write specs that engineers can build rules engines and ledgers from.
  • You influence without authority across product teams, marketing, commercial and finance.
  • You are fluent in French and Arabic.
  • Nice to have
  • You have worked in Algeria or another cash-heavy market.
  • You know how e-money rules treat promotional balances.
  • You have hands-on experience with CleverTap or a similar engagement platform.
  • You have built corporate gift card or B2B rewards programs.
  • You have shipped a paid subscription product.

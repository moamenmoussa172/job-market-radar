About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

Elastic, the Search AI Company, empowers everyone to find answers in real-time, leveraging all their data at scale, and fueling the potential of businesses and individuals. The Elastic Search AI Platform, trusted by over 50% of the Fortune 500, unifies the precision of search with the intelligence of AI to accelerate impactful results.

The Software Engineer will join the self-service cloud marketplaces team, driving the expansion of our marketplaces business through existing partnerships and new integrations. This role directly influences the growth and reach of Elastic Cloud across key marketplace ecosystems.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Working as a full stack engineer, contributing both to the backend and frontend of the product onboarding and trial experience.
  • Designing and implementing new scalable features and APIs in our product.
  • Working closely with PMs and designers to plan and design the features.
  • Implementing new integrations and adding new marketplaces.
  • Expand the usage and capabilities of the existing marketplaces.
  • Designing and implementing data evolution strategies to manage schema changes without introducing breaking changes.

What You Bring

  • Strong proficiency in React and JavaScript/TypeScript.
  • Strong proficiency in Node.js server frameworks like Hapi and Next.js.
  • Front-end tech like HTML5, CSS3, and responsive design is in your wheelhouse.
  • You know your way around RESTful APIs and async programming.
  • If you've dabbled in state management libraries (like NgRx), that's a big bonus.
  • Git and other code collaboration tools don't scare you.
  • Experience deploying and operationalizing software in production.
  • Ability to plan, design, implement, and lead projects independently.
  • Problem-solving is your superpower, and you're all about the details.
  • Knowledge in NoSQL databases, such as DynamoDB and Elasticsearch.
  • You're an awesome communicator and a great team player.
  • Fast-paced environments? No problem for you.
  • You're a nerd (in a good way) when it comes to staying on top of industry trends and best practices.

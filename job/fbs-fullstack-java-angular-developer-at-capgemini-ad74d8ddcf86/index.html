About the job

As a Fullstack Developer you would need to design, develop, test, and support enterprise applications using Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, and Cloud technologies. The role involves application modernization, REST API development, cloud deployments, production support, and collaboration with business and technical teams in an Agile environment. Experience in Insurance and enterprise-scale application development is preferred.

Responsibilities

  • Front-End Development. Translate wireframes and UI/UX designs into clean, responsive web pages using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frameworks.
  • Back-End & APIs. Assist in building and maintaining server-side logic, RESTful APIs, and third-party service integrations.
  • Write basic database queries, handle data validation, and support minor schema updates.
  • Write unit and integration tests, troubleshoot bugs across the stack, and participate in peer code reviews.
  • Collaboration & Documentation. Work within an Agile/Scrum workflow, participate in daily stand-ups, and maintain clear technical documentation.
  • Managing the complete software development process from conception to deployment
  • Maintaining and upgrading the software following deployment
  • Managing the end-to-end life cycle for the production of software and applications
  • Overseeing and guiding the analyzing, writing, building, and deployment of software
  • Actively seeks out development opportunities to further develop T-shaped skills and become a more cross-functional contributor.
  • Practices continuous exploration, continuous integration and continuous deployment necessary for building the Continuous Delivery Pipeline and implementing DevOps.
  • Offers innovative ideas within area of focus to improve processes.
  • Conducts ongoing assessment of current state and seeks out opportunities for improvement.
  • Conducts research, design, prototyping and other exploration activities which fosters innovation and builds alignment between the customer need and what is delivered. Implements innovative ideas within area of focus.
  • Actively participates in Agile Dev Team Member Community of Practice (CoP) and elicits contributions from group members.
  • Plans and conducts CoP sessions.
  • Effectively manages the communications and action items resulting from the sessions.
  • Performs other functions as assigned.

Requirements

  • 7+ Years of experience working as a software engineer or equivalent
  • 7+ years of experience working with Java and Spring Boot
  • 6+ years of experience working with Angular and Typescript.
  • Knowledge on cloud environments such as AWS or equivalent.
  • Experience with CI/CD with Jenkins and Dynatrace
  • Experience on the insurance industry

Nice to Have

  • Experience on Copilot and any other AI Agent

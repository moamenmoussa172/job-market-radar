About the job

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) embeds directly inside a strategic enterprise customer to design, build, deploy, and run AI-powered production systems alongside the customer's engineering team. FDEs write production-grade software and own outcomes end-to-end, from prototype through enterprise-scale deployment combining the technical rigor of an AWS SDE with the urgency and ownership required to deliver measurable customer business outcomes. They create reusable architectures, patterns, and engineering mechanisms that scale the entire FDE practice.
Key job responsibilities
Lead technical delivery across customer engagements. Own the technical strategy and execution for complex, multi-workstream customer AI programs. Make architecture decisions that span multiple services and organizational boundaries. Align technical approach with the customer's long-term technology strategy. Requires up to 25% travel.
Architect and build production AI systems at scale. Design the foundational architectures — multi-agent orchestration, enterprise retrieval systems, decision intelligence platforms — for the most complex customer engagements. Drive clarity where neither the business problem nor technology approach is fully defined. Build production systems that handle enterprise-scale traffic and reliability requirements.
Own production excellence across workstreams. Define operational standards, evaluation frameworks, and production readiness criteria for AI systems across your engagement scope. Establish mechanisms (testing strategies, observability standards, incident response playbooks) that ensure AI systems remain healthy.
Scale the team's effectiveness. Define engineering patterns, contribute to the interview and hiring process, and mentor junior FDEs into independent contributors. Coach others on system design, customer engagement, and production operations.
Accelerate customer transformation. Identify systemic opportunities to expand AI adoption across the customer organization. Create accelerators and reusable platforms that reduce time-to-value from months to weeks. Feed learnings back into AWS services and the FDE practice.

A day in the life
You get AI systems working in production for customers. Most days you sit with their engineers, on site or on a call.
You might start by checking how last week's agent handled overnight traffic, find why it failed on an unexpected request, and agree a fix by lunch. Then you write code, deciding what the model should do and what plain code does better.
Later you meet the executive funding the work. They want a second use case, and you tell them what their data can support.
Some weeks are mostly coding. Others you're presenting to leadership or helping colleagues.
About the team
We help companies turn AI into business results. Many have run pilots that never reached production. We work side by side with their engineers to redesign a priority piece of work end to end, and put an agentic system into production in weeks. Each project builds on the last, so the next one starts further ahead. When we step back, the customer's team runs and extends what we built.
You'll work with engineers, scientists and product managers who built and run AI at AWS scale, for customers from financial services to manufacturing and energy. We value ownership, candour and shipping.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you`ll do

Following are the key tasks that this role will perform:

  • Act as named SAP engineer for large and strategic EOS customers in the region, addressing architecture related topics and challenges throughout the customer lifecycle
  • Interact with Pre-Sales and Sales on deal requirements (customer focused solution design)
  • Support ECS Delivery and ECS Customer Delivery Engagement in critical customer situations and projects
  • Engineering and prototyping of new services as part of the ECS CTO innovation and engineering process

What you bring

  • Degree in Engineering or Computer Science, Business Administration
  • High proficiency in customer communications, project management and presentations
  • High proficiency in the SAP product portfolio, offerings, and industry solutions.
  • Good understanding of SAP solutions (releases, platform interdependencies etc.)
  • Good understanding of ITIL based operational aspects (Application or Technical) of SAP solutions in a cloud environment.
  • Good understanding of capabilities of at least one Hyperscaler (AWS, MS Azure or GCP)
  • Willingness to travel customer related.
  • Fluent English is a must. Other languages are beneficial.

Meet your team

SAP GCO ECS is the organization that manages the Private Managed Cloud Business, first and foremost supporting customers business transformation with the RISE with SAP offering. We support 5000+ customers and continue to expand massively. CAE Service Engineering is focusing on new solution enablement in Cloud Architecture and Engineering.

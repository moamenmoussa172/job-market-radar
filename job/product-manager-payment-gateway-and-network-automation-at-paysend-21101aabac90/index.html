Product Manager – Payment Gateway & Network Automation

About the job

About The Role:

  • We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager (Payment Gateway & Network Automation) to help scale the infrastructure that moves money for Paysend. As our team grows, you’ll play a key role in optimising and automating our global payment network and partner ecosystem. You’ll have real ownership in a lean team, where your decisions directly shape the speed, cost and reliability of every payment. You’ll own our payment gateways end to end. That means fine-tuning routing and configurations, digging into gateway logs, evaluating partner performance, and working closely with Engineering to ship improvements fast. Every improvement you ship is felt instantly by millions of users and businesses.
    What You'll Do (Responsibilities):
  • Partner & Gateway Prioritization: Evaluate, prioritize, and manage relationships with payment gateways and financial partners based on conversion, reliability, and coverage.
  • Partner Performance & Factor Analysis: Conduct regular factor analysis of partner efficiency (success rates, authorization rates, uptime, costs, chargebacks) to drive business and routing decisions.

Technical & Operational Management of Payment Gateways:

  • Method Setup & Configuration: Onboard and configure payment methods including currencies, transaction limits, geo-restrictions, regular expressions (regex for card routing/bin/fields), and localization.
  • Fee Management: Set up and manage partner commission structures and client-side fee models.
  • Partner Mandates Implementation: Define specs and create tasks for engineering teams to implement partner mandates and regulatory requirements.
  • Testing & Debugging: Lead end-to-end testing of payment methods, analyze payment gateway logs, and troubleshoot errors/issues identified during testing or production.
  • Traffic Routing: Configure and optimize smart traffic routing rules across countries, regions, and partners to maximize authorization rates and minimize costs.
  • FX Markups: Define and manage FX markup settings and foreign exchange configurations.
  • UX/UI Payment Interface Optimization: Design and structure the checkout interface layout (grouping payment methods into Popular, Fast & Easy, etc.) to enhance user conversion.
  • Engineering Collaboration: Act as the primary product owner working directly with dedicated development teams—defining backlogs, writing technical specs, running sprints, and overseeing delivery.

What You’ll Need To Be Successful In This Role:

  • Experience: 5+ years of experience as a Product Manager in FinTech, Payments Processing, Payment Gateways, or E-commerce Infrastructure.
  • Payment Operations Expertise: Hands-on experience with payment gateway configuration (routing, currencies, limits, geo, localization, fees, and FX markups).
  • Technical Skills & Log Analysis: Ability to analyze payment gateway logs/payloads, inspect API responses, debug transaction flows, and troubleshoot technical errors independently.
  • Analytics & Performance Tracking: Strong experience in conducting factor analysis and data-driven evaluation of payment partner efficiency and authorization metrics.
  • Software Engineering Collaboration: Proven track record of working closely with software engineering and QA teams in an Agile/Scrum environment.
  • Checkout UX/UI Knowledge: Understanding of payment UX/UI best practices, placement strategies (Popular, Fast & Easy, etc.), and conversion optimization.
  • Communication & Stakeholder Management: Excellent organizational skills and fluent spoken and written English.
    Nice to Have
  • Experience with network automation tools or automated routing engines.
  • Hands-on experience writing regex patterns for BIN routing and input validations.
  • Deep knowledge of global card scheme rules (Visa/Mastercard mandates) and local payment methods across multiple regions.

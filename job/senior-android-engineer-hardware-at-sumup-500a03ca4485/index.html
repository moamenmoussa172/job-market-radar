About the job

Team description

The Hardware tribe builds the technology that turns everyday devices into secure, reliable card readers, from Bluetooth-connected dongles to fully in-house Tap to Pay on Android. You'll join a team solving genuinely hard problems: keeping payments working across flaky Bluetooth connections, handling online and offline flows without losing a transaction. It's a chance to go deep on Android engineering in a space where reliability really matters, since every merchant depends on these readers to get paid.

What you'll do

  • Build and maintain Android applications for card readers, SDKs, and the SuperApp, covering both new features and improvements to existing systems
  • Work on Bluetooth Low Energy connections, online and offline payment flows, and Tap to Pay functionality
  • Refactor and modernise legacy code to improve reliability, performance, and maintainability
  • Contribute to testing practices, including unit and integration tests, to raise overall code quality
  • Collaborate with peers and stakeholders across the team to manage priorities and shape solutions to technical challenges

You'll be great for this role if...

  • Several years of professional Android development experience, with solid proficiency in both Java and Kotlin
  • Strong knowledge of clean architecture principles and hands-on experience maintaining Android SDKs
  • Experience of writing unit and integration tests, and comfort refactoring legacy code without breaking existing functionality
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, such as GitHub Actions, and an interest in improving build and release processes
  • Ability to adapt to ambiguity and shifting priorities, with strong communication skills for working independently with stakeholders

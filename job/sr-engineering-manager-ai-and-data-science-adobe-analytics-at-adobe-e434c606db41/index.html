About the job

The Opportunity

We have a fantastic opportunity for an Sr. Engineering Manager to join our Adobe Analytics team based in Bucharest.

Analytics is moving from building reports to asking questions. Adobe is building AI assistants and agents that let a marketer or analyst ask a question in plain language – “Why did conversions drop last week?” – and get a correct, explained answer based on their own data, together with the next step to take.

Our team builds the data science and AI “skills” behind these experiences, and makes sure the answers customers get can be trusted.

We are looking for an exceptional technology leader to nurture and grow a multidisciplinary team of engineers and data scientists, shaping the future of AI-powered analytics within Adobe Experience Cloud.

Your team will be responsible for developing the AI and machine learning capabilities behind conversational analytics, with a strong focus on the accuracy, quality, performance and reliability of AI-generated insights. You will partner with global Analytics engineering teams, Product Management and AI platform teams to bring these capabilities into production at scale.

Adobe Analytics is a market-leading analytics solution that enables businesses to understand customer behavior, measure performance and make data-driven decisions across digital and offline channels. With Customer Journey Analytics and the integration of AI assistants and agents, Adobe is transforming how customers interact with their data, moving from traditional reporting towards conversational, intelligent and actionable insights.

What you'll do

  • Hire, lead, coach and grow a multidisciplinary team of engineers and data scientists, fostering a culture of engineering excellence, continuous learning, knowledge sharing and customer focus.
  • Drive the development and delivery of AI and machine learning capabilities for Adobe Analytics, including forecasting, anomaly detection, root-cause analysis and conversational analytics.
  • Own the team's engineering roadmap, including scope, estimates, prioritization, execution and predictable delivery, ensuring clear communication with senior leadership.
  • Drive the quality and accuracy of AI-generated insights by establishing measurable evaluation criteria, monitoring production performance and leading continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Provide technical leadership and participate in architectural decisions, technology choices, design reviews and testing strategies.
  • Ensure the scalability, performance, reliability and security of AI and data science services, driving operational excellence across deployment, monitoring, on-call and incident management.
  • Foster direct engagement with customers, translating real-world challenges and feedback into engineering and product improvements.
  • Develop and evolve engineering practices, including AI-assisted development, to improve team effectiveness and collaboration.
  • Partner closely with Product Management, AI platform teams and Analytics engineering teams across geographies to define priorities, align roadmaps and establish clear ownership

What you need to succeed

  • B.S. or M.S. degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science or equivalent.
  • 10+ years of experience in software engineering, data science or machine learning.
  • 3+ years of people management experience, with a proven ability to lead and develop engineering or data science teams, ideally of six or more people.
  • Strong technical background with hands-on experience in data science, machine learning or backend engineering.
  • Solid understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs), AI agents and AI evaluation methodologies, including test datasets, evaluation metrics and human review.
  • Experience with Python and cloud-native technologies, including AWS or Azure, Kubernetes, Docker and CI/CD.
  • Experience developing, deploying and operating scalable production services, including monitoring, on-call and incident management.
  • Strong project management and execution skills, with the ability to define scope, estimate effort, prioritize effectively and manage technical risks.
  • Data-driven decision-making skills, with the ability to define meaningful metrics, measure outcomes and communicate decisions effectively.
  • Strong business acumen and customer focus, with the ability to translate business requirements into engineering priorities.
  • Proven ability to mentor, motivate and develop engineers and data scientists while maintaining high standards of technical excellence.
  • Experience working with geographically distributed engineering teams and Product Management.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to engage effectively with both technical audiences and senior leadership.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken.

Bonus skills:

  • Experience managing software development using modern engineering practices that incorporate AI-assisted coding, code review, testing, and documentation.
  • Familiarity with AI development workflows, including prompt engineering, evaluation, testing, and integration of AI services into software applications.
  • Ability to assess technical tradeoffs related to AI adoption, including security, privacy, governance, and responsible AI considerations.

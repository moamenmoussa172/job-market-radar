Department Overview

At Motorola Solutions, everything starts with our people. We are a global, close-knit community united by our purpose of helping people be their best in the moments that matter, and our mission of "Solving for safer." Within this mission, the Pronto Team serves as the UK's leading provider of mobile policing applications, supporting 50% of the country's 45 police forces and over 70,000 active officers every single day. The Hosted Applications Team takes primary responsibility for managing the Pronto application across the hosted environment. Our dedicated group remotely oversees these Pronto instances for all hosted customers, directly supporting platform stability, driving optimal performance, and maintaining continuous operational oversight.


Job Description

Job Description

Operating primarily remotely within Motorola Solutions’ Hosted Applications Team (with occasional travel to offices and customer sites), the Senior Systems Engineer holds a pivotal, highly technical role combining application lifecycle engineering with key client-facing responsibilities. Leveraging extensive engineering expertise, you will maintain end-to-end accountability for managing and sustaining the operational resilience of the mission-critical Pronto Digital Policing application for frontline emergency services and police forces across the UK. We foster an inclusive culture built on collaboration, technical mentorship, and equivalent practical experience—welcoming candidates who bring deep diagnostic curiosity and leadership presence to high-stakes environments.

In this position, you will drive key deployment initiatives for standard product enhancements and customised client features. You will serve as the primary operational liaison between Software Engineering and Shared Services teams while maintaining direct customer engagement. Key responsibilities involve analysing complex software issues, directing patch rollouts and release cycles, and translating customer feedback into actionable application enhancements. Serving as the main technical point of contact, you will manage client-facing operations, align infrastructure and application workflows, and lead technical discussions during weekly meetings and service reviews. You will also evaluate platform health, observe key performance metrics to uphold strict Service Level Agreement (SLA) standards, and partner with customer technical project managers to resolve complex issues. Additionally, you will oversee and maintain enterprise database clusters housing vital Pronto application data, supporting data security, data governance, and reliable operational processes tailored to the daily needs of emergency service teams.

Key Responsibilities

Platform Resilience & Data Governance

  • Observe and analyse system performance against agreed contractual SLAs, proactively driving improvements to minimise downtime and prevent service-impacting incidents
  • Oversee and maintain SQL Server clusters and customer data lifecycles, conducting regular audits, integrity checks, and compliance reviews to support high availability and data protection
  • Collaborate with the Incident Management Team to investigate and resolve complex system faults, driving effective solutions and long-term preventative measures

Release Management & Technical Leadership

  • Serve as the primary operational contact for the core Pronto Software Engineering Team, gathering operational insights and coordinating release management activities including application hotfixes, feature rollouts, and platform deployments
  • Partner with the internal Shared Services Team to convert application demands into technical specifications and initiate change requests as necessary
  • Serve as a primary technical resource for the team, identifying and bridging knowledge gaps by providing mentorship, guidance, and training to engineers across critical technical areas

Client Engagement & Architecture Documentation

  • Participate in and lead technical sections of regular customer service reviews and weekly meetings with key police force stakeholders to present system performance, address operational concerns, and drive customer satisfaction
  • Author and maintain customer-facing Interface Control Documents (ICDs) defining precise technical specifications and protocols required for external system integration with the Pronto platform
  • Partner with customer technical Project Managers to resolve complex operational challenges and translate frontline feedback into actionable product enhancements


Basic Requirements

Job Requirements

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or Systems Engineering
  • Extensive experience in Systems Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering, or Hosted Application Operations within enterprise or public safety environments
  • Must be eligible for and maintain regional security vetting, including UK NPPV Level 3 and SC Clearance for public safety cloud environments

Technical Expertise

  • Enterprise Architecture & SDLC to oversee end-to-end system design and complete software lifecycle management
  • Cloud & Hosting Environments to maintain hosted applications across AWS, Azure, or Private Cloud infrastructure
  • Containerisation Technologies such as Docker or Kubernetes to support application deployment and scalability
  • Windows & Linux Administration to deliver technical support for operating systems, web and application server architectures, SSL/TLS certificates, and CMDBs
  • Enterprise Relational Databases to configure, administer, and resolve issues across platforms such as SQL Server, Oracle, or PostgreSQL
  • Automation & Scripting using Python, Bash, or PowerShell to build automated workflows, schedule tasks, and observe system health
  • Observability & Telemetry solutions including Grafana, Prometheus, and the TIG stack to evaluate platform metrics and operational visibility
  • English Language Proficiency with an excellent command of spoken and written English to author technical documentation and lead client-facing service reviews

Professional Experience

  • Incident Resolution & Diagnostics to efficiently troubleshoot and resolve intricate operational and application faults under strict SLA standards
  • Stakeholder Alignment & Communication to translate complex technical data for diverse audiences, set clear expectations, and maintain strong client relationships
  • Technical Leadership & Mentorship to drive engineering best practices across departments while developing junior team members
  • On-Call & Field Readiness including availability to participate in an on-call rotation for emergency operational needs, alongside a valid UK driver's license and ownership of a vehicle for travel to customer sites

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Advanced hands-on experience administering high-availability SQL Server clusters in mission-critical public safety environments

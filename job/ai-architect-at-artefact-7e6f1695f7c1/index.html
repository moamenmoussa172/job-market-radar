About the job

Mission

Join the most advanced community of AI makers.

You design how the world’s leading companies run AI at scale, and you make sure it can be built. You start from what the client’s business needs and design the data and agentic platforms that deliver it within their IT landscape, on premise, in the cloud or both. You then stay with the delivery teams until the solution runs in production.

For example, a client’s teams launch each new agent on a shared platform that already handles access rights, monitoring and costs, instead of rebuilding them for every use case. [Example to add: one architecture the chapter delivered, the client’s industry and what changed, without client names.]

This role is for you if you want to see the whole system and own the decisions that are hard to reverse.

Artefact is the trusted AI deployment team to the world’s leading companies: 3,000 AI makers in more than 30 countries and more than 1,000 clients. In France, you join its Engineering department, about 300 people led by the CTO, in the AI Architect chapter.

Activities & Responsibilities

Who you work with

You work in project teams with AI Applied Engineers, who build and run what you design, and with AI Product Managers, who manage the product with the client. You agree on the target with the client’s business leaders. You also work with their IT, security, network and operations teams, so that the architecture fits their landscape and they support it.

You review designs with delivery teams, coach architects and engineers, and share what works with the rest of the chapter. You also work with technology partners and other Artefact teams.

What you do

  • Turn the client’s business objectives, constraints and deadlines into an architecture that business and IT stakeholders can follow, then present and defend your choices.
  • Design data platforms and agentic platforms, on premise, in the cloud or hybrid.
  • Fit the solution to the client’s organisation by defining technical perimeters that match how their teams work.
  • Shape proposals: scope the solution, estimate effort and cost, and structure the delivery roadmap.
  • Guide delivery: review designs, arbitrate trade-offs during implementation, and make sure the teams can build what you designed.
  • Define the chapter’s reference patterns and best practices, and keep up with what changes in AI, cloud and data.

Required Skills

Must-haves:

  • You have designed a data or AI architecture that went into production, and you can explain the options you rejected and why.
  • You know at least one major cloud (Google Cloud, Azure or AWS) in depth, and you apply security and network fundamentals: IAM, secrets management, network architecture.
  • You have written high-level and low-level design documents that a delivery team built from.
  • You code in at least one language, such as Python, and you use coding agents in your daily work. You know what it takes to keep a system in production: CI/CD, monitoring, incident management.
  • You have scoped a solution and estimated its effort and cost, for example for a proposal or a project plan.
  • You link business value, technical choices and a delivery plan, and you explain them clearly to business and IT stakeholders.

Bonus, not required:

  • Agentic platform architecture: RAG, multi-agent orchestration, choosing technologies layer by layer.
  • Data governance at enterprise scale: data mesh, access management.
  • On-premise, hybrid or sovereign platforms built on open-source technologies or Kubernetes.
  • Run standards: SLAs, disaster recovery and business continuity plans.
  • Experience as a CTO, as a tech lead or in a client-facing role.
  • A cloud architecture certification.

You don’t need to tick every box to apply. If you match most must-haves and want to learn the rest, we want to hear from you.

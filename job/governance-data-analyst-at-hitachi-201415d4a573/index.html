Job Description:

The Opportunity:

At Hitachi Energy our purpose is advancing a sustainable​ energy future for all. We bring power to our homes, schools, hospitals and factories. Join us and work with fantastic people, while learning and developing yourself on projects that have a real impact to our communities and society. Bring your passion, bring your energy, and be part of a global team that appreciates a simple truth: Diversity + Collaboration = Great Innovation.

Within our Corporate Controlling organization, we are looking for a young talent to join us as Authorization and Approvals analyst. This position will strengthen our Authorization and Approvals CoE team in Hitachi Energy, contributing to our application landscape will comply with Company Regulations and Corporate/ Business Unit / Local Authorization Tables leveraging our Digital Core.

Our flexible work practices help you optimize personal and business performance while creating an environment where all employees can develop their skills and grow.

If you are proactive and data oriented professional now with a strong background in governance matters and process optimization, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity.

How You'll Make an Impact:

  • You will support in streamlining and optimizing critical business processes related to approvals and authorisation within Hitachi Energy.
  • Creating and maintaining detailed process maps for approval and authorization processes.
  • Documenting standard operating procedures (SOPs) and guidelines.
  • Supporting testing of workflow related controls and be contact person for internal and external audits.
  • Leverage technology solutions to enhance approvals and authorizations processes and work closely with IT teams to integrate workflow automation tools and platforms.
  • Support the CoE delivering hight quality and timely solutions

Your Background:

  • Bachelor’s degree in business administration, information systems, finance, project management or a related field.
  • Relevant certifications in process improvement (e.g., Lean Six Sigma) and governance tools (e.g., ServiceNow, SharePoint) will be a benefit.
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
  • Experience in working in an agile and dynamic environment, and the ability to adapt to changing requirements and priorities.
  • Excellent communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.
  • Knowledge of SAP S4 Hana and good knowledge of Microsoft Office (especially Excel) and another relevant software application.
  • Proficiency in English language.

About the Role

As a Data Engineer, you will act as the bridge between raw data sources and actionable business intelligence. You will develop robust batch and real-time data pipelines, contribute to modern data architectures, and build data platforms. You will work closely with data scientists, analysts, product teams, and other engineering teams to build high-quality data products and platforms. You will take end-to-end ownership of your solutions, ensuring their reliability, scalability, performance, and cost efficiency.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable batch and real-time data pipelines and processing systems.
  • Design, build, and evolve data platforms that enable data ingestion, processing, analytics, and observability across the organization.
  • Build APIs and data services to expose processed data to downstream applications and teams.
  • Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, product teams, and other engineering teams to deliver trusted data products.
  • Monitor, optimize, and maintain data pipelines and processing systems to ensure high performance, reliability, scalability, and cost efficiency.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of data services and pipelines, including deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and incident resolution.
  • Expected Qualifications
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, MIS, Mathematics, or a related field.
    2+ years of experience as a Data Engineer or in a similar data-focused role.
  • Solid understanding of distributed systems, parallel processing, and fault-tolerant architectures.
  • Strong software engineering skills in at least one of Python, Go, or Java.
  • Hands-on experience with ETL/ELT processes and big data processing frameworks such as Spark or Flink is a plus.
  • Advanced SQL skills and experience with structured and semi-structured data formats.
  • Understanding of data modeling concepts and analytical data structures.
  • Experience in developing data platforms and modern data architectures, including data lakes, data warehouses, and lakehouses.
  • Experience with streaming technologies, Change Data Capture (CDC), and workflow orchestration.
  • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and Infrastructure as Code.
  • Experience with automated testing, data quality validation, monitoring, and observability practices.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and clear written and verbal communication in English.

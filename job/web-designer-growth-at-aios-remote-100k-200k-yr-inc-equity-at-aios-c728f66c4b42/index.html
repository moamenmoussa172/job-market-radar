About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

About AIOS

AIOS is building the world’s first full-stack AI doctor.

We’re at $450M ARR, growing from $7M/yr in April 2025. We’re faithfully serving 200k/mo patients via Bolt Pharmacy, our main UK brand. We’ve just launched Bolt Med, our international brand. We’re profitable.

Our strategy exists at the intersection of two strong theses:

  • The AI doctor that wins will get to escape velocity using GLP-1s, the fastest growing consumer product in history.
  • The $2T European healthcare market is overlooked by the most talented builders.

Our master plan:

  • Step 0 → $100M/yr by end of 2025: We went from $10M to $100M in 6 months serving the UK GLP-1 market.
  • Step 1 → $1B ARR by end of 2026: Since April 2025 we've grown from 3k to 200k UK active GLP-1 patients. We’ll continue this growth curve to hit $1B ARR.
  • Step 2 → $10B ARR by end of 2028: Blitzscale Europe. We’ll be Europe’s largest GLP-1 provider.
  • Step 3 → $100B/yr by end of 2030: Get regulatory approval across Europe for our Full Autonomous Prescribing (FAP) system. Win contracts at scale with European payers to mass replace human labor. We’ll be the dominant full-stack AI doctor in Europe.
  • Step 4 → $1T/yr by end of 2035: With one line of code and zero human time, you can use AIOS to treat any patient globally with any medication.

In so doing, we’ll become the world’s first trillion-dollar healthcare company.

We’re a young, founder-led company. This is still Day 1 and all our work is ahead of us.

You can read more about working with us here: Working at AIOS

Being a Web Designer, Growth at AIOS

We are building a world-class team.

As Web Designer, Growth at AIOS, your fundamental role is to turn cold clicks into customers, designing the pages that take a stranger from an ad to a purchase.

Visual polish matters, and for this role it comes second. Mastery here is conversion craft: knowing what goes on the first screen and in what order, turning a product into an offer, putting proof exactly where the doubt shows up, and removing risk at every step. You write and test your own headlines. You carry the ad’s promise and look all the way through to checkout.

You’ll own pre-conversion across our brands: landing pages, advertorials and listicles, quiz-to-plan flows and checkout, for our GLP-1 business and for the new non-Rx products we’re launching. You’ll ship your own pages to production with AI tools, without waiting on a developer, and you’ll live with each funnel through round after round of tests until you know exactly what moves the number.

You’ll:

  • Turn every growth bet into a live, high-converting page in days, not weeks
  • Close the gap between our funnels and best-in-class DTC, starting with the friction after our UK quiz
  • Launch new products with pages built to sell to cold traffic from day one
  • Keep us ahead of what’s new in DTC, and have our version live the same week

Great performance in this role means our pages win on persuasion, not just polish, and every new product and market launches with a funnel designed to convert.

This is a full-time, fully remote role, and you’ll work mostly async in the timezone of your choice, as long as you’re around until midday Pacific Time every weekday. This is an intense, hands-on IC role within the Growth team.

You’ll report directly to our Head of Growth (David Charles).

You’ll also work most closely with:

  • CRO Lead (Michael McCann)
  • Head of Design (Ryan Wu)
  • Growth (Jake)
  • CEO (Richie)

Key responsibilities

You’ll own pre-conversion end to end, from the ad click to the purchase.

  • First screen and page order: Decide what goes on the first screen and in what order, so a cold visitor knows what this is, why it’s for them and what to do next within seconds.
  • Offer design: Turn a product into an offer: the plan, the price anchor, the value stack, bonuses and the guarantee. Plan cards, best-value picks, struck prices and gifts inside the selected plan are your tools, not afterthoughts.
  • Proof and risk reversal: Put proof where the doubt shows up, not in a reviews block at the bottom, and remove risk at each step: what happens next, who the doctor is, cancel anytime, when it arrives.
  • Headlines and copy: Write and test your own headlines rather than laying out copy someone else handed over, and explain why one beats another.
  • Carrying the scent: Carry the ad’s promise and look through to the page, working with the growth team so the creative and the landing page feel like one journey.
  • Presells and quiz funnels: Design and iterate advertorials, numbered listicles, story pages and our quiz-to-plan flow, including the results moment and a plan page that feels built for one person.
  • Engaging pages: Use native-looking media, UGC, motion and before-and-afters where compliance allows, so the page holds attention the way the ad did.
  • Ship and iterate: Ship your own pages to production with AI tools, then live with the funnel through many rounds of tests, cut what loses and double down on what wins. Recent examples in motion: the UK post-quiz back end, the DE listicle, and Wave 1 non-Rx launches (skin, hair, ED, non-clinical).
  • Stay ahead of DTC: Track new formats across ecommerce as they appear (like RYZE’s Instagram-story listicles) and bring the best of them to our funnels fast.

Need to have

  • Live pages with real spend: Your best work is URLs that ran behind paid traffic. You can share live pages, the brand, rough monthly spend and the result.
  • Direct response ecom at scale: You’ve designed pages that sold a product to cold traffic with real spend behind them. Lead gen, info products, brand sites and app UI don’t count on their own.
  • Test history on one funnel: You’ve spent real time on one funnel at scale, on staff or in a long engagement, and can walk through a series of tests: what won, what lost, by how much and what you’d try next.
  • Direct response taste: You can name brands outside telehealth that do this well and explain why. Your acquisition references are DTC brands, not Apple, Airbnb or Whoop.
  • Offer thinking: You talk about the plan, price presentation, bundles, guarantees and gifts as naturally as you talk about layout.
  • Copy: You’re proficient in DTC and direct response copy and write your own pages. You may not be an S-tier copywriter, but you’re at least solid, and always hungry to improve.
  • CRO: Hands-on experience running or designing for A/B and multivariate tests, and using the results to decide what to try next.
  • Ownership: You own the results your pages generate. You’re a leader who gives a sh*t, not just an internal services function.
  • Ship it yourself, fast with AI: You ship your own pages to production with AI tools, without waiting on a developer, and can have a new format live the same week you spot it.
  • Visual craft at our bar: Required, and checked last. Your pages are clean, fast and on-brand within our design system.

‼️

NOT THE RIGHT FIT IF: you’re an agency designer with no test history, an app or product designer, or your work is all corporate ecom, brand sites or Dribbble-style concepts with no conversion numbers.

Nice to have

  • Quiz funnels: Experience designing long quizzes that build commitment and end on a personalised plan page.
  • Health and supplements: Experience on supplement, wellness or telehealth DTC pages, including compliant claims (FTC, ASA).
  • Analytics: Comfortable in behaviour and testing tools, and reading funnel data to find where people drop off.
  • Ad-to-funnel congruence: You understand carrying congruence from ad to funnel, and build accordingly. You think in awareness stages.
  • Motion and UGC: Able to cut native-looking video, motion and UGC assets for the page yourself.
  • Multi-market: Experience adapting funnels for different countries and languages.

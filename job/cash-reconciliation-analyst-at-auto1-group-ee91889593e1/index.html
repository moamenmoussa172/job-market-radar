About the job

Our Cash Reconciliation team plays a key role in ensuring the accurate allocation and posting of bank transactions within our Global Accounting department. As a Cash Reconciliation Analyst, you will contribute to maintaining accurate financial records, reconciling accounts, processing payments, and resolving discrepancies.

Main Tasks:

  • Reconcile bank and clearing account balances as part of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial closing processes.
  • Allocate bank transactions to the corresponding business transactions.
  • Process and post financial transactions in our accounting system, Oracle.
  • Investigate and clarify account discrepancies and payment-related issues.
  • Contribute to projects focused on process optimization and improving accounting efficiency.

Qualifications:

  • A completed commercial apprenticeship or a degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
  • Initial professional experience in accounting or a similar role.
  • A structured, independent, and reliable working style, with a strong understanding of numbers and good analytical skills.
  • A high affinity for IT systems and proficiency in common Office applications, particularly Excel.
  • Very good knowledge of English, both written and spoken.

The place of work will be at AUTO1 Albania headquarters, Rruga Dritan Hoxha, Kompleksi E88, Tiranë, Albania.

If you are detail-oriented, comfortable working with numbers, and interested in developing your career in credit analysis and financing operations, we would love to hear from you.

Please apply by uploading your CV in English.

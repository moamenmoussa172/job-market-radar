About the job

Job Description:

Airbus Commercial Aircraft is looking for a Industrial Cyber Sec. Specialist (f/m) to join our Industrial Cybersecurity Operations department based in Toulouse, France.

You will be part of a team protecting aircraft manufacturing processes against cyber threats. As part of the local ICS operations, you will be involved in bridging plant operations with security technologies aiming at systematically controlling and mitigating risks for the Toulouse St. Martin manufacturing facilities).

Your working environment:

Global capital of aeronautics and European capital for space research, Toulouse is a dynamic city in the southwest of France served by an international airport. Ideally located between the Mediterranean sea and the Atlantic ocean and close to the Pyrenees mountains, it offers plenty of options for outdoor activities!

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, agreements on success and profit sharing schemes, employee savings plan abounded by Airbus and employee stock purchase plan on a voluntary basis.
  • Work / Life Balance: Extra days-off for special occasions, holiday transfer option, a Staff council offering many social, cultural and sport activities and other services.
  • Wellbeing / Health: Complementary health insurance coverage (disability, invalidity, death). Depending on the site: health services center, concierge services, gym, carpooling application.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, expert career path, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

At Airbus, we support you to work, connect and collaborate more easily and flexibly. Wherever possible, we foster flexible working arrangements to stimulate innovative thinking.

Your challenges:

  • Carry out industrial cyber solutions (such as ISP) and service implementation projects in cooperation with plant stakeholders.
  • Conduct Industrial Cyber Security Maturity Assessments (ICMA) and deliver resulting local initiatives.
  • Coordinate local response actions to cybersecurity incidents and supervise change control for OT and IACS.
  • Manage local resources, budgets, work packages, and support the vulnerability management process.
  • Maintain plant security metrics and develop communication plans to periodically report the cybersecurity posture to local management.

Your boarding pass:

  • Degree in Cybersecurity, Systems Engineering, Industrial Automation, IT, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Proven expertise in Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Operational Technology (OT) cybersecurity.
  • Solid background in cyber incident detection, response, and information systems security architectures.
  • Strong grasp of NIST CSF (Manufacturing Profile, 800-53, 800-82) and ISA/IEC 62443 standards.
  • Familiarity with regulatory frameworks (EASA PART-IS, NIS2, EBIOS) and Security-by-Design principles.

Not a 100% match? No worries! Airbus supports your personal growth with customized development solutions.

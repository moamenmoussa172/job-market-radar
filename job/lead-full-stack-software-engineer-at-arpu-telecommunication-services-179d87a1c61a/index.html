About the job

Role Purpose in one of our Business units CloucCom

Lead the design and development of front-end and back-end services, API products, integration adapters, and platform components that power the Network API Gateway. This role combines hands-on software engineering with technical leadership to deliver scalable, secure, and high-performing solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the design, development, and enhancement of backend microservices for API exposure, mediation, partner onboarding, usage tracking, and platform administration.
  • Develop and maintain front-end applications and back-end services that support Network API Gateway products and internal platform tools.
  • Implement CAMARA-style APIs and TM Forum API integrations in line with business and technical requirements.
  • Build and maintain integration adapters connecting the gateway to operator BSS/OSS platforms and network systems.
  • Create, review, and maintain OpenAPI specifications and related technical documentation.
  • Write clean, testable, maintainable, and scalable code following engineering best practices.
  • Provide technical guidance, perform code reviews, and mentor team members to ensure high development standards.
  • Collaborate with QA to support automated testing, contract testing, regression testing, and release readiness.
  • Support production troubleshooting, bug fixing, performance optimization, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Work closely with Product, Architecture, DevOps, and cross-functional teams to deliver reliable platform capabilities.

Benefits

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • 7 to 8 years of hands-on software engineering experience, including both front-end and back-end development.
  • Strong experience with full stack technologies such as Angular, .NET Core, Java, Go, Python, Node.js, or similar technologies.
  • Solid understanding of REST APIs, JSON, OpenAPI specifications, and microservices architecture.
  • Experience with databases, message queues, caching technologies, and distributed systems.
  • Experience with Telecom APIs, TM Forum standards, CAMARA frameworks, or API Gateway platforms.
  • Hands-on familiarity with Kubernetes, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Experience leading technical initiatives, guiding developers, and driving engineering best practices.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

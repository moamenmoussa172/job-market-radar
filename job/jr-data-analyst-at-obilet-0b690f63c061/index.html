About the job

Founded in 2012, Obilet.com is a comprehensive travel and tourism platform offering bus, flight, and ferry ticketing, along with hotel bookings and car rentals.

With its advanced SaaS Inventory Management System (IMS) supporting bus carriers, over 25 million monthly visitors, and more than 15 million app downloads, Obilet is the leading player in both B2B and B2C markets in Türkiye and the Eastern European region.

Obilet has reached an agreement with BlaBlaCar for a strategic investment in exchange for a majority share in 2024. With more than 50 million transactions projected for 2026, Obilet continues its international expansion with the ambition of becoming one of the world's leading travel tech companies.

Main responsibilities will include:

  • Understanding the data requirements of the company, and conducting thorough analysis to achieve business objectives
  • Collaborating with various teams, including top management and marketing to identify data analysis project requirements
  • Creating dynamic dashboards and insightful reports using PowerBI to contribute to effective decision-making processes
  • Communicating data analysis findings clearly and effectively to stakeholders
  • Tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) to assess the success of business strategies and initiatives
  • Providing recommendations based on data to solve business challenges
  • Ensuring the effective functioning of business intelligence systems and tools, and taking necessary actions in case of issues

To be successful in this role, you need to have:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics or a similar field (a certificate on business intelligence, data science or data mining will be a plus)
  • Long Term Internship or 1-2 years experience
  • Proficiency in English, both written and verbal
  • Experience with MS Office tools and data analytics
  • Experience with visualization tools like Tableau, PowerBI, Qlik Sense is a plus
  • Knowledge of SQL is a plus
  • Knowledge on Python and experience with Machine Learning and AI models will be an added advantage
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Eagerness to learn, multitasking skills, attention to details, and ability to deliver high quality results

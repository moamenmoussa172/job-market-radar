Job Description & Summary

At PwC, our people in data and analytics focus on leveraging data to drive insights and make informed business decisions. They utilise advanced analytics techniques to help clients optimise their operations and achieve their strategic goals.
In business intelligence at PwC, you will focus on leveraging data and analytics to provide strategic insights and drive informed decision-making for clients. You will develop and implement innovative solutions to optimise business performance and enhance competitive advantage.

• Drive end-to-end process improvement & mining initiatives — from scoping and

data preparation to analysis, value quantification, and solution design.

• Build and interpret process mining dashboards and uncover improvement

opportunities in operational, financial, and cross-functional business processes.

• Support process reorganization and redesign initiatives, translating analytical

insights into optimized target processes and sustainable operating models.

• Leverage AI-powered capabilities to help clients accelerate their enterprise

transformation and build future-ready processes.

• Facilitate client workshops, process assessments, and root-cause analyses to

identify inefficiencies and translate findings into actionable recommendations.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support the implementation of process

improvements and monitor realized value.

• Support clients in establishing a scalable, data-driven process excellence approach

and embedding continuous improvement practices.

• First experience (Associate) or several years of experience (Senior Associate) in process mining, process consulting, business transformation, or similar data-driven improvement fields.

• Strong expertise in at least one core end-to-end process, such as Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, Record-to-Report.

• Ideally, broader process knowledge across additional E2E processes or crossfunctional business areas.

• Experience with SAP Signavio, Celonis, or comparable process mining technologies.

• Ability to translate complex data and process insights into clear, value-focused recommendations and actionable process redesigns.

• Solid analytical skills; familiarity with SQL or scripting Python.

• Strong communication, facilitation, and stakeholder-management skills typical of consulting environments.

• Fluency in German and English (both required) — you communicate confidently with stakeholders at all levels in both languages.

• Ability to work with Microsoft Office and Power Automate (its a plus)

• A structured, proactive, and impact-oriented working style.

Nice to Have

• Experience with AI-driven process optimization or automation technologies.

• Knowledge of process modeling standards (e.g., BPMN) or KPI frameworks.

• Exposure to enterprise transformation programs or E2E process design initiatives.

• Experience working in cross-functional or international teams.

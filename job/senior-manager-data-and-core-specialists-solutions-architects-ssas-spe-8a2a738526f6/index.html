About the job

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is looking for an experienced people leader to manage a team of Specialist Solutions Architects (SSAs) focused on Data and Core Services(e.g., Databases, Analytics, Storage, Compute, Networking). In this role you will lead a geographically distributed team that serves customers across Germany, Switzerland and CEE areas, helping them adopt and optimize AWS services at scale.
As a Sr Manager, Specialist Solutions Architecture, you will combine deep technical acumen with strong leadership skills to recruit, develop, and retain world-class technical talent. You will set the technical vision and technical go-to-market strategy for your domain areas, enable your team to build trusted-advisor relationships with customers, develop thought leadership assets, and partner closely with Service Teams, Sales, and Partner organizations to drive adoption and revenue growth.
Key job responsibilities
People Leadership
Build, lead, and develop a high-performing, diverse, and inclusive team of Specialist Solutions Architects distributed across three geographical areas.
Manage performance, career development, and succession planning.
Foster a culture of customer obsession, ownership, technical excellence, and continuous learning.
Drive hiring excellence — define hiring bar, calibrate interviewers, and attract top technical talent.
Technical Strategy & Go-to-Market
Define and execute the technical go-to-market strategy for Data and Core Services domains within the assigned geographies in tight alignment with the Specialist Sales team leadership
Identify trends, whitespace opportunities, and customer adoption patterns
Partner with Service Teams to influence roadmaps based on field feedback and customer requirements.
Develop and scale mechanisms (workshops, immersion days, proof-of-concept frameworks) that accelerate customer adoption.
Customer & Partner Engagement
Engage directly with strategic customers at CxO / VP level to drive large-scale adoption of AWS Data and Core Services.
Establish technical thought leadership through public speaking, blog posts, reference architectures, and best-practice guidance.
Support the broader partner ecosystem by enabling APNs, GSIs, and ISVs in the Data and Core Services space.
Operational Excellence
Drive operational mechanisms (WBR, MBR, QBR) to track team health, pipeline contribution, and service adoption metrics.
Manage cross-geo coordination to ensure consistent execution, knowledge sharing, and follow-the-sun support models.
Collaborate with Sales leadership on account coverage, and specialist engagement models.

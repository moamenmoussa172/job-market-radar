Robinhood is looking for a Senior Client Platform Engineer to join our Enterprise Technology organization. The Client Platform Engineering team builds and scales the systems that manage, secure, and support employee devices across Robinhood. We're focused on pushing the boundaries of traditional endpoint management through automation, AI-powered tooling, and modern engineering practices to create a more secure, efficient, and seamless employee experience.

In this role, you'll work at the intersection of endpoint engineering, software development, and AI-driven automation. You'll build and maintain the infrastructure, tooling, and automation that power device management across our global workforce, with a strong focus on macOS environments. From developing custom tools and debugging complex systems to leveraging AI to accelerate engineering workflows, you'll have the opportunity to solve challenging technical problems and drive meaningful improvements at scale. You'll join a lean, highly technical team where creativity, resourcefulness, and engineering ownership are essential. You'll partner closely with Security, IT, and Engineering to develop scalable solutions, improve platform reliability, and help shape the future of endpoint management at Robinhood.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build, and scale endpoint management infrastructure supporting macOS, Windows, and mobile devices across Robinhood's global workforce.
  • Develop and maintain custom automation, internal tooling, and configuration management solutions using technologies such as Munki, SaltStack, and MDM platforms.
  • Write, debug, and optimize code to improve device provisioning, configuration management, software deployment, and ongoing endpoint operations.
  • Leverage AI-powered development tools to accelerate automation and engineering workflows, while critically evaluating, troubleshooting, and refining AI-generated code.
  • Build and improve automated testing, deployment pipelines, and workflows to increase the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of endpoint management systems.
  • Monitor device management infrastructure, investigate logs, troubleshoot complex technical issues, and drive root cause analysis and long-term solutions.
  • Identify opportunities to eliminate repetitive manual processes through creative engineering solutions and scalable automation.
  • Partner with Security, IT, and Engineering teams to strengthen endpoint security, improve platform reliability, and support evolving business needs.
  • Provide technical guidance and support to other team members, sharing engineering best practices and helping drive continuous improvement.

What you bring

  • 5+ years of relevant experience in Client Platform Engineering, Endpoint Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a similar technical role supporting enterprise device environments.
  • Strong hands-on experience managing enterprise endpoint environments, particularly macOS, using tools such as Munki, SaltStack, and FleetDM solutions.
  • Strong programming and scripting skills, ideally in Python, with demonstrated experience building, maintaining, and debugging custom tools and automation.
  • Proven experience developing automation solutions for device provisioning, configuration management, software deployment, or endpoint lifecycle management.
  • Experience managing and troubleshooting endpoint infrastructure at scale, ideally supporting thousands of devices across multiple operating systems.
  • Ability to understand, troubleshoot, and improve complex codebases, including code generated with AI-powered development tools.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools and an interest in applying emerging technologies to improve engineering productivity and infrastructure automation.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, with the creativity and resourcefulness to develop practical solutions to complex technical challenges.
  • Experience working across multiple endpoint platforms, with a strong preference for macOS expertise.
  • An engineering-first mindset, strong technical ownership, and a passion for building scalable systems rather than relying on manual processes.

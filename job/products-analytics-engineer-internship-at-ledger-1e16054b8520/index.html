About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger.

At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries.

The team

As a Product Analytics Engineer Intern, you will join the Product Analytics team supporting Ledger Wallet across Product, Business, and Growth/CRM teams. Reporting directly to the Product Data Analytics Manager, you will play a key role in ensuring tracking data quality, and scaling our analytics infrastructure to support company-wide data-driven decision-making.

What you’ll be doing

  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Product Managers, Data Engineers, and Data Scientists to deliver insights and support Growth/CRM personalization pipelines.
  • Experimentation & A/B Testing: Specify and support A/B test setups, funnel evaluation, and advanced statistical analysis for product features.
  • AI Governance & Automation: Help establish governance, guardrails, and custom skills for users querying analytics platforms (Mixpanel, Snowflake MCP) via AI tools, and contribute to building end-to-end AI-assisted tracking workflows.
  • Tracking Quality & Investigations: Triage, investigate, and follow up on tracking issues to maintain data sanity.
  • Documentation & Enablement: Develop comprehensive documentation and data models (dbt/data mart) to empower stakeholders
  • Privacy & Compliance: Maintain standard privacy-aware constraints across all data modeling and analytics workflows.

What we're looking for

  • Final-year student in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical field seeking an end-of-studies internship.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL, Python scripting, and data modeling concepts.
  • Analytics engineering mindset with a focus on data quality, reliability, reproducibility, and documentation.
  • Understanding of A/B testing methodologies, statistical analysis, and funnel evaluation.
  • Familiarity with Product Analytics tools (e.g., Mixpanel, Amplitude) and data visualization tools is a strong plus
  • Hands-on experience or deep curiosity regarding AI agents and workflow automation is a strong plus.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Interest in Web3, digital assets, and crypto is a plus.

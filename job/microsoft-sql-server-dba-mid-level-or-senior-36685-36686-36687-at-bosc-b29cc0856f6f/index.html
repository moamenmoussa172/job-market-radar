About the job

We are looking for Database professionals with experience in Microsoft SQL Server to work on the administration, architecture, support, and continuous improvement of critical database environments within a global technology organization.

This role plays an important part in ensuring the availability, security, and performance of database environments, while contributing to technology modernization, automation, and Cloud migration initiatives.

The professional will collaborate with international teams and stakeholders, contributing to the technical evolution of database environments and continuous process improvement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Administer, install, configure, and monitor Microsoft SQL Server environments, ensuring availability, security, and performance.
  • Perform troubleshooting, analysis, and resolution of database and infrastructure-related incidents.
  • Design, implement, and maintain high availability, backup, and disaster recovery solutions.
  • Create and manage databases, access policies, and security controls in compliance with corporate standards.
  • Develop automation scripts and solutions using PowerShell and other tools, leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) capabilities to optimize processes.
  • Participate in technology modernization and migration projects from On-Premises environments to Cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP).
  • Contribute to governance, change management, information security, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Collaborate with global teams and international stakeholders, participating in technical discussions and projects conducted in English.
  • Share knowledge and best practices, contributing to the team's technical development.
  • Participate in support and on-call rotations, ensuring the continuity of business-critical environments operating 24/7.

Key Challenges

  • Technology Modernization: Contribute to the evolution of database architecture and migration initiatives to Cloud environments.
  • Availability and Resilience: Ensure the stability of critical database environments integrated with global manufacturing operations.
  • Automation and Innovation: Develop solutions that reduce manual activities, improve operational efficiency, and leverage AI capabilities.
  • Technical Excellence: Strengthen database architecture, security, performance, and governance standards.
  • Global Collaboration: Work closely with international specialists and teams, sharing knowledge and technical solutions.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, Systems Analysis and Development, or related fields.
  • Professional experience in Microsoft SQL Server database administration.
  • Knowledge of T-SQL, monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance tuning.
  • Experience with backup, restore, and database recovery procedures.
  • Knowledge of database security, administration, and governance.
  • Experience with Windows and/or Linux environments.

Preferred Qualifications and Experience

  • Experience with high availability and disaster recovery solutions, such as Always On, Failover Cluster, Log Shipping, and Replication.
  • Experience administering large-scale SQL Server environments and databases with high data volumes.
  • Knowledge of virtualized environments, such as VMware and Nutanix.
  • Experience with databases integrated with SharePoint.
  • Knowledge of data architecture, ETL, Data Warehousing, and dimensional modeling.
  • Experience with Cloud environments (Azure, AWS, or GCP).
  • Knowledge of PowerShell, automation, DevOps, and Artificial Intelligence tools.
  • Familiarity with ITIL practices, incident management, and change management.
  • Experience in global corporate environments, preferably in industrial or manufacturing sectors.
  • Experience in technical leadership, mentoring, and knowledge sharing.

Nice to Have

  • Experience in database migration projects from On-Premises environments to Cloud platforms.
  • Experience managing databases at the terabyte scale.
  • Knowledge of Containers and Kubernetes.
  • Experience with operational process automation and proactive monitoring.
  • Microsoft SQL Server-related certifications and ITIL certification.

Language Requirements

  • Advanced or fluent English.

The seniority level (Mid-Level or Senior) will be determined based on the candidate's professional experience and technical expertise.

About the job

Within the Experience Validation organization, the Field Integration and Testing (FIT) team pioneers end-to-end network field validation, testing Leo's full signal path from the customer terminal through gateway infrastructure to the internet, in real customer environments that labs cannot fully replicate. We are looking for a Technical Program Manager to drive the execution of FIT's core testing operations and its automated Solution Lab remote test fleet. You will be managing the remote test automation and debugging and you are the connective tissue between FIT and Leo engineering, keeping testing running with a fast-moving release cadence of new customer terminals, firmware, and mobile app builds.
Key job responsibilities
- Own fleet management of remote Solution Labs across EMEA, a core FIT workstream that keeps automated testing and verification infrastructure operational for engineering and product teams.
- Manage and modify AWS container-based workloads to meet evolving test requirements while maintaining security bar.
- Coordinate execution of country-specific functional test cases (like regional IP routing and country specific service plans) and B2B/B2C use cases across Solution Labs.
- Drive dependencies end-to-end, including Beta Ops provisioning, hardware delivery, and spot enablement.
- TPM-found issues with relevant stakeholders, manage schedules, risks, and open items, and maintain data-backed status reporting for leadership.
- Support Solution Lab and demo center expansion into future markets.
A day in the life
You might start your morning with your daily Solution Lab health report. It shows a test case failing at a remote site in another country. You find no known issue that matches, so you remote into the lab and confirm the failure is real. You capture the logs and open a ticket for engineering, with the evidence they need to act quickly. Next, a product team asks for access to another Solution Lab so they can validate their latest release in a real end-to-end environment. It's their first time in the lab, so you walk them through how to use it and set them up to succeed. After lunch, you join a planning session with the international expansion team, who are eager to get their new Solution Lab running. You brief them on the status of the technical enablers and gather their feedback, so the site also works as a customer-facing demo center. Later, you lead the issue review, where you summarize the top issues across the labs and work with engineering teams to drive improvements. Throughout it all, you turn signals from the field into action and help teams ship with confidence.
About the team
Our team validates the Amazon Leo customer experience in the field and helps Leo teams test in the real world. Our mission is to find potential issues before end users see them. We run a global network of Solution Labs and field test sites where teams across Leo validate their releases end to end, under real conditions. Collaboration is central to how we work. You'll partner closely with engineering, product, and operations teams to turn field insights into a better product. If you want to see first-hand how Leo performs for real customers, this is the team for you.

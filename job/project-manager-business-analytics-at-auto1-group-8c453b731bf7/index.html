About the job

As a Junior Project Manager – Business Analytics, you'll support the strategy, growth, and day-to-day operations of AUTO1's Branch Real Estate & Expansion team across Europe. Reporting directly to the Head of Real Estate & Expansion Europe, you will:

  • Convert Data to Decisions: Query and interpret data (SQL and beyond) to track branch and expansion performance, surface trends, and turn raw numbers into clear, actionable insight for the team.
  • Drive High-Impact Analysis: Take ownership of analytical workstreams within larger real estate and expansion projects, from data gathering through to recommendation.
  • Build Tools: Prototype dashboards, trackers, and lightweight internal tools — including using AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude Code — to automate reporting and solve operational problems without waiting on engineering resources.
  • Optimize Operations: Identify inefficiencies in branch or expansion processes and help design and implement scalable, data-backed improvements.
  • Coordinate Initiatives: Work closely with Purchasing, Expansion, Real Estate, and other cross-functional teams to keep projects aligned and moving.

This is a great opportunity for an analytically minded, hands-on professional to grow into project management while working on real, high-impact problems across a fast-growing international branch network.

We're looking for a curious, technically comfortable mind who's more energized by data than by process for its own sake. You'll thrive in this role if you have:

  • 1–3 years of experience in business analytics, consulting, engineering, or a similarly analytical role (strong recent graduates with relevant internships or project work are welcome to apply).
  • A background in Business Analytics, Management, Economics, Engineering, or another quantitative field.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to turn messy data into clear recommendations.
  • Solid SQL skills and no hesitation writing or adapting your own queries.
  • Comfort experimenting with AI-assisted coding tools (e.g., Claude Code) to build small internal tools — no formal software engineering background required, just a willingness to get hands-on.
  • Familiarity with BI or reporting tools (e.g., Looker, Tableau, Power BI) is a plus.
  • Fluency in Spanish and English (additional languages are a plus).

If you're a proactive, data-driven professional who wants to build things, not just report on them, we'd love to hear from you!

About the SAP Internship Experience Program
The SAP Internship Experience Program is SAP’s global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

Three reasons to intern at SAP

  • Culture of collaboration: meet with mentors, make new friends across the globe and create a thriving personal network.
  • Project-driven experience: gain cross-functional skills from our virtual and in-person learning sessions, diverse subject matter experts, and project deliverables.
  • Gain visibility: with SAP Internship Experience Program in your title, you’ll have a global network of SAP leaders, entrepreneurs and career development opportunities at your fingertips.

About the team

We are a friendly multidisciplinary team that likes to experiment, simplify and amplify the message to our customers. Our design create connection with the customer whoever it is. We emphasize content over form without exception.We will advise the best practice for any case. We will help our colleagues to win.

What you’ll do:

Position title: Design Office Nordic and Baltic student
Location: Oslo, Norway
Expected start date 15 August - 30 Sept, 2026

In this role, you’ll:

Learn to convey customer requirements and how to propose relevant visual design solutions to business messages.
Further, develop your design skills and experience and challenge yourself.
Learn how to leverage your creative skills and talents in a business-driven environment.
Tasks and Expectations

  • Produce marketing and communication materials across digital and presentation formats
  • Translate ideas and concepts into visual narratives — from early sketches to polished engagement experiences
  • Collaborate with Customer Innovation and Solution Advisory teams across the Nordic market
  • Work within SAP's brand framework while adapting to the visual identities of different customers
  • Contribute to live projects already in motion — iterating, refining, and delivering
  • You’ll be part of the local student community, helping to support SAP Next-Gen events. This provides an excellent opportunity to expand your network and connect with talented peers from a variety of disciplines.

Who you are:

We’re looking for someone who takes initiative, perseveres, and stay curious. You like to work on meaningful innovative projects and are energized by lifelong learning.

Education and Qualifications / Skills and Competencies

We are looking for the following profile:

  • Proficiency in Adobe Creative Cloud
  • Solid working knowledge of PowerPoint — you know it's a design tool, not just a slideshow builder
  • Familiarity with Figma and user-centered design thinking
  • Interest in AI-assisted design workflows — tools like Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, or AI-first presentation platforms are a plus; genuine curiosity counts for more than mastery

Personal skills:

  • Currently in your 2nd year of a bachelor's in design, communication, or a related field
  • Fluent in English, with strong written and verbal communication
  • Curious about how AI is reshaping visual production — and motivated to experiment with it in a professional setting
  • A team player who takes feedback well and improves from it
  • Organized enough to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines without losing the creative thread

Additional benefits:

Being a part of our SAP Internship Experience Program and Local Early Career Talent Committee will give you an opportunity to participate in various virtual events and trainings specially tailored for you. You will have the ability to contribute and organize different local projects for other interns, thus upskilling your project management, communication and presentation skills while expanding your professional network at SAP.

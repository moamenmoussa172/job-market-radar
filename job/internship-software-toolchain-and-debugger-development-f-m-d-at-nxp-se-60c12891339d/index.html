About the job

Looking for a student job where you can put your programming skills into practice and gain real-world software development experience?

Join our Toolchain team at NXP in Gratkorn and work alongside experienced engineers on software tools and debugger technologies. This part-time position gives you the opportunity to apply what you learn at university, explore different programming languages and technologies, and build valuable industry experience alongside your studies.

Your Responsibilities:

  • Develop, test and debug software tool components using Java, Python, C, C++, Rust and C#
  • Support the automation of build and test systems using Jenkins
  • Create and maintain technical documentation
  • Contribute to debugger development by analyzing and reproducing reported issues and verifying fixes
  • Collaborate with experienced engineers and gain hands-on experience in professional software development

Your Profile:

  • Ongoing studies in Computer Science, Information and Computer Engineering, Software Development, Mathematics, or a related field
  • Good programming skills in Java and C++
  • Experience with Eclipse, Python, C, C#, LLVM/compilers, Git, SVN and/or Jenkins is a plus
  • Good spoken and written English
  • An independent, structured and accurate way of working
  • Motivation to take initiative, learn and adapt in a dynamic technical environment
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Valid work authorization required
  • Availability of approximately 20 hours per week (duration over 6 months)

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.

The minimum salary for this position is EUR 2.800,- gross/month for full-time equivalent at NXP Austria.

Moreover, we provide attractive benefits to our employees like home office, flexible working time, meal benefits and more.

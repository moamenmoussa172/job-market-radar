Job Description

Beauty Care is one of the company’s sectors, merging categories of Skin, Personal Care and Hair Care with billion dollar brands like Olay, Old Spice, SK-II, Pantene, Head & Shoulders and Herbal Essences. As Beauty Care IT Hub in Warsaw we provide IT services and solutions to Beauty Care business globally. The roles in Warsaw focus around Data & Analytics, DevOps and Architecture, which includes Software Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Data Asset Management and Product Management.

We are looking for a Lead Data Engineer & DevOps to join our Beauty Care IT team in Warsaw to lead the JBP with central, upskill our technical resources, define best practices/processes, etc.

Responsibilities:

As a Lead Data Engineer & DevOps you will be responsible for:

  • Define Long-Term Platform Architecture: Partner with Data Asset Manager, Solution/Enterprise architects to establish a resilient, future-proof technical roadmap.
  • Lead Data Pipeline Engineering: Architect and optimize robust, high-throughput batch and streaming data pipelines leveraging the Google Cloud ecosystem (current solution). Assembling large, complex data sets which meet functional and non-functional business requirements. Acquiring, cleansing, transforming, validating and publishing data from a wide variety of sources.
  • Champion DataOps & Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Own the DevOps strategy for data platforms to provision and manage cloud infrastructure, ensuring environments are reproducible, version-controlled, and modular.
  • Automate End-to-End CI/CD: Build, maintain, and standardize automated CI/CD pipelines incorporating automated testing, data contract validation, and zero-downtime deployment practices.
  • Drive GCP FinOps & Observability: Implement SRE-style monitoring, logging, and alerting to maintain high availability while actively managing and optimizing GCP cloud spend and resource allocation.
  • Embed Security & Governance into Code: Implement enterprise security controls and data governance standards directly into deployment pipelines and infrastructure configurations.
  • Technical Leadership & Mentorship: Guide and upskill engineering teams across both modern data engineering techniques and DevOps principles, establishing best practices for code reviews, architectural documentation, and production readiness.

Job Qualifications

  • Proven senior experience in data engineering and/or management
  • Experience with Azure and/or Google Cloud Platform
  • Demonstrated knowledge of: SQL, Python, PySpark, coding standards
  • Client management and communication skills
  • Hands on experience writing clean, effective code

​

Nice to have:

  • Experience with Databricks and Power BI
  • DAMA certification
  • SCRUMand DevOps methodologies
  • Using or building CI/CD tools

  • Big Picture understanding of P&G IT and Product Supply organization and its Services in global multi-functional teams with several locations across continents

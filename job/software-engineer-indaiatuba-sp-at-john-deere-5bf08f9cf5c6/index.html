About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Technology (CA)
Title: Software Engineer - Indaiatuba/SP - 124852

As a Software Engineer located in Indaiatuba, Brazil you will contribute to the design, development, and delivery of enterprise-scale software platforms using a modern software engineering technology stack. This role supports key initiatives aligned to HR/People Tech & Corporate Functions business areas.

This role requires strong hands-on engineering skills, collaboration across product and architecture teams, and a focus on building secure, reliable, and scalable cloud-native solutions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Deliver technical excellence in the team: code quality, testing strategy, performance tuning, and operational readiness.
  • Build and operate backend APIs, including performance/latency benchmarking, observability (metrics/logs/traces), and safe rollouts.
  • Apply responsible DevOps practices on the services you build (evaluation, monitoring, safety/PII controls, incident playbooks) and participate in improving team playbooks.
  • Maintain system documentation and configuration standards;
  • Troubleshoot and resolve system issues.

What Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in technology discipline (Computer Science, Software Engineering);
  • Advanced English (broad vocabulary, strong grammar, able to lead meetings, argue, negotiate, and conduct training sessions);
  • Experience integrating systems and designing or consuming APIs;
  • Solid experience working with REST APIs and system integrations;
  • Experience working as a Software Engineer;
  • Strong programming skills in Python;
  • Familiarity with cloud environments, preferably AWS;
  • Understanding of software engineering fundamentals: design, testing, debugging, and maintainability.

What makes you stand out:

  • Full-stack experience (JavaScript/TypeScript, React/React Native, and Node.js) is a plus.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, and infrastructure as code;
  • Experience with Microsoft Power Platform.

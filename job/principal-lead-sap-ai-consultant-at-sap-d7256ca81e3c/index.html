About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

1. Where You Will Belong

You will join a high-performing, innovation-driven SAP AI architecture and engineering team at the intersection of enterprise SAP technology and artificial intelligence. Our team operates with a bold ambition: "Architect the Autonomous Enterprise, Develop and Deliver Today." We are a group of intellectually curious, outcome-obsessed SAP technologists who challenge conventional thinking, embrace complexity, and turn ambitious ideas into scalable, production-grade SAP solutions.

The preferred candidate will possess insatiable curiosity, unbounded creativity, and a growth mindset to navigate ambiguity and deliver clarity within complex SAP environments. Driven by a passion for mentoring and a deep desire to teach and learn, they will naturally translate business problems into elegant SAP-native architectures and solutions while actively giving and receiving feedback to maximize the team's collective knowledge and best practice development.

2. What You Will Do

a) Customer-Focused Outcomes

As a Senior SAP AI Engineer, you will be a key driver of customer value delivery, translating SAP AI architectural blueprints into fully functional, scalable, and production-ready solutions. Specifically, you will:

· Translate business requirements into technical solutions through workshops, prototyping, and iterative delivery

· Collaborate with customers across the delivery lifecycle (workshops, reviews, UAT) to refine and optimize solutions

· Design and implement production-grade AI solutions on SAP AI Foundation, covering data, models, APIs, front-end integration, agent-to-agent communication workflows

· Deliver Generative AI use cases on SAP BTP (e.g., RAG pipelines, chatbots, document processing, AI-driven workflows)

· Build and manage SAP AI Core pipelines, including training, model serving, and monitoring for production readiness

· Develop conversational and agent-based AI solutions (e.g., SAP Joule) to enable natural language interaction with SAP processes

· Build end-to-end SAP applications using CAP and Fiori, creating intuitive, AI-augmented user experiences

· Design and orchestrate agent-based workflows across SAP and non-SAP systems for intelligent automation

b) Functional Contributor for the Team

Beyond individual delivery, you will play an important role as a senior technical voice and enabler within the development team. Specifically, you will:

· Define and promote SAP AI development standards, including coding practices, reusable components, and deployment guidelines

· Conduct code reviews and peer assessments, providing high-quality feedback to improve solution quality and team capability

· Mentor and support engineers through knowledge sharing, pair programming, and technical coaching

· Contribute to pre-sales by developing and presenting PoCs and technical demos showcasing SAP AI capabilities

· Support Agile delivery processes (e.g., sprint planning, backlog refinement, estimation) with strong technical input

· Stay current with SAP AI and broader AI ecosystem trends, driving continuous innovation within the team

3. What You Will Bring

a) Education & Track Record

· Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Information Systems, or a related field

· 7+ years of software development experience, including 5+ years in SAP BTP development and/or AI/ML engineering in enterprise SAP environments

· Proven track record of delivering production-grade SAP AI solutions across full project lifecycles (design, development, deployment) using modern frameworks (e.g., CAP, Fiori, Integration Suite)

· Professional fluency in German and English (C1+)

b) Core Architecture & Engineering Skills

· Solid understanding of SAP architecture principles and proven ability to design robust integration architectures

· Strong Python skills for AI/ML development (e.g., LangChain, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy) in SAP AI Core environments

· Solid experience in SAP BTP development and subaccount management

· Proficiency in SAP CAP (Node.js/Java) for building scalable, cloud-native applications and AI-enabled services

· Experience with SAP Fiori/UI5 to develop and extend AI-powered user interfaces

· Understanding of S/4HANA extensibility patterns (side-by-side, in-app, key-user) aligned with Clean Core principles

· Strong API development skills (REST, OpenAPI, versioning) and SAP API Management

c) SAP AI & Agentic AI Skills

· SAP AI, Agentic AI & Generative AI Expertise

o Hands-on experience with SAP AI Foundation, including model deployment, serving, and workflow orchestration

o Experience with SAP Generative AI Hub for managing LLM providers (e.g., Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI)

· Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems

o Strong knowledge of agentic AI frameworks (e.g., LangChain, LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI)

o Experience designing multi-agent architectures (task orchestration, state management, delegation, error handling)

o Ability to build conversational AI systems with context management, memory, and multi-turn interactions

o Familiarity with SAP Joule integration and extending it with custom skills and agents

· Generative AI & LLM Engineering

o Expert-level prompt engineering skills (e.g., few-shot learning, prompt chaining, context optimization)

o Hands-on experience with RAG architectures (chunking, embeddings, vector search, retrieval strategies)

c) Data & Integration Skills

· Proficiency in SAP BDC, including data product design, data integration, and use of Datasphere data in AI pipelines

· Experience with SAP Integration Suite (e.g., Cloud Integration, API Management, Event Mesh) to connect AI services with SAP processes

· Proficiency in SAP Cloud Connector and Destination Service for secure connectivity between BTP and backend systems

· Experience with SAP OData services and RAP for exposing and consuming S/4HANA data in AI-enabled applications

d) AI Quality & DevOps Skills

· Experience with SAP AI Core MLOps capabilities (e.g., training pipelines, model management, and inference endpoints)

· Proficiency in SAP AI Launchpad for monitoring and managing AI scenarios across BTP environments

· Strong experience with CI/CD pipelines on SAP BTP (e.g., SAP CI/CD service, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps)

· Knowledge of SAP BTP security best practices (e.g., OAuth2/XSUAA, secret management, encryption, Responsible AI compliance)

e) Soft Skills, Project Management Skills & Leadership Skills

· Strong communication skills, able to explain complex SAP AI concepts to both technical and business stakeholders

· Strong ownership mindset, ensuring quality, reliability, and timely delivery

· Analytical and structured problem-solving approach

· Adaptability and continuous learning mindset within evolving SAP AI landscapes

· Solid understanding of Agile and SAP Activate methodologies

· Mentoring and knowledge-sharing mindset, supporting team growth

· Customer-focused approach, aligning development with business outcomes

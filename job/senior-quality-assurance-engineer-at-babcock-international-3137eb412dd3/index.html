About the job

Job Title: Senior Quality Assurance Engineer

Compensation: From £44,600 Depending on Experience + Benefits

Role Type: Full time / Permanent

Role ID: SF78450

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Senior Quality Assurance Engineer at our Devonport Royal Dockyard site.

The role

As a Senior Quality Assurance Engineer, you’ll play a key role in supporting complex submarine support, engineering services, infrastructure and production programmes. Working within a highly regulated environment, you'll provide expert quality assurance guidance, helping to ensure compliance with customer, regulatory and business requirements while maintaining the highest standards of safety and operational excellence.

Day-to-day, you’ll have an opportunity to influence projects that contribute directly to national security and critical infrastructure. You'll work alongside project teams, operational departments, suppliers and stakeholders, driving continuous improvement, mitigating quality risks and helping deliver work that makes a meaningful difference. In return, you'll gain exposure to challenging programmes, professional development opportunities and the chance to grow your career within one of the UK's leading engineering organisations.

  • Providing quality assurance support across production, project and supply chain activities.
  • Conducting audits, supplier assessments and surveillance activities to ensure compliance with quality standards.
  • Supporting project teams by identifying quality risks and driving corrective and preventive actions.
  • Reviewing quality and technical documentation to verify compliance with customer, regulatory and business requirements.
  • Promoting continuous improvement and quality excellence across submarine support and engineering programmes.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at Devonport Royal Dockyard.

Essential experience of the Senior Quality Assurance Engineer

  • Quality Assurance experience within the nuclear sector or another highly regulated industry, with a strong understanding of quality management systems and ISO 9001 requirements.
  • Experience conducting audits, supplier assessments and surveillance activities across products, processes and supply chains.
  • Ability to identify, investigate and resolve non-conformances, manage quality risks and drive corrective actions and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Experience reviewing technical and quality documentation and supporting quality assurance activities across projects and production environments.
  • Strong analytical, organisational and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with customers, suppliers, regulators and internal teams.

Qualifications for the Senior Quality Assurance Engineer

Essential:

  • IRCA Certified Lead Auditor qualification.
  • Relevant Level 5 qualification, recognised professional status, or significant experience in a relevant engineering or scientific discipline supported by a Level 4 qualification.

Desirable:

  • Additional quality management, engineering or auditing certifications.
  • Membership of a relevant professional institution.

Security Clearance

The successful candidate must be a sole UK National who is able to achieve and maintain Security Check (SC) security clearance for this role.

Many of the positions within our company are subject to national security clearance and Trade Control restrictions. This means that your eligibility for certain roles may be affected by your place of birth, nationality, current or former citizenship, and any residency you hold or have held. Further details are available at United Kingdom Security Vetting: clearance levels - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk).

Role

As a PhD Research Associate, under the Industry PhD program (IPP), you will be at the forefront of innovation in Business AI. You will work fulltime at the SAP Business AI unit while pursuing your PhD degree at the National University of Singapore (NUS). You will conduct cutting-edge research in generative AI and machine learning and publish your findings in top academic conferences and journals aligned to internal guidelines. Possible PhD topics, informed by real industry problems, include AI agents based on large language models (LLMs) and vision models, robust LLM-based evaluation, resilient multimodal retrieval-augmented-generation (RAG), document AI, multimodal LLMs for tabular AI, and synthetic data for anomaly detection. Your research will be supervised by a mentor at SAP and an academic supervisor at the university.

While your focus will be on research and completing your PhD, you will be able to gain industry exposure through working on projects with our data scientists and developers to embed the latest advancements in AI/ML into our software products. This unique opportunity combines academic pursuit with practical experience, allowing you to gain valuable insights into the real-world impact of your research. Successful candidates need to be accepted into the PhD program by NUS.

Expectations and tasks

Your tasks include:

  • Push the frontiers of what is possible in AI in an SAP-defined research project
  • Explore, understand, and implement recent research approaches and find ways to improve it
  • Understand business processes and assess business relevance of AI solutions
  • Create excellence both in terms of research results quality and system scalability
  • Communicate the relevance of implemented systems and achieved results in a visual and consistent way
  • Work closely with the data science and engineering teams to transfer research results into product code base
  • Ability to work in global teams with different time zones
  • Immerse yourself quickly into new topics, terminology, and development tasks
  • Complete all academic requirements and your PhD degree on time

Educational and experience requirements

Required

  • Undergraduate or master's degree in computer science, mathematics, statistics, or equivalent fields
  • Acceptance to NUS PhD program
  • Experience with machine and deep learning software packages and frameworks
  • Hands-on with programming languages such as Python, C++, Java, Scala
  • Experience in Large Language Models, Generative Pre-trained Transformer models, foundational models etc.
  • Strong desire to overcome obstacles and make your work benefit SAP's customers
  • Excellent written and communication skills in English language

Optional

  • Publication track record on deep learning and machine learning
  • Operational knowledge of Linux distributions
  • Familiar with Agile/Scrum methodologies

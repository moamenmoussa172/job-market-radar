About the job

  • Work with teams across Europe to gather, prepare and analyze data for our top management
  • Analyze the financial performance of our business lines across Europe and generate insights to facilitate decision making for our top management
  • Build our budgeting and forecasting processes by reviewing and evaluating financial data
  • Develop short and long term financial models for our business units and our products
  • Support our management in delivering high quality strategic and tactical reports for key stakeholders
  • You are recent graduate from University, preferably in Business, Economics, Finance, or in another relevant field
  • Relevant working experience in financial modeling and/or controlling through previous internships/working students positions
  • You are available for a six-month internship.
  • You do not just dig numbers, but you are also outspoken and like to communicate
  • An analytical mindset paired with excellent Excel skills; SQL and Python are a plus
  • Ability to fluently communicate verbally and written in English

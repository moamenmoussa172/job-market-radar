About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Brazil.

Our Client is one of the United States’ largest insurers, providing a wide range of insurance and financial services products with gross written premiums well over US$25 Billion (P&C). They proudly serve more than 10 million U.S. households with more than 19 million individual policies across all 50 states through the efforts of over 48,000 exclusive and independent agents and nearly 18,500 employees. Finally, our Client is part of one the largest Insurance Groups in the world.

Job Summary

We are seeking Data Governance Advisors with a forward-thinking approach to data governance who can help drive the continuous modernization of data solutions. This role will support and implement data governance capabilities, working closely with business stakeholders to improve data quality, metadata management, lineage, and governance practices across the organization.

Key Responsibilities

  • Implement and support data governance initiatives, including cataloging, profiling, metadata management, and data lineage.
  • Develop and maintain data governance standards, policies, and best practices.
  • Partner with business stakeholders to promote data governance principles and demonstrate business value.
  • Support data quality and observability initiatives to improve trust and reliability of enterprise data.
  • Execute governance-related projects and deliverables across assigned business domains.
  • Translate governance concepts into practical solutions and communicate recommendations effectively.
  • Contribute to the modernization of data governance processes and solutions.

Requirements

Required Qualifications

  • 4-6 years of experience in data governance, data management, analytics, or a related field.
  • Bachelor's degree.
  • Fluent English proficiency.
  • Advanced experience with:
  • Python
  • Snowflake
  • Intermediate experience with:
  • Agent AI
  • Data Quality & Observability
  • Metadata Management & Data Lineage

Required Competencies

  • Communication & Storytelling (Advanced)
  • Product Mindset (Intermediate)
  • Influence Without Authority (Intermediate)
  • Business Acumen (Intermediate)

About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Valeo Wiper Systems Plant in Skawina specializes in the design, manufacturing, painting and assembly of wiper blades, arms and mechanisms. Additionally, in 2013 production of motors for wipers has began. Plant’s main activity is supported by a modern research and development center which is currently employing about 100 staff members.

What you will be doing:

  • Development of Wiper motor for new Wiper system projects (P1)
  • Technical support for analysis related to Motor in different areas (Production, Warranty, P0s, etc)
  • Evaluating Technical Requirements (Valeo & different OEMs) to define the best Motor solution considering product and market constraints
  • Managing technical interfaces with multiple disciplines and customer engineering teams, and communicating clearly on project progress and technical risks to division-level stakeholders
  • Close coordination and collaboration with CAD team for new components and motor design
  • Leading Design Reviews for new Motor components with suppliers and complete product level (Motor & Wiper system) with customer and internal stakeholders.
  • Proposing optimized and innovative test cases and validation scope to ensure a robust product design.
  • Continuously learning and improving your technical skills
  • Using our digital tools on a daily basis to work faster and suggesting your own ideas on how we can improve our internal processes.

Job Requirements:

  • Engineering degree, with a preference for mechanical, material or electrical engineering.
  • 1 to 3 years of professional experience in electric motors or similar fields.
  • Technical Knowledge :
  • Background on electromagnetic behavior & electrical motor concepts
  • Understanding of mechanical and material technologies for automotive components
  • Technical Skills (Preferred):
  • Experience managing technical requirements from original equipment manufacturers OEMs.
  • Experience in the automotive industry is a plus, but candidates from other sectors such as aerospace or home appliances are welcome.

  • Experience in the automotive industry is a plus, but candidates from other sectors such as aerospace or home appliances are welcome.
  • Capability to work effectively within multidisciplinary teams.

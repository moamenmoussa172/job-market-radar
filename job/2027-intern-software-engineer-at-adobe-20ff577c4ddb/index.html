About the job

Internship Details

  • Location: Basel, Switzerland
  • Duration: 6 months full-time (1st July 2027 to 31st December 2027)
  • Eligibility: EU or Swiss students enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. degree program, graduating in 2028, and residing in Switzerland
  • Hybrid arrangement: 3 days onsite at the office, 2 days remote work from home
  • Annual Pay: 96,000 CHF

The Opportunity

Adobe is seeking dedicated and curious Software Engineer interns to help design, build, and evolve the systems that power our products and platforms.

As an intern, you’ll work in an AI-enabled development environment, where modern tools accelerate how software is built and tested. You’ll collaborate with experienced engineers and multi-functional partners, including designers and product managers, to contribute to real-world systems while continuing to build your technical and problem-solving skills.

In Basel, we build Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), the industry leader in Experience Management. AEM helps our customers develop, manage, and deliver high-quality digital experiences for the world’s biggest brands.
We’re also building something new: Adobe Brand Visibility, a product that helps brands understand and improve their visibility across AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and others. As people shift from searching to asking, brands need to know whether they’re being mentioned, how they’re described, and what to do about it. This is a genuinely new market, with open problems and few established playbooks.

Join us for an internship in one of several open project areas: Backend Development (Java, Node.js, Python), Frontend Development (HTTP, HTML, JavaScript), Cloud Engineering, or Data Science.

What's in it for you?

  • Real impact: You’ll participate in important engineering projects that have a direct effect on Adobe’s products and platforms. Work alongside knowledgeable teams, address authentic challenges, and grow skills that count.
  • Unforgettable memories: Our Internship Program is built to accelerate both personal and professional growth through curated learning sessions, mentorship, and peer networking.
  • Authentic culture: At Adobe, you’re encouraged to be yourself. Our values: Create the future, Own the outcome, Raise the bar, and Be genuine build a culture where innovation thrives, ownership is celebrated, and authenticity is respected.

What are we looking for?

  • Curiosity and drive: you’re eager to learn, explore new ideas, and contribute to a global, market-leading company.
  • Currently enrolled full-time and pursuing a Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience is required. The anticipated graduation date should be in 2028, and the student should be located in Switzerland.
  • Enthusiastic about programming with a solid grasp of ideas like Data Structures, OOP, Algorithms, and Problem Solving.
  • Excited to work across data science, AI, and analytics. Help prepare and explore datasets. Experiment with machine learning models. Develop insights that support data-driven decisions across products.
  • Solid knowledge of web technologies.
  • Excellent verbal and written English communication skills; can effectively articulate complex ideas and influence others through well-reasoned explanations.
  • Team collaboration: You enjoy working with others to deliver thoughtful, end-to-end solutions.
  • Big picture thinking: You can connect the dots between day-to-day tasks and broader organisational goals.
  • Ability to participate in a full-time internship between July and December.

What to expect from the recruitment process?

Our selection process typically includes the following stages: a 60-minute online assessment, followed by a visit to our office where we conduct a 60-minute technical interview with the team, and a 45-minute soft-skills conversation with the Hiring Manager.

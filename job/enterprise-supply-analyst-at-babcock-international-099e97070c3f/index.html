The Role

As an Enterprise Supply Analyst, you'll join a newly established Enterprise Integrated Business Planning function, helping to create the first enterprise-wide view of supply capacity and capability across the In-Service Submarine Maintenance Programme. This is a unique opportunity to influence strategic decision-making on one of the UK's most critical defence programmes.

Working at the heart of enterprise planning, you'll act as the vital link between supply providers and the wider planning process, bringing together data, assumptions, risks and insights that help ensure successful programme delivery. This role offers excellent exposure to senior stakeholders, the opportunity to shape a growing capability, and the chance to develop your career within a business that delivers work of national importance.

What you’ll do:

  • Collating and maintaining relevant supply capacity and capability data from multiple providers across the enterprise.
  • Acting as the primary point of contact between key supply providers and the Enterprise Integrated Business Planning function.
  • Attending supply reviews and planning meetings to identify risks, constraints and opportunities.
  • Liaising with enterprise value chain partners to secure accurate, validated supply information and planning assumptions.
  • Ensuring supply data, assumptions and confidence levels are accurately captured, maintained and communicated to support strategic decisions.

What you’ll need:

Essential Experience of the Enterprise Integrated Business Planning Process Owner

  • Significant experience in Supply Planning, Capacity Planning, Operations Planning or Integrated Business Planning within a complex engineering, manufacturing, defence or infrastructure environment.
  • Proven ability to gather, validate and consolidate supply capability and capacity data from multiple stakeholders.
  • Experience identifying risks, constraints and planning assumptions to support informed business decision-making.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills with the ability to build trusted relationships across organisational boundaries.
  • Experience leading planning reviews and working effectively with operational teams, suppliers and senior decision makers.

Qualifications of the Enterprise Integrated Business Planning Process Owner

Essential:

  • A-levels or equivalent qualification in Mathematics and/or a related subject.
  • Extensive experience within a planning discipline, preferably supply planning.
  • Experience delivering Integrated Business Planning and Sales & Operations Planning processes.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influencing capability.
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.

Desirable:

  • SCOR-P or another recognised supply chain professional qualification.
  • Recognised Integrated Business Planning qualification or training.

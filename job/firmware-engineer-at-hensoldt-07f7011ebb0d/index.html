About the job

About the Role

The Firmware Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, testing and maintaining firmware for embedded systems and electronic products within the HENSOLDT Optronics product portfolio. The role applies advanced electronic engineering principles, FPGA technology and embedded software development to deliver innovative, reliable and high-performance product solutions. Working closely with multidisciplinary engineering teams, the incumbent ensures firmware meets technical specifications, product lifecycle requirements, manufacturing standards and customer needs.

Qualifications and experience:

  • BEng in Electronic Engineering or BSc in Electronic Engineering.
  • 5–8 years' experience working with Xilinx FPGA and Zynq SoC platforms.
  • 5 years' experience in electro-optical product design will be advantageous.
  • Experience in firmware development using DO-254 standards will be advantageous.

Key technical skills:

  • Strong foundation in mathematics, physical sciences and electronic engineering principles.
  • Knowledge and experience in FPGA-based Digital Signal Processing (DSP) algorithm development.
  • Firmware development, embedded systems design and hardware-software integration.
  • Proficiency in VHDL coding and Xilinx FPGA development tools.
  • Experience with Xilinx Vivado, Xilinx SDK, ISE and HDL Designer.
  • Experience with ModelSim, QuestaSim and simulation environments.
  • Knowledge of high-speed digital design and JESD204B.
  • Experience with MATLAB, Octave or similar tools for signal processing modelling.
  • Knowledge of TCL scripting and embedded modelling tools.
  • Requirements analysis, technical specification development and design documentation.
  • Experience with electronic design tools such as Altium Designer and HyperLynx.
  • Knowledge of reliability engineering, component derating, FMEA and HALT.
  • Firmware testing, debugging, verification and validation.
  • Understanding of configuration management, version control and engineering change control.
  • Knowledge of manufacturing processes, product industrialisation and engineering lifecycle requirements.

Key responsibilities:

Product Development and Firmware Execution

Design, develop and enhance firmware for embedded electronic products by translating product and system requirements into functional specifications, executing firmware design activities, conducting simulations, prototyping and design reviews, and preparing solutions for manufacturing to ensure reliable, high-quality products that meet technical and business requirements.

Embedded Systems Design and Integration

Develop and integrate embedded firmware solutions by collaborating with Electronic Engineers to define system requirements, select suitable microcontrollers, processors and hardware components, design firmware architectures, develop low-level code, drivers and algorithms, and resolve hardware-software integration challenges to ensure optimal system functionality, performance and reliability.

Firmware Testing, Verification and Qualification

Develop and execute comprehensive firmware testing and qualification activities by performing unit, integration and system-level testing, using appropriate simulation, debugging and hardware analysis tools, identifying and resolving technical issues, and verifying firmware against defined requirements to ensure product reliability, maintainability and compliance with applicable engineering standards.

Product Documentation and Configuration Management

Develop and maintain comprehensive firmware documentation, design specifications, architectural diagrams, technical manuals and development procedures by establishing product baselines, implementing version control and change management processes, and maintaining traceability between firmware releases, design changes and documentation to ensure product data integrity, configuration control and lifecycle compliance.

Engineering Process Improvement and Technical Support

Contribute to continuous improvement of firmware development and product lifecycle processes by applying engineering standards, design review requirements, manufacturing best practices and quality assurance principles, while providing technical support and collaborating with multidisciplinary engineering and production teams to improve product performance, manufacturing readiness and long-term product sustainability.

About the job

Who we are

VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.
As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. This partnership is a unique, industry-first model which brings together the best of in-house and 3rd party capability.
We work with customers across 28 countries from 10 VOIS locations: Albania, Egypt, Hungary, India, Romania, Spain, Turkey, UK, Germany, Ireland, and with a network of teams in Czech Republic, Italy, Greece, and Portugal.

About this Role

Lead the Innovation & AI Agents unit within Vodafone Business, shaping and executing the portfolio strategy for the next generation of AI-powered propositions for SMEs. The role owns the path from strategic thesis and customer discovery through experimentation, productisation, market launch and scale. It combines portfolio leadership, deep AI product judgement, commercial ownership and people leadership to create new revenue streams and measurable customer value.
The Unit Lead will define where Vodafone should build, co-build, orchestrate or resell; establish a coherent roadmap across voice AI, digital workers, agent marketplaces and adjacent SaaS innovation; and lead a high-performing team that can operate effectively in ambiguity while meeting Vodafone standards for security, privacy, responsible AI and operational readiness.

What you’ll do

•Define and continuously evolve the Innovation & AI Agents portfolio strategy, translating Vodafone Business priorities into a coherent set of customer propositions, capabilities and strategic bets.
•Own portfolio segmentation, opportunity sizing, competitive and ecosystem scanning, investment prioritisation and lifecycle decisions across incubate, launch, scale, partner, pivot and stop.
•Create a joined-up roadmap across voice AI, AI agents, digital workers, marketplaces and adjacent SaaS categories, ensuring alignment with core products, managed services and channel capabilities.
•Set the product strategy for agentic AI, including use-case prioritisation, human-in-the-loop design, multi-agent orchestration, interoperability, memory, permissions, grounding, observability and lifecycle governance.
•Ensure technical decisions support secure APIs, identity and access management, enterprise integration, portability and operational supportability without requiring the role holder to be the solution architect.
•Establish a test-and-learn operating model using prototypes, design partners, controlled betas and paid pilots, with explicit hypotheses, success thresholds and evidence-based investment gates.
•Define product KPIs covering activation, task completion, containment, quality, adoption, retention, customer value, NPS, unit economics and operational risk.
•Build investment cases and own the commercial logic for new propositions, including market sizing, revenue, gross margin, cost-to-serve, partner commitments, sensitivity analysis and path to profitability.
•Develop repeatable partner assessment and marketplace curation standards covering product quality, security, compliance, integration readiness, commercial viability and concentration risk.
•Lead, coach and develop product managers, setting clear outcomes, decision rights, development plans and high standards for product craft and commercial accountability.

Who you are

• Significant product management experience, including leadership of a portfolio, product line or innovation unit with accountability beyond a single feature set.
• Demonstrable experience developing portfolio and product strategy, translating market insight into investment choices, roadmaps and measurable commercial outcomes.
• Hands-on understanding of generative AI and agentic AI concepts, product patterns, limitations and risks, with experience taking AI-enabled propositions from discovery to production or scaled adoption.
• Strong commercial acumen, including business cases, P&L drivers, pricing and packaging, partner economics, margin, cost-to-serve and scenario analysis.
• Experience leading cross-functional delivery across technology, design, data, security, legal, privacy, operations, marketing, sales and finance.
• Excellent executive communication and influencing skills, including the ability to simplify complex technology and make clear recommendations under uncertainty.
• Experience working across multiple markets, channels or operating companies in a matrix organisation.
• Proven people leadership, with experience setting direction, coaching product managers, managing performance and building new capability.
• Experience in B2B or SME SaaS, telecommunications, unified communications, managed services, marketplaces or platform businesses.
• Knowledge of voice AI, conversational AI, contact-centre or business-messaging ecosystems and the operational metrics that govern them.
• Familiarity with AI evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, orchestration, observability, model risk and responsible-AI control frameworks.
• Experience with strategic partnerships involving Microsoft, Google, AWS, OpenAI, Meta, RingCentral or specialist AI vendors.
• Relevant training or certification in product leadership, cloud/AI platforms, responsible AI, finance or people leadership.

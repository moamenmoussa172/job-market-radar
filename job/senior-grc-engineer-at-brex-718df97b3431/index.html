What you’ll do

As a Senior GRC Engineer at Brex, you’ll lead the IT security compliance workstreams that connect our technical control owners to the people who examine our controls: external auditors, Internal Audit, state regulators, and Finance. You’ll coordinate and test the technical controls behind our financial reporting (SOX/ITGC), our annual SOC 1 Type 2 report, our state money transmitter examinations, and our FINRA IT requirements. You’ll also run the annual business continuity and disaster recovery (BC/DR) testing calendar.

As part of the GRC team you may also contribute to the following operational tasks:

  • Third party risk management
  • Risk register maintenance and reporting
  • Customer Trust / GTM due diligence support
  • Tooling access requests
  • Assist with requests related to other certifications like SOC 2 Type 2, PCI DSS, or ISO 27001

Where you’ll work

This role will be based in our Seattle office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

Responsibilities

  • LeadIT SOX/ITGC test procedures, walkthroughs, evidence, workpapers, and remediation across logical access, change management, computer operations, and IT governance.
  • Manageour annual SOC 1 Type 2 engagement, including external auditor coordination, control descriptions, evidence, deficiency tracking, and review of draft report sections.
  • Support state money transmitter (MTL) examinations by working with Legal, Compliance, and Finance on IT documentation, examiner requests, and tracking of IT findings.
  • Test quarterly user access reviews, privileged-access evidence, and segregation-of-duties remediation, and verify that in-scope changes are complete and properly authorized.
  • Run the annual BC/DR testing calendar, including tabletop and failover exercises, and document results, evidence, and remediation.
  • Prepare FINRA-related IT control documentation, including business continuity, books-and-records, systems access reviews, and readiness testing.
  • Track findings through to closure with clear control owners, deadlines, and evidence, and keep audit coordination separate from control operation.
  • Support broader GRC team operational tasks related to evidence collection, access requests, and risk management.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in IT audit, SOX/ITGC testing, GRC, or technology compliance, in fintech, financial services, or public accounting.
  • Hands-on experience with ITGC testing across logical access, change management, and computer operations, and with documenting results in audit workpapers.
  • Working knowledge of SOC 1 / SSAE 18, including Type 2 reports, control descriptions, and deficiency management.
  • Familiarity with PCAOB standards.
  • Experience working with external auditors, regulators, or examiners and responding to their requests on deadline.
  • Strong organization and project management skills, with the ability to run several audit workstreams on different calendars at once.
  • Clear communication, and comfort holding technical control owners accountable for evidence and remediation.

Bonus points

  • CISA, CIA, CPA, or CISSP certification.
  • Experience with state money transmitter examinations or FINRA IT and books-and-records requirements.
  • Experience coordinating BC/DR testing, including tabletop and failover exercises.
  • Experience supporting audit readiness during an acquisition or integration.
  • Familiarity with COSO 2013 and COBIT.
  • Experience with GRC or audit workflow tools, or with SQL or scripting for control testing.

About the job

We help the world run better

At SAP, we enable you to bring out your best. Our company culture is focused on collaboration and a shared passion to help the world run better. How? We focus every day on building the foundation for tomorrow and creating a workplace that embraces differences, values flexibility, and is aligned to our purpose-driven and future-focused work. We offer a highly collaborative, caring team environment with a strong focus on learning and development, recognition for your individual contributions, and a variety of benefit options for you to choose from.

What you build

  • Develop and enhance web applications, internal tools, and AI-powered solutions
  • Implement features using JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, Python, HTML/CSS, and APIs
  • Build and integrate LLM-based applications and AI agents
  • Work on agent-to-agent communication and multi-agent workflows
  • Support LLM tuning, prompt optimization, evaluation, and model performance improvement
  • Integrate external services, APIs, and AI models into existing applications
  • Support testing, debugging, and quality assurance
  • Work with Git and modern software development practices
  • Contribute to dashboards, analytics, and reporting solutions such as Power BI
  • Translate technical requirements into practical solutions
  • Take ownership of smaller development tasks and collaborate with developers and project teams

What you bring

  • We are looking for an experienced Working Student who is currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Informatics, AI, or a related field and already brings practical software development experience.
  • Currently enrolled in a relevant university degree
  • Previous hands-on experience through working student roles, internships, university projects, or personal projects
  • Solid understanding of software engineering fundamentals
  • Experience with web development and API integration
  • Interest or initial experience in Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, and multi-agent systems
  • Familiarity with LLM tuning, prompting, evaluation, or RAG-based applications is a plus
  • Independent, structured, and proactive way of working
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills
  • Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies quickly

Role setup

  • Part-time position alongside studies
  • Intended for a student who already has practical technical experience
  • Opportunity to work on real software and AI use cases
  • Strong focus on hands-on development, experimentation, and ownership

Where you belong

The role is in the SAP COO board area, Corporate Processes & IT (CPIT) and the unit AI, Data & Platform (AIPD). Our vision is to bring the Autonomous Enterprise to life at SAP and act as Customer Zero to SAP.
SAP’s Intelligent Data Management (IDM) unit owns SAP’s enterprise data management and runs product management for master data, and within IDM, one team drives various strategic topics, especially leveraging AI for automation.
They are a team of Project Management and Subject Matter Experts with knowledge and expertise on Intelligent Data Management Strategy Programs and Projects across AIPD, CPIT and SAP. Their core tasks include project management, stakeholder management and connected communications.

Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.

This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

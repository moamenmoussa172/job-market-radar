About the job

About Synechron

Synechron is a global digital transformation consulting firm partnering with leading financial institutions to accelerate their business and technology transformation.

We combine deep Financial Services expertise with capabilities across technology, digital, data and AI to help our clients design, build and deliver complex transformation programmes.

Our teams operate internationally, bringing together business consulting, technology expertise and global delivery capabilities to support some of the world's leading banks and financial institutions.

Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced Calypso Technical Analyst to contribute to the development, customisation, integration and enhancement of the Nasdaq Calypso platform within a banking environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop and customise Calypso solutions using Java and Calypso APIs.
  • Translate functional requirements into technical specifications and solutions.
  • Develop and maintain workflows, interfaces, scheduled tasks and reporting components.
  • Support integrations with internal and external systems.
  • Perform technical analysis, troubleshooting and root-cause investigations.
  • Contribute to testing, releases, deployments and production support.
  • Collaborate with Business Analysts, developers and business stakeholders.

Required Skills

  • Proven experience as a Calypso Technical Analyst or Calypso Developer.
  • Strong Java, Calypso APIs and SQL expertise.
  • Experience in Calypso customisation, workflows and integrations.
  • Good understanding of financial markets and trading systems.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Calypso upgrades or migrations.
  • Unix/Linux, scripting and CI/CD.
  • Agile delivery experience.

About Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all.

Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce.

What You’ll Be Doing & Why It Matters

Solution Engineers (SEs) at Salesforce act as trusted partners to our customers, collaborating with sales, product, FDE and professional services teams to drive the technical sale of our products and help customers maximise value from their investments.

If you enjoy a dynamic environment full of challenges, and opportunities to build compelling solutions, a Solution Engineering role may be right for you. You must have a passion for blending both technical and business skills, including the ability to drive business discovery, design technical solutions, recommend best practices, and configure and successfully demonstrate solutions that drive tangible business value for our customers.

As a Specialist Solution Engineer, you will serve as a key pre-sales technical expert across Service Cloud and Salesforce Field Service solutions for our enterprise accounts. Operating as a strategic advisor and hands-on expert, you will leverage Salesforce Field Service, Service Cloud and other Salesforce capabilities to create compelling solutions, remove technical objections, and build credibility with stakeholders, transforming how companies engage with their customers.

This role requires deep Field Service expertise, with experience of Service Cloud considered highly advantageous. You will drive enterprise deal execution while contributing to broader strategic initiatives. Beyond running discovery, architecting solutions and building rapid prototypes to polished demos, you will provide feedback to inform product direction, share UK and Ireland market insights, and support enablement across our sales and partner ecosystem. You will translate complex customer requirements into compelling AI-driven solutions, and provide occasional advisory post-sale guidance to ensure long-term customer success.

If you love Field Service, live for the technical win, and want to be part of a team building the future of agentic enterprise operations — this role is for you.

Core Responsibilities & Scope

  • Partner with sales: to achieve maximum value and technical success for our customers. Support account planning activities, RFI/RFP response and deal qualification to prioritise highest-impact opportunities that drive business growth and customer retention.
  • Immersive Discovery: Lead business and technical requirements discovery through interactive methods - including customer workshops, ride-alongs, reverse demos, and collaborative ideation sessions.
  • Presentations and Demonstrations: Showcase the value and capabilities of Salesforce Field Service and Service Cloud including covering: Optimisation and Planning, Mobile, Asset Management, Workforce Management, Reporting and Analytics. Using AI tooling build rapid prototypes and tailored demos, crafting high-impact presentations and playbacks that provide clarity on solution, articulate business value, and align with desired customer outcomes.
  • Executive Presence - Comfortable delivering product demonstrations and presenting technical recommendations to C-suite and VP-level stakeholders
  • Technical Advisor & Relationship Building: Combine deep technical expertise and integrity to be a trusted advisor to our customers, building relationships with senior stakeholders and technical leads.
  • Path to Production: Guide customers on implementation options ensuring smooth handover to delivery teams. Provide post-sale advice on best practices and support resources available to drive adoption.
  • Scale and Multiplier: Support Salesforce Field Service enablement across sales teams, partners, and customers through training sessions, marketing events and by creating reusable assets. Contribute to our technical communities, and share field feedback with Product Management to inform product roadmaps.

Minimum Requirements

  • Multi-year relevant experience in the IT industry, either pre-sales, technical consulting or architecture/implementation of enterprise CRM software / customer service solutions.
  • Excellent communication / presentation skills with proven ability to engage business and technology leaders, explain complex technical concepts in simple terms, go deep when needed, and articulate the value of technology solutions
  • Confident and willing to get hands-on to build and deliver complex demos and prototypes.
  • Understanding of AI capabilities and how they can be applied to customer service use cases, including LLMs, agentic workflows, RAG, prompt engineering and observability.
  • Willing and able to travel regularly for customer engagements and events
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, STEM field, or equivalent practical industry experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Salesforce certifications - Salesforce Certified Agentforce Field Service and Operations Consultant, Agentforce Service Consultant, Platform Administrator, Platform Developer, Agentforce Specialist, or Data Cloud / 360 Consultant.
  • Experience of Salesforce Field Service implementation projects
  • Hands-on experience with Agentic AI tools and practices - Claude Code, Cursor, CODEXAgentforce, Meshmesh or equivalent.

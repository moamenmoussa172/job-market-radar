About The Role

As a Senior Data Scientist based at the John Deere Regional Office in Indaiatuba, SP, you will collaborate with business and analytics leaders to generate insights and solve complex business challenges using advanced analytics techniques, including statistical analysis, predictive modeling, forecasting, optimization, data visualization, and machine learning. You will analyze moderately complex datasets from multiple sources, validate findings through experimental approaches, and partner with cross-functional teams to deliver data-driven solutions. In addition, you will support data governance initiatives and lead small projects, including those involving global stakeholders, to drive innovation and business impact across the organization.

Main Responsibilities

• Define technical strategy and architecture for complex data science, machine learning, AI, and advanced analytics initiatives across multiple products or business areas.
• Lead solutions from problem definition and experimentation through production deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.
• Personally design and build models, algorithms, data pipelines, and production-ready analytical systems for difficult or ambiguous problems.
• Translate customer and business needs into scalable technical solutions with measurable outcomes.
• Provide technical direction across projects, ensuring solutions are reliable, maintainable, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards.
• Create reusable tools, frameworks, and technical practices that improve quality and accelerate delivery across teams.
• Partner with product, engineering, and business leaders to shape roadmaps, evaluate tradeoffs, and guide technical investments.
• Mentor data scientists and engineers through design reviews, code reviews, technical coaching, and knowledge sharing.
• Communicate technical recommendations, risks, and results clearly to technical teams and senior leaders.
• Promote engineering excellence, responsible AI, data governance, security, and technical innovation.

What Skills You Need

  • Completed Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics, Operations Research, or a related technical discipline.
  • Advanced or Fluent English proficiency, with the ability to collaborate effectively with global teams.
  • Availability to work onsite at the John Deere Regional Office in Indaiatuba, SP, three days per week.
  • Availability to travel approximately 10% of the time, as required to support business and project needs.
  • Proven experience developing and deployingproduction-grade machine learning, deep learning, AI, advanced analytics, optimization, or data-intensive software solutions.
  • Advanced programming skills and hands-on experience with modern data science, machine learning, and software engineering practices.
  • Experience working with large and complex datasets, such as machine telemetry, geospatial data, imagery, time-series, operational, or agronomic data.
  • Strong knowledge of machine learning, statistics, experimentation, model validation, and model performance evaluation.
  • Experience designing scalable data pipelines, model workflows, and production architectures.
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals, including version control, automated testing, CI/CD, reproducibility, and maintainable code development.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence technical decisions and establish direction across multiple teams without direct authority.
  • Proven track record of solving ambiguous business problems and translating technical solutions into measurable business value.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management, collaboration, and technical mentoring skills.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Expertise in computer vision, geospatial analytics, remote sensing, time-series modeling, optimization, robotics, autonomy, simulation, or precision agriculture.
  • Experience with cloud platforms, Databricks, Spark, Kubernetes, distributed computing, MLOps, model registries, or production model monitoring.
  • Experience building reusable platforms, frameworks, libraries, or shared capabilities adopted by multiple teams.
  • Familiarity with responsible AI, privacy, data governance, cybersecurity, and model governance.
  • Experience defining technical strategy across a portfolio of products or initiatives.
  • Record of innovation through patents, publications, research collaborations, or novel production solutions.

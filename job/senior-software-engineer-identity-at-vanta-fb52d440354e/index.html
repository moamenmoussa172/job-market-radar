About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

At Vanta, our mission is to help businesses earn and prove trust.We believe that security should be monitored and verified continuously, and we empower companies to practice better security and prove it with ease. Vanta has a kind and talented team, and while some have prior security experience, many have been successful at Vanta without it.

Visit our Vanta Engineering Blog to learn more about what our team is working on!

The Identity and Access Management (IAM) team owns Vanta's authentication and authorization platform. As Vanta's product surface grows in complexity and our customer base expands upmarket, the company looks to IAM team to deliver enterprise features that open new opportunities, and execute at a quality bar that minimizes risk to Vanta and our customers. Our mission is to make correct authentication and authorization the default path for every engineer at Vanta, with clear guardrails, fast onboarding, and predictable performance at scale.

As Vanta expands its agentic capabilities across surfaces like MCP, CLI, and the Vanta Agent, the IAM team is also at the center of a new set of problems: defining what it means for an agent to act as a user's proxy, how an agent acts on its own behalf, enforcing consistent permission checks across every invocation surface, and building an attribution model that makes agent-assisted actions auditable and trustworthy. You'll help design the identity primitives that make autonomous agent behavior safe and governable at scale.

As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll shape the direction of these areas in the IAM pillar, and your technical decisions will create leverage across the engineering organization.

What you’ll do as a Senior Software Engineer at Vanta:

  • Architect and evolve Vanta's authentication and authorization platform, setting technical direction for how identity, permissions, and access are enforced across the entire product
  • Lead the design of identity primitives for Vanta's agentic capabilities: defining proxy identity models for agents acting on behalf of users, agent identities for agents acting on their own behalf, enforcing consistent permission checks across MCP, CLI, and the Vanta Agent, and building attribution frameworks that make agent-assisted actions auditable and governable at scale
  • Lead initiatives that raise the security and reliability bar for auth primitives, spanning API-layer enforcement, enterprise identity protocols (SAML, SSO, SCIM), and access control at scale
  • Define the developer experience for auth at Vanta by building frameworks, patterns, and tooling that make it easy for product engineers to build securely by default, reducing onboarding friction and time-to-correct
  • Establish shared standards for access control and identity that reduce inconsistency and increase velocity for teams across the organization
  • Own complex system design decisions across authentication flows, authorization models, and identity lifecycle management, designing for correctness, extensibility, and long-term maintainability
  • Mentor engineers across the IAM team and the broader Vanta engineering org, raising the technical bar through design reviews, code feedback, and establishing engineering best practices

How to be successful in this role:

  • Expertise in authentication and authorization systems, including SAML/SSO, SCIM, OAuth, OIDC, token-based auth, and multi-tenant access control
  • Strong system design and backend architecture skills, with demonstrated experience building developer-facing platform abstractions that reduce complexity and create lasting leverage for engineering organizations
  • Thinking in terms of developer experience for platform systems- you design for the engineers who consume your platform, not just the underlying infrastructure
  • Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills, with the ability to align engineering, security, and product leadership around a shared technical vision
  • Strong technical foundation in modern backend technologies; experience with TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, and event-driven architecture is a plus
  • Use AI in day-to-day work to amplify skills and strengthen outcomes, applying it across routine workflows, actively building fluency through experimentation, and exercising sound judgment to ensure AI-assisted work meets a high quality bar

About Vanta

We started in 2018, in the wake of several high-profile data breaches. Online security was only becoming more important, but we knew firsthand how hard it could be for fast-growing companies to invest the time and manpower it takes to build a solid security foundation. Vanta was inspired by a vision to restore trust in internet businesses by enabling companies to improve and prove their security. From our early days automating security monitoring for compliance standards like SOC 2, HIPAA and ISO 27001 to creating the world's leading Trust Management Platform, our vision remains unchanged.

Now more than ever, making security continuous—not just a point-in-time check— is essential. Thousands of companies rely on Vanta to build, maintain and demonstrate their trust— all in a way that's real-time and transparent.

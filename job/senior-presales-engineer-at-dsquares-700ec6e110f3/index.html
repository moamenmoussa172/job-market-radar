Senior Presales Engineer

About the job

As a Senior Product Pre-Sales Engineer, you will help us win enterprise loyalty deals across

the MEA region by showing clients exactly what our platform does and how it fits their business.

You will demo the product, translate client requirements into platform capabilities, and write the

functional and technical sections of RFPs and proposals. We are looking for someone who

understands software products and the data behind them, and can explain both clearly to business

and technical audiences.

- Core Mission: Win deals by explaining and demonstrating what the product does,

accurately and convincingly.

- Work Style: Hybrid (3 days on-site, 2 days remote).

- Team: Part of the Presales team, working under the Senior Presales Manager and

alongside Sales, Product and Delivery.

What You Will Do Day to Day

  • Run live product demos for client business and technical stakeholders, tailored to their use cases.
  • Map client requirements to platform modules and capabilities, and identify gaps early.
  • Draft accurate functional and technical answers for RFPs, RFIs and proposals using our knowledge base.
  • Build and maintain demo scenarios, using sample customer and transaction data to show campaigns, rewards and reporting.
  • Join discovery calls, capture requirements and turn them into a clear solution outline.
  • Provide technical input to SOWs and pricing, and hand over sold solutions to Delivery with complete context.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Solution Discovery: Join discovery workshops with client business and technical teams to understand objectives, data sources and success measures.
  • Proposal Ownership: Own the technical drafting of RFP, RFI and proposal sections assigned to you, with high accuracy and consistency with the knowledge base.
  • Product Demonstrations: Deliver confident, clear demos that connect platform features to the client’s business outcomes.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Sales, Product and Delivery so that what we sell is what we can deliver.
  • Team Contribution: Share best practices, reusable content and lessons learned, and support junior team members.

Requirements

  • Experience: 5–10 years in product-focused pre-sales, solutions consulting or product consulting on a SaaS or data platform (for example CRM, CDP, martech, loyalty, payments or BI).
  • Product Understanding: Proven ability to learn a software product in depth and explain how it works end to end, with live demos.
  • Data Fluency: Working knowledge of customer and transaction data models, SQL basics, segmentation, and reporting and analytics.
  • Integration Awareness: Able to read and explain REST APIs and integration flows at a functional level (for example with POS, CRM and payment systems).
  • Communication: Explains technical concepts clearly to non-technical audiences, in writing and in person.
  • Delivery Under Pressure: Has written RFP or proposal responses to deadlines and handled several opportunities in parallel, with strong attention to detail.
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems or Business.
  • Language: Professional fluency in English and Arabic (French is a plus for African market coverage).

Benefits

  • Comprehensive Wellbeing: Enjoy peace of mind with our private medical insurance and life cover, plus extra days off for every year you're part of the team.
  • Financial Growth: Reap the rewards of your hard work with our annual profit share and competitive compensation packages.
  • Work-Life Balance: Achieve optimal well-being with our hybrid work environment, generous maternity and paternity benefits, and regular team-building events.
  • Continuous Development: Expand your horizons through our commitment to learning and development opportunities.
  • Perks and Privileges: Celebrate your journey with us through birthday offs, mobile allowances, transportation support, and a host of other employee-centric perks.

About the Team

The Adoption Team develops intelligent agents and capabilities that enable customers to manage Workday efficiently and maximize their overall experience. As a distributed-systems focused engineering group, we build and scale the agentic administrative tooling which supports Workday's core product suite.

About the Role

In this individual-contributor role as a Machine Learning Engineer, you will apply your growing ML expertise alongside a team of distributed-systems engineers, contributing to automation and intelligence initiatives across Workday's administration platform. Working under the guidance of senior engineers, Engineering leads, and Product Managers, you will help build and deploy ML components, develop your technical skills, and collaborate with core platform teams to integrate machine learning into Workday's administrative systems.

You will work within a collaborative ecosystem, helping translate technical work into measurable value for Workday customers while growing your expertise in applied ML engineering.

Key Responsibilities

  • Ability to work individually and as part of a team to build and help productionize machine learning models and pipelines that integrate with Workday's platform-level systems.
  • Follow and help apply established best practices for ML engineering, including model development, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring.
  • Work with distributed-systems engineers to help ensure ML solutions scale reliably across Workday's multi-tenant architecture.
  • Contribute to components of the ML lifecycle — including data preparation, model training, deployment support, and monitoring in production.
  • Collaborate with product management, data engineering, and platform teams on well-scoped ML-driven automation initiatives.
  • Participate in code reviews and knowledge-sharing sessions to build technical skills and support team quality standards.
  • Stay current with new tools, frameworks, and techniques relevant to the team's ML practices.
  • Support the team's technical roadmap for ML capabilities within the Adoption team.
  • Develop and deploy Generative AI capabilities and Large Language Model (LLM) applications

About You

Basic Qualifications

  • 4+ years of experience in machine learning engineering, applied ML, or a closely related discipline, including exposure to deploying ML models to production.
  • Solid software engineering fundamentals, with some experience in distributed or production systems.
  • Familiarity with common ML frameworks and languages (e.g., Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow) and basic MLOps practices (model versioning, monitoring).
  • Ability to execute well-defined technical tasks with increasing independence, escalating and collaborating with senior engineers as needed.
  • Good collaboration and communication skills, with willingness to learn from and work closely with more senior engineers.

Other Qualifications

  • Exposure to large-scale, multi-tenant SaaS platforms.
  • Interest or coursework/project experience in applying ML to enterprise/administrative platform problems (e.g., anomaly detection, automation, intelligent configuration).
  • Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and data processing systems.
  • Practical experience developing generative AI capabilities and integrating LLMs, with a preference for orchestration frameworks such as LangChain and agentic architectures.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together. Those in our remote "home office" roles also have the opportunity to come together in our offices for important moments that matter.

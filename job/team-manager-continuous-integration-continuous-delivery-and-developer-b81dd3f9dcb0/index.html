About the job

As a team manager at Ubisoft Montréal, you will support a team that improves development workflows, continuous integration and continuous delivery, build systems, and the tools used to deliver Rainbow Six.

You will work with the technical lead and partner teams to reduce development friction, improve the quality of deliverables, and make delivery more reliable. You will also support the gradual adoption of artificial intelligence in the team’s workflows.

What you’ll do:

  • Manage and support the team in its work and the growth of its members.
  • Align the roadmap and priorities with the technical lead.
  • Prioritize initiatives based on their impact on productivity, quality, and delivery reliability.
  • Plan and track the team’s work, including dependencies.
  • Identify workflow barriers with partner teams.
  • Facilitate collaboration with development, quality assurance, online, production, and core technology teams.
  • Check the value of solutions for internal users and how well they meet identified needs.
  • Improve practices for builds, continuous integration and delivery, test automation, development tools, and system monitoring.
  • Support the adoption of artificial intelligence for automation, problem review, code quality, and faster feedback.
  • Foster collaboration and continuous improvement within the team.

What you bring to the team:

  • Experience managing and supporting a team in a software, technical, or game development environment.
  • Understanding of continuous integration and delivery, build systems, automation, and development tools.
  • Ability to turn technical direction into a work plan with technical partners.
  • Experience planning, prioritizing, and tracking delivery, including dependencies.
  • Ability to work across teams in a complex production environment.
  • Knowledge of software quality practices, test automation, system monitoring, or internal tools.
  • Ability to support team members and communicate clearly.
  • Interest in practical uses of artificial intelligence in software development.

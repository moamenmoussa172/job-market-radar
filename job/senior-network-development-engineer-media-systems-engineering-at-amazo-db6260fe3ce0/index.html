About the job

Are you interested in shaping the future of entertainment?
Amazon MGM Studios is the movies and television development, production, and distribution arm of Amazon. It is our role to produce original content in service of Amazon's Prime Video customers. We need your passion, innovative ideas, and creativity to help take us to new heights.
Amazon MGM Studios is seeking a Senior Network Development Engineer to lead the design, architecture, and operation of secure, high-performance network and wireless infrastructure across our global production facilities. You'll work at the intersection of entertainment and technology ensuring that the networks powering content creation for Prime Video, Amazon MGM Studios, and our original productions are reliable, scalable, and secure.
Key job responsibilities
- Own the network architecture roadmap across studio production facilities.
- Design and optimize enterprise network and WiFi for high-density studio and stage environments.
- Work with Amazon Security to build zero-trust network solutions and maintain compliance with content protection standards.
- Support and troubleshoot network, compute and storage systems (NAS, SAN, media servers) on-premises and cloud.
- Build automation to reduce manual work and keep environments consistent.
- Manage vendor relationships, contracts, and SLAs for connectivity services.
- Plan capacity for upcoming productions, facility growth, and new tech rollouts.
- Mentor engineers and set technical standards for the team.
- Connect on-premises production environments to cloud services.
- Provide white-glove support for active productions, troubleshooting network, WiFi, and infrastructure issues with urgency.
- Respond to escalations and work with vendors to resolve outages quickly.
Please note this role will require a flexible schedule including weekends, nights, and holidays due to the nature of Studio based work
A day in the life
We are looking for innovative minds to join our fast-paced team. You will work in an agile environment to develop advanced solutions that revolutionize both our Studio operations and the broader entertainment industry. You will be challenged to think broadly and pioneer innovative approaches while contributing to Amazon's strategic vision. You will also be supported by a talented team of peers that work each day to positively impact our customers. This is an evolving business where creativity and drive can make a lasting impact. If you are energized by rapid growth and transformative technology, we want to hear from you.
About the team
The Media Systems Engineering team designs and delivers production and post-production environments, technology solutions, and hybrid infrastructure that automate, streamline, and optimize audio and video workflows throughout the content creation lifecycle at Amazon MGM Studios and Prime Video.
- Knowledge of IP networking fundamentals such as BGP, OSPF and other R&S technologies
- Experience with industry standard tools and scripting languages (Python or Perl) for automation
- Bachelor's degree, or experience in a relevant field (e.g., Computer Science, Networking Engineering)
- Experience and proven troubleshooting skills, specifically in the Wi-Fi, switch and router areas
- Demonstrated industry experience designing and operating enterprise wireless networks at scale (Cisco, Aruba, or equivalent; 802.11ax/WiFi 6/6E)
- Expert level knowledge of enterprise firewall platforms (Palo Alto, Cisco ASA, or equivalent)
- Experience with network monitoring, observability, and capacity planning tools
- Experience working for a production company, studio, or television network
- Experience with infrastructure as code, ops automation, and configuration management tools such as Chef, Puppet, or Ansible
- Knowledge of security best practices
- Familiarity with AV-over-IP protocols and high-bandwidth media workflows (NDI, SMPTE 2110, Dante)
- Previous experience with AWS networking services (VPC, Direct Connect, Transit Gateway, Cloud WAN)
- Experience with SD-WAN, MPLS, or dedicated circuit management across international sites
- CCNP, CCIE, or equivalent professional certification

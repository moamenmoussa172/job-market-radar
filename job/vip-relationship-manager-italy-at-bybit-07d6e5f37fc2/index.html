About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Europe.

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 90 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

We are looking for an enthusiastic Relationship Manager to join our VIP team in Italy, focusing on acquiring and engaging Italian-speaking clients. As the primary point of contact for a portfolio of VIP clients, you will enhance the customer experience, foster long-term client loyalty, and support business growth in the Italian market.

Location: Italy. Candidates must be based in and reside in Italy.

Responsibilities

  • Acquire new VIP clients and manage a portfolio of high-net-worth individuals and institutional clients, with a focus on the Italian market.
  • Build, maintain, and expand strong client relationships through regular, proactive, and personalised communication.
  • Develop a deep understanding of clients’ trading preferences, needs, and expectations to deliver tailored support and identify opportunities to strengthen engagement.
  • Contribute to the development and improvement of VIP client service processes, ensuring consistent service quality and effective portfolio management.
  • Analyse client behaviour, anticipate needs, and prepare reports on key accounts, client engagement, and business opportunities.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve client enquiries and deliver an excellent, seamless customer experience.
  • Stay informed about developments in the Italian market and the evolving crypto industry.

Qualifications:

  • Must be based in and reside in Italy, with a strong understanding of the local market and client expectations.
  • Proven experience in relationship management, sales, or client services within financial services, banking, investments, brokerage, or trading.
  • An established client network or portfolio of high-net-worth individuals, institutional investors, private banking clients, or professional traders is highly desirable.
  • Experience working with trading platforms or cryptocurrency exchanges is a strong advantage.
  • Fluency in Italian and English, with excellent verbal and written communication skills in both languages.
  • Knowledge of Spanish is a plus.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to build trust and maintain long-term relationships with demanding clients.
  • Ability to work under pressure, manage multiple priorities, and meet tight deadlines while maintaining high service standards.
  • A genuine interest in the crypto industry and a proactive approach to staying informed about market trends, products, and innovations.

Why Join Us

At Bybit, we are committed to fostering a supportive and enriching work environment. Our benefits include:

  • Study Growth Fund: We support your professional development and continuous learning.
  • Internal Events: Participate in regular team-building activities, workshops, and events designed to promote collaboration and innovation.
  • Global Collaboration: Be part of a diverse, international team, working alongside colleagues from around the world.
  • Career Advancement: Access opportunities for growth and advancement within a rapidly expanding global company.
  • Internal Mobility: Grow with us- Your long-term development is important to us. We offer internal job opportunities to help build your career path.

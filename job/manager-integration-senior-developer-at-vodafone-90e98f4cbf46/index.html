About this Role

We are seeking an experienced integration professional with 7–10 years of experience to lead the setup, operational readiness and end-to-end technical delivery of Apache Kafka and AWS/Amazon MSK platforms. The role combines hands-on expertise across Kafka, Java, Spring Boot and integration technologies with technical leadership, production support, stakeholder management and engineering mentorship. Experience with TIBCO BusinessWorks (BW) would be advantageous.

What you’ll do

  • Lead Apache Kafka and Amazon MSK platform setup and operational readiness.
  • Define and implement networking, IAM, encryption and monitoring requirements for reliable Kafka environments.
  • Establish deployment automation and recovery planning for the platform.
  • Own end-to-end technical delivery, from design and development through testing and production release.
  • Conduct design and code reviews to maintain engineering quality.
  • Provide support for critical production issues and contribute to service recovery.
  • Apply Java, Spring Boot and integration expertise to solution design and implementation.
  • Mentor engineers and encourage effective engineering practices.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to maintain alignment on technical delivery and priorities.
  • Identify and manage technical and delivery risks.
  • Ensure timely and high-quality delivery throughout the development and release lifecycle.

Who you are

  • You have 7–10 years of integration experience.
  • You have strong hands-on expertise in Apache Kafka.
  • You have experience setting up and operating AWS/Amazon MSK environments.
  • You understand networking, AWS IAM, encryption and monitoring within Kafka/MSK environments.
  • You have experience with deployment automation and recovery planning.
  • You have strong development expertise in Java and Spring Boot.
  • You have experience with integration technologies and patterns.
  • You can lead design and code reviews, testing, releases and critical production support.
  • You can mentor engineers and collaborate effectively with technical and business stakeholders.
  • You can identify and manage delivery and technical risks.
  • TIBCO BusinessWorks (BW) experience would be advantageous.

What's in it for you

  • Opportunity to take end-to-end ownership of technically complex Apache Kafka and Amazon MSK platform delivery.
  • Exposure to cloud-based integration, automation, security, monitoring and recovery practices.
  • Opportunity to combine hands-on engineering with technical leadership and mentorship.
  • Experience working closely with technical and business stakeholders across delivery and production environments.

What skills you will learn

  • Broader expertise in operating Amazon MSK and Apache Kafka environments at scale.
  • Enhanced knowledge of AWS networking, IAM, encryption and monitoring in an event-streaming environment.
  • Advanced deployment automation and recovery-planning practices.
  • Greater experience in technical leadership, stakeholder alignment, risk management and production support.

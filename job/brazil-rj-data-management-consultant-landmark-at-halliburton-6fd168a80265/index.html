Job Duties

Responsibilities

  • Support the management, integration, and maintenance of E&P geoscience data, ensuring data availability, integrity, consistency, and quality across geoscience applications and databases.
  • Perform data loading, quality control, transfer, manipulation, archiving, backup, restoration, and data lifecycle management activities for geological, geophysical, petrophysical, and spatial datasets.
  • Implement and follow data naming conventions, standards, best practices, and data management workflows to ensure data quality, confidentiality, security, traceability, and integrity.
  • Manage and manipulate a variety of geological, geophysical, petrophysical, and geospatial datasets, identifying data-quality issues and implementing appropriate corrective actions.
  • Support users in E&P data management and geoscience application workflows, particularly using OpenWorks® and DecisionSpace® Geosciences, including data loading, validation, organization, transfer, and integration.
  • Perform technical reviews and quality control of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and their integration with geoscience databases and E&P datasets.
  • Support the management and integration of spatial information using Esri ArcGIS and related GIS technologies.
  • Provide technical guidance and support regarding data structures, formats, databases, metadata, data loading procedures, and data management practices used throughout E&P geoscience workflows.
  • Work with multidisciplinary teams, including geologists, geophysicists, petrophysicists, engineers, GIS specialists, and IT professionals, to support integrated subsurface and data management workflows.
  • Contribute to data governance, standardization, process improvement, automation, and continuous-improvement initiatives related to geoscience data management.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Geology, Geophysics, Petroleum Engineering, Cartographic Engineering, Geography, Oceanography, Data Science/Data Analytics, or another relevant technical discipline related to Geosciences and E&P Data Management.
  • Professional experience in Geoscience Data Management, E&P Data Management, or a related technical role.
  • Good understanding of the Oil & Gas Exploration and Production lifecycle, with the ability to connect geoscience concepts, data, and technical applications.
  • Advanced English proficiency, with the ability to communicate effectively in technical and business environments.

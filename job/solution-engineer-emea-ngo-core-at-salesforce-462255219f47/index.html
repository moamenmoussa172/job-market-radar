About Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all.

Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce.

Join the NGO EMEA Solutions team and help non-profit organisations (NGOs) get more done with Salesforce and AI.

The Core Solutions Engineer is the technical person on the sales team for NGO customers, mostly in the Netherlands and Belgium. You show customers how Salesforce can help them work with donors, members, volunteers and clients. You work with the Account Executive and with our other SEs, specialists and supporting teams, and you talk with both business and IT people at the customer.

Customers want to see people and AI working together on real tasks, using data they can trust, and they want to see it working quickly. You will use AI tools every day to build examples fast, show customers how you use them yourself, and spend more time with customers and less time on slides.

Key Responsibilities

  • Talk to customers: Spend time in front of customers, in person where possible. Listen first. Find out what they want to achieve, what slows them down and what has not worked for them so far.
  • Show what works: Show real examples, build demos around the customer's own examples, including AI agents, and show how quickly something useful can work. Use AI tools to prepare faster, and always check the results yourself.
  • Explain how it fits: Help business and IT teams see how a solution works and how it works with the systems they already use.
  • Work with colleagues: Work with the Account Executive and with other SEs, specialists and supporting teams to put together one clear proposal for each customer. Ask for help early, share what you know, and help the team reach its regional targets.
  • Support existing customers: Join calls with current customers and help them start using new features. Know our customer stories and use them in your conversations.
  • Pass on what NGOs need: Tell our Product teams what NGO customers ask for and what is missing. Keep demos and tips in a shared place so colleagues can reuse them.
  • Keep learning: Follow new releases and technology trends and try them out. Present at customer events and webinars when needed.

Essential Requirements

  • Experience in a Solutions Engineer, pre-sales, consulting or technical implementation role.
  • Good working knowledge of Salesforce, or a similar CRM.
  • You enjoy being in front of customers and can explain technical topics in plain language.
  • You use AI tools every day, and know where they help and where they get things wrong.
  • You keep up with technology trends and are willing to learn. You do not need to know everything on day one.
  • You are organised, can manage several customers at once, and like working with colleagues in other teams.
  • Fluency in Dutch and English. You will work with customers in the Netherlands and Belgium.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have)

  • Industry Experience: Direct experience of working for, or selling to, NGOs, charities, care organisations or the wider non-profit sector.
  • French: French is a plus. You will spend a lot of time with customers in Belgium, and many of them speak French.
  • Salesforce Skills: Knowledge of Nonprofit Cloud, Service Cloud or Agentforce, or Salesforce certifications.
  • Building and Connecting: Experience building prototypes, or connecting Salesforce with systems such as ERP, finance or planning tools.

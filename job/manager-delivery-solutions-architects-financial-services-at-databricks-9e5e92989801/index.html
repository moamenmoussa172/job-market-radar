About the job

CSQ427R268

You will build and lead a team of Delivery Solution Architects (DSAs) responsible for the post-sales success of Databricks' largest Financial Services customers. This is one of the most consequential delivery leadership roles in the company. The customers in your portfolio anchor a strategic vertical for Databricks, and your team's work directly determines how quickly Data and AI transforms how they do business.

As a DSA Manager you set the technical strategy across your portfolio, decide where to invest your team's capacity, and partner with Sales, Pre-Sales, Professional Services, and our Systems Integrator ecosystem to move use cases from kickoff to production. You are a hands-on technical sponsor and trusted advisor to a select group of customers, including their C-suite. You build the systems, operating cadences, and playbooks that make your team and the broader DSA practice sharper over time.

The DSAs you lead are senior technical talent, drawn from principal-level solution architecture, top-tier consulting, or senior engineering backgrounds. Hiring, coaching, and growing this team is the heart of the job.

The impact you will have:

  • Hire, coach, and develop a high-performing DSA team. Own career growth, calibrated performance management, and individual development plans.
  • Own capacity planning and investment forecasting across the FINS portfolio. Defend investment decisions with data, alongside sales and field leadership.
  • Partner with Sales, Pre-Sales, Professional Services, and Systems Integrator partners to accelerate use case delivery and reduce customer risk.
  • Drive measurable customer outcomes: use-case throughput, time-to-production, consumption growth, and renewal rates. Forecast each across your portfolio.
  • Bring a programmatic approach to running the business. Build the systems, operating cadences, and playbooks that scale the DSA practice.
  • Shape and promote the value proposition of the DSA role with our internal partners and our customers.

What we look for:

  • 5+ years of experience managing a customer-facing technical team (DSA, Solution Architect, Technical Account Manager, Customer Success Engineer, or equivalent).
  • Demonstrated leadership of a team of pre-sales or post-sales technical talent.
  • Track record of putting data and AI workloads into production at scale, e.g., as a delivery architect, project manager, DevOps engineer, or service or product owner.
  • Conversant with the business problems and Data and AI use cases in financial services (banking, capital markets, insurance, or asset management preferred).
  • A bias for building systems, not just running them. A bias for growing people, not just managing them.
  • Track record of overachievement against quota, Goals or similar objective targets
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or equivalent experience through work experience
  • Can travel up to 30% when needed

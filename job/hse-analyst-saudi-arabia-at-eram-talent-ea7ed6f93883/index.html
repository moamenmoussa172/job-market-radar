About the job

Eram Talent is seeking a detail-oriented HSE Analyst to join our dedicated team in Saudi Arabia. As an esteemed Talent Acquisition Consultancy specializing in the Oil & Energy sector, we are committed to connecting talented professionals with leading companies in the industry.

The HSE Analyst will be responsible for analyzing and interpreting health, safety, and environmental data, preparing reports, and supporting the implementation of HSE programs and initiatives. This role requires a strong analytical mindset, attention to detail, and a deep understanding of HSE regulations and standards.

This position is based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Occupational Health and Safety, Environmental Science, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in HSE analysis or a related role within the Oil & Energy industry.
  • Thorough knowledge of HSE regulations and compliance requirements.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret complex data.
  • Familiarity with HSE management systems and data analysis software.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for effective collaboration with various departments.
  • Certifications in HSE (e.g., NEBOSH, IOSH) are preferred.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
  • Willingness to travel to different sites as needed.
  • Collects, analyzes, and interprets data to improve workplace safety, environmental performance, and regulatory compliance.

Senior Analog/Mixed Signal IC Layout Engineer
Location: Edinburgh, Scotland or Limerick, Ireland

About Analog Devices (ADI)

Analog Devices (NASDAQ: ADI) designs and manufactures semiconductor products and solutions. We enable our customers to interpret the world around us by intelligently bridging the physical and digital worlds with unmatched technologies that sense, measure, and connect.

The Position

Analog Devices is seeking a senior analog/mixed signal IC layout engineer for its Data Center & Energy (DCE) group in Edinburgh, Scotland or Limerick, Ireland. Within DCE, we deliver high‑reliability power, connectivity, and analytics solutions for modern data centers and energy infrastructure.

The successful candidate will be working on cutting-edge new products with exposure to the full range of precision technology including ADCs, DACs, converters, references, amplifiers, and switches.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Take responsibility/ownership for layout of large-scale analog / digital sub blocks, including scheduling, floorplanning, verification and tapeout.
  • Work collaboratively with the design team and support teams (foundry, assembly, reliability, failure analysis etc.) to achieve the desired product performance and quality.

Minimum Qualifications:

The ideal candidate should have a strong knowledge of layout techniques, be self-motivated, be willing to accept responsibility, and show initiative in identifying and solving challenging layout problems.

  • Degree in Electrical/Electronic Engineering, or equivalent
  • Ideally have 3+ years of experience of full custom IC Layout specializing in analog/mixed-signal electronics
  • Experience in use of Cadence Virtuoso including proficiency in use of VXL
  • High proficiency in interpretation and debug of verification (LVS/DRC/ANTENNA etc.)
  • Expertise in standard layout practices such as layout matching, parasitic, noise, shielding, supply consideration, latch up and ESD
  • Excellent planning and organizational skills
  • Good interpersonal, communications skills and report generation
  • Ability to work well in a global team environment

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience with deep submicron technologies (FinFET)

Experience with precision analog circuits such as Amplifiers, Voltage references, Current Mirrors, etc

What you’ll do:

This is an internship position as SAP EMEA Regional President Office Support, based in Milan, with an expected duration from September 2026 to September 2027 and a minimum commitment of six months.

In this role, you’ll:

  • Work with Regional President team on day to day matters, customers meeting preparations and follow up on actions, lease with SAP Market MU offices, help to drive many urgent topics to be delivered on time working with President office customer officer, Leadership meeting agendas and follow ups, Support RP office operational tasks and deadlines. Work with a Senior leaders on drafting customers responses.
  • Help to deal with day to day multiple asks organized and delivered on time. Ensure customers meeting are well prepared and timely follows up leading customer satisfaction.

Who you are:

  • We’re looking for someone who takes initiative, perseveres, and stay curious. You like to work on meaningful innovative projects and are energized by lifelong learning.
  • University student who can work in fast pace in a sales environment and drive multiple tasks in a timely manner and can adapt to work with multi cultural team.
  • Undergraduate in science, technology or economics.
  • knows digital world and can learn SAP tools with learning agility, Should be able to basic AI tools.

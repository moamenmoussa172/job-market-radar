Role Overview:

We are looking for a Business Analyst who goes beyond documentation and actively drives solution clarity across cloud-based applications and API-driven ecosystems.

This role operates at the intersection of business, architecture, and engineering, ensuring that requirements are not only well-defined, but also aligned with solution design and delivered accurately in production.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Elicit, analyze, and document business requirements for cloud applications and API integrations.
  • Translate business needs into clear, well-structured user stories on Jira, with defined acceptance criteria that are ready for development and testing.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams on API design, integration flows, and system interactions.
  • Support solution design by understanding data flows, system dependencies, and cloud architecture fundamentals.
  • Facilitate workshops with stakeholders to align on requirements, priorities, and expected outcomes.
  • Collaborate closely with Solution Architects, development, and QA teams to validate requirements against the target architecture, ensure accurate implementation across applications and APIs, and support end-to-end testing and quality assurance.
  • Track requirements and user stories on Jira throughout the lifecycle, ensuring full traceability from design to delivery.
  • Identify risks, gaps, and dependencies early and drive proactive mitigation.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with business owners post-development, ensuring features are business-approved, production-ready, and aligned with expected outcomes.
  • Required Qualifications:
  • 2–5 years of experience as a Business Analyst in technology or digital transformation projects.
  • Proven experience working on:
  • Web & mobile applications
  • API integrations (REST, JSON, etc.)
  • Cloud-based environments (GCP & Azure)
  • Strong understanding of:
  • System integration concepts
  • Data flows and service interactions
  • Agile delivery methodologies
  • Technical & Functional Skills:
  • Hands-on experience with:
  • Jira & Confluence
  • API tools (e.g., Postman)
  • Basic SQL or data analysis
  • Ability to understand and interpret:
  • API documentation
  • System workflows and architecture diagrams
  • Strong documentation skills with clarity, structure, and precision.
  • Soft Skills:
  • Strong stakeholder communication and expectation management.
  • Ability to translate technical complexity into clear business language.
  • High ownership and accountability mindset.
  • Comfortable operating in fast-paced, high-pressure delivery environments.

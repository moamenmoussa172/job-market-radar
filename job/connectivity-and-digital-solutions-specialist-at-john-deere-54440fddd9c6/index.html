Title: Connectivity & Digital Solutions Specialist
Primary Location: New Zealand - Tokoroa
Job Grade: 7
Hiring Manager: Douglas Thomson

The Opportunity

At Waratah, we're committed to staying at the forefront of forestry technology innovation.

As our Controls Engineer - Digital Solutions, you will own and lead the digital and connectivity side of Waratah's control systems, focusing on the technologies that sit beyond direct machine control and enable smarter, more connected operations.

This role is responsible for identifying, developing, and supporting digital technologies that add value across the entire production system. Working closely with customers, internal teams, and John Deere Forestry (JDF), you'll help ensure Waratah remains a clear technology leader while contributing to the future of machine connectivity, analytics, digital twins, and autonomy.
As connectivity solutions such as 5G, JDLink, Starlink, and remote diagnostics continue to mature, this role will play a critical part in scaling digital solutions and transforming machine data into actionable customer insights.

What You Will Be Responsible For

Leading the development and support of online and offline mapping capabilities.

Managing hardware, configuration, administration, and integration into machine data systems.
Supporting and advancing connectivity solutions, including Starlink, JDBoost, and JDLink.
Growing Waratah's analytics and reporting capabilities.
Develop and enhance Waratah's customer-facing mobile applications.
Supporting digital modelling and simulation environments, including 3D head models, digital twins, and virtual testing platforms.
Coordinating Waratah's Technology Development Process (TDP) initiatives and technology roadmap activities.
Collaborating with John Deere Forestry and cross-functional teams to ensure seamless digital integration across products and platforms.
Identifying emerging technologies and digital opportunities that improve customer outcomes and operational performance.

What Will Make You Successful In the Role

Success in this role comes from your ability to bridge the gap between machine control systems and the broader digital ecosystem.

You'll bring:
Strong understanding of data flow, system integration, connectivity, and cloud-based solutions.
The ability to work independently and quickly learn new technologies.
Confidence collaborating across functions, disciplines, and international teams.
Strong analytical thinking and the ability to transform complex machine data into meaningful insights.
A practical, customer-focused mindset that turns innovative concepts into scalable solutions.
The ability to balance day-to-day delivery with longer-term technology development and strategy.

As the role evolves, you'll also help shape the future of analytics, machine connectivity, remote diagnostics, AI-enabled insights, and the data foundations that support increasing levels of automation and autonomy.

We Are Looking For Someone Who

Holds a qualification in Engineering, Computer Science, Software Development, Data Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
Has experience working with connectivity, telematics, IoT, industrial equipment, machinery, automotive, or related technology environments.
Understands software development, APIs, cloud services, and systems architecture.
Has experience integrating hardware, software, and data systems across multiple teams and platforms.
Is comfortable working with data, analytics, and digital technologies.
Is naturally curious and enjoys solving complex problems through practical innovation.
Takes ownership and follows projects through to successful deployment.
Can work autonomously while remaining highly collaborative.
Enjoys experimenting with new technologies and learning emerging tools, including AI and advanced analytics.
Is motivated by solving real customer problems and creating technology solutions that make a measurable impact.

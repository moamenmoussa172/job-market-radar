THE ROLE

As a Senior SAP ABAP Developer on Everpure's expanding Enterprise Applications team, you will architect the scalable, modern SAP S/4HANA backbone that powers our global business operations. Bridging complex enterprise requirements with clean-core architecture, you will partner closely with cross-functional business leaders, functional experts, and Application Management Services (AMS) teams to deliver high-impact extensibility and AI-driven automation. Your technical leadership will directly safeguard system stability, accelerate business workflows, and future-proof Everpure's global enterprise footprint.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Architect clean-core custom ABAP solutions, Core Data Services (CDS) views, OData services, and Fiori applications within SAP S/4HANA to deliver high-performance, upgrade-safe extensions for global business teams.
  • Lead end-to-end technical delivery across major implementation and enhancement initiatives, partnering with functional leads to translate business specifications into optimized, production-ready code.
  • Build secure, scalable integrations connecting SAP and non-SAP enterprise systems via SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), RESTful Application Programming (RAP), and modern APIs to streamline cross-platform data flow.
  • Design and deploy SAP Business AI, Joule, and autonomous agent workflows across S/4HANA, BTP, and external foundation models to automate complex enterprise processes and unlock intelligent operations.
  • Champion long-term platform resilience and operational excellence by guiding AMS teams through complex Tier-3 escalations, establishing rigorous code review standards, and maintaining comprehensive architectural documentation.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Proven mastery in architecting and delivering enterprise-grade custom solutions using SAP ABAP, Object-Oriented ABAP (OOABAP), CDS Views, and OData within an SAP S/4HANA landscape.
  • Demonstrated capability implementing modern SAP extension strategies, including Clean Core principles (developer and side-by-side extensibility), Flexible Workflow frameworks, and SAP BTP services.
  • Deep hands-on proficiency in enterprise system integration (IDOCs, RFCs, REST/Web Services) and Fiori UI engineering (SAPUI5/Fiori Elements) to create seamless, end-to-end user experiences.
  • Strong analytical and diagnostic capabilities in performance tuning, advanced debugging, and transport management to ensure peak reliability across mission-critical enterprise software.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, US office in compliance with Everpure's policies, unless you are on PTO, work travel, or other approved leave.

ABOUT THE ROLE

Most AI assistants live in an app. This one you wear. The Cosmic Jam team is building an AI assistant around a connected device people wear every day: one button, your voice, and an assistant that turns a passing thought into a finished task. You’ll own the product end to end. What the assistant does with a spoken sentence. How it organizes someone’s day. What it connects to. And how we know it’s genuinely helping. You’ll work closely with engineering, design and Codeway’s infrastructure teams. This role is for builders who obsess over the first minute. People who know what makes someone talk to a device every day. Who read model outputs as closely as dashboards. Who ship, measure and improve, fast. The best assistants feel invisible. You’ll build one.


WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Own the app end to end, from the first minute of use to a daily habit.
  • Define what the assistant does with what people say, and how its quality is measured and improved.
  • Shape the space where captures become to-dos, reminders, notes and plans.
  • Connect the assistant to the tools people already use, from their calendar to their own AI.
  • Build the membership and the premium features that make it worth paying for.
  • Track the numbers that prove it works, from daily captures and completed actions to retention and conversion.
  • Work closely with engineering, design and AI teams to ship continuously.
  • Own recording consent and privacy, together with legal.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • You’ve shipped an assistant or agent product built on language models to consumers, and improved it from real usage.
  • You’re hands-on with the model layer: you write prompts and evals, read logs and prototype with AI tools.
  • You have a feel for voice and capture, and you know what brings people back every day.
  • You’ve shipped and grown a consumer subscription app.
  • You write clearly in English and thrive in a small team with little process.

NICE TO HAVE

  • Experience with voice notes, meeting notes or dictation products.
  • Experience with task, reminder or calendar products.
  • Experience with wearables or other devices paired with an app.
  • Experience building integrations or connectors for AI assistants.

THE RECRUITING PROCESS

We are committed to keeping our recruitment process short and transparent. Here’s how it looks like:

  • Application: Send us your CV or LinkedIn profile. You can also just write a few words about yourself.
  • Talent & Culture Interview: Let’s talk about your experience & expectations and see what we can achieve together.
  • Technical Interview: You will meet with your future team lead to go over the case and your technical capabilities in detail.
  • Case Study: We might send you a task to solve.
  • Final Interview: A final interview with one of our senior leaders.
  • Welcome Aboard! You are now a part of the team.

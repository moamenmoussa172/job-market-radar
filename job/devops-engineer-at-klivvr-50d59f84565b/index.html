About the job

We’re looking for a DevOps Engineer to own and evolve our cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and reliability practices. You’ll play a key role in enabling fast, secure, and resilient product delivery while working closely with engineering, product, and security teams.

What you'll do:

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Azure)
  • Own CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
  • Improve system reliability, availability, and performance (SRE mindset)
  • Implement Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, etc.)
  • Enhance monitoring, logging, and alerting systems.
  • Ensure security, compliance, and best practices across environments.
  • Support production systems and lead incident response when needed.
  • Contribute to DevOps best practices across teams.
  • To succeed in this role, you'll need to have:
  • 3+ years of professional experience in DevOps Engineering.
  • Strong experience in cloud platforms (AWS preferred)
  • Solid background in Linux, networking, and system architecture.
  • Experience with containers and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Proficiency in CI/CD tools.
  • Hands-on experience with IaC tools.
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes is a must.
  • Familiarity with monitoring tools.
  • Strong scripting skills (Bash, Python, or similar)
  • Experience in high-availability, production environments, Fintech is a plus.

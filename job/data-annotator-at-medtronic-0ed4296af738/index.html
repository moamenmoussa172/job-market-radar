About the Position

We are looking for a Data Annotator to join our team and support a variety of data annotation projects. This role is critical to helping create high-quality datasets that enable the development of innovative AI solutions for surgical applications.

As a Data Annotator, you will review, tag, and label surgical images and videos according to detailed guidelines and instructions.

No prior data annotation experience is required. Full training and project-specific guidance will be provided. While clinical knowledge or an interest in healthcare is advantageous, it is not essential.

This role is fully remote and can be carried out from anywhere within the UK.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Annotate surgical images and videos according to established project guidelines using tools such as bounding boxes, polygons, key points and timestamps.
  • Ensure annotations are accurate, consistent, and meet quality standards.
  • Follow project specific instructions and complete tasks within agreed deadlines.
  • Review and validate annotations completed by other team members to maintain high-quality datasets.
  • Identify potential issues within datasets and escalate concerns where appropriate.
  • Provide feedback and suggestions to improve annotation processes, workflows, and guidelines.
  • Maintain a high level of concentration and attention to detail while performing repetitive tasks.
  • Support the creation of training datasets for AI algorithms used in healthcare and surgical technologies.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong observational skills and excellent attention to detail, with an interest or background in a relevant field such as Biomedical Sciences, Healthcare, Clinical Sciences, Life Sciences, Medical Imaging, Design, Photography, Visual Effects, or Engineering.
  • No previous data annotation experience required.
  • Basic computer literacy and confidence using online systems and tools.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to producing accurate work.
  • Comfortable working independently and as part of a collaborative team while maintaining high-quality results.
  • Ability to manage repetitive tasks while maintaining quality and focus.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills.
  • Willingness to adapt to change and handle multiple projects at once.
  • Reliable, proactive, and able to meet deadlines.
  • Good written communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Interest in healthcare, clinical technology, artificial intelligence, or medical innovation.
  • Basic understanding of anatomy, clinical environments, or surgical procedures.
  • Previous experience performing quality assurance, data review, or administrative tasks requiring attention to detail.
  • Experience working remotely and independently.
  • Familiarity with image or video annotation tools is beneficial but not required.

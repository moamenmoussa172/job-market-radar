About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Product Designers at Stripe are strategic partners to Engineering and Product Management. Together, we define user-facing experiences, then translate that thinking into complete products that can be tested, shipped, and refined. We’re responsible for creating powerful, well-crafted, beautiful products that users love and are eager to recommend to others.

Treasury brings business banking and payments together on Stripe. We help businesses receive, store, send, and spend their money in one place — with faster access to funds, global money movement, and cards for everyday spending. Instead of stitching together banks, payment processors, and financial tools, businesses can manage their money where they already run their payments.

Our ambition is to make managing business finances feel as intuitive and thoughtfully designed as the best consumer products. That means making sophisticated capabilities accessible, bringing clarity to complex workflows, and paying close attention to the details that help people feel confident and in control of their money.

What you’ll do

As a Product Designer on the Treasury team, you’ll help rethink an essential part of running a business. Your work could help businesses access their earnings sooner, understand their cash flow, move money across currencies and borders, or manage everyday spending — all within a connected experience on Stripe.

You’ll work across new and existing products, shaping zero-to-one experiences and refining the details of what’s already in users’ hands. You’ll partner closely with Product and Engineering to define problems, explore possibilities, and bring high-quality experiences to life. This work involves substantial ambiguity and technical and regulatory complexity; your role is to make the experience feel simple without losing the rigor that financial products demand.

Responsibilities

  • Own the end-to-end product design process, from understanding user needs and defining problems through prototyping, detailed design, launch, and iteration.
  • Design intuitive, cohesive experiences that help businesses receive, store, send, and spend money with confidence.
  • Bring clarity to ambiguous problems, using research, exploration, and prototypes to establish direction and turn early ideas into products people can use.
  • Deliver exceptional interaction and visual design, bringing a consumer-grade standard of usability, polish, and care to complex financial tools.
  • Collaborate with Product and Engineering to shape the roadmap around meaningful, measurable user outcomes.
  • Partner with Legal, Compliance, Risk, and other stakeholders to understand constraints and find thoughtful solutions that protect users without introducing unnecessary friction.
  • Work with user researchers, content designers, and other product designers to validate ideas and create a consistent experience across Treasury and the broader Stripe Dashboard.
  • Work closely with engineers through implementation, refining interactions and details so the shipped experience meets the intended quality bar.
  • Share work regularly in design reviews, explain your decisions clearly, and use feedback to strengthen your work.

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • Have 6+ years of relevant product design experience at a technology, product-driven company.
  • Have strong visual design, interaction design, and prototyping skills, demonstrated through shipped experiences with a high bar for craft and usability.
  • Can make complex products feel simple and approachable, with thoughtful information hierarchy, clear interactions, and meticulous attention to detail.
  • Are comfortable working through substantial ambiguity: defining problems, exploring alternatives, and making progress when requirements are incomplete or evolving.
  • Can engage deeply with technical and regulatory requirements, working with cross-functional partners to translate constraints into clear, trustworthy user experiences.
  • Have worked autonomously on substantial product initiatives, partnering with Product and Engineering to shape both direction and execution.
  • Have experience working with and contributing to an established design system, balancing consistency with the needs of new experiences.
  • Have experience collaborating closely with engineers to deliver high-quality interfaces.
  • Communicate your thinking clearly through prototypes, user flows, and design rationale, and actively seek feedback.
  • Can prioritize effectively across projects at different stages, balancing speed, scope, and quality.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience shaping zero-to-one products, from early exploration through launch.
  • Experience designing financial products, particularly business accounts, money management, payments, or spending tools.
  • Experience designing consumer products, or bringing a comparable standard of simplicity and craft to complex business software.
  • Experience connecting fragmented tools or workflows into a cohesive product experience.
  • Enjoy collaborating with other designers, sharing feedback, and helping one another grow.

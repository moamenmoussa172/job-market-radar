About the job

Come and build the future with us as we change the way the world sees the Cloud!
CloudWatch is the observability platform of AWS, built for developers, system operators, site reliability engineers (SRE), and IT managers. CloudWatch collects monitoring and operational data in the form of logs, metrics, and events, providing a unified view of AWS resources, applications and services that run on AWS, and on-premises servers. Customers use CloudWatch to set high resolution alarms, visualise logs and metrics side by side, take automated actions, troubleshoot issues, and discover insights to optimise their applications, and ensure they are running smoothly. The CloudWatch team in Dublin is growing and we are looking for exceptional software development engineers to create the next generation CloudWatch experience.
CloudWatch is one of the largest real time data platforms in the world, processing more than 68 quadrillion metric observations per month and growing fast! This requires teams in CloudWatch to innovate at an unprecedented scale. In CloudWatch we love giving customers what they want: high quality, highly usable, highly available services. It's humbling and energizing to work with excellent, dedicated engineers to build and deliver world-leading service monitoring solutions at AWS scale.
We're looking for a new teammate who is enthusiastic, empathetic, curious, motivated, reliable, and able to work effectively with a diverse team of peers; someone who will help us amplify the positive & inclusive team culture we've been building.
Key job responsibilities
As a Software Development Engineer for the CloudWatch organisation, you will work in an agile team to design, build and operate highly available, massively scaled systems used by millions of users worldwide. CloudWatch is central to how AWS customers observe and operate their applications and infrastructure. Our services ingest, store, analyse and act on metrics, logs, traces and events at huge scale. You will own complex distributed software end to end, from design through to operation, and hold it to the highest quality.
Key responsibilities:
Work with experienced engineers, product managers and other Amazonians across disciplines to design, build and operate innovative services that delight our customers, and help move technical discussions towards solutions.
Use GenAI and AI-powered tools to make development more productive, and build AI-powered features into CloudWatch that help customers find, understand and resolve issues faster, while keeping up with emerging technologies.
Design and develop scalable solutions using cloud-native architectures and microservices in a large distributed computing environment.
Build and maintain resilient distributed systems that are scalable, fault-tolerant and cost-effective.
Produce clean, maintainable code that follows best practices and design patterns, take part in code reviews and contribute to technical documentation. When issues arise, dive deep to find the root cause and apply fixes that stop them from happening again.
Take part in your team's operational responsibilities, including on-call, and help raise the bar on operational excellence. Mentor junior engineers and help grow the technical capability of the team.
A day in the life
On a typical day you might root-cause an issue affecting a fleet of hosts, ship a new feature, or design a solution to a performance or footprint problem you have never seen before. You translate customer and internal requirements into designs, weigh the tradeoffs, and break the work into incremental deliverables your team can ship. A strong community of senior and principal engineers gives direct feedback and pushes your thinking. You are surrounded by people who are passionate about observability and who believe there is still a lot of ground to cover in helping developers, SREs, and operators understand their systems.
About the team
CloudWatch is the observability service that AWS customers rely on to monitor, understand and operate their applications and infrastructure. Our teams build and run the systems that collect, store, analyse and act on metrics, logs, traces and events from millions of resources across every AWS Region, as well as the alarms, dashboards, insights and AI-powered features customers use to find and fix issues. Our mission is to give every AWS customer a clear, reliable view of how their systems are performing, at any scale.
- Experience (non-internship) in professional software development
- Experience designing or architecting (design patterns, reliability and scaling) of new and existing systems
- Strong programming skills in at least two of the following: Rust, Go, Java, C++, Python
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent
- Experience with full software development life cycle, including coding standards, code reviews, source control management, build processes, testing, and operations
- Open-source enthusiasm and contributions (e.g. a GitHub portfolio, or contributions to projects such as OpenTelemetry)

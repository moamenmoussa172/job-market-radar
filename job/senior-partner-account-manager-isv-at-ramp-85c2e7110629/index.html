About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the Role

Embedded partnerships put Ramp inside the software our customers already use to run their businesses. Partners host the Ramp application, manage budgets and limits in their own UI, and trigger payments inside their own workflows. Our first partners are live or launching now, and the job from here is to activate their customers at scale.

As our first Embedded Partner Account Manager, you'll own our signed partners from signature onward and carry their activation number. ISV Account Executives sign new partners and hand them to you with a 24-month activation forecast you've co-signed. From there, you run the relationship, the activation plan, and the joint go-to-market.

You'll work with our Technical Partner Manager, Partner Consulting, Growth, Sales, and CX. They own integration design, bank approvals, customer onboarding, and technical support, so your time goes to activation. Most customers will activate inside the partner's product without talking to Ramp, so you'll make that funnel convert and work the accounts that get stuck.

What You'll Do

  • Agree on the number, then own the partner - co-sign the 24-month activation forecast before signature, take the handoff from the ISV AE, and run the kickoff with our Technical Partner Manager. From there, you're the partner's day-to-day and executive contact.
  • Run the partner cadence - weekly during launch, monthly once live, and a quarterly business review on activation vs forecast. Keep one joint action plan with a Ramp owner, a partner owner and a date on every row, and escalate when dates slip.
  • Own each partner's activation plan - based on the motion (product-led, sales-led, or migration) and set funnel targets from the forecast model. Build the joint marketing calendar with Growth, from co-branded campaigns and webinars to event follow-up. Audit every place Ramp shows up in the partner's product, and get the customer lists and account mapping our campaigns need.
  • Enable both sales teams - run seller kickoffs and keep battlecards, FAQs, and pricing guidance up to date in the partner's sales hub. Give our AEs a partner demo account and the integration story. Make sure every rep on both sides knows the customer offer and their incentive.
  • Run joint selling and work the exceptions - review shared pipeline with the partner's sales team weekly, and pair Ramp and partner reps on strategic accounts. Keep one deal channel where our AEs and theirs coordinate every shared deal. Work stuck applications, approved-but-not-onboarded accounts, and priority customers, routing sales-led deals to Ramp AEs and self-serve wins to CS.
  • Report the number every week - activated customers and contribution profit vs forecast, by partner and by motion. Show where the funnel stalls between lead and activation, with the blocker and its owner. Keep attribution clean so partner credit and rev share are right.
  • Grow the partnership - own amendments, renewals, and new products inside each agreement, including rev share and attribution terms. Bring in Partner Consulting for any new build.
  • Write the playbook - turn what works with our first partners into the activation kit, enablement and launch checklist every new partner starts with. Automate repetitive work so each new partner takes less of your time.

What You Need

  • 4+ years in account management, partner management, customer success or B2B sales.
  • You've grown a book you inherited, not just closed new logos - adoption, activation or expansion against a quota.
  • Fluency with funnels and data. You can read a cohort, find where customers stall, and pull your own numbers from Salesforce and dashboards.
  • You can run a relationship with a partner's executives and their product, sales, and marketing leads simultaneously, and hold both sides to their dates.
  • Comfort with APIs and integrated products. You don't write code, but you can walk a customer's path through a partner's product and tell a product gap from a process gap.
  • Clear, structured writing. Your weekly update tells a partner exec what moved, what's stuck and what you need from them.
  • You've carried a number while building the process around it. The playbooks, dashboards and handoffs for this motion are still being written, and you'll write a lot of them.
  • You use AI every day to research accounts, build reports and draft enablement.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience in embedded finance, payments, card programs or fintech partnerships
  • Experience managing ISV, platform or channel partners
  • Experience growing a self-serve funnel inside a partner's product, such as in-product placements, marketplace listings or partner-hosted sign-up
  • Experience enabling a partner's sales team or launching a product through a partner
  • Experience negotiating amendments, rev share or partner incentives
  • Familiarity with Salesforce, Crossbeam and Gong

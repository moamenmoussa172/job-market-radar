About the job

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

The Assistance Engineering team builds world-class tools for Airbnb's global network of support agents, so guests and hosts get a great experience 24/7, 365 days a year. Airbnb's operational staff must scale with our user base, and we own the entire tech stack behind their success.

Within Assistance Engineering, Assistance Foundations builds the shared platforms that support runs on: case management, the agent workspace, quality and coaching systems, money tooling, data services, agent routing, provisioning and training, and reliability. We are forming a dedicated Machine Learning Engineering group so that intelligence is built once on this foundation and reused across support, rather than rebuilt by each team. We use machine learning to understand our complex data landscape, drive structured insights, and resolve customer issues faster and more accurately. The work is highly visible and has company-wide impact.

Some of what we are building and shipping:

  • LLM-based quality evaluation that scores support cases against the quality standard, at scale
  • AI coaching that turns quality evidence into coaching plans for team leads
  • Simulation environments that train agents on realistic support scenarios
  • Natural language search and chat tools that help agents and supervisors find answers faster

The Difference You Will Make:

As Engineering Manager, you will build and lead the founding group of ML engineers who bring machine learning and LLM-based systems to Assistance Foundations: quality, coaching, case intelligence, and decision support for agents.

  • Lead and mentor: Hire, manage, and grow a new team of machine learning engineers, setting clear expectations, coaching careers, and building a collaborative, high-performing culture.
  • Set ML strategy: Define the roadmap for ML across Assistance Foundations, balancing a shared core ML platform with close partnership to the teams you serve (case platform, quality, money, data services, routing, and agent training). Cover LLM evaluation, quality and coaching intelligence, retrieval, routing and matching, capacity forecasting, and decision support, with a focus on quality, scalability, safety, and reliability.
  • Deliver at pace: Own end-to-end delivery of ML and LLM-based systems, batch and real-time, from experimentation through production, A/B testing, and operation at scale.
  • Partner across the org: Work closely with platform, backend, and frontend engineers, data scientists, product managers, and support operations and quality teams to identify high-impact opportunities, prioritize requirements, and quantify results.
  • Decide with data: Evaluate technical trade-offs and drive decisions grounded in experiments and measurable business impact.
  • Raise the technical bar: Lead design and architecture reviews, and uphold standards for ML best practices such as rigorous offline and online evaluation, minimizing training/serving skew, feature and model selection, and incremental, test-driven delivery.

Your Expertise:

  • 8+ years of industry experience building and shipping applied machine learning, including 3+ years managing ML engineers or applied scientists, ideally building a new team or shared ML platform that serves multiple product teams
  • BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related technical field
  • Depth in Natural Language Processing and LLM-based systems, with hands-on experience taking models to production
  • Strong programming (Python, Java, or Kotlin) and data engineering skills
  • Experience with 3 or more of: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Kubernetes, Spark, Airflow (or equivalent), Kafka (or equivalent), and a data warehouse such as Hive
  • Experience building end-to-end ML infrastructure and productionizing ML models, both batch and real-time
  • Strong grasp of ML best practices: evaluation design (offline metrics, human labeling, LLM-as-judge), training/serving skew minimization, A/B testing, feature engineering, and feature and model selection
  • Familiarity with architectural patterns of large, high-scale systems: well-designed APIs, high-volume data pipelines, and efficient algorithms
  • Track record of test-driven development, incremental delivery, and safe deployment
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, and the ability to work with stakeholders at every level and navigate ambiguity

Nice to have

  • Experience with LLMs in production, including evaluation, grounding and guardrails, and alignment techniques (SFT, RLHF, DPO)
  • Experience with ranking, matching, forecasting, or optimization systems (for example search, recommendations, routing, or capacity planning)
  • Experience applying AI to customer support, quality assurance, workforce tooling, trust and safety, or fraud

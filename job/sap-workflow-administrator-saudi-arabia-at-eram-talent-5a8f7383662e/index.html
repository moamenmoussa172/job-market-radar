About the job

Eram Talent is seeking a skilled and experienced SAP Workflow Administrator tMonitor the overall health of the workflow engine to ensure processes run smoothly and within defined SLAs. Troubleshoot system bottlenecks by restarting errant workflows, reassigning orphaned items, and correcting misdirected approvals.

Manage and schedule standard workflow batch jobs (e.g., event linkage, work item rule monitoring, dead work item handling).

Configure system settings, email notifications, and event queues for workflow environments.

Manage Archiving process for some tables if necessary.

Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Experience: 3+ years of specific experience in SAP Business Workflow design and administration.
  • Proficiency in SAP Workflow Builder, Business Object Builder and Flexible Workflows.
  • Working knowledge of ABAP coding, debugging, and RICEF objects.
  • Familiarity with SAP S/4HANA, Fiori Apps, and Cloud workflow management.

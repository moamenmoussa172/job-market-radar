About the job

Job Description & Summary

About your team

Join our Risk Assurance Services (RAS) innovation and transformation team, where we help clients unlock value through AI, automation, and digital transformation. We combine consulting expertise with technology to improve document-heavy processes and develop scalable solutions.

You will join the Document Automation team and support its day-to-day operations, reporting, communication, and the continued development of Document Bot, our document automation solution built on the Legito platform. You will work with colleagues and users across different countries and gradually gain exposure to data analytics, AI, product development, testing, and project delivery.

About your manager

Monika has been part of the Document Automation team for nearly six years. She works under Martin, who leads the entire team, including Product and Business Development for both internal Czech and international PwC clients. Monika is directly involved in Product and Business Development, where the team delivers complex solutions using top-tier technologies. She is responsible for selected projects, particularly integration projects, as well as reporting, billing, and other related operational activities. You will work closely with her, and she will help you get familiar with the team’s processes, tools, and Document Bot.

Job description & Summary

As a Business, Data & Support Analyst, you will help our team turn data into clear insights, support regular reporting and billing activities, and improve recurring processes through AI and automation.

This is not a purely administrative position. We are looking for someone who enjoys working with data, thinks systematically, notices inconsistencies, and can prepare clear and structured outputs. You will also learn how Document Bot works and gradually become involved in its testing, documentation, user support, and selected project activities.

You will:

  • Prepare and regularly update reports, data summaries, trackers, charts, and simple dashboards.
  • Process and validate data related to projects, working hours, costs, rates, billing, and team capacity.
  • Use AI tools and agents to support reporting, data checks, content preparation, and other recurring activities, while critically reviewing the accuracy and completeness of their outputs.
  • Prepare billing inputs, check recorded hours against projects and applicable rates, and maintain billing and invoice trackers.
  • Create clear tables, reports, written summaries, and initial presentation drafts for internal and client meetings.
  • Prepare regular system updates and other internal or client communications in Czech and English.
  • Gradually learn how Document Bot and the underlying Legito platform work, including key user scenarios and document automation processes.
  • Help maintain user guides, training materials, and internal documentation and, once sufficiently experienced, provide basic support to Document Bot users.

What matters to us

  • Analytical and logical thinking, strong attention to detail, and good Excel skills. You enjoy working with data, identifying inconsistencies, and turning information into clear, structured outputs.
  • A proactive and organised approach, curiosity about AI and automation, and a willingness to learn new technologies, understand business processes, and take responsibility for your work.
  • Good communication and presentation skills, with the ability to prepare professional written materials in Czech and English.

What will help you stand out

  • Experience with reporting, data analysis, project support, billing, or financial reporting.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Copilot, or other AI and automation tools.
  • Experience with application testing, requirements management, documentation, or user support.
  • Experience working with an external technology vendor.
  • Interest in document automation, product development, technology, or project management.
  • Experience with Microsoft Teams or SharePoint.

  • Support throughout your learning and development journey, including training in business, communication, presentation, data, AI, Power BI, and other digital skills.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.

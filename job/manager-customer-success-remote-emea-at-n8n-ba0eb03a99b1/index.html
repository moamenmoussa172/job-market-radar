About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and 11 more countries.

The AI orchestration of your wildest imagination.

n8n is the open workflow orchestration platform built for the new era of AI. We give technical teams the freedom of code with the speed of no-code, so they can automate faster, smarter, and without limits. Backed by a fiercely inventive community and 500+ builder-approved integrations, we’re changing the way people bring systems together and scale ideas for impact.

Since our founding in 2019, we’ve grown into a diverse team of over 260 - working across Europe and the US, connected by a shared builder spirit and with our centre of gravity in Berlin. Along the way, we’ve:

  • Cultivated a community of more than 650,000 active developers and builders
  • Earned 190K+ GitHub stars, making us one of the world’s Top 40 most popular projects
  • Backed by top investors, from Sequoia’s first German seed to our SAP's recent strategic investment - bringing us to a $5.2bn valuation

That’s the company we’ve built. Now we’d love to see what you can build. If you’re applying, try n8n out - whether you’re technical or not - and share a screenshot of your first workflow with us. The easiest place to start is here: app.n8n.cloud/register.

We’re in a defining moment of an incredible journey. Come and build with us.

🎯 Your main goal will be to lead and grow our EMEA Customer Success team, helping n8n's most strategic enterprise customers drive adoption, retention, and expansion while scaling the team and operating model for our next stage of growth.

You'll start with a team of 4 CSMs, which will grow in line with our 2027 hiring plans. To make that happen, you'll combine hands-on customer leadership, deliberate people development, and strong operational execution:

LEAD OUR MOST STRATEGIC ENTERPRISE ACCOUNTS IN EMEA

  • Partner with CSMs to navigate complex customer challenges and develop strategies for the region's largest and highest-risk enterprise accounts.
  • Build trusted executive relationships with key customers and step directly into customer conversations when additional leadership is needed.
  • Stay hands-on with strategic accounts while the team scales, including personally owning customer relationships where needed.

BUILD AND DEVELOP A HIGH-PERFORMING EMEA TEAM

  • Hire and onboard the additional CSMs needed as the EMEA team grows, building on the strong team already in place.
  • Coach and develop your team through structured 1:1s, clear development plans, and deliberate skill-building.
  • Create a high-accountability, supportive environment where people receive clear feedback and can continuously raise the bar.
  • Lead a distributed team across multiple countries and time zones.

DRIVE OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE

  • Run a consistent operating cadence across forecasting, account health, and structured risk reviews.
  • Build repeatable Customer Success motions across onboarding, QBRs, adoption, retention, and expansion.
  • Use customer and team insights to identify risks early, raise issues proactively, and continuously improve how the team operates.

SHAPE THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY WITH CROSS-FUNCTIONAL PARTNERS

  • Partner closely with Sales, Product, Support, TAMs, Renewals, and GTM leaders to deliver a strong end-to-end customer experience.
  • Collaborate with Customer Success leaders across the US, DACH, and Scaled CS to evolve shared playbooks and ways of working.
  • Contribute to global CS initiatives and help build scalable methodologies as n8n's Customer Success organization continues to grow.

REQUIREMENTS

Must-haves

  • 👥 People leadership: You have 2–3+ years of people management experience, with a strong track record of hiring, developing, and retaining Customer Success talent.
  • 💼 7-10 years of experience in Customer Success, Professional Services, Account Management, or other customer-facing roles, including supporting large, complex, high-risk enterprise customers.
  • 💻 Tech industry experience and relevant Enterprise subject-matter expertise: You’ve worked in an enterprise IT or technology environment and understand the needs and expectations of technical buyers and customers.
  • 📊 Operational discipline: You’re comfortable running forecasts, account-health cadences, and structured risk reviews to keep a complex book of business on track.
  • 🛠️ Builder mindset: You thrive in environments where not everything is already defined and are willing to roll up your sleeves to solve problems directly.
  • 🤝 Low-ego collaboration: You enjoy working within a shared direction, partnering closely with peers, and improving existing processes.

Nice-to-haves

  • 🚀 High-growth leadership trajectory: You've progressed from a senior CSM role into first-line leadership and have demonstrated strong potential to take on increasing scope.
  • 🤖 Automation and AI familiarity: You're familiar with n8n or the broader automation and AI tooling ecosystem.
  • 🏗️ Leadership across environments: You've held Customer Success leadership responsibilities across more than one company and have seen different approaches to scaling teams.

Why join us?

At n8n, you'll have the opportunity to own and shape a new leadership layer in our EMEA Customer Success organization. Our EMEA CS team has grown from 1 to 9 people this year, and you'll lead it through its next stage of growth, working with some of our largest and most technically sophisticated enterprise customers. You'll have real influence over how we evolve our customer journey, operating model, and CS playbooks as the company scales rapidly, without having to start from zero.

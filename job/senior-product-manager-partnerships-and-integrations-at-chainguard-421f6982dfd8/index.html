About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Chainguard is the trusted source for open source. By delivering hardened, secure, and production-ready builds of all the open source software engineers and AI agents rely on, Chainguard helps organizations build faster, stay compliant, and eliminate risk.
Our customers include Fortune 500 enterprises and global industry leaders, including Anduril, Canva, Fortinet, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, OpenAI, Snap Inc., and Snowflake.
Chainguard is venture-backed by leading investors, including Amplify, IVP, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Mantis VC, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Spark Capital.

The role in a nutshell:

Chainguard is building the most trusted source for open source software. To be that, we have to meet customers and developers where they already work: inside their SDLC, their existing tool stack, and increasingly their agent workflows.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the product strategy and execution for Chainguard's partnerships and integrations. You'll make sure someone can pick up Chainguard and start running within their toolchain on day one, and that Chainguard becomes a keystone in how they build, secure, and deliver software. This is a foundational, high-impact role at the intersection of product, partnerships, and go-to-market. You'll build bridges with the hyperscalers, developer tools, CI/CD systems, registries, security platforms, and AI agent ecosystems our customers depend on. You'll tie every integration to business impact, move with urgency, and lead largely through influence across internal teams and external partners.

What you’ll do:

  • Integration roadmap. Own the vision and roadmap for how Chainguard plugs into the customer's software delivery lifecycle, from source and build to registry, deploy, and runtime. Define the problem spaces where integrations unlock the most value, and map every integration to clear business impact: adoption, activation, pipeline, retention, and expansion
  • Meeting customers where they are. Make Chainguard seamless to adopt inside the tools developers and platform teams already use, including CI/CD systems, artifact registries, package managers, IDEs, security scanners, and cloud marketplaces. Reduce the time from "first look" to "running in production" by removing friction at every point where Chainguard touches an existing workflow
  • Agent & AI workflow integrations. Define how Chainguard shows up in AI-assisted and agentic development workflows, so agents can discover, pull, and use trusted Chainguard artifacts by default. Stay ahead of how these ecosystems evolve and position Chainguard as a natural building block within them
  • Partner product engagements. Partner with hyperscalers, developer tool vendors, and technology partners to scope, build, and launch joint integrations. Define clear rules of engagement for each partnership that serve the customer use case today while protecting Chainguard's future product plays. Seek win-win arrangements that create durable value for both sides
  • Sales & partner team alignment. Work hand in hand with Sales, Partnerships, Solutions Engineering, and Marketing to prioritize integrations that unblock deals and accelerate adoption. Equip field and partner teams with the positioning, enablement, and feedback loops they need to land and expand with integrations
  • Cross-functional leadership. Work closely with Engineering, Design, GTM, and other Product leaders to turn customer, partner, and business needs into a focused roadmap. Make fast, well-reasoned decisions, drive alignment through influence, and clearly convey the impact of the work to internal and external stakeholders

What we're looking for:

  • 5+ years of Product Management experience, ideally with developer-facing, platform, infrastructure, or enterprise products
  • Experience owning integrations or partner-built product experiences from problem definition through launch and measurable business impact
  • Experience working closely with Sales and Partner teams to prioritize, launch, and drive adoption of integrations
  • Experience defining rules of engagement with partners on product integrations, balancing customer use cases without eroding future product plays
  • Experience working with hyperscalers (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure) and developer tool ecosystems
  • Strong sense of urgency and decisive judgment, with the ability to make sound tradeoffs quickly and with incomplete information
  • A win-win mindset in negotiations, with a track record of building partnerships that create value for both sides
  • Enjoys working in a high-impact, and collaborative role spanning internal teams and external parties

Bonus points:

  • Experience with developer tools, infrastructure, supply chain security, or other highly technical products
  • Experience launching products through cloud marketplaces or co-sell motions with hyperscalers
  • Background in technology partnerships, business development, or solutions engineering alongside product management
  • Experience with B2B products that serve both individual developers and large enterprise customers
  • Experience building integration platforms, APIs, or SDKs that partners and customers build on

About the job

Work with HelloFresh in Warsaw and its HelloTech organisation, HelloFresh’s global technology backbone with more than 1000 people, building the digital products that power our end-to-end food experience. From meal kits and ready-to-eat meals to specialty offerings like pet food and premium meat & seafood, HelloTech creates the platforms that bring tailored food solutions to millions of customers every month.

Our subscription-based, direct-to-consumer model relies on technology at every step, from customer-facing apps and personalization logic to pricing, forecasting, supply chain optimization, and initiatives that help reduce food waste. While our brands operate independently to serve distinct customer needs, they are united by shared platforms, data, and operational excellence built by HelloTech.

HelloTech works in autonomous, cross-functional alliances, each owning a specific product or domain end to end. By working with our Warsaw office, you will help shape scalable, data-driven products used across our markets, working with a modern tech stack and international teams to continuously improve how people discover, order, and enjoy HelloFresh’s products, today and in the future.

About the role: What's in the Box

Our mission is pricing and plans that flex with the customer, not against them. The squad directly impacts revenue across web, iOS, and Android—owning the surfaces our global brands depend on: weekly menu pricing, plan building, bundles, and the cancellation flow. When pricing logic or an experiment misbehaves across markets, we find and fix it.

Our React Native codebase is shared across the engineering organization, with our squad owning the pricing and plan surfaces and the underlying backend services. Sitting inside the Consumer Mega Alliance, we build scalable logic across multiple brands (HelloFresh, EveryPlate, GreenChef, Factor, and more), currencies, and pricing models.

This is a mobile-first, senior role: most of your time will be spent in our shared React Native codebase shipping to iOS and Android. We are an AI-first engineering team looking for a senior engineer who can own problems end to end, elevate our mobile architecture, and set the standard for testing and observability.

At HelloTech, flexibility and cross-functional collaboration are core to how we work. While this role is aligned to a specific Alliance, strong candidates may also be considered for opportunities across different teams or projects.

What you’ll do: The Recipe

  • End-to-End Ownership: Lead feature delivery from technical design and implementation to phased rollout behind feature flags and post-launch measurement.
  • Mobile Architecture: Build and refine high-performance React Native surfaces across brands, focusing on modularity, offline resilience, and cross-market price correctness.
  • Full-Stack Fluency: Primarily work with React Native and TypeScript, while comfortably navigating our GraphQL APIs and backend pricing data when needed.
  • Quality & Observability: Maintain high test coverage (unit, integration, and Maestro E2E) and establish proactive observability (metrics, traces, alerts) for critical revenue paths.
  • Mentorship & Experimentation: Run and analyze A/B experiments, unblock mid-level engineers through thoughtful reviews, and share mobile patterns across chapters.
  • AI-First Delivery: Leverage AI tools across the entire engineering lifecycle—from scoping and design to coding and test generation—while exercising strong critical judgment on generated code.

What you’ll bring: The Ingredients

  • Core Experience: Senior-level software engineering experience, including 2–3+ years of production React Native alongside TypeScript and modern React.
  • Architecture & Systems: Proven ability to manage complex state, modular architectures, and API integrations (GraphQL/REST) in large-scale shared codebases.
  • Testing & Release Rigor: Hands-on experience with unit, integration, and E2E testing (e.g., Jest, React Native Testing Library, Maestro), paired with solid CI/CD release hygiene.
  • AI-Assisted Workflow: Proven track record of using AI tools to accelerate delivery, with clear insight into validation, error correction, and trade-offs.
  • Autonomy & Collaboration: Strong cross-functional partnership with product, design, and analytics, with the ability to drive technical decisions independently.
  • Bonus Points: Experience in subscription/pricing systems, mobile performance profiling, experimentation platforms, or observability tools (Honeycomb, OpenTelemetry).

Above all, we are looking for individuals who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at global scale, supporting 6+ millions of customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to workspace at Warsaw Centre Point. The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

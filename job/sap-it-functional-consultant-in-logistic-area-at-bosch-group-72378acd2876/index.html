SAP IT Functional Consultant in Logistic Area

Bosch Group · Remote

Fully remote · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 29 October 2026 · Visa / relocation

Agile / ScrumSAP S/4HANA

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Responsible for material flow processes with focus on Logistics Execution, especially for the modules/capabilities:
  • Logistics Execution (LE) within our S/4 HANA transformation project with focus on Outbound/Inbound Processes
  • Requirement clarification with process Experts, functional analysis and creation of functional requirement specifications.
  • Ensure that technical IT requirements match business requirements in close cooperation with IT Product Owner and Lead Developer.
  • Design of technical solutions and creation of technical specifications for development.
  • Coordination of necessary developments with the Developer Team.
  • Reads, understands and applies the procedures from area of responsibility and other interdepartmental procedures, applicable within the company;
  • Fulfils any other tasks related to area of responsibility, as requested by direct superior.
  • Language: English
  • University degree in Business Administration, Computer Science or related scientific or technical fields.
  • Strong experience as a Functional Consultant or similar role.
  • Senior project experience in configuring the SAP system by customizing (mandatory requirement)
  • Long term experience in SAP supply chain projects with excellent skills in SAP Sales and Distribution (SD) with focus on Logistics Execution (LE outbound, inbound).
  • Experience in Integration of Logistics Execution Module with Warehouse Systems (SAP EWM, SAP WM).
  • Ability to analyze complex use cases in SAP systems by e.g., debugging or tracing.
  • Customer oriented Supply Chain Consulting experience.
  • Knowledge of industry business models and related supply chain processes.
  • Know-how and practical experience with Agile methods.
  • Deep expertise in SAP S/4HANA core modules with a strong architectural understanding of logistics and supply chain flows.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex logistics requirements into technical specifications.
  • Effective time management and strong interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to convey technical information to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Willingness to learn new things, be open to challenges, and thrive on success.
  • Readiness to travel internationally (EU).
  • Experience in Advanced Shipping and Receiving (ASR) would be an asset.
  • Experience in EWM / SAP Transportation management integration scenarios

About the job

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive.

Lyft connects people to transportation to change the way we live and get around our communities. We are looking for Technical Program Managers who bring exceptional technical and analytical skills to drive execution. Come be part of a team at Lyft focused on enabling and empowering engineering teams to deliver at scale.

Technical Program Managers at Lyft drive cross-functional initiatives, leveraging strong leadership, planning, communication, and collaboration skills. Our TPMs are technically strong with deep domain expertise. They are problem solvers who make things happen around them by setting clear goals and inspiring teams to deliver. TPMs at Lyft are both strategic and tactical and do what it takes to successfully deliver key programs that have a material impact on the business.

The TPM in this role will be supporting the Corporate Engineering & IT organization, with a deep focus on sales and financial systems infrastructure and integrations across advertising and B2B organizations. You will collaborate with Finance, Sales, Legal, Engineering, Product, Data Science, Analytics, and Operations on technology programs that help to support our internal Financial priorities to deliver on Lyft company objectives.

Responsibilities:

  • Lead iterative delivery of strategic cross-functional initiatives from concept to ship, through focus, transparency, communication, visibility, and accountability
  • Leverage deep Quote-to-Cash and Financial systems expertise to build comprehensive plans, to identify risks, and to ensure smooth project launches with a goal of meeting our Financial needs
  • Work with stakeholders in Finance, Legal, Sales, and Operations to design and optimize systems and processes to accelerate deal approval and processing for billing and revenue recognition
  • Work cross functionally to develop processes & policies, tools, training and change management
  • Identify and resolve systemic issues impacting our Lyft Financial solutions and aggressively take action to resolve

Experience:

  • At least seven years of Quote-to-Cash and Financial systems experience.
  • Thorough understanding of system architecture, contribute valuable insights to technical discussions and assist various teams (Accounting, BIS, Sales Ops, AR Ops) in finding optimal solutions that cater to defined requirements.
  • Experience with Zuora, Salesforce CRM, Oracle, and, Hubspot
  • Experience leading cross-functional teams to deliver complex projects iteratively with multiple dependencies and constraints, in a highly dynamic and agile environment
  • Proven ability to operate effectively and autonomously across multiple teams in situations of extreme ambiguity, with only high-level direction
  • Have experience designing and implementing program governance frameworks in fast-paced, high-growth environments with complex revenue models and multi-system architectures including roadmaps, release plans, project plans with a thorough understanding of dependency management
  • Able to translate highly technical problems and solutions to all levels from engineer to partner to C-level executives
  • Able to influence, negotiate and inspire others in a matrixed environment. Excellent organization, planning skills, and attention to detail
  • Experience compiling and presenting business & technology options, backed by data & experimentation, to decision makers
  • Demonstrate strong problem-solving abilities in managing Finance and Sales domain challenges and can navigate complex integrations while maintaining focus on delivery excellence and business outcomes

  • Mental health benefits

The policy allows team members to take off as much time as they need (with manager approval). Hourly team members get 15 days paid time off, with an additional day for each year of service
  • Lyft is proud to support new parents with 18 weeks of paid time off, designed as a top-up plan to complement provincial programs. Biological, adoptive, and foster parents are all eligible.
  • Subsidized commuter benefits and Lyft ride credits

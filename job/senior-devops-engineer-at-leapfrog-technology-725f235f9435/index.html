About the job

About Leapfrog

About the role

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer to join our growing team and take ownership of the design, implementation, and smooth operation of our infrastructure. This role requires deep technical expertise in cloud computing, automation, and infrastructure management, along with a proactive mindset to drive operational excellence and mentor junior engineers. You will be responsible for maintaining reliable, scalable, and secure production environments, with a strong focus on AWS cloud infrastructure. You'll also lead infrastructure automation, contribute to architectural decisions, and ensure our systems run efficiently and securely.

Job Description

  • Ensure smooth operation, accounting, and reliability of the overall infrastructure.
  • Deploy, monitor, and maintain production environments and cloud-native solutions.
  • Design, plan, and manage highly available and secure AWS cloud infrastructure.
  • Identify and implement automation opportunities in deployment, infrastructure provisioning, and system management.
  • Oversee infrastructure monitoring, scaling, and cost optimization using industry best practices.
  • Design, configure, and monitor secure and scalable network infrastructure.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve issues in Linux-based remote servers and local workstations (Linux & Windows).
  • Perform in-depth analysis of security incidents and anomalies using logs, alerts, and monitoring tools.
  • Automate infrastructure provisioning and resource scaling for optimal efficiency.
  • Maintain and manage application, web, and database servers including Apache, Nginx, MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQL Server.
  • Support the deployment, operation, monitoring, and scaling of AI/Generative AI workloads, and identify opportunities to leverage AI for infrastructure automation, troubleshooting, observability, and engineering productivity.
  • Mentor junior DevOps engineers, fostering a collaborative and growth-oriented team culture.
  • Drive adoption of organizational processes, DevOps standards, and best practices.
  • Contribute to technical and architectural decisions related to cloud infrastructure, automation, security, reliability, and AI-enabled platforms.
  • Stay up to date with evolving technologies and trends in cloud, DevOps, AI/ML, Generative AI, and infrastructure engineering.
  • Minimum of 4 years of experience as a DevOps Engineer or in a similar role.
  • Strong hands-on experience with AWS (preferred), Azure, or GCP.
  • AWS Certifications (e.g., Solutions Architect Associate / Professional, DevOps Engineer Professional) are a strong plus.
  • Proficiency with Infrastructure as Code (IaC), preferably Terraform.
  • Strong knowledge of containerization using Docker and orchestration using Kubernetes.
  • Experience with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, Travis CI, Jenkins, etc.
  • Competent in configuring and managing databases like MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQL Server.
  • Experience managing application and web servers such as Apache and Nginx.
  • Solid Linux administration skills (Ubuntu, CentOS, Amazon Linux), including installation, package management, user and process control, and system monitoring.
  • Proficient in scripting and automation using Bash, Python, Go, or similar languages.
  • Understanding of networking concepts including TCP/UDP, DNS, DHCP, routing protocols, load balancing, HTTP/HTTPS, and network security.
  • Strong grasp of security principles and familiarity with web-based security tools and methodologies.
  • Experience with source control systems such as Git.
  • Hands-on experience with observability and monitoring tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, Nagios, Datadog, New Relic, or equivalent
  • Working knowledge of Generative AI concepts and architectures, including LLMs, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, AI agents, inference, and model serving.
  • Ability to deploy, operate, monitor, and scale AI/GenAI workloads using cloud-native technologies, with an understanding of compute/GPU requirements, latency, reliability, observability, and cost optimization.
  • Experience using AI-assisted development and DevOps tools to improve engineering productivity, automation, troubleshooting, incident analysis, documentation, and infrastructure management.
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills; able to present ideas clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Strong mentorship mindset with the ability to guide junior engineers and foster team growth.
  • Self-driven and highly motivated individual capable of working independently and taking ownership of initiatives.

Skills

  • AWS, Terraform, Docker, CI/CD, Linux, Jenkins, Kubernetes, Github Action, Logging And Monitoring, Prometheus, Grafana, Bash, Python

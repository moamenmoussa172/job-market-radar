About this Role

We are seeking a Finance Analytics & Insights Specialist to support the transformation of Finance Operations through advanced reporting, analytics and data-driven decision-making. The role focuses on translating business requirements into scalable reporting solutions, shaping reporting strategy, identifying opportunities for operational improvement, and delivering innovative analytics capabilities that enhance business performance and customer experience. The successful candidate will work closely with business stakeholders, technology teams and delivery partners to develop reporting solutions, generate actionable insights and drive adoption of data-led decision making across Finance Operations.

What you’ll do

  • Contribute to the delivery and continuous improvement of the Finance Insights & Reporting strategy.
  • Partner with business stakeholders to define reporting requirements, scope analytics solutions and recommend effective delivery approaches.
  • Work closely with technology, process and delivery teams to design and implement high-quality reporting and analytics solutions.
  • Provide expert guidance on complex reporting requirements, solution design and analytics opportunities.
  • Identify reporting gaps and opportunities where insights can improve efficiency, effectiveness and business performance.
  • Translate business requirements into data models, user stories and reporting frameworks.
  • Develop prototypes and rapid insight solutions to support urgent management decisions and scalable reporting capabilities.
  • Collaborate within agile teams to create innovative reporting products and strengthen data-driven decision-making across Finance Operations.

Who you are

  • You have 5-8+ years of relevant experience in finance reporting, analytics, solution design or business intelligence environments.
  • You possess strong expertise in data, analytics, reporting and the translation of business requirements into practical solutions.
  • You can convert complex reporting needs into user stories, requirements and scalable reporting solutions.
  • You have experience in at least two of the following areas:
  • Data transformation using tools such as Alteryx, SQL or similar platforms.
  • Cloud-based analytics environments such as Google Cloud Platform or Google BigQuery.
  • Data visualisation tools including Qlik, Tableau, Looker or similar solutions.
  • SAP BW, SAP ECC reporting or broader business intelligence technologies.
  • You are a qualified accountant or data specialist with experience designing and utilising reporting and insights solutions.
  • You have a strong understanding of finance processes, with SAP knowledge considered advantageous.
  • You demonstrate excellent stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills across different organisational levels.
  • You are comfortable solving complex challenges, working independently and driving continuous process improvement.

What's in it for you

  • Opportunity to influence the future reporting and analytics strategy within Finance Operations.
  • Exposure to global stakeholders across central teams and local markets.
  • Experience working with modern analytics, reporting and cloud-based data technologies.
  • Opportunity to drive business transformation through data-led innovation and insight generation.
  • Visibility with senior decision-makers and the chance to contribute to strategic business initiatives.

What skills you will learn

  • Enterprise reporting strategy and analytics leadership.
  • Advanced data modelling, data transformation and business intelligence techniques.
  • Cloud-based analytics and visualisation solutions.
  • Agile product delivery and stakeholder engagement in complex international organisations.
  • Strategic problem-solving, process improvement and data-driven innovation.

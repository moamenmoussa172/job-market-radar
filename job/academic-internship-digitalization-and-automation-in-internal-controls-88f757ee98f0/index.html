About the job

Your contribution to something big:

  • Support the development of automation solutions for Internal Control System (ICS) processes, including automated controls and monitoring activities.
  • Design and develop Python-based scripts, applications, bots and other automation solutions, using technologies such as SQL and Power BI.
  • Develop AI assistants or agents for selected ICS processes.
  • Work with structured and unstructured data, supporting data extraction, transformation, validation and analysis.
  • Develop digital control concepts and prototypes, from initial idea and proof of concept through testing and implementation.
  • Collaborate with ICS experts to translate business and control requirements into effective technical solutions.
  • Support the testing, documentation, maintenance and continuous improvement of developed solutions.
  • Contribute to the development of the digital and AI roadmap for Internal Controls.

What distinguishes you:

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a comparable technical field.
  • Strong programming skills, preferably in Python, with practical scripting experience and an understanding of clean, structured and maintainable code.
  • Good knowledge of databases and data processing.
  • Experience or strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and AI agents.
  • Knowledge of data analytics and visualisation tools, such as Power BI, is an advantage.
  • Familiarity with APIs, Git/version control, JSON, REST services, automation frameworks, cloud environments or Microsoft Power Platform is an advantage.
  • Strong analytical and logical thinking, with a hands-on, developer and problem-solving mindset.
  • Ability to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Independent, structured and proactive working style, with curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies and business processes.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills in an international environment.
  • Good written and spoken English; German is an advantage.
  • Previous experience in ICS, audit or governance is not required.

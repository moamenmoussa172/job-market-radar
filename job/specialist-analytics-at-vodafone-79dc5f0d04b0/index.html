Role Purpose/Business Unit:

To deliver accurate, timely and repeatable commercial analytics that enable the CBU Analytics function to understand business performance, diagnose material variances and support evidence-based commercial decisions.

The role executes defined analytical work across customer, product, channel, region, segment and network dimensions. It develops and maintains models, dashboards, scorecards and analytical outputs within approved methodologies, KPI definitions and governance standards.

The role is accountable for specialist analysis and recommendations within an assigned portfolio. It does not set the functional analytics strategy, own pricing governance or lead enterprise-wide analytical priorities.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Commercial performance analytics: Execute recurring and ad hoc analysis across revenue, ARPU, margin, gross additions, quality gross additions, churn, retentions, migrations, mix and customer value.
  • Performance decomposition and diagnostics: Identify the customer, product, channel, regional and segment drivers of material performance movements and provide evidence-based root-cause analysis.
  • Analytical modelling: Build and maintain analytical models, scorecards and reusable frameworks using approved definitions, data sources and methodologies.
  • Trend and scenario analysis: Undertake trend, cohort, segmentation, sensitivity and scenario analysis for defined commercial questions and initiatives.
  • Forecasting and planning support: Maintain analytical models and assumptions that support target setting, budgeting, reforecasting and initiative evaluation.
  • Pre- and post-implementation measurement: Measure assigned commercial initiatives against agreed baselines and quantify realised customer, revenue or margin outcomes.
  • Dashboard and reporting delivery: Develop and maintain fit-for-purpose dashboards, scorecards and performance reports within agreed service levels.
  • Data validation and controls: Perform reconciliation and reasonability checks, investigate anomalies and escalate material data-quality concerns.
  • Big Data and Technology interface: Clarify data and analytical requirements for assigned use cases and validate that outputs meet agreed commercial needs.
  • Analytical governance: Document calculation logic, assumptions, data lineage and methods to support repeatability, governance and auditability.
  • Automation and continuous improvement: Improve analytical efficiency through controlled automation, reusable templates and fit-for-purpose BI solutions.
  • Stakeholder support: Translate complex analysis into concise findings and recommendations for review by the Analytics Lead and use in commercial performance forums.

The ideal candidate for this role will have:

  • 3-year degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Econometrics, Data Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Commerce, Finance or a related discipline.
  • Postgraduate qualification or relevant analytics certification is advantageous.
  • Hands-on capability in SQL, advanced Excel and BI visualisation tools.
  • Practical knowledge of data validation, KPI design, analytical modelling and performance reporting.

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Minimum 3 to 5 years relevant experience in commercial analytics, business intelligence, revenue management, finance, data analytics or a related field.
  • Strong understanding of commercial performance measures and the ability to connect analytical findings to business outcomes.
  • Demonstrated experience in performance diagnostics, modelling, dashboarding and data visualisation.
  • Proficiency in SQL and advanced Excel, with practical experience in Power BI or an equivalent visualisation platform.
  • Working knowledge of statistical analysis and analytical programming in Python or R is advantageous.
  • Experience in telecommunications or another high-volume consumer environment is advantageous.
  • Ability to communicate analytical findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • High attention to detail, structured problem-solving, sound judgement and delivery discipline.
  • Ability to work independently within defined frameworks and escalate material issues appropriately.

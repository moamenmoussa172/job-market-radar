About the job

P-1344

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

The Runtime Observability team builds and maintains the telemetry, data pipelines, and debugging tools that provide insight into the behavior of the Databricks Spark Engine. The Spark Engine runs millions of workloads scheduled daily by Databricks customers and is a highly critical component of the Databricks product. The team's mission is to provide reliable, fresh, product-centric runtime insights that enable faster detection of regressions, troubleshooting of failures and performance issues, and data-driven product decisions.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Design and implement highly critical monitoring infrastructure that provides visibility into the Databricks Spark Engine, supporting millions of daily workloads.
  • Build systems that scale globally with high performance and reliability, balancing performance, scale, and reliability requirements for infrastructure underpinning the Databricks Engine.
  • Develop a monitoring and debugging platform used by engineers across Databricks to monitor and troubleshoot production issues across major products.
  • Own complex technical projects end-to-end, from design through delivery and operational excellence.
  • Drive technical decisions and influence architectural direction within the team and across related product areas.
  • Mentor engineers and elevate team engineering practices through code reviews, design feedback, and technical leadership.

What We Look For

  • 8+ years of software engineering experience.
  • Strong backend development experience with one or more programming languages including Java, Scala, Python, or Go.
  • Experience designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems in production environments.
  • Experience building and operating critical systems, including on-call responsibilities for production services.
  • Deep understanding of system reliability, performance optimization, and operational excellence at scale.
  • Ability to lead complex technical projects involving multiple teams or system boundaries.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to influence technical direction through clear articulation of tradeoffs and design decisions.
  • B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

About Databricks

Databricks is the Data and AI company. More than 20,000 organizations worldwide — including adidas, AT&T, Bayer, Block, Mastercard, Rivian, Unilever, and 70% of the Fortune 500 — rely on the Databricks Data + AI Platform to build and scale data and AI apps, analytics and agents. Headquartered in San Francisco with 30+ offices around the globe, Databricks offers a unified platform that includes Genie, Lakebase, Agent Bricks, Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Unity Catalog.

About the job

Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

You are joining the Base Transceiver Station (BTS) software engineering team within Dimetra Software Engineering department. We use agile frameworks and handle a dense roadmap of new features with a full product lifecycle responsibility.


Job Description

We offer our Dimetra technology for a variety of clients, mostly within governmental but also commercial space (for example you will develop systems used by different emergency services across Europe).


Basic Requirements

  • Communicative english (B1+)
  • Analytical skills and willingness to learn.
  • Commercial experience with a proven track record in developing software using the C/C++ 11/14/17
  • Basic knowledge of networking protocols (TCP, UDP)
  • Basic knowledge of object-oriented design techniques and best practices, coding principles and multi-threaded development.
  • Availability 32 hours per week (minimum)
  • 15 months Contract (UoP)
  • Availability to work 2 days per week in our office in Kraków (Czerwone Maki 82)

Optional Experience:

  • Python, Powershell or bash knowledge is an advantage
  • Knowledge of unit testing and TDD

In return for your commitment, we will support you with development every step of the way. You will be learning from experts and face a steep learning curve from day one. Also, we trust you will you work hard to success on the job and we can offer:

  • Contract of Employment
  • Private medical & dental coverage.
  • Life insurance.
  • Employee Stock Plan.
  • IP Tax Relief.
  • Possibility to participate in Hackathons and Alpha Projects.
  • Strong team-oriented culture.
  • Comfortable work conditions (high class offices, parking space).
  • Volleyball field and grill place next to the office.
  • Lots of sport activities such as Moto football league,
  • Wakeboarding, Snowboarding, e-gaming league etc.
  • Training and broad development opportunities.

About the job

About the opportunity

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager for our Investments team to take our Wealth products to the next level. Our Investments team is dedicated to empowering retail investors by building accessible, transparent, and innovative investment products. We believe that investing should be intuitive, fair, and aligned with long-term financial success.

As a leader within our Product team, you’ll drive the evolution of our core investment offerings, ensuring they are best-in-class for retail investors while also being scalable, compliant, and well-integrated with partners and platforms. You'll work cross-functionally with engineering, design, business, data, compliance, and external stakeholders to build investment products that meet the needs of our customer base.

This role requires deep domain expertise—you should have a strong grasp of investment products from a retail investor’s perspective and an understanding of the ecosystem, partnerships, and infrastructure required to deliver these products at scale. You’ll need to balance customer needs, regulatory requirements, while driving execution and delivering results.

In this role, you will:

  • Define and execute product strategy and roadmap for the team, aligning with company goals and market opportunities.
  • Lead end-to-end product development for investment products, from ideation to launch and iteration.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across the company to build and scale investment solutions.
  • Analyze data to continuously refine product offerings and identify new opportunities.
  • Drive growth, engagement, and retention of investment products.
  • Foster a data-driven, customer-first culture.

What you need to be successful:

Background:

  • 5+ years of product management experience, with at least 2+ years focused on investment products or wealth management.
  • Experience at a fintech company, brokerage, robo-advisor, asset management firm, or investment platform is strongly preferred.
  • Experience working with third-party partners, regulatory bodies, and financial institutions to bring investment products to market.

Skills:

  • Strategic Thinker: Able to craft a long-term product vision while executing effectively in the short term.
  • Execution-Focused: Strong ability to ship products, drive operational excellence, and navigate complex financial ecosystems. Biased for action.
  • Analytical & Data-Driven: Strong ability to interpret investment data, market trends, and user insights to inform product decisions.
  • Autonomy & Ownership: Thrives in an entrepreneurial, fast-moving environment with a high level of independence and responsibility.
  • Strong Stakeholder Management: Experience working cross-functionally with engineering, compliance, legal, marketing, and finance teams.
  • Communication & Leadership: Excellent ability to articulate product decisions, drive alignment, and influence at all levels of the organization.
  • Regulatory & Risk Acumen: Ability to navigate the complex regulatory landscape of investments while balancing user experience and compliance requirements.

Bonus points if you have:

  • Experience working with embedded finance, direct indexing, crypto, or alternative investments.
  • Familiarity with portfolio construction, risk modeling, or tax optimization strategies.
  • Experience with investment-related APIs and integrations.

What’s in it for you:

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors 🚀.
  • Employee benefits that range from a competitive personal development budget, work from home budget, discounts to fitness & wellness memberships, language apps and public transportation.
  • Come together with your team in the office for a dedicated day of teamwork each week, plus another day of your choice, and enjoy the flexibility of remote work the rest of the time. Some roles may require additional in-office presence.
  • As an N26 employee you will have access to a Premium subscription on your personal N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members.
  • Additional day of annual leave for each year of service.
  • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, life experiences and family statuses.
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it.

Who we are

N26 has reimagined banking for today’s digital world. Technology and design empower everything we do and it’s how we are building the global banking platform the world loves to use.

We've eliminated physical branches, paperwork, and hidden fees for an elegant digital experience and supreme savings. Giving people the power to live and bank their way is what gets us out of bed in the morning and inspires the work that we do.

We are headquartered in Berlin with offices in multiple cities across Europe, including Madrid and Barcelona, and a 1,500-strong team of more than 80 nationalities.

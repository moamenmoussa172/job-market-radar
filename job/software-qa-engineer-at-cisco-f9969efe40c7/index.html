About the job

Meet the Team

Join our Silicon One automated systems team as a System QA Software Engineer and help validate the networking systems behind the growing AI infrastructure. You’ll use our existing automation environment to create and run tests based on realistic customer scenarios, working across the SDK, system behavior, and hardware.

In this role, you’ll connect the dots between how customers use the product and how its software and hardware behave. You’ll translate customer use cases into meaningful system-level tests, examine how features interact, and share your findings with software and hardware engineers as you investigate failures. Along the way, you’ll gain hands-on exposure to SDK internals, ASIC architecture, and the technologies behind AI networking. You may also contribute improvements to the test framework when they make tests more reliable, reusable, or efficient.

Your Impact

  • Translate customer use cases into clear, repeatable automated test scenarios.
  • Develop, run, and maintain system-level tests within the existing automation environment.
  • Design tests that examine interactions between features and their effects on overall system behavior.
  • Use your knowledge of software and systems to investigate test failures and help identify root causes across hardware and software.
  • Work with engineers across the organization to improve test coverage and product quality.
  • Make targeted enhancements to the automation framework when they improve test reliability, reuse, or efficiency.
  • Share findings and collaborate with engineering teams to resolve issues and strengthen validation.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field.
  • 3-5 years of experience in software development, QA automation, system testing, or a related engineering role.
  • Ability to write clear, maintainable code and work effectively within an established codebase.
  • Experience investigating complex issues through structured analysis and debugging.
  • Ability to reason about how features interact and how their combined behavior affects a system.
  • Familiarity with parallel programming or concurrent execution.
  • Experience with Python and common software design patterns, and algorithms.
  • Fluent in English

Preferred Qualifications

  • Understanding of networking concepts and protocols.
  • Experience of validating complex software systems.
  • Exposure to SDKs, hardware-software integration, or ASIC-based products.
  • Openness to feedback and ability to work independently while collaborating across teams.

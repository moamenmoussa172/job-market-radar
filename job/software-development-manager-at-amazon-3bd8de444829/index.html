About the job

AWS Infrastructure Services owns the design, planning, delivery, and operation of the world's largest and most advanced cloud network. The organization develops the software that underpins the entire lifecycle of this network, from initial capacity planning and hardware deployment to decommissioning and replacement. It tackles complex engineering challenges at an unprecedented scale, including automating the deployment of thousands of network devices across more than 30 geographic regions, orchestrating real-time configuration changes without customer impact, and building intelligent systems that anticipate and respond to demand. This work is critical to ensuring that hundreds of millions of active customers and millions of developers worldwide experience the seamless, reliable connectivity they expect from AWS.
We are looking for an experienced Software Development Manager to lead a team of engineers in designing, developing, and delivering scalable software solutions that address complex technical and business challenges. In this role, you will lead and develop engineering talent, drive technical excellence, and oversee the delivery of reliable, high-quality software systems. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to define priorities, establish technical direction, and deliver solutions that improve efficiency, scalability, and customer experience. You will thrive in an inclusive environment that encourages innovation, ownership, and end-to-end execution. If you are passionate about building high-performing teams, solving complex engineering problems, and delivering impactful software solutions at scale, this is an opportunity to make a significant impact.
Key job responsibilities
Team Leadership & People Management
• Lead, mentor, and grow a team of software development engineers across multiple experience levels.
• Foster a culture of ownership, customer obsession, and engineering excellence aligned with Amazon’s Leadership Principles.
• Recruit top-tier talent; build a diverse and inclusive team that reflects the global nature of our customer base.
Software Delivery & Technical Execution
• Own the delivery of large-scale, high-availability software systems.
• Drive technical design reviews and ensure architectural decisions balance long-term maintainability with short-term delivery velocity.
• Set and track meaningful engineering metrics; use data to continuously improve team velocity and quality.
Operational Excellence
• Maintain a relentless focus on system reliability; own operational dashboards, alarms, on-call rotations, and runbooks.
• Drive root-cause analysis (COE) for operational events and implement systemic improvements to prevent recurrence.
• Champion automation to eliminate toil and reduce manual intervention across the network lifecycle.
Strategic Planning & Roadmap
• Define the 1–3 year technical roadmap for your area in partnership with principal engineers and senior leadership.
• Translate business and infrastructure requirements into well-prioritized engineering backlogs.
• Identify opportunities to innovate — leverage machine learning, advanced analytics, and modern cloud-native architectures to improve network lifecycle automation.
Cross-functional Collaboration
• Partner closely with stakeholder teams including Network Engineering, Supply Chain and Operations teams to deliver integrated solutions.
• Communicate effectively with senior leadership and stakeholders; provide clear status updates, risk assessments, and proposals.
• Influence technical direction across organizational boundaries through written documents (PRFAQs, design documents, narratives).

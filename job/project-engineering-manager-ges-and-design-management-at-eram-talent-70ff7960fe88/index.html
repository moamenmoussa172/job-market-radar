Project Engineering Manager (GES and Design Management)

Eram Talent · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Onsite · Lead / Principal · Engineering Leadership · Posted 13 October 2026

Apply on Eram Talent's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

The project Engineering Manager is Lead and manage multidisciplinary engineering and design activities for Saudi Aramco GES+ projects. Ensure engineering deliverables meet Saudi Aramco standards, project schedules, budgets, and quality requirements. Coordinate with the Client, PMT, licensors, vendors, procurement, and construction teams. Provide technical leadership across all engineering disciplines and ensure timely resolution of engineering issues throughout project execution.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead multidisciplinary engineering teams covering Process, Mechanical, Piping, Civil, E&I, and Telecom.
  • Manage FEED verification, detailed engineering, design development, and IFC deliverables.
  • Ensure all engineering activities comply with Saudi Aramco standards, specifications, and procedures.
  • Coordinate engineering interfaces with the Client, PMT, licensors, vendors, procurement, and construction teams.
  • Monitor engineering progress, man-hours, resource utilization, and deliverable schedules.
  • Lead design reviews, constructability reviews, HAZOPs, and technical workshops.
  • Manage Technical Queries (TQs), design changes, engineering risks, and technical issues.
  • Provide engineering support throughout procurement, construction, and commissioning phases.

Requirements

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, preferably Process or Mechanical Engineering.
  • Minimum 15 years of relevant engineering experience.
  • Minimum 8 years of experience in Engineering Management.
  • Proven experience working on Saudi Aramco GES+ projects.
  • Strong knowledge of Saudi Aramco engineering standards, specifications, and procedures.
  • Strong experience in multidisciplinary design management and project engineering.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, coordination, and stakeholder management skills.

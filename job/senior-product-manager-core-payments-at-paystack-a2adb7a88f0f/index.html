About the job

About Paystack

Paystack’s mission is to power African ambition. Over 300K businesses across Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and Kenya use Paystack’s modern payments gateway, including Qatar Airways, MTN, Burger King, UPS, Africa World Airlines, AXA Mansard Insurance, FairMoney, PiggyVest, Crocs, Under Armour, Richemont Lifestyle Group, and many others.

Over the last 10 years, we’ve built products that have helped shape online payments in Africa, from automated recurring payments to direct bank payments and automated chargebacks. Today, Paystack is part of The Stack Group (TSG), a family of technology brands building modern infrastructure across payments, banking, consumer products, and emerging technologies.

At Paystack, we hire talented people, treat them with genuine respect, and give them the space, resources, and support to do the best work of their lives. We’d love your help.

About the Senior Product Manager role

Our team is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own core payments at Paystack.

Core payments is the shared layer under every Paystack market: the global rails, the core payment engine and the services every market and product integrates into.

Country teams own local partner integrations and market detail. You own what sits beneath them: the engine, the endpoints and hooks they build on, and the integrations that are done once, centrally, for every market. You will own the cohesion of Paystack's payments products as they roll out to new markets, so a merchant gets the same product, the same behaviour and the same quality wherever they collect.

This is a processing role focused on the centre, not the edge. The job is to make the core reusable, reliable and easy for every market to build on.

The role reports to the Head of Product. It is strongly preferred if the candidate is based in Lagos, Nigeria.

We’ll trust you to

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for core payments across every Paystack market.
  • Own the global rails, including card schemes, global processors and wallets, and every integration that is done once, centrally, for all markets.
  • Own the core payment engine and shared services, such as routing, gateway selection, card BIN lookup and transaction status, that every market and product depends on.
  • Define the endpoints, hooks and standards that country teams build their local integrations on, and make them easy to adopt.
  • Decide what belongs in the core and what belongs at the edge, and move repeated country work into shared services.
  • Keep payments products consistent as they roll out to new markets, so merchants get the same behaviour and quality everywhere.
  • Work with country PMs in every market to bring their needs into the core roadmap, and push back when a local fix would fragment the platform.
  • Improve success and authorisation rates, latency, uptime and failover across the core.
  • Hold global processing partners to performance standards, and evaluate new global partners and rails.
  • Make sure the core is ready for each new market launch before the first local integration is built.
  • Work with Financial Systems, Trust & Safety and Merchant Experience & Onboarding so the core plugs into settlement, safety controls and the merchant experience.
  • Use transaction data to find failure points and set priorities.
  • Define and track the metrics: success rate, authorisation rate, uptime, latency, time to launch in a new market, and adoption of shared services by country teams.

You’ll thrive in this role if you

  • 7+ years of product management experience, including shipping complex technical or platform products
  • Deep experience in payment processing, such as acquiring, gateways, routing, switching, card schemes or processor integrations
  • Experience building platform products or shared services that other teams build on
  • A track record of improving success rate, authorisation rate or uptime, with the numbers to show it
  • Experience working directly with card schemes, global processors or acquirers
  • Experience taking a payments product into more than one market
  • Strong systems thinking, and the judgement to know what belongs in a shared platform and what should stay local
  • Technical fluency to discuss APIs, architecture, performance and reliability trade-offs with engineers
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort working with transaction data
  • Experience leading or supporting incident response on payment systems
  • Strong stakeholder management across country teams, engineering and partners
  • Prior experience at a payments company operating in several African markets, which is a strong asset

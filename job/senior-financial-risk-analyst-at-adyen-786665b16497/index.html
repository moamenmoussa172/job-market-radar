Senior Financial Risk Analyst

As a Senior Financial Risk Analyst on our Merchant Potential Liability Team you will be part of a team of high-performing professionals that evaluates and manages financial risk (i.e. chargebacks) arising from a portfolio of enterprise merchants, as well as assessing the risk of new products.

As a member of our team, you will help us scale and innovate our financial risk management framework and processes. You will be working closely with the Commercial, Product and Legal teams while collaborating with some of the biggest merchants processing on the Adyen platform, all with one overarching goal: limiting Adyen’s exposure to credit losses. Our team is highly motivated, extremely innovative, and thrives on shared success. As we operate globally, our team is part of an international team with diverse backgrounds and skill sets. Our ideal new team member has a proven track record of solving problems, is a great communicator with an affinity for technology and a passion for finance.

What you’ll be doing

  • As Senior Financial Risk Analyst on our Merchant Potential Liability team, you will work closely with our very diverse merchant portfolio, to assess and analyse their business model and financial health, and provide clear and concise credit risk advice to the Adyen credit risk committee.
  • You will drive continuous improvement of our risk management framework, including policies, tools and processes, to further improve the quality and scalability of our risk management objectives.
  • You will lead projects from beginning to end, and help us to find optimal solutions for our merchants by collaborating with various teams and ensuring we are aligned with our risk management framework;
  • You will share your credit risk knowledge to Product, Legal and Compliance teams to ensure credit risk is properly considered in new commercial product development;
  • You will share Adyen’s approach to credit risk management to merchants, commercial teams, and regulators.

Who you are

  • You have at least 5 years of working experience in a financial risk environment and a keen interest in business and financial markets;
  • You are an excellent communicator, able to share ideas and collaborate with our merchants, other team members and colleagues across cultures and time zones;
  • You are innovative, with strong problem-solving capabilities and able to define efficient and effective processes and procedures;
  • You are able to lead projects by working closely with different teams and setting clear goals and deadlines;
  • You thrive in an evolving, fast-paced environment, challenging the status-quo with a ‘can-do’ mentality.
  • Payments or Fintech experience is not required, but you must be able to communicate your interest in this space.
  • Strong data and analytic skills, which you capitalize to improve our risk management framework such as experience in: SQL, Python, R is not required but considered a strong plus; and,
  • University degree preferred in business economics, statistics, finance or another quantitative field.

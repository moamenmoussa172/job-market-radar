About the job

The Investment Analyst will support the BeVentures investment team across the investment lifecycle, including market research, deal sourcing support, preliminary screening, financial analysis, investment documentation, and portfolio monitoring. The role will contribute to identifying and evaluating technologies and business models that align with Bapco Energies’ strategic priorities, support Strategic Business Unit needs, and advance BeVentures’ mandate to source, validate, invest in, and scale strategically relevant energy and industrial innovation.

The investment analyst will also support portfolio company engagement and growth initiatives, including coordination with various internal business units, pilot initiation, follow ups, execution and monitoring, stakeholder engagement, and tracking opportunities across Bapco Energies.

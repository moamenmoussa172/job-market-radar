About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

Field Sales Engineer Automotive

Requirements

- 5-10 years experience as a Field Sales Engineer in Automotive or in similar sales/client relationship role
- Demonstrated success in managing and growing key accounts
- Strong understanding of sales principles and customer service practices
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Strong negotiation and conflict resolution skills
- Ability to analyze and interpret sales data and account performance metrics
- Proficiency in CRM software and MS Office Suite
- Strategic thinking and problem-solving aptitude
- Ability to multitask and manage multiple clients simultaneously
- Strong organizational and time management skills
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team
- Willingness to travel as needed to meet with clients
- Experience in the relevant industry or sector
- Proven ability to achieve sales targets and KPIs
- Strong presentation and public speaking skills
- High level of professionalism and integrity
- Ability to mentor and support junior team members
- Up-to-date with the latest industry trends and competitive insights

- B category driver’s license

Responsibilities

- Build and sustain strong relationships with key clients
- Schedule and conduct regular client meetings
- Identify and act upon growth opportunities within existing accounts
- Coordinate with internal teams to fulfill client needs
- Prepare and deliver client presentations
- Negotiate and finalize contracts and agreements
- Monitor and report on account performance metrics
- Stay informed about industry trends and competitive activities
- Address client issues and complaints promptly
- Develop strategic account plans aligning with sales targets
- Ensure timely delivery of solutions that meet client expectations
- Track and analyze key account metrics
- Develop new business opportunities with existing and new clients
- Serve as the primary contact for account-related matters
- Manage account budgets and oversee billing and invoicing
- Oversee the completion of large contracts
- Represent the company at industry events and conferences

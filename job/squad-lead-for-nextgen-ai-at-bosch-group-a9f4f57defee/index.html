SQUAD LEAD FOR NEXTGEN AI

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

Hybrid · Lead / Principal · LLM / GenAI Engineer · Posted 5 November 2026

IAMLLMs / GenAI

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Lead Bosch’s NextGen AI initiative and drive enterprise-wide adoption of Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions.
  • Define AI strategy, roadmap, governance principles, success metrics, and service offerings.
  • Own the end-to-end lifecycle of ChatGPT Enterprise and related AI services, from demand management and implementation to operations and continuous improvement.
  • Translate business requirements into secure, scalable, and compliant AI solutions.
  • Serve as the technical advisor for ChatGPT Enterprise and the broader NextGenAI ecosystem, providing solution design, integration guidance, and implementation support.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, architects, engineers, security, compliance, procurement, vendors, and executive leadership.
  • Drive responsible AI adoption by ensuring alignment with governance, privacy, security, and regulatory requirements.
  • Manage stakeholder engagement, vendor relationships, service performance, and business value realization.
  • Promote AI innovation, knowledge sharing, capability building, and organizational upskilling.
  • Monitor emerging AI technologies, trends, and regulations to continuously evolve Bosch’s AI ecosystem.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree
  • English Fluency
  • Strong expertise in Generative AI, including ChatGPT Enterprise, Large Language Models (LLMs), Prompt Engineering, AI Agents, and Agentic Workflows.
  • Knowledge of enterprise AI platforms and ecosystems, including OpenAI, MCP, A2A, MLOps, multi-agent architectures, and cloud-native AI infrastructure.
  • Proven experience in Product & Service Management, including product lifecycle management, service portfolio management, roadmap planning, demand management, budgeting, financial management, and vendor management.
  • Ability to design and implement secure, scalable, and business-oriented AI solutions.
  • Strong knowledge of cloud and SaaS platforms, APIs, system integrations, Identity & Access Management (IAM), and enterprise architecture principles.
  • Solid understanding of AI governance, Responsible AI, information security, data protection, risk management, GDPR, EU AI Act, and related compliance requirements.
  • Familiarity with Bosch AI Governance, Bosch Digital platforms, and Product & Service lifecycle processes.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, consulting, communication, and influencing skills across technical and business environments.
  • Strong analytical mindset, innovation focus, and commitment to continuous learning in a rapidly evolving AI landscape.

Internal Bosch candidates must be part of the TP or TPR program.

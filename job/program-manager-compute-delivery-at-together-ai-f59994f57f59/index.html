About the job

About the Role

Together AI runs on a large and fast-growing fleet of compute capacity, sourced from partners across many sites and regions. Our Compute team makes sure that capacity lands on time and that every team across the company works from the same accurate plan. We're looking for a Program Manager who will own the programs that take new infrastructure from commitment to production: running timelines and dependencies, holding partners and teammates to their commitments, surfacing risk early, and building AI-native processes that make delivery predictable as we scale.

Responsibilities

  • Own the end-to-end delivery plan for new infrastructure capacity, from commitment through production, so GTM, Finance and Engineering work from one trusted view of what’s landing and when
  • Run multiple concurrent infrastructure programs: milestones, dependencies, owners, decisions and handoffs as capacity is validated and brought into service
  • Act as the day-to-day operating counterpart to external infrastructure partners; hold them to commitments and escalate when things slip, while maintaining productive relationships
  • Maintain the tracking, dashboards and reporting cadence that give leadership clear visibility into program status and blockers, and turn it into concise updates up to the executive team
  • Build AI-native workflows and repeatable playbooks from day one: use agents and automation to replace manual tracking, reporting and follow-ups, so the team scales without adding headcount for coordination

Requirements

  • 5+ years in program management, TPM, BizOps or operations, managing complex, cross-functional programs with multiple parallel workstreams, external partners and shifting priorities
  • Hands-on experience with physical or cloud infrastructure: data centers, cloud capacity, or hardware deployment
  • AI-native in how you work; a track record of using agents and automation to replace manual work, with a high bar for accuracy
  • Strong communicator; able to translate technical and operational detail into a clear picture for non-technical audiences
  • Comfortable holding partners and senior colleagues accountable without formal authority, in an environment where processes are still being built and priorities shift quickly

Nice to Have

  • Familiarity with data center power, cooling and networking fundamentals, and how physical infrastructure decisions affect deployment timelines
  • Experience managing supply chain risk on infrastructure deployments: long-lead equipment, vendor delays, and working with cloud or colocation partners to recover schedule

About Together AI

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, is purpose-built for AI engineers. AI application developers get high-performance inference that scales reliably, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for creating frontier-level specialized models, and pre-training at massive scale for fully custom intelligence, all around a marketplace of leading open models that teams can run, adapt, and own. Trusted by Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, Together serves 400+ trillion tokens a month.

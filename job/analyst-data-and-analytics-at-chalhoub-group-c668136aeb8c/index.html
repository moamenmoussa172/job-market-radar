About the job

INSPIRE | EXHILARATE | DELIGHT

For over seven decades, Chalhoub Group has been shaping luxury experiences in the Middle East. As a leading hybrid luxury retailer, the Group brings together over 16,000 talented professionals and partners with more than 400 international brands across fashion, beauty, jewellery, watches, eyewear, and lifestyle.

With a people-at-heart culture, a commitment to innovation, and a passion for developing talent, we continue to shape the future of luxury retail while creating opportunities for the next generation to grow and thrive.

  • What You'll Be Doing
    The candidate will need to become a specialist in our Enterprise Analytics Platform powered by Looker and Google Cloud. He/She will support the business across the group to make the best use of our data products and tools. Your responsibilities will include (but are not limited to):
  • Perform analysis of the business performance across the different data domains: Commercial, Supply Chain, Ecommerce, Customer....
  • Identify and define the relevar KPIs for the business and build dashboards to monitor them in Looker.
  • Maintain and evolve the data products using LookML, Looker's programming language.
  • Support analysts and business users across the group to correctly utilize the platform and data products.
  • Support analysts with SQL and Python
  • Support the analyst across the group by reviewing their merge requests and performing code reviews

What You'll Need to Succeed

  • 2+ yrs experience in working in a technology related environment with of documenting processes and requirements.
  • Awareness of Retail Operations related systems ( In - Store, Logistics, Supply Chain etc.)
  • Knowledge of Oracle Retail MOM applications ( RMS, RPM, ReSA and ReIM) ( desirable but not mandatory)
  • Exposure to Oracle EBS suite and integration with Oracle Retail applications (desirable but not mandatory)
  • Good understanding of Retail domain concepts and appreciation of how technology can drive value in the industry.

Role summary

Responsible for providing technical assistance. In Charge of resolving daily users’ / shop hardware and software problems; monitors the operation of the company’s computer systems.

What you'll be doing

  • Provide day-to-day technical support to employees across the Head Office, retail stores, warehouses, and Cairo Airport Duty Free.
  • Receive, manage, and resolve IT support tickets raised by employees, ensuring timely follow-up and resolution.
  • Troubleshoot basic hardware and software issues, including computers, laptops, printers, Windows operating systems, and iOS devices.
  • Provide technical support for retail POS systems, barcode scanners, and other store-related equipment.
  • Assist with basic network and connectivity troubleshooting, escalating complex technical issues when needed.
  • Conduct onsite technical visits to different locations whenever required to resolve hardware, software, or connectivity issues.
  • Support IT setup and technical requirements for retail stores, warehouses, and other business locations.
  • Follow IT procedures and maintain accurate records of incidents, resolutions, and IT equipment.
  • Participate in a rotational 24/7 on-call schedule to support critical business operations for Cairo Airport Duty Free.

What you’ll need to succeed

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Information Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 1+ years of experience in IT Support, Helpdesk, Technical Support, or a similar role.
  • Basic knowledge of Windows operating systems, computer hardware, software troubleshooting, and networking fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with POS systems, printers, barcode scanners, and iOS devices is an advantage.
  • Good command of English, with the ability to communicate effectively with employees and stakeholders.
  • Willingness to work between Head Office, retail stores, warehouses, and Cairo Airport Duty Free whenever required.
  • Positive attitude, strong communication skills, eagerness to learn, and a proactive approach to problem-solving.

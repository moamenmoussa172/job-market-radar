About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Role Description

As a Senior Full Stack Product Software Engineer on Dropbox’s Web Developer Experience (Web DX) team, you will help design and develop the application platform, developer tooling, and shared foundations that enable engineers to build reliable web experiences. You will take ownership of key technical systems, designing scalable architectures and delivering improvements that make software easier to develop, test, and ship.

You will work closely with product and infrastructure engineering teams to improve build systems, language tooling, and development workflows. Your work will include accelerating TypeScript feedback, automating migrations, simplifying development environments, and evolving shared application foundations. As AI changes how software is built, you will help create the reliable builds, fast tests, and repeatable workflows that support engineers and coding agents.

Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver ongoing engineering impact through ownership of developer tooling, application foundations, and web-platform systemsImprove typechecking, builds, tests, and local development to shorten feedback loops and increase reliability
  • Build automation for upgrades, code transformations, and migrations using repeatable validation and gradual rollout
  • Collaborate with product and infrastructure teams to balance technical requirements, developer needs, and business goals
  • Lead technical initiatives from architecture and planning through rollout and adoption, measuring their impact on developer experience
  • Mentor engineers and contribute to team development through technical guidance, code reviews, and shared engineering standards
  • Support the quality and operability of the systems you deliver through diagnostics, failure investigation, and agreed support arrangements

Requirements

  • 9+ years of professional experience in full-stack development
  • BS degree or higher in Computer Science, a related field, or equivalent experience
  • Strong experience designing, developing, and scaling web applications or the platforms that support them
  • Strong programming and debugging skills in JavaScript, TypeScript, or another language, with experience working across front-end and back-end systems
  • Experience designing maintainable software, including component interfaces, dependencies, testing, and failure modes
  • Experience delivering software to production, including validation, rollout, and ongoing improvements
  • Demonstrated technical leadership, including architecture, prioritization, cross-team collaboration, and mentoring
  • Ability to investigate developer and system problems using measurements, profiling, logs, tests, and user feedback

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with JavaScript/TypeScript tooling, compilers, bundlers such as Rspack, or build systems such as Bazel
  • Experience with CI, test infrastructure, dependency graphs, or developer environments
  • Experience with web infrastructure, application performance, service integration, or platform adoption
  • Experience with codemods, framework upgrades, legacy-system migrations, or AI-assisted automation supported by reliable validation

Durable Skills

AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it. We believe people with these skills will thrive as work and technology continue to evolve:

  • Awareness: Understand yourself and others.
  • Judgment: Evaluate information and make decisions in complex situations.
  • Adaptability: Learn, adjust, and stay effective through change.
  • Connection: Communicate, collaborate, and build trust.

To learn more about why these skills matter and what the data shows about thriving through change, read this blog post from our Chief People Officer, Melanie Rosenwasser.

About the Team

We are an infrastructure engineering team within Workday's observability organisation. Engineering teams worldwide rely on our platform to monitor and operate their production systems.
What we own: Metrics ingestion and alerting infrastructure, from the pipelines that collect and process metrics to the platform services that detect issues and deliver notifications
How we build: Distributed systems at scale across Workday's global production environments, with a focus on reliability and long-term operability
Agentic engineering:We build tooling and agent-facing interfaces that help engineers and AI agents understand and operate the platform, and we use them in our own day-to-day work.
How we work: Close collaboration with partner teams across engineering and site reliability, with engineers taking end-to-end ownership of the problems they solve.

About the Role

We build and operate observability infrastructure for metrics ingestion and alerting across Workday's global production environments. You will work on distributed systems at scale, collecting, processing, and acting on operational data, with a focus on reliability, performance, and simplicity.
You will also contribute to agentic engineering, building tools and workflows that help engineers and AI agents operate the platform more effectively.
In this role, you will:

  • Design, build, and debug distributed systems across cloud-native production environments
  • Develop platform services for metrics ingestion, processing, and alerting at scale
  • Shape how AI agents interact with observability infrastructure through tooling, APIs, and operational workflows
  • Collaborate across teams to deliver infrastructure that is resilient, observable, and easy to operate

About You

Basic Qualification

  • 6+ years of professional software engineering experience with production-grade systems
  • 5+ years designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems on public or private clouds
  • Strong understanding of distributed systems fundamentals, including concurrency, fault tolerance, high availability, and failure-mode analysis
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

Other Qualification

  • Agentic Engineering:Interest or experience building MCP servers, agent-facing APIs, or tooling that helps engineers and AI systems operate complex platforms
  • Metrics & Observability Platforms:Experience with Prometheus-style monitoring ecosystems, time-series metrics pipelines, or large-scale observability platforms in production
  • Go & Python:Strong proficiency in Go for production services; experience with Python for tooling, automation, data processing, or ML workloads
  • API & Architecture:Experience designing and implementing robust, scalable HTTP and gRPC APIs in multi-tenant environments
  • Kubernetes & Infrastructure as Code:Experience deploying and operating services on Kubernetes using infrastructure-as-code and platform configuration workflows such as Helm, Jsonnet, Terraform, or GitOps-style deployment pipelines
  • Quality & Testing:Strong testing discipline across unit, integration, and end-to-end tests for distributed systems
  • Collaboration & Leadership:Demonstrated ability to own problems end-to-end, work closely with SRE and partner engineering teams, and mentor other engineers
  • Technical Communication:Excellent written communication skills for architecture designs, operational runbooks, and technical documentation

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together.

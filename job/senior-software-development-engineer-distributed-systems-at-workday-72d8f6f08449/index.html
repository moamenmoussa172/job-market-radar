About the Team

The MIA team owns Workday's primary time-series monitoring and alerting platform, a very high scale distributed service supporting all engineering teams and data centres worldwide.
The team is entirely based in Dublin and operate under a DevOps model whereby we build, deploy, maintain and support our services.

About the Role

You will help run and evolve monitoring platform, at very large scale alongside a highly collaborative team of talented engineers.
You will own technical direction on the hardest problems we face in order to support AI driven demand: ingestion resilience, write & read performance, API hardening, security compliance, budget management in a high growth demand, etc.
You will also lead designs and RCAs, and mentor mid-level engineers.
What we're looking for:

  • Solid DevOps and operations experience, including CI/CD, automation, on-call, and incident response.
  • Familiarity with observability tooling and practices such as metrics, alerting, SLOs, and dashboards.
  • A good grasp of distributed systems: scaling, resilience, and failure modes.
  • Clear communication and the ability to guide design decisions.
  • proficiency at leveraging AI enhancing engineering tooling and processes

Major plus:

  • Kubernetes
  • Python
  • Go
  • Prometheus/ Grafana ecosystems

About You

Basic Qualification

  • 8+ years experience in software development engineering.
  • 4+ years experience designing and developing distributed system components, demonstrating knowledge of principles such as consistency, availability, and fault tolerance.
  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.

Other Qualification

  • Demonstrated ability in Algorithmic Thinking to design efficient and scalable solutions.
  • Solid experience with API Development
  • Good understanding of Code Testing methodologies and experience with doing so in a distributed environment.
  • Strong understanding of Distributed Systems Software principles.
  • Working knowledge of High Availability strategies and their implementation in distributed systems.
  • Experience with Large Scale Data Processing technologies and frameworks such as Kafka within a distributed architecture.
  • Good understanding of Large Scale Systems design principles.
  • Good understanding of Object-Oriented Design (OOD) principles and their application in building modular and maintainable distributed components.
  • Working experience with Source Control Management (SCM) tools such as GHE and branching strategies for collaborative distributed development.
  • Basic understanding of System Security principles and best practices relevant to distributed environments.
  • Proven ability in Team Collaboration within a distributed software development team.
  • Good skills in creating Technical Writing Documentation for technical proposals, knowledge shares and user documentation.

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together.

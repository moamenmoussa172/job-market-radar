About the Role

Camunda partners with global system integrators, regional consultancies, and technology partners to help enterprises orchestrate complex processes and AI agents. This role builds and runs the engine behind that partner business: the program, the investments, the data, and the operating rhythm. The Principal Program Manager is the strategic operating partner to the VP of Partnerships & Alliances and leads an AI-first approach to how the partner team works.

Partner program, fund, and data work currently sits with the VP and the partner managers, which limits scale and pulls partner managers away from selling with partners. Partner-sourced and influenced pipeline, MDF spend, and the Strategic GSI Co-Investment Program all need consistent governance and reporting that no one owns today.

What You Will Do

  • Partner with the VP to run the organization: own the annual partner plan, budget and headcount planning, the team's operating rhythm, and executive and board reporting.
  • Drive team performance and accountability through clear OKRs, scorecards, and pipeline reviews.
  • Own the global partner program: partner types, tiers, reseller and commercial models, onboarding, benefits, agreements, and the annual program refresh.
  • Manage MDF and co-op budgets end to end: planning, approvals, claims, proof of performance, and ROI reporting.
  • Run the program office for the Strategic GSI Co-Investment Program: funded head scorecards, quarterly KPI gates, investment release decisions, and executive steering.
  • Manage priority partners through program parameters, and give Regional and GSI Partner Managers the standards, data, and tools they need.
  • Run the Partner Advisory Board and the annual partner calendar, including webinars, newsletters, and program communications.
  • Serve as the partner team's liaison to Marketing and Marketing Ops, Legal, Finance, Sales Ops, Enablement, and Product.
  • Own partner operations and tools, including Impartner (PRM) and Salesforce: deal registration, attribution, data quality, and dashboards. Support hyperscaler marketplace and co-sell operations.
  • Design and ship AI agents and automations across the partner lifecycle, and measure the time and cost they save.

What You Will Bring

  • 8+ years in partner, channel, or alliances roles at enterprise software or SaaS companies, with 3+ years running partner programs or partner operations.
  • Direct experience managing MDF and GSI co-investments or funded head programs, including ROI tracking and stage-gated funding.
  • Deep knowledge of partner types, tiering, and reseller and route-to-market models.
  • Hands-on experience as a builder (not just a user) of a PRM platform, ideally Impartner, and Salesforce.
  • Proven use of AI tools, agents, and automation to redesign workflows, with examples you can show.
  • Experience running a partner advisory board or a similar executive partner forum.
  • Strong analytical skills: builds own reports and can explain the numbers to executives and Finance.
  • Strategic judgment and executive presence, with experience as a trusted strategic operator to a senior leader.
  • Clear written communication and the ability to run programs across regions and time zones.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with hyperscaler marketplaces and co-sell programs.
  • Background in process orchestration, automation, or integration software.
  • Experience at a high-growth company scaling from early partner motion to a global program.

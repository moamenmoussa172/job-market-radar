About the job

What you’ll build

  • Develop mission-critical order-to-cash execution capabilities that can run reliably on remote servers in disconnected and intermittently connected operational environments.
  • Design and implement end-to-end SD business processes covering customer inquiries and quotations, sales orders, availability checks, picking, packing, outbound delivery, shipping, goods issue, billing, and invoicing.
  • Build high-quality solutions using ABAP Objects, ABAP CDS, RAP, OData, SAP Fiori elements, SAPUI5, and relevant S/4HANA extensibility technologies.
  • Adapt and enhance standard SD applications, business objects, APIs, workflows, output management, pricing, document flow, and status management for remote execution.
  • Implement robust synchronization, data consistency, error handling, conflict resolution, and reconciliation mechanisms between central and disconnected S/4HANA systems.
  • Deliver functionality from technical design through implementation, automated testing, release, stabilization, and production support.
  • Improve performance, security, upgrade safety, maintainability, and operational resilience in resource-constrained remote environments.

What you bring

  • Strong hands-on expertise in ABAP Objects, modern ABAP, ABAP CDS, RAP, OData services, enhancement frameworks, APIs, and performance-oriented development.
  • Practical experience with SAP Fiori elements, SAPUI5, annotations, draft handling, side effects, value helps, authorizations, and backend–frontend integration.
  • Solid functional knowledge of SAP S/4HANA Sales and Distribution, including sales document processing, pricing, ATP, delivery processing, picking, packing, shipping, goods issue, billing, invoicing, document flow, and output management.
  • Experience integrating SD with Materials Management, Warehouse Management or EWM, Finance, Transportation Management, Business Partner, and product master data.
  • Understanding of distributed-system concepts such as synchronization, transactional consistency, retries, duplicate prevention, conflict handling, and offline-capable processing.
  • Experience with ABAP Unit, integration testing, test automation, debugging, root-cause analysis, secure development, and agile delivery.
  • Strong ownership, analytical ability, and willingness to work across technical and functional boundaries in mission-critical customer scenarios.


Where you belong

You'll take end-to-end technical ownership of critical systems and contribute to engineering excellence across the team. Mentoring less-experienced engineers, guiding design and code quality, and building strong technical capabilities within the team are all part of the role. The team operates across global, cross-functional groups in a culture that values continuous improvement, operational excellence, and knowledge sharing.

Skills used in this role: Learning Agility, Creative Thinking, Complex Problem Solving, Effective Communication, Collaboration, AI-Assisted Engineering, Application Security, Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), High Availability Engineering, Troubleshooting (Problem Solving), Agentic AI, Day-to-Day Practice, AI Adoption Capability, AI Output Quality Assurance, Context Engineering, AI-Assisted Automation and Prototyping

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Netherlands.

Set a new pulse for healthcare!

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to join the Doctolib Connect team in Amsterdam.

Doctolib Connect is a secure medical communication app designed for healthcare professionals. More than 1M healthcare professionals use Doctolib Connect to discuss and coordinate patient care with colleagues, both within and beyond their organizational walls. Today, Doctolib Connect is the largest medical network in Europe. You'll work closely with a talented team to develop user-friendly products that meet market demands, such as tele-expertise or patient referral flows.

What you'll do

As a Senior Product Manager on the Doctolib Connect team, you will drive the development of products that strengthen care cooperation among healthcare professionals. Working at the heart of a cross-functional team, you will shape products that serve over 900,000 users across our network, combining a user-centric and data-driven approach with a focus on network effects and long-term impact. Success in this role means growing Doctolib Connect's network, launching products that achieve product-market fit, and delivering measurable value for both our users and the business.

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Contributing to our product strategy to grow Doctolib Connect, identifying opportunities that strengthen the network and deliver user and business value
  • Defining, shipping, and iterating on new products and features by analysing data, conducting user interviews, researching competitors, and leveraging cross-functional insights
  • Continuously measuring adoption, impact, and network health, iterating as needed to achieve desired outcomes for your product and company goals
  • Collaborating with engineering, design, and research teams to translate insights into hypotheses, experiments, and features that delight users and grow Doctolib Connect's network
  • Working closely with cross-functional teams including sales, marketing, support, and company leadership to ensure solutions serve users and meet business needs across target markets

Who you are

Are you convinced that you have exactly what it takes for the position? Then we encourage you to apply. Even if you don't meet every requirement, you might be exactly the right future Doctoliber for this or other positions!

You could be our next team mate if you:

  • Have 4+ years of Product Management experience, including at least 1 year working on mobile applications, with a track record of shipping products that have delivered meaningful business results and proven user value
  • Have experience developing SaaS or mobile products, ideally including a network effect component such as social networking, communication services, or collaborative workplace tools
  • Approach problems with an analytical mindset, balancing quantitative and qualitative insights to make data-informed decisions
  • Communicate with clarity and conviction to align diverse stakeholders, both technical and non-technical, driving vision and articulating user value and business impact
  • Are highly organised and disciplined in project management, with strong attention to detail and the confidence to ask tough questions and say no when needed

Now, it would be fantastic if you:

  • Have experience with health tech, marketplace, or platform products
  • Are familiar with operating in multi-market environments
  • Have worked in a scale-up or fast-paced product organisation

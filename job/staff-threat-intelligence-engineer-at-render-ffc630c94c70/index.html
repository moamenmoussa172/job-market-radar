About the Role

We’re looking for a Staff Threat Intelligence Engineer to build Render’s threat intelligence program and turn what we learn about attackers into protections and products customers can use.

As a hands-on technical leader within External Security, you’ll connect research, platform telemetry, and external intelligence to detect malicious activity earlier, track campaigns, and help teams act. You’ll establish the program’s priorities and operating model, build its core systems, and help recruit and develop the engineers and researchers who grow it.

Your first 90 days will focus on detecting anomalous network activity and delivering actionable leads to Trust & Safety, who will own the resulting investigations. You’ll build the initial detection capability, provide supporting evidence and workload context, and use investigator feedback to improve signal quality.

You’ll also work directly with customers and Product to turn repeatable capabilities into supported offerings. Initial opportunities include an IP threat feed, actionable threat notifications, and in container RASP, and intelligence that supports incident response. You’ll validate which opportunities solve a customer problem and can be operated sustainably.

What You'll Do

  • Build and operate anomalous network activity detections, delivering evidence and affected workload or account context to Trust & Safety for investigation.
  • Establish intelligence requirements with Security, Trust & Safety, Engineering, and customer-facing teams; prioritize threats to Render and its customers.
  • Build reliable collection, enrichment, correlation, and distribution pipelines that combine platform signals with external reporting.
  • Investigate attacker behavior and related accounts, infrastructure, and campaigns; document evidence, confidence, and alternative explanations.
  • Turn research into tested detections, investigation workflows, and response recommendations with clear ownership and measurable results.
  • Build partnerships with researchers, intelligence providers, and peer platforms to improve early warning and coordinated response.
  • Productize useful intelligence through maintained APIs, feeds, tools, or customer-facing capabilities with documentation, quality controls, and support.
  • Meet customers alongside Product and Sales to validate demand, adoption, willingness to pay where relevant, and delivery economics.
  • Publish useful research and represent Render externally in coordination with Legal and Communications.
  • Define the team’s skill mix and staffing plan, help hire, mentor contributors, and establish peer review and shared operational ownership.
  • Measure detection quality, time to action, responder effort, customer adoption, and operating cost.

What We're Looking For

  • A track record of building or materially advancing a threat intelligence program and delivering operational results.
  • Strong software engineering skills and experience operating production data pipelines, services, or APIs.
  • Depth in threat research, campaign analysis, and detection engineering, including reasoning under uncertainty.
  • Experience converting intelligence into protections used by responders, engineering teams, or customers.
  • Staff-level technical leadership: setting direction, gaining cross-team commitment, mentoring others, and delivering complex work.
  • Product judgment and experience validating user needs before committing substantial engineering effort.
  • Clear writing, sound evidence handling, and the judgment to communicate uncertainty without overstating attribution.
  • Pragmatism, empathy, and ownership of long-term system health and customer impact.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience with cloud platforms, containers, malicious workloads, phishing, C2 infrastructure, or shared IP reputation.
  • Experience shipping intelligence feeds, security APIs, or customer-facing detection products.
  • Established researcher partnerships and a record of useful public research.
  • Experience recruiting and developing an early team.
  • Familiarity with Go, TypeScript, SQL, Kubernetes, and large-scale security telemetry.

If this role excites you but you don’t meet every single requirement, we’d still love to hear from you—your unique experience might be just what we need.

Principal Enterprise Architect Professional Services (Germany)

About the job

The opportunity

From the moment you wake up in the morning until you go to bed at night consider the media you consume, the adverts you see, the apps you use, the websites you browse and almost all of the shopping you do online throughout the day. Chances are that every single one of those interactions, every single one of those experiences, was touched by an Adobe product.

We have a fantastic opportunity for a Principal Enterprise Architect Professional Services to join our team based in Germany.

As a Principal Enterprise Architect Professional Services, you will guide architecture through the transformation lifecycle. You will build executive confidence, govern design decisions, lead architecture build, and make sure technology choices support long-term value.

What you’ll do

You will bridge business goals and technical delivery by designing target architectures, planning transitions, and creating decommissioning strategies. Using change management frameworks such as ADKAR, you'll support adoption and sustainability.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assess and advance the architectural intent crafted during pre-sales.
  • Provide architectural leadership across the full transformation lifecycle and guide platform adoption and evolution.
  • Convert the success roadmap created during pre-sales into a comprehensive, managed transition plan featuring clear sequencing, decision points, and risk controls.
  • Formulate robust, industry-specific architectural perspectives and translate them into target-state architecture, reference models, and phased implementation plans.
  • Foster and maintain executive trust in architectural direction, serving as a strategic advisor to senior business and technology collaborators.
  • Collaborate with licensing and services sales teams to improve major sales initiatives.
  • Coordinate architectural efforts among delivery teams, subject matter experts, and partners to ensure build integrity and alignment during execution.
  • Establish and lead architecture governance and assurance forums.
  • Partner closely with pre‑sales teams to ensure lessons learned, build patterns, and reference architectures continually improve future pursuits and delivery outcomes.

What you’ll need to succeed

  • Proven experience providing enterprise architecture leadership across large-scale transformation programmes, from early visioning and pre-sales through delivery, adoption, and platform evolution.
  • Strong grounding in TOGAF principles and lifecycle-based architecture governance, applied pragmatically in complex enterprise environments.
  • Broad understanding of digital marketing, data, and activation ecosystems.
  • The ability to craft compelling narratives that translate architectural choices into business-relevant results and messages suitable for executives.
  • Experience operating as a trusted architectural advisor to senior executives.
  • Strong understanding of AI, automation, and emerging agentic models, and their implications for enterprise platforms.
  • Experience working closely with delivery teams, partners, and vendors to maintain build integrity.
  • Ability to apply structured change and adoption approaches (including ADKAR) to support the human and organisational aspects of architectural evolution.
  • A collaborative, outcome-focused leadership style, with a relentless focus on value and adoption.
  • Fluency in English and German is required. Any other european language would be a plus.

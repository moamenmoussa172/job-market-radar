About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

Who Are We❓

We Are Foodics! a leading restaurant management ecosystem and payment tech provider. Founded in 2014 with headquarter in Riyadh and offices across 5 countries, including UAE, Egypt, Jordan and Kuwait. We are currently serving customers and partners in over 35 different countries worldwide. Our innovative products have successfully processed over 6 billion (yes, billion with a B) orders so far! making Foodics one of the most rapidly evolving SaaS companies to ever emerge from the MENA region.

The Job in a Nutshell💡

Foodics Financial Services builds the money products that help restaurants and merchants across the region run their businesses: payments, capital, and cashflow tools. We operate in a regulated environment , which means we care deeply about reliability, security, and correctness. We also move fast and ship products merchants actually feel.

We're looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to work as a Forward Deployed Engineer: an engineer who lives closest to the problem. You'll sit with merchants, partner banks, and our own Operations, Risk, finance and Sales teams, see where the money flows break or slow down, and build the fix yourself, from a quick internal tool to a production feature. You'll be the shortest path between what happens in the field and what our platform does next.

What You Will Do❓

  • Go where the problem is. Spend real time with merchants, acquiring and banking partners, and internal teams (Settlement, Risk, Onboarding, Sales, Customer Success). Watch how our products are used, and where people work around them.
  • Turn field problems into shipped software. Take a messy, real-world problem, scope it, and deliver it end to end: backend services, APIs, data, and the frontend that people actually use.
  • Build integrations and custom flows. Lead technical delivery for strategic merchants and partners: payment and settlement integrations, onboarding flows, reporting, and data exports, done to production standard.
  • Prototype fast, then harden. Get a working version in front of users in days, learn from it, then make it reliable: idempotency, consistency, auditability, security, and observability for anything that touches real money.
  • Build the internal tools that unblock operations. Reconciliation helpers, dispute and chargeback tooling, merchant support consoles, and automation that removes manual work from Ops and Finance.
  • Be the voice of the field in product. Bring evidence back to Product, Design, and the core engineering teams. Spot patterns across customers, and push one-off fixes into reusable platform capabilities.
  • Work AI-first. Use AI coding assistants, agents, and LLM APIs to multiply your output, and find places where AI can remove friction for merchants and internal teams, then build it.
  • Own it in production. Support what you ship, take part in incident response for the systems you own, and keep stakeholders informed in plain language.

What Are We Looking For❓

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a track record of shipping and supporting production systems.
  • Genuine full-stack ability: you design backend services, APIs, and data models, and you build clean, usable frontends.
  • Customer-facing comfort: you have worked directly with customers, partners, or business users, and you can run a requirements session as well as a code review.
  • A product mindset: you care about the merchant and the business outcome, and you can explain why something should be built, not only how.
  • Bias for action: you deliver working software quickly, under ambiguity, without cutting corners on correctness.
  • Integration experience: REST APIs, webhooks, third-party systems, and the debugging that comes with them.
  • Technology-agnostic: you have worked across several languages and stacks and pick up new ones quickly.
  • AI-first habits: you use AI coding assistants, agents, and LLM APIs daily and know where they help and where they don't.
  • Clear written and spoken communication with technical and non-technical people. English required; Arabic is a strong plus.

Who Will Excel❓

  • Previous experience as a Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, or technical implementation lead.
  • Experience in fintech, payments, lending, or other regulated domains (PCI DSS, SAMA, or similar).
  • Hands-on work with settlement, reconciliation, card acquiring, or banking integrations.
  • Experience building AI-powered product features (LLM integrations, RAG, agents, evaluation).
  • Cloud infrastructure and DevOps experience (containers, CI/CD, observability).
  • Experience with B2B, merchant-facing, or restaurant and retail products.

What We Offer You❗

We believe you will love working at Foodics!

  • We offer highly competitive compensation packages, including bonuses and the potential for shares.
  • We prioritize personal development and offer regular training and an annual learning stipend to tackle new challenges and grow your career in a hyper-growth environment.
  • Join a talented team of over 30 nationalities working in 14 countries, and gain valuable experience in an exciting industry.
  • We offer autonomy, mentoring, and challenging goals that create incredible opportunities for both you and the company.

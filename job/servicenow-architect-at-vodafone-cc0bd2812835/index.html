About this Role

We are seeking a highly experienced ServiceNow Architect with over 10 years of relevant ServiceNow expertise to lead the design and architecture of enterprise-grade solutions. The role demands deep knowledge of ITSM, CMDB, CSM, integrations, and service portals, with a strong foundation in scripting and system performance. The individual will be responsible for creating high-level and low-level designs, driving innovation through proof-of-concepts, and translating complex business requirements into scalable ServiceNow solutions.

What you’ll do

  • Lead the design of ServiceNow solutions across ITSM, CMDB, CSM, Event Management, and Service Portal modules.
  • Architect integrations involving multiple systems with ServiceNow as the core platform.
  • Develop high-level and low-level design documents, internal component designs, and technical documentation.
  • Conduct mapping and modelling exercises to support solution architecture.
  • Manage system upgrades, cloning, and performance optimisation activities.
  • Apply advanced scripting skills, particularly in JavaScript, to develop and customise ServiceNow applications.
  • Utilise core technologies including HTML/XML, MySQL, Glide objects, and ServiceNow scripting components.
  • Implement and manage ServiceNow integration interfaces such as REST/SOAP APIs, JDBC/ODBC, JSON, and SAML2.0.
  • Design and configure UI elements including pages, macros, scripts, policies, actions, and widgets.
  • Drive innovation through rapid proof-of-concepts aimed at digital transformation.
  • Communicate technical solutions effectively to key stakeholders and business partners.

Who you are

  • A ServiceNow expert with 10+ years of relevant experience.
  • Proven experience in designing solutions involving multiple systems.
  • Skilled in creating HLDs, LLDs, and technical documentation.
  • Strong understanding of system behaviour, performance, and architecture.
  • Proficient in JavaScript and ServiceNow scripting components.
  • Holder of at least two mainline ServiceNow certifications.
  • Experienced in global corporate services and PaaS/SaaS/IaaS implementations.
  • Able to translate business requirements into sustainable ServiceNow solutions.
  • Technically adept in ServiceNow integrations, UI configurations, and database structures.

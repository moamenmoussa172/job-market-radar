About the job

Do you aspire to be a world class Systems Development Engineer, and learn from experts in the field? Are you passionate about operating highly available and large-scale distributed systems? Do you want to be a part of a fast-paced environment and contribute to one of the most visited sites on the Internet?
As a Systems Development Engineer intern, you would join ML Infrastructure and Platform team within Edge AI (Pylon) under Amazon Devices & Services. The team builds and maintains the foundational infrastructure that enables machine learning teams to train, compile, simulate, and deploy neural network models onto custom AI accelerator hardware. The team’s work directly supports the AI capabilities that ship on Amazon’s hardware products, meaning the systems you help build would underpin how ML models go from research to on-device inference across millions of devices.
Key job responsibilities
- Build and improve platform tooling and services that ML engineers rely on daily, such as training orchestration pipelines, model registry and versioning systems, experiment tracking infrastructure, or automated evaluation frameworks
- Design and implement scalable infrastructure components that support the full ML lifecycle from data ingestion and preprocessing through training, validation, packaging, and deployment to edge devices
- Build and maintain infrastructure that supports the model compilation pipeline, including tooling for converting open-source model formats into hardware-specific representations, managing compiler configurations across multiple accelerator architectures, and orchestrating end-to-end compilation workflows
- Develop and improve the infrastructure surrounding hardware simulation and validation, including systems that run compiled models through functional simulators, perform bitwise equivalence checks between compiler output and simulator behavior, and surface discrepancies to engineers automatically
- Build automation and tooling to streamline model onboarding, reducing the manual effort required to bring new neural network architectures into the compilation and deployment pipeline, including format conversion, weight mapping validation, and architecture constraint checking
- Maintain and improve CI/CD infrastructure that spans a complex ecosystem of interdependent packages including the core compiler, functional simulators, performance estimators, neural network format specifications, and validation tools
- Debug and resolve defects across distributed systems, including investigating failures in training pipelines, compilation workflows, or model serving infrastructure, which requires methodical root cause analysis across multiple services and layers
- Develop an understanding of the team’s infrastructure and its dependencies, including compute orchestration, storage systems, CI/CD pipelines, monitoring and alerting frameworks, and the interfaces between platform services and downstream ML and hardware teams
- Participate in team design, scoping, and prioritization discussions around topics like platform scalability, resource optimization, developer experience improvements, and infrastructure reliability across the hardware-software co-design workflow
- Understand business impacts arising from technical decisions recognizing that reducing model compilation turnaround time, improving simulation throughput, or streamlining model onboarding directly accelerates how quickly new AI capabilities can reach customers on Amazon devices.
A day in the life
As an intern, you will be matched with a mentor who will help you ramp up on the team’s platform architecture and the ML-to-hardware workflows it supports. Your internship project will be scoped to deliver a meaningful contribution whether that is a new platform feature, an infrastructure reliability improvement, a simulation pipeline enhancement, or a developer experience tool that helps ML engineers get models onto custom hardware faster. You will also have the opportunity to engage with other Amazonians for personal and professional development, expand your network, and participate in activities with other interns throughout your internship.
• Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Math/Statistics/Engineering or other equivalent quantitative discipline
• Experience with at least one functional scripting language (e.g., Perl, Python, C, C++, Java, Ruby, shell scripting) and open-source technologies (Linux, Spring, React, etc.)

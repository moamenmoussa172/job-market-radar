About the job

This role is hybrid remote with 60% monthly in-office presence in Mount Prospect, Illinois. (2-3 days in office per week)

In this dynamic, cross-functional role, you will be responsible for new product development by addressing portfolio gaps utilizing the user driven product requirements and time to market stage gate process, as well as life-cycle management of assigned product category. This exciting role will help shift and accelerate the innovation landscape for Bosch power tool development.

You will do so through the following activities:

  • Creating business cases and preparing project financials for new product development
  • Leading roadmap development forkey power tool categories
  • Assisting in the development of end user driven products from concept ideation to commercialization.
  • Driving development of operating instructions with our safety and engineering teams
  • Working with international teams in a personal and virtual environment
  • Assisting with setup of product/pricing information, i.e. Maintenance of Product Databases
  • Monitoring category KPIs to ensure business plan targets are being met
  • Supporting product improvements, address quality concerns and seek cost reduction opportunities
  • Utilizing and implementing agile work methods in order to reduce time to market
  • Passionate about providing solutions to meet end user needs through power tool applications
  • Utilize interactive UX processes to rapidly develop regional and global power too solutions
  • Lead the commercialization efforts for new product launches
  • Train our training team to effectively sell newly launched products
  • Minimum of 5 years of Product Marketing and/or Sales experience working in an international team environment in the Consumer Goods industry (Power Tools industry experience preferred)
  • Bachelor's degree or commensurate experience; postgraduate degree preferred
  • Prior experience working in an international entity with cross-functional collaboration experience preferred
  • Outgoing personality, hands on, driven, able to maintain a sense of urgency and thrive in a process-driven environment
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills; ability to influence leadership and key stakeholders
  • Excellent organizational skills
  • Agile with the ability manage multiple tasks
  • Proficient with all programs within the Microsoft Office suite
  • 30% travel including some international travel

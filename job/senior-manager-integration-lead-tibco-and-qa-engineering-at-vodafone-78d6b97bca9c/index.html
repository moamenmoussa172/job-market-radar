About this Role

We are seeking an experienced TIBCO & QA Engineering Lead to support the VF CZ Integration Chapter – VBTS Transformation project within Telco IT. This role combines middleware expertise, particularly TIBCO, with strong QA engineering capabilities and hands-on workflow development using the Flowable BPM platform.

You will design, develop, implement and test business workflows, ensuring scalable and high-quality solutions that integrate effectively with existing systems. The role also involves end-to-end functional and integration testing, defect resolution, deployment support and collaboration with cross-functional teams as the project progresses towards Agile delivery.

This opportunity is suited to candidates with 10+ years of experience, strong TIBCO/middleware knowledge, QA engineering expertise, telecom domain exposure and an in-depth understanding of the software development lifecycle (SDLC).

What you’ll do

Develop, configure and implement business workflows using the Flowable BPM platform.Translate business requirements into technical designs and BPM process models.Develop and maintain integrations with external systems using REST APIs.Apply TIBCO and middleware knowledge to support integration requirements.Prepare and execute functional, integration and regression testing.Investigate, troubleshoot and resolve defects to maintain solution quality and stability.Support deployment, release management and production-readiness activities.Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver agreed project milestones.Contribute to Agile delivery practices and the team's transition towards Agile ways of working.Support continuous improvement and automation initiatives throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Who you are

  • 10+ years of relevant professional experience.Strong knowledge of TIBCO and middleware technologies.
  • Expertise in QA engineering practices and processes.Experience or knowledge relevant to business workflow development and BPM solutions.
  • Understanding of functional, integration and regression testing.
  • Knowledge of REST API-based system integrations.
  • Telecom domain knowledge.In-depth understanding of SDLC.
  • Experience working collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
  • Graduate or postgraduate qualification, preferably in a technical discipline such as Engineering.

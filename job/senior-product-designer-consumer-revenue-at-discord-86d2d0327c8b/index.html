About the job

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

As a Senior Product Designer on Consumer Revenue, you'll help shape how millions of people discover, experience, and engage with Discord's paid products and offerings. You'll work on experiences that create meaningful value for our users while helping grow Discord's consumer business - from exploring new ways to monetize to improving and evolving existing revenue products.

You'll be involved in the full product development process from end to end, from identifying opportunities and shaping product strategy to prototyping, experimentation, and launch. You'll bring a strong balance of product thinking, interaction design, and visual craft, and partner closely with Product, Engineering, Data Science, Research, and Marketing to understand user needs, test hypotheses, and turn insights into impactful experiences.

We're looking for a designer who is excited by the intersection of consumer behavior, product experience, and business growth - someone who can simplify complex problems, explore new ideas, and design experiences that feel distinctly Discord while driving measurable impact.

If you're excited to help define the future of Discord's consumer business and build products used by millions of people around the world, we'd love to hear from you.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Embed within a Consumer Revenue squad and partner with engineering, product, data science, and research to own the experience of our paid products from concept to experiment to launch - and keep improving them after release.
  • Connect consumer and business goals through design decisions you can clearly articulate, from squad discussions to leadership reviews.
  • Effectively communicate the business and user-centered rationale behind design choices in forums such as design reviews, project-team meetings, and leadership discussions.
  • Use experimentation, funnel data, and research insights to refine your designs and inform ship/no-ship decisions.
  • Deliver high-quality work across desktop and mobile, with comprehensive documentation and interactive prototypes, while contributing back to our Design System.
  • Help foster a culture of design excellence through critique, knowledge sharing, and mentorship.

What you should have

  • 5+ years designing and shipping consumer products across desktop and mobile, including experience as the design lead for multiple squads or end-to-end initiatives.
  • Strong product thinking - you care more about the "why" than the "how," and can turn complex business requirements into simple, intuitive experiences.
  • Comfort digging into experiment data and user feedback to shape iterations and make ship decisions.
  • Mastery of visual and interaction design, with an eye for the details that build delight and trust - especially critical in revenue experiences.
  • Expert-level Figma and prototyping skills; experience working within and contributing to a design system.
  • A working understanding and curiosity of AI-assisted design workflows - from ideation and prototyping to generative UI - and the design judgment to know when AI output needs a human hand before it ships.

Bonus Points

  • Experience building monetization products

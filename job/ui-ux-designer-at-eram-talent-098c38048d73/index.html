About the job

Eram Talent is seeking a talented UI/UX Designer to join our team. As a UI/UX Designer, you will be responsible for creating intuitive and visually appealing user interfaces and experiences for our clients' websites and applications. You will collaborate closely with our development team and clients to understand user needs and translate them into effective design solutions.

Eram Talent is a leading talent acquisition company that provides exceptional recruitment solutions to clients across various industries. Join our team and contribute to our mission of connecting top professionals with great companies.

Responsibilities:

A solid understanding of the user-centered design process and related methodologies. Ability to design highly usable interfaces for multiple devices and channels - internet, intranet, mobile devices, tablets and touch screens etc. Ability to produce high-quality creative designs to tight deadlines.

Requirements

a) Bachelor's Degree in Graphic Design, Computer Science, Human Computer Interaction, Digital Art, Management Information Technology (MIS) or an equivalent major.

b) Minimum 3 years’ experience as a UI/UX Designer.

c) Previous experience in user experience and creative design for web applications, portals and mobile applications.

d) A solid understanding of the user-centered design process and related methodologies. Ability to design highly usable interfaces for multiple devices and channels - internet, intranet, mobile devices, tablets and touch screens etc. Ability to produce high-quality creative designs to tight deadlines.

d) Proficient with design tools (such as Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, Illustrator, Photoshop).

About the job

Overview

Get Paid to Help Shape the Future of Technology

We are building a community of participants for upcoming in-person data collection projects in San Francisco Bay Area. These projects help improve the next generation of smart devices and technology and we are looking for everyday people to take part.

No experience is required. No special skills are needed. Simply attend your scheduled session, follow a few simple instructions, and get paid for your time.

What You'll Do

Participants take part in short, moderated sessions involving simple, everyday activities such as walking, sitting, or moving through a space while smart devices record the session. A friendly moderator guides you through every step. A trained moderator will guide you through the entire process.

Project Details
Job Type: Freelance/Independent Contract

Time Commitment: Approximately 1 hour

Location: San Francisco Bay Area (On-site)
Project Type: Short-term, paid, in-person research sessions

Who Can Apply?

We welcome people of all ages and backgrounds from across the San Francisco Bay Area.

To participate, you should:

  • Adults and minors are eligible (participants under 18 require parent/guardian consent)
  • Be comfortable following simple instructions in English (non-native English speakers are welcome).
  • Feel free to register with friends or family, you are welcome to participate together.

About the Role

The Fivetran information security organization is a motivated and innovative team responsible for ensuring the continuous integrity, confidentiality, and availability of customers’ data. Our customers trust us with all their most sensitive information, and we consider maintaining that trust to be a critical, core component of our product and business. Fivetran is seeking a motivated candidate for a GRC Senior Engineer of the Security Team. The role requires a compliance-focused engineer with knowledge and expertise in building data sets that will be used to turn our compliance into a continuous data-driven function. The position reports to the Director, GRC.
Will build and own a security data lake that aggregates evidence and telemetry from across our environment, and use it to power continuous control monitoring (CCM) that replaces manual control testing with automation. You will also support core GRC operations, including internal and external audits, risk management, and vendor assessments. Acts as a subject matter expert within specific responsibility area(s) and applies internal audit best practices and keeps current with applicable rules and regulations for security compliance frameworks, as well as any authoritative pronouncements issued by relevant governing bodies. Advanced security risk or audit certifications are required and leading cybersecurity certifications are preferred.

This is a full-time position based out of our Oakland office. Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration, including two days in the office each week to connect and build as a team.

Technologies You’ll Use

AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, Secureframe, Wiz, GitHub

What You’ll Do

  • You will build and maintain a security data lake on Fivetran itself, using our own platform to centralize the data that powers continuous control monitoring.
  • Govern the lake itself: enforce data quality, retention, least-privilege access, and sensitive-data handling
  • Build compliance-as-code: automated evidence collection, continuous control monitoring, and policy-as-code checks across cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure), Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, identity, and SaaS systems
  • Integrate and extend GRC tooling and internal platforms with APIs, scripts, and workflows that replace manual audit work and give real-time visibility into control health
  • Support Fivetran's certifications and attestations: ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, ISO 42001, PCI DSS, SOC 2, and HITRUST

Skills We’re Looking For

  • 5+ years in GRC, security engineering, data engineering, or a related fieldUnderstanding and ability to perform control testing, documentation, and control design
  • Strong SQL and proficiency in Python (or similar) for automation and data transformation
  • Hands-on experience with a modern data stack (cloud warehouse/lake, ETL/ELT, orchestration, transformation tooling)
  • Experience automating control testing or replacing manual compliance processes with code-driven checks
  • Working knowledge of several of: ISO 27001 family, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HITRUST
  • Familiarity with cloud security controls in [AWS / GCP / Azure] and common SaaS security configurations
  • Ability to translate control language into technical tests, and technical results into audit-ready narratives
  • Clear written communication and comfort working across Security, IT, Engineering, Legal, and Finance

Bonus Skills​

  • Experience in dealing with external audits
  • Professional audit or information security certifications preferred (CISA, CISSP, AWS, SANS GIAC, etc.).
  • An understanding of control frameworks Fivetran follows: SOC 1/2, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, HITRUST, FedRAMP

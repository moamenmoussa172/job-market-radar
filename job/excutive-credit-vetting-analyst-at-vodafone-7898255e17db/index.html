About this Role

We are seeking a Credit Vetting Analyst to conduct credit and identity verification checks for new and existing business customers across SME, SOHO, LSME and Corporate segments. The role supports Vodafone’s credit risk framework through customer due diligence, credit assessment, credit limit management and fraud prevention.
The role also supports compliance with Credit and Collections policies, Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements, audit obligations and regulatory standards, helping protect Vodafone from bad debt and fraud exposure

What you will do

Complete credit assessments for new business account applications and assess customer creditworthiness using available assessment data.
Review and validate company information and legal entity details.
Conduct credit reference agency checks using approved credit bureau tools.
Recommend appropriate risk classifications and escalate high-risk applications to Credit Risk Management for approval.
Review existing customer accounts where credit limits have been exceeded.
Review and validate company registration, director and business ownership information, proof of identity and proof of address documentation where required.
Verify directors and authorised signatories against approved identification sources and ensure KYC documentation is complete before account activation.
Review requests for new or increased credit limits and support quarterly reviews of existing customer limits.
Ensure credit limits remain aligned with customer risk profiles and exposure levels, with final credit limit setting approved by the Credit Risk Manager.
Maintain complete audit trails and supporting evidence and assist with internal and external audit activities.
Support control monitoring and remediation activities and ensure security deposit requirements and risk controls are applied consistently.

Who you are

Previous administration experience.
Experience reviewing customer documentation and performing data validation.
Experience in transactional tasks and customer due diligence.
Experience handling confidential customer information.
Basic knowledge of Microsoft Excel and Office applications.
Ability to prioritise workload effectively and work within agreed service levels.
An understanding of customer documentation, credit assessment and KYC processes relevant to the role.

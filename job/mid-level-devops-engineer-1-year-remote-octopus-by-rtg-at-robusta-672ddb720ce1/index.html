About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in India.

We are looking for a Mid-Level DevOps Engineer with solid experience in cloud infrastructure, automation, CI/CD, and deployment processes. The ideal candidate will work closely with development and operations teams to improve system reliability, automate processes, and ensure efficient and scalable software delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines for automated application build, testing, and deployment.
  • Manage and maintain cloud infrastructure and environments.
  • Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, and deployment processes.
  • Monitor system performance, availability, and reliability, and troubleshoot technical issues when needed.
  • Implement and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices.
  • Work with development teams to support application deployments and improve release processes.
  • Manage containerized applications and deployment environments.
  • Implement security and best practices across infrastructure and deployment processes.
  • Troubleshoot production and infrastructure issues and support incident resolution.
  • Contribute to improving system scalability, reliability, and operational efficiency.
  • Work in an Agile environment and collaborate with cross-functional teams.

Requirements

  • 3–5 years of experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or a similar role.
  • Strong experience with CI/CD tools and practices.
  • Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud platform such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Good understanding of Infrastructure as Code, preferably using Terraform.
  • Experience with scripting and automation using tools such as Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
  • Good understanding of Linux/Unix systems and networking fundamentals.
  • Experience with monitoring, logging, and troubleshooting production environments.
  • Understanding of Git and version control.
  • Good understanding of DevOps and software development lifecycle practices.
  • Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments.
  • Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.

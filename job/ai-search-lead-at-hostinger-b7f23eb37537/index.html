Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles.

Your role at Hostinger

Join Hostinger's Organic Marketing team and help shape how millions of people discover, evaluate, and choose our products through AI-powered search.

As AI Search Lead, you'll own our AI Search strategy end to end, driving how Hostinger appears across ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, and emerging AI platforms.

This isn't a traditional SEO role. You'll turn uncertainty into experiments, test new approaches in days rather than months, and scale what delivers measurable business impact. Working closely with Marketing, Product, and Tech teams, you'll shape our AI Search direction and drive initiatives that keep Hostinger ahead as AI-powered discovery evolves.

Curious to learn more? Meet your future Team Lead: Emma Young, Head of Organic Marketing

Your day-to-day

  • Lead our AI Search strategy company-wide. Set the vision and priorities for how Hostinger shows up in AI-powered discovery, built around user segments and the full funnel (with products as a strong second layer). Work across Marketing, Product, Tech, Brand, PR, and Affiliates, and make the calls that keep everyone moving.
  • Run a weekly experiment cadence. Study how AI platforms discover, cite, and recommend brands, track platform changes and competitor moves as they happen, and turn them into tests within days. Scale what works, stop what doesn't.
  • Own our footprint beyond our own website. Find where AI platforms draw their consensus from (communities, social, PR, forums) and work with the teams that own those channels to close the gaps.
  • Own honest measurement. Define the metrics that matter, use tools like Profound, Ahrefs, GSC, and Amplitude to connect visibility and citations to business outcomes, and only call something a win when it's real.
  • Build lean systems. Create automation and lightweight playbooks, but only after something has been proven to work.

What will help you succeed

  • 3+ years in SEO, or a proven track record of self-driven AI Search experimentation (side projects, public tests, results you can show), with hands-on experience improving visibility in AI-assisted platforms.
  • Deep, practical knowledge of how LLMs retrieve, cite, and recommend brands, and what actually influences it: entity understanding, citations, authority signals, and off-site presence.
  • A track record of shipping experiments in days, not months: forming hypotheses, acting on imperfect data, scaling what works, and killing what doesn't.
  • Decisive under uncertainty: you make the call with incomplete information, can say how confident you are and what would change your mind, and hold your ground when challenged (even by senior people).
  • Scrappy and AI-native by default: you use AI assistants, scripts, and automation daily to do more with a lean team.
  • Strong SEO fundamentals and analytical chops, with experience in AI Search monitoring tools like Profound, alongside Ahrefs, GSC, Amplitude, or similar, and the ability to tie visibility to business outcomes.
  • Influence without authority: you can move Marketing, Product, and Tech teams without needing a title to do it.

Meet your team

You'll join Hostinger's Organic Marketing team, which helps millions of people discover our products and solutions through organic channels.

The team combines SEO expertise, data-driven experimentation, and emerging technologies to grow Hostinger's visibility and reach new audiences. As AI transforms how people search for information and make decisions, we're exploring new ways to connect our products with the right users.

You'll work closely with SEO specialists and collaborate with Product, Tech, Brand, PR, and Affiliate teams to turn insights into initiatives that deliver measurable growth.

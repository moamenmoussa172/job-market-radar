About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger.

At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries.

The team

To sustain the company's growth, you'll join the Security Engineering team and will be the hands-on security architect for the applications and SaaS tools Ledger runs on: identity, collaboration, customer support, finance and the vendors behind them. You set the architecture standards, take on the hardest threat models yourself, and make sure data exposure through a vendor or an integration is found by us first.

What you’ll be doing

  • Lead threat modelling and risk assessment of enterprise applications, SaaS tools and their integrations, hands-on, starting with the most critical tools and suppliers.
  • Set the security architecture standards and reusable patterns for enterprise systems: identity and SSO, access and least privilege, endpoints, network access, logging, data protection and integration tokens.
  • Review new applications, SaaS purchases and major changes at design stage, and agree the conditions for going live.
  • Design data masking and least-privilege access for customer support and customer-data tools.
  • Use offensive security techniques to challenge every design the way an attacker would, and work with the Red Team and external testers to validate it.
  • Turn findings into owned, dated actions, with the GRC team for the risk register.
  • Mentor the security engineers and help them grow into architecture work.

What we’re looking for

  • 10+ years in security engineering or architecture, including 5+ years in architecture and threat modelling for enterprise and cloud environments, and still hands-on.
  • Solid knowledge across the security disciplines: AWS, networking, endpoints, identity and access management (SSO, OAuth, OIDC), application and API security, and SaaS security.
  • Knowledge of offensive security techniques: how attackers abuse identities, integrations, tokens and endpoints, and how they move once inside.
  • Ability to challenge engineering and IT leads, and to be heard.
  • Fluent English is a must; French is a plus.

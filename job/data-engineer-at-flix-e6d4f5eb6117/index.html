About the job

89-119 PLN/hour

We are looking for an experienced Data Engineer to join Flix on a service agreement basis (B2B contract). This is a contractor engagement for a hands-on data engineering professional who wants to work on impactful data infrastructure powering global marketing operations across 40+ markets.

Scope of Services

Bring expertise in data pipeline development, marketing data integration, and workflow automation. Work across Flix's marketing data environment to build and maintain reliable pipelines connecting data systems to the channels and tools Flix's global teams depend on — from affiliate attribution and ad campaign automation to AI-generated content delivery.

About the Engagement

  • Develop and maintain reliable data pipelines using Python, SQL, and Airflow that connect Flix's marketing data to business-critical channels and tools
  • Build and maintain campaign automation systems that synchronise Snowflake data models with live ad platforms including Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and Microsoft Ads at scale across global markets
  • Contribute to Flix's AI content pipeline, integrating LLM-based content generation with Snowflake-backed data flows and connecting outputs to Flix's web surfaces
  • Collaborate with marketing stakeholders across SEO, PPC, and Affiliate to translate business requirements into scalable data solutions
  • Maintain shared data infrastructure including Airflow platform components, CI/CD templates, Docker image dependencies, and shared pipeline operators
  • Ensure data quality through proactive validation, alerting, and monitoring using DataDog
  • Document pipeline architecture, configurations, and operational procedures

About You

  • 3–5 years of hands-on data engineering experience with Python and SQL in a production environment
  • Solid experience building and operating Apache Airflow DAGs, including scheduling, custom operators, and failure alerting
  • Proficiency with Snowflake or a comparable cloud data warehouse, with working knowledge of data modelling concepts
  • Working knowledge of AWS services and experience with Docker and CI/CD pipelines in a containerized, Kubernetes-deployed environment
  • Clear English communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively in a distributed team environment
  • Experience with ad platform APIs (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Microsoft Ads) or affiliate marketing data platforms such as Awin is a plus
  • Background in marketing data or a related domain is a plus

Collaboration Model

  • Contract type: Cooperation based on a service agreement (B2B/freelance contract), not an employment contract
  • Working model: Independent contractor responsible for delivering agreed services and submitting invoices

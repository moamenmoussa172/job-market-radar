Senior Program Manager – Financial Engineering (FinE)

Spotify · New York, United States

Hybrid · Senior · Other tech role · Posted 25 November 2026

Apply on Spotify's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Spotify's Platform team creates the technology that enables Spotify to learn quickly and scale easily, enabling rapid growth in our users and our business around the globe. Spanning many disciplines, we work to make the business work; creating the infrastructure, tooling, frameworks, and capabilities needed to welcome a billion customers.

Financial Engineering (FinE) is part of Spotify's Platform Mission, sitting at the intersection of technology and finance. FinE's mission is to accelerate Spotify's growth through a trusted financial platform that delivers valuable data and insights to support the business.

As Spotify continues to scale, FinE is evolving the technology and ways of working that power our financial operations, with AI-enabled transformation creating new opportunities to improve how we work.

We're looking for an experienced Senior Program Manager to join the SAFE Alliance, reporting directly to the VP of SAFE. You'll initially focus on driving AI-enabled transformation across Financial Engineering and Finance, while supporting broader technical programs and strategic initiatives across Platform as priorities evolve.

You'll bring strategic thinking, hands-on delivery expertise, and strong change leadership to navigate complexity, align stakeholders, and turn ambitious transformation goals into meaningful outcomes.

What You'll Do

  • Own the end-to-end delivery of complex, cross-functional technical programs, translating strategic priorities into actionable plans and driving execution through implementation and measurable outcomes.
  • Drive AI-enabled business transformation across Financial Engineering and Finance, partnering with key stakeholders to unlock new capabilities and fundamentally evolve how the business operates.
  • Strengthen collaboration between engineering and finance teams, aligning priorities, managing dependencies, and building shared accountability for delivery.
  • Establish and evolve effective delivery practices, including intake, prioritization, planning, roadmap alignment, and operational reviews.
  • Design and execute change management strategies that build stakeholder commitment, drive adoption of new technologies and ways of working, and embed sustainable organizational improvements.
  • Proactively identify and resolve risks, blockers, and competing priorities to maintain momentum across complex initiatives.
  • Define meaningful program metrics and reporting, providing senior leadership with clear visibility into progress, decisions, and outcomes.
  • Champion continuous improvement, using data, feedback, and lessons learned to strengthen execution across FinE and the broader Platform organization.
  • Who You Are
  • An experienced Senior Program Manager with a proven track record of independently leading complex, multi-team technical programs from strategy through delivery.
  • A hands-on delivery leader who brings structure to ambiguity, drives accountability, and consistently turns plans into tangible outcomes.
  • Experienced in organizational transformation and change management, with the ability to influence adoption and embed lasting improvements.
  • Comfortable working in technical environments, partnering closely with engineering and product teams to navigate complex initiatives.
  • Strong understanding of AI technologies and their practical application in technical and business environments, with the ability to identify opportunities, navigate implementation challenges, and drive meaningful adoption.
  • Skilled at building trusted relationships across technical and business organizations, balancing competing priorities and influencing senior stakeholders.
  • Experienced in establishing effective planning frameworks, delivery processes, and metrics to drive alignment and execution.
  • Curious, adaptable, and collaborative, with strong ownership and a willingness to roll up your sleeves. Experience in Finance or Financial Engineering is valuable, but not essential.
  • Where You'll be
  • This role is based in New York
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home
  • The United States base range for this position is $163,412 - 233,447 , plus equity, plus bonus or commission. The benefits available for this position include health insurance, six month paid parental leave, 401(k) retirement plan, monthly meal allowance, 23 paid days off, 13 paid flexible holidays, paid sick leave. These ranges may be modified in the future.

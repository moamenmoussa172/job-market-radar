About the job

Powering Great Work

We started as the place to find a trusted trade. Now, as we shape the future of home improvement, we're building the tech to transform that landscape for everyone - better products, smarter data, AI where it actually helps - so trades can spend less time on admin and consumers can find someone they trust, first time. That's Powering Great Work, and it's why every team here exists:

🔧 Trades spend less time on admin and more on winning work and growing their business

🏠 Homeowners get a trusted trade, first time, every time

🚀 We get to build products that change homes and lives across the country

As our next Marketplace Analytics Manager you'll sit at the heart of how Checkatrade connects homeowners with tradespeople. You'll decide, with the matching team, which trades appear for which jobs, and you'll keep the marketplace in balance: the right trades in the right places, great matches for homeowners, and real returns for the trades who join us. You'll be data-first, commercially sharp, and trusted by senior leaders to tell them which lever to pull and why.

The job, unfiltered

  • Own marketplace health: define what healthy looks like for homeowners and trades, forecast supply and demand by region, category and season, and set clear priorities for the teams that grow our network.
  • Own metric integrity and diagnose what’s driving marketplace performance: define what our numbers mean, find the cause, size the impact, lay out the trade-offs, and track whether the change worked.
  • Decide when to send a lead, to whom and how, so trades see real value and stay engaged.
  • Understand how trades respond to lead delivery, and use simulation to choose which lever to pull and by how much.
  • Own the matching roadmap with Product and Applied Science, prioritised by commercial impact and value to our trades.

The fit

  • 5+ years in marketplace, growth or commercial analytics, with a track record of finding the cause and driving the change that moved the outcome.
  • Super strong with data: fluent in SQL, with a solid grounding in experiments.
  • Commercial instinct: you can weigh both sides of a marketplace and size the revenue and retention impact of a decision.
  • Experience forecasting supply and demand and defining marketplace health measures, ideally with price sensitivity or elasticity.
  • Thrives with ownership: you can influence Product, Sales, trade acquisition, CRM and senior leaders, and explain complex findings in a way that moves a decision.
  • Experience in a marketplace, subscription or lead-generation business is a bonus, as are Python, simulation, and tools like Tableau or Looker.

(Don't self-select out because you don't tick every box. We'd rather meet you.)

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Partner with finance teams across the organization to support capital deployment strategies
  • Lead corporate capital allocation governance processes
  • Support the development of investment business cases and promote an ROI-driven culture
  • Maintain oversight of approved and prospective investments, including periodic tracking, reporting and audits
  • Coordinate and facilitate capital investment approvals across the organization
  • Contribute to the annual budgeting process and quarterly forecasting cycles
  • Prepare financial analyses and respond to ad hoc management requests

How to thrive in this role?

  • Hold a bachelor’s degree in accounting
  • Hold an accounting title (CPA : CA, CMA, CGA) or in the process to obtain it
  • Have a minimum of five years of relevant experience
  • Are recognized for your autonomy, resourcefulness, and collaborative approach
  • Demonstrate proficiency in MS Office and SAP; experience with Power BI and Power Apps is considered an asset
  • Are bilingual (English and French)

THE ROLE

As a Senior Technical Program Manager for Hyperscale Operations, you will serve as the primary technical coordination lead for integrating Everpure’s Direct Flash Modules (DFMs) into next generation hyperscale storage platforms. Working closely with hyperscale customers and ODM partners, Everpure’s hardware and firmware engineering, product engineering, quality, operations, you will drive the end-to-end technical execution required to ensure DFMs are seamlessly integrated into customer-designed storage servers and L10/L11 manufacturing. You will lead cross-functional program execution from architecture alignment and platform qualification through manufacturing ramp, driving issue resolution, root-cause analysis, and technical risk mitigation to deliver reliable, high-quality deployments that meet aggressive customer milestones.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Orchestrate Hardware NPI Execution: Lead cross-functional engineering teams through EVT, DVT, and PVT milestone gates for complex storage server and L10/L11 rack systems, delivering scalable, high-yielding hardware platforms to high-volume manufacturing.
  • Orchestrate Technical Issue Resolution: Lead cross-functional triage across hardware, firmware, product engineering, and quality teams to drive root-cause analysis and resolve system-level qualification issues and platform bottlenecks.
  • Accelerate Strategic Partner Performance: Direct global contract manufacturers and system integrators on technical test coverage, power and thermal validation, and yield optimization to meet strict schedule, cost, and quality targets.
  • Harden Hardware Infrastructure Readiness: Proactively identify architectural bottlenecks, signal integrity constraints, and supply chain dependencies early in the lifecycle, establishing data-driven recovery plans that protect program timelines.
  • Synthesize Technical Trade-Offs: Translate complex server architecture, electrical trade-offs, and program health metrics into clear actionable insights for engineering leadership to drive rapid decision-making in fast-paced environments.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Storage & Server Hardware Engineering Mastery: Deep technical grounding in enterprise SSDs, NAND flash, NVMe/PCIe protocols, and L10/L11 server platform integration with hands-on experience troubleshooting board-level, electrical, and signal integrity issues.
  • End-to-End NPI Program Ownership: Proven track record leading complex hardware programs through design qualification, hardware debug, and volume production ramp within agile and waterfall execution frameworks.
  • Strategic Vendor & ODM Governance: Expertise driving contract manufacturers, system integrators, and component suppliers on technical test plan auditing, thermal/power validation, and manufacturing yield improvement.
  • Cross-Functional Technical Leadership: Demonstrated ability to influence and align multidisciplinary hardware, electrical, firmware, and supply chain engineering teams through technical authority, clear communication, and structured risk mitigation.

We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara office in compliance with Pure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

About the job

Meet the Team

As a Software Engineer SRE at Cisco, you will operate as an experienced engineering professional responsible for driving the reliability, availability, scalability, and performance of Cisco’s global collaboration cloud infrastructure. Operating with high autonomy within an agile SRE pod, you will determine the best technical approaches to solve operational challenges, build automated platforms to eliminate toil, lead high-severity incident investigations and blameless post-mortems, and mentor developing engineers across the organization.

Your Impact

• Software Delivery & Automated Operations: Design, write, and maintain production-grade software (in Java, Python, or Go) to enable zero-downtime deployments, automated canary rollouts, and self-healing cloud infrastructure. Build resilient APIs, microservices, and internal developer tooling to support broader service outcomes and eliminate repetitive operational toil.

• Telemetry & Real-Time Data Pipeline Architecture: Manage, scale, and optimize distributed streaming and telemetry platforms powered by Apache Kafka (and Kafka Streams), Apache Flink, and ClickHouse. Design end-to-end telemetry pipelines to ensure sub-second alerting and high-throughput ingestion of platform health metrics.

• Infrastructure as Code & Cloud Orchestration: Architect and maintain multi-region containerized infrastructure using Docker, Kubernetes (K8s), Helm, Terraform, and AWS. Code in multiple languages across varied infrastructure environments to optimize regional service delivery and ensure high fault tolerance.

• Incident Management, Post-Mortems & On-Call Leadership: Serve as an experienced member in the Tier-3/4 on-call rotation for mission-critical collaboration services. Lead blameless post-mortems, perform deep-dive Root Cause Analyses (RCAs), and track corrective preventative actions across development and operations teams.

• Scale, High Availability & Resiliency Engineering: Design, implement, and validate High Availability (HA) and Disaster Recovery (DR) strategies to systematically identify and eliminate Single Points of Failure (SPOFs). Conduct capacity modeling, load testing, and chaos engineering exercises to gauge system limits ahead of peak demand.

• Platform Visibility & Monitoring Tooling: Design and implement advanced monitoring and observability frameworks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Splunk, ELK) that provide deep visibility into service health, latency, and SLO adherence.

• Security, Governance & Mentorship: Incorporate security design inputs into architectural reviews, maintain cluster compliance, and actively support security incident response. Act as a technical mentor and resource for junior and developing engineers

Minimum Qualifications

• Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or related technical field with 5+ years of related industry experience, OR Master’s degree with 3+ years of related industry experience, OR PhD in Computer Science or related field with 0+ years of experience, OR 8+ years of equivalent relevant professional software / systems engineering experience.

• Software Engineering: Demonstrated hands-on experience designing, developing, and debugging production-grade software in Java (plus proficiency in Python or Go).

• Observability & Incident Leadership: Proven experience configuring observability frameworks (Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, OpenTelemetry), diagnosing production degradations, and leading Root Cause Analysis (RCA) investigations and post-mortems.

• Systems & Operational Acumen: Strong practical understanding of distributed systems architecture, Linux/Unix internals, CI/CD automation, and site reliability engineering best practices (SLOs, error budgets, toil reduction).

• Autonomy & Mentorship: Demonstrated ability to work independently, determine the best technical approach to accomplish project goals, and act as a mentor for less experienced engineers.

Preferred Qualifications

• Distributed Streaming & Data Infrastructure: Proven practical experience operating, scaling, or developing against distributed streaming systems (Apache Kafka, Kafka Streams, Apache Flink, or ClickHouse).

• Container Orchestration & Cloud: Strong hands-on proficiency with Kubernetes (K8s), Docker, Terraform/Ansible, and public/private cloud environments (AWS).

• Professional Certifications: Professional-level certification such as Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS), AWS Solutions Architect, or CCNP.

• Datastores & Caching: Experience designing and operating distributed databases, caching layers (Redis), or vector databases.

• AIOps & Tooling: Familiarity with AIOps frameworks, automated anomaly detection, or AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Claude, Kosmos, Codex).

• Networking Protocols: Deep understanding of high-throughput networking protocols (TCP/IP, gRPC, HTTP/2, WebSockets, SIP, and TLS termination).

• Resiliency & Chaos Engineering: Track record of executing chaos engineering drills and disaster recovery automation across multi-cloud or hybrid-cloud regions.

About the job

Logistics Transformation Project Analyst - VIE Contract

  • Location: Spain, Barcelona
  • Target start date: 01/01/2027

iMove, the Sanofi VIE Program, is available to citizens of the European Economic Area (EU + Norway, Liechtenstein and Iceland) aged between 18 and 28.

PLEASE NOTE that since this program is primarily an international development program, candidates cannot apply to a VIE assignment in their own country of citizenship.

PLEASE NOTE that applications that are only submitted in French cannot be considered by our non-French speaking partners at Sanofi worldwide. Therefore, only applications that are submitted in English will be considered. Please make sure to apply with your personal email address.

About the job

As Logistics Transformation Project Analyst VIE within our Global Logistics Transformation team, you’ll support the delivery of two strategic digital transformation projects: the CO2 Logistics Accounting & Management Solution Implementation and the Coldstream Select Temperature Monitoring Solution Upgrade. You will provide project management support, coordinate cross-functional activities, and contribute to business case development, working closely with the Project Lead, vendors, and internal stakeholders across HSE, Digital, Transportation, Procurement, and SC Sustainability.

This is a great opportunity for a young professional to gain exposure to global supply chain operations, vendor management, cross-functional governance, and sustainability/compliance-driven digital transformation within a leading global healthcare company.

What we offer:

  • An international assignment in Barcelona within a global healthcare leader.
  • Hands-on experience in project coordination, governance management, and business case development.
  • Direct collaboration with cross-functional teams (HSE, Digital, Transportation, Procurement) and external vendors.
  • A collaborative, learning-oriented environment supporting your professional growth.

Ready to get started?

About Sanofi

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main responsibilities:

1. CO2 Logistics Accounting & Management Solution Implementation:

  • Support the Project Lead in coordinating activities across Sanofi teams (HSE, Digital, Transportation, Procurement, SC Sustainability) and vendor (VesselBot).
  • Attend project meetings, capture key decisions and action items, and ensure timely follow-up with stakeholders.
  • Track project milestones against the implementation timeline (Project Planning, Build & Integration, UAT, Go-Live, Rollout).
  • Support coordination of logistics provider onboarding activities and data integration follow-ups.
  • Prepare status updates and reporting materials ahead of governance meetings (Steering Committee, Program Management Committee, Delivery Team, All Hands, Technical Working Sessions).

2. Coldstream Select Temperature Monitoring Solution Upgrade:

  • Support the Project Lead in coordinating activities related to the Sensitech CCM to Sensitech Select upgrade.
  • Attend project meetings, document key outcomes, and ensure follow-through on action items across Digital, Sites, and Quality stakeholders.
  • Contribute to building the business case for real-time logger implementation and control tower monitoring.
  • Prepare status updates and reporting materials ahead of governance meetings.
  • Support tracking of project budget, scope, and timeline (Go-Live Oct 2026, Target Architecture Feb-Jul 2027, Real Time Loggers within 2027).

About you

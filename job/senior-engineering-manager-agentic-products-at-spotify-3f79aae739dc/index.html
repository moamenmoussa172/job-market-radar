Senior Engineering Manager - Agentic Products

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

The Personalization team makes deciding what to play next easier and more enjoyable for every listener. From Blend to Discover Weekly, we’re behind some of Spotify’s most-loved features. We built them by understanding the world of music and podcasts better than anyone else. Join us and you’ll keep millions of users listening by making great recommendations to each and every one of them.

Generative AI is changing what Spotify’s products can do and how they’re built. When listeners can describe what they want in natural language, our products can meet more needs and build richer connections between listeners and the things they love. As Senior Engineering Manager for Agentic Products, you’ll lead multiple teams that prototype, design, and build Spotify’s consumer-facing AI products, and you’ll set technical strategy in a fast-moving space alongside partners across the company.

What You'll Do

  • Lead several teams building Spotify’s agentic experiences and put powerful new capabilities directly into the hands of listeners.
  • Contribute to an AI technology strategy that keeps Spotify innovating in the field.
  • Work closely with a range of product partners to make sure we meet users’ needs in a sustainable and architecturally sound way.
  • Cultivate a balanced, collaborative engineering culture, and develop new ways of working that give teams flexibility and speed without trading off team health.
  • Directly manage around 5–10 people across 3–4 teams.
  • Be part of a leadership team that makes sure we collaborate closely, work in healthy ways, and make forward-looking technical investments.
  • Who You Are
  • You have a track record of delivery. Your teams experiment, learn quickly, and ship products to market, and you understand the value of prototyping and innovation.
  • You’re enthusiastic about trying new things, can motivate your team to adopt new technologies and approaches, and are curious about agentic features.
  • You have solid mentorship and coaching skills, and enjoy helping individuals and teams reach their full potential.
  • You’re effective at bringing several teams together, developing and growing teams and their leaders while leading delivery.
  • You can distill complex information into easy-to-understand concepts, and know how to lead and motivate a team through ambiguity and change.
  • You’re comfortable with uncertainty and provide clarity and direction in complex situations.
  • Where You'll Be
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! For this role, it can be within the North America region in which we have a work location.
  • This team collaborates across the Eastern time zone
  • The United States base range for this position is $203,410 - 290,586 plus equity.

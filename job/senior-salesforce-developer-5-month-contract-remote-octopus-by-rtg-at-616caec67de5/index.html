About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Jordan.

About the Role

We are seeking a Senior Salesforce Developer to join our team on a 4-month remote contract. The ideal candidate is an experienced Salesforce professional who can seamlessly balance business-as-usual (BAU) support with the delivery of new enhancements. You will play a key role in maintaining platform stability while developing and implementing new features to meet evolving business needs.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide ongoing support and maintenance for Salesforce applications and existing functionality.
  • Develop, test, and deploy enhancements across the Salesforce platform.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues, ensuring minimal disruption to business operations.
  • Work closely with business stakeholders, administrators, and technical teams to understand requirements and deliver scalable solutions.
  • Participate in the full software development lifecycle, including analysis, development, testing, deployment, and post-release support.
  • Ensure solutions follow Salesforce development best practices and coding standards.
  • Perform code reviews and contribute to continuous platform improvements.
  • Maintain technical documentation for new developments and enhancements.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of hands-on Salesforce development experience.
  • Strong experience developing custom Salesforce solutions using Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Visualforce, and Salesforce platform technologies.
  • Proven experience providing ongoing Salesforce production support (BAU).
  • Experience delivering platform enhancements and new features in a live Salesforce environment.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience working with Salesforce integrations and APIs is an advantage.
  • Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Comfortable working independently in a fully remote team.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Salesforce Platform Developer I or II certification.
  • Experience with Salesforce Service Cloud and/or Sales Cloud.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and version control tools (Git, Azure DevOps, GitHub, etc.).
  • Experience working in enterprise Salesforce environments.

Contract Details

  • Employment Type: Contract
  • Duration: 4 Months
  • Location: Remote
  • Start Date: Immediate or as soon as possible

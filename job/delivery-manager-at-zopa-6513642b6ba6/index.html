About the job

Our Story

A bank that listens to what our customers don’t like about finance and does the opposite. We’re redefining what it feels like to work in finance. Our vision for a new era of banking puts people front and centre — we’ve built a business that empowers everyone to aim high, every day, to move finance forward. The team

The Delivery function is responsible for the first-class delivery of priority projects across product and engineering. As a Delivery Manager, you will work with product and technical leads and various stakeholders to provide structure, coordination, visibility and momentum for some of the most exciting and impactful changes happening at Zopa Bank. You will work on a variety of projects and engage multiple areas of the business, often expected to lead multiple projects in simultaneously.

A day in the life:

  • Project Planning & Scheduling: You would develop detailed project plans, schedules, and timelines. You would work with product and engineering teams to define project scope, goals and deliverables.
  • Stakeholder Management: You would Identify and engage with all stakeholders, communicate project expectations and updates to stakeholders, and facilitate steering committees.
    Risk Management: You would work with the project team to surface risks and issues. You would develop and implement risk mitigation strategies.
  • Leadership: You would lead and motivate project team members, providing guidance and support as needed.
  • Project Governance: You would ensure adherence to risk and governance processes, and facilitate project governance meetings.
  • Relationship Building: You would build and maintain strong relationships with key project stakeholders, contributors and sponsors.
  • Communication: You would ensure effective communication within the project team and with external stakeholders, and prepare and present regular status reports.
  • Change Management: You would work with key stakeholders to embed change effectively.
  • Coaching & Mentoring: You would coach individuals and teams to address dysfunctions and improve delivery outcomes. You would also mentor junior colleagues in industry best practise and Zopa ways of working.
  • About you:
  • Experience working in a delivery-related role.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with a range of people.
  • Experience managing medium to large projects involving multiple teams.
  • Experience delivering both technical and non-technical projects.
  • Experience working closely with product managers and software engineers.
  • Ability to create and maintain detailed project plans.
  • A keen problem solver who can navigate ambiguity and find practical solutions.
  • Experience managing relationships with senior and C-level stakeholders.
  • A solid understanding of change management principles.
  • Ability to prioritise effectively and recognise when to escalate for guidance.
  • A proactive approach to identifying and addressing personal development needs.
  • Willingness to work from the office at least twice a week, or as required.

Our approach to AI in interviews At Zopa, AI isn't something we're testing out — it's part of how we work every day. As a proud partner of Jobs 2030, we're committed to building AI fluency across our workforce, and we expect Zopians to use AI as part of how they do their jobs. Because of that, we want to be transparent about how we think about AI use during our hiring process. Behavioural and competency-based interviews: please don't use AI. These conversations are designed to understand you — your experiences, your judgment, and how you've approached real situations. An AI-generated answer can't tell us that. What it can do is get in the way of us finding out whether we're the right fit for each other. Technical interviews: it depends on the role. Some technical stages actively welcome AI use, others don't. Your Talent Partner will let you know what's expected at each stage. Where AI is part of the assessment, we'll be interested not just in the outcome, but in how you used it – the tools you chose, your reasoning, and the decisions you made along the way.

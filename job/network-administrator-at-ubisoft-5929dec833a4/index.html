About the job

The infrastructure department of our global network team is seeking a Network Administrator who can bring their expertise to life in our dynamic team of gaming & tech enthusiasts.
As Network Administrator, you will be responsible for installing and maintaining IT solutions and services as well as proposing improvements and optimisations to the current infrastructure. As a senior administrator, you will also be responsible to solve complex issues brought to us by our partners

Mission

Key tasks related to the mission include:

  • Install and maintain various network equipment, including switches, routers, firewalls and load balancers;
  • Deploy and maintain private network links and dedicated peering;
  • Assist in evaluating new requirements, technical design and standards;
  • Participate to the definition of standards, guidelines, best practices and metrics as directed;
  • Drive adoption of automation to enhance operational efficiency and consistency
  • Monitor and troubleshoot network infrastructure and services;
  • Enforce network security policies and procedures;
  • Perform proactive network capacity management activities;
  • Maintain documentation regarding network hardware and software;
  • Assist in evaluating new requirements, technical design and standards;
  • Answer user’s query and service requests;

Tasks

  • Act as a mentor and point of reference within the team, particularly with new administrators;
  • Contribute to the planning of the evolution and expansion of the network & systems;
  • Optimize network performance;
  • Carry out all other related tasks.

Relevant experience

  • Extensive experience as a Network Administrator
  • Expert level in routing technologies such BGP and OSPF;
  • Expert level in switching technologies such as as VLANs, STP, EtherChannel, VTP, and Layer 2/Layer 3 switching protocols
  • Expert level in developing techniques and methodologies to resolve unprecedented problems or situations

Nice to have

  • Good Linux environment knowledge
  • Strong expertise in automation using Python and Ansible, with hands-on experience in CI/CD pipelines
  • Good understanding of other infrastructure concepts like Cloud Network knowledge ( AWS,AZURE)

Assets

  • Ability to communicate effectively with all levels of management
  • Ability to make complex information accessible and understandable.
  • Demonstrates critical thinking and proactively flags governance or prioritization concerns to maintain operational clarity and focus.
  • Honesty, integrity and confidentiality must be always maintained.
  • Have great interpersonal communication skills

Ubisoft Benefits

💰 Profit-sharing and an annual company savings plan. 25 days of paid vacation + 12 additional RTT days.

🎁 50% reimbursement of your Navigo pass, meal vouchers (€9/day), health insurance for you and your family, as well as many additional Ubisoft benefits.

👶 20 weeks of maternity leave, 7 weeks of paternity/co-parental leave.

📍 Our brand-new offices are located in Saint-Mandé (Metro line 1, Saint-Mandé station). A gym is available in the building.

Recruitment Process

  • [45 min; remote] – Interview with a Talent Acquisition Specialist
  • [60 min; remote] – Interview with the hiring manager + key stakeholder
  • 60 min; remote/onsite] – Interview with team
  • [60 min; remote] – Interview with Sponsor

Skills and abilities can be expressed in different ways and may be built through a variety of experiences. That’s why we strongly encourage you to apply, even if you do not meet all of the criteria listed above.

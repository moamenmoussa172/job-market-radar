The Opportunity

Our team owns Oyster’s APIs, connected architecture (including Model Context Protocol services) and identity and access management systems. In this role you’ll build and maintain the Ruby on Rails APIs that help our internal teams get work done more effectively.

You’ll partner directly with colleagues across the business to understand how they work, find where an API can remove manual effort, clarify requirements and deliver real productivity improvements. You’ll take well-scoped projects from design through release, with the team’s support on design choices and on unfamiliar or higher-risk areas. Along the way, you’ll grow your ownership of projects within the team.

What You’ll Build & Own

  • Internal API use cases, from discovery to delivery. Work with internal stakeholders to understand their workflow, define acceptance criteria and break the work into manageable increments.
  • Production Rails APIs. Design, implement, test, review and release changes within established architecture and security practices, raising dependencies and risks early.
  • Secure, careful code. Handle authentication, authorization and sensitive data carefully, and seek review for unfamiliar access-control or integration changes.
  • Reliable, maintainable systems. Troubleshoot defects and API failures using evidence, add regression coverage and contribute to maintainability improvements.
  • Measurable impact. Evaluate delivered workflows with stakeholders to confirm adoption and productivity gains.
  • A strong team culture. Contribute to domain modeling and design discussions with Product and senior engineers. Give constructive code reviews, document API behavior and decisions for async collaboration, support less-experienced colleagues and help simplify unnecessary process.

What You’ll Bring

Essential

  • 3+ years of professional software development experience with a focus on Ruby.
  • Proven experience developing and maintaining Ruby on Rails applications. You can explain your API implementation choices and use Git effectively.
  • The ability to refine straightforward stakeholder requirements into well-scoped work, and to ask for support when risk or dependencies exceed the agreed scope.
  • Strong code quality habits: readable code, automated tests (TDD where appropriate), continuous integration, meaningful reviews and secure coding awareness.
  • Fluency with AI-assisted development, including independent verification of generated code, tests and assumptions.
  • Clear communication and effective collaboration in an agile, remote team. You adapt as priorities change and document decisions so others can work asynchronously.

Nice to have

  • Front-end experience with React and TypeScript.
  • Exposure to internal developer-facing APIs, identity and access management, or Model Context Protocol services. This is not required.

Why Join Us

  • Direct, visible impact. Your users are your colleagues, so you’ll see how your work changes their day.
  • Growing ownership with real support. You’ll own scoped projects end to end, with experienced teammates backing you on the unfamiliar parts.
  • A meaningful technical domain. You’ll work across APIs, connected architecture and access management, areas where careful engineering really matters.
  • Thoughtful remote culture. You’ll join a globally inclusive, async-friendly team that values documentation and sustainable working boundaries.

How YOU work

With diverse locations, cultures, and needs, we created How YOU Work; a program supporting your whole human experience at Oyster:

  • Work from anywhere: Oyster has no borders or HQ. As long as work is timely, your team is supported, and you're authorized to work where you live, you can work from anywhere.
  • Paid time off: Enjoy 40 days off per year (including holidays and vacation), or more if required by your country.
  • Mental health support: Access Plumm, our mental well-being service.
  • Wellbeing allowance: Each month, receive a wellbeing allowance in your ThanksBen wallet to spend on a wide range of options.
  • Flexible parental leave: All new parents are eligible for at least three months’ paid leave, with job protection for up to 12 months or as required locally.
  • WFH stipend: Receive a stipend for your laptop and home office equipment to get you set up quickly.

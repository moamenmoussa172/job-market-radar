Hardware Integration Engineer_EM

Bosch Group · Suzhou, China

Onsite · Integration / API Engineer · Posted 4 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • 支持客户需求分析，负责硬件需求分析和子模块需求分解；
  • Support customer requirement analysis, responsible for hardware requirement analysis and sub-module requirement decomposition;
  • 硬件系统架构设计，主功率拓扑选择，设计，分析和优化；
  • Hardware system architecture design, main power topology selection, design, analysis, and optimization;
  • 硬件模块电路WCA计算，设计分析和仿真评估；
  • Hardware module circuit WCA (Worst Case Analysis) calculation, design analysis, and simulation evaluation;
  • 电路原理图集成，与CAD工程师一起进行layout设计，电子物料受控与维护；
  • Circuit schematic integration, conduct layout design with CAD engineers, electronic component control and maintenance;
  • 项目硬件域批产受控（FMEA、特殊特性定义，供应商评审），支持物料管理以及样品生产等活动。
  • Project hardware domain mass production control (FMEA, special characteristics definition, supplier review), support material management and sample production activities.
  • 产线生产以及客户应用问题定位、原因分析和措施实施。
  • Production line and customer application problem localization, root cause analysis, and implementation of corrective actions.
  • 有车载电源(Flyback,Buck-Boost，移相全桥，PFC,LLC拓扑)开发经验优先；
  • Experience in automotive power supply (Flyback, Buck-Boost, Phase-Shifted Full Bridge, PFC, LLC topology) development is preferred;
  • 熟练掌握电力电子知识，功率半导体器件应用，驱动和散热设计经验优先；
  • Proficiency in power electronics knowledge, experience in power semiconductor device application, driver, and thermal design is preferred;
  • 熟练掌握原理图和PCB Layout设计规则优先；
  • Proficiency in schematic and PCB Layout design rules is preferred;
  • 有模块设计(模拟、数字、功率电路)经验优先；
  • Experience in module design (analog, digital, power circuits) is preferred;
  • 有失效分析(EOS分析，产线支持)经验优先；
  • Experience in failure analysis (EOS analysis, production line support) is preferred;
  • 有功能安全设计(安全概念，FMEDA计算)经验优先。
  • Experience in functional safety design (safety concept, FMEDA calculation) is preferred.

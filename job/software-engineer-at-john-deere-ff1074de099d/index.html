Your Responsibilities

As a Software Engineer for JD North American Parts Distribution Center located in Milan, IL you will:

• Lead/assist with the translation of business requirements into detailed user stories for planning processes utilizing SPM and other technical platforms with thorough understanding of Servigistics system architecture, features and capabilities, variables/parameter setting options.
• Provide ongoing maintenance, support and enhancements in existing systems and platforms
• Provide technical integration solutions with SAP and mainframe systems to ensure transparent technological solutions to bridge processes between technology platforms.
• Drive data feeds scoping. Work with the business for proper data cleanup to ensure the feeds are accurate.
• Supports system variables/parameters setup simulating and analyzing various scenarios for best planning output.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

• 5 or more years of understanding of applications development environment, database, data management and infrastructure capabilities and constraints.
• 4 or more years of experience in the design, development, testing and integration of high complex software solutions in Java web development with Sprint and Restful APIs
• 2 or more years of proven experience in an Agile/Scrum team environment
• Oracle DB, PL/SQL and SQL Skills
• Experience with ETL tools (Informatica, SAP Data Services (BODS), Palantir etc.)
• Operating System level scripting across Unix, Linux, and Windows
• Proven deep experience leveraging DevSecOps and lean development principles such as Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery such as GitHub and Jenkins
• AWS (RDS/S3/EC2)

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Capable of collaborating with multiple business stakeholders to provide information technology solution.
  • Reporting experience such as BI Tools / EDL / R / Python
  • Detailed knowledge of logistics/business processes in one or more of the following areas specifically related to the After Market Service Industry and/or Supply Chain Industry
  • Inventory Management
  • Supply Chain Planning and Management
  • Knowledge of SAP – two years

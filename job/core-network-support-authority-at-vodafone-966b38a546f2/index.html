What you’ll do

The 3rd Line CSDB Voice Core Specialist is responsible for leading, managing and supporting Ericsson CSDB Core network elements and Nokia One-DS and 3DS nodes to ensure high availability, performance, and reliability of voice and multimedia services. This role involves proactive fault finding, incident troubleshooting and resolution, configuration management, 24x7 On-call incident support, upgrades, re-deployment and collaboration with cross-functional teams and vendors.

Responsibilities:
Core Network Support

  • Support CSDB network elements: Ericsson CCDM, CCSM, HSS, CUDB, HLR, vUPG, EIR, USSD, and EDA2 and Nokia One-DS and 3-DS.
  • Ensure service availability and performance through maintenance and incident resolution.
  • Participate in 24x7 On-Call rotation for incident and proactive assistance.
  • Automating the daily and complicated tasks.

Incident & Problem Management

  • Respond promptly to service failures and major issues.
  • Conduct root cause analysis and implement workarounds.
  • Collaborate across teams to resolve problems effectively.

Performance & Availability Management

  • Act as system owner for platform support.
  • Check KPIs and analyse performance trends.
  • Contribute to improvement meetings and ensure changes protect service availability.

Proactive Service Assurance

  • Perform regular health checks and KPI reviews to protect network integrity.
  • Conduct resilience failure analysis and performance management.
  • Engage with vendors for continuous service improvements.

Network Configuration Work

  • Configure new number ranges, GT’s, APNs and Roaming Realms onto the VF network.
  • Certificate management.

Supplier & Third-Party Management

  • Assist in vendor issue resolution and monitor performance against SLAs.
  • Participate in audits and maintain compliance.

Change Management & New Service Introduction

  • Ensure network changes are implemented without unplanned outages.
  • Follow change management processes including testing and documentation.

Software Rollout (LCM)

  • Load new software onto the lab and rollout to live once tested.

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Strong knowledge of CSDB architecture Ericsson CCDM, CCSM, HSS, CUDB, HLR, vUPG, EIR, USSD, EDA2 and Nokia One-DS and 3-
  • Knowledge on upgrades, re-deployment of VNFs, of above-mentioned nodes.
  • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes.
  • Proficiency in troubleshooting using nodes logs, and Wireshark traces,
  • Ability to work under pressure in 24x7 environments.
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills.
  • Adaptability to emerging technologies (NFV, SDN, Cloud).

Must have technical/professional qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Telecommunications/Electronics/Computer Engineering.
  • 7 years+ of experience in CSDB operations.

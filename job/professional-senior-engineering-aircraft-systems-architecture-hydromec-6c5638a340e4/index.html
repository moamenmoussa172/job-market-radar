About the role

We are looking for systems engineers with aircraft level system development and integration experience to join the Aircraft Systems Architecture Development and Integration team in the Research & Technology organization.

As an Engineering Professional Senior in the role of aircraft system architect within the Product Integration (Research & Technology / Aircraft System Architecture Development & Integration) team, you will lead the aircraft-level systems engineering activities in the area of hydro-mechanical systems (hydraulic power, landing gear, flight control actuation), from the definition and optimization of aircraft-level architecture to product integration and validation.

Your role as aircraft system architect will focus on enabling next-generation operational capabilities through innovative system design and aircraft integration solutions. You will drive the development of advanced system architectures and incorporate emerging technologies to support future Bombardier aircraft.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Develop the aircraft-level hydromechanical system architectures, including hydraulic power, landing gear system and flight control actuation, and associated aircraft-level requirements that best respond to the market and program management needs.
  • Lead aircraft-level systems tradeoffs resulting in the definition of the product configuration and architecture.
  • Elaborate and maintain aircraft level requirements with the support of various disciplines.
  • Be responsible for aircraft-level interface requirements and definition (functional, logical and physical).
  • Lead aircraft-level validation and verification activities from the definition to the execution including the associated documentation.
  • Lead cross-system integration activities including aircraft integration activities.
  • Have responsibility for Validation and Verification summaries. Exercise technical oversight of verification and validation reports from various disciplines to ensure completeness at aircraft level.
  • Have responsibility for aircraft-level certification and safety activities and generation of the associated artifacts for the field of responsibility. Need to provide lead role for cross domain/function certification subjects.
  • Technical coordination of aircraft level change management process (cross functional issues) and associated build plan (including V&V regression activities).

How to thrive in this role?

  • You hold a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Aerospace or Mechanical Engineering or a related field. Formal training in Systems Engineering is an asset.
  • You have more than 8 years of relevant experience in aircraft or complex-system development and certification, including systems engineering, conceptual design, architecture definition and requirements-based verification.
  • You have more than 5 years of experience with functional, logical and physical architecture development of hydromechanical systems, including landing gear, hydraulic power generation and distribution, and flight control actuation, and their aircraft-level requirements validation and verification.
  • You have strong knowledge of complex systems development process methodologies and associated practices such as ARP4754 and ARP4761.
  • You are effective at bringing disciplines together, clarifying responsibilities, structuring complex problems, and ensuring that aircraft-level decisions are properly documented, justified, verified, and certifiable.
  • You ideally have some exposure to new or emerging technologies in hydromechanical systems, especially where these technologies affect aircraft architecture, certification, safety, integration, or development assurance aspects.
  • You have practical experience with model-based systems engineering (MBSE) practices and tools.
  • You are open-minded, curious, rigorous and methodical.
  • You strive for harmonious cohesive teamwork and support knowledge sharing amongst team members.
  • You possess strong analytical and systems-thinking skills to structure complex problems and maintain clarity across disciplines.
  • You have solid written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to interface effectively with different stakeholders internally and externally.
  • You communicate technical concepts, project status and risks clearly and succinctly to stakeholders at all organizational levels.

About the job

Develop at Ubisoft is a mentorship program for students and recent graduates (with less than two years of experience) looking to grow their skills in Game Design, Audio, Programming, or Cybersecurity.

This initiative supports individuals from genders historically underrepresented in gaming and STEM.

Develop at Ubisoft is one of several talent programs we offer to engage and support different audiences across the industry.

Please note: this program is a mentorship program and is not tied to any hiring process.

From January to May 2027, selected mentees will work on a project based on a given theme. They will be mentored by volunteer Ubisoft developers, including from our mobile studios, who will provide feedback and guidance throughout the mentorship to help them develop their own personal project.

In the Cybersecurity track, you will complete a series of Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges that simulate real-world security scenarios. You will investigate vulnerabilities, analyse systems, and solve technical challenges while developing practical cybersecurity skills under the guidance of Ubisoft cybersecurity professionals.

Eligibility criteria:

  • At least 18 years old
  • Currently enrolled in studies, or graduated in Spring 2024 or later
  • Fewer than 2 years of professional experience
  • Legally entitled to work in the country of application
  • Applications may be submitted to only one location
  • Individual entries only – team entries will not be considered

This program is designed to support early-career professionals from underrepresented genders – female and nonbinary – in the industry. By submitting an application, you confirm that you meet and accept the eligibility criteria outlined above.

Apply with your resume and cover letter.

Please note that this track is only open to applicants based in Montreal, Quebec.

Application period: October 1st – October 31st

About the job

Mission

Join the most advanced community of AI makers.

New models arrive every month, open or closed: you choose which ones are good enough for production, and you prove it. You build applications and agents on top of large language models (LLMs) and make the model part work. Our clients use these systems to answer customers, process documents and search their own knowledge.

You decide what context the model gets, how it retrieves knowledge and which tools it can call. You measure how good its answers are, and choose the model that fits the cost and quality target.

This role is for you if you want to know whether an AI system gives correct answers, not only whether it runs.

Artefact is the trusted AI deployment team to the world's leading companies. In France, you join its Engineering department, about 300 people, in the AI Applied Engineer chapter and the AI Engineer archetype.

Activities & Responsibilities

Who you work with

Product Engineers build the application around the model; you own how the model behaves inside it. Context Engineers prepare the knowledge your agents retrieve, and Platform Engineers own the shared harness, runtime and model-serving infrastructure; you own the agent logic and behaviour that runs on it.

With the AI Product Manager and the client's domain experts, you define what a good answer is and review results together. You review code with your peers and help newer colleagues.

What you do

  • Build Machine learning applications, such as scoring or time series forecasting, with all the necessary steps: data wrangling, training loops, cross validation, evaluation, and deployment.
  • Build LLM applications and agents: context design, tool calling, multi-step workflows.
  • Design how agents use knowledge: retrieval queries, re-ranking, citations.
  • Build evaluation sets and pipelines: reference answers, automated scoring, review by domain experts.
  • Track answer quality in production and fix what degrades.
  • Choose models by quality, cost and latency. Fine-tune or distil a smaller model when prompting is not enough.
  • Add guardrails against wrong answers, data leaks and prompt injection.
  • Process documents with LLMs: extraction, classification, summaries.

Required Skills

Must-haves:

  • You have built an application or agent that calls an LLM, and you can explain where it worked and where it failed.
  • You write tested, maintainable code.
  • You already use coding agents in your daily work and check what they produce.
  • You measure AI output quality with test sets and metrics, and you know how to limit made-up answers, prompt injection and data leaks.
  • You know what it takes to put an LLM application in production and keep it there: deployment, monitoring, cost and latency.
  • You start from the business problem, not the model, and you can explain to non-engineers when AI is not the right tool.

Bonus, not required:

  • Fine-tuning or distillation of language models.
  • A background in machine learning or statistics, for example from data science.
  • Agent frameworks or protocols such as MCP.
  • Multimodal inputs: documents, images, speech.
  • LLM observability tools: tracing, cost tracking.

You don't need to tick every box to apply. If you match most must-haves and want to learn the rest, we want to hear from you.

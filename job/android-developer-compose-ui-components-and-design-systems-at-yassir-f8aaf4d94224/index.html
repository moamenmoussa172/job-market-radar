About the job

Yassir is the leading super App in the Maghreb region set to changing the way daily services are provided. It currently operates in 45 cities across Algeria, Morocco and Tunisia with recent expansions into France, Canada and Sub-Saharan Africa. It is backed (~$200M in funding) by VCs from Silicon Valley, Europe and other parts of the world.

We offer on-demand services such as ride-hailing and last-mile delivery. Building on this infrastructure, we are now introducing financial services to help our users pay, save and borrow digitally.

Helping usher the continent into a digital economy era. We’re not just about serving people - we’re about creating a marketplace to bring people what they need while infusing social values.

About the Role:

We are looking for an experienced Android (Kotlin) Developer (3–5 years) with deep Compose expertise (2+ years of experience) to focus on building robust, reusable UI components following exist structure. This role is UI-focused and requires strong collaboration with design, a solid understanding of Figma files and design systems, and a high standard for accessibility and clean code.

You will be working primarily on the presentation layer, ensuring UI components are scalable, accessible, and aligned with design tokens and system guidelines, with high performance, and less decompositions if not needed

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain reusable Compose components aligned with a design system
  • Implement UI respecting current structure
  • Translate Figma designs into production-ready Compose reusable component
  • Apply design tokens (colors, typography, spacing, states) consistently in code
  • Ensure following accessibility standards
  • Support multiple screen sizes, orientations, and language direction, and dark/light settings
  • Collaborate with designers to refine layouts, animations, and interactions
  • Maintain high standards of code clarity, modularity, and testability
  • Review and refactor UI code for maintainability and performance
  • Required Qualifications
  • 3–5 years of professional Android (Kotlin) development experience
  • 2+ years experience in Compose
  • Experience working in Design Systems
  • Proven ability to build modular, reusable UI componentsStrong knowledge of accessibility standards and best practicesPractical experience working with Figma design files, including: Components and variants, Design tokens (colors, typography, spacing, states), Auto-layout and responsive behavior
  • Experience supporting multiple device sizes and orientations
  • High attention to detail in UI behavior, layout precision, and animations
  • What We Value
  • UI craftsmanship and consistency
  • Strong alignment between design and implementation
  • Accessibility as a non-negotiable requirement
  • Clean architecture and long-term maintainability
  • Clear communication with designers and engineers
  • Ownership of UI quality from design to delivery

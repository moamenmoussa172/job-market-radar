About the job

We are looking for a Public Cloud Ops Administrator to join our Public Cloud Operations team and play a key role in building, automating, and operating our multi-cloud platforms.This role combines strong hands-on technical expertise with a collaborative mindset, ensuring our cloud environments are reliable, secure, scalable, and aligned with modern operational best practices.

As a member of the team, you will drive automation initiatives, design and maintain CI/CD pipelines, and strengthen our Linux-based and cloud-native platforms. You will also contribute to team growth by sharing knowledge, promoting best practices, and supporting an automation-first and reliability-driven culture.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Public Cloud Operations

  • Operate and support public cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP or equivalent), including monitoring, troubleshooting, cost awareness, and day-to-day operational excellence.
  • Ensure platform reliability, availability, and security in production environments.

Automation & CI/CD

  • Design, implement, and maintain GitLab CI/CD pipelines to streamline deployments, testing, and infrastructure provisioning.
  • Promote automation to reduce manual operations and improve delivery speed and reliability.

Infrastructure as Code

  • Build and manage cloud infrastructure using Terraform, Ansible, and other automation tools to ensure consistency, traceability, and repeatability.

Cloud-Native Operations

  • Deploy, operate, and optimize containerized workloads using Docker and Kubernetes in public cloud environments.
  • Support cloud-native patterns and platform integrations.

Linux Administration

  • Administer and optimize Linux systems (Ubuntu, CentOS/RHEL) with strong expertise in networking, storage, and virtualization.
  • Perform performance analysis, troubleshooting, and system hardening.

On-Call Support

  • Participate in an on-call rotation to ensure timely response to critical production incidents and service availability.

Collaboration & Knowledge Sharing

  • Actively contribute to team discussions, documentation, and knowledge sharing.
  • Support peers and help promote DevOps and SRE best practices across the organization.
  • Experience operating public cloud environments in production
  • Solid background in Linux system administration and low-level performance troubleshooting
  • Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Strong expertise in CI/CD pipelines (e.g. GitLab CI) and automation tools (Terraform, Ansible)
  • Good understanding of networking concepts such as overlays, load balancing, and virtual networking
  • Interest in cloud platform architecture, reliability, and operational excellence
  • Experience with multi-cloud or hybrid cloud environments is a plus
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills in an international team environment
  • Proactive mindset, with a strong focus on automation, reliability, and continuous improvement
  • Good written and spoken English

About the Role

We are looking for a detail-oriented and proactive Global Payroll Analyst to support and manage payroll operations across multiple countries. This individual will play an important role in ensuring accurate, timely, and compliant payroll processing while helping scale and improve our global payroll infrastructure.

The Global Payroll Analyst will partner closely with People Operations, Finance, Accounting, Benefits, and external payroll providers to manage payroll inputs, review payroll calculations, reconcile payroll results, and resolve employee and provider inquiries.

This role requires someone who is comfortable navigating the complexities of multi-state and multi-country payroll, managing competing deadlines, identifying discrepancies, and continuously looking for opportunities to streamline processes through automation and operational improvements.

Key Responsibilities

Global Payroll Operations

  • Support and manage end-to-end payroll processing across multiple countries, including the US, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France and additional international markets.
  • Coordinate payroll calendars, deadlines, employee changes, new hires, terminations, compensation changes, bonuses, commissions, and other variable compensation.
  • Collect, validate, and audit payroll inputs to ensure accuracy and completeness before each payroll submission.
  • Review payroll previews and reports, identifying discrepancies and resolving issues before payroll approval and funding.
  • Ensure payroll is processed accurately and on schedule in accordance with local requirements and company policies.
  • Partner with global payroll providers and Employer of Record (EOR) partners to resolve payroll discrepancies and address employee-specific issues.
  • Support payroll funding, payment confirmations, and post-payroll reconciliations.

Payroll Compliance & Reconciliation

  • Maintain an understanding of applicable payroll tax, statutory deductions, employer contributions, and reporting requirements across assigned countries.
  • Reconcile payroll registers against HRIS records, accounting data, and prior payroll periods.
  • Review year-to-date balances, taxable earnings, deductions, employer contributions, and statutory limits.
  • Assist with quarterly and annual payroll activities, including tax filings, year-end reporting, and audits.
  • Identify and investigate discrepancies related to payroll taxes, benefits deductions, retirement contributions, and employee earnings.
  • Maintain accurate payroll documentation and support internal and external audits.

Systems & Process Improvement

  • Help maintain and optimize our global payroll platform, HRIS, and integrations with other systems.
  • Identify opportunities to automate manual processes, improve data accuracy, and create more scalable workflows.
  • Develop and maintain payroll procedures, checklists, process documentation, and internal controls.
  • Assist with system implementations, payroll migrations, testing, and ongoing platform enhancements.
  • Partner with internal stakeholders to ensure employee data and payroll changes are accurately reflected across connected systems.

Employee & Cross-Functional Support

  • Serve as a point of contact for employee payroll inquiries, providing timely and accurate responses.
  • Partner with People Operations on onboarding, offboarding, employee changes, and payroll-related benefits administration.
  • Collaborate with Finance and Accounting on payroll reporting, journal entries, accruals, and reconciliations.
  • Work closely with Benefits and retirement plan providers to ensure accurate payroll deductions, employer contributions, and funding.
  • Build strong relationships with external payroll vendors and proactively follow up on outstanding issues.

Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in payroll operations, preferably supporting multi-country or global payroll.
  • Experience processing payroll in multiple countries
  • Working knowledge of payroll taxes, statutory deductions, benefits deductions, and employer contributions.
  • Experience reviewing payroll registers, conducting reconciliations, and identifying payroll discrepancies.
  • Familiarity with payroll systems and HRIS platforms; Rippling experience is a plus.
  • Strong Google Sheets skills, including the ability to work with formulas, lookups, pivot tables, and large datasets.
  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple payroll calendars and competing deadlines.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting payroll across the US, Canada, UK, and/or European countries.
  • Experience working with global payroll providers, EOR platforms, and managed payroll solutions.
  • Familiarity with payroll accounting, general ledger reconciliations, and payroll funding.
  • Experience with payroll system implementations, integrations, and process automation.
  • Exposure to benefits administration and retirement programs, including 401(k), RRSP, and workplace pension contributions.
  • Experience supporting payroll audits, year-end processes, and statutory reporting.

About the job

Your contribution to something big:

As a Development and Test Engineer for Hydraulics in Heat Pump development, you will be at the heart of our green-tech innovation. You won't just run tests, you will design the sustainable heating solutions of tomorrow.

  • Pioneer Hydraulic Systems: Lead the development, virtual modeling, and physical testing of cutting-edge hydraulic and heat pump systems.
  • Bridge the Physical & Virtual Worlds: Create robust simulation models (using Matlab/Simulink) to virtually test thermal components, accelerating our time-to-market.
  • Own the Validation Lifecycle: Design comprehensive test plans, build experimental setups, execute physical laboratory testing, and analyze complex psychrometric and thermodynamic operational data.
  • Spearhead Documentation & Quality: Author and review clear technical specifications and system documentation, translating customer and safety requirements into solid technical concepts.
  • Drive Efficiency: Collaborate on cost-reduction initiatives for existing appliance platforms through smart component selection and design improvements.

What distinguishes you:

Education

  • Master of Science or Engineering Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Physics, HVAC Technology, Thermal Power Engineering, or an equivalent field of study.

Experience & Know-how

  • Solid theoretical background in fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, psychrometry, and refrigeration cycles.
  • Practical experience with system simulation tools (such as Matlab Simulink) and testing management workflows (e.g., Atlassian XRay)
  • Familiarity with standard quality and development methodologies (such as FMEA, DFMA, FAM)
  • Prior professional experience in refrigeration, heat pump hydraulics, or HVAC design is highly valued.
  • Proficient with standard Microsoft Office tools (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, OneNote) for documentation.

Languages

  • Strong communication skills with fluent English language capabilities (written and verbal)

Working Style and Methods

  • An analytical, structured approach to problem-solving with a drive to take initiative and own your tasks from start to finish.
  • Enthusiastic about working in a highly collaborative, agile, and cross-functional team environment.

Personality

  • Results oriented and pro-active attitude
  • Openness to occasional travel to collaborate with international teams and test centers

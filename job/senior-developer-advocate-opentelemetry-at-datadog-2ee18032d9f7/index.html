About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Developer Advocates at Datadog are engineers who share their expertise with practitioners across the community, customers and non-customers alike. Our Senior Developer Advocates are technical leaders and mentors who own projects, connect teams, and help engineers solve real-world challenges.

In this role, you’ll build on Datadog’s established work in OpenTelemetry (OTel), working alongside engineers who build and maintain components of the project and fellow community members. You’ll combine hands-on engineering with advocacy, bringing the challenges developers face into the project, sharing what you learn, and helping others contribute. It’s an opportunity to deepen your impact in OTel while strengthening the tools, shared knowledge, and community that everyone builds on.

What You’ll Do:

  • Work with Datadog’s product and engineering teams to turn feedback from OTel users and contributors into product improvements, and bring proposed changes back to the community for discussion and feedback.
  • Contribute to OTel through code, reviews, documentation, and working groups, collaborating with maintainers and helping new participants get involved.
  • Explore instrumentation and observability in AI applications and agentic workflows, using hands-on work to inform upstream development, product improvements, and practical guidance for developers.
  • Create technical content in the formats that best serve the community, drawing on hands-on work and the challenges practitioners encounter.
  • Partner with and coach engineers and community members to share their work through clear technical writing, presentations, and other public engagement.
  • Strengthen Datadog’s participation in OTel by partnering with engineering teams to identify opportunities, support upstream work, and assess our collective impact to drive measurable improvements for the project and its users.
  • Identify and pursue opportunities for collaboration, events, and other community work that builds trust and helps the OTel project grow.

Who You Are:

  • You have 10+ years of software engineering experience, ideally working with distributed systems, observability, or open-source projects, and deep, hands-on experience with OTel.
  • You have a publicly visible track record of contributions to the OTel or cloud-native community, ideally as an active contributor or maintainer in the OTel project.
  • You work comfortably across open-source communities and internal engineering teams, helping people understand different perspectives, navigate technical tradeoffs, and turn feedback into improvements.
  • You have 3+ years of experience creating technical content across several formats, with publicly available writing, presentations, workshops, videos, or other examples that developers can learn from.
  • You enjoy helping other engineers grow through thoughtful reviews, mentoring, and supporting their participation in technical communities.
  • You work hands-on with AI applications and agentic workflows, ideally with experience instrumenting or observing them.

Datadog values people from all walks of life. We know not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That’s okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your experience, we encourage you to apply.

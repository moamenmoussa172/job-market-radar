Channel Sales Engineer, KSA

Location: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

Territory: Middle East

Languages: Arabic and English (mandatory), French (preferred)

The role

Proofpoint is looking for a Channel Sales Engineer to join our EMEA Channel Engineering team, based in Saudi Arabia, with responsibility for developing our partner ecosystem across the Middle East.

Your focus will be helping partners independently identify, win and grow customer opportunities across Proofpoint's email, data and AI security portfolio.

We're looking for someone with strong, direct customer-facing sales engineering experience who knows how to uncover customer needs, qualify opportunities and turn technical capability into business value. You'll bring that experience to our partner ecosystem, understanding how each partner operates, building on its existing strengths and developing its ability to generate new business and expand existing accounts across the region.

You don't need to have held a channel-specific role. What matters is that you've worked effectively through partners and helped them become more capable, confident and self-sufficient.

What you'll do

· Understand each partner's business and build a focused plan: Get to know your partners' target markets, customer relationships, go-to-market approach and the people who influence how they sell and deliver solutions. Understand the capabilities of their sales, technical, practice and services teams, not just in Proofpoint, but across adjacent technologies.

Work with Channel Sales, regional Sales Engineering and partner leaders across the Middle East to turn that understanding into practical technical development plans, tied to the revenue priorities and opportunities in each partner's business plan.

· Help partners identify, qualify and win new customers: Develop partners' ability to recognise customer problems that Proofpoint can address and translate product knowledge into effective discovery, qualification and solution positioning.

Coach partner teams through technical demonstrations, technical assessments and proofs of concept, using live opportunities to build their judgement and confidence. Engage directly where it adds value, with a clear aim of moving partners from joint execution to independently leading technical sales engagements.

· Create growth within existing accounts: Help partners uncover cross-sell and upsell opportunities by understanding their customers' environments, security priorities and unmet needs.

Build on partners' existing practices and expertise in complementary technologies to identify relevant use cases for Proofpoint. Turn these into repeatable account reviews, discovery conversations and assessments that create qualified expansion opportunities.

· Develop partner technical champions and lasting capability: Identify and nurture technical champions who can influence customer decisions, support colleagues and strengthen their organisation's Proofpoint practice. Invest in their development over time, connect them with the right resources and build relationships with the practice leaders who support their success.

Use workshops, hands-on labs, coaching and certifications selectively to address specific capability gaps and commercial priorities. Follow through to establish whether partners can apply what they have learned, not simply whether they attended the training.

· Build partner services and delivery capability: Help partners develop commercially viable, repeatable services around Proofpoint, including assessments, deployment, health checks and managed offerings.

Work with Customer Success and Professional Services to strengthen partner delivery capabilities and support customer adoption, expansion and renewal. The objective is to build services that partners can confidently sell and deliver, reducing reliance on Proofpoint for routine engagements.

What you'll bring

· Strong direct customer-facing experience in sales engineering, solutions engineering or technical pre-sales. Experience in cybersecurity or a related technology segment is preferred.

· Evidence of developing partners, not just supporting their deals. You've helped partner teams improve their technical judgement, lead customer engagements and contribute to new business or account growth.

· Technical credibility and commercial judgement. You can connect customer needs to relevant technology, distinguish a promising opportunity from a poor fit, and explain value to technical and business audiences and teach partners to do the same.

· Relevant security or adjacent platform experience. This could include email, data, cloud, network or endpoint security, or DSPM. Experience with technologies from CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, DSPM providers or similar vendors is welcome. Prior Proofpoint experience helpful but is not mandatory.

· The ability to coach and influence across a partner organisation. You're comfortable working with engineers, sales teams, practice leaders and executives, and can turn an agreed plan into sustained action.

· Professional proficiency in both Arabic and English, including technical discussions, presentations and written communication with partners and their customers.

· Willingness and ability to travel across the Middle East to engage with partners and support regional business priorities.

What success looks like

Your impact will be visible in partner-sourced qualified pipeline, contributions to new-business and expansion revenue, greater independence in partner-led technical engagements, and services that partners are ready to sell and deliver.

Ultimately, success means building a stronger, more self-sufficient Middle East partner ecosystem that consistently creates and captures new opportunities for Proofpoint. Enablement activities and certifications support those outcomes; they are not the end goal.

About this Role

We are seeking a Commission Systems Analyst to support the effective delivery and operation of commission schemes for the Greek market. Based in Albania in a hybrid working arrangement, you will coordinate new commission requirements across the complete delivery lifecycle, from initial clarification and solution design to testing, implementation and post-launch support.

You will connect Sales, Commercial, Finance and IT teams, translating business needs into clear commission logic and implementable system specifications. The role combines business analysis, project coordination, system change management and operational commission support.

What you will do

• Coordinate new commission requirements from initial clarification and solution design through development, testing, implementation and post-launch support.
• Act as a key point of contact between Sales, Commercial, Finance and IT teams, ensuring that requirements are clearly understood, feasible and aligned.
• Maintain effective delivery governance through accurate documentation, status reporting, testing evidence and change traceability.
• Identify and manage risks, dependencies and delivery issues, escalating them promptly when required.
• Support commission reviews, calculations, accruals and reporting for direct and indirect sales channels.
• Contribute to the configuration and ongoing maintenance of commission systems according to operational requirements.
• Provide clear and timely updates on requirements, progress, decisions and outcomes to local and international stakeholders.
• Support stable and timely commission processing through effective controls, documented testing and compliance-focused working practices.

Who you are

• You hold a university degree in Information Technology, Economics or an equivalent field.
• You are fluent in spoken and written English and nice to have a good knowledge of Greek.
• You have substantial experience in channel commissions and IT-related project delivery.
• You can translate business requirements into clear commission rules, processes and system specifications.
• You have strong analytical skills and consistently demonstrate attention to detail.
• You are experienced in coordinating cross-functional stakeholders across Business, Finance and IT.
• You work in a structured way and can manage several priorities while maintaining high-quality outcomes.
• You communicate clearly and transparently with stakeholders at different organisational levels.
• You can work independently and navigate complex or ambiguous requirements.
• Knowledge of financial operations, advanced Excel functionality or automation tools would be advantageous.

What's in it for you

• The opportunity to work with local and international stakeholders across Sales, Commercial, Finance and IT.
• Exposure to the complete lifecycle of business and technology change, from requirements analysis to post-implementation support.
• The chance to contribute to commission processes that directly support customer acquisition, retention and commercial performance.
• Experience working in a complex international telecommunications environment where automation, harmonisation and operational quality are important.

What skills you will learn

• End-to-end coordination of commission-system requirements and changes.
• Translation of commercial requirements into system logic and functional specifications.
• Testing governance, implementation readiness and post-launch support.
• Commission calculation, accrual, reporting and control processes.
• Risk, dependency and change-management techniques.
• Cross-functional stakeholder management in an international environment.
• Methods for improving commission processes through automation and harmonisation.

JUNIOR TO MID-LEVEL - EDI DEVELOPER ANALYST

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

Hybrid · Junior · Data Analyst · Posted 6 November 2026

ExcelJavaScriptJiraREST APIsSAP S/4HANA

About the job

  • Develop and maintain translation maps using Axway Business Object Modeler (BOM).
  • Work with data transformation tools and mapping specifications to convert between EDI and other data formats.
  • Analyze and resolve incidents related to data transformation maps.
  • Ensure the quality and consistency of system integrations.
  • Collaborate daily with global teams on requirements analysis, support, and implementation of improvements.
  • Contribute to technical documentation and the continuous improvement of integration solutions.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor’s degree in Systems Analysis, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.
  • Fluent Portuguese.
  • Knowledge of EDI (Electronic Data Interchange).
  • Experience with EDI mapping or data transformation.
  • Knowledge of XSLT and/or JavaScript.
  • Strong programming logic and ability to develop technical solutions.
  • Knowledge of data integration between systems.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of Jira.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office, including Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Familiarity with AI tools, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or similar solutions.
  • Knowledge of SAP R/3 IDoc structures.
  • Knowledge of EDI standards/formats, including ANSI X12, EDIFACT, ODETTE, and RND.
  • Knowledge of communication and integration protocols such as SFTP, AS2, OFTP2, RESTful APIs, and others.

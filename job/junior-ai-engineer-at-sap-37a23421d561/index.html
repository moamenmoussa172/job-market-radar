About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

We are developing an agentic AI platform for Cloud Operations: autonomous agents that classify alerts, run operational playbooks, investigate incidents, and progressively refine their own skill library. The platform is organised into two layers — a control plane covering agent registration, skill governance, and human-in-the-loop orchestration, and the agents themselves, which execute on SAP BTP / Kyma.

This position lives at the boundary between AI engineering and platform engineering. We are hiring someone who ships agents that operate reliably in production — not proofs of concept.

What You'll Build

  • Designing and delivering LLM-driven agents based on skill-routing patterns (LangGraph, Claude API / Anthropic SDK, or comparable stacks)
  • Curating and evolving skill library — self-contained skill definitions with clearly declared tool bindings, trust levels, and confirmation checkpoints
  • Wiring agents into the systems already in production use: ServiceNow, alerting pipelines, Kyma / BTP APIs, and our internal knowledge sources
  • Contributing to shared agent memory and the feedback loops that lift skill quality over time
  • Packaging, deploying, and operating agents on SAP BTP / Kyma
  • Performing Agent testing and ensure Agents are production-ready, including authorizing tests that assess decision quality, not merely whether a task ran to completion

Results and Outcomes

  • Delivers agents and skills within an established architecture autonomously
  • Performs comprehensive testing on agents they build
  • Effectively works in accordance with established compliance, security and governance requirements
  • Understands ITSM and ITIL practices in the context of Cloud Operations and has them in mind to ensure Agent RoI, performance and relevance

Mindset

  • You focus on efficiency, value add and customer-centric experience
  • You actively communicate and collaborate with AI CoE and relevant teams to ensure compliance and effectiveness of AI Agents developed
  • You document your work — agent designs, skill contracts, and the reasoning behind decisions
  • You reason in terms of boundaries and contracts: what an agent is authorized to do unattended, what it must propose for approval, and what it must never do
  • You embed input from Operation team testing in Agent workflows prior implementation
  • You continuously work to develop a skill definition and initiate data-driven improvements over time

What You Bring

Essential

  • Strong command of Python (primary language for agent work), with working fluency in TypeScript / Node
  • Practical experience building LLM applications that go well beyond prompt crafting — including tool invocation, structured output handling, and agent control loops
  • A clear grasp of agentic design patterns: skill routing, human-in-the-loop workflows, and state-machine-driven execution
  • Working familiarity with at least one agent framework — LangGraph, LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI, or an equivalent
  • Comfort with containers and foundational Kubernetes concepts, since agents run on SAP BTP / Kyma
  • A sharp production-safety focus: recognizing the failure modes that matter — hallucination, tool misuse, runaway loops, breaches of trust boundaries

Strongly Preferred

  • Hands-on exposure to SAP BTP, the CAP framework, or enterprise integration patterns.
  • Familiarity with ITSM and ITIL concepts — ServiceNow, the incident lifecycle, SLA management — since this is the domain in which the agents operate
  • Experience designing how AI systems are evaluated and measured
  • Hands-on experience in Cloud Operations or SRE, with a proficient understanding of what "safe remediation" actually requires
  • A track record of contributing to a shared skill or tool library consumed by multiple agents

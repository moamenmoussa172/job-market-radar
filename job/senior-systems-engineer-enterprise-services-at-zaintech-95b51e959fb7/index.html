About the job

The Senior Systems Engineer – Enterprise Services is responsible for the administration, implementation, maintenance, and optimization of enterprise IT infrastructure and Microsoft-based technology solutions across ZainTECH's customer environments. The role ensures the availability, performance, security, and reliability of critical infrastructure services while providing advanced technical support and maintaining operational standards in accordance with customer requirements and industry best practices.

Working closely with customers, technical delivery teams, Project Managers, and other infrastructure specialists, the Senior Systems Engineer supports the full lifecycle of enterprise infrastructure solutions, including deployment, configuration, migration, troubleshooting, and ongoing maintenance. The role also contributes to infrastructure improvements, technical documentation, and knowledge transfer to ensure effective service delivery and long-term operational stability.

Responsibilities:

Enterprise Infrastructure Administration & Operations

  • Administer, maintain, and support enterprise IT infrastructure and Microsoft-based systems across customer environments.
  • Perform daily, weekly, and monthly operational activities to ensure the availability, performance, and reliability of critical infrastructure services.
  • Monitor system health, capacity, and performance, proactively identifying and addressing potential operational issues.
  • Implement and maintain system configurations, security controls, and operational procedures in accordance with established best practices.
  • Support the administration of enterprise infrastructure services, ensuring secure and reliable access for authorized users.
  • Perform preventive maintenance, system updates, and routine operational checks to minimize service disruptions.

Technical Support & Troubleshooting

  • Provide advanced technical support to enterprise customers through on-site and remote troubleshooting.
  • Investigate, diagnose, and resolve infrastructure incidents, system failures, and performance-related issues.
  • Support major customer accounts through proactive technical engagement, issue resolution, and ongoing service improvement.
  • Troubleshoot Microsoft infrastructure technologies, including Active Directory, Exchange Server, Microsoft 365, virtualization, and cloud services.
  • Escalate complex technical issues to relevant specialists or vendors where required, ensuring appropriate follow-up and resolution.
  • Provide technical guidance and knowledge transfer to customer IT teams to support effective system administration and operational continuity.

Infrastructure Implementation & Migration

  • Install, configure, deploy, and integrate enterprise infrastructure solutions in accordance with approved technical designs and customer requirements.
  • Implement Microsoft infrastructure technologies, including identity services, messaging platforms, virtualization, and cloud-based solutions.
  • Support and supervise infrastructure migrations, upgrades, and system modernization activities while minimizing disruption to customer operations.
  • Configure and optimize infrastructure environments to meet performance, availability, and security requirements.
  • Coordinate implementation activities with Project Managers, technical teams, and customer stakeholders to ensure successful delivery.
  • Validate infrastructure configurations and ensure deployed solutions meet agreed technical specifications and operational requirements.

Microsoft Cloud & Enterprise Services

  • Administer and support Microsoft Active Directory Domain Services, identity management, authentication, and access control solutions.
  • Support Microsoft Exchange Server and Microsoft 365 services, including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, and associated collaboration technologies.
  • Configure and maintain Microsoft Hyper-V virtualization environments and related infrastructure services.
  • Support Microsoft Entra ID (Azure Active Directory), Azure AD Connect, Active Directory Federation Services (ADFS), and Privileged Identity Management (PIM).
  • Administer Microsoft Azure infrastructure services, including virtual machines, virtual networks, storage accounts, and related cloud resources.
  • Support Microsoft Intune, information protection, and enterprise mobility and security capabilities.
  • Implement and maintain monitoring, backup, and disaster recovery solutions using Microsoft Azure and associated technologies.

Testing, Documentation & Reporting

  • Perform system integration testing, configuration validation, and operational readiness assessments for implemented solutions.
  • Prepare and maintain technical documentation, including system configurations, implementation records, operational procedures, and troubleshooting guides.
  • Document infrastructure changes, upgrades, and maintenance activities to ensure accurate technical records.
  • Provide regular technical status updates and progress reports to Project Managers, supervisors, and relevant stakeholders.
  • Communicate incidents, technical risks, and operational issues clearly, escalating matters requiring management attention.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives to enhance infrastructure performance, security, and service delivery quality.

Our Culture & Code of Conduct:

At ZainTECH, we take pride in a culture built on collaboration, innovation, and uncompromising integrity. We are looking for individuals who share these values and are committed to customer-centricity and ethical excellence. All employees are expected to uphold our Code of Conduct, which serves as a guiding framework for responsible behavior across everything we do — from ho we work with each other to how we engage with clients and partners globally.

Requirements

Experience & Qualifications

  • 4–6 years of experience in Microsoft infrastructure administration, enterprise systems engineering, or related IT infrastructure roles.
  • Hands-on experience administering and supporting Microsoft Active Directory Domain Services, including user management, authentication, and directory services.
  • Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Exchange Server and Microsoft 365 services, including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, and related collaboration platforms.
  • Experience with Microsoft Hyper-V virtualization technologies.
  • Working knowledge of Active Directory Federation Services (ADFS) and hybrid identity environments.
  • Experience configuring and supporting Microsoft SQL Server infrastructure.
  • Familiarity with Microsoft System Center products and enterprise infrastructure management technologies.
  • Hands-on knowledge of Microsoft Azure services, including:
  • Azure Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS): Virtual Machines, Virtual Networks, Storage Accounts, and related infrastructure services.
  • Identity & Access Management: Microsoft Entra ID, Privileged Identity Management (PIM), and Azure AD Connect.
  • Enterprise Mobility & Security: Microsoft Intune, Azure Rights Management, and associated security capabilities.
  • Monitoring & Operations: Azure Monitor, Log Analytics, and related infrastructure monitoring services.
  • Backup & Disaster Recovery: Azure Site Recovery and Azure Backup.
  • Experience supporting infrastructure implementation, system upgrades, migrations, and technical troubleshooting.
  • Good understanding of enterprise infrastructure security, system availability, performance optimization, and operational best practices.
  • Strong technical documentation and reporting skills.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills in both English and Arabic.
  • Bachelor's degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.

About the job

Linedata is seeking an experienced Product Manager who combines direct commercial banking or lending experience with strategic product management expertise.

The ideal candidate began their career working in commercial lending, credit analysis, underwriting, loan origination, or credit risk within a bank or lending institution before transitioning into a Product Management role within financial technology.

We are looking for a commercially minded Product Manager who understands how banks operate, the challenges facing lending professionals, and how technology can improve lending processes.

This is a business-focused, strategic Product Management position, responsible for identifying market opportunities, understanding customer needs, shaping product strategy, and helping drive the roadmap for Linedata's Capitalstream lending platform.

The Product Manager will work closely with customers, sales, product leadership, and engineering to ensure product investments deliver meaningful value to financial institutions and support Linedata's commercial objectives.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities

Product Strategy & Roadmap

Help define and execute the Capitalstream product strategy and roadmap based on market opportunities, customer needs, and business priorities.

Apply firsthand banking and lending experience to identify opportunities to improve commercial lending operations and workflows.

Evaluate market trends, customer feedback, competitive offerings, and industry developments to identify opportunities for new product capabilities.

Prioritize product investments based on customer value, market demand, competitive differentiation, and commercial potential.

Partner with product leadership to establish product priorities and support long-term growth objectives.

Contribute to product positioning and differentiation within the commercial lending technology market.

Commercial Lending Expertise & Customer Engagement

Serve as a subject matter expert in commercial lending, credit analysis, underwriting, loan origination, and credit risk.

Engage directly with commercial lenders, credit officers, underwriters, and banking executives to understand their business challenges and technology needs.

Translate banking workflows and customer requirements into product opportunities and business requirements.

Identify gaps in existing product capabilities and recommend enhancements that improve customer outcomes.

Develop strong customer relationships and maintain an understanding of evolving banking practices and industry regulations.

Product Management & Cross-Functional Collaboration

Lead product discovery and requirements discussions with customers and internal stakeholders.

Define business requirements and product priorities that guide development efforts.

Collaborate with engineering and product delivery teams to ensure solutions address customer and business objectives.

Evaluate proposed product enhancements based on customer impact, business value, and market relevance.

Support product launches and communicate product direction, capabilities, and benefits to internal teams and customers.

Work closely with sales, marketing, professional services, and support to identify customer needs and opportunities for product improvement.

Commercial & Market Support

Support sales teams in customer and prospect discussions, bringing lending industry knowledge and product expertise.

Contribute to product positioning, competitive analysis, and customer value propositions.

Identify opportunities to expand product adoption, enhance customer satisfaction, and support revenue growth.

Provide input into product investment decisions, pricing considerations, and commercial priorities.

Evaluate strategic partnerships and third-party solutions that complement Capitalstream's offerings.

Required Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field.

Direct experience working for a bank or lending institution in commercial lending, credit analysis, underwriting, loan origination, or credit risk.

Subsequent experience in strategic Product Management within financial technology, preferably supporting commercial lending or credit management solutions.

Demonstrated career progression from a hands-on banking or lending position into a business-focused Product Management role.

Strong understanding of the commercial lending lifecycle, including origination, underwriting, credit assessment, approval, pricing, collateral, documentation, and funding.

Proven experience defining product strategy, influencing roadmaps, identifying market opportunities, and prioritizing product enhancements.

Ability to translate banking workflows, customer needs, and market opportunities into business requirements and product direction.

Experience engaging directly with banking customers and senior business stakeholders.

Strong commercial awareness, strategic thinking, and decision-making skills.

Excellent communication, presentation, and relationship-building abilities.

Ability to influence cross-functional teams and collaborate effectively with product leadership, sales, and engineering.

Ability to work independently and manage competing priorities.

Willingness to travel approximately 5–10%.

Preferred Qualifications

MBA or relevant advanced degree.

Five or more years of direct commercial banking, underwriting, credit analysis, or lending experience.

Five or more years of strategic Product Management experience within financial technology.

Experience managing commercial lending, loan origination, or credit risk software products.

Experience contributing to product strategy, commercial planning, pricing, or product investment decisions.

Knowledge of banking regulations, credit policies, and commercial lending market trends.

Familiarity with AI and automation opportunities within commercial lending.

Experience evaluating third-party partnerships or financial technology providers.

Compensation & Benefits

The base salary for this role ranges from $135,000 to a maximum of $170,000. Final compensation will be determined based on experience, track record, and market expertise.

Your total compensation includes:

  • Base salary
  • Performance-based bonus

Comprehensive benefits package including extended health and dental coverage, paid time off, wellness programs, and participation in applicable government-sponsored benefits such as the Canada Pension Plan (CPP) and Employment Insurance (EI).

Linedata regularly reviews compensation programs to support equity, reward performance, and reflect evolving market conditions.

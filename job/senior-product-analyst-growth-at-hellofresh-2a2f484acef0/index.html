About the job

About the role: What's in the Box

As a Senior Product Analyst, you will be a trusted analytical partner to the Growth Alliance product managers, turning deep dives into customer behavior into decisions that improve the HelloFresh product and drive growth. Data-driven decision making is at the core of our company culture and your strong experience and knowledge across analytical and business related topics will enable us to make better and faster decisions. You will work with product teams across the Growth Alliance - for example Conversions, Payments, Pricing/Benefits, Communications and Adtech - by proactively identifying opportunities, advising on experiments and building self-serve analytics and automations that scale. You will be an influential member of the Growth Analytics team, shaping the analytical strategy of the product areas you support, mentoring other team members and driving the adoption of new technologies and AI-enabled ways of working.

What you’ll do: The Recipe

  • Deep-Dive into Customer Behavior: Explore and analyze customer behavior and product performance data across the funnel to detect growth opportunities. You will turn complex findings into clear recommendations that product managers can act on.
  • Advise on Experimentation: Partner with product managers to design A/B experiments, define success metrics and sample sizes, and interpret results. You will go beyond the headline number to understand what drove the impact and what to test next.
  • Build Scalable Self-Serve Analytics: Create dashboards, automations and reusable data products that let product managers and analysts answer recurring questions independently. You will work with analytics engineers on the underlying data models and keep what you build accurate and easy to use.
  • Scale Insight Generation with GenAI: Apply GenAI and LLM-integrated workflows to automate analysis and expand the insights available to the product organization. You will validate the quality and accuracy of AI-generated outputs before they reach stakeholders.
  • Shape Product Decisions with Evidence: Influence product priorities and roadmaps by bringing data-backed evidence, and promote a culture of evidence-based decision making. You will challenge proposals and conclusions the evidence does not support, and communicate the so-what clearly to product, engineering and UX stakeholders.
  • Plan & Deliver Cross-Functional Projects: Partner with Alliance product managers to gather requirements, define objectives and translate business goals into analytical plans. You will apply project management skills to deliver data-driven projects with clear ownership and timelines.
  • Own Data Quality & Tracking: Work with analytics engineers and data engineers to make sure the tracking and data behind your analyses are accurate, documented and trusted. You will be responsible for finding paths forward in ambiguous situations where both the "what" and the "how" may be unclear.
  • Mentor & Support the Team: Proactively mentor other product analysts. You will propose and drive changes in ways of working to improve delivery speed and analytical quality across the team.

What you’ll bring: The Ingredients

  • Experience in Product Analytics: 5+ years of experience in quantitative analysis roles, including at least 3 years working closely with product teams, with a proven track record of owning analyses from question to recommendation.
  • Technical Analytics Skills: Advanced SQL and Python skills for both analysis and efficient manipulation of large data sets. Ideally experience working with Databricks.
  • AI Proficiency: Day-to-day experience in an AI-first environment, comfortable using AI tools and agents to automate and scale analysis. Expertise with GenAI for analytics and LLM-integrated workflows, including how to evaluate the quality of AI-generated insights.
  • Experimentation Expertise: Experience setting up A/B experiments with product stakeholders, applying sound statistical methods, interpreting results and deep-diving into the impact of individual experiments.
  • Strong Communication & Influence: Proven ability to take complex business problems, frame them as analytical problems and present findings clearly and precisely, influencing product managers and aligning stakeholders on actions.
  • Data Visualization & Self-Serve: Advanced knowledge of visualization tools such as Databricks dashboards, used to build self-serve dashboards that convey complex data clearly and keep stakeholders informed without manual reporting.
  • Pragmatism & Flexibility: While you apply rigorous methods, you have the business acumen to know when a fast directional answer serves the business better than a perfect one.
  • Outcome Orientation & Change Agency: You don’t just accept the status quo; you actively seek to automate repetitive work, simplify reporting and turn insights into actions that create measurable business value.

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at global scale, supporting 6+ millions of customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to workspace at Warsaw centre. The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

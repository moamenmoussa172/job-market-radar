About the job

Senior Country Engineering Process and Project Manager oversees a team of Maintenance and Process Engineers, effectively bridging the gap between technical execution and business strategy. Role involves two Technology sites above 60k sqm and 700+ employees with the logistics and manufacturing activities. Acting as both a strategic leader and a mentor, this role guides the engineering team through the design, development, and delivery of complex manufacturing projects while actively fostering individual career growth. This position blends leadership, coaching, robust project planning, and technical oversight to ensure high performance and the delivery of quality products. This position will actively support New Product Introduction (NPI) and the new site launch process across our Technology Sites in Poland. For Zary site position will also act as a security in-charge designated lead having mandated additional responsibilities to meet Group requirements.

About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :

  • Own and prioritize the team backlog: ServiceNow features (catalog items, flows, portal and ITSM enhancements), ATF suites and CI/CD quality gates.
  • Turn business needs into clear epics, stories and acceptance criteria with stakeholders, developers and testers, including the automated tests each story needs.
  • Accept delivered work against acceptance criteria and test results, and plan releases with the team.
  • Track delivery and quality metrics (velocity, automation coverage, pass rates, defect leakage) and use them to set priorities.
  • Make sure features and regression suites are ready for platform upgrades and transition wave go-lives.

Educational qualification:

B.E - Computer Science or IT

Experience :

  • 6 to 10 years in IT, with at least 3 years of ServiceNow development or testing and at least 2 years as a Product Owner or team lead.
  • Good knowledge of ServiceNow development: Service Catalog, Workflow Studio/Flow Designer, portals, integrations and ITSM.
  • Hands-on ATF knowledge (test steps, suites, templates, scheduled runs).
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and Agile DevOps teams.

Mandatory/requires Skills :
Product Management, Leading Teams

Preferred Skills :
PSPO, CSPO or SAFe POPM; ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA); Certified Application Developer (CAD) or ISTQB Foundation.

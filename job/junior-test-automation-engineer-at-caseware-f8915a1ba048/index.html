About the job

Caseware combines over 35 years of industry expertise with AI-powered technology to transform how audit and accounting professionals work. Serving organizations across 130 countries, we bring intelligent automation directly into audit and financial reporting workflows—helping professionals reduce manual work, navigate complexity, and focus on informed decisions. Join our global team and help build technology that strengthens trust in financial information.

The Software Developer in Test is part of the Caseware team. Our platform, which streamlines and automates many of the tasks that accountants, auditors, and tax professionals perform every day around the world, supports the development of additional applications, add-ins, and extensions to meet the needs of these professionals across industries globally.

📍 Copenhagen, Denmark.

Contact: Dana Liulica - Talent Acquisition Partner

What you will do:

  • Design, implement, and maintain automated testing frameworks for Caseware’s web-based SaaS products
  • Perform and coordinate functional, integration, regression, and exploratory testing
  • Collaborate with developers and product specialists to refine user stories, acceptance criteria, and testability
  • Analyze requirements and technical designs to identify risk areas and critical test scenarios
  • Investigate, document, and track defects with clear reproduction steps and impact analysis
  • Improve test coverage, test reliability, and overall QA processes
  • Mentor and coach other QA peers on regional nuances
  • Promote and advocate for quality best practices across the engineering organization
  • What you will bring: Mid-level (3–5 years) experience in QA, test automation, or SDET roles
  • Demonstrable experience testing complex web applications, preferably SaaS products
  • Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills, with a structured approach to problem-solving
  • Hands-on experience with test automation using modern tools and frameworks, specifically Cypress
  • Solid understanding of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Agile methodologies
  • Ability to write clear, maintainable, and well-structured test cases and code
  • Comfortable working within cross-functional, international teams
  • Strong coordination skills and the ability to promote continuous QA awareness within the team
  • Good command of both Danish and English
  • Nice to have:
  • Experience in domains such as audit, accounting, financial reporting, or compliance software
  • Experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., GitHub Actions) and Atlassian products such as Jira and Confluence
  • ISTQB or other QA-related certifications
  • Knowledge of cloud platforms and modern web architectures
  • Experience contributing to test strategies or quality improvement initiatives
  • What's in it for you:
  • Shape what’s next with AI: Help transform audit and accounting through AI-powered technology. Explore new ways to bring AI into our own workflows, rethink how work gets done, and turn ideas into meaningful improvements for our teams and customers.
  • Make an impact that matters: Contribute to solutions that help professionals navigate complexity, make informed decisions, and strengthen trust in financial information around the world.
  • Bring your voice. Expand your perspective: Collaborate with a diverse global team where your ideas are valued, knowledge is shared, and different perspectives lead to better outcomes.
  • Build a career that grows with you: Develop your skills, take on new challenges, and explore career opportunities as our business and technology evolve.
  • Thrive at work and beyond: Enjoy competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits, recognition programs, and performance bonuses, alongside flexible work options, remote opportunities, and generous time off.

Job Description:

The Opportunity

As Global Head of Technology, you will lead the identification, exploration, and implementation of technology and product development strategies for Hitachi Energy's Business Unit Transformers, helping to strengthen and secure the company's global market position and profitability over the medium and long term. As a key member of the leadership team, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the future technology roadmap while advancing your career in a highly visible global leadership position. This is a unique opportunity to drive innovation, inspire high-performing teams, and deliver transformational results within a forward-thinking organization. The location for this role is flexible globally and will be determined during the selection process.

How you will make an impact

  • Shape and execute the strategic direction of R&D in coordination with Company R&D and the business unit’s management team.
  • Develop and oversee a cutting-edge technology roadmap that drives innovation, supports growth, and keeps the business ahead of market trends.
  • Manage large-scale R&D budgets and resources to deliver impactful, time-sensitive projects that align with strategic priorities.
  • Lead transformative innovation initiatives by fostering a culture of creativity, intrapreneurship, and continuous improvement.
  • Drive cross-functional collaboration to ensure seamless project execution, adherence to standards, and optimal delivery within scope and budget.
  • Cultivate strategic partnerships with global research institutions, industry stakeholders, and external collaborators to accelerate technological advancements.
  • Develop and implement robust IP strategies to protect competitive assets and proactively manage associated risks. Inspire and develop a high-performing R&D team, cultivating leadership, collaboration, and a relentless focus on breakthrough solutions in the energy sector.

Your background

  • Master’s or Doctorate degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Minimum of 20 years of experience in R&D, technology management, or product development, with at least 7 years in a senior leadership role.
  • Strategic thinking with strong ability to develop and execute technology roadmaps.
  • Proven leadership in managing multidisciplinary and multicultural teams.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Strong innovation mindset and experience in translating market trends into technology
  • undefined

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

We are currently looking for a highly skilled UI Designer to join our team and help enhance the visual appeal and user-friendliness of our interfaces. At Jisr, we strive to deliver exceptional user experiences, and as a UI Designer, you will play a crucial role in achieving this goal. Working closely with our Product Designers, UX Researchers and Frontend Engineers, you will have the opportunity to contribute to the development of a cutting-edge user interface that sets us apart from the competition.

In this role, you will have the responsibility of maintaining and enhancing our existing design system, micro-interactions and the interface overall, ensuring that our products are consistent and of the highest quality. We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented individual who is passionate about creating innovative and intuitive designs.

  • Collaborate with Product Designers, UX Researchers to understand user needs and translate them into innovative and visually appealing designs
  • Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes to communicate design direction and interactions to stakeholders
  • Conduct usability testing and gather user feedback to refine and improve designs
  • Collaborate with the development team to ensure the successful implementation of the designs
  • Maintain and enhance the design system, including UI components, style guides, and patterns
  • Ensure consistency and quality across all products and platforms
  • Proven experience as a UI Designer with a strong portfolio showcasing your work.
  • Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, or Principle.
  • Familiarity with design systems and atomic design principles.
  • Understanding of responsive design principles.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to articulate design decisions.

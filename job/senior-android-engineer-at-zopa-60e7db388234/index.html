About the job

The team

We're on a mission to take our mobile app to the next level, and we're looking for someone to help shape the future of our Android experience in Kotlin. You'll join one of our mobile engineering teams, working across the technical foundations that power the app and the customer experiences built on top of them: architecture, shared libraries, core experiences, build systems, analytics, and the features our customers use every day.

Depending on where your experience and our needs best align, you could focus more on shared foundations (app architecture, our design system, observability, developer tooling and CI/CD), or more on owning and evolving customer journeys end to end. Wherever you land, you'll help shape the direction of mobile engineering at Zopa, raise the bar on quality and developer experience, and work closely with product, design and engineering partners to deliver an app that is reliable, scalable and a pleasure to use.

Our app is built natively in Swift and Kotlin, and we're continuing to invest in strong native foundations and a best-in-class Android experience. This is an exciting opportunity to be part of that journey!

A day in the life:

  • You will report to the Engineering Manager for the Zopa mobile team.
  • You will work closely with other native mobile engineers to design, build, and evolve our Android app.
  • You will play a key role in evolving our native Android app, building in Kotlin with strong architectural foundations and industry best practices.
  • You will collaborate with the wider mobile engineering community to define best practices for native development and mentor engineers as they grow their native Android skills.
  • You will improve the performance, robustness, and overall quality of the app by identifying bottlenecks, addressing technical debt, and fixing defects to optimise the user experience.
  • You will collaborate with cross-functional partners – including product managers, designers, and backend engineers to design and deliver features that fully leverage native Android capabilities.
  • About you:
  • You are an experienced mobile app developer with a strong track record of building and shipping production Android applications in Kotlin, including publishing apps to the Google Play Store.
  • You have a solid understanding of Android frameworks, platform APIs, and native app development best practices.
  • You have worked on modern, high-engagement mobile applications, with a strong focus on performance, reliability, and delivering delightful user experiences.
  • You treat quality as a first-class concern and have a proven history of raising engineering standards through thoughtful design, testing, and maintainable code. You take pride in your craft and in doing things right.
  • You are motivated by opportunities to have broad impact, introducing better processes, tooling, and automation to raise the bar for mobile engineering across teams.
  • You have a strong customer focus and a genuine interest in Zopa's mission.
  • Added bonus (but not essential):
  • Experience applying mobile security best practices in production Android applications.
  • Experience working with CI/CD pipelines for mobile apps, including build, test, and release automation.
  • Experience collaborating closely with iOS (Swift) and/or React Native engineers in a cross-platform mobile environment.

Our approach to AI in interviews At Zopa, AI isn't something we're testing out — it's part of how we work every day. As a proud partner of Jobs 2030, we're committed to building AI fluency across our workforce, and we expect Zopians to use AI as part of how they do their jobs. Because of that, we want to be transparent about how we think about AI use during our hiring process. Behavioural and competency-based interviews: please don't use AI. These conversations are designed to understand you — your experiences, your judgment, and how you've approached real situations. An AI-generated answer can't tell us that. What it can do is get in the way of us finding out whether we're the right fit for each other. Technical interviews: it depends on the role. Some technical stages actively welcome AI use, others don't. Your Talent Partner will let you know what's expected at each stage. Where AI is part of the assessment, we'll be interested not just in the outcome, but in how you used it – the tools you chose, your reasoning, and the decisions you made along the way.

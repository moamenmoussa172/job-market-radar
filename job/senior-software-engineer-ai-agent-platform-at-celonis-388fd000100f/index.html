About the job

Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet.

The Team:

We build the engineering foundations that enable AI agents to work with enterprise tools and workflows.

Our work focuses on the Agent Platform, which coordinates agent execution and provides controlled access to tools and execution environments. Our platform directly powers the Celonis Context Model, meaning we work as a product team in close collaboration with product managers and other roles rather than an infrastructure team. Expect to work close to the end-user experience, shipping tangible product improvements in rapid iteration loops.

The Role:

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design, build, and operate backend services that serve the platform with the AI capabilities they need to perform useful work.

You will own complex features from understanding the problem and defining the technical approach through implementation, deployment, and ongoing improvement. The work combines Python development, asynchronous execution, data processing, and practical application of large language models.

You will help the team make thoughtful architectural decisions, balance delivery with maintainability, and turn evolving requirements into dependable software.

The work you'll do:

  • Build agent execution capabilities: Develop services for agent sessions, execution state, streaming responses, tool integrations, and controlled execution environments.
  • Design for reliable execution: Handle concurrency, cancellation, retries, partial failures, and recovery so that long-running operations behave predictably.
  • Protect customer data and actions: Implement authorization, tenant isolation, input validation, and appropriate controls around agent access to tools.
  • Own complex features through delivery: Clarify requirements with stakeholders, document design choices, break work into manageable increments, and follow through to production.
  • Measure and improve quality: Develop automated tests and evaluations, investigate failures, and improve latency, resource usage, and operational visibility.
  • Strengthen the engineering foundation: Refactor complex code where needed, improve development and deployment tooling, and contribute reusable APIs and libraries.
  • Help the team succeed: Review code, share knowledge, support less experienced engineers, and work with product and engineering partners to define success.

The qualifications you'll need:

  • Substantial professional experience building and operating backend software with Python, typically 5+ years, with demonstrated ownership of complex features or services.
  • Proven track record in technical architecture, high-level software design, and scalable system patterns.
  • Practical experience deploying agentic frameworks, machine learning models, or supporting backend ML infrastructure.
  • Experience developing production APIs using FastAPI or a comparable framework.
  • Experience deploying and troubleshooting services in a cloud environment, using containers, CI/CD, logs, metrics, and tracing.
  • Ability to work through ambiguous requirements, explain technical tradeoffs, and balance customer needs with long-term system health.
  • Clear communication in English and a collaborative approach to working with technical and non-technical colleagues (German is a plus).
  • Interest in building dependable AI applications and the ability to learn new tools and concepts as the field evolves.

Experience That Would Be a Plus:

  • Building applications with LLMs, tool calling, agent orchestration, or the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
  • Evaluating agent behavior using representative datasets and regression checks.
  • Working on multi-tenant SaaS systems, authorization boundaries, sandboxed execution, or defenses against prompt injection.
  • Familiarity with technologies such as Pydantic AI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, message queues, object storage, and Kubernetes.

You do not need experience with every technology listed. We value strong engineering judgment, ownership, and the ability to learn.

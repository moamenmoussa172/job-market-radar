About the job

Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Quantitative Analyst, you'll leverage your expertise in data analysis, statistical modeling, and business strategy to develop and implement advanced quantitative models. Your insights will play a critical role in driving data-driven decision-making, enhancing business performance, and identifying growth opportunities.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and implement quantitative models to analyze and forecast business performance
  • Perform deep statistical analysis on large datasets to uncover trends, patterns, and actionable insights
  • Build and maintain robust financial models and quantitative tools to support strategic decision-making
  • Monitor and analyze market trends and competitor performance to identify gaps and opportunities for innovation
  • Formulate and test hypotheses using financial and market data to recommend strategic improvements
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate analytical findings into practical business strategies
  • Provide insights into market data and financial trends to guide the planning and development of new projects or products.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Data Science, Physics, or a related discipline
  • Strong analytical skills with proven experience in analyzing large and complex datasets
  • Proficiency in statistical programming languages such as Python, R, or MATLAB
  • Advanced knowledge of statistical methods, machine learning, and predictive modeling techniques
  • Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) to present insights effectively
  • Familiarity with financial modeling, forecasting, and quantitative risk analysis
  • Knowledge of market analysis tools and techniques in finance or related industries.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills with a proactive and innovative mindset
  • Strong communication skills to present complex findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment.
  • Attention to detail with a commitment to delivering high-quality results

Our benefits

  • Medical insurance
  • Provident fund
  • In-house gym with a personal trainer
  • Free daily lunch catering, snacks, and beverages
  • Company discount card for various products & services
  • 21 days of annual leave and 10 days of sick leave annually
  • Shuttle bus service from Limassol
  • Birthday certificate program

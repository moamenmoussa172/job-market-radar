KBR is looking for an Information /Data Management Coordinator for a PMO portfolio PMC projectto be based in Abu Dhabi.

Role Purpose

The Information/Data Management Coordinator is responsible for managing project information, engineering documentation, data governance, document control systems, and digital information workflows across the PMC project. The role ensures integrity, accuracy, accessibility, security, and timely distribution of project data and documentation in accordance with client and project requirements.

Key Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and manage project information and data throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Maintain and administer document management systems (DMS) such as Aconex, EDMS, SharePoint, OpenText, or similar platforms.
  • Ensure compliance with project information management procedures, standards, and client requirements.
  • Control document workflows, transmittals, approvals, revisions, and distribution matrices.
  • Monitor and maintain accurate metadata, document numbering, and filing structures.
  • Generate periodic reports on document status, overdue reviews, and project KPIs.
  • Support engineering, procurement, construction, and project controls teams with information and data requirements.
  • Manage data quality checks and ensure consistency across project databases.
  • Coordinate with contractors, vendors, and client teams for timely submission and retrieval of project documentation.
  • Implement data governance and information security requirements.
  • Support project handover activities, including compilation of final documentation and as-built records.
  • Assist in developing information management procedures, workflows, and best practices.
  • Provide training and user support on information management systems when required.

Experience required:

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in Information Management, Data Management, Document Control, or related IT-based roles within Oil & Gas, EPC, PMC.
  • Strong IT background with experience in managing enterprise information systems and project data environments.
  • Ability to work effectively in a multi-disciplinary, multi-contractor project environment.
  • ADNOC project experience is an advantage.
  • Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering is a MUST.

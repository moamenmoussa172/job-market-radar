About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the Role

In this role, you will be a front-line manager to a group of Account Managers who are responsible for hunting, pitching, and expanding the existing customer base at Ramp.

You'll lead a group of 6-8 Account Managers, each carrying a book of 60-70 Enterprise customers with real latitude over how they grow it. This is a build role, not a maintain role: the playbooks, coaching systems, and performance bar you set are the ones this team runs on next year. Your ceiling is set by how well you turn individual AM judgment into a repeatable, scalable motion that grows the segment.

What You'll Do

  • Build and run a high-performing team of 6-8 Account Managers - setting the bar for culture, morale, and daily execution.
  • Get embedded in the relationships that matter. Engage directly with key customers and top accounts alongside your team, building trust and long-term partnership.
  • Own the number. Manage quota performance and pipeline generation across your team to hit spend activation, product activation, retention, and expansion goals.
  • Forecast and report team performance to senior leadership with clear-eyed visibility into what's working and what isn't.
  • Hire and develop the next generation of AMs - training on product, buyer personas, competition, and tooling through role-plays, game film, and real deal work.
  • Coach relentlessly. Run daily 1:1s, dissect call feedback, lead enablement sessions, and build career paths for every rep on your team.
  • Turn what works into a system. Continuously refine team processes and tooling to improve output and efficiency as the segment scales.
  • Represent Account Management cross-functionally, partnering with Sales, CX, CSM, Risk, and Product to remove blockers and drive outcomes.

What You Need

  • Minimum 5 years of quota-carrying sales or account management experience as an individual contributor, with a proven, consistent track record of exceeding goals (new business or cross-sell/upsell experience mandatory)
  • Minimum 5 years building and leading account management or sales teams, with a track record of exceeding goal (new business or cross-sell/upsell experience mandatory)
  • Prior success in fast-paced, results-oriented GTM environments, ideally at SaaS or AI companies
  • A genuine passion for hiring, with a thoughtful approach to talent development
  • Ability to articulate contractual, technical, and financial value to customers, including C-Suite and executive leaders
  • Strong track record negotiating, structuring, and executing complex agreements
  • Proficiency with data analytics tools (Salesforce, Looker, Excel) and a bias toward using data to drive decisions
  • Strong collaboration and influencing skills, backed by excellent communication and presentation ability.
  • High agency and adaptability - no two days are the same, and the only constant is change
  • A desire and willingness to jump headfirst into complex, challenging problems for the good of the business.

Great to Haves

  • Experience at a high-growth, top SaaS/AI startup
  • Bachelor's degree from an accredited university

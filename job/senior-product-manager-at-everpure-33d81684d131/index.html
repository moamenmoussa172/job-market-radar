About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

As Senior Product Manager for FlashArray Foundation, you will shape and accelerate the core platform roadmap for Purity//FA, the operating system powering our industry-defining enterprise storage solutions. Your mission is to redefine storage simplicity and resilience by delivering high-performance, zero-downtime capabilities directly to global enterprise data centers. Collaborating closely with Engineering, Hardware, and Office of the CTO architects, you will pioneer next-generation hardware/software co-design that keeps mission-critical applications running continuously.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Drive Core OS & Platform Strategy: Own the end-to-end vision, roadmap, and business impact for Purity//FA core services, delivering non-disruptive upgrades, high-availability failover, and operational simplicity to meet enterprise SLAs.
  • Architect Protocol & Hardware Integration: Translate enterprise market needs across FC, iSCSI, and NVMe-oF/TCP into actionable product definitions, partnering with hardware and software engineering to execute seamless controller and media co-design.
  • Turn Telemetry into Scalable Performance: Evaluate customer fleet telemetry, benchmark data, and workload trends to pinpoint latency and throughput bottlenecks, prioritizing high-impact enhancements that measurably improve fleet-wide reliability and speed.
  • Lead Cross-Functional Alignment & Execution: Unify Engineering, QA, Hardware, and Architecture teams through crisp decision frameworks, balancing customer value, technical debt, release velocity, and risk mitigation from concept to GA.
  • Enable Field & Market Adoption: Partner with Technical Marketing, Solutions Engineers, and sales teams to deliver compelling technical positioning, architectural deep-dives, and competitive intelligence that showcase FlashArray's operational advantages.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Enterprise Infrastructure Leadership: Demonstrated track record of managing enterprise storage, systems, or infrastructure products from initial requirements through commercial launch, with an ability to anchor complex architectural choices to clear business outcomes.
  • Deep Storage Systems & Protocols Mastery: Applied understanding of block storage protocols (FC, iSCSI, NVMe-oF, NVMe/TCP) and storage OS principles (such as controller/media behaviors, high-availability clustering, and non-disruptive upgrades), enabling peer-level credibility with principal engineers and architects.
  • Data-Informed Technical Acumen: Ability to analyze systems-level telemetry, IOPS, latency profiles, and failure modes to define clear prioritization metrics, acceptance criteria, and roadmap trade-offs.
  • Collaborative Influence & Strategic Communication: Exceptional ability to synthesize diverse stakeholder inputs, build consensus across distributed cross-functional teams without direct authority, and communicate technical narratives clearly to both executive and technical audiences.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Bangalore, India office in compliance with Pure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

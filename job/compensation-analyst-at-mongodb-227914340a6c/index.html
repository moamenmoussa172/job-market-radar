About the job

As a Compensation Analyst, you will help deliver MongoDB’s global compensation programs and core processes, including merit, bonus, market benchmarking, and pay structures. You will work closely with the Compensation team and partners across HR, Recruiting, Finance, and HRIS to coordinate end-to-end execution, maintain reliable compensation data, and improve the systems and processes that support our work. You will also contribute to broader global Compensation projects.

The successful candidate is analytical and detail-oriented, handles sensitive information with discretion, and is motivated to make recurring processes more effective and scalable.

We are looking to speak to someone who is based out of Austin for our hybrid working model.

Responsibilities

  • Support the design, administration, and end-to-end delivery of global compensation programs and cyclical processes, including merit and bonus programs.
  • Coordinate compensation market benchmarking, including participation in salary surveys, consolidation and review of market data, and support for pay-range and pay-structure development.
  • Maintain compensation data and processes in SuccessFactors; perform audits and validation to identify discrepancies and help ensure data accuracy and integrity.
  • Develop and maintain compensation reporting and dashboards, and prepare, validate, and consolidate compensation data for Board materials and other business needs.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, and automate compensation processes; help implement improvements that support scale and efficiency.
  • Create and maintain clear standard operating procedures (SOPs) for recurring compensation activities.
  • Partner with Compensation colleagues and cross-functional stakeholders, including HRBPs, Recruiting, Finance, and HRIS, to coordinate work and support global programs and projects.
  • Contribute to other compensation activities and projects as needed.

We are looking for someone with:

  • 4+ years of experience in compensation analysis and operations.
  • Strong Excel skills and the ability to analyze compensation data accurately.
  • Strong operational judgment and an interest in improving, scaling, and streamlining processes.
  • Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills, with high attention to detail.
  • Effective relationship-building and communication skills, with a flexible and consultative approach to cross-functional partnership.
  • Ability to manage work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Sound judgment and discretion when handling confidential compensation and HR information.
  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience.

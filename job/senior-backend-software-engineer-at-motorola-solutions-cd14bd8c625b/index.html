Department Overview

Motorola Solutions’ Avigilon Alta Video brings cloud video security systems to the next level. Alta’s cloud-native video security platform delivers industry-leading analytics and exceptional operational insights to help organizations observe, oversee, and analyse their environments to protect people, property, and places in real time.
The Alta Video Engineering Team injects intelligence into our approach to safety and across every solution we engineer. Our team designs, builds, and scales intelligent video microservices and distributed cloud infrastructure that empower human decision-makers at the centre, actively advancing our mission of "Solving for safer".
Our engineering hub is based in Uxbridge, West London, operating under a purposeful hybrid model with two collaborative days together on-site each week.


Job Description

As a Senior Back End Software Engineer, you will contribute to the core ecosystem powering our next-generation Avigilon Alta Video platform. Our close-knit engineering team values curiosity, high technical ownership, and pragmatic problem-solving. In this role, you will design scalable microservices, expand analytical capabilities, and collaborate across disciplines to deliver cloud solutions that matter.

We embrace an inclusive culture where every voice contributes to our collective impact. We recognize that talent takes many paths; candidates demonstrating equivalent practical experience alongside a strong track record of engineering delivery are encouraged to apply.

Key Responsibilities

System Architecture & Cloud Engineering

  • Design and implement high-throughput backend services and APIs that support real-time video management and intelligent analytics
  • Optimize distributed cloud architectures for resilience, scalability, low latency, and operational efficiency
  • Guide technical decisions across our service ecosystem, driving code quality and robust testing automation

Feature Delivery & Assisted Intelligence Integration

  • Expand video intelligence pipelines, integrating "Priority Intelligence™" features to deliver an Assisted Experience that augments human decision-making
  • Develop end-to-end device integration workflows, supporting multi-camera configurations, sensor inputs, and advanced analytic rule processing
  • Implement reliable telemetry, logging, and operational instrumentation to observe and diagnose distributed cloud workloads

Collaborative Ownership & Technical Leadership

  • Partner closely with frontend, firmware, QA, and product teams in an agile environment to shape technical roadmaps and feature delivery
  • Conduct structured code reviews and provide thoughtful engineering mentorship to uphold engineering standards and continuous team growth
  • Champion secure development lifecycles, helping to ensure that system designs align with modern enterprise cybersecurity baselines


Basic Requirements

You’re not expected to have all of the following skills, but they will be useful in performing your job.

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or Software Engineering
  • Substantial software development experience in backend engineering or distributed systems

Technical Expertise

  • Programming Foundations to architect services in modern backend languages, with proficiency in Golang, Python, C, C++, Rust, or Java
  • Distributed Cloud Systems to design scalable, fault-tolerant microservices and RESTful or gRPC APIs
  • Automated Testing & CI/CD to build resilient test suites and maintain continuous integration pipelines
  • English Proficiency to articulate technical concepts and collaborate across cross-functional distributed teams

Professional Experience

  • Technical Mentorship to conduct rigorous peer reviews and elevate team engineering practices
  • Agile Collaboration to partner across firmware, web, and infrastructure disciplines from design through delivery
  • Problem Solving to isolate and resolve performance bottlenecks across complex cloud video ecosystems

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Hands-on development experience building production services in Go (Golang)
  • Familiarity with TypeScript and modern web architectures
  • Practical background working with video streaming, computer vision workflows, or containerized cloud deployments (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Experience with Machine Learning, Computer Vision, AI technologies.

The Avigilon Alta Video teams default to programming using Go for backend and Typescript for frontend, but also use a wide variety of different languages and frameworks. It is not expected that you would be familiar with Go (or any of the other languages we use) but you should be enthusiastic and willing to learn new things!

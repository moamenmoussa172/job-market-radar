About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Data and Analytics (CA)
Title: Business Intelligence Analyst - Indaiatuba/SP - 125681

As a Business Intelligence Analyst for John Deere Financial, based in Indaiatuba/SP you will be responsible for developing, delivering and validating automated reports and dashboards in support of JDF’S credit operations. This role works with a large volume of financial data sets covering many aspects of equipment financing. You will be based in Indaiatuba, Brazil and align with the JDF Risk Analytics team headquartered in Johnston-Iowa-USA, aiming to execute and deliver the analytics and insights needed to support the business units. Join a team that is passionate about making a difference by applying different techniques to solve problems and leverage data as an asset.

*Please attach your resume in English*

Main Responsibilities

  • Assist with requirements gathering and ongoing stakeholder support across multiple John Deere Financial domains (Accounting, Customer Management, Collections, Litigation, Operational & Risk Analytics);
  • Build and maintain SQL queries in enterprise data platforms such as Databricks, becoming a subject matter expert in credit operation data sets used by John Deere Financial to support installment loans, leases and revolving accounts for equipment purchases;
  • Create analytic dashboards, craft visualizations and deliver automated reports with SAP Business Objects, SAP Web Intelligence and Excel;
  • Learn extensively from senior team members and work with other BI analysts in a cooperative paired programming environment;
  • Engage with data scientists, data engineers and data catalysts to design, build and support analytics solutions across the whole lifecycle (research and development, proof-of-concept, pilot, production);
  • Implement ongoing enhancements to improve cost, quality, and performance of analytics products;
  • Support operational needs of production reporting (documentation, monitoring, alerting, automation, change management, security, on-call).

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Data Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or related fields;
  • Advanced English: broad vocabulary, strong grammar, able to lead meetings, argue, negotiate, and deliver training sessions;
  • Experience and desire to create and maintain documentation and diagrams for analytics products;
  • Experience in one or more Business Intelligence reporting & visualization tools such as SAP Business Objects, Tableau, PowerBI and Databricks - with interest to learn others;
  • Intermediate to advanced SQL;
  • Basic to intermediate in report building and graphing/visualization;
  • Intermediate Microsoft Office skills (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Sharepoint);
  • Analytical skills to identify, analyze, debug and interpret trends or patterns in complex data sets;
  • Technical aptitude to learn a large set of business systems and associated data sets;
  • Advanced problem-solving ability.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with SAP Business Objects – report design, universe design;
  • Seeking a highly motivated individual with aspirations to become a domain knowledge expert in the area of John Deere Financial systems, data and credit operations over the next years;
  • Keen interest in maintaining high quality analytics products for others to consume an aptitude for diving into and resolving discrepancies in data – attention to detail is paramount for financial reporting and analytics.

Role summary

As an SAP Functional Consultant (MM/SD) joining The Transform Program, you will play a pivotal role in delivering our large-scale enterprise transformation across the Middle East. Working closely with internal business teams, technical leads, and system integrators, you will ensure seamless end-to-end process design, cross-system integration, and operational readiness across critical program phases.

What you'll be doing

  • End-to-End SAP MM/SD Ownership: Provide functional expertise across SAP Materials Management (MM) and Sales & Distribution (SD), managing upstream and downstream process impacts across procurement, inventory, order management, and retail operations.
  • Cross-System Integration: Support and validate complex integrations between SAP and surrounding ecosystem applications, including Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), legacy ERPs, and Demand Planning platforms.
  • Specifications & Design Review: Review, challenge, and validate Functional Specifications (FSDs), solution designs, and configuration documentation to ensure alignment with retail business requirements and Group standards.
  • Testing & Quality Assurance: Drive System Integration Testing (SIT) execution and defect resolution, and actively support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) preparation, test script validation, and business user coordination.
  • Data Migration & Cutover Support: Assist with data mapping, cleansing rules, validation, and reconciliation activities throughout mock runs and production cutover phases.
  • Reporting, Analytics & Security: Collaborate with BI/analytics and authorization teams to validate operational reporting requirements, data structures, and role-based security/access controls.
  • Program Delivery: Operate effectively across multiple project phases (build, test, deploy, hypercare) within a fast-paced, high-pressure transformation environment.

What you’ll need to succeed

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Supply Chain, Business Administration, or a related discipline.
  • 10+ years of hands-on experience working with SAP MM and SD modules, with a deep understanding of upstream and downstream dependencies.
  • Proven consulting background, with demonstrated experience advising business stakeholders, challenging designs, and translating complex operational needs into scalable SAP solutions.
  • Solid retail industry experience, ideally spanning omnichannel, luxury, fashion, or FMCG distribution environments.
  • End-to-end integration experience connecting SAP with WMS, legacy ERP systems, and Demand Planning tools.
  • Large-scale transformation track record, having delivered across multiple lifecycle phases (Blueprint/Explore, Realization, SIT/UAT, Data Migration, Cutover, and Go-Live).
  • Hands-on willingness and ability to roll up your sleeves across specification reviews, SIT, UAT preparation, data migration, reporting/analytics, and SAP security/authorizations.
  • Ability to thrive under pressure, manage competing priorities, and maintain high delivery standards in a fast-moving program environment.
  • Middle East experience (UAE, KSA, and broader GCC), including familiarity with regional retail operations, localization, and tax/customs flows.
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA retail/fashion implementations.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills across diverse, cross-functional teams and external implementation partners

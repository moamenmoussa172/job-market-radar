About the job

Strong coordination & digital skill for the QMS processes, including:

  • Support in exploring & applying AI tools/AI agent solutions to streamline repetitive tasks & optimize workflows.
  • Support to update process documentation on HcP Process Compass, ensuring consistency & compliance with Bosch central directives.
  • Support to coordinate & follow up Quality method topics (internal & external audit, QMS KPI updates,…).
  • Support the quality management team in various projects and initiatives related to digital and non-digital QMS e.g Preventive Quality, Gap Analysis, Q-Mindset, QMS Excellence.
  • Contribute to organize QMM7 informal & formal events. (Q mindset, PSD, Teambuilding, Diversity,…).
  • Other admin task as assigned in ad-hoc cases.
  • Last year student major in Management Information Systems (MIS), Information Technology, Quality Management or a related field.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams.
  • Strong data-driven & process mindset with a keen interest in applying AI & automation tools to optimize workflows.
  • Self-motivated and eager to learn, with a proactive and positive attitude.
  • Problem-solving and critical-thinking skills, with the ability to propose innovative solutions.
  • Good at reading, writing and communication in English.
  • Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint); familiarity with Power BI.
  • Basic design skill/video making and familiarity with digital solutions in documentation are a plus.
  • A learning-driven, professional, inclusive multinational work environment fosters personal growth and expands professional networks.
  • An attractive monthly allowance.
  • Bus transportation and lunch are provided daily.
  • Full-paid leaves for Vietnam public holidays, birthday, sickness and 01 day per working month.
  • Opportunities to become a Bosch associate after graduation.

About the job

Do you want to be part of a team of builders and innovators, directly responsible for the expansion and upgrade of Amazon network across AMET (Africa, Middle East and Turkey) region? Keep reading.
This Engineering Project Management role is dedicated to develop warehouse innovation and automation solutions. The individual will lead end-to-end automation program delivery, from feasibility assessment and business case development through commissioning and operational handover. The role will support multiple business segments across Amazon supply chain: Fulfillment, Sort Centers (FC/SC), Delivery Stations (DS), Sub-Same-Day (SSD) and Quick Commerce (QC).
The role requires high level of Ownership, Invent & Simplify, Deliver Results and Frugality to drive impactful results. Functionally, the role requires a deep expertise of fulfillment operations, automation, technologies, and AI.
Key responsibilities:
- Proactively scout new technologies and industry trends, participating in major industry exhibitions worldwide and evaluating their potential applicability to Amazon.
- Evaluate technical feasibility and vendor capability for automation opportunities across all types of Amazon sites.
- Develop ROI models, payback analyses, and capital investment plans to support automation business cases.
- Create, submit, and track CAPEX requests to ensure all projects are delivered within budget and at the best overall value.
- Partner with Software, IT, Safety, Operations, Maintenance, Procurement, Change Management, and Finance teams to ensure seamless integration of automation and innovative solutions into existing warehouse operations.
- Lead pilots of selected technologies in operationally live environments and analyze the actual benefits achieved.
- Coordinate the regional deployment of successful technologies and innovations across 10+ sites.
- Communicate project status, risks, and outcomes to senior leadership, driving escalations on potential risks and delays across multiple teams.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance system reliability, throughput, safety, and cost efficiency.
- Synchronize international innovation efforts, primarily across the MEATR, LATAM, and IN teams. Support the development of Amazon's low-cost automation and frugal innovation initiatives.
- Become the Subject Matter Expert for a specific piece of technology or equipment used at Amazon, advising Engineering teams internationally.
The role will be based in Dubai, with expected travel within the MEATR region and beyond up to 50% per year.
- 5+ years of industrial engineer, operations engineer, manufacturing engineer, or project engineer in a manufacturing environment experience
- Bachelor's degree
- Experience in English-language communication skills, both written and verbal
- Knowledge of AutoCAD, VBA, and SQL at the intermediate level
- Experience working with and managing third party vendors
- Experience with Six Sigma, lean manufacturing
- Experience leading cross-functional teams across engineering, operations, and field execution through launch readiness and go-live phases
- Ability to speak English is a must. Other languages such as French, German, Polish, Arabic, Portuguese, Turkish, Zulu, Chinese (Mandarin) are considered preferred qualifications.
- Experience in managing cool chains, refrigeration equipment and installations, temperature-controlled environments are considered a strong plus.

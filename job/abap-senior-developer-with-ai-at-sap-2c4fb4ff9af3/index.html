About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you'll bring

  • Minimum of 10 years of programming experience in the Software Development Lifecycle
  • Deep experience with distributed systems and scalable application design, including resiliency and fault tolerance.
  • Strong, hands-on LLM engineering: prompt design, function/tool calling, structured outputs, multi-turn reasoning, and agentic workflow design.
  • Proven application of advanced RAG patterns (retriever optimization, indexing strategies, grounding, guardrails) and multi-agent orchestration.
  • Experience in architecting tool integrations via MCP across multiple products/teams.
  • Working knowledge of SAP BTP AI services — Generative AI Hub, AI Core, and Joule Studio — including agentic orchestration for production deployments.
  • Familiarity with the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol for designing bidirectional, interoperable multi-agent architectures across SAP and external agent ecosystems.
  • Hands-on experience with agentic AI orchestration frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph to build, test, and operate production agent workflows.
  • Expertise in performance tuning, cost management, and debugging complex production systems.
  • Solid grounding in SRE principles: service-level objectives, runbooks, observability (logs/metrics/traces), incident management, and postmortems.
  • Experience designing and operating evaluation frameworks for AI systems to validate model behavior and reliability.
  • Technical leadership: mentoring, driving design reviews, raising code quality, and influencing engineering practices.
  • Excellent communication skills; able to translate business processes into agentic workflows and clearly articulate AI value to non-technical stakeholders.
  • A problem-solving mindset with rigorous troubleshooting and root-cause analysis skills.
  • Strong understanding of SAP S/4HANA architecture, data models, released APIs, and end-to-end business processes; Finance domain knowledge is an advantage.
  • Hands-on expertise in ABAP Objects, modern ABAP, ABAP CDS/VDM, RAP, OData, enhancement frameworks, APIs, and performance-oriented development.

What you'll build

  • Design and deliver production-grade AI agents that autonomously orchestrate SAP S/4HANA business processes, including multi-agent patterns and advanced RAG pipelines.
  • Define and own AI evaluation and benchmarking frameworks to measure quality, reliability, safety, and performance.
  • Architect and implement tool integration using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) across multiple product teams and systems.
  • Build A2A-compliant agent architectures enabling structured, bidirectional communication between Joule and BTP agents.
  • Wrap OData services and S/4HANA APIs as MCP-consumable tools via the SAP Integration Suite MCP Gateway, making them securely accessible to AI agents across teams and products.
  • Optimize system latency, throughput, resilience, and cost across services and workloads; continuously tune performance and efficiency.
  • Establish and evolve observability (logs, metrics, traces), alerting, incident response, and post-incident reviews in line with SRE best practices.
  • Partner with platform, data, and AI teams to design robust, scalable integrations and harden systems for production.
  • Build high-quality SAP solutions using ABAP/OO ABAP, modern ABAP, RAP, CDS/VDM, OData services, enhancement frameworks, and APIs.
  • Deliver features through the full lifecycle: technical design, implementation, automated testing, release, stabilization, and ongoing production support.
  • Collaborate with product, design, and cloud operations to shape roadmaps and ship value; drive automation in CI/CD and multi-level test strategies for frequent, reliable releases.
  • Troubleshoot complex issues, conduct root-cause analysis, implement preventive actions, and continuously improve security and reliability.

Where you belong

You’ll join the SAP S/4HANA Finance team, take end-to-end technical ownership of critical systems and contribute to engineering excellence across the team. Mentoring less-experienced engineers, guiding design and code quality, and building strong technical capabilities within the team are all part of the role. The team operates across global, cross-functional groups in a culture that values continuous improvement, operational excellence, and knowledge sharing.

About the job

Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet.

The Team:

The Data Analytics and Transformation (DAT) team builds one trusted data foundation driving strategic data-driven decisions across Celonis. We operate through horizontal capability hubs (Data Science & AI, Data Infrastructure, and Process Excellence) alongside vertical domains delivering deep functional insights.

The Data Science & AI hub leverages diverse data (product telemetry, infrastructure, sales motions and much more) to generate governed analytics signals like customer health scores, product depth, and churn. We formulate hypotheses, build predictive models and apply causal inference for actionable insights. Additionally, we develop internal AI agents for diverse use cases utilizing a shared semantic layer to empower organization-wide strategic decision-making.
The Role:

As a Senior Data Scientist in the Data Science & AI team, you will play a key role as a dependable senior contributor who owns complex projects end-to-end. Partnering closely with stakeholders, you will frame ambiguous business problems and drive strategic decisions in a hyper-growth environment. You will leverage your deep analytical and data science expertise to build predictive solutions, design rigorous experiments, and develop GenAI applications (RAG, agents, evals). Beyond modeling, you will translate complex technical findings into clear, actionable narratives, facilitating data-driven discussions and influencing engineers, managers, and executives across the organization.

The work you’ll do:

  • Develop scalable AI/ML solutions, including predictive models, GenAI agents, and advanced decision-making applications for internal use to optimize operational processes and business workflows.
  • Translate ambiguous business problems into technical solutions. Own the full modelling lifecycle: from data gathering and pipeline creation to deployment, continuous post-launch monitoring, and model iteration.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional partners (Product, Engineering, Growth) to drive initiatives by aligning technical capabilities with business goals and influencing decisions through compelling data storytelling and visualizations.
  • Continuously engage to maintain the health, integrity, and automation of data science operations. Refine deployment pipelines and operational workflows to ensure high data quality and rigorous model governance.
  • Act as the dependable expert on the team, elevating technical standards and mentoring junior data scientists in modeling and experimentation best practices.


The qualifications you need:

  • Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field (e.g., Computer Science, Math, Economics) or equivalent practical experience.
  • 5+ years of Data Science or ML Engineering experience, with a track record of deploying models into production at scale (SaaS/Enterprise software experience is a plus).
  • Advanced proficiency in Python (Pandas, NumPy) and SQL. Experience with Big Data (PySpark, Databricks) and BI visualization (Tableau, Looker, Power BI).
  • Deep theoretical foundation in statistics and ML, paired with hands-on expertise in frameworks like XGBoost, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.
  • Experience building with LLMs, prompt engineering, Vector DBs, and modern GenAI frameworks (LangChain, Hugging Face, FastMCP, Pydantic).
  • Strong command of MLops and model lifecycle management tools like MLflow, Docker, and Kubernetes.
  • Outcome-driven with strong decision-making skills and the ability to prioritize multiple goals while meeting deadlines.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills; exceptional attention to detail.

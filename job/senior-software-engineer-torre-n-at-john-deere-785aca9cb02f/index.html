Title: Senior Software Engineer - Torreón

Primary Location: Torreón

Function: Technology (CA)

John Deere is a foremost leader in the ag equipment business, and we continue to put a solid stamp on other industries. For over 185 years, the company’s portfolio of groundbreaking products has grown to include industries related to and outside of agriculture, including Turf, Construction and Forestry and Wind Technologies. The image of John Deere is no longer that of a signature green tractor meandering across a field; it’s been replaced with an image of acceleration, swift progress, and innovative leaping toward the company’s 200th year. The product line alone serves as proof with advances in machine performance, equipment automation, fleet management, and John Deere’s ongoing investment in emerging markets.

Note: All candidates should have their tax situation certificate (Constancia de Siituación Fiscal) up to date with the salary regime to be eligible for the hiring process.

Main Responsibilites:

  • Lead and participate in efforts to modernize the products technology stack;
  • Mentor junior developers and perform code pairing with other members of the team and perform proper code reviews with team members.
  • Collaborate with Product Management and team members on establishing a product roadmap to ensure the development work is prioritized in line with business requirement.
  • Follow industry-standard agile software design methodologies.
  • Grow your own and help grow others technical talents.
  • Learn new skills and technologies as required to support existing and evolving products.
  • Engaging in all aspects of the software delivery cycle including the design, build, test, deploy, and support of software applications.

What Skills You Need:

  • 4+ years of experience building front end applications using JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Ajax and jQuery with UI frameworks like React.
  • 5+ years of experience designing and developing microservices using and creating/consuming RESTful web services.
  • Hands-on experience with containerization using Docker and orchestration with Kubernetes.
  • 3 years of experience using Amazon Web Services (Lambda, API Gateway, ECS etc.) and understanding of infrastructure as code using Terraform or CloudFormation.
  • 2 years of experience designing and developing service-based architecture and/or micro-services.
  • Hands-on experience working with relational and no relational databases.
  • Proven experience working in an Agile Scrum team environment.

What Makes You Stand Out:

  • AWS Solutions Architect Associate Certification.
  • Experience working with SpringBoot or similar Java Frameworks.
  • Experience working with Micro-frontends.
  • Strong communication skills.
  • Appetite for continuous learning and willingness/dedication to develop deep technical skills needed to further develop, transform, and modernize processes and solutions.

Education:

  • Bachelor’s degree with equivalent experience.

This position will be posted for a minimum of 10 business days and unposted when a qualified pool of candidates is achieved.

Candidates must have the necessary work permits to work in the country.

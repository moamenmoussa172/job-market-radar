About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you’ll build

You’ll help customers reimagine their business through the power of SAP. Partnering closely with the sales team, you’ll identify, qualify, and win opportunities by showing customers the real, measurable value of their digital transformation.

As a trusted advisor across the full sales cycle, you’ll:

  • Drive sales success — partner with Account Executives on account strategies, lead discovery, and support deal strategy, competitive positioning, and RFP/RFI responses.
  • Advise and demonstrate — design and deliver compelling presentations, demos, workshops, and proof-of-concepts that turn customer needs into SAP solutions and clear business value.
  • Engage executives — build trusted relationships with C-level stakeholders and position SAP as a strategic transformation partner.
  • Share expertise — stay ahead of SAP solutions, industry trends, and technologies like cloud, AI, and analytics, sharing insights with customers and colleagues.
  • Collaborate and enable — work across sales, consulting, product, and partner teams, and mentor others along the way.

What you bring

  • A Bachelor’s degree in Business, IT, Computer Science, Engineering, Finance, Supply Chain, or a related field.
  • 5+ years in pre-sales, solution consulting, enterprise software, or business consulting.
  • Experience with SAP solutions (e.g., S/4HANA, BTP, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Analytics Cloud).
  • Strong grasp of business processes and enterprise transformation.
  • Excellent presentation, communication, and storytelling skills, with proven executive engagement.
  • Strong analytical thinking and the ability to juggle priorities in a fast-paced setting.
  • Willingness to travel as needed.
  • Fluency in English (B2/C1 at least)

Nice to have: SAP certifications, cloud ERP and transformation experience, industry expertise (Public Sector, Manufacturing, Retail, Healthcare, Financial Services, and more), and knowledge of AI, analytics, and automation.

You’ll thrive here with a strong customer focus, business acumen, executive presence, consultative selling style, and a passion for innovation and continuous learning.

Where you belong

You’ll join a collaborative, forward-thinking team where your expertise directly shapes how customers adopt SAP. More than a solution expert, you’ll be a trusted advisor helping organizations transform for the future. If you’re energized by solving complex challenges, engaging senior decision-makers, and staying at the cutting edge of enterprise technology—this is where you belong.

Length: 6months fixed term contract

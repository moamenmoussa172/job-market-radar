About the job

Take the role of a Component & System Test Engineer and the required competences for operating Test Automation projects.

Tasks

  • Analyze specified requirements
  • Design and implementation of test cases
  • Execution of tests, retests and regression tests
  • Evaluation of results
  • Documentation and archiving of work products
  • Creation of bug tickets

Rights

  • Approval of component & system requirements specifications
  • Review test designs & implementations
  • Veto regarding local deviations from company standards regarding architecture
  • Identification and application of suitable tools for the development of SW test architecture
  • Prototypical implementation of innovation ideas, especially in terms of testing

Responsibilities

  • Finding errors in the device under test (as many errors as possible with as little effort as necessary)
  • Ensuring the re-usability, applicability, availability, reproducibility and quality of the work results
  • Ensuring the information distribution of detected errors, their correction and lessons learned in the global environment
  • Graduated in Computer science/ Electrical engineering/ Embedded electronics development/
  • More than 1 years of experience in embedded software testing, ideally in automotive or industrial domains
  • Good knowledge of test methodologies, test levels, and defect management processes
  • Experience with embedded systems, hardware–software integration, and test environments
  • Experience with Python/C++
  • Familiarity with test management tools, defect tracking systems, and CI/CD pipelines
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership of quality outcomes
  • Professional proficiency in English – spoken and written.

This position will be contracted through Bosch’s external vendor under a 1-year contract. Salary and benefits will be discussed during interview

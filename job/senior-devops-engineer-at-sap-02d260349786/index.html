We are looking for a Senior DevOps engineer to join our infrastructure team. Challenge yourself in the fast-paced world of cloud infrastructure and become an integral part of the group that directs SAP’s strategy to move to the cloud.

What you’ll do

  • Develop and maintain a monitoring system that overlooks our build landscape.
  • Identify and address security and performance issues. Maintain a strong focus on quality and customer satisfaction.
  • Ensure the systems we build limit exposure to single points of failure and security vulnerabilities.
  • Design, implement, and manage DevOps capabilities using CI/CD toolsets and automation.
  • Analyze the existing infrastructure and provide expertise on containerization and migration to Kubernetes (K8S).

What you bring

  • 5yrs (T3) Experience with developing, deploying and maintaining solutions using microservice architecture.
  • A cloud mindset with an affinity for automation (GoLang or Bash or Shell or Python).
  • Knowledge of continuous integration, continuous testing, and continuous deployment.
  • Experience working in datacenter, cloud, and network environments.
  • Troubleshooting and support experience in system monitoring.
  • Fluency both written and spoken in English

Tech you bring

  • Public Cloud Services: Managed Kubernetes clusters, Managed Database clusters.
  • Containerisation & Microservices: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm.
  • Source control & CI/CD tooling: Flux, Git, GitHub Actions.
  • Observability & Monitoring tools: Splunk, Dynatrace, Grafana, Prometheus, Loki.

Tech you'll learn

Successful candidates will have the opportunity to work with our existing pool of talented engineers to improve their skills on any of the tools/utilities mentioned above.

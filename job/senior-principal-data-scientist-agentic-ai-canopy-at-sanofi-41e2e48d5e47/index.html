Job title: Senior Principal Data Scientist, Agentic AI Canopy

  • Location: Marcy L'Etoile, France

About the job

As Agentic AI Senior Principal Scientist within our Vaccines R&D Data and Computational Sciences (DCS) organization, you’ll define and execute the Agentic AI strategy for computational design across Sanofi's entire vaccine development portfolio. Ready to get started?

Join the team protecting half a billion lives every year with next-gen science, mRNA innovation, and AI-driven breakthroughs. In Vaccines, you'll help advance prevention on a global scale - and shape the future of immunization. Join the engine of Sanofi's mission — where deep immunoscience meets bold, AI-powered research. In R&D, you'll drive breakthroughs that could turn the impossible into possible for millions. You'll serve as a scientific leader and cross-functional collaborator responsible for implementing agentic AI workflows across four capability areas: bioinformatics, nucleic acid design, protein AI models, and viral strain surveillance supporting also the build of AI agents for Translational Data Science workflows. This role sits at the intersection of cutting-edge science, advanced technology, and strategic leadership.

About Sanofi:

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main responsibilities:

  • Define and execute Vaccines R&D agentic AI strategy — Implement and lead the adoption of agentic AI workflows across four capability areas: bioinformatics, nucleic acid design, protein AI models, and viral strain surveillance
  • Leverage and contribute to Sanofi's enterprise AI infrastructure — Champion the development and deployment of AI and machine learning methods, including autonomous AI agents, multi-step reasoning systems, and large language model workflows to accelerate computational design processes
  • Collaborate across functions — Drive cross-functional collaboration with R&D, Digital, CMC, and Clinical teams to ensure computational design outputs are actionable, integrated, and aligned with downstream development needs
  • Define and adopt best practices for agentic AI — Establish and enforce best practices for code generation, software engineering, data governance, model validation, and reproducibility across all computational workflows
  • Pioneer AI-assisted workflows — explore autonomous workflows for scientific computing, data analysis, and application development; build internal capabilities that can be industrialized and scaled across Research, CMC, Translational and Clinical contexts.
  • Harmonize organization-wide agentic AI practices — build a community of agentic AI experts to implement best practices across the broader DCS organization; mentor scientists in adopting AI-assisted workflows for their R&D activities.

About you

  • Experience: Progressive experience in computational biology, bioinformatics, or related fields, with a demonstrated track record of scientific leadership and innovation in pharmaceutical, biotech, or technology environments
  • Soft and technical skills:

• Deep expertise spanning at least two domains: bioinformatics and genomics, nucleic acid sequence design, or protein structure prediction and design

• Experience developing, productionizing, and deploying AI and machine learning models applied to molecular property prediction

• Demonstrated experience with autonomous AI systems, large language models, or AI agents applied to scientific research or drug discovery workflows

• Strong strategic thinking and business acumen, with ability to translate scientific vision into executable roadmaps and communicate priorities to stakeholders

• Exceptional cross-functional collaboration skills with experience at the interface of R&D, digital, and CMC functions

• Fluency in scientific computing (Python, R) and familiarity with cloud-based computational infrastructure (AWS, Azure, or GCP)

• Outstanding communication, presentation, and scientific writing skills with ability to distill complex computational concepts for diverse audiences including non-technical executives

  • Education: PhD in Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, Structural Biology, Computer Science, or a closely related quantitative discipline; postdoctoral training is a plus
  • Languages: Fluent in English; French is a plus

  • Serve as a strategic thought partner to shape the future of AI-powered drug development in one of the world's leading vaccine companies.

Operations Analyst

ShopUp · Dhaka, Bangladesh

Onsite · Business / Domain Analyst · Posted 21 October 2026

About the job

You'll work directly with CEO on high-impact projects that shape the company's future. This role offers a unique blend of operational strategy and hands-on technology work.

  • You have experience writing code and posses a strong understanding of databases. You must have professional experience leveraging these skills.
  • Currently work in operations and genuinely enjoy operational challenges. Previous experience in technical product management is a significant advantage.
  • You have entrepreneurial experience—whether running your own startup, business, or side project—that's a definite plus.
  • Have less than 7 years of total professional experience.
  • You must be good at managing many projects simultaneously.

Benefits:

  • You work with a diverse group of people (ex BAT, Unilever, Grameenphone, Bkash )
  • PF & GF
  • Subsidised Lunch

Disadvantages:

  • Work on Saturdays, sometimes Fridays.
  • Have to travel outside Dhaka or Country.
  • You may need to work with 50+ different teams across six countries.

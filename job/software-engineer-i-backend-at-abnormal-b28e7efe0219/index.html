About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Singapore.

What you will do

Abnormal AI is looking for a growth-oriented Backend Software Engineer to join our Critical Email Products team in Singapore. The team builds email security products that help customers protect sensitive information. One of your first opportunities will be to work on rule-based outbound data loss prevention (DLP), which helps organisations enforce policies on what sensitive information can be sent by email.

You’ll build backend features and integrations, improve the reliability of distributed systems that process email at scale, and help prepare new capabilities for production by integrating them with other systems and improving their stability before release. You’ll work with product partners, engineers, and other stakeholders to understand customer problems and shape the features that address them. You’ll use AI-assisted development tools as part of your engineering workflow. With guidance and feedback from experienced engineers, you’ll take ownership of well-scoped features and learn how to ship and operate them in production.

Must Haves

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Applied Sciences, Information Systems, or a related engineering field.
  • 1+ years of industry experience designing and building backend software, including experience with distributed systems.
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate regularly with teammates.
  • Experience working with distributed teams and communicating clearly in writing and asynchronously.
  • Experience building features or components from scratch and supporting them in production while balancing feature development with operational work.

Nice to Haves

  • Experience with one or more technologies in our stack, including Python, Go, AWS, Kubernetes, Django, Spark, and Prometheus.
  • 1+ years of experience programming in Go.
  • Familiarity with AWS services and deployment practices.
  • Experience leading a small team to build a feature or component from scratch.

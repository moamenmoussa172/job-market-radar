Job Description:

The opportunity
You will be building and growing the operations administrator plays a critical role in ensuring efficient and effective operational activities. Responsible for overseeing administrative workflows, improving internal processes, supporting strategic initiatives, facilitating cross-departmental collaboration. You will be acting as a key support to leadership by ensuring compliance, tracking operational KPIs, and driving process optimization.

How you’ll make an impact

  • Coordinating and overseeing day-to-day operational administrative activities and support Operations Management in strategic planning, budgeting, reporting.
  • Analyzing operational workflows and propose continuous improvement initiatives and monitor operational KPIs and generate performance reports.
  • Liaising across departments to streamline communication and operations and supervise and mentor junior administrative staff.
  • Managing procurement documentation, supplier communications, and vendor follow-up and organize meetings, draft agendas, record minutes, and manage action points.
  • Assisting in audits, compliance checks, and enforcement of corporate policies and Support onboarding processes, training logistics, and HR administrative tasks.
  • Assisting in risk management initiatives and business continuity planning and take ownership of special projects assigned by management.
  • Coordinating between the team leaders and Project managers for logging and following the NCRs on the system and updating the competency matrix for the organization in coordination with team leaders.
  • Supporting the employees with their admin requests such as the traveling and hotel related and support the training activity for the team and related administration task.
  • You will be responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable external and internal regulations, procedures, and guidelines.
  • Living Hitachi Energy’s core values safety and integrity, which means taking responsibility for your own actions while caring for your colleagues and the business.

Your background

  • A bachelor’s degree or diploma in electrical engineering is preferred.
  • More than 5-8 Years of experience in technical sales role and having knowledge and understanding on Grid Automation (Automation and communication) portfolios.
  • Previous Field work experience in Grid Automation systems (Automation and communication) will be an added advantage.
  • We are looking for an experienced, self-motivated and reliable team player with an independent and result oriented working style, as well as strong communicative and collaborative skills.
  • Able to work independently and collaborate in a team set up.
  • You consider yourself to be an energetic, supportive and self-confident person who can handle both ups and downs and manage problems that may occur.
  • Proficiency in both spoken & written English language is required.

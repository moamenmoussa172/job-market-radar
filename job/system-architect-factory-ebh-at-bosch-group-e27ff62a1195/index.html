About the job

Tasks / Responsibilities:Capture requirements from global and local factories and Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) partners. Consolidate them into traceable system requirements and assess justified local deviations.Define reusable architecture connecting product electronics, factory test systems, production lines, and IT backends, including system boundaries, components, interfaces, and end-to-end data flows.Standardize production workflows for firmware flashing, bootloader interaction, automated calibration, testing, and production data exchange.Evaluate architectural alternatives based on cost, complexity, scalability, and business value.Model architecture and interfaces in CAMEO using UML/SysML, maintain requirements traceability in Polarion, and document constraints, dependencies, and Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).Align factory, product, platform, IT, and supplier teams throughout implementation, integration, verification, and production ramp-up.Provide third-line technical support for production issues, analyse manufacturing and test data, and drive root cause resolution.Incorporate lessons learned into the standard architecture to improve production efficiency, reliability, and reuse across factories and EMS partners.Expected skill set: Factory Ecosystem & Appliance Manufacturing: Deep understanding of global and local factory setups, manufacturing processes, and electronics industrialization. ODM/EMS Collaborations: Strong understanding of working with Original Design Manufacturers (ODM) as Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS). Systems & Interface Architecture: In-depth knowledge of system boundaries, interfaces, and data flows between product platforms and factory test-systems, using UML and SysML frameworks to structurally represent complex architectures Polarion & UML/SysML Specification: Designing precise, reusable system architectures and standardized interfaces using UML and SysML, ensuring full requirements traceability directly within Polarion and BSH internal Product Data Management System. Industrial Networking & Bus Systems: Specifying wired/wireless Ethernet technologies and BSH specific Communication Bus Systems to establish reliable data paths between electronics and test-systems. Production Flashing & Calibration: Designing robust, high-speed Firmware-Flashing sequences, bootloader interactions, and automated Calibration protocols for physical production lines. End-to-End Interface Deployment: Driving the deployment and generic integration of interfaces across product development platforms, electronics, and global production lines. Big Data Production Analytics: Analyzing large-scale manufacturing and test datasets for root-cause investigations, providing expert 3rd-line support for production.. Systemic & Abstract Thinking: The ability to abstract highly complex local manufacturing processes into generic, standardized system interfaces. Stakeholder Alignment & Collaboration: Bridging the gap between factories, development projects, platforms, IT, and management. Balancing Global vs. Local Needs: Ability to evaluate and balance global standardized platforms with justified local factory requirements. Independent Ownership & End-to-End Responsibility: Taking full ownership of the target architecture from definition to successful ramp[1] Influence without Authority: Persuading and aligning diverse, cross-functional stakeholders without direct disciplinary power. Bachelor's/Master’s degree in Electronics Engineering, or related field.

About the job

About Us:
AI needs a new infrastructure layer. We're building it at Modal.

Every era of computing brought new workloads that previous infrastructure couldn't support: mainframes, databases, and the cloud. Each time, the company that rebuilt the layer underneath defined the decade. AI is no different, except it touches everything instead of one slice, and the window to build the layer underneath it is open right now.

Our customers include category-defining companies like Ramp, Cognition, DoorDash, and Suno. They rely on Modal for instant GPU access, sub-second container starts, and native storage, so it's simple to serve low-latency inference, fine-tune models, and access production-ready sandboxes at scale.

We've passed $1B in annualized run rate, up 15x from a year ago. In October 2026 we raised a $750M Series D led by Accel at a $15.75B valuation.

Our team includes creators of popular open-source projects (e.g., Seaborn, Luigi), academic researchers, international olympiad medalists, and experienced engineering and product leaders with decades of experience.

The Role:

As Strategy & Analytics Lead, you will build and lead the function that gives Modal a single, trusted picture of how the business is performing, and turn that picture into better decisions. This is a company-wide role, not a single-team one. You will support leadership across Go-to-Market, Finance, Product, and Infrastructure, own the metrics that matter most, and be a core contributor to how we plan, allocate capacity, and report to our board.

This is a player-coach role. You will stay hands-on in the data while building a small, high-caliber team and the standards it works by. You will work directly with company leadership and have a large say in how Modal runs.

What You’ll Work On:

Own the metrics that matter most

  • Define, document, and maintain Modal’s core company metrics, such as revenue, usage, growth, retention, margin, and capacity utilization, in partnership with Finance, Product, and Infrastructure
  • Create one trusted source for each metric, retire conflicting reports, and make sure leaders across the company are working from the same numbers
  • Build the dashboards and operating reviews that leadership uses to run the business week to week

Board and executive reporting

  • Own the analytics behind the board package and other investor materials, from the underlying data to the charts and narrative
  • Make executive and board reporting accurate, repeatable, and fast to produce, with clear explanations of what changed and why
  • Prepare deep dives on the questions leadership and the board care most about

Capacity planning and allocation

  • Build the analytics that inform how Modal plans, prioritizes, and allocates GPU capacity across customers and workloads
  • Forecast demand, track utilization and efficiency across the fleet, and help leadership weigh decisions about bringing new capacity online
  • Give the team clear, data-backed inputs for prioritization decisions when demand is high, so those decisions are consistent and defensible

Go-to-Market analytics

  • Support forecasting, pipeline reviews, and account planning with reliable data on pipeline, conversion, and win rates
  • Analyze how usage turns into revenue, including expansion, retention, and churn, across our usage-based and committed customers
  • Track forecast accuracy and explain misses, and help Sales focus on the accounts with the most potential

Strategic analysis across the business

  • Lead analyses on pricing, unit economics, product adoption, and customer cohorts, and turn them into clear recommendations
  • Partner with Product on usage and adoption metrics, and with Finance on reconciling usage, revenue, and margin
  • Answer the hard, ambiguous questions that cut across teams, and follow up on whether the recommendations worked

Data foundations and team

  • Work with Data Engineering on reliable, well-documented data models and quality checks across product, billing, CRM, and infrastructure data
  • Hire and coach a small team of analysts, and set the bar for rigor, documentation, and speed
  • Use AI and agentic tools in day-to-day analysis, and help the team and the rest of the company do the same

You May Be a Fit If:

  • You have 8+ years in analytics, business intelligence, strategy, or strategic finance, including several years supporting senior leadership at a high-growth technology company
  • You have a track record of defining company-level metrics with Finance and Product and getting an organization to trust them
  • You have experience preparing board, investor, or executive reporting
  • You have experience analyzing usage-based or hybrid commit-plus-usage business models
  • You have advanced SQL and BI skills, and comfort with statistical or forecasting models
  • You have experience leading or coaching analysts while staying hands-on yourself
  • You have strong business judgment: you can turn a messy question into a clear recommendation and challenge assumptions with data
  • You have excellent written and verbal communication for executive audiences

Nice-to-Have:

  • Experience at a cloud infrastructure, AI infrastructure, or developer platform company
  • Experience with capacity planning, supply and demand forecasting, or operations analytics
  • Experience building a metrics or semantic layer with modern analytics-engineering tools
  • Experience during a period of hypergrowth
  • Familiarity with Salesforce, billing, and product usage data

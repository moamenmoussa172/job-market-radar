About the job

Bloomreach is building the world’s premier agentic platform for personalization.We’re revolutionizing how businesses connect with their customers, building and deploying AI agents to personalize the entire customer journey.

  • We're taking autonomous search mainstream, making product discovery more intuitive and conversational for customers, and more profitable for businesses.
  • We’re making conversational shopping a reality, connecting every shopper with tailored guidance and product expertise — available on demand, at every touchpoint in their journey.
  • We're designing the future of autonomous marketing, taking the work out of workflows, and reclaiming the creative, strategic, and customer-first work marketers were always meant to do.

And we're building all of that on the intelligence of a single AI engine — Loomi — so that personalization isn't only autonomous…it's also consistent.From retail to financial services, hospitality to gaming, businesses use Bloomreach to drive higher growth and lasting loyalty. We power personalization for more than 1,400 global brands, including American Eagle, Sonepar, and Pandora.

We are looking for a Senior AI Product manager to join the Bloomreach Clarity product team and help build the next generation of conversational shopping. Working in one of our Central European offices or from home on a full time basis, you’ll become a core part of the Product Team, and report to the head of product for Clarity.

Clarity is an exciting rapidly growing product in an emerging market opportunity, built on the latest in LLM technology. This is an amazing opportunity to be at the frontier of AI-native product development, and to build a product that sets the bar for the next generation of commerce interfaces.We operate in a rapidly evolving landscape and value an entrepreneurial mindset, rapid iteration, sharp thinking, curiosity, and a laser focus on outcomes.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Drive the development of new AI-native capabilities within Clarity, from ideation through to launch, with a focus on unlocking real business value.
  • Collaborate closely with engineers, designers, and product leaders to rapidly prototype and iterate on features powered by Generative AI and LLMs.
  • Engage deeply with customers to understand their evolving needs in the AI space and translate those into innovative, intelligent product solutions.
  • Partner with Customer Success and Data teams to ensure successful rollout of new AI driven features, streamlining workflows and gathering both qualitative and quantitative feedback.
  • Stay on the pulse of AI and GenAI advancements, market trends, and competitive offerings, translating insights into strategic bets and product direction.
  • Set the gold standard for how AI features are delivered, ensuring strong user adoption, ethical implementation, and customer satisfaction.
  • Monitor and report on AI product usage, performance, and business impact using a data-first approach, ensuring every decision is backed by insight.
  • Regularly synthesize findings and communicate progress, opportunities, and outcomes to leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.

Personal Attributes:

  • A product first, AI curious mindset, driven by impact and always looking for ways to push boundaries.
  • Deep empathy for users and a data informed, experimental approach to solving real world problems with emerging tech.
  • A natural collaborator who thrives in high autonomy environments and loves owning outcomes.
  • Crystal clear communicator able to make complex AI and product decisions understandable to stakeholders across the business.
  • Grit, creativity, and a constant drive to learn, especially in fast evolving fields like GenAI.

Professional Experience:

  • 3–6+ years in B2B SaaS Product Management, with experience in high growth, innovative product environments.
  • Proven success delivering AI powered or GenAI driven features, or a strong demonstrable interest and understanding of how LLMs, embeddings, and conversational interfaces drive product value.
  • Hands on involvement in product led growth initiatives and scaling usage of intelligent product experiences.
  • Demonstrated comfort working with engineers, data scientists, and researchers in cross functional, global teams.
  • Familiarity with ethical considerations and responsible AI implementation is a strong plus.

About this Role

We are seeking an experienced SAP BW4HANA & Datasphere Developer to join the AI & Data Service Tower (AIDA) within Corporate Analytics. This role offers an opportunity to design and deliver enterprise reporting and analytics solutions using SAP BW4HANA, SAP HANA and Datasphere technologies. Based in Pune, the successful candidate will collaborate with global stakeholders, support end-to-end solution delivery, and contribute to business-critical reporting across Finance, Logistics and HR domains. The role is ideal for professionals with strong technical expertise, analytical capabilities, and a passion for delivering high-quality data and reporting solutions.

What you’ll do

Prepare requirement specifications and develop new reports aligned with business needs.Design and develop reporting and analytics solutions using SAP BW4HANA and Datasphere.Create and implement end-to-end technical specifications.Develop, test and validate solutions through unit testing and product testing activities.Support user acceptance testing and collaborate with stakeholders to ensure successful outcomes.Participate in cutover, deployment and hyper-care activities.Build and design reporting solutions using SAP BW4HANA, Datasphere and SAP HANA technologies.Develop and work with new BW4HANA objects including Composite Providers, ADSOs and CDS View-based DataSources.Utilise ABAP, SQL and SQL Script to develop, enhance and optimise reporting solutions.Work across multiple projects while managing priorities effectively.Proactively identify and mitigate risks, issues, process gaps and technical debt.Engage with stakeholders across varying levels of seniority and technical expertise.Contribute to continuous improvement and the adoption of new technologies and business capabilities.

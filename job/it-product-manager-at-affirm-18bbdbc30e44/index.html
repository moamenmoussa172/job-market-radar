IT Product Manager

Fully remote · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 17 November 2026

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

About IT

The IT team enables Affirmers to work securely and efficiently. We manage internal devices, networks, and systems, ensuring employees can collaborate and perform at their best. Our team supports onboarding, troubleshooting, and scaling technology across locations. We focus on usability, reliability, and security to maintain a productive work environment. The IT team ensures that Affirmers have the tools and support they need—wherever they work.

About the role

As an IT Product Manager, you'll help Affirmers embrace new ways of working and get more value from the technology they use every day. Partnering with IT leadership and teams across Affirm, you'll translate employee needs into product priorities, shape technology roadmaps and lifecycle decisions, and drive adoption of collaboration, productivity, and emerging AI-powered capabilities.

What You'll Do

We are looking for a collaborative, adaptable IT Product Manager to help Affirmers work more effectively by shaping how we manage, use, and adopt collaboration, productivity, and AI-powered workplace technologies.

  • You will partner with IT leadership to define and manage priorities for Affirm's collaboration and productivity tools, including Slack, Notion, Google Workspace, and emerging AI-powered technologies.
  • You will turn employee needs, business priorities, and technical constraints into clear requirements, practical solutions, and actionable roadmaps that guide technology improvements.
  • You will help guide the technology lifecycle, from evaluating new solutions and developing business cases to optimizing existing investments, supporting vendor renewals, and recommending when to consolidate or retire tools.
  • You will lead the rollout and adoption of new tools, features, and AI-powered capabilities, partnering with Internal Communications, business leaders, and employee champions to help Affirmers navigate change with confidence.
  • You will connect Affirmers with practical guidance, self-service resources, and learning opportunities—leveraging vendor expertise, existing materials, and on-demand options—to help them build confidence and get more value from workplace technology.
  • You will define success measures, use employee feedback, adoption data, and support trends to evaluate outcomes, and recommend improvements that enhance the employee experience and inform technology priorities.
  • You will partner with IT Engineering, Security, Legal, Procurement, and vendors to deliver solutions that balance employee needs, business value, technical feasibility, cost, and risk.

What We Look For

You bring hands-on technology experience, a product mindset, and a people-first approach to solving problems, collaborating across teams, and helping others embrace change.

  • You have 5+ years of relevant experience in IT product or program management, workplace technology, technology-focussed organizational change management, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and practical experience.
  • You have experience identifying and delivering improvements to employee workflows through enterprise collaboration and productivity platforms, such as Slack, Notion, Google Workspace, or similar technologies, and are eager to apply that same approach to emerging AI capabilities.
  • You have experience gathering stakeholder needs, defining requirements, prioritizing opportunities, and developing actionable plans or roadmaps for technology solutions.
  • You have experience leading technology rollouts, organizational change initiatives, or employee adoption programs that help people confidently embrace new tools and ways of working.
  • You communicate clearly with different audiences and have experience identifying, organizing, and sharing resources that help employees understand and adopt workplace technologies.
  • You use data, employee feedback, and support trends to evaluate technology adoption, measure outcomes, and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • You build effective relationships across technical and business teams, using sound judgment to balance competing priorities, navigate ambiguity, and influence decisions without direct authority.

Benefits designed for you
Our benefits reflect our commitment to care, transparency, and flexibility. Here are a few highlights:

  • Health coverage at no cost: We cover 100% of premiums for employees and their dependents.
  • Spending stipends: Monthly stipends support your tech setup, and the ability to choose health and wellness options that are right for you.
  • Time off to recharge: Flexible time off and generous holiday calendars help you rest when you need to.
  • Own a piece of what you build: Our employee stock purchase plan (ESPP) lets you buy Affirm stock at a discount.

We’re committed to providing an inclusive interview process, including accommodations for candidates with disabilities. If you need support, we’re happy to help.

About the job

Senior program role leading the standardization and simplification of processes across projects, portfolio and business operations, engineering, project delivery, asset management, and digital platforms. The role brings the systems, data, and user groups involved onto common ways of working that fit Masdar’s business operating models and deliver clear business outcomes. In close partnership with Finance, Techno-Commercial, Enterprise Risk, and Health & Safety, as well as future regional business units, the role works from an understanding of system, data, and process flows to define and solution cross-functional, cross-system processes and end-to-end digital-thread programs across the asset lifecycle, from investment, engineering, and project development through construction, operations, and long-term asset management.

The Digital Thread Program Manager translates complex, cross-functional business requirements into integrated system solutions, workflows, documents and controls, and data models, ensuring seamless connectivity across engineering tools, project systems, EAM, ERP, and analytics platforms. Working with CDO Center-of-Excellence (CoE) teams, Enterprise Architecture, business operations, and external partners, the role reduces process duplication and handoff friction, promotes reusable standards across assets and geographies, and delivers transparent, automated, insight-driven workflows that improve productivity, decision-making, and portfolio performance. The program leader will also design interim solutions for short- and medium-term digital capabilities and processes while the CoE teams continue to deploying enterprise systems and platforms

Key Accountabilities:

  • Define and continuously refine Masdar’s digital-thread programs and roadmap across engineering, project lifecycle, construction, O&M, and asset management, while considering integrated processes with finance and enterprise risks.
  • Identify cross-functional process flow, critical data flows, system touchpoints, and decision nodes across the lifecycle and design integrated solutions.
  • Serve as the solution authority for threading systems, data, and processes across distributed platforms.
  • Act as a trusted partner to Techno-Commercial organization, as CDO subject-matter-expert (SME) in system platforms, data/ml platforms, digital products, and processes
  • Translate operational and portfolio requirements into clear system capabilities, integration needs, and workflow designs.
  • Bridge business and technology by converting requirements into implementable solutions with measurable outcomes (efficiency, cost, cycle time, risk reduction).
  • Partner with Enterprise Architects & operations IT team, and drive process solutioning integration and orchestration across the engineering lifecycle (design tools, EDMS, BIM, digital plants), project lifecycle (planning, scheduling, cost, risk), construction lifecycle (field systems, contractor platforms), investment lifecycle (capital planning, portfolio analytics), and O&M lifecycle (EAM, monitoring, performance analytics).
  • Design and deploy automated workflows and decision-support processes, leveraging Microsoft, Azure, low-code automation, and enterprise SaaS tools.
  • Drive standardization and reuse of processes across assets and geographies.
  • Lead and coordinate delivery across CoE teams, digital teams, operations, and external system integrators.
  • Define backlog, priorities, and execution roadmap for digital-thread initiatives.
  • Enable a single, connected view of asset and portfolio data, ensuring consistency across systems.
  • Define key business metrics, dashboards, and reporting frameworks tied to lifecycle performance.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field (Master’s preferred).

Preferred Experience:

  • 12-15+ years in digital transformation, systems integration, six sigma black belt or program management within utilities / renewable energy, infrastructure / asset-heavy industries, or leading consulting / investment platforms supporting such sectors.
  • Experience in solution architecture across enterprise platforms, as well as proven system implementation experiences across multiple lifecycle domains, including ERP, engineering, project delivery, and operations.
  • Demonstrated track record delivering complex, cross-system integrations, digital-thread–like initiatives, and value-driven use cases/products in utilities, power generation, or asset-heavy industries
  • Experience operating in a multi-vendor, partner-enabled delivery model; exposure to large-scale renewable or power-generation portfolios highly preferred.

Job-Specific Skills:

  • Strong understanding of engineering systems (BIM, EDMS, design tools), project and construction systems, EAM / O&M and asset-performance systems, and ERP / enterprise data architecture.
  • Proven ability to map and design end-to-end processes, data flows, and system integrations.
  • Exceptional business-translation capability — converting operational needs into technical solutions; strong stakeholder management and influencing across project delivery, business operations, engineering, and digital teams.
  • Experience with workflow automation, AI-enabled processes, and analytics-driven decision support; systems-thinking mindset operating at both strategic and execution levels.
  • Team Player and Clear Communicator who can influence peers, key users and stakeholder

Plus factors: background in utilities (e.g., NextEra, Enel, Iberdrola, TAQA, ACWA, DEWA, etc); familiarity with SAP, Oracle, O&M, IFS, Document-control, Construction SaaS, Azure, digital-twin or advanced-analytics ecosystems.

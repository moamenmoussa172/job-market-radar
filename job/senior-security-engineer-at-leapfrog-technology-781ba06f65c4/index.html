About the job

  • Lead the design, implementation, and maintenance of security controls and countermeasures
  • Stay up-to-date with the latest security threats, trends, and best practices
  • Collaborate with the SecOps team and other departments to ensure security is integrated into processes and systems
  • Drive the adoption of security tools, processes, and automation across the organization
  • Develop and maintain the organization's security policies and procedures
  • Perform security assessments, vulnerability scans, and penetration testing
  • Lead incident response and investigation efforts in case of security breaches or incidents
  • Ensure the organization's security practices and systems are compliant with relevant regulations and industry standards
  • Develop and maintain security-related documentation and knowledge-base articles
  • Mentor and guide junior SecOps engineers in their professional development
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of the SecOps team's processes and tools
  • Stay current with emerging security technologies and recommend their adoption when appropriate
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in security engineering, cloud infrastructure, or a similar field.
  • Strong understanding of security principles and industry best practices
  • Proven experience with security technologies, such as firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and encryption
  • Experience with scripting languages and development frameworks
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms and containerization technologies
  • In-depth knowledge of security regulations and industry standards
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • Experience in leading and mentoring security engineering teams

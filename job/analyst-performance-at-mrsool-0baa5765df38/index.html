The Job in a Nutshell💡

Performance Analyst joining Mrsool’s Performance & Analytics team. In this role, you will transform business questions into clear, data-driven insights that support decision-making across multiple verticals including food delivery, services, and logistics. You will analyze performance trends, diagnose operational and business issues, build analytical models, and deliver actionable recommendations. This role requires strong analytical thinking, hands-on problem solving, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, data-driven environment.
If you're eager to take on this rewarding opportunity, we’d love to hear from you. Apply today!

What You Will Do💡

  • Own analysis end-to-end across the business: scope the question, pull and validate the data, run the analysis, and land on a clear answer
  • Analyze business and operational performance across verticals; identify and quantify the drivers behind changes in orders, revenue, margin, and customer behavior
  • Run deep-dive investigations into performance trends and problems, separating signal from noise and diagnosing the underlying root cause rather than the symptom
  • Build financial and operational models to size opportunities and evaluate the impact of pricing, promotions, incentives, and product or operational changes
  • Conduct unit economics, profitability, cohort, retention, and trend analysis across customer segments, order types, and business initiatives
  • Pressure-test assumptions and business cases with data, and challenge conclusions that the numbers do not support
  • Translate analytical findings into clear, concise insights and concrete recommendations for stakeholders
  • Support a broad range of analytical projects and exploratory analysis as business priorities evolve

Requirements

What Are We Looking For❓

  • 3+ years of experience in an analytical role within technology, marketplace, operations, finance, or management consulting
  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with a proven ability to break down complex, ambiguous problems and structure them into clear analysis
  • Advanced SQL, with the ability to write and optimize complex queries and validate results independently
  • Strong Excel skills, including financial modeling, scenario analysis, and clear data presentation
  • Solid understanding of marketplace or on-demand delivery economics, including unit economics and profitability drivers
  • Experience with cohort, retention, and behavioral analysis
  • Strong analytical judgment: the ability to challenge assumptions, assess data quality, and interpret results critically
  • A rigorous, detail-oriented approach to analysis, paired with the commercial sense to know which numbers actually matter
  • Comfort working with ambiguity and limited briefs, and the initiative to move analysis forward independently
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to structure complex findings clearly for non-technical audiences

Who Will Excel❓

  • Experience in a high-growth or fast-paced business environment
  • A background in management consulting or a similarly structured problem-solving environment
  • Multi-vertical or multi-product analytical experience
  • Proficiency in Python or R
  • Experience with BI tools such as Metabase, Looker, or Tableau
  • Familiarity with product analytics, experimentation, or marketing analytics

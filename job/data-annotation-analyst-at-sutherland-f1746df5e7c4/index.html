About the job

Role Overview

Seeking detail-oriented specialists to support data annotation for a global technology platform. The role involves reviewing, labeling, and classifying datasets to support AI/ML models, abuse detection, and operational intelligence initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review, classify, and annotate data as per defined policies and quality standards
  • Support spam, phishing, and mail abuse detection initiatives
  • Work across multiple tools, dashboards, and workflows simultaneously
  • Identify anomalies, edge cases, and escalate trends where required
  • Maintain accuracy, productivity, and SLA targets in a 24x7 environment
  • 1+ year experience in Data Annotation/Data Labeling/Trust & Safety operations
  • Experience working in a 24x7, multi-vendor environment is essential
  • Strong eye for detail and ability to comprehend detailed instructions
  • Comfortable working across multiple tools and systems
  • Strong analytical thinking and pattern recognition skills
  • Flexibility to work rotational shifts as required
  • Must have PDP score of Exceeds Expectations or Outstanding Tenure of at least 18 months
  • No Active DA Interested candidates
  • Must be willing to work onsite in Taguig

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.

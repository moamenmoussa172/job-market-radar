Join Novartis Neuroscience and help advance innovative medicines that have the potential to transform the lives of people living with severe neurological conditions. As a Principal Scientist within our Neuroimmunology group in Basel, you will lead cutting-edge research into neuroinflammation and neurodegenerative diseases, translating scientific discoveries into promising therapeutic opportunities. Working alongside multidisciplinary teams, you will combine human biology, advanced disease models, biomarker research and translational science to validate novel therapeutic targets and accelerate drug discovery.

Job Description

Key responsibilities

  • Lead Neuroinflammation Research: Drive scientific research into novel neuroinflammation targets associated with neurodegenerative diseases, generating and interpreting experimental data to support target validation and therapeutic development.
  • Drive Drug Discovery Strategy: Lead the biology strategy and scientific execution of multidisciplinary drug discovery programs, establishing critical experiments, milestones and decision criteria to advance promising therapeutic targets.
  • Advance Human Cellular Models: Design and oversee in vitro studies using human iPSC-derived disease models, co-culture systems and innovative neuroimmunology platforms to investigate disease mechanisms and therapeutic opportunities.
  • Lead Preclinical Research: Design and support in vivo studies to investigate neuroinflammatory pathways, evaluate therapeutic interventions and generate robust proof-of-mechanism data for pipeline programs.
  • Integrate Translational Science: Connect insights from clinical datasets, biomarker research and emerging technologies, including NULISA-based proteomics, with preclinical findings to strengthen target validation and support reverse translational research.
  • Provide Scientific Leadership and Mentorship: Supervise, coach and develop scientists, fostering scientific excellence, innovation and a collaborative research environment while building productive internal and external partnerships.
  • Drive Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with multidisciplinary teams across Neuroimmunology, Immunology, Translational Medicine, Data Science and other functions to align scientific strategies and accelerate research programs.
  • Ensure Scientific Excellence and Communication: Maintain high standards of experimental design, data integrity and scientific documentation, effectively communicating research findings, recommendations and programme progress to project teams and scientific leadership.
  • Shape Neuroimmunology Innovation: Contribute to the development of scientific capabilities, emerging technologies and research strategies that advance understanding of neuroinflammation and immune-mediated neurological diseases.

Essential requirements

  • Education and Scientific Experience: PhD in Neuroimmunology, Neuroscience or a closely related scientific discipline, with relevant postdoctoral and/or pharmaceutical industry research experience.
  • Neuroinflammation Expertise: Strong scientific expertise in neuroinflammation, neuroimmunology and neurodegenerative diseases, particularly the role of glial cells and immune-mediated mechanisms in disease progression.
  • Human Cellular Models: Demonstrated experience developing and applying human iPSC-derived disease models, including neuronal and glial cell systems, co-culture assays and other relevant in vitro neuroimmunology platforms.
  • Target Validation and Drug Discovery: Proven experience in therapeutic target identification and validation, including genetic perturbation approaches and the evaluation of different therapeutic modalities using in vitro and in vivo experimental systems.
  • Preclinical and Translational Research: Strong understanding of preclinical disease models, experimental pharmacology and translational research approaches, with the ability to integrate mechanistic findings to inform drug discovery decisions.
  • Experimental Design and Data Interpretation: Demonstrated ability to formulate scientific hypotheses, design rigorous experiments, analyse complex biological datasets and translate findings into actionable research strategies, supported by a strong scientific publication record.
  • Scientific Leadership and Collaboration: Experience leading scientific projects and collaborating effectively with multidisciplinary teams, with the ability to influence research direction, mentor colleagues and contribute to complex drug discovery programs.
  • Communication and Scientific Problem-Solving: Excellent analytical, communication and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate complex scientific findings clearly, navigate scientific challenges and make evidence-based recommendations to diverse stakeholders.

Desirable requirements

  • Advanced Proteomics and Biomarker Research: Experience applying emerging proteomics technologies, including NULISA-based platforms, biomarker discovery approaches or integrated multi-omics analysis to neurodegenerative disease research.
  • Industry Drug Discovery Experience: Experience advancing neuroimmunology or neuroscience programs within pharmaceutical or biotechnology research, including cross-functional target validation, translational strategy development and progression towards clinical development.

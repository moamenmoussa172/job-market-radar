About the job

Senior IT-OT Engineer - APICOM

Location: APICOM Facility, College Park, Dublin, Ireland

Competitive salary and benefits

Introduction to role:

Are you ready to bridge IT and OT to keep a next-generation API facility running flawlessly—and help deliver medicines to patients faster? In our brand-new manufacturing hub in Dublin, you will build and protect the digital backbone that powers automated production, real-time data, and rigorous compliance. Your work will directly influence production reliability, product quality, and speed to patients.

You will join a multidisciplinary environment where cloud platforms, control systems, and data tools come together to create an intelligent, resilient manufacturing ecosystem. From DeltaV, PAS-X, Aveva PI, to a range of IT-OT Systems. You will make critical systems talk to each other securely and at scale. Do you want to own the interfaces that turn process data into decisions while ensuring zero interruption to manufacturing?

Accountabilities:

IT Infrastructure Support: Coordinate preventive maintenance and performance tuning to ensure high availability for manufacturing-critical IT systems; partner closely with enterprise IT on network, server, and storage reliability; monitor private cloud environments to meet performance benchmarks and production SLAs.

OT System Support: Harmonise IT services with OT needs so production systems run uninterrupted; support applications such as DeltaV, AMS, PAS-X, SIPAT, PharmaMV, and OEM/SSPE without introducing downtime; proactively remediate issues before they impact batches or schedules.

Cybersecurity Implementation: Protect IT and OT assets with fit-for-purpose controls that safeguard performance and compliance; implement OT cybersecurity measures and collaborate with security specialists on a unified strategy; identify and resolve vulnerabilities in accordance with regulatory expectations.

Data Integration: Engineer connectivity and seamless data flow between OT and enterprise platforms; integrate OT data into Aveva PI and downstream analytics or business intelligence systems; ensure process and production data is captured, stored, and accessible with integrity.

System Administration: Maintain operational efficiency of production-related applications and services; drive timely incident resolution to protect yield and throughput; lead root cause analysis and performance optimization to prevent recurrence.

Vendor Management: Coordinate and supervise vendors to deliver robust solutions and services; act as the single point of contact for production teams on critical IT/OT issues; hold partners accountable for quality, compliance, and uptime.

Compliance and Best Practices: Ensure all IT/OT activities meet regulatory and internal standards; maintain GxP compliance and 21 CFR Part 11 readiness; document systems, configurations, and procedures to pharmaceutical industry standards.

Continuous Improvement and Innovation: Pursue improvements in monitoring, analytics, and automation to enhance performance; initiate changes that reduce downtime and increase observability; contribute to digital transformation projects that scale our intelligent manufacturing vision.

Essential Skills/Experience:

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Experienced working on ATEX environments and able to demonstrate specifying system requirements for automation systems.
  • Hands-on experience with systems like DeltaV Batch, DeltaV Live, Emerson AMS, Korber PAS-X, SIPAT, SIEMENS TIA/WinCC, Aveva PI, PharmaMV, and Kepware OPC.
  • Able to demonstrate PAS-X configuration and system administration
  • Configuration and administration of HMI thin client systems
  • Database management and system integration
  • Setup and configuration of automation infrastructure to connect all OT systems.
  • Significant experience in managing cloud-based systems, network infrastructure, and cybersecurity.
  • Knowledge on using AI for systems management.
  • Solid understanding of IT and OT convergence.
  • Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
  • Knowledge of cybersecurity standard methodologies in both IT and OT environments.
  • Familiarity with pharmaceutical manufacturing regulations and standards (e.g., GxP, 21 CFR Part 11).
  • Good interpersonal skills to work effectively with multi-functional teams.
  • Ability to prioritise and manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
  • High attention to detail and a commitment to operational excellence.

Desirable Skills/Experience:

  • Experience supporting greenfield manufacturing start-ups, commissioning, and site readiness.
  • Practical knowledge of ISA/IEC 62443 controls and OT incident response processes.
  • Scripting and automation skills (e.g., PowerShell, SQL, etc.) for orchestration and monitoring.
  • Experience with virtualisation and thin client platforms (e.g., VMware, Citrix, Visunet).
  • Significant contribution to validation, audit readiness, and documentation in regulated settings.
  • Track record of Lean or continuous improvement initiatives in IT/OT environments.
  • Strong vendor and contract management experience across OT ecosystems.
  • Additional certifications in Operational Technology (OT) and/or Cybersecurity.

Lead a major finance transformation and shape the future of enterprise systems

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a Finance SAP Implementation Lead at BVE Walsall, near Walsall and also Bristol.

The role

As aFinance SAP Implementation Lead, you'll play a pivotal role in delivering a major SAP ERP transformation across our Engineering & Systems Integration and Mission Technologies businesses. This is an exciting opportunity to lead the finance workstream for a complex business-critical programme, helping to modernise systems, improve processes, enhance financial controls, and support manufacturing operations across the business.

Working closely with finance teams, project stakeholders, and business leaders, you'll drive the successful design, configuration, testing, deployment, and adoption of SAP solutions. This role offers the chance to make a lasting impact while developing your expertise in large-scale digital transformation within a leading defence, aerospace, and engineering organisation.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Leading finance process design activities to support SAP implementation.
  • Ensuring robust financial controls, governance, and compliance requirements are embedded throughout the programme.
  • Developing reporting structures, chart of accounts, cost centres, profit centres, and master data requirements.
  • Creating finance training materials, user guides, and operating procedures.
  • Delivering training sessions and workshops to finance users and key stakeholders.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements with 3 days onsite however, this is flexible and can change with business needs.

Essential experience of the Finance SAP Implementation Lead

  • Experience leading or supporting SAP ERP implementation projects from a finance perspective.
  • Strong finance process improvement and transformation experience.
  • Demonstrable finance expertise combined with business process knowledge.
  • Experience working within complex change or transformation programmes.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement, communication, and workshop facilitation skills.

Qualifications for the Finance SAP Implementation Lead

  • Finance expertise required but no formal qualification

About the job

AWS Applied AI Solutions (AAIS) is building toward a future where every business innovates with Amazon AI teammates. To get there, we build AI solutions that improve human capabilities and transform entire business functions. We create end-to-end products that surprise and delight out-of-the-box, making complex things easy and hard things possible, with no cloud experience required. We start with customers who embrace the future and build bridges to meet the rest where they are. We pursue ambitious opportunities with conviction, and we are looking for builders who share that mindset.
AWS is responsible for driving revenue, adoption, and growth from the largest and fastest growing small- and mid-market accounts to enterprise-level customers including public sector. An AWS Cloud Operations Architect (CoA) is a trusted advisor and cloud operations architect for our Enterprise Support customers. As a trusted advisor you'll have a direct impact in helping our customers gain the most value from cloud technology. You'll craft and execute strategies to drive our customers' adoption and use of AWS services. You will provide advocacy and technical guidance to help plan and build solutions using best practices and proactively keep your customers' AWS environments operationally healthy. This is a post-sales advisory role within our Enterprise Support team. The close relationships developed with your customers will allow you to understand their business and operational needs and technical challenges and help them achieve the greatest value from cloud technologies. You will work with Public Sector customers in Norway - including government agencies, municipalities, and public institutions - providing strategic technical guidance helping plan and build solutions using best practices and proactively keeping their AWS environments operationally healthy. Our Cloud Operations Architects partner with some of the most impactful organizations in the Nordic public sector. You'll provide advice on architecture, support strategy, project, and launch planning as well as ongoing operational issues. As we continue to expand you'll have plenty of opportunities to develop your technical consulting and leadership skills. You'll work with talented cloud technologists whilst expanding your knowledge of AWS products.
Key job responsibilities
• Provide customers with technical expertise across multiple technology domains to achieve operational excellence in security resilience and efficiency • Serve as a trusted advisor for Enterprise Support Public Sector customers in Norway driving technical discussions regarding incidents trade-offs and risk management • Provide architectural guidance and lead reviews using the AWS Well-Architected Framework to ensure solutions are designed for successful deployment in the cloud • Proactively identify and mitigate risks driving efficiencies and simplification in customer operations and product adoption • Collaborate with Solutions Architects Account Managers and other internal teams to solve your customers' most challenging technical problems • Develop trusting relationships with customers across multiple levels understanding their business needs and technical challenges • Uplift customer capabilities by running workshops enablement sessions and operational reviews • With a bias for action proactively find opportunities for customers to gain additional value from AWS

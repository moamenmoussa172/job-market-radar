About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Are you passionate about learning languages? About helping people connect and communicate across cultures, for travel, friendship, or career advancement? About making an impact to our company and our 25MM+ learners? Come join our NY-based team as a Product Manager, Marketing Tech

About the Role:

We are looking for a Product Manager, Marketing Tech to help Babbel’s marketing teams reach learners with reliable, privacy-conscious marketing technology. Working closely with the Growth Product Director and Engineering Manager, you’ll own the roadmap for the MarTech team and enable a global team of marketers to grow Babbel’s subscription base.

The MarTech team maintains and improves Babbel’s marketing tech stack. It enables marketing teams to integrate with partner solutions and to launch, manage, and optimize campaigns efficiently, while ensuring data fidelity and compliance. The team also bridges our offices in Berlin and New York and the departments that depend on marketing data. You’ll be the product lead for an agile, cross-functional team and work day to day with Engineering, Data & Analytics, Marketing, Legal and Privacy, and external partners, from discovery through delivery and measurement.

What you’ll do

  • Set direction and priorities: Own the marketing technology roadmap, aligned to company strategy and OKRs. Run end-to-end product development and make trade-offs clear across user needs, business value, effort, and risk.
  • Improve tracking and measurement: Guide web and app tracking, including event definitions, session logic, data enrichment, and attribution, across client-side, server-side, and server-to-server setups. Partner with Engineering and Analytics to keep data accurate and useful for marketing decisions.
  • Embrace AI-assisted development: Work with fast-moving engineers. Use AI tools to write and refine features and user stories, and build AI prototypes to test ideas early.
  • Develop audience and activation capabilities: Shape how consented customer data flows across channels through Tealium CDP. With Marketing, Data, and Engineering, define use cases, data needs, identity and consent rules, integrations, and success measures.
  • Support partner measurement: Improve Adjust integrations for mobile attribution and partner performance. Coordinate conversion events, postbacks, API changes, and data-quality checks with internal and external partners.
  • Own consent and privacy tooling: Manage OneTrust product needs, including consent experiences, configuration, testing, and SDK and event alignment, to support GDPR, CCPA, and other requirements. Work with Legal and Privacy on interpretation and implementation.
  • Run effective discovery and delivery: Work closely with marketers to understand their needs, define problems and requirements, and write concise documentation. Take work from hypothesis and MVP through launch, monitoring, and iteration.
  • Measure outcomes: Set hypotheses and success metrics before delivery. Use experiments, dashboards, and user feedback to evaluate and improve features.
  • Strengthen platform reliability: Keep tracking and tag management in Google Tag Manager and Tealium maintainable, with consent-safe triggers, good performance, and redundant or unreliable tags removed.
  • Build shared understanding: Lead agile rituals and decisions, share progress and risks, and help resolve dependencies. Lead with clear context, constructive challenge, and reliable follow-through.
  • Improve ways of working: With Engineering, keep improving team engagement and velocity. Keep documentation, integration plans, and support workflows up to date, and look for repeatable fixes that cut manual work.

Tools & Systems

  • Core: OneTrust, Adjust, Tealium, Google Tag Manager, web and mobile tracking SDKs, partner APIs and conversion events, and payment integrations such as Stripe.
  • Analytics and experimentation: Google Analytics 4, Amplitude, Tableau, StatSig, and proprietary tools.
  • Data: SQL-based warehouses such as DataBricks.

What you bring

  • 3+ years of experience in product management, ideally working with backend engineers.
  • Experience implementing ad tracking for websites and apps, and integrating third-party services via client-side, server-to-server, and via API connections.
  • In-depth knowledge of client-side and server-side marketing tracking with tag management tools (Google Tag Manager and Tealium).
  • Hands-on experience with consent and privacy management systems such as OneTrust, and a working understanding of GDPR and CCPA.
  • A good understanding of marketing and online user behavioral analysis.
  • Strong analytical skills: you enjoy working with data and pay attention to detail.
  • Clear communication and proactive stakeholder management across marketers, engineers, legal partners, and external vendors.
  • Strong project and time management, with the ability to juggle multiple stakeholders, priorities, and contexts in parallel.
  • A drive to keep learning across product, engineering, and marketing, especially tracking, APIs, and marketing automation.
  • Comfort working with colleagues in European time zones, including starting early to collaborate with the team in Berlin.

Nice to have

  • Experience with customer data platforms, ideally Tealium CDP
  • Previous experience at an AdTech or MarTech company
  • Previous experience as a backend or full-stack software engineer, or as a data analyst or engineer
  • Knowledge of event-driven architecture
  • Knowledge of deep link tracking
  • Basic SQL (DataBricks, MySQL) and good knowledge of JavaScript
  • Multilingual, or interested in language learning

How you’ll work

You’ll bring structure to complex, cross-functional problems while keeping the process approachable. You’ll listen carefully, make evidence-informed decisions, explain trade-offs in plain language, and create space for teammates and partners to contribute.

Success means marketing teams can act on dependable data, consent is respected across customer journeys, and the MarTech platform is easier to operate and improve.

Benefits

  • Join a global company with purpose
  • Competitive compensation, PTO, benefits
  • Have an Impact on Babbel's product, company, and learners
  • Career growth and skill development

This position is located in NYC and requires a minimum of 1-2 days/week in office.

Salary Information

In New York City, the reasonable annualized full-time salary for this role is $100,000 - $120,000k placement within this range will vary based on several factors including, but not limited to experience, education, licensure/certifications, training and skill level.

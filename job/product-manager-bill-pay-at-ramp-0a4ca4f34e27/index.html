About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the Role

Every business pays bills, and many of them still do it by hand. Someone opens the invoice, types it in, codes it, chases approvals, pays it, matches it and syncs it to the books. It's one of the largest manual workflows left in finance.

We're building the version where software does that work. Bills come in and get read, coded, matched, approved and paid, and a person steps in only when judgment matters. Bill Pay is already one of Ramp's fastest-growing products. The next few years decide who owns AP for the next decade, and we plan for it to be us.

We're looking for a PM who wants to build that.

  • Please note that this role requires in-person presence at our NYC or SF office at least 2 days/week.
  • Level will be determined based on the candidate’s interview performance and previous scope of responsibility.

What You’ll Do

  • Own strategy, roadmap and results for a major part of Bill Pay.
  • Spend real time with AP clerks, controllers and CFOs, and turn what you see into product.
  • Prototype with customers, set quality bars with Engineering and Data, and ship automation people trust.
  • Measure success by adoption, automation and time saved, then use the data to decide what comes next.
  • Work with Procurement, Accounting, Payments and Risk so a bill moves from inbox to payment to the books without gaps.

Problems you'll work on

  • How do we get from "AI drafts the bill" to "AI handles the bill" without customers losing trust or control?
  • When should an agent act on its own, when should it ask, and how do we show our work so a controller signs off with confidence?
  • How do we make one product that a 10-person startup sets up in minutes and a multi-entity enterprise with an ERP and an AP team runs at scale?
  • How do we catch the wrong bill, the duplicate and the suspicious vendor change before the money leaves?
  • How do we use what Ramp knows across millions of transactions to help customers pay smarter, not just faster?

What You Need

  • You have 5+ years building products, and you've owned at least one from first customer conversation to real adoption.
  • You've built with AI hands-on, whether in products, prototypes or side projects, and you have opinions about evals, failure modes and user control.
  • You're technical enough to go deep with engineers on data models and integrations, and you pull your own data.
  • You can make complex workflows simple without stripping out what power users need.
  • You learn new domains fast. You don't need to know AP today, but you'll want to know it better than anyone in six months.
  • You write clearly, decide quickly and get teams to ship.
  • You want a hard problem, a high bar and more scope as soon as you've earned it.

Nice-to-Haves

  • You've worked on AP, payments, accounting or other finance software.
  • You've shipped AI to production, especially document understanding or agent workflows.
  • You've built for enterprise customers: ERPs, multi-entity setups, complex approvals and permissions.
  • You've worked at a high-growth startup and know what it takes to scale a product that's working.

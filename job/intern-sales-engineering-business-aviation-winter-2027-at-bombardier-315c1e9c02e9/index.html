What are your contributions to the team?

Sales Engineering Intern — Business Aviation

You’re eager to learn and make an impact. You’re excited by aircraft, eager to dive into sales engineering, and passionate about working hard to support a dynamic team. You bring curiosity, energy, and a desire to grow in business aviation.

Your role

  • Support sales engineers by helping research products and competitors to uncover key advantages and what sets us apart.
  • Assist in crafting clear, persuasive presentations and comparisons that highlight our value to customers.
  • Help prepare concise, mobile-friendly materials and participate in customer trainings and internal briefings.

What you’ll do

  • Contribute to building and delivering customer presentations, comparisons, and business-case stories in collaboration with the team
  • Work alongside Sales on pursuits, RFPs, and executive briefings; learn by doing
  • Help translate data into visuals and talking points that are clear and memorable
  • Assist in keeping content and competitive intelligence up to date across the sales funnel
  • Conduct route studies and mission analyses to evaluate aircraft performance on real-world customer missions.
  • Create range maps, competitive analyses, and technical presentations to support aircraft sales campaigns.
  • Perform takeoff, landing, and airport performance studies, considering runway and operational constraints.
  • Develop and maintain automation tools, databases, and dashboards using Excel, Power BI, Power Query, Think-Cell, and other data analysis tools to improve efficiency and support decision-making.

What you bring

  • A strong passion for aviation and motivation to work hard and learn quickly
  • Clear, enthusiastic communication skills and a willingness to ask questions
  • Interest in simplifying complex topics and sharing what you learn with others
  • Basic familiarity with slides and data; eager to develop presentation skills
  • A collaborative mindset, good judgment, and self-motivation

Nice to have

  • Coursework, projects, or hobbies related to business aviation, aircraft performance, or sales engineering
  • Experience working in teams or supporting group projects

Why this team?

We bring fresh ideas and deep market knowledge to help shape customer perceptions. You'll learn from experienced professionals while making meaningful contributions.

Location: Hybrid

We’re an equal opportunity employer. We welcome talent from all backgrounds and experiences. If you’re passionate about aviation and excited to work hard and learn, we’d love to hear from you.

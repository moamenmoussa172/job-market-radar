About the job

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Role Overview
Responsible for the planning and delivery of the AI Customer Service product line, covering user-facing AI chatbots, agent-facing AI assistance tools, and AI quality assurance platforms, driving continuous improvement in customer service efficiency and user experience.
Core Responsibilities

  • AI Customer Service Chatbot: Lead conversational AI product design, including intent recognition, dialogue management, self-service flow optimization, and multi-region/multi-language customer service capability building
  • Agent AI Assistance Tools: Design and iterate intelligent tools for customer service agents, including real-time reply suggestions, knowledge base/SOP intelligent retrieval, session auto-summarization, and multi-language translation
  • AI QA Platform: Own the AI-driven customer service quality management system, including automated scoring, QA calibration, DSAT (customer dissatisfaction) case handling, and strategy optimization

Requirements
Must Have:

  • 5+ years of internet product management experience, with 2+ years focused on AI/LLM/conversational AI products
  • End-to-end delivery experience building and scaling AI customer service or intelligent dialogue products from 0 to 1 with continuous iteration
  • Excellent PRD writing skills; able to independently produce high-quality product requirement documents and drive cross-functional execution
  • Data-driven product mindset; familiarity with core CS metrics such as resolution rate, CSAT, and human escalation rate

Preferred:

  • Product experience in crypto or fintech industry
  • Hands-on experience using LLM tools to augment product workflows

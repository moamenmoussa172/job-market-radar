Junior Associate Scientist (Intern USP)

Sanofi · Gent, Belgium

Onsite · Intern / Student · Data Scientist · Posted 20 November 2026

  • Location: Ghent, Belgium
  • Start date: February 2026
  • Duration: 4 to 6 months
  • Work Model: onsite, full time

About the job

As a Junior Associate Scientist (Intern USP), you will support the development of innovative NANOBODY® medicines by working with microbial cultures, fermentation processes and laboratory techniques used to produce therapeutic molecules. You will gain hands-on experience with bioreactors and scientific analysis, contributing to real research projects while developing practical skills in biopharmaceutical research and upstream processing.

Please note: This is an unpaid internship designed to provide students with hands-on experience in pharmaceutical marketing, exposure to a global healthcare organization, and opportunities for professional development through practical projects, coaching, and mentorship.

About Sanofi

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main responsibilities:

  • Cultivate microbial cultures and support the growth of Pichia pastoris.
  • Participate in fermentation processes and bioreactor operations
  • Perform laboratory activities, including microscopy and cell viability assessments.
  • Support sample processing, clarification and harvesting activities.
  • Perform protein analysis using techniques such as SDS-PAGE and Western Blot.
  • Analyse experimental results and document findings in electronic laboratory notebooks.
  • Contribute to ongoing research projects and present results to the team.

About you

To excel in this role you will need to have:

  • Be currently enrolled in a professional Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Biotechnology, Life Sciences, Chemistry or a related field.
  • A genuine interest in biopharmaceutical research and drug development.
  • A hands-on approach and willingness to learn new laboratory techniques.
  • Analytical thinking and attention to detail when working with experimental data.
  • Ability to document laboratory work and communicate scientific findings.
  • A collaborative mindset and ability to work both independently and as part of a team.

SaaS Implementation Project Manager

Onehealthineers · Remote

Fully remote · Technical Project / Program Manager · Posted 19 November 2026

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.
Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

Location: UK or Portugal | Travel: 10–15% | Full-time

We’re looking for an experienced SaaS Implementation Project Manager to lead customer implementations across Varian’s oncology software portfolio throughout EMEA.

This is a customer-facing role where you’ll own complex SaaS implementations from kick-off through to go-live, bringing together customers, Implementation Engineers and cross-functional teams to ensure successful delivery.

You won’t perform the technical installation yourself. Instead, you’ll make sure the customer, technology, resources, risks, decisions and timelines all come together successfully.

What you’ll do

  • Lead SaaS implementation projects from kick-off to go-live.
  • Manage project plans, milestones, resources, dependencies and Statements of Work.
  • Act as the primary project contact for customers across clinical, IT and operational teams.
  • Identify risks early, manage issues and drive escalations through to resolution.
  • Coordinate customer onboarding and implementation readiness.
  • Partner with SaaS Implementation Engineers and teams across Sales, Customer Success, Product, Engineering and Service.
  • Lead project meetings, ensuring clear decisions, actions, owners and deadlines.
  • Manage multiple customer projects across EMEA while maintaining clear communication and delivery visibility.

What we’re looking for

  • Experience managing SaaS, cloud or enterprise software implementations.
  • Strong customer-facing project management experience.
  • Excellent stakeholder, risk and project governance skills.
  • Confidence coordinating technical and non-technical teams.
  • The ability to manage multiple projects and competing priorities.
  • Strong communication skills, including the confidence to navigate challenging customer conversations.
  • Experience within healthcare, oncology or another regulated environment would be an advantage, but isn’t essential.

Location & languages

We welcome applications from candidates based in Portugal and the UK, subject to local employing arrangements. Fluent English is essential, and Spanish, Portuguese or Polish language skills would be particularly valuable.

You should also be comfortable travelling approximately 10–15% across the region.

Why join us?

This is an opportunity to work at the intersection of healthcare, technology and customer delivery, leading SaaS implementations that support oncology organisations across Europe. If you enjoy bringing structure to complexity, building strong customer relationships and driving technology projects through to successful go-live, we’d love to hear from you

About the job

Team description

You'll keep our warehouses and transport network across the US and Canada running smoothly, so the right stock is in the right place at the right time. This role has real room to make a difference: you'll raise inventory accuracy, move our returns process into our warehouse management and SAP systems, and take full ownership of how our logistics providers perform. If you enjoy digging into data, finding the root cause of a problem and seeing your fix change how a whole region works, this role gives you the scope and the trust to do it.

🎥 Take a look inside our São Paulo office

What you'll do

  • Run daily warehouse and transport performance for the US and Canada, spotting risks and bottlenecks early and fixing them before they affect customer
  • Lead improvement projects of medium to high complexity, including automating the returns flow in our warehouse management and SAP systems
  • Track the measures that matter, such as cost, lead time, inventory accuracy, on time and in full delivery, and service level, then dig into the root cause when one slips
  • Own provider performance and governance, and manage import and export flows for inventory transfers and supplier deliveries in line with regulations
  • Run the procurement process from request to order, balancing speed, cost and quality

You'll be great for this role if…

  • Fluent English, written and spoken, makes it easy to work with teams and stakeholders in different regions.
  • Strong problem solving skills help in finding root causes and putting practical fixes in place.
  • Strong command of Excel, SQL and Power BI or Tableau, with experience in warehouse and transport management systems and the ability to build an analysis from scratch.
  • Comfort making decisions based on data and using metrics to keep improving how things run.
  • Ability to juggle several stakeholders, prioritise competing requests and stay hands on, while helping teammates grow their own skills.

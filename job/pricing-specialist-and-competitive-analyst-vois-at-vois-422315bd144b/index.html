About this Role

We are seeking a Pricing Specialist & Competitive Analyst to develop and execute pricing strategies that support sustainable business growth, profitability and market competitiveness.

Based in Pune or Bangalore, this role combines pricing strategy, commercial analysis, cost modelling and market intelligence. The successful candidate will analyse internal cost structures and external market dynamics, monitor competitor activity and industry trends, and translate insights into commercial decisions that strengthen competitive positioning

What you’ll do

Develop pricing models and tools: Design, build and maintain solutions for pricing calculations, analysis and reporting.Monitor pricing performance: Track key pricing metrics, identify trends and provide actionable insights to support management decision-making.Manage annual pricing refreshes: Update cost inputs, overheads and target margins, revise price books and communicate changes across VOIS.Support digital pricing solutions: Provide commercial expertise and define business requirements for digital pricing tools.Maintain data accuracy: Ensure pricing tables, business logic and cost information within standard pricing tools remain accurate and up to date.Improve tool adoption: Support adoption and effectiveness of standard pricing tools across the organisation.Deliver pricing analysis: Conduct research and data analysis to identify trends, patterns and opportunities for pricing optimisation.Shape deal pricing: Develop commercial strategies that support profitability while considering price positioning, customer requirements and overall deal structure.Support cost modelling: Define and analyse programme metrics, financial-year targets and strategic priorities, and review cost models to support competitive pricing.

Who you are

You bring a strong combination of commercial judgement, analytical capability and pricing expertise, with the ability to turn complex data into clear, actionable recommendations.

You should have:

  • 8+ years of experience working within a global organisation.
  • 8+ years of experience in similar functions, including experience working with or leading virtual teams.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capability with a data-driven approach.
  • Demonstrable experience in pricing strategy, pricing models, profitability optimisation and competitive analysis.
  • Experience collecting, interpreting and presenting pricing and commercial data.
  • Strong understanding of business and competitive dynamics.
  • Experience with market intelligence and the ability to identify commercially relevant trends and opportunities.
  • Experience in deal shaping and negotiation.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work across teams and stakeholders.
  • Experience managing pricing-related projects from initiation through completion.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel expertise, including using advanced functionality to support analysis, reporting and business decision-making.
  • Understanding of service-industry pricing principles and practices.
  • Understanding of organisational complexity and its potential impact on customer proposals.
  • A bachelor’s degree in business, telecommunications or a related discipline.
  • An MBA or relevant professional qualification is desirable.
  • Familiarity with finance processes is desirable.

This role offers the opportunity to work across pricing strategy, commercial decision-making, competitive intelligence and digital pricing tools within a global environment. What skills you will learn

Through this role, you will have opportunities to further develop your capabilities in:

  • Advanced pricing and profitability optimisation.

  • Commercial deal shaping and cost modelling.

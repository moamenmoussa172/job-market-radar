About the job

Amazon Flex works directly with independent contractors – called delivery partners – to make deliveries to our customers. With Amazon Flex, individuals can now be their own boss delivering Amazon packages. Amazon Flex is powered by a mobile app that works in concert with our advanced logistics systems that allows delivery partners to on-board, schedule delivery times, guide them through their deliveries with in-app mapping and navigation, and more. Amazon Flex is seeking an exceptional Program Manager to lead our UK/EU Service Level Standards (SLS) program. This role will be responsible for defining, implementing, and continuously improving service level standards across UK/EU Amazon Flex, ensuring world-class delivery partner experience and customer satisfaction.
Key job responsibilities
Strategic Leadership
• Define and own the UK/EU strategy for Flex Service Level Standards.
• Develop the overall program vision, mission, and tenets that align with Amazon Flex's long-term objectives
• Create scalable mechanisms and frameworks that can be adapted to regional requirements while maintaining global consistency
• Partner with senior leadership to set objectives, analyze data, and drive improvements quantified with metrics
Program Execution
• Establish world-class evaluation processes for delivery partners that ensure consistent service quality
• Identify root causes and recognize trends through customer and delivery partner feedback to solve ambiguous problems
• Proactively mitigate risks before they become roadblocks to program success
• Drive cross-functional collaboration with Operations, Technology, Product, and Marketing teams globally
- Experience using data and metrics to determine and drive improvements
- Experience owning program strategy, end to end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience in program or project management
- Advanced degree, or Bachelor's degree
- Experience working with and influencing senior level stakeholders
- Experience working in a program management capacity and/or leading large-scale complex projects
- Experience in English-language communication skills, both written and verbal
- Experience leading process improvements
- Advanced degree, or MS degree
- Experience in program management, logistics, operations, supply chain, transportation, or equivalent
- Experience with operations/supply chain
- Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen, Prince2 certifications, or any other project management certification

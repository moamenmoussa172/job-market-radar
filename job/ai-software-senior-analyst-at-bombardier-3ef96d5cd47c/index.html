What are your contributions to the team?

  • Act as the subject matter expert for Methods applications and AI-enabled tools, maintaining a clear view of the organization’s technology landscape.
  • Partner with cross-functional teams (Production, Quality, Methods, Planning, Health & Safety, Customer Delivery) to identify operational needs and opportunities for digital enablement.
  • Lead application portfolio assessments to identify redundancies, gaps, and opportunities to consolidate, standardize, or retire tools.
  • Establish and enforce governance for tool selection and adoption, including the evaluation and approval of any new applications to ensure alignment with enterprise standards and IT strategy.
  • Deploy and integrate new and existing AI models into enterprise systems, including SAP S/4HANA. Ensure performance, scalability and responsiveness of AI-powered applications to drive measurable business value.
  • Contribute to model lifecycle management, including monitoring, evaluation and continuous improvement. (fix responsibilities so that it's not deployment and desgining but more with feedback above and notes)
  • Maintain clean, modular and well-documented code following best practices.
  • Engage with business stakeholders to gather requirements, incorporate feedback, and translate them into effective technical solutions.
  • Stay current on advancements in AI/ML and Software Engineering to proactively introduce relevant innovations to the organization.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You have 10+ years of experience in solution architecture, enterprise systems, or application landscape management, including experience with AI and data-driven technologies.
  • You have a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, or a related technical field.
  • You have strong proficiency in Python, SQL, React, PySpark, REST APIs, and full-stack integration.
  • You have hands-on experience implementing machine learning models, including time series forecasting, predictive analytics, image recognition, and natural language processing (NLP).
  • You have experience with cloud platforms, preferably Microsoft Azure, including Azure Databricks, Azure Machine Learning, AI Foundry, App Services, AKS, and Blob Storage.
  • You have proficiency with Agile methodologies, Azure DevOps, and application lifecycle management tools.You have experience scripting in Linux and/or PowerShell.
  • You have strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and the ability to make sound decisions.
  • You have excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to present technical concepts to both technical and business stakeholders.

About the job

Why join us?

At Bombardier, we design, build and maintain the world’s peak-performing aircraft for the world’s most discerning people and businesses, governments and militaries. We have been successful in setting the highest standards by putting our people at the heart of it all, and defining excellence, together.

Providing services to Bombardier means operating at the highest level. Every day, you are part of a team that delivers superior experiences and products, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in our industry and beyond. By prioritizing continuous growth and development, we empower everyone to reach their full potential on their own terms, because the greatest results happens when you are free to be yourself and share your unique expertise.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • You will carry out, from defined standards and processes, various tasks relating to the management of documentation
  • You will participate in the implementation and updating of document filing plans and the retention schedule
  • You will organize, audit and archive records
  • You will follow aviation regulations as well as internal processes related to document management
  • You will use the classification plan as well as the retention schedule allowing the identification, indexing, codification, classification of documents according to established standards
  • You will prepare or participate in the preparation of the contents of the boxes for their transfer to the filing of documents in the archives, purifies the contents, identifies, classifies the documents, audit records.
  • You will check the conformity of the boxes, arrange, and classify the boxes according to the established system; and is called upon to lift boxes of documents

How to thrive in this role?

  • You have a college diploma combined with 1 year of experience in aeronautics and/or documentation management
  • You have document and archive management techniques
  • You are fluent in spoken and written French and English and have good communication skills
  • You know office techniques and IT tools related to document management
  • You know how to show judgment, tact, and courtesy
  • You know how to show great rigor, team spirit and flexibility

Now that you can see yourself with the Bombardier Team!

About the job

Lead end-to-end testing activities for ADAS sensor projects (Radar, Camera, Ultrasonic) across customer & platform projects.

Define and drive overall test strategy, test architecture, validation roadmap, coverage, KPIs, and acceptance criteria.

Plan, manage, and execute System, Integration, SIL, HIL & Vehicle-level testing.Ensure alignment with ISO 26262, ASPICE, & customer-specific quality and safety requirements.

Manage complete test lifecycle including planning, scheduling, resource allocation, risk management, and budget tracking.

Drive execution and delivery of ADAS validation programs with focus on quality, timelines, and customer commitments.Monitor and report project status, test progress, defect metrics, and quality KPIs to stakeholders.

Identify risks and bottlenecks early and implement mitigation strategies.Provide technical leadership for test architecture, automation frameworks, and validation environments.Oversee HIL/SIL setups using tools.

Ensure effective usage of automotive communication protocols (CAN, LIN, FlexRay, Ethernet) and restbus simulation.

Guide diagnostics validation activities using UDS, XCP, and DoIP protocols.Drive development and adoption of scalable test automation frameworks using Python and C/C++.

Integrate testing into CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins, GitLab, or similar tools.

Ensure reusability and standardization of platform-level test solutions.Act as primary customer interface for all testing-related discussions, reviews, and escalations.Present test strategies, validation results, and quality status to customers and internal stakeholders.

Handle technical discussions, requirement changes, and issue escalations effectively.

Ensure compliance with functional safety and process standards (ISO 26262, ASPICE).

Maintain high-quality test documentation, traceability, and audit readiness.Lead, mentor, and manage cross-functional and distributed test teams.

Define team roles, responsibilities, and competency development plans.

Drive continuous improvement in test processes, automation, and frameworks.

Ensure effective log analysis, trace evaluation, and data validation.Analyze sensor data (camera, radar, ultrasonic) for validation and issue identification.

Support debugging, root cause analysis, and problem resolution for complex system issues.

Collaborate closely with development, system engineering, and integration teams.Ensure strong alignment between platform solutions and customer-specific implementations.

Drive innovation in test methodologies, including scenario-based and simulation-driven validation.

B.E / B.Tech / ECE / EEE / CS

11 - 15 years

About the job

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Job Overview This role requires a deep understanding of trading system architecture, order matching logic, user experience, and on-chain assets. Additionally, the ideal candidate should possess forward-looking insight and a strong interest in the trend of financial asset tokenization (such as Tokenized Stocks, Pre-IPO, and RWA Yield).

Job Responsibilities

1. Trading Product Design & Optimization

  • Responsible for the planning, design, and continuous iteration of spot trading products.
  • Lead the implementation of new trading features and continuously optimize the user trading experience.
  • Explore AI-driven trading tool design and applications (e.g., smart order execution, market analysis assistance).
  • Deeply understand core trading system mechanics, including order matching engines, order lifecycle management, and order book depth management.

2. CeDeFi & RWA Innovation & Exploration

  • Drive innovation in on-chain spot operations, bringing DeFi innovations into centralized exchanges (CEX).
  • Promote the launch of CeDeFi products, bridging on-chain and off-chain trading experiences.
  • Monitor and advance the productization of emerging asset classes such as Real-World Assets (RWA), Tokenized Stocks, and Pre-IPO assets.

Qualifications

Requirements:

  • 3+ years of experience in trading products; familiar with spot/derivatives trading systems.
  • Deep understanding of order-matching engines, trading workflows, and core technical mechanics.
  • Outstanding product design capability, with the ability to independently manage end-to-end (0 to 1) product planning and delivery.
  • Strong, up-to-date knowledge of the crypto asset market and industry trends.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have:

  • Experience building on-chain trading platforms or integrating DeFi protocols.
  • Hands-on experience or deep research in RWAs, tokenized stocks, or on-chain yield products.
  • Hands-on exploration in AI + trading integration.
  • Familiarity with major public chain ecosystems and smart contract interaction logic.

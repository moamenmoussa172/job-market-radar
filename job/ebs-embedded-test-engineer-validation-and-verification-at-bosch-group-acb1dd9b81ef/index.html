About the job

  • Analyze Customer and Internal requirements, design and develop test cases
  • Plan and Perform Manual and Automation software and system tests;
  • Clarify Open Points, Analyze defect and report defects
  • Set up test environment: SIL (Software in Loop) & HIL (Hardware in Loop)
  • Configure, and maintain LabCar test bench hardware and associated equipment
  • Coordinate team activities, and handle day-to-day work efficiently and independently
  • Troubleshoot and resolve hardware, wiring, communication, and integration issues on LabCar/HIL systems to ensure test bench availability, ECU connections, CAN/LIN/Ethernet communication, measurement devices, and test automation infrastructure
  • Major in Computer Engineering/ Information Technology/ Electrical-Electronics/ Electronics-Telecommunication/ Mechatronics Engineering/ Automotive or related fields
  • Have 0.5 + years of experience in Software and System Testing
  • Have knowledge about software life cycle
  • Strong in 1 of following programming language: C/C++/Python/VBA/Perl scripting language, API programming
  • Have basic knowledge of automotive system, micro-controller and embedded system
  • Hands-on understanding of electronic hardware, circuit fundamentals, sensors, actuators, and automotive ECU architecture
  • Able to read wiring diagrams, hardware schematics, pinouts, and technical documentation
  • Knowledge of automotive communication protocols such as CAN, LIN, FlexRay, and/or Automotive Ethernet
  • Ability to diagnose and resolve hardware-related issues using debugging and measurement tools such as oscilloscopes, CAN analyzers, multimeters, and logic analyzers
  • Good English communication skills

It would be BIG advantage if you have:

  • Automotive domain knowledge
  • Exposure to emulators, simulators, debugging
  • Knowledge of Braking system and test design techniques
  • ISTQB Foundation certificate
  • Chinese or Japanese language

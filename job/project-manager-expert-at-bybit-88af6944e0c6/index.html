Position Overview

As a Senior PMO, you will act as a strategic partner to the business, driving the company’s project portfolio management and governance at an enterprise level. You will independently coordinate cross-functional resources, steer complex project life cycles, and ensure strict alignment between strategic initiatives and business objectives. This role requires exceptional leadership without authority, advanced organizational agility, and the ability to seamlessly bridge executive-level strategic thinking with rigorous, detail-oriented execution.

Key Responsibilities

1. Project Portfolio Management & Resource Strategy

  • Lead and own end-to-end (full lifecycle) project management for multiple high-impact, company-level strategic initiatives, ensuring high-quality and on-time delivery.
  • Directly manage complex project portfolios, define priorities, and independently resolve resource contentions and critical path conflicts.
  • Optimize resource utilization globally; formulate strategies for manpower, time, and budget allocation to maximize ROI.
  • Drive project initiation reviews, establish priority frameworks, and enforce rigorous schedule tracking methodologies.

2. Stakeholder Management & Communication

  • Act as the primary bridge across business, product, R&D, QA, and other departments, fostering high-performance synergy and aligning diverse teams on shared deliverables.
  • Deliver executive-level, structured reports and presentations to senior management regarding project health, strategic risks, and mitigation strategies.
  • Proactively navigate and resolve complex project conflicts using advanced stakeholder management skills—balancing consensus-building (soft skills) with firm decision enforcement (hard skills).

3. Process Governance & Continuous Improvement

  • Establish, standardize, and scale organizational project management processes, methodology frameworks, and deliverable standards.
  • Lead periodic audits and health checks to ensure strict project compliance with organizational governance standards.
  • Drive continuous closed-loop optimization; track the effectiveness of implemented improvements across ongoing and new projects, shaping a culture of excellence.

4. Data-Driven Governance & Tooling

  • Design, implement, and maintain comprehensive KPI dashboards and metrics for enterprise project governance.
  • Conduct advanced data analysis and visualization to preemptively identify project bottlenecks, financial risks, and schedule variances.
  • Leverage, configure, and optimize project management toolchains (e.g., Jira, Lark, BDOT) to streamline task breakdown, automated tracking, and workflow efficiency.

5. Strategic Alignment & Business Acumen

  • Demonstrate profound understanding of the company’s industry, market dynamics, and business models.
  • Proactively identify opportunities where project outcomes can better serve business strategy, influencing product and business roadmaps.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or above, preferably in Computer Science, Business Administration, or related disciplines. (PMP, ACP, or Prince2 certification is highly preferred).
  • 3-5+ years of proven experience in PMO, Project/Program Management, preferably within a fast-paced tech or Internet environment.
  • Exceptional analytical, risk management, and problem-solving skills, with a proven track record of delivering complex technical or business projects from 0 to 1.
  • Advanced proficiency in configuring and utilizing project management toolchains (e.g., Jira, Lark, Confluence, BDOT).
  • Demonstrated resilience and adaptability to thrive and lead in a dynamic, highly ambiguous environment with shifting priorities.
  • Bilingual proficiency in Chinese and English (both written and spoken) is required, with the ability to influence and negotiate across global, diverse teams.

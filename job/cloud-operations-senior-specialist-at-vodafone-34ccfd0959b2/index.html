About the job

Join Us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers – we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact.

What you’ll do

• Responsible for designing, planning, executing technology refresh and consolidation tasks for cloud infrastructure, while troubleshooting any arising issues.
• Design, develop, and deploy modular cloud-based systems.
• Develop and maintain cloud solutions in accordance with best practices.
• Ensure efficient functioning of data storage and processing in alignment with company security policies and best practices in cloud security.
• Simplify existing platform architectures and eliminate legacy platforms by creating new designs and migration procedures.
• Maintain a comprehensive inventory of all managed software and hardware.
• Identify, analyze and resolve infrastructure vulnerabilities and application deployment issues.
• Regularly review existing systems and provide recommendations for improvements.
• Conduct periodic monitoring of systems, performance tuning, capacity planning, and trend analysis.
• Evaluate new versions, patches, firmware, and security updates, implementing necessary upgrades and patches regularly.
• Manage the partner ecosystem to facilitate data center/application migration or management processes, collaborating with vendors to implement new service platforms.
• Interact with customers, providing cloud support and making recommendations based on their needs.
• Perform troubleshooting, incident management, and error handling for various systems.
• Maintain operational readiness to ensure complete recovery in the event of a disaster; participate in disaster recovery (DR) tests regularly as per company policies and procedures to validate DR functionality, updating policies and procedures as required.

Who you are

• Minimum of 3 years of experience in cloud infrastructure operations.
• Very good command of English.
• Deep understanding and experience with cloud technologies: IaaS, SaaS, PaaS, Multi Cloud, and Hybrid Cloud.
• Experience developing and implementing cloud solutions on cloud directory platforms.
• Proficient knowledge and experience with VMware products (vSphere, vCloud Director, NSX, vRealize Operations, Log Insight).
• Strong knowledge and experience in virtualization, storage, SAN, and networking.
• Experience in a large enterprise environment managing 200+ hypervisors.
• Familiarity with investigating issues at the vSphere Command-Line Interface (CLI) level.
• Understanding of vSphere security hardening and compliance requirements.
• Proficient in PowerShell or PowerCLI for problem investigation, resolution, and automation tasks.
• Knowledge and experience with backup and replication software (Veeam, Zerto, VCA).
• Good understanding of server, storage, and backup hardware systems.
• University degree in Electronic, Computer, Telecommunication, or Mathematics Engineering.
• Military service completed (for male candidates).
• Excellent interpersonal relationship-building skills.
• Strong analytical skills.
• Ability to understand and capture both business and technical requirements.
• Should be a collaborative team player with the ability to perform well under stress.

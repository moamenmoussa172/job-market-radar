About the job

During your internship you will:
►Assist in managing cloud infrastructure on Azure.
►Support infrastructure automation and CI/CD activities using tools such as Terraform, Ansible,
Jenkins, or Azure DevOps.
►Monitor cloud environments, troubleshoot basic issues, and support security and cost
optimization activities.
►Collaborate with the team on cloud operations, documentation, and process improvements.
►Continuous improvement of DevOps/GitOps deployment practices.

You would be a perfect match to our team if:
► You are a final-year student in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
►You are motivated, proactive, and ready to work in an international team.
►You have good communication skills and enjoy working collaboratively.
►You have basic understanding of cloud computing and familiarity with Azure and/or AWS.
►You have foundational knowledge of scripting (Python or Bash) and Infrastructure as Code
(Terraform or Ansible is a plus).
►You have basic understanding of DevOps and CI/CD concepts is an advantage.
►You have strong willingness to learn, problem-solving skills, and good teamwork and communication skills.
► Strong English language skills, both written and verbal.

Why you'll love to work with us:
► Experienced colleagues, international environment
► Being part of a great team will challenge you to grow personally and professionally
► Paid full time internship with flexible working hours so you can pass all the exams and be in track with faculty activities

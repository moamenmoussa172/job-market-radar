About the job

AI Native Software Engineer (Analyst / Senior Analyst)

📍 Location: Vilnius, Lithuania
🕒 Full-Time | 40 Hours per Week

Shape the Future of AI-Native Engineering

We're building the next generation of AI-native software engineers who use AI as a core part of how they build, test, and deliver software, not as an add-on.

As an AI Native Software Engineer (Analyst / Senior Analyst), you'll design, develop, and deploy production-grade software across the full stack while leveraging AI-assisted development tools as part of your everyday workflow. You'll work on real client projects across multiple industries, building applications that integrate with modern AI systems, including agentic architectures, LLM-powered solutions, and enterprise AI platforms.

This is not an entry point into AI, it's an opportunity to build a long-term career in one of the fastest-growing and most sought-after areas of technology.

Why Join Us?

We offer something few organizations can match:

  • Exposure to projects across diverse industries and enterprise environments
  • Opportunities to work with leading AI technologies and cloud platforms
  • Access to learning and certification pathways in AI and software engineering
  • Collaboration with specialists across Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and other AI ecosystems
  • A clear development path toward advanced AI Engineering and Forward Deployed Engineering roles

What You'll Do

  • Use AI coding assistants daily to accelerate software delivery and improve quality
  • Design, build, and maintain modern full-stack applications
  • Integrate Large Language Model (LLM) APIs into production applications
  • Apply AI throughout the software development lifecycle, including testing, debugging, code reviews, and documentation
  • Evaluate and validate AI-generated outputs to ensure quality, reliability, and security
  • Define and track metrics that demonstrate the effectiveness of AI-assisted development
  • Deliver solutions end-to-end, from design and development through deployment and production support
  • Work closely with Agile teams and client stakeholders
  • Develop APIs, interfaces, and application components that connect business applications with AI and agentic systems
  • Contribute reusable components, engineering standards, and internal AI best practices

What We're Looking For

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field
  • Commercial software development experience, or equivalent experience gained through internships, academic programs, or personal projects
  • Strong programming skills in at least one backend language:
  • Python
  • Java
  • TypeScript
  • Experience using AI tools as part of day-to-day software development
  • Understanding of web development fundamentals, including JavaScript, HTML, and CSS
  • Familiarity with cloud technologies (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
  • Experience working with databases (SQL and/or NoSQL)
  • Understanding of Agile delivery methodologies
  • Awareness of software development best practices, CI/CD pipelines, and containerization technologies such as Docker

AI & Emerging Technology Experience

We are particularly interested in candidates who can demonstrate:

  • Practical use of AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity and delivery outcomes
  • Experience integrating LLM APIs into applications
  • Understanding of token management, latency considerations, and cost optimization when working with AI services
  • Ability to assess, refine, and improve AI-generated outputs
  • Knowledge of AI limitations, risks, and responsible AI practices
  • Familiarity with agentic systems, RAG architectures, LangChain, LangGraph, or similar frameworks
  • Understanding of how modern applications connect with AI-driven systems and workflows

What Success Looks Like

You'll thrive in this role if you are:

  • Passionate about software engineering and emerging AI technologies
  • Curious, adaptable, and eager to continuously learn
  • Comfortable combining strong engineering fundamentals with AI-assisted development
  • Able to balance innovation with sound engineering judgment
  • Excited to work on challenging projects across different industries and technology stacks

Locations

  • Vilnius, Lithuania

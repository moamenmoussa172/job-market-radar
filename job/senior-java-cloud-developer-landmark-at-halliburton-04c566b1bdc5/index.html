About the job

We are looking for the right people — people who want to innovate, achieve, grow and lead. We attract and retain the best talent by investing in our employees and empowering them to develop themselves and their careers. Experience the challenges, rewards and opportunity of working for one of the world’s largest providers of products and services to the global energy industry.

Job Duties

We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer to help build and evolve our seismic processing platform. This role is primarily focused on backend engineering, distributed compute systems, cloud-native services, and scalable microservice architectures. The successful candidate will contribute to high-performance processing workflows, platform scalability, and modern engineering practices while also supporting web-based application development when needed.

The ideal candidate has strong Java experience, is comfortable working with distributed processing and cloud-native services and actively leverages AI-powered development tools to improve engineering efficiency and software quality. Experience developing modern web applications and collaborating across cross-functional engineering teams is highly desirable

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.
  • 7+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Strong Java and Cloud services experience.
  • Experience utilizing AI-assisted engineering tools in day-to-day software development.
  • Experience designing and supporting microservice-based systems.
  • Experience with distributed compute platforms or large-scale processing systems.
  • Experience with Kubernetes, containers, and cloud-native deployments.
  • Experience building and consuming REST APIs.
  • Experience contributing to modern web applications using JavaScript/TypeScript-based frameworks.
  • Experience working within Agile development teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Oil & Gas industry experience
  • Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, Kinesis, Event Hubs, etc.).

Soft Skills

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, evolving environment.

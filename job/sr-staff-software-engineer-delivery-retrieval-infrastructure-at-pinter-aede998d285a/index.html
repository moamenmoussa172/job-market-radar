The Delivery Retrieval Infra team builds the foundational retrieval infrastructure and algorithms that power ads and core delivery experiences across Pinterest. As a Senior Staff Software Engineer, you’ll set technical strategy and lead the evolution of high-throughput, low-latency retrieval systems that enable product innovation while improving reliability and infrastructure efficiency.

What you’ll do:

  • Set and drive a multi-year technical strategy for delivery retrieval infrastructure, partnering with engineering leaders to align investment and execution with organizational OKRs.
  • Lead the architecture and delivery of large-scale distributed systems across retrieval, targeting and indexing.
  • Scale retrieval systems toward 10x capacity while improving latency, freshness, reliability and cost efficiency.
  • Evaluate emerging infrastructure approaches and translate relevant innovations into practical, high-impact technical initiatives.
  • Partner with machine learning, product, infrastructure and other engineering teams to turn ambiguous technical and business needs into clear, actionable plans.
  • Provide technical leadership, architectural guidance and mentorship that strengthens execution across a 20–30 engineer organization.
  • Use AI to accelerate analysis and iteration on system designs and technical proposals, while applying judgment and verification to ensure correctness and quality.
  • Use AI to synthesize technical information and streamline repeatable documentation or analysis tasks, while maintaining ownership of final decisions and deliverables.

What we’re looking for:

  • Minimum of 10 years of relevant industry experience in delivery retrieval, search infrastructure or distributed systems.
  • Experience building and scaling high-throughput, low-latency distributed retrieval or online serving infrastructure.
  • Experience with modern retrieval paradigms, including embedding-based retrieval, approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) techniques and indexing.
  • Experience troubleshooting and optimizing complex performance, latency and capacity challenges in high-volume production environments.
  • Experience leading technical strategy, driving cross-functional alignment and guiding complex projects from definition through execution.
  • Experience mentoring and upleveling engineers through technical guidance and feedback.
  • Proficiency in backend systems programming with C++, Java or Python, with strong C++ experience.
  • Demonstrated ability to use AI to improve speed and quality in relevant workflows, critically evaluate and verify AI-assisted work and protect sensitive data while remaining accountable for final decisions and deliverables.
  • Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science, or equivalent experience.

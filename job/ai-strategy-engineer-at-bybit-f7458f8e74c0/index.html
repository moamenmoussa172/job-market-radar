About the Role

Crypto financial markets generate enormous arbitrage opportunities, signals, and price dislocations every day — but most users lack the expertise to act on them. The AI Strategy Engineer we're looking for is someone who can transform these opportunities into executable AI-powered applications: one-click arbitrage, automated yield optimization, AI trading signals — giving ordinary users access to strategy capabilities that were previously reserved for institutional players.

You will lead strategy research, design, and deployment — developing deep expertise in funding rate arbitrage, basis trading, stablecoin yield strategies, cross-exchange arbitrage, and more. You will combine quantitative strategy logic with AI Agent execution frameworks to build automated systems that directly drive user retention and asset growth. This isn't a pure quant role, nor a pure engineering role — you need to understand markets, understand strategies, and know how to turn them into products users can actually use.

Key Responsibilities

  • Quantitative Strategy Research & Design: Research executable arbitrage opportunities in crypto markets including funding rate arbitrage, basis trading (perpetuals vs. delivery), stablecoin yield strategies (stablecoin lending loops, Launchpool hedging), and cross-exchange arbitrage. Complete strategy logic design, historical backtesting, and risk-adjusted return validation.
  • AI Strategy Application Development: Package quantitative strategies into user-facing AI applications. Design execution workflows, trigger conditions, position management, and risk controls for scenarios such as one-click arbitrage, automated yield optimization, and promotional opportunity capturing. Integrate AI Agent capabilities to enable automated strategy analysis, decision-making, and execution.
  • Market Signal & Intelligence System: Build a multi-source market intelligence capability integrating exchange market data, on-chain data, and community sentiment signals. Use AI models to identify hot opportunities, entry timing, and take-profit/stop-loss strategies, providing users with disciplined, AI-assisted trading signals.
  • Strategy Risk Control & Position Management: Design risk frameworks for each strategy class, including maximum drawdown controls, dynamic position calibration, and circuit breakers for extreme market conditions. Regularly analyze live strategy performance, identify degradation factors, and drive iterative optimization.
  • Strategy Productization for User Scenarios: Develop a deep understanding of different user personas (conservative yield-seekers vs. active traders) and translate strategy capabilities into perceivable product value — defining the core interaction logic, return visualization, and risk communication for strategy applications.
  • Cross-System Strategy Integration: Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate strategies with exchange APIs (including third-party exchanges), and drive the engineering delivery of automated strategy execution with unified cross-exchange asset management.

Requirements

  • Quantitative Strategy Experience: 3+ years of quantitative strategy research or trading experience, with at least one strategy class delivered end-to-end in production (funding rate arbitrage, basis trading, statistical arbitrage, market-neutral, etc.); proficient with backtesting frameworks and able to independently conduct strategy validation and attribution analysis.
  • Deep Crypto Market Knowledge: Strong command of crypto derivatives market mechanics including perpetual contract funding rates, delivery futures basis dynamics, stablecoin protocol yield structures (USDE / USD1 / BYUSD etc.), and DeFi protocol return mechanisms. Live trading strategy experience is strongly preferred.
  • Programming & Data Analysis: Proficient in Python for strategy development and data analysis (Pandas / NumPy / backtesting frameworks); experience with exchange API integration; able to work with market tick data, funding rate history, and other quantitative datasets.
  • AI / Agent Integration: Hands-on experience building LLM applications or AI Agents; able to encapsulate strategy logic as agent-callable tools and construct automated strategy pipelines from signal analysis through decision-making to execution.
  • Business Acumen & Product Thinking: Able to identify actionable strategy opportunities from a user-value perspective and translate complex quantitative strategies into understandable, actionable product experiences; capable of independently driving strategy concepts from 0 to 1.

Nice to Have

  • Live trading experience with cross-exchange arbitrage (funding rate, cross-exchange spread, basis arbitrage) or on-chain DeFi strategies.
  • Familiarity with on-chain analytics tools (Dune / Nansen / Glassnode) for mining strategy signals from on-chain indicators.
  • Experience developing and operating automated trading systems (quant trading bots, grid strategies, DCA systems, etc.).
  • Understanding of AI-driven signal generation, with practical experience combining market sentiment and quantitative models.

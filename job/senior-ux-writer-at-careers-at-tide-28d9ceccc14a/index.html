About the job

ABOUT TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts:

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

ABOUT THE TEAM:

The UX Writing team delivers clear and concise copy to help Tide members run and grow their business. We work closely with cross-functional teams, including Design, User Research, Product, Engineering, CCM, and Legal, to create and deliver intuitive user experiences.

ABOUT THE ROLE:

We're looking for a dynamic and detail-oriented Senior UX Writer (based in the UK) to work across a range of our products and international markets. You’ll help to shape the future of our products and guide members through journeys with intuitive copy. Your work will have a significant impact, helping members to streamline their admin and get paid for their services.

Responsibilities:

  • Deliver English copy for end-to-end user journeys.
  • Adapt our English copy for feature launches in international markets.
  • Collaborate with Design, User Research, Product, Engineering, CCM, and Legal throughout the product development life cycle.
  • Advocate for the value of UX writing and user-centered design across the business.
  • Communicate the rationale for your copy approach to stakeholders.
  • Contribute to Tide’s copy guidelines to raise the bar for copy across the business.
  • Stay updated on best practices and industry trends to spot opportunities to improve our processes.
  • Carry out content ops tasks in Lokalise, our translation management software.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

  • A solid past or current experience as a UX Writer or Content Designer, ideally within a similar sector.
  • Native-level English, excellent writing skills, including the ability to distil complicated concepts into plain language.
  • Understanding of user research and testing.
  • Creativity and problem-solving skills.
  • Quick learner with an ability to deal with ambiguity and grasp local administrative requirements across international markets.
  • Great communication skills.
  • Comfortable working with style guides to ensure consistency.
  • Experience of balancing multiple priorities with frequent context switching across squads.
  • Adept at stakeholder management, and working in a very fast-moving environment.
  • Passion for inclusive, accessible, user-centred design.

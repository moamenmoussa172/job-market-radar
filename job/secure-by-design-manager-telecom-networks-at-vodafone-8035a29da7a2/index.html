About the job

Join Us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers – we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact.

A Secure by Design Manager in the Vodafone Secure & Privacy by Design team acts as the interface between the technical disciplines and the business to carry out technically orientated security assessments, setting security requirements for products and services, and assessing compliance against agreed security requirements, policies and standards. The Secure by Design Manager also has an important advisory role across the business and is required to provide security design and architecture guidance as well as general consultancy, acts as coach to agile projects and programme teams to ensure that future infrastructures or products for Vodafone globally are secure.

In this role you will act as Secure by Design representative and support the Telecom Networks programmes in Vodafone by providing security expertise.

What you’ll do

  • Ensure networks, infrastructure, products, and services are Secure by Design, meeting appropriate technology security standards;
  • Identify, assess, and mitigate security risks to an acceptable level, protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems and data;
  • Provide security advice and guidance to internal stakeholders, customers, suppliers, and third parties;
  • Define and communicate security requirements, ensuring suppliers and third parties understand and comply with Vodafone security standards;
  • Collaborate closely with business, architecture, design, engineering, and operations teams to embed security throughout the technology lifecycle;
  • Partner with specialist security teams, including Risk & Compliance, Ethical Hacking, Security Operations, and Corporate Security;
  • Manage external security resources and coach Security Champions within Agile teams, strengthening security ownership across the organisation;
  • Champion a security-enabling mindset, demonstrating how Secure by Design can support innovation and act as a business differentiator;
  • Translate complex technical security requirements and risks into clear, practical guidance for both technical and non-technical stakeholders, including senior executives;
  • Provide clear direction on the implementation of effective security controls in complex technology environments.

Who you are

  • University degree in Information Security or equivalent;
  • Radio network expertise like access and radio networks;
  • One or more of the following: CISSP, CISM, CISA, CRISC, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor, GIAC, TOGAF, SABSA or equivalent;
  • 5+ years work experience in the field of cyber security;
  • Strong professional experience in mobile and telecom network technologies, including RAN, Core, Transport and IP/MPLS, with a solid understanding of associated cybersecurity risks in large-scale carrier environments;
  • Proven expertise in applying Secure-by-Design principles to telecom networks, including virtualised environments, threat modelling and attack surface analysis;
  • Experience defining, assessing and enforcing network security requirements, including security baselines, hardening guidelines, risk registers and security risk documentation;
  • Ability to assess the security posture of network vendors, suppliers and managed services, ensuring secure deployment models and compliance with internal and regulatory telecom security standards;
  • Expert knowledge of Cyber Security, with practical experience integrating security into Agile and DevSecOps ways of working;
  • Fluency in English.

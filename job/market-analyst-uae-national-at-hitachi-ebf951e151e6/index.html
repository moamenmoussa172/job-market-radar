Job Description:

The opportunity

Are you naturally curious, analytical, and excited by uncovering insights that shape business decisions? Join Hitachi Energy as a Market Analyst and play a key role in helping us identify opportunities, understand market trends, and drive strategic growth across the region.

In this role, you'll transform data into meaningful insights, helping teams make informed decisions that influence product strategies, customer engagement, and business performance. You'll work closely with colleagues across sales, marketing, strategy, and business development, gaining exposure to a wide range of projects and stakeholders.

This is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious UAE National who enjoys problem-solving, exploring new ideas, and leveraging technology to uncover market opportunities. You'll have the chance to develop your analytical expertise, attend industry events, engage with customers and partners, and contribute to projects that support the energy transition and a more sustainable future.

If you're eager to learn, enjoy working with data, and want to make a real impact within a global technology leader, we'd love to hear from you.

How you'll make an impact

  • Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and emerging customer needs.
  • Analyze market, customer, and business data to generate actionable insights and recommendations.
  • Develop reports, dashboards, forecasts, and presentations that support strategic decision-making.
  • Monitor industry developments, technological innovation, and regulatory changes affecting the energy sector.
  • Partner with sales, marketing, strategy, and business teams to integrate market insights into business plans.
  • Support the evaluation of new market segments, partnerships, and growth opportunities.
  • Utilize tools such as Power BI, Salesforce, and Microsoft Copilot to enhance reporting and analysis.
  • Represent Hitachi Energy at industry events, conferences, and customer engagements when required.

Your background

  • UAE National (Emirati) with a Family Book.
  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Economics, Statistics, Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Experience in market research, business intelligence, analytics, strategy, or a similar field is preferred.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience using data analysis and reporting tools such as Power BI and Excel; familiarity with Salesforce is advantageous.
  • Curious, self-motivated, and willing to learn, travel, and collaborate in a dynamic environment.

About the job

Together We Can

Discover global graduate program is a 12 month program to accelerated development that enables talented fresh graduates to develop and grow within Vodafone Egypt. Ultimately these extraordinary graduates become Vodafone’s future leaders through; customized job rotations in the Technology - Cyber Security department, challenging business exposure, continuous learning journey/development, & corporate networking.

Who can apply?

Fresh graduates with an outstanding academic record (Fresh Grads: any graduates of the year 2025, or 2026 or January 2027, as for males who completed their military service either in 2024 or 2025
A fresh graduate with diverse extracurricular & internship experiences

Competencies and Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity, or a related field.
  • Good understanding of networking fundamentals, particularly TCP/IP, DNS, ports, protocols, and basic network concepts.
  • Basic knowledge of programming and scripting fundamentals, such as Python, PowerShell, Java, or similar technologies.
  • Basic understanding of operating systems, particularly Windows and Linux environments.
  • Awareness of cybersecurity concepts such as authentication, authorization, access control, and least privilege.
  • Knowledge of User Access Management (UAM) or Identity and Access Management (IAM) is preferred, but not mandatory.
  • Good analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Strong willingness to learn and develop within the cybersecurity and identity security domain.
  • Good communication skills and ability to work effectively within a team.

About the job

Real influence. Real support. No limits. As a (Senior) Analyst, International Markets, you’ll work alongside our global management team in Berlin to shape strategy and profitability across 65+ markets. You’ll own the annual budgeting process and turn data into recommendations for senior decision-makers. Your analysis will directly influence which initiatives we prioritise and where we put our resources.

The International Markets team sits at our Berlin HQ and partners with regional and central leadership across 65+ markets.

Be part of the team that shapes Delivery Hero’s strategy across our markets worldwide. In the International Markets Team, you’ll contribute to high-impact projects that drive value, support profitability, and unlock growth opportunities on a global scale. Working closely with regional and central leadership, your work will directly influence how we prioritize initiaties, allocate resources, and execute strategies that keep us ahead.

  • Work with our global management team at HQ in Berlin to define strategy and ensure high-quality execution worldwide
  • Partner with stakeholders at all levels across HQ, regional teams and local markets to improve operational and financial performance
  • Own the annual budgeting process, working with entity and functional teams to set profitability and financial targets across portfolio markets
  • Identify best practices from our operations in 65+ countries and promote their adoption
  • Turn data into insights and present clear recommendations on critical topics to key decision-makers
  • Give senior leadership regular updates on business performance, key projects, strategic initiatives and progress toward strategic goals
  • 3 years of professional experience in management consulting and/or fast-paced tech or e-commerce companies (excluding traineeships and internships)
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving skills: you break complex, ambiguous business problems into clear, actionable parts
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort modelling complex business and financial scenarios, using both data and judgment to make recommendations
  • Project management skills, with experience driving and executing complex projects across multiple stakeholders globally
  • Excellent communication skills, with a track record of working with and influencing senior leaders, including C-level executives, and building trusting relationships with colleagues from diverse cultural and functional backgrounds (English is our primary business language)

Nice to have

  • 1-2 years of experience at a top-tier management consulting firm
  • Experience with SQL, BigQuery or other languages used to query and process data

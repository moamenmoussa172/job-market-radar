The Role

Docker’s Professional Services team partners with customers to turn AI and cloud-native deployments into working production outcomes.

We are looking for a technically fluent Delivery Program Manager to operate delivery engagements. You will be embedded as an overlay alongside technical sales, solution architects, forward deployed engineers, and delivery partners, translating customers’ technical and business requirements into scoped service engagements and managing them through delivery.

You will be both builder and operator: designing repeatable service offerings where none exist today, and equally willing to roll up your sleeves to scope a detailed Statement of Work or unblock a stuck engagement. If you thrive in ambiguity, love building order from chaos, and operate with high autonomy and low ego, this role is for you.

_______________________________________________________________________

What You'll Own

Delivery operations

  • Operationalize the end-to-end engagement lifecycle, from scoping and kickoff through delivery, handoff, and retrospectives.
  • Directly manage customer engagements: plan, run governance cadences, track milestones, manage risks and dependencies, and escalations.
  • Review technical assumptions in scopes and SOWs with solution architects (environments, integrations, identity, network and security constraints, customer prerequisites) so estimates and timelines hold up in delivery.
  • Own customer readiness and handoff: environment prerequisites, acceptance criteria, and clean transition to Support and Technical Account Managers.

Offerings and reusable assets

  • Partner with solution architects to create reusable delivery assets: playbooks, runbooks, statement of work templates, reference architectures, and onboarding workshops/guides.
  • Turn repeatable customer patterns into packaged, fixed-scope offerings with defined outcomes, durations and pricing inputs.
  • Build and maintain the sales-cycle engagement model and intake process for when and how Services are engaged across sales stages.

Governance, tooling, and metrics

  • Build the program management and governance infrastructure: intake frameworks, capacity planning models, risk registers, delivery dashboards, and milestone tracking.
  • Define and report delivery KPIs: on-time delivery, utilization, engagement margin, customer satisfaction.
  • Own SOW amendment and change-order processes: define when and how scope changes are documented, approved, and reflected in project tracking.
  • Partner with Legal on SOW frameworks and language.

Why This Role

Building a PS function from the ground up is rare. This is an opportunity to shape how Docker serves its most strategic customers, define what great looks like for technical delivery, and leave a lasting operational footprint on a high-growth organization. You will have broad scope, executive access, and the room to build something that matters.

  • Audit the existing SOW process and template library to identify gaps in standardization, approval routing, and tracking.

What to Expect in the First 60 Days

  • Manage first customer engagement directly, with support from the existing team.
  • Identify the highest-priority SOW template gaps for standardization.
  • Document the current delivery framework as it exists today capturing what's working, and what needs to change.

What to Expect in the First 60 Days

  • Autonomously managing customer engagements.
  • Support leadership with quarterly review cycles on active engagements and retrospectives.

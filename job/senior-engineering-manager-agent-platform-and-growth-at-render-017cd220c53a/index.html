About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

At Render, we’re building the modern cloud platform for developers creating AI-native, full-stack, multi-service applications. Our mission is to eliminate the tradeoff between the power of hyperscalers and the simplicity of developer-friendly platforms—so teams can ship fast, scale reliably, and focus on their product, not infrastructure.

Unlike complex hyperscalers or ephemeral edge/serverless solutions, Render offers a developer-first experience with persistent compute, dynamic autoscaling, built-in orchestration, and observability, allowing teams to launch, scale, and manage real-world applications without writing infrastructure code or managing servers. Whether you're building LLM-powered applications, scalable SaaS products, or async processing pipelines, Render empowers teams to move fast and scale confidently from MVP to millions of users.

Our platform is trusted by over 7 million developers worldwide and continues to grow rapidly. In February 2026, we raised an additional $100M in Series C financing, bringing our total funding to $260M, to accelerate our vision of making cloud infrastructure both powerful and intuitive—designed for the speed of modern AI development.

We’re a diverse and talented team that values craft, velocity, and user experience. If you’re excited to help shape the future of the intelligent cloud and empower developers everywhere, we’d love to hear from you.

About the Role

As Senior Engineering Manager for Agent Platform & UX, you will lead two teams reimagining Render's onboarding and activation experience for both humans and AI agents.

One team owns the experience a person sees the first time they come to Render: signup, onboarding, activation, and what drives them to adopt more of the platform. The other team builds the equivalent experience for agents, which reach Render through APIs rather than the UI, integrating with providers like Codex, Claude, and Cursor so agents can use Render the way humans do. You'll take an already-forming org through its next stage of growth.

What You'll Do

  • Lead and grow two teams, UX (human onboarding and activation) and Agent Platform (the equivalent experience for agents via programmatic interfaces like the MCP, CLI, API, SDK, and Blueprints)
  • Own the roadmap for Render's self-serve user journey, from first touch through activation, initial monetization, usage expansion, new feature adoption, and PQL graduation
  • Drive the agent-facing side of the platform: build the API-driven experience that lets agents integrate with and operate on Render
  • Partner with the dedicated product and design partners on both teams to align roadmap with customer, growth, and platform needs
  • Hire and develop engineers to support planned team growth, while keeping both teams' identities and focus areas clear as they scale
  • Make key architectural and product decisions, balancing long-term platform investment against iterative improvements to onboarding and activation
  • Build a strong performance management and career development practice for a team that already includes senior ICs

What We're Looking For

  • Strong people leadership - the top priority for this role
  • Hands-on enough to review code, prototype, and contribute directly when it helps the team
  • Comfort operating in ambiguity, with adaptability and a growth mindset
  • Proven experience building and scaling more than one team, ideally growing a group from a handful of engineers into a larger org
  • Experience managing and developing senior (IC5+) engineers, including hiring and performance management
  • Ability to partner closely with product and design without needing to fill those functions yourself
  • Strong product instincts, with the ability to connect engineering investment to growth and activation outcomes
  • Background from either a startup or a larger company is fine. What matters is experience and ability to operate in a fast-paced environment

Nice-to-Haves

  • Prior growth-focused engineering experience (onboarding, activation, funnel optimization)
  • Experience building developer- or agent-facing product experiences, and integrating with AI coding tools such as Codex, Claude, or Cursor
  • Background in developer platforms or DevTools
  • Experience helping a team split or reorganize as it scales into multiple teams

If this role excites you but you don’t meet every single requirement, we’d still love to hear from you—your unique experience might be just what we need.

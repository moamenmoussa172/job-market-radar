About the job

As a Senior QA Engineer at Seera you will be part of a huge QA team focused on delivering the best experiences for our customers using your testing skills, as well as ensuring the best
experience through fast and working software. You’ll be the confidence aspect of the team.

  • Quick-thinker/learner, quality enthusiast who understands the importance of releasing great software
  • Pair with engineers, product manager, and scrum master to identify issues, bugs, and how the system is broken
  • Use your full throttle experience
  • Exposed to a great team of engineers
  • Exposed to the latest technologies and testing techniques
  • Ability to introduce new tools and frameworks to your team
  • Solve customer problems and make their experience seamless
  • Exposure to a full autonomy environment
  • 4+ years hands-on experience with testing of APIs
  • 3+ years hands-on experience with related automation frameworks (E.g. Rest assured)
  • Familiar with testing methodologies/types and when to execute each
  • Programming experience is a must (E.g. Java)
  • Non-functional testing (E.g. load, performance, Usability) experience (E.g. Jmeter, Gatling, Vegeta)
  • Professional experience across the entire Software Development Life Cycle
  • Experience with source control and CI/CD tooling (E.g. Git, GitHub, Jenkins)
  • Experience with observability and monitoring tools (E.g. Runscope)
  • Excellent communication skills; explaining technical problems succinctly and clearly
  • Willingness to challenge existing practices in order to make them better

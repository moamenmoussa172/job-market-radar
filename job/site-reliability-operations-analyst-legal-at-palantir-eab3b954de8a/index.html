About the job

A World-Changing Company

Palantir builds the world’s leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. By bringing the right data to the people who need it, our platforms empower our partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more.

Core Responsibilities

  • Partner with leaders to clarify priorities, frame problems, define outcomes, and sequence important initiatives.
  • Establish and run a lightweight operating rhythm, including planning sessions, team reviews, agendas, and pre-reads. Track decisions, action items, milestones, and commitments, and provide concise updates that surface risks early and clarify next steps.
  • Drive cross-functional initiatives from planning through completion. Align stakeholders, milestones, dependencies, and decisions so work continues to move.
  • Prepare leaders for decisions with concise briefings that clarify context, options, tradeoffs, open questions, and required decisions.
  • When a critical initiative goes off track, quickly establish the facts, identify an owner and path to resolution, coordinate the response, and keep stakeholders informed.
  • Anticipate team needs, remove friction, and manage critical handoffs across groups.
  • Improve processes, workflows, and tools based on recurring pain points and lessons learned.
  • What We Value
  • Strong judgment and composure when priorities shift or high-stakes issues arise.
  • The ability to build trust and communicate clearly with leaders, peers, and partner teams.
  • A lightweight, creative approach to program management that brings clarity without adding unnecessary process.
  • Meticulous attention to detail, including accurate records and diligent tracking of decisions, commitments, and key metrics.
  • Proactive ownership, resourcefulness, and a willingness to make the first move when work is blocked.
  • Discretion and care when handling sensitive information.
  • What We Require
  • Three or more years of experience in project or program management, business operations, executive operations, chief of staff work, or a related role, preferably in a fast-moving environment.
  • Experience coordinating complex initiatives across teams and following work through to measurable outcomes.
  • Strong written and verbal communication, including the ability to prepare clear, concise materials.
  • Ability to manage competing priorities, bring structure to ambiguity, and make progress with limited direction.
  • Sound judgment and discretion when working with confidential or sensitive information.
  • Prior work on a legal team or compliance team is a plus.

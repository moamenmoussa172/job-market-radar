About the job

About Qashio

Qashio is a global fintech company, dual-headquartered in Dubai and Dublin, helping businesses simplify corporate spending, payments and financial operations.

We're growing quickly and looking for talented people who want to help build secure, reliable products and be part of our journey.

The Role

We’re looking for an exceptional, hands-on Senior Security Engineer to take ownership of Qashio’s technical security as we continue to scale internationally.
This is a highly technical, individual contributor role for someone who loves building, breaking and fixing things. You’ll work closely with our Engineering and DevOps teams to secure our cloud infrastructure, applications and APIs, build security tooling and respond to real-world threats.
We’re not looking for someone who simply identifies problems or recommends solutions. We want someone who rolls up their sleeves and fixes them.
As our dedicated Security Engineer, you’ll have the autonomy, responsibility and opportunity to make a significant impact from day one.

What You'll Do

  • Cloud Security: Build, secure and continuously improve our AWS infrastructure, including IAM, logging, encryption and security guardrails.
  • Application & API Security: Identify, exploit in authorised testing and remediate vulnerabilities across web applications, mobile applications and APIs.
  • Security Engineering & Automation: Write code and build tools to automate security processes, improve detection and reduce manual intervention.
  • DevSecOps: Embed security into CI/CD pipelines, making security an integral part of the engineering lifecycle.
  • Incident Response: Own security incidents end to end, from detection and investigation through to containment, remediation and prevention.
  • Offensive Security: Proactively test our systems, identify weaknesses and implement effective fixes before they can be exploited.
  • Security by Design: Partner with Engineering and Product teams to ensure new features, products and infrastructure are secure from inception.
  • Regulatory Security: Implement and maintain technical security controls that support PCI DSS and applicable financial services regulatory requirements.

What We're Looking For

  • 6–12 years of experience in IT or cybersecurity, with at least 4 years in hands-on security engineering.
  • Strong practical experience building and securing AWS environments.
  • Experience identifying and remediating vulnerabilities across web, mobile and API environments.
  • Strong programming or scripting skills in Python or Go.
  • Experience integrating security scanning and automated controls into CI/CD pipelines.
  • Demonstrable experience independently investigating and resolving security incidents.
  • Previous experience within fintech, payments or banking.
  • A strong understanding of PCI DSS and security within regulated environments.
  • The ability to work autonomously, make sound technical decisions and take full ownership of outcomes.Bonus Points
  • Experience with Terraform and infrastructure as code.
  • Offensive security certifications such as OSCP, OSWE or CRT.
  • An active bug bounty history through platforms such as HackerOne or Bugcrowd.
  • Published CVEs or participation in CTF competitions.
  • Experience within card issuing or payment processing.
  • Contributions to open-source security projects, technical blogs, GitHub repositories or industry conferences.

Who You Are:

  • A builder: You write code, automate processes and create solutions rather than simply operating security tools.
  • Naturally curious: You enjoy understanding how things work, how they break and how to make them more secure.
  • Hands-on: You’re equally comfortable finding a vulnerability and implementing the fix.
  • An owner: You don’t wait for someone else to solve a problem.
  • Commercially aware: You understand how to balance security, engineering velocity and business growth.We value demonstrable technical ability and real-world achievements over a long list of certification.

If you’re excited by the opportunity to take ownership of security engineering within a fast-growing global fintech, we’d love to hear from you.

Why Join Qashio?

We're at an exciting stage of growth, and you'll have the opportunity to shape how security supports our products, teams and customers as Qashio scales.

You'll work across the business, take ownership of meaningful security challenges and help build practical foundations that protect a growing fintech company.

If you're passionate about security engineering and enjoy helping teams build secure, reliable services, we'd love to hear from you.

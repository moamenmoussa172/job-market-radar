About the job

The Associate Director, Enterprise Data Solution Analysis leads the business analysis that turns enterprise data and AI ambitions into well‑defined, deliverable solutions. Operating within Enterprise Data Programmes, this role translates business needs into clear requirements, quantifies value and supports AI solutions end to end, from discovery through to production and adoption, in partnership with the project leadership, platforms and business teams.

Scope of accountability:

You will lead the analysis for defined programme workstreams and AI initiatives:

  • Opportunity identification: Assess and prioritise high‑value data and AI opportunities, balancing value, feasibility, risk and strategic fit.
  • Requirements and solution definition: Elicit and document business, functional and non‑functional requirements; translate business problems into testable user stories and success metrics.
  • End‑to‑end AI solution analysis: problem framing, data readiness, technique evaluation, validation, deployment and post go‑live performance.
  • Business case and benefits: Develop lightweight business cases and track realised value against agreed measures.
  • Governance and responsible AI: Work with privacy, security, quality, risk and GxP (where applicable) to reflect policy and regulatory expectations in requirements.

Key accountabilities:

Business analysis and requirements:

  • Engage stakeholders to understand needs and desired outcomes; maintain traceable requirements and prioritised backlogs for assigned workstreams.
  • Translate programme strategy into scoped use cases and prepare directional proposals for governance forums.

Discovery, analysis and value quantification:

  • Perform process, data and landscape analysis to quantify value hypotheses (e.g., cycle‑time reduction, quality uplift, productivity gains).
  • Assess data availability, quality, lineage and access early, and flag dependencies affecting feasibility.

AI solution delivery:

  • Partner with engineers and data scientists to define evaluation criteria, thresholds and human‑in‑the‑loop checkpoints.
  • Support POCs, analyse results against cost, performance and risk criteria, and recommend whether to scale.

Governance, controls and risk:

  • Ensure privacy, security, quality and responsible AI requirements are captured, testable and evidenced.
  • Identify and escalate risks and issues; contribute to stage gates and assurance reviews.

Adoption, transition and benefits:

  • Define UAT approaches and readiness criteria; work with change management to embed solutions and gather post go‑live feedback.
  • Track and report benefits realisation and adoption, feeding lessons learned into future planning.

Stakeholder management and reporting:

  • Coordinate with leadership on dependencies, stage gates and reporting; provide clear status, risks and benefits tracking.
  • Engage with vendors and partners as needed; manage to agreed deliverables and quality.

Essential skills and experience:

  • Undergraduate degree in Computer Science, Data Management, Business or a related discipline, or equivalent experience.
  • Experience in AI and Data is mandatory for this role.
  • Approximately 3 - 5 years of business analysis experience delivering requirements, process analysis and solution definition for data, analytics or technology programmes in a global, matrixed environment.
  • Demonstrable experience in the AI market, with solid understanding of AI/ML and generative AI capabilities, limitations, use cases and the vendor landscape.
  • Ideally a proven track record of delivering at least one AI solution end to end from use case definition through to production and adoption.
  • Strong skills in requirements elicitation, process and data analysis, and data quality criteria; able to translate business problems into designs and measurable success metrics using best practice frameworks, including BPMN and ER modelling.
  • Good communication in technical aspects, translate technical information depending on the audience, persuasive,
  • Working knowledge of the data lifecycle and of privacy, security and responsible AI considerations.
  • Clear written and verbal communication; effective collaborator with engineers, data scientists and assurance partners; experience working to agile principles.

Desirable:

  • Experience in life sciences or other highly regulated industries.
  • Knowledge data management, cataloguing/lineage, metadata standards and data quality frameworks.
  • Business analysis or agile certification (e.g., CBAP, PMI‑PBA, CSPO).

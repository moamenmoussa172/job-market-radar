We are looking for an Indirect Procurement & Sourcing Analyst to join our European Sourcing Center of Excellence (SCoE). The role will primarily support procurement activities for the Italian business across various indirect spend categories, including Facilities, Services, Equipment, Laboratory Consumables and MRO.

As part of a European indirect procurement team, the successful candidate will collaborate closely with sourcing professionals across multiple countries, contribute to cross-country sourcing initiatives, and provide backup support for other European markets when needed. The role offers a combination of local stakeholder engagement and participation in broader European procurement projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage sourcing activities from requirement definition through supplier selection, contracting and purchase order placement.
  • Lead and support RFQs/RFPs, supplier evaluations, commercial negotiations and sourcing recommendations.
  • Identify cost-saving opportunities and ensure compliance with procurement policies and processes.
  • Act as the main procurement contact for stakeholders in Italy, providing responsive and customer-focused support.
  • Maintain accurate contract records in contract workspace and ensure proper documentation and process compliance
  • Monitor procurement requests and tickets, ensuring timely follow-up, resolution and escalation when needed
  • Collaborate with European sourcing colleagues on regional projects, best practice sharing and process improvements.
  • Support procurement activities across other European countries as business needs require.

Qualifications

  • 1-3 years of experience in procurement, sourcing or purchasing.
  • Experience managing RFQs/RFPs, supplier evaluations and negotiations.
  • Fluent in Italian and English.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work independently in a multinational, virtual team environment.
  • Good organizational skills and familiarity with procurement systems (SAP Ariba or similar tools preferred).

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to support Italy and work within a European procurement organization with exposure to multiple countries and stakeholders.
  • Participation in both local and regional sourcing initiatives.
  • Broad indirect procurement experience and development opportunities in a dynamic international environment.

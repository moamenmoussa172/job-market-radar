About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of cmpanies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Our organization is on a mission to unlock local payment methods (LPMs) at scale. As a growing share of e-commerce moves to non-card payments, LPMs represent a massive opportunity for Stripe that’s measured in billions of dollars. We support high-volume payment methods and the platform that will enable Stripe to onboard new LPM partners at scale.

Our team is the connective tissue between product engineering, payment partners, and support. We own reliability and customer outcomes for the payment methods we operate. We integrate new LPMs, ship security and reliability enhancements, and eliminate toil at its source so Stripe can grow its LPM portfolio to match its ambitions.

What you'll do

You'll ship code to eliminate recurring operational work and integrate LPMs on our standardized platform. You'll own those integrations beyond launch, using automation and root-cause fixes to make them easier to operate as Stripe adds more payment methods. Alongside integration work, you'll own security and reliability enhancements for the systems our pillar owns.

You'll balance project work with a business-hours support rotation, answering technical questions from merchants and internal teams. This role does not include a 24/7 pager or incident on-call.

Responsibilities

  • Unblock merchants and internal teams by independently investigating technical issues through code, logs, and data
  • Remove operational barriers to LPM growth by automating recurring work and fixing its root causes
  • Own security and reliability enhancements across our pillar's systems, shipping code changes and infrastructure migrations with product engineering teams
  • Integrate LPMs on our standardized platform and own their reliability and customer outcomes beyond launch
  • Resolve technical problems efficiently with AI tools, checking outputs against source evidence and validating code changes before deployment
  • Enable scaled support teams to resolve repeatable issues independently through runbooks, tooling, and training
  • Prioritize operational improvements with product engineering teams, agree on ownership, and measure the outcomes

Who you are

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

TechOps IRE roles combine software engineering, data analysis, AI systems development, and cross-functional partner management. The ideal candidate brings experience across technical systems, data investigation, and complex project ownership. They find genuine satisfaction in making things run reliably at scale through both human judgment and intelligent automation.

Minimum requirements

  • Strong technical background, including 2+ years of experience designing, building, and operating software systems
  • Highly fluent with developer tooling (e.g. version control, CI/CD, real-time observability metrics and logs) and an ability to make well-scoped code changes in a large codebase
  • Ability to develop a deep understanding of complex systems through reading and writing code, studying documentation and partner API specifications, running debugging tools, reading logs, querying raw data, and asking precise questions of domain experts
  • Experience building with AI and LLM tooling with established clear outcomes. You don't need experience with any specific framework, but you should be able to pick up new tools quickly and think clearly about where automation adds value versus where human judgment is required
  • Strong SQL skills and proficiency navigating a variety of data systems to diagnose and scope issues
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to frame messages appropriately for both technical and non-technical audiences
  • An instinct to build solutions that increase the leverage of the people around you—not just to solve the problem in front of you, but to eliminate the class of problem

Preferred qualifications

  • Knowledge of and experience with payment technologies, open banking, and financial data ecosystems
  • Experience designing or operating agentic or LLM-powered workflows in a production environment
  • Proficiency with Presto, Splunk, Logscale or similar data querying and log analysis tools
  • Experience managing technical relationships with external partners or vendors

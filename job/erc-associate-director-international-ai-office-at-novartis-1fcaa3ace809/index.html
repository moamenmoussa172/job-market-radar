Are you passionate about shaping the future of artificial intelligence while ensuring it is used responsibly and ethically? As ERC Associate Director, International AI Office, you will play a pivotal role in guiding some of Novartis' most transformative digital and AI initiatives, including the Customer Engagement of the Future program. Working at the intersection of innovation, ethics, risk, and compliance, you will partner with senior business, data, digital, and technology leaders to help bring groundbreaking solutions to life while embedding trust, integrity, and compliance by design every step of the way. This is a unique opportunity to influence how AI is adopted at scale across international markets and help redefine the future of healthcare.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

· Partner with business leaders to enable compliant innovation across artificial intelligence and digital transformation initiatives.

· Collaborate with business and AI Office to establish AI Governance framework and guardrails supporting responsible adoption of artificial intelligence and data solutions.

· Provide strategic ethics, risk, and compliance guidance for customer engagement and data-driven programs.

· Bring a forward-looking ERC perspective to emerging technologies (e.g., CEFUT), customer engagement models, advanced analytics, and AI capabilities, balancing innovation with appropriate governance and risk management.

· Embed compliance and ethical principles into product design, governance, and operating models from concept to deployment.

· Anticipate, assess, and mitigate emerging compliance and ethical risks associated with evolving technologies.

· Collaborate with regional and country teams to align global initiatives with local regulatory requirements.

· Role‑model the Novartis Code of Ethics and provide credible, informed challenge across business initiatives

· Influence strategic decisions and foster a strong culture of ethics, integrity, and accountability across stakeholders.

Essential Requirements

· Degree in business, law, or another compliance-related discipline.

· Significant experience in ethics, risk and compliance, legal, or related business partnering roles.

· Deep understanding of pharmaceutical industry regulations, industry codes, and anti-bribery requirements.

· Experience advising cross-functional stakeholders on complex compliance and risk management matters. Country business partnering experience will be a plus.

· Strong knowledge of artificial intelligence concepts and responsible technology governance principles.

· Excellent communication, influencing, and stakeholder management skills across global organizations.

About the job

Build the Future of Intelligent Workflows

Join Bosch Vietnam’s Global Product & Service team to help build next-generation workflow platforms used by 500,000+ users worldwide. Built on modern Low-Code / No-Code technologies and evolving with AI and GenAI capabilities, our platform powers critical enterprise business processes at global scale.

We are looking for proactive and high-potential interns passionate about Product Development, Business Analysis, and innovation. This is not a passive internship — you will gain hands-on experience in real product environments and collaborate with global teams.

What You Will Do (Learning by Doing)

  • Support requirement gathering, analysis, and product documentation
  • Translate business needs into user stories and backlog items
  • Support workflow analysis, UAT, and Agile delivery activities
  • Explore user behaviors and product usage data to identify improvement opportunities
  • Participate in product discovery, design thinking, and innovation workshops
  • Collaborate with Product Managers, developers, and global stakeholders
  • Contribute ideas for workflow automation and AI-driven capabilities

Must-Have

  • Final-year or senior student in Computer Science, IT, Business Information Systems, or related fields
  • Available for a 6-month full-time internship (Monday to Friday) or at least 4 working days per week.
  • Able to provide a university recommendation or introduction letter for the internship
  • Good English communication skills
  • Basic understanding of software development lifecycle and requirement analysis
  • Strong logical thinking, problem-solving, and learning mindset
  • Proactive, responsible, and teamwork-oriented

Nice to Have

  • Exposure to Agile / Scrum
  • Interest in Product Management, Business Analysis, AI, or workflow automation
  • Experience in IT/product-related university projects or activities

How to Apply - please submit:

  • Your CV
  • A cover letter covering:
  • Why do you want to build a career in IT Product Business Analyst / Product Owner?
  • What does “product innovation and product impact” mean to you in real work?

WHY BOSCH?

Because we do not just follow trends, we create them. Together we turn ideas into reality, working every day to make the world of tomorrow a better place.

Do you set high expectations for your learning journey? So do we. At Bosch, you’ll explore, grow, and challenge yourself in a dynamic and innovative environment.

Internship Benefits & Learning Opportunities at Bosch

• Monthly Internship Allowance, plus Meal and Parking support.
• 1 paid leave day + 1 sick leave day for each internship month.
• 1 birthday leave day if your birthday falls during the internship period.
• Accident insurance provided throughout the internship.
• Opportunity to observe and engage in international projects and innovation.
• Access to our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professionalism.
• Participation in various company activities such as football, yoga, badminton, and team building.

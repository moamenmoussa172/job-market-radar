About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, United Kingdom.

We’re looking for a Senior Go Engineer to join our tech team and help build the future of neuro-contextual advertising at global scale.

Who We Are

At Seedtag, our mission is to transform advertising by proving that effectiveness and user privacy can truly coexist.

As the leading Neuro-Contextual Advertising Company, we combine Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and neuroscience to understand not only what content is about, but how it makes people feel and what they intend to do next.

Our proprietary AI, Liz, enables brands to connect with audiences across the open web and Connected TV without cookies or user tracking. Founded in 2014 by two ex-Googlers, Seedtag has grown to 500+ Seedtaggers in 17 countries, backed by €250M in funding, and operates today as a global ad-tech leader.

If you enjoy solving complex engineering challenges and building AI-driven systems at scale, you’ll feel right at home here.

Your Challenge

You will build and run the Go services behind Seedtag’s AdExchange, where every ad request is decided in milliseconds. You’ll join one team of engineers that owns the real-time bidding exchange end to end.

  • Scale real-time bidding services, balancing ultra-low latency, cost and reliability as traffic grows.
  • Integrate new demand and supply partners, from capacity planning to traffic shaping and performance validation. Bringing new formats and channels to one exchange.
  • Design auction and pricing mechanics, including bid floors, dynamic margins and ML models served in real time.
  • Build our audience data platform. Onboard third-party data providers, from data modelling and ingestion to real-time lookups.
  • Build event pipelines and reporting that data science and AI teams rely on to analyse, debug and train models.
  • Own projects end to end, from design to production metrics. You make technical decisions and explain them to Product and other stakeholders.

Tech Stack

Our stack includes:

  • Languages: Go, TypeScript, Python
  • Data & messaging: Kafka, Redis, MongoDB, PostgreSQL
  • Cloud & infra: Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Terraform, ArgoCD
  • Observability: Grafana, Victoria Metrics,
  • Analytics: Trino, BigQuery
  • Tooling: GitHub Actions, Claude Code, Jira, Notion, Slack

What You’ll Need to Succeed

5+ years building backend systems, with recent years writing Go in production.

  • Experience with high-throughput, low-latency services. You have measured and improved p99 latency or serving cost on a real system.
  • Distributed systems: message queues, caches, data stores, and how they fail. Concurrency, consistency and backpressure.
  • Kubernetes and cloud deployments, CI/CD and production observability.
  • Ownership. You take a fuzzy problem to a measured outcome and keep the service healthy after launch.
  • A data-driven mindset. You validate hypotheses with real data.
  • Resilient design: You have designed scalable, robust and self-healing services for high-transactional, low-latency workloads and applied the patterns on real systems: circuit breakers, timeouts, retries with backoff and jitter, bulkheads, load shedding, graceful degradation and fallback paths, idempotent event processing and caching strategies.
  • Architecture patterns in production. On the architecture side, you know when to reach for stateless, horizontally scaled services, sharding, health probes with autoscaling and canary deployments so the platform recovers without a human in the loop. You can explain what failed in production and how the design absorbed it.
  • AI-native workflow. You use AI coding tools daily, automate where you can and review their output critically when necessary.
  • Clear communication with engineers, Product and non-technical stakeholders, in English.

It would be a plus:

  • AdTech experience: OpenRTB, auctions, bid floors, SSP/DSP integrations or CTV
  • Serving ML models in real time
  • Mentoring others and raising the bar

Why Join Seedtag?

  • A key moment of growth with real ownership and global impact.
  • Flexible work model with 100% remote or hybrid options.
    (Remote contracts available in Spain, Italy, the UK, Belgium, the Netherlands, France, and Germany.)
  • Continuous learning through a learning platform and optional language classes.
  • A supportive, trust-based culture that values well-being.
  • Team activities, offsites, and opportunities to connect beyond work.

Additional Perks

  • Home office setup budget up to €1,000
  • Paid trips to our HQ in Madrid
  • MacBook Pro M3

At Seedtag, we create an environment where everyone can thrive. If you need accommodations during the hiring process, let us know and we’ll ensure a positive experience.

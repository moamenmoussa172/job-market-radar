About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland and 2 more countries.

Why Design at Langfuse

Your work will shape how engineers build with AI.

Langfuse is the primary interface engineers use to debug, evaluate, and improve their LLM applications. The experience you ship will be used for hours each day by thousands of developers and non-developers. They'll tell you about it in GitHub issues, on X, and in our community Slack channels. Everything we build is open source (MIT-licensed) and immediately visible.

The design challenges here are genuinely hard. You're designing for technical users and business users alike who care about information density, speed, and precision. For example, traces contain dozens of nested LLM calls with megabyte long prompts and completions. Users need to set up evaluators, manage skill versions, configure our AI gateway or integrations, and build monitoring dashboards before they even start analyzing results. You need to make both the setup and the daily use feel intuitive and easy.

You will also have direct exposure to how cutting-edge LLM applications are built. Deeply understanding the problems our users solve will make you a better designer and along the way makes you an expert on LLM engineering yourself.

For the past few years, we focused mainly on building LLM infrastructure that scales to massive volumes and stays cheap to run. Over the last year, we made design a cornerstone of our product team, because we learned that great infrastructure only pays off when the product feels seamless to use.

You will grow at Langfuse by

  • Owning our product design end-to-end. You will be assigned to the most challenging and business critical product launches we work on at Langfuse. To understand how to make the feature great, you will talk to users, our marketing team, and our engineers to understand what needs to be solved. You are then responsible for making the experience for our users as seamless as possible.
  • Making data-dense interfaces intuitive. Langfuse surfaces traces, scores, costs, latencies, token counts, prompt versions, and evaluation results. You'll figure out how to present complex, nested data in ways that help engineers find what they need fast.
  • Simplifying complex workflows. Evals, experiments, prompt and skill versions, and monitoring involve a lot of steps today. We're making Halo, our in-app agent, the place where they come together, with AI-generated UI and workflows built into many parts of Langfuse. You'll design these new ways of working so they feel obvious in hindsight.
  • Collaborating directly with product engineers. You'll pair with engineers, whiteboard solutions together, and ship designs that work with our design system. You'll create reusable, consistent UI patterns that the whole team can build with.
  • Shipping design straight to code. You'll use AI coding tools to implement smaller design fixes, build components for the design system, and polish UI details yourself. We are not looking for a perfect engineer here but for someone who can help themselves.

What we're looking for

  • 7+ years of experience in designing complex web applications, incl. a portfolio showcasing product work that users loved
  • Experience taking products from features from zero to one in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong product mindset, with a focus on UX and clear understanding of tradeoffs
  • The ability to think in systems. Your patterns and components hold up across the whole product and stay consistent as it grows
  • Excited about open source software and dev tools
  • We are launching new features on a daily basis. While you focus on your primary project, engineers will ask for help on smaller topics continuously. You need to be able to prioritize yourself and understand where to go deep and where to go with an 80:20 approach.

Bonus points:

  • Leverage AI in your workflows to design, build, test, and ship to production
  • ML/AI experience or familiarity with the LLM tooling ecosystem
  • Former founder or early startup experience
  • Contributions to open source projects or design systems
  • Side projects that show curiosity
  • Stellar visual craft

No candidate checks all boxes. If you feel you are a good fit for this role, please go ahead and apply.

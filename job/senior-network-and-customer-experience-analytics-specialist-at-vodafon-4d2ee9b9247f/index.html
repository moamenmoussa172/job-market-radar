About the job

Role Purpose and Key Responsibilities

Role purpose:

  • To analyze large volumes of network data using analytics tools to generate actionable insights that improve customer experience and optimize network performance.
  • Work with Marketing and CVM teams to translate network experience data into actionable business opportunities

Key Responsibilities:

  • Analyze large volumes of data from different network elementsto identify patterns, trends, and anomalies affecting customer experience
  • Support use case development for: Upsell/cross-sell based on customer experience and service differentiation based on Quality of Experience insights
  • Translate data insights into actionable insights for network optimization and customer experience improvement
  • Provide analytics to enable revenue protection and growth initiatives (e.g., churn reduction, retention triggers)
  • Collaborate with Network, Customer Business Unit,Vodacom Business Unit, IT, Customer Experience and Service Operation Centre (SOC) teams to ensure data-driven decisions across the organization

Qualification, Core Competencies, Knowledge and Experience

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Data Science / Computer Science/Telecommunications Engineering/Electrical and Electronics Engineering
  • Minimum 3–5 years relevant experience in
  • Data analytics / data mining / Data science
  • Telecom performance analytics or customer experience management
  • Strong technical skills in:
  • Spark SQL
  • Carbon Database
  • Python
  • Shell script
  • Experience with:
  • Data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau)
  • Handling and processing large datasets
  • Strong analytical and logical reasoning skills with ability to insights from complex datasets
  • Ability to translate technical data into customer-centric and business/commercial insights
  • Good understanding of telecom network domains (RAN, Core, Transmission, IP)
  • Experience in applying analytics to customer behaviour, segmentation, and value management
  • Experience handling large datasets and applying data science techniques
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills across technical and commercial teams

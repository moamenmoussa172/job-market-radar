Position title: SAP iXp Intern - Support Engineer at Technical Support - Data & Platform ABAP

Location: São Leopoldo, Brazil [hybrid position, 3 days per week working from the office]

Please, upload your resume in English

About the team

Support intern who is driven by a desire to provide our customers with outstanding technical customer service. The primary responsibility is to assist our customers in resolving issues related to the implementation, deployment, functionality and usage of SAP Products. The intern will need the ability to support complex environments that include a variety of operating systems, platforms and databases.

In this role, you’ll:

  • Monitor critical support incidents, ensure SLAs adherence and 24x7 support.
  • Perform root cause analysis, analyze and resolve incidents reported by global customers.
  • Create and share technical knowledge with colleagues and customers.
  • Report bugs and system errors to Development Support department.
  • Participate in local or global projects.

What You’ll bring:

A curious and agile mindset, with a passion for learning and the resilience to adapt in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Emotional intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and thoughtful stakeholder engagement. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies—especially artificial intelligence—paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take initiative.

Requirements:

  • University students enrolled in Information Technology, Computer Science, Accounting, HR or related areas, expecting to graduate in the next 2 years
  • Fluent English (oral and written) that can be in contact with customers and colleagues globally via email, system and phone.
  • Strong customer focus and communication skills (oral/written).
  • Team player and contributes for a great environment.
  • Programming logic
  • Object Oriented Programming (ABAP, Java, C++, etc.) is desirable

Nice to Have:

  • SAP HCM Business Processes
  • Payroll experience/knowledge
  • Problem solving/analytical skills
  • Spanish language would be a benefit

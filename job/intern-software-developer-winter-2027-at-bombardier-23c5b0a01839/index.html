About the job

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

What are your contributions to the team?

In your role, you will:

  • Participate in all aspects of agile software development, from implementation to deployment of web-based applications.
  • Help in development new features in support of evolving business and project requirements.
  • Participate in all aspects of the SDLC from a project’s initiation to release and post-release support.
  • Write clean & efficient code using best practices.
  • Write and validate test procedures. Write unit tests.
  • Communicate risks and issues to the delivery manager and propose potential mitigation plans.

How to thrive in this role?

As our ideal candidate:

  • You possess knowledge of two or more of the following: Node.js, REACT, Python and SQL.
  • You have completed at least one internship in software development.
  • You have an understanding of an Agile Software Development environment.
  • You are pro-active, flexible and have solid critical thinking skills. You have a strong sense of accountability and autonomy, and you focus on outcomes. You always look for ideas and suggestions to make improvements.

What you’ll do
In this role, you'll provide technical leadership and oversight across OSS data exchange and interface delivery, helping teams turn complex business and network requirements into scalable, high-quality solutions. You'll work closely with architects, programme teams, delivery partners, and operational stakeholders to ensure successful end-to-end delivery while continuously improving processes, standards, and service performance.

  • You'll lead the design and delivery of OSS data exchange and interface solutions, ensuring alignment with architectural principles, operational requirements, and business objectives.
  • You'll gather, analyse, and translate complex technical and business requirements into effective integration and interface designs.
  • You'll create, review, and govern key technical artefacts, including High-Level Designs (HLDs), Low-Level Designs (LLDs), test strategies, and Transfer to Operations (TTO) documentation.
  • You'll collaborate with internal teams, business partners, system integrators, and vendors to deliver solutions throughout the full project lifecycle.
  • You'll ensure all deliveries meet operational acceptance criteria, contractual commitments, technical KPIs, and agreed milestones.
  • You'll provide technical guidance on risk management, issue resolution, resource planning, and budgeting activities.
  • You'll establish and maintain data standards, governance frameworks, and integration practices that support consistency and data quality across platforms.
  • You'll drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance service assurance, delivery quality, and operational resilience.
  • You'll act as a trusted link between business stakeholders, architects, engineering teams, and external delivery partners.
  • You'll support RFP and technology evaluation activities, helping identify solutions that deliver sustainable value.
  • You'll contribute to the successful delivery of network engineering initiatives and related applications.
  • You'll build productive relationships with senior stakeholders and communicate progress, risks, priorities, and opportunities clearly and effectively.
  • You'll help foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.

Who you are

  • You have significant experience working within the telecommunications industry.
  • You have strong expertise in designing, implementing, and delivering OSS solutions in complex environments.
  • You have a solid understanding of OSS domains such as Service Management, Service Assurance, Inventory Management, and Customer Experience Management.
  • You bring strong knowledge of integration technologies and industry standards, including SNMP, NETCONF/YANG, and TM Forum Open APIs.
  • You have experience working with Master Data Management (MDM), semantic data modelling, and multi-vendor telecommunications ecosystems.
  • You have experience developing and applying data governance frameworks, standards, and policies.
  • You understand mobile network architecture and the processes involved in network design, deployment, and operations.
  • You can confidently gather requirements and translate them into practical, effective technical solutions.
  • You are an analytical thinker who can navigate complexity, identify root causes, and develop actionable solutions.
  • You communicate clearly and adapt your approach to engage a diverse range of technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • You build strong partnerships and can work effectively across teams, functions, organisations, and suppliers.
  • You have experience influencing outcomes and creating value within matrix-managed environments.
  • You balance strategic thinking with practical execution and can prioritise competing demands effectively.
  • You are comfortable engaging with senior stakeholders and helping build alignment around shared goals and priorities.
  • You have experience supporting technology transformation programmes involving multiple partners and stakeholders.
  • You hold, or are working towards, certifications such as TOGAF, ITIL Foundation, or PRINCE2 Practitioner.
  • You have knowledge of TM Forum frameworks, including TAM, eTOM, and SID.
  • You encourage collaboration, welcome diverse perspectives, and contribute to an inclusive working environment.
  • You demonstrate curiosity, adaptability, and a commitment to continuous learning and professional growth.
  • You are motivated by achieving meaningful outcomes, improving customer experiences, and delivering sustainable business value.

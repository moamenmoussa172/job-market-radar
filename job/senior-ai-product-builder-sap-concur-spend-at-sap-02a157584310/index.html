About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

We are looking for a product professional who can operate in a new paradigm where AI is foundational to how products are discovered, designed, built, and delivered.

As a Product Builder for SAP Concur Spend, you will play a key role in shaping the next generation of AI-native spend management experiences. You will work at the intersection of customer insight, product strategy, experience design, and intelligent automation, translating real-world customer problems into scalable, commercially viable, and high-impact product capabilities.

What you'll bring

You bring strong product management experience with end-to-end ownership of product discovery, roadmap development, delivery, and post-launch adoption. You have practical experience designing AI-native products and applying Generative AI, Agentic AI, intelligent automation, and AI-assisted product development techniques to solve real customer problems. You are comfortable independently identifying customer needs, challenging assumptions, validating hypotheses, and driving product decisions with minimal oversight. You possess a strong understanding of human-in-the-loop design, human-agent interactions, context engineering, and Responsible AI principles, and can apply these concepts to create effective and trustworthy user experiences. You have experience rapidly prototyping solutions using AI-assisted tools to visualize concepts, validate customer demand, and accelerate product discovery. You excel at customer engagement, market research, and competitive analysis, transforming insights into differentiated product experiences. You leverage data, experimentation, and meaningful success metrics to inform decisions and measure customer and business outcomes. You are skilled at prioritization, making build-versus-buy decisions, and defining product scope by balancing customer value, technical feasibility, and commercial viability. You are an effective collaborator and communicator who can influence stakeholders across cross-functional and global teams. Experience with spend management, expense management, finance systems, ERP platforms, or enterprise SaaS solutions is advantageous.

What you'll build

You will independently own assigned product areas end-to-end, shaping and evolving the product roadmap to align customer needs, business priorities, and emerging AI-driven opportunities. You will identify opportunities to simplify complex workflows, reduce manual effort, enable intelligent decision-making, and deliver differentiated AI-native experiences within SAP Concur Spend. Working across traditional web, conversational, voice, and agentic interfaces, you will lead product discovery, design, and iteration efforts, selecting the most effective interaction model for each customer problem. You will leverage AI-assisted rapid prototyping, design thinking, customer validation, and experimentation to transform ideas into outcome-driven MVPs, while designing and validating human-agent journeys that balance automation, autonomy, human oversight, trust, and accountability. In close partnership with engineering, design, and data science teams, you will translate product concepts into scalable, secure, and enterprise-grade solutions. You will define meaningful success metrics and AI evaluation frameworks that consider output quality, accuracy, reliability, explainability, adoption, operational efficiency, and measurable customer value. By establishing continuous feedback loops, you will evaluate AI performance, identify opportunities for improvement, and drive ongoing product optimization after launch. You will own product scope, backlog prioritization, and execution alignment, making informed trade-offs to deliver high-quality outcomes at speed. You will ensure products are useful, intuitive, accessible, consistent, and aligned with Responsible AI principles, localization needs, and enterprise compliance requirements. Additionally, you will drive launch readiness, customer adoption, and enablement in partnership with customer-facing and cross-functional teams, while contributing to broader product strategy and representing your product area with customers, partners, and internal stakeholders.

Where you belong

You will be part of the SAP Concur Spend Product team, shaping the future of intelligent spend management for global enterprises.You will own the product direction, quality, adoption, and customer outcomes for your assigned scope, serving as a trusted point of contact for complex product and experience decisions.Working closely with engineering, design, data science, and customer-facing teams, you will bring together product thinking, technical depth in AI-native design, and commercial awareness to transform customer problems into meaningful product experiences.Your success will be defined not only by the capabilities you deliver, but by the measurable value they create for customers and the business.

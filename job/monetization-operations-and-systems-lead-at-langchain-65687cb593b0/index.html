About the job

About the Role

The ways customers buy from us are multiplying as fast as the business is growing. Enterprise order forms. Private offers through AWS, GCP, and Azure. A self-serve business running on usage-based pricing, with commits, drawdowns, and true-ups behind it. Each one is a distinct path from signature to cash, and each one runs today on some mix of manual steps and automation that grew up with the business.

This role owns what comes next, and it's hands-on. You'll run order operations end to end - booking, provisioning, invoicing, amendments, and renewals - and you'll be the business-side admin for Metronome, our metering and billing platform, configuring it to make that work. The rest of the stack is Salesforce for CRM and Rillet for ERP. The stack is modern, but the processes on top of it were built to keep up with growth, not designed to scale. Your job is to decide what each one should become: keep it, rebuild it, automate it, or retire it.

Expect the work to split roughly 80/20. Most of it is operations and process: running the order-to-cash path, fixing what breaks, and getting partner teams onto one roadmap. The other 20% is hands-on systems work: building products and rate cards in Metronome, tracing how deal terms flow from Salesforce into billing, and querying billing data to find what doesn't reconcile. You don't need to be an engineer. You do need to be the kind of operator who opens the admin console and the API docs yourself instead of filing a ticket and waiting.

This is a senior individual contributor role. You'll own order operations and billing configuration, working closely with Deal Strategy and Operations, Product, GTM Systems, Finance and Accounting, and Enterprise Engineering. Our bias is to invest in scalable automation rather than a growing queue of manual work, and getting that right is what lets order volume grow by an order of magnitude without ops growing with it.

What You'll Do

  • Own order-to-cash end to end: intake, booking, contract setup in Metronome, provisioning, invoicing, amendments, and renewals across Salesforce, Metronome, and Rillet
  • Serve as Metronome's primary business admin, shared with Enterprise Engineering: products, rate cards, and custom fields
  • Partner with Deal Strategy and Product to translate new pricing models and non-standard terms into Metronome configuration
  • When a deal won't bill cleanly, decide whether to push back, propose an alternative, or accept a manual process (with a path to automation, or manual by design)
  • Partner with GTM Systems on the data flow from Salesforce to Metronome to Rillet
  • Run cloud marketplace operations across AWS, GCP, and Azure, from private offers to disbursement reconciliation
  • Own self-serve billing: plan changes, dunning, credits, rebills, and refunds
  • Be the go-to expert for tracking down and resolving errors in the billing pipeline and Metronome configuration
  • Set the automation roadmap: ship what you can yourself, and get the rest onto partner teams' priorities
  • Build the playbook and audit-ready controls: approvals, segregation of duties, reconciliations, and exceptions
  • Own three metrics: touchless order rate, signature-to-provisioned cycle time, and billing accuracy

What You'll Bring

  • 5+ years in billing operations, order-to-cash, revenue systems, or billing platform implementation at a B2B SaaS or usage-based company
  • Hands-on admin experience with a usage-based billing platform (Metronome, Stripe Billing, Orb, Chargebee, Zuora, or similar)
  • Working SQL for reconciling billing, usage, and CRM data
  • Comfort with APIs and integrations (no production code required)
  • Deep understanding of usage-based pricing: commits, drawdowns, overages, and true-ups
  • Working knowledge of ASC 606 and month-end close
  • A track record of learning new systems quickly
  • A history of automating manual work out of your own job
  • Ability to drive cross-team decisions without formal authority

Nice to Have

  • Metronome experience
  • Cloud marketplace experience (AWS, GCP, or Azure private offers)
  • Python or other scripting
  • Building agents or LLM workflows for internal operations, ideally on the LangSmith Platform
  • Audit prep or SOX readiness

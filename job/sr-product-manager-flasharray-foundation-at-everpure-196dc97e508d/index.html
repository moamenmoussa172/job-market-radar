THE ROLE

As Senior Product Manager for FlashArray Foundation, you will define and drive the core platform roadmap for Purity//FA, the operating system behind Everpure’s flagship enterprise storage appliance. You will bridge software and hardware co-design—owning controller enablement, flash media integration, high availability, and non-disruptive upgrades. Partnering closely with Engineering, Hardware, and the CTO office, you will turn complex storage protocols and hardware innovations into resilient, high-performance capabilities that maximize customer uptime and operational simplicity.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Core OS & Platform Ownership: Drive the end-to-end vision and product requirements for Purity//FA core services, balancing high-availability failover, non-disruptive upgrades, and data reduction capabilities to consistently exceed enterprise SLAs.
  • Protocol & Hardware Integration: Translate technical needs across storage protocols (FC, iSCSI, NVMe-oF, NVMe/TCP) and next-generation hardware into actionable product definitions that accelerate time-to-market for new storage innovations.
  • Data-Driven Performance Optimization: Analyze system telemetry, benchmark metrics, and support trends to identify latency and throughput bottlenecks, converting findings into a prioritized, high-impact product backlog.
  • Cross-Functional Execution: Partner with Platform PMs, Engineering, QA, and CTO architects to establish evaluation criteria and execution plans that ensure seamless hardware and software co-design.
  • Commercial & Field Enablement: Equip Solutions Engineers and enterprise customers with technical positioning, competitive analysis, and architectural deep-dives that showcase FlashArray’s core reliability and performance advantages.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Infrastructure Product Leadership: Proven expertise taking enterprise software or storage products from requirements to launch, with a strong track record of translating complex technical trade-offs into shippable features.
  • Storage Systems & Protocol Architecture: Deep technical command of block storage protocols (FC, iSCSI, NVMe-oF/TCP) and storage OS internals, including high-availability failover, upgrades, and controller/media interaction.
  • Analytical & Performance Mindset: Exceptional ability to evaluate system metrics (IOPS, latency, availability, failure domains) and use telemetry data to guide roadmap prioritization and architectural choices.
  • Cross-Functional Influence: Strong communication skills with a history of aligning Engineering, Hardware, and field teams around shared objectives without direct authority.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, CA office in compliance with Everpure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

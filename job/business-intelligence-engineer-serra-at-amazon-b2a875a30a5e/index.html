About the job

Are you passionate about turning raw data into meaningful insights that drive real business decisions? Do you thrive when building dashboards, writing queries, and uncovering patterns that help teams move forward with confidence? At Amazon, Business Intelligence Engineers play a critical role in shaping how we understand our customers, our operations, and our opportunities.
In this role, you will build and deliver business intelligence solutions including data sets, queries, reports, dashboards, and analyses that answer key business questions and empower stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions. You will work with well-defined requirements, collaborate with cross-functional partners, and apply your technical skills in SQL, ETL, data modeling, and visualization tools to create reliable, well-documented solutions. If you are curious, resourceful, and motivated by the chance to see your work make a tangible impact, we want to hear from you.
Key job responsibilities
- Build small to mid-sized business intelligence solutions data sets, queries, reports, dashboards, and analyses or components of larger solutions that answer business questions with data, delivering end products on schedule.
- Write secure, stable, testable, and maintainable SQL and code to query, process, and present data; automate manual processes where possible and maintain ETL pipelines to support reliable data flows.
- Create and populate data structures using schema definition languages, and use industry visualization tools such as QuickSight, Tableau, or Power BI along with statistical methods to deliver actionable insights to stakeholders.
- Partner with business teams, data scientists, and other internal customers to understand requirements, align deliverables with expectations, and refine BI solutions to meet evolving business needs.
- Troubleshoot data quality issues, research root causes, validate outputs against source data and business logic, and document solutions clearly to ensure ease of use, interpretation, and long-term maintainability.
A day in the life
You will start your day reviewing data pipelines and dashboards, ensuring the metrics your stakeholders rely on are accurate and up to date. From there, you might refine a report based on new business questions, write and optimize SQL queries against large data sets, or meet with partner teams to scope out an upcoming analysis. You will regularly validate your work against source data and participate in design and prioritization discussions with your team, building your understanding of both the technical landscape and the business context behind the numbers.
About the team
Our team is focused on enabling smarter, faster decisions across the business by delivering trustworthy data and clear, actionable intelligence. We collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, data scientists, and business leaders to ensure our solutions address real customer and operational needs. We value curiosity, thoughtful problem-solving, and a commitment to quality. As we continue to grow, you will have the opportunity to expand your technical skills, deepen your business knowledge, and contribute to projects that have a meaningful impact on how Amazon serves its customers.
- Experience in analyzing and interpreting data with Redshift, Oracle, NoSQL etc.
- Experience with data visualization using Tableau, Quicksight, or similar tools
- Experience with one or more industry analytics visualization tools (e.g. Excel, Tableau, QuickSight, MicroStrategy, PowerBI) and statistical methods (e.g. t-test, Chi-squared)
- Experience with scripting language (e.g., Python, Java, or R)
- Experience with SQL
- Experience in the data/BI space
- Master's degree, or Advanced technical degree
- Knowledge of data modeling and data pipeline design
- Experience with statistical analysis, co-relation analysis

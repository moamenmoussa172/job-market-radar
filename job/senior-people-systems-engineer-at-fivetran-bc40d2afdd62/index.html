About the Role

We're looking for a Senior People Systems Engineer to help shape how AI transforms our People Systems and employee experiences. You'll bring working knowledge of the Workday and Greenhouse ecosystems and a record of turning AI and automation opportunities into secure, useful production workflows.

You'll work across People, Engineering, Security, and business teams to build the integrations, automations, and agentic workflows that connect our People platforms. This role is scoped at the feature level: you'll independently design and deliver team-level features, contribute to architecture discussions, and develop subsystems with guidance from senior technical leaders. You'll also help establish patterns for safe and reliable AI use and move teams from experimentation to measurable results.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build, and operationalize agentic workflows and automations across Workday, Greenhouse, Pigment, and connected systems.
  • Independently write designs for team-level feature requests, seek feedback early, and incorporate it.
  • Participate in design reviews, identify design deficiencies, and propose solutions that weigh the tradeoffs.
  • Contribute to architecture discussions for integrations and solutions, and develop subsystems with supervision from senior technical leads.
  • Partner with People teams to identify high-value use cases and turn them into scoped, measurable delivery plans.
  • Independently structure project plans and execute on them; write technical specs and document common components.
  • Apply and contribute to reusable patterns for agent permissions, human review, auditability, privacy, testing, and production monitoring.
  • Produce configuration and code that serves as an example for teammates; coach peers and help ensure accepted technical practices are followed.
  • Manage dev infrastructure and environments on cloud and automation platforms.
  • Track emerging Workday, Greenhouse, and AI capabilities and share recommendations on where to adopt, extend, or integrate them.
  • Monitor workflow quality, reliability, and adoption, and report on business impact.
  • Communicate technical options, risks, and tradeoffs clearly to stakeholders and cross-functional teams.

Skills We're Looking For

  • Solid technical experience with Workday and ATS platforms, including integrations and the systems' roles across the employee lifecycle.
  • Experience building and delivering AI or agentic workflows, or comparable automations, that address real business needs.
  • Strong integration and automation experience, including APIs, web services, middleware, or custom applications.
  • Experience working with cloud platforms and managing dev infrastructure.
  • Ability to independently write designs and technical specs for team-level features, and to actively seek and apply feedback.
  • Experience handling sensitive employee or recruiting data in a secure, reliable way.
  • Good judgment in evaluating AI solutions, including quality, failure modes, human oversight, and operational support needs.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, strong documentation habits, and a collaborative approach that keeps stakeholders informed and helps coach peers.
  • Comfort making progress amid ambiguity, changing priorities, and incomplete information.

Bonus Skills

  • Experience with Workday Extend, Workday AI capabilities, MCP, or agent orchestration frameworks.
  • Experience building Slack or other employee-facing applications connected to People Systems.
  • Familiarity with Greenhouse APIs, recruiting operations, or recruiting workflow automation.
  • Experience with scripting, CI/CD, observability, or automated testing.
  • Exposure to responsible AI governance or evaluation practices for enterprise workflows.

Technologies You'll Use

Workday HCM, Workday Extend and integrations, Greenhouse, Pigment, APIs and web services, integration platforms, Slack and employee collaboration tools, large language models, AI agents, MCP-based tools, and cloud or automation platforms.

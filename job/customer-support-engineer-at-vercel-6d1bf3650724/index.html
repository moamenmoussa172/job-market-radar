About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Australia.

About Vercel:

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience.

Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

About the Role:

As a Customer Support Engineer, you’ll resolve technical problems and questions for Vercel customers, and build the internal tooling and fixes that help them get the most from the platform. You’ll specialize in a product area and own it within the team, working alongside engineering. Success means fixing root causes and improving the customer experience through engineering. This role includes evening, weekend, and on-call work on a rota.

For candidates based in Sydney, the role requires a hybrid schedule, three days per week in our Sydney office.

What You Will Do:

  • Resolve technical cases from Vercel customers, including issues raised outside regular case channels
  • Build and improve internal tools alongside engineering, and complete engineering work regularly
  • Specialize in a product area and own it within the team
  • Give advice and assistance to the Vercel community
  • Support Customer Success Managers on Enterprise requests, and improve internal documentation and processes

About You:

  • Relevant frontend development experience (ideally 1+year)
  • Hands-on experience with cloud technologies and serverless computing
  • Engineered fixes for the root causes of technical problems, not workarounds
  • Available for a weekend and on-call rota
  • Experience working on a fully remote, globally distributed team

Bonus if You:

  • Have hands-on experience with React and Next.js
  • Have backend development experience, especially Node.js, or have worked with monorepos and custom CI/CD pipelines
  • Have set up CDNs, DNS, or cloud infrastructure, or handled incident management

Benefits & Perks:

  • Competitive compensation and stock options.
  • Flexible working style - 100% remote, with teammates located throughout the globe.
  • Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills.
  • Unlimited PTO - 4 weeks recommended per year. Take time when you need it.
  • Generous Gear Credit.

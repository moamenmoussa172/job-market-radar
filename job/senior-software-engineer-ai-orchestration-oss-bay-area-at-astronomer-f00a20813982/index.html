About the job

Apache Airflow is the standard for data orchestration. We're now making it the standard for data and AI orchestration. You'll join Astronomer's open-source engineering team to help bring agentic and AI workloads into Airflow, working closely with Airflow's project leadership. You don't need to know Airflow already; we'll teach you. What we need is someone who has built and run AI systems in production and wants to shape how the open-source ecosystem supports them.

This is a hybrid position that includes regular in-person collaboration.

What You Get To Do

  • Design and implement Airflow’s AI and agent orchestration features
  • Bring real-world knowledge of deploying and running agents to the project’s AI efforts
  • Help shape how agent developers can write and orchestrate their TypeScript workloads natively in Airflow
  • Iterate and improve on Airflow’s developer experience
  • Participate in the Airflow community (via Slack, mailing list and GitHub)
  • Work closely with team members to deliver high-quality results
  • Liaise with customers, troubleshoot issues and provide solutions
  • Contribute to the Airflow ecosystem and steward the open-source community
  • Promote new Airflow features at conferences and events

What You Bring To The Role

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience, with strong TypeScript skills
  • Hands-on experience deploying and operating AI agents or LLM-backed systems in production, including dealing with things like retries, cost, latency, evals, and failures nobody predicted
  • Deep familiarity with agent frameworks and LLM tooling (such as Pydantic AI, Anthropic SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK, MCP or similar), both building with them and using them day to day
  • Experience with Kubernetes & Docker
  • Experience in troubleshooting complex issues, including across layers you didn’t write
  • Experience building scalable distributed systems that recover from failure without redoing work
  • Clear, persuasive communication, both in public open-source forums and with internal product and engineering peers

Bonus points if you have:

  • Contributions to open-source projects
  • Experience running agents or LLM-backed systems on GPU infrastructure
  • Experience with Apache Airflow in production
  • Experience with Python or Go

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Build something people love

Wealthsimple is Canada’s leading financial innovator. The company offers a full suite of simple, sophisticated financial products across managed investing, do-it-yourself trading, cryptocurrency, tax filing, spending and saving. Wealthsimple currently serves more than 4 million Canadians and holds over $169 billion in assets under administration. The company was founded in 2014 by a team of financial experts and technology entrepreneurs, and is headquartered in Toronto, Canada.

About the Role

Our ideal hire has a builder mindset, is detail oriented, action oriented and proactive. They own their role and continuously help identify ways to improve operations processes and controls. It is essential that this person manages time well, keeps on top of deliverables, and has the potential to grow with us. Experience in credit products, prepaid cards or other spend operations functions is a strong plus.

We manage billions of dollars, so the stakes are high. We want to deliver extraordinary experiences that meet our client's on-demand expectations, while being a compliance leader. At Wealthsimple, we are maker-owners, we believe that simplicity is better, and we do what's right for each client.

We are seeking an analytical, detail-oriented professional with strong SQL skills, advanced Excel expertise, and a passion for process improvement to join our Treasury team. The ideal candidate will excel in problem-solving, dashboard creation, and effective communication across all levels of the organization.

In this role, you’ll have the opportunity to:

  • Develop comprehensive financial analytics and reports to support regulatory compliance and strategic decision-making.
  • Leverage system to extract, analyze, and report on treasury data, ensuring accuracy and timeliness of deliverables.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (including Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Finance) and use in house AI tools to optimize workflows and drive project success.
  • Design, build, and refine intuitive, data-driven dashboards that provide actionable insights for various stakeholders.
  • Maintain or develop multiple reporting deliverables with varying frequencies, managing stakeholder expectations and ensuring consistent quality standards.
  • Support the team on complex projects within Treasury Cash and Collateral Management, facilitating communication and problem-solving across teams.
  • Conduct in-depth analysis using SQL and other tools to inform critical business decisions related to Treasury operations and strategy.
  • Identify and implement process improvements that enhance efficiency and accuracy in financial reporting and analysis.
  • Translate business requirements into technical reporting solutions, working closely with IT and data teams to optimize report performance.

What You'll Bring:

  • Relevant experience: Demonstrated success in Treasury and financial operations, banking and payments, or other operations roles in related fields.
  • Report Management: Proven experience overseeing end-to-end report lifecycle management, from requirements gathering to delivery. Ability to manage multiple reporting deliverables with varying frequencies and stakeholder groups, ensuring accuracy and timeliness.
  • Treasury Systems Expertise: Work with Treasury Management Systems, including report configuration, data extraction, and workflow optimization.
  • Organization & planning: Plans, organizes, and schedules in a productive manner, allowing for a focus on key priorities and regular updates
  • Collaboration: Excellent interpersonal skills and willingness to work in collaboration with various team members
  • Strong written and verbal communication: Speaks plainly and truthfully. Communicates complex ideas in a simple & easy-to-understand way for any audience (e.g. engineering, 3rd party vendors, growth/operational teams, executive team)
  • Problem solving: If something seems off, you want to figure out what's going on; you enjoy navigating uncertainty, bringing clarity to ambiguity and don't wait for a playbook to be written
  • Proactive & Action Oriented: Acts without being told what to do; taking full ownership of work, and proactively seeks help and input when needed to provide well supported solutions
  • Efficiency: Ability to produce significant output with minimum wasted effort; finds the path of least resistance to the problem definition, solution and execution
  • Honesty/integrity: Doesn't cut corners; does what is right, not what is politically expedient
  • Data & analytics: Has a strong proficiency in SQL, and a history of using data to answer complex problems. Ability to translate business requirements into technical reporting solutions.
  • Stakeholder Management: Experience with complex reporting processes and stakeholder requirements, with proven ability to deliver financial reports to senior leadership and various business units.
  • Financial modeling, coding/automation, ability to contribute to a data model, and experience with business intelligence tools (Power BI, Superset) is preferred

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, or related field
  • 3+ years of experience in financial analysis or similar role
  • 2+ years of hands-on experience with Kyriba Treasury Management System
  • Advanced proficiency in SQL and Excel
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent verbal and written communication abilities
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities effectively

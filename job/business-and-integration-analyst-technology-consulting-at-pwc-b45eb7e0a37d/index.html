Job description & summary

As a Business & Integration Analyst in Technology Consulting, you will lead business and integration analysis activities across digital transformation and system implementation projects. Acting as a bridge between business and technology teams, you will help clients translate strategic objectives into practical, scalable solutions.

  • Facilitate workshops and stakeholder interviews to gather, assess, challenge, and document business requirements.
  • Analyse business processes and identify opportunities for improvement through technology-enabled transformation.
  • Translate business needs into use cases, process flows, data models, interface definitions, and integration specifications.
  • Collaborate with solution architects, developers, and technology leads to design and deliver integrated solutions.
  • Support system implementation projects from analysis and design through testing and deployment.
  • Ensure alignment between business objectives and technical solution design.
  • Coordinate requirements across multiple stakeholders and workstreams.
  • Support testing, validation, and change management activities throughout the delivery lifecycle.
  • Create high-quality project documentation, including business requirements, process maps, and interface specifications.
  • Contribute to the successful delivery of large-scale transformation programmes within the Financial Services sector.

What matters to us

  • 3+ years of experience in business analysis, integration analysis, or system implementation projects.
  • Understanding of integration architectures, APIs, and data exchange formats such as REST, SOAP, JSON, and XML.
  • Experience with enterprise systems (ERP, CRM, core banking solutions, or similar) and related integration scenarios.
  • Experience working on projects within banking, financial services, or fintech environments.
  • Fluent Czech and English language skills (C1 level).

What will help you stand out

  • Proven ability to create detailed business and technical documentation, including requirements, interface specifications, and process flows.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Experience working in Agile, Waterfall, or hybrid delivery environments.
  • Knowledge of digital transformation and system integration best practices.

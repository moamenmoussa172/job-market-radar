About the job

Big systems. Exciting challenges. Real impact. As a Staff Software Engineer, you’ll be the technical lead for your team and a key voice across the Dmart domain. You’ll own the architecture behind the decision platforms that run assortment, pricing, and availability, from low-latency Golang services to the data flows behind them. You’ll align teams around coherent technical strategies and raise the engineering bar for everyone around you.

Across a DMart network of 800+ stores and 1M+ SKUs, your mission is to set the technical direction for the intelligence layer behind our operations, across Category Management, Store Management and Network Management. As Staff Engineer for our DMart teams, you'll shape the architecture of the decision platforms that automate assortment, pricing, store operations and inventory flow. You'll also build the shared data and platform foundations they all depend on. You'll stay hands-on with the hardest problems, raise the engineering bar across teams, and drive measurable impact across all our global markets.

Be part of redefining how customers experience quick commerce. You’ll help build technology that scales our non-food offerings, reaching new market segments and driving revenue growth. By innovating within our Quick Commerce Team, you’ll make Delivery Hero the go-to platform for a broad range of products, helping us grow faster and deliver more value to customers around the world.

  • Own multiple complex solutions in our Dmart decision platforms (primarily Go), with domain-level impact, and act as technical lead for your team.
  • Build and improve real-time services for assortment, pricing, and availability: scalable low-latency APIs and event-driven data flows across many stores and markets. Stay hands-on, splitting your time between coding, design, and architecture where it has the most impact.
  • Own architectural decisions across the domain and tribe, weighing business context and the financial implications of each choice. Lead design reviews, write and review RFCs, and build consensus by laying out pros and cons.
  • Break ambiguous initiatives into simple milestones that deliver impact early and often, and align teams on how to deliver them. Partner with product managers, data scientists, and operations stakeholders in Category Management and Store Management.
  • Set the standard for engineering excellence in the domain (observability, scalability, cost efficiency, code quality, testing). Drive resolution during incidents, review post-mortems, and foster a blameless culture.
  • Mentor engineers across your team and the domain, take an active role in hiring, and keep the engineering community up to date on new technologies and practices, including agent-assisted engineering.
  • Extensive backend engineering experience with deep expertise in Go and database systems, including owning high-throughput, low-latency production services and stateful APIs (gRPC/REST).
  • Experience designing event-driven architectures (e.g. Kafka, SNS/SQS) and infrastructure as code such as Terraform.
  • Experience operating critical services in production on AWS and/or GCP and Kubernetes, with observability, alerting, and on-call processes in place, and experience leading incident response.
  • A proven track record of leading architecture across multiple components or teams, and of leading design and delivery in highly ambiguous problem spaces with long-term impact.
  • Excellent written and verbal English, and the ability to explain trade-offs clearly to engineers, product, and business stakeholders, build consensus, and stay open to changing your mind.
  • Experience mentoring several engineers, taking part in hiring, and educating engineers on new technologies and practices, including where they help and where they fall short.

Nice to have

  • Experience with data-intensive or decision platforms, such as pricing, assortment, inventory, or supply chain systems.
  • Experience in quick commerce, e-commerce, or retail environments.
  • Familiarity with A/B testing and experimentation platforms.

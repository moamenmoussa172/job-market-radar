About the job

Job Description:

About This Talent Pool

We are expanding our AI Technologies engineering teams within Connected Intelligence and will be opening multiple official positions. By applying to this talent pool, your profile will be reviewed directly by our Talent Acquisition Partners and potentially matched to the upcoming roles that best align with your technical strengths.

Your location

At the Airbus site in Immenstaad , you work right where the future of Connected Intelligence, Space, and Air Power takes shape. In an environment that offers a broad perspective, you will drive technological innovations across multiple domains.

Our site thrives on dialogue: On campus, you will encounter a unique network of experts in satellite technology, military aviation, cyber security, Artificial Intelligence, as well as the Combat Cloud and Ground Support of tomorrow. Our collaboration is equally dynamic—working in agile teams, we tackle the most complex challenges, ranging from AI-driven Earth observation to secure cloud architecture. Those seeking technological excellence and interdisciplinary collaboration will find the ideal environment here.

Our Benefits for You

  • Fair Compensation & Extras: Attractive remuneration and individual additional benefits, such as company pension schemes, mobility offers (e.g., bike leasing), or corporate discounts with partner companies in accordance with our current guidelines.
  • Time for You (and Your Loved Ones): 30 days of annual leave based on our collective agreement (35-hour week); work-life balance through flexible working hours (flextime), mobile working, part-time options, job sharing, and sabbatical opportunities.
  • Growth Made Easy: Excellent professional development opportunities and international, group-wide career perspectives.
  • Feel Good at Work: On-site company doctor; comprehensive health programs (sports courses, preventive care, etc.), canteen and cafeteria, and local childcare facility
  • Diversity Connects: Work in a diverse environment with more than 140 nationalities, where exchange, mutual support, and inclusion are part of our DNA.

Mission

Within the AI Technologies domain at Connected Intelligence, we bridge frontier AI research, production software engineering, and resilient compute infrastructure. As an AI Software Engineer (m/f/d) or Platform Engineer (m/f/d), you will help turn prototype AI concepts into production-ready, scalable, and dependable workflows for next-generation defence and aerospace systems.

Depending on your specialization track, you will focus on production AI application engineering, frontier agentic and autonomous systems, or cloud/on-premises platform engineering—always built with rigorous monitoring, zero-trust security, and human oversight.

Key Responsibilities

(Your day-to-day scope will be tailored to your core specialization track below)

  • AI & Software Application Development: Translate product requirements into production software features and AI-assisted workflows. Build microservices, APIs, and data pipelines for batch and streaming analytics, implementing prompting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), tool use, LLM alignment/fine-tuning, and agentic orchestration.
  • Frontier AI, Agentic Research & Robotics: Design next-generation agentic AI capabilities, including memory compression, context propagation, and autonomous feedback loops for hypothesis testing. Architect near-real-time systems, Edge AI pipelines, hardware-software co-design, and decentralized/swarm robotics for collaborative sensing and decision-making.
  • Platform Engineering, Distributed Compute & DevSecOps: Provision, operate, and maintain resilient compute, networking, storage, and container platforms across Linux, Kubernetes, and hybrid/on-premises environments using Infrastructure as Code (IaC). Enforce identity and secrets management, network segmentation, and automated DevSecOps CI/CD pipelines.
  • Verification, Security & Human Oversight: Design comprehensive test suites for software behavior and AI output quality—covering edge, adverse, and failure cases—while applying privacy, access controls, and human-in-the-loop governance to AI-enabled actions.
  • Performance Optimization & Cross-Functional Delivery: Monitor latency, capacity, reliability, and operational costs to continuously refine solutions based on operational evidence. Document engineering standards and collaborate closely with system architects, AI specialists, and domain stakeholders.

Your Boarding Pass (Requirements)

Core Foundation

  • Completed university degree (Bachelor's, Master's, or equivalent) in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, or a related technical discipline.
  • Solid foundation in software engineering principles, modern application architecture, clean code standards, and technical documentation.
  • Hands-on programming experience in Python, C/C++, or Rust (particularly for high-performance, systems-level, or embedded development).

Domain Specialization (Demonstrated experience in at least one of the following tracks)

  • Track 1 – AI Software & Workflow Engineering: Production experience with LLMs, RAG, prompting, tool use, fine-tuning, agentic orchestration, API/microservice development, distributed data processing, and AI output validation.
  • Track 2 – Frontier AI, Mathematics & Swarm Robotics: Strong background in mathematics and control theory; experience with agentic AI research, Edge AI, near-real-time systems, decentralized/swarm robotics, or emerging paradigms (such as quantum or neuromorphic computing).
  • Track 3 – Platform Engineering, Cloud & DevSecOps: Proficiency in Linux, Kubernetes, cluster computing, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), identity/access management, and automated CI/CD pipelines (Nice to have: FPGA development using VHDL, Verilog, or HLS).

Mindset & Languages

  • Structured, rigorous approach to rapid prototyping, system verification, and failure-mode testing within complex, multi-stakeholder engineering environments.
  • Strong communication skills to articulate technical solutions clearly across cross-functional engineering and business teams.
  • Languages: Professional working proficiency in English; German language skills are a strong advantage.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

The AI orchestration of your wildest imagination.

n8n is the open workflow orchestration platform built for the new era of AI. We give technical teams the freedom of code with the speed of no-code, so they can automate faster, smarter, and without limits. Backed by a fiercely inventive community and 500+ builder-approved integrations, we’re changing the way people bring systems together and scale ideas for impact.

Since our founding in 2019, we’ve grown into a diverse team of over 260 - working across Europe and the US, connected by a shared builder spirit and with our centre of gravity in Berlin. Along the way, we’ve:

  • Cultivated a community of more than 650,000 active developers and builders
  • Earned 190K+ GitHub stars, making us one of the world’s Top 40 most popular projects
  • Backed by top investors, from Sequoia’s first German seed to our SAP's recent strategic investment - bringing us to a $5.2bn valuation

That’s the company we’ve built. Now we’d love to see what you can build. If you’re applying, try n8n out - whether you’re technical or not - and share a screenshot of your first workflow with us. The easiest place to start is here: app.n8n.cloud/register.

We’re in a defining moment of an incredible journey. Come and build with us.

🎯 Your main goal will be to build, lead, and scale a high-performing Solutions Engineering team that accelerates enterprise growth, delivers exceptional technical sales experiences, and helps customers unlock the full potential of n8n's AI-powered automation platform.

As we rapidly expand across Americas, you'll play a key role in developing our Solutions Engineering function and enabling your team to succeed. Here's how you'll make an impact:

Lead and develop a high-performing Solutions Engineering team

  • Manage, coach, and develop a regional team of Solutions Engineers, fostering a culture of ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
  • Support team growth through hiring, onboarding, mentoring, and career development, ensuring your team is equipped to succeed as we scale.
  • Build effective team structures, processes, and ways of working to support regional expansion and evolving business needs.

Drive technical excellence throughout the sales cycle

  • Guide your team through complex enterprise sales cycles, from initial discovery and technical validation to negotiations and deal closure.
  • Coach Solutions Engineers on delivering impactful product demonstrations, proofs of concept, and technical solutions that address customer challenges.
  • Provide strategic and hands-on technical guidance when needed, helping your team navigate complex opportunities and accelerate new business and account expansion.

Partner across teams to accelerate revenue growth

  • Collaborate closely with Sales and Sales leadership to align technical strategies with commercial priorities and support successful enterprise deals.
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Product Marketing to share customer insights, resolve technical challenges, and strengthen sales enablement.
  • Coordinate with post-sales teams and Forward-Deployed Engineers to ensure smooth customer handoffs and support technical validation for expansion opportunities.

REQUIREMENTS

Must-haves

  • 👥 Experienced pre-sales leader: You have at least 2 years of experience managing and developing pre-sales or Solutions Engineering teams, ideally within a high-growth B2B software environment.
  • 🚀 Proven people manager: You have a track record of coaching, mentoring, and developing technical talent while building high-performing teams and supporting their growth.
  • 🤝 Enterprise sales expertise: You have strong experience navigating complex technical sales cycles, including discovery, demonstrations, proofs of concept, technical validation, and negotiations.
  • 💻 Strong technical foundation: You have the technical depth to understand complex software solutions, advise your team, and engage confidently with technical stakeholders.
  • 🔗 Relevant industry experience: You've worked with technical software products in areas such as data platforms, databases, data integration, or data flows, and understand the challenges faced by technical buyers.
  • 🌍 Regional location: You're based in the UK, Ireland, or Germany to support our growing EMEA Solutions Engineering organisation.

Nice-to-haves

  • 🤖 AI-first mindset: You're excited about the potential of AI-driven automation and have experience working with AI technologies or within an AI-focused company.
  • 👨‍💻 Coding experience: You have hands-on experience with programming or scripting, enabling you to better understand technical integrations and automation workflows.
  • ⚡ Technical software vendor experience: You've worked at a technical software vendor, ideally in a fast-growing SaaS environment, and understand how to scale pre-sales teams alongside a rapidly expanding sales organisation.

Why join us?

At n8n, you'll have the opportunity to shape and scale a critical function at one of Europe's fastest-growing startups. You'll build and develop a regional Solutions Engineering team, influence how we win complex enterprise opportunities, and help bring the power of AI-driven automation to businesses worldwide.

You'll have real ownership over how your team grows, develops, and operates, with the visibility and cross-functional influence to make a meaningful impact on our commercial success. You'll join a collaborative, remote-first company that values autonomy, experimentation, and continuous learning, where your leadership will directly contribute to our next stage of growth.

Sound like a challenge you're excited to take on?

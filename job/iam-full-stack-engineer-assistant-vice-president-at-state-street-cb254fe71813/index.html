About the job

State Street’s Identity and Access Management division is seeking a senior developer with creativity; desire to lead design, development and support of business critical and complex enterprise-level security platform.

Job Responsibilities

o Work with business team/Development Lead to understand and analyze new requirements.

o Drive architectural and design discussions towards solution perfection.

o Hands on architecture, analysis, design, review and execution of code.

o Assist in QA and testing of existing and/or new features.

o Participate in release, build and deployment activities.

o Write and/or review software and system documentation.

o Provide subject matter technical domain expertise in addressing complex production issues that encompass a wide range of internal and external systems, components, and processes.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or engineering.
  • At least 7+ years of hands-on development experience in 3-tier or service oriented application architecture.
  • Exhibit integrity and ability to follow through on commitments and deadlines.
  • Ability to work with team member manage delivery and provide feedback.
  • Excellent analytical and interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent verbal, written communication and presentation skills.
  • Team player, Ethical, Self-driven and self-motivated, goal and results oriented.

Technical Skills

o At least 7 year Programming experience with Java/J2EE.

o At least 2 year Programming experience with Oracle.

o Experience with UNIX/Windows Shell/batch scripting.

o Experience with GitHub, Clearcase source code repository.

  • Experience with Apache Open source.
  • Experience in Hibernate, Web Services and Cloud architecture.

o Experience in evaluating software estimating cost and delivery timelines.

Additional Preferred Experience

  • Vendor software experience such as ServiceNow, Azure/AWS Cloud.

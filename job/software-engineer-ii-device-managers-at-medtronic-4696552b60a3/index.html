A Day in The Life Of:

As a Software Engineer II, you will contribute to the design, development, verification, and maintenance of software for medical device products. Working within cross-functional engineering teams, you will help deliver high-quality software solutions while building technical expertise and supporting continuous improvement initiatives throughout the product development lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design, develop, and verify software for medical device products in compliance with applicable software development standards.
  • Collaborate with product engineers, software engineers, software test engineers, and technology architects to deliver high-quality software solutions.
  • Participate in software design, implementation, debugging, and verification activities throughout the product development lifecycle.
  • Contribute to project planning, effort estimation, and execution of assigned software development activities.
  • Conduct technical reviews of requirements, specifications, designs, source code, and other engineering deliverables.
  • Apply software engineering best practices, coding standards, and development processes to deliver reliable and maintainable software.
  • Develop reusable software components and solutions that improve team productivity and consistency.
  • Partner with internal and external stakeholders to support the delivery of compliant, high-quality software products.
  • Utilise AI-assisted tools to accelerate implementation, code review, and debugging; holding AI-generated contributions to the same quality and safety bar as hand-authored code.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree (Level 8) in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline and a minimum of 2 years of relevant software engineering experience; or an advanced degree with relevant technical experience.
  • Experience with software design, development, debugging, and testing within a structured software development lifecycle.
  • Experience working in Agile development environments and familiarity with Test-Driven Development (TDD) principles.
  • Proficiency in one or more of the following technologies: XML, C++, Java, JavaScript, HTML, C#, .NET, or Python.
  • Experience participating in software design reviews, code reviews, or software verification activities.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience developing software within regulated environments, including medical device development.
  • Knowledge of software development standards and regulatory expectations applicable to medical device software.
  • Experience working within cross-functional product development teams.
  • Experience developing reusable software frameworks, components, or design patterns.
  • Demonstrated ability to learn and apply new technologies to solve technical and business challenges.

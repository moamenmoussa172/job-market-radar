About the job

The Group Senior Infrastructure Engineer is a senior technical lead who owns the design, automation, security, and operational health of infrastructure across Ports and Terminals products hosted on Microsoft Azure, on-premises, and hybrid environments. Working in a hybrid Engineering and Operations model, the engineer builds platforms and remains accountable for their availability and continuous improvement in production. They partner with Solutions Architect, Development, Database, Security, and DevOps teams to keep platforms scalable, resilient, secure, cost-efficient, and aligned with enterprise architecture. The engineer drives automation and standardization across the estate using tools such as Terraform, Ansible, PowerShell, and Python, establishes golden images and reference architectures, and systematically reduces manual work.

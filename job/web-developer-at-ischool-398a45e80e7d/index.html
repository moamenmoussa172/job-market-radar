About the job

Overview

As a Front-End Web Developer, you’ll join our academic services team helping to deliver a suite of proprietary educational services. Keypath’s core expertise lies in online education where we partner with universities to deliver a suite of student and academic lifecycle services, including; research, marketing, student recruitment, course development, retention and career services. You will be part of a global team creating exceptional performance, cross-platform code & smart, beautiful and frictionless user journeys.

What you’ll be doing as a Front End Web Developer

  • Work in a creative team environment to develop responsive html/CSS/JavaScript (jQuery) code to form page templates, prototypes and production code, that is performant and accessible across all common browsers
  • Provide diligent user testing and ensure your work delivers an intuitive and memorable user experience
  • Advise on user interface best practices to meet performance and client requirements
  • Develop images for courseware through image creation/manipulation software packages
  • Develop animations and interactive elements to enhance student engagement within the online environment
  • Contribute to continuous improvement

Education: Bachelor’s degree

Experience: 1+ years' experience in the following:

  • Front-End Web Development experience
  • Excellent working knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite with expertise in Photoshop and Illustrator in particular
  • Proficient in client-side technologies including (X)HTML5, AJAX, CSS3, JavaScript (jQuery, Vue.js)
  • Experience in working collaboratively with wireframes and technical specifications for interactive websites
  • A preference for working in a creative team environment, complimented by an ability to self-manage for deadlines
  • Strong HTML skills, and an ability to create elegant, semantically clean mark-up
  • Strong understanding of CSS, its cross-browser quirks and a keen eye for details
  • Demonstrated experience in managing maintainable, scalable, cross-browser code that is a delight to experience on any device
  • Experience working with learning management systems such as Blackboard, Moodle, Canvas and others is strongly preferred
  • Ability to work with multiple deadlines and multiple projects simultaneously in a fast-paced, changing environment
  • Ability to adapt and to solve problems proactively and creatively
  • Experience with databases and PHP (a plus)

Skills and Competencies:

  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Excellent attention to detail
  • Proofreading skills
  • A critical thinker
  • Curious and confident to ask questions and learn quickly
  • Ability to manage conflicting priorities and multiple tasks
  • Team player
  • Always demonstrate the Keypath values – Commitment, Collaboration, Innovation and Lifelong Learning

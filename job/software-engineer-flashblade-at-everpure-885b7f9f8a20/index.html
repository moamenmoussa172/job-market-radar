About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE
Join our Everpure FlashBlade engineering team in Prague to build backend services that power our high-performance, distributed storage platform. Your mission is to take ownership of production systems that drive reliability, scalability, and seamless orchestration for global customers. You will solve complex distributed-systems challenges and collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams across Prague and the US. This role empowers you to shape core architecture and directly impact the Everpure Platform's developer experience in a fast-paced environment.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Design and architect scalable backend services and platform components to enhance orchestration and resiliency across the Everpure Platform.
  • Develop and maintain distributed systems capable of operating flawlessly under high concurrency, scale, and failure scenarios to maximize customer reliability.
  • Create clean, stable APIs and evolve service interfaces that provide a seamless developer experience and ensure long-term system maintainability.
  • Partner with engineering and product teams across global offices to drive infrastructure projects from initial design through production support and quality improvement.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Proven expertise in software engineering fundamentals, specifically designing, building, and deploying backend or system-level components in production environments.
  • Proficiency in Python, Go, or another object-oriented programming language, accompanied by a strong willingness to master Go for primary development.
  • Deep understanding of distributed systems, concurrency, and scalable service design to independently evaluate and resolve infrastructure challenges of diverse scope.
  • Strong debugging, API design, and problem-solving skills paired with clear communication abilities to collaborate effectively across international teams.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Prague office in compliance with Everpure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT FROM US:

  • Innovation: We celebrate those who think critically, like a challenge, and aspire to be trailblazers.

  • Team: We build each other up and set aside ego for the greater good.

And because we understand the value of bringing your full and best self to work, we offer a variety of perks to manage a healthy balance, including flexible time off, wellness resources, and company-sponsored team events. Check out http://benefits.everpuredata.com/ for more information.

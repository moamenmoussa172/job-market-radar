About the job

As a member of NXP’s Product Security Incident Response Team, you will lead the operational response to reported vulnerabilities affecting NXP hardware and software products. You will own and drive security cases from initial intake through assessment, remediation coordination, customer communication, and responsible disclosure.

This position focuses on program leadership, case management, stakeholder coordination, communication, and continuous improvement. You will bring together engineering, product management, quality, legal, communications, customer-facing teams, and external researchers to ensure that security issues progress toward a timely resolution.

What you'll do: A snapshot of your key responsibilities and impact.

  • Own the operational lifecycle of product vulnerability cases from intake through closure and disclosure.
  • Establish the response plan, milestones, ownership, dependencies, escalation path, and communication cadence for each assigned case.
  • Coordinate impact assessments and remediation planning across engineering, product management, quality, legal, communications, and customer-facing organizations.
  • Track decisions, evidence, approvals, outstanding actions, and delivery risks.
  • Facilitate vulnerability review meetings and drive actions to completion.
  • Coordinate responsible and coordinated vulnerability disclosure with researchers, customers, partners, and relevant external organizations.
  • Manage CVE reservation and publication workflows in coordination with technical subject-matter experts.
  • Coordinate the creation, review, approval, and publication of security advisories and customer communications.
  • Improve PSIRT procedures, templates, governance, and audit readiness.
  • Support readiness for upcoming regulations such as the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), future standards and vulnerability-reporting obligations.

What you'll bring:

  • Experience in Product Security, Embedded Security, Semiconductor Security, Cybersecurity, vulnerability management or technical engineering programs.
  • 3 + years of relevant work experience
  • Strong understanding of vulnerability management, coordinated disclosure, incident handling, or product risk management.
  • Ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders across engineering, product management, quality, and customer-facing teams.
  • Demonstrated ability to coordinate complex investigations involving multiple technical and business organizations.
  • Strong project or program management skills, including dependency management, risk tracking, escalation, prioritization, and reporting.
  • Excellent communication skills and a passion for protecting innovative technology at scale.
  • Familiarity with embedded products, semiconductor products, firmware, automotive, industrial, or IoT environments is advantageous.
  • PMP, PgMP, Scrum Master, SAFe, or other project/program management certifications are preferred, but not required.
  • Fluent in English

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.

About the job

Do you want to help Media, Entertainment and Games companies reinvent how they create content, reach audiences and build new experiences with cloud and AI? AWS is looking for an Account Manager to join our Media, Entertainment and Games team in Central and Eastern Europe. You will manage a portfolio of customers and help them use AWS to innovate faster, scale globally and turn AI into real business results.
Location: The role is posted in Athens, Greece. Our preferred long-term location is Cyprus. Ideally, the successful candidate will be based in Cyprus over time.
You will work with C-level executives, technology leaders and developers, working backwards from their business goals to shape solutions that deliver measurable impact. You will work closely with Solutions Architects, AI specialists, AWS Partners and Professional Services to deliver for your customers. On a typical day you might run a business review with a CTO, build an account plan, help a games company launch a generative AI pilot, or bring together specialists and partners to scale a streaming platform.
AWS Global Sales drives adoption of the AWS cloud worldwide, enabling customers of all sizes to innovate and expand in the cloud. Our team empowers every customer to grow by providing tailored service, unmatched technology, and consistent support. We dive deep to understand each customer's unique challenges, then craft innovative solutions that accelerate their success. This customer-first approach is how we built the world's most adopted cloud. Join us and help us grow.
Key job responsibilities
- Manage and grow a portfolio of Media, Entertainment and Games customers across Central and Eastern Europe as their trusted advisor
- Build strong relationships with C-level executives, IT leaders and developers
- Create and execute account plans with clear goals for revenue growth and service adoption
- Find, qualify and close new opportunities while keeping a healthy pipeline and an accurate forecast
- Help customers adopt AI, including generative and agentic AI, to create new products, improve audience engagement and run more efficiently
- Build value propositions for content creation, streaming, game development and data and analytics workloads
- Work with Solutions Architects, specialists, Professional Services and AWS Partners to deliver customer outcomes
- Support go-to-market activities with AWS Partners and AWS Marketplace
- Ensure customer success from first workload through migration and modernization

SENIOR IT PRODUCT OWNER - SAP MDG Platform

About the job

The position acts as the IT/Product Owner for the SAP MDG (Master Data Governance) platform within
Bosch Home Confort.
In this role, you drive the global strategy, implementation, and scalable operation of our
central master data governance solution.
Your mission is to establish a "Single Source of Truth" across global and regional systems by governing critical master data domains.
Operating at the intersection of process harmonization, IT architecture, and corporate data strategy, you ensure high-quality master data replication, support ERP rollout waves and collaborate closely with
integration and analytics teams to enable robust downstream business processes.
Core responsibilities

  • Define and communicate the global product vision and strategic roadmap for the SAP MDG platform in alignment with the overarching Data Management and Analytics strategy.
  • Manage and prioritize the product backlog in an agile environment, translating complex business rules and data requirements into actionable user stories.
  • Establish global master data standards, rulebooks, and validation workflows.
  • Define and monitor key data quality KPIs, driving continuous optimization of duplicate checks, address validation, and automated master data maintenance workflows.
  • Partner with integration architects, ERP template owners, and downstream platform leads (e.g. Databricks) to design and secure robust master data replication patterns.
  • Ensure stable, secure, and compliant platform operations, including SLA monitoring and release/upgrade management for SAP MDG.
  • Manage external implementation partners and software vendors, steering development teams and coordinating resources for project deliveries and support.
  • Professional experience as an IT Product Owner, Solution Architect, or Lead Consultant in master data management (MDM) with a clear focus on SAP MDG.
  • Proven track record of managing technical engineering teams in agile frameworks (Scrum/Kanban) within international environments.
  • Deep architectural knowledge of the SAP MDG platform (including MDG-M, MDG-BP/C/S, custom objects).
  • Hands-on expertise in data modeling, UI modeling, workflow design, and the SAP Data Replication Framework (DRF).
  • Solid understanding of master data processes within core SAP modules (SD/MM) and S/4HANA environments.
  • Outstanding consulting and negotiation skills, acting as a credible translator between central platform engineering, data stewards, and business stakeholders.
  • Strong analytical mindset with a passion for process standardization.
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken).

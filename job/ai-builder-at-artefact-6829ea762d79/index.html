About the job

Mission

As an AI Builder Manager, you steer AI Adoption missions and programs that take AI from experimentation to the transformation of business units. Working directly with executive leaders and business unit heads, you define initiatives for upskilling, agent building, and change management. You are the engine of our missions: you ensure their quality, impact, and profitability, help your teams grow, and develop your accounts alongside Directors and Partners.

You start from the challenges of business users, their processes, and their pain points to transform AI possibilities into tangible results. You remain a maker: you do not stop at the slide. You design engaging learning experiences, challenge and build functional AI solutions (no-code, low-code, vibe coding), and mobilize teams to take these solutions from prototype to sustainable use.

You support organizations in transforming tasks, roles, responsibilities, and skills in the AI era. You articulate field initiatives (bottom-up) with large-scale transformation programs (top-down), in collaboration with our AI Strategy and Engineering teams. Your ambition: make AI a driver for transforming work, with a concrete, measurable, and sustainable impact on teams and their performance.

Activities & Responsibilities

I. Analysis & scope definition

  • Lead the scoping with L&D, HR, sponsors, and business units: define target audiences, priorities, observable objectives, and differentiated learning paths.
  • Design a multi-pronged strategy combining sessions, digital content, coaching, champions, and handover to internal teams.
  • Steer a portfolio of operational use cases: value, feasibility, risk, build capacity, and sprint sequencing.
  • Organize discovery workshops and have dependencies, access, or governance matters arbitrated by authorized stakeholders.

II. Solution development & deployment

  • Oversee budget, planning, staffing, pedagogical quality, and satisfaction; make the necessary trade-offs and grow the teams.
  • Supervise pedagogical assets, facilitate high-visibility sessions, and ensure alignment between exercises, platform capabilities, and business needs.
  • Supervise low-code/no-code solutions, standards, game tests, documentation, and quality gates adapted to the technical scope and practice standards.
  • Design with Analytics Engineers the program reporting architecture, define visualization and KPI standards, coach teams on data storytelling, supervise their dashboards, and drive their adoption.

III. Adoption & community facilitation

  • Coordinate user adoption, training, and client handover: managerial relays, communities, rituals, and office hours.
  • Steer feedback loops, value metrics, and, if assigned, the change management plan on a targeted project.

IV. Structuring & steering the change approach

  • Define competency, autonomy, and usage indicators; present results to sponsors and adjust the approach.
  • Contribute to pre-sales, estimates, costings, and proposals, identifying relevant extension opportunities with clients.
  • Assess the need for co-delivery with Solutions Engineering or IT, without encroaching on their technical authority.

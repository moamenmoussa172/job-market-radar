About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France, Poland, Switzerland, United Kingdom and Europe.

About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

  • ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
  • ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
  • ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

About the role

We are looking for a Full-Stack Engineer to make building with ElevenLabs easier, from a developer's first API key to a successful production integration.

Developer Growth owns the surfaces developers and their agents use: our SDKs, API, documentation and ElevenAPI dashboard. This role focuses on the dashboard and product flows, helping self-serve and enterprise developers get started, understand their usage and get more value from ElevenLabs.

You will join a small, remote team and own features across frontend and backend. You will use direct user feedback and experiments to decide what to build, combining strong engineering with a careful eye for developer experience.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Improving the ElevenAPI dashboard so developers can manage integrations and understand how their applications and agents use ElevenLabs.
  • Streamlining onboarding, helping developers create API keys, get started with agents and reach their first successful integration.
  • Designing and shipping experiments to improve activation, API key creation and self-serve subscriptions, then using the results to guide further changes.
  • Owning features from discovery and design through frontend, backend, deployment and iteration, working closely with the team and learning from users.

There is scope to grow into technical and team leadership over time, depending on your experience and impact.

Requirements

  • Strong full-stack engineering skills, with particular strength in frontend development and confidence building backend services and APIs.
  • Strong product judgement and attention to UX, with evidence of making complex workflows intuitive for developers.
  • Experience using user feedback, product data and experimentation to prioritise work and improve measurable outcomes.
  • Independent, end-to-end ownership, clear communication and genuine enthusiasm for developer tools and developer experience.
  • Experience managing or mentoring a small team of software engineers

Prior generative audio experience is not required. Experience building developer-facing products, API dashboards or onboarding flows is particularly valuable. Previous technical or team leadership experience is a plus.

Location:

This role is remote and can be executed globally. If you prefer, you can work from our offices in London, New York, San Francisco, and Warsaw.

Note: All candidates should have their tax situation certificate (Constancia de Siituación Fiscal) up to date with the salary regime to be eligible for the hiring process.

Main Responsabilities

Senior Software Engineer provides technical leadership in the design, development, and support of software products that enable and sustain John Deere manufacturing operations. This role is responsible for development, debugging and resolving complex, cross system issues, ensuring high availability and reliability of factory critical applications. The engineer collaborates closely with product owners, UX, and cross functional teams, contributing hands on code while guiding technical decisions and best practices. Working within an Agile framework, this position supports continuous improvement, mentors other engineers, and ensures solutions deliver measurable value to the factories while meeting quality, security, and performance expectations.

What Skills You Need

  • 5+ years of experience in Software Development enterprise grade, production systems (Java, .Net, etc)
  • 4+ years of experience with JavaScript / TypeScript for backend and frontend development
  • 4+ years of experience working with relational databases such as SQL Server, PostgreSQL, or equivalent
  • 4+ years of experience designing, developing, and maintaining RESTful APIs 3+ years of hands on experience with Docker for application containerization
  • 4+ years of experience supporting mission critical production systems, including on call rotations.
  • 3+ years of hands-on experience with AI agents and agentic development patterns, including AI-assisted software engineering, tool integration, orchestration, evaluation, and deployment of production-grade AI solutions that improve developer and end-user productivity.

What Makes You Stand Out

Demonstrated ability to provide technical leadership, guide design decisions, and influence architecture

Experience mentoring engineers and promoting best practices in code quality, security, and reliability

Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)

Knowledge of observability tools (logging, monitoring, tracing)

Education

BS in Computer Systems Engineering or equivalent experience

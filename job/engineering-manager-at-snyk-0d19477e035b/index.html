Engineering Manager

We’re looking for an experienced, hands-on engineering manager to lead the Snyk API & Web engineering organisation, a group of several technical teams building key technology capabilities for our product which helps enterprise and non-enterprise customers stay secure.

The role will be focused on solving challenges including:

  • How we keep scaling the product as we onboard larger, more demanding enterprise customers, without compromising on reliability or the customer experience.
  • Delivering with both velocity and quality: building a robust system, automating as much as we can, and steadily paying down technical debt.
  • Keeping support and day-to-day operations sustainable, in close partnership with the product team.

You’ll spend your time:

  • setting priorities and direction with the product team, and turning them into plans the teams can execute
  • managing engineers across several disciplines
  • providing regular feedback, coaching and mentoring, and building a strong engineering culture
  • guiding the technical direction through architecture and design reviews, and shaping how the teams build with AI coding agents, from tooling to review practices and quality guardrails
  • staying close to customers, including getting directly involved in complex support cases
  • owning delivery and operational health

You should apply if you:

  • have experience managing and mentoring engineers across several disciplines, ideally including team leads or managers
  • have a strong technical background and can challenge and empathise with architectural decisions
  • have built and run a SaaS platform for enterprise customers, and are comfortable owning its reliability
  • have hands-on experience with AI-assisted development and want to embed AI in how we build, test and operate
  • have a track record of delivering with both velocity and quality, and of cultivating a strong engineering culture in an iterative environment
  • are comfortable working directly with customers, support, sales and product teams
  • can manage multiple priorities and make clear trade-offs

We’d especially love to hear from you if you:

  • have worked on application security or other security testing products
  • have run a product where a data-heavy backend and a customer-facing web app ship together
  • are excited by Snyk’s mission and by applying AI to security testing

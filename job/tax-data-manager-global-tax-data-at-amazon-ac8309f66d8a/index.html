About the job

Are you ready to tackle complex tax challenges at a global scale? Amazon is looking for a Tax Data Manager to join our Global Tax Data Team where you will play a critical role in shaping tax strategy, ensuring compliance, and driving operational improvements. In this role, you will serve as a subject matter expert, providing data-driven insights and recommendations that influence business decisions. You will collaborate with cross-functional partners across finance, legal, and accounting to address tax risks and opportunities, while building scalable solutions that support Amazon's continued growth. If you are a self-starter who thrives on solving intricate problems and communicating complex concepts to varied audiences, we want to hear from you.
Key job responsibilities
• Reconcile large-scale datasets across tax calculation and reporting systems, ensuring accuracy and data integrity from source systems through transformation to final outputs
• Configure and maintain business rules for tax reporting across multiple jurisdictions, ensuring mathematical models align with tax engine logic and invoicing requirements
• Manage the complete lifecycle of data flows, identifying and resolving data gaps while driving reporting requirements and validating system output attributes
• Serve as a strategic business partner for new e-reporting initiatives, bridging communication between technical teams (engineering, data) and business stakeholders (tax, finance, operations) to influence interim solutions and drive scalable implementations
• Strong understanding of tax compliance principles, regulations, and reporting requirements across jurisdictions
• Demonstrated analytical capabilities to identify discrepancies, data quality issues, and validate data integrity across complex system environments
• Proven ability to partner with and influence business stakeholders on strategic programs, with strong communication skills to engage effectively with both technical and non-technical personnel
• Experience supporting change management for new system implementations
- Knowledge of Microsoft Office products and applications at an advanced level
- Knowledge of strong understanding of tax compliance principles, regulations, and reporting requirements across jurisdictions
- Experience in taxation in a corporate environment, public accounting firm or major law firm
- Experience in international indirect tax planning and strong understanding of or practical experience with tax laws in other countries
- Experience with VBA, Python, SQL, or other database language
- Experience a corporate tax advisory role in a multinational and/or professional organization
- Experience working in a large public accounting firm or multi-national corporate tax department
- Experience building strategic relationships with stakeholders, including communicating and collaborating across teams and functions

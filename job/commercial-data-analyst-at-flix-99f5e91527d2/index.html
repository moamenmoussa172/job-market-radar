About the job

At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable.

As a Commercial Data Analyst, you will part of the Revenue Management Americas Team, where you will support business teams in optimizing revenue, ensuring profitability, and fostering sustainable growth through data, reporting, and actionable insights.

This is a highly visible role that requires technical excellence, rigor, self-organization, autonomy, leadership, a hands-on mentality, and an entrepreneurial spirit.You will work on a wide range of analytical and reporting tasks while collaborating closely with business stakeholders across the region.

About the Role

  • Support HUB stakeholders, including Directors, Experts, CMLs, and CMMs, on data-related topics.
  • Collect, structure, and provide data for local and regional stakeholders.
  • Support data-driven projects across the Revenue Management Americas Team.
  • Implement, maintain, and optimize reporting tools created in Power BI and Excel.
  • Proactively identify new reporting needs in collaboration with the team and business stakeholders.
  • Optimize current reporting processes and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Maintain accurate documentation of data products, processes, reports, and business logic.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and translate them into clear data solutions.
  • Communicate findings, insights, and recommendations to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Actively contribute to the team’s development by sharing knowledge, best practices, and analytical know-how.

About You

  • At least 5+ years of experience as a Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Commercial Analyst, or in a similar role.
  • Excellent SQL knowledge, ideally connected to Snowflake.
  • Excellent Power BI knowledge, utilizing DAX.
  • Advanced Excel skills, including formulas and macros.
  • Experience communicating with internal and external stakeholders, including non-technical audiences.
  • Ability to explain complex data findings in a clear, structured, and business-oriented way.
  • Preference for collaborating with teams of analysts within Agile frameworks.
  • Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills.
  • High level of autonomy, ownership, and self-organization
  • Fluency in English is required, both spoken and written.
  • Python knowledge is nice to have.
  • Microsoft Power Platform experience is nice to have

We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!

What We Offer

  • Travel perks: 12 free Flix vouchers + 12 discount vouchers for friends & family.
  • Work from (M)Anywhere: Depending on your role, work from another location for up to 60 days per year.
  • Hybrid work model: We are an office-first company, but we offer flexibility to balance work and life.
  • Wellbeing support: Access confidential 1:1 counselling, courses, and stress management for yourself and up to four family members.
  • Learning & Development: Take advantage of language classes, training courses, and expert-led sessions to grow your skills.
  • Mentoring Program: Connect with experienced colleagues to gain insights and accelerate your career.

As we continue to expand across the globe, you can make a real difference in how we work.

If you’re ready to grow and lead your journey, Flix is the place for you!

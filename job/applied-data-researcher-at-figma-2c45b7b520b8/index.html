About the job

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us!

We’re hiring Applied Data Researchers for opportunities across Figma. We’re looking for people who pair rigorous quantitative thinking with curiosity about products and a drive to put ideas into practice. Your work may span product research and experimentation, AI evaluation, and applied data tools and capabilities. You’ll collaborate with partners in Data Science, Engineering, Product, Design, and AI Research, working closely with product teams and across the broader organization. We’ll consider your interests, experience, and strengths when exploring the right opportunity, with responsibilities and scope reflecting the role.

This is a full time role that can be held from one of our US hubs or remotely in the United States.

What you’ll do at Figma:

  • Frame research questions with partners, uncover opportunities through product data, and help teams decide where to invest through exploratory analysis, experimentation, and applied research
  • Design measurement approaches for products and AI-powered experiences, using methods such as experiments, causal inference, and evaluation frameworks to understand quality, behavior, and customer value
  • Use code to investigate hypotheses and develop analyses, models, prototypes, or research tools, choosing approaches that fit the question and make results reproducible
  • Own research projects at a scope appropriate to your experience, contribute to technical decisions, and share expertise with colleagues through collaboration, feedback, and mentorship
  • Work with engineering and product partners to apply research in practice, from improving instrumentation and guiding launches to integrating useful methods and tools into ongoing workflows
  • Develop metrics, datasets, and reusable approaches that help teams learn over time; assess adoption and impact, and refine your work as evidence and product needs evolve
  • Explain results and their implications clearly, helping partners understand methodological limits, weigh options, and make informed product and business decisions

We’d love to hear from you if you have:

  • A strong foundation in statistics, experimentation, machine learning, causal inference, or a related quantitative discipline, with experience applying appropriate methods to real-world questions
  • Extensive experience automating data workflows or building generative AI features and tools using large language models
  • Proficiency in SQL and a programming language such as Python or R, with the ability to explore complex data, implement methods, and produce reliable, reproducible work
  • Experience taking an ambiguous question from initial framing through analysis or method development to a practical recommendation, product improvement, or useful tool
  • A record of influencing product or technical decisions through research and collaborative communication, with ownership and impact appropriate to your experience, whether within a product team or across an organization — including the ability to build product context, question assumptions, discuss uncertainty, and develop strong relationships with cross-functional partners

While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have:

  • Experience evaluating generative AI, including human feedback, automated evaluation, or multimodal systems, and connecting evaluation results to how people experience a product
  • Experience building evaluation systems or infrastructure to measure and monitor the quality of AI-powered features in production
  • Experience developing research prototypes, interactive applications, or reusable tools that help others explore data, test ideas, or apply your findings
  • Familiarity with collaborative software, creative tools, or design systems, or experience working with large datasets and the infrastructure used to analyze them
  • Graduate study in a quantitative field, published research, or comparable depth developed through applied work

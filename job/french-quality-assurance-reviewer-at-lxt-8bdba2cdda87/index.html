French Quality Assurance / Reviewer

LXT · Remote

Fully remote · QA / Test Engineer · Posted 24 November 2026

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

LXT is an emerging leader in AI training data to power intelligent technology for global organizations. In partnership with an international network of contributors, LXT collects and annotates data across multiple modalities with the speed, scale and agility required by the enterprise. Our global expertise spans over 145 countries and coverage for more than 1000 language locales. Founded in 2010, LXT is headquartered in Toronto, Canada with presence in the United States, UK, Egypt, Turkey and Australia. The company serves customers in North America, Europe, Asia Pacific and the Middle East. Learn more at lxt.ai.

Are you detail-oriented, analytical, and passionate about maintaining high-quality standards?

LXT is looking for reliable and motivated individuals to join our Quality Assurance (QA) team and help support the delivery of AI data collection projects.

About the Role

As a French (France) Quality Assurance / Reviewer, you will review project submissions, identify quality issues, and ensure that delivered data meets client requirements and quality standards. This role is ideal for individuals with experience in data annotation, content review, moderation, or quality control.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review completed project submissions for quality and compliance.
  • Identify errors, inconsistencies, and guideline violations.
  • Provide accurate and objective feedback based on project requirements.
  • Evaluate completed tasks and document findings accurately.
  • Escalate quality concerns when necessary.
  • Support quality improvement initiatives and maintain quality targets.
  • Follow confidentiality and data security requirements at all times.

Requirements

  • Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Ability to follow detailed guidelines and instructions.
  • Reliable internet connection and access to a computer.
  • Must be at least 18 years old.
  • Active Clickworker account, or willingness to create one before applying.
  • Experience with data annotation, content review, quality control, moderation, or similar work is preferred.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous QA, moderation, annotation, or data review experience.
  • Familiarity with AI data collection and data labeling projects.
  • Ability to work independently and consistently meet deadlines.
  • Strong organizational and documentation skills.

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to work on exciting AI and data collection projects.
  • Flexible working hours.
  • Remote work environment.
  • Professional growth opportunities within the Clickworker community.

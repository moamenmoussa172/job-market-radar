Title: Senior Forward Deployed Engineer - SAP Business AI Platform - Indaiatuba/SP - 126054

John Deere is building capabilities on the SAP Business AI Platform to help teams apply AI to real business needs. As a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)at John Deere in Indaiatuba, you’ll partner with business stakeholders, SAP teams, and platform engineers to understand challenges in their day-to-day work and turn them into secure, useful software.

You’ll take solutions from discovery through deployment and adoption—using SAP BTP and related AI tooling to build integrations, workflows, and AI-powered applications. This role combines software engineering with hands-on problem solving: you’ll work in the context where the needs arise, validate ideas quickly, and share what you learn to help evolve the platform for broader use.

Main Responsibilities

  • Partner with business and technical teams to identify workflow needs, define outcomes, and assess feasibility;
  • Build, test, and deploy SAP BTP and Business AI solutions—from prototypes to production—including SAP/non-SAP integrations;
  • Explore and apply AI agents, generative AI, SAP Joule, and automation to validated business needs;
  • Explain technical decisions and tradeoffs to business and technical audiences;
  • Contribute to engineering standards, automated testing, CI/CD, and DevSecOps practices;
  • Mentor colleagues and help strengthen the team’s technical practices.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related field;
  • Advanced English proficiency for verbal and written communication in a global environment;
  • Solid experience designing and developing software, including object-oriented programming;
  • Experience building or integrating solutions in SAP environments, such as SAP S/4HANA or SAP BTP;
  • Experience developing APIs, integrations, or distributed applications using modern software architecture patterns;
  • Experience with cloud platforms and cloud-native development;
  • Knowledge of automated testing, CI/CD, and DevSecOps practices;
  • Ability to work through ambiguous problems, learn unfamiliar systems, and deliver practical solutions iteratively;
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, including the ability to work with business users, engineers, and global stakeholders.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with SAP Business AI, SAP AI Core, SAP Joule, AI agents, generative AI, or large language models;
  • Experience using AI services and integrating them into enterprise workflows;
  • Proficiency in Python, JavaScript, Node.js, Java, or similar languages;
  • SAP certifications or other relevant technical certifications;
  • Experience taking prototypes or proofs of concept into production;
  • Experience working in multicultural, globally distributed teams.

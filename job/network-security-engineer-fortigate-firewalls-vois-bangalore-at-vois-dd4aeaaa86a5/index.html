About this role

We are seeking a skilled Network Security Engineer with expertise in Fortigate Firewalls to deliver secure, resilient, and efficient network operations. The individual will work within an ITIL-driven environment, ensuring incident resolution, change implementation, and proactive support across customer-facing and internal infrastructures. This is a key operational role requiring strong troubleshooting capability, stakeholder collaboration, and a commitment to continuous learning

What you will do

  • Resolve network security incidents within critical SLAs through effective troubleshooting and root-cause identification.
  • Perform configuration and housekeeping activities including Layer 2/3 analysis, device upgrades, replacements, and performance optimisation.
  • Plan and implement internal and customer changes aligned with ITIL processes.
  • Raise and manage hardware vendor tickets, coordinating replacements and returns as per SLA while following escalation pathways where required.
  • Document technical procedures and share knowledge by coaching and mentoring team members.
  • Review and approve change requests ensuring adherence to security and operational guidelines.
  • Drive self-development by working towards industry-recognised certifications across supported technologies and expanding skills in emerging network security tools.

Who you are

  • Proficient in Fortigate Firewalls (Primary Skill).
  • Knowledgeable in FortiManager, FortiAnalyzer, IDS/IPS (Alternate Skills).
  • Experienced in troubleshooting and resolving network issues in a structured, SLA-driven environment.
  • Comfortable working within ITIL-aligned operational processes.
  • Collaborative, detail-oriented, and committed to continuous improvement.
  • Able to learn new technologies quickly and adapt to evolving technical landscapes.

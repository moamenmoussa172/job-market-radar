About the job

SOOR a subsidiary of Master Works, helps organizations stay ahead of evolving cyber threats through advanced cybersecurity and secure infrastructure solutions. SOOR delivers resilient, scalable, and regulation-aligned security across governance, cloud, networks, and managed defense.

SOOR is looking for a Business & Technical Solutions, Specialist to act as a bridge between business and technology teams by supporting business analysis, solution design, integration, testing, and implementation activities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Gather and document business and technical requirements.
  • Support solution design and system integration activities.
  • Conduct business process analysis and improvement assessments.
  • Prepare BRDs, functional specifications, process maps, and use cases.
  • Support testing, troubleshooting, and issue resolution activities.
  • Coordinate with stakeholders, vendors, and technical teams.
  • Maintain solution and integration documentation.
  • Support change management and user adoption activities.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 7 years of experience.

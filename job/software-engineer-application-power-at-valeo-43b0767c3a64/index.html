About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Key Responsibilities:

  • End-to-End Development: Design, plan, and implement advanced software tools and platforms tailored for automotive engineering workflows.
  • Technical Excellence: Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code while upholding high standards through rigorous peer code reviews.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with multidisciplinary teams to define requirements and deliver integrated software tooling solutions.
  • Systems Evolution: Provide lifecycle support, maintenance, and strategic enhancements for existing desktop and web-based platforms.
  • Problem Resolution: Rapidly troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues to ensure uninterrupted development pipelines.
  • Continuous Innovation: Proactively recommend process improvements and stay at the forefront of emerging technologies and industry trends.

Your Profile: Technical Rigor & Innovation

We are seeking a graduate or experienced engineer who thrives on solving complex structural problems and building high-quality software products.

Education & Core Skills:

  • Academic Background: B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical field.
  • Engineering Principles: Deep mastery of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and a solid command of Design Patterns with demonstrated practical application.
  • Language Proficiency: Strong command of at least one core language (e.g., C#, Java, C++, Python, or JavaScript).

Specialized Experience:

  • Application Development: Proven experience in building Windows Desktop Applications (using .NET or QT) and Web Applications (utilizing Angular paired with .NET or Python).
  • Automation & DevOps: Strong scripting capabilities (Python, Windows Batch, or Shell) and experience working within CI/CD environments.
  • Analytical Mindset: Exceptional problem-solving skills and the ability to navigate complex software architectures.
  • Communication: Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to articulate technical concepts to diverse stakeholders.

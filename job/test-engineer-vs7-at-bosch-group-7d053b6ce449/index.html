About the job

New product/ process introduction:

  • Equipment and process definition, machine acquisition support, installation and validation on production environment.
  • Develop procedures and instructions for processes within his/her responsibility.
  • Technicians training, Theoretical and practical.
  • Corrective, preventive and predictive maintenance procedures definition.

Production support:

  • Tracks KPI’s to identify areas of improvement and monitor upgrades.
  • Solves problems related to manufacturing processes.

New Product / Processes:

  • Active participation in new project introduction activities: process definition, acquisition, transfer, acceptance and validation processes.
  • Assure that Bosch and customer standards for release process and equipment installation are met.
  • Fulfill APQP requirements: EWAK runs, Pre-PV’s PV, and SOP.
  • Participate with Lead Plant / Development departments on manufacturing process definition and acceptance criteria.
  • Assure that general delivery specifications (GDS) for new equipment are met, Technical availability, cycle time, software & hardware design etc.

Production Support:

  • Contribute with Value Stream Main KPI’s achievement. Quality -Cost - Delivery
  • Create procedures, instructions, reaction plans and training material for machine troubleshooting.
  • Responsible to reach technical availability, machine cycle time and defect cost targets for the equipment under his/her responsibility.
  • Ensure manufacturing process compliance with customer requirements, Bosch standards and divisional process specifications.
  • Lead problem solving and improvement activities related to manufacturing processes under his/her responsibility.
  • Manufacturing Periodically meetings participation (DMM /DLR).

Functional area specific activities

  • Define, display and update process parameters in compliance with development specifications, divisional directives, general process specification and CoC recommendations. (DMD’s, VVT’s, DI’s…)
  • Comply with equipment capability studies, calibration, ESD and mech. stress requirements.
  • Define and apply TPM (total productive maintenance) activities, schedule, and training.
  • Machine spare parts analysis and control system information upload / actualization.
  • Develops / lead continues improvement projects based on the area KPI’s results.
  • Technical Downtime, MTTR, MTBF and MOC.
  • Development of Quick Change Over standards.
  • Define reaction plans, troubleshooting guides, and problem-solving techniques for technicians.
  • Set standards and methods for process confirmation.
  • Active exchange of lessons learned from other and to other Value Stream and IPN Plants.
  • Use the appropriate problem solving techniques to find root cause for problems related to manufacturing processes /equipment.
  • Follow up and promote the use of the Quality, Engineering and BPS tools. Use Bosch 14Q principles, A3, CIP, PCR’s, ECR, PFMEA, CP.
  • If applicable: OT Planner/OT Technician

EDUCATION:

Minimum education level: Bachelor’s degree, Eng. Mechatronic, Electronic, Systems, or similar)

SKILLS:

  • Experience (3-5 years) as a testing engineer.
  • Good knowledge in electronics (Analog/Digital).
  • Software programming knowledge (C, C++, VB or Delphi).
  • Analysis skills.
  • Self-directed.
  • Teamwork.
  • Problem solving techniques.
  • Electronic manufacturing Test Equipment knowledge (ICT , Flash programming ,
  • Functional test , leakage, DMC scanners, vision systems, multimeter , curve tracer).
  • Automotive industry standards (IATF, APQP, PPAP, MSA).
  • Electronic manufacturing industry standards.
  • Problem solving techniques.
  • Industry 4.0 , IoT knowledge.
  • IT components, Data security partner and connectivity knowledge (Role for IT-Planner).

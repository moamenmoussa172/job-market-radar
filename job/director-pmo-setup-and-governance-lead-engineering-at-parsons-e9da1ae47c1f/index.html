Job Description:

Parsons is looking for an amazingly talented Director – PMO Setup and Governance Lead to join our team! In this role, you will be seconded to a major governmental entity to help them establish and govern their PMO.

What You'll Be Doing:

  • Define the required PMO model and implementation roadmap by assessing the client's needs and maturity.
  • Establish the PMO vision, mandate, organizational structure, and operating model.
  • Define project, programme, and portfolio governance requirements.
  • Standardize project management methodologies, procedures, templates, and controls.
  • Establish integrated planning, cost management, risk, change, reporting, and assurance processes.
  • Provide executives with reliable performance information and early warnings.
  • Establish a scalable PMO capable of supporting the organization’s current and future project portfolio.
  • The successful candidate should be expected to produce, approve, or oversee the following deliverables:
  • Initial Mobilization Deliverables: PMO Charter, vision and objectives, PMO operating model mobilization plan, governance map and decision rights matrix.
  • Management Framework Deliverables: Project management framework, stage-gate framework, project execution plan template, change-control procedure, and reporting and dashboarding standard.

What Required Skills You'll Bring:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or a related discipline from a recognized university.
  • At least 17- 20 years of professional experience
  • Proven experience in infrastructure, master planning, and road design management.
  • Proven experience working within or establishing a Project Management Office (PMO).
  • Experience on projects in the Middle East preferred.
  • Arabic speakers preferred as the role involves regular interaction with government stakeholders.

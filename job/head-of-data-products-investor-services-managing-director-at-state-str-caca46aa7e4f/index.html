About the job

Role Purpose

The Head of Data Products is the senior Product leader responsible for the strategy, development and commercialisation of Investment Services' data, Data Services, analytics and AI-enabled product portfolio.

The role owns the end-to-end Product lifecycle, from understanding business, client and market needs and defining Product strategy, through investment and development, to launch, adoption, pricing and commercialisation.

A core mandate is to build a common Data Platform and portfolio of reusable Data Products that create value across Investment Services. These products support internal users across Operations, Client Service and Product, enable advanced analytics and AI, and provide the foundation for client-facing commercial Data Services.

Externally, the role is responsible for building and growing the commercial Data Services business, converting Investment Services' data, servicing capabilities and domain expertise into scalable products that solve client problems and create new commercial value.

The role works extensively across enterprise functions, including the Chief Data & AI Officer (CDAIO) organisation, Technology, Architecture and Enterprise Data Management, to co-create common capabilities, influence enterprise priorities and ensure Investment Services can leverage and contribute to enterprise data, analytics and AI capabilities.

Key Accountabilities

Product Strategy & Portfolio

  • Own the strategy, roadmap and investment priorities for the Investment Services data, Data Services, analytics and AI portfolio.
  • Manage products end-to-end, from discovery and business case through build, launch, adoption and optimisation.
  • Translate business, operational and client needs into clear Product requirements, priorities and measurable outcomes.
  • Define Product propositions and roadmaps that balance internal business value and external commercial opportunity.
  • Partner with Technology and Architecture to deliver scalable products, with Product owning the what and why.
  • Drive reuse and rationalisation, reducing bespoke solutions, duplicate pipelines and legacy capabilities.
  • Establish clear Product ownership and success measures across the portfolio.

Data Products & Data Platform

  • Own the Data Platform from a Product perspective, providing a common, governed and reusable data foundation across Investment Services.
  • Build domain-aligned Data Products that serve multiple internal and external consumers rather than individual use cases or applications.
  • Develop Data Products that improve Operations, including workflow, automation, exception management, reconciliation and operational decision-making.
  • Enable Client Service teams with timely, consistent and trusted data to improve client servicing, transparency and issue resolution.
  • Enable Product teams with trusted data and insights to support Product management, client engagement and business decisions.
  • Ensure Data Products can be reused across operational, analytical, AI and external client use cases.
  • Drive common definitions, semantics, quality, lineage and entitlements so data is consistent and trusted across consuming channels.
  • Rationalise legacy products and pipelines as strategic capabilities mature.

Advanced Analytics & AI

  • Own the Investment Services Product strategy for advanced analytics and AI-enabled capabilities.
  • Enable advanced analytics by making governed, high-quality and well-structured data readily available for analytical consumption.
  • Develop capabilities that support descriptive, diagnostic and predictive analytics, enabling deeper insight into clients, products, operations and business performance.
  • Create trusted, AI-ready Data Products with the data, semantics, metadata and controls required for scalable AI adoption.
  • Identify opportunities for generative AI, semantic models, knowledge graphs and agent-based capabilities to improve internal workflows or create differentiated client propositions.
  • Move analytics and AI from isolated use cases toward repeatable Product capabilities that can be reused across Investment Services.
  • Measure analytics and AI investments through adoption, productivity, operational efficiency, client experience and commercial value.

Commercial Data Services

  • Build and grow the commercial Data Services offering for Investment Services.
  • Convert Investment Services data, domain expertise and servicing capabilities into differentiated client products and services.
  • Develop scalable propositions across data integration, interoperability, distribution, managed data services, analytics and AI-enabled services.
  • Move from bespoke client solutions toward repeatable, configurable and commercially sustainable products.
  • Develop solutions that connect Investment Services data with clients’ wider investment and technology ecosystems.
  • Own Product packaging, service models, pricing and Product economics.
  • Use strategic client implementations to build reusable capabilities and accelerate the broader Product roadmap.
  • Measure commercial success through pipeline, adoption, revenue, reuse and client value.

Client, Market & Commercial Engagement

  • Act as the senior Data Product expert with clients, prospects and strategic partners.
  • Maintain direct engagement with clients to understand evolving data, operating model, interoperability, analytics and AI requirements.
  • Lead the data and AI response for strategic RFPs, RFIs, new-business opportunities and client due diligence.
  • Translate recurring client requirements and market signals into Product propositions and roadmap priorities.
  • Co-create new propositions with strategic clients, validating demand and value before significant Product investment.
  • Support solution design, commercial negotiations and senior client engagements.
  • Partner with Sales, Coverage and Relationship Management to develop pipeline and take new Data Services to market.
  • Use client, competitor and market intelligence to continuously evolve Product strategy.

Pricing & Product Economics

  • Own pricing and commercial models for Data Services in partnership with Finance, Sales and business leadership.
  • Define scalable pricing across Data Products, integration, distribution, managed services, analytics and AI-enabled capabilities.
  • Distinguish between strategic Product investment, client implementation charges and recurring service fees.
  • Manage Product economics across build, implementation and operating costs, revenue potential and reuse.
  • Use adoption, pipeline, revenue and economics to inform ongoing Product investment and prioritisation.

Enterprise Co-Creation & Partnerships

  • Act as the senior Investment Services Data Product partner to CDAIO and other enterprise functions.
  • Co-create enterprise data, analytics and AI capabilities, ensuring Investment Services requirements and domain expertise shape enterprise strategy and investment.
  • Bring Investment Services use cases, business semantics and client requirements into the design of enterprise capabilities.
  • Leverage common enterprise platforms, standards and tooling rather than creating unnecessary duplication within Investment Services.
  • Identify Investment Services capabilities that can be reused more broadly across the enterprise, creating value beyond the immediate business.
  • Establish clear ownership between enterprise capabilities and Investment Services Product responsibilities.
  • Partner across Technology, Architecture, Enterprise Data Management, Operations and other businesses to solve shared data challenges and accelerate delivery.

Leadership & Executive Governance

  • Build and lead a high-performing team of Data Product leaders and domain experts.
  • Establish clear Product ownership and accountability across the Investment Services data portfolio.
  • Lead core executive governance forums, including ExCo and SteerCo, setting strategic direction, driving investment and prioritisation decisions and maintaining accountability for outcomes.

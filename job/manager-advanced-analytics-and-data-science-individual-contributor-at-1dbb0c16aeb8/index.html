About the job

*For Pooling Purposes*

What You'll Do

Advanced Analytics & Business Insights

  • Perform deep-dive analyses to uncover trends, patterns, opportunities, and risks across business operations.
  • Develop descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive analytics solutions to support strategic decision-making.
  • Translate complex business questions into actionable analytical frameworks and recommendations.
  • Deliver executive-level insights through compelling storytelling, visualizations, and presentations.

Data Science & Machine Learning

  • Design, build, and deploy predictive models, including forecasting, classification, clustering, and recommendation models.
  • Apply statistical methods to identify business drivers and improve performance outcomes.
  • Evaluate model effectiveness and continuously refine solutions to maximize accuracy and business value.
  • Leverage Python, SQL, and modern analytics tools to support scalable data science initiatives.

AI, Text Analytics & Automation

  • Analyze unstructured data sources such as customer interactions, transcripts, feedback, and operational documentation.
  • Utilize Natural Language Processing (NLP) and AI-based methodologies to identify sentiment, themes, behaviors, and emerging trends.
  • Support the development of intelligent analytics solutions that enhance operational performance and customer experience.

Business Consulting & Stakeholder Management

  • Partner directly with business leaders to define requirements, align priorities, and identify opportunities for data-driven improvements.
  • Serve as a trusted advisor, helping stakeholders convert insights into measurable business actions.
  • Lead analytical discussions and effectively communicate technical concepts to non-technical audiences.
  • Manage multiple strategic initiatives while maintaining high-quality delivery standards.

Modern Data Platforms & Reporting

  • Work with large-scale data environments, cloud-based analytics platforms, and enterprise reporting solutions.
  • Support analytics modernization efforts by translating legacy reporting logic into scalable data solutions.
  • Collaborate with data engineering teams to ensure data quality, governance, and accessibility.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, or a related quantitative discipline.
  • At least 4 years of experience in Data Science, Advanced Analytics, Business Analytics, or a related field.
  • Strong expertise in SQL and Python for data analysis and model development.
  • Experience building predictive and statistical models, including forecasting, regression, classification, and segmentation.
  • Solid understanding of data visualization and business intelligence concepts.
  • Experience working directly with business stakeholders and translating business challenges into analytical solutions.
  • Strong problem-solving, presentation, and communication skills.
  • Experience using Git or similar version control tools.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience with cloud-based analytics platforms such as Databricks or similar technologies.
  • Knowledge of Spark SQL, PySpark, or large-scale data processing frameworks.
  • Experience in Natural Language Processing (NLP), text analytics, or AI-driven modeling.
  • Familiarity with modern machine learning frameworks and model deployment practices.
  • Exposure to optimization modeling, resource planning, workforce optimization, or operational analytics.
  • Experience supporting high-volume customer-facing, sales, service, or operations environments.

Why Join Sutherland?

  • Drive high-impact analytics initiatives that influence business strategy and operational excellence.
  • Work with modern data technologies, advanced modeling techniques, and AI-powered solutions.
  • Partner directly with senior leaders and decision-makers across the organization.
  • Enjoy the autonomy and visibility of a leadership-level individual contributor role.
  • Be part of a collaborative, innovation-focused environment where your insights create measurable business value.
  • Contribute to large-scale analytics transformation programs and future-focused data strategies.

Ready to make data matter?

Join Sutherland and help shape smarter business decisions through advanced analytics, machine learning, and data-driven innovation.

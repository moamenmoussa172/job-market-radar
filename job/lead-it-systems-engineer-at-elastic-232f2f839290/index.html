About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

The Lead IT Systems Engineer will be tasked with leading our work across many fronts including identity and access management, data loss prevention, and insider risk engineering. They will also provide hands-on leadership around our SaaS native landscape and automation in our IT Operations space, working closely with the Director of IT Operations to keep pace with a quickly growing company.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Work with the director of IT Operations to set the strategy and drive action across a handful of areas including relevant build vs buy decisions, identity and access management, data loss prevention, and insider risk posture.
  • Acts as a thought leader to introduce and assess emerging capabilities and technologies
  • Drives consolidation and simplification of applications and technologies
  • Works with the team to establish dashboards, reports, and KPIs to manage the environment and drive efficiencies from
  • Looks for ways to use AI both for the company and within the team to increase employee satisfaction and productivity
  • Mentor teammates to help grow acumen and technical capabilities within the group
  • Build automation capabilities to reduce the load on the team, increase user satisfaction, and set the company up for successful scaling.
  • Working knowledge of fleet management platforms (e.g., Jamf Pro for macOS, Microsoft Intune for Windows) and modern Zero Trust network access (ZTNA/SASE) architectures.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and company policies related to IT operations and data security.
  • Promote a culture of information security awareness and best practices among employees.
  • Partner with Infosec in the rollout, maintenance, and support of information security tools, controls, and best practices
  • This is a hands-on role and the right candidate will have the ability to do the work and teach, grow, and mentor others.

What You Will Bring

Education and Experience:

  • Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 15 years of experience in IT operations, with at least 10 years in a lead role.
  • Knowledge of IT infrastructure, including network management, endpoint management, office and cloud infrastructure.
  • Experience with user provisioning, application support, and access management.
  • Experience working in a SaaS/remote environment
  • Experience managing an Okta and Gsuite environment
  • Knowledge of information security principles and practices.
  • Experience in leveraging Elastic for the delivery of operational processes is a plus

Skills:

  • Top-notch communication and social skills.
  • Well-developed analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to lead multiple projects and priorities in a dynamic environment.
  • Ability to appropriately apply AI / ML to business challenges
  • Experience running scale out identity and access management and DLP solutions

Personal Attributes:

  • Strategic problem solver with an initiative-taking and solution-oriented mentality.
  • Organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to influence and inspire change across the organization.
  • You maintain composure and clarity when navigating complex or high-stakes challenges.

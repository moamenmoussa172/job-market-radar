About the job

About Gusto

At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve.

All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy.

AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process.

About the Role:

As a Staff Engineer for the Agent & Partner Experiences team, you’ll be a key technical leader empowering internal and partner developers to build reliably and efficiently on Gusto Embedded Payroll. You’ll collaborate across product and engineering teams to design, build, and evolve tooling, APIs, SDKs, documentation, and workflows that reduce friction and improve velocity for those who integrate with Gusto Embedded Payroll.

This role combines hands-on engineering with strategic thinking about how developers interact with our products—identifying root causes of friction, validating solutions with users, and delivering scalable improvements.

Your influence will span teams, including product, platform, and partnerships, while your primary impact will be through execution and technical ownership rather than setting org-wide policy.

About the Team:

Gusto Agent & Partner Experiences is how Gusto shows up wherever our customers want to work. We build the APIs, SDKs, and AI interfaces that let partners embed payroll directly into their own software, and let AI agents act on payroll on a customer's behalf. That means a small business can run payroll from the tools it already uses, whether that's a vertical SaaS product, a fintech or neobank app, or an AI-native experience. We've taken Gusto's payroll infrastructure and opened it up beyond Gusto itself, so anyone can build the best payroll experience for their customers, with Gusto powering it behind the scenes.

Learn more about Gusto Embedded Payroll here.

Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day:

  • Own and deliver high-impact initiatives that improve the experience of external developers integrating with Gusto Embedded Payroll and Solutions Architects supporting those integrations, balancing partner needs, business impact, and technical complexity.
  • Partner with Solutions Architects to understand recurring integration challenges and translate them into scalable platform, tooling, or documentation improvements.
  • Design, build, and evolve developer-facing APIs, SDKs, reference implementations, and tooling with a strong focus on clarity, consistency, backwards compatibility, and long-term maintainability.
  • Improve onboarding, debugging, and operational workflows for partners through better docs, examples, error messages, observability, and feedback loops that inform iteration.
  • Engage directly with external developers and partners to gather feedback, validate solutions, and evaluate effectiveness based on real-world usage.
  • Work closely with product and engineering teams to ensure partner and Solutions Architect needs are actively advocated for and appropriately weighted in technical decisions and tradeoffs.
  • Lead by example through hands-on development, shipping well-tested, maintainable code that supports long-term platform health.
  • Mentor engineers and collaborate with peers to raise the quality, consistency, and shared technical direction of developer-facing systems across teams.

Here’s what we're looking for:

  • Experience: 8+ years of professional software development experience, with ownership of complex initiatives from problem definition and prioritization through delivery and iteration.
  • Technical Expertise: Strong backend engineering expertise, with deep experience working in large, production systems (Ruby on Rails experience is a plus). You’re comfortable working across multiple layers of abstraction and making pragmatic tradeoffs.
  • Developer First: A developer-first mindset, with deep empathy for integrators and a focus on clear contracts, predictable behavior, actionable error messages, and high-quality documentation.
  • Mentoring: You enjoy mentoring and raising the technical bar for your team and adjacent teams through guidance, collaboration, and example.
  • Effective Communicator: You possess the ability to explain technical tradeoffs and constraints to both technical and non-technical stakeholders and can influence technical direction through execution, collaboration, and technical leadership rather than formal authority.
  • AI-native Curiosity: Curiosity in an AI-native engineering environment, leveraging intelligence, automation, and experimentation to improve developer tooling, workflows, and partner-facing payroll APIs at scale.

Please note that travel to Gusto's office for onboarding is required for this position. If you have any questions regarding this requirement, please share with your recruiter.

Our cash compensation amount for this role is targeted at 163,000–204,000 in Denver, and 197,000–247,000 for San Francisco & New York. Stock equity is additional. Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors including candidate experience and expertise and may vary from the amounts listed above.

At Gusto, we don't use titles the traditional way, as a signal of rank or hierarchy. Instead, our titles describe two things at once — your craft (what you do, e.g. Engineering, Design) and your scope of ownership (what you're accountable for) — which is why you'll see something like "Head of X" instead of a rank word like "Director" that tells you neither. Because of this, the title on this posting may look different from what you'd expect based on the scope and seniority of the role, and from how similar roles are typically titled elsewhere. If you have questions about how this role compares to your current title or level, your recruiter can help translate.

Gusto has physical office spaces in Denver, San Francisco, and New York City. Employees who are based in those locations will be expected to work from the office on designated days approximately 2-3 days per week (or more depending on role). The same office expectations apply to all Symmetry roles, Gusto's subsidiary, whose physical office is in Scottsdale.

Note: The San Francisco office expectations encompass both the San Francisco and San Jose metro areas.

When approved to work from a location other than a Gusto office, a secure, reliable, and consistent internet connection is required. This includes non-office days for hybrid employees.

Reliable, consistent presence: We expect regular and predictable attendance, whether you're working in-office, hybrid, or fully remote, so that you stay closely connected to your team, customers, and stakeholders in real time and ensure colleagues and customers receive the timely support and responsiveness they deserve.

Our customers come from all walks of life and so do we. We hire great people from a wide variety of backgrounds, not just because it's the right thing to do, but because it makes our company stronger. If you share our values and our enthusiasm for small businesses, you will find a home at Gusto.

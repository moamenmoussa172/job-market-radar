Keep Critical Defence Training Systems Running at Their Best

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as an IT Support Technician at HM Naval Base Clyde, near Helensburgh, Argyll and Bute.

The role

As an IT Support Technician, you'll play a vital role in supporting the technology that underpins world-class defence training. This is an opportunity to work in a unique and highly secure environment where your technical expertise will directly support operational readiness and national defence capabilities. You'll be part of a collaborative team, solving complex challenges, improving user experiences, and ensuring critical IT systems remain secure, reliable, and available when they're needed most. Whether you're resolving technical issues, supporting infrastructure improvements, or enhancing cyber security compliance, your work will make a genuine difference while helping you develop your technical skills and career within a leading engineering and defence organisation.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Provide first and second-line technical support, resolving incidents and service requests within agreed service levels.
  • Monitor, maintaining and supporting IT infrastructure including servers, desktops, networks, printers and specialist training systems.
  • Install, configuring and troubleshooting hardware, software and operating systems to ensure reliable service delivery.
  • Support system upgrades, maintenance activities and continuous improvement projects across the IT environment.
  • Maintain secure and compliant IT services through effective documentation, asset management, change control and cyber security activities.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and is based on site at Clyde, Faslane.

Essential experience of the IT Support Technician

  • Delivering first and second-line IT support within a service desk or technical support environment.
  • Strong knowledge of Microsoft Windows Server environments and enterprise IT infrastructure
  • Troubleshooting hardware, software, networking and user access issues within business-critical environments.
  • Working within IT service management frameworks and achieving performance against agreed service levels.
  • Managing competing priorities whilst building effective relationships with users, stakeholders and technical teams.

Qualifications for the IT Support Technician

  • Strong knowledge of Microsoft Windows Server environments (2019 and newer versions).
  • Understanding of core networking technologies, including network addressing, name resolution, device connectivity and network infrastructure.
  • Knowledge of virtualisation platforms, particularly VMware environments and virtual server management.
  • Understanding of cyber security, data protection and information assurance principles.
  • Familiarity with IT service management best practice and service lifecycle processes.

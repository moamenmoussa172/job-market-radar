About the job

Your contribution to something big:

As an experienced SAP Consultant for Order-to-Cash (O2C) process, you will play a crucial role in Bosch Home Comfort's operations as we migrate from SAP R/3 to S/4 HANA.

In this dynamic role, you will:

  • Actively contribute to the division-wide migration project, ensuring seamless transition and optimization (from R/3 to S/4 HANA) with focus on S/4 HANA template and roll-out projects,
  • Work on design, harmonization and requirements in SAP ERP R/3 and S/4
  • Analyze and translate order-to-cash and sales-related insights into actionable IT work packages and make them transparent to the team,
  • Consult worldwide operating sales organization & business units regarding integration and onboarding of new sales, master data processes, and applications in a customer oriented, re-usable and supportive manner,
  • Execute requirement workshops, create specifications, support the implementation in SAP, train and support our key users,
  • Actively communicate, change management & align with related stakeholders worldwide.

What distinguishes you:

Education:

  • Degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, or a related field.

Experience and Knowledge:

  • Several years of experience in consulting or industry with focus on sales process areas (Order to Cash, Lead to Order), relevant in-depth IT solution experience in both SAP R/3 and SAP S/4 HANA, experience with international roll-outs.
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP SD Order-to-Cash (O2C) processes in R/3 and S/4 HANA. Purchasing or logistics process expertise (e.g., MM, EWM or GTS) and technical acumen are a plus.
  • Good project management skills.

Languages:

  • Very good communication skills in English. Skills in Portuguese are a strong plus.

Working Style and Methods:

  • Adaptable and open-minded in fast-paced environments.
  • Collaborative team player with strong presentation and coordination skills.

Personality:

  • Proactive, customer-oriented mindset with a focus on delivering results.
  • Willingness to work with cross-functional teams in an international environment

Mobility:

  • Willingness to travel 20–30% expected.

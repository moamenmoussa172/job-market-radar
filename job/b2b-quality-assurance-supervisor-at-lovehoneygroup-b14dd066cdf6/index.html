We are currently looking for a B2B Quality Assurance Supervisor who will be integral to driving high-level performance across Lovehoney Group. Partnering closely with the B2B Warehouse team, your focus will be on ensuring the accuracy, quality and compliance of outbound B2B customer orders, while identifying recurring issues and supporting continuous improvement across the operation. In this role, you will combine your strong attention to detail with your ability to challenge non-compliant practices, investigate quality issues and support consistent operational standards.

What you will do

  • Conduct quality checks on outbound B2B orders, verifying SKUs, quantities, product condition, labels, packaging, pallets and customer-specific requirements before dispatch.
  • Maintain accurate QA records and prevent non-compliant orders from being shipped, ensuring issues are resolved or escalated appropriately.
  • Monitor outbound errors and quality performance, investigate root causes and work with the Warehouse team to implement corrective and preventative actions.
  • Support distribution compliance for key US brands, including perishable product checks, batch and expiry controls, storage requirements and temperature monitoring.
  • Support inventory and wider warehouse activities, including cycle counts, stocktakes and investigations, while coaching colleagues on quality standards and promoting safe, professional and organised working practices.
  • What you should bring
  • Experience working in a Warehouse, Distribution or Fulfilment environment, with proven experience in B2B or Quality Assurance within a fast-paced operational environment.
  • Strong working knowledge of WMS, ERP systems and Microsoft Office, including Excel; NetSuite experience is preferred.
  • Exceptional attention to detail, with strong analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills and the ability to identify trends and improve quality and service levels.
  • Excellent communication skills and confidence to challenge non-compliant practices, make decisions independently, resolve issues and escalate appropriately.
  • Reliable and able to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced environment, with the confidence to coach and support colleagues in achieving consistent quality and operational standards.

Your role at Hostinger

We're looking for a hands-on Linux Systems Engineering Lead to lead our Web Hosting SysAdmin team in Indonesia.

Our team operates more than 1,600 bare-metal hosting servers across 16+ data centres, supporting millions of customers worldwide.

You'll lead an experienced team of Linux Systems Administrators responsible for maintaining, troubleshooting and continuously improving our global hosting infrastructure.

This is not a traditional management role. You'll spend approximately 50% of your time working hands-on with Linux infrastructure, solving complex technical challenges, improving automation and driving infrastructure reliability. The other 50% will focus on leading, mentoring and developing your team.

You'll own the team's operational performance, shift coverage and incident response during the Asia-Pacific daytime window of our 24/7 global operations, working closely with our teams in Lithuania and Brazil.

You'll also collaborate with engineering, development, Customer Success and product teams to improve the reliability, security and performance of our hosting services.

If you're an experienced Linux engineer who enjoys solving complex infrastructure problems and helping other engineers grow, this is an opportunity to make a real impact at scale.
Curious to learn more? Meet your future Manager: Teodoras Celencevičius

Your day-to-day

  • Lead and develop the Indonesian Web Hosting SysAdmin team, providing technical guidance, mentoring and support.
  • Manage team shift coverage and coordinate daily operations to ensure reliable 24/7 global infrastructure support.
  • Take ownership of incident response during your team's operational window, including incident acknowledgement,status page updates, troubleshooting, postmortems and follow-up within SLA.
  • Act as a technical escalation point for Customer Success on complex hosting, infrastructure and security cases.
  • Stay hands-on with our hosting infrastructure, working with CloudLinux, LiteSpeed, cPanel/WHM and MariaDB to diagnose, optimise and resolve complex technical issues.
  • Improve infrastructure reliability, performance and security through engineering initiatives and automation.
  • Build and maintain automation using Ansible/AWX, Bash, Python and monitoring tools such as Prometheus and Grafana.
  • Explore and implement AI-driven solutions for automated ticket resolution, AI-assisted troubleshooting and smarter operational workflows.
  • Participate in hiring, onboarding, mentoring and developing team members.
  • Monitor team performance, operational KPIs and service quality, identifying opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Collaborate with our SysAdmin teams in Lithuania and Brazil, sharing knowledge, improving processes and making technical decisions that benefit our global infrastructure.

What will help you succeed

  • You have 4+ years of experience in Linux system administration, infrastructure engineering or a similar technical role.
  • You have a strong understanding of Linux environments, server administration, troubleshooting and performance optimisation.
  • You have experience working with web servers such as Apache, Nginx or LiteSpeed.
  • You have familiarity with web hosting technologies such as cPanel/WHM, CloudLinux (LVE, CageFS) and MariaDB/MySQL.
  • You understand infrastructure security, server hardening and malware investigation. Experience with tools such as Imunify360 or Monarx is an advantage.
  • You have experience with Ansible or other configuration management and automation tools.
  • You are familiar with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus and Grafana.
  • You have experience mentoring engineers, leading technical projects or managing a team.
  • You can coordinate technical operations, prioritise incidents and make decisions in challenging situations.
  • You have experience with incident management, operational processes and service-level agreements.
  • You have good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with distributed international teams.
  • You have professional working proficiency in English, both written and spoken.
  • You enjoy staying hands-on while helping other engineers improve their technical skills.
  • You take ownership, think proactively and continuously look for ways to improve systems and processes.
  • You're curious about new technologies and excited about using AI and automation to improve infrastructure operations.
  • You care about uptime, security, teamwork and delivering reliable services to customers.

Meet your team

The Web Hosting SysAdmin team keeps the infrastructure behind Hostinger's hosting services healthy, stable, and ready to support millions of customers worldwide. We are distributed across Indonesia, Lithuania and Brazil, which gives us round-the-clock coverage and expertise from three markets.

Each location has its own team and manager, but we work as one: shared runbooks, shared incidents, shared goals. In Indonesia, you will lead an experienced sysadmin team who care deeply about Linux and about making our hosting environment better every week.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Chainguard is the trusted source for open source. By delivering hardened, secure, and production-ready builds of all the open source software engineers and AI agents rely on, Chainguard helps organizations build faster, stay compliant, and eliminate risk.
Our customers include Fortune 500 enterprises and global industry leaders, including Anduril, Canva, Fortinet, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, OpenAI, Snap Inc., and Snowflake.
Chainguard is venture-backed by leading investors, including Amplify, IVP, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Mantis VC, Redpoint Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Spark Capital.

The Global Systems Integrator (GSI) Partner Solutions Engineer is a partner-facing technical role focused on Chainguard's relationships with GSIs. GSIs sell and deliver large cloud, security, and platform modernization programs, so this role works within partners' practices, delivery teams, and pursuit teams to make Chainguard part of how those programs are designed, scoped, and delivered. The GSI PSE builds technical champions across practices and regions, turns Chainguard into repeatable GSI offerings and accelerators, and supports joint pursuits that drive partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue.

This role combines technical depth, partner enablement, and go-to-market collaboration.

Our partner engagement model follows the BEPIC framework:

  • Belief – Build technical conviction in Chainguard
  • Executive Sponsorship – Align with partner leadership
  • Propensity to Embed – Integrate Chainguard into partner architectures
  • Integrated Value Proposition – Deliver compelling joint solutions
  • Cadence – Maintain consistent partner engagement

Key responsibilities

Partner technical strategy and enablement

  • Build and maintain technical champions across each GSI's practices (cloud, security, platform engineering, application modernization) and regions, so Chainguard becomes the default recommendation for secure software supply chain work.
  • Design enablement for delivery scale: train-the-trainer programs, role-based learning paths, and certification tracks for architects, engineers, and pre-sales staff, supported by hands-on labs, Capture The Flags, and hackathons.
  • Create and maintain reusable assets that delivery teams can use directly in engagements: reference architectures, migration runbooks, demo environments, solution briefs, and pursuit-ready content.

Offerings, accelerators, and solution design

  • Work with GSI practice leads to turn Chainguard into packaged offerings, such as vulnerability reduction assessments, container and library migration factories, secure platform build accelerators, and managed-service add-ons.
  • Embed Chainguard in GSI AI and agentic development offerings, such as AI factory builds and secure-by-default foundations for AI coding assistants; work with Chainguard product and engineering to bring them to life.
  • Serve as the technical lead for Chainguard Centers of Excellence inside each assigned GSI, partnering with the GSI Partner Manager to standardize delivery and roll offerings out globally.
  • Contribute to scoping, effort estimates, and statement-of-work language for Chainguard-related services.

Pursuit support and go-to-market acceleration

  • Support GSI pre-sales and capture teams on active pursuits, including RFI and RFP responses, solution design, technical orals, and proof-of-value plans.
  • Run technical workshops and assessments that the GSI can deliver to its own customers, to shorten sales cycles, strengthen the value proposition, and increase win rates.
  • Collaborate closely with Partner Managers and Sales to attach Chainguard to GSI-led opportunities, drive co-sell motions, and grow partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline and revenue across each GSI's key accounts and geographies.
  • Co-author blogs, webinars, talks, and solution briefs with GSI practices to amplify the joint value proposition.

Account planning, alignment, and roadmap

  • Own the technical plan for assigned GSIs: map practices, regions, and key delivery accounts, identify executive sponsors (global alliance leads, practice heads, CTO office), and set quarterly joint goals aligned to the BEPIC framework.
  • Shape each GSI's progression from introduction to deep-dive, certification, and service build-out, and track delivery readiness: certified practitioners, active engagements, and offerings live.
  • Keep regional teams and global alliance teams aligned on one message and one plan across a matrixed GSI organization.
  • Capture and feed back partner and field learnings to internal teams to influence product, content, and partner strategy.

Required skills

  • 5+ years in solutions engineering, partner solutions architecture, sales engineering, or a similar customer-facing technical role (consulting experience welcome).
  • Strong hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), containers and Kubernetes, and software supply chain or cloud security.
  • Experience working at or with a GSI or consulting firm, and an understanding of how practices sell and deliver: pursuits, SOWs, delivery models, utilization, and certification.
  • Proven track record enabling partners through training, workshops, and joint solution development, ideally at scale across large, distributed teams.
  • Experience with large-scale modernization or migration work, such as container adoption, platform engineering, or application modernization.
  • Comfortable working across product, engineering, sales, marketing, and partner managers, and across a matrixed partner organization.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills: can explain complex technical topics to both engineers and executives, and enjoys public-facing activities (workshops, webinars, conferences).
  • Ability to travel up to 50%.

Ideal candidates possess

  • Familiarity with regulated and public-sector requirements, such as SBOMs, FedRAMP, and compliance frameworks.
  • Existing relationships with GSI alliance, practice, or pursuit leaders.
  • Experience with hyperscaler co-sell and marketplace motions.
  • Additional language skills

OTE range: $237,000 - $275,000

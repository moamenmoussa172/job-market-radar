About the team

Embedded Engineering Capability: Empowering Innovation in the World of Embedded Software.

Join Accenture to shape a future looking Software-Defined Vehicle Platform that unifies Linux, Android, QNX, AUTOSAR & Adaptive AUTOSAR into one powerful developer ecosystem, sustaining innovation in ADAS and Infotainment, building safe, secure, and continuously upgradable vehicles.

We are looking for an experienced Embedded Linux/Android Engineer with strong C/C++ and Linux expertise to work on embedded Android platforms. The role focuses on bringing Android up on custom hardware, integrating and adapting BSP components, device drivers and Hardware Abstraction Layers (HALs), and debugging complex issues across hardware, kernel, and Android system layers.

You will work close to the hardware, contributing to platform bring-up, system integration, optimization, and platform upgrades.

How you will make an impact

  • Bring up Android/AOSP on custom hardware
  • Integrate and adapt BSPs, device drivers, and Android HALs
  • Debug issues across hardware, kernel, and Android system layers
  • Customize Linux/AOSP builds and integrate vendor components
  • Optimize performance, power consumption, and system stability
  • Support platform upgrades and hardware/software integration

What you need to succeed:

  • Must Have
  • Strong C/C++ and Linux knowledge
  • Experience with Embedded Linux and/or Android/AOSP
  • Hands-on experience with board bring-up, BSPs, device drivers, and HALs
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills
  • Experience with AOSP/AAOS, automotive communication protocols, Java, Kotlin, or Rust.
  • Experience with vehicle prototypes, test benches, or development boards.
  • Nice To Have

Yocto, U-Boot, Device Tree, ARM, SELinux, Soong, ADB/logcat.

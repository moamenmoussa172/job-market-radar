Senior Analyst - Procurement Operations

The Opportunity:

The Sr Analyst - Procurement Operations plays a critical role in enabling procurement operational excellence through data governance, system administration support, reporting, compliance monitoring, and continuous process improvement. This highly analytical, detail-oriented individual contributor serves as a steward of procurement data, systems, workflows, controls, and reporting accuracy across the enterprise.

This role is responsible for maintaining the integrity of procurement platforms and related data structures, ensuring audit readiness, monitoring compliance with procurement policies and delegated authority frameworks, and delivering actionable insights that improve transparency, governance, and business decision-making. The Procurement Data Analyst partners closely with Procurement, Finance, Accounting, Internal Audit, Legal, Information Security, Business Technology, and other cross-functional stakeholders to strengthen operational controls, improve process effectiveness, and support enterprise risk management objectives.

Unlike traditional procurement roles focused on sourcing, contracting, supplier negotiations, or category management, this position is centered on procurement operations, analytics, governance, controls, reporting, and technology enablement. The successful candidate will combine strong analytical skills, business acumen, and technical expertise to identify risks, uncover process inefficiencies, improve data quality, and support ongoing transformation initiatives across the procurement function.

What you’ll do…

Procurement Data Governance & System Integrity

  • Serve as a primary steward of procurement master data, ensuring accuracy, completeness, consistency, and integrity across procurement systems and platforms.
  • Maintain and monitor supplier records, procurement hierarchies, approval structures, delegated authority matrices, user permissions, organizational relationships, and workflow configurations.
  • Conduct recurring data quality reviews to identify duplicate records, incomplete data, stale records, policy violations, approval anomalies, and system inconsistencies.
  • Support procurement technology governance activities by evaluating system configurations, controls, and operational effectiveness.
  • Develop and maintain governance standards related to procurement data management, user access, and system administration support.


Operational Reporting & Analytics

  • Create recurring and ad hoc reports that provide visibility into procurement activity, workflow performance, stakeholder adoption, approval cycle times, supplier onboarding metrics, and policy compliance.
  • Monitor reporting accuracy and consistency to ensure executive decision-making is supported by reliable and trustworthy data.


Compliance, Audit & Controls Management

  • Support internal and external audit activities by preparing audit-ready documentation, evidence repositories, reporting packages, and control validation materials.
  • Track compliance exceptions and control failures while ensuring timely remediation activities are completed.
  • Maintain documentation supporting audit readiness, control design, process ownership, and operational procedures.
  • Assist in control effectiveness testing and remediation planning.


Workflow Monitoring & Operational Excellence

  • Monitor procurement intake channels, approval workflows, supplier onboarding activities, and operational processes for accuracy, efficiency, and compliance.
  • Analyze workflow performance data to identify bottlenecks, delays, approval routing issues, and process inefficiencies.
  • Monitor procurement technology adoption rates and user engagement trends.


Process Improvement & Automation

  • Support system implementations, upgrades, workflow redesign efforts, and procurement transformation initiatives.
  • Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT), validation activities, release testing, and post-implementation support.
  • Document business requirements, process maps, operating procedures, and system enhancement recommendations.

Who you are?

  • Bachelor’s degree in business administration, Finance, Accounting, Supply Chain, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Management Information Systems, Economics, or related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in procurement operations, procurement analytics, business operations, governance, compliance, finance operations, data management, or related analytical functions.
  • Experience developing dashboards, scorecards, reports, KPIs, and executive-level analytics.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills include PivotTables, Power BI, Power Query, complex formulas, and data modeling.
  • Experience working with procurement, ERP, workflow, or spend-management platforms. Zip experience is a plus.
  • Demonstrated experience analyzing large data sets and identifying operational insights.
  • Experience supporting audit, compliance, governance, or internal control activities.
  • Strong understanding of business processes, workflow controls, and operational risk management.
  • Excellent written, verbal, and presentation communication skills.
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Demonstrated ability to independently identify problems, perform root-cause analysis, and drive solutions to completion.
    Preferred Skills and Experience
  • 5+ years of experience supporting procurement operations, procurement technology, business analytics, finance operations, governance, or enterprise systems.
  • Experience supporting procurement platforms including Zip, Coupa, SAP, Ariba, Workday, Oracle, ServiceNow, Jaggaer, Ivalua, or related solutions.
  • Advanced experience with Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Alteryx, Power Automate, Power Query, or other similar platforms.
  • Experience supporting SOX controls, internal audits, risk management programs, or regulatory compliance initiatives.
  • Knowledge of procurement processes including intake management, supplier onboarding, approvals, purchase requisitions, purchase orders, and procurement governance.
  • Experience with enterprise workflow platforms and approval routing configurations.
  • Familiarity with master data management principles and enterprise data governance frameworks.
  • Process improvement certification or methodology exposure (Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen, Agile, PMP, or similar).
  • Experience developing executive dashboards and operational performance scorecards.

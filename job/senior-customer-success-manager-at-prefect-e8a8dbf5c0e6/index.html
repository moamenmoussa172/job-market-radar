About Prefect

Prefect builds automation for an unpredictable world. The last decade of automation was about protecting workflows from unpredictability; in the agentic era, unpredictability is the point. Our mission is to give people confidence in automated work, whether that work is a mission-critical data pipeline or a fleet of AI agents.

In August 2026, Prefect and Dagster, competitors for eight years, joined forces to build the next generation of automation infrastructure. Today, our ecosystem includes Dagster, the data platform; Prefect, the agent platform; and FastMCP, the leading open-source developer framework for the MCP ecosystem. Together, our open-source and commercial products are trusted by Fortune 500 companies, data innovators, and high-growth technology companies around the world.

We think of our culture as the operating system of the company. We’ve carefully created a supportive, high-performance environment that empowers our team to do the best work of their careers, have meaningful impact, and continue growing personally and professionally. We’re a remote-first team that values high standards, ownership, and thoughtful collaboration.

Role Summary:

We are seeking a skilled Senior Customer Success Manager (Expansion) to drive growth within our existing customer base. In this role, you will own revenue growth and retention for our mid-market and enterprise customers, working in close partnership with Account Executives and Sales Engineering. As the commercial point of contact post-initial sale, you'll be in charge of onboarding and identifying and closing upsell and cross-sell opportunities while ensuring high customer satisfaction and retention.

The ideal candidate is a relationship-driven "farmer" who excels at building strong customer partnerships and strategically growing B2B accounts, with the technical aptitude to engage credibly with developer audiences. You'll own customer relationships across both Prefect and Dagster, developing an understanding of each product's use cases, technical audiences, and adoption paths to identify opportunities for growth and expansion. You'll need to participate confidently in technical conversations, knowing when to provide direct guidance, leverage existing resources, or bring in Sales Engineering expertise. Your ability to continuously deepen your knowledge of both platforms and translate technical capabilities into business value will be essential to your success.

You'll report to Prefect's Director of Expansion and Partnerships, Alejandro Palencia.

What You'll Do:

  • Own and grow a portfolio of mid-market and enterprise customer accounts, developing deep understanding of their business objectives and use cases
  • Lead the onboarding process for new customers, ensuring a smooth transition from sales to success by providing clear guidance, product training, and strategic recommendations to drive early adoption and value realization.
  • Engage confidently in developer product discussions while knowing when to leverage Sales Engineering expertise, documentation, or other resources to address the most complex technical questions beyond your knowledge
  • Drive upsell and cross-sell revenue by identifying, qualifying, and closing expansion opportunities within your accounts
  • Manage the renewal process for all contracts in your portfolio, ensuring high retention rates and expanded commitments where possible
  • Emphasizes proactive account ownership, orchestrating internal teams, connecting technical adoption to business value, and identifying opportunities through usage trends.
  • Build and maintain strategic account plans that map customer needs to Prefect solutions
  • Meet or exceed quarterly and annual targets for expansion ARR and Net Dollar Retention
  • Strong emphasis on technical credibility, understanding complex customer environments, turning usage data into actionable recommendations, and owning outcomes across stakeholders.

Your Qualifications:

  • 5+ years of experience in B2B SaaS account management, customer success, or consultative sales, with a focus on expansion and renewals
  • 2+ years of experience working in account management at a developer tools/technical B2B SaaS company
  • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding revenue growth targets in an existing customer context
  • Technical proficiency to engage credibly in developer product conversations, with the judgment to know when to answer directly, point to resources, or loop in Sales Engineering
  • Proven ability to continuously self-educate on technical products and translate that knowledge into expansion opportunities
  • Experience serving as a bridge between technical users and business decision-makers in B2B SaaS environments
  • Excellence in relationship building and maintaining high customer satisfaction
  • Well-developed organizational skills and ability to manage multiple accounts and opportunities simultaneously
  • Demonstrated ability to navigate ambiguity, independently solve complex problems with minimal oversight, and build scalable processes in fast paced, high growth environments.

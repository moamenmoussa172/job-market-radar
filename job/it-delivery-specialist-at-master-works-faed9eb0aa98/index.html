IT Delivery, Specialist

About the job

SOOR a subsidiary of Master Works, helps organizations stay ahead of evolving cyber threats through advanced cybersecurity and secure infrastructure solutions. SOOR delivers resilient, scalable, and regulation-aligned security across governance, cloud, networks, and managed defense.

SOOR is looking for an IT Delivery, Specialist to support the planning, coordination, and successful delivery of IT projects and technology initiatives. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, vendors, and stakeholders to ensure projects are executed efficiently while maintaining high-quality standards and meeting business objectives.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the delivery of IT projects and technical initiatives.
  • Coordinate implementation activities with internal teams and external vendors.
  • Track project milestones, risks, and deliverables.
  • Support project reporting and status updates.
  • Participate in project meetings and follow up on action items.
  • Assist in issue and risk management.
  • Support testing, deployment, and transition activities.
  • Maintain project documentation and records.
  • Ensure project activities align with organizational standards and timelines.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, MIS, or a related field.
  • 3–5 years of experience in IT project delivery, project coordination, or a similar role.
  • Proven experience supporting IT projects from planning through implementation.
  • Experience working with cross-functional teams and external vendors.
  • Strong understanding of project management methodologies and best practices.
  • Excellent organizational and coordination skills.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Professional proficiency in English (written and spoken).

About this Role

We are seeking an experienced cybersecurity professional to join the Application In-Life Assessment (AILA) Germany team, based in Pune or Bangalore.
In this role, you will conduct security-focused architecture reviews and design and technical security assessments to evaluate the risks and compliance of Vodafone products and services. You will work across network and architecture security, technology risk management, cloud, data centre and application security while ensuring alignment with Vodafone security policies, controls and recognised security frameworks.
You will also contribute to AILA leadership decisions on security and risk management, collaborate with stakeholders across Vodafone global entities, improve assessment processes and help identify opportunities to develop the VOIS cybersecurity practice.
The position requires 10 to 15 years of IT security experience, including substantial experience in security architecture and perimeter security technologies such as firewalls, VPNs, proxies and network engineering or management

What you’ll do

erform Application In-Life Assessments and deliver security and risk assessments for received requests.
Understand business and technical requirements and translate them into appropriate security requirements.
Conduct security-focused architecture, design and technical assessments of products and services.
Analyse solution designs and recommend practical measures to achieve compliance with Vodafone security policies, controls and recognised practices while supporting business requirements and documenting risks.
Review products and services, identify applicable security controls from established control sets and assess compliance against those controls.
Identify opportunities to enhance controls and maintain compliance with Vodafone policies, standards and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
Provide expertise and input into AILA leadership decisions relating to security and risk management.
Coordinate process setup activities and support consistent assessment delivery.
Manage stakeholder expectations and communications across Vodafone global entities.
Proactively escalate security issues, concerns and risks to relevant stakeholders.
Work collaboratively with Cyber Security, Technology, project and business teams to support secure products, services and solutions.
Lead new initiatives and process improvements to increase operational efficiency and support continuous optimisation across teams.
Contribute to the development of new business opportunities and the strategic growth of the VOIS cybersecurity practice.
Use data to produce reports and presentations that accurately communicate performance and trends.

Who you are

10 to 15 years of IT security experience with in-depth understanding of security architecture and perimeter security, including firewalls, VPNs, proxies and network engineering or management.
Expertise in information security, particularly network and architecture security.
Expertise in technology risk management and experience conducting information security risk assessments.
Good understanding of cloud security, data centre security and application security.
Knowledge and practical experience of information security standards and frameworks such as ISO 27001, ISO 22301, ISO 31001, NIST 800-53 and OWASP.
Good understanding of Security by Design, OWASP principles and risk frameworks.
Understanding of security controls and their role within an organisation.
Ability to analyse complex problems, structure and simplify them, and develop effective solutions.
Excellent communication, influencing, interpersonal, negotiation and stakeholder management skills.
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.
Ability to analyse data and create clear reports and presentations covering performance and trends.
Ability to communicate recommendations effectively, influence change and establish effective processes and structures.
The following experience or qualifications would be advantageous:
Cybersecurity certifications such as CISA, CISM, ISO 27001 or ISO 31001.
Knowledge of information security assurance frameworks, including NIST SP 800-37 and COBIT 5.
Knowledge of data privacy, information protection and GDPR.
Experience within the telecommunications industry.
In-depth knowledge of telecommunications networks, IP networks, IT infrastructure, applications and relevant security technologies, standards, regulations and methodologies.

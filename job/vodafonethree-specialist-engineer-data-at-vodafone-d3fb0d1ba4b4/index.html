VodafoneThree - Specialist Engineer - Data

About the job

Location: Remote with Customer presence + Location Independent Worker
Salary: Excellent basic salary plus bonus and Vodafone benefits
Working hours: Full time 37.5 hours per week – Monday to Friday + On-Call - 1 in 4 Rota
Security Clearance: This position requires the ability & willingness to undergo and obtain a Secure Vetting (SC) clearance.
Location Independent Worker
As a Location Independent Worker, for operational reasons you may be required to perform your duties, either temporarily or permanently, from any of our offices or customer sites within a reasonable geographic area of your home address. Our “Office in a Box” home working kit will provide you with everything you need, no matter where you are.

What you’ll do

  • Assure/ Incident Management of Cisco Managed LAN and WLAN
  • Management of day-to-day project and provide activities
  • Achieve agreed performance levels in line with business targets, customer requirements and functional SLA’s
  • Escalate issues and potential delays through Line Management channels in accordance with standard procedures to ensure that SLA’s are not breached
  • Maintenance of site documentation
  • Liaise with Project Engineers, Project Managers, Service Desk and High Care Delivery colleagues on a day to day basis
  • Attend and repair customer faults within agreed timescales
  • Role is predominantly remote based, however there will be the requirement to travel to customer sites in and around the West Midlands area and sometimes South of the UK on an adhoc basis, whilst also working on a standby rota once fully up to speed in the role.

Who you’ll be

  • Extensive experience with Cisco LAN and Wireless LAN technologies, including switching and IP addressing best practice.
  • Good understanding Cisco Voice Technologies.
  • Experience in configuring networks in a managed data-networking environment, ideally coupled with a networking qualification.
  • Self-motivating individual with the ability to develop strong customer relationships as well as functioning in a team environment.
  • Good interpersonal skills with effective communication.
  • Holds valid SC Clearance, if not then minimum must be SC clearable, as the role will require the successful candidate to be SC Cleared.

About the job

Senior Data Scientist

Hej, Truecaller is calling you from Stockholm, Sweden! Ready to pick up?

Our goal is to make communication smarter, safer, and more efficient, all while building trust everywhere. We're all about bringing you smart services with a big social impact, keeping you safe from fraud, harassment, scam calls, or messages, so you can focus on the conversations that matter.

  • Top 20 most downloaded apps and the leading caller ID and spam-blocking service, powered by AI and used by over 420 million people every month.
  • Founded in 2009 and listed on Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap, with a strong track record of innovation, sustainable growth and profitability.
  • A team of around 400 people representing 35+ nationalities, based in Stockholm and across offices in Bangalore, Mumbai, Gurgaon and Tel Aviv.

As a Senior Data Scientist for the Search team, you’ll lead high-impact initiatives that directly shape user experiences across core features like Caller ID, Spam & Fraud Protection, and Name Resolution. Your work will drive innovation in spam detection, name accuracy, and large-scale experimentation. Ideal for someone who excels at the crossroads of data science, product, and engineering— leveraging ML, stats, and data engineering to solve meaningful, real-world problems.

The impact you will create:

  • Exploring the problem space and finding data science solutions that provide step increases in customer experience.
  • Planning data science work for the team, with an emphasis on collaboration to achieve common goals.
  • Lead efforts to detect and prevent scam, ensuring user safety and platform trust.
  • Drive the development and optimization of name election algorithms to improve identity accuracy.
  • Design and run large-scale experiments to improve user experience and feature performance.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and analytics teams to implement data-driven solutions.
  • Translate complex data insights into clear strategies and actions.

What you bring:

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Machine Learning, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, or a related field.
  • 7+ years of experience applying data science and machine learning to real-world problems.
  • Experience leading data science projects, collaborating across teams and driving solutions from idea to production.
  • Strong programming skills in Python and SQL.
  • Solid understanding of Python development tools, including IDEs, testing frameworks and static code analysis.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain complex data and insights to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Strong problem-solving skills, a customer-focused mindset and the ability to navigate ambiguous requirements.

It would be great if you also have:

  • Experience scaling machine learning solutions in production to large user bases.
  • Experience querying and working with very large datasets (we use BigQuery).
  • Experience collaborating with senior stakeholders and C-suite executives.
  • Experience working with mobile app data.
  • Knowledge of graph models.
  • Experience with fraud detection or recommendation systems.
  • Experience with agentic AI.

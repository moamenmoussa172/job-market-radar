About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

The Growth Platform (Marketing Technology) team’s vision is to drive long term sustainable growth for the Airbnb community. Our mission is to build best-in-class agentic systems, and capabilities to support the growth of all Airbnb products, current and future.

The External Search & AI Optimization team's mission is to make Airbnb the most visible and trusted destination wherever people search for travel, from traditional search engines to AI answer engines and assistants. We build landing pages, structured content, and platform that help travelers discover stays, experiences, and services across hundreds of thousands of destinations and a broad range of languages and locales. As a product, infrastructure, and growth team within Marketing Technology, we own our stack end to end: CDN, frontend, backend, data pipelines, and AI agents. We partner closely with Product, Design, Marketing, and Data Science, as well as engineering teams across the company, to grow Airbnb's visibility, organic traffic, and bookings.

The Difference You Will Make:

As the Engineering Manager for External Search & AI Optimization, you will lead a team of engineers, from early-career to staff, working across frontend, backend, and data. You will own the engineering strategy and roadmap as travel discovery expands from search results to AI-generated answers, balancing continued growth in traditional search with new investment in making Airbnb easy for AI products to read, cite, and recommend. In your first year, success means measurable growth in Airbnb's visibility, traffic, and bookings from search and AI, a single roadmap shared across functions, and a healthy team whose engineers are growing.

A Typical Day:

  • Lead, coach, and grow a team of full stack and data engineers, and create an environment where they own initiatives end to end.
  • Set technical strategy and roadmap with Product, Design, Marketing, and Data Science, and keep many concurrent cross-functional projects prioritized and moving.
  • Guide the team in building fast, reliable landing pages, content systems, and data pipelines that serve travelers, search crawlers, and AI agents across many languages and locales.
  • Drive responsible use of LLMs to generate, translate, and improve content at scale, with strong evaluation and quality practices.
  • Build the measurement and experimentation needed to show how search and AI referrals drive bookings, and communicate progress and impact to senior leadership.
  • Stay close to the work through design reviews and technical deep dives, and uphold a high bar for engineering quality and reliability.

Your Expertise:

  • 9+ years of relevant software engineering industry experience, including 5+ years of engineering management experience leading teams that own large-scale, user-facing systems.
  • Track record of hiring, growing, and retaining a diverse, high-performing team, including staff-level engineers.
  • Technical breadth across web frontend, backend services, and data pipelines, with the judgment to guide architecture decisions.
  • Experience applying machine learning or LLMs in production, and using experimentation to measure impact.
  • Experience driving growth for a consumer product; search engine optimization, AI answer engine optimization, or other organic growth experience is a plus.
  • Strong communication and influence with technical and non-technical partners, including Product, Marketing, Data, Finance, and Legal.
  • Comfort leading through ambiguity, balancing short-term business outcomes with long-term technical and team health.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience with technical SEO and search engineering, including crawling, indexing, rendering, structured data, sitemaps, internal linking, and search performance optimisation.
  • Understanding of how SEO and SEM work together, and how engineering can drive automation and optimisation at scale.
  • GEO/AEO and optimisation for AI-powered search experiences.
  • Agile Practice for AI Production: Experience with the entire AI product development lifecycle from incubation to production at scale, following agile practices in the Applied AI/ML domain.
  • Infrastructure Acumen: Experience building robust testing frameworks for agent behavior validation and continuous improvement, and driving architectural requirements on agentic systems

How We'll Take Care of You:

The base pay range is subject to change and may be modified in the future. This role may also be eligible for bonus, equity, benefits, and Employee Travel Credits.

A Note on Recruiting Scams: Scammers sometimes pose as Airbnb recruiters to get money or personal information from candidates. A few things that will always be true for Airbnb’s hiring process: our open roles are posted on Airbnb’s Career’s Page at careers.airbnb.com, and our recruiters correspond only from @airbnb.com or @ext.airbnb.com email addresses. Our recruiters will never ask for your Social Security number, bank account details, passport, or payment app information while you are interviewing. We’ll also never ask you to pay a fee, send money, deposit or cash a check, or purchase work-related equipment (such as a company laptop) during the interview process. We encourage candidates to remain vigilant of these recruiting scams and not share sensitive information if you do not believe an individual is actually affiliated with Airbnb.

About this Role

We are seeking an Enterprise Network Operations Lead to provide advanced technical support across enterprise network infrastructure, with a focus on resolving complex incidents, improving network performance and supporting operational excellence. The role is based in Pune within the VNO function and GNO Enterprise Connectivity-Tech Ops department, supporting resilient, secure and cost-effective IT infrastructure across routing, switching, wireless, data centre and security technologies

What you’ll do

•Provide third-line support for complex enterprise network environments, ensuring timely investigation and resolution of critical incidents.
•Lead troubleshooting activities across technologies including VRF, BGP, OSPF, EIGRP and Cisco ACI.
•Manage and maintain enterprise network infrastructure consisting of Cisco Catalyst 3700, 3900, 4500 and 9000 series switches, Cisco Nexus 7000 and 9000 platforms, Cisco ASR, Cisco Catalyst Center and Wi-Fi 6/7 wireless networks.
•Monitor network performance and proactively identify opportunities to improve stability, availability and capacity.
•Analyse capacity and performance reports and recommend improvements to network quality and operational efficiency.
•Conduct root cause analysis (RCA) and contribute to continuous service improvement initiatives.
•Support and mentor Network Operations Centre teams through technical guidance and knowledge sharing.
•Collaborate closely with Design, Engineering, NOC, Security, Core, IPBB, Change Management, MIM and CSB teams.
•Support the implementation of network changes, upgrades and new technology deployments.
•Interface with technology vendors to resolve hardware and software issues through effective escalation management.
•Ensure compliance with security, governance and operational standards.
•Participate in change management activities and support risk mitigation during deployments.
•Identify opportunities for automation and digitisation of operational processes and documentation.
•Maintain accurate network documentation, including diagrams, configurations and operating procedures.

Who you are

•Experience supporting and managing large-scale enterprise network infrastructures.
•Strong technical expertise in routing, switching, wireless networking and data centre technologies.
•Hands-on knowledge of Cisco Catalyst, Cisco Nexus, Cisco ASR, Cisco ACI, Cisco Catalyst Center and Wireless LAN technologies.
•Strong understanding of network protocols including VRF, BGP, OSPF and EIGRP.
•Experience with VSS switching environments and enterprise wireless solutions.
•Familiarity with network monitoring and diagnostic tools such as SolarWinds, NetFlow and Wireshark.
•Strong analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
•Ability to manage high-severity incidents and perform effectively in a fast-paced operational environment.
•Experience working with cross-functional technical teams and external vendors.
•Understanding of network security and operational compliance requirements.
•Exposure to automation and scripting technologies such as Python and Ansible would be advantageous.
•Customer-focused mindset with a strong commitment to improving service delivery and reducing MTTR.
•Professional networking certification such as CCNP.

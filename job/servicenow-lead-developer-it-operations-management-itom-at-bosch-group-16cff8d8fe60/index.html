About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Lead the design, development and technical delivery of ServiceNow IT Operations Management (ITOM) solutions across Discovery, Service Mapping, Event Management/AIOps, MID Server and monitoring integrations. Remain hands-on with platform configuration, scripting and integrations; improve CMDB-aligned service visibility, event-to-action automation and production reliability while guiding developers and coordinating cross-team delivery.

Key Responsibilities

Discovery, MID Server & Service Mapping Delivery

  • Discovery Engineering: Lead development and troubleshooting of Discovery schedules, credentials, classification, patterns, probes and sensors across approved infrastructure.
  • MID Server Reliability: Configure and troubleshoot MID Servers, access, capabilities, certificates and secure connectivity; resolve discovery and integration failures.
  • Service Mapping: Lead implementation and maintenance of application service maps, dependency relationships and service impact paths aligned with CMDB/CSDM.

Event Management, Integrations & CMDB

  • Event Management/AIOps: Implement monitoring event ingestion, event rules, alert binding, deduplication, correlation, grouping and incident/automation triggers.
  • Monitoring Integrations: Build and troubleshoot supported connectors, REST APIs, webhooks and MID Server-based integrations with enterprise monitoring tools.
  • CMDB Data Integrity: Improve CI identification, relationships and IRE-aware ingestion; address duplicate, stale or missing CIs with CMDB stakeholders.

Technical Leadership, Quality & Operations

  • Hands-on Technical Lead: Translate requirements into designs, estimates and deliverables; develop and review JavaScript/Glide API solutions and reusable automation.
  • Quality & Release Delivery: Guide peer reviews, functional/automated testing, source control, deployment readiness and secure, upgrade-safe implementations.
  • Production Ownership: Lead root-cause analysis of complex ITOM incidents, optimize performance and reliability, and mentor developers in troubleshooting.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications

  • Experience: 13–16 years in software/IT operations engineering, including extensive hands-on ServiceNow ITOM development and technical leadership.
  • Discovery & MID Server: Proven expertise in patterns, schedules, credentials, probes/sensors, MID Server configuration, diagnostics and infrastructure connectivity.
  • Service Mapping: Strong practical experience developing service maps, CI dependencies and CMDB/CSDM-aligned service models.
  • Event Management: Experience with event ingestion, alert correlation, CI binding, AIOps capabilities and enterprise monitoring integrations.
  • Development & Integration: Strong JavaScript/Glide APIs, REST/JSON, scripting and knowledge of SSH, WMI, SNMP and secure integration patterns.
  • Engineering Leadership: Experience in design reviews, developer mentoring, troubleshooting, testing, releases and L2/L3 production support.

Additional / Preferred Experience

  • Advanced ITOM: Experience with alert noise reduction, cloud discovery, Service Graph Connectors and service-impact driven automation.
  • Automation & Infrastructure: Exposure to Python/PowerShell, IntegrationHub, Workflow Studio/Flow Designer, CI/CD and Linux/Windows administration.
  • Cross-Domain Experience: Familiarity with ITSM incident/change processes, CMDB health, IRE rules and CSDM adoption.

Certifications

  • Preferred: ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Application Developer (CAD).
  • Highly Desirable: Relevant ServiceNow ITOM implementation certification(s), e.g. Discovery, Service Mapping or Event Management.

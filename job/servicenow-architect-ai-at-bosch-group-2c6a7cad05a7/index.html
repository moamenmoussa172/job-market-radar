About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Own the target architecture, technical standards and cross-domain design decisions for a scalable ServiceNow platform. Provide architectural direction for Workflow Studio, IntegrationHub, App Engine, portals, AI capabilities and enterprise data integration while safeguarding security, maintainability, CSDM alignment and operational resilience.

Key Responsibilities

Architecture and technical governance

  • Platform Architecture: Define platform reference architectures, scope boundaries, integration patterns, design standards and reusable solution blueprints.
  • AI & Emerging Technology Architecture: Define solution architectures leveraging Now Assist, Platform Intelligence, AI agents, Workflow Studio, Integration Hub, and App Engine.
  • Architecture Governance: Lead architectural design reviews, guard against unnecessary customizations and decide when native functionality is preferable to custom development.

Integration, Application & AI Solution Design

  • Integration & Application Architecture: Design complex flows, Scripted REST APIs, Service Portal/Employee Center architecture, scoped application isolation, ACLs and secure integration patterns.
  • AI & Agentic Integration: Architect Now Assist, AI agents, and intelligent workflows across ServiceNow and enterprise systems.
  • Connectivity & Security: Govern MID Server connectivity, data flows, authentication/secrets handling, performance, monitoring and enterprise release architecture.
  • CSDM & ITIL Alignment: Align application data models to CSDM 5.0 principles, ServiceNow platform governance and ITIL 4 service processes.

AI Architecture Leadership, Governance & Modernization

  • AI Strategy & Roadmap: Translate business outcomes into ServiceNow AI and automation roadmaps, assessing business value and AI consumption cost, risk, scalability, and non-functional requirements before development.
  • Technical Leadership & Assurance: Provide architecture direction, establish design guardrails and quality gates, and govern complex AI, integration, and platform solutions.
  • AI Adoption & Modernization: Drive responsible adoption of Now Assist, AI agents, and emerging capabilities aligned with security, entitlements, governance, and measurable business value.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications:

  • ServiceNow Architecture: Extensive experience in enterprise ServiceNow architecture, platform design, integrations, and technical governance.
  • AI & Agentic Architecture: Strong expertise in Now Assist, Platform Intelligence, AI agents, GenAI, AI Search, and intelligent workflow design.
  • Workflow & Integration: Deep expertise in Workflow Studio, IntegrationHub, App Engine, REST/Scripted REST APIs, and enterprise integration patterns.
  • AI Security & Governance: Experience designing secure, scalable AI solutions with appropriate ACLs, data access, governance, compliance, and responsible AI controls.
  • Platform Experience: Strong knowledge of Employee Center, scoped applications, upgrade-safe extensions, and platform security.
  • CSDM & Service Management: Strong understanding ofCSDM, CMDB and ITIL 4, with architecture focused on data quality, reliability, compliance, and scalability.
  • ITSM, ITOM & ITAM: Good architectural knowledge acrossITSM, Discovery, Service Mapping, Service Graph, CMDB, and asset lifecycle, enabling AI use cases across service operations.
  • Technical Leadership: Proven ability to define architecture standards, evaluate emerging AI capabilities, lead design reviews, and guide multidisciplinary delivery teams.

Additional / Preferred Experience:

  • AI-Enabled Service Operations: Experience leveraging ITSM, ITOM, ITAM, and CMDB/CSDM as foundations for AI-driven and autonomous service operations.
  • Enterprise AI Integration: Experience integrating ServiceNow AI capabilities with CMDB data, enterprise platforms, APIs, and automation ecosystems.

Certifications:

  • ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Application Developer (CAD) – mandatory.
  • At least two Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) certifications (e.g. CIS – Data Foundations, CIS - ITSM, CIS - Discovery, CIS - Event Management)
  • ServiceNow Certified Technical Architect (CTA) or ServiceNow Certified Master Architect, if held, is highly desirable

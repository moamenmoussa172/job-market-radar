About the job

SAP GTS Compliance Consultant is responsible for designing, configuring, testing, and supporting global trade compliance processes within SAP Global Trade Services (GTS).

The focus is on Compliance Management (Sanctioned Party List screening, export/import legal control, licensing, and embargo checks). However, the candidate must possess a solid functional overview of all core GTS modules (Customs Management, Preference / Trade Risk Management)

The role requires a strong technical-functional bridge mindset: the consultant must be capable of understanding technical changes made in the system (e.g., ABAP code/exits, BADIs, RFC connections, IDoc/web services, XML structures, and batch job schedules), debugging baseline functional issues, and effectively communicating requirements with ABAP developers and system architects.

Roles & Responsibilities :

Capable to visualize the requirement and validate if SAP standard can be used.

  • Configure and support Legal Control & Export/Import Controls (licensing determination, license types, update strategies, and embargo checks).
  • Translate international trade compliance requirements and legal regulations into robust functional specifications and SAP GTS customizing.
  • Conduct gap analyses between standard SAP GTS capabilities and business processes, proposing standard-first solutions whenever possible. Ability to discuss with business and technical teams on GTS requirements.
  • Discuss and provide solution for the new or change in existing business processes.
  • Optimize the current solution for the business processes.
  • Discuss, review and interpret technical specifications, ABAP enhancements, user exits, BADIs (e.g., /SAPSLL/ enhancements), and custom logic implemented by development teams.
  • Define and execute functional unit test cases, end-to-end (E2E) integration test scenarios, and regression test suites.

Educational qualification:

  • B.E., B.Tech, On equivalent

Experience :

  • 5 - 9 years

Mandatory/requires Skills :

  • SAP GTS Compliance

Preferred Skills :

  • SAP GTS Customs, Preference, SAP SD.

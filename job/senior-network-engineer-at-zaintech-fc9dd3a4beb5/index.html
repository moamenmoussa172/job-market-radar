About the job

The Senior Network Engineer (L3) is responsible for engineering, maintaining and securing ZainTECH’s private and public cloud infrastructure across multiple data centers in the Middle East. The role owns the most complex network and security incidents, leads root cause analysis and design-level changes in a multi-tenant environment, and acts as the final technical authority for escalations from L1 and L2 engineers.

The Senior Network Engineer is a customer-oriented expert in networking and security who designs solutions and mentors the wider team.

Responsibilities:

Incident, Problem & Change Management

  • Own and resolve the most complex customer network and security incidents, and act as the final technical escalation point for L1 and L2.
  • Perform root cause analysis (RCA) on major and recurring incidents and drive permanent fixes through problem management.
  • Review, approve and lead high-risk change requests for managed service customers, including impact assessment and rollback planning.
  • Work with ServiceNow to manage incident, problem and change records, ensuring high-quality documentation.

Network & Security Engineering

  • Design, deploy and optimize firewall platforms (FortiGate, ASA, Palo Alto) and VPN solutions (SSL & IPSec VPN), including high availability and routing.
  • Architect and tune load balancers and WAF (Kemp & FortiADC) for performance, security and application availability.
  • Lead security policy audits, rule-base clean-up and optimization, and define policy standards for customers.
  • Analyze traffic at packet level (packet captures and flow analysis) across TCP/UDP/ICMP and stateful/stateless protocols.

Vendor Management & Documentation

  • Engage and manage vendor TAC cases (Fortinet, Cisco, Palo Alto, Kemp, etc.) through to closure.
  • Create and maintain network designs, runbooks, SOPs and knowledge base articles.

Team Development & Continuous Improvement

  • Mentor and train L1 and L2 engineers, review their work and lead technical knowledge sharing.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to enhance network security, performance and scalability.
  • Obtain and maintain up-to-date certifications for supported technologies, including CCIE where applicable.
  • Provide input to improve products and services, including automation and service catalogue enhancements.

Our Culture & Code of Conduct:

At ZainTECH, we take pride in a culture built on collaboration, innovation, and uncompromising integrity. We are looking for individuals who share these values and are committed to customer-centricity and ethical excellence. All employees are expected to uphold our Code of Conduct, which serves as a guiding framework for responsible behavior across everything we do — from how we work with each other to how we engage with clients and partners globally.

Requirements

  • 6+ years working in network engineering or network security roles, including at least 3 years in an escalation or senior engineering role.
  • Advanced expertise in firewall design, troubleshooting and policy management (FortiGate, ASA, Palo Alto).
  • Proven experience designing and deploying SSL VPN and IPSec VPN solutions at scale.
  • Advanced knowledge of load balancers and WAF (Kemp & FortiADC), including SSL offloading and application-layer troubleshooting.
  • Strong knowledge of public cloud networking (AWS and Azure), including VPC/VNet design, transit and hybrid connectivity.
  • Expert knowledge of routing protocols (BGP, OSPF) and multi-ISP environments.
  • In-depth knowledge of packet flow, the OSI model and the TCP/IP stack, with hands-on packet capture analysis.
  • Experience supporting large multi-tenant environments and managing complex escalations under SLA.
  • Excellent communication skills, able to explain complex technical issues clearly to customers and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Ability to work in a shift-based support environment and participate in on-call rotation.
  • Proven experience producing technical designs, documentation and mentoring junior engineers.
  • Required Certifications: Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP) Enterprise or CCNP Security (minimum requirement).
  • Preferred Certifications: CCIE Network (Enterprise Infrastructure) or CCIE Security; vendor expert-level certifications such as Palo Alto PCNSE or Fortinet NSE 7; IPT/VoIP qualifications

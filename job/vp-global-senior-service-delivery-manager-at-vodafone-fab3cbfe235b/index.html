About the job

Who we are

VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.
As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. This partnership is a unique, industry-first model which brings together the best of in-house and 3rd party capability.

About this Role

We are seeking an experienced service delivery leader to drive operational excellence, customer satisfaction and business performance across Vodafone Business Enterprise Run Operations. Supporting more than 3,000 global customers, this role is accountable for the end-to-end delivery of critical services across multiple markets and delivery locations. The individual will lead operational performance, service resilience, customer outcomes, transformation initiatives and commercial effectiveness while ensuring services consistently deliver value, improve customer loyalty and support Vodafone’s growth ambitions.

What you’ll do

  • Lead the end-to-end delivery and operational performance of Enterprise Run Operations services across global markets, ensuring service excellence, customer satisfaction and business continuity.
  • Manage and optimise multiple service towers including Connectivity, Unified Communications, Technical Services, Mobility and Enablement functions.
  • Drive service performance through effective governance, service reviews, risk management, escalation management and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Own service transition, new product onboarding and operational readiness activities to ensure successful customer adoption and service stability.
  • Monitor and improve customer experience through service performance metrics, customer satisfaction measures and proactive improvement plans.
  • Lead financial performance activities including budget management, cost control, revenue management, commercial planning and business performance optimisation.
  • Develop and execute operational transformation initiatives leveraging automation, artificial intelligence, analytics and digital platforms to improve productivity and reduce complexity.
  • Build strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders, customers and suppliers to ensure alignment of business priorities and service delivery outcomes.
  • Lead vendor and partner performance management to ensure contractual obligations, service commitments and customer expectations are consistently achieved.
  • Provide leadership for governance, quality management, compliance activities and operational reporting frameworks, including recognised quality standards.

Who you are

  • Significant experience in global service delivery, operational management or customer operations within a large and complex organisation.
  • Strong understanding of enterprise technology services, telecommunications solutions and service management principles.
  • Demonstrated experience leading transformation, automation and continuous improvement programmes that deliver measurable business outcomes.
  • Strong analytical, commercial and strategic thinking skills with the ability to manage complex operational challenges and executive-level escalations.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills, with the ability to collaborate across diverse global teams.
  • Proven experience managing large operational teams, suppliers and cross-functional delivery environments.
  • Strong knowledge of customer experience management, operational governance and service performance management.

What's in it for you

  • Opportunity to lead service delivery for a large global customer portfolio across multiple markets and regions.
  • Exposure to senior business stakeholders and strategic operational decision-making.
  • Ability to influence customer experience, operational transformation and business performance at scale.
  • Opportunity to lead digital transformation initiatives incorporating automation, analytics and artificial intelligence.
  • Global collaboration with cross-functional teams, customers, suppliers and business leaders.
  • Leadership responsibility for operational performance, governance and continuous improvement programmes.

What skills you will learn

  • Advanced service delivery and operational leadership in a global environment.
  • Enterprise customer experience and customer success management.
  • Financial management, commercial planning and operational governance.
  • Service transformation through automation, analytics and emerging technologies.
  • Vendor management, commercial negotiations and partner governance.
  • Strategic stakeholder engagement and executive communication.
  • Agile operating models and large-scale operational change management.

About the job

Join Proton and build a better internet where privacy is the default

Founded in 2014 by scientists who met at CERN, Proton was built on the belief that privacy is a fundamental human right. What started as an encrypted email service has grown into an ecosystem of privacy-first products, including Proton Mail, VPN, Drive, Pass, and Calendar, used by millions of people worldwide.

We’re profitable, independent, and building for the long term. Free from venture capital control and advertising-driven business models, we focus on what matters most: our users. Our work helps protect digital freedom, from everyday privacy to supporting journalists and activists in challenging environments.

Today, we’re an international team of 700+ people, with offices across 9 countries. We have high standards for the people we hire and the work we do. You’ll work alongside talented, driven colleagues who challenge each other, take ownership, and care about getting things right.

Explore our open-source projects.

The Design Team

Proton's Design team shapes the visual and interaction layer across Mail, Drive, VPN, Pass, and Calendar — products used by millions of people, many of whom rely on them in high-stakes moments. We work at the intersection of security and simplicity: our products must inspire trust without feeling intimidating, and feel refined without sacrificing clarity. Designers are embedded within engineering teams and involved from the very beginning — problem framing, planning, implementation, and quality assurance — not brought in at the mockup stage. The team is organized as a guild: high autonomy, shared standards, and real influence over how design is practiced across Proton.

We hold a high craft bar. Typography, layout, spacing, color, iconography, motion — the details most companies treat as polish, we treat as the product. If your best work is the work itself — pixels you're proud to sign — you'll be in good company here.

The role

Proton Mail and Proton Calendar are used by millions of people who chose privacy over convenience. Our job is to make sure they never have to choose. We don't sell user data, use dark patterns or design for engagement at any cost. We build tools people trust with their most personal communication, and we want them to feel as good as, or better than, anything else they could use.
As a Senior Product Designer on the Inbox team, you'll shape the core experience of Mail and Calendar: how people read, write, organize, search and plan, on web and mobile. You'll also design the moments that help people get started, see the value of Proton and bring more of their digital life with them. For us, growth means earning trust, not pushing for clicks: every sign-up is someone choosing privacy, and every upgrade helps fund an internet that doesn't depend on surveillance.
We're looking for someone with real visual taste and strong product thinking, who can turn complex problems into experiences that feel simple, calm and polished. You'll work closely with Product Managers and Engineering as a true partner. You'll help decide which problems we solve and how we solve them, and you'll care just as much about the final pixel as about the overall flow.

What you'll do

  • Design end-to-end experiences across Mail and Calendar, turning complex problems like triage, search and scheduling into intuitive, polished experiences.
  • Set a high visual bar with precise typography, layout, color and hierarchy, and use motion and micro-interactions to guide attention and give the product character.
  • Shape key growth moments, such as onboarding, activation, upgrade and cross-product adoption, so they feel trustworthy and helpful, not pushy.
  • Run experiments with Product Managers and Data, from hypothesis to decision, without lowering the craft bar.
  • Partner with Product Managers and Engineering to frame problems, shape the roadmap and make tradeoffs.
  • Use AI throughout your design process, from framing problems and synthesizing research to exploring variations and prototyping with real models and code, and hold the output to the same quality bar as everything you ship.
  • Help define how AI shows up in Mail and Calendar: clear about what it's doing, easy to undo and private by default

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years designing and shipping digital products at scale.
  • A portfolio with exceptional visual craft and taste (typography, spacing, color, hierarchy, iconography) and strong product thinking.
  • A track record of turning complex problems into simple experiences, including interaction design, motion and micro-interactions.
  • Experience building and evolving a design system.
  • Close partnership with Product Managers and Engineers on strategy and roadmap.
  • Experience with onboarding, activation or monetization flows, running experiments and A/B tests, and good judgment about the line between persuasion and manipulation.
  • Hands-on maker with a strong AI workflow: including prototyping with real models, and the judgment to know when the output is good enough to ship.
  • Clear, confident communication: you can explain and defend your design decisions to any audience.

Nice to have

  • Experience with productivity, communication or calendar products.
  • Strong illustration or icon design skills.
  • Experience working across a multi-product ecosystem.
  • Familiarity with privacy-focused products or designing with strong ethical constraints.
  • Experience shipping production code is a plus.

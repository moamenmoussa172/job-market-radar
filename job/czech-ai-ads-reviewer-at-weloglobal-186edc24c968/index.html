Czech AI Ads Reviewer

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Czechia.

Location: Czech Republic, Remote (Work from Home)

Type: Freelance, Part-time, Flexible Hours

Payment: $11 USD/hour

🚀 About the Role:

Are you a tech-savvy, internet-loving individual who’s fluent in Czech? Join our team as a Czech AI Ads Reviewer In this role, you’ll evaluate online ads to ensure they’re accurate, relevant, and resonate with end users.

This is the perfect opportunity for someone who enjoys exploring the web, uses major search engines daily, and has a knack for understanding what people are looking for with just a few keywords. If you're curious, detail-oriented, and ready for a flexible, fun side job, this could be the role for you!

🛠 Key Responsibilities:

- Review and evaluate Czech-language online ads for quality and relevance.

- Analyze ad content and placement to ensure they meet guidelines and criteria.

- Offer actionable feedback to enhance ad quality and user experience.

- Conduct keyword research to refine ad targeting and user intent.

- Work collaboratively to identify trends and optimize ad delivery strategies.

🌟 What You’ll Bring to the Team:

- Fluency in Czech and a deep understanding of Czech culture and online behavior.

- Strong command of English for communication and reporting.

- A daily user of search engines like Google or Bing, and active on social media.

- Excellent research skills and the ability to process information quickly.

- Detail-oriented with strong problem-solving and analytical abilities.

- A reliable internet connection and a comfortable home working setup.

💡 You’re an Ideal Fit If You:

- Love surfing the web and are familiar with various online platforms.

- Have experience navigating major search engines.

- Can understand user intent based on minimal information or keywords.

- Enjoy exploring new topics and learning through online research.

- Want a flexible, remote opportunity that fits your lifestyle.

🎁 Perks of the Role:

- Flexible hours—work on your schedule!

- Remote work—you can be anywhere in Czech Republic.

- Learn the inner workings of the digital advertising world.

- Sharpen your research and analytical skills.

- Join a collaborative, forward-thinking team.

📥 How to Apply:

If this sounds like the perfect opportunity for you, don’t wait—send us your resume by clicking “Apply” below. We’re excited to hear from you!

To be successful in the process, candidates must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement to protect client confidentiality and pass learning modules and a required quality test designed by our client before starting work.

*Please note, the official title of this position is: Ads Quality Rater. *

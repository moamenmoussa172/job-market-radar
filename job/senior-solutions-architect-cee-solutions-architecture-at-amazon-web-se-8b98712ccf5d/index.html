About the job

Do you like helping customers architect systems and solve challengin technical problems? Would you like to do this using the latest cloud computing technologies and develop solutions using AI? Do you have a knack for helping companies understand application architectures and integration approaches, and the consultative and leadership skills to launch a project on a trajectory to success?
As a Senior Solution Architect, you will have the opportunity to help shape and deliver on a strategy to build mind share and broad use of AWS services within different areas like AI, Big Data & Analytics, compute and more.
Your broad responsibilities will include: owning the technical engagement and ultimate success around specific implementation projects, and developing a deep expertise in the AWS technologies as well as broad know-how around how applications and services are constructed using the AWS platform.
*Please note, this opportunity is open also for Cyprus based applicants*
Key job responsibilities
- Serve as a key member of the Solutions Architecture team in helping to ensure customer success in building applications and services on the AWS platform.
- Scope customer engagements, with attention towards clear and well-defined objectives and success criteria.
- Own each engagement, and help ensure a timely and successful delivery of value.
- Capture and share best-practice knowledge amongst the solution architect community
- Develop prototypes and proof of concepts using AI technologies
About the team
The team is a multi disciplinary team of technologists coming from many different backgrounds. The mission of the team is to help customers achieve their business objectives by leveraging AWS and Amazon technologies
- has experience in one or more specific technology domain areas (e.g. software development, cloud computing, systems engineering, infrastructure, security, data & analytics).
- has experience in design, implementation, or consulting in IT applications or infrastructures.
- has experience in the Fintech industry
- hands-on experience building Machine Learning and Generative AI applications and deploying them end-to-end, from PoC through to production.
- has professional Russian and written language skills

About the job

About Paystack

Paystack’s mission is to power African ambition. Over 300K businesses across Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and Kenya use Paystack’s modern payments gateway, including Qatar Airways, MTN, Burger King, UPS, Africa World Airlines, AXA Mansard Insurance, FairMoney, PiggyVest, Crocs, Under Armour, Richemont Lifestyle Group, and many others.

Over the last 10 years, we’ve built products that have helped shape online payments in Africa, from automated recurring payments to direct bank payments and automated chargebacks. Today, Paystack is part of The Stack Group (TSG), a family of technology brands building modern infrastructure across payments, banking, consumer products, and emerging technologies.

At Paystack, we hire talented people, treat them with genuine respect, and give them the space, resources, and support to do the best work of their lives. We’d love your help.

About the Senior Product Manager role

Our team is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own trust and safety at Paystack. Merchants trust us with their money, their customers and their accounts. This role keeps them safe.

This is a product role, separate from Paystack's risk and compliance function. That function sets policy and runs reviews and investigations. You build the products and systems that keep merchants safe by design, so protection does not depend on case-by-case intervention.

You will lead a new central Trust & Safety product team. What you build serves every Paystack market, and market teams tailor it to local rules and fraud patterns. The role covers fraud prevention, the security of merchant accounts and API keys, the controls on when merchant funds settle or are held, and ongoing AML and merchant monitoring once a merchant is live. A safe merchant is a merchant who keeps getting paid. A blocked good payment or an unexplained hold hurts a merchant as much as fraud does, so both count against the role.

The role reports to the Head of Product. It is strongly preferred if the candidate is based in Lagos, Nigeria.

We’ll trust you to

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for trust and safety across every Paystack market.
  • Build shared safety infrastructure, including rules, limits, risk scoring, monitoring and case management, that market teams configure for local needs.
  • Stop fraud across cards, bank transfers, direct debit and payouts before money leaves Paystack.
  • Protect merchants from account takeover and leaked API keys, with stronger controls on high-risk actions such as charging saved cards and sending transfers.
  • Own the controls that decide when merchant funds settle, are paused or are held, and make every hold clear to the merchant, with a path to resolve it.
  • Monitor merchants after they go live, and catch sudden changes in volume, velocity or recipients in real time.
  • Build AML, sanctions screening and transaction monitoring that meet regulatory obligations through systems, not manual checks.
  • Tune rules and limits so good transactions go through, and treat every false decline and unnecessary hold as harm to a merchant.
  • Keep fraud rates within card scheme thresholds and avoid scheme fines.
  • Give Paystack's risk and compliance teams the tools to review, investigate and decide quickly, and turn their recurring manual work into product.
  • Give every financial control a clear owner, a roadmap and a metric, so money movement security runs as a product and not as a set of one-off fixes.
  • Lead the product side of fraud incidents, then close the gaps they expose, not just the immediate hole.
  • Work with Merchant Experience & Onboarding, Financial Systems and Core Payments so safety controls plug into their systems and merchants hear about them the right way.
  • Define and track the metrics: fraud loss rate, false decline rate, funds held in error, time to detect, time to contain and funds recovered.

You’ll thrive in this role if you

  • 6+ years of product management experience, with at least 3 years in trust and safety, fraud, risk or AML at a payments company, bank or fintech
  • A track record of reducing fraud losses or false declines, with the numbers to show it
  • Experience building rules engines, risk scoring, limits, monitoring or case management tools
  • First-hand knowledge of how fraud works in African payments, including card fraud, account takeover, social engineering and mule accounts
  • A working understanding of AML, sanctions and transaction monitoring obligations for payment companies
  • Strong SQL and data skills, and comfort working with data scientists on models and thresholds
  • Technical fluency with APIs, authentication, event streams and real-time decisioning
  • Experience leading or supporting incident response under pressure
  • A merchant-first view of safety, and sound judgement on the trade-off between protection and conversion
  • The ability to get risk, compliance, legal and engineering teams to agree on one control
  • Clear writing that explains a safety decision to a merchant, an engineer and a regulator
  • Prior experience with acquiring-side or merchant fraud, which is a strong asset

About this Role

Location: Newbury or Paddington (hybrid)

We are seeking an AI Platform Engineer to design, build and evolve the engineering platforms, automation capabilities and developer experiences that enable AI-native software delivery at scale. This role is ideal for individuals with strong software and platform engineering expertise who are passionate about improving engineering productivity, software quality and delivery outcomes across multiple teams. The successful candidate will help create secure, scalable and reusable engineering capabilities that support the safe adoption of AI-assisted software development practices throughout the software development lifecycle.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build and maintain engineering platform capabilities that enable AI-native software delivery at scale.
  • Develop reusable delivery controls, templates, pipelines, automation and engineering accelerators.
  • Embed automated validation, quality checks and secure defaults directly into software delivery workflows.
  • Build and evolve reusable patterns that support the safe and scalable adoption of AI-assisted development.Improve developer experience through streamlined workflows, tooling integration and self-service capabilities.
  • Develop observability and measurement capabilities to provide insights into engineering productivity, quality and platform adoption.
  • Collaborate with Technical Leads and AI Software Engineers to identify recurring engineering challenges and implement platform-based solutions.
  • Enable responsible use of AI-generated code, testing, documentation and engineering artefacts through automated validation.
  • Drive continuous improvement across CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, security scanning and deployment processes.
  • Ensure platform capabilities remain secure, scalable, maintainable and operationally resilient.Evaluate emerging AI engineering tools and recommend adoption where appropriate.
  • Contribute to engineering standards, platform roadmaps and developer enablement initiatives.

Who you are

  • Experienced professional with 7+ years of software engineering, platform engineering or developer tooling experience.
  • Skilled in designing and operating developer platforms, CI/CD capabilities and engineering automation solutions.
  • Strong understanding of cloud-native architectures, distributed systems and modern software delivery practices.
  • Experience building reusable platform capabilities that improve developer productivity and software quality.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted software development tools and engineering automation practices.
  • Good knowledge of AI technologies including models, agents, evaluation frameworks and automated validation tooling.
  • Understanding of LLM cost optimisation, including token monitoring, caching strategies and model selection considerations.
  • Knowledge of approaches for managing and reducing token consumption costs.Deep understanding of observability, automated testing and software delivery tooling.
  • Knowledge of platform security, governance and operational controls.
  • Strong programming, automation and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Passionate about improving developer experience and engineering effectiveness.
  • Able to apply systems thinking to solve organisational engineering challenges through reusable solutions.
  • Effective communicator who can collaborate with engineering teams, technical leaders and platform stakeholders.

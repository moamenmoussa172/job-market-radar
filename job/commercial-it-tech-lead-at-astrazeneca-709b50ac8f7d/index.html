About the job

Commercial IT Tech Lead

ABOUT ASTRAZENECA

AstraZeneca is a global, science-led, patient-focused biopharmaceutical company that focuses on the discovery, development, and commercialisation of prescription medicines for some of the world’s most serious diseases. But we’re more than one of the world’s leading pharmaceutical companies.

Join a high-performing team, empowered to cut through the noise and drive real change. Our agility and pace, coupled with scientific focus, powers us to spot opportunities in the market that keep us moving forward and into new spaces.


SITE DESCRIPTION

AstraZeneca is experiencing a significant transformation in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, driven by a commitment to advancing healthcare, innovation, and sustainable growth. Building on our global legacy, AZ KSA is rapidly evolving its operations to better serve patients, collaborate with healthcare stakeholders, and contribute to the ambitious goals outlined in the Saudi Vision 2030. We are investing in talent, digital solutions, and new ways of working; all designed to enhance our impact on patient outcomes and reinforce our leadership in therapeutic areas. This dynamic environment calls for visionary, agile professionals who thrive in change and are motivated to help shape the future of healthcare in KSA.

ROLE SUMMARY

The Commercial IT Tech Lead is the hands-on technology partner and delivery lead accountable for translating the market Commercial IT roadmap into secure, compliant, resilient and scalable technology solutions and services. The role leads enterprise platform deployment and configuration, local product and solution delivery, technical readiness, service performance and lifecycle management across commercial applications, MarTech and related integrations. It partners closely with AI & Digital Excellence and business owners, while preserving the boundary between business-owned digital strategy and IT-owned technology delivery and operations.

What you’ll do

  • Technology partnership, portfolio and delivery
  • Translate the Commercial IT roadmap into a prioritized delivery plan with clear outcomes, owners, milestones, dependencies, capacity, investment and success measures.
  • Lead end-to-end delivery of local and enterprise capabilities pulled into the market, managing scope, quality, timeline, budget, risk, release readiness and transition into operations following SDLC principles.
  • Shape technical options within delegated authority, reuse strategic platforms and approved patterns, and route non-standard choices through architecture and risk governance.
  • Coordinate International and Global capability teams, suppliers and business stakeholders to deliver agreed outcomes and measurable value.
  • Deliver structure project management approach to ensure clear ownership and accountability across teams, include timeline management and delivery. Managing and escalating risks and opportunities where necessary.

Enterprise platforms and local products/solutions

  • Lead deployment, localization and adoption enablement of enterprise commercial platforms, including CRM, MarTech, workflow, analytics-facing applications and market solutions.
  • Own technical design, configuration, testing, release readiness and operational handover for applications in scope.
  • Ensure APIs, interfaces and data flows follow approved architecture and integration patterns, partnering with the Data & Integration Lead where that role exists.
  • Manage local product backlogs and technical roadmaps, balancing business demand, platform standards, lifecycle risk and available capacity.

Technology operations and lifecycle

  • Own service health, lifecycle, configuration, technical debt and operational performance for solutions in scope.
  • Manage incidents, problems, changes, releases and recurring demand, using service telemetry, root-cause analysis, knowledge, and self-service to improve reliability.
  • Apply approved AI for IT, automation, observability and AIOps with human oversight, least-privilege access, auditability, testing and controlled production change.
  • Maintain accurate application, interface, asset, license, service and supplier records and drive simplification, reuse and decommissioning.
  • Maintain optimal uptime and support relating to key solutions used by the business, and linking to global support teams where required to ensure optimal performance of these solutions.

Technology governance, suppliers and financial control

  • Execute enterprise cyber, privacy, quality, compliance and technology-risk controls; coordinate required architecture, privacy, security, third-party and quality assessments.
  • Document approved data flows, hosting, remote access, access reviews, resilience arrangements and remediation actions; escalate material risks and control gaps promptly.
  • Manage supplier performance, statements of work, SLAs, deliverable acceptance and issue of escalation, without committing spend or contractual risk beyond delegated authority.
  • Support forecasting, contract and license management and transparent value tracking for initiatives in scope.

Business partnership and team leadership

  • Translate business priorities into clear technical requirements, solution choices, and delivery commitments.
  • Provide technology architecture, platform, cyber, privacy, delivery and operational expertise to business-led digital, customer experience, analytics and AI initiatives.
  • Agree business ownership, technical ownership, acceptance criteria, operational readiness and outcome measures before delivery.
  • Lead or coordinate employees, secondees and supplier resources through line or matrix arrangements; build engineering, DevSecOps, service and AI-enabled delivery capability.

Outcomes and success measures

  • Milestone predictability, budget variance, release quality and benefits enabled jointly with business owners.
  • Service availability, incident recurrence, mean time to restore, change success, and completion of resilience actions.
  • Platform reuse, adoption readiness, reduction of technical debt and retirement of redundant applications or manual processes.
  • Timely vulnerability remediation, access reviews, control actions and audit-ready evidence.
  • Support to ensure project / solutions benefits realization and illustration back to business stakeholders, illustrating that solutions continue to support the greater business objectives and benefits.

Typical accountabilities

  • Conduct all activities in line with company values, the Code of Ethics, applicable policies, standards and required training.
  • Implement applicable GxP, quality, privacy, cyber security, records-management, third-party risk, identity and access, business-continuity and disaster-recovery controls.
  • Maintain audit-ready evidence, promptly report material risks or control gaps, and obtain approvals from authorised control functions before go-live or material change.
  • People-management responsibilities apply only where formally assigned and may include objective setting, performance and development management, talent planning, recruitment, safety and delegated financial approvals.

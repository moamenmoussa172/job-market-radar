About the job

Overview

We are seeking a mid-level Data Consultant to design, implement, and maintain robust data models and data exchange solutions supporting manufacturing and plant logistics operations. In this role, you will work hands-on with business and functional teams to translate operational supply chain workflows into logical, physical, and dimensional models, while developing the underlying Oracle PL/SQL mechanisms and data integration pipelines.

Roles & Responsibilities :
Data Consultant (Data Modeling & Logistics Solutions)

Data Modeling & Architecture: Design, implement, and maintain logical and physical data models, dimensional schemas (star/snowflake), and data vault structures using Oracle Data Modeler or equivalent tools.

  • Implementation Projects: Actively contribute to and execute end-to-end data modeling and engineering implementation and rollout lifecycles for business-critical operational applications.
  • Database Programming & Customization: Develop and maintain stored procedures, packages, functions, and triggers in Oracle PL/SQL tailored to business requirements and operational logic.
  • Data Integration & Exchange: Design and implement robust data interface and delta-capture mechanisms, ETL routines, and automated file exchange processes (SFTP/FTP) between ERP, WMS/MES, and downstream data repositories.
  • Performance Tuning & Debugging: Perform query optimization, indexing strategies, debugging, and execution-plan tuning to ensure high performance and data accuracy.
  • Data Movement & Utilities: Leverage database utilities such as SQL*Loader, Data Pump, and export/import tools for initial loads, migrations, and regular feeds.
  • Stakeholder Consulting: Work closely with cross-functional logistics teams and business analysts to gather requirements, establish data dictionaries, and validate business rules.

Experience :

5+ years of hands-on experience in data modeling, engineering and database development, including participation in at least 1–2 implementation projects.

Title: Data Consultant – Data Modeling
Experience Level: 4 – 6 Years (5+ years preferred)
Domain: Manufacturing Operations, Supply Chain & Plant Logistics
Practice Area: Data Solutions & Consulting (Application/Solution Modeling)

Mandatory/requires Skills :
Data Modeling Paradigms:

  • Strong conceptual and practical knowledge of dimensional, logical, and physical data modeling; understanding of Data Vault architecture is a strong plus.
  • Practical proficiency with Oracle Data Modeler or equivalent modeling tools
  • Oracle & SQL Development:
  • Strong hands-on proficiency in SQL and advanced PL/SQL (stored procedures, packages, functions, triggers).
  • Strong RDBMS fundamentals, schema design, and constraints management.
  • Solid working knowledge of SQL*Loader, Data Pump, and data movement utilities.
  • Proven debugging techniques and query/performance tuning methodologies.
  • Integration & Automation: Experience building data interfaces, ETL workflows, SFTP/FTP data transfer mechanisms, and basic automation scripting.
  • Domain Knowledge (Preferred): Working knowledge or exposure to manufacturing logistics, plant supply chain, inventory movement, or warehousing (WMS/ERP interfaces).
  • Interpersonal Skills: Strong oral and written communication skills with the ability to articulate technical data designs to business and functional teams.

Preferred Skills :
Databricks, Oracle APEX, PowerBI

Educational qualification:

B.E. / B.Tech / MCA / M.Sc in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.

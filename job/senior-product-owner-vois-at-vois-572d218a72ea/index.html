About this role

We are seeking an experienced Product Owner to lead the end-to-end product lifecycle for new and existing digital products. You will help shape the product vision and roadmap, guide feature development and continuous improvement, define success measures, and ensure delivery remains aligned with customer needs and business objectives. You will collaborate with product teams and stakeholders across different organisational levels while providing guidance to less experienced Product Owners.

What you’ll do

Contribute to the product vision and strategy, define features, and prioritise the product backlog according to customer and business value.Communicate the product roadmap, expected business benefits, delivery priorities, and dependencies clearly to stakeholders and delivery teams.Plan product releases and manage dependencies across short-term deliverables and longer-term product goals.Assess how proposed features may affect product strategy, operations, business processes, and the customer experience.Define product objectives, KPIs and OKRs, monitor progress, maintain roadmap alignment, and review work completed by less experienced Product Owners.

Who you are

You have at least six years of relevant experience, supported by a qualification in Computer Science, Engineering, or an equivalent combination of education and professional experience.You have previous experience working as a senior-level Product Owner and leading or mentoring other Product Owners.You have expert knowledge of Agile practices and software development, with experience managing product backlogs, roadmaps, releases, and complex technical dependencies.You can define and deliver Minimum Viable Products, conduct A/B tests, interpret data, identify root causes, and make evidence-based product decisions.You use analytical thinking and creative problem-solving to address detail while maintaining a clear view of wider product goals. You can establish new workstreams, squads, or business areas and communicate confidently with stakeholders at different organisational levels.

What's in it for you

The opportunity to influence products throughout their complete lifecycle, from initial concept and feature development to continuous improvement.Responsibility for shaping product strategy, roadmaps, delivery priorities, KPIs and OKRs.Exposure to complex digital transformation initiatives, new workstreams, multidisciplinary squads, and varied business areas.The opportunity to collaborate with stakeholders across multiple organisational levels.Scope to strengthen your product leadership capabilities by mentoring Product Owners and supporting consistent delivery standards.

What skills you'll learn

Advanced product strategy, roadmap planning, prioritisation, and lifecycle management.Release planning and dependency management across complex technical environments.Data-informed product development through experimentation, A/B testing, KPI analysis, and root-cause assessment.Stakeholder communication and influence across delivery teams and senior organisational audiences.Product leadership, coaching, quality oversight, and the onboarding of new squads, workstreams, and business areas.

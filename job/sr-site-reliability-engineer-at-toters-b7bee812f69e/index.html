About the job

The Company

Toters is an on-demand e-commerce and delivery platform and operates a service that enables customers to get anything in their city at the highest level of convenience.

At Toters, technology is at the heart of everything we do. We have product teams that are working hard every day to create products that make our customers' lives easier. Our engineers are also continuously creating solutions to make our processes more efficient, all in an effort to get to our customers fast and at the best cost. If you are interested in working in a high growth startup environment, and look to be part of a team that will potentially change the way customers shop in the Middle East, apply now.

About the role

Toters runs a realtime delivery platform that customers, shoppers and merchants depend on every minute. As a Senior SRE you’ll own production reliability: lead incidents, define what “reliable” means for our users, and engineer the platform so failures are rare, contained and quick to recover from.

What you’ll do

  • Lead incidents. Act as Incident Commander during major outages. Coordinate diagnosis, communication and recovery, then run blameless postmortems that lead to real fixes.
  • Define reliability. Set SLIs, SLOs and error budgets for critical user journeys, and use them to guide alerting, release decisions and engineering priorities.
  • Make systems observable. Design metrics, logs and traces so that questions about production can be answered quickly. Alert on symptoms users feel, not on noise.
  • Engineer out toil. Automate recovery, remediation and routine operations, and treat on-call load as a signal to fix the system, not just staff it.
  • Make change safe. Improve how code and infrastructure reach production through progressive delivery, fast rollbacks and infrastructure as code.
  • Plan for scale and failure. Own capacity planning, load behavior, dependency failure modes and cost efficiency.
  • Raise the bar. Work with backend and platform teams so reliability is designed into services from the start.

What we’re looking for

  • 5+ years in SRE, platform or infrastructure engineering, running production systems at meaningful scale.
  • You’ve led real incidents and can explain how you’d handle one: triage, mitigation before root cause, communication, and follow-through.
  • A strong grasp of reliability fundamentals: SLOs and error budgets, latency percentiles, saturation, cascading failures, retries and backpressure, and graceful degradation.
  • A clear understanding of how distributed systems fail, including networking, databases, queues, caches and the edge.
  • Hands-on experience with containerized workloads and orchestration, and with managing infrastructure as code.
  • Solid experience with at least one major cloud provider.
  • You write real code for automation and tooling, in Python, Go or similar.
  • Strong Linux and networking fundamentals.

Nice to have

  • Experience building an SLO program from scratch.
  • Progressive delivery (canary, blue/green) in production.
  • Operating databases at scale (upgrades, replication, performance).
  • Relevant certifications, if they reflect real practice.

About the role

We are looking for an experienced technology recruitment leader to lead our existing team of recruiters globally.

This is a hands-on leadership role combining team management with active recruitment delivery. You will set priorities, develop the team and partner with technology leaders on their hiring needs, while personally managing a portfolio of technology vacancies.

The ideal candidate will have led technology recruitment teams within regulated financial services, preferably fintech, and will bring strong stakeholder management skills alongside a proven ability to deliver specialist technology hires.

Responsibilities

  • Lead, coach and develop a globally distributed team of technology recruiters.
  • Partner with technology leaders to understand hiring requirements and translate them into clear recruitment priorities.
  • Personally manage end-to-end recruitment for technology roles, from role briefing and sourcing through assessment, offer and close.
  • Balance team leadership with hands-on delivery, allocating workloads and adapting priorities as business needs change.
  • Build talent pipelines for specialist and difficult-to-fill technology positions.
  • Strengthen recruitment practices, including sourcing strategies, interview processes and candidate assessment.
  • Use recruitment data to monitor delivery, identify bottlenecks and improve hiring outcomes.
  • Ensure a consistent, professional candidate experience and recruitment practices aligned with applicable regulatory requirements and internal policies.

Requirements

  • 10+ years experience in both agency & inhouse roles.
  • A proven track record of leading technology recruitment teams within regulated financial services organisations.
  • Strong hands-on experience recruiting technology professionals across a range of disciplines and seniority levels.
  • Demonstrated ability to combine people management with personal recruitment delivery.
  • Experience managing geographically distributed teams and working with stakeholders across regions.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, with the confidence to advise and constructively challenge senior hiring managers.
  • A structured, data-informed approach to recruitment planning and delivery.
  • Fluent English, with strong written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred

  • Technology recruitment leadership experience within fintech or crypto.
  • Mandarin language proficiency.

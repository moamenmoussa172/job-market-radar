About you and this role

We have an exciting opportunity for you as a Global Workforce Management Automation Leader within Human Resources based in Terneuzen, the Netherlands, with additional location options in Midland, United States; Mumbai, India; São Paulo, Brazil; and Shanghai, China.

In this strategic leadership role, you will shape the future of Workforce Management by advancing intelligent automation, productivity, and end-to-end process excellence. You will lead a globally connected team of specialists, own the Workforce Management automation agenda, and represent the function in enterprise initiatives that deliver measurable value.

You will bring together lifecycle process ownership, technology, data, governance, and skills to transform fragmented, manual processes into an integrated, technology-enabled service model. Through this transformation, you will help improve speed, quality, and employee experience while creating sustainable value across Human Resources and the wider organization.

Responsibilities

  • Establish the Workforce Management productivity and automation strategy, create end-to-end visibility across workforce lifecycle processes, and drive simplification and standardization.
  • Own the Workforce Management automation roadmap across Workday, ServiceNow, Power Platform, document automation, and data, guiding opportunities from discovery through implementation at scale.
  • Lead a globally distributed team of onboarding-to-offboarding business process owners and specialists, setting clear accountability and strengthening succession and knowledge continuity.
  • Define a Workforce Management capability framework and close priority skill gaps through learning, stretch assignments, and communities of practice.
  • Represent Workforce Management in enterprise programs and lead workstreams where workforce data, processes, or controls are central.
  • Build portfolio governance, value cases, and baselines to support leadership decision-making and measurable value realization.
  • Strengthen controls related to data, security, and auditability while supporting responsible innovation and sustainable change.
  • Partner with Workforce Management leadership and specialists, Technology, Digital and Service Delivery, other Human Resources disciplines, Data Privacy, and Security to accelerate automation, simplification, and governance.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Information Systems, Industrial Engineering, or related fields.
  • 10+ years of experience in workforce lifecycle automation and people leadership.
  • The critical competencies required for success are: Strong knowledge of HR technologies such as Workday, ServiceNow, Microsoft Power Platform, Copilot/AI, automation, and data & analytics, combined with the ability to simplify workforce processes, identify automation opportunities, and translate business needs into scalable digital solutions, leveraging AI and emerging technologies across the employee lifecycle.

Your Skills

  • Strategic thinking: You connect strategy, workforce lifecycle processes, technology, data, and people to identify priorities and shape effective solutions.
  • Digital transformation: You apply practical knowledge of intelligent automation, technology-enabled operating models, and analytics to improve how work gets done.
  • Global leadership and influence: You demonstrate the ability to lead globally distributed specialists and influence effectively across organizational boundaries.
  • End-to-end ownership: You take ownership from strategy through execution, applying disciplined delivery, governance, and value realization.
  • Process excellence: You identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, and improve end-to-end workforce processes.
  • Stakeholder influence: You frame complex choices clearly, mobilize stakeholders, and translate ambition into executable roadmaps.
  • Governance and value realization: You establish clear controls, measures, and baselines to support decisions and demonstrate sustainable value.

Additional notes

Relocation and visa sponsorship are not available for this role.

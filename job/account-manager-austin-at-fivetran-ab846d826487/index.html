Account Manager (Austin)

About the role

The Account Manager (AM) plays a critical role in Fivetran's growth by driving customer expansion, cross sell and usage. AMs own their book of business and manage customer contracting, upsells and renewals. The AM will manage customer contracts from planning through negotiation to execution. AMs are responsible for tiering accounts, strategizing growth use cases via connector and usage addition.

Location: Austin, TX (hybrid). This is a full-time position, and candidates must be located in Austin and able to work in the office two days a week (Tuesday and Wednesday).

What you'll do

  • Own incremental growth of your book of business by retaining and expanding customers, with a focus on growing consumption.
  • Approach your book of business with a strategic mindset and tier your book of business to prioritize and refine your outreach and planning.
  • Sell across the full dbt Labs + Fivetran portfolio, identifying and closing cross-sell opportunities that bring multiple products into each account.
  • Identify and drive increased usage of dbt Labs + Fivetran products to help our customers realize positive business outcomes from our modern data solution. Research and plan tailored outreach to customers to drive consultative discussions around potential new use cases and cross-sell opportunities.
  • Drive expansion by guiding customers through testing and proofs of concept (POCs) for new use cases, partnering with Sales Engineers to turn successful pilots into increased consumption.
  • Actively prospect into new business units, departments, and teams within your existing accounts to build new champions and open new use cases.
  • Guide customers through pricing changes and plan conversions, communicating changes clearly and keeping customers confident and engaged through the transition.
  • Work cross-functionally with Customer Success Managers, Sales Engineers, Marketing, Product and other teams to drive a positive customer experience, while establishing deep and meaningful relationships with our users.
  • Establish and maintain strong relationships with our Partner and SI counterparts to execute on customers’ data architecture plans.
  • Own all contracting within your book of business including renewals, upsells and amendments.
  • Provide accurate, timely forecasts for renewals and amendments, ensuring SFDC and forecasting tools are updated in real-time.
  • Gather customer insights and feedback to share with sales, product, and leadership teams.
  • Develop customer relationships with the goal of creating customer advocates.

Tools you'll use: Salesforce, Catalyst, Slack, Looker, Sigma, Clari, Hex, ChatGPT, Workday, Zendesk, Gong, Outreach, Slab, G Suite, Jira

What you bring

  • 2+ years of proven track record in SaaS sales, customer success, or account management, with experience in managing contract renewals and negotiations.
  • Hands-on experience with Salesforce (SFDC) is required. We are not able to provide training on this tool.
  • Experience selling in a consumption-based pricing model, including driving usage growth and forecasting consumption.
  • Experience selling multiple products or cross-selling across a portfolio.
  • Experience working customers through pricing changes, plan migrations, or conversions.
  • Comfort with prospecting into new teams and business units within an existing customer account.
  • Exceptional organizational skills for managing multiple renewals, and strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to assess contract terms, identify upselling opportunities, and resolve issues proactively.
  • Effective interpersonal skills for working with cross-functional teams, and clear, concise communication.
  • Skill in prioritizing tasks and managing multiple deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Understanding of key SaaS metrics and experience in forecasting revenue impacts, optimizing renewals, and managing bookings.
  • Commitment to a seamless customer experience, anticipating needs and aligning solutions with customer and company objectives.

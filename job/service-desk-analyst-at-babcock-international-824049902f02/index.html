The Role

As a 3rd Line Service Desk Analyst, you'll play a key role in delivering advanced application support across critical business systems that underpin major defence and engineering programmes. Working within the Digital Solutions team, you'll be part of the New Product introduction supporting our new applications and environments for our Technical Partners, providing technical expertise, resolve complex incidents, support service improvements, and act as an escalation point for application-related issues.

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT support professional to further develop their career within a complex and highly regulated environment. You'll work closely with support teams, technical specialists, developers and stakeholders, helping to ensure business-critical applications remain reliable, secure and available.

What you’ll do:

  • Leading the investigation and resolution of complex application incidents and service requests.
  • Supporting and mentoring Application Support Analysts to deliver high-quality customer service.
  • Managing major incidents, carrying out root cause analysis, and driving service improvements.
  • Administering and supporting business-critical applications and IT service management tools.
  • Collaborating with technical teams and stakeholders to maintain service availability and performance.

What you’ll need:

  • Experience providing advanced application support within an IT service management environment.
  • Experience managing and resolving complex technical incidents and customer issues.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including root cause analysis.
  • Experience working with SQL and performing database queries for investigation and support activities.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to work across technical and business teams.
  • Degree-level qualification in Information Technology, Project Management, Programme Management or a related discipline.
  • TIL Version 4 certification - Desirable

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at Babcock Technology Centre. Set working times are between 7am and 7pm, Monday to Friday to cover operational hours

About the job

This position is needed to gather requirements, design solutions, test functionalities, and provide configuration support across the recruit-to-hire employee lifecycle.The successful candidate will partner closely with Talent Acquisition (TA), People Technology, and business stakeholders to design and optimize technology solutions anchored around Greenhouse. While Greenhouse serves as the primary system of record for global hiring, the role will also oversee connected tools within the recruit-to-hire lifecycle, including Workday, Eightfold, HackerRank, LinkedIn, HireRight, and specialized candidate fraud and sourcing platforms.

Responsibilities

In this role, you’ll:

  • Participate in requirements gathering sessions with TA and HR stakeholders to understand business processes and translate them into technical specifications.
  • Lead follow-up requirements gathering clarification and demos to stakeholders to ensure recruit-to-hire business outcomes are being achieved.
  • Analyze existing talent acquisition systems and workflows to identify areas for improvement, optimization, and fraud prevention.
  • Work closely with vendors and internal teams to configure, design, and implement solutions across TA platforms including Workday, Eightfold, Greenhouse, LinkedIn, HireRight, and HackerRank.
  • Develop detailed functional design documents and use cases tailored to candidate sourcing, screening, and onboarding.
  • Define test strategies, author comprehensive UAT (User Acceptance Testing) scripts, coordinate testing cycles with recruiters, and hands-on log/triage defects.
  • Manage and execute testing activities including system, integration, and user acceptance testing (UAT) to ensure quality delivery across the hiring tech stack.
  • Provide post-implementation support, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement recommendations for all TA applications.
  • Serve as the liaison between TA business units and People Technology Product Managers or Owners, ensuring that technology solutions meet complex hiring needs.
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of recruitment technologies, sourcing tools, and industry best practices to inform system enhancements.
  • Create user guides, release notes, and documentation for new features, facilitating training and knowledge transfer sessions for TA users and administrators as needed.

Qualifications

Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply. If your career is just starting or hasn't followed a traditional path, don't let that stop you from considering Twilio. We are always looking for people who will bring something new to the table!

*Required:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Business Administration, Human Resources, or related field; Master’s preferred.
  • 5+ years of experience in HR technology/business systems analysis with a heavy focus on Talent Acquisition applications.
  • Proven expertise and experience supporting core recruitment systems such as Eightfold, Greenhouse, Workday, HackerRank, LinkedIn, HireRight, and candidate fraud platforms.
  • Strong expertise in the software development lifecycle (SDLC), including standard business analysis methodologies, requirements gathering, data modeling, process mapping, UAT execution, and documentation.
  • Experience working with internal and external audit teams and understanding of SOX controls to support recurring audit requests and walkthroughs.
  • Experience with configuration, testing, and implementation of HR technology systems.
  • Excellent communication skills, capable of interfacing effectively with both technical and non-technical TA stakeholders.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Deep knowledge of HR processes strictly related to recruitment, candidate lifecycle management, sourcing strategies, and pre-hire compliance.

Desired:

  • Experience working in an Agile/Scrum environment using tools like Jira or Confluence, and ServiceNow.

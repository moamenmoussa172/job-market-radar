About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Lithuania.

Join the team building an AI-first company.

Hostinger serves more than 5.5 million clients across 150 countries, but we're still excited about what we're creating next.

AI is changing how we build products, support customers, work together, and solve problems. It helps us move faster, spend less time on repetitive work, and focus on bigger ideas.

If you want your work to influence both what we build and how we build it, we'd like to meet you.

Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles

Your role at Hostinger

The website is where most customers first meet Hostinger, and where they decide whether to stay. As Web Designer you'll own the end-to-end experience of our web pages and purchase flows: how people discover products, understand what we offer, and move from curiosity to conversion. It's work that mixes storytelling with real commercial impact, where small design decisions move real numbers.
AI is changing this space too, from faster experimentation to smarter personalisation of what visitors see. You'll be designing those experiences, not just reacting to them.

Your day-to-day

  • Own the design of website and landing page experiences seen by millions of visitors.
  • Craft conversion-focused flows that feel clear and trustworthy.
  • Work closely with marketers, PMs, and engineers in a cross-functional team, from concepts through to shipped pages.
  • Use AI tools to move faster: prototype more ideas, test more hypotheses, and generate variants at scale.
  • Iterate based on data: monitor performance after launch, run A/B tests, and keep improving.
  • Run and synthesise user research and usability testing to understand what makes visitors hesitate and why.
  • Be autonomous and initiative: pitch your ideas, take ownership to improve working processes and outcomes, innovate on brand display.

What will help you succeed

  • You have a 3+ years experience as UI/UX designer.
  • Generalist mindset: you're comfortable with research, interaction design, and visual craft without needing to specialise in just one.
  • You can collaborate with different skillset specialists like copywriters, graphic designers, product designers, developers, QA specialists, etc.
  • You use AI tools in your daily workflow: whether that's speeding up exploration, generating variants, or synthesising research faster.
  • Comfortable with HTML/CSS basics, enough to prototype in browser and collaborate with engineers.
  • Strong communication skills: you can present decisions, take feedback, and push back when needed.
  • Familiar with analytics and testing tools (Amplitude, Hotjar, or similar).
  • A portfolio showing real work: process, thinking, and outcomes, not just polished screens.

Benefits you'll love

🚀 360° Growth: You'll never stop learning here. Get access to Reforge and CoachHub, go to global conferences, and borrow from our physical and digital libraries. Depending on your location, you can mentor or be mentored through TesoXchange. People give each other open feedback, share what they know, and take on new projects as their skills grow.

🎯 Freedom & responsibility: Work where you do your best work: our offices in Vilnius, Kaunas, Jakarta, or Luxembourg, your home, or anywhere in the world. You plan your own day and own your results, and good ideas go live fast.

💪 Wellness simplified: Your wellbeing comes first. You get time off to recharge, a Headspace subscription, and events focused on wellbeing. Depending on your location, you also get health insurance from day one, a gym membership, regular health checks, and clubs for sports, arts, and hobbies.

🎉 Work hard – play hard: We celebrate wins together at Summerfest & Winterfest, Town Halls, team-buildings, and workations. Meet the Client sessions connect you directly with the people you build for. Milestones outside work matter too, so we celebrate weddings, new parenthood, and graduations with you.

Compensation starts from €3,500 EUR/month, depending on your experience and skills.

We're always looking for experienced talent to raise the bar. If you're excited about the role but the listed compensation range doesn't align with your expectations, we still encourage you to apply — we're open to adjusting it based on the impact and value you bring.

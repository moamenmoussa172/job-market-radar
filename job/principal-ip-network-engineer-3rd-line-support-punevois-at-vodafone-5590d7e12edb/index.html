About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Principal IP Network Engineer to provide expert-level 3rd Line support for complex IP/MPLS and transport network environments. This role is responsible for leading the technical resolution of major incidents, critical core network outages, and customer-impacting service disruptions across multi-vendor service provider networks.

The successful candidate will act as the highest operational escalation point before vendor engagement, taking ownership of service restoration, root cause analysis, stakeholder communication, and continuous service improvement. This opportunity is ideal for a networking professional with deep expertise in IP routing, MPLS technologies, Segment Routing, transport networks, and customer-facing incident management within Telecom, ISP, or Service Provider environments.

What you’ll do

Incident Management & Service Restoration
•Lead the technical resolution of major incidents and critical core network outages.
•Take end-to-end ownership of incidents until full service restoration is achieved.
•Perform advanced troubleshooting across IP/MPLS, transport, and optical network domains.
•Provide Level 3 escalation support to Network Operations Centre (NOC), Service Operations, Engineering teams, and customers.
•Lead technical bridge calls during critical incidents and provide regular stakeholder updates.
•Ensure adherence to incident management, escalation, and communication processes.
•Conduct post-incident reviews, root cause analysis (RCA), and corrective action planning.
•Identify recurring faults and drive long-term problem resolution initiatives.
•Participate in on-call support during evenings, weekends, holidays, and emergency situations.
Customer & Stakeholder Engagement
•Act as a senior technical point of contact for customer escalations.
•Participate in customer reviews, incident discussions, and service improvement activities.
•Communicate complex technical issues clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
•Build confidence through proactive communication, accountability, and ownership during critical incidents.
•Support customer audits, technical reviews, and optimisation initiatives.

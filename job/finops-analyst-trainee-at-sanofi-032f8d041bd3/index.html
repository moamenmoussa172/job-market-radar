About the job

Job title: FinOps Analyst Trainee

  • Location: Budapest – Sanofi Budapest HUB, Váci Greens

About the job

Looking to launch your career at the cutting edge of healthcare? Join Sanofi for a chance to develop with mentoring and guidance from inspirational leaders while helping to make an impact on the lives of countless people worldwide.

You will play a pivotal role in shaping Sanofi's Central FinOps data governance strategy — designing, owning, and developing technical solutions on Sanofi's standardized cloud platforms. Representing the Central FinOps team, you will work closely with the Apollo team to define and implement FinOps processes across a range of data objects and technical solutions.

In this role, you will build high-quality, seamless data pipelines that power our FinOps data and analytics initiatives, while supporting FinOps financial analysts with data requests and analysis. You will be instrumental in redefining Apollo, Sanofi's go-to Cloud & Infrastructure reporting platform.

We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. We’re also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started?

Main responsibilities:

  • Performing ad-hoc analysis on FinOps-related datasets
  • Monitoring and reporting on FinOps-related backend processes
  • Collaborating with the Apollo team to develop and maintain FinOps reports, dashboards, and KPIs for Digital / Business Unit Leadership
  • Leveraging cloud services to support FinOps initiatives, while ensuring strong knowledge transfer across the FinOps & Apollo team
  • Partnering with cross-functional teams across Digital / Business Units to help them understand their cloud spend and meet their FinOps goals
  • Creating training materials and documentation on backend Cloud FinOps processes for various Sanofi teams

About you

Experience:

  • Ongoing studies in BSc or MSc (active student status) at the fields of Finance, Business Administration, Accounting, Economics, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering or Information Technology Management
  • min. 20-40 hours availability/week

Soft and technical skills:

  • Advanced MS office skills
  • Knowledge with SQL, Python, Sharepoint and PowerAutomate
  • Problem solving oriented
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Pro-active attitude
  • Able to present challenges on a constructive way
  • General interest in working with data and system
  • Experience with data analysis and data visualization tools (PowerBI, BigQuery, Excel etc.)

Languages: Fluent in English mandatory (written and spoken)

What we offer?

  • A dedicated mentor and day-to-day manager to guide your learning throughout the internship
  • Structured onboarding to help you get up to speed quickly
  • Regular feedback sessions to support your professional development
  • The opportunity to contribute to real projects with tangible business impact
  • A collaborative, inclusive team culture within the Budapest Hub
  • Networking opportunities across Sanofi's broader community

Why choose us?

  • Bring the miracles of science to life alongside a supportive, future-focused team.
  • An international work environment, in which you can develop your talent and realize ideas and innovations within a competent team
  • Flexible home office policy
  • You'll face challenging tasks with professional responsibilities

  • Join a great community & special event (Weekly Yoga/Pilates classes, Monthly Board Game Nights, Summer/Winter Events, Well-Being Lectures & Sport Clubs)

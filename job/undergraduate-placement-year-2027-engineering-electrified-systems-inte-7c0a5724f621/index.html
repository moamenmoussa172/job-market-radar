About the job

Join us for your placement year in summer 2027! Our internships are designed to give you an insight into what it's like to work for a world-renowned brand with a culture of support and community.

Sitting within the Research & Development department, this role is to support both the mechanical and electrical Engineer(s) in the Electrified Solutions team at Bosch Home Comfort UK Ltd. It is aimed at someone wanting to get an in-depth engineering experience In this role, you will be responsible for:

  • Working within a project team to assist with various tasks such as drawings, CAD 3D models, ECR and BOMs to ensure the overall project goals are achieved.
  • To support the development test team with test regimes of our future products.
  • Update any reports on the design status on a regular basis.
  • Using knowledge of both mechanical and electrical design to aid with topics ranging from drawing/modifying structures, testing performance of future products, to helping develop electrical harnesses, across a variety of projects.
  • Employ sound problem-solving and analysis to help identify issues as well as options for mitigation of issues.

Interested? Upload your CV and a Cover Letter today!

Please note that to be eligible for this role, you must be an undergraduate student seeking a placement year as part of your degree - if you do not meet these criteria please consider applying for other positions.

Our ideal candidate should have:

  • Be a genuinely self-motivated individual.
  • Be meticulous with an eye for detail.
  • Be flexible and agile with their approach to completing tasks being able to swap between tasks with ease.
  • Have the confidence to keep asking questions until they understand the task.
  • Be curious and ask ‘what happens if we do this’.
  • Have the confidence to raise their own ideas in the presence of others.
  • Be able to take constructive feedback.
  • Have perseverance and patience.

Right To Work: please ensure you have a valid Right To Work in the UK status for the entirety of the placement year upon submitting an application.

Deadline for Applications: 27/11/2026

Please note, we reserve the right to close this vacancy at any time so we encourage candidates to submit their applications as soon as possible.

Expected Start Date: July 2027

Please note that this date is advisory and can be flexible within reason.

Contract Duration: 1 year

Working Hours: 39 hours per week – standard working pattern of 08:00 – 17:00 Monday to Thursday and 08:00 – 16:00 Friday with a 1-hour lunchbreak

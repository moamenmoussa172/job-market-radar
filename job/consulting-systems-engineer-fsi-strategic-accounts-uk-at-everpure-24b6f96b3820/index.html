About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

Location: London, UK | Hybrid

Are you a disruptive thinker with a passion for technology, customer transformation and cultural change?

Everpure is seeking a senior Consulting Systems Engineer to work with strategic Financial Services and Insurance (FSI) clients across the UK and globally.

You will help organisations modernise their data platforms, move beyond traditional infrastructure models and unlock greater business value from their data. Working closely with senior business and technology stakeholders, you will translate complex challenges into compelling, outcome-based solutions.

What you’ll do

  • Develop end-to-end solutions and use cases for banking, insurance, capital markets, payments and fintech clients.
  • Build trusted relationships with C-suite, business, technology and operational leaders.
  • Shape value propositions, TCO models and commercial strategies aligned to customer outcomes.
  • Guide Sales teams through opportunity development, positioning and competitive engagements.
  • Lead workshops, proofs of concept, demonstrations and executive presentations.
  • Collaborate with Sales, Engineering, Product, Partners and Systems Engineering teams.
  • Track FSI trends including AI, cloud transformation, cyber resilience, data sovereignty, regulatory change and operational resilience.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentoring and market insight across Everpure.
  • Represent Everpure at industry events and contribute to an inclusive, customer-first culture.

What you’ll bring

  • Strong experience across infrastructure and data technologies, including storage, compute, databases, networking, virtualisation and cloud-native architectures.
  • Knowledge of mission-critical FSI workloads, data management, security, governance and operational resilience.
  • Experience working with large or strategic enterprise accounts.
  • Ability to connect business strategy, technology architecture and measurable customer value.
  • Confidence presenting to C-level executives, senior business leaders and technical teams.
  • Excellent English communication skills, emotional intelligence, empathy and diplomacy.
  • Familiarity with data and analytics, DevOps, automation, infrastructure as code and modern IT operating models.
  • A growth mindset and commitment to ongoing learning, certifications and industry engagement.

This is a high-impact role for a commercially minded technical leader who can operate from boardroom to technical workshop and help shape Everpure’s FSI growth strategy.

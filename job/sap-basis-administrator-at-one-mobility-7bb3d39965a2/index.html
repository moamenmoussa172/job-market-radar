About the job

Join us in driving connectivity forward!

At One Mobility Group, we bridge worlds—combining nearly a century of German automotive engineering precision with the innovation power of the globe's largest electronics and ICT manufacturer. This unique positioning makes us the strategic partner for end-to-end mobility solutions—from initial concept design to large-scale manufacturing. Our comprehensive portfolio spans the full spectrum of electrified mobility, including power distribution systems, high- and low-voltage modules, safety components, and advanced charging solutions. We specialize in integration expertise that seamlessly connects data, power, and sensing – redefining what’s possible in intelligent, connected mobility.

At One Mobility, you will join a vibrant international team of 10,500 people, working together in 17 countries across Europe, Asia, North America, the Middle East, and Africa. Step into a culture where inclusivity is the foundation, where your ideas are trusted and supported, your passions are nurtured, and the spirit of collaboration is encouraged.

We move fast, think differently, and empower our people to deliver breakthrough solutions.

We are looking for

SAP Basis Administrator

at one of our locations inEMEA region - Hungary (Budaörs)

Your tasks:

  • Operational support, administration and continuous optimization of the SAP system landscape within a SAP RISE environment
  • Coordination with SAP as the Managed Service Provider
  • Monitoring of the SAP system landscape
  • Incident, Problem, and Change Management
  • Transport Management
  • User and Authorization Administration
  • Interface Monitoring
  • Support of upgrades and releases
  • Performance and availability monitoring
  • Management of external service providers
  • Support of projects and rollouts
  • Bachelor’s degree in IT/Business Economics and/or 5+ years experience in SAP Basis
  • Several years of experience in SAP Basis Administration
  • Experience with SAP ECC and SAP S/4HANA environments
  • Experience with SAP Solution Manager and/or SAP Cloud ALM
  • Knowledge of Transport Management (TMS)
  • Several years of experience with user and authorization administration
  • Knowledge of Incident and Change Management processes
  • Understanding of SAP integration technologies
  • Experience working with external service providers
  • System monitoring (CCMS)
  • Output management (Printing – mailing)
  • Fluent in English (spoken and written)

Advantage if you have:

  • Experience in international projects
  • German knowledge
  • SAP RISE experience
  • Basic SAP BTP knowledge
  • SAP Fiori administration and SAP Signavio user administration experience
  • SAP Cloud Connector knowledge
  • Experience with SAP Templates
  • Experience with SAP SuccessFactors, Ariba, or SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)
  • Knowledge of samQ and setQ (Voquz Labs)

Our offer:

  • A permanent, full-time position
  • A mixed team of fresh talent and long-standing employees
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • A structured induction program
  • An attractive remuneration package

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

1Password is growing. We’ve surpassed $500M in ARR and we’re continuing to accelerate, earning a spot on the Forbes Cloud 100 for four years in a row and teaming up with iconic partners like Oracle Red Bull Racing.

About 1Password

At 1Password, we’re building the foundation for a safe, productive digital future. Our mission is to unleash employee productivity without compromising security by ensuring every identity is authentic, every application sign-in is secure, and every device is trusted. We innovated the market-leading enterprise password manager and pioneered Unified Access Management, a new cybersecurity category built for the way people and AI agents work today. As one of the most loved brands in cybersecurity, we take a human-centric approach in everything from product strategy to user experience. Over 180,000 businesses, from Fortune 100 leaders to the world’s most innovative AI companies, trust 1Password to help their teams securely adopt the SaaS and AI tools they need to do their best work.

If you're excited about the opportunity to contribute to the digital safety of millions, to work alongside a team of curious, driven individuals, and to solve hard problems in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, then we want to hear from you. Come join us and help shape a safer, simpler digital future.

About the role

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the Admin Workflows area of 1Password's Enterprise Password Manager (EPM). With our API first approach to EPM, you will champion API centric workflows and deliver features that help administrators manage, monitor, and support their organizations at scale. You'll shape a roadmap spanning admin capabilities, admin dashboards and reporting, notification systems, account recovery and user confirmation workflows, and agentic (MCP) capabilities — connecting deep platform decisions to customer and business outcomes. This role sits at the center of the enterprise admin experience, with dependencies across many partner teams, so strong cross-functional judgment and communication are essential.

This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US.

How we're using AI today

Our Engineering, Product, and Design teams are thoughtfully integrating AI across the full software and product development lifecycle to move faster without sacrificing quality or security. In practice, that looks like engineers using AI-assisted coding tools to accelerate reviews and catch bugs earlier, product managers synthesizing user research at scale, and designers rapidly prototyping and iterating with AI-generated mockups. We approach AI the same way we approach security: with clear principles, human accountability at every consequential decision point, and rigorous evaluation before anything ships to customers.

Our Product philosophy

Our philosophy on Product consists of five core pillars:

  • Focus and Execution — can you ship quickly and measure results?
  • Customer Empathy — are you motivated first and foremost to solve customer problems?
  • Effective Communication — can you bring the entire organization — horizontally and vertically — along on the journey?
  • Go To Market — can you understand and drive the business outcomes through product decisions?
  • Technology — articulating how our technology beats our competitors.

Product Managers play a highly visible role in the company and are critical to the development and execution of our product strategy. As PMs, we are responsible for setting and championing the high bar for our products. You must excel at prioritization, collaboration, and communication, while holding yourself and others accountable for measuring quality and business results. You'll work with other PMs to understand customer problems and define requirements so your engineering counterparts can create great solutions, and you'll take a holistic view of customer needs that cross buyers, users, and administrators. You will work with Engineering, Design, Marketing, and GTM teams to ensure the quality, security, and usability of the service.

What we're looking for:

  • 5+ years of product management experience owning a product or a set of features and being responsible for its ultimate success, in B2B or enterprise software
  • A track record of shipping and improving software and driving business impact through the features or products you owned
  • Experience building for administrator or IT personas — workflows, dashboards, APIs, or enterprise governance capabilities
  • Strong experience with API first product design, API development, and API management workflows, with the technical depth to engage directly with system architecture and developer documentation
  • An action-bias that won't wait to create change
  • Obsession with internal and external customer problems, and the ability to represent the voice of the customer to motivate the team
  • Strong presentation and leadership skills
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills that scale from technical to non-technical audiences
  • Ability to inspire and bring together different teams and stakeholders to drive forward, solve problems, and remove roadblocks
  • Ability to prioritize tasks and use data — both qualitative and quantitative — to drive decision-making
  • Strong background and experience in cybersecurity is needed.
  • Experience training internal teams as part of bringing products to market, particularly sales, support, and customer success

What you can expect:

  • Working with a passionate, highly motivated Product team that is invested in your success and the success of your initiatives
  • A dynamic, highly collaborative environment
  • Empowerment to contribute to, and help drive, overall product strategy and the relative priority of the features you own on the roadmap
  • Being a part of the team that drives the success of 1Password, our products, and business to the next level
  • Having fun working in a fast-paced environment and being part of a team that prioritizes close collaboration over rigid processes, while continuously embracing an entrepreneurial culture

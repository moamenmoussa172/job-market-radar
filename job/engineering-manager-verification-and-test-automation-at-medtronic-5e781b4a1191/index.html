About the job

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact.

A Day in the Life

Medtronic Digital Technologies is creating ambitious new products that bring connectivity, real-time guidance, and decision support to the operating room. To support this mission, we are developing compact, high-performance, embedded systems that enable edge computing and connectivity. Working closely with other teams across Medtronic, we are creating a connected ecosystem and leveraging the state of the art in computer vision to provide the most comprehensive view of surgery.

If you’re interested in using cutting-edge technology to improve the standard of care in surgery on a global scale, this could be the place for you.

As an Engineering Manager, you’ll lead the verification and test automation team, owning the verification strategy for hospital computing and real-time connectivity systems. You’ll work alongside development teams to design for testability and share responsibility for product quality, from the first architecture decision through release.

The role requires working from our London office alongside the team at least three days a week.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Hire and develop verification engineers with strong software automation skills. Coach them to design tests that uncover product risks and build regression suites that identify failures early.
  • Embed AI into the team's ways of working. Develop automation across all workflows, including test design, coverage analysis and failure investigation, while ensuring these automations are appropriately validated.
  • Expand the hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) test cluster. Prioritise automated checks that run on real devices as part of continuous integration, covering a broad spectrum of operating conditions and failure scenarios.
  • Drive design for testability with software and hardware engineers before and during implementation. Determine what should be tested in isolation, what requires end-to-end testing on hardware, and where exploratory testing can uncover behaviours that scripted tests may miss.
  • Lead test planning and release-readiness reviews. Work alongside the team in the lab to investigate difficult-to-reproduce defects, clearly communicating what the test evidence demonstrates and which risks remain.

Required Qualifications

  • Proven experience leading and developing a test or verification team, including coaching engineers, building technical capabilities and defining effective, risk-based verification strategies.
  • A strong track record of personally designing, developing and implementing automated integration and system-level tests that are reliable, maintainable and trusted by development teams.
  • Hands-on experience verifying software on physical devices, working closely with developers to improve system observability, troubleshoot issues and make failures easier to reproduce and diagnose.
  • Experience working with regulated or safety-critical products, with an understanding of quality requirements, verification processes, risk management and the importance of robust test evidence.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience developing, testing or verifying medical devices under IEC 62304 and ISO 14971, particularly software-driven products used in clinical or hospital environments.
  • Experience with real-time video testing, including video streaming, processing, image quality, latency and performance validation.
  • Familiarity with embedded systems, edge computing and connected devices, particularly in environments where reliability, performance and real-time responsiveness are critical.

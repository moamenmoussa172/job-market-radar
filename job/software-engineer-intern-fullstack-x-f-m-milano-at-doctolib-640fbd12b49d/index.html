About the job

Software Engineering Intern - Fullstack (x/f/m)

Set a new pulse for healthcare!

We are looking for a Software Engineering Intern to join the Engineering team.

Your mission will be to build innovative products and features that improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. You will work in a feature team developing solutions that directly impact healthcare professionals and patients across Europe, contributing to our Operating System powered by AI to manage all clinical and administrative activities.

Working in the tech team at Doctolib means building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients.

What you'll do

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Build and own features alongside experienced engineers — from design and development to testing and shipping — with a direct impact on real users
  • Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and designers to solve complex technical challenges and build scalable, secure, and user-friendly healthcare tools
  • Write clean, efficient code following industry best practices in design, testing, and peer review
  • Solve real-world problems that directly enhance patient care and improve doctor-patient relationships
  • Explore new tools and technologies, including AI and distributed systems, as part of your continuous growth
  • Contribute to a culture of learning and collaboration by sharing knowledge and engaging actively with your team

Who you are

Before you read on: if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

You'll be a great fit if you:

  • You love problem-solving and are driven by curiosity — you're not afraid to dive into complex issues and find creative solutions
  • You thrive in a collaborative environment and enjoy working with cross-functional teams, sharing knowledge, and learning from your peers
  • You have a passion for technology and product development, and always keep the user experience in mind
  • You are comfortable coding, with the help of AI agents like Claude Code, in multiple languages and eager to learn our tech stack
  • You are in your final year of a degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field and looking for a 6-month full-time internship
  • Eager to learn and work in a team of builders
  • You are fluent in English

It would be fantastic if you:

  • Have experience with web development or building products in a fast-paced environment
  • Have worked on side projects or contributed to open-source communities
  • Are familiar with AI-assisted coding tools and eager to integrate them into your daily workflow.

Life at Doctolib Tech

  • Our solutions are built on a single fully cloud-native platform that supports web and mobile app interfaces, multiple languages, and is adapted to country and healthcare specialty requirements.
  • Our stack is composed of Rails, TypeScript, Java, Python, Kotlin, Swift, and React Native.
  • We leverage AI ethically across our products to empower patients and health professionals. Discover our AI vision here.

Want to learn more about our tech culture and environment? Visit theDoctolib Tech site.

What we offer

  • Tech masterclasses throughout the internship
  • Free breakfast at the office
  • Access to the best AI tools for coding and development, along with dedicated training
  • Supplementary health insurance, with the option to include cohabiting family members
  • Reimbursement of public transportation
  • Food benefits: electronic meal vouchers (€8.00 per working day), Foorban fridges, and breakfast at the office
  • Wellbeing and sports: access to mental health services in partnership with Moka.care; free access to the gym at our Milan office; and a partnership with Wellhub
  • For employees caring for dependent family members and employees with disabilities, a support package including adapted remote-working arrangements, additional paid leave for medical reasons, and psychological support
  • Partnership with Ciao Elsa for access to pension funds
  • Annual flu vaccination offered during the winter, along with various preventive healthcare initiatives throughout the year
  • A fixed monthly allowance for internet and electricity
  • Corporate partnerships with various providers across Italy, including Corporate Benefits and Ennevolte, offering dedicated discounts and promotions

Our interview process

  • Coding Game (1h)
  • Recruiter Interview (30 minutes)
  • Feature Building Interview (1h)
  • Behavioral Interview (1h)

Job details

  • Internship (6 months)
  • Milano
  • Total compensation : 1000 euros
  • Hybrid work setup (up to 2 remote days per week)
  • Start date: March 2027

About the job

We are currently seeking a highly skilled Senior Project Manager and a great team player to join our PMO team.

If you are curious by nature, adaptive, collaborative, and always one step ahead, then join us and be a Commencer!

Responsibilities

  • Lead, coordinate, execute, and monitor projects to ensure successful delivery.
  • Ensure projects align with company strategy, goals, and commitments.
  • Develop and maintain project plans, revising them as needed to meet changing requirements.
  • Deliver projects within the defined budget, timeline, and scope constraints.
  • Identify, assess, and proactively mitigate project risks.
  • Ensure all project deliverables meet established quality standards.
  • Leverage AI-powered tools, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and NotebookLM, to improve project planning, reporting, risk management, documentation, and overall delivery efficiency.
  • Identify opportunities to integrate AI-driven solutions into project management processes and workflows.
  • Promote AI adoption, knowledge sharing, and continuous learning within project teams.
  • Support the effective delivery of AI-driven initiatives, ensuring alignment with business objectives, delivery timelines, and quality standards.
  • Continuously evaluate processes and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Collaborate closely with Product Management, Design, and Engineering teams to achieve project objectives.
  • Identify, acquire, and allocate necessary resources while assigning individual responsibilities.
  • Serve as the main point of contact for daily client communications.
  • Set and manage client expectations effectively.
  • Develop and nurture new business opportunities with existing clients.
  • Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering; an MBA or a master's degree is a plus.
  • Minimum 7 years of professional experience in IT and project management.
  • Experience in international projects.
  • Fluency in Russian and English, both written and spoken, is required.
  • Strong knowledge of software development processes.
  • Familiarity with Generative AI technologies and hands-on experience with AI-powered tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and NotebookLM.
  • Ability to leverage AI tools to enhance project delivery, reporting, decision-making, and operational efficiency.
  • AI-related certifications or ongoing professional development in AI technologies are highly valued.
  • Willingness to continuously develop AI competencies and adopt emerging AI tools and practices.
  • Strong quality mindset, great team spirit, and attention to detail.
  • Ability to thrive in a dynamic work environment.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills to engage effectively with stakeholders at various levels.
  • Strong documentation and analytical problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to understand and evaluate competitive products.
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure.
  • Excellent interpersonal skills and the ability to collaborate in a high-performing team.

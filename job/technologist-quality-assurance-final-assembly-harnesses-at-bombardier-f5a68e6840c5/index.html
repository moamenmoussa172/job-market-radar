What are your contributions to the team?

In this role, you will:

  • Ensure compliance with manufacturing processes and standards.
  • Manage and support the disposition of non-conforming material.
  • Support continuous improvement and Lean initiatives, including Kaizen activities.
  • Ensure product and process conformity to regulatory and company requirements.
  • Participate in problem-solving activities to eliminate customer-reported non-conformances.
  • Support the implementation of improvement projects within your area.

How to thrive in this role? Skills, knowledge & experience:

  • You have a bachelor's degree in Aeronautical Engineering, Electrical engineering, Mechanical engineering or related
  • You have 3 to 5 years of manufacturing experience in aerospace, automotive, or a related industry.
  • You hold hands-on experience with electrical harness installation and testing on aircraft assemblies.
  • You have an advanced proficiency level of English (reading, writing, and speaking).
  • You count with strong problem-solving abilities and knowledge of continuous improvement and quality tools.
  • You have experience using measuring instruments and interpreting engineering drawings, GD&T, and Bombardier specifications.
  • You hold knowledge of Quality Management Systems and quality control processes.

About this Role

We are seeking an RPA Developer to design, develop, test, implement, and document secure, scalable, and reusable Robotic Process Automation solutions that increase automation and operational efficiency within VNO.

In this role, you will analyse automation opportunities and requirements, design process workflows, and ensure that solutions meet development and quality standards throughout their lifecycle. You will collaborate with Agile teams, internal stakeholders and suppliers to deliver RPA initiatives successfully, while also contributing to business cases and the identification of further automation opportunities.

What you’ll do

  • Design, develop, code, test, deploy, and document RPA workflows and reusable automation components.
  • Create solution designs and develop automation solutions, including required changes throughout the solution lifecycle.
  • Apply development standards, conduct code reviews and help ensure the quality, security, scalability and reusability of automation solutions.
  • Analyse and design process workflows in collaboration with colleagues to implement RPA initiatives.
  • Support the development of business cases and identify opportunities that can increase the adoption of RPA solutions across the organisation.
  • Participate in process walkthroughs and support automation assessments.
  • Share experience, knowledge and solutions aligned with relevant industry best practices.
  • Support all application development phases and the activities required to deploy solutions into production.
  • Maintain the reusable components library and consolidate similar components into common reusable interfaces.
  • Regularly report programme progress and maintain accurate records.
  • Report relevant KPIs, analyse programme gaps and recommend alternative solutions where appropriate.
  • Build effective working relationships across Agile teams, Vodafone colleagues and suppliers.

Who you are

  • You hold a degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a relevant professional certification.
  • You have relevant RPA Software Developer certification(s).
  • You have advanced Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel, macros, and VBA.
  • You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills and can translate process requirements into effective automation solutions.
  • You communicate and present effectively in written and spoken English.
  • You are comfortable working in a dynamic, innovative, and business-focused environment.
  • Knowledge of scripting, coding, SQL, relational databases, and application development would be an advantage.
  • You are familiar with Agile/Scrum best practices.
  • Knowledge of the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) would be an advantage.

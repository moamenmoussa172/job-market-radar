About the job

Who we are:

Charles River Development (CRD) is the FinTech division of State Street. Together with State Street’s Middle and Back-office services, Charles River’s cloud-based Front Office technology forms the foundation of the State Street Alpha® Platform, the first front-to-back solution in the industry.

Our vision is to be the world’s leading investment platform, shaping the future of the industry and driving new levels of technology-led innovation, resiliency, efficiency and growth for investors and the people they serve.

CRD Engineering organization is innovating and transforming the platform by moving away from monolithic software to utilising next generation cloud-based technology that leverages Microsoft Azure, Kafka, Snowflake, etc.

Industry momentum has seen CRD grow over 135% in headcount over the last 5 years, and we continue to grow. As a digital innovation leader, we invest into our solutions, processes, systems, and talent.

We are actively hiring in Dublin as this is an exciting time to join our innovative engineering teams as we are investing in growing out our European presence and leadership to help drive our next phase of growth.

Position Summary:

Act as a Full Stack Engineer responsible for enhancing and maintaining Alpha/Charles River Development modules as part of a agile scrum team. The role primarily requires strong Java, React, and relational database skills. Provide engineering and troubleshooting support to customer support and development teams across Charles River.

Responsibilities:

  • Analyse, design, develop, test, and support software enhancements across Alpha/Charles River Development modules and shared frameworks.
  • Collaborate with Business Analysts, Product Managers, and engineering colleagues to deliver simple, sustainable solutions to business and technical problems.
  • Lead the investigation and resolution of complex technical issues within assigned areas, including mission-critical client implementations.
  • Develop applications and services using Java, Spring Boot, React, TypeScript/JavaScript, SQL, and related technologies. C# and .NET experience is advantageous but not required; the role may include some exposure to these technologies.
  • Design and maintain relational database schemas, SQL queries, stored procedures, and data transformations, with a focus on performance, integrity, and scalability.
  • Build quality into delivery through code reviews, unit testing, automated testing, and appropriate manual validation.
  • Follow enterprise development standards and contribute to improvements in engineering practices and the software development lifecycle.
  • Participate actively in Agile delivery practices, including planning, refinement, reviews, daily coordination, and retrospectives.

Qualifications:

Recommended Education:

  • Bachelors degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience required.

Recommended Experience:

  • 4+ years of professional Java development experience, ideally including Spring Boot or a comparable modern framework.
  • 2–4 years of experience developing modern web applications with React and TypeScript/JavaScript.
  • Strong relational database experience, including data modelling, SQL, query optimisation, indexing, transactions, and performance troubleshooting; Microsoft SQL Server is preferred.
  • Experience designing and maintaining SQL queries, stored procedures, and data transformations.
  • Experience working with object-oriented design, RESTful APIs, automated testing, and collaborative code-review practices.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative Agile team.

Desirable Experience:

  • C# and .NET/.NET Core; familiarity with WinForms or other desktop UI technologies is also beneficial.
  • Building or contributing to CI/CD pipelines
  • Financial services knowledge
  • Working with event-driven architectures, observability tools such as Dynatrace, data migration, or Azure cloud technologies.

Working Arrangements:

Employees are expected to work from the office 4 days a week.

Job Purpose

Support warehouse and materials management at Majnoon Oil Field by receiving, recording, storing and issuing materials and spare parts. The role helps make sure stock records are accurate, materials are available when needed, and the warehouse is run safely.

Job Responsibilities

  • Receive incoming materials, check them against purchase orders and delivery notes, and report shortages or damage.
  • Enter receipts, issues and transfers accurately in the inventory system.
  • Issue materials and spare parts against approved requisitions.
  • Store materials correctly, including labelling, bin locations and segregation of hazardous items.
  • Take part in regular stock counts and help investigate and correct stock differences.
  • Maintain warehouse files, records and stock reports.
  • Coordinate with procurement, logistics and end users on material status and deliveries.
  • Keep the warehouse clean and orderly and follow all HSE rules, including safe manual handling and lifting.
  • Report slow-moving, expired or damaged stock to the warehouse supervisor.

Technical Qualification & Skills

  • Bachelor's degree or diploma in any discipline.
  • 1 to 3 years of experience in warehouse, stores, materials or data administration work.
  • Good data entry accuracy and computer skills in MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook).
  • Basic knowledge of inventory control and stock recording practices.
  • Experience with an inventory or ERP system is an advantage.
  • HSE awareness training is an advantage.
  • Working level of spoken and written English; fluent Arabic.

Soft skills

  • Organised and accurate with records.
  • Responsible and trustworthy in handling company property.
  • Works well with others and communicates clearly.
  • Safety-minded in a warehouse environment.
  • Able to work in a remote field environment on a rotational schedule.

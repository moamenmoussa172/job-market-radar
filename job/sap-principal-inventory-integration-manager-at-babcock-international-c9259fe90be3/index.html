Shape the Future of Defence Logistics and Digital Transformation

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a SAP Principal Inventory Integration Manager at any of our main UK locations, including Oakhanger, Hawthorn, Bristol or Devonport, with travel as required.

This is a unique opportunity to play a leading role in a major SAP transformation programme that will modernise and enhance inventory management capabilities across our Mission Technologies business. Working at the heart of a significant business change initiative, you'll help ensure that critical inventory, logistics and supply chain processes are successfully integrated into a future-focused SAP environment that supports defence and security operations.

The role

As a SAP Principal Inventory Integration Manager, you'll act as the key link between operational Inventory Management teams and the SAP implementation programme. You'll use your expertise to ensure existing inventory processes, controls and business requirements are understood, documented and effectively embedded within the new SAP solution.

This role offers the opportunity to influence a large-scale business transformation programme while working with a diverse range of stakeholders across logistics, supply chain, inventory management and digital systems. You'll be joining Mission Systems, a fast-growing and innovative business delivering advanced solutions and services that support defence and national security customers across the UK and internationally.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Act as the Inventory Manager within the SAP programme.
  • Document current Inventory Management processes, controls and workflows.
  • Act as the conduit between operational teams and SAP implementation partners.
  • Ensure Inventory Management requirements are reflected within SAP solutions.
  • Develop training materials, user guides and operating procedures.
  • Deliver SAP training and knowledge transfer to operational teams and drive business readiness and adoption activities.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements with 2 days onsite with travel required and 3 days working from home.

Essential experience of the SAP Principal Inventory Integration Manager

  • Significant experience within inventory management, materials control or supply chain environments.
  • Experience supporting business transformation, ERP or systems implementation programmes.
  • Strong understanding of inventory control processes, governance and operational best practice.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Experience analysing, mapping and documenting business processes.

Qualifications for the SAP Principal Inventory Integration Manager

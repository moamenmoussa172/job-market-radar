Software Engineer - Global Memberships Platform

Join us in our mission to transform the way people shop and eat, where impact, innovation and growth drive everything we do. Our Engineering teams tackle complex technical challenges across a global, three-sided marketplace, building and scaling systems that serve millions of customers, riders and partners every day.

From real-time logistics to resilient infrastructure and marketplace optimisation, we design, build and operate technology that powers growth at a global scale for all three of our brands: Deliveroo, DoorDash and Wolt.

Our team, Global Membership Platform, owns the core platform capabilities that drive the subscription model, which is key to our business. We build and run the shared services behind how customers join, manage and benefit from our membership offerings, giving every brand a single, reliable and scalable foundation to launch and evolve its membership proposition. Because membership is a paid product, we work closely with the Money team on payments, from how members are charged and billed to how payment outcomes feed back into their membership, so correctness and reliability are central to what we build. As a new team, we have a clear mandate and plenty of room to shape it: designing platform services that work across brands and regions, keeping a business-critical, always-on system dependable, and partnering with engineers, product and design teams across Deliveroo, DoorDash and Wolt to bring membership together as one global platform.

We’re looking for a Software Engineer to join our London team. In this role, you’ll help us build and maintain high-performance systems that power our global marketplace, ensuring a seamless experience for millions of users. This role is based in our London office with a hybrid setup, typically 3 days in the office.

Get to know our Engineering team — what drives us, how we work, and what you can expect.

What You’ll Be Doing

You’ll be joining a multi-disciplinary engineering team focused on technical execution and collaboration. As a Software Engineer at Deliveroo, your individual work contributes to achieving goals across your team, simplifying the maintenance of production systems while ensuring operational readiness and health.

Here’s what your day-to-day might look like:

  • Drive Technical Excellence: Improve code structure and architecture across your team’s remit, developing, releasing and running large-scale web applications whilst transitioning our monolithic codebase to a microservice-based architecture
  • Lead Project Planning: Foresee dependencies and risks, partnering closely with Product and Design to ensure smooth delivery of complex features, whilst defining the backend architecture and tech stack for a product area
  • Optimise Operations:Improve performance, reliability, scalability and security for our backend systems
  • Collaborate & Influence: Build relationships across global engineering teams to identify collaboration opportunities and influence the wider technical roadmap.
  • Mentor & Hire: Support the personal growth of your colleagues through technical guidance and take an active role in conducting engineering interviews to scale our team.

What You’ll Need to Thrive

Our ideal candidate will bring strong expertise in some of these areas and curiosity to grow in others:

  • Proficiency in using AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) in the full software development lifecycle, including designing, generating code, testing, monitoring and releasing software
  • Strong Engineering Principles: Deep pride in writing readable, well-designed, and well-tested software.
  • Backend Expertise: Significant experience building web-based applications (we use Go, Ruby/Rails, Python, Scala, and Rust).
  • Data Proficiency: Practical experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and "NoSQL" backends like Redis, DynamoDB, or ElasticSearch.
  • Systems at Scale: Proven ability to manage web architecture at scale (handling 20krpm and above).
  • Technical Leadership: Experience breaking down large pieces of work and guiding implementation choices for a team of engineers.

Why Join Us?

At Deliveroo, you’ll do work that matters—solving real-world problems in a three-sided marketplace that’s constantly evolving. We’re food lovers, problem solvers, community builders and more, brought together by a shared drive to make things better. Working here you can expect to:

🔧 Solve meaningful problems at real scale Work on a complex, always-on marketplace that impacts millions every day.

🌱 See your impact, fast Ship, test and improve ideas quickly in a low-hierarchy, high-ownership environment.

🧠 Grow through challenge and ownership Take on big, ambiguous problems and accelerate your career with strong support.

🌎 A culture built for builders High standards, collaboration, flexible working and continuous learning.

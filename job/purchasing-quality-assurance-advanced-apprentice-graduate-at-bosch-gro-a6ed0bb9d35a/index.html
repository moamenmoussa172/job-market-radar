Purchasing Quality Assurance Advanced Apprentice (Graduate)

About the job

Your Purpose:

  • Support the Purchasing Quality Assurance team in their day-to-day activities.
  • Work with a national and international Purchasing Quality team and support the business of Home Comfort in developing the supply base.

Role Scope:

  • Support the Purchasing Quality Assurance team in dealing with quality topics from purchased parts.
  • Support the problem solving and investigation of internal component complaints.
  • Dealing with internal and external cross departmental teams.
  • Dealing with external suppliers as required.
  • Support the returns to supplier & cost collection process.
  • Support the data management of quality complaints and supplier data.
  • General administrative work when required.

Key Focuses:

  • Support Production dealing with quality issues of purchased components.
  • Containment actions and problem-solving activities.
  • Inspection of delivered goods.
  • Returns to suppliers & reimbursement process.
  • Supplier data management in SAP.

Additional Information about the role:

  • Provide technical support to production and other departments regarding non-conformances.
  • Analysis, update and development of Purchase Quality databases as necessary.
  • Participate in the Continuous Improvement Process.
  • Other general projects or quality engineering duties as required.
  • Be the type of person people like interacting with, this role relies on great working relationships!
  • Very good communication and networking skills.
  • Determined and resilient, able to deal with challenges and find solutions. Attention to detail.
  • Possess strong analytical skills and critical thinking.
  • Be hands-on and have an agile mindset.
  • Proficient skills in MS Excel as a starting point, with experience / knowledge of more office and software applications.
  • Be target orientated, and able to meet deadlines.
  • Forward thinking, with strong organisational skills.
  • Proactive and self-motivated, able to work independently.
  • A University (academic) education, and studies in Engineering are a must to this role preferably HND or BEng in Mechanical or Manufacturing Engineering.

Deadline for Applications: Please note, we reserve the right to close this vacancy at any time so we encourage candidates to submit their applications as soon as possible.

Working Hours: 39 hours per week – standard working pattern of 08:00 – 17:00 Monday to Thursday and 08:00 – 16:00 Friday with a 1-hour lunch break

Working with us comes with the following benefits:

  • Office based role
  • 25 days annual leave per year plus bank holidays (pro-rata for your first year)
  • Eligibility for our Company Performance Bonus (up to £2,000 gross per annum)
  • Enhanced pension contributions
  • Access to the Perkbox and My Benefits platforms giving access to discounts, group income protection and death in service benefits
  • Employee Assistance Programme and Virtual GP
  • Discounts on products from across the Bosch Group
  • Subsidised on-site café and canteen
  • Health & well-being support including: over 200 trained mental health first aid champions
  • Access to Bosch Social Club membership offering you and up to 5 friends and family discounts on excursions and events
  • Volunteering days
  • Policy to support growing families
  • Access to self-service training platform containing over 200 Bosch approved programmes
  • Networking opportunities across Bosch UK including a mentoring programme
  • And more!

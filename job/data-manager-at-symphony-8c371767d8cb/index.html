About the job

Symphony is an AI-first global product, design, and development company with 800+ full-time employees. Our team works across engineering offices in Southeast Europe and Latin America, business development offices in the U.S. and Western Europe, and a Remote hub that spans Latin America, Africa, and Eastern Europe. In the last 20 years, we have delivered over 400 products for the world's leading startups, enterprises, and Fortune 500 companies. We are a preferred technology partner to more than 10 of the world's biggest brands and work with 50+ private equity funds.

Our team has a proven track record in helping clients increase revenue, reduce time-to-market, improve end-user experiences, and scale. We have domain expertise spanning financial services, banking, retail, technology, healthcare, and the VC and PE ecosystem, among others.

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Data Manager to shape Symphony's internal data function, lead and grow a team of data professionals, and build a unified data foundation that supports business decision-making, reporting, and AI initiatives across our global organization.

What would be your day-to-day responsibilities, you ask?

  • Own the data layer as the single source of truth for company data: architecture, models, quality, access and documentation
  • Lead and grow a team of data analysts into analytics and data engineers: set standards, review their dbt work, plan their development
  • Bring the remaining sources into the lake (ERP and finance, HR, Google Drive documents and others) and decide what is integrated, rebuilt or switched off
  • Own the business-logic (mapping) layer so that org and process changes can be handled through configuration, not code
  • Move company reporting off Power BI and the legacy warehouse onto the lake, report by report, without breaking what Finance and leadership rely on today
  • Define and enforce data ownership: who owns each source, metric and definition, and what "correct" means for each
  • Own everything downstream of the HubSpot CDC feed; data hygiene at source in HubSpot sits with SalesOps
  • Serve consumers across the company: Finance, Workforce Management, Delivery, Sales, leadership, and AI agents such as Baton
  • Set data governance and access rules for sensitive data (people, compensation, client financials)
  • What are we looking for?
  • 7+ years in data engineering, analytics engineering or BI, with at least 2 years leading people or a data function
  • Hands-on, current experience with Snowflake (or a comparable cloud warehouse) and dbt
  • Strong SQL and data modelling: dimensional modelling, medallion or similar layered design, slowly changing dimensions
  • Experience with CDC and ELT tooling and with orchestration
  • Experience replacing a legacy reporting estate with a governed model, including reconciling numbers with Finance
  • Working knowledge of data quality testing, lineage and documentation practice
  • Comfortable with business data: CRM (ideally HubSpot), ERP, HR and resource allocation data
  • Ability to turn vague business questions into clear definitions and models
  • Python for pipelines and tooling
  • Fluent written and spoken English
  • Nice to have:
  • Experience in a professional services or IT outsourcing company (utilisation, bench, rates, margin)
  • Exposure to serving data to LLM agents or semantic layers
  • Power BI background, to read and migrate existing reports
  • Infrastructure as code and CI/CD for data
  • You will easily fit into Symphony culture if you:
  • Prefer one source of truth over many quick fixes
  • Can explain a data decision to a CFO and to a junior analyst
  • Enjoy building a team, not only a platform
  • Take ownership and push work across team boundaries without waiting to be asked

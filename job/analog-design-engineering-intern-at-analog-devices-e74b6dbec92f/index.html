ADI helps customers bridge the physical and digital worlds with unmatched technologies that solve real-life problems and make the world smarter, healthier, greener, and safer. ADI specializes in high-performance analog, mixed-signal, power, and digital signal processing (DSP) integrated circuits that sense, measure, interpret, connect, power, secure and transform how the world experiences the world around them.

In our growing High-Performance Power Group in Milan, Italy, Analog Devices we offer position for Internships including the possibility of Master Thesis in the fields of Analog Mixed Signal Design. This group designs (between others) ICs in Display, Lighting and HV Power applications.

Our challenge is to get highest precision analog designs withstanding different application requirements. During an internship in our experienced mixed signals design team, you will learn how to

  • Transistor-level circuit design and simulation
  • Learn, implement and analyze trade-offs of analog blocks
  • Understanding of device-level operation and physics
  • System and block level circuit simulation and modelling

About the job

Rate: 90 - 120 PLN/hour (B2B)

We are looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to support Flix on a project basis through a service agreement collaboration.

Scope of Services

The consultant will provide expertise in designing, automating, monitoring, and maintaining cloud infrastructure for our Cloud Platform team within the Foundation Division. The Cloud Platform team provides tools, infrastructure, expertise, and best practices that enable software engineering teams to build, deploy, and operate secure, robust, and efficient applications with minimal operational overhead.

About the Engagement

  • Design, deploy, monitor, and maintain cloud infrastructure running on AWS and Kubernetes
  • Support the further development of Infrastructure as Code using tools such as Terraform
  • Automate operational processes and infrastructure workflows using scripting or programming languages such as Bash, Python, or Go
  • Support CI/CD processes and help engineering teams make effective use of the cloud platform stack
  • Troubleshoot infrastructure and platform issues using monitoring tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or Datadog
  • Contribute to platform reliability, incident response, and disaster recovery improvements
  • Evaluate relevant technologies and provide recommendations for implementation
  • Support developer experience improvements by enabling teams to work more efficiently with platform tools
  • Where agreed, participate in operational support or on-call activities to ensure platform availability

About You

  • Strong professional experience as a DevOps Engineer, Platform Engineer, or Cloud Engineer
  • Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code, ideally Terraform
  • Experience with public cloud environments, preferably AWS
  • Good knowledge of Linux, Kubernetes, and CI/CD processes
  • Experience with at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, Go, or Bash
  • Good technical communication skills and the ability to collaborate with engineering teams
  • Experience delivering services independently within cloud, infrastructure, or platform engineering projects
  • Professional proficiency in English

Nice to Have

  • Experience with GitOps tools such as FluxCD or ArgoCD
  • Experience with Istio, Datadog, Prometheus, or Grafana
  • Experience improving Developer Experience in engineering organizations
  • Experience working in agile or product-oriented technology environments
  • Experience with API Gateways
  • Contributions to open-source projects or upstream repositories

Collaboration Model

  • Contract type: Cooperation based on a service agreement (B2B/freelance contract), not an employment contract.
  • Working model: Independent contractor responsible for delivering agreed services and submitting invoices.
  • Location: Open for contractors based in Poland.

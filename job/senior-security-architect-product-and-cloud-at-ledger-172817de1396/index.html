About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger.

At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries.

The team

You'll join the Security Engineering Team and will be the Senior Security Architect for Ledger's products, the cloud they run on and the partners built into them, from our consumer apps and services to our enterprise custody platform. You shape how our products are designed, how environments are separated and how secrets and keys are handled, so that security is built in rather than added later.

What you’ll be doing

  • Lead threat modelling and design reviews for Ledger's products and services, hands-on: new features, APIs, backend services and the enterprise custody platform.
  • Set the cloud security architecture: account and network design, separation of development, test and production, identity and access, and guardrails for Kubernetes and serverless workloads.
  • Define how secrets, keys and credentials are managed across environments.
  • Design and own the key ceremonies and the processes around them, with the teams that operate our HSMs.
  • Review the path to production with engineering: CI/CD, code signing and release integrity.
  • Assess the Web3 and fintech partners behind the features we deliver to users, such as transaction checks, card, swap and buy/sell: how they protect our users' data and transactions, what we share with them, and what happens if one is compromised.
  • Use offensive security techniques to challenge every design the way an attacker would, and work with the Red Team and external testers to validate it.
  • Turn findings into owned, prioritised engineering work, and track them to closure.
  • Mentor the security engineers and help them grow into architecture work.

What we’re looking for

  • 10+ years in security engineering or architecture, including 5+ years in product or cloud security architecture, and still hands-on.
  • Solid knowledge across the security disciplines: AWS (or another major cloud), networking, endpoints, identity and access management, Kubernetes, and application and API security.
  • Experience designing secrets management and environment separation at scale.
  • Knowledge of offensive security techniques: how attackers move from a test system, a leaked secret or a vulnerable service to production.
  • Understanding of applied cryptography (secure channel protocols, signing schemes, HSM-based flows and key management), ideally gained on custody, signing or payment platforms.
  • Low-level development experience (C or Rust, embedded or secure-element code), enough to read the code running on our custom OS and in our HSMs.
  • Ability to challenge engineering leads, and to be heard.

Revenue Enablement Program Manager, AMER

Notion · Remote

Fully remote · Project Manager · Posted 10 November 2026

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

About the Role:

At Notion, we believe in building software that adapts to how people work, not the other way around. As we scale globally, we need someone who can ensure our revenue teams are equipped with the knowledge, tools, and processes to succeed across every geography while maintaining our core values and approach to customer relationships.

We're looking for a Revenue Enablement Program Manager to turn our global enablement strategy into repeatable programs that drive measurable changes in how our revenue teams work. You'll maintain a consistent global core while tailoring examples, facilitation, sequencing, and practice to the market.

You'll partner with Sales Leadership, Revenue Operations, Product Marketing, onboarding teams, and frontline managers to equip the field with the skills, workflows, and just-in-time guidance to execute our GTM strategy. You'll design for practice and proficiency, enable managers to coach and reinforce new behaviors, and use adoption and business signals to continuously improve programs.

What You'll Achieve:

Global Program Development & Implementation

  • Champion our global operating cadence to ensure the AMER region is successfully executing our GTM strategy
  • Design, develop, and program manage comprehensive enablement programs that can be deployed globally and execute them in AMER while maintaining consistency in our selling methodology and GTM goals
  • Partner with Revenue Operations, Sales Leadership, Product Marketing, and subject matter experts to build scalable curricula, hands-on practice, certification, and continuous learning programs that demonstrate role-based proficiency
  • Contribute to and maintain the global sales playbook in partnership with its owners, ensuring it reflects practical, repeatable practices across markets
  • Use AI, automation, and reusable agent-enabled workflows to deliver just-in-time guidance and reinforcement in the tools and workflows revenue teams already use
  • Define the behaviors each program should change and measure leading indicators such as completion, proficiency, adoption, manager activation, and resource usage alongside downstream outcomes such as time to ramp and productivity. Review results regularly, distinguish correlation from causation, and recommend corrective action when adoption or performance is off track
  • Lead cross-functional initiatives to align product releases, go-to-market strategies, and enablement rollouts
  • Partner with onboarding teams to reinforce role-based ramp milestones through hands-on application, practice, and proficiency checks
  • Build technical and workflow fluency programs that help the field uncover customer needs, translate business problems into practical Notion workflows, and demonstrate their value
  • Activate frontline managers first with previews, toolkits, coaching prompts, and consistent reinforcement and inspection cadences

AMER activation & regional adaptation

  • Collaborate with regional segment leads to proactively identify segment & market dynamics, competitive landscapes, and customer behavior patterns
  • Adapt global programs to address regional needs while maintaining core methodology and brand consistency
  • Create region-specific content, case studies, and competitive battle cards that resonate with local markets
  • Build feedback loops between regional teams and global program development to continuously improve our approach
  • Partner with regional managers to coach, inspect, and reinforce priority behaviors in existing team cadences; use field feedback to improve adoption and program design

Skills You'll Need to Bring:

Experience & Expertise

  • 7-10+ years of experience in sales enablement, revenue operations, or similar go-to-market roles
  • Proven track record of building and scaling enablement programs across multiple geographies, with a consistent global core and effective regional adaptation
  • Expertise in adult learning methodologies and designing multimodal experiences that build and assess proficiency through practice, application, and reinforcement
  • High proficiency with the enablement tool stack including emerging AI and automation solutions (ideally AI is already embedded into your workflow)
  • Experience working with global sales teams and understanding of diverse market dynamics
  • Strong analytical skills and experience defining target behaviors, tracking leading indicators and business outcomes, and improving programs based on evidence

Skills & Qualities

  • Demonstrate ability to build and automate Enablement and field work through AI workflows
  • Strategic thinker who can balance global consistency with local relevance
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, comfortable working with senior leadership
  • Natural collaborator who thrives in cross-functional environments
  • Data-driven approach to decision making with strong attention to detail
  • Adaptable and comfortable with ambiguity in a fast-growing environment
  • Passionate about developing people and helping teams reach their potential

Technical Requirements

  • Proficiency with AI tools for workflow development, gamification, content creation, data analysis, and process automation
  • Strong project management skills with experience managing multiple initiatives simultaneously
  • Experience enabling frontline managers to coach and reinforce new behaviors
  • Ability to build or support technical and workflow fluency programs that connect customer needs to practical solutions
  • Comfortable with sales analytics and performance metrics

Nice to Haves:

  • Experience at a high-growth SaaS company
  • Background in sales, customer success, or revenue-generating roles
  • International work experience or familiarity with diverse cultural business practices
  • Experience with Notion or similar productivity tools
  • Certification in sales methodologies (MEDDIC, Challenger, etc.)

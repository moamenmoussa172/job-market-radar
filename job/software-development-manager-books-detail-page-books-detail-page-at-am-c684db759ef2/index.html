Software Development Manager, Books Detail Page, Books Detail Page

Amazon · Madrid, Spain

Onsite · Lead / Principal · Engineering Leadership · Posted 24 November 2026

About the job

Every time a customer considers buying a book on Amazon, they land on a page your team owns. The Books Detail Page is where millions of readers make decisions about print, Kindle, and audio formats across a catalog spanning hundreds of millions of titles. As a Software Development Manager in the Books Detail Page team, you'll lead a team of talented engineers building critical customer experiences. You'll be responsible for the technical direction, team development, and operational excellence of your team while contributing to the broader organizational strategy.
Key job responsibilities
- Lead and develop a team of software engineers building customer-facing Books detail page experiences, fostering technical excellence and career growth
- Own the technical roadmap and architecture decisions for your team's area, including A/B experimentation, page performance, and feature delivery across print, digital, and audio formats
- Drive hiring and team development to build a strong engineering culture
- Balance operational excellence with new feature development, maintaining high availability for one of Amazon's highest-traffic store pages
- Partner with Books product, content, and business teams to define and execute technical strategy for discovery and purchase experiences
- Ensure delivery of high-quality software solutions across a global, multi-marketplace customer base
A day in the life
As an SDM, you'll focus on both technical leadership and people management. Your day includes mentoring engineers, reviewing technical designs, and ensuring operational health of your systems. You'll participate in planning sessions, conduct 1:1s with team members, and collaborate with other leaders to align on technical direction. You'll spend time on hiring, team development, and ensuring your team has what they need to succeed.
About the team
Books Detail Page is the cornerstone of Amazon's Books eCommerce funnel. Our teams build the experiences that help customers choose their next book: contextual features that surface the right information at the right moment, content evaluation tools that ensure quality and richness across our catalog, and discovery signals that connect readers to formats and titles they'll love. After a phase focused on modernizing our technical foundation, we're now in an exciting period of customer-focused innovation, with the architecture to move fast and the scale to make an impact.
- Knowledge of engineering practices and patterns for the full software/hardware/networks development life cycle, including coding standards, code reviews, source control management, build processes, testing, certification, and livesite operations
- Experience in leading the definition and development of multi tier web services
- Experience managing engineers
- Experience partnering with product and program management teams
- Experience in engineering
- Experience in communicating with users, other technical teams, and senior leadership to collect requirements, describe software product features, technical designs, and product strategy
- Experience in recruiting, hiring, mentoring/coaching and managing teams of Software Engineers to improve their skills, and make them more effective, product software engineers

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Stripe MALPB operates in a complex, fast-moving regulatory environment. The Risk and Compliance team is responsible for ensuring that our expansion is grounded in a bulletproof governance framework that matches the speed of our technology.
Having successfully established our foundational ERM policies, taxonomy, and risk registers, we are entering the integration phase. This team is building the future of "AI-Native" risk management—where governance frameworks are embedded into daily business operations through automated data pipelines and LLM orchestration.

What you’ll do

We are seeking a risk professional who combines strong enterprise risk management (ERM) experience with an analytical mindset and an interest in technology. Grounded in risk, audit, or compliance, you’ll help strengthen risk management across Stripe MALPB by bringing independent judgment, cross-functional collaboration, and clear communication to complex business challenges. ERM expertise is the primary focus, complemented by SQL skills and curiosity about how AI and automation can improve risk and control processes.
We are looking for a rare breed of professional: someone with the executive presence to stand up a banking governance program, combined with the hands-on engineering capabilities to automate it themselves.
As Enterprise Risk Management Lead, you will lead MALPB’s ERM program end-to-end, translating its established framework into consistent practices across the bank. You will provide independent second-line oversight and challenge, deliver clear reporting to management and the Board, and improve how the bank identifies, assesses, monitors, and responds to risk.

Responsibilities

  • Lead risk assessments. Run the annual Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) cycle, providing independent second-line challenge of first-line risk assessments, risk ratings, and control effectiveness.
  • Maintain core risk infrastructure. Maintain the Master Control Library, Central Risk Register, and enterprise risk taxonomy, with effective version control and change management to reflect evolving operations and emerging risks.
  • Monitor risk appetite and remediation. Aggregate, analyze, and validate Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) against Board-approved risk appetite thresholds. Escalate breaches, require appropriate Management Action Plans (MAPs), and track remediation.
  • Deliver decision-ready reporting. Combine quantitative risk metrics with qualitative assessments of emerging risks to produce the quarterly Enterprise Risk Scorecard and present clear, actionable insights to the Management Risk Committee and Board of Directors.
  • Apply data-driven analysis. Use basic SQL queries and existing reporting tools to extract and sample data for control verification, partnering with technical teams on more complex analysis, automation, and system integrations.
  • Use AI to improve execution. Explore and apply AI tools to support control monitoring and testing, partnering with technical teams to develop agents that flag potential anomalies or control deficiencies for expert review.
  • Advance continuous monitoring. Progressively supplement point-in-time testing with automated dashboards and ongoing monitoring of key operational and regulatory controls.

Who you are

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 8+ years of experience in enterprise risk management, operational risk, or risk advisory within a regulated financial institution, fintech company, or Big Four technology-risk practice.
  • Demonstrated experience implementing and embedding ERM programs—including RCSAs, KRIs, risk registers, and control libraries—within active, fast-paced business operations.
  • Strong analytical skills, including the ability to interpret large datasets, assess data quality, and translate findings into meaningful risk insights.
  • Working knowledge of SQL, including the ability to write basic queries for data extraction and analysis.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including experience constructively challenging senior business leaders and preparing concise, Board-level risk reporting.
  • Familiarity with AI tools and a willingness to learn how to build and apply AI agents to risk management processes.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience in a regulated fintech company, digital bank, or complex global payments processor.
  • Familiarity with governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platforms, such as ServiceNow or Archer, including how their configuration can support a scalable ERM program.

About the job

Are you a problem solver looking for a hands-on internship position with a market-leading company that will help develop your career and reward you intellectually and professionally?

About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

At ADI, you will learn from the brightest minds who are here to help you grow and succeed. During your internship, you will make an impact through work on meaningful projects alongside a team of experts. Collaborating with colleagues in an environment of respect and responsibility, you will create connections that will become a part of your professional network.

ADI’s culture values aligned goals, work-life balance, continuous and life-long learning opportunities, and shared rewards. The internship program features various lunch-and-learn topics and social events with other interns and full-time employees.

At ADI, our goal is to develop our interns so they are the first to be considered for full-time roles.

Apply now for the opportunity to grow your career and help innovate ahead of what’s possible.

The Business:

ADI’s Data Center & Energy BU is at the forefront of enabling AI, cloud computing, electrification, and grid modernization. As part of a long sustainable growth plan, ADI is expanding its design team, supporting the development of highly integrated power solutions.
Data Center & Energy BU will present the successful candidate a great opportunity for continuous career development as part of a highly motivated and supportive worldwide engineering team.

The Group:

The Integrated Voltage Regulators (IVR) Group at ADI is exponentially expanding its activities and engagements, growing its power solutions for modern data center.

As data center workloads increasingly demand more power and speed, existing power delivery architectures are no longer viable. ADI’s Integrated Voltage Regulators power the AI revolution with its leading-edge technology by reducing the energy footprint and total cost of ownership of data centers. Our transformational integrated voltage regulators deliver on-demand scalable power with the speed, precision and signal integrity required by AI processors. ADI’s power-management architecture shrinks solution footprint, height and component count, achieving True Vertical Power Delivery with unprecedented power density and efficiency.

The Team:

The Integrated Voltage Regulators Design Team has the mission to expand the ADI products portfolio with high-performance Power Management ICs by designing best-in-class, scalable, efficient, compact and ultra-fast fully-integrated voltage regulators, custom passives and advanced System-in-Packages. As part of the team, the successful candidate will contribute to laboratory automation solutions used for the characterization, validation and evaluation of next-generation Power Management IC prototypes.

The scope of the job includes some or all these activities:

  • Develop software and automation frameworks for an electronic laboratory dedicated to Power Management IC prototype evaluation
  • Interface and control laboratory instruments including oscilloscopes, electronic loads, power supplies, source-measure units, digital multimeters and data acquisition systems
  • Develop automated measurement procedures for electrical characterization, validation and performance testing
  • Create reusable Python tools for automated test execution, instrument configuration and measurement sequencing
  • Implement automated data collection, processing, visualization and report generation
  • Develop user-friendly graphical interfaces for test execution and result visualization
  • Support the setup, bring-up and debugging of evaluation boards and laboratory test environments
  • Improve the efficiency, repeatability and traceability of laboratory measurements
  • Analyze measurement data and support engineers in identifying performance trends and anomalies
  • Develop structured solutions for storing and managing characterization data and measurement results
  • Document software solutions, measurement methodologies and laboratory processes
  • Clearly communicate project status and progress to local team members and management

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Currently pursuing an MSc degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering or a related technical discipline
  • Strong programming skills in Python
  • Basic knowledge of analogue and digital electronics and laboratory measurement techniques
  • Familiarity with electronic laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies, electronic loads and digital multimeters
  • Good understanding of software development principles and structured code organization
  • Familiarity with data analysis and visualization tools
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary engineering team

Preferred Qualifications & Experience:

  • Experience with instrument automation using different interfaces
  • Experience with Python libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, PyQt, Tkinter or similar frameworks
  • Familiarity with Git and collaborative software development workflows
  • Experience with databases and basic firmware development/debug
  • Experience with basic PCB bring-up or hardware debugging: SMD soldering, etc.

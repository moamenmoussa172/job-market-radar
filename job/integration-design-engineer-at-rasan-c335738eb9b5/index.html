About the job

Job Summary

We are looking for a highly skilled Engineer - Integration Design to join our Integration Design Unit. In this role, you will create thorough integration guides that cover detailed web services specifications and overall design documents. Your responsibilities will include collaborating closely with connected vendors, communicating technical requirements to the development team, participating in technical discussions with vendor technical teams, and conducting basic analysis and troubleshooting. Proficiency in web services, JSON, XML, and Open API is vital for success in this position.

Job Objectives

  • Design integration solutions and integration guides that enable seamless connectivity between our systems and those of connected vendors and customers.
  • Act as the technical liaison between our organization and vendors/customers, gathering requirements and ensuring smooth integration.
  • Translate technical requirements, including architecture, data formats, protocols, and security considerations, clearly to the development team.
  • Identify and resolve integration issues quickly through effective analysis and troubleshooting.
  • Deliver integration and boarding projects on time and keep stakeholders informed through clear tracking and reporting.

Job Responsibilities

Integration Design & Documentation

  • Design integration solutions and develop integration guides: create detailed integration guides that include web services detailing, overall design documents, and technical specifications to facilitate seamless integration between our systems and those of connected vendors.

Vendor / Customer Collaboration

  • Collaborate with vendors/customers: engage in discussions with connected vendors/customers to gather requirements, understand their systems, and ensure smooth integration. Act as a technical liaison between our organization and the vendors/customers.
  • Attend technical discussions: participate in technical discussions with vendor technical teams to understand their integration capabilities, address technical queries, and ensure alignment between our systems and theirs.
  • Communicate technical requirements: clearly communicate technical requirements to the development team, including system architecture, data formats, protocols, and security considerations. Provide support during the implementation phase to address any integration-related issues.

Analysis & Troubleshooting

  • Perform analysis and troubleshooting: conduct basic analysis and troubleshooting to identify and resolve integration issues. Collaborate with the development team, vendors, and other stakeholders to troubleshoot and debug integration problems effectively.

Project Delivery & Reporting

  • Plan, manage and execute Integration and Boarding Projects: plan project deliveries, follow up on delivery progress, and report any related achievements/challenges.
  • Track and report Integration solution status: track the status of integration solutions and activities, and prepare periodic/on-demand reports.

Job Requirements

Educational Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent education and experience.

Licenses & Certifications

N/A

Previous Work Experience

  • 1 to 3 years of relevant work experience in the integration development domain.
  • Previous experience in designing and implementing integration solutions, including building integration guides and overall design documents.
  • Familiarity with industry-standard integration patterns and best practices.
  • Project management skills and experience are a plus, for effectively managing integration projects, coordinating tasks, and ensuring timely delivery.

Skills & Abilities

  • Technical Expertise: Strong understanding and hands-on experience with web services, including RESTful APIs and SOAP. Proficiency in JSON and Open API specifications is necessary.
  • Technical Documentation: Proficiency in creating clear and concise technical documentation, including UML, integration guides, design documents, and API specifications.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills to effectively convey technical concepts and requirements to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Ability to facilitate productive discussions and build strong relationships with vendors.
  • Problem-Solving Abilities: Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills to identify and troubleshoot integration issues. Ability to think critically and provide innovative solutions.
  • Team Collaboration: Strong teamwork and collaboration skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams, including developers, project managers, and business stakeholders. Ability to adapt to changing priorities and work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Attention to Detail: Meticulous attention to detail to ensure accuracy in integration design and documentation. Ability to identify potential risks and suggest mitigations.
  • Continuous Learning: Eagerness to stay up to date with the latest advancements in integration technologies, industry trends, and best practices.

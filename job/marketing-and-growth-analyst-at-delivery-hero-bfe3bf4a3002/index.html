About the job

ID: JR0089317

YOUR MISSION

Glovo is looking for a talented and ambitious Growth Analyst to join our Caucasus Growth team, playing a key role in accelerating acquisition and engagement for the country. Are you looking for a challenging experience in on-demand delivery? Do you want to contribute to building a product that's disrupting how people shop, eat, and get things delivered?

THE JOURNEY

  • Own and drive the projects and analysis behind Caucasus’s growth strategy, identifying opportunities to grow user base, orders, and retention through pricing, promotions, CRM, and other marketing levers.
  • Execute local marketing campaigns and support the localization of creative assets, working cross-functionally with CRM, Design, Commercial, Operations, and Finance teams (promo creation/auditing, creative asset requests, partner campaigns).
  • Build, maintain, and scale reporting tools to track KPIs (NC, CVR, RPO, CPO, retention) using SQL and BigQuery, keeping them accurate and up to date.
  • Translate data into clear, actionable recommendations for the local Growth teams, and monitor the competitive landscape to inform strategy.
  • Support ad-hoc analyses and projects based on business needs identified by local and global Growth & Marketing Teams.

WHAT YOU WILL BRING TO THE RIDE

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Economics, Engineering, Math, Stats or a related quantitative field
  • 1-3 years of experience combining analytical work with hands-on campaign execution or project management
  • Strong analytical mindset, genuine curiosity for numbers, patterns, and turning data into decisions
  • Proven ability to run projects end-to-end, from ideation and analysis into implementation
  • SQL proficiency is nice to have
  • Strong expertise with Excel/Google Sheets; knowledge of visualization tools like Tableau or Looker is a plus
  • High willingness to learn and grow fast in a hands-on, fast-paced environment
  • Proficiency in English
  • Hands-on attitude. You'll have to roll up your sleeves!

EXPERIENCE OUR GLOVO LIFE BENEFITS:

  • Monthly Glovo credit to spend on our restaurant products and zero delivery fee on all Glovo orders!
  • Flexible time off (take the time you need) and hybrid working model (own your time)
  • Online therapy and wellbeing benefit
  • Discounted gym memberships

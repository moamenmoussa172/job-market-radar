About The Opportunity

As a Technical Expert, you will own the core build-out of the Operations Automation & Cloud Management Platform, focusing on the unified management and intelligent orchestration of multi-cloud products. By integrating with cloud provider APIs (Aliyun, AWS, Azure), the role builds full-lifecycle management capabilities across compute, storage, network, database, and middleware resources—combined with CMDB, Event Center, orchestration engines, and AI / LLM capabilities—to evolve from manual operations toward intelligent control and automated change, continuously improving operational efficiency and stability.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Design and develop the Operations Automation & Cloud Management Platform, building full-lifecycle management capabilities for multi-cloud resources across compute, storage, network, database, and middleware;
  • Integrate with cloud provider APIs (Aliyun, AWS, Azure) and Terraform to enable unified onboarding, automated provisioning, configuration changes, and cost optimization of cloud products;
  • Own the design and development of core systems including CMDB, Event Center, and orchestration engines, building a unified data foundation for resources, configurations, and events, and develop workflow engines, task schedulers, and approval mechanisms;
  • Build intelligent cloud management capabilities, leveraging AI / LLM for intelligent orchestration, change-risk assessment, and capacity/cost prediction, driving the evolution from manual operations to intelligent control;
  • Explore the application of AI / LLM in cloud management, building an intelligent ops assistant (ChatOps / AI Copilot) to support natural-language-driven cloud resource operations and smart ticket handling;
  • Design and maintain unified RESTful / gRPC interfaces and SDKs, and participate in platform security (OIDC, IAM Role, RBAC, audit) and operations.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related fields;
  • Proficient in Java, familiar with Spring / Spring Boot / Spring Cloud;
  • Development or integration experience in multi-cloud resource management (Aliyun, AWS, Azure) and cloud provider APIs / Terraform;
  • Strong API design and system integration skills, with understanding of distributed architecture;
  • Experience designing or developing CMDB, orchestration engines, or event centers;
  • Knowledge of AI / LLM application development (Prompt Engineering, RAG, Agent), able to integrate AI into cloud management and operations scenarios;
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and strong code-quality awareness.

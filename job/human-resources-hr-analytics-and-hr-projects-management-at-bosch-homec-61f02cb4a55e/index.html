About the job

Key Responsibilities

1. HR Analytics & Reporting

  • Design, develop, and maintain HR dashboards, scorecards, and reports covering key metrics such as:
  • Headcount, attrition, retention
  • Hiring, time-to-fill, cost-per-hire
  • Performance, engagement, learning effectiveness
  • Diversity, equity & inclusion (DEI)
  • Perform trend analysis, predictive analytics, and workforce modeling to support strategic workforce planning.
  • Translate complex HR data into clear insights, narratives, and executive-ready presentations.
  • Ensure data accuracy, integrity, and governance across HR systems.
  • Partner with HR stakeholders to define KPIs aligned to business goals.
  • Automate recurring HR reports to improve efficiency.

2. HR Project Management

  • Lead and manage end-to-end HR projects, including:
  • HR digital transformation initiatives
  • Process improvement and standardization
  • Policy rollout and change management
  • Implementation or enhancement of HR systems (HRMS, ATS, LMS, Performance tools)
  • Develop project plans, timelines, budgets, risks, and dependency matrices.
  • Drive cross-functional collaboration with HR COEs, IT, Finance, and external vendors.
  • Track milestones, risks, issues, and benefits realization.
  • Prepare project governance updates for senior leadership and steering committees.
  • Ensure projects are delivered on time, within scope, and aligned to business objectives.

3. Stakeholder Management & Communication

  • Act as a trusted advisor to HR leadership on analytics findings and project execution.
  • Facilitate requirement-gathering workshops and stakeholder alignment sessions.
  • Communicate progress, insights, and impact in a structured and persuasive manner.
  • Support change management through training, documentation, and communication plans.

4. Process Improvement & Innovation

  • Identify opportunities to improve HR processes using data, automation, and analytics.
  • Leverage digital tools, AI/ML insights (where applicable), and best practices.

Establish and continuously improve HR analytics frameworks and project management standards.

  • Bachelor’s degree in:
  • Human Resources
  • Business Administration
  • Analytics / Statistics
  • Engineering / Management

Postgraduate degree or certification (MBA / PGDM / Analytics / PM) is preferred.

  • HR Analytics, People Analytics, or MIS
  • HR Project or Program Management
  • Technical & Functional SkillsHR Analytics SkillsStrong proficiency in:
  • Excel (advanced formulas, Power Query, Pivot Tables)
  • Power BI / Tableau / other BI tools
  • Experience with HR systems such as:
  • SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, or equivalent
  • Basic understanding of statistics and predictive modeling is an advantage.
  • Ability to build KPIs and metrics aligned to business needs.

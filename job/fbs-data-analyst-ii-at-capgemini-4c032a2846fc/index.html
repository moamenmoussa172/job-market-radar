About the job

Provide data in response to one-time or recurring requests for reports or analysis. Understand customer objectives and processes; effectively communicate system capabilities and limitations; and deliver available data. Analyze data quality and take corrective action. Analyze impact of system changes to data/reporting infrastructure (support testing and implementation activities). Contribute data and expertise to the success of complex analysis projects.

Essential Job Functions

Extract and manipulate data to solve business problems: • Respond to new requests for reports/data: understand customer objectives and processes; review and refine requirements; and commun icate estimated hours and timeline to complete; • Extract data using Cognos ReportNet, OBIEE Answers, or similar business query interfaces; • Extract data directly from relational databases (e.g. Oracle, SQL Server), data warehouses, or other data stores using SQL; • Format data for business use in Excel, Access, or similar format; • Produce reports manually and through automated scripting; create and maintain scripts and macros used to automate reports; • Support application testing for enhancements or system upgrades; identify and communicate data quality issues or data differences; • Scope and deliver data for analytic purposes (data designed for the construction of advanced mathematical models); measure, document, and address data quality issues; maintain datasets and dataset documentation; and contribute knowledge and expertise to the success of the project; and • Act as liaison between business and IT; collaborate on technical issues that require IT involvement and business priority to fix. Communicate effectively: • Be concise and precise in written communication; • Incorporate best practices in developing intuitive reports to share with a broad audience; and • Describe and document unique aspects or limitations of data to aid understanding. Demonstrate business understanding: • Develop in-depth knowledge of data associated with multiple lines of business (LOBs), and effectively support the data needs of those LOBs; and • Demonstrate commitment to lifelong learning. Exemplify good teamwork: • Proactively communicate with customers and team members; • Provide excellent customer service by making realistic commitments and keeping them; • Work productively on assigned tasks; • Collaborate effectively with other team members; • Demonstrate accountability to management and customers for hours spent on assigned tasks; • Diligently strive to deliver value in the fewest possible hours; and • Share effective SQL or other data extraction/manipulation techniques with team members; debug problems with report automation or queries; propose and implement remedies

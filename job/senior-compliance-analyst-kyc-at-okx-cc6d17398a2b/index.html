About the job

Who We Are

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

About the Opportunity

The Compliance function at OKX is responsible for the overall compliance culture at the company. We are a team of risk- minded problem solvers who advise the business on the company’s regulatory obligations and enterprise risk. Our KYCAdvisory team, in particular, is responsible for the strategic design, implementation, and oversight of the Global KYCProgram across all business lines

What You’ll Be Doing

You will be required to integrate subject matter and industry expertise within this functional domain. You will appropriatelyassess risk, coordinate with key stakeholders, such as the Business (e.g. Product, Engineering, Design, etc), Operations, Legal, and other Compliance teams, as well as demonstrate consideration for OKX's reputation by driving compliance withapplicable policies, laws, and regulations.In this role you will:

  • Assist the KYC Advisory Lead with the strategic design, implementation, and oversight of the Global KYC ProgramDraft, maintain and advise on the Global KYC Standards and procedures, including supplements and addendumsas necessary
  • Provide timely guidance to the key stakeholders, and act as a point of escalation for complex KYC issuesPartner with key stakeholders to continually evaluate, maintain, enhance, and implement critical KYC controls, systems and frameworks
  • Support the identification and assessment of process deficiencies, enhance reporting capabilities and providerecommendations for corrective actions while satisfying regulatory and audit commitments
  • Assist with special projects, with the ability to work in a fast-paced and ever-changing environment
  • Conduct targeted KYC-related training as needed
  • Stay up-to-date on relevant global, regional and jurisdictional regulatory guidance, enforcement actions, andindustry best practices

What We Look For In You

  • Substantive experience in AML/KYC compliance within the financial services, FinTech or crypto industries
  • Demonstrable experience of managing teams, overseeing processes and leading AML/KYC related initiatives
  • Sound knowledge and understanding of various regional and jurisdictional AML/KYC regulations and requirementsfor the crypto industry (e.g., APAC, EU, LATAM, MENA, US, etc.)1
  • Excellent research, analytical and due diligence skills
  • Strong communication and presentation skills and ability to interact and lead discussions
  • Proven ability to work within and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Self-motivated, critical thinker and adept at working independently and in a dispersed team environment coveringmultiple and varied tasks
  • Intuitive, highly ethical and comfortable to question and challenge
  • Solid understanding and familiarity with the crypto industry
  • Experience working in a global and/or remote environment
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience
  • CAMS, CFE, or equivalent certification is a plus

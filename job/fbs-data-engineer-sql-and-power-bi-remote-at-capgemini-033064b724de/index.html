Requirements

Summary:

We seek a Data Engineer with a strong P&C insurance background to partner with business stakeholders, product owners, and technology teams to translate business needs into clear, actionable requirements for financial loss reporting and analytics solutions. The ideal candidate will analyze business data stories, document functional requirements, support data product certification, validate business rules, and ensure delivered solutions meet reporting, data quality, and downstream consumption needs. Core Responsibilities: Gather, analyze, and document business requirements; facilitate discussions with SMEs and stakeholders; translate business data stories into functional specifications; support source-to-target mapping and business rule validation; assist with UAT planning and execution; track issues, decisions, and approvals; and collaborate with data engineering teams to ensure requirements are implemented accurately.
Responsibilities:
•Develops moderately complex value-based analytics solutions for quality, business process functionality, or other strategic/data/analytic initiatives of moderate complexity.

•Collaborates with cross-functional teams to identify and establish analytics requirements.

•Creates detailed documentation and business plans that address stakeholder needs.

•Liaises between IT and Product Management for data solutions.

•Develops and implements moderately complex enterprise-wide data integration strategies that utilize various data-marts and data warehouse applications to enhance reporting.

•Continuously works to improve data platforms, dashboards, and reports to meet emerging and changing business demands and guidelines.

•Consults with limited guidance from Principal on UAT, technical specifications, strategic planning, contract design, technical support, testing updates, and trainings.

•Uses intermediate understanding of data elements, structures, standards typically involved in financial services transactions, reporting, and cloud-based data storage/sharing to adopt and apply standards in a way that satisfies and balances regulatory and compliance requirements with business needs.

•Manages data from multiple sources and uses project management skills to complete moderately complex projects with minimal coaching and guidance.

•Transmits data and proactively works to ensure data quality. Troubleshoots data issues and devises creative and effective ways to avoid or mitigate issues.

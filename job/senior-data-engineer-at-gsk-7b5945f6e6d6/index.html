About the job

About the role

GSK’s Enterprise AI (EAI) organisation is building the data foundations that enable AI to operate reliably and responsibly at scale across GSK.

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to provide technical leadership in the design and delivery of production-grade data systems that support AI products and capabilities across the organisation.

In this role, you will lead the design of data pipelines, platforms and AI-grade data infrastructure across your team’s domains. You will establish the architectural, engineering, quality and governance standards that underpin how data is built, deployed, monitored and maintained.

A key part of the role will be helping shape how AI-enabled digital workers are incorporated into modern data engineering. You will define appropriate engineering standards, controls and human oversight while ensuring the quality and reliability of what is delivered.

You will also help develop the wider engineering capability within the team, mentoring engineers and partnering closely with ML engineers, forward deployed engineers and AI product teams.

Key responsibilities

In this role, you will:

  • Lead the architecture and design of complex, production-grade data pipelines and systems supporting multiple downstream consumers and AI applications.
  • Establish engineering standards for the use of AI-enabled digital workers within data engineering, including appropriate testing, quality controls, evaluation and human review.
  • Design how digital workers integrate with orchestration, monitoring, observability and incident-management processes.
  • Provide technical leadership during significant data quality or pipeline incidents, identifying root causes and driving sustainable improvements.
  • Establish semantic and contextual data standards across your domains, including definitions, data contracts, classification, metadata and lineage.
  • Define and drive adoption of data quality and governance standards, with measurable improvements in areas such as data reliability, AI performance and audit readiness.
  • Lead the design of AI-grade data infrastructure, including evaluation datasets, retrieval architectures and real-time data flows that support reliable AI systems in production.
  • Lead cloud data infrastructure design and champion infrastructure-as-code practices, improving deployment reliability, observability, cost management and operational support.
  • Build reusable platform components, patterns and tooling that improve consistency and accelerate the delivery of new data products.
  • Set high standards for engineering documentation, code quality, testing and operational excellence.
  • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through technical guidance, pairing, code reviews and constructive feedback.
  • Partner with ML engineers, forward deployed engineers, AI product teams and other stakeholders to translate complex requirements into robust production data solutions.

Basic Qualifications

We are looking for professionals with these required skills to achieve our goals:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Significant professional experience in data engineering, including experience leading the design, development and operation of complex production data systems.
  • Strong experience with modern data warehouse or lakehouse technologies, orchestration and transformation frameworks, and batch and/or streaming architectures. This could include technologies such as Databricks, BigQuery, Apache Airflow, dbt or Apache Spark.
  • Experience designing data infrastructure for production AI or machine-learning systems, such as evaluation datasets, retrieval architectures or real-time data flows.
  • Experience managing data throughout the lifecycle of AI systems, including areas such as versioning, monitoring, governance or data/model drift.
  • Demonstrated experience defining and implementing data quality or governance standards across a team, platform or data domain.
  • Experience designing cloud-based data infrastructure and applying infrastructure-as-code practices.
  • Hands-on experience designing or operating AI agents, LLM-powered tools or AI-assisted capabilities within data engineering workflows, including areas such as tool design, evaluation, observability and human-in-the-loop controls.
  • Experience providing technical leadership and influencing engineering standards across teams or complex technical environments.

Preferred Qualifications

If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Experience acting as the technical lead for a data platform or significant data domain, taking solutions from initial design through to production and scale.
  • Experience building or improving internal developer platforms and data engineering tooling, such as self-service pipeline capabilities, agent runtimes, lineage solutions or data-quality services.
  • Experience with data catalogue, metadata, semantic or lineage infrastructure used by AI or machine-learning systems.
  • Experience working in a regulated or high-stakes data environment such as pharmaceuticals, life sciences, healthcare or financial services.
  • Experience mentoring other data engineers and helping establish engineering practices across a team.
  • Experience working in multidisciplinary teams alongside ML engineers, AI engineers, product teams or similar technical functions.

Why join us?

This is an opportunity to help define how modern data engineering operates in an AI-enabled organisation.

You will work on some of the foundational capabilities that allow AI systems to access and use data with the appropriate context, quality, permissions and governance. You will have the opportunity to influence engineering standards, shape the adoption of AI-enabled development practices and build capabilities that can be reused across GSK.

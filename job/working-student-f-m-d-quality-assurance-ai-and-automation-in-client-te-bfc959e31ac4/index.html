About the job

We help the world run better

At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you'll bring

You're studying computer science, information systems, or a related field and are drawn to quality assurance, automation, and AI. You enjoy exploring new technologies and turning ideas into practical solutions.

  • A genuine interest in Power Apps, Power Automate, and the Microsoft ecosystem
  • Curiosity about Azure DevOps and software testing approaches
  • Strong analytical thinking and a structured, independent way of working
  • An eagerness to learn tools such as Tricentis Tosca — no prior experience required
  • Good English communication skills, both written and spoken
  • A customer-oriented mindset that keeps end-users in mind
  • At least 2 semesters of studies till graduation left
  • Working from Walldorf office is required

We also look for people who communicate clearly, keep the customer in mind, and enjoy finding smart solutions to problems — whatever stage of your career you're at.

In addition to the role-specific skills above, all student and early talent positions at SAP look for candidates who are open to working with AI tools — including agentic AI — to automate everyday tasks, prototype ideas, and build confidence in AI-assisted ways of working from day one.

What you'll build

You'll actively shape and develop an internal QA ecosystem built on Microsoft technologies — building real features that improve how the team works every day.

  • Develop and extend a Power App integrated with Azure DevOps Test Plans
  • Design and optimise Power Automate workflows and low-code process automations
  • Identify and prototype AI-supported improvements to QA processes
  • Take features from initial concept through testing to productive use
  • Document solutions and present results to team members and stakeholders

Where you belong

You'll join IT Client Services – Client Technology Workplace, a team that has built its own QA ecosystem and is actively evolving it. You'll receive structured onboarding and are encouraged to challenge the status quo and contribute your own ideas. The role is based in Walldorf, Germany, with up to 20 hours per week during the semester and full-time during semester breaks. When you join SAP, you'll be a part of SAP Next Gen — a global community of students and early professionals with access to mentors, development programmes, and peers.

Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please also describe your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.

This is a SAP global, strategic, paid working student position that provides students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

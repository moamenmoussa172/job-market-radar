About the job

As a Site Reliability Engineer, you will join a team dedicated to delivering high‑quality solutions that meet and exceed customer expectations. Flightscape, powered by CAE, is the ultimate decision-making platform for airline Operations Control Centers. Designed to meet the demands of today’s complex aviation environment, Flightscape delivers real-time insights in a single view that help teams anticipate disruptions, respond with precision, and continuously optimize performance. By supporting seamless adaptation and better resource management, Flightscape enables airlines to maintain high standards of safety, quality, and sustainability.

With Flightscape, make operational excellence your most significant competitive advantage. Because when you're ahead of what’s next, every challenge becomes an opportunity.

To learn more: Flightscape | CAE

Description

  • Oversee application tasks related to in‑house applications supporting various airline processes.
  • Operate production systems under general direction.
  • Design and build automation to ensure system scalability and efficiency.
  • Develop tools and scripts using various scripting languages for monitoring, deployment, and automation.
  • Design and define highly complex technical solutions and align cross‑functional teams to support strategic objectives.
  • Build and use automation tools supporting the release management process.
  • Support and enable DevOps teams, processes, and IT Service Management disciplines.
  • Act as a consultant or technical architect by performing analysis, providing guidance, and delivering system solutions based on customer or stakeholder requirements.
  • Collaborate with key technical managers and experts across the organization to understand the existing technology landscape, business complexity, IT requirements, and validate proposed architectures and solutions.
  • Ability to work in a hybrid model from our Kraków office (Wadowicka 3).
  • Willingness to participate in the on‑call rotation.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in software or system engineering, including 2+ years in SRE or platform reliability.
  • Experience working in cloud environments with complex requirements.
  • Hands‑on experience with AWS, Kubernetes, and Terraform.
  • Knowledge of data security, network security, and information security best practices.
  • Experience developing and integrating with APIs.
  • Strong expertise in automation and DevOps tools.
  • Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python, Ruby, Bash, or PowerShell.
  • Knowledge of orchestration technologies and techniques.
  • Strong analytical skills with a data‑driven and process‑focused mindset.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Ability to work with minimal supervision.
  • Proactive attitude.

Preferred Qualifications

  • CKA / CKAD certification.
  • AWS certifications.
  • HashiCorp Terraform certifications.

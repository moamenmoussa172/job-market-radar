Job Description:

This opportunity is well suited for an ambitious UAE National who enjoys problem-solving, exploring new ideas, and leveraging technology to uncover market opportunities while contributing to projects that support the energy transition and a more sustainable future

In this role, you will support market assessment and business growth by gathering, analyzing, and interpreting market trends, customer insights, and competitor activity to help shape marketing and product development strategies. The role is suited to a curious and analytical professional who can work independently with general supervision, solve difficult but generally non-complex problems, and influence others through clear communication of facts, insights, policies, and practices.

At Hitachi Energy, our purpose is to advance a sustainable energy future for all. We bring power to our homes, schools, hospitals, and factories. Join us and work with fantastic people, while learning and developing yourself on projects that have a real impact on our communities and society. Bring your passion, bring your energy, and be part of a global team that appreciates a simple truth: Diversity + Collaboration = Great Innovation

How You Will Make an Impact

  • Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify market trends, opportunities, and emerging customer needs.
  • Analyze market, customer, and business data to generate actionable insights and recommendations that support strategic decision-making.
  • Monitor market conditions, competitor behavior, pricing trends, and sector developments to support business and product planning.
  • Develop reports, dashboards, forecasts, and presentations to support management decisions and business growth initiatives.
  • Monitor industry developments, technological innovation, and relevant market changes affecting the energy sector.
  • Partner with sales, marketing, strategy, and business teams to integrate market insights into business plans and commercial activities.
  • Support the evaluation of new market segments, partnerships, and growth opportunities.
  • Utilize tools such as Power BI to enhance analysis, reporting, and insight generation.
  • Represent Hitachi Energy at industry events, conferences, and customer engagements when required, and gather market intelligence from external interactions.
  • Support the review of marketing initiatives and assess results against objectives where applicable.
  • Live Hitachi Energy’s core values of safety and integrity, which means taking responsibility for your own actions while caring for your colleagues and the business.

Your Background

  • UAE National (Emirati) with a Family Book.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business, Marketing, Economics, Statistics, Data Analytics, or a related discipline.
  • Experience in market research, business intelligence, analytics, strategy, commercial analysis, or a similar field is preferred.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills.
  • Good computer skills, including experience with Power BI and Excel.
  • Exposure to or willingness to use Microsoft Copilot and other digital productivity tools.
  • Curious, self-motivated, enterprising, and willing to learn in a dynamic environment.
  • Willingness to travel and attend events as required.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work collaboratively across teams and stakeholders.
  • Fluency in English

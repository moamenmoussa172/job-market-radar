Senior Director, Infrastructure Engineering

About the job

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive.

Every ride on Lyft runs on infrastructure. Every match between a rider and a driver, every price, every payment, every map and every arrival estimate depends on platforms that have to be fast, reliable and efficient at a global scale. This role leads the organization that builds and runs those platforms.

Why this role matters

This is one of the most impactful engineering leadership roles at Lyft. You will be accountable for one of Lyft's largest operating investments: the cost of hosting, running and scaling the entire company on a public cloud, including our growing AI infrastructure spend. This role spans the full infrastructure stack, carries one of Lyft’s largest operating budgets, and directly determines how well Lyft’s product works and what it costs to run.

Reporting to Lyft’s Chief Technology Officer, you will lead an organization of approximately 200 engineers, engineering managers and senior technical leaders to efficiently execute on the right goals to drive impact while maintaining a sense of community.

Responsibilities:

You will lead the organization that builds and runs the platforms every Lyft engineer depends on. You’ll guide a team of engineering managers and senior technical leaders, and you’ll be accountable for the reliability, scalability and cost-efficiency of the systems that keep riders and drivers moving.

What you’ll own

  • Core hosting infrastructure. The hosting and compute foundation Lyft runs on in the public cloud, including our Kubernetes clusters and container platform, networking, storage, and the core services every product team depends on.
  • Data infrastructure. The databases, data stores and foundational data services that keep rider, driver and marketplace data available, durable and fast.
  • Developer platform and experience. The internal platforms, tools, libraries, software development kits (SDKs), and build and release systems that determine how quickly and safely every Lyft engineer can ship.
  • Mobile and client infrastructure. The shared build, release, testing and client platforms behind Lyft’s Android, iOS and web applications.
  • AI and machine learning infrastructure. The compute, serving and platform capabilities that let Lyft’s teams train, deploy and run machine learning and AI workloads, including the capacity, reliability and cost discipline that AI product development requires.
  • Observability and reliability. Stats, logging, metrics and tracing across Lyft. You’ll also define and champion engineering-wide standards for reliability and operational excellence, and build the mechanisms that make them stick: service level objectives (SLOs), error budgets, incident review and production readiness.
  • Infrastructure economics. A significant operating budget across hosting, cloud and AI spend, including capacity planning, efficiency, unit economics, and the tradeoffs between cost, reliability and speed. You’ll drive sustained efficiency without compromising reliability, and make the cost of scale visible and understandable to the teams that create it, in close partnership with Finance and executive leadership.

How you’ll lead

  • Set strategy and plan. Turn company priorities into a multi-year infrastructure strategy and roadmap, and own org-level planning, including headcount and budget, to put resources against the highest-leverage problems.
  • Drive major platform evolutions. Lead cross-functional initiatives and platform changes, and make sure architectural decisions scale with Lyft’s growth.
  • Balancing speed and safety with continuous improvement. Shape a culture where Lyft ships quickly without compromising production stability. Make the hard calls when the two are in tension, and instill continuous improvement in architecture, process and operational practices across your teams and beyond.
  • Represent infrastructure to executive leadership. Communicate risks, tradeoffs and investment needs in business terms.
  • Build and develop the organization. Hire, grow and retain strong engineering managers and senior technical leaders, building a deep bench and an inclusive, accountable organization with clear ownership and a high bar for performance

Experience:

  • 15+ years of progressive experience, including at least 5 years leading teams.
  • Strong technical background. You contribute to planning and design discussions and earn the respect of senior engineers on consequential architecture decisions.
  • Large-scale public cloud infrastructure. You’ve led infrastructure for a product that couldn’t afford to be down, including Kubernetes and container platforms, distributed systems, networking and storage.
  • Deep experience in several of the following: data infrastructure (databases, data stores, foundational data services); developer platforms and experience; mobile and client infrastructure for Android, iOS and web; and/or infrastructure for machine learning and AI workloads.
  • Understanding of Infrastructure economics at scale. You’ve owned a large cloud, hosting or infrastructure budget, delivered efficiency and cost outcomes without sacrificing reliability, and can speak fluently in business terms to the tradeoffs.
  • Leadership at scale. You’ve led managers of managers and senior technical leaders in a large organization at comparable scale, and guided teams through planning, prioritization, execution and major platform migrations.
  • How you work. You focus on impact, execution and internal/external customers. You build teams and products that scale, collaborate well, instill a strong sense of ownership, and plan ahead.

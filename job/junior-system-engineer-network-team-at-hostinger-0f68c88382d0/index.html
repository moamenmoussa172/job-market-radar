Your role at Hostinger

As a Junior Network Engineer, you’ll help operate and grow Hostinger’s global network of nearly 1,000 network devices across data centers worldwide.

You’ll work hands-on with Linux networking, BGP, peering, automation, observability, and DDoS protection, while learning how large-scale production networks are built and operated.

We automate repetitive work, avoid manual configuration where possible, and focus on building a network that is reliable, scalable, and easy to operate.

Curious to learn more? Meet your future Team Lead: Tomas Vilemaitis, Engineering Manager

Your day-to-day

  • Help maintain Hostinger’s global network and learn how we keep it reliable.
  • Learn how networks connect - including BGP routing, peering, transit services and Internet Exchange Points (IXPs) - with guidance from experienced teammates.
  • Use Python, Ansible and NetBox to help automate routine work and keep network information organized.
  • Support IP address management by helping with address allocation, capacity tracking and up - to - date records.
  • Monitor network health with Prometheus and Grafana, and learn how sFlow telemetry helps us spot issues.
  • Help investigate packet loss, routing problems and performance issues alongside experienced engineers.
  • Contribute to improvements in DDoS protection and other network security controls.
  • Build practical experience with Linux networking, routers, switches and network services.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, hardware, security, monitoring and product teams.
  • Help improve documentation and runbooks so network tasks are easier to understand and follow.

What will help you succeed

  • Basic Linux and networking knowledge - you do not need to be an expert, but a solid foundation is important.
  • A strong willingness to learn - there will be a lot to learn, and we do not expect you to know everything from day one.
  • Confidence to ask questions when something is unclear instead of being afraid to speak up.
  • A collaborative mindset and willingness to work closely with the rest of the team.
  • A problem-solving mindset - willingness to investigate, test, and understand the root cause instead of only fixing the symptom.
  • A preference for automation over repetitive manual work.
  • Willingness to take ownership of tasks, follow them through, and learn from mistakes.
  • Willingness to participate in a weekly 24/7 on-call rotation once you are fully onboarded and ready to handle production incidents independently.

Meet your team

The Network Team makes sure traffic flows smoothly and reliably across Hostinger’s global network. We work closely with product and infrastructure teams to improve connectivity, introduce new technologies, and support Hostinger’s growth. You’ll join a team of junior to senior network engineers based in Vilnius and Kaunas, working and learning together.

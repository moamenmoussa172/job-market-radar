Role summary

We’re looking for an Analyst - FP&A to join our finance team. You’ll work with Accounting and teams across the business to resolve posting and expense queries, understand what’s driving monthly results, and support reporting, budgeting and forecasting across markets, brands and channels.

This role would suit an experienced accountant who enjoys getting into the detail behind the numbers and wants to build their experience in FP&A. You’ll bring your accounting expertise from day one and develop your planning and analysis skills with guidance from the team.

What you'll be doing

  • Work with Accounting and other departments to resolve queries on receivables, payables, invoice postings, accruals, reversals and advance payments.
  • Advise on the correct business unit and general ledger account to use for purchase orders and invoices, and on how to account for operating expenses and capital expenditure.
  • Support month-end close by reviewing entries and following up with Accounting on discrepancies.
  • Review the monthly P&L, investigate unusual expense movements and help prepare management reports explaining variances against budget and prior year results.
  • Support budgeting and forecasting across markets and brands, taking into account different reporting requirements and planning cycles.
  • Work with Sales and Marketing to assess the expected financial impact of promotions and launches and track marketing spend against budget.

What you’ll need to succeed

  • At least five years of hands-on accounting experience, with the ability to work independently.
  • A bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance. ACCA, completed or in progress, is highly preferred.
  • A good understanding of how accounting entries affect the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow.
  • Proficiency in Excel. Experience with SAP would be an advantage.
  • Good communication skills in English and the ability to explain accounting matters clearly to colleagues across teams.
  • Curiosity to investigate discrepancies and an interest in developing your FP&A skills.
  • We welcome applicants looking to make their first move into FP&A.

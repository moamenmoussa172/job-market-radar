About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Docker

Docker has been one of the most loved brands in developer tooling, trusted by more than 20 million monthly users and over 20 billion container image pulls. From solo founders to the world's largest companies, developers rely on Docker to build, share, and run their applications across our suite of products including Docker Desktop, Docker Hub, and Docker Scout.
We are a globally distributed, remote-first team building the tools that define how software gets built and delivered. As AI agents redefine software development, Docker is at the center of that shift, providing the sandboxed environments, verified images, and secure infrastructure that make autonomous workflows trustworthy by default.

The Role

The Senior Principal Software Engineer is responsible for shaping and evolving the company’s technical landscape. In addition to designing, developing, and maintaining core systems, the Senior Principal Software Engineer provides strategic guidance to align technology initiatives with business goals. This role is vital in ensuring that the platform is secure, scalable, and performant—capable of supporting rapid growth and driving innovation.

_______________________________________________________________________

Responsibilities

Technical Strategy & Architecture

  • Define and drive the overarching technical vision, ensuring the platform meets current and future scalability, performance, and security needs
  • Evaluate and select tools, frameworks, and architectures to maintain a state-of-the-art technology stack

Leadership & Mentorship

  • Provide technical leadership and guidance to the engineering organization, setting best practices and standards.
  • Coach and mentor engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and collaboration

Innovation & Research

  • Investigate emerging technologies, frameworks, and best practices
  • Lead prototyping initiatives and proof-of-concepts that can transform the platform’s capabilities

Scalability & Performance

  • Ensure the platform is designed for high performance and can seamlessly handle increasing user loads
  • Oversee performance optimization, load balancing, and capacity planning strategies

Reliability & Availability

  • Design fault-tolerant, highly available systems that minimize downtime and deliver a seamless user experience Infrastructure Automation
  • Advocate for and implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles, CI/CD pipelines, and automation best practices
  • Continuously refine deployment, testing, and operations processes to improve efficiency

Security & Compliance

  • Collaborate with security experts to ensure platform integrity, compliance with industry standards, and protection of sensitive data
  • Proactively address security vulnerabilities and champion secure coding practices

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Work closely with product managers, designers, and other stakeholders to ensure technical initiatives support business objectives
  • Drive alignment between engineering roadmaps and product strategies

Technical Problem-Solving

  • Tackle complex architectural and technical issues, providing clear direction and innovative solutions
  • Serve as an escalation point for critical system challenges

Code Quality & Reviews

  • Uphold coding standards through thorough code reviews, ensuring high-quality, maintainable codebases
  • Establish and enforce best practices around testing, documentation, and version control

Documentation & Knowledge Sharing

  • Produce comprehensive technical documentation, including architectural diagrams, design guidelines, and operational runbooks
  • Promote a transparent engineering culture by sharing insights and successes across the organization
  • Take part in on-call rotation for your team, respond to incidents, debug production issues, and drive continuous improvement of system reliability

Qualifications

● 12+ years of software engineering experience with demonstrated expertise across multiple domains (SDLC, AI engineering, corporate systems, etc)

● Extensive software engineering experience, with a track record in designing and implementing large-scale, distributed systems

● Deep technical expertise: Proficiency in multiple programming languages, cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP), and modern development frameworks

● Leadership acumen: Demonstrated ability to lead diverse engineering teams, inspire innovation, and drive successful outcomes

● Architectural vision: Proven skill in defining and driving architectural roadmaps that align with evolving business needs.

● Problem-solving ability: Expertise in diagnosing complex system issues and designing robust, scalable solutions

● Communication skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, capable of influencing both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

● Security conscious: Solid understanding of security best practices, compliance standards, and data protection

● Continuous improvement mindset: Passion for optimizing processes, toolchains, and workflows

● Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

As a Senior Principal Software Engineer, you will not only architect innovative solutions but also shape the technical culture. Your leadership and expertise will be pivotal in building a resilient, cutting-edge platform that supports rapid growth and drives long-term success.

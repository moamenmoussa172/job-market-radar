About the job

We are seeking an experienced and highly motivated Linux System Administrator to join our ICT team. The successful candidate will be responsible for the administration, maintenance, security, and optimization of Linux-based infrastructure supporting critical business applications and services. The role requires strong troubleshooting skills, automation experience, and the ability to work within complex enterprise environments.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Install, configure, administer, and maintain Linux servers (Red Hat Enterprise Linux, Rocky Linux, Ubuntu, or CentOS).
  • Monitor system performance, availability, and capacity to ensure optimal operation.
  • Manage server security, patching, vulnerability remediation, and hardening activities.
  • Administer virtualization platforms such as VMware vSphere and virtual Linux environments.
  • Manage storage connectivity including SAN, NAS, and enterprise backup solutions.
  • Configure and maintain web servers, application servers, and middleware components.
  • Implement and support automation tools and scripting using Bash, Python, or Ansible.
  • Monitor and troubleshoot server, network, and application issues.
  • Support business continuity, disaster recovery, and high-availability environments.
  • Maintain system documentation, procedures, and operational standards.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, security, database, and application teams to support business initiatives.

Requirements

Technical Requirements:

  • Minimum 5 years of hands-on Linux administration experience.
  • Strong knowledge of:
  • Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL)
  • Rocky Linux / Ubuntu
  • Shell Scripting (Bash)
  • Ansible Automation
  • VMware vSphere
  • Apache, Nginx
  • DNS, DHCP, NTP
  • SSH, SELinux, Firewalld
  • SAN and NAS storage technologies
  • Monitoring tools such as Zabbix, Nagios, Grafana, or Prometheus
  • Experience with backup and recovery solutions.
  • Familiarity with container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes is an advantage.
  • Knowledge of cybersecurity best practices for Linux environments.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
  • Professional certifications are preferred:
  • Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA)
  • Red Hat Certified Engineer (RHCE)
  • Linux Professional Institute Certification (LPIC)
  • VMware VCP (Advantage)

Personal Skills:

  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills.
  • Team-oriented with a proactive and problem-solving mindset

About the job

Introduction to role:
Are you motivated by making complex processes simpler, faster, and compliant? As an Engagement Analyst, you will support external expert engagements from contracting through payment, ensuring accurate execution, strong compliance, and a smooth stakeholder experience.

You will join a global team focused on improving ways of working through automation, data, and continuous improvement, helping the business operate with greater speed, consistency, and transparency.

Key Accountabilities:

  • Manage end-to-end contracting workflows, from application review and approvals to contract execution.
  • Perform compliance and due diligence checks in line with policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Coordinate payment-related data and support accurate transparency reporting.
  • Partner with budget owners, payments teams, and cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Ensure data quality and support effective purchase-to-pay processes.
  • Identify process improvements and contribute to training and operational best practices.

Essential Skills & Experience:

  • Bachelor’s degree and 1+ year of experience in an operational, contracting, or related role.
  • Strong organisational and project management skills, with excellent attention to detail.
  • Experience with Salesforce, Excel, and Word.
  • Understanding of procurement and purchase-to-pay processes.
  • Excellent written and verbal English and strong stakeholder management skills.
  • High ethical standards and ability to handle sensitive information appropriately.

Desirable Skills & Experience:

  • Experience managing contracts or operational projects.
  • Background in biopharma, consulting, Compliance, Legal, Finance, Procurement, or related functions.
  • Knowledge of transparency and compliance requirements, such as EFPIA or the Physician Payments Sunshine Act.
  • Experience in risk management, auditing, quality, or SOX environments.
  • Experience working across global and cross-cultural teams.

Why AstraZeneca:

At AstraZeneca, you will combine operational excellence with meaningful impact. You will work with diverse global teams, use technology and data to improve how we operate, and help create faster, simpler, and more transparent ways of working, while maintaining the highest standards of compliance.

Call to Action:

Ready to bring your operational mindset, attention to detail, and collaborative approach to a global organisation? Apply now and help us shape simpler, smarter, and more effective ways of working.

Communication & Onboarding Analyst

stc · SA, Saudi Arabia

About the job

Job Purpose

The role holder is responsible for assist in developing and executing a creativity management strategy aligned with inspireU's vision to enhance brand awareness in collaboration with Corporate Relations. The role holder shall carry out his duties in accordance with the stipulated business policies and procedures.

Job Responsibility

• Assist in developing and executing a creativity management strategy aligned with inspireU's vision to enhance brand awareness in collaboration with Corporate Relations
• Support the building and refinement of communication plans in accordance with inspireU programs’ activities.
• Assist in shaping and distributing communication materials about inspireU and its startups monthly across various channels, including press releases, internal newsletters, and social media posts in collaboration with Corporate Relations
• Provide prompt responses to audience inquiries, comments, and messages across communication channels, fostering a responsive and interactive online environment.
• Support the establishment and continuous updating of a protocol and media appearance guide for inspireU and its startups, ensuring consistent and on-brand communications.
• Regularly assist in updating the inspireU website content and design to ensure it is informative, innovative, engaging, and up-to-date.
• Help provide incubated startups with tailored marketing plans, advanced marketing resources, and expertise to accelerate their market entry and growth.
• Help organize and execute periodic events, including inspireU academies, demo days, pitch sessions, and industry-specific forums, to showcase inspireU and the accomplishments of incubated startups in collaboration with Corporate Relations
• Help manage the sponsorship of events, ensuring strategic alignment with corporate partners and sponsors in collaboration with Corporate Relations
• Assist in managing the onboarding process for startups across all programs
• Help build and update a plan to enhance the international exposure of startups through participation in global events and conferences, providing comprehensive support before, during, and after international trips.

Job Responsibility (cont.)

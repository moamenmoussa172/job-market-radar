About the job

We are looking for a Senior AI Engineer with over 4 years of experience to lead our AI initiatives. In this role, you will be responsible for driving the design, development, and deployment of cutting-edge AI solutions. You will provide technical leadership to a team of AI engineers, mentor junior team members, and collaborate closely with stakeholders to deliver impactful AI-driven products. This position requires deep expertise in machine learning, data engineering, and software development practices.

Responsibilities:

* Lead the end-to-end development of AI models.

* Architect scalable AI systems and infrastructure to support large-scale data processing and model training.

* Mentor and coach junior AI engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and technical excellence.

* Collaborate with product managers and business stakeholders to define AI strategy and roadmap.

* Conduct research and stay abreast of advancements in AI/ML fields to drive innovation.

* Ensure robustness, scalability, and performance of AI solutions in production environments.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 4+ years of experience in AI, machine learning, or data science roles, with a proven track record of delivering complex projects.
  • Proven experience designing and deploying Generative AI (GenAI) systems using large language models (LLMs).
  • Expertise in Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or other ML frameworks.
  • Strong knowledge of distributed computing, data engineering, and cloud platforms.
  • Hands-on experience with RAG architectures, vector search engines, and embedding models.
  • Deep experience with prompt engineering and LLM fine-tuning.
  • Experience building intelligent agent-based systems using frameworks like LangChain, LangGraph, or CrewAI.
  • Familiarity with LLM evaluation, observability, and cost/performance optimization.
  • Hands-on experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills.

Preferred Qualifications:.

  • Experience working with multi-agent architectures, planning/execution agents, or tool-using LLMs.
  • Contributions to open-source GenAI projects, or participation in applied research involving LLMs, embeddings, or agentic reasoning.

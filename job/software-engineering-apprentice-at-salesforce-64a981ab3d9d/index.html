About Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all.

Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce.

As an apprentice, you will have the opportunity to learn on the job with world class software developers and actively contribute to our exciting mission to deliver ever more reliable and resilient software solutions supporting our infrastructure. The Infrastructure Software Teams leverage cutting edge technologies to code, design and debug complex solutions to automate operational tasks. You will have the opportunity to operate as a fully functioning member of the team and gain valuable real world experience.

During this experience, you'll be part of a wide apprentice community; spanning across Dublin and London, and across a variety of business units and roles. You'll also have access to a broader group of people that will support you throughout to develop both personally & professionally. The program will run in collaboration with Fast Track into Information Technology for two years and will provide you with the experience and knowledge to become a proficient software developer.

What are we looking for?

Below are some of the skills we expect Infrastructure Engineering Apprentices to demonstrate:

  • Exposure to AWS, GCP or AZURE
  • Excellent analytical, conceptual and problem-solving skills
  • Self-starting, proactive and organised, as well as being capable of long-term thinking
  • Passion for scaling systems and software
  • Excellent English communication skills - written and oral
  • A keen interest in the world of software development

What’s in it for you?

  • 25 days annual leave and additional
  • Health insurance, dental insurance, travel insurance, vision benefit
  • Monthly wellbeing reimbursement and Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
  • Pension package & Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

Location

Applicants must live within a reasonable commuting distance to Dublin and must be willing to attend college in person.

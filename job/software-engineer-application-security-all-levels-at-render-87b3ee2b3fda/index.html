About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

At Render, we’re building the modern cloud platform for developers creating AI-native, full-stack, multi-service applications. Our mission is to eliminate the tradeoff between the power of hyperscalers and the simplicity of developer-friendly platforms—so teams can ship fast, scale reliably, and focus on their product, not infrastructure.

Unlike complex hyperscalers or ephemeral edge/serverless solutions, Render offers a developer-first experience with persistent compute, dynamic autoscaling, built-in orchestration, and observability, allowing teams to launch, scale, and manage real-world applications without writing infrastructure code or managing servers. Whether you're building LLM-powered applications, scalable SaaS products, or async processing pipelines, Render empowers teams to move fast and scale confidently from MVP to millions of users.

Our platform is trusted by over 7 million developers worldwide and continues to grow rapidly. In February 2026, we raised an additional $100M in Series C financing, bringing our total funding to $260M, to accelerate our vision of making cloud infrastructure both powerful and intuitive—designed for the speed of modern AI development.

We’re a diverse and talented team that values craft, velocity, and user experience. If you’re excited to help shape the future of the intelligent cloud and empower developers everywhere, we’d love to hear from you.

About the Role

Render hosts applications for millions of developers, from solo builders to enterprises with strict security requirements. Our Application Security team keeps our product, and the internal apps we run, safe. It also builds the security features our customers use to keep their apps safe.

We are an engineering team. We build patterns and guardrails so product teams move faster and safer, rather than adding review gates. This is not a role where you will run a scanner and file tickets. You'll build production features that ship to customers, and partner with other teams to build solutions for sign-in, secrets management and how AI agents access resources on Render.

What You'll Do

Keep Render's Applications Secure at Scale

  • Own code and dependency scanning and tune it to be high signal and autonomous.
  • Operate Render's bug bounty program.
  • Build secure code patterns to accelerate development.
  • Protect Render's software supply chain from vulnerable dependencies and malicious packages.
  • Harden authentication and authorization for both the Render product and internal applications.

Build security into the product

  • Partner with product engineering teams to inform and build security features for our customers.
  • Directly ship products that give customers visibility and protection for what they are running on Render.
  • Make storing and injecting secrets into applications on Render safer.
  • Create safe access patterns for agents to autonomously use Render.

What We're Looking For

  • Strong software engineering skills. You've built and shipped production web applications, APIs, or developer-facing products.
  • Experience with running application security at scale.
  • Strong judgment toward security risks and tradeoffs.
  • A sense of ownership to take projects from idea to shipped.
  • Empathy for our engineering team and our customers.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience with any of the technologies the Render product runs on: Go, Typescript, Kubernetes, Postgres, Terraform, Temporal.
  • Experience securing build systems and container images
  • Offensive security experience through pentesting, AI red-teaming or exploit development.
  • Experience securing LLM or agent-based products from vulnerabilities such as prompt injection.
  • Contributions to the security community through research, talks, or open source.

If this role excites you but you don’t meet every single requirement, we’d still love to hear from you—your unique experience might be just what we need.

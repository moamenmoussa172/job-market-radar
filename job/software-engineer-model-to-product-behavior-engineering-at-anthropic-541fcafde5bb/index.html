About the job

About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

We're looking for engineers to join Model-to-Product Behavior Engineering, the team responsible for getting Claude's behavior right across Anthropic's products. You'll work with product, research, and safeguards teams to make sure Claude behaves in ways that are consistent, safe, and genuinely helpful, whether in a new feature, a new model, or a launch.

Evals are one of the sharpest tools we have for getting Claude's behavior right, but not the only one. You'll build evals to see clearly where Claude shines and where it slips, use what you learn to inform how to close the gap, and build the harness improvements that get us there. That takes your own intuition and judgment about model behavior, both to decide what is worth building and to know what to do with what you find. Your work will shape launch decisions and give research and product teams a signal they can trust.

You'll also build the infrastructure behind our evals, along with tooling that makes it easy to test and ship prompt fixes. And you'll create self-serve tools that let other teams build their own evals and improve Claude's behavior in their products, so that getting model behavior right scales well beyond any one team.

You'll work across many projects and product surfaces at once, holding a high bar for the details while adapting as model capabilities change.

Key responsibilities

  • Build evals that measure Claude's quality, behavior, and capabilities across Anthropic's products and model releases, with sound methodology: deciding what to measure, validating datasets and graders, and interpreting noisy results with statistical rigor
  • Apply judgment to model outputs to separate real regressions and capability gaps from noise, and turn findings into clear recommendations
  • Support model launches by catching regressions before they reach users and giving decision-makers a clear read on model readiness
  • Act on what the evals show: build and ship improvements to the product harnesses Claude runs in so that the behavior is right in the product
  • Design, build, and operate the infrastructure our evals run on, and the tooling to test and ship prompt fixes
  • Build self-serve tools and frameworks that let other teams develop and run their own evals and improve model behavior with confidence, and help product engineers build their first evals
  • Set technical direction for our eval and harness systems, and lead projects that span product and research teams
  • Partner with product, research, and safeguards teams to ensure new features and models meet quality and safety standards

Minimum qualifications

  • Strong software engineering skills in Python or a similar language, including version control, testing, and CI/CD
  • Experience owning production systems end to end, from design and architecture through launch and ongoing operation
  • Hands-on experience designing and building evaluations for LLMs, such as datasets, graders, metrics, or eval harnesses
  • Understanding of evaluation methodology for AI systems, including how to validate an eval, the limitations of LLM-based grading, and how to reason about statistical significance
  • Experience building infrastructure, platforms, or developer tools that other engineers or researchers rely on
  • Good judgment about model behavior: you can read model outputs and form a well-calibrated view of what is a real problem and what can be improved
  • Clear communication, in writing and in discussion, including explaining complex model behavior to diverse stakeholders
  • Ability to lead ambiguous, cross-team projects from defining the problem through delivery, and to prioritize across several at once

Preferred qualifications

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience
  • Experience using eval results to find and fix problems in an AI product, such as changing a harness and showing the fix worked
  • Background in machine learning, NLP, or a related field
  • Experience with Claude or other frontier AI models in production settings
  • Experience with A/B testing and experimentation frameworks
  • Working knowledge of prompting and context engineering
  • Familiarity with AI safety and alignment considerations
  • Curiosity about how language models behave, and comfort defining success metrics for novel AI capabilities where no standard exists

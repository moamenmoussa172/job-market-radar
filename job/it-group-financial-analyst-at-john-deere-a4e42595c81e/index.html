Your Responsibilites

As the IT Group Financial Analyst for Global IT and Global Law Services & Regulatory Affairs (GLSRA) this role is onsite in Moline, Illinois.

  • Advise Global IT and GLSRA leaders by providing financial analysis, insights, and recommendations for business and investment decisions
  • Lead annual budgeting and oversee financial reporting, variance analysis, performance tracking, and governance for overhead expenses and capital projects.
  • Manage legal liability contingency forecasting and related financial reporting and audit requirements.
  • Advance improvements and automation within IT accounting processes.
  • Collaborate with global, cross-functional teams across Accounting & Finance, Legal, and IT to support strategic initiatives.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

  • 5 or more years of accounting / finance experience
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Knowledge of GAAP and financial management principles such as financial forecasts, financial analysis, and capital budgeting
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to translate complex financial information into clear, actionable insights
  • Agile mindset, comfortable with constant change in business priorities
  • High energy, positive attitude, and intellectual curiosity

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with SAP, CFIN, and Power BI
  • Proven ability to present complex financial information clearly and confidently to business leaders
  • Demonstrated experience leading change through process, reporting, or analytical improvements
  • Cross-functional collaboration experience

Education

Ideally, you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

  • Bachelor's degree in accounting or finance

What you’ll do

As a Senior Automation Quality Assurance Engineer, you will work in a team of QA Engineers to design and implement Vodafone’s Core IT applications. You will take technical ownership of test automation and help embed quality throughout delivery, using AI-enabled testing practices with appropriate security and human oversight.
Alongside hands-on engineering, you will coach other QAs and work closely with the Quality Assurance Chapter Lead to shape the team’s future direction, automation capabilities and ways of working, improving customer experience and delivery quality.
You will work across multiple interconnected systems and complex end-to-end flows, requiring a strong understanding of system architecture, APIs, integrations, dependencies and data flows across parallel systems.
More specifically you will:
• Review requirements, specifications and technical designs early in delivery, challenging gaps and defining clear acceptance criteria and risk-based test coverage.
• Design, implement and maintain reusable automated tests for user and technical stories across Core IT applications, APIs and system integrations, covering functional, integration and regression testing, using tools and frameworks such as Karate, Playwright and SoapUI where appropriate.
• Build and maintain automated API testing solutions, validating REST/SOAP services, requests, responses, data, integration points and end-to-end system behaviour.
• Investigate failed and flaky tests, distinguish automation issues from product defects, and work with developers to resolve root causes and improve test reliability.
• Estimate, prioritise, plan and coordinate testing activities within the team, balancing delivery priorities, quality risks and opportunities for automation.
• Take technical ownership of test automation frameworks for Core IT applications, APIs and system integrations, improving their architecture, maintainability, scalability and integration into Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.
• Develop and maintain automation solutions using TypeScript and/or JavaScript, applying good coding practices to create reusable, maintainable and scalable test automation.
• Collaborate with engineers and product colleagues to understand testing needs, embed quality into development and provide timely feedback on defects and release risks.
• Understand system architecture and dependencies across multiple interconnected applications, enabling effective testing of complex end-to-end business flows and identifying appropriate integration points, test boundaries and automation opportunities.
• Identify and implement QA improvements, using evidence such as automation coverage, test reliability, execution time and escaped defects to guide priorities.
• Use AI-assisted tools to support test scenario design, automation code creation, test maintenance and failure analysis; critically review outputs and protect sensitive data, maintaining human accountability for quality decisions.
• Evaluate and pilot AI-enabled testing approaches, assessing their value and limitations before recommending adoption and sharing reusable practices with the team.
• Coach and mentor other QAs through pairing, automation code reviews and knowledge-sharing sessions, helping them build testing, automation and responsible AI skills.
• Partner with the Chapter Lead to shape the team’s future quality strategy and automation roadmap, identify capability gaps and propose improvements to tools, skills and ways of working.
• Help the Chapter Lead translate improvement priorities into practical team initiatives, making technical trade-offs and dependencies visible and reviewing progress towards sustainable delivery.

Who you are

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Engineering or another related field.
• 4-6 years of experience in software testing, including strong hands-on experience in test automation, API testing and integration testing. Practical experience with Karate, Playwright and/or SoapUI is highly desirable.
• Hands-on coding experience in TypeScript and/or JavaScript, with the ability to write clean, reusable and maintainable automation code and review contributions from other QAs.
• Strong experience in API testing, including REST and/or SOAP services, request/response validation, authentication, data handling and testing integrations between systems.
• Experience integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines and using version control, defect tracking and test management tools.
• Good understanding of system architecture, system integrations and dependencies, with the ability to understand and test end-to-end flows that span multiple systems operating and interacting in parallel.
• Ability to analyse complex technical flows and quickly understand new applications, architectures, interfaces and technologies.
• Practical experience using AI-assisted tools for testing or coding, with sound judgement in validating outputs, recognising limitations and handling sensitive data responsibly.
• Ability to coach and mentor other QAs, provide constructive technical feedback and share automation practices effectively.
• Experience applying an agile mindset and iterative ways of working within an engineering team, including incremental delivery, feedback, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
• Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with QA Engineers, Developers, Business Analysts, Architects, Product teams and other technical stakeholders.
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Greek and English, with the ability to communicate clearly with both technical and business audiences.
• A fast learner, comfortable working in a complex technology landscape and able to quickly understand unfamiliar systems, business flows and technical dependencies.
• Fulfilled military services (for male candidates).

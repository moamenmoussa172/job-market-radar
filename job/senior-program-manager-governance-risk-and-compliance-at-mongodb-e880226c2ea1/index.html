About the job

The Internal Data team is on a mission to power data-informed decisions and applications across MongoDB, oriented around the business areas of Customer, Product, Finance, and Employee. We build and scale the data products MongoDB employees rely on every day, including data pipelines, datasets, analytics, APIs, ML models, and frameworks that form the backbone of our internal data ecosystem.

We treat data as a product. That means applying the same rigor, user focus, and outcome orientation to internal data that MongoDB brings to its external-facing platform and products. As MongoDB scales, our financial data footprint has grown exponentially. You will inherit the challenge of providing the focus and product management needed to transform complex, high-volume consumption data into clean, compliant, and near-real-time financial products; moving our Finance teams away from manual workflows and toward automated, AI-driven forecasting.

This role will be based remotely in United States.

What You'll Do

  • Own the overall vision, strategy, prioritization standards, and investment direction across the Finance data product line and domain
  • Act as a trusted advisor to senior business and Finance leaders, resolving cross-functional conflicts, aligning competing needs, and shaping expectations across multiple functions
  • Institute repeatable execution practices, metrics, and standards for product-led ways of working across data teams and pods
  • Mentor and coach Product Managers on applying product thinking, rigorous discovery, and outcome-based prioritization to internal platforms
  • Arbitrate domain-level portfolio trade-offs and remove systemic blockers across teams to scale delivery and enablement playbooks
  • Define domain-level AI strategy for financial product workflows, ensuring AI capabilities are consistent, governed, responsible, and tied to measurable outcomes
  • Influence IT-wide architecture and adoption approaches while establishing enterprise trade-off decisions and success criteria before work begins

What We're Looking For

  • 12+ years of experience with deep domain expertise in financial data platforms and enterprise systems
  • Proven track record operating autonomously and setting standards that other teams and product managers follow
  • Deep cross-system fluency across complex financial architectures, enterprise ERPs, and data integration patterns
  • Demonstrated capability as an enterprise-minded leader who can institutionalize product-led practices across a domain
  • Hands-on experience with ERPs and core finance systems, with a strong understanding of journal entries, AR/AP, and aging schedules
  • Familiarity with FP&A and Workforce Planning (WFP) ecosystems
  • Experience handling sensitive data and SOX compliance, with the judgment to balance risk controls with velocity
  • Strong communication skills, with a track record of providing clear product visibility to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • High autonomy, with the ability to take full ownership of workstreams once priorities are aligned
  • Experience at top-tier software companies with consumption-based business models is a major plus

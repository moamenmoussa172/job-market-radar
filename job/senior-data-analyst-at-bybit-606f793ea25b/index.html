About the job

About Us

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own end-to-end analytics for the fiat business line — P2P, One-Click Buy (credit card / balance / money deposit), fiat Spot pairs, and fiat FTD — covering conversion funnels, corridor-level user behavior, and revenue structure
  • Build and maintain dashboards and automated reporting to track fiat OKR metrics
  • Analyze fiat revenue composition end to end — trading fees, spread, and hedging PnL — and construct full-chain revenue models from gross revenue through channel cost and hedging cost to net revenue
  • Monitor fiat liquidity supply and demand: order-book depth and spread by currency, maker/taker supply structure, and competitive benchmarking against other exchanges and local platforms
  • Evaluate payment channel and P2P partner performance — conversion, cost, failure reasons, settlement reconciliation — and support commercial negotiations with data
  • Deliver data support for regional entities and regulatory requirements, where accuracy standards are externally auditable
  • Build operational monitoring for fiat workflows: order/ticket SLA and aging metrics, queue and pending-stage breakdowns, and anomaly alerting
  • Support product, operations, regional, compliance, and finance teams with ad-hoc analysis, pre/post-launch impact assessment, and A/B or quasi-experimental evaluation of pricing and fee changes
  • Provide actionable recommendations on pricing, currency and corridor prioritization, maker incentive design, and channel strategy to support fiat growth and monetization

Required Qualifications

  • Fluent in English and Mandarin (written and spoken)
  • 5+ years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, or a related analytical role
  • Background in payments, fiat on/off-ramp, FX, cross-border remittance, banking, or fintech required
  • Strong proficiency in SQL and at least one analytical programming language (Python or R)
  • Experience with BI/visualization tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or similar)
  • Solid understanding of conversion funnel and transaction-flow analysis — drop-off diagnosis, failure attribution, cohort-based conversion
  • Strong analytical thinking with the ability to translate complex data into clear, actionable insights
  • High attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy, particularly for externally reported or compliance-facing figures
  • Comfortable working across different time zones

Preferred Qualifications

  • Familiarity with crypto fiat gateways — P2P marketplaces, card acquiring, local bank rails, stablecoin settlement
  • Experience with two-sided marketplace dynamics (maker/taker supply, pricing competitiveness, ad ranking) or market microstructure and order-book depth analysis
  • Experience supporting regulated entities and regulatory reporting
  • Experience in fraud, AML, or market-abuse analytics
  • Experience with statistical analysis, forecasting, quasi-experimental design, or machine learning techniques
  • Prior experience at a crypto exchange, payment institution, or digital asset platform

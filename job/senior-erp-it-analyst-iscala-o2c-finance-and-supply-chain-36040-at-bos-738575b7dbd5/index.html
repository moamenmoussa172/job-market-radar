About the job

The iScala ERP Techno-Functional Analyst will be part of the Bosch Home Comfort IT organization,
responsible for delivering standardized, scalable, and business-aligned iScala ERP solutions across
Order-to-Cash (O2C), Finance, and Supply Chain domains.
This role spans the end-to-end product lifecycle, including strategy, architecture, design,
implementation, deployment, and continuous optimization of iScala ERP solutions.
The candidate will act as a functional and technical leader, driving solution architecture, product
roadmap, and delivery governance, ensuring alignment with Bosch standards and global templates.
Additionally, the role supports business–IT alignment, process standardization, and ERP
modernization initiatives, including integration with enterprise platforms.

Role & Responsibilities
Strategy & Conceptualization:
• Align iScala ERP solutions across Order-to-Cash (O2C), Procure-to-Pay (P2P), and Record-to-
Report (RTR) with overall business and IT strategy
• Collaborate with Business Process Owners (Finance, Commercial, Supply Chain) to understand
business needs and translate them into scalable system solutions
• Identify opportunities for the process optimization, automation, and standardization across
global operations
• Support demand management, prioritization, and roadmap planning for ERP initiatives
• Contribute to ERP transformation programs, including multi-country rollouts and coexistence
strategies
Design & Planning:
• Lead requirements gathering, analysis, and documentation across O2C, P2P, and RTR processes
• Conduct Fit-Gap analysis to evaluate business requirements against iScala capabilities and
recommend optimal solutions
• Define end-to-end solution architecture aligned with global templates, enterprise standards,
and regional compliance requirements
• Act as Subject Matter Expert (SME) across iScala modules including Order Management, Billing,
AR, AP, GL, Fixed Assets, Procurement, and Inventory
• Design and ensure integration with upstream and downstream systems (CRM, SAP, Oracle, banking
systems, and third-party platforms)
• Review functional specifications and collaborate with technical teams to deliver custom
developments, reports, and interfaces
• Support project planning, scoping, and vendor coordination activities

Build, Deploy & Transition:
• Support and execute full lifecycle delivery (Design → Build → Test → Deploy → Operate) for
iScala implementations
• Configure and validate system functionality across O2C, P2P, and RTR modules
• Collaborate with development teams on customizations, integrations, and enhancements
• Participate in system integration testing (SIT), user acceptance testing (UAT), and defect
management
• Oversee data migration activities, including master data and transactional data (customers,
vendors, AR/AP balances, GL data)
• Support cutover planning, go-live execution, and post-go-live stabilization activities
• Ensure proper documentation and knowledge transfer to business and support teams
Run & Operate:
• Support production operations by ensuring system stability and reliability
• Collaborate with operations and support teams to resolve issues and ensure smooth business
continuity
• Ensure adherence to enterprise support models, SLAs, and operational standards
Optimization & Continuous Improvement:
• Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance system performance, process efficiency, and
user experience
• Promote standardization and automation across financial and supply chain processes
• Ensure adherence to regional regulatory, tax, and statutory compliance requirements
• Maintain data quality and governance across master and transactional data
• Support system upgrades, enhancements, and innovation initiatives
Phase-Out & Decommissioning:
• Support ERP transformation and legacy system decommissioning strategies
• Plan and execute data migration, archival, and retention for finance and supply chain data
• Ensure business continuity during system transitions and decommissioning activities
• Conduct post-implementation and post-decommissioning reviews to capture lessons learned

Qualifications & Experience
• Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Business, or related discipline
o MBA or a relevant Master’s degree preferred
• ERP experience, with strong focus on iScala / Epicor ERP
• 2–3 full lifecycle iScala implementations
• Experience in global template design and multi-country deployments preferred

Core Skills & Expertise
Strong techno-functional expertise in:
o iScala O2C (Order Management, Pricing, Billing, Accounts Receivable)
o iScala Finance (General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management)
o iScala P2P (Procurement, Inventory Management, Supplier/Vendor Management)
• Strong understanding of end-to-end enterprise processes (O2C, P2P, RTR/Finance cycles) across
global environments
• Hands-on experience with iScala ERP configuration, implementation, and production support
• Ability to translate business requirements into functional designs and techno-functional
solutions
• Strong experience in solution architecture across functional, technical, integration, and data
layers
• Expertise in integration architecture including APIs, web services, middleware, and batch
interfaces
• Strong knowledge of integration with CRM, SAP, Oracle, banking systems, and third-party
platforms
• Experience in data migration, master data management, and data governance (Customer, Vendor,
Financial data)
• Exposure to technical concepts including SQL, data extraction, debugging, and system analysis
• Understanding of ERP architecture, scalability, performance, and security principles in multi-
entity environments
• Experience supporting multi-country deployments with tax, statutory, and regulatory compliance
requirements
Desejáveis:
• Experience with Epicor ecosystem and modernization initiatives
• Exposure to ERP-to-cloud transformation or coexistence strategies
• Experience with Agile / SAFe delivery models
• Understanding of DevOps and CI/CD concepts
• Familiarity with enterprise architecture frameworks

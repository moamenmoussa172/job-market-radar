About the job

Bosch Digital is looking for an IT Development Manager (SAP) to drive the evolution and support of business-critical solutions, leading development and digital transformation initiatives across Latin America. The professional will be responsible for software development activities, fostering technological innovation, and ensuring the delivery of scalable, secure solutions aligned with business needs. The role will work closely with business stakeholders, Squad Leads, Product Owners, Architects, and multidisciplinary teams to translate complex requirements into sustainable, high-value solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the development, implementation, and continuous evolution of business-critical applications and digital solutions.
  • Translate business requirements into scalable and sustainable technical solutions aligned with corporate standards.
  • Lead end-to-end development activities, including planning, estimation, development, testing, deployment, and support.
  • Act as a technical reference, ensuring quality, governance, and alignment with Bosch Digital engineering practices.
  • Support strategic initiatives related to SAP ERP (R/3), SAP S/4HANA, Brazil Localization, Brazilian Tax Reform, Foreign Trade, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Generative AI (GenAI).
  • Coordinate technical activities across regional and global teams.
  • Define and govern technical requirements, solution architecture, documentation, and software quality standards.
  • Promote Agile and DevOps practices, as well as the use of AI technologies to increase productivity and efficiency.
  • Contribute to the execution of the organization's digital transformation roadmap.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Advanced English proficiency to work effectively in a global environment.
  • Solid experience in enterprise solution development and technical management of development initiatives.
  • Experience with SAP ERP (R/3) and SAP S/4HANA.
  • Broad knowledge of SAP processes, with a strong focus on SAP SD (Sales & Distribution).
  • Experience with system integration using APIs, EDI, SAP PI/PO, and SAP CPI / Integration Suite.
  • Experience with ABAP development and integrations across SAP modules.
  • Knowledge of Brazil Localization, taxation, electronic invoicing, and regulatory requirements.
  • Experience or participation in initiatives related to the Brazilian Tax Reform.
  • Knowledge of solution architecture, software development lifecycle, and enterprise integrations.
  • Experience with Agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) and DevOps practices.
  • Knowledge of IT governance, software quality, compliance, and auditing.
  • Experience in stakeholder management and multicultural environments.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Knowledge of additional SAP modules, such as MM, FI/CO, WM/EWM, PP, QM, or similar.
  • Experience with SAP BTP, SAP Fiori, and SAP cloud technologies.
  • Knowledge of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI (GenAI), and AI-assisted development platforms.
  • Experience with environments and tools based on Claude or other advanced AI platforms.
  • SAP, Agile, Scrum, ITIL, or DevOps certifications.
  • Experience in global projects or large-scale corporate transformation initiatives.
  • Intermediate or advanced Spanish proficiency.
  • Knowledge of manufacturing, logistics, supply chain, sales, or operations processes.

About the job

We're looking for an Engineering Manager to lead our Loyalty team. The team builds the platforms behind how inDrive rewards users across the Super App: offers and promotions, rewards and partner benefits.
You'll lead the team and own the platforms that serve millions of users.

  • Lead and grow a team of backend, mobile and QA engineers, supporting their development and careers.
  • Own delivery of the team's roadmap with product and business partners, balancing scope against capacity.
  • Run reliable platforms that handle real value, keeping every reward and redemption correct and auditable at scale.
  • Build flexible offer and benefit engines so product and marketing teams can launch promotions, rewards and perks through configuration, not code.
  • Shape the architecture with your tech lead, keeping the platform configurable across verticals and markets.
  • Work with payments, app, data, anti-fraud and partner teams to ship features end to end.
  • Tie engineering work to retention and business outcomes and use experiments to learn fast.
  • 7+ years in software engineering, including 2+ years managing engineering teams.
  • Experience growing engineers and attracting strong senior talent.
  • A track record of delivering high-load backend systems, ideally in promotions, loyalty, billing or payments.
  • A solid grasp of distributed systems: idempotency, consistency and reconciliation.
  • Clear communication with engineers, product and business leaders.
  • Fluent English
  • Experience working in an international environment.

Nice to have

  • Experience with loyalty, promotions or benefits products, such as offer or rules engines.
  • Working with personalisation or ML teams to target offers to the right users.
  • Integrations with external partners or payment providers.
    Go, Kafka, Kubernetes, MySQL or PostgreSQL.
  • Leading distributed teams in emerging markets.

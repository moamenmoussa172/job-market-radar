About this Role

We are seeking a Technical Product Owner to lead product ownership and technical delivery, translating business requirements into effective technical solutions. You will own and prioritise the product backlog, shape the product vision and strategy, and work closely with technical teams and business stakeholders to deliver high-quality features aligned with customer and business objectives.

What you’ll do

  • Own and manage the product backlog, defining, prioritising and communicating the product vision.
  • Translate business requirements into technical solutions in close collaboration with developers.
  • Lead sprint planning and ensure tasks are clearly aligned with product goals.
  • Drive product strategy while balancing technical debt, feature development and customer needs.
  • Collaborate with technical teams on architecture and tool selection, ensuring designs follow best practices and customer requirements.
  • Encourage technical innovation and continuous product improvements.
  • Guide and support the technical team in connecting business priorities with technical solutions and foster a collaborative working environment.
  • Support quality through code reviews, unit testing, coding standards and maintainability practices.
  • Provide stakeholders and business leaders with regular updates on product progress, risks and technical challenges.
  • Deliver high-quality features within agreed scope and adapt effectively to changing timelines and business requirements.

Who you are

You are a product-focused technology professional who can effectively connect business requirements with technical delivery. You are comfortable owning product priorities, working closely with development teams and communicating technical considerations to business stakeholders.

You bring experience or capability in:

  • Product ownership and backlog management.
  • Sprint planning and prioritisation.
  • Translating business needs into technical requirements and solutions.
  • Working with software development teams.
  • Technical architecture and tool-selection discussions.
  • Software quality practices, including code reviews, unit testing and coding standards.
  • Managing technical debt alongside feature development.
  • Stakeholder management and reporting.
  • Technical mentoring and collaborative team leadership.
  • Identifying and communicating technical risks and delivery challenges.

This role offers the opportunity to influence both product direction and technical delivery, working closely with business and technology stakeholders. What skills you will learn

Through the responsibilities described in the role, you will have opportunities to further develop:

  • Technical product ownership and backlog management.
  • Product strategy and prioritisation.
  • Sprint planning and agile product delivery.
  • Stakeholder communication and risk management.
  • Architecture and technology decision-making.
  • Technical quality management and maintainability practices.
  • Technical mentoring and collaborative team leadership.

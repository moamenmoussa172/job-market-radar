About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who we are

We’re Ethena, an AI platform that’s revolutionizing the Compliance space. Our team of agents helps Compliance and People leaders run proactive Compliance programs that drive real behavior change.

From Policies and Disclosures to Training, Hotline, and Analytics, our agents work together to drive smarter, faster, human-led decision-making. Hotline and training data rolls up into fully customizable, board-ready risk reports. Our AI call taker synthesizes data to recommend improvements to company policies and training. Every signal lands in one place, so Compliance leaders can spot risk early and fix it before it becomes a problem. The agents do the legwork, and people make every judgment call.

More than 2,000 organizations, from Pinterest and Zendesk to the Salvation Army, run their programs on Ethena. That covers millions of employees, from the warehouse floor to desktop workers. Learners give our training a 93% approval rating, 96% of customers renew, and our business is growing quickly with 50%+ YoY revenue growth.

Lots of companies call themselves AI-forward. We’ve actually built the tools to prove it. Come help us build what’s next.

How we work

We’re a team that values diversity in all its forms and loves adding new perspectives. We ask thoughtful questions, challenge assumptions, and build with empathy — for customers and each other. We're passionate about the work we do, love feedback, and lead with high agency and accountability. When we see something that could be better, we step up to the plate and do something about it. We take our work seriously, but are careful not to take ourselves too seriously. Because doing great work should be fun.

Our workforce is fully remote, and your personal working hours can be based on your own timezone. All team-wide meetings are scheduled to be inclusive of all North American time zones.

Who we’re looking for

Ethena is looking for a Customer Success Engineer who sits at the intersection of technical implementation, product expertise, and AI-powered workflows. This isn't a traditional CSM role — you'll be central to how new customers get onboarded and configured, using AI tools to move faster and build smarter than a traditional implementation function. You'll work closely with our Account Management team to ensure customers go live quickly, integrate deeply, and expand confidently.

What You'll Do

  • Own technical implementation for your book of customers — HRIS integrations, SSO setup, LMS connectors, API configurations, and assignment automation logic.
  • At the Associate level you'll start with mid-market accounts and grow into enterprise; at the Senior level you'll own complex enterprise implementations from the start.
  • Use, extend and build AI-powered workflows to automate repeatable implementation tasks (e.g., launch requirements, config QA, onboarding documentation generation)
  • Partner with AMs to run discovery on technical requirements during kickoffs and translate them into scoped implementation plans
  • Serve as the primary technical point of contact during onboarding, reducing time-to-value for new customers
  • Diagnose and resolve integration issues, working cross-functionally with Product and Engineering when needed
  • Work from our implementation playbooks and documentation, and flag where the process could be more efficient. You'll also own improving them, using AI tooling like AI Training Builder, internal Compass workflows, and vibe-coding your own apps
  • Contribute to the scaled enablement motion by building reusable templates, guides, and self-serve assets
  • What We're Looking For
  • Experience in a Customer Success, CS Engineering, Solutions Engineering, Implementation Consulting, or Technical CSM role — ideally at a SaaS company
  • Hands-on technical experience with tools like HRIS platforms (Workday, ADP, HiBob), SSO (SAML 2.0), or API-based integrations — or a track record of picking up unfamiliar technical systems fast
  • Demonstrated use of AI tools to improve workflow efficiency and solve customer problems — not just "familiar with AI," but actually building workflows that save time and reduce manual work
  • Excitement to take troubleshooting work off an engineer’s plate and solve a problem yourself
  • Comfort operating in ambiguous environments and interest in building process where none exists
  • Strong written communication skills; you can explain technical concepts to non-technical compliance buyers
  • Passion for what we're building at Ethena and strong alignment with our core values.
  • Bonus: experience in compliance, HR tech, or legal tech
  • We're open to a range of backgrounds. If you're earlier in your career with strong technical instincts, or coming from an adjacent role and ready to go deeper on implementation, we'd like to see your application.

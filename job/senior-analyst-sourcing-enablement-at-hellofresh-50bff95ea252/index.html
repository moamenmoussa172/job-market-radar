About the job

We’re hiring a Senior Analyst to join our Sourcing Enablement Team within Supply Chain Enablement & Insights at HelloFresh. In this role, you will partner closely with purchasing leaders, using data analytics, project management, and strategic communication to drive high-impact sourcing initiatives and optimize our tooling and processes. To succeed, you should thrive on turning ambiguous datasets into actionable business insights, supporting negotiations, and leading cross-functional alignment. We are looking for a proactive problem solver who can dive into complex details and deliver clear, strategic recommendations.

You will …

  • Collaborate with Procurement category leaders to facilitate high-impact sourcing events and manage data analysis for $100M+ in RFP spend.
  • Synthesize complex datasets into clear, actionable business insights for senior leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Develop, scale, and maintain decision-critical data products and AI tooling to streamline procurement strategy and process management.
  • Execute rapid, ad-hoc spend and cost analytics to identify savings opportunities and support ongoing category initiatives.
  • Represent North American team interests in the global rollout of procurement tools, programs, and process enhancements, leading end-to-end adoption by gathering business requirements (BRDs), establishing audit and approval controls, and driving team compliance through training and enforcement mechanisms.

You are…

  • Data-driven – You can organize, analyze, and synthesize unstructured data into actionable insights to better inform operational decision-making.
  • Intellectually curious – You have an insatiable desire to understand how things work, why they work the way they do, identify data-driven insights, and consistently challenge the status quo.
  • Detail-oriented – You possess strong organizational skills and consistently demonstrate a methodical approach to all your work.
  • A self-starter – You are highly motivated and passionate. You’re eager to take on new projects without knowing exactly what you will uncover.
  • Agile – You thrive in fast-paced and dynamic environments and are comfortable working autonomously.
  • A structured-thinker – You use logic to identify issues and opportunities to solve complex problems.
  • An excellent communicator – You can explain complex technical information to a variety of audiences.

You have…

  • 2-4 years of relevant work experience in an advanced analytical capacity (preparing complex reports, presentations, and data visualizations) and/or 1-2 years of management consulting experience
  • Undergraduate degree in Supply Chain, Business, Math, Engineering, Data/Computer Science, or other relevant areas of study
  • Advanced fluency in spreadsheet modeling and data analytics
  • Basic proficiency in SQL required; advanced SQL and/or Python experience is a plus
  • Fluent in English

Flexible Hybrid Approach

At HelloFresh, we know that flexible work arrangements are essential in enabling you to do your best work, while balancing your personal and life needs. Offering remote work flexibility, along with the opportunity to interact and collaborate in the office are all a part of creating a great employee experience.

To meet these needs, we are pleased to provide Flexible Hybrid work. Flexible Hybrid is a people-first approach that is based on choice, trust, personalization, and empowers teams to choose when and how often they work from the office and work from home, in addition to team days and company days. This means a minimum of 2 days in office per week, with most teams in office between 2-3 days a week.

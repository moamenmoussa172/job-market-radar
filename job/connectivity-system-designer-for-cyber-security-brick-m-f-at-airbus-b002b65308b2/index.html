Connectivity System Designer for cyber security brick (m/f)

About the job

Job Description:

Airbus Commercial Aircraft is looking for a Connectivity System Designer for cyber security brick (f/m) to join our Engineering department based in Toulouse, France.

You will be part of a team developing dedicated cybersecurity components for embedded information and connectivity systems. As part of this team, you will be involved in system design and security enforcement across interconnected architectures, aiming at delivering resilient and fully compliant next-generation avionics solutions.

Your working environment:

Global capital of aeronautics and European capital for space research, Toulouse is a dynamic city in the southwest of France served by an international airport. Ideally located between the Mediterranean sea and the Atlantic ocean and close to the Pyrenees mountains, it offers plenty of options for outdoor activities!

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, agreements on success and profit sharing schemes, employee savings plan abounded by Airbus and employee stock purchase plan on a voluntary basis.
  • Work / Life Balance: Extra days-off for special occasions, holiday transfer option, a Staff council offering many social, cultural and sport activities and other services.
  • Wellbeing / Health: Complementary health insurance coverage (disability, invalidity, death). Depending on the site: health services center, concierge services, gym, carpooling application.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, expert career path, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

At Airbus, we support you to work, connect and collaborate more easily and flexibly. Wherever possible, we foster flexible working arrangements to stimulate innovative thinking.

Your challenges:

  • Identify and drive system development life cycle activities required to engineer cybersecurity bricks in compliance with programme standards and airworthiness regulations.
  • Establish and conduct design checkpoints and technical reviews aligned with program phases and architectural complexity targets.
  • Deliver technical datasets, interface descriptions, and detailed system requirements enabling seamless implementation across target architectural entities.
  • Coordinate security verification, communication protocol robustness, and system connectivity validation.
  • Interface with architecture, product security, and programme stakeholders to secure compliance and delivery milestones.

Your boarding pass:

  • 3+ years of hands-on professional experience in communication systems engineering, network design, or embedded connectivity platforms.
  • Solid background in cybersecurity architecture applied to industrial, embedded, or critical operational environments.
  • Proven experience with structured systems engineering processes (e.g., ARP4754A, DO-326A / ED-202A, ISO 27001, or equivalent frameworks).
  • Demonstrated ability to author formal system specifications and lead technical gating reviews.
  • Professional proficiency in both French and English in a multicultural operational setup.

Not a 100% match? No worries! Airbus supports your personal growth with customized development solutions.

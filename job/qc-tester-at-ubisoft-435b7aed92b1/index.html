About the job

*Please note that a work permit for Spain obtained prior to applying for the position is mandatory.*

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Assist team in testing the product before release to market and subsequent updates with a close to defect free status goal.
  • Working effectively in completing assigned QC tasks: test plan completion, feedback on features, investigative tasks, live ops checks, ad hoc testing etc.
  • Work closely with assigned game and tools teams to identify areas to test and construct relevant test cases.
  • Ensure that all issues are recorded in an efficient, complete and informative manner.
  • Respond to the development team’s requests in line with the project e.g., providing more information, balancing, build installation etc.
  • Writing QC reports in a professional manner for QC Leads or senior management.

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Fluent english is mandatory
  • At least 1 year of experience in a Quality Assurance/Control position.
  • Understanding of bug/defect management systems (like JIRA) along with exceptional bug reporting skills.
  • Experience with version control software such as perforce and git.
  • Experience working on all main mobile platforms e.g., Android, iOS, Amazon.
  • Experience with creating new testing scenarios based on project’s documentation or existing builds.
  • Have a basic understanding of the server-client architecture.
  • Computer literacy and familiarity with Microsoft software (Word, Excel and Outlook)
  • Have a proactive attitude, able to identify issues and provide solutions.
  • Exceptional attention to detail.
  • Real team player: we use agile development methods, where the team members are in permanent contact.
  • Open-minded, respectful attitude towards others and their work and good communication skills are a must.
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written English.
  • Passion for games and some knowledge of the mobile F2P gaming industry.

Bonus / Nice to Haves

  • Experience with test automation is a big plus.
  • Basic knowledge of Jenkins, XRay, TelNet and SSH.
  • Bachelor's degree in any Video Games related field
  • Good knowledge of Spanish or Catalan is a plus.

YOUR LIFE AT UBISOFT BARCELONA MOBILE (and PERKS)

  • Ticket Restaurant: 220€ per month, and a flexible compensation plan (ticket transport & ticket nursery free of taxes).
  • Flexible working hours: 40-hour workweek with start times between 8 AM and 10 AM (or from 7:30 AM when working onsite to avoid traffic jams), and early leave from 4 PM (Fridays from 2 PM).
  • Reduced schedule (35 hrs per week) in July and August, Easter, Christmas holidays, and special days like Sant Jordi and Revetlla de Sant Joan.
  • Personal Matters Hours: 24 hours paid for by Ubisoft that you can use for personal matters during your work schedule
  • Holidays: 25 days per year + 1 day on yourUbi-Birthday.
  • 5 extra holiday days on the year you celebrate your 5th/10th/15th/20th year with us.
  • Health insurance with Cigna, including physiotherapy and psychology sessions at a reduced price.
  • Life Insurance with AXA.
  • Office Gym open from 7 AM to 8 PM
  • Mobile Device Grant of 400 EUR starting in your 1st year in the company.
  • Language training for English, Spanish, and French.
  • Professional development training & programs, based upon request.
  • Ubisoft games are available at a discount , and Ubisoft Connect in PC is free
  • Locally roasted craft coffee by INCAPTO (it’s very, very good).

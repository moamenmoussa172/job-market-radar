About the job

The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. About Elastic

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables government agencies and enterprises to turn massive, disparate data into real-time operational intelligence at scale. The Elastic Search AI Platform brings together the precision of search and the speed of AI to accelerate mission-critical outcomes across the public sector. By unifying structured and unstructured data while maintaining strict security, compliance, and data sovereignty standards, Elastic’s cloud and hybrid solutions for enterprise search, observability, and cyber threat hunting help agencies deliver on the promise of modern AI and IT modernization.

Thousands of organizations worldwide—including federal agencies like NASA, DHS, VA, US Army, US Airforce, GSA, as well as enterprise leaders such as Barclays, Cisco, eBay, Fairfax, ING, Goldman Sachs, Microsoft, The Mayo Clinic, The New York Times, Wikipedia, and Verizon—rely on Elastic to power their most vital infrastructure. Founded in 2012, Elastic is a distributed company with Elasticians supporting missions around the globe. Learn more at elastic.co.

The Opportunity

Are you ready to apply your technical expertise to high-impact public sector missions?

As a Federal Civilian Solutions Architect (SA) at Elastic, you will serve as the primary technical strategist and trusted advisor to Federal Civilian agencies, system integrators, and channel partners. You will map complex agency mission requirements—ranging from cyber defense and infrastructure monitoring to enterprise search and analytics—directly to the Elastic Stack.

In this role, you will lead technical engagements across the sales lifecycle, advocate for agency needs within Elastic product teams, and serve as a technical thought leader in the local D.C., Maryland, and Virginia (DMV) federal ecosystem.

We’re looking for architects and engineers with experience in influencing technical product, design and architecture decisions on technical merit, but also on value to the mission. Design experience with AI systems, foundation models, and closed LLM are highly desirable, especially when using data systems to provide secure enterprise context. Our solutions for Cyber security SIEM and XDR are highly relevant to mission objectives today that align to Federal cyber strategies, such as M2614 and Zero Trust. Experts in these topics should have the ability to thrive in this role and lead our customer engagements.

“We question the status quo, debate the best ways to accomplish our goals, and hold ourselves to the highest standards of performance. We take pride in understanding our public sector customers’ unique mission needs and delivering recommendations built on proven global deployments. Join this team if you are looking for a collaborative, high-growth environment where you can drive meaningful federal impact while elevating your career.”

— VP, Worldwide Solution Architecture

Location: Washington, D.C. / Baltimore, MD / Tysons Corner, VA Metropolitan Area (Hybrid – periodic in-office collaboration at the Elastic office in Reston/Tysons, VA required)

Travel: Up to 30% within the Federal Civilian territory, with occasional national/international events.

What You’ll Do

  • Serve as Mission Strategist: Act as the lead technical advisor for your assigned Federal Civilian accounts, working alongside Account Executives to drive territory strategy from initial discovery to the technical win.
  • Architect Value-Driven Solutions: Develop a deep understanding of agency modernization mandates, IT goals, and operational pain points, articulating how Elastic delivers immediate mission value and cost optimization. Demonstrating knowledge, expertise and critical thinking to apply Elastic and integrate it with the overall enterprise data landscape.
  • Build Executive & Technical Trust: Cultivate lasting relationships across all levels of customer leadership, from program managers and enterprise architects to Agency CIOs, CISOs, and CTOs.
  • Drive Demonstrations & Proofs of Concept (POCs): Deliver compelling technical demonstrations, tailored POCs, and custom architectural prototypes to validate Elastic’s capabilities across search, observability, and security use cases.
  • Partner & Ecosystem Collaboration: Work closely with Federal System Integrators (FSIs), VARs, and strategic partners on joint account plans, RFIs/RFPs, and program captures.
  • Voice of the Federal Customer: Advocate for federal-specific requirements—such as compliance standards, air-gapped environment needs, and security controls—directly to Elastic product management and engineering.
  • Community Engagement: Represent Elastic at regional public sector events, federal user groups, and tech summits across the DMV area.

What You Bring

  • Location & Presence: Based in the Washington, D.C., Baltimore, MD, or Tysons Corner / Northern VA area, with the ability to work in a hybrid capacity and collaborate in-person at the Elastic Virginia office.
  • Presales Experience: 4+ years of experience as a Solutions Architect, Systems Engineer, or Technical Presales Consultant in the enterprise software or federal high-tech space.
  • Federal Domain Knowledge: Familiarity or direct experience supporting federal mission areas, with demonstrated experience in:
  • Healthcare & HHS
  • Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) & Regulatory Oversight
  • Federal Data & Analytics Initiatives
  • Financial Systems & Federal Reserve
  • International Affairs, State Department, and Civilian NGOs
  • Proven Track Record: Demonstrated success leading technical evaluations and analysis of alternatives (AOA), overcoming technical objections, and articulating the ROI of platform modernization and software re-architecture.
  • Technical & Strategic Influence: Proven ability to influence technical decision-makers and inspire confidence in both small technical teams and large executive audiences.
  • Continuous Curiosity: A passion for self-driven learning, keeping pace with emerging open-source technologies, cloud-native architecture, and AI/ML trends. Experience in Machine Learning (supervised and unsupervised), data resilience and failover architectures, systems designs are a plus.

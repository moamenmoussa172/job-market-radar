About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications using natural language. With millions of users worldwide, Replit is democratizing software development by removing traditional barriers to application creation.

About the Role

Over the past year, hundreds of thousands of non-technical knowledge workers from startups to the worlds’ largest companies have used Replit to build apps, dashboards, and other critical tools that help them do their jobs better.

Our early enterprise success has led us to a more ambitious vision: building the operating system for self-driving companies. We believe the shift already underway in software engineering will soon extend to every business function: people will set the vision, and teams of AI agents will work together to carry it out.

We are hiring a Staff/Senior Engineer to join the Enterprise product team and build the platform for companies to create, manage, and govern their own AI teammates. You’ll help build the primitives that allow Agents to work proactively, collaboratively, and learn over time, all while respecting the strict governance and security requirements of enterprise customers.

This is a tiger team that combines complex backend platform engineering with enterprise product needs and touches every major Replit launch. You’ll help decide what products we ship, not just how they are built, and you’ll see your work impact real teams at the largest organizations in the world.

In this role you will:

  • Talk to customers and shape the product. Own technical design and delivery for enterprise-facing features end-to-end, from ideation to delivery.
  • Work on a frontier coding agent. Contribute to the harness and build core primitives that let Replit Agent operate reliably and securely on real-world enterprise systems and data.
  • Define the UX for multiplayer agents. Balance the needs of users and enterprise admins while constructing entirely new, multiplayer AI experiences. No one knows what the ideal collaboration model for agentic work is; you will help us discover it.
  • Build the habitat for enterprise agents. Design and build the permissions, governance, and administration capabilities that enterprises need to trust Replit with mission-critical data and systems. Architect the platform where enterprise customers can build maximally capable, autonomous agents, without sacrificing control.
  • Use the product you build every day. We exclusively use Replit to build Replit. Our industry and tooling is changing rapidly; you get to experience the future before everyone else does.

Required skills and experience:

  • Full-stack systems thinking. You’re comfortable across the stack - from databases and APIs to frontend frameworks. You need not have experience in any particular language (Agents write the code anyway 🙂), but your fundamentals must be rock-solid.
  • Personal and technical excellence. You hold a high bar for yourself and expect the others around you to do the same. You sweat the small stuff, because you know the micro-decisions compound into delightful user experiences.
  • Extreme ownership. You have a record of shipping high-impact features on aggressive timelines because you refuse to be blocked. Nothing is “someone else’s job”. If it’s broken, you fix it.
  • Product judgement. You can distill ambiguous customer needs into well-defined technical solutions. You are comfortable wearing the PM hat when needed and can act decisively, with conviction in your own judgement.
  • Enterprise empathy. You have experience building B2B or enterprise software products. You understand the needs of different personas in F500 companies, and know what it takes to get complex enterprise features deployed at scale.
  • Genuine passion for our mission. You are excited about shaping the future of human<>agent collaboration and the opportunity to transform how billions of people work. You are motivated by extremely ambitious goals and are excited to tackle the hard problems that come with them.

Nice to Have:

  • Experience with enterprise identity, authorization, permissions, or governance systems (SSO, SCIM, RBAC, ReBAC, PBAC).
  • Experience designing distributed services, APIs, and data models, with an understanding of reliability, consistency, scalability, and performance tradeoffs
  • Ex-founder or previous experience in a high-growth startup environment

Tools + Tech Stack for this role:

  • Replit

This role may not be a fit if:

  • You prefer working exclusively on backend systems without user interaction
  • You're uncomfortable with the pace and changing priorities of a startup environment
  • You require highly structured requirements and aren't comfortable with ambiguity
  • You were a late adopter of coding agents or are hesitant to trust them with complex scope.
  • You are looking for a comfortable and easy job

This is a full-time role that is held from our Foster City, CA office. The role has an in-office requirement of Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

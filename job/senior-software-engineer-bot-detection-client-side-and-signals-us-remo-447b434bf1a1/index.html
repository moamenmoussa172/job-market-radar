About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

At Hugging Face, we're on a journey to democratize good AI. We are building the fastest growing platform for AI builders with over 20 million users who collectively shared over 4 million models, 1 million datasets & 1.5 million apps. Our open-source libraries have more than 700,000 stars on Github.

About the Role

Hugging Face hosts millions of models, datasets and Spaces, and serves a very large community of developers. That scale also brings automated abuse: fake accounts, scraping, spam, credential stuffing, and bots gaming downloads, likes and trending signals. We're building a small, dedicated bot-detection effort inside our Trust & Safety team, and we're looking for an engineer to own the signal-collection side of it.

You'll design and ship the mechanisms that tell humans from automation across every surface where people and machines touch the Hub: web, API, git/LFS, CLI and client libraries. "Front" here is broad. It covers browser instrumentation, but also what we can learn from network, TLS and protocol behavior, and from how clients talk to our infrastructure.

What you'll do

  • Design, build and deploy detection signals in production: browser and device fingerprinting, client-side challenges, TLS/HTTP fingerprinting, behavioral telemetry
  • Analyze how real-world bots and automation frameworks work (headless browsers, anti-detect browsers, residential proxies, CAPTCHA farms) and build countermeasures that hold up when attackers adapt
  • Work with our data-focused bot-detection engineer to turn raw signals into reliable features and decisions
  • Respect user privacy and keep legitimate developers, researchers and API users free of friction
  • Partner with infra, security and Hub engineers to roll out mitigations (rate limiting, step-up challenges, enforcement) safely at scale
  • Contribute openly where it makes sense: tooling, write-ups and research shared with the community

Requirements

  • You have solid hands-on experience in bot detection, anti-fraud, browser fingerprinting, web security or a closely related field, and you've shipped things that ran in production
  • You understand browser internals and the web platform deeply (JavaScript, DOM and rendering APIs, WebGL/Canvas/Audio, extensions), as well as networking protocols (HTTP/2/3, TLS)
  • You know the attacker's toolbox from the inside (Puppeteer/Playwright/Selenium, stealth plugins, automation frameworks) and you like taking it apart
  • You're comfortable across the stack: TypeScript/JavaScript, and at least one backend language (Python, Go, Rust…)
  • You're pragmatic: you prefer a signal that works today and improves tomorrow over a perfect system that never ships
  • You care about privacy and about not punishing legitimate users

Nice to have

  • Published work or talks on fingerprinting, bot detection or web measurement (academic or industry). A research background is welcome as long as you like seeing your work in prod
  • Experience at a bot-management, anti-fraud or CDN/WAF company
  • Open-source contributions

What you'll be doing

Own the Quality Governance capability, defining enterprise quality standards, managing release quality gates, and tracking defect metrics to continuously improve delivery quality across engineering teams.

  • Define and own enterprise quality standards and QA methodologies across engineering teams, ensuring consistent, high-quality delivery.
  • Manage release quality gates, governing entry and exit criteria so releases meet defined quality and business requirements before go-live.
  • Track and analyze defect metrics and quality analytics using tools such as TestRail to identify trends and drive continuous improvement.
  • Own release management from a quality perspective, ensuring quality sign-off and release readiness across releases.
  • Own UAT readiness, quality sign-off, and post-release defect triage for major launches, ensuring business and technical stakeholders have clear entry and exit criteria.
  • Lead and develop the QA team, set technical direction, mentoring engineers, and building QA capability.
  • Report quality posture, release risk, and defect trends to engineering leadership through concise dashboards and agreed improvement actions.
  • Partner with engineering and product teams to embed quality practices early in the SDLC and report on quality posture to leadership.
  • Drive continuous improvement of QA processes, incorporating AI-assisted defect analytics and predictive quality insights to prioritize testing effort.
  • Plan, design, execute, and document functional, integration, end-to-end, and regression testing for assigned scope.

What you’ll need to succeed

  • 10+ years of hands-on experience in quality engineering and QA, including defining quality standards and managing release quality gates.
  • Strong command of QA standards, QA methodologies, release management, and defect analytics, with hands-on experience using tools such as TestRail.
  • Proven experience leading or mentoring QA engineers and driving continuous improvement in delivery quality.
  • Understanding quality metrics and reporting to support data-driven quality decisions.
  • Experience supporting quality governance in ecommerce or retail technology environments with complex integrations, releases, and business cutovers
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted defect analytics and predictive quality insights to enhance testing effectiveness.
  • Experience with organizing testing for multiple integrated systems

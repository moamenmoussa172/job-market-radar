Help Shape the Future of Secure Global Communications

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as an IT Infrastructure Engineer at Harrogate, near York, North Yorkshire.

This is an exciting opportunity to join our Mission Systems business, supporting the delivery of next generation Beyond Line of Sight (BLOS) Radio Frequency communications solutions for UK and international customers. You'll become part of a growing engineering team delivering critical technology that helps enable secure communications in some of the world's most challenging environments.

The role

As an IT Infrastructure Engineer, you'll provide engineering lifecycle support across complex and innovative communications programmes. Working within a multidisciplinary engineering team, you'll play a key role in designing, developing, testing and supporting critical infrastructure solutions that directly contribute to national security and defence capability.

This role offers the opportunity to work on cutting-edge technology programmes, collaborate with highly experienced engineering professionals and develop your technical expertise across a diverse range of infrastructure, systems and network technologies.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Generate detailed specifications, designs and other technical deliverables, working closely with peers to support engineering change and ensure adherence to process.
  • Support system design delivery, including requirements management, justification and decomposition.
  • Undertake interface decomposition and studies between systems, subsystems and functions.
  • Support operational engineering teams with in-service systems, troubleshooting technical faults and managing problems through to resolution.
  • Participate in validation, verification, testing and acceptance activities, including writing and running test events.
  • Contribute to technical interactions with the customer, internal and external stakeholders

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements with typically 3 days a week onsite but may change due to business needs.

Essential experience of the IT Infrastructure Engineer

  • Experience administering and supporting Windows Server and Linux environments
  • Experience managing Active Directory infrastructure and services
  • Experience with virtualisation and containerisation technologies such as VMware vSphere, Hyper-V, KVM, Docker or Kubernetes
  • Experience with infrastructure automation and configuration management tools such as PowerShell, Python, Bash or Ansible
  • Experience working within complex engineering, defence, telecommunications, infrastructure or technology environments

Qualifications for the IT Infrastructure Engineer

  • Degree or equivalent in Telecommunications or Electronic Engineering or HNC/HND with equivalent, relevant experience is valued and welcomed
  • Incorporated / Chartered Engineer or working towards

Security Clearance

The successful candidate must be able to achieve and maintain Developed Vetting (DV) security clearance for this role.

Many of the positions within our company are subject to national security clearance and Trade Control restrictions. This means that your eligibility for certain roles may be affected by your place of birth, nationality, current or former citizenship, and any residency you hold or have held. Further details are available at United Kingdom Security Vetting: clearance levels - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk).

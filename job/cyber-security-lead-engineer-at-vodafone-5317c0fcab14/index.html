What you’ll do

  • Drive the assurance and effectiveness of CHARM controls, ensuring alignment with Vodafone Group Cyber Security requirements and targeted security maturity levels.
  • Perform security gap and maturity assessments, identify control weaknesses, and drive technical remediation activities through to closure.
  • Track CHARM effectiveness, KRIs, security gaps and remediation progress, providing accurate input to monthly C-level and Group reporting.
  • Translate Vodafone Group Cyber Security requirements and baselines into technical security controls, improvement actions and projects.
  • Provide technical guidance and security challenge to teams where additional cyber security expertise is required, supporting consistent and effective control implementation.
  • Work closely with technology, Cyber Defence, audit, fraud, investigation and other stakeholders to address security risks and control gaps.
  • Maintain strong engagement with Vodafone Group Security teams, representing Vodafone Türkiye in relevant security reviews, assurance activities and technical discussions.
  • Assess cyber risks related to projects, systems and technology changes, providing risk-based security recommendations and decisions.
  • Coordinate security assessments and testing where required and ensure identified security findings are appropriately remediated.
  • Support the effectiveness, resilience and continuous improvement of security capabilities and platforms, including logging, DLP, UAM and data classification.
  • Support internal and external cyber security audits and assurance activities, coordinating required technical evidence and remediation actions.
  • Work with vendors and third parties to ensure security requirements and expected control effectiveness are maintained.
  • Maintain security documentation and procedures and contribute to knowledge sharing and technical guidance across the team and relevant stakeholders.

Who you are

  • University degree (BS/MS) in Engineering discipline (Electrical-Electronics, Computer Science or similar)
  • Fluent in English both written and spoken
  • 10+ years of work experience in information security or IT security
  • Certified on ISMS systems and industry spesific standards preferred (CISSP, CEH, ISO27001)
  • Strong knowledge on IT security controls
  • Knowledge on OS, DB, Microsoft systems and hardening baselines regarding to each
  • Understanding of networking, having CCNA/CCNP certificates is an advantage
  • Knowledge on Log Management, PUAM, DLP, Phishing, AV/EDR systems
  • Telecommunications industry experience is an advantage.
  • Having low level security understanding but ability to present it in a high level
  • Strong analytical deduction
  • Extensive problem solving and being result oriented
  • Ability to work in tight deadlines and delivering solutions within defined time periods
  • Team working
  • Customer oriented
  • Effective verbal and written communication skills and strong interpersonal skills, good in reporting
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively with 3rd party suppliers and internal stakeholders.
  • Male candidates should have completed or postponed military service.

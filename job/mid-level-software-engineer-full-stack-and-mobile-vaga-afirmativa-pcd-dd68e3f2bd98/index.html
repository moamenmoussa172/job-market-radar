Title: Mid-level Software Engineer (Full Stack & Mobile) - Vaga Afirmativa PcD - Indaiatuba/SP - 125048

We are looking for a Mid-Level Full-Stack Software Engineer to join our team at the Regional Office in Indaiatuba, SP. You will transform business requirements into scalable architectures, developing robust backends, resilient REST APIs, and cutting-edge mobile applications. Collaborating directly with global Product Managers and cross-functional teams, you will independently drive features from technical discovery to production. Join us to combine technical excellence with continuous innovation, modern cloud services, and AI-enabled software engineering.

Build and Deliver

  • Develop business applications and digital solutions.
  • Deliver enhancements and new capabilities.
  • Participate in solution design discussions.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues.

Enterprise Platforms

  • Configure and extend enterprise platforms.
  • Support application lifecycle activities.
  • Participate in upgrades and modernization efforts.
  • Ensure platform stability and reliability.

API & Integration Engineering

  • Design and develop integrations.
  • Build secure and maintainable APIs.

  • Improve interoperability across systems.

Mobile Engineering

  • Develop and maintain mobile applications.
  • Support Android and Flutter-based solutions.
  • Improve user experience and application performance.

Cloud & Automation

  • Leverage cloud services and platform capabilities.
  • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines.
  • Implement automation opportunities.
  • Support observability and monitoring practices.

AI & Innovation

  • Utilize AI-assisted engineering practices.
  • Contribute to intelligent automation initiatives.
  • Explore AI-enabled business solutions responsibly.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent discipline;
  • Advanced English;
  • Experience in software development, including hands-on work with a server-side language such as Python and a modern front-end framework such Flutter or any similar framework;
  • Experience developing or consuming REST APIs and working with relational or non-relational databases;
  • Working knowledge of cloud services and automated deployment pipelines;
  • Working knowledge of unit testing, secure coding, version control, and CI/CD practices;
  • Foundational understanding of AI concepts and experience using AI-enabled development tools or integrating an AI service into an application;
  • Ability to break down defined problems, communicate progress and blockers, and collaborate with stakeholders and engineers;
  • Curiosity, learning agility, and a disciplined approach to experimentation;
  • Availability to work in a hybrid model (3 days per week on-site in Indaiatuba/SP).

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with workflow automation, low-code orchestration, or enterprise system integrations;
  • Experience with PL/SQL development or foundational understanding of this language;
  • Experience with prompt design, retrieval-augmented generation, AI evaluation, or responsible AI practices;
  • Understanding of Product Management, discovery techniques, or service design;

About the job

Will provide technical and engineering leadership for the development, enhancement, modernization, and support of Alpha Data Platform Azure-based microservices and Charles River Investment Management Suite (IMS) modules, components, and related applications. Will own engineering outcomes for significant areas of these platforms and work across Engineering, Product Management, Architecture, Client Support, and other teams to deliver scalable, resilient, secure, and maintainable solutions for complex, mission-critical financial systems.

Specific duties of the position include:

  • Owning the technical direction, architecture, and successful delivery of significant Alpha Data Platform and Charles River IMS capabilities.
  • Leading engineering execution across the complete software development lifecycle, including architecture, design, development, testing, deployment, production support, and continuous improvement.
  • Designing and delivering scalable, resilient, observable, and maintainable distributed systems and Azure-based cloud-native microservices.
  • Providing technical leadership for complex system-design decisions and critical engineering challenges involving Java, distributed computing, databases, data platforms, and enterprise integration.
  • Driving modernization of existing applications and services through cloud-native architectures, microservices, event-driven processing, automation, and modern software-engineering practices.
  • Partnering with Product Management and Business Analysts to translate business, client, and product priorities into executable technical solutions and engineering roadmaps.
  • Establishing and promoting engineering standards for software design, code quality, automated testing, observability, security, performance, resilience, and operational readiness.
  • Owning software quality and ensuring engineering teams deliver solutions that meet functional and non-functional requirements while proactively identifying, managing, and reducing technical debt.
  • Providing technical leadership for troubleshooting and resolution of complex production issues affecting mission-critical client implementations.
  • Evaluating system performance, scalability, data integrity, operational risk, and architectural constraints and guiding engineering solutions to address identified issues.
  • Leading and coordinating engineering activities across multiple scrum teams and functional groups to manage dependencies, resolve technical blockers, mitigate delivery risks, and achieve strategic commitments.
  • Developing technical designs, architecture recommendations, implementation strategies, test approaches, and engineering standards for significant software capabilities.
  • Leading technical and architectural reviews, including reviews of software designs, code, testing strategies, implementation plans, and production-readiness criteria.
  • Driving continuous improvement of engineering processes, development practices, automation, and team efficiency across the software development lifecycle.
  • Leveraging artificial intelligence and AI-assisted engineering capabilities to improve software development, testing, troubleshooting, automation, and engineering productivity.
  • Applying emerging technology trends and industry innovations to improve the scalability, maintainability, resilience, and capabilities of State Street and Charles River technology platforms.
  • Building engineering capability by mentoring and developing engineers and technical leads, sharing technical expertise, and fostering a culture of engineering excellence and accountability.
  • Collaborating across organizational boundaries to support strategic initiatives, critical client requirements, and the continued evolution of Alpha Data Platform and Charles River IMS.

Minimum Requirements

Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Engineering, or a related technical field, plus significant professional software-engineering experience developing and supporting enterprise-scale production systems.

Position requires demonstrated experience in:

  • Java and object-oriented software design and development;
  • Distributed systems and distributed computing;
  • Relational databases, SQL, and data-model design;
  • Cloud-native architectures and Azure-based services;
  • Microservices and event-driven architectures;
  • Designing scalable, observable, resilient, and maintainable enterprise systems;
  • Software architecture, system design, troubleshooting, performance analysis, and production support;
  • Automated testing, software quality, observability, and modern CI/CD and DevOps practices;
  • Agile software-development and full software-development lifecycle methodologies; and
  • Technical leadership of complex software-development initiatives involving multiple engineers or teams.

Must Also Have

  • Demonstrated ability to design and deliver large-scale, mission-critical software systems.
  • Deep understanding of object-oriented design, data structures, relational databases, distributed systems, and modern software architecture.
  • Demonstrated experience modernizing enterprise applications using cloud-native, microservices, or event-driven architectural patterns.
  • Ability to make and communicate complex technical and architectural decisions while balancing scalability, resilience, maintainability, security, delivery timelines, and business requirements.
  • Experience identifying and managing technical debt and establishing engineering practices that improve software quality and long-term platform sustainability.
  • Strong understanding of financial-system requirements, including data integrity, consistency, reconciliation, auditability, and traceability.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead technical initiatives and coordinate engineering activities across teams and organizational boundaries.
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor engineers and technical leads and contribute to the development of high-performing engineering teams.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to communicate technical concepts and recommendations to engineering, product, architecture, and business stakeholders.
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively in an Agile, collaborative, globally distributed engineering environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • At least 10 years of experience with full lifecycle of project development for Java applications from similar industry
  • Experience developing enterprise data platforms or other large-scale, data-intensive systems.
  • Experience developing or modernizing technology within financial services, investment management, or FinTech.
  • Experience with high-throughput and low-latency distributed systems.
  • Experience with cloud-native systems on Microsoft Azure.
  • Experience applying artificial intelligence or AI-assisted engineering capabilities to software development, testing, troubleshooting, or automation.
  • Experience leading engineering teams responsible for enterprise-scale production systems.

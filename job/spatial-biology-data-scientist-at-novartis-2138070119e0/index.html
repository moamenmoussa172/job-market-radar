The Immunology Data Science and Molecular Pathology teams in Biomedical Research at Novartis work at the intersection of multi-omic spatial profiling, digital pathology, and advanced spatial & imaging AI models. We are recruiting a data scientist with deep expertise in spatial data analysis (imaging and/or spatial transcriptomics), experience with extracting biological insights from such data, and a strong interest in translational research.
The role will apply AI models and develop analysis pipelines for spatial profiling data. The scientist will become part of a multidisciplinary team of pathologists, data scientists, technology experts, and drug discovery teams to generate and test hypotheses, identify pathogenic mechanisms, and help discover biomarkers and clinically relevant biological features. In addition to contributing to individual drug discovery programs, the scientist will help shape the organization's spatial biology and digital pathology strategy, ensuring that emerging technologies, data standards and analytical capabilities enable integrated, scalable and AI-ready approaches to understanding tissue biology.

Job Description

Key responsibilities:

  • Design and contribute to spatial biology studies addressing biological and translational questions from drug discovery projects.
  • Develop analysis approaches, best practices and computational pipelines for spatial profiling data, including multiplexed imaging and spatial transcriptomics.
  • Modify and apply AI approaches to extract biologically meaningful insights from complex multimodal datasets.
  • Support the onboarding, assessment, and use of spatial data analysis methods, platforms and databases.
  • Stay current with advances in spatial profiling data analysis and AI modeling, propose and test new methods, work in close collaboration with data scientists to harmonize analytical approaches.
  • Work closely with pathology and biology experts to ensure spatial findings are interpreted in their appropriate morphological and disease context.
  • Support scientific publications and present at external and internal conferences.

Requirements

  • Ph.D. in Machine Learning, Computational Biology or related field. Candidates with MSc in Machine Learning, Computational Biology or related field, with 4+ years of relevant industry experience will also be considered.
  • Experience with spatial transcriptomics, spatial proteomics, multiplex tissue imaging, digital pathology and/or related tissue-based technologies.
  • Hands-on experience with common data analysis frameworks and data formats, and with standard image analysis tools used in pathology labs.
  • Excellent programming skills both in python and R; openness to learning new tools and technologies; familiarity with models and harnesses for AI-assisted coding.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to communicate complex data insights and recommendations to cross-functional teams.
  • Demonstrated contribution to biological interpretation of spatial profiling data, including scientific publications and conferences.

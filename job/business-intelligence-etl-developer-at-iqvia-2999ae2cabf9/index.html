About the job

Business Intelligence ETL Developer

About the Team & Role

You will join a team within IQVIA's Global Health System Solutions organization, working on an established data warehouse product supporting hospitals across Belgium.

The team collects, processes and structures data from various hospital systems, creating a centralized data warehouse that enables further business intelligence and analytical solutions.

As a Business Intelligence ETL Developer, you will focus on the backend side of data warehousing, working with data transformation, cleansing, processing and modelling. You will contribute to the further development of the existing product while also helping maintain and improve established solutions.

This is a hands-on development role combining new development activities with ongoing maintenance and support of an existing product. It offers an opportunity to work with complex datasets and gradually expand your knowledge of healthcare data.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Develop, maintain and improve ETL processes and data warehouse solutions.
  • Write and optimize SQL queries, scripts and data transformations.
  • Work with data modelling, data cleansing, validation and processing.
  • Investigate data issues, identify inconsistencies and troubleshoot existing solutions.
  • Support the maintenance and continuous improvement of an established data warehouse product.
  • Collaborate with developers, product stakeholders and local teams to translate requirements into technical solutions.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to development quality.
  • Use AI-assisted development tools to support coding, problem-solving and development efficiency.

What We're Looking For

  • Approximately 1–3 years of experience in software development, data engineering, BI development or a related technical environment.
  • Good knowledge of SQL and relational databases, preferably Microsoft SQL Server.
  • Basic understanding of data warehousing, ETL/ELT processes and data modelling.
  • An analytical mindset and genuine curiosity about data, including the ability to investigate data structures, identify patterns and understand relationships between datasets.
  • Some experience with scripting, preferably Python or PowerShell, would be an advantage.
  • Familiarity with Git and collaborative development practices.
  • Practical experience using AI-assisted development tools, such as GitHub Copilot or Cursor, is an advantage.
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies.

Languages

  • English: good working proficiency.
  • French or Dutch: required.

Internship in GenAI Knowledge Assistant for Engineering

About the job

  • During your assignment, you will collect, structure and preprocess engineering knowledge sources such as technical documents, specifications, test reports and structured datasets.
  • You will evaluate suitable Databricks-native and open-source tools (e.g. RAG, Text-to-SQL, AI Agents, Vector Search, Genie) for different types of engineering content.
  • Based on this, you will design and implement natural language workflows on Databricks that allow hardware engineers to query both documents and structured data.
  • To deliver reliable, source-grounded answers, you will apply prompt and context engineering and ensure result quality and traceability (e.g. citations, evaluation, guardrails).
  • Additionally, you will collaborate closely with hardware engineers to understand use cases and iteratively improve the solution.
  • Finally, you will communicate progress and findings through visualizations, short presentations and technical documentation.
  • Education: master studies in the field of Energy Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, HVAC, Control Systems, Computer Science, Data Science, or a comparable technical field with good grades– a combination of engineering domain knowledge and AI/data skills is ideal
  • Experience and Knowledge:
  • practical knowledge of large language models and Generative AI concepts such as RAG, embeddings, vector databases, prompt and context engineering, or AI agents
  • solid programming skills, ideally in Python and SQL
  • engineering background as a plus
  • experience with Databricks (e.g. Vector Search, Genie, Mosaic AI Agent Framework, MLflow), LangChain, LlamaIndex or other Generative AI frameworks as an advantage
  • understanding of heat pump technology and operating principles as an asset
  • Personality and Working Practice: you are curious and self-driven, bringing a structured approach and strong communication skills to engaging with engineers from different backgrounds, understanding their needs and translating them into technical solutions
  • Work Routine: we offer you the opportunity to work in a hybrid setup with regular on-site presence
  • Enthusiasm: passionate about bridging AI and hardware engineering and making complex technical knowledge easily accessible through natural language
  • Languages: very good in English

Start: according to prior agreement
Duration: 6 months

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Role Description

As an Infrastructure Software Engineer on Dropbox’s Orchestration team, you’ll build and operate the platforms that help engineering teams run services reliably, efficiently, and at scale. The team provides internal cloud capabilities for compute, storage, deployment, orchestration, and multi-tenancy across on-premises and public-cloud environments.

You’ll contribute to systems such as Kubernetes, OpenSearch, Rook/Ceph, and deployment automation, with opportunities to deepen your expertise in distributed systems, storage, observability, automation, and production operations. Your work will help improve the reliability and efficiency of infrastructure used by teams across Dropbox.

Join us in redefining the future, where your passion for building scalable, reliable systems will drive meaningful change on a global scale.

Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here.

Responsibilities

  • Build and improve reliable, usable, and efficient orchestration platforms.
  • Develop tooling that helps teams deploy, operate, and scale workloads in multi-tenant environments.
  • Contribute to Kubernetes, OpenSearch, Rook/Ceph, deployment automation, and related systems.
  • Help support databases and other stateful workloads on Kubernetes.
  • Troubleshoot production issues and improve observability, automation, and resource efficiency.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, security, networking, database, and service engineering teams.
  • Participate in design discussions, code reviews, testing, deployments, documentation, and incident response.

On-call work may be necessary occasionally to help address bugs, outages, or other operational issues, with the goal of maintaining a stable and high-quality experience for our customers.

Requirements

  • BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science or related technical field involving coding (e.g., physics or mathematics), or equivalent technical experience
  • 5+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Experience developing or operating software, infrastructure, backend services, or developer platforms.
  • Programming experience in Go, Python, Java, C++, or a similar language.
  • Working experience with Kubernetes, including deploying workloads, troubleshooting issues, or building related tooling.
  • Working knowledge of Linux systems, debugging, and software or production operations.
  • Foundational knowledge of distributed systems, networking, storage, databases, reliability, or observability.
  • Ability to write maintainable code and documentation and learn unfamiliar technologies.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Kubernetes operations, scheduling, upgrades, multi-tenancy, or resource management.
  • Experience applying AI-assisted or agentic workflows to infrastructure operations, automation, troubleshooting, or developer productivity.
  • Experience with Ceph, Rook, OpenSearch, or similar technologies.
  • Experience with CI/CD, deployment automation, infrastructure provisioning, AWS, or hybrid infrastructure.
  • Experience improving resource utilization, operational efficiency, or infrastructure cost.

Durable Skills

AI fluency means using these tools to amplify human judgment, not replace it. We believe people with these skills will thrive as work and technology continue to evolve:

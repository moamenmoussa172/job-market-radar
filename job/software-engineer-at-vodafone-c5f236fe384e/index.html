About this Role

We are seeking a Generative AI Backend Software Engineer to join our Infrastructure Engineering team in Romania. You will design and build modern, scalable back-end applications and services using Python and Django, developing RESTful APIs and data-access layers within a microservices architecture.

A key part of the role is integrating Generative AI and Large Language Model (LLM) capabilities into back-end services, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, agentic workflows and AI agent tooling through APIs and MCP servers.

Alongside hands-on engineering, you will provide technical consultancy to clients, advise on suitable AI use cases and engineering best practices, and help stakeholders make informed technical decisions.

What you will do

  • Design, develop and maintain high-quality, well-tested back-end services and RESTful APIs using Python and Django.
  • Build and maintain scalable microservices and integrations with databases, search and logging infrastructure.
  • Develop and maintain AI-enabled software capabilities, including integrations with LLM providers.
  • Build and integrate RAG pipelines that connect back-end applications, data sources and LLM-based functionality.
  • Develop agentic workflows and expose or consume tools for AI agents through APIs and MCP servers.
  • Apply responsible AI and security practices to LLM-based functionality, including PII controls and safeguards against data leakage and prompt injection.
  • Collaborate with front-end developers to define and integrate API contracts.
  • Work with product managers and other stakeholders to deliver project objectives and features on schedule.
  • Contribute throughout the engineering lifecycle, including planning, solution design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance.
  • Review peer code for quality, performance and adherence to agreed coding standards.
  • Debug and troubleshoot technical issues and implement appropriate solutions.
  • Improve back-end performance through caching, query optimisation and efficient data-access techniques.
  • Follow and promote established engineering practices, including version control, code reviews and continuous integration.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, operating procedures and solution or architecture designs to support effective knowledge sharing.
  • Prepare and deliver knowledge-transfer sessions and contribute actively to the onboarding of new colleagues.
  • Provide technical support, mentoring and guidance to colleagues across different levels of experience.
  • Contribute to relevant team, departmental and organisational initiatives, including technical investigations, analysis, process documentation and knowledge transfer.

Who you are

  • You have 5+ years of back-end development experience, with a strong focus on Python and Django.
  • You have strong practical proficiency in Python and the Django framework.
  • You have experience integrating LLM provider APIs, such as OpenAI, Anthropic or Azure OpenAI.
  • You have experience designing and developing RESTful APIs.
  • You have experience working with relational databases and SQL.
  • You have a strong understanding of microservices architecture and integration patterns.
  • You understand prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, agentic patterns and LLM output evaluation.
  • You understand Agile/Scrum ways of working and are comfortable contributing within collaborative, fast-paced environments.
  • You bring effective problem-solving skills, attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • You can communicate complex technical concepts clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • You continuously develop your technical knowledge and skills.

Nice to have:

  • Understanding of containerisation technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • Experience with message brokers and asynchronous or background processing.
  • Familiarity with observability practices, including logging, metrics and tracing.
  • Experience with testing frameworks.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines.
  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.

About the job

Job Description & Summary

The Opportunity

PwC is one of the leading professional services firms in the world, offering industry-focused Tax, Assurance and Advisory services to public and multinational companies.

We are currently looking for a highly motivated SAP Senior Treasury Consultant to join our SAP Technology team.

The selected candidate will support the design and delivery of SAP S/4HANA Treasury solutions, helping clients optimize treasury operations, cash and liquidity management, financial transactions, and risk management processes. A key focus of the role is In-House Bank (IHB), Advanced Payment Medium (APM) and Netting.

What you will be doing:

  • Lead or contribute to end-to-end SAP S/4HANA Treasury implementations, from requirements gathering and solution design through configuration, testing, go-live, and support.
  • Collaborate with client stakeholders and project teams, prepare functional documentation, and support testing, training, and post-go-live activities.
  • Ensure integration with SAP FI/CO, payments, bank communication, market data, and other relevant systems.
  • Support financial risk management processes, including exposure management, hedging, valuations, and related accounting requirements.
  • Configure and support SAP Cash and Liquidity Management, including cash positioning, liquidity forecasting, and bank account management.
  • Configure and support SAP Treasury and Risk Management (TRM), including Transaction Manager processes for money market, foreign exchange, debt, investments, and derivatives.
  • Design, configure and support SAP In-House Bank (IHB), including internal bank accounts, intercompany payments, and in-house cash processes.
  • Implement and support Advanced Payment Medium (APM), including payment formats, payment runs, and bank communication.
  • Design and configure Netting processes (multilateral and bilateral intercompany netting) and their integration with In-House Bank and payments.

What we need from you:

  • University degree in Economics, Finance, Accounting Administration, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • Strong analytical, stakeholder-management, and communication skills, with very good command of English; SAP certification and knowledge of SAP Fiori or S/4HANA transformation projects are considered an advantage.
  • Experience with SAP Treasury integration with SAP FI/CO, payments, bank communication, market data, and financial reporting.
  • Participation in at least two end-to-end SAP implementation projects, including requirements gathering, solution design, configuration, testing, and go-live support.
  • Good understanding of corporate treasury processes, including cash and liquidity management, debt and investments, foreign exchange, derivatives, financial risk management, and hedge accounting.
  • At least 5 years of relevant SAP consulting or implementation experience, with hands-on knowledge of SAP S/4HANA Treasury and Risk Management (TRM), SAP Cash and Liquidity Management, In-House Bank, Advanced Payment Medium (APM), and Netting.
  • Proven project experience implementing In-House Bank, APM, and Netting scenarios, including integration with SAP FI/CO, payments, and bank communication.

We Offer:

  • Opportunities to impact: Dive into diverse projects and seize international assignments that let you shape the future.
  • Mentoring: Thrive in a supportive culture with regular feedback, personalized training programs, and clear career development paths.
  • Work-life balance: Enjoy our hybrid working policy, flexible hours.
  • Extra perks: Indulge in office massages, a Multisport card, medical center membership, and exclusive discounts with partner companies.

We are committed to building a diverse and welcoming/ safe environment, so we’re excited to receive applications from people of all cultures and backgrounds.

We appreciate the interest shown by all candidates. However, we wish to advise that only suitable candidates will be contacted.

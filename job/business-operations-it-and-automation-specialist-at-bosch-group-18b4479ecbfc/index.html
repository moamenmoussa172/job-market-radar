About the job

Robert Bosch (Pty) Ltd is looking to employ an experienced Business Operations IT and Automation Specialist to join their Power Tools department in the Midrand offices. Duties will include but will not be limited to the following:

IT-related duties:

  • IT Partner, key user and internal IT support: technical roll out, maintenance and support for relevant processes and general administration of IT systems (e.g. EDI, IPOS, BI@PT)
  • Responsible for access & identity management

Automation-related duties:

  • Expert-ship in a specific functional area (process mining, robotic process automation (RPA), workflow automation, reporting automation etc.)
  • Support by gathering and vetting the business requirements for use cases in the area of responsibility. Support by roll-out and acceleration of adoption by continuous development and expansion of use cases
  • Process optimization, standardization, and automation options within PT Business Operations, including operational day-to-day steering of internal or external service providers
  • Access management ERP related tasks (BR ticket creation, roll outs support)
  • Projects support: CCC, Celonis, Marketplaces, automation etc.
  • BOTs: creation, adaptation, tests during new roll outs
  • Active member of expertise network

Requirements

  • Completed Matric / Grade 12 &
  • University degree in Business Administration, Computer Science or related field

Experience

  • 3 to 5 years process execution experience, incl. deep SAP and tool know-how / best practice application, in Lead-to-cash or adjacent areas
  • Experience in roll out ERP, EDI, digital platforms or automation
  • Good knowledge of Sales processes & Business operations management in a demanded area
  • Service and results orientation for internal and external customers

Skills

  • Strong analytical skills
  • Ability to collaborate across hierarchies, divisions, functions, and cultures
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and autonomous working manner

By choice, we are committed to a diverse workforce and are an equal opportunity employer. Robert Bosch welcomes and encourages applications from people with disabilities. Accommodations are available on request for candidates taking part in the selection process.

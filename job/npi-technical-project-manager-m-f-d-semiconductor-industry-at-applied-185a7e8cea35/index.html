The NPI - Project Manager (m/f/d) within our product management team is responsible for overseeing the development and implementation of new e-beam (electron beam) technology projects. This role involves coordinating cross-functional teams, managing project timelines, ensuring technical requirements are met, and driving innovation in e-beam applications. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in project management experience and would be responsible for the transfer of new electron-optical developments from an R&D prototype/pilot into a product ready for serial production.

Please note that this role is offered on a fixed-term basis for 12 months

Key responsibilities:

Project Management:

  • Create project schedules/plans for New Production Introduction (NPI) phase (transfer from R&D to Production) by adhering to the timelines, key milestones for prototype readiness, as well as ramp to pilot and volume.
  • Develop and maintain detailed project plans, including schedules, budgets, and resource allocation.
  • Coordinate cross-functional teams, including engineering, manufacturing, quality assurance, and supply chain, to ensure project milestones are met.
  • Monitor project progress and implement corrective actions as necessary to stay on track with project timelines and objectives.

Technical Leadership:

  • Provide technical expertise and guidance on e-beam technology and applications.
  • Oversee the design, development, testing, and validation of e-beam components and systems.
  • Collaborate with R&D teams to drive innovation and improvements.
  • Ensure that all technical requirements and specifications are met or exceeded

Stakeholder Communication:

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for all project-related communications.
  • Prepare and present project status reports to stakeholders, including senior management.
  • Facilitate regular project meetings and ensure clear communication of project goals, progress, and challenges.

Risk Management:

  • Identify potential project risks and develop mitigation strategies.
  • Conduct risk assessments and ensure that risk management plans are implemented and followed.

Documentation and Compliance:

  • Ensure all project documentation is complete, accurate, and compliant with regulatory and company standards.
  • Maintain thorough records of project development processes, decisions, and changes.

Qualifications & Skills:
- A degree in engineering or in any scientific field
- 3-5 years of project management experience
- Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- PMP or equivalent project management certification is an added advantage, not a must
- Experience working in a cross-functional, global team environment

Senior Account Manager

Unifonic · Istanbul, Turkey

Onsite · Senior · Account Manager · Posted 24 November 2026

About the job

Proudly recognized as a Great Place to Work®, we are an AI-native customer experience platform purpose-built for emerging markets. With more than 25 years of conversational AI research and development, we serve over 5,000 enterprises across MENA and are pioneering sovereign, agentic AI through our Agentic Studio platform. As the category takes shape, the way Unifonic is perceived — by buyers, analysts, media, and the broader market — is a strategic asset. This role owns that perception.

We thrive on creativity, collaboration, and fun, where every idea is welcomed and celebrated. As shareholders in the company, we’re more than just communicators—we’re innovators shaping the future together. We are Unifones. Join us and be part of something extraordinary!

Our Sales team at Unifonic plays a vital role in driving revenue growth, building strong customer relationships, and delivering value through consultative selling. From managing key accounts and developing new business opportunities to strengthening customer engagement and identifying growth opportunities, our team works closely with cross-functional departments to ensure our solutions meet customer needs and support their success, contributing to the company's continued growth and market expansion.

As a Senior Account Manager, you will lead and grow strategic relationships with mid-market and enterprise customers, acting as a trusted advisor to drive customer success, retention, and revenue growth. You will take end-to-end ownership of your customer portfolio, leveraging commercial insights, data-driven strategies, and a deep understanding of eCommerce and SaaS to maximize customer value, strengthen long-term partnerships, and identify opportunities for business expansion.

Help us shape the future of communication by:

  • Managing a portfolio of mid-market and enterprise accounts, serving as the primary commercial and strategic point of contact.
  • Building trusted, long-term relationships with key stakeholders, business leaders, and decisionmakers.
  • Understanding customer business objectives, challenges, and growth opportunities to develop tailored account strategies.
  • Overseeing the end-to-end customer lifecycle, from onboarding and adoption to engagement, renewals, and expansion.
  • Driving product adoption and maximizing the business value customers achieve through Segmentify's solutions.
  • Leading quarterly business reviews (QBRs), presenting performance insights, ROI analysis, and strategic recommendations.
  • Monitoring account health, customer engagement, and product utilization to identify growth opportunities and potential risks.
  • Providing strategic guidance, product training, and best practices to optimize customers' eCommerce performance.
  • Driving customer retention, renewal success, and recurring revenue growth across the assigned portfolio.
  • Identifying and developing upselling and cross-selling opportunities aligned with evolving customer needs.
  • Leading renewal discussions and commercial negotiations in collaboration with internal stakeholders.
  • Developing account growth plans and maintaining accurate renewal and expansion forecasts.
  • Proactively identifying churn risks and implementing retention strategies to protect recurring revenue.
  • Collaborating with Sales, Product, Technical Support, and Marketing teams to deliver a seamless customer experience.
  • Representing the voice of the customer internally and translating feedback into actionable product and service improvements.
  • Coordinating the resolution of technical and operational issues, ensuring clear communication and accountability.
  • Maintaining accurate account records, commercial opportunities, and customer interactions within CRM systems.
  • Bachelor's degree in business, Marketing, Economics, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 4+ years of experience in Account Management, Customer Success, or Client Relationship Management, preferably within B2B SaaS, eCommerce, MarTech, or digital marketing.
  • Demonstrated success in managing and growing a portfolio of business customers.
  • Proven experience driving customer retention, renewals, upselling, and cross-selling.
  • Experience working with mid-market or enterprise customers and managing multiple stakeholders.

AI Proficiency:

  • Minimum Level: L2-L3 — Basic to practical understanding of AI tools with ability to apply them in sales-related tasks.
  • Expected: Use AI for sales prospecting, outreach personalization, CRM insights, and pipeline management. Familiarity with tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Gemini.

Craft & Toolkit

  • B2B SaaS & eCommerce
  • Strategic Account Management
  • Customer Retention & Renewals
  • Upselling & Cross-selling
  • Revenue & Account Growth
  • Commercial Negotiations
  • Customer Lifecycle Management
  • Product Adoption & QBRs
  • SaaS Metrics (ARR, MRR, NRR & Churn)
  • eCommerce Metrics (AOV, CLV & ROI)
  • CRM Tools (Zoho, HubSpot & Zendesk)
  • eCommerce & Marketing Automation Platforms
  • AI-powered SaaS Solutions

Character Traits:

  • Analytical thinking and problem-solving – approach problems clearly, use data, and find solutions.
  • Ownership and accountability – take responsibility and follow projects through to completion.
  • Communication – explain ideas clearly and listen to others across teams.
  • Collaboration – work well with others and support team goals.
  • Adaptability and learning – adjust to change and keep learning new skills or tools.
  • Mentorship and knowledge sharing – help others grow and share what you know.
  • Resilience – stay calm under pressure and handle setbacks constructively.
  • Quality and attention to detail – do work carefully and strive for improvement.
  • Advocacy and innovation – encourage best practices, efficiency, and new ideas

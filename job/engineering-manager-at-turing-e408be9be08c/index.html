About Turing

Engineering Manager, (Technical Team Lead)

Location: Remote, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina

Status: Full-time

Role Overview

As a Engineering Manager (Technical Team Lead) at Turing, you will be a pivotal part of our engineering leadership for AI initiatives. This is not a pure people management role. You will be expected to be hands on: able to operate on the data, organize the stack, set up infrastructure, and personally deliver a project if no one else is available. You will lead a team through a rapid iteration, startup paced environment with high expectations and short timelines, while also driving the technical vision and execution of complex projects.

The ideal candidate is someone who takes ownership beyond what their role technically requires, has a track record of delivering under pressure, and is excited by the intensity of a startup environment rather than looking for a more comfortable, longer timescale management track.

Key Responsibilities

Hands On Technical Ownership:

  • Operate directly on data, organize the technical stack, and set up infrastructure as needed.
  • Be the technical fallback who can personally deliver a project if no one else is online to do it.
  • Guide the team in architecture, design, and implementation of scalable, maintainable codebases.
  • Build and use AI agents, and review the team's agent based work, since this role requires evaluating agent output directly rather than only reviewing human written code.

Prompt Generation and Innovation:

  • Lead innovation efforts in product features and processes.
  • Manage idea and prompt collection and prioritization.
  • Drive experimentation and iteration for optimal outcomes.

Work Stream Assignment Across Pods:

  • Allocate projects based on skills and performance.
  • Prioritize tasks to meet business goals.
  • Coordinate with cross functional teams for project alignment.

Team Throughput and Task Completion:

  • Monitor progress using metrics like velocity and cycle time.
  • Optimize development processes for efficiency.
  • Ensure tasks meet quality standards before deployment.

Spot Reviews on Tasks:

  • Perform quality control through task reviews.
  • Provide actionable feedback to maintain high standards.
  • Identify risks early and adjust processes accordingly.

Identifying High and Low Performers:

  • Conduct performance analysis through reviews and metrics.
  • Provide mentorship, feedback, and talent management.
  • Identify high performers for advanced roles and support development for others.

People and Project Leadership Under Pressure:

  • Lead teams through urgent, high pressure delivery schedules, not just steady state work.
  • Push delivery timelines in a rapid iteration startup context while maintaining quality.
  • Take direct ownership of outcomes, including being responsive when urgent needs arise.

Technical Skills

  • Expert level, hands on experience in Node.js, with React and NestJS specifically, including a deep understanding of their ecosystems, libraries, and best practices.
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and GCP, including setting up and operating cloud infrastructure directly.
  • Ability to build and use AI agents, with enough fluency to review and evaluate a team's agent built work.
  • Experience leading projects that leverage Python for data analysis, backend development, or AI and ML applications, as well as JavaScript and TypeScript for front end and full stack development.
  • Proficiency in data analysis techniques, including statistical analysis, data visualization, and data manipulation using tools like Pandas, NumPy, and SQL.
  • Strong focus on data quality, including data validation, cleansing, and enrichment.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Data Engineering, or a related field. Electrical engineering backgrounds are acceptable. AI or data science background is a meaningful plus (helps significantly) but not a dealbreaker if diligence and ownership are evident elsewhere.
  • 5+ years of experience in software development.
  • At least 2 years in a leadership role is the bar for an Engineering Manager managing teams of 3 to 6. For this Senior Engineering Manager (Technical Team Lead) role, candidates should have experience managing meaningfully larger teams than that, not just the 5 to 6 person threshold.
  • Proven track record of leading teams through urgent, complicated technical work, ideally in a startup setting, and delivering on time or ahead of time.
  • Demonstrated ownership that exceeds role expectations: a history of taking on more than the job required, high personal output, and strong initiative.
  • Strong problem solving skills, with a focus on delivering results in a fast paced, dynamic environment.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across cross functional teams.

What Success Looks Like

You are the kind of leader who is excited to do this work. You know it will be intense, that you'll manage a lot of people, process, and technology at once, and you welcome it. You have taken on something genuinely hard, led people through it, and can clearly explain what made it difficult and how you delivered — a truly hard technical milestone, reached in a leadership setting, that you're proud of.

Values

  • We are client first: We put our clients at the center of everything we do, because their success is the ultimate measure of our value.
  • We work at Start-Up Speed: We move fast, stay agile and favor action because momentum is the foundation of perfection
  • We are AI forward: We help our clients build the future of Al and implement it in our own roles and workflow to amplify productivity.

Advantages of joining Turing

  • Work at the frontier of AI, helping the world’s leading AI labs improve their most advanced models by building expert datasets, RL environments, and first-of-a-kind benchmarks.
  • Contribute to leading-edge AI research and showcase your work at top conferences such as ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS.
  • Bring frontier AI innovation to the enterprise, applying lessons learned from leading AI labs to solve real-world business challenges.
  • Collaborate with and learn from exceptional colleagues with deep AI experience from Google, Meta, Amazon, and other leading technology companies.
  • Move at the pace of AI innovation, with the speed, ownership, and impact of a startup.

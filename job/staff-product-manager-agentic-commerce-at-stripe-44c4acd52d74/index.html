About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Agentic payments is a team that’s building new API and integration experiences for agentic commerce online. We think that the proliferation of consumer-facing AI experiences will unlock new ways that consumers buy things online, and our existing payments experiences need to evolve to new commercial models that cutting-edge builders are starting. Our first launch, Instant Checkout in collaboration with OpenAI, took the industry by storm and we’re bringing experiences like that to the entire universe of AI agents, and sellers.

What you’ll do

As a Staff Product Manager on Agentic Commerce, you’ll own the vision and execution of transformative products that redefine how commerce works, making it more fluid, automated, and aligned with user intent. You’ll collaborate closely with engineering, design, data science, and marketing to unlock new opportunities for our customers and partners globally.

Our north star provides a new integration shape for AI agents who are looking to build agent-powered online commerce experiences, via their Stripe integration. They will have access to our entire suite of connected sellers, and can focus on the experience with the end-user. Enabling an AI agent to do this requires new API primitives, new dashboard surfaces, and deep thought about what financial and e-commerce complexity Stripe can simplify on their behalf.

Responsibilities

  • Define and drive the product roadmap for agentic commerce solutions that empower users, from automated merchant tools to intelligent purchasing workflows and beyond.
  • Build full stack solutions across Stripe's ecosystem, including back end services, dashboard interfaces, money movement systems, and integrations with other Stripe primitives
  • Partner deeply with engineering and design to ship high impact features that increase user autonomy and reduce friction.
  • Strong analytical skills: comfort diving into data to test hypotheses and make informed trade-offs.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally across Stripe’s broader platform and product teams to integrate agentic commerce capabilities seamlessly.
  • Engage directly with users (particularly large enterprises) to understand their business needs and translate requirements into novel product shapes.

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 12+ years of Product Management experience
  • Computer Science background or equivalent technical experience
  • Demonstrated experience of leading initiatives across large engineering organizations, spanning multiple teams to influence tech roadmap planning and execution
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, structuring ambiguous concepts clearly and from first principles
  • Thrive in a collaborative environment involving different functions, stakeholders, and subject matter experts
  • Deep empathy for users balancing autonomy and guidance in digital commerce experiences.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience building APIs or new integration experiences for users
  • You enjoy using social media to interact directly with customers for feedback
  • You’ve founded a business / startup, taking a product from 0-1
  • Experience building APIs or new integration experiences for users
  • Experience in eCommerce

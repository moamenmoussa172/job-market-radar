About the job

Intern Software Engineer

Company:

Boeing Poland sp. z o.o. EFS

An exciting opportunity has become available to join Boeing Poland as an Intern Software Engineer.

Position overview:
Boeing Poland Software Engineering develops and produces avionics and electronics systems designed for use on aircraft and other platforms for purposes such as navigation, flight controls, information systems, and other core avionics functions. We have set our sights high – to design, develop, and manufacture the most technically advanced and reliable avionic components in the world. We are innovating and developing new products to address the future of the urban air mobility as well as our next generation of commercial, tactical, and trainer aircraft markets.

As an Intern Software Engineer, you will join the Boeing Poland Software Engineering team consisting of highly skilled, high performing engineering team members supporting multiple projects across Software Engineering organization.

We’re planning to hire interns for the following paths:

  • C/C++
  • Java/Python
  • MBD

Hiring details:

  • Candidates can work from our office in Gdańsk or Rzeszów
  • This is a part time position

Position Responsibilities:

  • Utilizing modern programming techniques, tools, languages and agile software development methodologies to build software for delivered Boeing systems
  • Performing engineering activities including architecture design, software development, integration and testing
  • Proactively contributing to project execution plan to set and effectively achieve operational goals
  • Influencing, innovating and continuously improving the software engineering process as well as quality, safety and reliability of products and services provided by Boeing
  • Cultivating a positive staff culture, team motivation

Basic Qualifications (Required Skills/Experience):

  • 3+ months of professional experience/ documented students projects
  • Student status
  • English proficiency
  • Commitment to working minimum 20 hours a week

Preferred Qualifications (Desired Skills/Experience):

  • Familiarity with airborne software certification and tools qualification processes and standards
  • Design and systems thinking skills

as well as:

  • C/C++ path:

- Experience in C or C++

- Experience in containerization

- Familiarity with software development cycle such as version control, testing methodologies, agile development and CI/CD.

  • Java/Python path:

​- Experience in Java or Python

- Experience in containerization

- Experience in database development (PostgreSQL)

- Experience in developing web services (REST, SOAP, etc.)

- Familiarity with software development cycle such as version control, testing methodologies, agile development and CI/CD.

  • MBD path:

- Familiarity with Model Based Design, preferably in MATLAB and Simulink suite

- Knowledge of scripting languages like Python

- Elementary knowledge of building models, simulating systems, analyzing simulation results, and code generation in MBD approach.

- Familiarity with software development cycle such as version control, testing methodologies, agile development and CI/CD.

Join us if you want to:

  • Have flexible working hours
  • Broaden your experience in international avionics projects, technical workshops, and hackathons
  • Develop professional skills with our virtual learning tools and platforms (Degreed, Enterprise Mentoring, Communities of Excellence & Practice)
  • Enjoy and create with us friendly and inclusive atmosphere in workplace
  • Cooperate and exchange knowledge with international team where we support and respect each other

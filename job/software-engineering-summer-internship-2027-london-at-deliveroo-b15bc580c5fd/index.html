About the job

Our Global Structure

Deliveroo is now part of DoorDash, bringing together teams with even greater reach, scale, and ambition. Depending on your role, you may collaborate with teammates, systems, and leaders across DoorDash and Wolt. Together, we’re unlocking new possibilities as one global team.

About the Team

There’s a lot behind a morning coffee order. You could be part of it.

A local café, a courier on the way and a customer ready to start their day. At Deliveroo, we connect people with the food, shops and products they love. As a Software Engineering Intern, you’ll join a team building the technology that makes those connections possible. From creating user-friendly experiences to working on the systems behind them, you’ll help develop, maintain and ship products used by customers, merchants and riders.

You’ll work alongside experienced engineers to solve real problems, with support from a dedicated manager and mentor. Along the way, you’ll build your skills, learn how we develop products at scale and see the impact of your work.

This is a full-time, 12-week internship based at our London HQ, starting in summer 2027. It’s open to students currently enrolled in higher education and in the penultimate year of study.

What You’ll Be Doing

You’ll join a team of engineers building technology that improves the experience of consumers, merchants and Riders. During your 12-week internship, you’ll collaborate with your team to solve customer problems and help develop, maintain and ship products.

  • You’ll contribute to technical projects with support from your manager, mentor and team.
  • You’ll learn about the systems you work on, from user-friendly product experiences to scalable backend architecture.
  • You’ll develop your engineering skills through feedback, coaching and mentorship.
  • You’ll collaborate with your team to solve problems and contribute to business priorities.
  • You’ll take part in intern programming, including leadership speaker sessions and social events, both virtually and in person.
  • You’ll share what you’ve learned in a final presentation at the end of your internship.

What You’ll Need to Thrive

We’re looking for strong engineering foundations and curiosity to learn. Here’s what you’ll need to join us and thrive in the role:

  • You’re pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or an equivalent field and expect to graduate in 2028.
  • You have no more than two years of full-time work experience.
  • You’re available to start in June 2027 and work in person for the duration of the internship.
  • You have experience working with databases and technologies such as SQL.
  • You have a solid understanding of algorithms and data structures.
  • You’re proficient in at least one object-oriented programming language, such as Python, Java or Kotlin.
  • You have experience writing clean code, using version control and writing unit tests.
  • You’re authorised to work in the UK for the duration of the internship.

Preferred but not required:

  • Previous software engineering internship experience or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Curiosity about and experience using AI-powered tools to support software development, problem-solving and learning, with an understanding of how to evaluate outputs and use these tools responsibly.
  • An ability to analyse and improve the efficiency, scalability and stability of systems.
  • An interest in developing, releasing and running large-scale web applications.
  • Experience with systems monitoring, live deployments or continuous integration.
  • Experience solving real-time technology problems.
  • Experience with service-oriented architecture, writing APIs or designing systems.
  • Participation in technical projects or research, hackathons, conferences, student organisations, teaching assistant roles or other relevant extracurricular activities.

Why Join Us?

At Deliveroo, you’ll do work that matters - solving real-world problems in a three-sided marketplace that’s constantly evolving. You’ll be paired with a dedicated manager and a mentor while being supported by a community of engineers passionate about learning and helping each other grow.

We’re food lovers, problem solvers, community builders and more, brought together by a shared drive to make things better. Working here you can expect to:

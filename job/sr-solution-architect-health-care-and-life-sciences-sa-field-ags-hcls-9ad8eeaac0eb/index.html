About the job

Do you want to help pharmaceutical, biotech, medical technology and healthcare organizations bring new therapies and better care to patients faster? We are looking for a Solutions Architect with a background in Healthcare & Life Sciences (HCLS) who wants to shape how this industry uses the cloud, data and AI.
As a Solutions Architect in our HCLS team, you are the trusted technical advisor to some of the largest life sciences and healthcare companies in Germany. You work with IT and with business leaders in research & development, clinical operations, manufacturing, quality and commercial functions. You understand their industry challenges, from regulated validation processes to drug discovery economics, and turn them into architectures and programs that create measurable outcomes.
This role suits someone who is as comfortable discussing a customer's R&D strategy with a business executive as designing a validated data platform with their engineers. You identify where AWS can make the biggest difference for a customer, build the case for it together with the account team, and see it through to production.
Key job responsibilities
- Act as the lead technical advisor for a portfolio of HCLS customers, owning the technical relationship and long-term architecture direction
- Engage business and technology stakeholders up to executive level to understand their priorities, and translate them into use cases and an adoption roadmap.
- Proactively identify new use cases and workloads across the HCLS value chain: research, clinical development, regulatory, manufacturing and supply chain, commercial and patient services.
- Partner with account managers on account planning, and drive initiatives from first idea through proof of concept to production
- Design secure, compliant architectures for regulated environments (e.g. GxP, GDPR, health data regulations), including data platforms, analytics, HPC and AI
- Bring in AWS specialists, partners and service teams at the right time, and lead the overall solution
- Represent the customer voice to AWS product teams and share industry insights with the wider organization
- Build thought leadership in the HCLS community through workshops, events, publications and industry forums
A day in the life
You might start the day with a head of R&D IT discussing how to speed up candidate selection using simulation and protein models, continue with an architecture review for a GxP-validated data platform, and end it shaping next year's account plan with your account manager. You split your time between customer conversations, hands-on architecture work, and building the HCLS community inside and outside AWS.

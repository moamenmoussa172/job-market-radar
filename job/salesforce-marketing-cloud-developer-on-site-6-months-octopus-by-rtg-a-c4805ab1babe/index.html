About the job

We are looking for an experienced Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) Developer to join our team in the UAE. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience implementing, developing, and integrating enterprise-grade Salesforce Marketing Cloud solutions.

This role requires a combination of strong technical Salesforce expertise and client-facing capabilities. You will work closely with business stakeholders, functional teams, architects, and developers to translate business and marketing requirements into scalable and reliable SFMC solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, configure, and implement solutions using Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC).
  • Build and manage customer journeys using Journey Builder and marketing automation workflows using Automation Studio.
  • Develop and maintain email templates, dynamic content, landing pages, and personalized communications using AMPscript, HTML, CSS, and SQL.
  • Configure and manage Data Extensions, Contact Builder, segmentation, and audience management.
  • Develop integrations between SFMC, Salesforce platforms, and external enterprise systems using REST/SOAP APIs and other integration approaches.
  • Support enterprise-level Salesforce implementations, enhancements, and integration initiatives.
  • Translate business and marketing requirements into scalable technical solutions.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues related to SFMC campaigns, integrations, automation, and data.
  • Ensure solutions follow Salesforce development standards, security requirements, and implementation best practices.
  • Participate in solution design, technical discussions, testing, deployment, and production support.
  • Work directly with clients and business stakeholders to understand requirements, present solutions, and provide technical recommendations.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation for developed solutions and integrations.
  • Collaborate with Salesforce Architects, Developers, Marketing teams, and other technology teams.

Requirements

  • Strong hands-on experience in Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) development and implementation.
  • Experience delivering enterprise-level Salesforce solutions and integrations.
  • Good knowledge of key SFMC capabilities such as Journey Builder, Automation Studio, Email Studio, and Contact Builder.
  • Hands-on experience with AMPscript, SQL, and API integrations.
  • Relevant Salesforce certification(s) are required.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills with the ability to work directly with clients and business stakeholders.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with technical and functional teams.
  • Must be available to work onsite in the UAE.

About the job

SAP BI Data Loads Monitoring and Error Correction.

• The scope includes monitoring and Error Correction of all process chains in the production environment
• Monitor SAP BI data loads (Data loads running on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis) using the Unified Monitoring Console. In case of any failures, take prompt corrective actions to ensure timely data availability for reporting in production environment
• Analyze and fix long-running jobs to ensure on-time data availability
• Define and implement preventive measures for data load errors with approval from the BGSW Maintenance team
• Perform ad-hoc data load requests from the BGSW Maintenance Team as required, using the provided inputs
• Ensure Business data is available on time for Monthly Business Reporting(MBR)
• Proactively communicate and coordinate with the BGSW Maintenance Team in the event of system unavailability and data load issues(for exceptional cases), facilitating timely resolution and resumption of normal operations
• Optimize data load runtimes in the production environment
• Coordinate with IN and VN monitoring teams during shift handovers and provide all relevant updates
• Document and update the solution database for all identified unique data load issues to maintain a comprehensive knowledge repository

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree completed.
  • Advanced English proficiency, with the ability to communicate effectively in a global environment.
  • Advanced knowledge of BI/BO (Business Intelligence / Business Objects).
  • Knowledge of SAP systems.
  • Knowledge of ABAP.
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA.

Desirable Requirements

  • Previous experience in 2nd and 3rd level BI/BO support.
  • Experience in SAP Business Intelligence and reporting solutions.
  • Experience in project implementation, support, and maintenance.
  • Experience working in global IT environments and supporting users across different locations.
  • Knowledge of incident and problem management, as well as Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Experience in system performance monitoring, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement.
  • Experience integrating BI/BO solutions with SAP environments.

About the job

Synopsis

The Manager, Mobile Architecture & Engineering is a senior technical leader responsible for defining scalable mobile architecture and engineering strategy across iOS, Android, hybrid, and cross-platform applications. The role combines technical and people leadership with governance across security, quality, performance, observability, and CI/CD, while driving the adoption of AI and Generative AI across customer experiences and software development. A key focus is selecting the most effective technology approach based on business and engineering needs rather than advocating a single technology stack.

Accountabilities

  • Lead the implantation of mobile engineering and architecture strategy and standards across iOS, Android, and hybrid/cross-platform applications, with a focus on Ionic-Angular.
  • Define reference architectures, reusable components, SDK strategies, integration patterns, and engineering guardrails, including native versus hybrid criteria.
  • Drive API-first and event-driven integration patterns, ensuring scalability, reliability, offline capabilities, accessibility, localization, analytics, and security.
  • Drive AI-assisted development across requirements, design, code, test, security, release, and operations using tools such as GitHub Copilot.
  • Set engineering standards based on SOLID principles, clean architecture, code reviews, static analysis, dependency management, and automated quality gates.
  • Establish effective testing, automation, performance KPIs, telemetry, and observability across mobile applications.
  • Govern mobile CI/CD and DevSecOps, including automated build/testing, SAST, DAST, SCA, secrets detection, signing, and App Store/Google Play deployment.
  • Lead mobile security and privacy, including OWASP Mobile, OAuth 2.0/OpenID Connect, biometrics, encryption, secure storage, device integrity, and API security.
  • Govern architecture, engineering standards, technical debt, technology lifecycle, and modernization roadmaps across legacy, hybrid, native, APIs, cloud integrations, and design systems.
  • Provide technical leadership across Engineering Managers, Technical Leads, Architects, Mobile Engineers, QA and DevOps, while governing vendor engineering quality, standards, and knowledge transfer.
  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or an equivalent technical discipline.
  • 7+ years of software engineering experience, working with mobile application development.
  • 5+ years in technical leadership, engineering management, architecture, or equivalent senior engineering responsibility.
  • Experience with Swift and/or Kotlin, alongside Ionic-Angular or other hybrid mobile technologies using WebKit/WebView i.e., PhoneGap, Cordova.
  • Master’s degree in computer science, AI, Software Engineering, Technology Management, or a related discipline is desirable.
  • Relevant cloud, architecture, cybersecurity, mobile, or AI certifications are advantageous.
  • Demonstrated delivery of large-scale, customer-facing mobile applications.
  • Significant experience with native and hybrid/cross-platform mobile architectures.
  • Experience leading multiple engineering squads or technically governing a significant engineering portfolio.
  • Strong understanding of enterprise SDLC, DevSecOps, testing, security, observability, and production operations.
  • Practical experience adopting AI within software products and/or the engineering lifecycle.

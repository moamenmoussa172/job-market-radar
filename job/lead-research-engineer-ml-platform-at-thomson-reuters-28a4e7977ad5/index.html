About the job

Job Description

Thomson Reuters Labs builds and ships custom models for professional workflows requiring fiduciary-grade AI. The Thomson LLM family was developed by training leading open-weight models on decades of authoritative Thomson Reuters content, with thousands of hours of subject-matter-expert input. Owning the model layer is how we earn trust in the most regulated, highest-stakes professional workflows.

See the detailed technical report for more information on the latest model release: Thomson 1.0 Technical Report on Hugging Face.

The ML Platform team is responsible for building and maintaining the infrastructure which powers the training and evaluation of the Thomson family of models. The team operates high availability model training and evaluation infrastructure for complex LLM training and evaluation use cases, under tight constraints of cost, multi-region multi-cloud deployment strategies, and high-availability requirements. Additionally, the team is responsible for platform compliance in a heavily regulated domain.

As a lead research engineer on the team, you would be working with other technical leads, engineers and researchers to build and maintain the ML Platform powering Thomson development, keeping it stable and reliable and scaling with growing Thomson adoption and product integration use cases. You’ll be responsible for a critical part of the system that’s essential to keep Thomson model suite at the frontier of specialized professional LLMs.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan delivery and recommend staffing. Work with program and research leads to agree priorities. Break work into stages, estimate effort and cost, and recommend the skills and staffing needed. Identify dependencies and explain what can be delivered with the people and time available.
  • Build and operate the ML Platform infrastructure that enables Thomson model training, evaluation and experimentation
  • Implement and tune cost-optimization techniques to keep infrastructure running reliably with tight cost, availability and reliability constraints
  • Improve the platform’s cost-tracking and observability as call volume and the deployment surface grow.
  • Contribute to the ML Platform’s compliance readiness efforts: implement controls for ISO 27001 compliance paths, vendor capacity planning, GPU-capacity contingency planning
  • Make and document cost, latency, reliability tradeoffs, and bring them to the broader teams and stakeholders
  • Work directly with the training and evaluation pods so they can train and evaluate new model releases on the platform.
  • ML Platform operational support and reliability – provide operational support for the reliable functioning of ML platform
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders including researchers, leaders, other engineers, leadership and finance
  • Guide and mentor engineers. Review designs and code, help engineers solve problems, and explain technical decisions to research and product colleagues. Raise delivery risks early and propose changes to scope or approach when needed.

Required Qualifications

  • Hands-on infrastructure and large-scale distributed systems engineering experience with Kubernetes or similar
  • Experience owning and operating highly reliable ML platform infrastructure for LLM development across multiple use cases: peak load, scaling, disaster recovery and operational excellence.
  • Direct experience with model-serving infrastructure and/or inference optimization for large models, at whatever scale you've worked at so far.
  • Large-scale distributed systems experience, including scaling and reliability under production load.
  • Comfort debugging distributed systems and production incidents under real traffic.
  • Champion and enforce engineering best practices, while working with dynamic product requirements
  • Experience with working with senior collaborators and operating autonomously to deliver, while exercising technical and business judgment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Exposure to GPU-cluster capacity planning or multi-region/multi-cloud deployment.
  • Exposure to a regulated or compliance-heavy production environment (ISO 27001, SOC 2 or comparable)
  • Familiarity with systems development languages (Rust, Go, etc.)
  • Comfort with systems-level optimization - improvements to rate-limiting and routing approaches, and identifying bottlenecks across networking, hardware, and lower-level software, etc.

Even if you don’t have all the above requirements, we encourage you to apply to explore a mutual fit!

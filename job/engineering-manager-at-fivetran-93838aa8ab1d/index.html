About the Role

Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies.

We’re looking for an Engineering Manager to lead and scale a high-performing engineering team focused on building robust, high-throughput data movement services. In this role, you will lead a team of talented engineers responsible for developing, maintaining, and scaling mission-critical pipeline components, connectors, and distributed platform services operating on our global Kubernetes clusters.

You will balance technical depth with people-first leadership: coaching engineers in their career development, ensuring high engineering standards through architectural design reviews (TDDs), driving predictable execution with Product, and championing an AI-first development culture.

This is a full-time position based out of our Bengaluru office. Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration.

Technologies You’ll Use

Java, SQL, Postgres, AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, Docker, Kafka, Temporal, Buildkite, GitHub Copilot, Grafana, Jira

What You’ll Do

  • Lead and coach a team of software engineers, conducting regular 1:1s, providing actionable feedback, and supporting their career progression and skill development.
  • Drive delivery and agile execution, partnering closely with Product Managers and Technical Program Managers to translate product roadmaps (PRDs) into clear engineering milestones.
  • Maintain high architectural standards, reviewing Technical Design Documents (TDDs), providing design feedback, and ensuring systems are built for reliability, fault tolerance, and low latency.
  • Champion engineering excellence, fostering best practices in code quality, comprehensive automated testing, continuous integration, and technical debt management.
  • Own operational health and SLAs, establishing robust observability (metrics, alerting, dashboards) and driving root-cause incident reviews and post-mortems.
  • Integrate AI-first developer workflows, actively driving adoption of coding agents, AI code assist, and LLM-assisted workflows across SDLC stages to boost team velocity.
  • Partner with cross-functional stakeholders, collaborating with Customer Support, Product Operations, and Security teams to triage escalated issues and safeguard customer trust.
  • Grow the engineering team, partnering with Talent Acquisition to recruit, interview, and onboard top-tier engineering talent while building an inclusive culture.
  • Live our core values: embody One Team, One Dream, Get Stuck In, and Do the Right Thing across daily decisions and team leadership.

Skills We’re Looking For

  • Engineering Management Experience: 2+ years directly managing, coaching, and scaling software engineering teams in high-growth SaaS, cloud, or data infrastructure environments with a total experience of 10+ years in the software industry.
  • Strong Technical Background: Prior hands-on experience designing and building scalable distributed backend systems, with deep expertise in systems languages (e.g., Java, Go, C++).
  • Distributed Systems & Cloud Knowledge: Strong understanding of microservices, REST/Cloud APIs, and containerized cloud platforms (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS/GCP).
  • Execution & Project Delivery: Demonstrated ability to run predictable delivery cycles, scope roadmaps, manage risk, and deliver multi-quarter initiatives on schedule.
  • People & Team Development: Proven track record of hiring, retaining, and coaching junior to staff-level engineers, fostering a culture of high ownership, accountability, and psychological safety.
  • Operational Acumen: Experience leading production on-call operations, managing severity incidents, conducting blameless post-mortems, and improving uptime and MTTR.
  • Cross-Functional Communication: Excellent verbal and written communication skills to articulate complex technical trade-offs to product stakeholders, executives, and customers.
  • AI Tooling & Agent Fluency: Familiarity with leveraging modern AI tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Code) to accelerate authoring TDDs, code reviews, PRDs, and test plans.

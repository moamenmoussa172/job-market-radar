About the job

You will join Signals & Intelligence, part of TomTom's Location Intelligence for Enterprises organization. We turn TomTom's traffic, map and location data into decisions that customers pay for: how busy a road is, where to open a store, whether an insurance claim matches what happened on the road, which junction to fix first and many more.

We are a product development team, not a research lab. We run fast experiments with customers to create new products. We also maintain and improve a portfolio of mature products. We own the pipelines behind both. Fast experiments are our product development approach. Our three workstreams are Traffic Analytics, Places Analytics and Risk Analytics.

You will work with data scientists, software engineers and product managers as one product development team. We own our work from data extraction to release, monitoring and on-call.

What you will do:

  • Build data science solutions for our products that turn noisy, large-scale location data into reliable signals: traffic volumes, risk scores, claim validation, site selection and other analytics products.
  • Take product ideas from a first experiment to a production service, and keep improving the mature products we already sell. Decide with the team, using clear invest-or-stop criteria, when an idea is ready to scale.
  • Improve the quality of our products. Measure it with accuracy, error and coverage metrics, and with confidence scores that tell customers how far to trust each value.
  • Own a whole product, from first idea to long-term maintenance, including its production pipeline: input data, feature engineering, modeling, evaluation, release and monitoring.
  • Own the infrastructure of your product as well as its code: cloud setup, deployment, cost, security, reliability and on-call.
  • Work with probe and trace data, map content and points of interest. Solve problems in geospatial analysis, map matching, aggregation, sparse data and ground-truth quality.
  • Design experiments and tests that show whether a model works, and explain the results to technical and non-technical people.
  • Write production-quality code and review the code of others.
  • Use AI tools, such as Claude Code, for analysis, implementation and daily engineering work. Mentor less experienced colleagues and help with hiring.
  • What you will need:
  • 6+ years of professional experience as an applied scientist or data scientist with strong engineering skills.
  • A degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Geospatial Science or a similar quantitative field. A master's or PhD is a plus.
  • Strong fundamentals in statistics, machine learning, evaluation and experimental design.
  • Strong Python skills. You write code that others can read, test and run in production. Our stack can also include Kotlin, Kubernetes and React for the front end. Experience with any of them is a plus.
  • Experience with large-scale data processing, such as Spark or Databricks, and with cloud platforms.
  • Experience building data products: putting models or data pipelines into production, including monitoring.
  • Experience owning both code and infrastructure of a product: cloud services, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, CI/CD and operations.
  • Ability to work across the whole chain: data, modeling, evaluation and release.
  • Ability to turn a vague business question into a clear product and technical problem, and to work on it with little guidance.
  • Experience leading well-scoped technical projects and mentoring others.
  • Proficient in English.
  • You are already located in Madrid or nearby areas, and you are willing to commute to our Madrid office twice per week.
  • Nice to have:
  • Experience with traffic, mobility, geospatial or location data.
  • Experience with risk modeling, insurance, or time-series forecasting.
  • Experience with LLMs or agent-based tools in analytics work.

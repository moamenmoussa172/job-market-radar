About SAP Labs Latin America

SAP is one of the world’s leading technology companies, with more than 50 years of innovation helping organizations run better and improve people’s lives. Today, SAP is advancing the future of business through the combination of enterprise applications, business data, and responsible AI, with the vision of powering the Autonomous Enterprise.

Our Labs located in São Leopoldo, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, has been part of Tecnosinos since 2006 and is one of SAP’s global hubs, with nearly 3,000 employees. The site contributes to the development of solutions used by customers worldwide while fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning. SAP Labs Latin America is also recognized by Great Place to Work® as the Best IT Company to Work for in Brazil.

Do you want to explore your true potential, through a comprehensive learning journey designed to develop your technical and soft skills while fostering an AI mindset, guided by experienced professionals in a multicultural, diverse, and innovative environment?

Then the Rotation Program (STAR) is for you!

Recognized as one of the top internship programs in Brazil by FirstJob , the Rotation Program offers a unique two-year journey where you will rotate through three different areas of the company, working on impactful projects in a hybrid model.

You will also become part of SAP Next Gen, a global organization dedicated to developing early talents, joining a worldwide community of more than 1,300 STAR interns across 19 countries.

We are looking for #56 curious and ambitious students ready to explore areas such as Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, Technical Support, Business Operations, and Consulting.

Throughout your rotations, you’ll also have the opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI projects, gaining real-world experience in one of the most transformative technologies shaping the future of business and society.

Who can apply?

If you are an undergraduate student:

  • Currently enrolled from the 2nd semester onward in IT or business-related fields;
  • Have advanced English skills;
  • Expected graduation in April 2029 or later.

...then this opportunity is for you!

We don't require previous professional experience. What we value most is your curiosity to learn, a collaborative mindset, customer focus, adaptability, and out-of-the-box thinking.

Nice to have: Curiosity to explore AI-powered tools and an interest in learning how AI can support innovation and solve real-world business challenges.

Selection Process (Please, send your resume in English!)

The selection process will consist of four main stages:

  • Online Assessments
    Assessment 1: mandatory for all candidates and sent by email immediately after application.
    Assessment 2: required for candidates applying for technical positions who are pre-selected to move forward in the process.
  • HR / Talent Discovery Interview
    A conversation with the recruiter to assess the candidate’s motivation, IA adoption mindset, effective communication skills, interest in the opportunity, overall profile, and alignment with SAP.
  • Onsite Assessment | November
    Candidates selected after the HR interview will participate in an onsite assessment designed to evaluate key competencies such as collaboration, communication, learning agility, teamwork, and problem-solving.
  • Hiring Manager Interview | December
    In the final stage, selected candidates will have an interview with the Hiring Manager, who will assess their fit for the position and the team.

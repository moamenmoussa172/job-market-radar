About the job

Join IQVIA and use data, insights, technology and strategic thinking to help shape smarter approaches to clinical development.

In this role, you will work across a variety of opportunities and projects, bringing together data from multiple sources to assess clinical trial protocols, support protocol digitalization, and develop evidence-based strategies. You will turn complex scientific, operational and protocol information into structured, actionable insights that help our teams and customers make informed decisions around study design, planning and delivery.

You will collaborate with colleagues across Medical, Therapeutic Strategy, Business Development, Sales, Project Leadership and other cross-functional teams, giving you the opportunity to work with a broad range of experts and contribute to solutions that support both our customers and patients.

This is an opportunity to combine analytical thinking, digital innovation, collaboration and strategic problem-solving, while continuing to build your expertise within a global organization at the forefront of human data science.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the digitalization and structured interpretation of clinical trial protocols, translating protocol content into standardized, high-quality digital information that can enable downstream analytics, insights and study planning activities.
  • Review and validate digitally captured protocol information to ensure accuracy, completeness and consistency, applying clinical and analytical judgment to identify and resolve discrepancies.
  • With this role you will have the opportunity to grow as Design analytics lead and be more involved in deliver high-quality, data-driven protocol assessments that support new business opportunities, early engagements, re-bids and, where appropriate, awarded studies.
  • Explore and interpret multiple sources of data, scientific literature and therapeutic area research to identify relevant trends, opportunities and potential challenges.
  • Turn complex data, protocol information and analytics into clear, meaningful insights and recommendations that support effective decision-making.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional colleagues to interpret findings, challenge assumptions and shape compelling, client-ready recommendations and deliverables.
  • Develop strong expertise in IQVIA insights tools, data sources and digital solutions, selecting the most appropriate approaches to deliver robust assessments and strategies.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of protocol digitalization and analytics approaches by identifying opportunities to improve data quality, standardization, processes and ways of working.
  • Take ownership of project timelines and day-to-day stakeholder communication, ensuring activities progress effectively and to a high standard.
  • Continue to develop your analytical and consulting capabilities through hands-on project experience, cross-functional collaboration and ongoing learning opportunities.
  • Share knowledge and best practices with colleagues, while identifying opportunities to improve processes, strengthen ways of working and contribute to innovation across the team.

Why IQVIA?

  • At IQVIA, you’ll have the opportunity to work at the intersection of healthcare, data, technology and human ingenuity, helping turn insights into better decisions across clinical development.
  • You’ll collaborate with experts from different disciplines and geographies, work with rich healthcare data and advanced analytics, and contribute to innovative digital and analytical solutions for some of the most complex challenges in life sciences.
  • We offer an environment where you can grow your expertise, broaden your experience and make a meaningful contribution, while being part of a global organization focused on creating healthier outcomes for patients.

IQVIA is a leading global provider of clinical research services, commercial insights and healthcare intelligence to the life sciences and healthcare industries. We create intelligent connections to accelerate the development and commercialization of innovative medical treatments to help improve patient outcomes and population health worldwide.

Our world is changing. And so are we. From our commitment to zero-carbon flight (#ZEROe) to cleaning up space, sustainability is at the heart of our purpose. So what's your next change?

Airbus Defence and Space has an exciting opportunity for a Systems Administrator to join the Spacecraft Control Centre Support Team in Corsham.

Reporting to the Head of SCOF Engineering, you will play a critical role in maintaining the Spacecraft Control Centre and its functionality.

You will configure ground segment control computers, manage system monitoring, and provide essential engineering support to ensure the optimum performance of our mission-critical spacecraft operations.

HOW YOU WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THE TEAM

  • Maintain the Spacecraft Control Centre and its functionality, configuring ground segment control computers and diagnosing and rectifying system failures.
  • Perform routine system maintenance, housekeeping, data backups, archiving, and network management (system monitoring) to ensure optimum performance.
  • Deliver specialist LEOP tools (software analysis tools) and manage GMV installations at SAT (Production).
  • Support the Software Manager in system, software, and database configuration management activities.
  • Provide comprehensive SGS maintenance, engineering support, and ground segment software maintenance.
  • Create and review critical software build documentation and supplier documentation to uphold system integrity.

ABOUT YOU

  • Experience of supporting spacecraft operations or similar high-availability, mission-critical systems environments.
  • Experience of operating systems administration, specifically utilizing Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) and Windows 2022.
  • Experience of database administration and deploying modern configuration or containerisation tools, including Ansible and Docker (Portainer).
  • Experience of utilizing scripting and version control tools (such as Python and Git), alongside monitoring systems like Grafana.

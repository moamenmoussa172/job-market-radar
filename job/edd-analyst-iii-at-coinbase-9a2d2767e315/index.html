About the job

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? Coinbase is building the future of finance for people, institutions and their agents, with one place to trade crypto, equities, derivatives and prediction markets, use stablecoins, and connect AI agents with clear controls. The bar is high and the pace is intense, and we like it that way. This isn’t a place for complacency. It’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits and do the best work of your career. If you’re ready to build the future of finance with a championship team who refuse to settle for “good enough,” you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for in-person working sessions

About the Role

The CX Compliance team is essential in ensuring that our global CX Compliance Operations function efficiently, comply with regulatory requirements, and consistently deliver high-quality resolutions across all functions to maintain production and quality of the highest standard.

You will analyze customer information in line with defined processes and procedures and summarize the findings/facts/information to recommend appropriate next steps.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Supporting compliance policies and programs including investigating automated alerts from our transaction monitoring systems, and referrals sourced from law enforcement requests and other company lines of business
  • Gather and analyze customer due diligence documentation and information
  • Write detailed narratives as necessary
  • Incorporate feedback from Quality Assurance Team
  • Maintain detailed documentation to demonstrate compliance with regulations and internal policies and procedures
  • Remain current with industry standards and regulatory requirements

What We Look For In You

  • Demonstrates the ability to responsibly use generative AI tools and copilots (e.g., LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) in daily workflows, continuously learn as tools evolve, and apply human-in-the-loop practices to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in efficiency, cost, and quality
  • Flexible and adaptable to the evolving needs of a high-growth and fast paced organization environment
  • Ready to support in 24*7 environment
  • Organized with a high level of attention to detail

Nice to Haves

  • Proficiency with tools such as Google apps, JIRA, and Salesforce Service Cloud
  • Experience collaborating with external outsource business partners

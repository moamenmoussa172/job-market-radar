About the job

*For Pooling Purposes*

What You'll Do

Lead Enterprise Data Modernization Initiatives

  • Drive the migration of legacy reporting and analytics environments to modern cloud-based platforms.
  • Analyze existing BI models, SQL workloads, and reporting logic to identify optimization opportunities.
  • Convert complex business and reporting logic into scalable cloud-native data solutions.
  • Ensure data quality, accuracy, and seamless transition through comprehensive validation and testing.

Design & Build Scalable Data Platforms

  • Develop, enhance, and maintain enterprise-grade data pipelines and data products.
  • Build efficient data transformation frameworks using modern analytics engineering practices.
  • Optimize data models, processing frameworks, and reporting performance.
  • Establish scalable solutions that support future analytics, reporting, and machine learning initiatives.

Partner with Business & Analytics Leaders

  • Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, and business stakeholders to translate requirements into technical solutions.
  • Serve as a trusted advisor on data architecture, reporting strategy, and platform capabilities.
  • Support strategic and ad hoc analytics requests through reliable, well-governed data assets.
  • Communicate technical concepts effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Drive Operational Excellence

  • Implement best practices for data governance, version control, documentation, and deployment.
  • Monitor platform performance and proactively identify optimization opportunities.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives across analytics and data engineering functions.

What We're Looking For

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Analytics, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 4+ years of experience in Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, Business Intelligence, or Data Platform Development.
  • Strong expertise in SQL, including advanced query development, optimization, and stored procedure logic.
  • Experience working with Power BI, semantic models, and DAX-based reporting environments.
  • Hands-on experience with Spark, PySpark, Spark SQL, or similar big data technologies.
  • Proficiency in Python for data processing, automation, and analytics.
  • Experience using Git and modern software development practices.
  • Strong stakeholder management and requirements-gathering skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Databricks, Delta Lake, Databricks SQL, DLT Pipelines, and Unity Catalog.
  • Experience leading or contributing to cloud migration and data modernization initiatives.
  • Knowledge of modern data warehousing, lakehouse architecture, and analytics platforms.
  • Databricks, Azure, AWS, or related cloud certifications.
  • Experience supporting high-volume, customer-facing, or enterprise-scale operations.
  • Exposure to analytics, machine learning, or data science environments.

Why Join Us?

Own High-Impact Data Initiatives

Take the lead on strategic data transformation projects that directly influence business performance and decision-making.

Work Directly with Global Leadership

Partner with senior leaders and cross-functional teams in a highly collaborative, fast-paced environment where your expertise is valued.

Shape a Modern Analytics Ecosystem

Play a foundational role in building scalable cloud-based data platforms that enable advanced analytics, AI, and future innovation.

Accelerate Your Career

Gain exposure to modern technologies, enterprise-scale datasets, and complex business challenges while expanding your leadership influence as a senior individual contributor.

About the job

Capgemini is seeking a highly skilled Principal AI Platform Engineer to help build and scale enterprise-grade AI platforms that power next-generation solutions for banking, insurance, and payment operations.

This role focuses on designing and operating the foundational AI infrastructure that enables secure, scalable, and governed deployment of AI products. You will work on model serving, agent orchestration, evaluation frameworks, shared platform services, guardrails, and AI observability while driving innovation across the AI engineering lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

Platform Engineering

  • Design, build, and own core AI platform components, including:
  • Model gateways and routing layers
  • Agent runtimes and orchestration frameworks
  • Evaluation and testing platforms
  • AI guardrails and policy enforcement engines
  • Shared services such as tenancy, metering, case management, and enterprise connectors
  • Ensure platform reliability, scalability, security, performance, and cost efficiency.

AI Systems & Infrastructure

  • Develop AI infrastructure supporting LLMs, SLMs, RAG systems, and agentic workflows.
  • Build model serving, monitoring, and lifecycle management capabilities.
  • Enable seamless integration of both proprietary and third-party AI models.

Evaluation & Governance

  • Design and operate evaluation frameworks using golden datasets, regression testing, and automated release gates.
  • Implement AI observability, monitoring, and governance controls.
  • Establish quality, safety, and compliance standards for AI deployments.

Model Adaptation & Continuous Learning

  • Build pipelines for data collection, fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and model adaptation.
  • Support training, evaluation, and serving of domain-specific AI models.
  • Drive continuous improvement using production feedback and evaluation insights.

Collaboration & Leadership

  • Partner with product and engineering teams to develop reusable platform capabilities.
  • Mentor engineers and provide technical leadership across AI platform initiatives.
  • Promote AI-first engineering practices and developer self-service capabilities.

Requirements

AI Platform & LLM Infrastructure

Experience building and operating:

  • Model gateways (LiteLLM or equivalent)
  • AI model serving platforms (vLLM, Triton, managed endpoints)
  • Vector databases and retrieval systems
  • RAG architectures

Agentic AI Systems

Hands-on experience with frameworks such as:

  • LangGraph
  • OpenAI Agent SDK
  • Semantic Kernel
  • CrewAI

Strong understanding of:

  • Tool calling
  • Multi-agent systems
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP)
  • Agent orchestration patterns

AI Evaluation Engineering

Experience implementing:

  • Evaluation harnesses
  • Golden datasets
  • Regression testing frameworks
  • CI/CD quality gates
  • LLM-as-a-Judge methodologies

Model Fine-Tuning & Post-Training

  • Experience with one or more of the following:
  • Fine-tuning LLMs
  • Reinforcement learning pipelines
  • Synthetic data generation
  • Preference optimization
  • Domain adaptation techniques

AI-First Development

  • Demonstrated use of AI coding assistants and AI-enabled software engineering practices.
  • Experience leveraging AI tools to accelerate development and platform operations.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with AI observability and guardrail frameworks such as:

  • LangSmith
  • Braintrust
  • NeMo Guardrails
  • OpenTelemetry for GenAI

Experience in:

  • RLHF/RLAIF
  • Reward modeling
  • Model distillation and optimization
  • Small Language Models (SLMs)

Expertise in multi-tenant SaaS platforms, metering, and usage-based billing.

Prior experience in Banking, Insurance, Payments, FinTech, Cybersecurity, or other regulated industries.

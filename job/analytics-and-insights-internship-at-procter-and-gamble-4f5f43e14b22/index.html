Analytics & Insights Internship

Procter & Gamble · Rotterdam Office, Netherlands

About the job

Job Location

ROTTERDAM OFFICE

Job Description

You are a student looking for a meaningful internship? Come and live a P&G business as an Analytics & Insights Intern! Do you love diving into data sets and human psychology? We are passionate about understanding data and consumer needs and want more people who know how to use these elements in the best way to guide our decisions.

We hope you are up for the challenge!

In the Analytics & Insights Team, we are experts in data analysis, market research and consumer immersions. We use this expertise to directly influence business strategy, product design, pricing, shelf appearance, media investments and much more. We use our boundless curiosity to discover what consumers want, why they want it & how we can serve their needs better.

Internship available as of January 2027

As an Analytics & Insights Intern, you will be in charge of:

  • Actively seek to discover opportunities for delighting consumers by uncovering insights
  • Understanding behavioral patterns and working with business teams to turn these into new ideas and plans for our brands.
  • Become an expert in a broad range of analytical and insight development methods. One day you could be exploring human decision-making & how the brain works and another deep diving into big data sets to spot patterns and share key takeaways to shape P&G business strategies.
  • Be part of a multi-functional business team, working closely with Brand Management, Finance, Sales, Research & Development and others.
  • Combine soft skills with analytical skills and use integrative thinking to turn data findings into action.
  • Grow your skills day-in, day-out, by connecting with internal & external experts and by exchanging your findings with the Analytics & Insights family.

Job Qualifications

You are the right fit if you are/have:

  • Student currently enrolled in a Master’s Degree program in Marketing, Communications, Data Analytics, Psychology, Business Administration, Sociology, or any related field
  • Passionate about Data Analytics & Problem-Solving
  • Very good oral & written communications skills in both English and Dutch
  • You have a sense of leadership and a strong determination to get things done to achieve your goals.
  • You demonstrate agility, ingenuity, empathy and creativity.
  • You have a strong capacity for analysis and critical thinking.

What can we offer you?

  • Internship compensation: €1200/month;
  • Responsibilities as of Day 1 – you will feel the ownership of your projects from the beginning, and you will be given specific projects and responsibilities that matter;
  • Continuous coaching – you will work with passionate people and receive both formal trainings as well as day-to-day mentorship from your manager;
  • Dynamic and supportive work environment – employees are at the core, we value every individual and encourage initiatives, promoting agility and work/life balance;
  • Subsidized company restaurant with many options every day;
  • Flexibility in terms of working time;
  • Mobility solution; Mobility card for free public transportation or mileage reimbursement for days of the office with a maximum of 25km per day (one way)

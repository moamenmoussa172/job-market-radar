Qualifications & Experience

Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technology field.

Minimum of 3-5 years' experience in IT Infrastructure Administration within a large enterprise, telecommunications, financial services, fintech, or mission-critical environment.
Hands-on experience administering Linux (Red Hat Enterprise Linux) and Windows Server environments.
Experience managing

Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) / RHCE
VMware Certified Professional (VCP)
Kubernetes and Container Administration (CKA/CKAD)
Microsoft Certified: Windows Server or Azure Administration
ITIL Foundation Certification
Active-Active High Availability implementation experience
Knowledge of Payment Gateways

Knowledge of HP, Dell, Oracle hardware
At least 3+ years' experience in IT System Infrastructure Administration
VMware Tanzu, Kubernetes, Docker/containerization experience
Red Hat OpenShift administration experience

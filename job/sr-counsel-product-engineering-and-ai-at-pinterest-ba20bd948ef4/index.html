About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Pinterest:

Millions of people around the world come to our platform to find creative ideas, dream about new possibilities and plan for memories that will last a lifetime. At Pinterest, we’re on a mission to bring everyone the inspiration to create a life they love, and that starts with the people behind the product.

Discover a career where you ignite innovation for millions, transform passion into growth opportunities, celebrate each other’s unique experiences and embrace the flexibility to do your best work. Creating a career you love? It’s Possible.

At Pinterest, AI isn't just a feature, it's a powerful partner that augments our creativity and amplifies our impact, and we’re looking for candidates who are excited to be a part of that. To get a complete picture of your experience and abilities, we’ll explore your foundational skills and how you collaborate with AI.

Through our interview process, what matters most is that you can always explain your approach, showing us not just what you know, but how you think. You can read more about our AI interview philosophy and how we use AI in our recruiting process here.

As a member of Pinterest’s Legal team, you’ll help shape the products and experiences that bring that mission to life. We’re looking for an experienced, pragmatic, and strategic product lawyer to serve as a senior legal partner to teams building Pinterest’s core consumer experience. You’ll work across the full product lifecycle and a broad portfolio of interconnected experiences—from discovery and onboarding to identity, engagement, communications, and community. You’ll help business leaders make sound decisions on complex issues, influence product direction, and build approaches that scale as our products evolve.

We're also looking for someone who embraces AI as a core part of how they work—bringing curiosity, sound judgment, and an experimental mindset to using AI tools to drive efficiency, accelerate problem-solving, and operate with greater speed and impact.

What you’ll do:

  • Provide practical, strategic counsel across Pinterest’s consumer product journeys and the platform capabilities that support them, including user growth, discovery, onboarding, identity, communications, sharing, and collaborative experiences.
  • Advise on complex and emerging issues involving privacy and data use, consumer protection, AI, intellectual property and content, and applicable laws and regulations.
  • Shape product roadmaps and decisions by explaining legal considerations, framing options and trade-offs, and turning requirements into clear, actionable product and technical guidance.
  • Identify patterns and systemic risks across products; lead cross-functional work to develop durable principles, mitigations, and implementation plans.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders and influence decisions through sound judgment, clear communication, and strong product understanding.
  • Develop scalable legal resources, processes, and responsible AI-enabled workflows that improve the quality, speed, and consistency of advice.
  • Support product-related partnerships and integrations when they materially affect the consumer experience.

What we’re looking for:

  • Juris Doctorate with a minimum of 8 years of relevant work experience in a demanding and innovative legal environment; at least 2 years in-house at a tech company preferred.
  • A track record of advising consumer-facing product teams across the product lifecycle, including work involving complex, novel, or evolving legal questions.
  • Strong knowledge of privacy and data-use issues and consumer protection; experience with AI, intellectual property/content, or platform regulation is valuable.
  • The ability to understand technical and product details, connect implications across a broad portfolio, and give concise, actionable advice to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Excellent judgment and integrity, including the ability to assess uncertainty, prioritize material risks, and recommend practical paths forward.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence senior leaders, build strong cross-functional relationships, and lead complex initiatives to completion.
  • Strategic and solutions-oriented thinking, with the ability to balance legal obligations, user trust, product quality, and business objectives.
  • An AI-forward mindset, with experience using AI tools to accelerate analysis and deliver more effective legal advice.
  • Bar membership in good standing in at least one U.S. jurisdiction

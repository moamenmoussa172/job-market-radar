Job Description & Summary

Design intuitive, user-centered digital experiences for web and mobile applications while collaborating with German-speaking stakeholders, product owners, and development teams. The role combines UX research, interaction design, visual design, and hands-on delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct UX research, workshops, interviews, and usability testing.
  • Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI designs.
  • Build and maintain design systems and reusable UI components.
  • Collaborate with developers and product teams to ensure successful implementation.
  • Facilitate design thinking sessions and stakeholder workshops.
  • Review delivered solutions and drive continuous UX improvements.
  • Mentor junior designers and promote UX best practices.

Required Experience

  • 4-7 years of experience in UI/UX or Product Design.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design delivery.
  • Experience with enterprise, web, or mobile applications.
  • Ability to work independently and own design workstreams.

Technical Skills

  • Figma, FigJam, Adobe Creative Suite.
  • UX Research, User Journey Mapping, Information Architecture.
  • Wireframing, Prototyping, Design Systems.
  • Responsive Design and Accessibility (WCAG).
  • Agile delivery experience.

Other Skills

  • German (B2-C1+) mandatory

Preferred

  • Experience with German clients or German-speaking teams.
  • Consulting, digital transformation, or custom software development experience.
  • Experience facilitating workshops and stakeholder engagements

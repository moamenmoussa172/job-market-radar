Senior Product Manager, Data Platform

Lunar · København, Denmark

Onsite · Senior · Data Product Management · Posted 3 November 2026

Apply on Lunar's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Banking hasn’t changed much in decades. We’re changing that. One product at a time.

Lunar is a Nordic challenger bank building the future of financial life with AI at the core. Our engineers, designers, and product people work on problems that actually matter. Making money simpler, smarter, and more rewarding for millions of people across the Nordics.

Fully licensed and founded in 2015, with offices in Copenhagen, Aarhus, and Stockholm. We move fast, we build with purpose, and we’re just getting started.

As our new Senior Product Manager, Data Platform, you’ll join the Data Platform team based in Copenhagen, working to build the data foundation that powers everything from real-time financial reporting to AI and ML. You’ll be one third of a leadership trio alongside a Staff Architect and an Engineering Manager. Legacy infrastructure and EOL decisions sit with the Staff Architect, while you own the product problem and the roadmap.

What will you do?

  • Run discovery before anything gets roadmapped, working out whether a problem is worth solving now, later, or never, and keep the team focused on what actually changed for our users, our business, and our regulators, not just what got shipped.
  • Own data as a product by building and communicating a clear product vision for Matrix, backed by sharp problem definitions, OKRs, and a roadmap stakeholders can track.
  • Treat Finance, Financial Crime Prevention, Lending, and Product Analytics as real internal customers: set clear service agreements, manage inbound demand, sequence requests fairly, and flag capacity constraints before they become a problem.
  • Own data trust and discoverability, including GDPR compliance, the deletion engine, and DORA operational resilience requirements.
  • Help shape and deliver Lunar’s AI ambitions by making data accessible, governed, and agent-ready as AI use grows across the organization.

What are we looking for in you?

  • Strong product craft: you run discovery, frame problems clearly, prioritize ruthlessly, and measure success by what changed, not what shipped.
  • Comfort saying “not yet” and pushing back on requests that don’t solve a real problem.
  • Experience with event-driven architecture and modern data stacks, enough to ask sharp questions, not to be the expert in the room.
  • Experience leading through influence as a peer to architecture and engineering leads.
  • Experience in a regulated industry, ideally financial services, is a plus.

What you get (aka. Benefits and perks)

  • 4 days in the office. We’re a team that builds together, thinks together, and moves together. Showing up is part of how we work.
  • A well-equipped workspace. You’ll have a modern computer and a comfortable desk setup. We want you to have the tools you need to do your best work.
  • Pension, health insurance, and enhanced parental leave. From your financial future to your family moments. We’ve got you covered.

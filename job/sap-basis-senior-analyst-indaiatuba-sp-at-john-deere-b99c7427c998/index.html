About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Technology (CA)
Title: SAP BASIS Senior Analyst - Indaiatuba/SP - 125887

As a SAP BASIS Senior Analyst, you will be responsible for supporting, maintaining, and continuously improving SAP environments that enable critical business operations across the organization. Working as part of a global agile team, you will lead complex SAP Basis activities, including system administration, performance optimization, upgrades, troubleshooting, and technical implementations, ensuring the stability, security, and availability of SAP platforms.This role requires advanced SAP Basis expertise and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams, infrastructure partners, and business stakeholders. You will contribute to strategic initiatives, drive technical improvements, provide guidance to other analysts, and serve as a subject matter expert for SAP technologies and related enterprise systems.

Main Responsibilities

  • Administer, monitor, maintain, and optimize SAP landscapes, ensuring high availability, performance, stability, and security across production and non-production environments.
  • Perform SAP installations, upgrades, migrations, patching activities, and system refreshes in accordance with business and technical requirements.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex SAP Basis issues related to system performance, connectivity, transport management, interfaces, databases, and infrastructure components.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, database, security, and application teams to ensure seamless operation of SAP platforms and related technologies.
  • Support SAP S/4HANA and other SAP technology initiatives, contributing to architecture decisions, technical implementations, and continuous improvement efforts.
  • Monitor system health, capacity, and performance metrics, proactively identifying risks and implementing corrective actions to minimize business impact.
  • Manage transport processes and coordinate system changes across development, quality assurance, and production environments.
  • Contribute to disaster recovery planning, business continuity activities, and system compliance requirements, ensuring adherence to company standards and best practices.
  • Lead or support small to medium-sized technical projects, coordinating activities with global teams and stakeholders to ensure successful delivery.
  • Develop and maintain technical documentation, operational procedures, knowledge articles, and support materials to improve team effectiveness and system reliability.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and knowledge sharing to other analysts and support personnel, serving as a subject matter expert in SAP Basis administration.
  • Stay current with SAP technologies, industry trends, and best practices, identifying opportunities to improve platform performance, automation, and operational efficiency.

What Skills You Need

  • Bachelor's degree completed in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field
  • Advanced English communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with global teams and stakeholders
  • Availability to work in a hybrid model based in Indaiatuba, SP, Brazil
  • Extensive experience supporting and administering SAP landscapes, including system installations, upgrades, maintenance, and monitoring
  • Strong knowledge of SAP Basis administration across multiple SAP environments
  • Experience with incident management, problem resolution, performance tuning, and system optimization
  • Knowledge of database management, operating systems, and infrastructure concepts related to SAP environments
  • Experience working in Agile environments and collaborating with cross-functional teams
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver solutions with limited supervision
  • Experience providing technical guidance and mentoring to junior team members

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience supporting SAP S/4HANA environments and migration projects
  • Knowledge of SAP BTP (Business Technology Platform), SAP Cloud technologies, or hybrid landscapes
  • Experience with SAP Solution Manager, ChaRM, Focused Run, or similar SAP administration tools
  • Familiarity with automation, scripting, and monitoring tools to improve system reliability and operational efficiency
  • Experience working within global organizations supporting multi-region SAP environments
  • Knowledge of SAP security, authorization concepts, and compliance requirements
  • Cloud platform experience, including Microsoft Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud
  • SAP certifications related to Basis Administration, Technology, or SAP S/4HANA
  • Proven ability to lead complex initiatives and drive continuous improvement across SAP platforms

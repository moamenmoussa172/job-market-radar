Help protect a critical national capability through science and innovation

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as an Assistant Scientist Reactor Chemistry at our Devonport Royal Dockyard site.

The role

As an Assistant Scientist Reactor Chemistry, you’ll have a unique opportunity to build a career in science within one of the UK's most complex and highly regulated environments. Working as part of the Reactor Chemistry team, you'll support activities that are essential to the safe operation, maintenance and long-term sustainability of the UK's nuclear-powered submarine fleet. Combining laboratory science, technical analysis and operational support, this role offers the chance to develop specialist expertise while contributing directly to national defence and nuclear safety.

Day-to-day, you’ll provide scientific and technical support across reactor chemistry monitoring, surveillance and compliance activities. You'll work alongside experienced specialists, helping to maintain laboratory standards, manage technical records and support chemical and radioactive material control activities that are critical to safe and effective operations.

  • Supporting reactor chemistry monitoring, surveillance and analytical activities.
  • Maintaining accurate technical records, laboratory data and supporting documentation.
  • Assisting with the control, storage and inventory management of chemicals and controlled materials.
  • Following laboratory procedures and compliance requirements to maintain safety, quality and regulatory standards.
  • Contributing to the maintenance of laboratory standards and the long-term development of reactor chemistry capability.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at Devonport Royal Dockyard.

Essential experience of the Assistant Scientist Reactor Chemistry

  • Understanding of scientific or technical principles relevant to chemistry, analytical science or a related discipline.
  • Experience supporting monitoring, surveillance or analysis activities within a laboratory environment.
  • Ability to maintain accurate technical records, data and documentation.
  • Experience following established procedures and compliance requirements.
  • Strong communication, teamwork and attention to detail, with a commitment to maintaining high safety and quality standards.

Qualifications for the Assistant Scientist Reactor Chemistry

Essential:

  • GCSE (or equivalent) Maths and English at Grade 4/C or above.
  • Level 3 qualification in a scientific discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.

Desirable:

  • Further qualifications or training in Chemistry, Analytical Science, Nuclear Science or a related technical discipline.
  • Experience working within a dockyard, scientific, engineering or nuclear environment.
  • Full UK driving licence.

About the job

About Gusto

At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve.

All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy.

AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process.

What Product Management is like at Gusto:

  • Our Product team is lean, which means you'll have a high degree of impact and ownership. We believe in smaller, empowered teams that move quickly with less overhead. You'll move fast by pairing sharp product judgment with fluency in AI tools — automating what slows teams down and amplifying what makes them creative and high-performing.
  • We're boundaryless builders. Lines between roles are intentionally blurred, and our PMs do whatever it takes to deliver outcomes. You'll prototype, automate, design, and ship — using AI as your co-builder to turn problems into durable solutions that deliver customer value with urgency and care.
  • We're here to serve small and medium businesses. Gusto has a strong mission-driven culture, and we care deeply about lifting up these business owners — building technology for an AI-first world that gives them the superpowers to run and grow their businesses with confidence.
  • We're comfortable with change. Our environment moves fast, and PMs here thrive in ambiguity — blending curiosity, experimentation, and AI-native craft to shape how products (and work itself) are built at Gusto.

About the Team:

Gusto's Consumer Money team builds financial products for the millions of employees paid through Gusto every month. Gusto Wallet gives workers direct access to their pay and a set of tools to manage it: spend accounts, savings goals, paycheck splitting, and on-demand access to earned wages. Because Gusto runs payroll, we sit at the source of income itself, positioning us to be a powerful financial operating system for workers.

This role owns core Wallet experiences and works at the intersection of payroll and consumer banking. You'll build products that help workers smooth cash flow between paychecks, save automatically, and avoid predatory alternatives like payday lenders and overdraft fees.

Here’s what you’ll do day-to-day:

  • Own roadmap initiatives spanning Gusto financial services, helping workers make financial progress across spending, saving, credit, and money management.
  • Ship consumer banking experiences across the customer lifecycle - onboarding and activation, everyday usage, retention and engagement, and customer support - on all surfaces a customer wishes to engage.
  • Take responsibility for the business outcomes of your workstream as a true owner.
  • Be the fintech infrastructure and payments expert that reliably untangles complexity and ships durable systems.
  • Use Gusto's unique position in the payroll ecosystem to build consumer features that can only be built at the origination of income
  • Deeply understand our customers, particularly hourly and salaried workforces to craft product narratives and help create real financial progress.
  • Get in the trenches with cross-functional teams spanning Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Risk, Compliance, Legal, and Ops to ship resilient, compliant, and delightful products.
  • Evaluate and execute opportunities with external vendors (e.g., issuing banks, card processors, banking infrastructure providers) to deliver reliable, integrated experiences.
  • Measure what matters — define KPIs around activation, funding, engagement, and retention, and use data to drive prioritization, learning, and iteration.

Here’s what we're looking for:

  • 10+ years of product management experience, with directly related product management work in accounting, financial software, or an adjacent domain (mandatory).
  • Has familiarity with AI tools and comfortable using AI tools, including prototypes to accelerate the development lifecycle.
  • Is data oriented and experienced using data to inform decisions and track outcomes
  • Brings meaningful familiarity with accounting concepts (journal entries, reconciliation flows) and can ramp quickly into the accounting product space.
  • Is comfortable facing ambiguous problems.
  • Is able to wear multiple hats, for example conducting user research, forecasting product adoption or prototyping a new feature all in the same week
  • Can understand not just how an individual feature works, but how it fits into the broader accounting system.
  • Is comfortable working through detailed workflows, edge cases, data mappings, ledger concepts, and controls needed for accountant confidence.
  • Should have strong frameworks/foundations to meaningfully prioritize and deliver on customer needs and business outcomes.

Our cash compensation amount for this role is $179,000/yr to $220,000/yr in Denver, and $210,000/yr to $260,000/yr for San Francisco & New York. Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors, including candidate location, experience, and expertise, and may vary from the amounts listed above.

At Gusto, we don't use titles the traditional way, as a signal of rank or hierarchy. Instead, our titles describe two things at once — your craft (what you do, e.g. Engineering, Design) and your scope of ownership (what you're accountable for) — which is why you'll see something like "Head of X" instead of a rank word like "Director" that tells you neither. Because of this, the title on this posting may look different from what you'd expect based on the scope and seniority of the role, and from how similar roles are typically titled elsewhere. If you have questions about how this role compares to your current title or level, your recruiter can help translate.

Gusto has physical office spaces in Denver, San Francisco, and New York City. Employees who are based in those locations will be expected to work from the office on designated days approximately 2-3 days per week (or more depending on role). The same office expectations apply to all Symmetry roles, Gusto's subsidiary, whose physical office is in Scottsdale.

Note: The San Francisco office expectations encompass both the San Francisco and San Jose metro areas.

When approved to work from a location other than a Gusto office, a secure, reliable, and consistent internet connection is required. This includes non-office days for hybrid employees.

Reliable, consistent presence: We expect regular and predictable attendance, whether you're working in-office, hybrid, or fully remote, so that you stay closely connected to your team, customers, and stakeholders in real time and ensure colleagues and customers receive the timely support and responsiveness they deserve.

Our customers come from all walks of life and so do we. We hire great people from a wide variety of backgrounds, not just because it's the right thing to do, but because it makes our company stronger. If you share our values and our enthusiasm for small businesses, you will find a home at Gusto.

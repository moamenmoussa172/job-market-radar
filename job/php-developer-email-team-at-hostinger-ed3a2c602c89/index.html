Join the team building an AI-first company.

AI is changing how we build products, support customers, work together, and solve problems. It helps us move faster, spend less time on repetitive work, and focus on bigger ideas.

Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles

Your role at Hostinger

As a Software Engineer on the Email Team, you’ll build and run high-performance, high-scale public and private APIs and the internal services behind them, in Go and PHP: authentication, provisioning, webhooks, and websockets; webmail, contacts, calendar, email migration/import system; routing, policy, and spam filtering systems. None of it stops at deployment - you’ll be watching it in production and making it faster as it grows.
The stack is mostly PHP (Laravel), with the highest-throughput services built in Go, backed by MySQL, Redis/Valkey, Memcached, and ClickHouse, with Kafka/Redpanda moving events between them, and Grafana and Sentry watching it all in production. You don’t need Go coming in - just the willingness to learn it. Large parts of the platform are still being shaped, so you won’t just be shipping into an existing pattern - you’ll help set it.

Curious to learn more? Connect with your team: Edgaras Lukoševičius, Engineering Manager

Your day-to-day

  • Build across the platform. Provisioning, authentication, webhooks and websockets, email apps, policy, routing, and spam filtering, and the public APIs and SDKs built on top.
  • Own features end to end. Discovery to deployment, and the follow-up after - not just the pull request.
  • Work mostly in PHP, and in Go for the high-scale, high-availability pieces.
  • Make it fast. A slow query here isn’t a minor bug - it’s felt by millions of mailboxes at once.
  • Harden the product. Authentication, sessions, and the edge cases that become security issues if nobody catches them.
  • Watch it in production. Grafana, Sentry, and the alert that catches it before a client does.
  • Raise how the team builds. Tooling, automation, and docs that make the next feature faster to ship.
  • Review, and be reviewed. Raise the bar on both sides of a pull request.

What will help you succeed

  • Strong core software engineering principles. Clean architecture and sound engineering judgment, expressed mostly in PHP day to day, and in Go where the product needs it.
  • System design thinking - you can reason about trade-offs like consistency vs. latency, or sync vs. async, before choosing an approach.
  • A grasp of distributed, event-driven systems - queues, webhooks, retries, idempotency - the concepts behind systems that scale without quietly breaking.
  • Solid understanding of databases, caches, and queues - schema design, indexing, and knowing which tool fits the job.
  • REST API design principles - versioning, backward compatibility, and what makes an API pleasant for someone else to build on.

Nice-to-haves

  • Hands-on experience with Go.
  • Familiarity with email products and protocols (IMAP, SMTP).
  • Real-time systems - WebSockets, pub/sub, or similar.
  • Message queues and data pipelines - RabbitMQ, Kafka/Redpanda, or ClickHouse.
  • Comfortable working alongside AI coding assistants - Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot, or similar. We lean on them daily.

Meet your team

You’ll join the Email Team. The team owns Hostinger’s entire email platform end to end - inbound and outbound mail delivery, storage, spam and abuse defense, and the automation behind all of it - powering millions of client mailboxes.
You’ll work most closely with a tight-knit mix of system engineers, backend and frontend developers, and QA engineers, all building and running the same platform from different angles - infrastructure, product, and quality meeting in the same codebase. Because email underpins so much of the business, the team also works closely with Customer Support on investigating issues escalated by clients, and with Security, Network, Storage, and other infrastructure teams across Hostinger on keeping the platform safe and reliable.

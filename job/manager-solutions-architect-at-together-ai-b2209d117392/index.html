About the job

About the role

As Manager of Solutions Architecture at Together AI, you will lead the team of Solutions Architects who help our customers build and scale Generative AI applications on open-source models. You will hire, develop and coach a team of deeply technical, customer-facing engineers while staying hands-on with the most complex deals and deployments.

You will partner closely with Sales leadership to drive revenue, with Product, Engineering and Research to shape the platform, and with customers' technical and executive leaders as a trusted advisor. This is a high-impact opportunity for a technical leader who is passionate about AI, customer success and building teams in a fast-paced, innovative environment.

This is a hands-on leadership position. You should have experience deploying AI systems with customers, and your team trusts your technical judgment because of it. When a deal or deployment gets hard, you roll up your sleeves: engaging alongside your team on POCs, tuning performance, bringing in our researchers on custom inference systems and post-training work, and debugging next to your engineers.

Responsibilities

  • Hire, onboard and develop a high-performing team of Solutions Architects, building a culture of technical excellence, ownership and customer obsession
  • Align team members to accounts and opportunities, balancing strategic customers, POCs and new business
  • Coach and assist solutions architects on delivering a successful technical win across Together’s full stack (inference, post-training, and GPU clusters) including: call support, solution reviews, POC strategy/execution, and win/loss retrospectives
  • Partner with Sales leadership on territory planning, pipeline, forecasting and deal strategy
  • Serve as leadership sponsor for strategic customers and ensure their solutions hold up on goals around latency, throughput, cost, security and scale ahead of production
  • Own the technical win playbook and team metrics, including technical win rate, POC conversion and time to value
  • Collaborate on a voice of the customer program and feedback loop to provide customer insights to Product, Engineering and Research.
  • Help build enablement to ensure the team is keeping up with product changes and industry trends

Requirements

  • 8+ years in customer-facing technical roles, including 4+ years in pre-sales and 2+ years managing pre-sales or field engineering teams
  • Track record of hiring strong engineers and helping them advance in their careers
  • Proven success partnering with Sales to close digital native and AI-native deals
  • Hands-on builder who is comfortable working in python and leveraging/building AI agents to get work done.
  • Deep knowledge of post-training and inference for open-source LLMs, including serving engines such as vLLM, SGLang or TensorRT-LLM
  • Strong grasp of GPU and HPC infrastructure
  • Excellent communicator, effective with both executive buyers, ML engineers, and developers
  • Business judgment to focus your team on the highest-impact deals, plus experience building scalable processes for a growing team
  • Thrives in a fast-moving startup
  • Willing to work in San Francisco office 3-4 days a week and travel for customer meetings and team events

About Together AI

Together AI is a research-driven artificial intelligence company. We believe open and transparent AI systems will drive innovation and create the best outcomes for society, and together we are on a mission to significantly lower the cost of modern AI systems by co-designing software, hardware, algorithms, and models. We have contributed to leading open-source research, models, and datasets to advance the frontier of AI, and our team has been behind technological advancement such as FlashAttention, Hyena, FlexGen, and RedPajama. We invite you to join a passionate group of researchers in our journey in building the next generation AI infrastructure.

Compensation

We offer competitive compensation, startup equity, health insurance, and other benefits, as well as flexibility in terms of remote work. The US base salary range for this full-time position is: 300-325K OTE + equity + benefits. Our salary ranges are determined by location, level and role. Individual compensation will be determined by experience, skills, and job-related knowledge.

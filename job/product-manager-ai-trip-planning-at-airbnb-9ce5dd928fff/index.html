About the job

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

Our Product Managers imagine the ideal experience for our community and work backwards, creating innovative products that delight our guests and hosts. They deeply understand the needs of our users and translate these into a strategy that combines strong product thinking and deep research. They partner with talented cross-functional team members across engineering, design, operations, data science, and more, leading teams to execute a shared vision. They are incredibly detail-oriented and hands on.

The Guest product team owns and builds the end-to-end journey guests take to travel with us, from the first idea through the trip itself. Trip planning is where that journey becomes intelligent: a team of engineers, applied researchers, designers, and data scientists building an AI planning agent that can understand what guests are looking for, and reason across homes, experiences, services and more to assemble a real trip. We're building toward a future where Airbnb feels like a knowledgeable travel companion that plans alongside you.

The Difference You Will Make:

As Product Manager for AI trip planning, you will own the strategy and execution for this agent — one of Airbnb's most ambitious bets on an AI-native product. You'll define what it means for an agent to plan a trip well: how it asks the right questions, reasons about tradeoffs guests care about, and turns intent into a bookable trip spanning homes, experiences, and services. You’ll take the product from a demo to a system millions of guests rely on, and the work will be measured in bookings across categories and guest satisfaction at global scale. You’ll communicate these new AI features to the world by distilling the complex ideas into simple, delightful, and well-received products.

A Typical Day:

  • Define and drive the roadmap for a multi-turn agent that can plan trips with guests along their travel journey
  • Set the quality bar for agent behavior: what a good plan looks like, where the agent should defer to the guest, and how it recovers when it's wrong
  • Own the cross-category planning problem and sequence work with Homes, Experiences, Services, and Hotels so the agent can plan across the full inventory
  • Work with ML engineers and applied researchers on model strategy, evaluation frameworks, and experiment design
  • Make prioritization calls when guest trust, planning quality, latency, cost, marketplace health, and revenue pull against each other
  • Keep cross-functional partners such as Search & Discovery, Relevance & Personalization, Trips, Trust & Safety, Customer Support, Legal, and Privacy aligned on shared goals and sequencing
  • Design A/B experiments and offline evals, including proxy metrics for planning quality and measurement of long-term effects
  • Turn complex technical tradeoffs into clear decisions for engineering teams and concise narratives for leadership
  • Stay close to guests: synthesize user research, planning-session data, and competitive signals to sharpen your intuition and refine strategy
  • Partner with marketing and communications to bring each release to market, and craft the product narrative that explains it to leadership internally and to guests externally

Your Expertise:

  • 10+ years of consumer product management experience, including meaningful time on AI/ML, search, recommendations, or conversational products at scale
  • A track record of owning strategy, setting direction for technically complex systems, and making tough prioritization decisions
  • Experience shipping LLM-based products in production, including the evaluation, latency, cost, and guardrail work that stands between a good demo and a launch
  • Fluent in experimentation: you can evaluate A/B test results and offline eval honestly, and reason about proxy metrics versus long-term outcomes
  • Strong judgment of consumer product quality; you can tell the difference between an agent that sounds impressive and one that’s useful
  • Experience working on two-sided marketplaces or platforms where you balanced supply-side and demand-side tradeoffs
  • Ability to earn the respect of Staff and Principal-level engineers and researchers
  • Clear, structured communicator who can distill complex technical tradeoffs for executives and translate strategic intent into actionable guidance for engineers
  • Experience with agentic systems: tool use, planning and orchestration, retrieval, or multi-turn state management (preferred)

Your Location:

Due to the nature of this position, the successful applicant will need to be based in San Francisco, CA to be able to conduct their work.

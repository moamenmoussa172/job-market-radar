About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Spain, United Kingdom, United States.

About Docker

Docker has been one of the most loved brands in developer tooling, trusted by more than 20 million monthly users and over 20 billion container image pulls. From solo founders to the world's largest companies, developers rely on Docker to build, share, and run their applications across our suite of products including Docker Desktop, Docker Hub, and Docker Scout.
We are a globally distributed, remote-first team building the tools that define how software gets built and delivered. As AI agents redefine software development, Docker is at the center of that shift, providing the sandboxed environments, verified images, and secure infrastructure that make autonomous workflows trustworthy by default.

_______________________________________________________________________

The Role

You are a product security engineer with a strong engineering foundation. Embedded in the Desktop engineering team, you will bring an engineering-first perspective to the security of a complex, cross-platform product built at the intersection of identity, OCI runtimes, and Linux kernel internals. You will read and write Go, contribute security fixes and hardening to the codebase, and make security a first-class concern from the inside out: not as an outside reviewer, but as a contributing member of the team.

This role is for a security engineer who builds: someone who applies an engineer’s instinct to making Docker Desktop more secure by default.

_______________________________________________________________________

Responsibilities

  • 6+ years of experience in product security, application security, or software engineering with a security focus, including time embedded with or partnered closely with product engineering teams.
  • Proficiency in Go (or a comparable systems language), with the ability to review security-sensitive code and ship production-quality fixes and tooling.
  • Solid understanding of Linux fundamentals relevant to container security: namespaces, cgroups, capabilities, seccomp profiles, AppArmor/SELinux, rootless containers, and privilege boundaries.
  • Solid grasp of container runtime security (e.g. runc, containerd, BuildKit) and OCI specifications.
  • Working knowledge of identity and access management concepts: OAuth 2.0, OIDC, token handling, and auth flows in desktop or cloud-adjacent contexts.
  • Hands-on experience performing security design reviews and threat modeling for product features.
  • Familiarity with vulnerability management processes: CVE triage, CVSS scoring, coordinated disclosure, and working with external reporters.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills; comfortable bridging the gap between a dedicated security team and a product engineering team.

_______________________________________________________________________

Qualifications

  • 6+ years of software engineering experience, with meaningful exposure to security-sensitive systems or a strong personal interest in security.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
  • Strong proficiency in Go, with a track record of writing and shipping production-grade code.
  • Solid understanding of Linux fundamentals relevant to container security: namespaces, cgroups, capabilities, seccomp profiles, AppArmor/SELinux, rootless containers, and privilege boundaries.

What to Expect

First 30 Days

  • You will onboard into the team and get hands-on with the Docker Desktop codebase, architecture, and development workflow. You will meet your counterparts in the central security organization and learn how vulnerability reports are currently handled. The goal is to listen, ask questions, and build a clear picture of the product’s current security posture, not to change anything yet.

First 90 Days

  • You will be an active participant in design and code reviews, bringing a security lens to features in flight and landing your first security fixes in the codebase. You will have taken ownership of the vulnerability intake process, handling your first end-to-end triage cycles with minimal guidance. You will have a working relationship with the engineers on the team and a growing sense of where the most meaningful security investments should be made.

One Year Outlook (First Year)

  • You will be the team’s trusted authority on product security and a contributor to the codebase. You will have driven meaningful improvements to threat modeling practices, auth flows, container isolation defaults, or time-to-remediation, and shipped security improvements directly to the code. Your work will be directly visible in the security and resilience of a product used by millions of developers every day.

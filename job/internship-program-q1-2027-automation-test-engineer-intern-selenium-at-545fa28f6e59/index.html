About the job

Internship Duration: 04 Jan 2027 - 25 Jun 2027

Internship Model: Full-time or at least 4 days/week

Application Period: 1 Nov – 16 Dec 2027

Interview date: 10, 15 & 17 Dec 2027

  • Understand business requirement and take task assignments from leader
  • Support the design, development, and execution of automated test scripts
  • Assist in maintaining and updating existing automation test frameworks
  • Execute automated and manual test cases and report test results
  • Identify, log, and track defects using bug-tracking tools
  • Manage test documents and keep track topics
  • Prepare basic test documentation (test cases, test reports)
  • 3rd or final-year student in Computer Science, IT Software, related major
  • Official university recommendation letter with the university stamp (mandatory). This document will be required after you successfully pass Bosch's interview process.
    Exception: For students from HCM University of Science and HCM University of Social Sciences and Humanities, the university will send the recommendation letter directly to Bosch. You do not need to obtain a separate copy yourself.
  • Commitment: full-time or at least 4 days/week
  • Must have program skill using Java, .NET
  • Experienced in using Selenium (is plus)
  • Have knowledge or experience using other test tools as CodeUI, Cucumber, TPT (is a plus)
  • Ability to setup development environment for automation test
  • Ability to know how to use source control basically.
  • Ability to use build tool
  • Fair English communication skills

Important note:

  • Graduates or applicants who cannot submit a university recommendation letter (with official stamp from university) are not eligible to apply.
  • Due to the high number of applications received, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. Thank you for your understanding.

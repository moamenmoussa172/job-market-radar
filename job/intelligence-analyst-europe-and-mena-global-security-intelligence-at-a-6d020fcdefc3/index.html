Intelligence Analyst, Europe & MENA, Global Security Intelligence

Amazon · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Onsite · Security Engineer · Posted 23 November 2026 · Arabic required

SIEM / SOC

About the job

Come join an innovative team that delivers tailored products and services for the customer. Our ideal candidate will produce rapid assessments on risk, and conduct open-source investigations across the Middle East, North Africa, and Europe regions. They will bring deep subject-matter expertise spanning both MENA and European geopolitical and security landscapes. Our ideal candidate will demonstrate an ability to think critically, provide deep-dive analysis, and prioritize multiple requests in a fast-paced and challenging environment that requires significant attention to detail. They bring a genuine passion for delighting customers, collaborating across teams, and continuously raising the bar — always seeking opportunities to refine processes, close gaps, and deliver more impactful products.
Key job responsibilities
Collect, analyze, and interpret complex data with a strong understanding and application of analytical techniques across the MENA and Europe regions.
Proactively identify and analyze relevant threats and trends across both regions and produce written strategic analysis, oral briefs, and tactical/investigative assessments in a cohesive and concise format to serve customers across business lines.
Engage with internal customers to understand their priorities and translate the requirements into solutions.
Identify new solutions, methodologies, including using AI, to help improve information collection and reporting. Including the creation of maps or other visuals and tools to support the team's products.
Perform thorough quick-turn and deep dive open-source investigations and monitoring across MENA and European sources.
Support business continuity, crisis, and security management teams, including as needed outside typical work hours during security incidents and emerging crises.
- Experience handling confidential information
- Experience conducting investigations using Boolean logic, OSINT analysis, databases, and social media
- Master's degree or equivalent, or experience in a professional field or military
- Experience in Incident Management, Threat Management, Corporate Investigations, Security Operations, Crisis Management or a related field
- 3+ years in analytical, intelligence, investigative disciplines
- Experience in verbal and written communication across various levels including senior leadership
- Experience with the intelligence cycle, strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and effective communication abilities
- Demonstrated knowledge of the geopolitical, security, and threat landscape across both the Middle East/North Africa and Europe regions
- OSINT certification or equivalent training
- Proficiency in Arabic or Farsi and/or one or more European languages (e.g., French, German, Italian, Spanish, etc.)

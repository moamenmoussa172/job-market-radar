Job Description & Summary

Automation and Integration Lead Developer

Your impact

Technology creates opportunities to simplify the way we work, connect systems and build smarter solutions. At PwC, you will have the opportunity to turn those possibilities into meaningful impact.

As an Automation and Integration Lead Developer, you will combine hands on development with technical ownership.

You will design automation and integration solutions, help guide other developers and contribute to the technical direction of our Microsoft Power Platform and Azure environment.

You will have room to grow your expertise in a collaborative and inclusive environment where curiosity, new ideas and continuous learning are encouraged.

About the team

You will join our Products and Technology team within Internal Firm Services in Belgium, supporting reliable, secure and high performing IT services across the organisation.

Within our Application and Automation team (A&A), you will help build and improve solutions that automate processes and connect applications, systems and data.

You will work closely with developers, architects, business stakeholders and other technology teams to deliver scalable and reliable solutions.

What your day will look like

  • Design and develop automation and integration solutions using Microsoft Power Platform and Azure Integration Services.
  • Build solutions with Power Automate, Power Apps, Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions using C Sharp, Azure Service Bus and Azure Event Grid.
  • Translate business and technical requirements into effective and sustainable technology solutions.
  • Contribute to the technical direction of automation and integration solutions and ensure consistency across developments.
  • Provide technical guidance to developers, exchange ideas and share knowledge and best practices across the team.
  • Ensure solutions meet high standards for quality, scalability, security, reliability and maintainability.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, architects, developers and other technology teams to understand needs and deliver the right technical solutions.

Your profile

  • 3 to 7 years of relevant professional experience in software development, automation, integration or a related technology environment.
  • Strong knowledge of Azure Integration Services, including Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions, Azure Service Bus and Azure Event Grid.
  • Hands on experience with Microsoft Power Platform, including Power Automate, Power Apps and Dataverse.
  • Experience with API integrations and C Sharp, with good knowledge of REST, SOAP, JSON and XML.
  • Strong analytical and problem solving skills, with an understanding of how to build scalable, secure, reliable and maintainable solutions.
  • A collaborative and proactive mindset, with the ability to take ownership of technical deliverables, share knowledge and provide technical guidance to other developers.
  • You are fluent in Dutch or French and have a professional working proficiency in English.

What you can expect from us

At PwC, you will have the opportunity to grow through challenging and meaningful work while developing both your technical expertise and leadership skills.

You can expect a supportive and inclusive environment, continuous learning opportunities, flexibility and the chance to work with modern Microsoft technologies.

You will have autonomy in your work while being surrounded by colleagues who share knowledge and work together to deliver quality solutions.

Life at PwC

At PwC, people with different backgrounds, perspectives and expertise work together to solve important problems.

We value integrity, collaboration, curiosity, quality and continuous learning, and want everyone to feel respected and able to contribute.

We are committed to equal opportunities and an inclusive recruitment process. Personal data shared during recruitment is handled in line with applicable privacy requirements, including GDPR.

Ready to combine your technical expertise with greater ownership and help shape our automation and integration solutions? We would like to hear from you.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the team

The Connectors team is the bridge between ClickHouse and the broader data ecosystem. We build and maintain the integrations that make ClickHouse accessible to millions of developers, data practitioners and AI agents worldwide: data visualization plugins (Tableau, Power BI, Superset, Metabase), connectors for data frameworks (Apache Spark, Flink, Kafka Connect, Beam, Fivetran), transformation and orchestration tooling (dbt, Airflow, Dagster), and AI tooling.

Our work directly shapes how companies process massive datasets: real-time analytics platforms ingesting millions of events per second, observability systems monitoring global infrastructure, and the data warehouses and AI-powered applications redefining how teams work with data. We collaborate closely with the open-source community, internal teams, and enterprise users to ensure ClickHouse integrations set the standard for performance, reliability, and developer experience.

About the role

As a Senior Software Engineer focused on the data warehousing ecosystem, you will own how ClickHouse plugs into the modern analytics-engineering toolchain. This role sits at the intersection of high-performance database engineering, developer experience, and production operations. You will build the integrations that let data teams use ClickHouse as the warehouse behind the tools they already run, and you will own how those integrations are deployed and operated at scale.

You will drive architecture, performance, and feature direction across four areas:

  • Transformation: dbt, across both the Python-based v1 adapter and the Rust-based v2 (Fusion) engine; SQLMesh
  • Semantic layer: dbt Semantic Layer / MetricFlow, Cube, Apache Ossie (formerly Open Semantic Interchange)
  • Schema and metadata management: schema evolution, model contracts, migration tooling, catalog integration
  • Orchestration: Apache Airflow, Dagster, Prefect, Temporal, and how transformation jobs run inside them (Astronomer Cosmos, dagster-dbt)

We are looking for someone who has run the transformation layer of a real data warehouse, not just written models in it. You have operated dbt and its orchestration for many teams: CI/CD, environments, scheduling, credentials, observability, and cost. You know what breaks at scale because you were the one paged for it. You know what it takes to maintain code that other teams’ pipelines depend on – ideally because you’ve contributed to the open-source data warehousing ecosystem yourself (dbt and its adapters, orchestration providers, semantic-layer tooling or similar). You bring that practitioner's insight into what we should build and why, and you turn it into robust, production-ready integrations and the deployment artifacts (container images, Helm charts, reference architectures) that make ClickHouse the natural choice when data teams design their next warehouse.

What you'll do

  • Own and evolve dbt-clickhouse and related transformation adapters to enterprise-grade quality: correctness, performance, and reliability across dbt versions and engines
  • Design and build ClickHouse integrations for semantic-layer tooling (MetricFlow, Cube, Apache Ossie), making ClickHouse a first-class target for governed metrics consumed by BI tools and AI agents
  • Build and maintain orchestration integrations (Airflow provider, Dagster, Prefect) and the patterns for running transformation jobs inside them
  • Define how schema evolution, model contracts, and migrations work across the toolchain when ClickHouse is the warehouse
  • Own the production deployment and operations story for these integrations: container images, Helm charts, reference architectures, and runbooks for running transformation and orchestration workloads on Kubernetes against ClickHouse
  • Build and operate the Kubernetes-based CI, integration-test, and benchmark infrastructure
  • Engage actively with the open-source community: triage issues, support contributors, advocate for users, and shape the roadmap from real-world feedback
  • Collaborate with Product, Cloud, and core database teams to align integration work with broader priorities
  • Bring a practitioner's perspective to roadmap decisions, grounding prioritization in how data and analytics engineers actually operate a warehouse

About you

  • 7+ years of software development experience, including hands-on time as a Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, or Data Platform Engineer
  • Deep dbt experience from both sides: you have run large dbt projects in production, and you understand how an adapter plugs into dbt-core
  • You have built or operated an internal data platform that served multiple teams: CI/CD for transformation projects, orchestration, environment promotion, secrets and credentials, observability, and cost attribution
  • You have run dbt and an orchestrator (Airflow, Dagster or Temporal) in production on Kubernetes, and you’ve owned the deployment and reliability of both — not just the models.
  • Strong database fundamentals: SQL, data modeling, query optimization, and familiarity with OLAP or columnar databases
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication; comfortable collaborating across engineering functions and with open-source communities

Bonus points

  • Proven contributions to the open-source data warehousing ecosystem
  • Familiarity with ClickHouse or similar high-performance OLAP databases; MergeTree internals a plus
  • Hands-on experience with semantic-layer tooling (MetricFlow, Cube, Apache Ossie, AtScale) or BI semantic models
  • Experience exposing governed data to AI tooling: MCP servers, text-to-SQL grounding, agent-facing data APIs
  • Familiarity with Rust & the Data Fusion ecosystem
  • Familiarity with the JVM ecosystem

About the Team

We are looking for a Product Manager to help shape the future of Workday’s core data engine. In this role, you will define and execute the roadmap for Workday’s next generation core data and compute engine, driving strategic efforts that improve performance, scalability, and efficiency for our largest customers and most challenging workloads.You will work closely with senior product leaders, engineering, product pillar teams, and other cross-functional partners to help define priorities, and business needs, and translate strategy into actionable product plans. If you are passionate about enterprise data access, and building scalable platform capabilities that enable innovation across Workday, this role is for you.

About the Role

  • Collaborate with Workday’s development teams and stakeholders to synthesize and clearly document their needs and priorities. Apply structured frameworks to intake, assess, and rank the strategic importance of enhancements, guiding investment and development focus.
  • Define the product roadmap, with a focus on high-value outcomes and Jobs to Be Done. Provide well-defined requirements for each release based on user needs, technical architecture, and business strategy.
  • Work closely with the engineering organisation through design, development, testing, release, and maintenance of the product to ensure its quality and successful delivery. Support iterative delivery, validation, and test-and-learn approaches.
  • Coordinate and align requirements across multiple scrum teams operating in a sophisticated distributed software environment.
  • Act as a customer and stakeholder advocate by incorporating feedback loops, usage insights, and market signals into product decisions, planning and prioritization.
  • Communicate roadmap updates, product insights, trade-offs, and delivery progress to stakeholders and product leadership.
  • Get internal development teams to enthusiastically build on top of the product through education and evangelization.
  • Serve as the central resource and point of contact for other Workday teams including but not limited to Application Development, Documentation, and Education.
  • Championing a data‑driven culture by using performance metrics, benchmarking, and customer telemetry to inform prioritization, tradeoffs, and long‑term strategy.

About You

Basic Qualifications

  • 5+ years experience in product management, defining and delivering SaaS and/or cloud-based enterprise software products or features.
  • Strong individual initiative and ownership – driven by results, motivated by impact, and comfortable making thoughtful tradeoffs in a complex technical space.
  • Demonstrated ability with database engines and interfaces, including deep understanding of SQL, and familiarity with query optimization and performance tradeoffs.
  • Experience with distributed systems, cloud architectures, large‑scale data platforms, and designing scalable, reliable, business‑critical systems for enterprise customers.

Other Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Business, or related technical field.
  • Comfortable diving into complex, technical problem spaces (e.g., performance, reliability, data modeling, cost‑to‑serve) and distilling them into clear, prioritized product plans.
  • Proven ability to operate successfully across multiple functions, influence senior executives and peers alike, and mediate across teams with differing priorities.
  • Exceptional communication and storytelling skills, both written and verbal – you can explain complex technical concepts in a compelling, accessible way.
  • Flexible and adaptable to change, excited by innovation, and energized by working with a fun, creative, and high‑performing team at the heart of Workday’s performance and efficiency evolution.
  • Experience using AI-powered tools and platforms to inform product decisions.
  • Ability to break down complexity, clearly articulate product requirements, and prioritise enhancement requests
  • Excellent analytical abilities, including exploration, research, measurement, monitoring, and storytelling.
  • Excellent organisational, collaboration and communication skills
  • Strong self-motivation, problem solving, and execution skills

Our Approach to Flexible Work

With Flex Work, we’re combining the best of both worlds: in-person time and remote. Our approach enables our teams to deepen connections, maintain a strong community, and do their best work. We know that flexibility can take shape in many ways, so rather than a number of required days in-office each week, we simply spend at least half (50%) of our time each quarter in the office or in the field with our customers, prospects, and partners (depending on role). This means you'll have the freedom to create a flexible schedule that caters to your business, team, and personal needs, while being intentional to make the most of time spent together. Those in our remote "home office" roles also have the opportunity to come together in our offices for important moments that matter.

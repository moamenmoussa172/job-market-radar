About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the Role

Abnormal AI is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own high-priority initiatives within our core Cloud Email Security group — the flagship product area driving Abnormal's growth and market leadership. Our AI-native platform helps customers ranging from SMBs to Fortune 100 enterprises stay safe from complex cyberattacks that cause financial loss and disrupt business operations. In this role, you will have direct interaction with some of the largest enterprise customers in the world, setting strategy, driving execution, and helping Abnormal continue to protect customers from evolving and sophisticated attacks.

What you will do

  • Own and evolve a 9–12 month roadmap for a strategically critical initiative within Cloud Email Security, prioritizing against customer value, competitive positioning, and revenue impact
  • Lead cross-functional execution across Engineering, Design, Data, Sales, and Customer Success — from problem framing and PRDs through launch, instrumentation, and iteration
  • Run discovery with Fortune 500 enterprises to translate operational insights into clear problem statements and measurable outcomes; go deep with Engineering on architecture and infrastructure tradeoffs
  • Contribute to competitive positioning and GTM strategy, including customer-facing messaging, analyst conversations, and sales enablement
  • Define and track KPIs including adoption, competitive win rates, and revenue impact; serve as the domain expert in executive reviews, customer calls, and escalations
  • Leverage AI tools (e.g., Claude, v0, analytics platforms) and rapid prototyping to accelerate discovery, research, documentation, and UX exploration

Must Haves

  • 5+ years of product management experience in software, with a strong track record of owning complex technical product areas end-to-end in B2B SaaS or similarly technical domains
  • Proven experience working on deeply technical products, collaborating closely with engineering on architecture, scalability, or AI/ML-driven features, and reasoning through technical tradeoffs and constraints
  • Demonstrated ability to own product strategy, definition, and roadmap for a product line or multiple complex surfaces — including problem framing, opportunity sizing, prioritization, and success metrics
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with comfort using data to inform decisions and refine product direction in ambiguous spaces
  • Excellent communication and cross-functional leadership, with the ability to drive alignment across Engineering, Design, GTM, and executives, and represent the product in high-stakes customer-facing scenarios
  • High ownership, operational excellence, and attention to detail across documentation, planning, stakeholder management, and launch execution
  • Growth mindset with a willingness to learn quickly in a fast-paced, AI-native environment, including integrating new tools and workflows into day-to-day product practice

#LI-CP1

Nice to Have

  • Experience in enterprise cybersecurity, security operations, or email security
  • Prior experience as a software engineer, data scientist, or in another technical role, or equivalent hands-on experience with distributed systems or AI-driven products
  • Familiarity with AI-native workflows and rapid prototyping tools (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT, v0, Figma) to speed up discovery, ideation, and validation
  • Experience working directly on product pricing and commercials, as well as partnering with GTM teams on competitive positioning, customer-facing messaging, and sales enablement

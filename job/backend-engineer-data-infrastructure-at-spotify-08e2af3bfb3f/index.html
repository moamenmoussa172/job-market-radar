About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

The Platform team creates the technology that enables Spotify to learn quickly and scale easily, enabling rapid growth in our users and our business around the globe. Spanning many disciplines, we work to make the business work; creating the infrastructure, tooling, frameworks, and capabilities needed to welcome a billion customers.

We're looking for a Backend Engineer to join Babka, a team within Spotify's Data Infrastructure organization. Babka powers the analytics behind experiences like Spotify for Artists and Spotify for Creators, helping artists, creators, and music labels explore how their content performs through fast, interactive data insights.

Originally built to support music analytics, our platform now serves podcasts, audiobooks, and internal Spotify tools. As we expand our capabilities across Spotify, you'll join us at an exciting stage, with opportunities to shape our technical direction, improve our infrastructure, and make analytics easier for engineering teams to build and operate.

What You'll Do:

  • Build, maintain, and improve Java backend services, libraries, and data ingestion pipelines that power Spotify's analytics platform.
  • Operate and deploy Apache Druid, our distributed analytics database, enabling teams to deliver fast, reliable, interactive data experiences.
  • Help expand our data infrastructure platform to support new products, teams, and use cases across Spotify.
  • Build internal tools and shared services that make it easier for engineers to integrate with and use our analytics infrastructure.
  • Contribute to the reliability, performance, and scalability of our distributed systems, including monitoring, troubleshooting, and improving production services.
  • Collaborate with engineers and product teams across Spotify to understand their analytics needs and develop practical, scalable solutions.
  • Develop agentic workflows that both automate on-call operations and accelerate how teams build data analytics features on top of our platform.
  • Contribute to technical and architectural decisions as the team evolves, exploring opportunities to improve data modeling, infrastructure, and developer experience.
  • Who You Are:
  • You have 3+ years of experience developing backend services or working with distributed data systems.
  • You're comfortable programming in Java and enjoy building reliable, maintainable backend applications.
  • You have experience with distributed databases or data storage systems and understand the challenges of working with data at scale.
  • You understand data modeling, database design, and how data is organized and queried to support different use cases.
  • You care about building reliable services and have experience with monitoring, troubleshooting, and improving systems in production.
  • You're curious about analytical databases and technologies such as Apache Druid, ClickHouse, Pinot, BigQuery, or Snowflake. Experience with Apache Druid is a plus.
  • You've worked with, or are interested in learning, infrastructure technologies such as Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud platforms like Google Cloud Platform or AWS.
  • You enjoy collaborating with other engineers, communicating openly, and learning new technologies. You're comfortable taking ownership of your work while seeking feedback and sharing knowledge with others.
  • Where You'll Be:
  • This role is based in New York, NY
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.
  • The United States base range for this position is 132,948.00–189,927.00 USD, plus equity. The benefits available for this position include health insurance, six-month paid parental leave, 401(k) retirement plan, monthly meal allowance, 23 paid days off, paid flexible holidays, and paid sick leave.

Security & Compliance Analyst

Become a Security & Compliance Analyst at Bloomreach. You will join our Governance, Risk, and Compliance team and help maintain our compliance activities, resolve compliance-related issues, and support engineering and sales teams with customer requirements. Bloomreach provides the best digital experience for leading international e-commerce companies, and your work will help protect the trust of hundreds of millions of consumers who shop online.

Your tasks and responsibilities

As a Security & Compliance Analyst, you will:

  • Support periodic audits of company procedures and processes, and help prepare reports on compliance.
  • Investigate compliance issues, document findings, and support the implementation of appropriate actions.
  • Support the sales process by completing security and compliance sections of RFPs and customer questionnaires.
  • Help ensure that policies, standards, and related documentation are reviewed and kept up to date.
  • Lead the security training program.
  • Maintain process mapping and the responsibilities structure chart.
  • Support updates to compliance policies and related documentation.
  • Research relevant regulations, standards, and industry developments to support the team’s work.
  • Help communicate applicable regulations and compliance requirements to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Assist with the preparation of management reviews and supporting materials.
  • Communicate with clients across different regions, including EMEA and the US.

What we expect from you

  • Working knowledge of relevant industry compliance requirements and standards.
  • A good understanding of SaaS businesses and how they operate.
  • A good understanding of Governance, Risk, and Compliance in an IT environment.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with different stakeholders.
  • Previous experience as a compliance analyst, security analyst, compliance officer, or in a similar role.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Effective research and documentation skills.
  • The ability to organise your work and manage competing priorities.
  • Experience with ISO security standards or a willingness to develop your knowledge in this area.
  • Exposure to SOC 2 audits or similar compliance assessments

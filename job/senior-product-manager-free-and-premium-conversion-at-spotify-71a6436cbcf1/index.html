Senior Product Manager - Free and Premium Conversion

Spotify · Sweden, United Kingdom

Hybrid · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 18 November 2026

Apply on Spotify's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Hundreds of millions of people come to Spotify to listen for free. Our ambition is to make that experience great while helping listeners discover the value of Premium. Getting that balance right is one of the most important opportunities for Spotify's continued growth.

The Freemium Funnel team shapes the journey from Free listening to Premium subscription and beyond. We bring together Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Research, and Marketing to understand what listeners value, remove barriers to conversion, and create experiences that make Premium a compelling choice.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to join our Free and Conversion Surfaces team. You'll shape how listeners engage with Spotify Free, discover Premium, make the decision to subscribe, and experience their first moments as paying subscribers. From Premium education to conversion experiences and early retention, you'll work on meaningful product challenges that directly influence Spotify's growth.

This is an opportunity to think strategically, build at scale, and turn complex challenges into simple, engaging experiences that work for both our listeners and our business.

What You'll Do

  • Get close to our Free listeners to understand what they value, what gets in their way, and what could make Premium more compelling.
  • Own the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for a key part of the Free-to-Premium journey, balancing immediate opportunities with long-term impact.
  • Lead initiatives across multiple product areas, moving beyond individual experiments to build scalable capabilities that improve the conversion experience.
  • Connect insights and opportunities across teams, identifying what matters most and communicating how your work contributes to subscriber growth.
  • Partner closely with Engineering, Design, Data, and Research to explore ideas, run experiments, and make informed product decisions.
  • Bring clarity to complex problems, helping teams align on priorities, navigate trade-offs, and move forward with confidence.
  • Use AI tools and agents in your daily work to explore data, develop ideas, create product documentation, and work more effectively.
  • Who You are
  • You have experience building and launching consumer-facing products at scale, with a strong understanding of user needs and business outcomes.
  • You understand growth, conversion, or monetization challenges and know how to create experiences that deliver value for both users and the business.
  • You're comfortable owning complex product strategies and translating long-term goals into clear priorities and actionable roadmaps.
  • You think in systems, understanding how product decisions, shared capabilities, and user experiences connect across teams.
  • You use data, experimentation, and customer insights to make thoughtful decisions, knowing when to move quickly and when deeper investment is needed.
  • You take initiative, navigate ambiguity, and bring people together to solve problems without waiting for perfect information.
  • You communicate clearly and build strong relationships across Engineering, Design, Data, Research, and business teams.
  • You value transparency, collaboration, and thoughtful decision-making, and you're curious about how AI can improve the way you work.
  • Where You'll Be
  • This role is based in London, UK or Stockholm, Sweden
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.

About the job

Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. Valeo is a global technology company designing breakthrough solutions that reinvent mobility. We are a key automotive partner for car manufacturers and new mobility players worldwide. Our vision? To create greener and safer mobility through solutions focused on intuitive driving and the reduction of CO2 emissions. We are a leader in our business sectors and recognized as one of the world's most innovative companies.

At the Valeo plant in Czechowice-Dziedzice, we manufacture powertrain systems that support fuel consumption reduction and lower emissions—including starters, air throttles, exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) valves, electromechanical actuators, and innovative electric drives.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Testing & Analysis: Develop andperform validation tests for hydraulic products and analyze results according to lab standards.
  • Bench development: Develop and improve benches for validation tests.
  • Measurement Systems: Perform MSA and manage sensors for data collection
  • Equipment Oversight: Validate laboratory equipment and ensure it meets technical and safety requirements.
  • Problem Solving: Resolve equipment and product issues, supporting continuous improvement in the lab.
  • Reporting & Collaboration: Prepare technical reports and cooperate with project teams to meet customer requirements.

Requirements:

  • Education: Degree in Automation, Mechanical, Electrical or similar.
  • Experience: Min. 2 years in a similar role (Automotive preferred).
  • Technical Skills: Strong knowledge of mechatronics systems, measurement hardware.
  • Languages: Good English and Polish (working with international teams).

Nice to Have:

  • Bus protocols (CAN/LIN)
  • Vector tools (CANoe/CANape)
  • LabView or DIAdem (National Instruments).
  • Basic programming (Python/C)

  • Development opportunities through participation in internal and external training.
  • The chance to have a real impact on the development of modern automotive technologies and road safety.

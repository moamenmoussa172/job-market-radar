About the job

The HQ IT team is looking for a Data Developer to join our HR & IT Data Domain team. In this role, you will leverage your SQL, Python, and data engineering expertise to design, develop and optimize scalable data warehouse solutions that enable reporting and analytics. You will work across the full data lifecycle, from data integration and modeling to pipeline development and analytics enablement, building robust, well-documented data pipelines, enforcing data quality and governance standards, and continuously improving our data warehouse infrastructure in close collaboration with stakeholders.

What you'll do

  • Analyze and understand business requirements, translating them into scalable, efficient data warehouse solutions and data models.
  • Design, build, optimize and maintain ETL processes and data pipelines ensuring reliable, accurate and timely data delivery.
  • Perform data validation, testing and root cause analysis to identify and resolve data quality, performance and consistency issues.
  • Support troubleshooting and incident resolution for data integration and reporting issues.
  • Produce and maintain clear technical documentation for data models, ETL processes, reporting semantic models, data mappings and development activities.
  • Participate in project meetings, workshops, and solution design discussions, providing technical expertise and recommendations.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of data architecture, development standards and best practices.
  • Collaborate closely with business stakeholders, analysts and technical teams to ensure successful project delivery.
  • Communicate technical concepts effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences, ensuring alignment between business needs and technical solutions.

What you'll bring as technical knowledge

  • Strong experience in Data Warehouse development, including building, optimizing and troubleshooting ETL processes and data pipelines.
  • Hands-on experience with cloud-based data platforms, preferably Snowflake; exposure to Databricks is considered a strong advantage.
  • Strong SQL proficiency, with the ability to write efficient, maintainable and reusable code (including performance tuning and debugging).
  • Experience building, optimizing, and troubleshooting data pipelines for reporting and analytics.
  • Practical experience with workflow orchestration tools such as Apache Airflow (DAG design, scheduling, dependency monitoring).
  • Knowledge of DataOps practices, including CI/CD, version control (Git), testing, monitoring, and release management.
  • Experience with reporting and analytics tools such as Power BI, Tableau or similar, including optimizing models for dashboards.
  • Advanced level of English (written and spoken), for collaboration with international teams.

What you'll bring as soft skills

  • Autonomous and innovative
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Result oriented: respects the deadlines whilst maintaining a high-quality service
  • Adaptability – being able to cope with change, be it on the technological landscape or organizational wise

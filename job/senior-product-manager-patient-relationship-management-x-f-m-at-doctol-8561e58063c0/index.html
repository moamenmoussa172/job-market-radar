About the job

What we do

As a Senior Product Manager, your mission will be to lead the evolution of our patient messaging solution, transforming how healthcare professionals communicate with their patients. You'll work with our established messaging platform that launched in Italy in 2021 and now facilitates millions of care conversations monthly across multiple countries. You'll collaborate with a dynamic team of full-stack developers in Milan and Berlin to drive productivity improvements and enhance healthcare professional satisfaction.

What you will do:

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Define and execute the long-term product strategy for patient messaging and communication services
  • Lead cross-functional teams through technical transformations, including architecture improvements and AI integration
  • Drive the scalability of patient relationship management tools across multiple countries
  • Collaborate with senior stakeholders to align product vision with business objectives
  • Oversee integration with innovative products like AI-phone assistant and document sharing capabilities

Who you are

Before you read on, if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

  • You have a proven track record in senior product management roles with strategic impact
  • You have experience designing, launching, and scaling technical products in SaaS or B2B environments, preferably in healthcare or regulated industries
  • You have a strong analytical mindset with a data-driven approach to product decisions
  • You possess technical knowledge and experience with distributed systems and platform architecture
  • You are fluent in English and ready to work in a fast-paced, international environment

Now it would be fantastic if you:

  • Speak Italian (highly recommended for our Milan location)
  • Have experience with AI-powered product development
  • Bring knowledge of healthcare technology or regulated industries

What we offer

  • Supplementary Health Insurance
  • Partnership for retirement funds with Ciao Elsa
  • Parent Care Program: Various initiatives supporting parenthood, including 100% paid parental leave
  • Free mental health and coaching services through our partner Moka.care
  • For caregivers and workers with disabilities, a package including an adaptation of the remote policy, extra days off for medical reasons, and psychological support
  • A flexible workplace policy offering both hybrid and office-based mode
  • Work from EU countries and the UK for up to 10 days per year, thanks to our flexibility days policy
  • Sport Partnership with FitPrime
  • Reimbursement of public transportation

The interview process

  • Interview with a recruiter to discover your background, motivations and assess the fit of your skills with our needs
  • Meeting with our managers to explore your skills through concrete situations and present our team and mission
  • Discussion with our senior managers to deepen your application including business case simulation
  • At least one reference check

Job's details:

  • Job type: Permanent
  • Contract: Full-time
  • Location: Milan
  • Remote Policy: Hybrid
  • Total compensation range: €70.000 - €100.000 annually (including 10% performance-based bonus)

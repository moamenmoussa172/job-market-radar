The role

As a Financial Planning and Reporting Analyst, you'll play a key role in improving the quality, consistency and efficiency of reporting across UK Aviation. Working closely with finance leaders and stakeholders across the business, you'll consolidate financial and operational information, challenge assumptions, identify trends and help drive better business performance.

You'll support planning, forecasting and reporting activities while helping to modernise and enhance reporting processes. Through your analysis, you'll provide senior stakeholders with clear, actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and operational excellence across a dynamic and growing business.

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Coordinating monthly performance and operational reporting activities, ensuring information is presented consistently and accurately.
  • Reviewing and challenging financial submissions to strengthen data integrity, assumptions and reporting quality.
  • Analysing profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow and working capital performance to identify trends, risks and opportunities.
  • Translating complex financial and operational data into clear, actionable insight for senior leaders.
  • Supporting budgeting, forecasting and long-term planning activities while driving reporting process improvements.

This is a full-time, 37.5 hours per week and is based at the Babcock Technology Centre, Bristol. Flexible working patterns are available, with Bristol as the primary location, although candidates can be based across the South or Midlands.

Essential experience of the Financial Planning and Reporting Analyst:

  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including Power Query and modern data analysis functionality.
  • Experience analysing, reconciling and interpreting large and complex datasets.
  • Experience in management reporting, financial analysis and financial modelling.
  • Ability to communicate financial and operational insights clearly to a range of stakeholders.
  • Working knowledge of financial accounting and reporting principles.

Qualifications for the Financial Planning and Reporting Analyst:

  • Part-qualified accountant, qualified by experience, or holding an equivalent finance, analytical or data-related qualification.

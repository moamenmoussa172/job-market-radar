Flight Test Engineer Intern

As part of the Flight Operations group within Bombardier Flight Test Center, based in Wichita, KS, Flight Test Engineers (FTEs) are a key part of our ability to test and certify cutting edge business aircraft. FTEs are a key part of our ability to test and certify aircraft, working in a focal role for all flight operations on test aircraft.

FTEs will fly on every test mission alongside Test Pilots to conduct testing, operate test equipment and record results. FTEs are typically assigned to a single program or Flight Test Vehicle, and will work as part of a team under a senior FTE.

FTEs will generally build familiarity with all testing disciplines required to certify Part 25 aircraft, as well as becoming knowledgeable with the design and operation of the aircraft which they are assigned to. FTEs are expected to be fast learners, and comfortable with operating in a dynamic, flexible and technically challenging environment, with high levels of autonomy and responsibility. The onboard environment can be physically challenging, with a requirement to operate under elevated g, low cabin pressures, turbulence and extreme temperatures.

FTEs work closely with all parts of the support team at BFTC and the wider Bombardier community, including engineering, maintenance, instrumentation, project management, controllers and others. Excellent communication skills are required, and FTEs will be expected to display leadership abilities in all activities.

This position requires regular onsite support, and full time remote work is not available.

What are your contributions to the team?

Daily duties include assisting with test planning, readiness, execution and reporting, including but not exclusive to the following:

  • Flying onboard experimental aircraft as a crewmember following appropriate training.
  • Operating all cabin experimental provisions and mission specific test instrumentation, while conducting tests and recording results.
  • Communication and coordination of efforts relating to testing activities with all other disciplines
  • Test documentation and reporting, including technical review and generation of test plans, instructions, test limitations and post flight reports
  • Coordination of test aircraft and experimental equipment configuration for test.
  • Design and development of safety critical real time data displays and derived parameters
  • Proper use of safety and risk mitigation procedures as defined by company policies, and compliance with FAA and Transport Canada Regulations

How to thrive in this role?

  • The candidate must be working towards a Bachelor’s Degree or higher in an engineering discipline.
  • Product specific development and training will be provided prior to participation in on aircraft ground and flight testing under supervision of a permanent employee.
  • Previous flying experience (pilot or other crew member) is a strong asset but is not considered essential.
  • Interpersonal skills necessary to work effectively with a variety of individuals, departments and organizations. Strong leadership qualities and communication skills, both verbal and written are an asset.
  • Demonstrated capacity to prioritize, manage and absorb large quantities of data and information.
  • Ability to work with a variety of IT tools and equipment, with experience in the uses of programming for automation and workload reduction an asset.

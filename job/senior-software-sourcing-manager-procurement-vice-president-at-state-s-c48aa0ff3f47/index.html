Senior Software Sourcing Manager (Procurement), Vice President

State Street · Ireland, United Kingdom

Onsite · Head / Director · Other tech role · Posted 17 November 2026

Apply on State Street's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Who we are looking for

The VP of Software Sourcing is a key leadership role within State Street’s Global Procurement Team, responsible for leading and driving the end-to-end sourcing and procurement process, including strategy development, analysis, negotiation, and finalization, with a focus on software and SaaS categories.

The role will lead and execute global, regional, and local software sourcing strategies, independently managing complex and strategic sourcing engagements while partnering with senior business, technology, legal, risk, and other key stakeholders to deliver commercial value, manage risk, and achieve business objectives.

The role will be performed in a hybrid model 4 days onsite 1 day remote out of London, UK or Dublin, Ireland.

Why this role is important to us

The team you will be joining plays an important role in the overall success of the organization. Across the globe, institutional investors rely on us to help them manage risk, respond to challenges, and drive performance and profitability. To make that happen we need people like you to help navigate employees and the organization as a whole. In your role you will strive for cutting-edge solutions, that are straightforward and scalable. You will help us build resilience and execute day to day deliverables at our best.

Join us if making your mark in the financial services industry from day one is a challenge you are up for.

What you will be responsible for

As Senior Software Sourcing Manager, VP you will:

· Perform direct negotiations and manage large and complex agreements

· Solution requests and issues timely, and perform daily problem solving

· Draft and negotiation Master License and Cloud agreements with support from Legal

· Negotiate IT security schedules with support from IT Security and Legal SMEs.

· Based in one of our key technology offices this role is to develop face-to-face stakeholder relationships with senior IT and business stakeholder in Boston, London or Dublin

· Develop vendor relationship with key Infrastructure, Cyber Security and other critical vendors

· Independently develop software sourcing strategies, leveraging relevant data and market insights to drive informed sourcing decisions. Develop pipeline of strategic initiatives in support of any agreed strategy.

· Identify risks and opportunities related to achieving financial objectives; work with process partners to mitigate risks and realize opportunities

· Heavily contribute to the development and delivery of savings pipeline for both cost reduction and cost avoidance savings for the team

· Analyze data to identify opportunities to reduce existing cost base

· Coordinate, prepare and manage RFx/competitive bids to optimize cost and service

· Perform all duties in accordance with prescribed regulatory compliance guidelines

· Quickly learn the State Street end to end process, and work within the guidelines to gain saving and delivery results

· Develop and mentor team members in our US, Poland and India locations

What we value

These skills will help you succeed in this role

· Strong commercial skills with ability to deliver cost effective outcomes

· Top level negotiation skills with ability to influence cross-functionally

· Proven self-starter who can work independently and within a team while effectively juggling multiple priorities

· Strong strategic thinker and problem solver with attention to detail

· Personal resilience to work within an extremely fast-paced, ambiguous environment

· Established written and verbal communication skills

· Ability to manage challenging stakeholders diplomatically

· Good presentation skills and a keen eye for detail a must

Required experience

· About 10+ strategic sourcing experience in IT related spend with referenceable knowledge in cloud software is a must

· Proven track record of delivering P&L saving projects

· A strong understanding of all facets of global IT vendors

· Understanding of best in class procurement, risk, Information Security and payable processes

· Ability to create software sourcing strategies based on a deep understanding of industry trends and wider technology change

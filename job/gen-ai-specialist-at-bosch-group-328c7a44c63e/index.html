About the job

Roles & Responsibilities:

  • Design, develop, test, and deploy machine learning models and generative AI solutions using state-of-the-art algorithms with a strong focus on large language models
  • Build end-to-end AI/ML/NLP solutions including conversational AI, document understanding, and question-answering systems
  • Develop and fine-tune language models (LLMs, SLMs) using frameworks like LlamaIndex, Langchain, and Autogen, with expertise in RAG, prompt engineering, and model optimization techniques
  • Mine and analyze data using statistical methods to identify critical product insights and customer patterns
  • Translate analytic insights into concrete, actionable recommendations for business and product improvement
  • Define, deploy, and mentor best practices in machine learning, MLOps, and LLMOps across teams
  • Lead projects independently, provide technical direction to team members, and champion effective techniques through development
  • Partner closely with product and engineering leaders throughout the project lifecycle
  • Communicate data-driven insights clearly and concisely to stakeholders at all organizational levels through effective storytelling and visualization

Experience Required:

5-8 years of experience as a Data Scientist or GenAI specialist, with 2-3 years specifically in generative AI solution development

**Must have Skills:**

  • 5-8 years of experience as a Data Scientist or GenAI specialist, with 2-3 years specifically in generative AI solution development
  • Proficiency in Python, TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, and NLP libraries (NLTK, SpaCy, Gensim)
  • Hands-on experience with open-source LLMs (Llama, Gemma, Mixtral) and closed-source models (GPT, Azure OpenAI, Claude, Gemini)
  • Strong understanding of vector databases (Pinecone, Qdrant) and cloud services (Azure, GCP, AWS)
  • Expertise in NLP algorithms for intent recognition, entity extraction, text classification, summarization, and question answering

**Good to Have skills*

  • Prior experience in Mobility or Healthcare domains
  • Foundational knowledge of cloud platforms, API frameworks (Flask, FastAPI), and tools like Swagger/Postman
  • Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or related field from tier 1 institutions

BE, B Tech or ME in (Computer Science / Machine Learning/ Mechanical, etc)

About the job

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior iOS Engineer to design and build scalable, secure features for a flagship wealth management app at one of the UAE's largest banks. You will own feature architecture end to end, set the bar for code quality and security, and work closely with Android, backend, design and product teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Architect and deliver scalable, modular features in Swift and SwiftUI
  • Apply and enforce mobile app security standards across the codebase (secure storage, data protection, jailbreak detection, secure networking)
  • Find and fix memory leaks, retain cycles and performance bottlenecks
  • Integrate Firebase services for authentication, real-time data and push notifications
  • Work with Android engineers to keep feature behaviour consistent across both platforms
  • Use AI-powered developer tools and support AI feature integration where relevant
  • Review code, mentor engineers and contribute to architecture decisions

Must-Have Skills

  • 7+ years of native iOS development, with expert-level Swift
  • Strong hands-on experience with SwiftUI in production apps
  • Solid understanding of MVVM architecture
  • Proven experience with modular app architecture (SPM or framework-based modularisation)
  • Strong command of memory management: ARC, retain cycles, Instruments (Leaks, Allocations)
  • Hands-on experience with Firebase (Auth, Firestore or Realtime DB, FCM)
  • Working familiarity with Kotlin and Android, and the ability to collaborate across platforms
  • Exposure to AI integration or AI-assisted development tools (Copilot, Cursor or similar)

Good to Have

  • Banking, wealth or fintech app experience
  • Knowledge of Combine, async/await and Swift Concurrency
  • Experience with CI/CD for mobile (Fastlane, Bitrise, Xcode Cloud)
  • Familiarity with Jetpack Compose

Qualifications

  • Degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
  • Portfolio of published native iOS apps on the App Store (links required)
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills
  • Clear communication for working across teams
  • Adaptable to changing tech stacks

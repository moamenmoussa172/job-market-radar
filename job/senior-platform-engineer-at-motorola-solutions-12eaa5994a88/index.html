Job Description

Job Description At Motorola Solutions, everything starts with our people. As a Senior Platform Engineer on our Alta Video Backend team, you will make a defining contribution to our award-winning next-generation security solutions. You will work in a human-centered, collaborative R&D environment where your creative abilities drive real-world impact. We empower Motorolans to do their best work, innovate what's next, and shape the future of safety and security. Qualified candidates may demonstrate success through a Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.

Key Responsibilities

Cloud Platform Architecture & Scaling

  • Develop and scale our cloud platform across R&D and production environments utilizing Google Cloud Platform and AWS
  • Drive cloud architecture development, adopting Go programming where applicable to enhance platform capabilities
  • Implement continuous integration and continuous delivery/deployment processes using GitHub Workflows/Actions or Jenkins

Infrastructure Operations & Resilience

  • Manage day-to-day operations across cloud and on-premises environments utilizing Linux, Terraform, Kubernetes, Helm, Prometheus, Grafana, and Bash
  • Champion chaos-engineering principles to build robust, resilient systems that withstand operational stress
  • Maintain high standards across day 0, day 1, and day 2 operational lifecycles, balancing feature implementation with airtight testing

Security & Continuous Improvement

  • Embed a security-first mindset across all platform engineering, deployment, and operational workflows
  • Identify, develop, and share innovative tools and processes to refine and streamline team operations
  • Collaborate dynamically with software development teams to shape and evolve our unified security solution


Basic Requirements

  • Solid experience in platform engineering, systems design, or cloud infrastructure operations
  • Excellent command of English

Job Requirements

Technical Expertise

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Tools to manage and scale Linux, AWS, and GCP environments using Terraform, Kubernetes, Helm, Prometheus, Grafana, and Bash
  • Security & Testing to embed a security-first mindset and establish comprehensive operational tests across the full system lifecycle
  • System Resilience to apply chaos-engineering principles and optimize long-term operational health
  • English Proficiency to communicate effectively, present technical solutions, and collaborate with global R&D teams

Professional Experience

  • Cross-Team Collaboration to partner with software development teams in shaping scalable cloud systems
  • Process Improvement to evaluate existing workflows, suggest short- and long-term improvements, and implement modern tooling
  • Independent Execution to drive complex engineering projects from inception to completion

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Experience working within an AWS FedRAMP environment or AWS GovCloud
  • Hands-on programming experience with Go
  • Practical knowledge of CI/CD pipeline automation tools such as GitHub Actions or Jenkins

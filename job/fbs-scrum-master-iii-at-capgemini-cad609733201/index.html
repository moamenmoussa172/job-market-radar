About the job

Consistently models the advanced qualities of a servant leader and exhibits the behaviors of a Lean-Agile Leader with a Lean-Agile Mindset. Trains and coaches Agile teams and Stakeholders to embrace SAFe Core Values, adopt and apply SAFe Principles, and implement SAFe practices. Effectively removes impediments and actively communicates with leadership and outside stakeholders to protect the team from uncontrolled expansion of work while improving performance in the areas of quality, predictability, flow and velocity.

Requirements

Experience- 9 years Plus

Certification- Certified Advanced SAFe Scrum Master certification, Certified SAFe Practitioner or Agilist certification

  • Acts as a servant leader for multiple teams and their stakeholders. Aids the team and Product Owner in efforts to manage and sequence the backlog.
  • Leads more complex teams involving work with routine external team impact or dependencies including multiple system integration points.
  • Eliminates impediments and resolve issues and dependencies more independently with limited support from Release Train Engineer or other resources.
  • Assists in building a high-performing team and foster relentless improvement at the Team and Program level while improving team performance in areas of quality, predictability, flow and velocity.
  • Identifies SAFe anti-patterns and resolves with limited support and applies SAFe principles to facilitation, enablement, and coaching for multiple teams.
  • Utilizes intermediate to advanced experience using tools to create metrics that measure and report on team performance, backlog health and continuous improvement.
  • Utilizes advanced coaching capabilities to influence teams and advise on practices and procedures, soft-skills and Lean Agile mind-set.
  • With limited support from RTE and others, guides teams through the decision making process and teach techniques on how to problem solve, improve communication, self-manage and self-organize.
  • With limited support from RTE and others, Scrum Master supports a trusting and safe environment where problems can be raised. Helps manage interpersonal conflicts, challenges and opportunities for growth within the team.
  • Applies SAFe principles to facilitation, enablement, and coaching in a multi-team environment. Mentors and guides other scrum masters on SAFe principles and manifesto.
  • Organizes and leads a Community of Practice, and protects the team from uncontrolled expansion of work.
  • Leads problem solving techniques and continuously challenges norms to improve performance in quality, predictability, flow and velocity.
  • Independently participates, facilitates and manages the outputs of all agile ceremonies including Daily Stand Up meetings,
  • Sprint review, sprint retrospective, sprint planning, backlog refinement sessions, prepare for Program Increment Planning Sessions.
  • Participates in the Scrum Master Communities of Practice, Scrum Mastery Sync Sessions and Agile Release Train Sync Sessions.
  • Provides advanced mentorship to other scrum masters through Agile ceremonies. Provides back up support to RTE when needed. Trains and coaches
  • Agile teams and stakeholders to embrace SAFe Core Values, adopt and apply SAFe Principles, and implement SAFe practices.
  • Demonstrates ongoing thirst for knowledge and a desire for continuous improvement by creating an environment for continuous learning and builds improvement roadmaps for team(s).
  • Pursues continuous education with regards to everything Agile (e.g. visit user groups, attend conferences, read books, write blogs, etc.).
  • Continuously works with the Scrum Master Community of Practice to ensure standard procedures are in place and ultimately improve the organizations' product delivery capability.
  • Seeks advice and works occasionally with the Release Train Engineer to establish best practices and resolve challenging issues, risks, conflicts or impediments.

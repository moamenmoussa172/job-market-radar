Customer Success Manager, Strategic

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the team and role

Ramp’s Strategic Lifecycle Success team helps our largest and most complex customers turn their investment in Ramp into lasting business impact. After initial onboarding and implementation are complete, we help customers deepen adoption, continuously improve their Ramp setup, and successfully launch and activate additional products as their needs evolve.

As a Strategic Lifecycle Customer Success Manager, you’ll be a hands-on product expert and trusted partner to the finance, accounting, and operations teams using Ramp. You’ll own the ongoing customer success plan, proactively improve workflows, and lead onboarding and implementation when existing customers adopt new Ramp products. You’ll also lead adoption and value-focused business reviews, connecting how customers use Ramp to the outcomes that matter to their business.

You’ll work alongside an Account Manager, who owns commercial strategy and executive relationships, and partner with Solutions Consultants and Technical Consultants on complex technical needs. Your focus is helping customers get more from Ramp over time—from optimizing what’s already live to making each new product rollout successful.

What you’ll do

  • Own ongoing success planning for a portfolio of customers. After a structured handoff from initial onboarding and implementation, partner with a dedicated Account Manager to align on customer goals and define clear milestones, owners, and measures of success.
  • Serve as a trusted Ramp product expert. Stay current on new products, capabilities, and best practices, and proactively translate relevant releases into recommendations for each customer.
  • Continuously enhance customers’ Ramp environments. Understand their configuration and business processes, identify opportunities to improve policies, controls, approval flows, and workflows, and work hands-on with customers to put those improvements into practice.
  • Lead new-product implementations for existing customers. Own discovery, planning, configuration, training, launch, and sustained adoption, aligning customer goals, owners, and milestones. Lead standard workflow improvements; partner with Solutions Consultants on complex design and validation, Technical Consultants on integrations, APIs, ERP connections, and data flows, and Support on troubleshooting and defect escalation. Specialists own diagnosis and execution; you own customer communication, cross-functional follow-through, and validation of outcomes.
  • Lead adoption and value-focused business reviews. Define success measures and establish baselines with customers, then track improvements in adoption, spend consolidation, operational efficiency, and controls to demonstrate the value of their Ramp investment. Run regular adoption reviews with day-to-day stakeholders and partner with the Account Manager on executive QBRs and EBRs. Bring in the Account Manager or Solutions Consultant when useful, and coordinate joint reviews for Tier 1 accounts. Use evidence of progress to agree on next priorities.
  • Build customer champions and drive change. Develop a network of champions across finance, accounting, AP, operations, and business units. Create enablement and change-management plans, including train-the-trainer approaches where useful, that equip customer teams to adopt new capabilities and sustain new ways of working beyond individual stakeholders.
  • Manage customer health proactively. Monitor adoption, engagement, outcomes, and delivery risks; identify barriers early; and lead action plans to improve customer health. Equip your Account Manager with value evidence for renewal and growth conversations.
  • Identify customer success qualified leads (CSQLs). Recognize growth signals through customer relationships, product usage, and evolving business needs, and qualify and proactively surface expansion opportunities to your Account Manager with clear customer context, value evidence, and recommended next steps. The Account Manager owns commercial strategy and execution.
  • Shape a repeatable lifecycle success motion. Partner with Solutions Consultants, Technical Consultants, Support, and Product to resolve blockers and build playbooks for ongoing optimization and new-product activation. Translate recurring customer needs and workflow gaps into actionable feedback for Product, and close the loop with customers on relevant developments without making unconfirmed roadmap commitments.

What you need

  • A track record in enterprise customer success, including hands-on ownership of implementation, adoption, and ongoing value realization for complex customers.
  • Strong product aptitude and experience configuring and optimizing software to support customer business processes—not just advising from a distance.
  • Experience managing customer-facing implementations, from discovery and project planning through training, launch, and sustained adoption.
  • The ability to lead business reviews that connect product usage to financial and operational outcomes, such as stronger controls, more efficient workflows, greater visibility, and time saved.
  • Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional execution skills, including experience navigating large, matrixed organizations, aligning stakeholders with competing priorities, and prioritizing multiple concurrent workstreams. Comfortable discussing integrations, ERPs, and workflow design and knowing when to engage technical specialists.
  • Practical AI fluency: experience using AI tools to synthesize account information, prepare customer materials, and improve your work, with sound judgment about accuracy and appropriate use.

Nice to have

  • Experience with finance operations, accounting systems, or procure-to-pay workflows, including accounts payable, procurement, expense management, or ERP platforms.
  • Experience building customer success and implementation playbooks in a high-growth environment.

About the job

The Subscriptions Mission builds and scales the products, platforms, and business models that turn free listeners into lifelong Premium subscribers. We focus on attracting new subscribers, improving conversion and retention, and creating seamless commerce experiences that support Spotify's growth and bring fans closer to the creators they love.

At Spotify, subscription data helps shape the decisions and experiences behind our 700M+ monthly active users and 290M+ Premium subscribers. You'll join a collaborative team building the backend systems that power merchandising, personalized experiences, and subscription journeys at a global scale. Your work will help listeners discover the value of Premium and give them more reasons to stay.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable Java services that power Spotify's merchandising platform and help listeners discover and upgrade to Premium.
  • Work with your team to develop backend systems, APIs, and data models using Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Partner with product managers, data scientists, and engineers to turn complex challenges into practical, scalable solutions.
  • Build and improve data pipelines that support experimentation, recommendation logic, and machine learning applications.
  • Shape technical decisions that improve system reliability, performance, and maintainability as our platform grows.
  • Take ownership of production services, using monitoring, observability, and continuous improvement to deliver reliable experiences.
  • Share knowledge through code reviews, technical discussions, and collaboration to help your team learn and grow.
  • Who You Are
  • You have 5+ years of experience developing backend services with Java.
  • You have a solid understanding of computer science fundamentals and experience building distributed systems at scale.
  • You're experienced in designing, deploying, and maintaining cloud-based services using platforms such as GCP or AWS.
  • You know how to design APIs and work with partners to turn product needs into reliable technical solutions.
  • You understand what it takes to operate high-traffic services, including monitoring, performance optimization, and reliability.
  • You enjoy collaborating across disciplines, sharing ideas, and taking ownership of projects from design through delivery.
  • You value feedback, continuous learning, and finding better ways to build systems and work together.
  • You're familiar with technologies such as gRPC, BigQuery, Bigtable, content management system integrations, or machine learning applications.
  • Where You'll Be
  • This role is based in London, UK.
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.

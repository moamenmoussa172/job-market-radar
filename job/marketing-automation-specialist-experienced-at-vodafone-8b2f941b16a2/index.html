About this role

We are seeking an Experienced Marketing Automation Specialist in Hungary to plan, create and execute integrated digital marketing campaigns using marketing automation tools. You will work across email, webinars, landing pages and lead-nurturing programmes, using segmentation, testing, reporting and campaign insights to improve effectiveness and data quality. The role combines hands-on campaign delivery with analytical thinking, cross-functional collaboration and continuous optimisation.

What you’ll do

  • Support the design of integrated digital campaigns across email, webinars and landing pages.
  • Independently create and execute digital campaigns and lead-nurturing programmes using marketing automation tools.
  • Produce regular activity reports covering digital customer touchpoints, improve reporting and keep key stakeholders informed.
  • Create segmentation filters, groups and distribution lists to support campaigns, lead scoring and nurturing programmes while reducing irrelevant leads, contact fatigue and unsubscribe rates.
  • Use testing and marketing intelligence to continuously optimise campaign effectiveness.
  • Contribute to campaign planning and take ownership of campaign delivery, applying judgement and problem-solving skills during execution.

Who you are

  • You have 3–5 years of marketing experience, including creating and delivering digital campaigns through marketing automation platforms such as Oracle Eloqua, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Salesforce Pardot or Adobe Marketo, or comparable tools.
  • You bring 3–5 years of marketing automation experience.
  • You have strong reporting and analytical capabilities and can take a systems-thinking approach.
  • You communicate effectively and are fluent in English.
  • You have experience in customer relationship management, problem-solving, organisation, prioritisation and deadline management.
  • You hold a BA degree in Marketing, Communications or a related field.
  • You have excellent working knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

  • Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams and stakeholders while taking ownership of campaign execution.
  • The opportunity to contribute ideas and continuously improve digital campaign effectiveness and customer engagement.

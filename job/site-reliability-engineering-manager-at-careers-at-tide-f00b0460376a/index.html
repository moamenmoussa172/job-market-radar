ABOUT THE TEAM:
The SRE team is part of Platform Engineering. Its mission is to own the reliability system: the standards, tooling, and platform services that determine how reliable Tide is by default. We measure success by SLO attainment and the reliability our members experience.
ABOUT THE ROLE:
Tide is hiring an Engineering Manager to lead the SRE team that owns our reliability system.
As an Engineering Manager, SRE, you will:

  • Lead: Hire, coach, and set clear goals for the SRE team.
  • Define SLOs: Agree SLOs with service owners and run the error budget policy.
  • Own observability: Run the observability platform 24/7 in production, including SLOs and platform alerting.
  • Release safely: Build deployment guardrails such as canary rollouts and auto-rollback.
  • Build resilience: Detect partner degradation early with synthetic monitoring and vendor scorecards.
  • Remove toil: Automate manual incident work, such as recording incident timelines and drafting status updates.
  • Fix systemically: Turn post-mortem findings and incident patterns into cross-cutting platform fixes.
  • Measure outcomes: Track SLO attainment and member-facing reliability metrics.
  • Support: Advise product engineering teams on reliability.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

  • An Engineering Leader with a strong engineering mindset who simplifies problems and looks for optimal solutions
  • A Professional who wants to own real systems in production and be measured on outcomes
  • 10+ years in software engineering or infrastructure engineering roles
  • 3+ years in engineering leadership roles
  • Strong knowledge of SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, and error budgets
  • Hands-on experience instrumenting Java, Python, and Go services with OpenTelemetry
  • Experience using Terraform
  • Working knowledge of Kubernetes, AWS, and Java
  • Experience with canary rollouts, auto-rollback, and auto-scaling
  • Knowledge of reliability patterns such as retries, backoffs, DLQs, the transactional outbox, and feature flags
  • The ability to plan, prioritise, and manage expectations, particularly during incidents and retrospectives
  • Experience running blameless retrospectives that uncover the root cause of complex issues

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Arab Emirates.

We’re looking for a Product Manager to join our EasyVideo team and shape the future of AI-powered video creation for workplace learning.

EasyVideo turns presentations, existing content, and ideas into professional training videos in minutes, with AI-powered creation, intuitive editing, localization, and interactive learning.

This isn’t a role where you manage a backlog or execute a handed-down roadmap. We want someone who takes ownership, understands customer problems deeply, challenges assumptions, and cares about great user experiences. AI is central to where EasyVideo is going, so you’ll actively experiment with AI tools and tell real product value from hype.

You’ll own a meaningful product area, working closely with Engineering, Design, Analytics, and other teams.

What you’ll do

  • Own a product area: drive initiatives from discovery through delivery and iteration, and own the outcomes
  • Drive discovery: understand customers, their workflows, and unmet needs through research, data, and market insights
  • Shape direction: turn customer problems and strategy into clear priorities, hypotheses, and solutions
  • Build AI-powered experiences: find where AI simplifies workflows and creates real value, working with Engineering and Design
  • Deliver great UX: make smart scope and prioritization trade-offs to ship intuitive, high-quality solutions
  • Measure and improve: define success metrics and use insights to keep improving
  • Partner across the business: align with Product Marketing, Customer Success, Sales, and others

What we’re looking for

Must-have

  • 3+ years of product management experience, ideally in B2B SaaS (we’re open to exceptional candidates with less who show strong ownership)
  • Experience taking initiatives from discovery to launch and iteration, working closely with Engineering and Design
  • Strong product judgment: you frame problems, challenge assumptions, and make thoughtful trade-offs
  • Strong UX instincts and attention to detail
  • Analytical thinking: you combine customer insight, data, and business context
  • An ownership-driven mindset: you work independently and navigate ambiguity
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, and a willingness to learn and seek feedback
  • Hands-on experience with AI tools and a pragmatic approach to applying AI to real customer problems

Nice to have

  • Experience with AI-powered products, creative tools, video editing, or content creation platforms
  • Experience with enterprise SaaS or complex, collaborative workflows
  • Familiarity with EdTech, learning and development, or workplace training

What we offer

  • A high-impact role at a fast-growing EdTech scale-up
  • The chance to build AI-first products shaping workplace learning
  • Real ownership, autonomy, and growth alongside experienced product leadership
  • A multicultural team across multiple countries
  • Room to grow as we scale

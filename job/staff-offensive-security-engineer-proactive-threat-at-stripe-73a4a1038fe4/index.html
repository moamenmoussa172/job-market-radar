About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

The Proactive Threat team finds vulnerabilities and security weaknesses across Stripe's systems, applications, networks, and cloud infrastructure before adversaries do. We're a hybrid offensive function. We run penetration tests, emulate real-world threat actors in red team operations, and work closely with our defensive security teams to validate detection capabilities and improve Stripe's overall security posture.

We are builders first. We build custom tooling, automation frameworks, and internal platforms that scale our offensive work and make our assessments repeatable and high-fidelity. Increasingly, that includes AI agents that find and validate vulnerabilities at a scale no human team could reach. We believe the best offensive security engineers are equal parts hacker and engineer.

The team is distributed and works regularly with security, engineering, and product stakeholders across Stripe, including teams in Europe and Asia.

What you'll do

As a Staff Offensive Security Engineer on the Proactive Threat team, you'll set the technical direction for how Stripe tests itself the way a real adversary would. You'll be the team's most senior offensive practitioner. You'll stay deeply hands-on: you'll lead the hardest engagements yourself and write the tooling that makes everyone around you more effective.

You'll own the strategy and the operating foundation of our red team program, including its tradecraft, infrastructure, rules of engagement, and coordination with detection and response. You'll shape how offensive security scales at Stripe through automation and agentic systems. Your work will span multiple teams: you'll partner with detection engineering, incident response, vulnerability management, and product engineering so that what we find makes Stripe measurably harder to attack.

Responsibilities

Technical strategy and leadership

  • Set the multi-quarter technical strategy for offensive security at Stripe: where we test, how deep we go, and which threat actors and attack paths matter most to a financial infrastructure company
  • Define and own the red team program's operating foundation: rules of engagement, opsec standards, C2 and payload infrastructure, IR deconfliction, and repeatable engagement playbooks
  • Be the prime mover on large, ambiguous offensive initiatives that span multiple teams, from framing the problem to landing the outcome
  • Make fast, well-reasoned calls on scope, risk, and tradecraft, and explain them clearly to engineers, security leaders, and executives

Hands-on offensive work

  • Lead the team's most complex engagements yourself: objective-based red team operations, assumed-breach exercises, and deep assessments of critical payment, money-movement, cloud, CI/CD, and corporate systems
  • Emulate the TTPs of sophisticated cyber and criminal threat actors targeting financial services, including initial access, lateral movement, persistence, and data exfiltration
  • Find novel vulnerability classes and attack chains specific to Stripe's architecture, and turn them into systemic fixes rather than one-off findings

Scaling through engineering and automation

  • Design and build the offensive platforms, tooling, and agentic workflows that encode the team's expertise into repeatable, scalable systems
  • Apply LLMs and autonomous agents to reconnaissance, vulnerability discovery, exploit validation, and reporting, and set the bar for running them safely and reliably
  • Raise the engineering quality of the team's codebases through design reviews, code reviews, and example

Cross-team impact

  • Partner with detection engineering, threat intelligence, and incident response to run purple team operations, close detection gaps, and measure improvement over time
  • Work with vulnerability management and product engineering teams so findings lead to durable, systemic remediation
  • Help manage external offensive vendors and partners by setting scope, holding them to a quality bar, and integrating their results with our own work
  • Act as the subject-matter expert on adversary behavior for Stripe-wide security initiatives, incident investigations, and leadership decisions

Multiplying the team

  • Mentor and grow offensive security engineers at all levels, and raise the team's technical bar through pairing, design review, and knowledge sharing
  • Shape hiring for the team, including interview design and calibration
  • Represent Stripe in the broader security community through research, talks, and open-source contributions where appropriate

Who you are

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 8+ years of experience in offensive security, penetration testing, red teaming, or a related field, including significant time as a technical lead on complex engagements
  • A track record of setting technical direction for an offensive security program or capability, with impact across multiple teams
  • Strong software engineering skills in Python, Go, or similar languages, with a history of designing and shipping production-quality offensive tools, platforms, or automation that others rely on
  • Expert knowledge of adversary tradecraft and frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK, and experience planning and running full-scope red team operations, including C2 infrastructure, opsec, and deconfliction with defenders
  • Deep, hands-on expertise in at least two of the following: cloud (AWS, GCP, or Azure) attack techniques, web application and API security, identity and corporate infrastructure (e.g., Active Directory, SSO, endpoint), and CI/CD and supply chain attacks
  • Experience working with detection and response teams to turn offensive findings into measurable improvements in detection and response
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to explain complex attack paths as clear, risk-based recommendations to engineers and executives
  • Experience mentoring other engineers and raising a team's technical bar

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience leading offensive security in fintech, financial services, or other highly regulated environments, including payment and money-movement systems
  • Experience building or running a red team program from an early stage, including its operating procedures and infrastructure
  • Background in vulnerability research, exploit development, or CVE discovery
  • Hands-on experience building agentic or LLM-driven systems for security work, such as autonomous vulnerability discovery, exploit validation, or attack-path analysis
  • Experience testing AI/ML systems and LLM-based applications for security weaknesses (prompt injection, tool and agent abuse, data extraction, model manipulation, etc.)
  • Experience scoping and managing third-party penetration testing or red team vendors
  • Familiarity with big data and log analysis tools (Splunk, Databricks, PySpark, osquery, etc.) for threat hunting or investigative support
  • Published research, conference talks, or contributions to open-source security tools
  • Relevant certifications such as OSCP, OSWE, OSEP, OSED, OSEE, CRTO, CRTL, GXPN, or cloud security certifications

Location

This role is remote within the United States. While you are welcome to visit Stripe offices for team meetings, on-sites, and events, our expectation is that you would regularly work from home. The team works across US and European time zones, with regular collaboration with stakeholders in Asia.

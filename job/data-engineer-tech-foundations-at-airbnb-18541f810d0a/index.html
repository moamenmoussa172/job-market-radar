About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

Data Engineer, Enterprise Core Datasets, Tech Foundations

The Community You Will Join

At Airbnb, trustworthy data powers business decisions, product innovation, and operational efficiency. The Enterprise Core Datasets team builds the technical data foundations that help Airbnb understand how the business operates, where resources are invested, and how new technologies create value.

Our scope spans high-impact datasets across the organization: measuring AI adoption, usage, and cost; understanding revenue, costs, and profitability through Unit Economics; integrating non-platform data from Jira and other enterprise applications; and powering critical company metrics such as Gross Booking Value and Revenue.

You will join a team that values thoughtful architecture, engineering rigor, and operational ownership. We collaborate with engineering, product, analytics, finance, and business teams to turn complex requirements into dependable, reusable data assets. We welcome engineers who are curious about the business and eager to learn and solve problems across diverse domains.

The Difference You Will Make

As a Data Engineer, you will build and operate datasets that shape decisions across Airbnb. Your work will help the organization measure AI adoption and spending, understand the economics of its products, and connect enterprise application data to meaningful business and operational insights.

You will deliver well-scoped data engineering projects, from requirements through design, implementation, validation, and production operations. You will work closely with teammates and stakeholders, with guidance from senior engineers on larger or more ambiguous problems. You will build maintainable data models and pipelines that accommodate new AI tools, products, cost categories, and enterprise systems while preserving clear definitions, traceability, and reliable historical data.

You will work in an ecosystem that includes petabyte-scale datasets, and you will help improve correctness, freshness, performance, and cost efficiency. You will contribute reusable components that simplify source onboarding and reduce duplicated logic. You will grow your craft through design reviews, code reviews, and mentorship from senior teammates.

A Typical Day

  • Build, test, and operate data pipelines across AI usage and cost, Unit Economics, enterprise applications, and critical financial metrics.
  • Work with stakeholders and senior teammates to understand how AI adoption, consumption, and spending are measured, and implement data models that follow those definitions.
  • Extend Unit Economics datasets to connect revenue and diverse cost drivers.
  • Build ingestion and transformation pipelines for non-platform systems such as Jira and other BizTech enterprise applications.
  • Turn defined requirements into source contracts, reusable fact and dimension models, and technical designs for your projects, and get design feedback early.
  • Contribute to configurable pipelines and shared components that simplify onboarding new data sources and products.
  • Implement automated tests, reconciliation, and monitoring to protect data accuracy, completeness, and availability.
  • Support migrations and historical backfills by following dependency analysis, validation, and cutover plans.
  • Identify and fix performance and cost issues in distributed processing, storage, and queries.
  • Take part in the on-call rotation, respond to incidents, and improve runbooks for the datasets you own.
  • Participate in design and code reviews, share what you learn, and help improve Airbnb's Data Engineering Paved Path.

Your Expertise

  • 3+ years of relevant industry experience, with a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • A track record of delivering data engineering projects end to end, with increasing independence.
  • Strong SQL skills and programming skills in Python or Scala, with experience writing maintainable, well-tested code.
  • Hands-on experience with distributed processing such as Apache Spark, and familiarity with large-scale analytical storage systems.
  • Solid data modeling fundamentals, including fact and dimension design, slowly changing dimensions, incremental processing, and historical data management.
  • Experience working with varied data sources, such as APIs, enterprise applications, event data, and transactional systems, including handling schema changes.
  • Experience building reliable pipelines, and the ability to support migrations and backfills while preserving correctness and downstream compatibility.
  • Working knowledge of data quality checks, observability, lineage, and governance, and the ability to troubleshoot production data issues.
  • Ability to find and fix common performance bottlenecks in distributed computation, storage, and query execution.
  • Clear communication and collaboration skills, including asking clarifying questions of business partners and explaining your technical choices.
  • Willingness to give and receive feedback in design and code reviews, and to help onboard teammates.
  • Curiosity and sound judgment when learning new domains.
  • Experience with AI usage or cost measurement, cloud cost attribution, Unit Economics, enterprise application data, or financial and payments datasets is a plus. Familiarity with workflow orchestration, SQL transformation frameworks, and modern table formats such as Apache Iceberg is also valuable.

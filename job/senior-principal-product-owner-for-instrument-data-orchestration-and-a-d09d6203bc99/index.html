About the job

The Product Owner is accountable for a defined product or product area within Instrument Data Capture & Orchestration in Labs & CMC Tech — solutions that enable the capture, orchestration, integration and use of laboratory instrument data — translating the team's product vision into clear goals and working day to day with scientific and business stakeholders and the delivery team to build and prioritise a backlog that delivers real scientific and business value.

The Product Owner is building the foundations of trusted product leadership — developing domain knowledge, technical fluency and stakeholder relationships that will underpin their growth into more senior Product Owner roles.

The role operates within a multinational, matrix organisation, working across multiple products and initiatives and partnering with scientific, business, technology and external stakeholders.

Key responsibilities:

  • Backlog & prioritisation: Contributes team-level input to integrated roadmaps showing dependencies and value flows, aligning scientific, business and technology priorities, and keeps a well-ordered backlog linked to outcomes.
  • Opportunity identification: Helps identify and prioritise opportunities on scientific value, user impact, feasibility and strategic alignment, flagging dependencies and trade-offs early.
  • Delivery & value: Owns outcomes for their product area from discovery through adoption and continuous improvement, writing testable acceptance criteria and flagging slippage, dependencies and risks early. Defines measurable outcomes and demonstrates value and adoption, not just delivery, asking “what problem are we solving?” before “what should we build?”
  • Domain & user understanding: Builds foundational understanding of the domain and the real needs behind requirements, shadowing end users to learn laboratory instrument workflows and data.
  • Technology: Builds working knowledge of systems, data and architecture (connectivity, orchestration), contributes to evolving orchestration and instrument-data capabilities for scalable, AI-ready data, and knows when to involve engineering.
  • Business Intelligence & analytics: Supports BI and advanced analytics on instrument and equipment data to improve insights and decisions.
  • Quality, risk & compliance: Safeguards data integrity, maintains systems of record, follows applicable GxP/privacy/quality requirements, flags risks early, and supports responsible data and AI adoption, including human oversight.
  • Stakeholders & partnership: Builds trusted relationships with scientific, business, technology, Data and AI stakeholders, communicating priorities, investment choices, risks and trade-offs clearly. Contributes to discussions on digital, data, automation and AI opportunities, partners with Engineering and Architecture on measurable outcomes, helps stakeholders understand AI’s potential and limits, and supports alignment across functions and geographies.
  • Cross-product contribution: Identifies opportunities to simplify, reuse and standardise capabilities across laboratories, favouring reusable data, orchestration and AI over point solutions, coordinates priorities and dependencies with neighbouring Product Owners and delivery teams, shares best practice, and gives input to portfolio priorities.
  • AI-enabled ways of working: Uses AI tools to prototype, draft and synthesise early work and explore instrument data, following GSK’s responsible-AI guidance and validating outputs.
  • AI opportunities: Helps identify AI-enabled opportunities (including Generative and Agentic AI, assistants, automation) that simplify scientific workflows and how scientists use instrument data, and contributes to an AI opportunity pipeline that tests where AI beats traditional solutions before scaling.
  • AI adoption & governance: Drives adoption of frontier AI practices in their product area, applying measurable quality, cost and performance standards to vendors and partners through

Why You?
Basic Qualification
We are seeking professionals with the following required skills and qualifications to help us achieve our goals:

  • Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) with some relevant technology/product experience, ideally within a large pharmaceutical or similarly regulated organisation.
  • Exposure to laboratory instrument data, orchestration or data & analytics / BI, and to the Labs & CMC (manufacturing, quality or laboratory science) domain, processes or regulatory environment.
  • Experience in product or project delivery, including backlog management and stakeholder engagement.
  • Experience using AI tools to support day-to-day product work, such as analysing or summarising instrument data.

Preferred Qualification
If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Good facilitation and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both business and technical stakeholders.
  • A collaborative, curious approach with a genuine interest in using technology to improve outcomes for patients
  • Familiarity with Agile ways of working.

Working arrangement
This role is hybrid role. You will spend scheduled time on-site for collaboration and stakeholder engagement. Remote work is supported as part of a hybrid arrangement.

About the job

The Opportunity

We have a fantastic opportunity for an Engineering Manager to join our GenStudio team based in Bucharest.

We are looking for an exceptional technology leader to build, nurture and grow a highly motivated agile team working on the best in class solution for digital marketing multi-channel content management systems driving a billion-dollar revenue.

Your team will be responsible for developing the front-end user experiences while ensuring the back-end services are built for performance and reliability. You will partner with teams across the Marketing Cloud/ Adobe Experience Cloud and deploy these technologies across Web and Desktop.

Adobe GenStudio is a generative AI-first product that lets marketing teams quickly plan, build, manage, activate and measure on-brand content. It allows any team member to quickly find and generate assets, create variations, and optimize experiences based on real-time content performance insights.

What you'll do

  • Hire and directly lead world class engineers while being responsible for driving all aspects of implementation, quality assurance, continuous integration and deployment, and application monitoring.
  • Responsible to build and maintain fault tolerant and highly available (99.99%) web applications and the services hydrating its data.
  • Build and instill a team culture that focuses on engineering excellence, encourages deep engagement with our customers and out of the box thinking.
  • Mentor technical leaders and high bars for excellence in architecture and code design.
  • Participate in the design and review of features, technology choices, architecture, designs, time and budget estimates, and test strategies.
  • Handle project scope and risks with data, analytics and creative problem solving.
  • Develop and evolve engineering processes and collaboration models to optimize team efficiency and teamwork.
  • Work closely with teams and managers across Adobe, in different geographies.

What you need to succeed

  • B.S or M.S. degree in Computer Sciences, Engineering or equivalent.
  • 10+ years of software development experience.
  • 3+ years of people management experience.
  • Proven people management skills in leading teams of 8 or more and projects of 6 dev/months or more.
  • Experience with SaaS products.
  • Web development know-how. Knowledge of ETL pipelines.
  • Ability to quickly prioritize and an acute sense of urgency.
  • Ability to understand business context and requirements, strong customer focus.
  • Passion for software development and knowledge about software development methodologies.
  • Able to motivate, inspire, and grow team members.
  • Strong ambition and self-drive.
  • Top-notch communication skills, able to be credible and convincing in a simple phone conversation in English, able to deliver short but high-impact presentations and pitches.
  • Fluent in English (both written and spoken).

Additional

  • Experience managing software development using modern engineering practices that incorporate AI-assisted coding, code review, testing, and documentation.
  • Familiarity with AI development workflows, including prompt engineering, evaluation, testing, and integration of AI services into software applications.
  • Ability to assess technical tradeoffs related to AI adoption, including security, privacy, governance, and responsible AI considerations.

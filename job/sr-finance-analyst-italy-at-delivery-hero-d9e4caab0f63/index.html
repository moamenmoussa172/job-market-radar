About the job

Job Ref ID: JR0089175

Your mission
Based in Milan, you'll partner with Italy's Finance Lead to keep the country's financial plan sharp and current. From day one, you'll own the entire P&L in terms of forecasting and controlling, aiming at developing improved processes for all the lines impacting the economical performances.

The journey

  • P&L MEC & Best Estimate: own the country's P&L forecasting and controlling cadence end to end. Lead the monthly Best Estimate (BE) across all revenue and cost lines, support Accounting during Month-End Closing (MEC) on cut-offs and accruals, and contribute directly to the Annual Business Plan (BP).
  • Cross-functional POC: act as the strategic finance copilot for local leadership. Partner closely with the Commercial team to control major cost and revenue lines—such as Preferred Partnerships investments—and team up with Operations to dissect Riders’ Cost Per Order (CPO).
  • Process optimization: redesign controlling and forecasting workflows so that business leaders and finance speak the exact same language, eliminating data discrepancies and driving cohesive decision-making.
  • Tooling: build and maintain the dashboards able to ease finance workstreams. Daily use of AI tools (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, or similar) is required; not just to speed up analysis, but to build clearer frameworks and move faster.

What you'll bring

  • Strategic and hands-on: You take ownership; not just analysis, but making things move forward and land. You challenge assumptions, drive actions, and measure yourself by real-world impact.
  • Financial modeling: Comfortable building and maintaining forecast models from scratch; experience with cash flow, cost, or P&L modeling is a must.
  • Problem solving: Break issues into components, define hypotheses, run analysis, and translate findings into practical recommendations. Own the process end-to-end.
  • Curiosity & learning agility: Ask sharp questions, absorb feedback quickly, and grow fast.
  • Prioritization & ownership: Own your work streams end-to-end, from analysis to follow-through, and hold yourself accountable to outcomes, not just outputs.
  • Communication: Clear, structured storytelling, decision-ready one-pagers and crisp live readouts.
  • Collaboration: You elevate teammates, share context, and document logic and assumptions for repeatability.
  • Experience: 2–4 years in FP&A, strategy, controlling, consulting, or a fast-paced analytical role. Degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or related.
  • Tools: Gsheet, Excel, BigQuery/Looker and daily AI tool use required; SAP, or Business Central — nice-to-have.
  • Language: English required.

What you'll get

  • Velocity: High-pace environment with ownership from day one; your work has immediate visibility and impact.
  • Fast learning curve: Exposure to finance, strategy, and operations, with regular touchpoints across regional finance and beyond.
  • Stretch challenges: You'll operate outside your comfort zone regularly; that's by design, and it's how you grow fastest.
  • Coaching and mentorship: Direct access to the Finance Lead, invested in your development, not just your output.
  • Talent density: A team that thinks rigorously, moves fast, and raises the bar.
  • A lot of gas: The data, cross-functional access, and organizational reach to make things happen for real.

About the job

We are looking for a Staff Quality Engineer to build Data Quality Management for inDrive's data business end to end. Our users complete paid tasks in the app, and the audio, video and photo
data they create is prepared and licensed to AI labs. You will own the quality bar for every dataset we deliver, whether it is collected inside our own vertical or supplied by an external partner.
The role sits between what a customer contract promises and what the data actually is. You will work with the business lead and customers to turn requirements into measurable specifications, and with engineering, operations and partners to prove that each delivery meets them: complete, authentic and fit for training.
This is a deeply technical role. You will spend your time designing quality pipelines, analysing data, auditing deliveries and making accept-or-reject decisions, not only writing guidelines. We are
looking for someone who has built data quality systems for ML training data and can hold their own with ML engineers and with customers' research teams.
You will join the engineering team of the data vertical and set the quality standards that our internal teams and external data partners work to.

  • Building the Data Quality Management framework for audio, video and photo data: quality dimensions, metrics, thresholds and acceptance criteria per data type
  • Designing automated and human quality gates with engineering, and making quality observable through per-item provenance and per-batch reports
  • Ensuring quality and completeness of every delivery against the customer contract, and owning batch acceptance and sign-off
  • Organising work with suppliers and partners: quality requirements, scorecards and independent external audit of their data before it reaches customers
  • Detecting and preventing fraud and non-authentic data: synthetic, replayed, duplicated or borrowed content
  • Supporting the business lead, on request, in assessing the feasibility and quality of new dataset offers and showcases
  • Establishing a process for continuous improvement of quality metrics, and mentoring engineers and analysts working on data quality
  • Proven Experience. 5+ years in data quality, ML data engineering or quality engineering for data products, including 3+ years on training or evaluation data for machine-learning models
  • Quality Framework Ownership. Build a quality function from scratch: define what "good"means for each dataset, turn it into measurable criteria, and get it adopted across teams and partners
  • Multimodal Depth. Work hands-on with at least two of speech/audio, video and images, and understand the third. Audio: WER/CER, SNR, VAD, language identification, speaker embeddings and diarization, reference-free quality models. Video and images: technical conformance, near-duplicate and re-capture detection, model-based content verification
  • Authenticity and Fraud. Recognise and counter the failure modes of crowdsourced collection: multi-accounting, synthetic and replayed speech, borrowed content, device and identity signals, anti-spoofing
  • Statistical Rigor. Apply acceptance sampling, stratified sampling, confidence intervals, inter-annotator agreement and precision/recall of automated filters against human labels, so that thresholds are measured rather than assumed
  • Hands-on Engineering. Write strong Python and SQL; build analysis, validation tooling and pipeline prototypes yourself, and specify quality gates for pipelines that run ML models in batch at scale
  • Dataset Engineering. Own schemas and versioning, manifests and checksums, data lineage and provenance, and dataset documentation that a customer can audit
  • Contract Quality. Turn contract language into dataset specifications with coverage, diversity quotas, format and numeric acceptance criteria, and run root-cause analysis on rejected or disputed deliveries
  • Supplier Audit. Run supplier quality end to end: due diligence, SLAs and acceptance criteria in contracts, independent verification of delivered data, scorecards and corrective actions tracked to closure
  • Customer Partnership. Act as the technical counterpart for customers' data and research teams on pilots, acceptance and quality questions, and understand how data defects affect model training
  • Business Partnership. Work with the business lead to judge whether a proposed dataset is feasible and what quality evidence a buyer will expect
  • Privacy and Consent. Know how consent and privacy requirements for personal and biometric data (voice, faces) constrain collection, retention and resale across jurisdictions
  • Structure in Ambiguity. Create structure where none exists: quality standards, review practices, audit cadences and reporting for a new business line
  • Executive Communication. Report quality status, risks and trade-offs to engineering and business leadership in clear, decision-ready form.
  • Fluent English
  • Experience working in an international environment.
  • Craft Leadership. Mentor engineers and analysts and set the craft direction for data quality as the function grows
  • Nice to Have. Experience at an AI data provider or in the data team of an AI lab; multilingual and low-resource speech, including code-switching; egocentric or robotics video;

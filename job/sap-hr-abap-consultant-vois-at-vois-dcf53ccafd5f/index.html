About this Role

We are seeking a skilled SAP HR ABAP Consultant with over four years of experience in SAP HCM/HR technical development. This role is ideal for an individual with strong expertise in ABAP within HR modules such as Personnel Administration (PA) and Organisational Management (OM), alongside a working understanding of integration scenarios, including SAP Integration Suite (CPI).

What you’ll do

Design, develop, and support SAP HR (HCM) ABAP solutionsWork extensively with infotypes, payroll, time management, and organisational managementDevelop and enhance:

  • ABAP reports (classical and ALV-based)
  • Enhancements including user exits, BAdIs, and implicit/explicit enhancements
  • Forms such as SmartForms, Adobe Forms, and SAP Scripts
  • Interfaces including IDocs, web services, and APIs

Build and optimise HR-specific ABAP objects such as macros and function modulesPerform payroll-related enhancements including PU12Manage secure bank file transfers, including encryption processesDevelop data migration and data upload solutions using LSMW, BAPIs, or custom programsDebug, troubleshoot, and resolve production issues and performance bottlenecksCollaborate with functional consultants and business stakeholders for requirement analysisParticipate in code reviews, testing cycles, and deployment activitiesWork on IDoc-based integration interfaces

Who you are

Over four years of hands-on experience in SAP ABAP developmentStrong technical expertise in SAP HR (HCM) ABAPGood understanding of:

  • Infotypes and HR data structures
  • HR function modules (PA/OM)
  • Payroll and time management fundamentals

Proven experience in reports, enhancements, forms, and interfacesStrong debugging and performance optimisation capabilitiesFamiliarity with object-oriented ABAP (OOABAP)Strong analytical, problem-solving, and collaboration skills

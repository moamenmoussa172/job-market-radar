About the Role

Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies.

We’re looking for a Pricing & Order Operations Analyst to join our Order Operations & Monetization team. This role restores dedicated capacity for pricing and order operations testing, quote-to-cash (QTC) release validation, and order execution support.

You’ll help ensure system changes are tested, validated, and operationally ready before they go live. You’ll provide Pricing and Order Operations subject-matter expertise and execute assigned business validation across signature packets, bookings, marketplace offers, and booking-to-order management handoffs. This role partners closely with Order Operations & Monetization, IT’s business systems team, Pricing Operations, Sales, and Deal Desk.

Reporting & Prioritization

  • Reports to the Order Operations & Monetization lead, Jamie Calkins.
  • The Senior Pricing Operations Manager may help prioritize pricing-related test cases and QTC issues tied specifically to pricing and quoting.
  • The Order Operations & Monetization lead owns final prioritization of the analyst’s workload across order operations, assigned non-pricing UAT participation, and pricing-related support work.

This is a hybrid role, 1-2 days in the office.

Technologies You’ll Use

  • Salesforce CPQ
  • Jira
  • Suger and marketplace user interfaces, including AWS, Azure, GCP, and Snowflake
  • Notion
  • Metronome
  • Hex

What You’ll Do

Pricing Operations & QTC Support

  • Support pricing operations changes through business requirement review, data validation, configuration checks, and documentation, helping ensure pricing logic is accurately reflected in QTC workflows.
  • Validate pricing-related QTC changes across quoting, approvals, discounting logic, product configuration, and downstream order execution.
  • Help confirm that pricing changes are operationally ready before launch, including impacts to Sales, Deal Desk, Order Operations, Billing, Revenue, and marketplace workflows.
  • Partner with the Senior Pricing Operations Manager to prioritize pricing-related test cases, identify gaps in business requirements, and document expected system behavior.
  • Help maintain documentation for pricing- and order-related system configurations, known dependencies, and release-readiness criteria.

UAT & Testing

  • Participate as a Pricing and Order Operations subject-matter expert in UAT project-managed by Technical Program Management (TPM) and executed under RevOps coordination, ensuring relevant business scenarios and expected outcomes are represented.
  • Execute assigned Pricing and Order Operations test cases and collaborate with the GTM subject-matter experts identified by RevOps.
  • Partner with RevOps and relevant subject-matter experts to develop and maintain Pricing and Order Operations test cases, including scenarios for quoting, approvals, signature packets, bookings, marketplace offers, billing, and order management.
  • Document test results and defects with clear traceability from business requirement to test outcome, and provide domain-specific readiness input through the established UAT sign-off process.
  • Maintain the Pricing and Order Operations portion of the regression test suite for recurring QTC changes.

Order Operations

  • Operate under defined business requirements for order operations rules within the QTC flow, validating execution across signature packet generation and e-signature routing, booking rules, marketplace offer configuration—including AWS, Azure, GCP, and Snowflake—and the booking-to-order management handoff.
  • Partner with IT’s business systems team and the Proposal-to-Order Lead to keep order operations rules aligned with upstream pricing and quoting logic and downstream billing and revenue systems.
  • Ensure signature packets and marketplace offers accurately reflect the underlying order and pricing terms before execution.
  • Identify and troubleshoot order operations issues across bookings, signature packets, and marketplace offers, escalating systemic problems to the appropriate system owner.

Skills We’re Looking For

  • 2–3 years of experience in pricing operations, order operations, business systems analysis, or quality assurance within an agile environment, ideally involving quote-to-cash processes.
  • Working knowledge of Salesforce CPQ and order management or billing systems, with the ability to read and understand configuration logic without necessarily building it directly.
  • Strong attention to detail and comfort performing structured, repeatable testing work.
  • Comfort using conversational AI tools—such as Claude, ChatGPT, or internal chatbots—to support daily and project work, combined with sound judgment about when human review or decision-making is necessary.
  • Clear written communication skills, with the ability to document test cases, defects, and sign-off criteria for technical and business audiences.
  • A proactive and organized approach, with the ability to work across IT, Pricing and Order Operations, and Sales and Deal Desk without heavy oversight.

Bonus Skills​

  • Exposure to Revenue Recognition or Professional Services systems and processes.

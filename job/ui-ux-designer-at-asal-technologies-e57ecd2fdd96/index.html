About the job

Senior UI/UX Designer - Enterprise Web & macOS Applications

About the role

We're looking for a Senior UI/UX Designer who thrives in fast-moving product cycles and can make complex data feel simple.

You'll work closely with product and engineering to design experiences that are both powerful and intuitive.

What you'll do

  • Own design end-to-end - from early concepts to specs ready for engineering
  • Turn complex workflows and multi-layered data into clear, polished interfaces
  • Prototype fast, take feedback, iterate, and move on
  • Work daily with product, engineering, and stakeholders - communicate design decisions clearly
  • Manage your own workload with reliable estimates the team can count on
  • Use AI tools as part of your everyday workflow

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years designing enterprise web and macOS applications that handle complex, multi-layered data
  • Strong visual instincts - your work looks finished, not just functional
  • Fast prototyping - whiteboard to clickable mockup in hours, not days
  • Clear communicator - you can walk engineers and executives through a design and make it land
  • Comfortable with tight deadlines and frequent changes without dropping quality
  • Sharp attention to detail - you notice things others miss
  • Quick to pick up new tools and processes
  • Works well both independently and as part of a team
  • Open to critique and fast iterations - you improve quickly and don't get attached to first drafts
  • Proficient English speaker and writer - clear, precise communication in both spoken and written form

Nice to have

  • Background in graphic design, motion, or illustration
  • Experience with design systems at scale

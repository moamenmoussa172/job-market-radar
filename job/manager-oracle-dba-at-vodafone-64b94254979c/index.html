About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Oracle Application Database Administrator with approximately 7–10 years of relevant experience to support database administration, operational stability and critical database activities from SP Infocity, Pune.

The role focuses on Oracle database administration, SQL and database performance tuning, upgrades and migrations, backup and recovery, security and access management, automation, monitoring and high-availability environments. You will work closely with application teams to maintain end-to-end service stability and availability while contributing to continual service improvement.

The role also provides exposure to Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices, AI-driven optimisation, CRM and end-to-end operations, with a focus on improving operational efficiency.

What you will do

  • Administer and support production Oracle databases and associated database environments.
  • Analyse database and application performance bottlenecks, including scripts, code and user-generated queries that contribute to application slowness.
  • Perform database and SQL performance tuning, including support for DML operations.
  • Support database upgrades and migrations from older to newer versions, including integration with other RDBMS technologies.
  • Manage database configuration, architecture and day-to-day operational administration.
  • Work with memory, storage, cluster and high-availability architectures.
  • Support database backup and recovery activities.
  • Manage user access and contribute to security audits.
  • Participate in auditing and SOX compliance activities.
  • Monitor database environments using tools such as Oracle Enterprise Manager and Grafana.
  • Use observability and monitoring platforms such as Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Kibana, Splunk or Zabbix for log analysis, alert tuning, dashboards and proactive monitoring.
  • Work closely and collaboratively with application teams to support database operations.
  • Use ticketing tools to manage operational incidents and service requests.
  • Provide on-call support for high-priority database issues.
  • Develop and maintain UNIX, shell and other scripts to automate BAU operational activities and improve system stability.
  • Handle UNIX jobs and batch processes, tune existing processes and develop new processes where required.
  • Contribute to proactive monitoring, automation, reconciliation and continual service improvement.
  • Apply SRE practices and support AI-driven optimisation initiatives to enhance operational efficiency.

Who you are

  • You have 7–10 years of relevant application database support and administration experience, including at least seven years of proficient application database support operations experience.
  • You have hands-on experience administering Oracle databases across multiple versions.
  • You understand Oracle and non-Oracle database architecture.
  • You have practical expertise in database and SQL performance tuning.
  • You have experience configuring databases and providing operational support.
  • You understand memory architecture, storage architecture, cluster architecture and high-availability environments.
  • You have advanced UNIX knowledge and practical experience with shell scripting and at least one additional scripting language, such as Perl.
  • You have experience with database upgrades, migrations, backup and recovery, user access management and security auditing.
  • You understand proactive monitoring, automation, reconciliation and continual service improvement.
  • You have experience with monitoring and observability technologies such as Enterprise Manager, Grafana, Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Kibana, Splunk or Zabbix.
  • You can perform log analysis, alert tuning and dashboard-based proactive monitoring.
  • You bring effective analytical, communication, stakeholder-management and technical-leadership skills.
  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent relevant professional experience.
  • ITIL certification is desirable.
  • Knowledge of telecommunications would be an advantage.

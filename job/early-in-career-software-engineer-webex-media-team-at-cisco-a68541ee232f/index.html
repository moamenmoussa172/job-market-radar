Location: Krakow, Poland (We follow a hybrid work approach with a minimum office presence of 3 days per week.)

Meet the Team

Your Impact

Cisco Webex helps people and businesses across the entire world to connect and collaborate. As a member of the Webex media team, your work will ensure that every person using our products receives a truly immersive experience, regardless of their device or location.

On a day-to-day basis, this role involves:

  • Bringing to life new software functionality based on product requirements.
  • Performing peer code reviews and automating all development chores.
  • Participating in agile ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, planning, release, and retrospective meetings.
  • Broadening your technical and soft skills with the help of our comprehensive training programs.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • 1+ years of professional software development experience and a BSc (or equivalent) in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field. Equivalent experience accepted in lieu of degree.
  • Proficiency in C/C++ or Rust with strong knowledge of data structures, algorithms, and design patterns.
  • Uses AI responsibly to accelerate development.

Preferred qualifications

  • Professional verbal and written communication skills in English.
  • Knowledge of WebRTC/ SIP protocols
  • Experience with reviewing AI-generated code
  • Experience steering AI effectively via context and prompt design.
  • Collaborative and eager to learn.

Role Overview

The Staff Physical Design Engineer plays a key role in delivering physical implementation and signoff for digital blocks and SoCs. Working across collaborative and geographically distributed engineering teams, the role drives assigned RTL-to-GDSII activities, resolves implementation challenges, and helps shape design and methodology decisions that improve power, performance, area and schedule outcomes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Drive assigned RTL-to-GDSII implementation activities for digital blocks and SoCs, including floor-planning, power planning, CTS, routing, equivalence checking, timing closure, and physical verification.
  • Take ownership of assigned implementation and signoff deliverables, working with the wider team to manage technical risks, dependencies, priorities, and schedule commitments through tape-out.
  • Apply strong knowledge of synthesis and physical implementation to support design decisions and improve power, performance, area, and reliability.
  • Analyse and resolve timing, power, signal-integrity, IR/EM, DRC, LVS, and equivalence issues, with support from specialist teams where appropriate.
  • Drive implementation reviews and collaborate with RTL, DFT, and analogue teams to identify issues early and develop robust solutions.
  • Develop and drive improvements to physical design flows, automation, metrics, and best practices, supporting consistent and efficient execution across projects.

Qualifications

  • B.Tech., M.Tech., or equivalent qualification or experience in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Typically 7-10 years of relevant physical design or digital implementation experience.
  • Hands-on experience with industry-standard physical design or signoff tools, such as Cadence or Synopsys environments.
  • Demonstrated experience in timing closure, physical verification, power integrity, and implementation trade-offs in digital designs.
  • Ability to work collaboratively, take ownership of technical tasks, communicate clearly, and support colleagues.

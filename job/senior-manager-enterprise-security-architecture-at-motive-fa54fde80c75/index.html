About the Role:

Motive is seeking a Sr. Manager, Enterprise Security Architecture to own how we protect our most sensitive data across our cloud, data platforms, and media pipelines. Reporting directly to the CISO, you will be the founding leader of the Enterprise Security Architecture function, setting the technical standards for data governance, zero-standing-privilege access management, and security posture across our US and EU environments. You will hire, coach, and lead a small team of security architects while partnering closely with Platform, Data, AI/ML, Product, Privacy, Legal, IT, and GRC teams to embed security early into engineering design. You will ground decisions in hands-on validation and evidence, proving target architectures in working prototypes before turning them into scalable paved roads for engineering. This is a principal-engineer-style player-coach role: you will spend roughly half your time staying hands-on—designing reference architectures, writing infrastructure and policy as code, and building proofs of concept yourself—and the other half leading your team and driving cross-functional alignment. This is a high-visibility, 0-to-1 leadership opportunity to shape the foundational security architecture behind Motive’s data and AI platforms.

What You’ll Do:

  • Define data classification, ownership, retention, and handling standards for structured and unstructured data, including customer media, telemetry, and AI/ML training data.
  • Design reference architectures for data protection across AWS, Snowflake, and surrounding data platforms, including customer-managed encryption keys, key separation by data class, and multi-region (US and EU) data residency controls.
  • Architect access management that eliminates standing human access through least-privilege design, federated identity, just-in-time elevation, and approval-gated exceptions.
  • Define authentication, authorization, registration, review, and revocation standards for services, pipelines, and AI agents, alongside strong guardrails to prevent data exfiltration.
  • Run hands-on proofs of concept for data security posture management (DSPM), cloud and data access analytics, secure media review environments, and ephemeral compute to drive data-backed build-versus-buy decisions.
  • Write infrastructure as code, policy as code, and detection logic, and review engineering designs and pull requests to turn successful prototypes into documented, self-service paved roads.
  • Hire, coach, and grow a team of security architects focused on IAM, data governance, and AI security, setting technical direction, priorities, and the quality bar.
  • Partner across Platform, Data, AI/ML, Product, Privacy, Legal, IT, and GRC to embed controls early, and report posture metrics, risk trade-offs, and progress to security and engineering leadership.

What We’re Looking For:

  • 8+ years in security engineering or architecture, including at least 5 years designing cloud and data security for large, regulated, or data-intensive environments.
  • Deep AWS security expertise paired with hands-on experience securing Snowflake or comparable data platforms (such as Databricks or BigQuery), including RBAC design, masking, row access policies, private connectivity, data sharing, and audit controls.
  • Proven track record designing data governance and access management architectures, including data classification, least privilege, just-in-time elevation, workload identity, and privileged access.
  • Practical experience with data security posture management (DSPM) and data loss prevention (DLP), from running proofs of concept and tuning findings to operationalizing controls at scale.
  • Strong coding ability in Python or Go and Terraform to build prototypes, policy as code, and automation, combined with proficiency using AI coding tools to accelerate engineering solutions.
  • 3+ years leading or mentoring security engineers or architects, either formally as a manager or as a technical lead.
  • Ability to turn architectural standards into broad engineering adoption through clear design docs, cross-functional influence, and pragmatic risk communication.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

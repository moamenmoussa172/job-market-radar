About the job

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet.

About the team

We build the infrastructure layer that routes AI inference at scale. Our platform serves billions of daily requests across 100+ large language models, handling model selection, provider failover, load balancing, and cost optimization through a single unified API. Thousands of developers and enterprises depend on it to operationalize LLMs across research, product, and production workloads.

The security surface spans API key and provider credential management across cloud boundaries, user prompt data flowing through the routing layer, a billing pipeline processing billions of daily events with financial-grade accuracy requirements, and an authentication layer federating external identity providers. You will lead and scale the security team that owns this surface — hands-on in the architecture and code, setting the technical direction, and directly elevating the security posture of the platform through your own work.

What you'll do

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You manage a fast growing team of software and security engineers and make outsized impact through technical depth. Your focus will be cloud security infrastructure and modern AI development practices. This is a player/coach role: you’ll establish the roadmap and grow the team while continuing to design systems, write code, evaluate tools, and work through difficult technical problems.

  • Own the security architecture. Threat-model the edge compute layer, provider integration surface, credential management system, billing pipeline, and analytics store. Make the architecture decisions — API key lifecycle, secrets rotation, data classification, auth enforcement — and partner with product and infra teams through implementation.
  • Secure the credential layer. Design and implement controls for API key issuance, rotation, revocation, and abuse detection across user-facing keys and upstream provider credentials spanning multiple secrets engines and cloud boundaries. This is the highest-risk surface on the platform and requires deep expertise in cryptographic credential management, OAuth/OIDC federation, and token-based access control.
  • Build detection and response. Instrument the platform for security events across edge, async processing, and database tiers. Build alerting, runbooks, and forensic tooling. Lead incident response when it matters. You own time-to-detect and time-to-contain, not just the playbook.
  • Integrate the platform into Stripe's security programs. Bring the platform into SecRev, vulnerability management, and incident response processes. Adapt controls designed for Stripe's primary infrastructure to fit an edge-first, multi-cloud architecture. Bridge Stripe's compliance frameworks (SOC 2, PCI DSS) with a platform that routes data across external providers.
  • Lead and develop the team. Manage existing team members and help rapidly scale it, in line with business and technical needs. Set clear expectations, give direct feedback, and develop people.

Who you are

You’re an engineer and a people leader who is comfortable representing the security posture to executive leadership . You enjoy building things yourself, but you also know how to create an environment where other strong engineers can do their best work. You’re resourceful, comfortable with ambiguity, and willing to start with a practical solution and improve it as you learn

Minimum requirements

  • Software & security engineering depth. 10+ years in software engineering with deep expertise in at least two of: API security architecture, credential and secrets management, cloud security (IAM, network segmentation, encryption), or application security for high-throughput distributed systems.
  • Hands-on technical leader. Strong software engineering skills and the ability to contribute directly to production code, infrastructure, and technical design. You have directly designed credential management systems, authentication architectures, or data protection controls for production systems at scale, not just reviewed others' work.
  • Multi-cloud and edge security. Experience securing workloads that span multiple cloud providers or edge compute platforms: secrets management across trust boundaries, data classification for data in transit, and compliance in environments where you do not control the full stack.
  • Team leadership. Experience managing and growing a team of engineers, setting high expectations, and developing people. You have led teams where the quality of the work depended on your technical judgment, not just your organizational skills.
  • Communication. You explain security risks, architecture decisions, and compliance tradeoffs clearly to engineering, product, and executive audiences.

Thrives with ambiguity. A resourceful, pragmatic approach to execution: you can prioritize under constraints, make clear build-versus-buy decisions, and deliver useful improvements without waiting for perfect information.

Preferred qualifications

  • The ability to secure high-throughput proxy, API gateway, or edge compute systems
  • Experience designing, operating, or security infrastructure on Google Cloud
  • Deep knowledge of OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML, and federated identity architectures
  • Familiarity with LLM inference infrastructure, token-based billing, or AI/ML platform security
  • Experience with laptop, device and endpoint security, including device management and secure access.
  • Background in payments, fintech, or regulated industries (SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR) with strict data-handling standards
  • Experience integrating a newly acquired platform into an existing security organization and compliance framework

If you don't think you meet all of the criteria but are still interested in the job, please apply. Nobody checks every box, and we're looking for someone who is excited to join the team.

In-office expectations

In-Office: Stripe is a "Live" culture. You will be expected to spend at least 50% of your time in your local hub to foster high-bandwidth, in-person collaboration.

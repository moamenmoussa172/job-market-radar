About the job

Network Engineering & Architecture

  • Design, implement, and support enterprise LAN, WAN, WLAN, SD-WAN, and cloud networking solutions.
  • Develop and maintain network architecture standards, diagrams, and technical documentation.
  • Evaluate and recommend network technologies, hardware, and software solutions.
  • Lead network upgrades, migrations, and modernization initiatives.

Operations & Support

  • Provide Tier 2/3 support for complex network incidents and outages.
  • Perform root cause analysis and implement corrective actions for recurring issues.
  • Ensure network availability, performance, and reliability through proactive monitoring and maintenance.
  • Participate in on-call support and incident response activities as required.

Security & Compliance

  • Implement and maintain network security controls, including firewalls, VPNs, NAC, segmentation, and secure remote access.
  • Collaborate with cybersecurity teams to identify and remediate vulnerabilities.
  • Support compliance, audit, and risk management requirements.
  • Maintain network security standards and best practices.

Cloud & Infrastructure Integration

  • Design and support connectivity to Azure, AWS, GCP and OCI.
  • Integrate network services with enterprise infrastructure platforms.
  • Support hybrid-cloud and multi-cloud networking architectures.
  • Implement automation and infrastructure-as-code solutions where appropriate.

Project Leadership

  • Lead technical aspects of network-related projects from planning through implementation.
  • Coordinate with vendors, service providers, and internal stakeholders.
  • Mentor junior engineers and provide technical guidance across the team.
  • Develop project plans, implementation procedures, and testing strategies.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience.
  • 5+ years of enterprise network engineering experience.
  • Advanced knowledge of routing, switching, wireless networking, and network security technologies.
  • Experience with Cisco, Palo Alto, F5, or similar enterprise platforms.
  • Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills.
  • Experience supporting large-scale and mission-critical environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Cisco CCNP or equivalent certification.
  • Experience with Cloud networking.
  • Experience with SD-WAN and network automation technologies.
  • Knowledge of scripting and automation using Python, PowerShell, Terraform, or Ansible.
  • Industry certifications such as CCIE, CISSP, Azure Network Engineer Associate, or AWS Advanced Networking Specialty.

Technical Skills

  • BGP, MPLS, and routing protocols
  • Layer 2/Layer 3 switching technologies
  • Wireless networking and controller-based architectures
  • VPN, Firewall, IDS/IPS, and Zero Trust technologies
  • SD-WAN and Internet connectivity solutions
  • Cloud native and hybrid-cloud networking
  • Network monitoring and performance management tools
  • Automation and Infrastructure-as-Code methodologies

Key Competencies

  • Advanced troubleshooting and problem-solving
  • Technical leadership and mentorship
  • Project execution and delivery
  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Collaboration across infrastructure and security teams
  • Continuous improvement mindset
  • Customer service and operational excellence

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines. Role is expected to work US Eastern Standard Time hours

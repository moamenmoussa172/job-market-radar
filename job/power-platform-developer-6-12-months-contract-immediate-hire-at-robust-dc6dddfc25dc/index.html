About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

Role and engagement model

  • Work as an external delivery specialist embedded in the client's agile platform team.
  • Take ownership of defined work packages from refinement and design through build, test, deployment and stabilization.
  • Collaborate closely with developers, architects, product owners and business stakeholders while following the client's governance and engineering standards.

Key responsibilities

  • Design, build and enhancemodel-driven apps and Canvas apps using reusableand maintainablepatterns.
  • Create Power Automate cloud flows, approvals and integrations with appropriate error handling and monitoring.
  • Design Dataverse tables, relationships, security roles, business rules and solution components.
  • Integrate Microsoft and third-party services through standard connectors, custom connectors and REST APIs.
  • Deliver changes through managed solutions, source control and Azure DevOps based ALM.
  • Perform unit testing, peer reviews, defect fixing, technical documentation and production support.
  • Coach internal developers through pairing, code reviews and concise technical handovers.

Requirements

Mandatory experience

  • 5+ years of software or low-codedevelopment experience, including 3+ years of hands-on Power Apps delivery in enterprise environments.
  • Proven delivery of production-ready model-driven and Canvas apps, including complex Power Fx and reusable components.
  • Strong practical knowledge of Power Automate, Dataversedata modelling, security and integration patterns.
  • Experience deploying managed solutions across multiple environments using structured ALM.
  • Hands-on Azure DevOps, repositories, pipelines or comparable CI/CD experience.
  • Professional English and clear communication in an international team.

Preferred experience

  • Plugins, PCF controls, JavaScript or TypeScript, custom connectors, Power Pages or Azure Functions.
  • Dynamics 365 Sales or Customer Service.
  • Copilot Studio and AI Builder integration experience.
  • Microsoft Power Platform certification, particularly PL-400 or PL-600.

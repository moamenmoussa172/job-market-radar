About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in India.

About ClickHouse

Recognized on the 2025 Forbes Cloud 100 list, ClickHouse is one of the most innovative and fast-growing private cloud companies. With over 2,000 customers and ARR that has more than quadrupled over the past year, ClickHouse leads the market in real-time analytics, data warehousing, observability, and AI workloads. We're on a mission to transform how companies use data. Come be a part of our journey!

About the Team

The Managed Postgres team is building ClickHouse's fully managed Postgres service. It gives developers a fast, reliable Postgres that works natively alongside ClickHouse, so one platform covers both transactional and real-time analytical workloads. This is a strategic, high-visibility initiative, and the user experience we build will shape how thousands of developers create, manage, monitor, and scale their databases.

About the Role

We're looking for a Full Stack Software Engineer to help build responsive, performant, and elegant interfaces that make complex database operations feel simple. You'll work closely with product, design, and backend engineers, and you'll learn from senior engineers while owning real features from day one.

What You'll Do

  • Turn designs and user requirements into intuitive, high-quality interfaces for the Managed Postgres experience in ClickHouse Cloud.
  • Build features end-to-end: React and TypeScript on the frontend, plus integration with (and contributions to) backend APIs.
  • Build data-rich UIs for metrics, diagnostics, backups, replication, and performance insights.
  • Contribute to click-ui, our open-source design system, and follow frontend best practices: code quality, performance, accessibility, and testing.
  • Give product and design feedback from a developer's point of view.

About You

  • Experience: 1–4 years in frontend or full-stack development.
  • Core skills: Solid React, TypeScript, and modern frontend fundamentals (state management, component architecture, data fetching).
  • Backend exposure: Comfortable working with REST APIs; Node.js, Go, or similar is a plus.
  • Collaboration: You enjoy working closely with designers and backend engineers across a global team.
  • Developer empathy: You care about fast, clear, purposeful UIs for technical users.
  • Ownership: You take initiative, ask good questions, and carry work from idea to delivery.
  • Growth mindset: You're keen to learn and grow your scope quickly.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Postgres, SQL, or database tooling
  • Accessibility (WCAG) knowledge
  • Data visualization or dashboard experience
  • Open-source contributions

Why Join Us

  • Shape the future of the Postgres user experience inside one of the fastest-growing data platforms.
  • Real ownership and autonomy from day one.
  • Learn from experts in databases, distributed systems, and developer experience.
  • Solve meaningful problems in performance, scale, and usability.

Perks

  • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
  • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
  • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity.
  • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries.
  • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee.
  • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

Culture - We All Shape It

As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture.

About the job

Note: By applying to this position, your application will be considered for: London, UK.
Do you want to experiment with innovative technologies, including Cloud Computing, Machine Learning, and GenAI? Are you passionate about educating, training, designing, and building cloud computing solutions to help solve the most challenging problems?
Amazon Web Services (AWS), a leader in Cloud Computing, is seeking interns to join our AWS Professional Services Internship program. This is a unique opportunity to play a key role in a fast-growing business and to deliver value to AWS customers of all sizes from startups to global brands. The skills and experiences you gain will be highly sought after throughout the industry and give you the opportunity for the career of a lifetime.
The AWS Professional Services (ProServe) organization is a global team of experts that helps AWS customers realize their desired business outcomes when using the AWS Cloud. We deliver focused guidance through our global specialty practices, which cover a variety of solutions, technologies, and industries. In addition to working alongside our customers, we share our experience through technical artifacts and proof of concept demos.
As an Associate Delivery Consultant Intern, you will gain hands-on experience in cloud computing, develop business acumen, and learn about Amazon’s peculiar culture. You will work on internal projects, have the opportunity to obtain the AWS certifications, and attend professional development events. This internship can lead to a full-time role in London, UK, that has up to 75% travel associated. While many of our customers engage ProServe virtually, there is always the potential that a customer might choose to include travel in their contract.
Delivery Consultants in AWS ProServe are aligned by technical specialty. For this internship, we are looking for candidates that are interested in and fit to either the AIML or Modernization specialty.
AIML - AIML role supports our services that leverage data and produce business insights, which may include using Machine Learning/Artificial Intelligence/Generative Artificial Intelligence (ML/AI/GenAI). Helping our customers use and integrate Big Data services in what is arguably our industry’s most exciting space. The portfolio of services covers EMR (Hadoop), DynamoDB (NoSQL), MongoDB, Apache Cassandra, Amazon Q, and Bedrock.
Modernization - Modernization resources are specialists in designing applications that run natively in the cloud using programming languages such as Python, JavaScript, Typescript, etc. They are experts in building programs that run on any number of platforms including virtualized instances, containers, or server less architecture. They are full stack developers with backend, frontend, and UX skillsets.
Upon successful completion of the internship program, selected interns may receive a full-time offer to join the ProServe Associate to Consultant (A2C) Delivery. A2C Delivery develops the next generation of ProServe consultants who use generative and agentic AI to solve customer challenges and drive innovation within ProServe. A2C Delivery focuses on accelerating career development for recent graduates and early career professionals entering technical roles, advancing their skills and helping customers design and build flexible and resilient cloud-based solutions. While in A2C, you will learn key technical and professional skills from top AWS subject matter experts that you can use in your career working with AWS’s customers.
Amazon internships are full-time (40 hours/week) for 12 consecutive weeks, starting June 2027. Applicants should have at a minimum one quarter/semester remaining after their internship concludes. This role requires the intern to be onsite five days a week in London.
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. For an update on your status, or to confirm your application was submitted successfully, please login to your candidate portal. Please note that we are reviewing a high volume of applications and appreciate your patience.

About the job

We’re looking for a Tax Manager to join Spotify’s Global Transfer Pricing team, reporting to the Director of Global Transfer Pricing. In this role you’ll focus on analytics, modelling and AI. You’ll drive the development of Spotify’s global transfer pricing models and economic analyses, keeping our intercompany pricing accurate, efficient and defensible in every jurisdiction where we operate. You’ll also help lead the team’s use of AI tools and automation to change how transfer pricing work gets done, from forecasting and projections to audit support. You’ll join a team of skilled, dedicated colleagues in a fast-moving international company, with plenty of room to take on responsibility. Above all, your work will impact the way the world experiences audio.

What You'll Do

  • Develop, maintain and continuously improve Spotify’s global transfer pricing models for quarter-end and year-end closes.
  • Prepare and review quarterly and annual transfer pricing calculations, delivering them within internal and reporting deadlines.
  • Turn externally prepared benchmarking studies and economic analyses into practical inputs for our transfer pricing models.
  • Partner with FP&A and finance business partners on future transfer pricing outcomes and projections.
  • Drive the adoption of AI tools and automation to make transfer pricing processes more efficient, accurate and scalable.
  • Work with Spotify’s data and technology teams to transform how financial data for transfer pricing transactions is sourced and handled.
  • Collaborate with Tax, Treasury, Accounting and Legal on transfer pricing matters, and support tax audits with documentation, analyses and outcome scenarios.
  • Contribute to transfer pricing documentation, including local files and master files.
  • Who You Are
  • You have 5+ years of experience in transfer pricing, gained at a Big 4 firm, in industry, or both. Experience in the tech industry is a plus.
  • You hold a degree in accounting, finance, economics, business administration or law.
  • You have hands-on transfer pricing operations experience, including model-building, economic analysis, valuation analysis and benchmarking.
  • You have strong analytical and quantitative skills and can build and maintain complex financial models in Google Sheets or Excel. Experience with SQL, Python or other data tools is a strong plus.
  • You’ve worked with financial systems such as ERP, consolidation and management reporting tools. Knowledge of NetSuite is a plus.
  • You use AI tools to improve efficiency and quality in your daily work, and you’re curious about new ways to apply them.
  • You have supported the transfer pricing aspects of tax audits and prepared defence files.
  • You communicate clearly in English, both written and spoken, and you’ve worked well in cross-functional, cross-border teams.
  • You’re intellectually curious, eager to learn, and comfortable in a fast-paced, informal and often ambiguous environment.
  • Where You'll Be
  • This role is based in Stockholm.
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home. We ask that you come in 3 times per week.

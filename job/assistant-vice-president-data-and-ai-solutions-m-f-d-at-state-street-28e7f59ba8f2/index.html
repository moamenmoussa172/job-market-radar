Job Description: Assistant Vice President, Data Governance Lead - Data & AI Solutions (m/f/d)

We are looking for an experienced Data Governance Lead with strong business, regulatory and technical expertise to drive the execution of data governance within Finance and across relevant data domains. Acting as the primary point of contact for Data Stewards and partnering with Business Owners, Data Owners, Technology and the Data Governance function, the role ensures that data is governed, fit for purpose, traceable and supported by effective controls. The position combines Data Governance Lead responsibilities with Business Analyst capabilities like data modelling, cloud, analytics and AI to translate regulatory and business requirements into sustainable, controlled and scalable data solutions.

Responsibilities:

  • Act as the Data Governance Lead and first point of contact for Data Stewards within Finance and relevant cross-functional data domains, providing direction on policy, standards, roles and control requirements.
  • Partner with Business Owners and Data Owners to ensure adherence to the Data Governance Policy and the effective execution and documentation of governance responsibilities.
  • Manage and oversee Data Provider and Data Consumer relationships, including data ownership, fitness-for-purpose assessments, data sharing requirements and issue escalation.
  • Engage Business Owners, process owners and Technology to confirm that business and IT controls are designed, implemented, evidenced and operating effectively to mitigate data quality and data risks.
  • Drive the definition, documentation and maintenance of Critical Data Elements, business terms, data taxonomies, data ownership, metadata and data standards in the enterprise Data Catalog.
  • Partner with Technology Managers and data experts to capture technical metadata and link physical data structures to business definitions and Critical Data Elements in Collibra DIP.
  • Ensure end-to-end data lineage, including application inputs, transformations, aggregation and reporting outputs, is complete, accurate, approved and maintained in the Data Catalog.
  • Lead the identification, prioritisation, root-cause analysis, remediation and closure of data quality issues and exceptions in collaboration with business, technology and subject matter experts.
  • Define and oversee data quality rules, thresholds, controls, monitoring, dashboards, reconciliation requirements and remediation plans, including the use of Collibra Data Quality where applicable.
  • Participate in data integration, transformation and regulatory remediation initiatives to ensure that appropriate data is used, governance requirements are embedded and Data Catalog records remain current.
  • Support compliance with Section 25a KWG, MaRisk AT 4.3.4, BCBS 239 and ECB expectations for Risk Data Aggregation and Risk Reporting, with a focus on data ownership, quality, lineage, traceability and control evidence.
  • Design or review conceptual and logical data models supporting Finance, Treasury, Risk, analytical and regulatory reporting requirements, ensuring alignment with business definitions and governance standards.
  • Translate business, governance and regulatory requirements into scalable and controlled data solutions using platforms such as Databricks and analytical capabilities using SQL and Python.
  • Identify opportunities to improve governance execution, data issue management and operational efficiency through workflow automation, AI Agents, Generative AI, Databricks Genie, Alteryx or Workiva.
  • Prepare governance documentation, control evidence, management reporting and decision materials suitable for senior management, internal governance forums, Audit and regulatory review.

Skills, Requirements and Qualifications

  • Strong subject matter expertise in data governance policies, operating models, roles, standards and controls within banking or another highly regulated environment.
  • Proven experience acting as a Data Governance Lead, Data Steward Lead, Data Owner delegate or comparable role, including coordination of Data Stewards across multiple business or data domains.
  • Demonstrated knowledge of BCBS 239 compliance, ECB RDARR expectations, Section 25a KWG and MaRisk AT 4.3.4, particularly for data quality, lineage, traceability, aggregation and reporting controls.
  • Hands-on experience with Data Catalog and governance platforms, preferably Collibra Data Intelligence Platform and Collibra Data Quality, including metadata, lineage, data quality and workflow capabilities.
  • Strong understanding of Finance, Treasury, Risk and regulatory reporting data, data provider/consumer relationships and data sharing or service-level arrangements.
  • Ability to define and challenge data ownership, Critical Data Elements, business definitions, data quality rules, controls, thresholds, issue remediation and evidence requirements.
  • Good understanding of conceptual, logical and physical data models, data taxonomies, reference data and data standards.
  • Working knowledge of cloud-based data platforms such as Databricks, together with SQL and Python for analysis, validation and automation is advantageous.
  • Practical understanding of AI and Generative AI capabilities, including AI Agents, agentic workflows and natural-language analytics such as Databricks Genie, is advantageous.
  • Experience with Alteryx, Workiva, Business Intelligence or workflow automation tools is advantageous.
  • Strong stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills, with the confidence and tenacity to drive issues to closure across Business, Technology, Risk, Compliance and Audit stakeholders.
  • Excellent project and issue-management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and independently drive governance deliverables from requirement through implementation and closure.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Business, Information Technology, Data Management, Business Informatics, Computer Science or a comparable discipline.
  • Typically 5+ years of relevant experience in financial services, including at least 2 years in data governance, data management, risk management, regulatory remediation or a comparable control function.
  • Relevant certifications in data management, Collibra, Databricks or AI technologies are advantageous.

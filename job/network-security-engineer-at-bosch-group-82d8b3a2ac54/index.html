About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Provide last-level support for complex incidents, drive technical resolution, andensure timely restoration within agreed service targets
  • Perform root-cause analysis for high-impact or recurring issues; define,implement, and validate sustainable corrective measures
  • Monitor service stability, performance, capacity, and security posture;proactively improve reliability, service quality, and cost efficiency
  • Plan, implement, validate, and document production changes, includingstandard changes and feature introductions
  • Support client and platform upgrades, operational readiness checks, logcollection, storage-capacity monitoring, and four-eyes validation whererequired
  • Configure, operate, and optimize RAS and SSE controls, including remote-access VPN, proxy, cloud firewall, identity-aware access, traffic steering, andapplication-security configurations
  • Prepare, configure, and roll out network and security devices or servicecomponents within the assigned service scope
  • Deliver technical application onboarding and advise internal customers on theonboarding process, required connectivity, authentication, and securitycontrols
  • Maintain security compliance, vulnerability remediation, and required patchlevels for operated services and devices
  • Create, maintain, and govern operational documentation, runbooks, andknowledge articles throughout their lifecycle
  • Contribute to service improvements, automation, product upgrades, andassigned project deliverables in a global agile team.Participate in an on-call rotation and support planned maintenance or critical incidents outside standard office hours when required
  • Degree in computer science, information technology, engineering, or a comparable qualification; equivalent relevant professional experience is also accepted
  • Demonstrable experience operating enterprise network-security, remote-access VPN, secure web gateway, SASE/SSE, proxy, cloud firewall, or comparable connectivity services
  • Strong foundation in TCP/IP, ARP, NAT, routing, DNS, TLS/SSL, and packet-level troubleshooting
  • Practical knowledge of Internet and authentication protocols, including HTTP/S, FTP/SFTP, SSH, Kerberos, LDAP, OAuth, and basic authentication
  • Working knowledge of network technologies such as BGP, OSPF, SD-WAN, QoS, and load-balancer architecture and operations
  • Experience using packet captures and related diagnostic tools to investigate complex network and application issues
  • Knowledge of application vulnerabilities and security techniques
  • Broad understanding of security concepts, access controls, and secure service operations
  • Experience with IT service-management practices, ideally ITIL 4, including incident, problem, change, request, and knowledge management
  • Confident administration and troubleshooting skills in Linux and Windows environments
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and customer-consulting skills
  • Fluent written and spoken English

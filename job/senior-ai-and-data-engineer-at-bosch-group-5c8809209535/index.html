About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
You will work as part of our global DevOps teams to provide data solutions for our internal customers across the globe. In this role, you will design, build, and scale modern data platforms and AI-powered solutions that drive business value. You will work closely with data scientists, data engineers, product owners, and business stakeholders to develop robust data pipelines, deploy production-grade AI applications, and establish engineering best practices across the organization.

- Take responsibility: You will work with our internal customers and international team of developers for developing robust data platforms, implementing advanced AI applications, and ensuring production-grade deployment of data and AI products.

- Help shape the future: As an employee of the global cross-functional Business Intelligence team (xBI), you will work on the future steering of Bosch.

- Use freedom and creativity: We live agile values so that you can contribute your ideas and experiences for the benefit of the team and the company.

- Think entrepreneurially: As part of the leading in-house provider for data and analytics-based solutions within Bosch, you will experience the start-up spirit with us and make your contribution through entrepreneurial thinking and acting.

- Live Cooperation: You will work in an interdisciplinary and international team supported by internal and external service providers as well as in direct exchange with our internal custom.

-Project Work: The person will be working with the team to take up the tasks and make sure it is done without any issues for the done tasks.

Educational qualification:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.

Experience :

5+ years of experience in Data Engineering, AI Engineering, or related domains.

Mandatory/requires Skills :
Data Engineering & Platforms

  • Solid understanding of the Databricks ecosystem, including Unity Catalog, Genie Spaces, Delta Lake, MLflow, Databricks SQL, Vector Search, and Model Serving
  • Strong programming skills in Python, PySpark, and SQL, with a focus on scalable data processing and optimization
  • Hands-on experience building and operating data pipelines

AI Engineering Expertise

  • Proven experience designing and implementing AI solutions, with expertise in at least two of the following areas:
  • Development of MCP servers for AI applications
  • Use of modern AI frameworks such as LangChain, OpenAI SDK, or PydanticAI
  • Design and implementation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems
  • Implementation of tracing, monitoring, and observability for AI applications

Deployment & Engineering Excellence

  • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines for data and AI solutions using Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions
  • Strong understanding of automated testing, code quality, and build processes
  • Proven ability to deploy and manage applications in production using Docker, Kubernetes, and managed cloud services

Preferred Skills :

  • Experience working on enterprise-scale AI transformation initiatives.
  • Knowledge of MLOps practices and model lifecycle management.
  • Familiarity with Azure cloud services and modern data architecture.
  • Experience mentoring engineers and driving technical excellence within teams.
  • Strong problem-solving and stakeholder management skills.

Key Competencies

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and drive initiatives end-to-end.
  • Passion for emerging AI technologies and continuous learning.
  • Collaborative mindset with a focus on delivering business outcomes.

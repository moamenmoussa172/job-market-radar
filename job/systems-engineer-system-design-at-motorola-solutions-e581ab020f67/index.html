Department Overview

Motorola Solutions is a global leader in mission-critical communications and public safety technologies. Within our Defense & Tactical Systems business unit, we design, engineer, and integrate high-security, ruggedized, and interoperable voice and tactical data communications infrastructures (TETRA, mission-critical broadband, C4ISR, tactical radio systems) for armed forces and maritime security authorities. For our complex defense procurement, modernization, and system integration programs supporting the German Navy (surface vessels, submarines, and naval shore stations), we are seeking an experienced and dedicated Systems Engineer (System Design) (m/f/d).


Job Description

In this core systems engineering role, you will translate high-level functional and non-functional customer requirements into concrete, producible, and integrable system architectures for shipboard communications and tactical data transmission platforms:

  • Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE): Conceptualize, model, and detail both logical and physical system architectures using SysML/UML and modern MBSE toolsets (e.g., Enterprise Architect, Cameo Systems Modeler).
  • Requirements Decomposition & Specification: Decompose German Defence requirements baselines and author detailed subsystem- and component-level specifications (hardware, software, firmware) within the system design documentation framework per V-Modell XT Bund.
  • Detailed Interface Design (ICD): Define, coordinate, and maintain detailed Interface Control Documents (ICDs) governing data links, audio distribution networks, power supply architectures, and control protocols interfacing with third-party shipboard systems (such as Combat Management Systems / CMS, tactical intercoms, and navigation systems).
  • Naval Platform Integration & Mechanical Concepts: Design equipment racks, console integrations, antenna multicoupler architectures, and shipboard cabling layouts in strict compliance with naval environmental standards (shock, vibration, and climatic qualification per German VG standards).
  • RF & Cybersecurity Design Integration: Integrate cryptographic hardware/software, TEMPEST emission security and zoning principles in alignment with German BSI guidelines, and verify electromagnetic compatibility (EMC / VG 95373).


Basic Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s, Master’s degree, or Diploma in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Systems Engineering, Computer Science, Mechatronics, or an equivalent technical discipline.
  • Professional Experience: Minimum 3 years of hands-on engineering experience in system design, system architecture, or requirements-driven engineering for complex mechatronic or telecommunications systems—ideally within the defense, naval, or aerospace industry.
  • Methodologies & Tools: Strong command of Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE), SysML/UML modeling tools, and requirements management platforms (e.g., Jama Connect).
  • Technical Domain Knowledge: Solid understanding of communication networks (IP networking, tactical radio HF/VHF/UHF, TETRA, routing/switching, voice distribution) and fundamental principles of shipboard shock, vibration, and EMC engineering.
  • Standards & Lifecycles: Familiarity with structured systems engineering lifecycles (such as V-Modell XT) and defense engineering standards (MIL-STD, German VG standards, AQAP).
  • Languages: Professional business proficiency in German (for customer interface and technical documentation) and fluent English (written and spoken).
  • Security Clearance Requirement: Due to handling classified defense projects, the candidate must be eligible and willing to undergo a German security clearance (at least Ü2 according to the German Security Clearance Act – SÜG). German or EU citizenship is typically required.

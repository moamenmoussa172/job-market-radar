What can you expect from your 16 months internship at Bombardier?

From day one, you will contribute to the delivery of real projects using the latest technology to solve business problems. Over the course of your internship, you will receive one-on-one training and coaching from a team of talented experts and take on increasingly challenging tasks, while learning in a challenging and rewarding environment.

You will be part of the Air Systems Engineering / Pneumatics Systems discipline team where you will be involved in Aircraft Systems Engineering for Environmental Control, Bleed Air, Ice and Rain Protection, Cabin Pressurization, Fire/Smoke detection, Heaters, Oxygen, Flow distribution and Ventilation.

We are looking for a highly motivated Intern to assist aircraft integrators with researching technical issues, data analysis, interacting with various departments, facilitate meetings, progress tasks, maintain governance, and participate in technical discussions, while using various tools.

During your internship, you will contribute to:

  • Support Systems Engineering team in investigating technical issues & governance
  • Support data retrieval, analysis and presentation using various tools
  • Support engineering deliverables & governance by managing databases and following up on outstanding actions
  • Support Bombardier Operational Excellence System by tracking Key Performance Indicators (KPI) governance
  • Facilitate meetings, create presentation packages, issue meeting minutes in a timely manner
  • Develop good working relationships with various departments within Bombardier and with our system suppliers

During your internship, you will learn:

  • Working confidently on your own or as part of a team in a highly technical environment
  • Organization and support of technical meetings
  • Pneumatic systems and the ability to name components and their functions
  • System troubleshooting techniques and procedures
  • How to use available tools and databases
  • Aerospace component and aircraft certification standards

What we are looking for:

  • You are a student in aerospace, mechanical, or mechatronics engineering
  • You are working towards a Bachelor or Master Engineering degree
  • You have an understanding/familiarity in one or more of the following fields: aircraft system design, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, system control
  • You have good oral and written technical communication skills in English (French is an asset)
  • You are proficient in MS Office suite, especially Office365 applications and PowerBI
  • You possess good interpersonal skills in order to work well within the team and other groups
  • You are comfortable with the technical aspects of engineering as well as project management
  • You are able to work well independently and demonstrate drive and initiative to achieve the goals established
  • Previous technical work experience is an asset
  • Familiarity of component qualification (DO-160, MIL-STD-810) and airworthiness regulations (CAR 525, FAR 25 & CS 25) is an asset

Boarding Information:

  • Location: Global 7500/8000
  • Duration: 16 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. The base salary for this position is expected to range from CAD $17.95 to CAD $31.00 per hour. The applicable base salary will be determined based on various criteria, at Bombardier’s sole discretion, such as the candidate’s skills, experience and qualifications. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.

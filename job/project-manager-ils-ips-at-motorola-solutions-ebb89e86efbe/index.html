Department Overview

Motorola Solutions is a global leader in mission-critical communications and public safety technologies. Within our Defense & Tactical Systems division, we equip armed forces with resilient command, control, and tactical communications systems (TETRA, mission-critical broadband, C4ISR). To ensure long-term operational readiness, material maintenance, and comprehensive through-life support for our communication systems aboard German Navy platforms (frigates, corvettes, submarines, and naval shore stations), we are seeking a qualified Project Manager (ILS/IPS) (m/f/d).


Job Description

In this pivotal role, you will take full ownership of Integrated Logistics and Product Support across the entire system lifecycle—from development and procurement through commissioning, operational deployment, and naval in-service support:

  • Overall Logistics Planning: Develop, coordinate, and maintain the Integrated Logistics / Product Support Plan (ILSP / IPSP) in alignment with customer requirements (Defence), V-Modell XT Bund guidelines, and international specifications (e.g., ASD/AIA SX000i).
  • Logistics Support Analysis (LSA): Coordinate and conduct Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, and Safety (RAMS / FMECA) analyses in accordance with S3000L and MIL-HDBK-502 to optimize preventative maintenance routines and repair concepts.
  • Spare Parts Management & Provisioning: Define initial and replenishment spare parts packages (S2000M / NATO Codification) for onboard allowances and shipyard maintenance periods in coordination with naval arsenals and supply depots.
  • Technical Documentation: Supervise the development, quality assurance, and formal delivery of Interactive Electronic Technical Documentation (IETD according to S1000D and German Bundeswehr documentation standards) for system operation and maintenance.
  • Life Cycle Cost (LCC) & Obsolescence Management: Monitor life cycle costs, identify end-of-life/obsolescence risks early, and submit engineering change recommendations to the Change Control Board (CCB) in close collaboration with Configuration Management.
  • Stakeholder & Quality Assurance Liaison: Serve as the technical logistics point of contact for the German Defence project management team, the Bundeswehr Quality Assurance Service (under contractual AQAP quality standards), naval shipyards, and system sub-suppliers.


Basic Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Industrial Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Logistics Management, Systems Engineering, or a comparable military-technical background (e.g., former naval weapons/communications officer).
  • Professional Experience: Proven track record in Integrated Logistics Support (ILS), Integrated Product Support (IPS), or through-life technical service management for complex systems—ideally within the naval, defense, or aerospace industry.
  • Standards & Specifications: Thorough working knowledge of the ASD/AIA S-Series suite (S1000D, S2000M, S3000L) and familiarity with German defense procurement and lifecycle processes (CPM, V-Modell XT Bund).
  • System Understanding: Good technical grasp of shipboard communication and tactical radio networks, interfaces to combat systems, and the operating environments of naval platforms.
  • Methods & Tools: Experience with LSA databases, ERP systems, and PLM/ALM tools to ensure seamless data alignment for the As-Maintained configuration state.
  • Languages: Professional business proficiency in German (for customer negotiations and local naval documentation) and fluent English (written and spoken).
  • Due to the specific requirements of the project, candidates interested in this position will be required to undergo a background check in accordance with applicable legal requirements. German or EU citizenship may be required, depending on the applicable requirements.

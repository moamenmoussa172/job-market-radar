About the job

Who we are

Charles River Development (CRD) is the FinTech division of State Street. Our technology forms part of the State Street Alpha Platform, supporting investment managers around the world.

Our engineering organization is undertaking a major modernization of the Wealth Management platform, evolving a large, business-critical software estate toward a modern architecture and engineering model using technologies including Java, Azure, Kafka, Kubernetes and Snowflake.

And we're doing it while the business continues to run.

The challenge

This is a colossal engineering challenge.

We are modernizing a mature financial platform that our clients depend on every day. That means evolving architecture, introducing modern technologies and engineering practices, and reducing years of accumulated complexity without stopping the business.

There is legacy technology. There are dependencies between systems and teams. And yes, we are a large corporation, with the processes, controls and organizational complexity that come with operating technology in a highly regulated industry.

We are realistic about that. But we don't accept the status quo simply because "that's how things work in a large organization."

We want to simplify, automate and improve what we can, making engineering more effective and more enjoyable along the way.

For experienced engineers and leaders, this is an opportunity to learn something beyond another technology stack: how large-scale modernization actually works in the real world, where architecture, technology, business, risk and people all meet.

The role

As a Java Developer & Team Lead, you will combine hands-on engineering with technical and people leadership. You will lead a team contributing to the Wealth Management modernization while remaining close to architecture, design and implementation.

You will:

  • Lead a team of engineers from both a technical and people-management perspective
  • Design and deliver complex software changes using Java, Spring, Kafka, APIs and relational databases
  • Help evolve the platform toward a more modular, cloud-oriented architecture
  • Make pragmatic engineering decisions balancing modernization with the realities of a business-critical production system
  • Improve testing, CI/CD, observability, automation, reliability and developer experience
  • Coordinate technical work and dependencies across teams
  • Mentor engineers and support their technical and professional growth
  • Challenge unnecessary complexity and continuously improve how engineering gets done

Most importantly, we expect you to take ownership and help move difficult problems forward.

Qualifications

What we are looking for

  • 7+ years of professional Java development experience
  • 2+ years leading engineering teams, including people-management responsibilities
  • Strong knowledge of Java, Spring, SQL and distributed systems
  • Experience with Kafka, APIs and microservices
  • Experience leading teams of approximately 6–10 engineers

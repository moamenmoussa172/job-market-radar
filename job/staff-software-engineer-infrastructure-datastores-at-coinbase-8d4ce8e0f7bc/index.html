About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for "good enough," you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” Learn more about working at Coinbase.

The Datastores team owns Coinbase's production online databases and caches: Aurora, RDS Postgres, MongoDB Atlas, DynamoDB, ElastiCache (Valkey, Redis), and ClickHouse. We build the paved paths, tooling, and client libraries that let hundreds of engineering teams run reliable, secure, cost-efficient data layers serving millions of requests per second. We are hiring a Staff Software Engineer to help evolve our storage platform: consolidating how databases are provisioned and operated, building zonal resilience and disaster recovery into every production datastore, securing access to data, and giving teams well-paved ways to use it.

What you'll do

  • Shape the architecture and multi-year roadmap of Coinbase's storage platform across relational, document, key-value, and caching datastores.
  • Lead platform initiatives from design through production, such as consolidating databases onto shared, proxy-fronted clusters and building client libraries that make applications resilient to failover.
  • Drive resilience and disaster recovery for online datastores: zonal failover, backups and replication, restore tooling, and regular recovery validation.
  • Harden the security and compliance posture of our datastores: encryption and key management, least-privilege access, audit logging, and the controls and evidence that regulators and auditors expect.
  • Design for reliability, performance, and cost efficiency; set and defend SLOs, lead capacity planning, and eliminate recurring classes of incidents.
  • Partner with senior engineers and product-group leadership on database technology choices, and work directly with AWS, MongoDB, and other vendors.
  • Mentor engineers across the team, raise the bar on design reviews and operational excellence, and participate in the on-call rotation.

Required Skills and Experience

  • 8+ years of industry experience building and operating large-scale, highly available distributed systems in production.
  • Deep hands-on experience operating online databases at scale in production (e.g., PostgreSQL/Aurora, MongoDB, DynamoDB, Redis/Valkey, Memcached), including replication, failover, performance tuning, and version upgrades.
  • Solid understanding of distributed database architecture, consistency models, and the trade-offs between datastore technologies.
  • Experience securing data infrastructure in a regulated environment: encryption at rest and in transit, key management, access control, and audit requirements.
  • Strong backend development skills in Go, Java, or Python, with a track record of building database tooling, client libraries, or automation.
  • Experience leading cross-team technical initiatives, writing clear design documents, and influencing without direct authority.
  • Strong communication skills with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

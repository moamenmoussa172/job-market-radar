About the job

About Synechron

Synechron is a global digital transformation consulting firm partnering with leading financial institutions to accelerate their business and technology transformation.

We combine deep Financial Services expertise with capabilities across technology, digital, data and AI to help our clients design, build and deliver complex transformation programmes.

Our teams operate internationally, bringing together business consulting, technology expertise and global delivery capabilities to support some of the world's leading banks and financial institutions.

Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced Calypso Business Analyst to support functional analysis, business requirements and platform enhancements within a Capital Markets environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Gather, analyse and challenge business requirements.
  • Translate business needs into functional specifications and user stories.
  • Analyse Calypso functionalities, workflows and configuration requirements.
  • Conduct impact assessments and contribute to solution design.
  • Coordinate with technical teams, Product Owners and business stakeholders.
  • Support functional testing, UAT and issue resolution.
  • Contribute to Calypso enhancements and process improvements.

Required Skills

  • 8+ years of experience as a Calypso Business Analyst.
  • Strong functional knowledge of Nasdaq Calypso.
  • Understanding of Capital Markets products, including FX, Securities, Money Markets and Derivatives.
  • Experience in requirements gathering, functional specifications and testing.
  • Good SQL knowledge.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Calypso upgrades or migrations.
  • Unix/Linux knowledge.
  • Agile delivery experience.

About the job

In this role, you will be part of the creation of a new digital customer service hub in Brazil, established to support Bosch Mobility Aftermarket operations in North America. This strategic initiative aims to expand global customer support capabilities by combining operational excellence, customer proximity, and innovative digital solutions.

You will play a key role in building processes, implementing digital tools, and driving continuous improvement initiatives, while collaborating closely with global and cross-functional teams. Working in a data-driven environment supported by automation and artificial intelligence, you will contribute to enhancing service quality, operational efficiency, and the overall customer experience.

Key Responsibilities:

·Manage customer requests and inquiries through digital support channels, including email, chat, self-service platforms, and back-office processes.

·Ensure cases are handled efficiently, accurately, and with a strong customer focus, while meeting established service and quality standards.

·Support problem resolution, case follow-up, and escalation management in collaboration with internal stakeholders.

·Contribute to a professional, collaborative, and customer-centric team environment focused on service excellence.

#LI-MM1

Bachelor’s degree

·Knowledge of Microsoft Office, Power BI, and digital tools powered by Artificial Intelligence (AI).

·Low-code platform hands-on experience (e.g., Power Automate, N8N, Copilot Studio);

·Technical understanding of connecting automation workflows to external APIs;

·Knowledge and ability to write a basic SQL query to filter and retrieve structured operational data;

·Troubleshooting & Monitoring Deployed Automations: proactive maintenance, error handling, and debugging of active workflows

·Experience in customer service, customer operations, process support, process automation, or related areas.

·Experience in international companies or global operations is considered a strong advantage.

·Fluent English skills, with excellent written and verbal communication abilities.

·Reside in the Campinas region, with availability to work onsite at the office three times per week.

Key Competencies:

·Analytical and data-driven mindset, with experience in KPI analysis, problem-solving, and continuous process improvement.

·Strong customer orientation and commitment to delivering an excellent customer experience.

·Collaborative, organized, and proactive profile, with strong communication skills, results-oriented mindset, and the ability to adapt to dynamic and innovative environments.

Important:

- Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English.

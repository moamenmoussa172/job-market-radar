About the job

Internship Duration: 04 Jan 2027 - 25 Jun 2027

Internship Model: Full-time or at least 4 days/week

Application Period: 1 Nov – 16 Dec 2027

Interview date: 10, 15 & 17 Dec 2027

  • Work side by side with specialists and be entrusted with your own area of responsibility within the scope of your projects or day-to-day business.
  • Clarify and Analyze software requirement
  • Be responsible for software development, integration and test execution based on requirements
  • Support in low and high level design for software modules
  • Document results, report problems and debug if required
  • 3rd or final year student majoring in Computer Engineering/ Electrical - Electronics/ Telecommunications/ Automation/ Mechatronics or relevant background
  • Official university recommendation letter with the university stamp (mandatory). This document will be required after you successfully pass Bosch's interview process.
    Exception: For students from HCM University of Science and HCM University of Social Sciences and Humanities, the university will send the recommendation letter directly to Bosch. You do not need to obtain a separate copy yourself.
  • Strong in C/C++ programming language
  • Embedded knowledge is a must
  • Exposure to Micro-controllers or Microprocessors
  • Nice to have: scripting language (Python, Perl); Communication Protocols; Sensors
  • Good English communication skills

Important note:

  • Graduates or applicants who cannot submit a university recommendation letter (with official stamp from university) are not eligible to apply.
  • Due to the high number of applications received, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. Thank you for your understanding.

  • Engage in our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professional skills

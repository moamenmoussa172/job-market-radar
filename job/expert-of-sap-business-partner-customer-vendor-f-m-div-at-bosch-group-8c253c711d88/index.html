About the job

This role’s mission is to manage, protect, and maximize the value of an organization's data assets by ensuring
they are accurate, accessible, secure, and compliant. You will act as business custodians who implement data
governance policies, improve data quality, and bridge the gap between technical teams and business users.

Your contribution to something big:

  • Defines, aligns and executes product roadmap together with product owner and product teams;
  • Close alignment with product owner, central IT, agile master and external service provider to observe current and plan future sprints;
  • Responsible to steer the test team and approve user stories;
  • Operatively driving migration, including data cleansing, and 3rd level support (handling tickets, support to lower levels, bug fixing in daily business);
  • Supports technical and solution concepts based on business requirements and architectural guidelines with enterprise architects;
  • Responsible for the complete IT delivery of the B usiness Partner (Customer/Vendor) in an agile environment.

What distinguishes you:

Education

  • Master’s or Bachelor’s degree in Management, Business Administration, Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related discipline (or equivalent practical professional experience).

Experience

  • Around 3+ years of professional experience in a similar role combining enterprise SAP systems with business process design.
  • SAP Project Management (Functional Test, Integration Test, User Acceptance Test, technical Go-Live and Productive Go-live)
  • Demonstrated track record of leading or facilitating projects with close collaboration between IT units and business user departments.
  • Getting Requirements from business and implement the E2E Process/Concept into SAP (or ERP Systems)
  • High-level understanding and explanation (-Support for wording needed-) of IT-system architecture related to business partner flows

Know-how

  • Solid functional understanding of SAP S/4HANA, with practical exposure to key modules such as MM, SD, FI/CO, or the Business Partner (BP) model with their concepts.
  • Familiarity with enterprise data architecture concepts, IT infrastructure principles, or data management standards is a strong asset.

Languages

  • Fluent English and Portuguese (written and verbal) are required for effective daily collaboration across international teams.
  • Knowledge of German is considered a welcome advantage.

Working Style and Methods

  • Analytical, structured, and strategic problem solver capable of translating abstract requirements into actionable project goals.
  • Agile mindset with adaptability to evolving technological and business landscapes.

Personality

  • Collaborative team player who thrives in a diverse, global work environment and values purposeful in-person collaboration.

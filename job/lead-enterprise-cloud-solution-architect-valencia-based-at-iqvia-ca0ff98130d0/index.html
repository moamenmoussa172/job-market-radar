About the job

With approximately 90,000 employees in more than 100 countries, IQVIA is a leading global provider of advanced analytics, technology solutions, and clinical research services for the healthcare industry.

IQVIA is looking for a Lead Enterprise Cloud Solution Architect for the implementation of IQVIA HIS across 35 hospitals within the Valencia healthcare system and serve as Technical Design Autorithy for a major transformation program.

This strategic leadership position will provide end-to-end architectural governance across enterprise architecture, solution architecture, cloud infrastructure, interoperability, security, identity management and business continuity. The successful candidate will combine strategic vision with deep technical expertise to ensure a secure, scalable and resilient healthcare ecosystem.

Key Responsibilities

  • Define and govern the overall architecture strategy for the Valencia HIS program.
  • Act as the primary architectural authority across enterprise, solution and cloud architecture domains.
  • Lead the design, evolution and governance of AWS-based cloud environments supporting IQVIA HIS deployment.
  • Own infrastructure strategy, capacity planning, scalability, resilience, performance and cloud cost optimization activities.
  • Ensure seamless integration between IQVIA solutions, customer systems, interoperability platforms and third-party applications.
  • Provide architectural leadership across healthcare interoperability, identity management, cybersecurity, disaster recovery and business continuity domains.
  • Lead technical discussions with AWS specialists, customer stakeholders, vendors and delivery teams.
  • Identify architectural risks and define sustainable solutions supporting long-term evolution of the healthcare ecosystem.
  • Coordinate global IQVIA technical experts and ensure governance across all technical workstreams.
  • Support rollout planning and architectural readiness across 35 hospitals

Qualifications & Experience

  • Master's Degree (MECES Level 3), Engineering Degree, Bachelor's Degree or equivalent qualification officially recognized in Spain.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience as a technical lead or team leader in project delivery and technology programs.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience designing and implementing infrastructure supporting Electronic Health Records, Hospital Information Systems or comparable healthcare platforms.
  • Extensive experience in Enterprise Architecture, Solution Architecture, Cloud Architecture or large-scale digital transformation initiatives.
  • Strong healthcare IT background and experience working with mission-critical clinical systems.
  • Experience engaging executive stakeholders, healthcare organizations and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Experience with hybrid cloud environments combining cloud services and local infrastructure.
  • Professional knowledge of interoperability standards like HL7 and FHIR
  • Security Architecture, Identity and Access Management and cloud governance
  • Complex systems integration and API-led architectures
  • Technical leadership and ability to engage with different stakeholders
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Problem solving and decision making, customer focus attitude with ability to translate business requirements into sustainable actions
  • Native Spanish and Catalan, Professional English ( minimum B2)

Certifications

  • Required: AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional
  • Preferred: Microsoft Certified Azure Solutions Architect Expert
  • Preferred: Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect
  • Preferred: VMware Certified Professional – Data Center Virtualization (VCP-DCV)
  • Preferred: Azure Virtual Desktop Specialty or equivalent
  • Preferred: Certified Cloud Security Professional (CCSP) or equivalent

Location: Valencia, Spain (Hybrid Formula)

About the job

Marketing Analyst / Intern (Part-Time)

About Implicit

Innovation is at the heart of what we do. At Implicit, we have built the world's first No-Code Knowledge Engine for building AI Experts. Our platform enables teams to turn multiple content sources into powerful, production-ready AI systems without writing code.

Check out the app here (free account): app.implicitcloud.com/register

The Role

We're seeking a part-time Marketing Analyst or Intern to help build our marketing presence and create compelling content that communicates our value to customers. This is a hands-on role suited for someone who wants to learn B2B SaaS marketing while contributing meaningfully to a growing company.

Responsibilities

  • Build public AI experts that showcase Implicit's value
  • Plan and execute social media campaigns across LinkedIn, Reddit, X, and other relevant platforms
  • Track campaign performance and report on key metrics
  • Research competitors and industry trends to inform messaging
  • Support content creation for the website, blog, and email campaigns
  • Assist with event marketing and webinar promotion as needed
  • Nurture and support community of AI Expert builders

Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or related field
  • Strong writing skills with ability to translate technical concepts into clear, compelling content
  • Strong research skills to build public AI experts on diverse topics
  • Familiarity with social media platforms, particularly LinkedIn
  • Self-starter comfortable working independently
  • Interest in B2B technology and enterprise software
  • Experience with design tools (Canva, Figma) or marketing platforms (HubSpot, Hootsuite) a plus

Details

  • Type: Part-time (approximately 20 hours per week)
  • Duration: 8 weeks (possibility of extension)
  • Location: Remote
  • Compensation: $20 to $25 per hour (based on experience)
  • Experience Level: Student, recent graduate, or early-career professional

About this Role

We are seeking a Business Assurance Controls & Analytics Specialist to independently investigate financial and operational anomalies, execute evidence-based assurance controls, and deliver clear findings that help protect revenue, margin, billing accuracy and customer-account integrity.
Based in Albania within Finance Services and Business Assurance, the role combines financial control, investigation and reconciliation with practical data analytics. You will use tools including Excel, SQL and BI platforms and contribute to selected AI, Copilot and workflow automation opportunities that improve the efficiency and scalability of assurance activities.

What you will do

• Execute risk-based controls and reviews across contract-to-invoice processes, customer accounts, service configuration, usage, billing, charging and customer profitability.
• Investigate anomalies and potential financial leakage by tracing transactions, validating evidence, identifying root causes and documenting clear conclusions.
• Reconcile financial and operational data from multiple sources, ensuring results are accurate, repeatable and supported by a complete audit trail.
• Prepare financial-impact reports covering identified, prevented and recovered exposure, including associated risks, causes and recommended corrective actions.
• Contribute to reporting and automation improvements using SQL, BI, AI, Microsoft 365 Copilot or workflow tools where these can reduce manual effort and strengthen control execution.
• Work across Finance, Commercial, Billing, IT, Network, data and operational teams, using evidence-based findings to support corrective actions.

Who you are

• You have a strong investigation and financial-control mindset and can take an initial anomaly through evidence gathering to a substantiated root cause.
• You demonstrate excellent attention to detail, accuracy, ownership and persistence when handling complex, incomplete or high-volume information.
• You have strong financial reporting and reconciliation capabilities and can quantify financial impact and communicate findings clearly.
• You have advanced Microsoft Excel or equivalent skills, including pivot tables, lookups, data validation, reconciliation and structured reporting.
• You have practical SQL knowledge that enables you to retrieve, combine, validate and reconcile data. Advanced SQL is desirable rather than the primary focus of the role.
• You have experience with at least one BI or reporting solution, such as Power BI, MicroStrategy, Tableau or a comparable platform.
• Familiarity with Microsoft 365 Copilot, Power Automate, Copilot Studio or comparable AI and automation solutions is an advantage.
• Programming or scripting knowledge in Python, R, JavaScript, PHP or a similar language is desirable.
• You hold a bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, Data Analytics, Business Informatics or a comparable field.
• You bring at least three years of relevant professional experience in financial control, business assurance, investigation, reconciliation, data analysis or a comparable specialist role.
• You have practical experience analysing large volumes of financial and operational data and producing evidence-based reports.
• English at B2 level or above and Italian at B2 level or above.
• You can prioritise independently, meet deadlines and collaborate effectively with business and technical stakeholders.

What's in it for you

This role provides the opportunity to work on business-critical assurance activities that directly support billing accuracy, margin protection, reliable reporting and customer-account integrity.
You will work across Finance, Commercial, Billing, IT, Network, data and operational teams, giving you broad exposure to both business and technical stakeholders. The position also provides opportunities to apply SQL, BI, AI, Copilot and workflow tools to improve reporting and automate selected assurance activities.

What skills you will learn

Through this role, you will have opportunities to further develop:

• Advanced investigation and root-cause analysis across financial and operational data.
• Risk-based financial controls, reconciliations and evidence-based assurance techniques.
• Financial-impact assessment and reporting, including identification of prevented or recovered exposure.
• Practical applications of SQL and BI tools to assurance and reconciliation activities.
• Automation and productivity improvement using AI, Microsoft 365 Copilot and workflow technologies.
• Cross-functional collaboration across Finance, Commercial, Billing, IT, Network, data and operational teams.

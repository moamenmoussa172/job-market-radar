About the job

At Synechron, we harness the power of digital transformation to drive business success. As a global consulting firm, we combine creativity with advanced technology to deliver innovative solutions across industries. Recognized with multiple employer awards, we are committed to building talented teams and creating a dynamic work environment.

We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer

Hybrid working model - Novi Sad or Belgrade office

Join our dynamic team of 30+ skilled ML professionals!

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop, test, and deploy advanced machine learning models that transform business processes
  • Tackle complex challenges by designing innovative AI-driven solutions
  • Work closely with diverse teams from around the world to deliver breakthrough results
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define and implement new features
  • Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in cloud development and database technologies
  • Take advantage of extensive training programs and professional development opportunities to stay at the forefront of AI

Your profile:

  • A minimum of 4 years in software development with a strong focus on Python
  • Proficient in Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models
  • Experience with cloud technologies, Docker, REST APIs, and various database systems, including SQL, NoSQL, and vector databases
  • Proven ability to design, develop, test, and deploy high-quality, scalable software solutions
  • Strong problem-solving skills and an analytical mindset with keen attention to detail
  • A passion for exploring new technologies with a track record of implementing cutting-edge techniques
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively in a goal-oriented environment
  • Fluency in English is required
  • BA/BS in Computer Science or a related technical field
  • Experience working with Generative AI applications, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines and agent-based AI systems is considered a plus

What’s in it for you:

  • Compensation package based on experience and skills.
  • Flexible hours and hybrid work options to achieve a work-life balance
  • Private health insurance plan.
  • Access to continuous training, accredited certifications, and unlimited Udemy courses to support professional development.
  • Personalized leadership training program.
  • FitPass membership and mental health support programs.
  • Comprehensive Referral Rewards Program.

Note: One of the prerequisites for this position is that the prospective candidate must possess valid work permit for Serbia.

We appreciate the interest of all applicants. Please note that only those whose qualifications align closely with the position requirements will be contacted for the next steps in the selection process.

All applications will be handled with confidentiality.

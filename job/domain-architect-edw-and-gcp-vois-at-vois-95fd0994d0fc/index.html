About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Domain Architect specialising in Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) to join our Software Engineering – BIBDA team in Pune, India.
The role will provide end-to-end architecture leadership across cloud and EDW solutions, with responsibility for designing secure, scalable and resilient GCP architectures. You will lead cloud migration and modernisation initiatives, establish cloud governance practices, optimise data workloads and provide technical direction to engineering teams.
The role requires 18–23 years of overall experience, including significant architecture leadership experience and strong expertise across GCP, EDW and cloud-native technologies.

What you’ll do

Design and implement secure, scalable and resilient GCP-based architectures.
Analyse business and technical requirements and translate them into effective cloud-native solutions.
Lead cloud migration programmes covering lift-and-shift, modernisation and greenfield implementations.
Collaborate with application development, operations and security teams to deliver high-quality cloud-native applications.
Establish cloud governance practices and support compliance with security and cost-management policies.
Evaluate and recommend new cloud services, capabilities and technologies based on business and project requirements.
Optimise data workflows for performance, scalability and cost efficiency using Terraform, Cloud Monitoring and GCP-native services.
Drive innovation through proof-of-concepts and evaluation of emerging cloud technologies.
Provide technical leadership and mentorship to engineering teams.
Take accountability for end-to-end application scoping, planning, development and delivery against agreed quality standards.
Support project management with technical estimates, delivery timelines and allocation of technical resources.
Contribute to process improvement, resource optimisation and adoption of appropriate tools to improve efficiency and quality.

Who you are

You have 18–23 years of overall technology experience, with substantial experience in cloud architecture and engineering.
You bring 5+ years of experience leading architecture practices, including areas such as Domain Architecture, EDW, GCP and AWS.
You have strong hands-on and architectural experience with Google Cloud Platform, including extensive exposure to GCP architecture roles.
You have experience in Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) architecture and engineering.
You have extensive knowledge of GCP services including Compute Engine, Cloud Run, BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Cloud Functions, Dataflow, Dataproc, IAM and Cloud Storage.
You understand networking, cloud security, containers and CI/CD practices end to end.
You have experience with cloud monitoring, logging and performance tuning.
You are familiar with DevSecOps practices and technologies such as HashiCorp Vault.
You can provide architecture leadership while collaborating effectively across engineering, application, operations and security teams.
You hold a B.E./B.Tech, BCA/MCA, or BSc/MSc in Computer Science or a related discipline.

What's in it for you

Shape enterprise-scale EDW and GCP architecture within a complex technology environment.
Lead cloud migration, modernisation and cloud-native solution initiatives from architecture through delivery.
Work across modern GCP data and application services, including BigQuery, Dataflow, Dataproc, Pub/Sub, Cloud Run and Cloud Functions.
Influence cloud governance, security, performance and cost-optimisation practices.
Provide technical leadership and mentorship while helping drive innovation through proofs-of-concept and emerging cloud technologies.

What skills you will learn

Enterprise-scale GCP and EDW architecture.
Cloud migration and application modernisation strategies.
GCP-native data engineering and cloud-native application patterns.
Cloud governance, security and cost optimisation.
Infrastructure as Code using Terraform.
Cloud monitoring, logging and performance optimisation.
DevSecOps and secure cloud architecture practices.
Technical leadership, architecture governance and cross-functional solution delivery.

About the job

AI Teammates are Asana’s flagship AI innovation - autonomous agents embedded directly into enterprise workflows that reason, act, and proactively drive work forward. The AI Agents/Teammates Platform (AITP) team owns the execution engine and platform layer that makes Asana's AI Teammates and other AI Agents effective, reliable, and scalable. We build the core systems behind agentic execution: model evaluation and rollouts, proactive detection of blocked work, execution quality infrastructure, tool orchestration, and the platform APIs, events, harness and agentic memory that power AI across all of Asana engineering. Rather than building a monolithic feature, we build a composable foundation. We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to drive key architectural initiatives, design performant agentic runtime systems, and help build a reliable foundation that scales to millions of users.

Location: San Francisco, office-centric hybrid (in office Monday, Tuesday, Thursday). Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements.

What You’ll Achieve

  • Drive the technical design, architecture, and end-to-end delivery of medium-to-large projects across the AI Teammates execution engine, tool orchestration, and platform APIs & events.
  • Architect and own composable platform foundations, harness, memory, partnering with product engineering teams to enable them to build agentic capabilities into their domains in a self-serve manner.
  • Design robust, extensible, and performant systems that support agentic execution, prompt-driven automation, tool integrations, and strict enterprise-grade reliability boundaries.
  • Solve complex technical problems across model evaluations, latency tuning, and cost optimization while balancing technical debt, execution risk, and direct business goals.
  • Move quickly by shipping in small increments, validating with real customer usage, evals, and metrics, and adjusting based on what the data shows rather than assumptions.
  • Anticipate customer and architectural risks early, breaking down complex agentic product visions into clear engineering milestones and actionable tasks.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of and accountability for outcomes, not just delivery, from design through production, quality, and customer impact.
  • Proactively identify gaps and opportunities, then propose and drive new features, platform capabilities, and re-architecture, backed by customer feedback and usage data.
  • Champion technical excellence, write high-leverage design docs, establish evaluation methodologies, and mentor engineers across levels to raise the engineering bar.
  • Partner closely with Product Management, Design, Data Science, and external model providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) to define requirements and align technical direction.

About You

  • 6+ years building and running production distributed systems, microservices, or complex backend architectures.
  • A track record as Tech Lead or AoR owner, driving multi-contributor projects from ambiguity to delivery across teams and services.
  • High ownership and accountability: outcomes are yours, you follow through, and you raise risks early and openly.
  • Product and customer-first: you start from the customer problem, use feedback and data to shape direction, and bring ideas rather than waiting for specs. You understand how architecture and model behavior affect user trust, latency, and reliability.
  • Bias for action grounded in reality: you move fast and decide from customer feedback, production data, and eval results, and you know when a decision needs more rigor and when it can be reversed cheaply.
  • Strong design-doc skills and sound trade-off judgment (latency, cost, determinism, maintainability).
  • Hands-on experience or deep interest in LLMs, agentic patterns, eval pipelines, RAG, or real-time streaming.
  • A quick learner who ramps fast on new domains, codebases, and the AI landscape.
  • A passion for guiding and mentoring engineers with actionable code and design feedback.

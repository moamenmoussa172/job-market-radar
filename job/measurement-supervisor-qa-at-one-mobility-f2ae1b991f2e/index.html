We are looking for

Measurement Supervisor (QA)

at one of our locations in Asia region - in Vietnam (Hai Duong City)

Lab & Measurement Responsibilities

  • Manage and control all measurement equipment, including equipment registration, calibration, verification, maintenance, and status control.
  • Plan and monitor internal and external calibration activities, ensuring all measuring equipment remains within its calibration validity.
  • Define and establish appropriate measurement methods molded connector measurements based on product specifications, drawings, and customer requirements.
  • Develop and maintain measurement procedures, work instructions, and measurement standards in the measurement room.
  • Plan and conduct MSA studies
  • Manage dimensional measurement activities for molded connectors daily, including measurement method selection and evaluation of measurement results.
  • Interpret engineering drawings and apply GD&T requirements to define appropriate measurement methods and evaluate dimensional results.
  • Control and review measurement data to ensure accuracy, consistency, and traceability.
  • Co-operate with Lab team in Korea and internal team to manage reliability testing activities, including test planning, sample preparation, data collection, and reporting.
  • Train and provide technical guidance to measurement personnel on equipment operation, measurement methods, GD&T, and MSA.
  • Communicate and coordinate directly with customers on measurement-related matters, including measurement methods, GD&T, MSA, equipment, and dimensional measurement results.
  • Bachelor’s degree or college diploma in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Metrology, Automotive Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in measurement, metrology, laboratory, or quality-related
  • Strong knowledge of dimensional measurement and measurement methods for mechanical, plastic, and molded components.
  • Good knowledge of measurement equipment and instruments, calibration, verification, and maintenance requirements.
  • Strong working knowledge of MSA, GD&T and the ability to interpret engineering drawings and dimensional requirements.
  • Experience in defining and establishing appropriate measurement methods based on product specifications and GD&T requirements.
  • Knowledge and experience in reliability testing, including test planning, sample preparation, test execution, and data analysis.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills, particularly in evaluating measurement data and identifying measurement-related issues.

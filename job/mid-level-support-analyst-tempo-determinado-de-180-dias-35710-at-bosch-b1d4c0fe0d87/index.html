About the job

We are seeking a dedicated and detail-oriented Alteryx Operations Administrator to join our team, supporting our global Data & Analytics platform. This role is focused on the day-to-day operational management and maintenance of Alteryx environment, ensuring high availability, performance, and adherence to established protocols.

Main Responsibilities:

  • Execute daily operational tasks: Perform routine health checks, system monitoring, and verify the status of Alteryx Servers.
  • User and Permission Management: Process requests for user creation, modification, deletion, group assignments, and project-level permissions within established guidelines for Alteryx Servers.
  • Content Management Support: Assist with the promotion, deletion, and organization of workflows and data sources as per client requests and procedures.
  • Data Refresh Management: Monitor and troubleshoot scheduled data refreshes, identifying and resolving failures in a timely manner.
  • Incident Response: Respond to and resolve operational incidents related to Alteryx Servers, including server outages, service interruptions, and dashboard loading issues, following defined runbooks and escalation procedures.
  • Performance Monitoring: Track and report on key performance indicators (KPIs) and resource utilization for Alteryx environments using existing monitoring tools.
  • System Maintenance: Assist with scheduled maintenance activities such as restarts, cleanup tasks, and log management for Alteryx Server.
  • Documentation Adherence: Follow and update existing operational documentation, standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • Request Fulfillment: Process and fulfill operational requests from users and other IT teams related to the Alteryx platform.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with senior administrators and client IT teams to escalate complex issues and ensure seamless service delivery.
  • Testing: Testing and knowledge transfer for product enhancements
  • Consulting customers e. g. In ETL use cases

Minimum Qualifications

  • Min. bachelor’s degree in computer science, Mathematics, Engineering, Information Systems or a related field.
  • Practical experience in an IT operations or administrative role.
  • Hands-on experience with administering Alteryx or other Business Analytical Servers at an operational capacity.
  • Proficiency in executing routine administrative tasks within Alteryx environments.
  • Familiarity with server operating systems (Windows Server) for basic operational checks.
  • Experience with monitoring tools (preferred New Relic) for system health and performance.
  • Strong adherence to operational procedures and attention to detail.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills with a focus on quick resolution of operational issues.
  • Ability to work in a structured environment and follow established processes.
  • Fluent in English.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Basic understanding of SQL for data source verification and troubleshooting.
  • Hands-on experience with administering Alteryx or other Business Analytical Servers at an operational capacity.
  • Proficiency in executing routine administrative tasks within Alteryx environments.
  • Familiarity with ticketing systems (e.g., Jira, ServiceNow) for managing requests and incidents.
  • Basic understanding of network concepts (e.g., firewalls, proxies) relevant to server connectivity.
  • Knowledge of ITIL processes (Incident, Request, Problem, Change Management).
  • Experiences in KNIME which is like Alteryx or another Business Analytics environment

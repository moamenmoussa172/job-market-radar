About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
• OT Asset Discovery and Validation: Discover OT assets, connected devices and communication protocols across all in-scope plants, including unmanaged assets. Apply a safe mix of passive and active discovery and data collection methods and validate every finding with plant personnel.

• Asset Classification and Data Acquisition: Classify every discovered asset in the client's data acquisition template by asset type, function, criticality (Critical, High, Medium, Low), owner (business owner, technical owner, support team, OEM) and location (plant, shop, line, zone, station). Capture technical details such as OEM, model, controller, firmware/software versions, IP, switch and VLAN details, protocols, end-of-life status and internet connectivity.

• OT Network Mapping: Create visual OT network architecture diagrams, segment by segment, showing OT device local networks, OT system switches and OT device IP addresses. Identify segmentation gaps and undocumented connections.

• OT Risk Assessment and IEC 62443 Alignment: Define and apply the OT risk scoring methodology and provide a written rationale for every asset's criticality rating. Assess findings against IEC 62443, including zones and conduits and security levels, and produce a risk register with prioritized, production-safe recommendations.

• Methodology, Reporting and Stakeholder Management: Define assessment methodology, tools, validation process and report review process.

• Lead solution design and technical responses for RFPs, RFIs and proactive proposals across OT/ICS security, product security, AI security and penetration testing (PTEST).

• Conduct discovery workshops with client CISOs, security and engineering teams to capture requirements, pain points and compliance drivers.

• Architect end-to-end solutions covering assessments, managed services, secure product development and subscription-based commercial models; define scope, effort, staffing and delivery approach.

• Deliver client presentations, demos and proof-of-concepts; anchor technical due-diligence and clarification rounds.

• Map solutions to regulatory and standards frameworks such as IEC 62443, CERT-In, NCSC CAF, EU CRA, EU AI Act, FDA / EU MDR and IEC 81001-5-1.

• Collaborate with delivery partner and product teams (including AI security and OT IDS partners) to build reusable offerings, accelerators and reference architectures.

• Support pricing and commercial construct decisions with effort estimates, risk assessment and assumptions.

• Contribute to GTM collateral, thought leadership and analyst / partner engagements.

· As Solution Architect you are supposed to work with Cybersecurity Presales Team on RFP , RFI , Proactive Cybersecurity Opp to solution it. Work with SMEs to capture Technical inputs and stich it for overall Solution story line in the proposal.

· You will partner with sales, presales and business development to understand client Cybersecurity challenges, shape winning solutions, and translate BGSW’s cybersecurity portfolio into compelling proposals for clients across India, UK, EU, Nordics, Middle East and North America.

• BTech in computer science, Electronics & Instrumentation, Cyber Security, Engineering, or a related field.

• Demonstrated experience performing OT risk assessments (asset criticality, threat and vulnerability analysis, risk scoring and prioritized remediation).

• Strong working knowledge of IEC 62443, coupled with the ability to translate a compliance requirement directly into a technical control such as a firewall rule or network segmentation design. Familiarity with NERC CIP, NIST SP 800-82 and MITRE ATT&CK for ICS is a plus.

• Hands-on experience with OT discovery and security monitoring tools (for example Claroty, Nozomi Networks, Dragos, Tenable OT or Microsoft Defender for IoT), and with Wireshark and OT-safe use of Nmap.

• Foundational knowledge of networking protocols, firewalls, VPNs, IDS/IPS and industrial switching, with a strong understanding of network segmentation and the Purdue Model applied to OT environments.

• Deep understanding of how industrial protocols function, such as Modbus, EtherNet/IP, PROFINET, OPC UA and MQTT.

• Working knowledge of common industrial control platforms (PLC, CNC, HMI, SCADA and robot controllers) and an understanding of the risks of scanning fragile or legacy devices.

• Certifications such as CISSP, CISM, GICSP, ISA/IEC 62443, OSCP, CEH or CCSP.

• Exposure to automotive, manufacturing, energy & utilities, or healthcare / MedTech sectors.

Experience :

• 10-14 years of experience in OT / ICS cybersecurity, with at least 2 years in presales, solution architecture or consulting roles, including hands-on delivery of OT asset discovery and network mapping in live manufacturing environments, ideally automotive or discrete manufacturing.

• Strong understanding of two or more domains: OT/ICS & IIoT security, product / embedded security, application security, cloud security, or AI/ML security.

• Hands-on familiarity with security assessments, penetration testing methodologies, threat modelling and secure SDLC.

• Working knowledge of relevant standards and regulations (IEC 62443, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, CERT-In guidelines, EU CRA / NIS2, MedTech cybersecurity guidance).

• Proven ability to write high-quality proposals and present to CxO and technical audiences.

• Experience engaging clients across multiple geographies; comfortable with travel and cross-time-zone collaboration.

• Excellent communication, stakeholder management and commercial acumen.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent written communication for reports and network diagrams.

• Willingness to travel to multiple plant locations and to follow plant safety and confidentiality requirements.

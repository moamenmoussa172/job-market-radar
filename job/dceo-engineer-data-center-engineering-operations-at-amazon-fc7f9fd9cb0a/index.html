DCEO Engineer, Data Center Engineering Operations

Amazon · Paris, France

Onsite · Other data role · Posted 20 November 2026 · Visa / relocation

AWS

About the job

We are looking for a Data Center Engineering Operations Technician to join our team at an AWS lease/hybrid data center in Paris. In this highly hands-on, field-based role, you will perform direct operation and maintenance of the electrical and mechanical infrastructure that powers AWS cloud services.
Unlike colocation environments, you will be responsible for hands-on preventive and corrective maintenance, equipment repairs, and emergency response. The role requires a motivated, self-managed individual who demonstrates initiative and proactively seeks solutions to problems while striving for operational excellence.
You will be part of the Engineering and Facilities team, responsible for maintaining availability for customers, ensuring maintenance and operation of technical infrastructure, and the upkeep of the data center facility.
Key job responsibilities
Safety
Follow and maintain the highest safety standards and encourage a world-class safety culture
Lead by example in implementing and maintaining safety protocols
Respond to emergency situations and coordinate with emergency services as needed
Engineering & Facility Operations and Maintenance
Hands-on operation and maintenance of electrical and mechanical equipment including UPS systems, switchgear, circuit breakers, automatic transfer switches, diesel generators, chillers, HVAC systems, exhaust fans, variable frequency drives, and transformers
Operate, monitor, and maintain fire detection and suppression systems including pre-action systems, VESDA, and other fire protection equipment
Perform routine inspections and testing of fire alarm panels, smoke detectors, and suppression systems
Troubleshoot operational events within internal Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
Take daily operational readings of all mechanical and electrical equipment through routine rounds/log-taking
Perform hands-on preventive and corrective maintenance activities on critical infrastructure
Execute equipment repairs, replacements, and facility modifications in accordance with standards
Respond to alarms and perform emergency troubleshooting and repairs
Coordinate and oversee contractor work when specialized services are required
Maintain accurate documentation of all maintenance activities and equipment performance
Manual handling and lifting of equipment, including working on ladders and at height, may be required (weight limits in accordance with health & safety regulations)
Leverage AI-powered tools to improve operational efficiency, troubleshooting, and data analysis
Generate and contribute to documentation relevant to business and facility operations, including Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
Work Schedule
This is a 100% in-person onsite position based in [Specific location]. The role operates on a shift-based schedule to ensure 24/7 coverage of data center operations, which may include day shifts, night shifts, weekends, and holidays.

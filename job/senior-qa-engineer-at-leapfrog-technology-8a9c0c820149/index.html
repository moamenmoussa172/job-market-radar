About Leapfrog

Leapfrog is on a mission to be a role model technology company. Since 2010, we have relentlessly worked on crafting better digital products with our team of superior engineers. We’re a full-stack company specializing in SaaS products and have served over 100 clients with our mastery of emerging technologies.

Hundreds of companies in Boston, Seattle, Silicon Valley, and San Francisco choose us to gain speed, agility, quality, and stability, giving them an edge over their competitors.

  • Total work Experience as QA should be 5+ years
  • Leadership:
  • Lead and mentor the QA team, providing technical guidance and best practices.
  • Establish and maintain QA workflows, processes, and strategies to ensure efficient testing and high-quality product delivery.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to ensure project goals are met.
  • Test Strategy & Planning:
  • Develop and execute comprehensive test plans, strategies, and test cases.
  • Identify, document, and manage test data requirements.
  • Automation Testing:
  • Design, develop, and maintain automation frameworks using tools such as Cypress, Selenium, Playwright and Page Object Model (POM).
  • Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines to integrate automated testing.
  • Write, execute, and maintain automated test scripts for web applications and APIs.
  • Manual Testing:
  • Conduct manual testing for exploratory, usability, cross-browser, and responsive UI testing when necessary.
  • Execute regression and sanity testing based on release plans.
  • AI Enablement Skills
  • AI-Assisted Test Design
  • AI-Assisted Automation Development
  • Prompt Engineering for QA
  • AI for Defect Analysis
  • AI-Driven Exploratory Testing
  • Defect Management:
  • Identify, report, and track defects to resolution using tools like JIRA and Test Rail.
  • Perform root cause analysis of defects and recommend improvements.
  • Documentation:
  • Prepare and maintain test documentation, including test plans, test cases, bug reports, and release notes.
  • Stakeholder Communication:
  • Provide application demos to stakeholders.
  • Communicate testing status, risks, and mitigation plans.

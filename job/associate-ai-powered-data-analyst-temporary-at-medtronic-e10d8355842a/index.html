About the job

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact.

A Day in the Life

The NextGen Academy is a 6-month immersive program designed to launch your career in medical technology, offering hands-on experience and collaboration with diverse teams to support therapies and introduce innovative products across the region. The program is designed to develop you into a confident and capable future AI-Powered Data Analyst, aligned with Medtronic's mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life.
Based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, this is a temporary position with a Flex working model. You will support the Executive Office by applying artificial intelligence (AI), automation, and data analytics to strengthen decision-making, productivity, and knowledge management.

As an AI-Powered Data Analyst within the Executive Office, you will gain hands-on experience developing AI-powered assistants, automating business workflows, and transforming data and organizational knowledge into actionable insights for senior leadership. You will work across technology, data, and business functions while building practical experience with Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, Power BI, SharePoint, and Power Automate.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Organize executive knowledge assets, including presentations, business reviews, meeting notes, and strategic documents.
  • Develop AI and Microsoft Copilot solutions to support leadership decision-making and productivity.
  • Automate business processes, reporting, meeting summaries, and action tracking.
  • Integrate data from Microsoft 365, Power BI, SharePoint, and other business systems.
  • Build dashboards and insights to support business reviews and strategic initiatives.
  • Support executive AI assistants and digital knowledge solutions.
  • Partner with IT, Digital, Data & Analytics, and business stakeholders to identify and implement AI and automation opportunities.
  • Maintain governance, confidentiality, and data security standards when handling executive information.

Required Qualifications

  • Experience or academic exposure to artificial intelligence, generative AI, data analytics, business intelligence, or automation.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Copilot Studio and Power Platform.
  • Knowledge of Power BI, SharePoint, and Power Automate.
  • Ability to work with confidential information and engage effectively with senior stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience developing AI-powered assistants or workflow automation solutions.
  • Experience integrating data across Microsoft 365 or other business systems.
  • Experience building dashboards, reports, or data-driven insights.
  • Understanding of how to translate business requirements into practical AI, analytics, or automation solutions.

Recruitment Fraud Alert

We are aware of phishing scams targeting job seekers. Please keep the following in mind:

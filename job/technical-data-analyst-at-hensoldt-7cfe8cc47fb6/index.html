About the job

About the Role

The Technical Data Analyst designs, develops, deploys and maintains data-driven models, automated workflows, dashboards and digital applications using programming and approved AI-enabled tools to optimise stock, demand and capacity planning, improve operational efficiency and support informed decision-making across the product development and production lifecycle.

Qualifications and experience:

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification in Data Science, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Statistics or a related technical discipline.
  • Minimum 4 years’ relevant experience in data analytics, modelling, workflow automation or data-driven solution development.
  • Proven ability to design, code, test, deploy and maintain data models, automated workflows and software applications.
  • Practical experience in statistical analysis, predictive modelling, forecasting and integrating and automating data from multiple sources.
  • Experience developing dashboards, applications and automated decision-support tools, from prototype through to deployment and ongoing support.
  • Practical experience using Artificial Intelligence (AI)-enabled development and analytics tools.
  • Experience in a high-technology engineering, manufacturing or product development environment will be advantageous.
  • Exposure to production and systems integration lifecycles will be advantageous.
  • Working knowledge of SAP R/3 and SAP data import and export interfaces will be advantageous.

Key technical skills:

  • Software and application development.
  • Data analytics and statistical evaluation.
  • Predictive modelling, forecasting and time-series analysis.
  • Demand planning, stock and inventory modelling.
  • Capacity and resource planning.
  • Data modelling, transformation and integration.
  • Workflow automation.
  • Data validation and quality management.
  • AI-assisted analytics and development.
  • Rapid application and Minimum Viable Product (MVP) prototyping.
  • Business intelligence, dashboards and decision-support tools.
  • Advanced spreadsheet modelling and automation.
  • Programming and scripting for data analysis and automation.
  • Database querying and data extraction.
  • Requirements analysis and process mapping.
  • Systems and process integration.

Key responsibilities:

Data Analytics, Modelling and Predictive Planning

Analyse operational, engineering and planning data to develop analytical and predictive models for stock optimisation, demand forecasting, capacity planning and resource allocation, improving planning accuracy, proactive decision-making and organisational resource utilisation.

Workflow Automation and Systems Integration

Design, develop and integrate automated workflows across stock, demand, capacity and operational planning processes to replace fragmented manual activities with reliable digital solutions that improve efficiency, consistency and data integrity.

AI-Enabled Solution Development and Rapid Prototyping

Develop, test, deploy and maintain analytical models, automation logic and internal applications using approved AI platforms, programming tools and development environments to accelerate digitalisation and deliver scalable alternatives to spreadsheet-based and manual processes.

Operational Data Optimisation and Decision Support

Collaborate with Engineering, Product Development, Production and Operational stakeholders to identify requirements and translate data gaps, planning constraints and business needs into structured analytical solutions, dashboards and decision-support tools that enable timely, evidence-based decisions.

Data Governance, Solution Support and Performance

Maintain the accuracy, integrity, traceability and reliability of data, models and automated solutions through validation controls, documentation, user support, performance monitoring and continuous refinement to ensure dependable solutions that meet evolving operational requirements.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

As a 24/7 Cloud Operations Engineer, you will be at the center of SAP's AI strategy — directly responsible for the reliability of the platforms that make it possible. Our team operates and safeguards AI Platform, AI Core, Joule, Business Data Cloud (BDC): the foundational components enabling SAP's AI offering for customers worldwide. The availability of these platforms is a prerequisite for SAP's AI adoption — and ensuring it is what your work is built around.
This role goes beyond traditional operations. We are looking for engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges, are curious about how public cloud systems work, and continuously seek opportunities to improve reliability through automation, operational excellence, and AI-driven operations such as human judgment, supervision, and accountability layer in an AI-assisted operational environment — working alongside Gen AI-driven communication and automated remediation agents, while maintaining clear ownership of production outcomes in line with SAP's Human–AI Collaboration principle.
What you’ll build:
• Monitor and operate SAP’s cloud services, ensuring high availability and service reliability.
• Investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve incidents across the technology stack, coordinating restoration activities with development and platform engineering teams.
• Drive effective communication during incidents, including stakeholder updates, technical bridge calls, and coordination across multiple Lines of Business.
• Collaborate with AI agents and leverage AI-driven automation workflows to accelerate incident detection, triage and resolution
• Leverage AI-powered operational tools, intelligent observability, and AIOps capabilities to accelerate incident resolution.
• Work alongside AI agents to automate repetitive operational tasks, enhance troubleshooting, and continuously improve operational efficiency.
• Identify opportunities to build and adopt AI-assisted workflows that improve service reliability, engineer productivity, and customer experience.
• Provision, maintain, and continuously improve cloud environments.
• Execute planned change activities within agreed maintenance windows
What you bring
We’re looking for engineers who are naturally curious, eager to learn, and motivated to solve challenging problems. You enjoy working in collaborative environments, embrace new technologies, and are excited by how AI is transforming cloud operations.
• Experience in Cloud Operations, Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, Infrastructure Operations, Technical Support or a related technical field.
• Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills, with the ability to investigate issues across multiple layers of the technology stack.
• Good understanding of Kubernetes, Cloud Foundry, and public cloud platforms (AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud).
• Experience with observability platforms such as Grafana, Kibana, Dynatrace, Prometheus, or similar solutions.
• A curiosity for emerging AI technologies and a willingness to leverage AI agents and automation to improve operational efficiency and accelerate incident resolution.
• A proactive mindset with a strong sense of ownership, continuous learning, and continuous improvement.
• Excellent collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively in an international environment.
• Fluency in English, both written and spoken.
The following would be considered an advantage:
• Understanding of Linux systems, networking fundamentals, distributed systems, and cloud-native architectures.
• Experience integrating AI copilots, AI agents, or GenAI-powered workflows into engineering or operational processes.
• Experience building automation using Python, Go, Bash, or similar languages.
• Experience with Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, and GitOps practices.
• Experience working in large-scale SaaS or mission-critical production environments.
Where you belong:
As part of SAP’s Analytics and Intelligent Technologies organization, you’ll join a 24/7 engineering team responsible for operating some of SAP’s most strategic cloud services, such as Joule, Business Data Cloud, SAP Analytics Cloud, Datasphere, AI Core etc.
Our mission is evolving beyond traditional cloud operations. We are embracing automation, intelligent observability, AIOps, and AI agents to build the next generation of cloud operations. Our engineers partner closely with development, platform engineering to create smarter operational workflows, reduce time to resolution, and continuously improve customer experience.
If you’re excited about cloud technologies, enjoy solving complex problems, and want to help shape the future of AI-enabled operations at SAP, we’d love to meet you.

Bring out your best
SAP innovations help more than four hundred thousand customers worldwide work together more efficiently and use business insight more effectively. Originally known for leadership in enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, SAP has evolved to become a market leader in end-to-end business application software and related services for database, analytics, intelligent technologies, and experience management. As a cloud company with two hundred million users and more than one hundred thousand employees worldwide, we are purpose-driven and future-focused, with a highly collaborative team ethic and commitment to personal development. Whether connecting global industries, people, or platforms, we help ensure every challenge gets the solution it deserves. At SAP, you can bring out your best.

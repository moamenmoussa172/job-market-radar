About the job

Key Responsibilities:

Design and develop ARM processor-based subsystems and IP blocks for SoC architectures.

Develop synthesizable RTL using Verilog/VHDL.Integrate

SoC components such as NoC, ARM processors, DMA, PCIe, Ethernet, and peripheral IPs.Perform

RTL linting, CDC/RDC analysis, and static checks using SpyGlass or equivalent tools.

Support synthesis, timing analysis, RTL debugging, and design optimization.

Collaborate with Design Verification, Validation, DFT, and SoC Integration teams.

Coordinate design activities and communicate effectively with global stakeholders.

Required Skills & Experience:

BE/B.Tech/ME/M.Tech in Electronics and Communication or related discipline.

Hands-on experience in RTL design using Verilog/VHDL.

Experience in IP development and SoC integration.

Hands-on experience with linting, SpyGlass, CDC/RDC, and synthesis flows.

Strong understanding of digital design concepts, RTL coding guidelines, and timing constraints.

Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

Good to Have experience in Automotive SoCs.

Knowledge of ISO 26262, low-power design (UPF), and formal verification.Exposure to FPGA prototyping and emulation flows.

BE / B.Tech / ME / M.Tech (Electronics and Communication)

6 to 10 years

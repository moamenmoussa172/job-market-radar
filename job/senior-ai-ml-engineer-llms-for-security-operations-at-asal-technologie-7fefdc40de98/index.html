About the job

About ASAL:

ASAL Technologies was established in 2000 and has grown to become a leading technology company across Jordan, KSA, UAE, and Palestine. With a workforce of over 450 engineers, ASAL offers a wide range of AI agents, services and products, Software and Digital Transformation services, Engineering and R&D services. ASAL Technologies is assembling a team of PD Engineers for a high-end multinational company. The team will be working with top notch engineers in the semiconductor industry from all over the world.

What you will do:

  • Own the AI layer of our SOC platform: cloud models (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic) and local models (Ollama) behind one interface.
  • Build evaluation sets from real security alerts and measure AI accuracy, cost and hallucination rates.
  • Build grounding checks so AI-written incident summaries only state facts that are in the evidence.
  • Defend the pipeline against prompt injection hidden in log data.
  • Improve anomaly detection (UEBA) and risk scoring, and ship new AI features for analysts.
  • Keep the service working when AI is switched off.
  • Red-team our own AI (prompt injection, data leakage) and build AI support for threat hunting.
  • 5+ years in ML / AI engineering, including 2+ years on production LLM applications.
  • Strong Python engineering: tests, code review, version control, APIs.
  • Hands-on experience with LLM evaluation and with making model output reliable.
  • Knowledge of attack techniques, threat hunting, vulnerabilities and defence methods, enough to judge AI verdicts on security alerts.
  • Good understanding of AI in attacks and red teaming: AI-written phishing, deepfakes, AI-assisted malware and reconnaissance, prompt injection, MITRE ATLAS, LLM red-teaming.
  • Strong written and verbal English skills.

Nice to have

  • Security or SOC domain experience.
  • Local model serving, quantisation and GPU sizing.
  • Property-based testing; RAG; AI-security (OWASP Top 10 for LLMs).

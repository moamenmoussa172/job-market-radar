What you’ll do

Lead network solution and software engineering activities to assess, design, plan, implement and evolve IMS network solutions & products, aligned with market priorities, technology evolution, business strategy, international standards, security/quality assurance, business continuity, regulation frameworks and customer experience excellence.
More specifically, you will:
• Lead IMS Core Network evolution, driving design and optimization through Cloud Native, Digital Automation, AI integration, API implementation and other innovative technologies.
• Lead Feasibility Analysis during demand assessment and drive High/Low Level Solution Design (HLD/LLD) during implementation.
• Lead IMS network planning and software engineering, addressing traffic growth and performance KPIs while optimizing network quality, customer experience, operational simplicity and ROI.
• Drive harmonization of IMS design, planning and deployment across markets, ensuring consistent technology introduction and evolution.
• Contribute to IMS software application strategy and roadmaps, ensuring solutions meet company needs for cost, efficiency, reliability, scalability and operational simplicity.
• Support TRS definition and vendor/supplier evaluations during RFI/RFQ negotiations for local and centralized IMS network evolution.

Who you are

You thrive in an international, cross-functional and multicultural environment, driving innovation, adapting to change and delivering positive business impact.
Education
• Degree in Electrical/Electronic, Telecommunications or Software Engineering.
• Excellent written and spoken English. German language is a plus.
Experience & Skills
• Deep knowledge of IMS architecture, networking, protocols and solutions, including tracing tools, vendor documentation, HLD/LLD and international standards.
• Strong IMS engineering experience within telecom operators, covering mobile/fixed services, network planning, dimensioning, solution design and new technology adoption.
• Understanding of telecom market, regulatory framework and technology evolution.
• Technical leadership in complex cross-functional projects, including end-to-end Solution Authority, design decisions and tactical/strategic solution assessment.
• Strong stakeholder management, influencing, negotiation, communication, planning and decision-making skills, including engagement with senior business stakeholders and suppliers.
• Software engineering and AI adoption skills, with strong interest in continuous up-skilling and re-skilling around new technologies.
• Customer Orientation, resilience, empathy and experience with agile ways of working.

About the job

Meet the Team

Join our Silicon One automated systems team and help validate the networking systems behind today’s growing AI infrastructure. As a Senior System QA Software Engineer, you’ll bring strong system QA experience to complex customer scenarios, working across the automation environment, SDK, networking features, and hardware.

You’ll take ownership of failure triage and test design and help junior engineers implement effective system-level tests. A key part of the role is understanding how features interact, how those interactions affect system behavior, and how to turn that understanding into reliable validation. You’ll work closely with engineering teams to investigate issues and improve product quality.

Your Impact

  • Triage test failures, assess their scope and impact, and drive investigation toward root cause.
  • Guide junior engineers as they implement automated tests and work through system QA challenges.
  • Design and implement test cases for complex customer scenarios using the existing automation infrastructure.
  • Identify interactions between features and create tests that validate their combined system behavior.
  • Apply system QA expertise to improve test coverage, reliability, and the usefulness of test results.
  • Communicate findings, technical reasoning, and recommendations clearly to engineering teams.
  • Contribute improvements to the automation infrastructure when they support better testing.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in system QA, test automation, experience writing and maintaining automated tests with pytest.
  • A structured approach to problem-solving, including the ability to investigate complex failures and identify likely root causes.
  • Ability to explain technical reasoning and findings clearly.
  • Ability to design tests that account for interactions between features and their effects on overall system behavior.
  • Experience guiding or supporting other engineers through test implementation or technical problem-solving.
  • Experience with Python and common software design patterns, and algorithms.
  • Fluent in English

Preferred Qualifications

  • Working knowledge of networking, including routing protocols such as OSPF, IS-IS, and BGP.
  • Experience of validating complex software systems.
  • Exposure to SDKs, hardware-software integration, or ASIC-based products.

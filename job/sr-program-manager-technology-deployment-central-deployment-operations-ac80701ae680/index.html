Sr. Program Manager - Technology Deployment, Central Deployment Operations

Amazon · London, United Kingdom

Onsite · Senior · Technical Project / Program Manager · Posted 24 November 2026

PMP / PRINCE2

About the job

The Central Deployment Operations team under RME Central (RP3) is seeking an experienced and highly organized Program Manager to lead the deployment of software and hardware across Amazon's fulfillment and logistics network. The Deployment team serves as a scalable execution function for technology programs across the network, partnering with product, engineering, operations, and other global teams to plan, execute, and scale deployments, including emerging technologies as they evolve. In this role, you will own the end-to-end deployment lifecycle for several tech programs, hardware and software, managing multiple concurrent deployment programs from planning through field execution, validation, and operational handoff. You will translate technical requirements into executable deployment plans, coordinate vendors and internal stakeholders, manage schedule and execution risks, and establish repeatable processes that enable technologies to scale across a large and geographically distributed portfolio.
The successful candidate will have experience delivering complex technical or infrastructure projects and operating effectively in environments with multiple stakeholders, competing priorities, and evolving technical requirements. This role requires a self-starter who can independently manage multiple projects, identify and resolve execution barriers, and influence stakeholders across organizational boundaries, with the ability to quickly understand unfamiliar technologies sufficiently to lead their successful deployment. This is a travel-based role. Travel across the globe up to 50% or more may be required depending on project needs.
Key job responsibilities
Build partnerships across product, engineering, operations, and other global teams to support deployment scalability.
Manage multiple concurrent technology deployment programs from planning through implementation, validation, and operational handoff.
Develop deployment strategies, project plans, schedules, milestones, and execution mechanisms that enable repeatable deployment across multiple sites.
Coordinate demand planning, site readiness sequencing, and supply chain activities including production forecasting, PO timing against long lead times, and inventory management to build executable quarterly deployment plans.
Translate engineering and technical requirements into executable field deployment plans in partnership with engineering teams, vendors, contractors, and site stakeholders.
Manage vendors and contractors, including performance, quality, schedule adherence, execution risks, and corrective actions.
Manage escalations, installation deviations, field constraints, and rework, driving issues through resolution with appropriate stakeholders.
Track deployment budgets and spend across hardware, installation labor, and site infrastructure, identifying funding headroom or risk as programs progress.
Analyze deployment data, including vendor KPIs, installation quality, and schedule performance, to identify systemic issues and improvement opportunities.
Develop mechanisms, standard work, documentation, and best practices that improve deployment consistency, quality, and scalability.
Write and maintain program management documents and own reporting cadences including WBR, MBR, and QBR to keep stakeholders and senior leadership informed on program health.
- Bachelor's degree in supply chain, business, engineering, finance or related technical or quantitative field
- Experience in program or project management
- Experience defining and executing program requirements
- Experience working cross functionally with tech and non-tech teams
- Experience owning program strategy, end to end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience using data and metrics to determine and drive improvements
- Experience managing multiple projects, prioritizing, planning, and managing time
- PMP certificate
- MBA
- Experience leading process improvements
- 5+ years of leading large complex programs experience
- Experience in a multi-site environment

About the job

About Paystack MFB

Paystack MFB is a CBN-licensed Microfinance bank and part of Paystack, one of Africa's leading payments companies. We give growing businesses one place to collect payments, hold and grow their money, access credit, and pay or get paid across borders. Thousands of businesses already rely on Paystack's payments platform every day. Paystack MFB extends that relationship into full business banking.

Over the last 10 years, we’ve built products that have helped shape online payments in Africa, from automated recurring payments to direct bank payments and automated chargebacks. Today, Paystack MFB is part of The Stack Group (TSG), a family of technology brands building modern infrastructure across payments, banking, consumer products, and emerging technologies.

At Paystack MFB, we hire talented people, treat them with genuine respect, and give them the space, resources, and support to do the best work of their lives. We’d love your help.

About the Relationship manager - Business Banking role

The Relationship Manager, Business Banking, builds and manages a profitable portfolio of SME relationships. The work has two sources:

  • Acquiring new SMEs through field sales who will run their daily transactions through the bank.
  • Moving existing payment merchants into full banking relationships with us.

We’ll trust you to

  • Convert existing payment merchants into banking customers: open accounts, move settlements and daily collections to the bank, and make us their primary operating account.
  • Prospect, onboard, and activate new SMEs in key sectors, including FMCG, mobile and electronics, fashion, household goods, building materials, retail chains, and service businesses.
  • Drive transaction activity across all collection channels (transfers, online payments, POS) so the bank sees each customer's daily cash flow.
  • Coordinate KYC/KYB documentation and account activation, ensuring full compliance with regulatory and internal onboarding standards.

Deposit mobilisation

  • Grow current account, savings, and term deposit balances across the portfolio.
  • Increase float and average balances by deepening wallet share and cutting leakage to other banks.
  • Meet or exceed assigned deposit volume, low-cost funding mix, and account growth targets.

SME lending

  • Originate and close SME and merchant loans, including working capital, inventory finance, and cash-flow-based facilities, using transaction data to support credit decisions.
  • Own the end-to-end loan process: prospecting, credit documentation, working with credit and risk, disbursement coordination, and post-disbursement monitoring.
  • Build and manage a high-quality loan book with strong utilisation, healthy repayment, and minimal delinquency.
  • Drive repeat borrowing and up-sell to strong performers in the portfolio.

Portfolio and performance management

  • Track daily, weekly, and monthly KPIs across deposits, lending, portfolio quality, transaction volumes, and international payments.
  • Visit customers regularly to deepen relationships, spot risks early, and find cross-sell opportunities.
  • Feed market, competitor, and customer insights back to product, pricing, and credit teams.
  • Keep an accurate pipeline and customer record in the CRM.

Key performance indicators

  • Deposit balances and growth, including low-cost deposit mix
  • Number of merchants transitioned and new SMEs acquired and activated
  • Transaction volume and value processed through the bank
  • Loan volume, value, and portfolio growth
  • Portfolio quality (PAR, repayment rate, delinquency)
  • International payments volume and revenue
  • Products per customer (cross-sell ratio)

You’ll thrive in this role if you

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Banking, Business Administration, Economics, Marketing, or a related field.
  • 8+ years' cumulative experience in commercial, business, or SME banking, including direct relationship management at a bank. Prior banking experience is required.
  • Proven track record of managing an SME lending portfolio of at least ₦2B, with strong repayment performance.
  • Demonstrated success in deposit mobilisation and meeting balance-sheet targets.
  • Strong grasp of cash-flow-based lending, SME credit analysis, and portfolio management.
  • An existing SME network in one or more of the target sectors is an advantage.
  • Commercially sharp, results-driven, and focused on execution.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation, and relationship management skills.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

Where you belong

Our Supply Chain Management Consulting unit supports SAP customers in planning, executing, and monitoring their supply chain processes. In doing so, we will guide our customers from the design of the solution through realization to go-live and subsequent optimization.

In addition, the department develops its own consulting solutions, which are used for more than 300 customers worldwide and provide additional analysis and controlling functions in addition to the SAP standard software. To do so, we address customer pain points and develop cross-industry solutions.

For our team, we are looking for highly motivated young talents (w/m/d) who are supporting us in this area and willing to work in a challenging but also varied environment.

What you´ll build

  • Further development of our consulting solutions in the SAP programming language ABAP
  • Support development projects from initial ideas to finished solutions and their implementation
  • Support developers in various activities related to this process

We offer:

  • Ability to learn ABAP from scratch
  • Self-employed, project-based work
  • Practical Tasks
  • Insights into the latest technologies SAP UI5, SAP S/4 HANA, and SAP Business Technology Platform
  • High chances to be taken over as working student
  • Flexible Working Hours

What you bring

  • Ongoing studies in software development (for example: business informatics, computer science, business administration with IT focus, information technology, software development, industrial engineering, mathematics)
  • Knowledge of at least one programming language required
  • Strong communication and presentation skills and autonomous, goal-oriented work (also in the virtual team)
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office is a prerequisite
  • ABAP knowledge or motivation to learn ABAP
  • Very good German and English skills (C1)
  • Interest in Supply Chain Planning
  • Technical Affinity
  • Passion for SAP Topics and Current IT Technologies

As an attractive employer we offer you a salary that is based on the current "Kollektivvertrag für Angestellte von Unternehmen im Bereich Dienstleistungen in der automatischen Datenverarbeitung und Informationstechnik", a minimum of 1703€ gross/month/full time for interns and 2473€ gross/month/full time for working students, in addition to generous additional benefits.

​​Your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a resume in table form, school leaving certificates, certificate of enrollment, current university transcript of records, copies of any academic degrees already earned, and if available, references from former employers (including internships). Please describe as well your experience and skills in foreign languages and computer programs / programming languages.​

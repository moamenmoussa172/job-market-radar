About the job

Who we are

VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.
As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth. With our strategic partner Accenture, we work alongside our Vodafone customers, other Telco and tech companies to drive transformation, meet the challenges of our industry and ensure we stay relevant and resilient. About this Role

We are seeking a highly experienced Senior Java Developer to design, develop, and enhance scalable microservices-based applications within the Telco IT domain. This role involves hands-on development, technical solution ownership, and collaboration across cross-functional teams, including DevOps, architects, and product stakeholders. The individual will contribute to delivering robust, high-performance systems and guide teams in implementing innovative and efficient technical solutions

What you’ll do

Develop and perform unit testing for Java/Spring Boot applications
Apply strong debugging skills to troubleshoot complex issues
Design and manage data models and database interactions
Conduct performance testing and tuning of applications
Lead and guide teams to identify and implement improved solutions
Translate business and functional Telco requirements into technical designs
Own solution design, configuration, and implementation across multiple teams
Define and configure interfaces for data exchange between services and front-end applications
Present high-level, solution, and technical designs to cross-functional teams
Analyse defects and coordinate resolution across teams
Handle incidents and provide workarounds in complex production scenarios
Conduct code reviews and approve pull requests for complex changes
Collaborate with Product Owners/Managers and System Architects for feature breakdown
Support system architecture design and high-level planning
Assist DevOps teams in deployment across environments

Who you are

Strong expertise in Java, Spring Boot, microservices, and database technologies (relational and non-relational)
Experience in technical architecture, system design, and solution development (minimum 6 years in relevant areas)
At least 2 years of experience working in a DevOps or cloud environment
Familiarity with tools such as Kafka, Grafana, AWS, GitHub, Jira, and Confluence
Experience working in Agile frameworks (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban, or hybrid models)
Working knowledge of Telco business domains (Cable/TV preferred)
Ability to manage stakeholders effectively and collaborate across teams
A proactive learner with a strong interest in evolving system landscapes and sharing knowledge
Educational qualification in Computer Science or a related discipline (B.E./B.Tech, BCA/MCA, BSc/MSc

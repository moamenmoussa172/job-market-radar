About the job

⠀

Flightscape is a high technology company, at the leading edge of digital immersion, providing solutions to make the world a safer place. Our flight services business delivers software solutions to more than 150 airline customers.

Join the engine that is changing Fligthscape, pointing towards the next horizon of growth through digital innovations to support our customers in their success.
Airlines and business aircraft operators face pressures like few others. In a heavily regulated, competitive, ultra-visible industry, they require a suite of digital tools to efficiently plan their operations and deploy their people while optimizing their performance, compliance, and customer satisfaction.

Work location: This is an onsite role based in our Kraków office.

Your role :

Front-end Software Engineer to develop the Unified Task Board web application. Best fit for Unified Task Board team would be a highly motivated individual with focus on quality and willingness to discover new technology.

Main responsibilities:

  • Designs and develops efficient front-end systems
  • Creates unit and integration tests
  • Works closely with team to understand and resolve problems
  • Assists in development of technical documentation
  • Deploys software tools, processes, and metrics
  • Identifies areas for modification and opportunities for improvement of the application
  • Participates in knowledge sharing

Necessary skills & requirements :

  • 3 or more years’ experience in programming
  • Experience in developing web applications using React and TypeScript (good understanding of hooks, state management, routing, async)
  • Knowledge of CSS
  • Experience with at least one library for stamen management (redux, zustand)
  • Experience with Rest API, Open API
  • Experience with Node.js, NPM
  • Experience in CI/CD tools such as Azure Devops, TeamCity or Jenkins
  • Experience with dev tools (GIT)
  • Experience in form development (react-hook-form, formic) and validation (zod, yup)
  • Knowledge of data fetching and caching
  • Willingness to learn and accommodate new business knowledge (airlines industry)
  • Experience with designs systems (MUI)

Nice to have skills & requirements:

  • Experience in building micro-frontends
  • Experience with playwright
  • Basics of designing API (OpenAPI specifications, BE/FE contracts)

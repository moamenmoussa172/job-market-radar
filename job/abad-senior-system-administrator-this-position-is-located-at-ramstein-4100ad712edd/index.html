Job Description:

Parsons is looking for an amazingly talented ABAD Senior System Administrator to join our team! In this role you will get to design, develop, and maintain multi-site communications networked infrastructure hardware. This position is located at Ramstein Air Base, Germany (International Assignment) *NO REMOTE WORK**

What You'll Be Doing:

  • Manage account creation and account administration on physical and virtual machines.
  • Analyzes, troubleshoots, and resolves system hardware, software, and networking issues.
  • Conduct virtual machine builds on physical server stacks.
  • Document and maintain servers, create monitoring reports and logs, and ensure functionality of systems.
  • Install and administer Windows and Linux servers.

What Required Skills You'll Bring:

  • Bachelor’s degree with 2+ years of related professional or associate's degree with 7+ relevant work experience or 10+ years of related experience in lieu of a degree.
  • IAT Level II certification required (CompTIA Sec +, etc.)
  • 2+ years of experience in a System Administrator role
  • Experience with virtualization
  • Experience with Red Hat Enterprise Linux
  • Experience with routers, switches, and firewall configuration
  • Understanding of STIG, SCAP, and RMF requirements
  • Secret clearance is required
  • **This position is located in Ramstein Air Base, Germany (International Assignment) *NO REMOTE WORK**

What Desired Skills You'll Bring:

  • Creating Gold images and server backups with the appropriate OS hardening, leveraging the Security Technical Implementation Guide (STIG) and Security Content Application Protocol (SCAP) scans
  • Conducting routing and switching within a server enclave, and integration of firewalls

Security Clearance Requirement:

An active Secret security clearance is required for this position.​

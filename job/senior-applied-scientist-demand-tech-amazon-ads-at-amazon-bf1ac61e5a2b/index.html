About the job

In Amazon Advertising, we apply machine learning at massive scale to optimize the prediction, ranking, and bidding behind every ad - deciding, in milliseconds, which ads to show shoppers and how to value them. We're looking for an experienced Applied Scientist to lead a science area within how we rank, value, and bid on ads for Amazon DVA+ (Display, Video, and Audio) - including how we judge whether an ad is a good fit for the page a shopper is on and for the shopper themselves.
It's high-scale, low-latency, customer-facing science: your models run live in front of millions of shoppers under tight real-time constraints. The questions are genuinely open - how do you tell whether an ad is relevant to someone, how do you balance what's good for shoppers, advertisers, and Amazon, and how do you keep getting that right as shopping behavior and inventory shift underneath you? You'll set the technical direction for how we answer them, and have room to shape where we take it.
A few things make this stand out: your models touch a huge share of the ads shoppers see every day, so even small improvements add up fast; you'll run modern ML live under strict latency limits, across regions and very different types of ad inventory; and the problem space is rich - from how we value and bid on ads, to keeping models stable as traffic shifts, to what makes an ad a good fit for a shopper.
Key job responsibilities
- Be the scientific point of reference for your area, mentoring scientists and engineers and raising the team's bar through code, design, and science review.
- Shape the science roadmap for your area, aligning partner teams and building consensus across science and business.
- Advance the state of the art in ranking, deep learning, and information retrieval, judging which problems are genuinely new versus solvable with existing approaches, and set the standards the team builds on.
- Own ambiguous problems end to end with limited guidance: frame them, break them down for yourself and others, prototype, experiment, and ship.
- Define how the team weighs competing objectives — shopper experience, advertiser and publisher value, and Amazon's business — and the metrics that keep models holding up across placements and marketplaces.
- Write and ship your own production code, moving fast with modern AI coding assistants and agents — we're all builders here.
A day in the life
You might start by digging into last week's experiment results, then use an AI coding agent to get your next prototype built and ready to test in production. In the afternoon you could be sketching a new way to measure ad relevance, reading a recent paper that bears on it, and reviewing a teammate's design - as you are the person the team turns to when a problem gets hard. You'll move between hands-on science, writing and shipping real production code, setting the direction for your area, and helping the scientists and engineers around you do their best work.
About the team
We're a group of scientists and engineers based in Edinburgh and London, working to make Amazon's ads more performant and relevant. We sit within a larger team spread primarily across New York City and the UK, and we have a broad mandate to build and experiment. You'll lead a science area within that mandate, with the data and infrastructure to do the work well and room to grow your scope over time - including opportunities to attend top conferences (NeurIPS, KDD, ICML).
- PhD or equivalent research experience
- Experience programming in Java, C++, Python or related language
- Experience in applied research
- Experience in building machine learning models for business application
- Experience with Machine and Deep Learning toolkits such as MXNet, TensorFlow, Caffe and PyTorch
- PhD in machine learning, statistics, mathematics, physics, engineering, computer science, operations research, econometrics, optimization, or equivalent quantitative field.
- Experience in recommender systems, performance advertising, or online auctions.

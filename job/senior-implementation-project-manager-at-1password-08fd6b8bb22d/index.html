About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

1Password is growing. We’ve surpassed $500M in ARR and we’re continuing to accelerate, earning a spot on the Forbes Cloud 100 for four years in a row and teaming up with iconic partners like Oracle Red Bull Racing.

About 1Password

At 1Password, we’re building the foundation for a safe, productive digital future. Our mission is to unleash employee productivity without compromising security by ensuring every identity is authentic, every application sign-in is secure, and every device is trusted. We innovated the market-leading enterprise password manager and pioneered Unified Access Management, a new cybersecurity category built for the way people and AI agents work today. As one of the most loved brands in cybersecurity, we take a human-centric approach in everything from product strategy to user experience. Over 180,000 businesses, from Fortune 100 leaders to the world’s most innovative AI companies, trust 1Password to help their teams securely adopt the SaaS and AI tools they need to do their best work.

If you're excited about the opportunity to contribute to the digital safety of millions, to work alongside a team of curious, driven individuals, and to solve hard problems in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, then we want to hear from you. Come join us and help shape a safer, simpler digital future.

As a Senior Implementation Project Manager, you’ll lead complex and enterprise customer implementations from discovery through launch and transition to ongoing success. Reporting to the Manager, Implementation Project Management, you’ll guide multiple workstreams, align customer and internal stakeholders, and turn technical and business goals into clear delivery plans and measurable outcomes. Your work will help organizations adopt secure, productive ways of working and advance 1Password’s mission to create a safer, simpler digital future.

How we’re using AI today

Across GTM, we’re transforming the way we work with AI. We expect every seller, CSM, and team member to become a power user, using it daily to work smarter and move faster. AI is already embedded in how we operate – from real-time coaching that sharpens how we sell and interact with customers, to surfacing insights that help us anticipate needs – creating more time for meaningful customer engagement. If you’re excited to reshape how you think, make decisions, and drive growth with AI, you’ll feel right at home here.

This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US, with a preference for Pacific Time coverage.

What we're looking for:

  • Bring 5+ years of experience in project management, implementation, professional services, or a related field within Business-to-Business Software as a Service (B2B SaaS).
  • Lead complex customer implementations involving multiple workstreams, changing conditions, competing priorities, and diverse stakeholder groups.
  • Demonstrate experience delivering paid professional services or implementation packages, including managing scope, tracking project time, and delivering within agreed timelines.
  • Translate customer goals into practical strategies, project plans, milestones, decisions, and next steps while surfacing risks and dependencies early.
  • Communicate clearly with technical and business stakeholders, including executive audiences, and lead difficult conversations with sound recommendations.
  • Bring strong technical curiosity and the ability to learn products quickly, with familiarity across SaaS, identity providers, single sign-on (SSO), System for Cross-domain Identity Management (SCIM), Active Directory, command-line interfaces (CLI), endpoint management, or device trust.
  • Hold a Project Management Professional (PMP) certification and demonstrate a commitment to mentoring colleagues, improving delivery practices, and using artificial intelligence (AI) responsibly to strengthen research, preparation, documentation, and workflows.

What you can expect:

  • Lead complex and enterprise 1Password implementation and onboarding projects from discovery through launch, owning scope, timelines, dependencies, risks, and next steps.
  • Establish clear operating models for customer engagements, including integrated plans, governance, communication rhythms, decision rights, escalation paths, and durable documentation.
  • Partner with customer executives, sponsors, champions, technical owners, and internal teams to understand business and security goals and guide implementation decisions.
  • Coordinate closely with Technical Account Managers, Customer Success Managers, Account Executives, Product partners, Implementation Consultants, and other specialists to address customer requirements and technical dependencies.
  • Lead kickoffs, discovery sessions, status meetings, executive updates, launch-readiness reviews, and transition activities for ongoing customer success.
  • Take ownership of ambiguous or at-risk engagements by diagnosing root causes, managing tradeoffs, creating recovery plans, and communicating recommended actions.
  • Mentor and coach project management colleagues, create reusable guidance and playbooks, and identify improvements that strengthen delivery consistency and the customer experience.

Our culture
At 1Password, we prioritize collaboration, clear and transparent communication, receptiveness to feedback, and alignment with our core values: keep it simple, lead with honesty, and put people first.
You’ll be part of a team that challenges the status quo, and is excited to experiment and iterate in search of the best solution. That said, 1Password is not for everyone. Our work is demanding, we strive for excellence, and the pace is fast. We need people who are keen to take on challenging problems, who seek feedback to grow, and who are driven to make an impact. If you're looking for a place where you can settle into a comfortable routine, this might not be the right fit for you. We’re looking for individuals who are proven experts in their fields, as well as those who are highly adaptable, can thrive in ambiguity and through change, are curious, and above all deliver results.
How we work with AI
We are committed to leveraging cutting-edge technology—including AI—to achieve our mission. We also understand that thinking critically about AI in its current forms will help us create better solutions for our customers and ourselves with its future forms, which will help us continue to close the gap between security and privacy and achieve our mission. We want team members at all levels to take the approach of actively learning AI best practices, identifying opportunities to apply AI in meaningful ways, and driving innovative solutions in their daily work. Embracing the future of AI isn't just encouraged—it's an essential part of how we will be successful at 1Password.

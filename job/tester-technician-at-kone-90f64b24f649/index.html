About the job

We are looking for a Tester Technician to join our team. You will be responsible for testing and commissioning elevator and escalator equipment, ensuring it meets KONE’s safety, quality, and technical standards before handover.

The role requires strong technical and troubleshooting capabilities, as well as the ability to work confidently with digital tools and systems used for testing, commissioning, diagnostics, and reporting.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform testing and commissioning of elevators and escalators according to KONE procedures and safety standards.
  • Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve electrical and technical issues during testing.
  • Ensure equipment meets required safety, quality, and performance standards before handover.
  • Read and interpret electrical drawings, wiring diagrams, and technical documentation.
  • Use relevant digital testing, diagnostic, commissioning, and reporting tools as part of day-to-day activities.
  • Report identified issues and coordinate with installation and technical teams to ensure timely resolution.
  • Complete required testing documentation and reports accurately.
  • Maintain testing tools and equipment in good working condition.
  • Follow KONE’s safety procedures at all times and contribute to a safe working environment.
  • Support installation teams with technical guidance when required.

What We’re Looking For

  • Technical Diploma or Technical Secondary School qualification in Electrical, Electronics, Mechatronics, Electromechanical, or a related technical field.
  • 3–4 years of relevant technical experience, preferably in elevators and escalators, testing, commissioning, maintenance, or electrical systems.
  • Good knowledge of electrical systems, wiring, control systems, and technical drawings.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Good digital skills and the ability to use digital tools and applications for testing, diagnostics, commissioning, documentation, and reporting.
  • Comfortable learning and working with new digital systems and technologies used in field operations.
  • Good attention to detail with a strong focus on safety and quality.
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with installation and site teams.

About the job

Is getting in the weeds, fixing what’s broken and coming up with innovative solutions your thing?

We are working on a genre-defining and charts-ruling hit mobile game and are looking for an experienced UX Designer to join our Top Eleven team and help us elevate the experience to the next level for our players.

If you enjoy solving problems, experimenting with new ideas, and working closely with a multidisciplinary team to make great games, let’s talk!

THE DIFFERENCE YOU’LL MAKE:

  • You’ll shape how players experience our game - from the way they navigate and understand its systems to how they interact with individual features
  • You’ll turn player needs, research, data, and team insights into thoughtful UX solutions that make the game intuitive, engaging, and enjoyable

WHAT YOU'LL DO:

  • Concept, design, wireframe, prototype, iterate, and optimize user flows for Top Eleven
  • Incorporate iterative user and team feedback into the development process
  • Create wireframes and prototypes for testing and communicating your interactive design solutions to key stakeholders and teammates
  • Create an amazing user experience for our players by defining information architecture and visual guidelines
  • Define and implement standards that help create a consistent, high-quality experience for players
  • Assist the Marketing team with user testing and incorporate the results into the iterative design process
  • Work with the User Research team to understand player needs and validate design decisions
  • Follow the latest design trends, AI-based UX/UR tools, and experiments aimed at discovering innovative solutions

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH:

  • You’ll be part of a multidisciplinary game team, working closely with Game Designers, UI Artists, Engineers, User Researchers, Analysts, and other disciplines
  • You’ll collaborate with the wider UX and Design community at Nordeus, sharing knowledge, exploring new approaches, and continuously improving how we design experiences for our players

WE ARE A MATCH, IF YOU:

  • Have 3+ years of relevant UX design experience
  • Have previous experience designing user experiences for games, ideally mobile games
  • Have a background in Web Design, Interaction Design, UI/UX, Human-Computer Interaction, Psychology, or another related field
  • Keep up to date with the latest UX trends and major platform interaction guidelines
  • Can combine research, data, and your understanding of gaming culture to empathize with players and develop solutions tailored to their needs.
  • Can create and animate prototypes that clearly demonstrate UI interactions
  • Understand how UX solutions translate into real products, including cross-platform implementation and technical limitations
  • Are a self-starter with a “get things done” attitude and a focus on working efficiently in a fast-paced environment
  • Love video games and are passionate about game development

WE CAN’T WAIT TO MEET YOU SO DON’T FORGET TO:

  • Share your latest portfolio, including at least one project that shows your full UX process- from initial brief to final outcome
  • Tip: Prioritize showcasing your UX thinking over visual design
  • Here’s what we’ll be looking at:
  • Your wireframing process
  • Iterations and how your ideas evolved
  • Research, analysis, and any user testing insights that informed your decisions
  • The final UX outcome (prototypes are a plus)

WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE:

  • The team behind the game: transparency and trust from day one, paired with a strong sense of teamwork- that's the essence of who we are. It's not just what you do - it’s how you do it and who you do it with. With 280+ teammates from around the world, we’re on a mission to bring the joy of winning to millions.
  • We make your life at Nordeus hassle-free: enjoy Nordeus provided breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages, a fully-equipped gym, organized sports activities (yoga, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, basketball, football), an ergonomic workstation, top-notch tech equipment (including laptop, mobile phone and bill coverage, and other tech), a kids’ playroom, a music corner, board and video games + latest consoles.
  • Perks? We’ve got plenty: premium private medical insurance for you and your family, flexible working hours, take-what-you-need vacation policy, offsites abroad for the whole company, fully-paid maternity and paternity leave, employee stock purchase plan, access to L&D platforms and opportunities and many more.
  • Make your mark on games played by millions: with over 300M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game, aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful mobile sports gaming experiences built to last.
  • Meaningful career experience: work with experienced game makers and mentors who will support you from day one, helping you map a career path that's true to you.
  • Impact beyond the screen: It is not just about us. We are part of something bigger than a job. We create a better future for generations to come through the efforts of the Nordeus Foundation.

GOOD TO KNOW:

  • This is a hybrid, on-site position based in Belgrade.
  • Our 3+2 hybrid model remains the standard - Monday to Wednesday in the office, Thursday and Friday are optional remote days. While we prefer working together from the office, we also understand that having some alone time or working from home (or some other location) can be beneficial. It’s about what works, not what’s prescribed.
  • If you are not residing in Serbia, learn more about our relocation package.

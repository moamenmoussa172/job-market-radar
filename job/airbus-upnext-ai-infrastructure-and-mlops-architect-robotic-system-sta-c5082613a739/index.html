About the job

Job Description:

Project description

Airbus UpNext demonstrator is pioneering the future of autonomous aircraft ground operations. By exploring new paradigms in robotics and Physical AI, we are building a connected ecosystem that bridges intelligent robots, ground ramp agents, and next-generation aircraft.


The AI Infrastructure & MLOps Architect - Robotic System Stack(M/F) position is within Airbus UpNext and it consists of a fixed-term contract (CDIC-C), starting ASAP till the End of June 2029.

Mission

Your main tasks and responsibilities will include:

  • Building the cloud-to-edge backbone that keeps our models training, deploying, and running without bottlenecks.
  • Designing AWS storage and GPU computing infrastructures specifically tailored to process massive multimodal robotics datasets (telemetry, point clouds, video) efficiently.
  • Building low-latency inference pipelines that run heavy foundation models seamlessly between cloud clusters and edge hardware on the physical test cell.
  • Automating CI/CD for model versioning, deployment, and rollbacks so the algorithmic team can ship updates daily without breaking the physical robot.
  • Ensuring reliability, observability, and driving the internal tooling strategy powering our agentic workflow infrastructure.

Your boarding pass

What you bring:

  • Proven expertise in designing and managing AWS cloud architecture and GPU computing clusters for heavy machine learning workloads.
  • Deep knowledge of MLOps pipelines, CI/CD automation, and deploying foundation models on edge devices.
  • Passion about technology, science.
  • Eagerness to take initiatives to propose simpler and efficient ways of working.
  • Impatience and burning desire to attain rapidly, along with a healthy skepticism for progress.
  • Values of transparency, humility, empathy.
  • Autonomy, Adaptability, Availability, Accountability.
  • Team-Player mindset.
  • Willingness to have fun.

What you get:

  • Expertise on product development scaling massive AI infrastructure for real-world robotic applications.
  • Knowledge in design, manufacture and deliver prototypes.
  • A fun and supportive working environment that not only fosters your development in many ways, but also rewards it with plenty of room to grow.
  • A shared open space office with highly skilled multicultural teams.
  • Ability to craft your calendar with flexible hours/Opportunity to work remotely.
  • UpNext communities bonding events.
  • A high-achieving, experienced and fun team.

The responsibilities and the technical scope of the job might evolve according to the agile working environment of the project, so being flexible is important.

About us

Founded in 2017, Airbus UpNext is actively shaping the future of aerospace by using demonstrators to evaluate, mature and validate new products and services. Our purpose is to pioneer game-changing technologies with Airbus.

In this role, you will embody the core Airbus UpNext values:

  • Care for Each Other: Ensuring a supportive and inclusive environment.
  • Be Simple: Finding the most efficient path to success.
  • Be Audacious: Challenging the status quo to make the unthinkable real.
  • Be Open: Collaborative solution-finding across diverse teams.
  • Speed of Execution: Moving at the speed of a startup.

Semiconductor Application Engineer – Automotive Radar & Ultrasonic Sensing

Bosch Group · bangalore, India

Onsite · Software Engineer · Posted 30 October 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Responsibilities:

Support customers in the evaluation, integration, and debugging of Radar and Ultrasonic sensor solutions.

Analyze and solve hardware, software, and system-level issues.

Perform electrical measurements, validation, and signal analysis.

Act as a technical interface between customers, application engineering, product development, and regional teams.

Drive problem resolution across multidisciplinary and international teams.

Technical Skills:

Good understanding of analog hardware development on PCB/system level.

Experience with hardware-near embedded software development.

Knowledge of signal integrity topics (timing, slew rates, interface behavior).

Basic understanding of digital signal processing.

Strong electrical measurement and debugging skills (oscilloscope, logic analyzer, etc.).

Basic knowledge of Functional Safety (ISO 26262).

Advantageous: Knowledge of electro-acoustics, ultrasonic transducers, and the piezoelectric effect.

Soft Skills

Strong communication and presentation skills.

Customer-oriented mindset and structured problem-solving approach.

Ability to resolve conflicts and align different stakeholder perspectives.

Willingness to work in an international environment and collaborate closely with customers.

Qualifications:

Degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Mechatronics, Physics, Computer Engineering, or a comparable field.

Experience in automotive electronics, sensing systems, or semiconductor applications is beneficial.

Bachelor’s / Master’s in Electronics

6-10 Years

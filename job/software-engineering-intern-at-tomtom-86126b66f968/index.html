About the job

AD Speeds makes the speed restriction data in TomTom maps: the maximum speed a driver may legally drive on each road. Car makers use it in navigation and in driver-assistance systems that warn drivers when they drive too fast.
You join COPS, the team that runs and maintains the speeds production system, in the pod that automates it. The pod builds the services that keep speed data correct and deliver it on time.

What you'll need:

Must have

  • Strong problem-solving skills: you break a problem down and find a way through it.
  • Working knowledge of at least one programming language.
  • SQL: you can query and change data in a relational database.
  • Git and GitHub: branches, pull requests, code review.
  • An understanding of service-based and microservice architectures.
  • Basic DevOps knowledge: containers (for example Docker) and how applications are deployed.
  • Located in Lodz or close by.
  • Enrolled in a Polish university.
  • Nice to have
  • Experience with AI-assisted and agentic programming tools, such as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot.
  • Study projects, personal projects and open-source work count as experience. What you can expect:
  • A small engineering team in Łódź that works on production systems used by global car makers.
  • A mentor from the team, regular check-ins and feedback during your internship.
  • Real tasks with real impact, scoped to your level, and space to learn.
  • Hybrid work: 1–2 days a week in the Łódź office, the rest remote.
  • Flexible hours: 8 hours a day within 08:00–17:00.
  • Hands-on experience with modern engineering practice, including AI-assisted development.

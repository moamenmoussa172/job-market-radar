About the job

Grocery Storefront is a new team at inDrive. The team builds a native grocery and food shopping experience inside the inDrive super app: the iOS and Android storefront and the mobile backend-for-frontend (BFF).
As a Senior iOS Engineer, you will own the iOS storefront from its first version: architecture, technical standards and delivery. You will work in a small team of iOS, Android, backend and QA engineers.

  • Design the architecture of the iOS storefront module in the super app: modules, UI layer, data layer and navigation
  • Build storefront screens, from the home page and catalog to the cart and checkout
  • Agree API contracts for the mobile BFF with backend and Android engineers. The BFF sets the content and the order of blocks on storefront screens
  • Keep long lists with images fast and smooth on all supported devices
  • Run A/B experiments, manage feature flags, and implement analytics events
  • Set the quality bar for the iOS code: code reviews, tests, CI checks
  • Mentor engineers and take part in hiring for the team
  • Build with AI tools (Claude Code, Copilot) as part of an AI-First approach
  • 5+ years of commercial iOS development with Swift
  • Production experience with SwiftUI and good knowledge of UIKit
  • Good knowledge of Swift Concurrency, MVVM or similar patterns, and DI
  • Experience with modular architecture (Tuist, SPM)
  • Experience with performance work: long lists with images, memory, app start time
  • Strong understanding of networking, caching and unstable connections
  • Experience with unit and UI tests (XCTest) and with CI for mobile apps
  • Experience as a technical owner of a feature or an app area, and mentoring experience
  • International work experience (experience working in international teams or with international markets)
  • English level: B2+

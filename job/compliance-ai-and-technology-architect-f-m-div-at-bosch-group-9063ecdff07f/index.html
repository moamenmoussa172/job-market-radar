Compliance AI and Technology Architect (f/m/div.)

Bosch Group · Lisboa, Portugal

Hybrid · Senior · Software / Solutions Architect · Posted 23 October 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Your contribution to something big:

As Compliance AI and Technology Architect, you will shape the technology strategy and architecture that enables Bosch Compliance to evolve in a rapidly changing digital environment. You will connect business needs, technology innovation and regulatory requirements to create secure, scalable and sustainable solutions for global Compliance operations.

Your responsibilities include:

  • Own the architecture, governance and strategic evolution of Compliance AI and technology platforms.
  • Design and govern technology capabilities supporting both manual and AI-enabled Compliance processes.
  • Drive enterprise-wide digital transformation initiatives across Compliance.
  • Define and implement technology strategies supporting global Compliance operations.
  • Enable the sustainable integration of AI, automation and data-driven capabilities into Compliance processes.
  • Establish and maintain technology governance, ensuring compliance with regulatory, security and risk requirements.
  • Translate Compliance and business requirements into scalable, secure and sustainable technology solutions.
  • Represent Compliance in enterprise architecture, technology governance and cross-functional transformation programs.
  • Lead complex global transformation and organizational change initiatives.
  • Collaborate with senior stakeholders across Compliance, IT and corporate functions.
  • Balance innovation, regulatory requirements, risk management and operational excellence to ensure future-ready Compliance operations.

What distinguishes you:

Education: Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Engineering or a related technical field.
Advanced training in Digital Transformation, Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Architecture or Compliance.

Experience:

  • 8+ years of professional experience in Digital Transformation, Technology Architecture, AI, Software Solutions or related fields.
  • Proven experience leading global, cross-functional transformation initiatives and programs.
  • Demonstrated experience defining and governing technology strategies for global business functions.
  • Experience with AI, automation and technology-enabled business solutions.
  • Proven experience managing senior / executive-level stakeholders across IT, Compliance and corporate functions.

Know-how:

  • Artificial Intelligence, AI-enabled solutions & Digital Transformation.
  • Enterprise Architecture & Automation Technologies.
  • Technology Governance.
  • Compliance Technology Solutions.
  • Risk-based technology governance and regulatory requirements.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into scalable, secure and sustainable technology solutions.

Working Style & Methods

  • Strategic and enterprise-oriented approach combined with strong execution.
  • Structured approach to architecture, governance and transformation.
  • Strong delivery and implementation focus.
  • Able to manage complex, global and cross-functional initiatives.
  • Strong change management and stakeholder management skills.
  • Comfortable balancing innovation with regulatory, security and risk requirements.
  • Able to operate effectively across business and technology environments.

Personality

  • Strong leadership and influencing skills.
  • High professional credibility and integrity.
  • Sound judgment, particularly in sensitive Compliance environments.
  • Strategic thinker with a pragmatic, solution-oriented mindset.
  • Confident working with senior and executive-level stakeholders.
  • Collaborative and able to drive alignment across different functions.
  • Curious and forward-looking, with a strong interest in emerging technologies and AI.

Languages: Fluent English is mandatory.

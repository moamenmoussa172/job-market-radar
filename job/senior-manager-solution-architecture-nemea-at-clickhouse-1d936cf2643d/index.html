About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

ClickHouse is on a mission to grow a vibrant user community and establish ourselves as a cloud-first company. We are seeking an experienced Solutions Architect Leader to continue to build, lead, and scale our Solutions Architecture function across NEMEA. This is a pivotal role for someone who thrives at the intersection of technical leadership, customer impact, and team development.

Joining at an exciting stage following the general availability of our serverless ClickHouse Cloud product, you will play a key role in shaping how ClickHouse is positioned, adopted, and scaled across the region. You will lead a growing team of Solutions Architects while remaining close to customers, partners, and the field.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Lead, mentor, and scale the NEMEA Solutions Architecture team, setting technical standards, best practices, and a strong customer-first culture.
  • Partner closely with Sales, Product, and Engineering leadership to drive successful outcomes across enterprise and commercial customers.
  • Serve as a senior technical advisor to customers, engaging at all levels from C-suite stakeholders to architects, data engineers, and developers.
  • Translate complex business and technical requirements into scalable, cloud-native solution designs, including architecture, migrations, workflows, and data visualisation.
  • Guide and support high-impact proofs of concept (POCs) and reference architectures that accelerate adoption and expansion.
  • Evangelise ClickHouse’s vision, architecture, and value proposition across the NEMEA market, including participation in executive briefings, events, and community initiatives.
  • Drive regional initiatives that help customers transform raw data into actionable insights aligned with clear business outcomes.
  • Provide feedback from the field to inform product strategy, roadmap priorities, and go-to-market execution.

What You Bring

  • Significant experience in Solutions Architecture, Sales Engineering, or a similar customer-facing technical leadership role, with prior people management experience.
  • Strong background working with open-source software business models and cloud-first technologies.
  • Hands-on experience designing and supporting scalable, highly available distributed systems.
  • Proven ability to balance strategic leadership with hands-on technical engagement when needed.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to bridge business goals and technical solutions.
  • Experience operating in fast-growing, high-change environments and building teams from early stages.

Bonus Points For

  • Experience deploying applications or selling into environments with major cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure).
  • Familiarity with database technologies, data modelling, and analytical workloads.
  • Familiarity with observability technologies.

Perks

  • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
  • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
  • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity.
  • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries.
  • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee.
  • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

Culture - We All Shape It

As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture.

Are you interested in finding out more about our culture? Learn more about our values here. Check out our blog posts or follow us on LinkedIn to find out more about what’s happening at ClickHouse.

Role Purpose

Drive operational excellence and customer experience improvement by mapping, analyzing, standardizing, and continuously enhancing end-to-end processes and customer journeys. The role ensures service consistency, identifies and addresses process gaps and root causes, maintains governance documentation and service standards, and tracks improvement initiatives through to successful implementation, enabling efficient operations and sustainable business performance.

Key accountabilities and decision ownership

  • Map and document end-to-end customer journeys, operational processes, handoffs and decision points.
  • Identify process gaps, customer pain points, control weakness and root causes of recurring failures.
  • Develop, review and maintain SOPs, Business Playbook, process maps and service standards.
  • Analyse customer complaints to identify trends and improvement opportunities.
  • Track improvement actions, risks and decisions through to completion.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to evaluate and enhance customer journeys, ensuring seamless, efficient, and customer-centric experiences across all touchpoints

Core competencies, knowledge and experience

  • Customer Centric: Keeps customer needs at the center of process decision and improvement
  • Business Writing: Ability to prepare clear, concise, and practical SOPs, reports requirements and performance updates.
  • Analytical thinking: Ability to analyse operational, customer, and performance data to identify trends and risks.
  • Attention to detail & Problem Solving: Ability to investigate recurring issues, assess root causes and develop sustainable solutions.
  • Stakeholder Management: Ability to align stakeholders, clarify ownership, and resolve process related conflicts without direct authority.

Must have technical / Professional qualifications

  • 1- Bachelor’s degree in business Administration, Engineering, Supply Chain, or related field.
  • 4-6 years of relevant experience in business excellence, process improvement, customer experience, e-commerce, or operations.
  • Experience writing and implementing SOPs, process maps, and service standards.
  • Strong data analysis skills, including proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Proven ability to conduct root-cause analysis and develop process improvements.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to coordinate improvements across teams.

About the job

The MongoDB Customer Organization Portfolio Management Office (PMO) orchestrates strategic initiatives and programs that advance customer experience, functional excellence, and technology innovation. Through comprehensive portfolio governance, the PMO optimizes investments and drives value realization across MDB’s Customer priorities to accelerate enterprise growth and enable customer success.

As trusted advisors to leadership, the PMO implements data-driven frameworks that strengthen decision-making and cross-functional alignment of our most business-critical initiatives. This systematic approach to benefit tracking and resource optimization positions MongoDB to capture emerging market opportunities, maximize stakeholder value, and set new industry benchmarks.

About The Role

As "Program Manager, Customer Strategy & Operations" you’ll play a critical role as part of our PMO in the scaling of our Customer Organization.

In this role, you'll be the driving force behind a portfolio of programs and strategic initiatives, each aimed at realizing customer value, optimizing our operations, and advancing our capabilities. You'll thrive on complexity and make swift, thoughtful, data-driven decisions that balance outcomes across the function and our customer org. Your ability to anticipate obstacles, work through developing problem spaces, influence cross-functional alignment, and realize strategic outcomes will be crucial in maximizing our collective impact.

We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Gurugram for our hybrid working model. The working model for this role can be in office (3 days a week) or flexible (2 days a week).

The ideal candidate will:

  • Be a Leader: Driven by an attuned sense of priority and urgency to deliver on the right things at the right time. Orchestrate multiple, interconnected projects, programs, and portfolios to maximize long term benefits. Drive high-level decision-making that aligns program objectives with organizational goals and customer needs
  • Own the Strategy: Establish the strategic vision and maintain comprehensive governance structures for your programs. Oversee all plans, timelines, and resources to ensure cohesive delivery across the entire portfolio
  • #BuildTogether: Build credibility and earn trust in all your interactions. Lead your teams, stakeholders organizations toward simple, coherent approaches. Facilitate high-level communications and strategic forums to align your programs and portfolio across all organizational levels
  • Have a knack for looking around corners: Recognize patterns in a sea of information and take action accordingly. Anticipate program-wide risks and opportunities. Develop strategies to mitigate issues across multiple projects and programs, manage interdependencies, and capitalize on synergies within the portfolio
  • Follow the data: Understand how data contributes to better story telling. Devise compelling program-wide metrics and analytics frameworks. Leverage cross-program data to inform strategic decision-making and drive continuous improvement across the entire portfolio
  • Detest surprises: Sweep nothing under the rug. Establish clear program objectives, scope, deliverables, and roadmaps in partnership with senior leadership and key stakeholders that account for dependencies, constraints, and conflicts. Ensure alignment between individual programs and overarching portfolio goals
  • Live in the future: Take a long term position in all that you do and help others see the big picture. Develop long-term program strategies that anticipate evolving business needs. Implement scalable frameworks and best practices that elevate program management capabilities across the organization
  • Be a good human: Leave your ego at the door. Model accountability and create the structures that help cultivate it in others. Provide mentorship and guidance to teams across your programs. Foster a culture of ownership, integrity, and collaboration that spans multiple teams and departments

Requirements

  • Experience: At least 5+ years in a program or portfolio management role overseeing a portfolio of complex, interdependent programs in a technical environment. Proven track record of delivering strategic outcomes through multi-program initiatives across various methodologies
  • Communication & Consensus: Exceptional ability to articulate program vision, status, and impact to diverse audiences. Orchestrate collaboration between senior leaders, cross-functional teams, and external stakeholders to ensure program outcomes align with organizational strategy and customer needs. Skilled at influencing without authority and facilitating high-level strategic discussions to drive results
  • Adaptability: Navigate complex, evolving program landscapes. Ability to pivot program direction in response to changing business priorities while maintaining overall strategic alignment across the portfolio
  • Improvement: Implement program-wide continuous improvement initiatives. Foster a culture of innovation and efficiency across multiple programs and portfolio teams
  • Optimization: Develop and execute strategies for optimal resource allocation across the program portfolio. Balance competing priorities and interdependencies to maximize overall program impact
  • Change: Lead large-scale organizational and structural changes through strategic program and portfolio initiatives. Ensure smooth adoption of new processes, tools, or methodologies across multiple teams and departments

Success Measures

  • In 3 months you will have gained a comprehensive understanding of MongoDB's ecosystem, fully integrated into Customer and PMO processes, and begun developing a strategic roadmap for your programs and portfolio
  • In 6 months you'll have established robust program governance structures, aligned multiple projects and programs with our organizational goals, and demonstrated initial strategic impact across your portfolio
  • In 9 months you'll have identified opportunities for program-wide operational improvements, built consensus among senior stakeholders for these initiatives, and begun implementing changes that enhance overall program and portfolio effectiveness to materialize organizational value

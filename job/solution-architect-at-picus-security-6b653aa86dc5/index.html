About Picus

Picus Security is an exposure validation company. One platform proves what attackers can exploit and what your defenses stop, turning every exposure into a defensible decision, autonomously and at the machine speed today's AI threats demand.

Picus validates attack surfaces, exposures, and security controls as one continuous loop: validate, decide, fix, re-validate. Instead of ranking findings by severity score, Picus proves which exposures are genuinely exploitable in your environment, including the business-critical, restricted, and air-gapped assets a live exploit can never safely touch, so teams act on what truly matters and resolve each exposure into a clear call to patch, mitigate, monitor, or accept with evidence.

The Picus Platform spans Autonomous Penetration Testing, Exposure Validation, and Breach and Attack Simulation, unified by Picus Swarm, a swarm of AI agents that runs the whole validation loop continuously. It reaches across on-prem, hybrid cloud, and endpoint environments, with 75+ integrations that ingest from scanners like Tenable and Wiz and operationalize through your EDR, SIEM, firewall, and ticketing tools.

The pioneer of Breach and Attack Simulation, Picus proves and improves the security controls you already own, doubling control effectiveness within three months. Picus holds a 95% recommendation rate, 4.9 on G2, and 4.8 on Gartner Peer Insights, and is the #1 Leader on Frost Radar for Automated Security Validation.

About The Role

We are looking for a seasoned, hardworking, innovative, and overachieving Solutions Architect with proven experience and a passion for selling technology solutions to senior executives. You will be responsible for selling to new prospects, upselling, cross-selling, and diligently handing off new customers to Customer Success to support customer satisfaction and contribute to the overall success of the sales team and the business. This position requires that the candidate be an industry subject matter expert with business acumen.

What You Will Do

  • Support Enterprise Account Executives in solution selling to the prospect account base,
  • Drive technical engagements with prospects to secure technical wins and accelerate the deal cycle,
  • Partner with sales executives to plan, prepare, and execute strategic deals in a complex sales environment,
  • Build compelling business cases that demonstrate ROI and strategic alignment,
  • Match customer challenges and requirements to tailored technical solutions,
  • Create and deliver persuasive presentations, demos, and RFP responses that clearly communicate the Picus platform's value proposition,
  • Manage PoC (Proof of Concept) projects with clear deliverables and timelines,
  • Stay current on competitive offerings and provide insights to product and marketing teams,
  • Act as a trusted advisor to customers, shaping strategic growth plans that drive loyalty, retention, and expansion,
  • Cultivate long-lasting relationships with key decision-makers and stakeholders to embed Picus as a critical part of their security strategy,
  • Support the technical onboarding process and ongoing engagements to ensure effective adoption and customer proficiency,
  • Work with internal teams to monitor customer health metrics and proactively take actions to improve value realization and prevent churn,
  • Identify and unlock upsell and cross-sell opportunities in collaboration with sales,
  • Inform customers about emerging cybersecurity trends and Picus innovations,
  • Represent the customer’s voice internally by funneling actionable feedback to product, R&D, and support teams,
  • Build strong relationships with technical contacts within partners and distributors,
  • Support and educate partners to deepen their technical knowledge of the Picus platform,
  • Collaborate on joint customer engagements and drive joint success through the channel.

What You Have

  • 5+ years of experience as a Cybersecurity Solutions Architect / Security Architect / Pre-Sales Engineer,
  • Solid technical background with understanding and/or hands-on experience in networking and cybersecurity,

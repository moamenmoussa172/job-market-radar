Recruitment Fraud Alert

We’ve learned that scammers are impersonating Commvault team members—including HR and leadership—via email or text. These bad actors may conduct fake interviews and ask for personal information, such as your social security number.

What to know:

  • Commvault does not conduct interviews by email or text.
  • We will never ask you to submit sensitive documents (including banking information, SSN, etc) before your first day.
  • Commvault recruiters may contact prospective candidates through LinkedIn and other professional networking platforms regarding career opportunities. If you are unsure whether a recruiting communication is legitimate, review the profile to ensure they have a badge verifying them as a Commvault employee.

If you suspect a recruiting scam, please contact us at wwrecruitingteam@commvault.com

About Commvault

Commvault (NASDAQ: CVLT) delivers continuous recovery for the AI era. We help organizations stay ready for constant disruption by returning critical data, applications, identities, and AI systems to a clean, trusted state. Our purpose-built recovery solutions span cloud-native and hybrid environments, leveraging AI to enable resilience at enterprise scale.

The Opportunity:

The Principal AI & Semantic Engineer is the senior technical authority responsible for connecting governed enterprise data and business meaning with scalable analytics and artificial intelligence solutions. This role defines the architecture, standards, and engineering patterns for semantic models, governed metrics, AI-ready data products, machine learning operations, and generative AI consumption.

Operating at the intersection of Analytics, Data Engineering, Data Science, Governance, and AI Platform Engineering, this role ensures that enterprise data, metrics, models, and knowledge assets can be reliably discovered, understood, deployed, monitored, and reused. The Principal AI & Semantic Engineer provides hands-on technical leadership, guides complex implementations, and mentors engineers and analysts while remaining an individual contributor.

What you’ll do…

Semantic Architecture & Governed Metrics

  • Define enterprise standards and reference patterns for semantic models, dimensional models, business metrics, KPIs, hierarchies, calculations, and reusable analytical assets.
  • Architect semantic consumption patterns across Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, Databricks Gold-layer data, and approved AI applications.
  • Partner with business domain owners, analysts, and Data Governance to translate business concepts into consistent, governed definitions and analytical models.
  • Lead design reviews for semantic models and analytical data products, balancing usability, reuse, performance, security, and maintainability.
  • Ensure semantic assets are documented with appropriate metadata, lineage, ownership, and change controls.

AI Engineering & MLOps

  • Define engineering standards for deploying, versioning, testing, monitoring, and governing machine learning and generative AI solutions.
  • Architect reusable MLand LLM patterns, including CI/CD, model and prompt versioning, experiment tracking, evaluation, observability, rollback, and production support.
  • Guide implementation of feature pipelines, model registries, inference workflows, retrieval pipelines, vectorized knowledge assets, and AI evaluation frameworks.
  • Partner with Data Scientists and AI product teams to move prototypes into secure, scalable, and supportable production services.
  • Implement technical controls for traceability, reproducibility, model performance, data drift, responsible AI requirements, and operational reliability.

AI-Ready Data & Knowledge Enablement

  • Design curated, governed data products and knowledge assets for predictive analytics, generative AI, copilots, agents, and retrieval-augmented generation.
  • Define how structured data, enterprise metrics, metadata, documents, embeddings, and retrieval indexes are linked and consumed.
  • Ensure AI applications use approved business definitions, metrics, access policies, and authoritative data sources.
  • Collaborate with Architecture and Governance teams on ontology, glossary, metadata, lineage, classification, and policy integration.

Technical Leadership & Collaboration

  • Serve as the technical escalation point for complex semantic, analytics, MLOps, and AI engineering decisions.
  • Create reference implementations, reusable frameworks, coding standards, and engineering playbooks.
  • Mentor Senior Analytics & AI Engineers, Semantic Engineers, Data Analysts, and Data Scientists.
  • Influence cross-functional roadmaps and communicate architecture decisions, trade-offs, risks, and business outcomes to technical and executive audiences.

Evaluate emerging technologies and recommend pragmatic adoption paths aligned with enterprise architecture and governance standards.

Who you are?

  • 10+ years of experience across analytics engineering, business intelligence, semantic modeling, data engineering, machine learning engineering, MLOps, or related disciplines.
  • Demonstrated experience serving as a principal-level technical leader or architect for enterprise data, analytics, or AI solutions.
  • Deep expertise in SQL, dimensional modeling, semantic model design, analytical performance optimization, and governed metric implementation.
  • Hands-on experience with Microsoft Power BI and/or Microsoft Fabric semantic models, including DAX and enterprise-scale model design.
  • Hands-on experience with Databricks or a comparable cloud data and AI platform, including production data pipelines and model lifecycle capabilities.
  • Experience designing or operating CI/CD, experiment tracking, model registries, monitoring, evaluation, and observability for ML or generative AI solutions.
  • Working knowledge of generative AI patterns such as embeddings, vector retrieval, RAG, prompt and model evaluation, and agent-based solutions.
  • Strong understanding of metadata, lineage, data quality, access control, business glossary, and responsible AI principles.
  • Proven ability to mentor technical professionals, lead architecture reviews, and influence stakeholders without direct authority.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Information Systems, or a related field; equivalent practical experience will be considered.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Microsoft Purview, Unity Catalog, MLflow, Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions, and cloud observability tooling.
  • Experience supporting enterprise Finance, GTM, Product, Customer, or Workforce analytics domains.
  • Experience establishing reusable semantic or AI platform capabilities in a federated operating model.

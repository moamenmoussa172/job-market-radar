About the job

The position acts as a Customer and Supplier Master Data (CMD/SMD) analyst within Bosch Home Confort.
The role takes global domain ownership for business partner master data, driving process standardization,
data quality, and compliance to ensure a reliable 360-degree view of customers and suppliers across global and regional systems.
Core responsibilities
• Take domain ownership of Business Partner Master Data (CMD/SMD), implement global "rulebooks",
data standards, and quality KPIs.
• Define and optimize global workflows for the creation, validation, duplicate checking, and
maintenance of customer and supplier master data.
• Provide system and functional expertise for CMD/SMD within ATHENA, core ERPs, and connected
SaaS applications (e.g. Salesforce, Ariba).
• Define and manage the data mapping from regional and legacy systems into the central ATHENA
platform.
• Align and collaborate with regional teams to enforce global standard adoption and drive process
harmonization.

• Professional experience in master data management (MDM) with a clear focus on
business partner domains (customers, suppliers/vendors).
• In-depth knowledge of business partner processes in SAP (SD/MM modules) and hands-on experience
with a centralized master data management platform is essential. Experience with CRM platforms (SAP,
Microsoft, Salesforce) or purchasing platforms (Ariba) is an asset.
• Strong understanding of business workflows dependent on business partner master data, such as
Order-to-Cash (O2C) and Procure-to-Pay (P2P).
• Strong analytical, structured, and detail-oriented approach with a passion for data quality and
process standardization.
• Fluent in English (written and spoken).

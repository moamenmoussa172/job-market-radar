About the job

Caseware is one of Canada's original Fintech companies, having led the global audit and accounting software industry for over 30 years, with more than 500,000 users across 130 countries and available in 16 different languages. While you might not have heard of us (yet) over 36,000 accounting and audit professionals list Caseware as a skill on their LinkedIn profiles!

Caseware is seeking a NetSuite Administrator/Developer to support and evolve our NetSuite ERP ecosystem. This role will focus on system administration, configuration, customization, and integrations that enable finance and business teams to operate effectively at scale. Working closely with Finance, RevOps, and IT, you will help optimize workflows, maintain data integrity across connected systems, and deliver solutions that improve automation, compliance, and operational efficiency.

The ideal candidate brings hands-on NetSuite expertise, strong integration experience, and a solid understanding of financial and commercial business processes. This is a great opportunity to play a key role in strengthening the systems that power core business operations.

❗This is a new vacancy.

❗This is a full-time, permanent position.

📍This is a hybrid role, requiring the successful candidate to work three days a week at our Toronto oﬃce (351 King St E, Toronto, ON).

What you will be doing:

  • Assist the Finance/Enterprise Systems team in administering and configuring the NetSuite ERP, including roles, permissions, workflows (SuiteFlow), saved searches, custom records and forms to support finance and business operations.
  • Design, build, and maintain integrations between NetSuite and other enterprise systems (e.g., Salesforce, billing, payroll, and data warehouse platforms) using SuiteScript, SuiteTalk (SOAP/REST), and middleware/iPaaS tools.
  • Partner with Finance, RevOps, and IT stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business processes into scalable NetSuite configurations and customizations.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve system issues, data discrepancies, and integration failures across connected financial systems, ensuring timely break-fix support.
  • Support quote-to-cash, order-to-cash, and lead-to-revenue workflows by maintaining data integrity between NetSuite and upstream/downstream systems like Salesforce.
  • Lead or support NetSuite upgrades, sandbox testing, and release management, minimizing disruption to financial operations.
  • Develop and maintain custom scripts (SuiteScript), scheduled scripts, workflows, and RESTlets to automate manual processes and extend NetSuite functionality.
  • Support integration of acquired entities into NetSuite, including data migration, chart of accounts alignment, and workflow standardization.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation, process runbooks, and training materials for system configurations, integrations, and end-user procedures.
  • Ensure system security, SOX/audit compliance, and adherence to data governance standards across financial systems.
  • Participate in sprint grooming, standups, and retrospectives to plan, track, and continuously improve delivery of enhancements and support work.

What you will bring:

  • Three (3)+ years of experience as a NetSuite Administrator and/or Developer, including hands-on configuration, customization, and scripting.
  • Proficiency with SuiteScript, SuiteFlow, SuiteTalk (SOAP/REST web services), and SuiteBuilder customizations.
  • Experience integrating NetSuite with third-party systems (e.g., Salesforce, Avalara, payment processors, data warehouses) via APIs or middleware/iPaaS platforms (e.g., Celigo, Boomi, MuleSoft).
  • Solid understanding of core financial and accounting processes, including general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, revenue recognition, and financial reporting.
  • Familiarity with commercial and financial workflows including quote-to-cash, subscription management, and lead-to-revenue.
  • Experience with data migration, ETL processes, and system integration testing.
  • Ability to manage and prioritize tasks/projects in JIRA, including supporting refinement and sprint planning.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to diagnose issues across integrated systems.
  • Strong communication skills, written and verbal, with the ability to translate technical concepts for business stakeholders.
  • NetSuite certification (e.g., SuiteFoundation, ERP Consultant) is an asset.

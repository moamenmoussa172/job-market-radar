About the job

Migration is the on-ramp to the AI era. Before customers can realise the full value of generative AI, they must exit their data centres, modernise their estates, and build on a cloud native foundation. As the EMEA Infrastructure Migration and Modernisation (IMM) GTM Strategy and Business Development Leader, you will own the go-to-market strategy for IMM across nine EMEA regions. The business spans data centre exits, VMware migrations via multiple pathways including our new Amazon EVS, and modernisation with AWS Transform. You will shape how EMEA's largest organisations move from legacy infrastructure to platforms built for the AI workloads of the future.
Reporting to the EMEA EAMM Specialist Leader, you will be the single-threaded owner (STO) of the EMEA IMM business, accountable for input and output goal attainment from pipeline creation through launch to realised revenue. You will be a trusted advisor to AGS Specialist Sales Leaders and Worldwide leaders up to VP level, and the primary interface to the Worldwide IMM team and AWS service teams, bringing them the customer and commercial insights that shape global roadmaps.
This is a matrixed leadership role. You will deliver results at scale with the 9 regional AGS Specialist teams, APO, adjacent specialist domains (Storage, EUC, Database), and other EMEA migration teams, influencing well beyond your own organisation. You will work backwards from customer outcomes, not technology, to shape migration and modernisation programmes that deliver measurable business value. You will lead with speed and conviction: make high-judgement calls with incomplete information, act early when the business is at risk, and course-correct quickly. The role demands high autonomy and high accountability in a complex, fast-moving environment.
Key job responsibilities
- Own and grow the EMEA IMM business, accountable for input and output goal attainment.
- Define the multi-year strategy, set goals and KPIs, and build scalable mechanisms (pipeline cadences, co-sell motions, funding frameworks, business reviews) with the Worldwide IMM team, the 9 regional AGS Specialist teams, APO, adjacent specialist domains, and other EMEA migration teams.
- Champion Customer Obsession across the region, working backwards from customer needs to define initiatives, prioritise investments, and bring the voice of the customer to Worldwide and service teams.
- Serve as Single-Threaded Owner (STO) of the EMEA IMM pipeline, from creation to realised revenue. Anticipate risks, drive Get-to-Goal plans, and lead the MBR and QBR cadence, representing EMEA in the Worldwide MBR.
- Allocate specialist capacity to the highest-value opportunities, positioning migration and modernisation as the foundation for cloud native and generative AI adoption.
- Build, coach, and retain a high-performing, multi-geography team of senior GTM specialists, and develop future leaders.
- Lead across a matrixed organisation, aligning Sales, Solutions Architecture, Business Development, Partners, and Marketing on shared outcomes.
- Own CxO relationships (CIO, CTO, Head of Infrastructure) and sponsor complex programmes, including data centre exits, VMware migrations (VMCCO), and modernisation with AWS Transform and Amazon EVS.
- Drive partner-led growth with GSIs, SIs, and technology partners, scaling RAPID and VMCCO and making AWS Transform the default partner tool.
- Act as a primary interface to AWS service teams, sharing customer insights that shape roadmaps, and represent AWS at executive briefings, industry conferences, and partner events.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent
- Experience building and leading large teams and have worked in matrixed operating structures
- Experience in Go-To-Market, Business Development, Sales, or Consulting
- Experience working and communicating with multiple stakeholders, C-level executives and cross functional teams or equivalent
- Experience in strategic thinking about business, enterprise software products, and new technology platforms and architectures or equivalent
- Experience working with Enterprise Application Modernization and Migration technologies, including, but not limited to VMware and Windows environments
- Experience managing and developing high performance teams
- MBA
- Multi-cultural leadership across European/EMEA markets

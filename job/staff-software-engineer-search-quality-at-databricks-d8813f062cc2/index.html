About the job

Staff Software Engineer (L6)

Location: Bangalore, India

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

As a Search Quality Engineer, you will build the contextual backbone that powers both AI agents and human users across the Databricks platform. You will own the quality of retrieval systems that enable LLMs to reason over data they weren't trained on, ensuring accurate, high-stakes business actions. Your work will connect structured and unstructured data sources, balancing traditional keyword-based search with semantic vector search to deliver high-recall, intuitive experiences.

The Impact You Will Have

- Own the design and implementation of hybrid retrieval systems that balance keyword-based and semantic vector search for both AI agents and human users
- Build and optimize ranking models that satisfy human readability requirements while providing LLM-ready context
- Develop evaluation frameworks and relevance scoring systems to ensure search quality and prevent AI hallucinations
- Drive technical strategy for search quality across heterogeneous data sources including SQL tables, unstructured documents, and real-time metrics
- Lead cross-team initiatives to improve data discovery and retrieval infrastructure at scale
- Mentor engineers and raise the technical bar for search and information retrieval across the organization

What We Look For

- 8+ years of software engineering experience
- Deep experience with information retrieval systems including search engines, ranking algorithms, and relevance optimization
- Strong understanding of hybrid retrieval approaches combining traditional IR methods with semantic search and embeddings
- Experience building and operating evaluation frameworks for search quality, including relevance metrics such as nDCG, MRR, and Precision@K
- Proficiency in technologies including Lucene, Elasticsearch, or similar search platforms
- Experience with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures and LLM-based systems
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to balance competing optimization goals across different user types
- B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

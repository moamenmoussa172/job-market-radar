About the job

Department: Platform Infrastructure

Location: Berlin, Germany

Team description

You’ll join the newly formed Developer Experience team, which improves how around 700 SumUp engineers build, ship, and observe software. The team builds the internal tools, services, and abstractions that make infrastructure invisible to product teams. That includes modernising an observability stack that has grown organically and shaping what useful debugging looks like for both engineers and AI agents. This role is software engineering first and DevOps aware second. You’ll define open-ended problems before solving them, and you’ll build agreement across product engineering, Cloud Infrastructure and Site Reliability Engineering.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build and evolve services for developer tooling, such as cost reporting, observability pipelines, internal APIs and command line tools
  • Build and extend the internal observability platform so systems are easy for developers to understand and act on
  • Take ownership of components in the observability stack (Loki, Grafana, Prometheus, Alloy and exporters) and improve them
  • Explore what telemetry and debugging context AI agents need, compared with human engineers
  • Create automation, self-service features, and integrations that reduce manual work for product engineering teams
  • Lead design discussions, write and review RFCs, mentor mid-level and junior engineers, and raise standards through code review

What you’ll bring

  • Strong software engineering fundamentals (Go preferred), with a proven track record of shipping production backend services, command-line tools, or platform tooling.
  • Solid understanding of observability and OpenTelemetry (metrics, logs, traces, SLIs and SLOs) and hands-on experience building observability tooling or integrations. Familiarity with Honeycomb or Grafana is a strong plus.
  • Working knowledge of Kubernetes as a user, and comfort with Terraform, Helm, ArgoCD, and CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions to deliver your own services.
  • Experience mentoring engineers and leading design discussions, supported by excellent written and verbal communication and a documentation-first mindset.
  • A platform engineering mindset that treats internal developers as customers, with a bias toward shipping and iterating, comfort with ambiguity, and a habit of digging into root causes.

About the job

The role

HelloFresh is going AI-first, and we need Staff-level strategists to set the direction for that shift across the UX organization. As a Staff UX Strategist & Researcher, you’ll take on the most ambiguous, highest-stakes questions across the customer experience. You’ll decide which problems are worth solving, set the research and strategy standards others follow, and turn uncertainty into decisions that hold up at the executive level. You’ll challenge leadership assumptions before investment is committed, and you’ll leave the organization better at making decisions, not just better informed.
You’ll bring a deep point of view on how AI changes research and strategy, and you’ll use it as a genuine partner across the product design lifecycle. Strategy and research are your deepest strengths, and you have enough breadth to set quality bars across interface design, service design, and content. You know when to raise the bar, when to delegate, and when to bring in a specialist. We’re looking for people who make the people around them better as much as they make HelloFresh better. If you don’t tick every box but have the judgment and track record for this level, we’d love to hear from you.

Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.

What you’ll do

  • Set the research and strategy agenda: Define the questions that matter across product, marketing, operations, and business, and align leaders on where to invest research effort before projects are scoped.
  • Lead strategic research across the portfolio: Own multi-quarter research and strategy programs end-to-end, from problem framing through insight delivery and adoption, balancing primary, secondary, qualitative, and quantitative methods.
  • Shape experiences across the ecosystem: Partner with senior leaders in product, design, marketing, operations, and business to shape the full customer and service ecosystem, not just individual touchpoints.
  • Challenge the bets before they’re made: Identify risks and weak assumptions in major initiatives early, and stress-test business cases against customer evidence, market context, and KPIs.
  • Influence at executive altitude: Shape decisions with VP, SVP, and C-level stakeholders, building opportunity frameworks, strategic directions, and concept artifacts that force a choice in the room.
  • Design and lead workshops at scale: Set the approach for co-creation and alignment sessions across multiple teams, and coach others to run them, from the room and agenda to the outputs.
  • Set the method standard: Define how the UX organization selects and applies research methods, rightsized to speed and rigor, and build shared playbooks others can use.
  • Lead AI-native practice: Set direction for how AI is applied across framing, research, synthesis, and strategy. Evaluate new approaches, and make the calls on where AI should and shouldn’t be trusted.
  • Build capability across UX: Raise the minimum quality bar across interface design, service design, and content by sharing what works, mentoring specialists and generalists, and removing barriers between disciplines.

What you’ll bring

  • At least 5 years of experience in UX research, UX strategy, Service Design, or a closely related field within a product or tech environment, with a track record of taking ambiguous work to outcome.
  • Deep mixed-methods expertise and the judgment to choose the right approach for each context, with the ability to explain those choices to others.
  • A proven record of influencing VP, SVP, and C-level stakeholders, changing decisions and investment rather than just informing them.
  • Experience designing and leading workshops and co-creation sessions across multiple cross-functional teams, and developing others to do the same.
  • Ability to prototype and visualize strategic direction independently, and to set the quality bar for concept sketches, lo-fi prototypes, service blueprints, and AI-assisted artifacts.
  • Strong strategic storytelling and visual communication, with a history of deliverables that move organizations, not just inform them.
  • A clear, demonstrated point of view on how AI should change research and strategy workflows, where its limits are, and practical experience leading AI adoption in real project contexts.
  • Business acumen at the level of portfolio trade-offs, connecting research to market context, commercial implications, and organizational priorities.
  • Depth plus breadth, and a track record of raising the standard for others. You can set quality bars across disciplines and know when a problem needs a specialist’s depth.

Flexible Hybrid Approach

At HelloFresh, we know that flexible work arrangements are essential in enabling you to do your best work, while balancing your personal and life needs. Offering remote work flexibility, along with the opportunity to interact and collaborate in the office are all a part of creating a great employee experience.

To meet these needs, we are pleased to provide Flexible Hybrid work. Flexible Hybrid is a people-first approach that is based on choice, trust, personalization, and empowers teams to choose when and how often they work from the office and work from home, in addition to team days and company days. This means a minimum of 2 days in office per week, with most teams in office between 2-3 days a week.

About the job

Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

About the team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Crypto Perpetuals team is building Robinhood’s advanced trading experience for customers who want fast, intuitive access to crypto derivatives markets. Our team powers the mobile workflows that help traders monitor price action, understand market depth, manage risk, and place trades with confidence in rapidly moving markets.

We work at the intersection of high-performance UI, real-time market data, and complex trading systems. From responsive charting and order entry to position management and risk-aware product experiences, our work directly shapes how active traders engage with Robinhood’s crypto platform.

As a Senior iOS Engineer on the Crypto Perpetuals team, you’ll design and build performant mobile experiences that support real-time data, sophisticated trading flows, and highly reliable execution. You’ll partner closely with product, design, and backend engineering to deliver intuitive tools for serious traders, while helping raise the technical bar across the team through strong architecture, execution, and mentorship.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA OR New York, NYoffice, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you'll do

  • 6+ years of professional experience developing iOS applications
  • Design and implement scalable iOS application architecture that supports advanced crypto trading experiences used by Robinhood's most active investors
  • Partner with product managers, designers, backend engineers, and data teams to deliver fast, reliable, and intuitive pro-grade trading tools on mobile
  • Improve application performance, real-time data handling, observability, and release stability across core crypto trading workflows
  • Lead technical planning and provide guidance during system design reviews, implementation discussions, and incident resolution efforts for high-stakes trading features
  • Mentor iOS engineers through code reviews, technical feedback, and structured development support while contributing to engineering standards and documentation

What you bring

  • Extensive experience building and maintaining large-scale iOS applications using Swift and modern iOS development frameworks
  • Experience designing mobile systems with clear architecture patterns, scalable component design, and maintainable testing strategies — particularly for data-intensive or real-time use cases
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with product, design, backend, and infrastructure partners to deliver customer-focused technical solutions
  • Experience leading technical initiatives across multiple teams and balancing short-term delivery needs with long-term platform improvements
  • Experience working on financial platforms, trading systems, or real-time market data products (preferred)
  • Clear communication skills and ability to translate complex trading concepts into intuitive mobile experiences
  • This position is restricted to US citizens or lawful permanent residents

  • Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits

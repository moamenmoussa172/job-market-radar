About this Role

We are seeking a Cyber Security Incident Management & Monitoring Specialist to join our Cybersecurity team in Bangalore. The role focuses on supporting the operational management of cybersecurity platforms, ensuring platform availability, monitoring service performance, and responding to cyber events, threats, vulnerabilities, and security risks. This opportunity is ideal for professionals with strong experience in cybersecurity incident management, security monitoring, GCP, Looker, and telecommunications roaming environments who are passionate about protecting business-critical services and enabling secure operations.

What you’ll do

  • Support the execution of Vodafone's cybersecurity strategy, operating model, and security controls in alignment with established cybersecurity standards.
  • Monitor cybersecurity platforms to ensure service availability, identify disruptions, and support incident resolution activities.
  • Contribute to the technical operation and lifecycle management of cybersecurity platforms.
  • Detect, investigate, and support responses to cybersecurity events, threats, risks, and vulnerabilities in accordance with established management response processes.
  • Participate in transformation, maintenance, and project activities to ensure security controls remain effective and aligned with business requirements.
  • Apply cyber defence best practices across the supported market environments.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to provide timely communication and support during incidents and service-impacting events.
  • Analyse operational and security-related data to support informed decision-making and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Translate business requirements into effective technical and analytical solutions.
  • Perform additional cybersecurity activities and responsibilities as required to support business objectives.

Who you are

  • Proven experience in cybersecurity incident management and security operations with over 5 years of relevant industry experience.
  • Strong hands-on expertise in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Looker.
  • Experience monitoring cybersecurity tools, platforms, uptime, and operational performance.
  • Good understanding of cybersecurity principles, threat detection, vulnerability management, and incident response processes.
  • Knowledge of telecom industry operations, with strong understanding of Telecom Roaming being highly desirable.
  • Ability to interpret business requirements and convert them into actionable technical analytics solutions.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making capabilities.
  • Effective communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to work in a collaborative, fast-paced, and technology-driven environment.

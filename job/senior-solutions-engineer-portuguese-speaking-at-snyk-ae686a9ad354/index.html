About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Brazil.

Unleash AI Innovators, Securely

Software is being rewritten in real time. AI agents are starting to write more code than humans do, at a speed no security team has ever had to match, and that single shift is opening up the biggest opportunity our industry has seen in a decade. We're not bracing for it, we're building the company that owns it.

Snyk is building the next generation security platform where security isn't a set of tools people operate. It's a team of security agents working alongside your engineers, finding, fixing, and governing risk at the speed software is now built. These agents work alongside both the company build and the agents they adopt. Security stops being a queue of work waiting for people, and becomes a coordinated workforce companies direct and trust.

Job Summary

We're looking for a Senior Solutions Engineer to lead the technical side of the sales cycle for Snyk's customers and prospects across Latin America, with a focus on Portuguese-speaking markets. You'll run demos, workshops, and proofs of concept for developers, security teams, and executives, advise organizations on securing code, open source dependencies, containers, and cloud infrastructure, and help them adopt AI-assisted development safely. You'll also carry the voice of the LATAM market back to our product and engineering teams. This is a high-impact role at a company building the security layer for an AI-native software world.

About the Team

You'll join Snyk's Solutions Engineering team, the technical bridge between our Sales, Product, Marketing, Partner, and Customer Success organizations. You'll partner daily with Account Executives covering LATAM and with channel partners, and work alongside Solutions Engineers around the world who share playbooks, demos, and lessons from the field.

What You’ll Do

  • Lead the technical aspects of the sales cycle for new and existing LATAM customers, from discovery and demos through proof of concept and technical close
  • Deliver tailored technical demonstrations and DevSecOps workshops in Portuguese and English to developers, DevOps teams, security practitioners, and senior technical and business leaders
  • Own and manage proofs of concept end to end, setting clear success criteria, keeping stakeholders aligned, and translating results into business value
  • Partner with Account Executives to shape account strategy across the region, including large enterprise organizations in industries such as financial services, telecommunications, and technology
  • Advise customers on developer and application security programs, open source and cloud native security, and the safe adoption of AI coding assistants and AI-generated code
  • Show customers how the Snyk platform fits into their developer workflows, and work with our Customer Success team to guide technical implementation after the sale
  • Work with channel partners and system integrators in the region to build their proficiency in selling and supporting Snyk
  • Build and maintain technical and business champions at key customers and across the ecosystem
  • Bring customer and partner feedback to Product and Engineering, influencing new features and regional requirements
  • Contribute to proposals and RFP responses, and help build documentation, localized demos, and reusable technical content for the wider team
  • Mentor teammates and raise the bar on demo quality, discovery, and proof of concept execution
  • Represent Snyk at customer meetings, industry events, and conferences across the region, with travel as needed

What you’ll bring

  • Fluency in Portuguese and English, written and spoken. You'll present to executives and technical practitioners in Portuguese and collaborate with global teams in English
  • 5+ years of experience in solutions engineering, sales engineering, development, or enterprise security consulting, ideally in developer tools, application security, or DevOps
  • A proven track record with global and enterprise organizations, managing and exceeding customer expectations across the full customer lifecycle
  • The ability to engage and present credibly at every level, from hands-on practitioners to executives, in large enterprise environments
  • Expert knowledge of the developer and DevOps ecosystems, CI/CD, cloud native practices, and application security best practices
  • Familiarity with a wide variety of programming languages and how they work
  • Working fluency with AI-assisted development tools such as Claude, Copilot, or Gemini, and the ability to speak credibly about the security implications of AI-generated code
  • Strong planning and communication skills: you set milestones, over-communicate, create alignment, and rally resources to satisfy stakeholders
  • Curiosity about new technologies and a habit of digging into how things work under the hood
  • A team-oriented mindset, a genuine appreciation for diversity, and a love of teaching and sharing knowledge
  • Nice to have: a developer background or hands-on coding experience, or deep security expertise
  • Nice to have: experience working with the channel ecosystem
  • Willingness to travel within the region to meet customers, partners, and teammates

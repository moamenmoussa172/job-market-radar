About the job

The Product Management team drives Asana’s product strategy and execution, translating customer needs and opportunities into a compelling roadmap and working cross-functionally to deliver impactful solutions for team productivity and collaboration. We’re helping to define the Work Management category and inventing new ways for teams to work more efficiently together and see the larger purpose behind their work. Our team works directly with Engineering, Design, User Research, and Data Science, as well as with Sales, Support, and Marketing to ship great products. You’ll take part in every type of product work here, from strategy to product to process improvements, conceptualizing, launching, and iterating on Asana itself for millions of teams around the world.

Joining our recently formed team in Warsaw, you’ll contribute to the product direction for Command, Asana’s new product for the agentic software development lifecycle and our bet on what the next $100M+ line of business looks like. As a Senior Product Manager, you’ll work from first principles to turn ambiguous problem spaces into sharp, opinionated workflows that feel fast, minimal, and “obviously right” to high-performing teams. You’ll validate your bets directly with demanding customers, from hypergrowth startups to global enterprises, and be accountable for turning those insights into a focused roadmap and real business impact.

This role is based in Warsaw with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday.

Our employees in Poland are employed under a contract of employment.

What You’ll Achieve

  • Deliver a high-craft product for engineering teams and developers, a new kind of customer for Asana beyond the business and ops teams we’ve traditionally served
  • Build product with strong passion and operational maturity
  • Balance priorities and use cases across customers, partner product teams, and enterprise compliance requirements
  • Negotiate with partner teams and deliver a compelling roadmap
  • Maximize clarity by bringing a data-driven mindset to setting concrete goals and milestones

This role is for someone who wants to build a category-defining product from the ground up - not optimize an inherited roadmap. You should be comfortable saying “no” often, shipping with strong opinions, holding the quality bar at the level of the best modern SaaS tools and innovating beyond it.

About You

What you need

  • 5+ years of product management experience.
  • Systems thinker: You’re just as comfortable modeling data relationships in a technical diagram as you are partnering with Design to walk stakeholders through a holistic UX flow.
  • Strategic: You can identify the most critical customer problems and scope pragmatic solutions that address them.
  • Get Stuff Done: You create, flex, and evangelize a roadmap, thoughtfully break down projects into MVPs and follow-up iterations, and ship with high velocity for business impact.
  • Customer-centric: You talk to customers directly, including technical users like engineers, dig into the roots of their needs, and synthesize research into a deep understanding and narrow definition of a problem.
  • Communication and collaboration: You partner with cross-functional teammates to deliver high-quality results. You speak with clarity and write sharp product specs. You are highly comfortable getting into deep technical discussions with engineers about the pros and cons of different approaches, and you pair with Design on complex user flows.
  • Craft: You have a strong sense of what makes a product great, can point to products you admire and explain why, and sweat the details in what you ship.
  • Growth Mindset: You lead with curiosity and are open to feedback in order to learn.

Nice to have

  • Background in B2B SaaS, complex data models, and human-centric UX
  • Experience building developer tools
  • Startup background

At Asana, we’re committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission. If you’re interested in this role and don’t meet every listed requirement, we still encourage you to apply.

What We’ll Offer

  • Generous, transparent and fair compensation system
  • Contract of Employment (and the option of 50% tax deductible costs for author’s rights usage in respect of applicable roles)
  • Health insurance with dental and travel coverage (Lux Med)
  • Meals reimbursement on the days that you work from the office
  • Career growth budget
  • Home office setup budget
  • Gym/Fitness reimbursement
  • Fertility healthcare and family-forming support with Carrot
  • Mental Health Support in Modern Health
  • Group life insurance
  • MacBooks with all necessary accessories

For this role, the estimated base salary range is between 29,000 - 31,935 PLN gross per month (subject to all taxes and necessary deductions). The actual base salary will vary based on various factors, including market and individual qualifications objectively assessed during the interview process. The listed range above is a guideline, and the base salary range for this role may be modified.

About Asana Command

Command is Asana’s new product for the agentic software development lifecycle, covering how engineering teams define, sequence, and track their work from initiative through delivery. Its core users are software engineers, tech leads, product managers, and engineering managers, a different audience from the business and ops teams who make up most of Asana’s customer base today. Command and Asana work as one connected experience, so business and R&D teams can plan from a shared source of truth instead of switching tools.

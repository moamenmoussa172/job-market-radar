About the job

Your tasks:

► Lead the design and development of scalable, secure, and high-performance web applications as a fullstack expert.

► Develop and design internal digital solutions that drive automation and directly support our long-term digitalization strategy — including advanced use cases with agentic AI for autonomous and intelligent systems.

► Mentor medior and junior developers, provide code reviews, and help define development standards.

► Make architectural decisions across backend (Java, Spring Boot) and frontend (React, Angular, or similar), balancing performance, maintainability, and user experience.

► Ensure seamless deployment and operations in containerized environments (Docker, Kubernetes) on cloud platforms (e.g. Azure).

► Take initiative in adopting and integrating AI-enhanced tools — from code copilots to advanced automation frameworks — to increase productivity and innovation.

► (Optional) Contribute your UX knowledge to help design intuitive and efficient interfaces.

► Bachelor’s / Master’s degree in Economical Informatics, Information Technology or Programming Mathematics OR equivalent working experiences

► 5+ years of experience in web application development field

► Java and/or Kotlin , Spring Framework /Boot, Spring Data, JPA / Hibernate

► Angular (typescript)/ React knowledge

► Knowledge in SQL & SQL-extensions (e.g. PL/SQL or TSQL); NoSQL & NewSQL Mongo DB experiences are advantages

► Docker and/or Kubernetes

► English intermediate level, German is an advantage

► Experiences in agile methodologies

► Logical and systems thinking

► Communicative and like to work in teams

► High motivation and flexibility for demanding tasks to achieve the goal

► Initiative and can-do attitude

► Experience with the productive operations of your developed code is a strong plus

We are looking to hire multiple employees for the advertised position, so more than one candidate will be selected.

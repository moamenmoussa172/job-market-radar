About the job

Job Location

WARSAW DOWNTOWN OFFICE

Job Description

We are seeking a highly skilled a Senior Azure & Network Automation Software Engineer to help drive innovation and transformation for their global enterprise network. Qualified candidates should have at least 5+ years of software design and implementation experience, working across wide range of network technologies and services including LAN, WAN, Security, wireless, SDWAN, load balancing, and cloud connectivity. The successful candidate will have extensive experience architecting and operating Azure Kubernetes environments, automating deployments through modern CI/CD frameworks, and provisioning cloud infrastructure using Terraform. They will leverage Python to build scalable automation solutions and possess practical experience implementing Agentic AI capabilities and AIOps strategies to improve operational efficiency, system reliability, and intelligent decision-making. The candidates must also exhibit strong professional skills including communications, self-motivated, adaptable and dependable.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain cloud infrastructure in Azure using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform).
  • Architect, deploy, and operate containerized workloads on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) including Helm charts, operators, and cluster lifecycle management.
  • Develop, maintain, and enforce CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions (or equivalent), integrating testing, security scanning, and automated deployments.
  • Build scalable automation solutions implementing Agentic AI capabilities and AIOps strategies to improve operational efficiency, system reliability, and intelligent decision-making.
  • Automate network and platform operations using scripts and code (Python, or similar) and integrate with configuration management systems.
  • Collaborate with network operations, security, and project teams to design resilient, observable, and secure solutions for hybrid cloud and on-prem environments.
  • Own infrastructure provisioning, environment lifecycle, and release processes with a focus on repeatability and reliability.
  • Troubleshoot production incidents, perform root cause analysis, and implement preventative automation.
  • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to operational runbooks, architecture documents, and knowledge sharing.

Desired Characteristics, Skills & Traits

  • Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
  • Clear documentation and communication skills. Ability to effectively prioritize and execute tasks in support of business-critical programs
  • Must be able to work independently to gather requirements and translate those into solutions
  • Strong analytical skills, able to leverage complex data to identify opportunities, recognize problems, and draw logical conclusions.
  • Ability to produce and present technical information to both technical and non-technical personnel
  • Good temperament, ability to manage stressful situations. Ability to provide customer-facing support in a professional manner.
  • Automator mindset — prefers code and automation over manual processes.

Job Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in software engineering, platform engineering, DevOps, or infrastructure automation, including hands-on use of GitHub Enterprise and modern version-control and pull-request practices.
  • Demonstrated experience designing, deploying, and supporting containerized applications on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), including networking, storage, ingress, RBAC, and cluster lifecycle management in enterprise environments.
  • Hands-on expertise with Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, including modules, state management, remote backends, and integration with enterprise CI/CD pipelines.
  • Practical experience applying Agentic AI technologies to automate workflows or improve operational efficiency and business processes.
  • Experience applying AIOps principles or tools to monitoring, incident response, root cause analysis, or platform operations.
  • Proficiency in Python, PowerShell, or a comparable programming or scripting language for automation and integration.
  • Experience implementing observability and security controls in cloud-native systems.
  • Understanding of data analytics, Agentic AI concepts, and DevOps practices.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Elastic Stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana).
  • Prior experience with network operational platforms such as Netbox, Zabbix, and Unimus.
  • Familiarity with agentic workflow automation tools such as Tines (agentic workflow management).
  • Familiarity with Terraform Enterprise or cloud-based state backends such as Terraform Cloud or Azure Storage.
  • Certifications such as Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator/Engineer, Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) or Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD), HashiCorp Terraform Associate.
  • Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams in agile environments.

What we offer

  • Responsibilities as of day 1. You will have project ownership and autonomy to deliver change and results from the beginning
  • Dynamic and encouraging work environment. At P&G our employees are at the core, we value every individual and encourage initiatives, promoting agility and work/life balance
  • Continuous mentoring, you will work with hardworking people and receive ongoing coaching and mentoring from your line manager and other colleagues. Corporate and functional training will enable you to succeed and develop from day one
  • Industry Certifications (ITIL, DevOps, MS portfolio etc), full additional benefit program like private health care, P&G Dynamic Living programs like sport cards, in-office fitness center, PG stock options, saving plans, lunch subsidy, regular salary increases and possible promotions, flexible work arrangements, mentoring programs & trainings
  • Big Picture understanding of P&G IT and Product Supply organization and its Services in global multi-functional teams with several locations across continents

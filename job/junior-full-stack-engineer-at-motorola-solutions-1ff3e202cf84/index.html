Department Overview
Motorola Solutions’ Avigilon Alta Video brings cloud video security systems to the next level. Alta’s cloud-native video security platform delivers industry-leading analytics and exceptional operational insights to help organizations observe, oversee, and analyse their environments to protect people, property, and places in real time.
The Alta Video Engineering Team injects intelligence into our approach to safety and across every solution we engineer. Our team designs, builds, and scales intelligent video microservices and distributed cloud infrastructure that empower human decision-makers at the centre, actively advancing our mission of "Solving for safer".
Our engineering hub is based in the heart of Edinburgh city centre, operating under a purposeful hybrid model with 2 collaborative days together on-site each week.


Job Description

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We empower each Motorolan to bring their authentic self to work, innovate with purpose, and build software that protects lives in the moments that matter. As a Full Stack Engineer joining our Edinburgh team, you will design, build, and scale resilient cloud-native applications and high-throughput services that help to provide critical safety intelligence across the globe.

In this role, you will be part of an inclusive, collaborative engineering environment where intelligent video analytics, event-driven microservices, and modern web interfaces intersect. You will partner across disciplines to translate complex operational workflows into performant interfaces and distributed services. We value practical problem-solving, technical curiosity, and foundational software engineering capability alongside equivalent practical experience.

Front-End Architecture & User Experience

  • Build responsive, high-performance web applications using modern TypeScript frameworks to deliver real-time video feeds
  • Partner with product designers and senior engineers to transform complex security workflows into clean, reliable interfaces that assist critical decision-making


Backend Microservices & Distributed Cloud Systems

  • Design, implement, and maintain resilient backend services and high-throughput APIs across a multi-language stack
  • Evolve cloud-native architecture to scale data ingestion and video processing pipelines across distributed microservices
  • Take end-to-end ownership of service lifecycle, contributing technical documentation and supporting engineering teams consuming your APIs

System Quality & Continuous Delivery

  • Invest in automated testing, observability, and robust CI/CD pipelines throughout the software development lifecycle to uphold system reliability
  • Integrate AI developer tooling across the engineering workflow to improve code quality, documentation accuracy, and delivery velocity
  • Participate constructively in code reviews, and cross-functional technical initiatives


Basic Requirements

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or similar discipline. Alternatively candidates with 1-2 years of industry experience will be considered.
  • Legal right to work in the UK and willing to commute 2 days a week to our office.
  • The Avigilon Alta Video teams default to programming using Go for backend and Typescript for frontend, but also use a wide variety of different languages and frameworks. It is not expected that you would be familiar with Go (or any of the other languages we use) but you should be enthusiastic and willing to learn new things!
  • We are looking for a team player who thinks holistically, enjoys solving complex problems and thrives in an agile, cross functional environment.

THE ROLE

Everpure is seeking a Hardware Engineering Manager to lead a team developing and supporting high-performance storage systems. This role oversees the full hardware lifecycle, from design through production and customer support. The ideal candidate is a strong technical and people leader who can set direction, deliver results, and collaborate across the organization.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of hardware engineers.
  • Set technical direction and execution plans for complex compute, storage, networking, and I/O systems.
  • Own team delivery across architecture, schematic capture, PCB layout, eDVT, qualification, failure analysis, and production release.
  • Harden and evolve hardware validation and qualification strategies, including automated testing, system stress testing, and coverage reviews.
  • Drive root-cause and failure-analysis efforts for internal issues and customer escalations, ensuring corrective actions improve product robustness.
  • Partner with Manufacturing, Operations, Test, Quality, and JDM/OEM partners to transition new hardware subsystems into production.
  • Build scalable engineering processes, automation, documentation standards, and “Quality by Design” practices.
  • Hire and retain top hardware engineering talent while creating a culture of accountability, collaboration, technical excellence, and continuous improvement.
  • Communicate program status, risks, tradeoffs, and resource needs clearly to engineering leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • A Degree in Electrical or Computer Engineering, with either:
  • Masters with 5+ years of professional work experience.
  • Bachelors with 7+ years of professional work experience
  • 3+ years as a Hardware Engineering Manager
  • Demonstrated experience managing, mentoring, or leading hardware engineering teams.
  • Strong background in hardware design, x86 architectures, networking, high-speed interfaces, and system-level integration.
  • Experience architecting or supporting complex systems involving compute, storage, networking, and I/O.
  • Strong understanding of signal integrity, power integrity, thermal performance, and electrical validation.
  • Experience qualifying CPUs, DDR memory, NICs, SSDs, power supplies, voltage-regulator circuits, and other system components.
  • Experience defining and automating hardware qualification processes.
  • Experience working with diagnostics engineers and providing technical direction on test coverage and validation strategy.
  • Strong written and verbal communication, organizational judgment, analytical skills, and the ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Experience in hyperscale, enterprise hardware, storage, server, or networking environments preferred.

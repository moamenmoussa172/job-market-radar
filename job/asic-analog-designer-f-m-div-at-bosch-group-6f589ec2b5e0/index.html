ASIC Analog Designer (f/m/div.)

Bosch Group · München, Germany

Hybrid · ASIC / FPGA Design Engineer · Posted 5 November 2026

About the job

At our Munich ASIC Development Center, we create the technology behind the next generation of smart consumer sensors. Join our team and turn innovative ASIC concepts into high-volume products:

  • As an ASIC Analog Designer, you work in leading-edge development projects for MEMS consumer sensor ASICs, contributing to top-level system architecture and block-level design of analog circuits for next-generation sensors
  • With your experience in analog integrated circuit design, you implement challenging blocks and mixed-signal sensor systems ready for high-volume production
  • Your technical expertise enables you to translate complex design requirements into robust architectures, block specifications and circuit solutions, while collaborating closely on layout and production test development
  • Rounding off your responsibilities, you take ownership of product validation and assess characterization and qualification results to ensure robust product performance
  • Personality: High level of enthusiasm and creativity; willingness to work in international and interdisciplinary teams
  • Working Style: Team player with a quality-oriented and independent working style
  • Experience and Knowledge: Minimum five years of professional experience in Analog IC design; preferably experience in system level architecture of sensor front-end in CMOS technology; deep knowledge of state-of-the-art EDA Software tools for IC design and system level modeling
  • Languages: Fluent English, German beneficial
  • Education: Master’s degree or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics or comparable qualifications

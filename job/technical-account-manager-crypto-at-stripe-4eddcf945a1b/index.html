About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Technical Account Managers are responsible for the technical relationship of Stripe Crypto's largest and most complex users. We lead cross-functional efforts across Engineering, Product, and go-to-market teams—balancing our users' business needs with technical constraints—and act as trusted partners and consultants who help businesses deliver more value to their customers, optimize their technical operations, and accelerate their global growth with Stripe.

What you'll do

As a Technical Account Manager for Crypto, you will own the technical relationship for Stripe's most strategic crypto-native users: high-growth companies building on Stripe's crypto and stablecoin products, including Bridge and Privy. You will be the technical expert your accounts trust and rely on—developing deep relationships with their key stakeholders, mobilizing cross-functional teams across Stripe to resolve their most complex technical challenges, and proactively identifying opportunities that help them succeed and scale.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary technical point of contact and trusted advisor for a set of strategic crypto accounts; build durable relationships with their Engineering, Payments Operations, and Product stakeholders.
  • Lead complex technical troubleshooting and incident response for assigned accounts; own communications and remediation until resolution, collaborating with Product, Support, and Engineering when needed.
  • Partner closely with GTM and other user-facing teams to deliver a coordinated experience across your accounts, pairing technical ownership with commercial strategy.
  • Translate customer and developer needs into actionable product feedback: aggregate patterns across accounts, size the business impact, surface it to Product and Engineering, and follow through to completion.
  • Develop and maintain account-specific operational runbooks, escalation paths, and success metrics (uptime, latency, transaction throughput, settlement timelines).
  • Proactively review your accounts' implementations to identify improvements and surface potential issues—across areas like performance, reliability, and compliance workflows—before they impact the user's experience, and flag growth opportunities to partner teams.
  • Deliver regular business and technical reviews with customers (quarterly reviews, post-mortems, roadmap previews) and drive action items to closure.
  • Create and maintain user-facing technical documentation, sample code, and troubleshooting guides that reduce friction and enable self-service.
  • Lead cross-functional projects and process improvements that reduce operational friction, improve SLAs, and scale TSC coverage and effectiveness.

Who you are

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 5+ years in enterprise-level, client-facing technical roles such as Technical Account Management, Technical Solutions Consulting, or Solutions Engineering.
  • Strong experience with RESTful APIs and webhooks, and the ability to explain API concepts, integration patterns, and troubleshooting steps to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Working familiarity with stablecoins and blockchain fundamentals, including chains, wallets, on-chain transaction lifecycle, and on/off-ramps, with genuine enthusiasm for going deeper.
  • Strong technical troubleshooting skills and demonstrated experience partnering with engineering teams to resolve production incidents.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication and stakeholder management. You can hold both business-level and deeply technical conversations, and influence cross-functional teams.
  • Experience owning complex projects across multiple teams, including timelines, dependencies, and deliverables.
  • Comfort operating in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment and prioritizing between reactive and proactive account work.

Preferred qualifications

  • Hands-on experience implementing crypto, stablecoin, or blockchain systems, including blockchain APIs and integrating digital assets with traditional financial systems.
  • Background in payments, fintech, or cross-border money movement, including how traditional rails and stablecoin rails interoperate.
  • Comfort with observability tooling for diagnosing production issues, such as Datadog or a comparable platform for log search, dashboards, monitors, and distributed traces.
  • Experience with tools like Postman and cURL, light scripting in Python or Node.js, basic SQL, and AI coding agents for debugging and root-cause investigation.
  • An operations-savvy mindset, with a track record of removing process friction and replacing one-off workarounds with scalable processes.
  • Strong product sense and comfort translating customer business objectives into technical implementations and product feedback.

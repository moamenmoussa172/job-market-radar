About this Role

We are seeking an IT Service Desk & Operations Specialist to support IT operations through voice and email handling, general IT troubleshooting, ticket management and escalation management. The role requires an understanding of data centre technologies and ITIL processes, effective communication skills, and flexibility to work in a 24x7 shift environment.

What you’ll do

  • Handle IT support interactions through voice calls and email.
  • Perform general IT troubleshooting.
  • Apply a high-level understanding of data centre technologies when supporting operational requirements.
  • Follow ITIL processes in day-to-day IT operations.
  • Use Remedy or similar ticketing tools to support incident and request management.
  • Handle and manage escalations effectively. Work flexibly across 24x7 shifts.

Who you are

  • You have knowledge of ITIL processes.
  • You have experience or knowledge of Remedy or another ticketing tool.
  • You communicate clearly and effectively.
  • You can manage operational escalations in a structured manner.
  • You have general IT troubleshooting capabilities, with A+ certification preferred.
  • You have a high-level understanding of data centre technologies.
  • You can work flexibly in a 24x7 shift environment.
  • Experience in data centre delivery, together with clear, solution-oriented thinking and effective communication, is relevant to success in the role.

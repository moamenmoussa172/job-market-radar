Unity Catalog Disaster Recovery (UC DR)

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

The Unity Catalog Disaster Recovery team helps customers keep their Databricks deployments resilient. When a cloud region goes down, our disaster-recovery manager coordinates replication of Unity Catalog metadata and data to a paired region so customers can recover quickly. Based in Berlin, the team works closely with Unity Catalog and Delta teams to ensure platform reliability at scale.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Design and build distributed systems that enable disaster recovery and high availability for Unity Catalog metadata and data across cloud regions.
  • Own complex technical projects end-to-end, from design through delivery, testing, and production rollout.
  • Drive technical decisions and contribute to architectural direction for disaster recovery capabilities.
  • Collaborate with Unity Catalog and Delta teams to ensure seamless integration and platform reliability.
  • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and reliability issues in production systems.

What We Look For

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience.
  • Experience building and operating large-scale distributed systems in production environments.
  • Experience with one or more programming languages including Java, Scala, C++, or Go.
  • Understanding of distributed systems fundamentals, including replication, consistency, and fault tolerance.
  • Experience working on a SaaS platform or with service-oriented architectures.
  • Experience with cloud technologies including AWS, Azure, or GCP.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively across teams.
  • B.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

AI Governance

The AI Governance team extends Unity Catalog's governance model to AI assets including models, agents, skills, MCP servers, and the tools or functions they use. You will build platform capabilities that enable organizations to manage AI assets with familiar governance controls, route AI requests through a central control plane with access policies and usage limits, and provide visibility into AI activity for monitoring and auditing.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Design and build scalable distributed systems that govern AI assets, runtime requests, and usage across the Databricks platform.
  • Own complex technical projects end-to-end, from design and implementation through testing, deployment, and production operations.
  • Build reliable services and APIs that handle AI governance workflows at scale across multiple cloud providers.
  • Make technical and architectural decisions that balance product requirements, system performance, and long-term maintainability.
  • Collaborate with product management, security, and platform teams to define and deliver governance capabilities for AI workloads.
  • Mentor engineers on the team and contribute to raising engineering standards through code reviews and technical guidance.

What We Look For

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience.
  • Experience building and operating large-scale distributed systems in production environments.
  • Experience with one or more programming languages including Java, Scala, Python, or Go.
  • Understanding of distributed systems fundamentals including concurrency, microservices architecture, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Ability to take ownership of technical domains, diagnose performance issues, and drive projects from design through delivery.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively across engineering, product, and cross-functional teams.

About the job

The Principal Solution Engineer – Cloud & DevOps is responsible for leading the design and delivery of cloud and DevOps engagements across Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). The role takes ownership of customer projects from requirements through to production hand-over, designing secure, scalable cloud-native solutions and building the automation, CI/CD and platform engineering practices that keep them reliable.

Responsibilities:

Project & Solution Leadership

  • Lead cloud and DevOps projects end to end, from discovery and scoping through design, implementation, testing and hand-over.
  • Own the technical plan, milestones, risks and quality of delivery.

Solution Design & Customer Engagement

  • Work with internal and external stakeholders to gather requirements, define technical scope and translate business needs into scalable, secure and cost-efficient solutions.
  • Produce architecture diagrams, design documents and trade-off analyses, and present recommended solutions for review and approval before implementation.

Multi-Cloud Infrastructure

  • Design, deploy and operate infrastructure on Azure, AWS and GCP, covering compute, networking, storage, databases, identity and security, for both greenfield builds and migrations.

DevOps & CI/CD

  • Design and implement CI/CD pipelines and release strategies using tools such as Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI and Jenkins, with automated build, test, security scanning and deployment.

Infrastructure as Code & Automation

  • Build reusable, version-controlled infrastructure using Terraform (primary), along with Bicep/ARM, CloudFormation, Pulumi or Ansible.
  • Automate operational tasks with Python, Bash or PowerShell.

Containers & Kubernetes

  • Architect, deploy and manage containerised workloads on Kubernetes, including AKS, EKS, GKE, Rancher and OpenShift.
  • Create Helm charts and manifests, and handle scaling, upgrades and cluster health.

Observability & Reliability

  • Define monitoring, logging, alerting and SLO practices using cloud-native tools (Azure Monitor, CloudWatch, Cloud Operations) and open-source stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, ELK).
  • Lead root cause analysis and drive improvements in uptime and performance.

Security, Governance & Compliance

  • Apply cloud security best practices, including IAM, network segmentation, secrets management, policy-as-code, DevSecOps and landing-zone governance.
  • Align to customer standards and frameworks such as ISO 27001, SOC 2 and CIS.

Cost & Performance Optimisation

  • Apply FinOps practices and right-sizing to keep cloud environments efficient, resilient and cost-effective.

Documentation & Knowledge Sharing

  • Produce clear technical documentation, runbooks and customer presentations.
  • Set engineering standards and best practices for the wider team.

Our Culture & Code of Conduct:

At ZainTECH, we take pride in a culture built on collaboration, innovation, and uncompromising integrity. We are looking for individuals who share these values and are committed to customer-centricity and ethical excellence. All employees are expected to uphold our Code of Conduct, which serves as a guiding framework for responsible behavior across everything we do — from how we work with each other to how we engage with clients and partners globally.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in cloud infrastructure, DevOps or platform/site reliability engineering, with at least 2 years in a lead or senior delivery role.
  • Hands-on, production-level experience in at least two of Azure, AWS and GCP.
  • Strong DevOps expertise: CI/CD design, Git-based workflows, release management and build/deployment automation.
  • Advanced Infrastructure as Code skills, with Terraform as a must-have.
  • Strong experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production, including managed services (AKS/EKS/GKE) and Helm.
  • Solid understanding of cloud networking and security: VPC/VNet design, load balancers, firewalls, WAF, VPN/private connectivity, IAM and identity federation.
  • Experience with Linux and Windows administration.
  • Experience with observability tooling such as Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, ELK, or cloud-native monitoring.
  • Scripting and automation skills in Python, Bash or PowerShell.
  • Proven ability to run technical projects, estimate effort, manage risks and deliver to agreed timelines.
  • Strong solution design and technical documentation skills, with the ability to clearly justify architectural decisions.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder management and problem-solving skills, including presenting to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Experience with Rancher or OpenShift, including air-gapped or private cloud deployments, is an advantage.
  • GitOps tooling such as Argo CD or Flux, and service mesh experience, is an advantage.
  • Experience with cloud migration and application modernisation programmes is an advantage.
  • Experience in a consulting, professional services or managed services environment is an advantage.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate
  • Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) or CKAD
  • Preferred Certifications: Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305) or Azure DevOps Engineer Expert (AZ-400), AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate/Professional or AWS Certified DevOps Engineer – Professional, Google Professional Cloud Architect or Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer

The Role

As an SAP Change Management Senior Manager, you will provide strategic leadership for the people and change agenda across complex, large-scale SAP transformation programs.

You will partner with senior client leaders and transformation teams to shape the Organizational Change Management strategy and ensure organizations are ready to adopt and sustain SAP-enabled business transformation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Shape and lead the end-to-end OCM strategy for large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformations.
  • Advise senior client stakeholders on Change Strategy, Business Readiness, Organizational Readiness, and User Adoption.
  • Lead executive and senior stakeholder engagement and establish strong leadership sponsorship for transformation.
  • Oversee enterprise-wide Change Impact Assessments across functions, processes, geographies, and stakeholder groups.
  • Define strategies for Stakeholder Engagement, Change Communications, Learning & Training, Change Networks, and Adoption.
  • Establish change governance and integrate OCM activities with the broader SAP implementation and transformation roadmap.
  • Facilitate executive workshops, leadership alignment sessions, and transformation discussions.
  • Build and mobilize Change Champion / Change Agent Networks.
  • Identify change risks, resistance, readiness gaps, and adoption barriers and develop appropriate interventions.
  • Lead multidisciplinary Change Management teams and coach Managers, Consultants, and junior team members.
  • Support proposals, business development, and client conversations related to SAP-enabled transformation and OCM.

Skills & Experience

  • Experience: 10+ years of relevant professional experience, including significant experience leading complex Change Management / OCM programs, ideally within SAP S/4HANA or other enterprise-wide transformations.
  • Extensive experience leading Organizational Change Management (OCM) within complex transformation programs.
  • Strong experience in SAP S/4HANA, SAP Transformation, SAP Implementation, or ERP Transformation.
  • Strong expertise in Change Strategy, Change Impact Analysis, Stakeholder Management, Communications, Training, Organizational Readiness, and Adoption.
  • Knowledge of established methodologies such as Prosci, ADKAR, or equivalent Change Management frameworks.
  • Strong executive stakeholder management and leadership influencing capability.
  • Excellent facilitation, communication, presentation, and storytelling skills.
  • Experience leading complex, cross-functional or multi-market transformations is highly desirable.

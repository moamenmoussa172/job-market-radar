About the job

Join Amazon Web Services (AWS) and launch your career in cloud computing through our Early Career Talent program. You'll gain hands-on expertise designing cloud solutions, learning from experienced architects, and working on real AWS projects that impact millions of customers worldwide.
Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world's leading cloud computing platform, helping millions of customers, from startups to global enterprises, build and run applications without managing physical servers. We're seeking motivated individuals for our Associate Solutions Architect role within Early Career Talent, a structured learning experience designed to develop the next generation of cloud solutions architects.
This program serves as a pathway to building foundational knowledge in cloud computing, AWS services, and solutions architecture principles in a supportive, education-first environment. You'll learn to understand customer requirements, build working cloud solutions, and communicate technical recommendations effectively while being mentored by experienced Solutions Architects.
Key job responsibilities
- Complete a structured training curriculum to build cloud architecture foundations and develop expertise in AWS services
- Analyze customer requirements and learn to translate them into technical solution recommendations
- Participate in solution design sessions and observe customer engagements with experienced Solutions Architects
- Work on real AWS projects, applying architecture principles and building hands-on experience with AWS services
- Develop communication skills by learning to present technical concepts effectively to diverse audiences
A day in the life
As an Associate Solutions Architect, you'll engage in a mix of structured learning and practical application. Your day might include attending training sessions on AWS services and the Well-Architected Framework, then applying those concepts to a real project you're building on AWS. You'll participate in solution design discussions, observing how experienced architects translate customer needs into technical recommendations. You might shadow customer engagements to see how solutions are presented to diverse audiences, then return to work on your own project, experimenting with AWS services and architecture principles. Throughout the program, you'll develop presentation skills by communicating technical concepts to colleagues and mentors, receiving feedback that helps you grow as a solutions architect.
About the team
We are the AWS Early Career Talent team, dedicated to nurturing emerging talent in cloud computing. Our team collaborates across the organization to create meaningful learning experiences that bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world cloud architecture. We work with experienced Solutions Architects who are committed to mentoring the next generation of cloud professionals. Together, we're building a community of architects who understand how to solve complex business problems through innovative cloud solutions. When you join us, you'll be part of a team that values your growth, supports your learning journey, and helps you develop the skills needed to succeed in cloud consulting and solutions architecture.

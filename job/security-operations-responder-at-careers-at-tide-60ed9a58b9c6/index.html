ABOUT THE ROLE

The Tide Security Engineering team is made up of three core areas: Product Security, Identity, Threat Detection & Response

Product Security consists of application and cloud security experts. Their mission is to protect the products we build, covering everything from secure design reviews to threat modelling and penetration testing, ensuring security is embedded from the ground up.

Threat Detection & Response (this role!) focuses on protecting the company by building a robust detection and automation platform. We’re proactive in our defence, constantly hacking ourselves to improve our security posture and staying ahead of emerging threats. Our goal is to make Tide resilient against the ever-evolving threat landscape.

Identity is responsible for managing Tide's staff identity platform, ensuring that access to systems and infrastructure is secure, seamless, and aligned with modern security practices. The team uses strategies like zero trust, multi-factor authentication, and granular role-based access controls to safeguard our internal operations.

While each area has its own focus, collaboration is key - it's why we share the same Slack channel and hold our standups together as one cohesive team, ensuring alignment and seamless communication across all security functions.

As a Security Operations Responder, you’ll help defend Tide day to day by investigating threats through our security data lake and SOAR platform. You’ll work alongside experienced responders and our Detection Engineering team, learning how to investigate effectively, when to escalate, and how to turn repetitive work into automation. Over time, you’ll help us reduce manual investigation and improve the true positive / false positive efficacy of what lands in the queue.

When the security queue is empty, you’ll be building – automations, enrichment and, with support, your own detections. This role is a great fit if you’re early in your security career, whether you’re a recent graduate with some hands-on experience or have spent a few years in a SOC or similar role. We care most about an open mind, a curious approach and a drive to learn; we’ll invest in growing the rest.

As a Security Operations Responder, you’ll be:

Investigating and responding to threats (your primary focus)

  • Triaging and investigating security alerts through our security data lake and SOAR platform, across AWS, GCP, SaaS (such as Google Workspace and Okta), internal applications and employee endpoints (Windows, macOS).
  • Working through investigations using our runbooks and investigation standards – gathering evidence, determining whether activity is malicious, and escalating to senior responders when something needs a second pair of eyes.
  • Supporting the response to security incidents, helping with evidence gathering, containment and remediation tasks alongside security, IT and engineering teams.
  • Documenting your investigations clearly so others can follow your reasoning, and suggesting improvements to runbooks as you learn.
  • Joining the security incident response on-call rota once you’re fully onboarded, with senior support behind you.

Scaling investigation through automation & Improving detection efficacy

  • Contributing to SOAR playbooks and security automations – from enriching alerts with useful context through to automated response actions, such as removing phishing emails from employee mailboxes. We want you to run with the “anything is possible” mantra.
  • Spotting repetitive steps in your investigations and proposing (and building) ways to automate them.
  • Flagging where missing logs or context in the data lake slow investigations down.
  • Feeding investigation outcomes back to the Detection Engineering team – flagging noisy alerts and false positives, and highlighting things we should have caught – to help improve TP/FP efficacy.

Building when the queue is clear

  • Writing and testing your own detections with guidance and review from Detection Engineering, learning our test-driven, detections-as-code practices.
  • Taking part in threat hunts, using threat intelligence and lessons from past investigations to look for activity our detections might miss.
  • Building small tools and scripts that make investigation faster and easier for the team.
  • Continuously developing your skills through hands-on work, mentoring from senior team members and structured learning.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • A degree in Cyber Security, Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Some hands-on security experience – from an internship, placement or graduate role through to a few years in a SOC, security operations or similar role.
  • A foundational understanding of common attack and defence techniques across Cloud (AWS, GCP), SaaS (such as Google Workspace and Okta) and desktop (Windows and macOS) environments, and familiarity with frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Exposure to log analysis and querying data in a SIEM or security data lake, such as Splunk, Scanner, Sentinel or SecOps.
  • Some scripting ability (e.g. Python) and a genuine interest in automating repetitive work.
  • An open mind and a curious approach – you enjoy pulling on threads and want to understand why something happened, not just what.
  • A methodical approach to problem-solving and the confidence to ask questions and escalate when unsure.
  • Eagerness to learn, take feedback and grow your skills quickly.
  • Clear spoken and written communication skills.

Nice to have

  • Hands-on exposure to SOAR platforms or automated workflows.
  • Experience writing or tuning detections.
  • A security certification such as CompTIA Security+, CompTIA CySA+ or Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1).
  • Evidence of learning outside work, such as CTFs, a home lab, or a repository showcasing scripts, automations or detections you’ve built.

About the job

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

The Assistance Engineering team builds world-class tools for Airbnb's global network of support agents, so guests and hosts get a great experience 24/7, 365 days a year. Airbnb's operational staff must scale with our user base, and we own the entire tech stack behind their success.
Within Assistance Engineering, Assistance Foundations builds the shared platforms that support runs on: case management, the agent workspace, quality and coaching systems, money tooling, data services, agent routing, provisioning and training, and reliability. We are forming a dedicated Machine Learning Engineering group so that intelligence is built once on this foundation and reused across support, rather than rebuilt by each team. You will be one of its founding engineers.

Some of what we are building and shipping:

  • LLM-based quality evaluation that scores support cases against the quality standard, at scale
  • AI coaching that turns quality evidence into coaching plans for team leads
  • Simulation environments that train agents on realistic support scenarios
  • Natural language search and chat tools that help agents and supervisors find answers faster

The Difference You Will Make:

As a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, you will design, build, and operate the LLM-based and agentic systems behind quality, coaching, case intelligence, and decision support for agents. The work spans prompt design, retrieval, fine-tuning, agent orchestration, dataset engineering, and evaluation. This is a product-building role: you will take systems from first experiment to production and own how they perform for real users.

A Typical Day:

  • Design, build, and ship LLM-based and agentic systems, from technical design through implementation, experimentation, and deployment.
  • Build offline and online evaluation (human-labeled sets, LLM-as-judge, A/B tests) so that quality is measured, not assumed.
  • Curate and engineer the datasets that prompts, fine-tuned models, and evaluations depend on.
  • Turn one-off models and prompts into shared components that other Foundations teams can adopt.
  • Collaborate with product, design, engineering, data science, and support operations and quality teams to find high-impact problems and quantify results.
  • Participate in design and code reviews, and mentor other engineers.

Your Expertise:

  • 5+ years of industry experience building and shipping applied machine learning, or a PhD in a related field plus 2+ years in industry
  • Recent, hands-on experience building LLM-based systems that shipped to production
  • Expertise in designing and iterating on prompts, fine-tuning, and LLM evaluation
  • Expertise in RAG patterns, memory, tool use, and agent planning
  • Experience integrating LLMs with APIs, vector databases, and orchestration frameworks
  • A solid foundation in machine learning: modeling, evaluation design, experimentation, and A/B testing
  • A track record of shipping AI products used by real users, not only research prototypes or offline models
  • Strong programming (Python, Java, or Kotlin) and the engineering skills to productionize your own work: model serving, monitoring, data pipelines, and safe deployment
  • Excellent collaboration and communication skills to work effectively across teams and domains
  • Bachelor's and/or Master's/PhD degree, preferably in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

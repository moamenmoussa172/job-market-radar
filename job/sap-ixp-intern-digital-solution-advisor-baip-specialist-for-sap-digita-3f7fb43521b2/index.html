Digital Solution Advisor – iXp BAIP Specialist for SAP Digital Hub

SAP Digital Hub is one of the most innovative teams in the company, built to deliver exceptional customer experiences in a scalable, speedy, and personalized way.You would be joining one of the most exciting teams in Latin America, working at the intersection of data, artificial intelligence, and business transformation. Here, you will be empowered to build, experiment, and create — contributing directly to how SAP engages with its customers across the region.

This Young Professional role is designed for students who are passionate about data, AI, and emerging technologies. You do not need prior SAP knowledge — we will provide the product context. What matters most is your curiosity, technical foundation, and ability to bring ideas to life.You will work alongside experienced Solution Advisors on the BAIP portfolio — covering Business AI, Analytics, Data Management, and Integration — building internal tools and assets that enhance how we demonstrate SAP solutions, run live demos, and prepare for customer engagements.

Key Responsibilities and Tasks

Area 1: Demo Engineering & ToolingYou will play a hands-on role in building and maintaining the technical assets that make our customer demonstrations more compelling and efficient. This includes developing scripts, automation routines, and data pipelines that feed demo environments with realistic, high-quality data. You will also help integrate AI-powered capabilities into demo scenarios, making it easier for Solution Advisors to showcase the full potential of SAP's BAIP portfolio in a live setting.

Area 2: AI & Data ProjectsYou will research and prototype internal applications using AI and data technologies — from building generative AI assistants that support presentation preparation, to creating analytical models that help the team track performance and customer engagement patterns. These projects will have direct impact on how the Digital Hub team operates day-to-day.

Area 3: Content & Presentation SupportYou will contribute to the creation of technical collateral for customer-facing events, proof-of-concept documentation, and executive briefing materials. Working with the team, you will help translate complex technical scenarios into clear, visual narratives that resonate with business decision-makers.

What We're Looking For

  • Currently enrolled in a degree program in Data Engineering, Computer Science, Information Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, or a related field
  • Hands-on experience or strong academic exposure to Python, SQL, or similar languages
  • Familiarity with AI/ML concepts — even at a foundational level (coursework, personal projects, or certifications count)
  • Experience with data visualization tools or BI platforms is a plus
  • Comfortable working in English (reading technical documentation; writing is a plus)
  • Curious, proactive, and able to work independently on open-ended problems

What You'll Find Here

  • Direct exposure to enterprise AI and data solutions used by leading companies across Latin America
  • Mentorship from experienced Solution Advisors and technical architects
  • A team culture that values experimentation, learning, and customer impact
  • Flexibility to run your own projects within a structured, high-performance environment

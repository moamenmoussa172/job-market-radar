Technical Support Engineer (Assistant Manager)

Bosch Group · Chennai, India

Onsite · Solutions / Support Engineer · Posted 12 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

1. As responsible engineering team member for additional launch release (ALR)/relocation/promotion/SDA (Sales Driven Activity) etc. projects mainly include:
- Clarify and define the product requirements with customers.
- Establish BOM and create new 2D/3D drawings.
- Work with QPV to define test plan, analyze test failures and improve design.
- Coordinate engineering activities like DFMEA and agency approval etc.
2. Support plant for daily maintenance of assigned product category.
3. Support plant for activities of cost reduction and quality improvement.
4. Design optimization for series products and components.
5. Conduct products updating to comply with the latest international and country standards.
6. Manage power tools product certification for new products and existing products in coordination with test houses, create and track product certification plan for new products.
7. Approves engineering change requests for product approval related components. Advises on impact of change for approval of affected products.
8. Supports local business teams with information and interpretation of approval requirements, directives, and standards.

• Bachelor's or master's degree in mechanical engineering or related majors.
• At least 3 years working experience in engineering related department.
• Familiar with Pro/E etc. design tool to create 2D/3D drawings, requirement engineering and FMEA etc. knowledge.
• Be familiar with the relevant certification process and standards on power tools or household appliances, such as IEC/EN/UL 62841, 60335 is preferred.
• Good knowledge in material and process, strength calculation & verification, failure analysis and problem solving.
• Having power tool or related industries experience is preferred.
• Self-motivated, strong ownership, open mind, fast learning and team player.
• Good communication skills in English (reading, writing, speaking).

About the job

About Bosta:

Launched in 2017, Bosta is an overnight delivery provider for E-commerce companies in Egypt. Leveraging technology to empower businesses is what we do best. Our goal is to disrupt the logistics industry by bringing technology and experience together to provide a one-stop shop for delivery solutions.

We rely on advanced technology to deliver orders to our customers in a fast way. Businesses can track and connect with couriers and follow up on their orders - all in one system.

Job Code: 2025FINA-080

Job Responsibilities:

  • Ownership of reporting &amp; planning activities for all areas of business, inclusive of monthly reporting package, budget and rolling forecasts
  • Build and maintain a long-term dynamic financial model to be used in Group’s annual budget and 5-years business plan
  • Coordinates with business units on annual budget and rolling forecasts, challenge assumptions and propose cost optimization initiatives
  • Identify trends, provide financial insights and recommend actions based on sound analysis.
  • Support Ad hoc financial and non-financial/competitive analysis and benchmarking reports.
  • Job Qualifications:
  • Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance. CMA / FMVA is a plus
  • 4-6 years of experience in financial planning and analysis or investment banking
  • Excellent modeling and analytical skills
  • Attention to Detail
  • Strong interpersonal skills, ability to work with diverse team and ability to communicate effectively and in writing
  • Thorough knowledge of applicable accounting/general ledger systems and procedures and financial chart of accounts
  • English language fluency

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. This isn't a place for complacency, it’s a place to be pushed past your perceived limits. If you're ready to build the future of finance alongside people who refuse to settle for "good enough," you belong here. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called “surges.” Learn more about working at Coinbase.

The CTO organization spans 700+ people across Platform, AI, Engineering, Enterprise Engineering and IT. The Chief of Staff to the CTO is a one-person force multiplier. You own the CTO's time, attention, decisions, and follow-through, and increasingly you build the AI agents that run their operating system.

What you'll do

  • Own the CTO's operating system: how their week, preparation, and decisions flow, with a running decision and commitment log so decisions get made once, stick, and each follow-up has a named owner.
  • Be where they cannot be: represent them in forums and cross-functional discussions across Platform, AI, Engineering, Core, and the executive team, bring back the signal, and unblock without escalation.
  • Carry their voice: write and edit town halls, AMAs, all-hands remarks, board materials, and Slack posts so their point of view lands crisply and on time.
  • Build and scale AI agents that absorb more of their operating system, freeing your hours for higher-leverage work.
  • Run the few things they own that have no organizational home, from framing to clean handoff, without adding process.

Required skills and experience

  • 6+ years in BizOps, strategy, consulting, or technical program leadership, including support of a senior technical executive.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication: you can write in an executive's voice and turn technical trade-offs into business terms.
  • Technical fluency: you follow architecture, infrastructure, and AI discussions with staff-plus engineers and ask the right questions. You do not need to be a practicing engineer.
  • Judgment and discretion with sensitive org, people, and strategy information, and a clear read on what to escalate, decide, or let go.
  • Influence without authority across senior engineering and cross-functional leaders, earned by being prepared and useful.
  • Personal operating rigor: you track commitments and close loops relentlessly, without building process.
  • Hands-on use of AI tools in your own work; experience in a platform, infrastructure, developer tooling, or AI-focused organization is a plus.
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

About the job

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact.

A Day in the Life

Responsibilities may include the following and other duties may be assigned.

  • Work with the business and therapy leaders, as well as cross functionally, to define, develop pricing and execute strategy across the product/service portfolio and customer segments.
  • Evaluate pricing trends and recommend actions to ensure pricing meets the Operating Unit strategic objectives. Conduct analysis and build consensus with key stakeholders to drive agreement on appropriate pricing levels.
  • Conduct diagnostics to determine key pricing levers and estimate pricing opportunities.
  • Set pricing aspirations/goals/targets, monitor compliance take corrective actions and assure system maintenance,
  • Work in close collaboration with the Western Europe, Japan, Australia/New Zealand country, and the global Operating Unit to develop, implement innovative business models and value-based healthcare programs.
  • Review key tenders and deals and ensure they are meeting the OU revenues and profit expectations. Provide recommendations to the country business unit, and senior management
  • Implement operating mechanisms across Western Europe, Japan/Australia to strengthen the focus on pricing as key strategic and profit lever.
  • Build consensus with the key stakeholders (Global, HQ, countries) and drive pricing mindset change in the organization.
  • Ensure compliance with internal procedures and government regulations.
  • Use sound judgment and strong financial skills to interact with OU leaders, regional Sales & Marketing Leaders and other stakeholders
  • Work in close collaboration with the EurAsia OLM Pricing strategy team to share best practices and support the execution of pricing initiatives cross Medtronic.

Required Knowledge and Experience: ·

  • At least 5 years of experience in pricing, namely pricing strategy, market optimization, product profitability, tender and reimbursements.
  • Significant experience is required in price, sales or downstream/operational marketing. The last one to two positions should have been in international pricing positions or in a major pricing consultancy firm.
  • Knowledge of tender and reimbursement systems in Europe
  • Excellent analytic and strategic skills - Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills, strong modelling skills (Excel)
  • Act with autonomy, performs work without appreciable direction.
  • Strong Leadership and influence skills, business acumen and financial understanding, as well as communication and negotiation skills
  • Experience in managing cross-functional, cross country and cross-business issues and generate impact through others within a matrix structure and an intercultural environment.
  • Fluent in English, any other European language is a benefit.
  • University degree or equivalent.

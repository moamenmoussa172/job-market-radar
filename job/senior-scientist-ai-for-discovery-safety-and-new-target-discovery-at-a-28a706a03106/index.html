About the Team

The AI for Discovery team builds and deploys AI capabilities — including agentic AI, predictive foundation models, and multimodal representation models — to accelerate target discovery, drug design, and safety assessment across AstraZeneca R&D. We are now establishing dedicated AI support for safety science, working alongside Clinical Pharmacology and Safety Sciences (CPSS) to build predictive models that help scientists assess and anticipate safety risks earlier in the drug discovery process. This is a new capability — you will help build it from the ground up.

The team partners closely with safety scientists, toxicologists, and translational teams, as well as AI engineering and AI research groups across the organisation.

What the Role Involves

This is a hands-on individual contributor role applying AI to safety science and new target discovery. You will apply your technical skills in AI and biology to build, evaluate, and deploy predictive models and agentic AI tools that support safety assessment and risk prediction across AstraZeneca’s portfolio.

Key responsibilities:

· Build, evaluate, and deploy predictive AI models that integrate multimodal data — including omics (transcriptomics, Cell Painting), chemical structure, and in vitro assay data — to predict safety outcomes.

· Develop and benchmark multimodal foundation models and representation learning approaches, combining diverse biological and chemical data sources into unified embeddings for downstream safety prediction.

· Build confidence and uncertainty quantification methods — calibration, conformal prediction, out-of-distribution detection — to help scientists judge when model outputs can reliably inform safety decisions.

· Contribute to the development of agentic AI tools and automated pipelines that support safety scientists in querying, integrating, and interpreting heterogeneous evidence.

· Write production-quality code, maintain reproducible experimental pipelines, and contribute to shared infrastructure alongside AI engineers.

· Collaborate effectively with safety scientists, toxicologists, and computational biologists — communicating technical findings to both expert and non-expert audiences.

· Contribute to AstraZeneca’s external visibility through publications, conference presentations, and scientific collaborations.

Essential Skills

· Degree in computational biology, bioinformatics, AI/ML, systems biology, cheminformatics, or a related quantitative discipline, with relevant research or industry experience. Candidates with additional post-doctoral experience are equally encouraged to apply.

· Experience with predictive AI modeling for biology or chemistry — foundation models, graph neural networks, multimodal representation learning, or related approaches.

· Understanding of drug discovery or safety science — including toxicology, mechanism of action, safety pharmacology, or translational science.

· Strong technical proficiency in Python (required); experience building production-quality code, pipelines, and tools for scientific applications.

· Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, or equivalent) for model development and evaluation.

· Experience working with large-scale biological or chemical datasets (e.g., transcriptomics, Cell Painting, high-content imaging, in vitro screening data).

· Effective communication of technical and scientific findings to diverse audiences.

· Enthusiasm and persistence in applying AI methods to complex biological and safety problems.

Desirable Skills

· PhD in a relevant discipline.

· Experience with uncertainty quantification, calibration, conformal methods, or out-of-distribution detection in predictive models.

· Experience with safety science, toxicology, or preclinical safety assessment.

· Experience with agentic AI, LLM-based reasoning systems, or multi-agent architectures applied to scientific problems.

· Experience with multimodal data integration (combining omics, imaging, chemical, and assay data).

· Experience training or fine-tuning foundation models on biological or chemical data at scale (GPU clusters, distributed training).

· Experience with cloud computing (AWS) and high-performance computing environments.

· Proficiency in R for statistical analysis or bioinformatics workflows.

· Experience working in pharma or biotech R&D.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Location(s): US (remote), Boston (hybrid), or New York (hybrid)

Bready* to make a change?

Toast is looking for a Software Engineer II to build guest-facing features across every surface of our consumer-facing digital dining product, Toast Local: the backend services, the web, and our iOS and Android apps. You’ll join a team of frontend, backend, and native mobile engineers working together to create the best digital restaurant experience for guests.

Who We Are

The Consumer team at Toast is all about creating exceptional consumer experiences for restaurants and their guests. We’re product-obsessed, focused on innovation, and committed to building scalable, reliable solutions that are used by millions of restaurant-goers every day. If you’re ready to make an impact on a massive scale, this is the place for you.

About this roll* (Responsibilities)

  • Build guest-facing features end to end, from the backend services to the web, iOS, and Android clients that use them
  • Pick up work wherever the team needs it most, and learn new parts of the stack as you go
  • Break features down into well-scoped, sequenced tasks and see them through to production
  • Monitor what you ship, fix what breaks, and improve the quality of the code you own
  • Work closely with product, design, and the engineers on other platforms to ship experiences guests love
  • Use AI coding tools every day to ramp up on unfamiliar parts of the stack and ship faster

Do you have the right ingredients*? (Requirements)

  • 2+ years of experience as a full-stack software engineer
  • Experience with at least one of: React (TypeScript/JavaScript), Java/Kotlin, or Swift
  • Willingness to work across the whole stack, including backend, web, iOS, and Android, not only the parts you know today
  • Experience building or consuming GraphQL or REST APIs
  • Ownership of your work from start to finish: build, test, ship, and watch how it performs
  • A passion for building high-quality consumer experiences and solving real problems for guests

Special sauce* (Nice to haves)

  • Experience working in a microservice architecture
  • Experience working with NoSQL databases (DynamoDB)

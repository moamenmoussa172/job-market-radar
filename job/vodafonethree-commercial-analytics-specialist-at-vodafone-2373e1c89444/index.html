What you’ll do
You’ll join Commercial Decision Support at a defining moment, as Vodafone and Three bring together their strengths to create the UK’s biggest mobile network provider. You’ll use data, commercial insight and curiosity to help shape smarter credit decisions, balancing customer needs, risk and sustainable growth. Your analysis will help us understand what’s driving bad debt and acceptance performance, and you’ll work with colleagues across the business to turn those insights into decisions that make a meaningful difference for our customers and our commercial performance.

  • You’ll explore bad debt drivers, acceptance outcomes and Credit Risk strategy performance, turning complex data into clear recommendations that will help improve vetting and decisioning.
  • You’ll build data-led commercial appraisals of Credit Risk strategies, considering profitability, customer impact, risk and long-term sustainability.
  • You’ll help optimise Conditional Acceptance criteria and migration pathways, supporting outcomes that will work well for both our customers and the business.
  • You’ll monitor Credit Risk initiatives and carry out post-implementation reviews (PIRs), helping us understand what’s working, what we’re learning and where we can improve.
  • You’ll keep a close eye on less-than-12-month Max Exposure performance, spotting trends, emerging risks and opportunities and helping colleagues understand what they mean.
  • You’ll bring the story behind the data to life, creating clear commercial narratives and insights that will support trading cycles, forums and decision reviews.
  • You’ll partner with Commercial and Trading teams, providing thoughtful, consistent analysis of bad debt drivers and their impact on profitability.
  • You’ll work collaboratively across Credit Risk, Trading and In-Life teams, bringing together different perspectives and helping create shared understanding around assumptions and decisioning.

Who you are

  • You’ll bring experience in commercial analysis, credit strategy, risk management or performance analytics, whether that’s in telecoms or another relevant, data-rich environment.
  • You’ll be comfortable working with large and complex datasets and will have experience with one or more tools such as SQL, Python, R, SAS or Databricks.
  • You’ll combine commercial curiosity with analytical thinking, considering financial impact, customer outcomes and risk when exploring different options.
  • You’ll bring knowledge or experience of vetting, acceptance, decisioning or credit policy frameworks, along with an openness to learn and develop as our organisation evolves.
  • You’ll enjoy getting beneath the numbers, asking thoughtful questions, recognising patterns and translating your findings into practical insights.
  • You’ll have experience reviewing the impact of initiatives, including through PIRs or similar approaches, and will use those learnings to help inform future decisions.
  • You’ll enjoy building positive, collaborative relationships with colleagues across areas such as Risk, Trading and Product, valuing different experiences and perspectives along the way.
  • You’ll be able to make complex information easier to understand, communicating clearly and bringing care, consistency and attention to detail to the work you’ll deliver.

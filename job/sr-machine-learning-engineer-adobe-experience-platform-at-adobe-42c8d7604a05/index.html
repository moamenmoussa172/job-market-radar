About the job


The Opportunity

Adobe Experience Platform powers real-time customer experiences for the world’s largest enterprises — unifying data, resolving identity at scale, orchestrating journeys, and activating audiences across every channel. Behind these capabilities are engineering teams that must understand both the platform’s architecture and the regulated, complex environments where customers operate.

We are seeking a Sr. Machine Learning Engineer to join the AI Quality Platform team, with a focus on scoring and evaluation of AI-powered experiences. The team develops methods and systems to measure the quality of large language models and AI agents, helping product teams understand performance, detect regressions, and prioritize improvements.
In this position, you will help build automated evaluators, train and calibrate scoring models, and develop reliable benchmarks. You’ll combine machine learning, statistical analysis, and human feedback to assess response quality and task success, working alongside senior engineers and data scientists to bring evaluation methods into production.

What You'll Do:

  • Develop scoring methods for dimensions such as correctness, relevance, completeness, groundedness, instruction following, and task success.
  • Build and improve automated evaluators using rubric-based scoring, LLM-as-a-judge methods, supervised models, and deterministic checks
  • Train and calibrate scoring models using human-labeled examples, pairwise preferences, and expert assessments.
  • Design evaluation datasets and benchmarks with representative coverage, challenging cases, and clearly defined success criteria.
  • Develop annotation guidelines and scoring rubrics; analyze disagreements to improve label consistency and evaluator reliability.
  • Validate automated scores against human judgments, measuring agreement, ranking quality, bias, robustness, and uncertainty.
  • Design reproducible experiments to compare models, prompts, and agent configurations, using appropriate statistical tests and confidence intervals.
  • Evaluate agent behavior, including tool selection, tool-call correctness, intermediate steps, and end-to-end task completion.
  • Build scoring pipelines and regression tests that integrate evaluation into development and release workflows
  • Analyze evaluation failures and communicate actionable findings to product, engineering, and data science teams.

What You'll Need to Succeed:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Statistics, Data Science, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 3+ years of relevant experience developing ML solutions, with hands-on experience in model evaluation, experimentation, or applied data analysis.
  • Strong Python programming skills and experience with ML and data analysis libraries such as PyTorch, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, and SciPy.
  • Solid understanding of supervised learning, classification, ranking, model validation, and appropriate performance metrics.
  • Working knowledge of statistics and experimental design, including sampling, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and sources of bias.
  • Experience preparing datasets and designing evaluations that avoid data leakage and support reproducible comparisons.
  • Familiarity with LLMs, prompt design, and the challenges of evaluating open-ended generated outputs.
  • Ability to write maintainable, tested code and explain evaluation methods, findings, and limitations clearly.
  • Strong grasp of distributed systems fundamentals: consistency models, failure modes, backpressure, eventual consistency trade-offs
  • Hands-on experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS and/or Azure)
  • Experience operating streaming or NoSQL data infrastructure (Kafka, Cassandra, HBase, or equivalents)
  • A track record of delivering SaaS products with serious quality signals: observability, graceful degradation, security threat modelling, load testing
  • Clear, direct communication — e.g. you can write a design doc that gets to the point and push back constructively in a review

Nice to Have:

  • Experience developing LLM-as-a-judge evaluators, learned scoring models, reward models, or preference-based ranking systems.
  • Experience calibrating automated evaluations against human judgments, including inter-rater agreement and disagreement analysis.
  • Familiarity with evaluator failure modes such as position bias, verbosity bias, prompt sensitivity, and susceptibility to manipulation.
  • Experience evaluating retrieval-augmented generation, factual grounding, tool use, or multi-step AI agents.
  • Experience with annotation workflows, synthetic evaluation data, adversarial test cases, or benchmark contamination detection.
  • Familiarity with pairwise comparison methods, score calibration, threshold selection, and slice-based performance analysis.
  • Experience building scalable evaluation pipelines using SQL, Databricks, Spark, or similar tools, and tracking experiments with MLflow or equivalent platforms.

System Requirement Engineer_BCSC

About the job

Job Summary

Responsible for customer requirement management, system requirement analysis, and requirement traceability in automotive projects. The role works closely with customers and cross-functional teams to clarify function definition, support solution discussion, and ensure high-quality requirement specifications. Good code reading capability, structured problem-solving skills, and solid understanding of engine and vehicle-related functions are expected. Experience in hybrid functions and basic OBD regulation knowledge are preferred.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze customer and system requirements, and support system solution discussion.
  • Manage customer requirements, perform requirement breakdown, and maintain requirements in relevant tools.
  • Define and review system requirements with HW/SW/Test/Architecture teams to ensure feasibility and consistency.
  • Manage customer change requests and lead impact analysis.
  • Clarify function definition with customers and provide appropriate technical suggestions.
  • Support PM/PCM/Architect/Tester and other stakeholders in requirement-related activities.
  • Ensure requirement traceability across different levels and related process areas.
  • Support requirement clarification and issue analysis through code reading and functional understanding.
  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering or a related field.
  • Knowledge of system engineering, software development, testing, and basic project management.
  • Familiarity with requirement management and traceability tools such as DOORS or JIRA.
  • Automotive industry experience is preferred.
  • Good code reading capability.
  • Familiarity with engine and vehicle functions.
  • Hybrid function analysis/development experience is preferred.
  • Basic knowledge of OBD-related regulations is a plus.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and structured thinking skills.

Personal Competencies

  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Customer-oriented mindset.
  • Strong willingness to learn.
  • Open-minded and proactive team player.

About this job

At Kantar Consulting, we help some of the world’s leading brands unlock new opportunities for growth. As an Analyst within Consulting UK, you will support clients in making confident decisions by connecting evidence, commercial context and strategic thinking.

You’ll play an important role in delivering high-quality consulting projects across brand, portfolio and cultural strategy, futures and foresight, innovation strategy, growth strategy, shopper strategy and organisational performance. Working with guidance from colleagues, you’ll gain experience in helping turn insights into action for our clients.

As an Analyst, you’ll take ownership of your work, manage your time proactively across tasks and develop your consulting toolkit across a range of projects, sectors and consulting offers.

What you’ll be doing

  • Supporting the delivery of consulting projects across a range of industries and client sectors.
  • Analysing market, consumer and business information to identify insights and implications.
  • Contributing your ideas and perspectives as part of a team creating clear, engaging and actionable deliverables to guide our client’s strategy
  • Taking ownership of tasks, managing your time proactively across them and delivering high-quality work to agreed timelines.
  • Building collaborative relationships across Consulting and the wider Kantar business.

The ideal skills and experience

  • Curiosity about brands, consumers, culture, and market dynamics
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to turn information into insight
  • Excellent written, verbal and visual communication skills.
  • A desire to learn quickly, build your consulting expertise and take increasing ownership over time.
  • Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities.
  • A collaborative mindset and willingness to learn from colleagues across different specialisms.
  • Confidence working with data and familiarity with Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel.

What’s in it for you

  • The opportunity to work on strategic growth challenges for some of the world's most recognised brands.
  • A supportive consulting environment focused on learning, development and career progression.
  • Opportunities to work across a wide range of sectors, clients and consulting offers.
  • A collaborative culture where diverse perspectives and continuous improvement are valued.
  • Exposure to Kantar's industry-leading data, tools and intellectual property.
  • Access to Kantar's broader benefits and wellbeing programmes.

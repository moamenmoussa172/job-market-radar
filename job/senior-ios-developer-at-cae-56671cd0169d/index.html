About the job

⠀

We are looking for an iOS Developer to join our Crew Access team, based in our Kraków office. You’ll contribute to modern mobile solutions used by airline cabin and cockpit crews around the world.

Crew Access is a platform-agnostic solution that helps airlines manage crew members throughout their operations. Our Kraków team consists of 14 passionate professionals focused on innovation, technology modernization, automation, and delivering rich content to airlines worldwide.

As part of the Crew Access Mobile team, you will:

  • Develop and enhance a native iOS application.
  • Deliver new features and advanced technological enhancements, including smart and dynamic user interfaces.
  • Analyze and investigate applications and systems.
  • Troubleshoot issues and resolve bugs.
  • Collaborate within an agile, cross-functional team.
  • Continuously learn and apply engineering best practices and new technologies.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Minimum 5 years of mobile application development experience.
  • Strong knowledge of Swift and iOS development.
  • Experience working with REST APIs.
  • Familiarity with agile development methodologies.
  • Good command of English.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Willingness and ability to learn and work with new technologies and programming languages as needed.

About the job

We are looking for a Full-Stack & Automation Engineer to join our team and contribute to the development of business applications, system integrations and automation solutions across the Bosch environment.

In this role, you will act as a bridge between enterprise systems, cloud platforms, APIs, automation technologies and business processes, delivering pragmatic and scalable solutions that simplify operations and improve efficiency.

You will combine Low-Code development, APIs, SAP integration and Pro-Code capabilities, using technologies such as OutSystems, SAP CPI / SAP Integration Suite and modern programming languages.

We are looking for an all-rounder with a strong problem-solving mindset, business understanding and the ability to quickly translate business needs into effective technical solutions. You will assess different technologies and approaches, balancing speed of delivery, maintainability, scalability and cost.

Your Responsibilities

Low-Code & Application Development

  • Design, develop and maintain business applications, primarily using OutSystems.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand requirements and rapidly prototype solutions.
  • Extend Low-Code applications with custom code whenever required.

SAP Integration & System Connectivity

  • Design, develop and maintain integration flows using SAP CPI / SAP Integration Suite.
  • Integrate SAP and non-SAP systems through REST, OData and SOAP APIs.
  • Work with JSON and XML data structures to ensure reliable data exchange across enterprise platforms.
  • Enable connectivity between SAP systems, business applications, automation and AI solutions.

Automation & AI-Ready Solutions

  • Identify opportunities to simplify, optimise and automate business processes.
  • Support and maintain existing automation solutions, including Automation Anywhere, where applicable.
  • Contribute to the evolution of automation towards modern, API-based and AI-enabled solutions.
  • Promote robust integration approaches, prioritising API-based connectivity over UI-based automation whenever appropriate.

Pro-Code & Solution Engineering

  • Develop APIs, services and custom components using technologies such as JavaScript/TypeScript, Groovy, Java or Python.
  • Apply Pro-Code development whenever Low-Code platforms or standard integration capabilities do not fully meet business requirements.
  • Evaluate and select the most appropriate technologies for each business challenge.
  • Develop reusable and maintainable solutions, following an iterative approach from prototyping and user validation to scalable implementation.

Education:

  • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Experience & Know-how:

  • Professional experience in software development, application integration or automation.
  • Experience or knowledge of SAP CPI / SAP Integration Suite.
  • Experience with Low-Code development platforms, ideally OutSystems.
  • Experience with REST and SOAP APIs, OData, JSON/XML and system integration concepts.
  • Programming knowledge with one or more of the following: JavaScript/TypeScript, Groovy, Java or Python.
  • Experience with automation technologies, ideally Automation Anywhere, is an advantage.
  • Familiarity with SAP and non-SAP enterprise environments.
  • Interest in AI-enabled solutions and modern integration architectures is a plus.

Working Style & Methods:

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a pragmatic and solution-oriented approach.
  • Ability to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Comfortable working across different technologies and adapting to changing priorities.
  • Ability to evaluate alternatives and select the most effective approach, balancing speed, quality and long-term maintainability.
  • Proactive mindset, with the ability to work independently and collaboratively with different stakeholders.

Personality:

  • Curious and eager to learn new technologies.
  • Flexible and adaptable, with an open mindset towards different tools and approaches.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ownership mentality and focus on delivering results.
  • Comfortable navigating complexity and turning ideas into practical solutions.

Job Description:

The opportunity

We are looking for an IT Business Analyst to support the implementation, adoption and development of SAP LeanIX across our Enterprise Architecture environment.

In this role, you will work with Enterprise Architects, Application Managers, Application Owners, Capability Owners and business stakeholders. Your focus will be on high-quality application portfolio data, effective governance and the continued adoption of LeanIX.

You will work with complex Enterprise Architecture processes and help translate business needs into practical improvements. The role combines business analysis, process design and a good understanding of Enterprise Architecture and technology.

How you'll make an impact

  • You will support the onboarding of applications, business capabilities and technology assets into SAP LeanIX.
  • You will work with Application Owners globally to collect, validate and improve application data.
  • You will monitor data completeness and quality and coordinate improvement activities.
  • You will configure LeanIX surveys, reports, dashboards and governance workflows.
  • You will support business capability mapping and application portfolio management activities.
  • You will gather business requirements and translate them into LeanIX capabilities and improvements.
  • You will support Enterprise Architecture standards, governance processes and application lifecycle management.
  • You will facilitate workshops and training sessions to support LeanIX adoption across different stakeholder groups.
  • You will support integrations between LeanIX and enterprise systems such as ServiceNow and configuration management databases.
  • You will monitor adoption and help measure the value delivered through LeanIX-supported processes.

Your background

  • You have at least 3 years of relevant experience in Enterprise Architecture, Application Portfolio Management, IT Consulting, Business Analysis or a related area.
  • You have practical experience with SAP LeanIX or another Enterprise Architecture platform.
  • You understand Enterprise Architecture Management and Application Portfolio Management processes.
  • You have experience working with application inventories, configuration management databases or technology governance processes.
  • You understand business capability mapping and application lifecycle management.
  • You have experience gathering requirements and translating them into processes, system capabilities or improvements.
  • You have experience facilitating workshops or training and can work effectively with different stakeholder groups.
  • You have good communication and stakeholder management skills and are comfortable working in a global environment.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

Location: Singapore

Duration: 5-6 months

Start Date: January 2027

SAP's Autonomous Enterprise is an AI-native operating model where governed AI agents orchestrate end-to-end business processes, with humans setting direction and AI executing the work. It provides the Business AI Platform to Build, Run, and Engage business agents. Over 200 specialized agents across finance, supply chain, HR, and more have been deployed. SAP’s Joule Studio, the AI-first development environment, empowers teams to build custom agents using no-code, low-code, or pro-code approaches, simply by describing a business outcome in plain language. SAP also offers comprehensive set of services on clouds, and Agentic developer experience for customers to build enterprise mobile solutions.

Through the internship, you will get to:

  • Learn the cutting-edge technologies in multiexpeience platform and Agentic development experience. For example, enterprise mobile services, cross-platform technology, cloud app development, MCP Servers for developers, Agentic development, Agent Evaluation, and SAP Joule Studio.
  • Work with the team to create innovative features or agents and implement defined functionality for the new Enterprise AI enabled mobile solutions – combined with Joule and Business AI across different platforms.
  • Work in AI way for your day-to-day work with AI tooling/skills/agents for coding, testing, trouble shooting and experiementing.
  • Develop features for powerful enterprise-grade native mobile app with SAP Fiori, the new user experience (UX) for SAP software that applies modern design principles, and SAP Joule AI capabilities.
  • Collaborate with development team members on best practices, code reviews, internal tools and process improvements
  • Be a contributing member of Empowered Product Team (EPT) and with practice of homonized product operation model.
  • Experiment new ideas and create prototypes for innovations
  • Learn homonized product operation model and process for product development and releases.

Skills Required:

  • Basic knowledge of cloud computing, AI tooling and Agents
  • Innovative Thinking, Team Work and Collaboration.
  • Basic skills in Project Management.
  • Higher programming language, e.g., JavaScript, Swift, Java, Python, SQL, iOS or Android Development.

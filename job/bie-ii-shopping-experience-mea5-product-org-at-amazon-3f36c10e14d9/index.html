About the job

We are looking for an experienced, self-driven Business Intelligence Engineer with strong analytical skills to identify meaningful data trends, uncover gaps in existing data, and build effective measurement frameworks that improve our shoppers’ experience.
The ideal candidate will combine data mining with statistical and machine learning techniques to support segmentation and prediction. They will be passionate about working with disparate datasets and bringing data together to answer complex business questions. Success in this role requires effective collaboration with internal stakeholders and cross-functional teams to solve problems, create operational efficiencies, and deliver results against high organizational standards. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to a dynamic, fast-paced environment.
Key job responsibilities
- Conduct end-to-end deep dives using exploratory data analysis and statistical methods to generate actionable insights.
- Apply a strong understanding of statistics and machine learning to identify patterns, segment customers, and develop predictive insights.
- Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to understand business needs and define data and metrics requirements.
- Develop interactive, flexible dashboards and reporting across products to drive adoption and increase visibility into key measures of business performance.
- Build data models and extract and transform data using ETL processes and SQL.
- 3+ years of analyzing and interpreting data with Redshift, Oracle, NoSQL etc. experience
- 2+ years of Tableau Desktop, Quicksight or other relevant data visualization software experience
- Bachelor's degree or above in business administration, finance, economics, computer science, data science, engineering, or other related field, or 2+ years of Amazon RME (BB/3P) Full Time Exempt experience
- Experience with data visualization using Tableau, Quicksight, or similar tools
- Experience with data modeling, warehousing and building ETL pipelines
- Experience in Statistical Analysis packages such as R, SAS and Matlab
- Experience using SQL to pull data from a database or data warehouse and scripting experience (Python) to process data for modeling
- Experience using SQL (Structured Query Language) to pull data from a database or data warehouse
- Experience using Python scripting to process data for modeling
- Master's degree or above in BI, finance, engineering, statistics, computer science, mathematics or equivalent quantitative field
- Knowledge of Microsoft Excel at an advanced level, including: pivot tables, macros, index/match, vlookup, VBA, data links, etc.
- Experience with AWS solutions such as EC2, DynamoDB, S3, and Redshift
- Experience in data mining, ETL, etc. and using databases in a business environment with large-scale, complex datasets
- Experience developing and presenting recommendations of new metrics allowing better understanding of the performance of the business

IT Support Technician, OTS

About the job

At Amazon, we strive to be Earth's most customer-centric company. We hire the world's brightest minds, and offer them an environment where they can relentlessly innovate for our customers. As a member of Ops Tech Solutions (OTS), you will have an opportunity to make a valuable contribution to that goal.
Amazon has an immediate opening for an IT Support Technician at our Fulfillment Center in Ensisheim (MLH1). The IT Support Technician is the face of IT for our customers. We are looking for individuals with a proven history of providing excellent customer service and IT support. We provide technical support in person and via remote channels for Windows, Macintosh and Linux systems, as well as MS-Office applications.
Key job responsibilities
• Windows/Mac/Linux OS imaging and support
• PC hardware troubleshooting and repair
• Supporting operational devices: RF scanners, handhelds/tablets, label printers (Zebra, HP), workstations and wireless access points
• Hardware procurement, receiving, RMA and asset management
• AV/VC support and room setups
• Resolving tickets by following standard operating procedures, under the guidance of more senior technical staff
• Creating and maintaining documentation
• Opportunity to support local infrastructure (network, phones, servers)
• Taking part in shift coverage and on-call rotation based on site operational needs
At Amazon it is always Day 1. We are looking for team members who are passionate about learning and applying new technology every day.
A day in the life
Your day starts with a quick check of the ticket queue and a handover from the previous shift. You'll be on the floor supporting Operations, Safety, HR and Learning teams: troubleshooting RF scanners, label printers and workstations, imaging laptops and fixing hardware so the site keeps running. Between tickets you'll manage spare parts, RMAs and asset records, set up meeting rooms, and help onboard new associates. You'll work closely with IT Support Engineers on network and infrastructure issues, follow SOPs, update documentation and help solve recurring problems before they slow down operations.
About the team
Ops Tech Solutions (OTS) keeps Amazon's operations running by delivering reliable, customer-focused IT support to our sites. At MLH1 in Ensisheim, you'll join a newly formed IT team of Support Engineers and Technicians who support every department in the building, from the operations floor to HR and Safety. As a new team, we're building our processes from the ground up, so your ideas will directly shape how we work. We value ownership, teamwork and continuous learning, and we invest in training and career growth within OTS.
- Experience supporting Windows, Macintosh or Linux operating systems
- Proficiency supporting office productivity applications (Outlook, Word, Excel)
- Experience using a ticket system for customer service requests
- Experience with PC imaging
- Experience with hardware troubleshooting
- Understanding of basic networking concepts (TCP/IP, switches and routers, WiFi)
- Ability to lift 11 kg with reasonable accommodation
- Speak, write, and read fluently in French
- Knowledge of network administration
- Working understanding of Microsoft Active Directory and Exchange
- Understanding of the basic concepts of Computer and Information Systems security
- Experience providing IT support in a medium to large company, ideally in a logistics or operations environment
- A demonstrated willingness to learn and apply new technology
- Certifications in IT (e.g. CompTIA A+/Network+, CCNA) are a plus

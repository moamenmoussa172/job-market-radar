About the job

Help keep Ubisoft's online world fair, secure and fun to play.

As a Game Security Programmer within Ubisoft Anti-Cheat, you will build and evolve technologies that help protect our games and players against cheating and manipulation. You will work where software engineering meets game technology. Day to day, you will tackle complex technical problems spanning Windows internals, game clients and large-scale online environments.

What you will do:

  • Design, develop and improve anti-cheat and game-security technologies used to protect Ubisoft multiplayer games
  • Investigate technical attack vectors and cheating techniques, then turn that understanding into practical protections and detection capabilities
  • Work with game production teams, researchers and platform specialists to integrate and operate our solutions across Ubisoft games
  • Build high-performance, reliable software with a strong focus on stability, compatibility, and player experience
  • Contribute to the technical direction of our anti-cheat technology and share your expertise across the organization
  • Apply your knowledge of Windows internals and systems programming to solve hard, low-level problems
  • Debug and profile complex products involving multiple interacting components
  • Keep up with developments in anti-cheat, reverse engineering, and the wider security landscape

We are looking for experienced software engineers interested in tackling game security challenges.

What you bring:

Core requirements:

  • Strong programming skills in C++, with solid software-engineering fundamentals.
  • Experience solving complex technical problems in systems programming or security-critical, low-level software.
  • Good knowledge of Windows internals and low-level debugging.
  • Practical experience with reverse engineering and binary analysis.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English.
  • A collaborative mindset and a proactive approach to solving problems and delivering secure, reliable software

Bonus skills and experience:

  • Experience with anti-cheat, anti-tamper, endpoint security or anti-malware technologies is a strong plus
  • Windows driver development
  • C# development experience
  • Game or engine development, especially for multiplayer or online titles
  • Python for analysis, automation, or tooling
  • Docker and Kubernetes
  • SaaS monitoring, observability, or site reliability engineering

What to send our way:

  • Your CV, highlighting your education, experience and skills
  • A cover letter including your earliest starting date, expected salary and why you would like to join us

  • Relocation support: We offer financial support in form of a cash allowance and assistance with accommodation search and settle-in support via our relocation partner, for international candidates we offer visa assistance
  • Ubisoft Blue Byte Academy: We offer multiple opportunities for you to discover your full potential and develop yourself in various areas. Guest speakers from the industry, workshops, and access to our training and development platform as well as to our in-house library
  • Free games: Free Ubisoft day one release copies and free games on Ubisoft Connect PC
  • Monthly Mobility Budget: 80€ per month that can be used on bicycle lease, parking spot rental or public transportation ticket
  • Up to 350€ childcare support: We offer up to 350€ net per child for preschool child day care costs
  • Company Pension Scheme: We offer an attractive company pension scheme
  • Gym subsidy: We contribute 50% (up to a maximum amount of 25€) towards the monthly cost of a gym membership of your choice
  • 26 days paid vacation per year
  • Corporate Benefits web portal: Employee discount program for affiliated retailers, shops, and service providers

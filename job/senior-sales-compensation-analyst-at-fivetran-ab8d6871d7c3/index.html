About the Role

Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies.

We’re looking for a As the Senior Analyst on the Sales Compensation team, you’ll own the systems and automation that power how Fivetran calculates and pays its sellers. Your focus is CaptivateIQ (or similar compensation platforms Xactly, SPIFF, Varicent), system enhancements, and using automation and AI to remove manual work, improve accuracy, and scale the commission process.

This role reports to the Manager, Sales Compensation Systems and Analytics and partners closely with Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Systems/IT, Analytics, Revenue Accounting, and Payroll. This is a full-time role with opportunities to connect and build as a team

This is a full-time position based out of our Bangalore office. [If hybrid] Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration, including two days in the office each week to connect and build as a team.

Technologies You’ll Use

CaptivateIQ (or similar compensation platforms Xactly, SPIFF, Varicent), Tableau, or other BI tools

What You’ll Do

  • Platform Ownership: Configure and maintain our compensation platform (CaptivateIQ or similar), including plans, crediting rules, quotas, territories, and data loads.
  • Systems Enhancements: Own the enhancement backlog end to end: document business requirements and current-state/future-state assessments, then build, test, and release changes with Systems/IT, and lead continuous improvement of program efficiency and best practices.
  • Automation: Automate commission calculation, data validation, reconciliation, and statement delivery using platform workflows, SQL, and Python, replacing manual spreadsheet processes.
  • AI Enablement: Build and maintain AI-assisted tools and workflows (e.g., LLMs, assistants, agents) for plan documentation, seller inquiries, QA, and anomaly detection, and validate that outputs are accurate.
  • Data Integrations & Management: Maintain integrations between the compensation platform, Salesforce, the data warehouse (e.g., BigQuery), and HRIS/Payroll; reconcile and analyze large datasets to ensure accurate quota allocation and performance tracking.
  • Plan Design Support: Support the annual compensation planning cycle from design to deployment: plan builds, quota loads, and modeling of plan changes with Sales Strategy and Finance.
  • Commission & Quota Operations: Run the monthly and quarterly cycle end to end, including quota distribution and adjustments, revenue crediting, and payout execution, with audit-ready documentation and SOX-compliant controls.
  • Stakeholder Support: Serve as the point of contact for sales managers and sellers, resolve complex inquiries and escalations, enforce incentive policy with diplomacy, and deliver training on plans and changes.
  • Reporting & Analytics: Build automated dashboards and seller statements that give sellers and managers clear visibility into attainment, earnings, and program performance, and stay current on compensation best practices.

Skills We’re Looking For

  • 7 years of experience in sales compensation, sales operations, or revenue operations, with hands-on administration of CaptivateIQ, Xactly, SPIFF, Varicent, or a similar platform
  • Strong system configuration skills: plan builds, crediting logic, data models, formulas, and integrations
  • Automation mindset: has replaced manual processes with repeatable, documented, auditable workflows
  • Working proficiency in SQL and Python or similar scripting; experience with Salesforce and data warehouses (e.g., BigQuery, Snowflake)
  • Expert-level Excel/Sheets modeling, meticulous attention to detail with large datasets, and strong command of commission logic and plan mechanics
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills for complex compensation scenarios, plus experience writing business requirements documents and current-state/future-state assessments, and testing and releasing system changes
  • Clear communicator who can explain plans and technical changes to non-technical stakeholders, with the backbone to enforce policy and the diplomacy to do it well

Bonus Skills​

  • Experience with usage-based or consumption pricing models
  • Built with AI: has personally used AI tools (LLMs, assistants, or agent and automation platforms) to build compensation tools or workflows others rely on, and can explain how they work and how outputs were checked
  • Experience with Looker, Tableau, or other BI tools
  • Experience at a high-growth tech company supporting a sales organization
  • Experience with SOX controls over commission processes

About the job

P2P Strategy & Design Analyst

Location: Warsaw

Hybrid model: 3 days per week from office in Warsaw

The P2P Strategy & Design Analyst supports the transformation of Purchase to Pay by helping make buying and paying simpler, more effective and increasingly automated. Combining strong S2P process knowledge with technology expertise and an innovative mindset, the role identifies opportunities to simplify, standardize and improve P2P processes through AI, automation and continuous improvement.

The role works across P2P, GFS, GBS, Procurement and IT to translate business challenges into practical solutions, from initial idea and solution design through experimentation, testing and implementation. A key focus is the hands-on exploration and co-development of AI agents and emerging technologies, ensuring new solutions deliver measurable business value while maintaining appropriate process controls, compliance and governance.

Through curiosity, experimentation and strong understanding of the end-to-end S2P landscape, the role helps shape new ways of working and supports P2P in building the capabilities needed for an increasingly digital and AI-enabled future.

Key responsibilities

  • Support and independently drive AI, automation and improvement initiatives across P2P.
  • Co-develop, configure and test AI agents and other digital solutions, translating business problems into requirements, use cases and test scenarios.
  • Build and test prototypes and proof-of-concepts using approved AI tools and platforms.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify P2P, reduce manual activities, remove unnecessary process steps and improve cycle times and user experience.
  • Apply structured problem-solving and root-cause analysis to P2P challenges.
  • Evaluate solution outputs critically, identifying defects, risks, exceptions and unintended process or control impacts.
  • Support pilots, implementation, adoption and post-implementation improvement of new solutions.
  • Actively follow developments in AI, agentic AI, automation and enterprise technology and translate relevant innovations into practical P2P opportunities.
  • Work closely with GFS, GBS, Procurement, IT and other S2P stakeholders to develop solutions across process and system boundaries.
  • Facilitate workshops and communicate effectively with operational, technical and senior stakeholders.
  • Ensure solutions respect P2P controls, compliance, information security, data privacy and appropriate AI governance.

Essential capabilities

  • Strong S2P/P2P process knowledge and Coupa literacy.
  • Advanced practical use of enterprise-approved generative AI and AI-enabled productivity tools.
  • Experience in process improvement, automation, digital transformation or solution design.
  • Ability to independently design and execute structured solution testing.
  • Excellent knowledge of BI, analytics and process data.
  • Very strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple initiatives and independently drive work from idea to outcome.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills at all organizational levels.
  • Innovative, curious and action-oriented mindset combined with strong awareness of controls and compliance.

Measures of success

  • P2P opportunities successfully progressed from idea through testing and implementation.
  • Measurable reduction in manual effort, process friction and cycle times.
  • Effective and controlled adoption of AI and automation solutions.
  • Growing pipeline of innovative P2P improvement opportunities.

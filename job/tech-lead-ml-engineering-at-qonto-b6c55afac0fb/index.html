About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France, Germany, Italy, Serbia, Spain.

[OUR MISSION AND CUSTOMERS] We are creating the freedom for SMEs to succeed by delivering Europe's leading finance workspace with banking at its core, augmented by financial tools. We are proud to be rated 4.7 on Trustpilot,based on 60,000 reviews. Our cultureputs customer satisfaction at the core of what we do (more about our culture here).

[OUR JOURNEY] Founded in 2016 and launched in 2017 byAlexandre and Steve, Qonto has grown to 1,700 Qontoers serving over 750,000 customers across 8 European countries. We have been profitable since 2023, and we are just getting started.

[OUR BELIEFS] We hire for skills and potential. With 80+ nationalities, 45% women, and 56% of women in our leadership team, diversity isn't a program; It's who we are. We've built a discrimination-free hiring process because the best teams are built on merit.

[AI AT QONTO] AI is deeply embedded in how we work (here) - Every Qontoer gets unlimited access to the best AI tools. We want people who experiment without waiting for permission, push AI beyond the obvious, know when to trust it, and when to question it.

Join us as Tech Lead, ML Engineering to lead the Document Intelligence stream that teaches Qonto to read new kinds of documents, and to understand the existing ones better.

Every day, 750,000 businesses drop supplier invoices, client invoices and expense receipts into Qonto. Document Intelligence, part of our AI Engines team, reads them, understands them and connects them to the right transaction, with in-house trained models and fine-tuned LLMs. Our North Star is the share of documents handled end to end, with no human touch. You will report to Benjamin Wolter, Head of AI Engines.

➡️ What you'll do

  • Lead and grow your team: you start with 2 ML engineers and build to 5 in 2027. You run the 1:1s and growth plans, set the hiring bar, hire and onboard.
  • Own your stream end to end: new document types, fields and internal flows for internal domains around banking, accounting, cash-flow management, plus performance improvement of the flows customers already rely on.
  • Stay hands-on: you design, train and deploy models into production yourself, next to your team. Frontier LLM, fine-tuned model or purpose-built extractor? You benchmark them per document type and per field on real production data, and ship what gives customers the best accuracy at the latency they need.
  • Plan with your stakeholders: you build the stream's plan with your Product Manager and align with backend and data engineers across product teams.
  • Own production and guide technical decisions: when a model drifts or breaks, your team knows first and fixes it. Architecture, standards and the roadmap are decided as a team with our Staff MLEs, and you help steer them.

➡️ What we're looking for

  • People management: 2+ years with at least 2 direct reports. You have hired, coached and managed performance, from growing strong performers to closing gaps.
  • Technical mastery: 6+ years as an ML engineer, with model architectures you designed and shipped yourself. You have kept models healthy in production: monitoring, retraining, rollback.
  • Delivery in a large, living system: you have delivered complex ML projects with many stakeholders, not only on greenfield projects.
  • Hands-on by choice: leading doesn't mean you stop building. You write Python you would be proud to see in production.
  • A sense for the right tool: you have worked with generative AI and with classical ML, and you know when each one wins. Nice to have: fine-tuning transformer models, document understanding (OCR, layout, extraction), fintech or accounting.

➡️ What we can offer you

  • A team you build: from 2 to 5 engineers in 2027, with your own hiring bar, onboarding and rituals.
  • One of Qonto's core AI platforms: AI Engines gathers 16 ML and data engineers who build shared engines that every product team plugs into, held to the reliability a bank needs. Your scope widens from invoices and receipts to bank and financial statements and legal documents.
  • A real stack: Python, FastAPI, AWS (SageMaker, Bedrock), MLflow, Airflow, Kafka, Kubernetes, Snowflake, PostgreSQL, Prometheus, ArgoCD, GitHub, Cursor, Claude Code. You can test any tool that helps us reach the target.
  • Strong peers: you work next to our Staff MLEs, and technical decisions are taken as a team.
  • Room to grow and visible impact: your scope and your team grow from day one, on the management track of our ML career framework. Our north star, automation rate, is felt by customers every day and reported to Product and Tech leadership.

➡️ Your future manager

Your Future Manager will be Benjamin, Head of AI Products.

His background?

After earning his PhD in Physics and leading ML Engineering and Data Science teams in established tech companies across last-mile logistics and digital marketing, Benjamin joined Qonto to lead our AI Products team!

What does he bring to the team?

With deep technical expertise in machine learning and data science, he brings practical experience in building scalable ML systems and guiding teams through complex technical challenges. Benjamin is committed to creating an environment of ownership and autonomy, supporting team members in their growth and development.

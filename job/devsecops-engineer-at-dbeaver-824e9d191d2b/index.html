About the job

At DBeaver, our team has a shared vision to integrate data into the routines and decision-making process of every individual. With a dedicated community of over 10 million active users worldwide who constantly inspire us, our open-source approach fuels innovation within.

We are looking for a DevSecOps Engineer to join our development team and help keep our infrastructure and products secure, focusing on cloud infrastructure, CI/CD security, and vulnerability management. You'll also help our engineering teams build secure products and have real influence over how we approach security.

Location: This role can be based in our hybrid offices in Belgrade, Serbia, or Budapest, Hungary; or remotely in Europe.

What You’ll Do:

  • Design, implement, and maintain secure cloud and on-premise infrastructure.
  • Integrate security controls into CI/CD pipelines and development workflows.
  • Automate security checks, compliance validation, and vulnerability management processes.
  • Secure containerized environments and Kubernetes clusters.
  • Implement and maintain secrets management, identity, and access control solutions.
  • Monitor infrastructure and applications for security threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Collaborate closely with developers, QA engineers, and platform teams to promote DevSecOps best practices.
  • Participate in security reviews, architecture discussions, and threat modeling activities.
  • Contribute to security standards, policies, and technical documentation.

What You Bring:

  • 5+ years in DevOps, DevSecOps, or infrastructure engineering, with recent focus on security.
  • Hands-on cloud security experience, preferably with AWS environments.
  • Experience working with pen testers or running security assessments, and following findings through to fixes.
  • Experience implementing vulnerability scanning, CVE triage, dependency management, and container security controls.
  • Understanding of application and infrastructure security principles.
  • Knowledge of networking fundamentals, IAM, secrets management, and secure authentication mechanisms.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production.
  • Experience with infrastructure as code, such as Terraform, and CI/CD platforms such as Jenkins or similar.
  • Knowledge of English (B2 level).

Bonus Skills:

  • Knowledge of security frameworks and standards such as ISO 27001 or NIST.
  • Experience creating security practices for AI use, not just using AI tools.
  • Experience with SIEM, monitoring, and observability platforms.
  • Security certifications such as AWS Security Specialty, CKS, CISSP, CCSP, or similar.
  • Experience contributing to open-source projects.
  • Understanding of database security concepts and data protection practices.

We Use the Following Tools:

  • AWS
  • Kubernetes
  • Docker
  • Terraform
  • GitHub
  • Prometheus
  • Grafana
  • ELK Stack

About DBeaver:

DBeaver Corporation provides a full-featured data solution that easily adapts to users' needs, from open-source tools to all-in-one enterprise software. Our mission is to make it possible for all kinds of people and organizations to leverage all kinds of data.

We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone feels welcomed and valued. If you are looking for an opportunity to join a collaborative and supportive team, we invite you to apply today.

About the job

At DBeaver, our team has a shared vision to integrate data into the routines and decision-making process of every individual. With a dedicated community of over 10 million active users worldwide who constantly inspire us, our open-source approach fuels innovation within.

We are looking for a DevSecOps Engineer to join our development team and help keep our products and cloud infrastructure secure. This is a security-focused DevSecOps role: you’ll work closely with our DevOps and development teams on CI/CD security, vulnerability management, and secure development practices.

Location: This role can be based in our hybrid offices in Belgrade, Serbia, or Budapest, Hungary; or remotely in Europe.

What You’ll Do:

  • Manage and maintain security controls into CI/CD pipelines and software development workflows.
  • Monitor vulnerabilities across products and infrastructure, coordinating prioritization, remediation, and verification.
  • Maintain AWS security best practices, including IAM, network security, secrets, encryption, logging, and monitoring.
  • Manage and support security incident detection, response, tracking, and post-incident reviews.
  • Coordinate penetration testing and track remediation of findings.
  • Manage access control principles and conduct regular access reviews.
  • Collaborate closely with engineering and operations teams to improve the company’s overall security posture
  • Support ISO 27001, SOC 2, and other security audits and certifications.

What You Bring:

  • 5+ years in DevSecOps, or DevOps or infrastructure engineering with a focus on security.
  • Hands-on cloud security experience, preferably with AWS environments (IAM, Organizations, VPC, CloudTrail, GuardDuty, Security Hub, KMS, and AWS Config).
  • Experience securing CI/CD pipelines and implementing SAST, DAST, SCA, secret scanning, and container scanning.
  • Experience with vulnerability management, risk assessment, remediation prioritization, and security reporting.
  • Knowledge of cloud networking, encryption, secrets management, logging, and monitoring.
  • Experience coordinating penetration tests and validating remediation results.
  • Experience with containers, container security, Docker, Kubernetes.
  • Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code security (preferably Terraform and CloudFormation).
  • Practical understanding of application security, including the OWASP Top 10, threat modeling, etc.
  • Understanding of identity and access management, RBAC, least privilege, and access review processes.
  • Scripting and automation skills in Python, Bash, or a similar language.
  • Experience with SIEM, security monitoring, incident investigation, and incident response.
  • Knowledge of English (B2 level).

Bonus Skills:

  • Working knowledge of ISO 27001, SOC 2, and common security frameworks such as NIST or CIS Benchmarks.
  • Certifications or professional training in ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor; and/or OWASP

We Use the Following Tools:

  • AWS
  • Kubernetes
  • Docker
  • Terraform
  • GitHub
  • Prometheus
  • Grafana
  • ELK Stack

About DBeaver:

DBeaver Corporation provides a full-featured data solution that easily adapts to users' needs, from open-source tools to all-in-one enterprise software. Our mission is to make it possible for all kinds of people and organizations to leverage all kinds of data.

We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone feels welcomed and valued. If you are looking for an opportunity to join a collaborative and supportive team, we invite you to apply today.

About the job

At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable.

We're looking for a motivated and driven IT Infrastructure Engineer (m/f/d) who will help us shape our team, drive the company to the next level, and have a direct influence on our success.

As an IT Infrastructure Engineer, you lead the efforts of maintaining and improving our global identity and access management platform as a coherent set of highly available services following our cloud-focused strategy. It is your responsibility to identify improvement opportunities, drive identity projects, and build a unified, scalable environment.

Based in Germany and the US, our IT Infrastructure Services team designs, implements, and manages global cloud and on-premises infrastructure solutions to provide Flix with internal business services such as identity and access management, security and compliance configuration, communication tools, file sharing, 3rd-level support, and infrastructure consulting.

About the Role

  • Integrate new internal and external applications into our identity infrastructure (e.g., SSO) following best practices
  • Work on projects to improve internal role-based access control concepts
  • Consult other teams and guide them to success, leveraging our services especially regarding identity and access management
  • Drive innovation and improve our services together with the team
  • Automate recurring tasks
  • Design the future IT infrastructure of Flix

About You

  • At least 3 years of experience with Microsoft Entra ID or similar identity and access management platforms
  • Advanced knowledge of modern authentication protocols (OIDC, OAuth 2.0, and SAML)
  • At least 3 years of scripting experience (ideally with Python)
  • At least 1 year of experience with IaC solutions (preferably Terraform)
  • Hands-on experience leveraging AI tools to streamline infrastructure automation, troubleshooting, and daily operations
  • Basic experience using APIs
  • Experience with containerization and orchestration platforms (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) is a plus
  • Experience working with IaaS solutions is a plus
  • Basic knowledge of modern frontend frameworks (e.g., React) or web technologies is a plus
  • Experience with M365 products (such as Exchange Online, SharePoint/OneDrive, and Microsoft Teams) is a plus

We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!

What We Offer

  • Travel perks: 12 free Flix vouchers + 12 discount vouchers for friends & family.
  • Work from (M)Anywhere: Depending on your role, work from another location for up to 60 days per year.
  • Hybrid work model: We are an office-first company, but we offer flexibility to balance work and life.
  • Wellbeing support: Access confidential 1:1 counselling, courses, and stress management for yourself and up to four family members.
  • Learning & Development: Take advantage of language classes, training courses, and expert-led sessions to grow your skills.
  • Mentoring Program: Connect with experienced colleagues to gain insights and accelerate your career.

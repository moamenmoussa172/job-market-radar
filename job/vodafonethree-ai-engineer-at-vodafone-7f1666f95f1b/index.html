What you’ll do
As an AI Engineer, you’ll turn ambitious AI ideas into production-ready products that make a real difference across VodafoneThree. Working alongside Senior and Principal AI Engineers, you’ll take ownership of building intelligent applications from concept through to deployment, gaining hands-on experience with agentic AI, RAG, LLM applications and responsible AI while helping improve efficiency and reduce costs across the organisation.

  • You’ll build and ship end-to-end AI-powered applications, working across front end, back end and data to deliver solutions that create measurable value.
  • You’ll design and develop agentic and multi-agent workflows, using tool calling, Model Context Protocol (MCP), memory and orchestration techniques.
  • You’ll implement RAG, context and prompt engineering, conversational interfaces and LLM integrations to create intuitive AI experiences.
  • You’ll build robust APIs and integrations that connect AI models with data platforms and existing business systems.
  • You’ll evaluate AI solutions using curated test sets, regression testing and LLM-as-judge techniques, helping ensure quality, accuracy and reliability.
  • You’ll embed responsible AI, security and safety through effective guardrails, content filtering, governance and data protection controls.
  • You’ll contribute clean, tested and maintainable code, support CI/CD and releases, and monitor performance, cost, latency and model behaviour in production.
  • You’ll work closely with users and stakeholders to understand their needs, gather feedback and continuously improve accessible, practical AI products.

Who you are

  • You’ll bring a Bachelor’s or MSc degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering or another relevant STEM discipline related to AI development.
  • You’ll have experience building and shipping AI-powered applications for real users, or strong personal or open-source projects that demonstrate comparable hands-on capability.
  • You’ll bring strong software engineering skills, ideally in Python or a similar programming language, with a focus on clean, secure and maintainable code.
  • You’ll understand modern LLM application patterns, including prompt and context engineering, RAG, agent frameworks, tool calling and orchestration.
  • You’ll understand how to evaluate LLM-based systems for quality, reliability and safety, and will take a thoughtful approach to responsible AI.
  • You’ll have experience or knowledge across APIs, application architecture, cloud services, containers, CI/CD and system integration.
  • You’ll be comfortable considering production factors such as latency, cost, security, safety and observability, while adapting pragmatically to an evolving technology landscape.
  • You’ll be a proactive and collaborative AI builder, curious about emerging technologies, comfortable experimenting with new ideas and ready to turn learning into high-quality products at pace.

Where you belong

Product Excellence:

Product Excellence is a team under the SAP Signavio functional unit where we enable product teams to build exceptional products that delight users and achieve business goals by championing a customer-centric and data-driven approach to product management.
We provide essential frameworks, tools and best practices necessary to craft strong product strategies, make informed decisions, and execute effectively. By fostering a culture of continuous improvement, we ensure sustained innovation and excellence across the organization

The Product Excellence team comprises multiple specialized sub-units that collectively enable high-performing, scalable, and customer-centric product teams. These include Business Analytics, which provides data-driven insights to support informed decision-making; Practices and Programs Management, which establishes governance, drives execution discipline, and ensures alignment across initiatives; and Curation of Best Practices, focused on defining, standardizing, and continuously improving processes to enable smooth project execution across cross-functional teams.

In addition, the team focuses on Voice of Customer initiatives to systematically capture, analyze, and embed customer feedback into product strategy and delivery. We also manage onboarding and upskilling programs to accelerate new team members’ productivity and continuously enhance the capabilities of existing talent. Finally, the team emphasizes the optimal utilization of available tools, frameworks, and resources to improve operational efficiency, reduce friction, and enable product teams to focus on delivering maximum value.

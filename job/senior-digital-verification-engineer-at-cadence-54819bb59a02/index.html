Job Title: T4 - Principal Design Engineer

Location: France, Rennes (preferably) or Paris

Reports to: Sr Design Engineering Manager

Job Overview: Senior Digital Verification Engineer

You will lead the development and execution of robust, reusable UVM verification environments for our products, ensuring functional‑coverage and first‑silicon success.

By driving verification strategy, automating large‑scale regression flows, and mentoring the verification team, the role directly safeguards the quality of high‑performance ASIC designs.

The work spans building scalable test‑bench architectures, integrating Cadence verification tools, and collaborating cross‑functionally with design and software teams to close verification gaps before RTL freeze.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Define and implement robust, scalable UVM test‑bench architectures for complex digital IPs
  • Create and maintain reusable verification components (sequencers, drivers, monitors, scoreboards, coverage models)
  • Develop directed, random, and constrained‑random test cases to achieve 100 % functional coverage and 100 % code coverage on target designs
  • Lead root‑cause analysis of simulation failures, performance bottlenecks, and silicon bring‑up bugs
  • Coordinate with design teams to close verification gaps before RTL freeze
  • Contribute to internal verification guidelines, templates, and training materials
  • Mentor junior verification engineers (T1‑T3) and provide technical guidance on UVM, test‑bench design, and debugging techniques
  • Participate in design/verification reviews, providing constructive feedback and risk assessment

Job Qualifications:

  • B.S. or M.S. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field
  • 7‑10 years of professional digital verification experience, including ≥ 5 years with UVM on ASIC or FPGA projects
  • Proficiency in SystemVerilog/UVM
  • Strong hands‑on experience with Cadence Xcelium
  • Knowledge of synthesis‑aware verification (e.g., constraint‑driven random verification, coverage‑driven closure)
  • Deep understanding of functional coverage, assertions (SVA), formal verification, and constrained‑random verification
  • Comfortable with large‑scale simulation environments, waveform analysis, and performance profiling
  • Excellent communication, problem‑solving, and leadership capabilities
  • Ability to influence cross‑functional teams and mentor junior engineers.

Additional Skills/Preferences:

  • Hands‑on with verification IP (VIP) development and integration
  • Exposure to low‑power verification (e.g., power‑aware UVM, clock gating checks)
  • Scripting in Python, TCL, Makefile or Bash for automation
  • Familiarity with Git and CI/CD tools

Présentation du poste : Senior Digital Verification Engineer

Le travail consiste à concevoir des architectures de bancs de test évolutives, à intégrer les outils de vérification Cadence et à collaborer de façon transversale avec les équipes de conception RTL et logicielles les afin de combler les lacunes de vérification avant le « RTL freeze ».

  • Coordonner avec les équipes de conception RTL pour résoudre les lacunes de vérification avant « RTL freeze »
  • Contribuer aux directives internes de vérification, aux modèles de banc de tests et aux supports de formation
  • Encadrer les ingénieurs vérificateurs juniors (T1‑T3) et fournir des conseils techniques sur la méthodologie UVM, la conception de bancs de test et les techniques de débogage
  • Participer aux revues de conception/vérification en apportant des retours constructifs et une évaluation des risques

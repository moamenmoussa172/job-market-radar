About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About the team

The Control Plane Platform team is building the shared foundation that every product in ClickHouse Cloud is built and shipped on. We give ClickHouse engineering teams one place to own and deploy their product, remove the overhead of rebuilding common plumbing, and keep the customer experience consistent as the number of integrating teams grows. The team owns API gateways and API standards, shared platform services, deployment and infrastructure patterns, testing and CI, local developer experience and Control Plane observability.

About the role

We’re looking for a platform software engineer that will work on developing the shared services and core of the platform. This is a high-impact, technical role at the center of our Cloud product, combining technical direction, scalability mindset, and operational ownership while maintaining the reliability customers expect.

You’ll work hand-in-hand with Product, Infrastructure, Security, and engineering teams across ClickHouse.

This role goes beyond engineering. As part of the platform team, you’ll help shape how new products and teams will integrate and deploy their applications in ClickHouse Cloud.

What You'll Do

  • Build and evolve the core platform surface: shared platform services (event bus, notifications, feature flags, websockets, audit records, Customer Data Platform), internal tools, CI and testing, local developer experience, and the software factory.
  • Build the Customer Data Platform that captures customer events from our web applications and APIs, and make that data reliably available to the teams and systems that depend on it.
  • Own the integration points that let teams host and ship their code in our monorepo, so each team can own and deploy its product without rebuilding common plumbing.
  • Treat integrating teams as your customers, and measure success by their delivery velocity and their adoption of the platform.
  • Raise the bar on architecture and design through thoughtful reviews of designs and code, including those of other senior engineers.
  • Land cross-cutting migrations, deprecations, and contract changes across teams without stalling product delivery.
  • Dive into the design and code of complex areas: gateway routing, spec-driven code generation, event delivery semantics and idempotency, and multi-region rollout safety.
  • Define and drive API standards across the platform so the customer experience stays consistent as the number of integrating teams grows.
  • Build and improve Control Plane observability, and take part in incident response and investigation to improve the platform's reliability.
  • Systematize support, reduce toil, and build self-serve and auto-healing capabilities: golden-path templates and generators, and documentation that gets a new team integrated in days rather than weeks.
  • Work alongside software engineers and DevOps/Infra engineers who build and maintain the platform's infrastructure.
  • Help shape the platform vision and turn it into a roadmap. Sequence bets for business impact, ship fast with tight feedback loops, and write the RFCs that build cross-org consensus before big changes land.

What you bring

  • 8+ years in software engineering, including 3+ years developing internal and external platforms.
  • Experience developing internal tools and infrastructure for other engineering teams, such as service catalogs, golden-path templates, and self-service app scaffolding.
  • Experience with large TypeScript/Node monorepos and the developer-experience problems that come with them: CI scale and flakiness, build and test times, local development environments, and code generation from specs.
  • Experience building scalable, highly available, and secure cloud infrastructure in AWS (EKS, SQS, DocumentDB, Redis, PostgreSQL, Lambda), with infrastructure as code (Terraform, Helm) and progressive delivery (Argo Rollouts or similar).
  • Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes), including patterns for hosting many independently deployed applications in a shared cluster.
  • Experience building event-driven systems (queues, event buses, notification pipelines) and a solid grasp of delivery guarantees, ordering, retries, and idempotency.
  • A track record of decomposing a monolith into independently deployable services without stalling product delivery.
  • Technical credibility to engage deeply in architecture discussions and incident investigations.
  • Sound execution judgment across ambiguity, parallel workstreams, risk, and long-term system health.
  • Strong written communication, including authoring RFCs and design docs that drive alignment across teams.

Nice to have

  • Familiarity with API platform work: OpenAPI-driven contracts, API gateways, and the shared concerns they centralize (authentication, RBAC, rate limiting, versioning, and deprecation).
  • Designed and implemented strategies for load balancing, auto-scaling, disaster recovery, and failover in cloud environments.
  • Experience building CI/CD at scale.
  • Proficiency with frontend frameworks (React).
  • Experience making APIs consumable by AI agents: MCP servers, CLIs, or skills generated from API specs.
  • Experience building or operating customer event tracking or analytics pipelines.
  • Experience with ClickHouse.
  • Previous experience developing analytics or data-intensive applications.

Why This Role

  • Shape how every feature team in ClickHouse Cloud develops, ships, and integrates with the others.
  • Operate with ownership, autonomy, and speed while keeping the company lean.
  • Work on problems that touch every product in the platform, with a direct line from your work to the delivery velocity of the whole engineering organization

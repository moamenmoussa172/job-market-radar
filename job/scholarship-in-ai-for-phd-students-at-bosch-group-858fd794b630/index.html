Scholarship in AI for PhD Students

Bosch Group · Cluj-Napoca, Romania

Hybrid · Other data role · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Bosch Engineering Center Cluj offers scholarships of 4000 RON/month over the academic year for enthusiastic enthusiastic Doctoral students. Students granted with scholarships have the possibility to work together with Bosch experts in Software and Hardware fields.

The research topics we are proposing to be approached together are:

  • AI embedded / edge AI;
  • AI chips design;
  • Neural networks optimizations;
  • HW friendly AI architecture;
  • Knowledge distillation from large to small embedded models;
  • Real-time physical AI;
  • AI training HW and training optimization techniques (stretched objective);
  • Hardware-Software co-design.
  • Students in a full-time doctoral program;
  • Year of study: 1st, 2nd, 3rd;
  • Having the annual doctoral studies conditions met (articles, conference, etc.);
  • Attending to one of the following research domains:
  • Computers and Information Technology;
  • Systems Engineering;
  • Electronic Engineering, Telecommunications and Information Technologies;
  • Electrical Engineering;
  • Mechanical Engineering;
  • Not enrolled in public funded projects.

Selection:

  • Submit your CV until the 6th of January
  • Technical interview period between 16th-21st of January
  • The subject of doctoral thesis must be in line with one of the topics proposed;
  • For more information contact us on ECCAcademicPrograms@ro.bosch.com.

Benefits of being in the Bosch Scholarship Program:

  • Scholarship of 4000 RON/month;
  • Mentorship for a project developed together;
  • Access to educational resources within Bosch;
  • Networking possibilities with IT experts.

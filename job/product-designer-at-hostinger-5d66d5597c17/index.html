About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Lithuania, Poland.

Join the team building an AI-first company.

Hostinger serves more than 5.5 million clients across 150 countries, but we're still excited about what we're creating next.

AI is changing how we build products, support customers, work together, and solve problems. It helps us move faster, spend less time on repetitive work, and focus on bigger ideas.

If you want your work to influence both what we build and how we build it, we'd like to meet you.

Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles

Your day-to-day

  • Work with cross-functional teams to solve customers problems.
  • Create innovative solutions that meet customer needs and are extremely easy to use.
  • Own and drive the entire design process for your area: be the advocate for the user, responsible for the journey from first sketch to shipped feature, and then for monitoring its performance and iterating.
  • Conduct extensive user research to inform design decisions.
  • Iterate on exciting solutions based on received feedback and data.
  • Keep up-to-date with current design trends, techniques, and practices.
  • Actively collaborate with the design guild to improve our existing design systems and design processes.
  • Be autonomous and proactive. You’re going to be taking full ownership and responsibility for your work.
  • Provide and get actionable feedback from teammates.

What will help you succeed

  • You have minimum 3+ years of experience designing for consumer-focused tech companies.
  • You have deep fluency in design thinking, user-centered methodologies, UX research, prototyping and iterative design — not just delivering UI screens, but understanding why and how those designs solve user & business problems.
  • You’re comfortable owning a design area end-to-end: making strategic design decisions, collaborating cross-functionally, balancing user needs, business goals and technical constraints, and driving execution through to launch.
  • You have excellent communication skills to clearly present your design ideas and solutions.
  • You can conduct user research, understand the data and validate ideas.
  • You have an excellent knowledge of design tools.
  • You have a great knowledge of design systems and can contribute to them.
  • You are driven, creative, and hungry for knowledge.
  • You have an impressive online portfolio that you’re desperate to show off.
  • Understanding of analytics tools such as Amplitude, HotJar.

Benefits you'll love

🚀 360° Growth: You'll never stop learning here. Get access to Reforge and CoachHub, go to global conferences, and borrow from our physical and digital libraries. Depending on your location, you can mentor or be mentored through TesoXchange. People give each other open feedback, share what they know, and take on new projects as their skills grow.

🎯 Freedom & responsibility: Work where you do your best work: our offices in Vilnius, Kaunas, Jakarta, or Luxembourg, your home, or anywhere in the world. You plan your own day and own your results, and good ideas go live fast.

💪 Wellness simplified: Your wellbeing comes first. You get time off to recharge, a Headspace subscription, and events focused on wellbeing. Depending on your location, you also get health insurance from day one, a gym membership, regular health checks, and clubs for sports, arts, and hobbies.

🎉 Work hard – play hard: We celebrate wins together at Summerfest & Winterfest, Town Halls, team-buildings, and workations. Meet the Client sessions connect you directly with the people you build for. Milestones outside work matter too, so we celebrate weddings, new parenthood, and graduations with you.

Compensation starts at 4,500 EUR/month, depending on your experience and skills.

We're always looking for experienced talent to raise the bar. If you're excited about the role but the listed compensation range doesn't align with your expectations, we still encourage you to apply — we're open to adjusting it based on the impact and value you bring.

Get ready to take your personal and professional growth to new heights! Join Hostinger today and be part of our journey 🚀

Three. Two. Onboard

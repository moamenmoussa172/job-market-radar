Role Overview

As a Forward Deployed Software Engineering (FDE) Manager, you will lead a high-performing team building solutions for customers' data and AI challenges using the Databricks platform. Your team will own the architecture, lead design decisions, rapidly prototype and implement end-to-end systems spanning apps, AI and data. Our focus is to combine engineering expertise with adaptability, curiosity, and a passion for working with customers and teammates to build solutions that drive measurable outcomes.

The impact you’ll have

  • Oversee the successful delivery of customer outcomes through high-quality FDE execution, as measured by key metrics including bookings, revenue, CSAT and impact on consumption
  • Connect your team’s day-to-day work to real customer outcomes and business impact. Build excitement about Databricks FDE by sharing customer wins and delivery successes, making each individual feel motivated and appreciated
  • Hire strong Software Engineer/FDE/Full Stack talent using structured interviews and practical delivery scenarios. Grow the team by ensuring continuous technical learning and experimentation. Understand your team’s strengths and growth areas to provide regular coaching, address performance gaps promptly by offering actionable feedback and maintaining clear accountability
  • Reinforce your team’s top delivery priorities. Help your team focus on high-impact technical work, redirecting effort away from low-value tasks and using customer impact, delivery data and KPIs to confirm focus areas
  • Apply your own builder and AI-first mindset to improve team productivity. Remove delivery blockers quickly, using automation or AI assistants to accelerate routine work. Run consistent inspection rhythms (standups, project reviews, retrospectives) and hold teammates strictly accountable to timelines and quality bars
  • Translate Databricks’ culture principles (Customer Obsessed, Truth Seeking, First Principles, Raise the Bar, Company-First, Bias for Action) into concrete day-to-day behaviors, ensuring standards are upheld in all customer interactions
  • Review delivery plans and architectures utilising practical knowledge of SWE/FDE/Full Stack approaches. Actively coach ICs on secure, scalable deployment patterns, governance, and production readiness.

What We Look For

  • Experience: 8+ years of total technical experience with 2+ years of experience managing, leading, or mentoring high-caliber, AI-first, Software Engineering, Full Stack or Forward Deployed Engineering teams
  • Technical Depth: Enough technical depth to engage on architecture and trade-offs with senior and staff engineers, and to hold a high-quality bar for our customer-facing outputs
  • Track Record: Evidence of leading, building and shipping tools/apps/platforms used by many end users, delivered using production-grade approaches
  • Leadership Skills: Proven ability to manage technical project delivery, timelines, scoping, conflict resolution, and executive customer relationships from technical ICs up to Director level
  • Comfort with Ambiguity: Experience leading teams through ambiguity, with competing priorities from customers, partners and platform dependencies, and making clear calls.

About Databricks

Databricks is the Data and AI company. More than 20,000 organizations worldwide — including adidas, AT&T, Bayer, Block, Mastercard, Rivian, Unilever, and 70% of the Fortune 500 — rely on the Databricks Data + AI Platform to build and scale data and AI apps, analytics and agents. Headquartered in San Francisco with 30+ offices around the globe, Databricks offers a unified platform that includes Genie, Lakebase, Agent Bricks, Lakeflow, Lakehouse, and Unity Catalog.

About the job

The COST (Cost Optimization, Strategy, and Technology) team within Ops Tech Solutions (OTS) Delivery is seeking a Technical Program Manager to own programs where the answer depends on getting the data and the systems right. The COST team delivers cost visibility, savings governance, and free cash flow improvement across a global Reliability and Maintenance Engineering (RME) and IT Operations cost base. Within COST, our team finds savings opportunities, checks whether savings are real, and gives leaders the cost reporting and comparisons they use to make decisions. You will own programs end to end, from defining the method to getting leaders to use the result. You will work with field leaders, finance, business intelligence and engineering teams across Europe and globally. You will trace numbers back through the systems that produce them, find where a process breaks, and put a mechanism in place so it does not break again.
Key job responsibilities
- Own technical programs end to end, from scope and schedule to risks, dependencies and communication
- Define methods and metrics that make cost comparable across regions and sites, and test them against the data yourself
- Trace financial and operational data through source systems, and build mechanisms that fix problems at the cause
- Build tools and automation yourself, including with generative AI, and validate the output against source data
- Write business cases and program reviews for senior leaders, and spend time with the operation you support, including site visits
A day in the life
You might trace why two regions report the same cost differently, agree a data definition with a finance partner, build a script to replace a manual report, review a program's risks with an engineering team, or present findings to a leadership review. Travel up to 10%.
About the team
We are a small team of program managers and business intelligence, spread across several countries and part of OTS Delivery. We work closely with the team that owns our data platform and delivers technical programs, and we document our work so anyone on the team can pick it up.
You will own a program from the method through to leadership reviews, build your own tools with generative AI, and work with finance, field and engineering leaders across regions, which gives you a broad view of how costs are run in a global operation.
- Experience in project management disciplines including scope, schedule, budget, quality, along with risk and critical path management
- Experience managing programs across cross functional teams, building processes and coordinating release schedules
- Experience building and evaluating system-level technical design
- Speak, write, and read fluently in English, and have the ability to take direction in English
- Experience working directly with engineering teams
- Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science or equivalent
- Experience using any of SQL or other analytical tools for conducting data analysis
- Experience working with finance organizations and financial data
- Experience leveraging AI-powered tools or data analytics platforms to drive quality improvements, root cause analysis, or process optimization
- Experience producing executive-level written materials and presenting to executive audiences
- Experience owning program strategy, managing end-to-end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience performing data analysis and troubleshooting data integrity issues

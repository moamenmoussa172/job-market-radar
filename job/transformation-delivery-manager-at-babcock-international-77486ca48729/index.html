Shape the future of transformation and innovation across defence, engineering and critical infrastructure

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Transformation Delivery Manager at our Andover site.

The role

As a Transformation Delivery Manager, you’ll have a role that’s out of the ordinary. You'll play a pivotal role in driving business transformation, operational improvement and strategic growth initiatives across our Land sector. This is an exciting opportunity to influence how services are delivered today while helping to shape future operating models, transformation strategies and customer solutions.

Day-to-day, you'll be:

  • Deliver business transformation initiatives that drive strategic change, operational improvement and service excellence.
  • Coordinate and monitor transformation projects, ensuring delivery against agreed timelines, budgets, benefits and governance requirements.
  • Facilitate stakeholder workshops and cross-functional collaboration to identify opportunities for innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Produce high-quality business cases, reports, implementation plans and executive-level presentations to support decision-making.
  • Support future growth by developing operating models, transformation strategies, customer proposals and integrated service solutions.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and is based at Andover with hybrid working arrangements.

Essential experience of the Transformation Delivery Manager:

  • Experience delivering business transformation, operational improvement or strategic change programmes within a complex organisation.
  • Strong understanding of project management and change management principles, including governance, risk and milestone management.
  • Proven ability to engage, influence and build relationships with stakeholders at all levels.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with experience producing business cases, reports and executive-level presentations.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively within fast-paced, collaborative environments

Qualifications for the Transformation Delivery Manager:

  • Professional Project Management qualification such as APM, PRINCE2, MSP, or an equivalent recognised certification.
  • Change Management qualification such as Prosci, APMG Change Management, or a similar recognised accreditation
  • Experience supporting business development, bids, campaigns, capture activities or customer proposal development.
  • Knowledge of the UK Defence sector, Ministry of Defence environments, Defence Equipment and Support, asset management or fleet management is desirable

Security Clearance

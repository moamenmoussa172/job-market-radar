About the job

SOOR, a subsidiary of Master Works, helps organizations stay ahead of evolving cyber threats through advanced cybersecurity and secure infrastructure solutions. SOOR delivers resilient, scalable, and regulation-aligned security across governance, cloud, networks, and managed defense.

SOOR is looking for an experienced Network Security, Senior Engineer with strong technical knowledge and hands-on experience in implementing, configuring, managing, and troubleshooting enterprise network and cybersecurity solutions. The candidate should be able to handle technical projects, provide professional services, and support customer environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Implement, configure, and maintain network security solutions.
  • Deploy and manage Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, Forcepoint and other vendors' security solutions.
  • Configure and troubleshoot firewall policies, NAT, IPsec/SSL VPN, SD-WAN, and High Availability (HA).
  • Deploy, configure, manage, and troubleshoot EDR, NDR, DLP, Proxy, and email security solutions, including integration with existing security infrastructure.
  • Implement and support network infrastructure, including switches, routers, VLANs, and wireless networks.
  • Perform security assessments, configuration reviews, and security hardening.
  • Troubleshoot complex network and security issues.
  • Experience with security hardening and vulnerability remediation.
  • Perform firmware upgrades, system migrations, and security solution integrations.
  • Prepare technical documentation, implementation plans, and as-built documents.
  • Coordinate with customers and internal teams to ensure successful project delivery.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in network security.
  • Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Good communication and technical documentation skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple technical tasks.

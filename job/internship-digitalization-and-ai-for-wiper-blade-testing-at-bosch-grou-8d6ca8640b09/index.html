Internship - Digitalization & AI for Wiper Blade Testing

Bosch Group · Tienen, Belgium

Onsite · Intern / Student · QA / Test Engineer · Posted 12 November 2026 · $11k a year (converted to USD)

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Your challenge

Join our wiper blade test department and help us make our testing processes smarter, more standardized and data-driven. You will work on practical digitalization and AI solutions together with our test engineers.

Your main tasks

  • Test specification standardization
  • Compare existing wiper blade test specifications and identify opportunities for complexity reduction.
  • Standardize test descriptions and required information.
  • Develop an AI-based solution to easily compare specifications and highlight differences.
  • AI for test reports
  • Develop an AI solution to search and analyze historical test reports.
  • Investigate how the quality and structure of existing data can be improved.
  • Create a standardized template for future test reports to enable effective AI-based analysis.
  • Digitalization of test equipment
  • Update the test equipment inventory, including equipment locations.
  • Support the preparation and entry of equipment data into the new laboratory management system planned for 2027.
  • Wiper arm digitalization & 3D scanning
  • Create an inventory of available wiper arms and link them to the existing wiper arm library.
  • Support the digitalization of wiper arms using 3D scanning.
  • Bachelor or Master student with a technical/engineering background, for example mechanical, automotive, electromechanical or industrial engineering.
  • A strong interest in digitalization and Artificial Intelligence.
  • An analytical and structured way of working.
  • Motivation to work with technical specifications, test reports and engineering data.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work closely with test engineers.
  • Good written and spoken English, as you will work with technical specifications, reports and colleagues in an international environment.

The internship requires that you have the nationality of a EU-country.

The personal experience of the interns is usually the following: The company employs 10-15 interns all year round from various countries. Therefore this is also a great opportunity to enhance your social and language skills. The interns live mostly in Tienen or in Leuven, known as a multicultural university city. You receive 800 € as expense reimbursement per month.

Practical Information

Duration: total 6 months

Location: Hamelendreef 80, 3300 Tienen, Belgium

About the job

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced QC Test Lead Engineer to lead testing activities across assigned products and delivery teams. The successful candidate will combine strong hands-on testing and automation expertise with the ability to plan, coordinate, review, and continuously improve testing work. The role is accountable for test strategy execution, risk-based coverage, defect management, quality metrics, and effective collaboration with engineering and business stakeholders. The Test Lead will also champion AI-enabled quality engineering, ensuring AI is used responsibly to accelerate test design, automation, analysis, and decision support while maintaining human ownership of quality outcomes.

Job Objectives

  • Lead and coordinate testing activities to deliver reliable, high-quality software across Web, Mobile, and API platforms.
  • Translate project requirements and testing strategies into practical test plans, estimates, priorities, and measurable outcomes.
  • Ensure effective test coverage, defect management, test execution, and clear quality reporting throughout the delivery lifecycle.
  • Guide the development and adoption of maintainable test automation frameworks and integrate automated testing into CI/CD workflows.
  • Provide technical direction, task coordination, coaching, and knowledge sharing to QC engineers within assigned teams.
  • Drive continuous improvement and the adoption of AI-enabled testing practices to increase productivity, coverage, and early defect detection.

Job Responsibilities

Test Leadership, Planning & Coordination

  • Lead day-to-day testing activities for assigned products, projects, or delivery teams, ensuring work is planned, prioritized, and completed in line with delivery commitments.
  • Develop and review test plans for relevant test levels, aligned with project testing strategy, scope, risks, and acceptance criteria.
  • Coordinate with Product Owners, Business Analysts, Developers, Delivery, and other stakeholders to clarify requirements and ensure testing solutions meet business and technical needs.
  • Perform or facilitate requirement ambiguity analysis and risk analysis; define testing priorities, scope, dependencies, and appropriate test approaches.
  • Prepare and review testing estimates, resource plans, and execution schedules; communicate assumptions, constraints, and delivery risks.
  • Assign and distribute testing tasks based on team capacity, skills, priorities, and project needs, and monitor progress to completion.
  • Provide direction to QC engineers, support decision-making, and escalate complex issues or resource constraints when needed.
  • Review test plans, scenarios, cases, scripts, and execution evidence to ensure completeness, accuracy, and alignment with expected quality standards.

Test Design, Execution & Defect Management

  • Oversee the design and execution of functional, integration, system, regression, smoke, exploratory, and end-to-end testing across Web, Mobile, and API solutions.
  • Ensure test scenarios and cases cover functional and non-functional requirements, business workflows, integrations, and relevant edge cases.
  • Ensure appropriate test design techniques, test data, environments, and traceability are applied across the testing lifecycle.
  • Review test execution results, investigate discrepancies, identify likely causes, and ensure findings are communicated clearly to relevant stakeholders.
  • Own and monitor the defect management process, including defect reporting quality, prioritization, triage, follow-up, verification, and closure.
  • Coordinate defect resolution with development and product teams and escalate critical quality risks that may affect delivery or production.
  • Ensure adequate test coverage and regression protection to reduce the likelihood of escaped defects and production issues.
  • Review and approve test summary reports and communicate testing progress, results, residual risks, and release readiness inputs.

Automation Strategy & Technical Direction

  • Lead the expansion and improvement of automated testing frameworks for UI, Mobile, and API testing.
  • Guide the team in designing, developing, reviewing, and maintaining reusable automation scripts, libraries, utilities, and framework components.
  • Promote suitable automation tools and frameworks, including Selenium, Appium, Playwright, Cypress, Robot Framework, and API testing solutions, based on project needs.
  • Ensure automation candidates are selected based on business value, risk, repeatability, execution frequency, and maintenance cost.
  • Coordinate integration of automated tests into CI/CD pipelines and support continuous testing and early feedback within delivery workflows.
  • Review automation results and failures, distinguish product defects from test or environment issues, and drive improvements in reliability and maintainability.
  • Evaluate new testing tools, techniques, and environments and recommend solutions that provide measurable value to current and future projects.

Quality Metrics, Reporting & Release Readiness

  • Own test coverage, test execution, defect, automation, and quality reporting for assigned products or teams.
  • Define, track, and analyze relevant testing metrics, identifying trends, bottlenecks, recurring defects, and opportunities for improvement.
  • Provide clear and timely status updates on testing progress, risks, blockers, dependencies, and expected completion.
  • Assess test completion against agreed entry and exit criteria and provide evidence-based quality inputs for release decisions.
  • Ensure testing assets, results, reports, and lessons learned are documented, maintained, and archived appropriately.

AI-Enabled Quality Engineering

  • Champion the practical adoption of AI-powered tools and techniques across the testing lifecycle to improve quality, speed, and team productivity.
  • Use AI-assisted analysis to review requirements, identify ambiguities and risks, and propose test scenarios, test conditions, and test cases.
  • Guide the team in using AI to accelerate test script creation, refactoring, maintenance, and automation coverage expansion.
  • Leverage AI for test data generation, log analysis, defect investigation, failure clustering, and root-cause analysis support.
  • Explore AI-enabled approaches such as risk-based test selection, intelligent regression prioritization, and automated test result analysis.
  • Evaluate AI-generated outputs for correctness, security, maintainability, and alignment with business requirements before they are adopted.
  • Identify opportunities to embed AI into QC workflows, define reusable prompts and practices, and share effective use cases across teams.
  • Monitor the impact of AI adoption through measurable outcomes such as reduced test design effort, faster execution analysis, improved coverage, and reduced rework.
  • Ensure human review and accountability remain in place for test decisions, defect conclusions, and quality or release recommendations.

Team Development, Collaboration & Continuous Improvement

  • Provide technical guidance, mentoring, and constructive feedback to QC engineers, helping strengthen testing, automation, analytical, and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Facilitate knowledge sharing, peer reviews, and collaboration within the testing team and across engineering communities.
  • Work effectively with distributed and cross-functional teams, ensuring alignment on priorities, dependencies, and delivery expectations.
  • Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies and contribute testing insights to planning, refinement, daily coordination, and retrospectives.
  • Advocate quality engineering best practices and lightweight preventive checks across development and engineering teams.
  • Identify inadequacies in testing methods, standards, tools, and workflows; recommend and lead practical improvement initiatives.
  • Organize or contribute to special engineering initiatives, tool evaluations, framework development, and reusable testing solutions.
  • Promote a culture of ownership, transparency, collaboration, and continuous learning.

About the job

Are you ready to lead a talented team that secures access for thousands of employees at one of the world's most innovative companies? We are looking for a Manager II, Support Engineer to lead our Security Support Engineering team and drive Identity and Access Management (IAM) initiatives that protect Amazon's corporate environment. In this role, you will manage the day-to-day operations of a team securing access at massive scale while providing direct support to business owners across the company.
You will set clear priorities, develop your engineers toward their next step, and ensure the team delivers high-quality outcomes against service level agreements. This is a hands-on leadership opportunity where you will bring technical depth to guide the team through complex access management challenges, make sound judgments on security outcomes, optimize workflows with a focus on root-cause resolution, and create an inclusive environment where your team thrives.
Key job responsibilities
Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
- Lead and develop a team of Security Support Engineers, setting clear priorities and performance expectations and providing regular coaching and feedback
- Own the team's operational results, making sure work is delivered on time, meets service level agreements, and holds a high quality bar
- Hire, onboard, and grow talent, creating development plans that stretch engineers and prepare them for their next step
- Guide the team through the investigation and remediation of complex business decisions regarding security outcomes, escalating security risks with good judgement
- Optimise team workflows and best practices, prioritising root-cause resolution and reducing manual effort through automation and tooling
- Partner with Security Engineers and stakeholders across the company to align on priorities and deliver the right customer outcomes
- Own team security metrics, using data to measure performance and drive improvements
A day in the life
You will lead both the people and operations of the Security Support Engineering team. On any given day, you might review the team's case queues and SLA adherence, coach an engineer through a difficult access management investigation, run a one-on-one focused on career development, and collaborate with partner teams to set priorities for upcoming security initiatives. You will identify recurring issues and direct the team toward automation and process improvements that resolve them at the root. When problems are complex or risks emerge, you will help the team find a path forward and decide when to escalate.
This role includes occasional on-call responsibilities.
About the team
Our team's mission is to make the secure way the easy way. We focus on Identity and Access Management improvements, and our remit covers both internal and external identity. We are committed to protecting Amazon's corporate environment while ensuring that employees have the access they need to innovate and deliver for customers.
We value collaboration, continuous learning, and operational excellence. As part of our team, you will work in an inclusive environment that encourages engineers to take initiative, solve complex problems, and grow their skills while delivering high-impact security outcomes at scale.

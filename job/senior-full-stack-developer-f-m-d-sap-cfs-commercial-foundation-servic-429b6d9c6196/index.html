About the job

What you’ll build

At CFS, we own and maintain UI applications that serve both internal SAP stakeholders and external customers. These applications play a critical role in delivering seamless user experiences and business functionality, requiring high availability, performance, and reliability.

We are looking for a passionate and skilled (Senior) Full-Stack Developer (f/m/d) to join our innovative team. In this role, you will play a key part in building intuitive, scalable, and high-performance web applications, using modern frontend technologies (e.g., Angular, JavaScript, SAP UI5) and integrating with backend services built in Java and Go. The ideal candidate is a strong team player who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is eager to contribute to products used by thousands of enterprise users worldwide.

You will do:

  • Develop SAP CFS services using cutting-edge technologies such as Angular, Node.js, Go, Java, Docker, Kubernetes, and other cloud technologies.
  • Work within an Agile team, applying Scrum methodologies and Agile development practices.
  • Act as a team player, contributing to team goals with flexibility and the ability to work across a range of topics.
  • Participate in code reviews, architecture discussions, and Agile ceremonies.
  • Leverage AI tools to support daily development work and build high-quality applications.

What you bring

  • B.Sc. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, with 3+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong experience with frontend technologies such as JavaScript/TypeScript, Angular, and UI frameworks (experience with SAP UI5 and end-to-end (E2E) testing frameworks is an advantage).
  • Strong backend development experience with object-oriented programming languages (Go / Java).
  • Hands-on experience with modern development tools (e.g., GitHub, Jenkins, JIRA) and familiarity with cloud/DevOps technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes - an advantage).
  • Fast learner, strong team player, and results-driven, with excellent communication and execution skills.
  • Experience in the SAP BTP domain - an advantage.

Where you belong:

At SAP CFS (Commercial Foundation Services), we deliver best-in-class end-to-end solutions that enable developers and Lines of Business (LOBs) to create, extend, and consume SAP applications on the SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) / Unified Services. We are responsible for developing and operating the core platform services of SAP BTP / Unified Services - services that all other components depend on.

Our unit provides foundational capabilities such as account and tenant management, provisioning, commercialization, automation, integration, and shared platform services, ensuring they are secure, scalable, and reliable for all BTP / Unified Services products and customers.

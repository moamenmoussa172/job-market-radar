Senior Product Manager, P2P & Local Transfers

Tabby · Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 30 October 2026

About the job

Tabby creates financial freedom in the way people shop, earn and save by reshaping their relationship with money. Over 25 million users choose Tabby to stay in control of their spending and make the most out of their money.
The company’s flagship offering allows shoppers to split their payments online and in-store with no interest or fees. Over 70,000 global brands and small businesses, including Amazon, Noon, IKEA, and SHEIN use Tabby to accelerate growth and gain loyal customers by offering easy and flexible payments online and in stores.
Tabby generates over $18 billion in annual transaction volume for its partner brands and is the highest-rated, most-reviewed, largest, and fastest-growing FinTech in the GCC region.
Tabby launched in 2019 and has since raised +$1 billion in equity and debt funding from global and regional investors, and is now valued at $6,5 billion.
About the role

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager located (or ready for relocation) in the UAE to lead the development and growth of our P2P and local transfer products in Tabby Cash. Since our vision is to become a financial service for daily use, we need a passionate, driven individual to make sending money to friends and family a daily habit for millions of Tabby customers.
You will lead a dedicated team focused only on P2P and local transfers and work cross-functionally with engineering, design, data, marketing, legal, compliance and our banking partners to deliver a seamless and scalable experience in the UAE and KSA.

  • Own the strategy and roadmap for money moving between people inside the country: P2P between wallets, local bank transfers and instant payments.
  • Launch new local payment rails and partner integrations end to end, from discovery and regulatory requirements to scale.
  • Invent creative, viral social money mechanics that people love to use and share: request money, split the bill, QR, payment links and sending to people who don't have a wallet yet.
  • Grow the network by turning millions of Tabby app users into active senders and recipients.
  • Work closely with legal, compliance, risk and core banking teams so products meet local regulations and security standards without killing innovation, because we are about balance.
  • Lead the launch of local transfers in KSA together with local partners.
  • Shape how the team works together with the Team Lead: planning, rituals and a predictable delivery cadence.
  • Set and track KPIs (senders, transfer frequency, recipients per user, retention) and run experiments to move them.
  • Keep an ear to the ground on market trends, user behaviour and best-in-class P2P products to keep us ahead of the game.
  • Plan launches with marketing and communications teams to ensure clear messaging and user adoption.
  • UAE residence or ready for relocation
  • 5+ years of experience as a Product Manager with hands-on expertise in P2P payments, wallets, instant payments or mobile banking
  • Proven track record of launching and growing consumer payment products with measurable results
  • Experience delivering products with banks, payment rails or other regulated partners
  • Resilience and ability to adapt to a rapidly changing environment
  • Fluency in English

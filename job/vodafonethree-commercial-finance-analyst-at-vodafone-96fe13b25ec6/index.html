What you’ll do

You’ll join Vodafone Business as a Commercial Finance Analyst supporting the Direct Sales team, where you’ll turn sales, customer and financial data into clear commercial insight. You’ll become a trusted finance partner to senior stakeholders, helping them understand what’s driving performance, where risks and opportunities are emerging and what actions will improve revenue, margin and long-term value. You’ll work across Finance, Sales and other commercial teams, giving you the opportunity to influence decisions, strengthen forecasting and help simplify how performance is managed. In this role you will:

  • Provide clear insight into Direct Sales performance across revenue, margin, sales volumes, pipeline, bookings, conversion, customer value, churn and other key commercial KPIs.
  • Translate sales activity and pipeline data into credible forecasts, challenging assumptions constructively and helping improve forecast accuracy and predictability.
  • Partner with Direct Sales and Finance leaders, presenting concise commercial insight that will support informed decisions and create clear accountability for actions.
  • Provide analysis for pricing, deal economics, customer and product decisions, channel investment and sales productivity, considering ROI and the wider P&L impact.
  • Identify emerging performance gaps, risks and opportunities, then work collaboratively with stakeholders to agree actions, owners and timelines.
  • Help maintain the accuracy and integrity of financial reporting, building a strong understanding of revenue, margin, unit economics, volumes and cost-to-sell drivers.
  • Collaborate across Sales, Commercial, Product, Marketing, FP&A, FinOps and Data & Analytics to create a consistent view of commercial and financial performance.
  • Help simplify, automate and standardise reporting and planning, improving data quality and reducing manual processes, including continued optimisation of IFRS 15 reporting.

Who you are

  • Vast experience in commercial finance, sales finance or FP&A, with the ability to influence commercial performance in a complex environment.
  • Showcase commercial and analytical thinking with the ability to turn sales, pipeline and customer data into straightforward financial insight and practical recommendations.
  • Demonstrable understanding of revenue, margin, pricing, deal economics, financial planning, forecasting and performance management.
  • You will be collaborative with the curiosity to ask questions, constructively challenge assumptions and help stakeholders reach better commercial decisions.
  • Showcase clear communication and have strong stakeholder management with stakeholders at different levels, adapting complex financial information into messages that will be easy to understand and act on.
  • You’ll bring strong attention to detail and will care about financial accuracy, effective controls and credible forecasts.
  • Skilled at working with multiple systems and data sources, extracting, analysing and presenting information in a meaningful and accessible way.
  • You’ll take a proactive and resilient approach, managing competing priorities calmly while keeping customers, sustainable results and long-term value at the heart of your decisions.

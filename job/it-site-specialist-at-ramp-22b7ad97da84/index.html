About the job

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the Role

We’re looking for an IT Site Specialist to join our IT Team at Ramp based in our NYHQ office! This is a contract to hire full-time on-site role that blends hands-on, in-person site support, including remote support for end users based out of smaller offices and hubs. You’ll be joining the IT Operations pod that owns desk-side support, onboarding/offboarding, and user and office technology. We’re looking for someone who takes pride in having a service mindset while delivering team oriented results.

What You’ll Do

  • Provide in-person support at our NYHQ 5 days/week and remote support for our offices.
  • Manage your IT ticket queue: triage incoming requests, set priorities, and keep response time fast without letting things fall through the cracks, while proactively seek ways to eliminate repeat requests through automation and process improvements
  • Support office technology, including printers, conference room AV, and company all hands events
  • Endpoint lifecycle: prep and facilitate provisioning of new hire laptops, fleet upgrades, retire end of life hardware, update asset records, and IT asset management
  • Reduce repeat work by writing and maintaining IT documentation and help articles, and by building smaller automations with guidance from the team
  • Take ownership of small and large projects, working with various internal/external teams to accomplish goals
  • Contribute to IT projects in partnership with People, Security, Engineering, and other teams, and look for practical ways to use AI to improve support and IT operations
  • Be an advocate for our users, understand their challenges, and look to remove blockers

What You Need

  • Minimum 3 years of hands-on IT support experience, ideally in a fast-paced or high-growth environment
  • Strong knowledge in our core tech stack including Okta, Google Workspace, Slack, JAMF, Intune, Zoom, and various modern SaaS tools
  • Experience with desk side support, ticket resolution, and knowing when to escalate to an engineering team
  • Some hands-on exposure to IT automation or workflow tools, and an eagerness to build more
  • Practical AV and conference room experience, including large event support
  • Demonstrated AI fluency: you use AI regularly in your work, have a clear point of view on where it helps and where it doesn’t, and are continuously raising your own bar
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical issues clearly to non-technical teammates
  • Empathic, humble, and posses a strong focus on elevating end user experience

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience in high-growth startups
  • Experience managing macOS/Windows in hybrid/remote environments.
  • Familiarity with lightweight scripting (Bash, Python, etc.) or exposure to APIs and integrations
  • Experience with AI-assisted support tooling and workflow automation (e.g., Serval or similar)
  • Experience coordinating hardware procurement with fulfillment vendors (e.g., Firstbase, SHI, CDW)
  • Google Workspace or Okta Administrator experience
  • Experience supporting onboarding and offboarding workflows alongside People Ops

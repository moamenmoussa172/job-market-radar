Overview

Instacart is looking for a Senior IT Support Specialist to join its IT Support team in Toronto or New York City. This person is the go-to technical partner for Instacart employees, resolving issues through the ticketing system and in person, and helping every teammate get back to work quickly and feel supported. This is a hybrid role with a set schedule for days in the office, working alongside a collaborative team of technicians, Corporate Engineering, and HR. It's a great opportunity to work with modern tooling, grow your skills, and have a real impact on the employee experience at Instacart.

About the Job

  • Resolve 70+ support tickets a week for Instacart employees through our ticketing system and in-person support, while keeping CSAT high.
  • Reach full independence within 90 days: self-resolve with minimal escalations, escalate efficiently when needed, and handle on-call responsibilities.
  • Support and administer core systems including Okta, Jira, Google Workspace, Slack, Jamf, Conductor One, and 1Password, and troubleshoot macOS, Windows, and ChromeOS.
  • Be a point of contact for your office, partnering with IT Support peers, Corporate Engineering, and HR.
  • Assist with onboarding new hires, including hardware deployment, equipment setup, and SaaS app access through our SSO tool.
  • Keep office AV equipment, including Zoom-enabled conference rooms, working properly.
  • Own or contribute to IT projects that help us maintain and scale our environment, and keep our knowledge base and documentation up to date.
  • Participate in a rotating on-call schedule for weekend P0 support.
  • Work a Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm local-time schedule, in the office 2 to 3 days a week as business needs require (currently 2).

About You

Minimum Qualifications

  • 3+ years of relevant IT support experience.
  • 3+ years supporting macOS, Windows, and ChromeOS.
  • 3+ years of hands-on experience with Google Workspace.
  • Hands-on administration experience with Okta, Jira, and Slack.
  • Working knowledge of AV systems, including Zoom.
  • Comfortable with a rotating weekend on-call schedule for P0 support.
  • Ability to occasionally lift 40 lbs.
  • Able to work in our Toronto office 2-3 days a week.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Hands-on experience with Jamf, Conductor One, and 1Password.
  • Experience at a tech company or startup of 1,000+ employees.
  • At least a year in a customer-facing role, such as Apple retail.
  • A track record of strong CSAT scores and high ticket volume.
  • You are inclined to Google things you don't know before asking someone.
  • You have a friendly personality and love to teach others.

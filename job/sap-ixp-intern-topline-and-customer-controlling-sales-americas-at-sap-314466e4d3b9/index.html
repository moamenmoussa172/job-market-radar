About the SAP Internship Experience Program

The SAP Internship Experience Program (iXp) is SAP’s global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

Three reasons to intern at SAP:

Culture of collaboration: meet with mentors, make new friends across the globe and create a thriving personal network.

Project-driven experience: gain cross-functional skills from our virtual and in-person learning sessions, diverse subject matter experts, and project deliverables.

Gain visibility: with SAP Internship Experience Program in your title, you’ll have a global network of SAP leaders, entrepreneurs and career development opportunities at your fingertips.

About the Team:

Organizationally located within Global Controlling, the Topline & Customer Controlling Sales (TCC) team is one trusted and reliable Controlling team that acts as a high-value business partner to SAP’s leaders worldwide. With more than 130 professionals across the globe, TCC Sales is an innovative and diverse organization that continuously strives for efficiency while offering a great place to explore, learn, and grow.

Topline & Customer Controlling (TCC) Sales Americas: The team provides operational support to Customer Success Business Leaders and CFOs across the Americas region. Focused on being Business Partners and Trusted Advisors to Sales leaders, we deliver financial insight and foresight that enables informed decision-making. Our insights and forward-looking guidance help steer the business toward SAP’s ambitious goals, through operational efficiency and a collaborative, high-performing team environment that allows our members to learn and grow.

What you’ll do:

Position title: SAP iXp Intern – Topline & Customer Controlling Sales Americas

Location: Sao Paulo, Brazil

As an intern in Topline & Customer Controlling Sales , you will:

Have the opportunity to gain deep insight into the Controlling function and its role as a strategic business partner within a global organization. You will support the full spectrum of controlling activities — from master data management to complex financial analysis, reporting, and process optimization — and directly contribute to SAP’s ability to make sound financial decisions that drive sustainable growth.

Your responsibilities will include:

· Support the team in recurring activities including month-end and quarter-end closing, forecasting, budgeting, and management reporting

· Assist with master data maintenance, validation, and clean-up

· Support ad hoc reporting and financial analysis requests from business stakeholders

· Work with SAP internal tools and platforms (including SAP Analytics Cloud and SAP S/4HANA) to extract, structure, and present data

· Contribute to the creation and maintenance of dashboards and standardized reports

· Document and publish process descriptions on SAP internal platforms

· Proactively communicate and interact with team members and internal stakeholders across functions and geographies

· Contribute to TCC Sales initiatives focused on simplification, standardization, and automation of key controlling processes

· Experiment with AI-powered tools to identify opportunities that enhance team productivity and streamline routine controlling tasks

· Support the preparation of presentations and management-level meetings

· Assist with other operational and project-based activities as needed

Who you are:

We’re looking for someone who takes initiative, perseveres, and stays curious — including a genuine interest in exploring how AI tools can make everyday work smarter and more efficient. You are energized by meaningful, innovative work and are committed to lifelong learning.

· Currently enrolled as a university student in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Accounting, Engineering or a comparable field

· Fluent in English, both written and verbal

· Proficient in Microsoft Office, with strong command of Excel and PowerPoint

· Exposure to or curiosity about data and analytics tools (e.g., SAP Analytics Cloud, Power BI, or similar) is a plus

· Eagerness to experiment with AI tools (such as SAP Joule, generative AI solutions, Claude Code) to improve productivity and the quality of financial analysis

· Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail

· Excellent organizational, verbal, and written communication skills

· Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial, and evolving environment

· Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team

· Quality- and results-oriented with a proactive and positive attitude

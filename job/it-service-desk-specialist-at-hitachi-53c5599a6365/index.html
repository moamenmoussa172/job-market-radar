Job description

Your impact

As an IT Service Desk Specialist at Hitachi, Ltd., you will provide essential first-line technical support to employees and help ensure smooth day-to-day business operations.

What you will do

  • Provide first-level technical support and troubleshooting for hardware, software, and network-related issues via phone, email, and chat.
  • Log, categorize, prioritize, and track support requests in the IT service management system.
  • Resolve common IT issues independently, including password resets, software installations, peripheral connectivity problems, and basic network troubleshooting.
  • Escalate complex issues to second-level support teams or subject matter experts, while ensuring timely follow-up with users.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation of solutions, procedures, and frequently asked questions in the knowledge base.
  • Support IT onboarding and offboarding activities, including account creation or deletion and equipment setup or retrieval.
  • Educate users on IT best practices and self-service options to promote efficient technology use.
  • Contribute to IT projects, system improvements, and upgrades as required.

What you bring

  • Associate's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field. A Bachelor's degree is preferred.
  • 1 to 3 years of experience in an IT service desk or technical support role.
  • Strong knowledge of Windows operating systems, Microsoft Office, and common business applications.
  • Familiarity with network fundamentals, including TCP/IP, DNS, and VPN.
  • Experience with IT service management tools such as ServiceNow or Jira Service Management.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical users.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a customer-focused mindset.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, collaborative team environment.

Nice to have

Relevant IT certifications, such as CompTIA A+ or ITIL Foundation, are an advantage.

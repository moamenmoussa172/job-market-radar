Group Product Manager - Core Products

Marqeta Inc · Remote

Fully remote · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 23 November 2026

Apply on Marqeta Inc's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

As Marqeta’s Group Product Manager, Core Products (API & Accounts), you will own the Marqeta API and the users, businesses and accounts that every Marqeta product is built on, setting the strategy that decides how fast customers launch and how confidently they scale. You will partner with engineering, architecture, GTM, card networks, sponsor banks and customers. This role typically owns API strategy, governance and versioning, SDKs and abstractions, and the lifecycle of users, businesses and accounts across card and non-card payments. It is an individual contributor role today, with the potential to build and lead a team of product managers as the business grows.

We work Flexible First. This role can be performed remotely. We’d love for you to join us!

The Impact You’ll Have

  • Own strategy and growth: define the multi-year vision and roadmap for the Marqeta API and for users, businesses and accounts, and own adoption, reliability and revenue goals. Turn trends like embedded finance, agentic payments and tokenization into differentiated product bets
  • Make the API a competitive advantage: set the quality bar and own governance (OpenAPI as the source of truth, standards enforced in CI, an API governance board) and company-wide policy for versioning, deprecation and breaking changes. Track consistency, spec accuracy, error handling, idempotency, performance and reliability through scorecards against best-in-class API platforms
  • Productize and monetize: decide what becomes a packaged, priced API product, SDK or higher-level abstraction, and own its adoption plan, pricing and lifecycle from beta to retirement. Turn network mandates and compliance requirements into supportable APIs delivered ahead of deadlines
  • Own users, businesses and accounts: design complete lifecycles (create, update, suspend, close, reactivate) and multi-account models, and evolve these services toward a domain-driven, event-driven architecture with engineering and architecture. Explore new product bets, such as data products built on card portfolio data, from customer validation through business case
  • Lead execution and influence at scale: guide multiple engineering squads from discovery through launch and adoption, equip GTM and support teams with clear positioning, and turn customer escalations into roadmap items. Use usage analytics, customer insight and financial models to make the case for investment to executives, and align teams across the company while mentoring PMs

Who You Are

  • 10+ years of product management experience, including 3+ years at a senior or group level owning a portfolio or multiple product lines with measurable business results
  • Proven experience owning an API platform or developer-facing product end to end, including API design, governance, versioning and deprecation, OpenAPI-based tooling, SDKs, abstractions and developer documentation
  • Deep understanding of payments and card issuing platforms and the full card lifecycle (issuance, provisioning, authorization, clearing, settlement, disputes), plus the identity and account models behind them, such as users, businesses, accounts and ledgers
  • Experience with regulated financial products, including KYC/KYB, sponsor bank models and card network requirements and mandates
  • Strong technical acumen: comfortable reviewing API contracts and data models, working through domain-driven design or event-storming sessions, and making architectural tradeoffs alongside engineers
  • Track record taking complex, ambiguous products from strategy to scale in an API-first, high-growth environment
  • Strong financial and analytical skills, with the ability to build business cases, shape pricing and present to executives
  • Excellent communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills with internal teams, customers and external partners, plus the ability to influence without authority and to simplify for customers

Nice-To-Haves

  • Experience with multi-account or ledger-based products, or with credit and prefunded program models
  • Experience setting up an API governance function or design review process
  • Experience shipping SDKs or higher-level abstraction layers over complex APIs
  • Familiarity with tokenization, digital wallets and dynamic credentials
  • Familiarity with network standards and regulatory frameworks (ISO 8583, EMV, PCI, KYC/AML, EU and UK payments mandates)
  • Experience validating and launching new products or data products with early customers

Compensation and Benefits

Marqeta calibrates pay to a competitive value according to working location. Compensation is aligned according to three tiers within the United States:

  • National: A baseline tier that applies to most of the geographic territory of the United States.
  • Premium: Slightly elevated from the National tier, and oriented toward a narrower set of higher cost-of-living areas, such as Los Angeles CA and Seattle WA
  • Premium Plus: A tier for the most expensive working areas, like the San Francisco Bay area and New York City.

Visit this page or consult with a recruiter to determine which tier would be applicable to you.

When determining salaries, we consider several factors including, but not limited to, skills, prior experience, and work location. The new-hire base salary range for this full-time position is:

  • National: 174,700 - 218,400
  • Premium: 189,000 - 236,300
  • Premium Plus: 205,500 - 256,900

We also believe in recognizing the contributions of our people. That's why we award annual bonuses to eligible employees, rewarding both individual performance and the success of the entire company.

Along with monetary compensation, Marqeta currently offers

  • Multiple health insurance options
  • Flexible time off – take what you need
  • Retirement savings program with company contribution and after tax contributions
  • Equity in a publicly-traded company and an Employee Stock Purchase Program
  • Family-forming benefits, fertility support and generous parental leave base salary top-up
  • Free therapy sessions, financial and professional coaching, and legal advice
  • Monthly stipend to support our remote work model
  • Annual “development dollars” to support our people growth and development

About Marqeta

Marqeta is on a mission to change the way money moves. We’re one of the earliest enablers of embedded finance, a market opportunity sized up in the trillions. Our card issuing platform provides unprecedented flexibility and control for companies to issue cards, authorize transactions, and manage payment operations in real time. Marqeta is powering the most well known brands in the new economy (Block, Cash App, Affirm, Instacart, Doordash, Uber, Walmart, etc). You don’t need to be a Payments expert to join the Marqeta Team, let us help you with that. This is the opportunity of a lifetime to work with innovators around the world and unlock equitable financial access for all.

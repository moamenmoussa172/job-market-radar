About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

This position can be filled in Garching, Walldorf, Berlin, Ratingen or Hamburg

Where you belong

You will join a high-performing, innovation-driven SAP AI architecture and AI consulting team at the intersection of enterprise SAP technology and artificial intelligence. Our team operates with a bold ambition: "Architect the Autonomous Enterprise, Develop and Deliver Today." We are a group of intellectually curious, outcome-obsessed SAP technologists who challenge conventional thinking, embrace complexity, and turn ambitious ideas into scalable, production-grade SAP solutions.

The preferred candidate will possess insatiable curiosity, unbounded creativity, and a growth mindset to navigate ambiguity and deliver clarity within complex SAP environments. Driven by a passion for mentoring and a deep desire to teach and learn, they will naturally translate business problems into elegant SAP-native architectures and solutions while actively giving and receiving feedback to maximize the team's collective knowledge and best practice development.

What you’ll build

a) Customer-Focused Outcomes

As a Principal SAP AI Consultant (f/m/d), you have the ability to deliver projects and services in a fast-paced environment when required, and you will be a key driver of customer value delivery, translating SAP AI architectural blueprints into fully functional, scalable, and production-ready solutions. Specifically, you will:

  • Lead enterprise-wide transformation programs and strategic initiatives that enable customers on their journey toward the Autonomous Enterprise
  • Implement AI solutions within SAP systems to enhance business processes and decision-making
  • Identify high-value use cases and design AI solutions that deliver proven business outcomes
  • Delivery AI Service of SAPs Success Plans to the customer
  • Translate business requirements into technical solutions through workshops, prototyping, and iterative delivery
  • Collaborate with customers across the delivery lifecycle (workshops, reviews, UAT) to refine and optimize solutions
  • Design and deploy scalable, secure, and high-performing AI powered applications on SAP Business AI Platform
  • Utilize SAP BTP services such as SAP Generative AI Hub, SAP AI Core, SAP AI Business Services, SAP Integration Suite etc. to develop innovative solutions.
  • Drive cross-functional alignment across SAP, customers, and ecosystem partners to maximize business outcomes

b) Functional Contributor for the Team

Beyond individual delivery, you will play an important role as a technical, strategic, and organizational leader within the team. Specifically, you will:

  • Mentor and support consultants through knowledge sharing and technical coaching
  • Establish coaching frameworks and knowledge-sharing mechanisms that accelerate delivery excellence
  • Contribute to pre-sales by developing and presenting PoCs and technical demos showcasing SAP AI capabilities
  • Support Agile delivery processes (e.g., sprint planning, backlog refinement, estimation) with strong technical input
  • Stay current with SAP AI and broader AI ecosystem trends, driving continuous innovation within the team

What you bring

a) Education & Track Record

  • A Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Information Systems, or a related field and 10+ years in principal technical customer-facing roles
  • Proven track record in consulting or customer-facing roles, including SAP BTP and/or AI/ML engineering in enterprise SAP environments
  • Proven track record of delivering SAP AI solutions across full project lifecycles
  • Demonstrated success leading enterprise-wide transformation initiatives with measurable business impact
  • Professional fluency in German
  • Professional fluency in English

b) Core Architecture & Engineering Skills

  • Proven experience as a Lead Architect, Principal Architect, Chief Architect, or Enterprise Architect with strong expertise in AI, ML and GenAI
  • Strong understanding of SAP architecture principles and proven ability to design robust integration architectures
  • Hands-on experience with Joule and SAP Generative AI capabilities
  • Hands-on experience in SAP BTP development
  • Proficiency in SAP CAP for building scalable, cloud-native applications and AI-enabled services
  • Understanding of S/4HANA extensibility patterns (side-by-side, in-app, key-user) aligned with Clean Core principles
  • Solid API development skills (REST, OpenAPI, versioning) and SAP API Management

c) SAP AI & Agentic AI Skills

  • SAP AI, Agentic AI & Generative AI Expertise
  • Hands-on experience with SAP AI Foundation, including model deployment, serving, and workflow orchestration
  • Experience with SAP Generative AI Hub for managing LLM providers (e.g., Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Vertex AI)
  • Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems
  • Strong knowledge of agentic AI frameworks (e.g., LangChain, LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI)
  • Experience designing multi-agent architectures (task orchestration, state management, delegation, error handling)
  • Ability to build conversational AI systems with context management, memory, and multi-turn interactions
  • Familiarity with SAP Joule integration and extending it with custom skills and agents
  • Generative AI & LLM Engineering
  • Strong prompt engineering skills (e.g., few-shot learning, prompt chaining, context optimization)
  • Hands-on experience with RAG architectures (chunking, embeddings, vector search, retrieval strategies)

c) Data & Integration Skills

  • Proficiency in SAP BDC, including data product design, data integration, and use of Datasphere data in AI pipelines
  • Experience with SAP Integration Suite (e.g., Cloud Integration, API Management, Event Mesh) to connect AI services with SAP processes
  • Proficiency in SAP Cloud Connector and Destination Service for secure connectivity between BTP and backend systems

e) Soft Skills, Project Management Skills & Leadership Skills

  • Exceptional executive communication and stakeholder management skills, including engagement at C-level and board level
  • Ability to explain complex SAP AI concepts to both business and technical stakeholders
  • Strong ownership mindset, ensuring quality, reliability, and timely delivery
  • Analytical and structured problem-solving approach
  • Adaptability and continuous learning mindset within evolving SAP AI landscapes
  • Solid understanding of Agile and SAP Activate methodologies
  • Strong mentoring and knowledge-sharing mindset, supporting team growth
  • Customer-focused approach, aligning development with business outcomes

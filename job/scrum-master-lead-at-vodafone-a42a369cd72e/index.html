Role Purpose

Enable cross-functional squads to deliver business outcomes effectively by embedding Agile and Scrum ways of working, facilitating collaboration, removing impediments and driving continuous improvement. The Scrum Master acts as a servant leader and Agile coach, helping the squad become increasingly self-organizing, accountable and predictable

Role Responsibilities

Ensure effective application of Scrum and Agile principles and maintain a consistent delivery rhythm across assigned squads.

Facilitate Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review and Retrospectives, ensuring ceremonies are focused, time-boxed and outcome-driven

Create an environment that enables the squad to self-organize, collaborate effectively and take accountability for delivery.

Proactively identify, track and remove blockers affecting squad progress, escalating systemic impediments where necessary.

Partner with Product Owners on backlog readiness, prioritization, sprint goals and clarity of expected outcomes.

Maintain visibility of sprint commitments, dependencies, risks, impediments and progress using Jira and/or Azure DevOps.

Facilitate resolution of cross-team dependencies and coordinate with other squads and enabling functions.

Coach squad members and stakeholders on Agile mindset, Scrum practices, accountability and continuous improvement.

Use retrospectives and delivery insights to identify improvement opportunities and ensure agreed actions are implemented

Communicate delivery progress, risks, dependencies and decisions transparently without creating unnecessary governance overhead.

Role Qualifications

  • 5+ years of relevant experience in Agile delivery, Scrum Master, project delivery or similar roles.
  • Hands-on experience working with cross-functional Agile squads in Technology, Digital, Telecom or transformation environments.
  • Strong practical understanding of Scrum, Agile principles, iterative delivery and servant leadership.
  • Working experience with Jira and/or Azure DevOps for backlog, sprint, dependency and delivery tracking.
  • Demonstrated capability in facilitation, coaching, stakeholder management and conflict resolution.
  • Experience managing delivery dependencies, risks, blockers and escalation paths.
  • PSM I, CSM or an equivalent Agile/Scrum certification is preferred.
  • A bachelor degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science or a related discipline is preferred.

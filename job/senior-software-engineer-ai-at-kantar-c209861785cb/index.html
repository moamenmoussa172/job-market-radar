Job Details

Why this job is important

You’ll join our Consumer Twins division, where we’re building scalable, AI-powered systems that simulate real consumer behaviour.

This isn’t a traditional full-stack engineering role. You’ll help create the distributed systems, event-driven architecture and AI platforms that allow thousands of AI-powered “digital twins” to operate concurrently. These systems will enable clients to test ideas, explore scenarios and receive meaningful insights in real time.

Reporting to the Director of Engineering, you’ll work closely with our product, data science and platform teams to turn emerging AI capabilities into reliable, production-grade products.

What you’ll be doing

Platform and backend engineering

  • Design and build scalable, distributed backend systems for AI-powered applications.
  • Develop production services using Node.js, TypeScript and/or Python.
  • Build event-driven services using Apache Kafka or comparable streaming technology.
  • Design REST and GraphQL application programming interfaces that support high-throughput, real-time workloads.
  • Create resilient systems with appropriate retry, idempotency and fault-tolerance patterns.
  • Build systems that can scale horizontally as workloads increase.
  • Improve the performance, maintainability and scalability of existing services.

AI systems and agent orchestration

  • Build systems that orchestrate thousands of AI agents or digital twins concurrently.
  • Integrate services with large language model providers such as OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex AI and Anthropic.
  • Develop Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines that connect AI models with relevant data and knowledge.
  • Implement prompt orchestration, prompt chaining and agent workflows.
  • Enable AI agents to use tools, services and data securely.
  • Optimise AI workloads for latency, throughput, reliability and cost.
  • Help turn AI prototypes into dependable, client-facing products.
  • Contribute to the responsible development and use of AI systems.

Data and event processing

  • Design and operate event-driven data pipelines.
  • Build services that process and respond to high-volume event streams.
  • Ingest and process large behavioural datasets.
  • Work with relational databases such as PostgreSQL.
  • Integrate services with document and NoSQL databases.
  • Work with vector databases and semantic search technologies where appropriate.
  • Support low-latency, high-throughput delivery of client insights.

Cloud and infrastructure

  • Build and deploy services using cloud-native architectures across Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform or Amazon Web Services.
  • Package applications using Docker.
  • Deploy and operate services through Kubernetes or serverless container platforms.
  • Contribute to infrastructure-as-code using Terraform.
  • Design services for resilience, observability and horizontal scalability.
  • Work with our platform teams to improve deployment and operational processes.

Engineering quality and collaboration

  • Write clean, maintainable and testable code.
  • Participate in technical design discussions and architectural decisions.
  • Review code and share practical engineering feedback.
  • Mentor other engineers and contribute to an inclusive engineering culture.
  • Collaborate with product, data science and platform colleagues to deliver end-to-end features.
  • Break down ambiguous technical problems and identify practical solutions.
  • Explore relevant developments in backend, cloud and AI engineering, recommending their use when they offer clear value.

The ideal skills & experience

You’ll bring strong backend engineering experience, together with an interest in building scalable systems for the next generation of AI-powered products.

Core experience

  • Experience designing and building distributed backend systems.
  • Production development experience with Node.js and TypeScript and/or Python.
  • Practical experience with Apache Kafka or another streaming or messaging platform.
  • Understanding of event-driven architecture and asynchronous processing.
  • Experience designing REST or GraphQL application programming interfaces.
  • Experience building systems for high-throughput or real-time workloads.
  • Understanding of concurrency, parallel processing and distributed workloads.
  • Knowledge of backpressure, rate limiting, retries, idempotency and fault tolerance.
  • Experience working with relational and/or NoSQL data stores.
  • Experience deploying software to Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform or Amazon Web Services.
  • Familiarity with Docker and container orchestration.
  • A track record of writing maintainable, well-tested production code.

How you’ll work

  • You can break complex or unclear problems into manageable technical decisions.
  • You use evidence and data to guide engineering choices.
  • You communicate technical ideas clearly to colleagues across engineering, product and data science.
  • You enjoy contributing to architectural discussions and challenging ideas constructively.
  • You’re comfortable collaborating across teams to deliver complete products, rather than isolated technical components.
  • You support other engineers through mentoring, code reviews and knowledge sharing.
  • You’re curious about AI engineering and motivated to develop your skills in this area.

You don’t need to meet every requirement. We’re interested in your core engineering experience, how you approach complex problems and your willingness to learn.

Experience that could help you succeed

These aren’t essential. We can support you in developing these skills as you grow into the role:

  • Python for backend services, scripting or data processing.
  • Experience integrating products with large language model application programming interfaces.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines.
  • Prompt design, orchestration and evaluation.
  • Agent workflows and tool use.
  • Vector databases such as pgvector, Pinecone or Weaviate.
  • Embeddings, semantic search and vector retrieval.
  • Knowledge graphs or graph databases such as Neo4j.
  • Ontologies and graph query languages.
  • AI development frameworks such as LangChain or LlamaIndex.
  • Data pipelines or Extract, Transform and Load processing for model-driven applications.
  • Testing tools such as PyTest or Playwright.
  • Performance profiling and optimisation for backend systems.
  • AI safety, governance and data privacy principles.
  • Experience prototyping AI agents, chatbots or recommendation systems.

What’s in it for you

  • Build products at the intersection of advanced software engineering, AI and human understanding.
  • Work on complex distributed systems with genuine scale, concurrency and performance challenges.
  • Develop practical AI engineering experience while building on your existing backend expertise.
  • Work with Kantar’s global human and synthetic datasets to create new forms of decision-ready intelligence.
  • Collaborate with software engineers, machine learning specialists, data scientists, product professionals and behavioural scientists.
  • Help shape technical architecture and engineering practices in a growing AI product division.
  • Join an environment where learning, mentoring and knowledge sharing are part of the role.
  • Contribute to technology that will change how brands explore ideas, understand audiences and make decisions.

Please be aware, the majority of our roles are hybrid, working three days a week in our office.

We’re not able to offer visa sponsorship or help with relocation support for this role. Please make sure you’ve got the right to work in the country where this role is located before applying.

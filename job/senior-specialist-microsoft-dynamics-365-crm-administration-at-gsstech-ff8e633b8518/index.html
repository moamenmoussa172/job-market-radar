About the job

Description

GSS Tech Group is hiring a Senior Specialist, Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM Administration, for a leading government financial institution in Abu Dhabi. You will own the administration, deployment and reliability of production Dynamics 365 CRM environments supporting customer service and complaint management operations.

This is a platform ownership role, not a development or L1 support role. We are looking for someone who has run production CRM environments, applied updates and handled incidents end to end.

Key responsibilities

  • Administer Dynamics 365 CRM environments (online and on premises): environment setup, user and licence management, security roles, business unit hierarchies and system configuration
  • Configure and maintain the Customer Service and Complaint Management modules, including entity relationships, data models and security roles
  • Manage the solution lifecycle (managed and unmanaged solutions, import and export) together with the CRM development team
  • Plan, execute and verify deployments, upgrades, cumulative updates and hotfixes across sandbox and production environments, including regression testing and rollback planning
  • Run migrations and environment refreshes with minimal business disruption
  • Design, configure and maintain workflows, process flows, business rules and approvals in response to business change requests
  • Monitor workflow health, error queues, system performance, availability and logs, and resolve issues proactively
  • Take part in incident management and on call rotation for high priority CRM issues
  • Support high availability and disaster recovery, including failover testing and DR drills
  • Maintain runbooks, SOPs, environment documentation and deployment notes
  • Coordinate with Microsoft, partners and vendors on administration matters

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems or a related field
  • Minimum 5 to 7 years of hands on experience administering Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM / Customer Engagement in production
  • Proven experience with deployment, patch management (cumulative updates and hotfixes) and environment management for Dynamics 365 CRM
  • Strong experience configuring and maintaining workflows, process flows, security roles and data management
  • Hands on experience with Dynamics 365 Customer Service; Complaint or Case Management experience is highly desirable
  • On premises Dynamics CRM experience (servers, SQL Server, Active Directory) is highly desirable
  • Experience with monitoring, incident management and HA/DR practices for enterprise applications
  • Working knowledge of REST/SOAP APIs and integrations supporting the CRM platform
  • Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Administrator or Application Administrator, or equivalent
  • Experience with Azure services (Logic Apps, Functions, Azure DevOps) and Power Platform (Power Automate, Power BI) is an advantage
  • Experience in banking, financial services or a government or regulatory environment is strongly preferred
  • Excellent written and spoken English

Please note: this role requires dedicated Dynamics 365 CRM / Customer Engagement administration experience. Profiles focused purely on development, Power Platform app building, or Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations will not be considered.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to own a business critical CRM platform for a leading government financial institution
  • Competitive monthly compensation
  • UAE employment visa and medical insurance
  • Long term engagement with growth opportunities through GSS Tech Group

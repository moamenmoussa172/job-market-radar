About the job

We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

At Motorola Solutions, everything starts with safety, and our unwavering commitment to "Solving for safer" drives every line of code we ship. Our International Mobile Applications organization builds mission-critical software solutions that help emergency first responders across public safety agencies worldwide perform at their best in the moments that matter.
Based in the West End of Glasgow, our Pronto team is the largest supplier of mobile police applications in the UK, providing software to more than half of the UK’s 45 police forces and supporting around 70,000 police officers every day. Originally founded as Kelvin Connect—a spin-out from the University of Glasgow—our 80-person Glasgow hub blends the agility and creative energy of a fast-moving product engineering studio with the global scale, resources, and stability of Motorola Solutions.


Job Description

We are looking for a deeply technical, product-minded engineering leader to guide and scale our core Pronto engineering teams in Glasgow. In this role, you will bridge the gap between product vision and hands-on technical execution—leading teams of roughly 8+ engineers (or mentoring existing team leads) to architect, build, and evolve our next-generation mobile, gateway, and cloud-hosted policing platforms.

You will thrive here if you bring a "product builder" mentality rather than a legacy maintenance mindset. While you won't be committing code every day, you remain close enough to the architecture to lead design reviews, debate system trade-offs, and earn the technical respect of seasoned engineers. We value equivalent practical experience gained through building complex, high-availability software products just as highly as formal academic pathways. Guided by our Employee Value Proposition—working in a people-first culture, innovating what's next, and pursuing your passion and potential—you will foster an inclusive, psychologically safe environment where engineers are empowered to experiment, challenge the status quo, and ship life-saving software.

Key Responsibilities

Technical Strategy & Architectural Oversight

  • Partner with Product Management and Principal Engineers to define technical roadmaps, lead system design reviews, and guide scalable architectural decisions across mobile, server, and cloud platforms
  • Champion modern software engineering practices, code quality, automated testing, and continuous delivery to accelerate product innovation across UK police deployments
  • Dive into complex technical challenges, performance bottlenecks, or incident reviews when needed to help the team unblock critical deliverables

People Leadership & Culture Building

  • Mentor, coach, and support engineers and emerging team leads across all career stages through regular one-on-ones, structured career mapping, and constructive performance feedback
  • Cultivate an inclusive, high-trust engineering culture built on psychological safety, continuous learning, and blame-free post-mortems
  • Partner with Talent Acquisition to attract, assess, and onboard high-caliber software engineers who add fresh perspective to our Glasgow engineering hub

Agile Delivery & Cross-Functional Execution

  • Own the "how" and "when" of software delivery across Agile/Scrum squads, balancing new feature velocity with platform resilience and technical debt reduction
  • Collaborate closely with our Hosted Applications and Deployment teams to help provide seamless integration and reliable rollouts for frontline police forces
  • Use engineering metrics and team health insight


Basic Requirements

  • Must be able to attain Non-Police-Personnel-Vetting Level 3 (NPPV3), which requires a minimum of 3 years continuous UK residency
  • Extensive commercial experience in full-stack software engineering, including architecting and delivering production-grade distributed systems
  • Proven track record of leading, mentoring, or managing agile software engineering teams while retaining strong hands-on technical credibility

Technical Expertise

  • Java and/or Kotlin to architect, review, and guide high-performing backend services and modern mobile applications
  • Client/Server & API Architecture to design resilient server/gateway applications, RESTful APIs, and secure data communications
  • Full-Stack & Mobile Foundations to oversee end-to-end product delivery across modern web frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue), relational databases (MSSQL/SQL), and smartphone platforms (Android/iOS)
  • CI/CD & Modern DevOps to champion automated build, testing, and deployment pipelines (such as GitHub Actions and Gradle)
  • English Fluency with an excellent command of written and spoken English to lead technical discussions, stakeholder reviews, and cross-functional planning

Professional Experience

  • Product-Driven Engineering Leadership to drive greenfield feature development and architectural modernization rather than passive legacy maintenance
  • Agile & Scrum Execution to guide cross-functional squads, optimize sprint workflows, and remove technical or organizational blockers
  • Software Design Patterns & System Scalability to evaluate algorithmic efficiency, microservice trade-offs, and high-availability requirements for mission-critical environments
  • Collaborative Stakeholder Management to align engineering execution with Product Managers, Deployment leads, and external public safety requirements

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computing Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical discipline
  • Hands-on experience with Spring Boot, AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, or Kotlin Multiplatform
  • Familiarity with distributed event streaming platforms (Apache Kafka, Azure Event Hubs) and modern identity/security protocols (OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, JWT)
  • Experience working with legacy enterprise technologies (Struts, Google Web Toolkit (GWT), XML/XSLT, Saxon, JAXB) to support ongoing platform modernization

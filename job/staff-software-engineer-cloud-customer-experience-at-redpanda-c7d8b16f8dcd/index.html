About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Redpanda is the first runtime and control plane for agent-data interaction — a unified platform that combines streaming, SQL analytics, and intelligent connectivity with the governance layer enterprise AI agents need in production.

Built on infrastructure already trusted by Fortune 500 companies and fast-growing startups, Redpanda enforces governance entirely outside the agent's data path, controlling what agents see, limiting what they do, and capturing a tamper-proof record of everything they touch, so organizations never have to choose between innovation and control.

About the Role:

We're looking for an experienced staff-level engineer to set the technical direction for a new Warsaw-based team that owns Redpanda Cloud's customer-facing journeys, including sign-up and authentication, account management, usage-based billing, payments, and cloud-marketplace integrations. You'll lead the architecture across these workstreams, take ownership of the systems that exist today, stabilize and deepen them, and then drive the product and technical roadmap that makes every customer interaction reliable, transparent, and trustworthy. You'll maintain a high bar for software in customer-facing systems, and partner across engineering, design, product, finance, and pre-/post-sales.

The Team:

You'll join a newly-formed small, cross-functional Cloud team based in our new Warsaw office that owns the services powering the customer-facing journeys at the heart of Redpanda Cloud. This is how every customer registers, manages their account, and pays for what they use. The team owns these critical workstreams:

  • Register / Sign-up / Sign-in
  • Account management
  • Usage-based billing (UBB) via Metronome
  • Credit-card management for self-service customers via Stripe
  • Cloud marketplace integrations (AWS, GCP, and Azure with more to come) for UBB, public offers, and private offers

The team is adopting ownership of these areas from the teams that built them, will come up to speed on what we have today, and then drive a product and technical roadmap that gives our customers confidence whenever they interact with our services. It's cross-functional and works closely with design, product, and finance. We hire for the skill mix across the team, not every skill in every person.

Everybody at Redpanda is reinventing how they work using the latest AI tooling and workflows.

This is a hybrid role based in our central Warsaw office, with a minimum of 3 days a week on-site.

You Will:

  • Lead the technical direction across the sign-up, marketplace, billing, and payments journeys on AWS, GCP, and Azure
  • Own the architecture for correctness-critical billing, metering, and payment flows, ensuring idempotency, reconciliation, and auditability
  • Drive multi-quarter product and technical roadmaps in partnership with product management, finance, and commercial stakeholders. Turn ambiguous product and commercial requirements into clear designs and executable workstreams
  • Lead the adoption of existing systems from other teams, assessing, stabilising, and planning evolution
  • Anticipate technical risk across the team's surface and proactively mitigate it
  • Set and uphold high standards for reliability, security, and privacy of customer PII and payment data
  • Raise the engineering bar, mentor the team, improve practices, and champion initiatives that lift operational excellence
  • Participate in the on-call escalation chain, reduce escalations and improve operational health

You Have:

  • Significant experience designing and operating large-scale, reliable systems in public cloud environments, and leading large technical projects end-to-end from design through execution and into production
  • Strong proficiency in Go (or comparable systems language with willingness to ramp)
  • A track record of leading large technical projects end-to-end, from design and planning, to execution and production operations
  • Enough frontend familiarity to own customer-facing UIs with design support (not a specialist)
  • Production experience using AI-assisted software development workflows (e.g. Claude Code, Codex, etc) to accelerate prototyping, delivery, and operations
  • Excellent communication; effective in a globally distributed, async environment; strong collaboration with product, design, finance, and customer-facing stakeholders

Strong Plus:

  • Depth in one or more of: Usage-based billing & metering (Metronome or similar), cloud marketplace integration (AWS/GCP/Azure marketplaces for UBB, public, and private offers), payments / Stripe, identity & auth
  • Building or operating SaaS billing/account platforms at scale

Nice to Have:

  • React / TypeScript for customer-facing UIs
  • Experience modernising inherited/legacy systems
  • Redpanda / streaming platforms as a user

This is a B2B contract role in Warsaw. The rate is 417,000–500,000 PLN per year (net + VAT). Your final rate will depend on your skills, experience, and interview performance. Your recruiter can share more details about the full package.

Join Redpanda if you’d enjoy being part of a fast-moving, diverse, people-first organization with team members around the globe and a culture based on trust, transparency, communication, and kindness. You'll dive into a nimble, high-impact team with the latest AI tools — and the budget to actually use them.

About the job

About the role
We are building the software foundation for our autonomous-driving work, and we are hiring the engineer who will start it. Today we collect street-level imagery and GPS tracks from vehicle cameras, including our own camera units with NVIDIA edge devices on board, and use them to update maps. Next comes a dedicated survey vehicle with a full sensor set. You will start with the imagery and map data systems we run today. As the sensor platform arrives, you will design and build the systems that turn its data into training datasets for autonomous-driving models.

  • Design and build the systems that move multi-sensor data (multi-camera video, IMU, LiDAR, GPS) from vehicles into cloud storage, including how the data is stored and organized.
  • Implement spatial algorithms to process and geo-reference vehicle data.
  • Build validation and monitoring for incoming vehicle data.
  • Build tools and services that prepare training datasets for autonomous-driving models.
  • Take over and develop the current imagery and GPS-track systems from vehicle cameras, and contribute to map data pipelines.
  • Write production-quality code: tests, code review, CI/CD, clear design docs.
  • 4+ years in software engineering, with experience in data-intensive or geospatial systems.
  • Experience with multi-sensor data from vehicles or robots: cameras, IMU or LiDAR, and keeping the streams in sync.
  • Large volumes of binary or media data in object storage (S3 or GCS); distributed processing (Spark or equivalent).
  • Experience implementing spatial algorithms.
  • Strong Python and SQL; solid software design and testing practices; AWS or GCP, Docker, Kubernetes.

Nice to have

  • Experience preparing training datasets for computer vision or autonomous-driving models.
  • Sensor data formats such as ROS2 bag or MCAP, and point cloud data.
  • Parquet, Delta or Iceberg;
  • Databricks.
  • Argo Workflows or Airflow.
  • OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps.
  • BigQuery or another cloud data warehouse.
  • QGIS.

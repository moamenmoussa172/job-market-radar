Your Responsibilities

As a Display Systems Engineer for John Deere's Construction & Forestry (C&F) division in Dubuque, IA you will:

  • Define and document display system requirements, user workflows, and functional specifications to support customer and product needs
  • Develop and maintain subsystem requirements, interface definitions, and technical documentation for vehicle display systems
  • Coordinate integration of displays with controllers, sensors, diagnostics, telematics, and other machine systems
  • Support system verification and validation activities, including test planning, issue investigation, and requirements traceability
  • Collaborate with software, product design, vehicle, and validation teams to deliver integrated display solutions

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

  • Understanding of systems engineering principles, including requirements development, requirements traceability, and system validation
  • Ability to define user workflows and functional requirements with a focus on operator experience and machine usability
  • Knowledge of vehicle communication networks and diagnostics, including CAN/J1939 protocols
  • Demonstrated problem-solving skills to investigate issues, evaluate alternatives, and support system integration activities
  • Effective communication and collaboration skills to work across software, vehicle, controls, validation, and product engineering teams
  • Ability and willingness to travel domestically and internationally, up to 10%

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Hands-on experience defining user workflows, interfaces, or system requirements through internships, design projects, or engineering teams
  • Demonstrated understanding of human-machine interaction and a passion for improving operator experience through intuitive display designs
  • Experience working with vehicle electronics, controllers, sensors, diagnostics, or CAN-based communication networks
  • Experience with embedded Linux, Qt/QML, graphics frameworks, or display application development
  • CAN, USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth communications experience
  • Ability to bridge technical and non-technical perspectives by effectively collaborating across software, vehicle, validation, and product teams
  • Experience with technology solutions used in the construction industry, such as grade control and GNSS, telematics, etc.
  • Familiarity with operating Construction, Forestry, or Ag off-road equipment

Education

Ideally you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

• Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or Electrical Engineering

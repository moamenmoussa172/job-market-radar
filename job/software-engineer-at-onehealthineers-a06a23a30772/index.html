About the job

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.
Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

In this role, you will contribute to the development and integration of X-ray image acquisition systems for our radiation therapy linear accelerators. You will design, implement, test, maintain, and enhance software solutions that support reliable and high-quality imaging in a highly technical medical environment.

Core Responsibilities

  • Development of real-time software for x-ray imaging in C++ running on a Intel CPU under VxWorks and real-time Linux
  • Integration of hardware modules for image acquisition and processing
  • Integration of x-ray imaging into medical products
  • Development of application software in C# for service application and production test system
  • Development of automated test software
  • In-house integration testing of image acquisition systems on real linear accelerators

Desired Qualifications

  • Engineering degree from a technical institute or university with experience in software development
  • At least 3 years of experience with C++, preferably C++17 or newer
  • Experiences in C#/.NET is an advantage
  • Experiences with real-time operating systems, such as VxWorks or Linux kernel, is desirable
  • Knowledge in electronics is a plus
  • Fluency in English is required, German language skills are considered an advantage.
  • Forward thinking mindset and a structured way of working with an emphasis on getting things done

You are analytical, communicative and focused. You work structured and have an eye for details. In addition, you can express your ideas and research in written form. You are used to working independently, open to discussing ideas and solutions with your colleagues. You thrive in an international work environment and in cultivating your contacts with partners in Europe and overseas.

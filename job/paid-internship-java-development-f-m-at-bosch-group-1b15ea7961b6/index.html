About the job

Kick-start your career in software development with Bosch!

Would you like to turn your university knowledge into hands-on experience and see how software is developed in a global organization? Join our international development team and contribute to one of Bosch’s core internal applications.

During your internship, you’ll work alongside experienced developers, architects, and process experts in an Agile environment. You’ll have the opportunity to contribute to real development tasks, learn the entire software development lifecycle, and see your work become part of solutions used within Bosch.

Your tasks

  • Maintain, improve, and extend the existing software stack by developing new functionalities.
  • Participate in the end-to-end software development lifecycle, from understanding requirements through design and implementation to testing and deployment.
  • Collaborate with developers, process experts, architects, and technical experts responsible for connected systems.
  • Take part in code reviews and learn software development best practices from experienced team members.
  • Contribute to writing clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
  • Work as part of an international and virtual development team using Agile methodologies.

We are looking for you if you:

  • are currently a 2nd–4th year student in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or another IT-related field.
  • are available for approximately 30 hours per week.
  • have practical knowledge of Java, including technologies such as Spring Boot, Hibernate, web services, REST, and APIs.
  • have basic knowledge of Agile methodologies, such as Scrum or Kanban.
  • are familiar with unit testing and tools such as JUnit, JUnit Jupiter, and Mockito.
  • have a basic understanding of Git and Git Flow.
  • are familiar with software development principles and practices such as SOLID, Clean Code, and Test-Driven Development (TDD).
  • are proactive, motivated to learn, and comfortable taking initiative.
  • enjoy collaborating with different experts in a virtual, international team.
  • have a good command both of Polish and English.
  • approach problems with an end-to-end mindset and are curious about how different parts of a solution work together.

Show us what you’ve built!

We’d love to see your code! Please include a link to your GitHub profile in your CV. It will help us better understand your coding approach, technical interests, and practical experience.

Don't have GitHub? No problem. You can also share a GitLab or Bitbucket repository, portfolio, personal website, or another example of your development projects.

Bosch’s culture of innovation and digital transformation offers you a fantastic platform to grow your skills and enhance your network. We are dedicated to building a warm, open, transparent, and inclusive work environment for all.

Benefits:

  • Paid internship based on a civil-law contract
  • Hybrid work mode with the office in Warsaw
  • Flexible working hours
  • Gaining practical knowledge in the international company
  • Substantive support from best experts
  • Prepaid Lunch card
  • Special conditions for the MultiSport Card

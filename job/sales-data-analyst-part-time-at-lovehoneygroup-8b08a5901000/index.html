Join us at Lovehoney, where pleasure combines with creativity, data, and innovation!

We are currently looking for a part-time Sales Data Analyst to join our Sales team and transform commercial data into actionable business intelligence. In this role, you will play a key part in sales reporting, performance analysis, forecasting, and opportunity tracking, enabling data-driven decision-making across the organization. Reporting directly to the Director, B2B Sales & Marketing (Americas) and collaborating closely with Account Managers, Sales Operations, Finance, and other key stakeholders, you will deliver analytical insights that drive revenue growth, improve forecast accuracy, and identify opportunities to optimize sales performance.

This role is offered on a part-time basis, with an expected commitment of 20 hours per week.

What you will do

  • Prepare and maintain weekly and monthly sales reports, tracking revenue YTD, actuals versus budget and forecast, and year-over-year performance.
  • Analyze sales performance by customer, region, brand, product, and SKU to highlight growth, declines, trends, and exceptions.
  • Produce customer-level performance summaries, including top and underperforming products, sales volumes, and opportunities for follow-up.
  • Support sales forecasts by consolidating Account Manager inputs, validating assumptions, and identifying gaps or changes versus plan.
  • Maintain new business and listing opportunity trackers, including expected launch timing, SKU assortment, estimated volumes, annualized value, and dependencies.
  • Help quantify the current-year and future-year revenue potential of new listings, distribution expansion, and customer initiatives.
  • Build and update Superset dashboards, NetSuite reports, Excel models, and standardized reporting templates that reduce manual work and improve consistency.
  • Monitor order, inventory, and fulfillment data where available to flag out-of-stock risks, delayed orders, and potential revenue impact.
  • Assist in preparing customer business reviews, sales presentations, and management updates with clear data visualizations and commentary.
  • Reconcile data across NetSuite, Superset, CRM records, and other sales sources; investigate discrepancies and maintain reliable reporting definitions.
  • Collaborate with Account Managers, Sales Operations, Finance, and other relevant teams to obtain data and clarify performance drivers.
  • Use AI-enabled tools responsibly to streamline recurring analysis, automate routine reporting tasks, and improve the speed and quality of commercial insights.
  • Maintain CRM pipeline data quality and work with Account Managers to track opportunities, deal stages, next steps, forecast timing, and key dependencies.
  • Help evaluate and improve CRM reporting and AI-supported workflows for sales forecasting, opportunity tracking, and account management.
  • What you should bring
  • Proven experience in Sales Analytics, Commercial Finance, Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, or a similar analytical role; B2B or consumer goods experience is an advantage.
  • Hands-on experience using NetSuite for sales, order, customer, and revenue reporting, including saved searches or equivalent reporting tools.
  • Experience using Apache Superset to navigate, build, or maintain dashboards and analyze sales performance; basic SQL skills are an asset.
  • Practical experience with CRM systems and sales pipeline management, including opportunity stages, data hygiene, forecasts, and reporting.
  • Comfortable applying AI tools to research, data analysis, reporting, and workflow automation, with sound judgment around accuracy, confidentiality, and validation.
  • Strong Microsoft Excel skills, including pivot tables, lookups, formulas, and structured data analysis.
  • Ability to interpret revenue, unit sales, margin, budgets, forecasts, and customer and product performance data.
  • Excellent attention to detail and ability to reconcile and validate information from multiple business systems.
  • Clear communication skills and ability to turn findings into concise, actionable insights for non-technical stakeholders.
  • Organized, proactive, and comfortable balancing recurring deadlines and ad hoc requests in a part-time schedule.

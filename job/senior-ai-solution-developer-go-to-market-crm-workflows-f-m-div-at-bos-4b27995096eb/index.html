About the job

Your contribution to something big:

  • End-to-end AI solution development: Design, prototype, deploy and continuously improve Generative AI products and agentic workflows throughout their lifecycle.
  • Hands-on implementation: Build and configure prompts, AI agents, workflows and tool integrations using technologies such as Python, enterprise AI platforms and low-code automation tools such as n8n.
  • System integration: Design and implement integrations using APIs, MCPs and webhooks. Define clear interface requirements for CRM and platform teams, including technologies such as OData, REST APIs and Microsoft Dataverse Web API.
  • Business and user alignment: Translate business requirements, user feedback and process needs into practical AI solutions while ensuring secure and responsible handling of CRM data.
  • Product ownership: Co-own the product lifecycle after rollout, including backlog prioritization, release coordination and continuous improvement.
  • Value creation: Identify opportunities to scale successful AI solutions and contribute to measurable improvements in CRM processes across multiple geographies.

What distinguishes you:

Education: Degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Data Science, Business Administration or a comparable field.

Experience:

  • Several years of experience developing and deploying GenAI, AI-agent or automation solutions from prototype to production.
  • Experience with CRM processes and platforms is a strong advantage.

Know-how:

  • Hands-on experience with Python, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, APIs and enterprise AI platforms.
  • Knowledge of CRM integrations, such as SAP CX, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Dataverse, OData or REST APIs.
  • Experience with Databricks or workflow automation tools such as n8n is a plus.

Working Style and Methods:

  • Hands-on, solution-oriented and comfortable turning business requirements into scalable technical solutions.
  • Able to work independently and collaboratively across technical and business teams.

Personality:

  • Curious, collaborative and passionate about AI, technology and creating business value.

Languages: Very good English skills.

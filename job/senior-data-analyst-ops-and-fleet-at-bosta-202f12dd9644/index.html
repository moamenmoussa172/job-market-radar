About the job

About Bosta:

Launched in 2017, Bosta is an overnight delivery provider for E-commerce companies in Egypt. Leveraging technology to empower businesses is what we do best. Our goal is to disrupt the logistics industry by bringing technology and experience altogether to provide a one-stop-shop for delivery solutions.

We rely on advanced technology to deliver orders to our customers in a fast way. Businesses can track and connect with couriers and follow up on their orders - all in one system.

Job Code: 2026DATA-071

Job Overview:

As a Senior Data Analyst, you will play a crucial role in extracting insights from complex datasets to inform strategic decision-making and drive business growth. With a keen eye for detail and strong analytical skills, you will transform raw data into actionable insights that empower stakeholders to make informed decisions. This role will have a strong focus on supporting our Operations and Fleet teams, helping them to analyze data, uncover insights, and make data-driven decisions.

Job Responsibilities:

  • 1-SQL Database Management:
  • Build and maintain SQL databases, ensuring data integrity, security, and optimal performance.
  • Oversee the maintenance of databases and coordinate with data engineers to address any performance issues.
  • Optimizing data performance.
  • Handling all data extraction requests by creating queries on our SQL database.
  • 2- Data Extraction and Visualization:
  • Develop and implement efficient data extraction processes from various sources.
  • Create visually appealing and insightful data visualizations to effectively communicate findings to stakeholders.
  • Apply data visualization best practices to enhance data-driven decision-making across the organization.
  • 3- Team Collaboration and Support:
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and provide data-driven insights and recommendations.
  • Work closely with the Operations and Fleet teams to understand their data needs, analyze operational and fleet data and operations challenges, and provide insights to improve efficiency and decision-making.
  • Partner with the planning/performance team.
  • 4- Additional Duties:
  • Stay updated on emerging trends, tools, and technologies in the field of data analytics.
  • Foster a data-driven culture within the organization, promoting the use of data for decision-making at all levels.
  • Job Requirements:
  • Bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as Data Science, Statistics, or Computer Science.
  • It's preferred to have tech startup experience.
  • 3-5 Years of experience as a Data Analyst.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL database management
  • Advanced proficiency in Tableau for building dashboards and visualizations (required).
  • Working knowledge of Python, Power BI or any other tools for data analytics is a plus.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret complex data and drive actionable insights.
  • Strong business acumen and the ability to translate business requirements into analytical solutions.

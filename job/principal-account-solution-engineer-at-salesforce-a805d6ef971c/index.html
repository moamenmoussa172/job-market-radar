About Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all.

Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce.

What You’ll Be Doing & Why It Matters
Salesforce is looking for several Pre-Sales Solution Engineers to join our dedicated Data Foundation Pre-Sales Solution Engineering team, who are instrumental to Data Foundation’s growth for Middle East.

Data Foundation brings together our MuleSoft integration and API platform with our Informatica data management, governance, and MDM capabilities — together, they turn connected, trustworthy data into the foundation AI agents need to act reliably at scale. Solution Engineers in this role help customers build that foundation, whether an engagement starts with MuleSoft, Informatica, or both.
Pre-Sales Solution Engineers provide technical sales partnership to our sales teams and align our solutions to our customers’ vision. The Data Foundation Sales team relies on the technical and product expertise of our Solution Engineers throughout the sales cycle. The Solution Engineering team plays an integral part of the success for both our customers and Account Executives. They are technologists and customer advocates that connect the dots between the technical solution and the business outcomes for the customer.
We are hiring for Pre-Sales Solution Engineers to focus across multiple industries such as Financial Services Consulting, Technology, Retail and Consumer Goods, Healthcare and Life Sciences, Manufacturing, or Automotive.
Core Responsibilities & Scope

  • Building trusted relationships with internal and external partners to qualify deals, identify business value, and provide technical guidance in order to set expectations to ensure customer success
  • Conducting technical discovery meetings to document, analyze, and clarify business and technical requirements to support a detailed solution proposal, managing customer engagements independently
  • Delivering architecture reframe and developing white-boarding skills through shadowing and customer-scenario role playing
  • Responding to Request for Information/Proposal documents
  • Working hands-on with prospects and customers to deliver standard, customized, and/or strategic solution demonstrations, white boarding, presentations, architecture workshops, and best practices that showcase the functional capabilities, differentiators, and business benefits of Data Foundation solutions — from demo and proof of concept to design and implementation
  • Supporting demos at marketing and field events
  • Collaborating with Product Management, Engineering, and Product Marketing to continuously improve Data Foundation’s products and marketing, including surfacing customer feedback on product gaps
  • Sharing best practices, content, tips, and tricks within your primary area of responsibility
  • Staying ahead of and immersing in ever-evolving technology landscape, maintaining a deep understanding of competing and complementary technologies and vendors, to position MuleSoft’s and Informatica’s value in relation to them
  • Staying current on relevant certifications
  • Actively leveraging AI and staying ahead of agentic concepts, tooling, code development, solution architecture and emerging trends in the AI space
  • Partner with the Customer Success Management (CSM) team on nurture activities, including technical advisory and workshops
  • Provide ongoing, post-sales technical guidance to the client’s technical team to drive adoption/utilization of Data Foundation’s solutions and digital transformation success

Minimum Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related discipline required (BS, Master’s, or PhD)
  • Minimum of 10 years of professional experience
  • Demonstrated presentation and composure abilities in front of all levels of technical and non-technical audiences
  • Experience with cloud technologies — iPaaS, SaaS applications, cloud infrastructure, etc. — is preferred
  • Working knowledge of hybrid deployment of software solutions, data warehousing, database, and BI concepts, plus core data management mechanics: master data match/merge and survivorship, data quality, and lineage
  • Experience building and delivering Proofs of Concept (PoCs) and responding to functional and technical elements of RFIs/RFPs independently
  • Knowledge of related applications, relational databases, and ERP technologies
  • Strong demonstrated oral, written, presentation, collaboration, and interpersonal communication skills
  • Fluency with AI-based development and agentic concepts, including supporting technologies (e.g. models, MCP, A2A, token management, etc.) and solutions
  • Ability to travel as needed (30-40%) and work flexible hours across multiple time zones
  • Experience leading architectural discussions
  • Proven experience establishing trust with clients, and the ability to influence key decision makers, influencers, coaches, and partners
  • Storytelling ability and experience tailoring communication and engagement with prospects to specific use cases
  • A passion for technology and an ability to translate that passion to impact business for our customers

Preferred Qualifications

  • Additional experience with Informatica related software platform/technology (e.g. API management, app integration, MDM, data governance, and data integration including AI-assisted capabilities such as Informatica CLAIRE) is a bonus.

  • You have experience in industries such as Financial Services, Consulting, Technology, Retail and Consumer Goods, Healthcare and Life Sciences, Manufacturing, or Automotive

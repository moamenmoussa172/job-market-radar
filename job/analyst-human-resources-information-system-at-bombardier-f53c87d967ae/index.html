What are your contributions to the team?

  • Interact with internal customers to understand needs and lead to technological solutions while maintaining excellent system design.
  • Analyze functional system testing ensuring solution satisfies business requirements.
  • Provide high quality user support with timely response to requests.
  • Conduct training to end-users & develop and maintain effective system documentation.
  • Troubleshoot HR system issues and find root causes.
  • Participate in team evaluating bi-annual SuccessFactors system upgrades.
  • Conduct testing of system design changes and vendor service upgrades.
  • Analyze existing systems for potential improvements and action as necessary.
  • Maintain system documentation, methods, procedures.
  • Assist with design and documenting business processes and system changes.
  • Effectively create necessary requests for third party system integrator to support required changes.
  • Promote open communication with process stakeholders and design teams.
  • Demonstrate complete discretion when dealing with confidential data and a mastery of employee data privacy best practices.

How to thrive in this role?

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Human Resources, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • 5+ years professional experience working with SAP SuccessFactors HR systems.
  • Knowledge in Recruiting (Smartrecruiters) and Performance module is an asset.
  • Experience with SuccessFactors implementation cycles and upgrade testing.
  • Kowledge of programming.
  • Knowledge in developing and conducting technological training.
  • Ability to multitask and be flexible to handle changes in meeting requirements and deadlines & to coordinate projects.
  • Knowledge in assisting with developing presentations.
  • Interpersonal skills necessary to establish and maintain effective working relationships with co-workers, other business areas, vendors and suppliers.
  • Knowledge of database design and how they inter-related with other systems, including third party systems.
  • Good understanding of HR processes and best practices.
  • Proficient skills with Microsoft products (Word, Excel, PPT..).
  • Understand project management terms and methodologies.
  • Knowledge of data privacy concepts.
  • Ability to stay current with industry trends and emerging technologies in HRIS.
  • Interest for AI technology.

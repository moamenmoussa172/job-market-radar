About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Lead hands-on development of reusable ServiceNow solutions across workflows, integrations, portal experiences and platform extensions. Convert technical designs into high-quality implementation, guide developers through code reviews and secure delivery, and help introduce Now Assist or AI Agent Studio workflows when the required capabilities are available.

Key Responsibilities

Technical design and hands-on development

  • Solution Design & Build: Lead solution design and build Scripted REST APIs, Flow Designer/Workflow Studio flows, IntegrationHub actions, custom scripts and scoped applications.
  • Portal Engineering: Design and implement custom portal components, Service Portal widgets or Employee Center extensions aligned to platform design standards.
  • AI Proofs of Concept: Develop MVP for emerging Now Assist/AI Agent Studio use cases to translate business outcomes into ServiceNow AI and automation roadmaps, assessing business value and AI consumption cost.

Code quality, integration and release

  • Code Quality & Reviews: Conduct design/code reviews, set conventions for reusable modules, error handling, observability and secure credential management.
  • Enterprise Integrations: Guide integration with external tools using REST/SOAP, MID Server and enterprise authentication patterns.
  • Release & Quality Assurance: Drive ATF/regression readiness, source-control and release discipline; troubleshoot critical defects and performance issues.

Engineering leadership and support

  • Technical Planning: Break requirements into technical stories, estimates, dependencies and acceptance criteria with product owners and architects.
  • Coaching & Delivery: Coach developers, resolve technical blockers, guide vendor contributions and maintain implementation documentation.
  • Production Stabilization: Own complex L3 diagnosis and production stabilization of delivered solutions in collaboration with operations.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications:

  • Experience: 12+ years; strong hands-on ServiceNow development and technical solution-design leadership.
  • Platform Development: Deep hands-on ServiceNow development including JavaScript, Glide APIs, Flow Designer and IntegrationHub.
  • Engineering Leadership: Demonstrated technical ownership of end-to-end ServiceNow solutions, code reviews and developer mentoring.
  • Portal Experience: Experience with custom portal components, Service Portal or Employee Center implementation.
  • Integration & Security: REST APIs, platform data model, security/ACLs, scoped app design and deployment practices.
  • Emerging AI Capabilities: Ability to evaluate Now Assist / AI Agent Studio use cases and deliver them under architectural governance.
  • ServiceNow ITOM, ITAM, CMDB/CSDM: Strong knowledge in Discovery, Service Mapping, Service Graph, asset lifecycle and CMDB governance. Ability to build integrated solutions across these capabilities and core ITSM processes.

Additional / Preferred Experience:

  • App Engine & DevOps: Experience with App Engine, Automated Test Framework, DevOps tooling and CI/CD.
  • AI-Enabled Workflows: Experience with AI-supported workflows, virtual agents, knowledge search and agentic orchestration.

Certifications:

  • ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) and Certified Application Developer (CAD)– mandatory.
  • Relevant ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist certifications – desirable.

Educational qualification:

BE/B-Tech/MCA

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

About the Role:

An AI agent is only as useful as what it knows about your company. For many of the world's leading companies, that knowledge lives in Notion. The AI Knowledge & Capabilities team makes sure every agent working in Notion – built by Notion, by customers, or by other AI companies – finds the right answer, quickly and at a cost that makes sense.

We own the systems that make this work: search, knowledge, and memory. We also own the agent harness – the tools, context, and model choices that turn a model into a working agent. We decide what to build using evals, customer feedback, and product judgment. Our work reaches users directly, and other AI teams at Notion build on it.

We recently shipped Memories, so Notion AI, Custom Agents, and agents connected through MCP can learn and remember across platforms. We also shipped Auto, a model router that delivers frontier quality at 90% lower cost. Next, we're bringing memory, search, and the harness together into one system. Nobody has figured out how to package that yet – we want any company to get a great knowledge system for its agents with a click or a few questions.

You will be a technical leader for the team. You'll set direction across search, knowledge, and the harness, starting hands-on with making agents better and cheaper, and help define how Notion measures AI quality.

This role can be based in either San Francisco or New York City. We work from our offices on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays (our Anchor Days) because we do our best thinking and building together in person. We’re looking for someone who’s excited to work alongside the team during those days.

What You'll Achieve:

  • Set technical direction for search, knowledge, and the agent harness – make the architecture calls, run design reviews, and decide what the team builds next.
  • Make Notion's agents better, faster, and cheaper – starting with the harness: which tools an agent can call, what context it gets, and which model handles each request.
  • Lead how we measure agent quality – evolve the metrics and evals that reflect real usage, and use them to choose what ships.
  • Turn company data into answers agents can use – build agentic search and memory that give every agent the context it needs, at the right latency and cost.
  • Work closely with research – partner with Notion's model research team on evals, model choice, and models trained for agentic work, and get what works into production. You'll also work with search platform and AI product teams on work that spans teams.
  • Grow the team – mentor engineers, review designs, and develop the next tech leads.

Skills You'll Need to Bring:

  • Production applied AI: You've shipped and iterated on production LLM or agent systems used by real users. You owned how their quality was measured, and you changed course based on what you found.
  • Quality, latency, and cost tradeoffs: You've traded quality against latency and cost in a production system, measured the result, and can explain what you gave up and why.
  • Technical leadership: You've owned the technical direction of a complex system, often across teams, and kept it sound as it grew. You don't wait for a roadmap – you find the problems worth solving, make the case for them, and go deep on the hardest ones yourself.
  • Product sense: You've shipped quality improvements users noticed, and you've shaped new products from scratch – from the data model to what users see. You can explain why they mattered.
  • Shipping something new: You've taken an idea from nothing to a product people use. You try new models and tools early, form your own views, and turn them into product decisions.
  • Making other engineers better: You mentor engineers at every level, guide their designs, and share what you know so it scales past you. Multiple engineers have grown by working with you.
  • Clear communication: You write clearly about tradeoffs, disagree directly, and change your mind when the evidence says to.

Nice to Haves:

  • You've improved LLM cost and quality directly – e.g. model routing, eval-driven harness or prompt tuning, or token efficiency in long-running agents.
  • You've founded a company or been an early engineer at a startup, and built systems that hold up at large scale.
  • Experience working closely with research or model training teams.

About the job

Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Everpure's growth depends on data our teams can actually trust. As Sr. Manager, Master Data Management & Data Governance, you'll own the enterprise vision for our most critical data domains — Customer, Product, Asset, and Vendor — and build the governance foundation that makes every downstream decision faster and better informed. You'll build the stewardship model and the team around it as the function grows, and use AI and automation to move master data quality from periodic cleanup to continuous, self-correcting control. Partnering with leaders across Sales, Finance, Operations, Product, and IT, you'll turn fragmented data into an AI-ready asset the whole company can build on. This is a highly visible role with a direct line to how Everpure runs.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Set and execute the enterprise strategy for Customer, Product, Asset, and Vendor master data, defining the governance and stewardship models that keep these domains consistent and trusted across every system that touches them.
  • Deploy AI and automation across matching, deduplication, classification, enrichment, and remediation, shifting master data quality from manual cleanup to continuous monitoring, anomaly detection, and automated correction.
  • Build the AI-ready data foundation, institutionalizing lineage, catalogs, dictionaries, and metadata, and ensuring master data flows cleanly across ERP, CRM, PLM, service management, and analytics platforms.
  • Establish the enterprise standards, ownership, and controls that govern master data quality, integrity, security, and compliance, partnering with senior business and technology leaders to make accountability real rather than nominal.
  • Own the program end to end — delivery, budget, and the executive scorecards that show data health and business impact — and grow the stewardship model and team as the domains mature.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • A track record leading enterprise Master Data Management programs end to end across global implementations, with deep command of Customer, Product, Asset, and Vendor domains and the ability to design governance and stewardship models that scale for each.
  • Hands-on command of enterprise MDM and data quality platforms such as Reltio and Informatica, and proven success integrating them with core enterprise systems — ERP, CRM, PLM, and analytics — to drive enterprise-wide consistency.
  • Demonstrated success applying AI and automation to data stewardship, enrichment, and predictive quality controls, with a clear view of where the discipline is heading.
  • Experience building and developing teams of data stewards and data management professionals, and growing capability that outlasts any single program.
  • The ability to align diverse stakeholders and still decide, translate technical complexity into business impact, and influence C-suite audiences with clarity.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, CA office in compliance with Everpure's policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

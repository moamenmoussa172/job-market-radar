About the job

Meet the Team

  • The Vodafone Global Account Team serves as the dedicated technical and strategic engine partnering with one of Cisco’s most critical global telecommunications clients.
  • Our function bridges high-level business strategy with advanced networking technology, directly integrating our work into Cisco's broader global service provider growth initiatives.
  • Composed of a collaborative and highly driven group of solution engineers, account leaders, and delivery experts, the team fosters an energetic, innovation-focused environment.
  • The most exciting aspect of our work is directly shaping next-generation global architectures like 5G, Telco Cloud, and AI networking at a massive scale.

Your Impact

  • Lead comprehensive strategic account planning and multi-domain architectural designs to align Cisco's technology transformation vectors with Vodafone's core business priorities.
  • Drive complex deals across the technical sales cycle by orchestrating cross-functional virtual teams to accelerate deal velocity and expand Cisco's market footprint.
  • Leverage deep market competitive insights and strategic positioning to differentiate Cisco solutions against competitor plays and secure crucial RFP/RFQ wins.
  • Provide actionable corporate feedback to Cisco Business Entities and Product Management to influence feature development based on emerging telecommunications and AI market trends.
  • Convert qualified technical opportunities into closed business across the Vodafone Group to ensure consistent revenue growth and long-term customer success.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Minimum of 7 years of field experience in technical sales or solution engineering within the Service Provider or telecommunications industry.
  • Demonstrated hands-on technical deployment experience with Cisco architectures or equivalent enterprise-grade competitor offerings.
  • Full professional fluency in written and spoken English.
  • Experience responding to complex telecommunications RFPs, RFIs, or structured technical tenders.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Deep expertise in Service Provider routing and switching, Telco Cloud, SDN architectures, Data Center fabrics, AI infrastructure, and carrier security.
  • Possession of relevant advanced industry certifications such as CCIE (Service Provider, Enterprise, Data Center, or Security) or CISSP.
  • Proven track record of translating complex service provider business challenges into scalable technical specifications with clear executive ROI.
  • Exceptional written, verbal, active listening, and executive-level presentation skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor peers and foster cross-functional collaboration between technical teams and external stakeholders.

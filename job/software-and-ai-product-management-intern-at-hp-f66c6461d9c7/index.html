About HP Barcelona

At HP Barcelona, we are committed to reshaping the future of work through technology. As one of the world’s largest R&D centers, we bring together more than 800 engineers and deliver over 150 inventions annually. Our site serves as the global headquarters for the Large Format Printing and 3D Printing Business Units, driving innovation across software, firmware, and hardware development.

Beyond engineering, our Sales, Operations, Marketing, Supply Chain, Customer Support, and Finance teams work collaboratively to deliver exceptional customer experiences and continuously optimize processes. This ensures that HP remains agile and prepared to meet future challenges.

Join us and be part of a team that is redefining how technology transforms work.

HP 3D Printing Business is looking for a highly motivated Software AI Product Manager to join the 3D Product Management incubation team.

This role combines technical development of SW AI solutions to massively and structurally amplify the Product Management insights data set. Such intel and capabilities will become essential to enable critical decision-making process related to HP 3D incubations product roadmap in both the digital and physical offering.

Key responsibilities:

  • Develop multiple AI solutions to continuously monitor market, competitors, and open-source technologies related to the 3D incubation.
  • Stay up to date with industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices in software product management.
  • Define and measure key product metrics, using data-driven insights to continuously improve the product's performance and user experience.
  • Support in the development of the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for assigned software products or features.
  • Engage in research, development, and deployment of software solutions, showcasing hands-on experience.
  • Model Total cost of ownership of multiple scenarios applicable in the 3D incubation.

Requirements:

  • Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Digital Marketing or related fields.
  • Technical Skills
  • AI Agent & Workflow Frameworks
  • LLM orchestration frameworks
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Python & Software Engineering
  • JIRA or other Agile methodologies/tools
  • Analytical modeling
  • Proficient Excel modeling
  • Product Management
  • Soft skills
  • Customer Centricity
  • Effective Communication
  • Results Orientation
  • Learning Agility
  • Digital Fluency
  • Fluent in English, both spoken and written

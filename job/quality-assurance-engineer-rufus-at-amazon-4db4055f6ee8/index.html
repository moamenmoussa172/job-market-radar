About the job

Rufus is Amazon's conversational AI shopping assistant, designed to make everyday shopping seamless through intelligent, AI-powered experiences.
We're seeking a passionate and talented Quality Assurance Engineer (QAE) to join the Rufus Quality Engineering team. In this role, you'll be instrumental in building scalable test automations, elevating the quality bar from the customer's perspective, and championing quality excellence across Rufus services.
You'll have the autonomy—and encouragement—to innovate, improve processes, and uphold the highest standards on behalf of our customers. Collaborating with teams across Amazon, you'll directly influence the end-to-end quality of the Rufus shopping experience.
You'll have the freedom (and encouragement) to improve, invent and insist on the highest standards on behalf of our customers. You will interact with teams throughout Amazon and have an impact on the end quality of our services for shopping using Rufus.
Key job responsibilities
· Experience in manual testing
· 4+ years experience working as a Quality Assurance Engineer
· Experience in automation testing
· Agile Testing Experience in an agile development environment (e.g. SCRUM/XP/Kanban) with experience writing acceptance criteria for user stories.
· Experience working with product owners defining, and refining user stories.
· Demonstrated experience in test planning, test design, test strategy, and test execution.
. Recommended experience of testing on mobile devices
- Experience in automation testing
- Experience in manual testing
- Experience as QA lead on medium to large sized projects
- Experience working as a Quality Assurance Engineer
- Experience with at least one automated test framework like Selenium or Appium
- Experience in gathering test requirements to create detailed test plans and defining quality metrics to measure product quality

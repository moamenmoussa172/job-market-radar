About the job

At Penumbra, innovation, teamwork, and initiative are more than values – they are the foundation of a workplace where every individual is encouraged to grow and make a real difference. We believe that the strength of our team lies in our diverse talents and shared passion for improving lives. If you’re looking for a role where your contributions matter, we invite you to join a company that values both personal development and impactful work.

As a leading MedTech company, we provide cutting-edge solutions for conditions such as stroke, pulmonary embolism, deep vein thrombosis, and acute limb ischemia. Headquartered in Alameda, California, Penumbra has over 4,500 employees worldwide and generated revenues of approximately $1.4 billion in 2025.

What You’ll Work On

As our FP&A Analyst, EMEA, you’ll support financial planning, forecasting, reporting and analysis across our EMEA business, with a strong focus on expense management and financial performance.

  • Own the preparation and coordination of annual and periodic expense forecasts and long-range financial plans, including P&L forecasting.
  • Track financial performance against management targets, forecasts and prior periods, identifying key variances and the business drivers behind performance.
  • Partner with EMEA business stakeholders to understand financial performance and spend, support business planning, and help expense owners understand their cost responsibilities and track spend effectively.
  • Lead workforce and non-workforce forecasting, including headcount planning, in collaboration with key business functions.
  • Oversee cost allocation, ensuring expenses are reflected in the correct cost centers and improving transparency of spend across the business.
  • Develop financial analyses, reporting and KPIs to support management decision-making and the evaluation of initiatives and investments.
  • Drive continuous improvement and standardization of FP&A processes, tools, templates and reporting.
  • What You Bring We’re looking for someone who combines strong financial and analytical skills with curiosity and a genuine interest in understanding the business behind the numbers.
  • A Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, Business Management or a related field.
  • 4+ years of relevant professional experience in FP&A, controlling, corporate finance, management accounting, audit or another relevant finance function.
  • Experience with financial data, analysis and reporting; exposure to budgeting, forecasting or management reporting is preferred.
  • Previous experience working within a multinational or international business environment is an advantage.
  • Strong analytical and numerical skills with a solid understanding of fundamental finance and accounting principles.
  • Strong Microsoft Excel skills; experience with financial modelling tools such as Anaplan is an advantage.
  • A curious, business-oriented mindset with an interest in understanding the commercial drivers behind financial results.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively across functions and in an international environment.
  • A structured and detail-oriented approach, with a strong focus on data accuracy and the ability to work effectively within recurring reporting, forecasting and budgeting deadlines.
  • Fluent English; additional European languages are an advantage
  • As we require fluent English skills and the process will involve English speaking colleagues, we are looking forward to receiving your CV in English.

About the job

Senior Manager, Credit and Analytics - (AI & Advanced Analytics)

Nairobi, Kenya

What if AI could change the way millions of people access credit?

You're accomplished. You understand credit risk, portfolio economics and the commercial decisions that determine whether a lending business grows sustainably.

You know how to interrogate a portfolio, identify emerging risks, understand the drivers of repayment behaviour and translate complex data into decisions that protect profitability.

But you also see something bigger.

You recognise that AI is fundamentally changing how businesses analyse information, identify patterns, make decisions and execute at scale. You don't simply use AI to make existing tasks faster. You know how to build intelligent workflows, leverage advanced AI capabilities and fundamentally rethink how analytical work gets done.

Now imagine applying that capability to a credit business serving millions of customers across Sub-Saharan Africa.

At M-KOPA, this is not a conventional credit analytics role. We are looking for someone who can bring together deep credit expertise, exceptional analytical thinking and sophisticated AI capability to transform how we manage, understand and optimise our credit portfolio.

We want someone who doesn't just work with data. We want someone who knows what AI can make possible with it.

The opportunity: credit performance at scale

M-KOPA has unlocked more than $2 billion in credit for 10 million customers across Sub-Saharan Africa. Our ambition is to continue expanding access to financial services while maintaining a sustainable, healthy and profitable credit portfolio.

As we scale, the complexity of managing that portfolio increases. Traditional reporting, manual analysis and conventional approaches to portfolio monitoring will not be enough.

We need to move towards a more intelligent, predictive and automated approach to credit management.

This is where you come in.

You will own Kenyan credit performance while driving the adoption of advanced AI and analytical capabilities across credit strategy, portfolio management, pricing, forecasting and business intelligence.

You will have direct exposure to the Managing Director, General Manager and Kenya Leadership Team, with the opportunity to influence decisions that shape the business.

What makes this role different?

Most credit analytics roles focus on producing reports, managing models or interpreting historical performance.

This role goes further.

We want you to challenge how things are currently done and explore how AI and advanced analytical technologies can unlock better, faster and more intelligent ways of working.

That could mean developing AI-enabled workflows that automatically interrogate portfolio performance, building intelligent agents that surface emerging credit risks, using machine learning to improve customer segmentation, or applying advanced analytical techniques to optimise pricing and repayment strategies.

You will be expected to move beyond experimenting with AI tools and demonstrate how you can translate their capabilities into practical, measurable business outcomes.

You will also retain accountability for the fundamentals: portfolio quality, credit risk, pricing discipline, profitability and sustainable growth.

What you'll bring

1. Deep credit and portfolio management expertise

  • A demonstrable track record in credit risk, portfolio analytics and performance management within financial services, ideally consumer lending, fintech or PAYGO businesses in emerging markets.
  • Strong understanding of credit lifecycle management, customer repayment behaviour, portfolio deterioration, collections, fraud and credit risk mitigation.
  • Experience translating portfolio insights into credit policy, pricing, risk appetite and commercial decisions.
  • A strong grasp of lending economics, including unit economics, margins, customer lifetime value and portfolio profitability.

2. Advanced AI capability and applied AI innovation

This is a critical differentiator for the role.

We are looking for someone with demonstrable, hands-on experience applying advanced AI capabilities to solve complex business and analytical problems.

You should be able to demonstrate how you have moved beyond basic generative AI usage and incorporated AI into actual business workflows and decision-making processes.

This includes:

  • Building and deploying sophisticated AI-enabled workflows, intelligent agents or multi-step automated processes that solve real business problems.
  • Applying advanced prompting, context engineering and AI orchestration techniques to extract meaningful insights and execute complex analytical tasks.
  • Using large language models (LLMs), generative AI and emerging AI technologies to transform data analysis, reporting, research and decision support.
  • Leveraging AI-assisted coding and analytical tools to accelerate the development of models, interrogate large datasets and automate repetitive analytical processes.
  • Understanding how machine learning and predictive AI can be applied to credit scoring, customer segmentation, early-warning indicators, collections optimisation and portfolio forecasting.
  • Demonstrating the ability to integrate AI into existing business processes and systems, rather than treating it as a standalone productivity tool.
  • Critically evaluating AI-generated outputs, validating results, managing hallucinations, and ensuring appropriate data security, governance and responsible AI practices.

We are particularly interested in what you have actually built, automated or transformed using AI, the complexity of the problems you have solved, and the measurable impact your work has delivered.

We are not simply looking for someone who uses ChatGPT or Copilot to improve personal productivity. We want someone who understands the technology, explores its possibilities and knows how to apply it meaningfully in a business environment.

3. Advanced analytics, modelling and business intelligence

  • Strong technical analytical capabilities, including experience working with complex, multi-source datasets.
  • Practical proficiency in SQL and Python or equivalent analytical programming tools.
  • Experience with BI and visualisation platforms, such as Power BI, Tableau or similar.
  • Experience developing predictive models, scenario analysis, forecasting methodologies and analytical frameworks.
  • Ability to design experiments, evaluate outcomes and translate findings into actionable business recommendations.
  • Experience improving data quality, reporting architecture, metric definitions and analytical processes.

4. Strategic leadership and business influence

  • Proven experience leading analytics, BI or credit intelligence teams, including managing and developing analysts and managers.
  • Ability to influence senior stakeholders and translate complex technical findings into clear commercial recommendations.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, particularly across Credit, Finance, Sales, Data and Technology.
  • A track record of driving transformation, challenging conventional approaches and embedding new ways of working.
  • Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to articulate insights, challenge assumptions and influence executive-level decisions.

A quantitative degree is preferred. Postgraduate qualifications or professional designations such as CFA, ACCA, CPA or FRM would be advantageous.

What your weeks will look like

Transforming credit intelligence through AI

You will identify opportunities to embed AI into credit and portfolio management, develop intelligent analytical workflows and drive the adoption of AI-enabled solutions. You will continuously explore emerging technologies and determine where they can deliver tangible business value.

Anticipating and steering portfolio performance

About the job

Money Fellows is transforming how people save, borrow, and invest. As the world’s first and largest Money Circles app, we’ve digitized the traditional ROSCA model to make it smarter, safer, and more accessible. 💸
Since 2018, we’ve empowered over 7 million users to take control of their financial futures with confidence. 🚀

  • Own production infrastructure: Operate, troubleshoot, and improve our cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes environments, ensuring reliability, availability, performance, and security.
  • Build and improve automation: Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines, GitOps workflows, infrastructure-as-code, and automation that make deployments and operations more consistent and efficient.
  • Eliminate repetitive work: Identify manual, error-prone, or time-consuming processes and turn them into reliable, reusable tools. We value engineers who recognize when a small automation investment can save the team hours or days of work every month.
  • Develop our internal developer platform: Help build and evolve internal tooling and platform capabilities that simplify workflows, improve developer experience, and enable engineering teams to work more independently.
  • Raise the team's technical bar: Support other DevOps engineers through technical guidance, code reviews, troubleshooting, knowledge sharing, and mentoring.
  • Drive problems to resolution: Investigate complex infrastructure and application issues, identify root causes, implement lasting fixes, and prevent recurring incidents.
  • Deliver pragmatic improvements: Prioritize work based on impact, ship usable solutions, and continuously improve existing systems without overengineering.
  • Grow into leadership: Help establish technical standards, improve engineering practices, and contribute to team priorities, with the opportunity to grow into a broader technical and people leadership role.
  • 3 - 6 years in DevOps, atleast 2 years as a Senior
  • Strong hands-on experience in DevOps, platform engineering, or SRE, with a track record of owning production systems.
  • Kubernetes: Experience operating, troubleshooting, upgrading, and maintaining production clusters—not just deploying applications.
  • AWS/OCI/HCloud: Solid understanding of cloud infrastructure, architecture, networking, security, and cost optimization.
  • Infrastructure as Code: Strong Terraform experience, including reusable modules and maintainable infrastructure patterns.
  • CI/CD and GitOps: Practical experience designing and improving deployment pipelines and working with tools such as GitHub Actions and Argo CD.
  • Automation and scripting: Proficiency in Python and Bash, with the ability to build maintainable tools that solve real operational problems.
  • Production troubleshooting: The ability to investigate complex issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable solutions.
  • Engineering ownership: Experience building or improving systems that other engineers depend on, and maintaining them beyond the initial delivery.
  • Pragmatic execution: A strong bias toward action, measurable outcomes, and simple solutions that solve the right problems.
  • Collaboration and mentorship: The ability to support teammates, communicate technical decisions, and help others grow.

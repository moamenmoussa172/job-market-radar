About the job

Why join us?

At Bombardier, we design, build and maintain the world’s peak-performing aircraft for the world’s most discerning people and businesses, governments and militaries. We have been successful in setting the highest standards by putting our people at the heart of it all, and defining excellence, together.

Providing services to Bombardier means operating at the highest level. Every day, you are part of a team that delivers superior experiences and products, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in our industry and beyond. By prioritizing continuous growth and development, we empower everyone to reach their full potential on their own terms, because the greatest results happens when you are free to be yourself and share your unique expertise.

What are your contributions to the team?

· Establish the needs of the various sectors of the plant in terms of layout or infrastructure.

· Present projects to internal entities and communicate their progress.

· Develop and choose innovative solutions while respecting customer needs.

· Respect the rules in terms of health, safety and the environment and intervene as needed.

· Ensure that deliverables comply with plans and specifications and meet deadlines.

· Plan several projects simultaneously while respecting the challenges and constraints of operations.

· Manage the work under your responsibility (HSE, Quality, etc.) by ensuring constant monitoring of external contractors.

· Respect budgets and inform Bombardier managers when required of any excess costs related to the project

How to thrive in this role?

· You have a university degree or college diploma in the field of industrial engineering, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, architecture / or Equivalent.

· You have more than 5 years of experience as a project manager in an industrial environment or Equivalent.

· You demonstrate an excellent spirit of synthesis; you easily popularize technical information in your written and oral communications while demonstrating an excellent command of French and a good knowledge of English.

· You have good computer skills in a Windows environment: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, MS Project, Teams etc.

· You are a good communicator; you easily interact with several stakeholders from different backgrounds.

· You are autonomous, innovative and dynamic.

· You show leadership and you have very good ability to work with multidisciplinary teams.

· You are a very organized person, you are results-oriented while remaining flexible to changing priorities and accustomed to working on several projects at the same time.

· You have experience in project management and ideally in drawing on Autocad.

​Now that you can see yourself with the Bombardier Team!

Please note: You don’t need all the skills, knowledge, and experience listed to contribute for this opportunities . We’re not seeking perfection, just passionate, committed individuals.

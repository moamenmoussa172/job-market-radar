Assistant Manager_eAxle System engineer

Agile / Scrum

About the job

**Responsibilities:**

  • Establish and execute comprehensive requirements-management processes and tool structures for eAxle projects
  • Develop and maintain project requirements-management plans aligned with organizational standards
  • Exchange and baseline requirements with customers, ensuring clarity and alignment across all stakeholders
  • Assign requirements to responsible domains and track assessment progress throughout project lifecycle
  • Moderate clarification of incomplete, conflicting, or non-testable requirements with cross-functional teams
  • Maintain traceability between customer, system, and component specifications to ensure end-to-end coverage
  • Report on requirements maturity, coverage, changes, and open points at project milestones
  • Support ASPICE assessments and drive continuous process improvement initiatives
  • Coordinate with engineering teams to ensure requirements are properly understood and implementable
  • Manage requirements change requests and conduct impact analysis on project scope and schedule
  • Lead requirements validation and verification activities to ensure compliance with customer expectations
  • Mentor and guide team members on requirements-management best practices and ASPICE compliance standards
  • Identify and mitigate risks associated with requirements definition and traceability gaps
  • Maintain comprehensive documentation and evidence for audit and certification purposes

**Qualifications:**

**Required:**

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics, or related engineering discipline
  • Minimum 6 years of professional experience in automotive requirements engineering or system development, preferably in electrified powertrain systems
  • Demonstrated expertise in requirements management processes, tools, and methodologies
  • Strong knowledge of automotive standards and ASPICE compliance frameworks
  • Proficiency with requirements management tools and software (e.g., DOORS, Requisite Pro, or equivalent platforms)
  • Excellent technical documentation and traceability skills
  • Proven ability to work effectively across cross-functional engineering teams

**Preferred:**

  • Advanced certification in systems engineering (CSEP, ASEP) or ASPICE assessment experience
  • Experience with eAxle, electric motor, or battery management system development
  • Knowledge of verification and validation (V&V) methodologies and testing protocols
  • Demonstrated experience in change management and impact analysis
  • Background in risk management and mitigation strategies
  • Experience mentoring junior engineers and leading process improvement initiatives
  • Familiarity with automotive development lifecycle and agile project management approaches

About the job

About the role
As a Mid-to-Senior Product Designer, you will be the champion of user-centered design, shaping the future of Bosch Digital products, services, and solutions. You will own the design journey from early-stage context mapping and user research down to high-fidelity prototyping, testing, and implementation.

Besides your skill in research, service and visual design, we need a strategic thinker who bridges the gap between complex software/system architectures, business goals, and real-world user expectations. Utilizing an iterative Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop, you will turn complex user workflows into highly intuitive, accessible, and delightful digital experiences.

Responsibilities & Tasks
Iterative End-to-End Design: Guide the product through the complete UX lifecycle (research, wireframing, detailed interaction design, prototyping, and validation testing) .

Conduct User Research & Audits: Observe users in their environments, moderate workshops, map customer journeys, and run UX audits or heuristic evaluations to spot usability pitfalls early.

Rapid Prototyping: Build both low-fidelity wireframes and interactive, high-fidelity prototypes (e.g., in Figma) to validate concept designs and test user flows .

UX Standards: Align design implementations with modern design principles, maximizing ease of navigation and screen clarity.

Maintain & Scale Digital Solutions: Actively work, expand, and enforce BD product, services and solutions, establishing reusable patterns and ensuring consistency across different applications.
Collaboration

Cross-Functional Synergy: Work in agile, interdisciplinary teams alongside Software Developers, Frontend/Backend Engineers, Product Owners, and System Architects to ensure technical feasibility and smooth handovers.

Stakeholder Alignment: Act as a critical interface with both internal stakeholders and external clients to translate customer business requirements into elegant, optimal UX designs.

Design & Dev Handover: Drive clear, traceable design handovers by creating annotated screenshots, interactive flows, and clear redlines in platforms like Figma to streamline developer execution .

#LI-MM1

  • Proven experience as a UX/UI Designer, Interaction Designer, or Product Designer working on complex digital platforms or enterprise software .
  • Rich Portfolio: A strong portfolio demonstrating your user-centered design process, clear problem-solving methodologies, user flows, and before-and-after case studies .
  • Agile & System Exposure: Experience working within fast-paced, agile environments and a strong familiarity with working alongside technical architectures and design frameworks.
  • Advanced proficiency with design tools such as Figma, Figma Make, Illustrator, Photoshop and Miro.
  • Knowledge in AI tools, adding it to your design process (Gemini, Copilot).
  • Expertise in wireframing, prototyping, and interaction design.
  • Solid understanding of user research methodologies and usability testing.
  • Knowledge of information architecture and design systems.
  • Proficiency in visual design principles, typography, and color theory.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication and presentation abilities.
  • Fluency in English.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Bachelor in Design, with experience with accessibility standards (WCAG) and inclusive design practices.
  • Familiarity with design thinking methodology.
  • Knowledge of data analytics and user behavior analysis tools.

The position may be filled at the Mid-Level or Senior level, depending primarily on:

  • Previous experience in Product Design, UX/UI, or Interaction Design;
  • Complexity and scope of digital products and platforms previously developed;
  • Level of autonomy and ownership throughout the design process;
  • Depth of experience in user research, wireframing, prototyping, interaction design, and usability testing;
  • Experience working with design systems, technical teams, and Agile environments;
  • Ability to manage stakeholders and clearly communicate design decisions;
  • Proficiency with tools such as Figma, Miro, Illustrator, and Photoshop;
  • For Senior profiles, demonstrated ability to lead complex design initiatives, influence product decisions, and act as a design reference for multidisciplinary teams.

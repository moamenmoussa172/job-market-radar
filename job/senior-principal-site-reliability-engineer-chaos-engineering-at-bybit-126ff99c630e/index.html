About This Role

As the Core Architect of Bybit’s Chaos Engineering Platform, you will be responsible for designing and building an enterprise-grade chaos engineering platform from the ground up (0 to 1), enabling fault injection and resilience validation in production environments across 2,000+ microservices.

Your mission goes beyond simply building a platform. You will drive continuous system resilience validation, with the ultimate goal of reducing the occurrence of P0/P-1 severity incidents by over 50%.

Core Responsibilities

  • Chaos Engineering Platform Architecture & Development (50%)
  • Design and build an enterprise-grade chaos engineering platform supporting multi-cluster (K8s + EC2 hybrid), multi-region, and multi-environment (testnet/mainnet) deployments
  • Core capability development:
  • Fault Injection Engine: Pod-level / Node-level / AZ-level fault simulation, network latency / packet loss / partition, dependency timeout / error injection
  • Production Safety Assurance: Blast radius control, one-click Kill Switch, automatic rollback, real-time impact monitoring
  • Traffic Isolation: Experiment traffic tagging and isolation to ensure fault injection does not impact real users
  • Fault Isolation: Precise impact scoping at service / cluster / AZ granularity
  • Design experiment orchestration capabilities supporting complex fault scenario composition (e.g., simultaneous network latency + downstream timeout + cache invalidation)
  • Deep integration with existing monitoring, alerting, and SLO systems to achieve an automated closed loop: inject fault → observe impact → determine pass/fail
  • Production Resilience Validation Framework (30%)
  • Define safety standards and approval workflows for mainnet fault injection
  • Design and drive routine chaos experiments:
  • Daily patrol-level experiments: Low-risk experiments executed automatically on a daily/weekly basis
  • Periodic validation experiments: Monthly/quarterly resilience verification of critical paths
  • Large-scale drills: Cross-AZ / cross-region disaster recovery failover validation
  • Establish a resilience scoring system to quantify system health based on experiment results
  • Deliver improvement recommendations and drive business teams to remediate identified weaknesses
  • Technology Selection & Team Enablement (20%)
  • Evaluate and select the technology foundation (Chaos Mesh / Litmus / custom components — hybrid strategy)
  • Develop chaos engineering best practices and playbooks to enable SRE teams and application developers
  • Mentor and grow the team (2–3 engineers) in chaos engineering capabilities
  • Stay current with industry developments and introduce cutting-edge practices (e.g., AI-driven fault scenario discovery)

Key Requirements

Must-Have:

  • 8+ years of backend / infrastructure engineering experience, with 3+ years dedicated to chaos engineering or stability engineering
  • Hands-on experience with large-scale fault injection in production environments (not just test environments), with deep understanding of production safety constraints
  • Expert-level proficiency in Kubernetes fault injection (Chaos Mesh / Litmus / custom solutions), familiar with CRD / Operator development
  • Proficient in at least one backend language (Go preferred), with platform-level system architecture design capability
  • Deep understanding of distributed system failure modes (network partitions, split-brain, cascading failures, data inconsistency, etc.)
  • Familiarity with observability tech stack (Prometheus / Grafana / Thanos / OpenTelemetry)
  • Excellent technical documentation and solution design skills

Nice-to-Have:

  • Experience in financial / trading system stability (understanding of transaction consistency and fund safety constraints)
  • Experience building SLO / Error Budget frameworks
  • Experience building automated fault recovery (self-healing) systems
  • Familiarity with AWS infrastructure (EC2 / EKS / Multi-AZ / Multi-Region)
  • Knowledge of Netflix Chaos Engineering / AWS Fault Injection Simulator / Gremlin
  • Open-source community contributions (Chaos Mesh / Litmus or similar projects)

Soft Skills:

  • Ability to balance "safety" and "validation depth" — not afraid of production injection, while maintaining strict risk control
  • Strong cross-team collaboration and influence — chaos engineering requires buy-in from business teams; this role demands persuasion skills
  • Self-driven, capable of independently planning and executing in ambiguous situations

岗位定位

作为Bybit混沌工程平台的核心架构师，负责从0到1构建企业级混沌工程平台，支撑2000+微服务在生产环境的故障注入和韧性验证能力。你的目标不是"搭个平台"，而是通过持续验证系统韧性，将P0/P-1级故障发生率降低50%+。

核心职责

  • 混沌工程平台架构设计与建设（50%）
  • 设计并构建企业级混沌工程平台，支持多集群（K8s + EC2混合）、多区域、多环境（testnet/mainnet）
  • 核心能力建设：
  • 故障注入引擎： Pod级/Node级/AZ级故障模拟，网络延迟/丢包/分区，依赖超时/错误注入
  • 生产环境安全保障： 爆炸半径控制、一键停止（Kill Switch）、自动回滚、影响范围实时监控
  • 流量隔离： 实验流量标记与隔离，确保故障注入不影响真实用户
  • 故障隔离： 按服务/集群/AZ粒度精准控制影响范围
  • 设计实验编排能力：支持复杂故障场景的组合编排（如：同时注入网络延迟+下游超时+缓存失效）
  • 与现有监控、告警、SLO体系深度集成，实现"注入故障→观测影响→判定通过/失败"的自动化闭环
  • 生产环境韧性验证体系（30%）
  • 制定主网故障注入的安全规范和审批流程
  • 设计和推动常态化混沌实验：
  • 日常巡检级实验（每日/每周自动执行低风险实验）
  • 定期验证级实验（月度/季度核心链路韧性验证）
  • 大规模演练（跨AZ/跨Region容灾切换验证）
  • 建立韧性评分体系：基于实验结果量化系统健康度
  • 输出改进建议并推动业务方修复发现的弱点
  • 技术方案选型与团队赋能（20%）
  • 评估并选择技术底座（Chaos Mesh / Litmus / 自研组件的组合策略）
  • 制定混沌工程最佳实践和Playbook，赋能SRE团队和业务研发
  • 培养团队（2-3人）的混沌工程能力
  • 与业界保持同步，引入前沿实践（如：AI驱动的故障场景发现）

任职要求

必需条件：

  • 8年+后端/基础设施开发经验，3年+混沌工程或稳定性工程专项经验
  • 有生产环境大规模故障注入的实操经验（非仅测试环境），深刻理解生产安全约束
  • 精通Kubernetes故障注入（Chaos Mesh/Litmus/自研方案），熟悉CRD/Operator开发
  • 精通至少一种后端语言（Go优先），具备平台级系统架构设计能力
  • 深入理解分布式系统故障模式（网络分区、脑裂、级联故障、数据不一致等）
  • 熟悉可观测性技术栈（Prometheus/Grafana/Thanos/OpenTelemetry）
  • 优秀的技术文档和方案设计能力

加分项：

  • 有金融/交易系统稳定性保障经验（理解交易一致性、资金安全约束）
  • 有SLO/Error Budget体系建设经验
  • 有自动化故障恢复（Self-healing）系统建设经验
  • 熟悉AWS基础设施（EC2/EKS/Multi-AZ/Multi-Region）

  • 有开源社区贡献经验（Chaos Mesh/Litmus等项目）

软性要求：

  • 能在"安全"和"验证深度"之间做好平衡——既不畏惧生产注入，也能严格控制风险
  • 有推动跨团队协作的能力——混沌工程需要业务方配合，这个岗位需要说服力
  • 自驱型，能在方向模糊时独立规划和推进

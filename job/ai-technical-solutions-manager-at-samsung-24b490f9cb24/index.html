About the job

Position Summary

Why join our team?
We are looking for a hands-on AI Transformation Specialist to drive AI adoption and business process innovation across our European subsidiaries. You will act as an internal consultant, analysing sales and operational processes to identify inefficiencies, proposing appropriate AI solutions, and implementing them directly. This role requires a combination of business domain knowledge and practical AI skills to bridge the gap between business needs and AI capabilities, from requirements analysis and PoC development to business adoption and rollout.

Role and Responsibilities

  • Project Discovery & Management: Lead the full lifecycle of AI initiatives within subsidiaries, from identifying use cases to pilot testing and full rollout. Monitor progress and ensure KPIs are met.
  • Internal Consulting: Analyse key business processes (Sales, Supply Chain, Marketing, etc.) in local subsidiaries. Work closely with business teams to diagnose bottlenecks and proactively propose AI-driven improvements
  • AI Solution Design & Development: Evaluate and recommend appropriate AI solutions and tools based on business requirements. Design and develop PoCs and workflow automation solutions using Generative AI, AI coding tools, APIs, and automation platforms.
  • Adoption Support: Work closely with local teams to ensure the successful adoption of new AI solutions and provide necessary guidance or training to business users.

Skills and Qualifications

To be successful, you will possess the following skills and attributes:

  • Proven experience in Sales Operations, SCM, IT Project Management, or AI-driven business transformation within the electronics or retail industry.
  • Domain Knowledge: Understanding of sales subsidiary workflows (e.g., demand planning, sales administration, customer service), with the ability to analyse business challenges and identify improvement opportunities.
  • Technical Acumen: Understanding of AI technologies (Generative AI, LLMs, RPA, etc.) and hands-on experience in developing PoCs or workflow automation using AI coding tools. Advanced coding skills are not required, but the ability to leverage AI tools to solve business problems directly is essential.
  • Problem-Solving & Communication: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a consulting mindset, and the ability to communicate effectively with business users and drive AI initiatives from concept to implementation.

Benefits

  • Hybrid working – 3 days in the office and 2 days at home per week
  • Bonus scheme linked to individual, team and company performance
  • Car allowance
  • Pension contribution
  • Three volunteering days each year
  • Holiday - 25 days plus bank holidays and an additional day off for your birthday
  • Access to discounts on a wide range of Samsung products
  • Access to a discount shopping portal
  • Partner Colleagues are not eligible for Samsung Enhanced Paid Sick Leave but may be eligible for statutory payments from their payroll agency
  • Up to 20 (pro-rata) Partner Absence days per calendar year to be used in times of need

Please note this is a Partner Colleague role where you will be engaged by one of our recruitment agency partners.

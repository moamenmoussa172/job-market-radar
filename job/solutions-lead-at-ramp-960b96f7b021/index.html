About the job

About Ramp

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

About the role

We’re looking for a Solutions Lead to help build Ramp’s solutions function for our international business, starting with the UK and Europe. This is a hands-on builder role. You’ll be a trusted technical partner to prospects, customers and channel partners, shaping how we sell, implement and support complex solutions as the business grows.

This is broader than a traditional pre-sales role. Winning complex opportunities is central to the job, but your impact won’t end at signature. You’ll connect technical discovery with implementation readiness, help resolve high-stakes customer challenges, enable partners, and build the demos, playbooks and technical expertise that make the wider team more effective.

You’ll combine technical depth, commercial judgement and a strong understanding of finance workflows to solve the most important problems yourself and build repeatable approaches so the business becomes less dependent on any one person. You don’t need an engineering background; what matters is your ability to understand systems, solve technical problems and communicate clearly.

You’ll also have the opportunity to help customers navigate an evolving platform, including areas such as Accounts Receivable, Stack and our AI-powered products: Router, Glass and Inspect.

What you’ll do:

Win complex opportunities and prepare them for implementation

  • Partner with Account Executives and Account Managers to lead technical discovery, tailored demos and solution design, translating finance and procurement requirements into practical workflows and integrations.
  • Communicate capabilities, gaps and trade-offs clearly; identify implementation risks early and establish clear ownership and dated handoffs to the implementation team.

Lead technical resolution of complex customer challenges

  • Diagnose high-impact product, configuration, accounting and ERP issues, coordinating with customer-facing and technical teams to drive a clear resolution path.
  • Turn recurring issues into better guidance and evidence-backed product feedback, rather than a permanent stream of one-off interventions.

Enable partners and build the international solutions function

  • Act as the technical counterpart to channel and accounting partners, supporting joint opportunities and equipping them with reusable knowledge and resources. Partnerships retains ownership of commercial relationships and partner strategy.
  • Work with Demo Architecture and Solutions teams to build market-relevant demos, playbooks and enablement for AEs, AMs and partners. Use lightweight intake and prioritisation to focus effort on customer risk, revenue impact and opportunities for leverage.

What you need

  • A strong track record, typically 5–7 years, in solutions consulting, sales engineering, solutions architecture or technical consulting for complex B2B software, or in accounting or an in-house finance role with relevant systems and technical problem-solving experience.
  • Experience leading discovery, explaining complex workflows or systems, and influencing decisions across multiple stakeholders; experience delivering tailored demos is valuable.
  • Strong understanding of finance and accounting workflows, with hands-on experience working with ERP or accounting systems and their integrations.
  • The ability to reason through APIs, data flows and configuration dependencies, investigate technical problems and translate your findings for technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Evidence of working beyond the sale: designing implementable solutions, navigating complex customer issues and collaborating effectively across commercial and technical teams.
  • A builder’s mindset, with examples of creating reusable tools, documentation, demos or enablement that improved how others work.
  • Sound judgement about where to go deep, when to bring in specialists and how to set boundaries across competing priorities.
  • Comfort operating independently in an evolving market while building strong relationships with regional colleagues and central teams.

Nice to have

  • Experience with fintech, spend management, procurement or procure-to-pay platforms.
  • Familiarity with UK and European accounting practices, multi-entity structures and multi-currency workflows.
  • Experience enabling accounting firms, channel partners or implementation partners.
  • Experience building or scaling a solutions motion in a new market.
  • SQL, scripting or other practical technical problem-solving skills.

What success looks like

In your first 6–12 months, you’ll establish a trusted technical path for complex opportunities and customer escalations; clearer procurement scoping and implementation handoffs; and stronger technical capability across our partner ecosystem.

You’ll also build reusable demos, enablement and playbooks that let the region scale without requiring you in every conversation, supported by clear visibility into solutions demand and outcomes.

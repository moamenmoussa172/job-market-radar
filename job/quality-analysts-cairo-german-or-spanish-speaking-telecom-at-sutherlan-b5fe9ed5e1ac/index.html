About the job

Sutherland Cairo is looking for 2 talented Quality Analysts to support a NEW Telecom program!! We're looking for German OR Spanish-speaking QAs!

As a QA, you'll be responsible for:

  • Completing all assigned transaction evaluation and attending all internal and external calibration sessions as assigned.
  • Defining Sutherland’s reputation: Oversee and manage performance and service quality to guarantee customer satisfaction.
  • Strengthening relationships: Establish and maintain communication with clients and/or team members; understand needs, resolve issues, and meet expectations.
  • Keeping management updated: Relay important information in the form of timely and accurate reports. Impact the bottom line: Produce solid and effective strategies based on accurate and meaningful data reports and analysis and/or keen observations.

Our most successful candidate will have:

  • 1 year of QA experience w/in the telecom domain
  • Ability work onsite at our Cairo location
  • German or Spanish fluency
  • Ability to work in MS Office
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Pro-active ability in developing trust and professional rapport with employees and team members; work as a team-player
  • Strong analytical skills; be able to interpret data, identify trends, and make suggestions for improvements
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills; be able to communicate in a clear, constructive, and professional manner

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.

About the job

Role and Responsibilities

  • Design tailored SAP Business Data Cloud architectures aligned with specific customer needs and strategic goals
  • Partner with business and IT teams to gather, interpret, and translate business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Define and enforce data security protocols to ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.
  • Establish and advocate best practices across architecture, process, and project execution methodologies.
  • Align solution strategies with stakeholder expectations, ensuring consistency with KPI benchmarks and long-term roadmaps.
  • Author comprehensive documentation, including requirement specifications, functional and technical designs, and project-related materials.
  • Collaborate across departments to design and implement complex business and technical requirements.
  • Apply standardized guidelines for project management, architectural integrity, and process optimization.
  • Evaluate cross-functional impacts of proposed solutions to maintain system coherence and project alignment.
  • Champion innovation, fostering creative problem-solving and continuous improvement.
  • Leverage automation tools and techniques to accelerate delivery and enhance project efficiency.
  • Manage full project lifecycle, from initiation through deployment and post-implementation support.
  • Deliver end-to-end consulting services, guiding clients through strategy, design, implementation, and optimization.
  • Support pre-sales initiatives, enabling sales teams with technical insights and driving successful RFP responses.

SAP Datasphere:

  • Extensive hands-on experience with SAP Datasphere, including at least two full-cycle implementations.
  • Design and development of data models, data flows, and integration pipelines to support enterprise analytics and reporting.
  • Creation and management of remote tables and Data Builder objects within the Datasphere environment.
  • Development of analytical models using graphical views, seamlessly integrated into SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) stories and reports.
  • Implementation of data transformations using Python scripting, enabling advanced logic and automation.
  • Proficient in designing and executing complex dataflows, ensuring scalability and performance across systems.
  • Utilization of Open SQL schemas and HDI containers for structured data access and modular development within Datasphere.

SAP Analytics Cloud:

  • Deliver architectural guidance and subject matter expertise for the SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) platform
  • Configure infrastructure prerequisites for establishing both live and import data connections with SAP Datasphere and SAP BW/BW4HANA systems.
  • Develop SAC objects, including public/private dimensions, account-based and measure-based models, along with customized story templates and visualizations.
  • Set up currency configurations within SAC to support comprehensive analytical and planning requirements.
  • Execute business planning scenarios using SAC’s built-in features such as Data Actions, Advanced Data Actions, and Allocation processes.
  • Manage planning activities, including scheduling via SAC Calendar, implementing data locking mechanisms, and defining validation rules.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in SAC’s predictive analytics and augmented intelligence capabilities to enhance decision-making.
  • Create geo-enriched visualizations by integrating data from HANA and S/4HANA (live and import modes) into SAC stories.
  • Implement AI and ML-driven features such as Smart Insights, Smart Discovery, and Search to Insight to enrich reports and visualizations

SAP BW/4HANA:

  • Design, develop, and implement SAP BW/4HANA solutions that meet client reporting and analytics requirements
  • Lead data modeling and ETL processes within SAP BW, ensuring data accuracy and integrity
  • Optimize performance of SAP BW queries and reporting using HANA capabilities
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications
  • Provide training and mentorship to junior consultants on best practices in SAP BW and HANA
  • Assist in project planning and management, ensuring timely delivery of solutions
  • Support troubleshooting and performance tuning for existing BW HANA environments

SAP HANA Cloud/HANA:

  • To develop applications using SAP HANA Cloud Development
  • To utilize HDI Containers for application development
  • To implement HANA OData Services (XSOData)
  • To work with HANA XSA/XSJS and SQL Scriptinga along with Node.js and CF XSUAA services
  • Manage HANA Transport Management System and experience with HANA Transport Container
  • Utilize HANA XS Advanced and HANA 2.0 version
  • CDS View development experience is added advantage.
  • Leveraging Knowledge graphs within HANA cloud for Generative AI scenarios.

SAP AI:

  • Deploy and manage AI solutions using SAP BTP AI Core or SAP Databricks services for scalable enterprise integration.
  • Expose and integrate APIs from AI Core or SAP Databricks for seamless consumption across SAP and non-SAP platforms.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to embed AI/ML capabilities into SAP BTP environments or SAP Databricks, ensuring business alignment and technical feasibility.
  • Design and implement advanced AI/ML models, emphasizing generative AI and large language model (LLM) architectures.
  • Build robust, scalable applications leveraging Python/Pyspark for model development, orchestration, and deployment.
  • Apply prompt/context engineering techniques to optimize LLM performance for targeted use cases and domain-specific tasks.
  • Architect and implement end-to-end machine learning pipelines, from data ingestion to model deployment.
  • Utilize CI/CD workflows, ensuring version control, automated testing, and streamlined deployment.MLFlow.
  • Develop containerized ML pipelines using Docker for portability, scalability, and efficient resource management.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in SAP Generative AI components, including vector engines and knowledge graph integration.

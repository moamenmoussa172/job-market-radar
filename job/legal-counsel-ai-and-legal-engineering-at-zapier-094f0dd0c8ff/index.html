Hi there!

We're looking for a lawyer who builds. Zapier's Legal team is small, strong, and moving toward an AI-native way of working, and we want someone to help lead that change: building the systems through which our legal work gets done, practicing law with them, and helping the team and the company do the same. Building isn't a side project here; it's foundational, and you'll always be building alongside the work. You’ll work closely with and report to the General Counsel.

If you want to advance your career at a fast-growing, profitable, impact-driven company, read on…

About You:

  • You build at the frontier. You haven't just built a tool once; you keep testing what new models can do, retire what they make obsolete, and rebuild. You treat AI as a new way of working, not a set of isolated use cases, and you can show us what you've built.
  • You're a practicing lawyer. You have sound commercial and legal judgment and want to keep doing real legal work. That's how you know what to build, and it's what makes what you build credible.
  • You can turn the unfamiliar into a practical recommendation. You research a new regulation or contract issue and land on advice that balances risk with business goals.
  • You love new challenges. You're comfortable with change and ambiguity and excited to learn areas of law that are new to you.
  • You're self-aware and have a high EQ. You bring people along, inside Legal and across teams, rather than mandating change.
  • You're a skilled communicator. You make legal and technical ideas easy to follow for a global, fully remote team that works mostly in writing, and you can explain what you've built to lawyers outside Zapier.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Juris Doctor degree
  • Admitted to practice and in good standing with one or more state bars in the US
  • 6 to 10+ years of legal experience across commercial, product, or general corporate matters
  • Demonstrated, current hands-on building with AI tools (a portfolio we can look at)
  • In-house experience at a SaaS or technology company preferred, but not required

Things You’ll Do:

  • Lead AI transformation for the Legal team: build and improve our legal operating system (shared context, decision records, and the loops that check both the work and what the system learns from it), and help each lawyer build workflows that fit how they work.
  • Rebuild how routine legal work gets done, starting with NDAs and procurement review, so less of it needs a lawyer's time.
  • Do real legal work alongside the building: commercial contracts, product questions, and day-to-day matters as the team needs, including procurement and mid-market coverage when it's needed.
  • Dogfood Zapier's own products (Next-Gen Zaps, Zapier MCP) for legal workflows, and give product teams feedback on what legal teams need.
  • Partner with Zapier's AI transformation team on patterns that help beyond Legal.
  • Help test a go-to-market experiment: share what we build with legal builder communities and connect interested legal teams with our sales team.
  • Other duties as needed to meet the ongoing needs of the legal team at Zapier.

How to Apply

At Zapier, we believe that diverse perspectives and experiences make us better, which is why we have a non-standard application process designed to promote inclusion and equity. We're looking for the best fit for each of our roles, regardless of the type of companies in your background, so we encourage you to apply even if your skills and experiences don’t exactly match the job description. All we ask is that you answer a few in-depth questions in our application that would typically be asked at the start of an interview process. This helps speed things up by letting us get to know you and your skillset a bit better right out of the gate. Please be sure to answer each question; the resume and CV fields are optional.

Education is not a requirement for our roles; however, if you receive an offer, you will need to include your most recent educational experience as part of our background check process.

After you apply, you are going to hear back from us—even if we don’t see an immediate fit with our team. In fact, throughout the process, we strive to never go more than seven days without letting you know the status of your application. We know we’ll make mistakes from time to time, so if you ever have questions about where you stand or about the process, just ask your recruiter!

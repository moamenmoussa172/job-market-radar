About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Hex

Hex is growing our team of builders on a mission to make everyone a data person. Our platform solves key pain points with today’s data and analytics tooling, and empowers anyone to explore data using natural language, with or without code, on trusted context. Thousands of customers like Ramp, Figma, Stubhub, Anthropic, and Gamma love Hex for our beautiful UI, agentic superpowers, and boundless flexibility.

Hex has raised over $100M from leading investors like Sequoia, a16z, Snowflake and Amplify. With office hubs in San Francisco and New York, as well as remote team members throughout the US and UK, we’re a high-agency team that ships fast and has fun doing it. Check out our Intro to Hex video and employee handbook to learn more about what it’s like to work at Hex.

About the role:

We are looking for an experienced engineering leader to drive the strategy, execution, and growth of Hex’s state-of-the-art agentic analytics and app platform.

The Agent & Analytics teams build the core workflows in Hex for exploring data, answering questions, and building interactive applications. Our vision is to bring the speed of building with AI to the data assets enterprises rely on every day. With Hex, users can seamlessly jump from data explorations to fully interactive applications and dashboards, review the logic, trace any number back to its source, manage changes over time, and deploy it at scale. As our platform grows we expect to expand into a much broader set of workflows, from orchestrating actions based on what the data tells you to embedding rich, interactive analytics directly in other products.

Building on top of our newly-developed Hex 4.0 architecture, this work spans across our agent orchestration layer, conversational chat and app-building experiences, external MCP and CLI workflows, data visualization, and collaborative version control.

What you will do:

  • Lead an organization of multiple teams working on Hex’s agentic analytics platform
  • Recruit exceptional engineering talent, growing to a team of at least 15 software engineers
  • Drive technical strategy, review designs, and occasionally get hands on to move the needle
  • Serve as people manager for line-level engineering managers, tech leads, and ICs, setting a high bar and empowering them to do their best work

About You:

  • 5+ years experience leading engineering teams working on complex, data-intensive, or AI-driven products and SaaS platforms
  • Exceptional ability to identify and design the right technical abstractions to accelerate an ambitious and evolving product vision
  • Deep curiosity for, and strong intuition about, state-of-the-art AI agent behavior and context systems
  • Proven track record as a people manager, with experience (or clear potential) managing other engineering leaders and scaling high-performing teams
  • Brings a clear playbook for building high-performing teams: setting clear technical standards, hiring and developing strong talent, and creating environments where extraordinary engineers can do their best work
  • Deeply technical leader who is not afraid to roll up your sleeves and get hands on when necessary

Location & Compensation

This is a full-time role, based out of our San Francisco, CA office. Employees are encouraged to come into the office at least twice a week, with Tuesdays and Thursdays being our main in-person days.

Salary range: $261,375-$375,000

About the job

We are looking for a UX/UI Designer to join a project focused on the development and improvement of Bosch's HomeCom Easy mobile application.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Project Migration: Support the transition of existing projects from Sketch to Figma, ensuring that design integrity and functionality are maintained.
  • Design and Prototyping: Create and prototype intuitive graphical interfaces, aligned with best design practices and user needs.
  • Usability Testing: Facilitate and support usability tests with end-users, gathering feedback to enhance their experience.
  • Continuous Improvement: Collaborate in a continuous improvement environment, contributing ideas and suggestions to optimize processes and products.
  • Information Architecture: Develop the information architecture and define interactions for new and existing features, ensuring a smooth and intuitive navigation.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work in collaboration with other designers and the development team, fostering a collaborative and creative work environment.
  • Academic Background: A degree in User Interface Design, UX Design, Communication Design, Multimedia Design, or Product Design.
  • Knowledge of Digital Interfaces: A solid understanding of best practices and trends in digital interface design.
  • Tools: Proficiency in creating user interfaces using tools such as Figma, Sketch, Illustrator, After Effects, etc.
  • User-Centered Design Experience: Familiarity with user-centered design methods and techniques, including wireframes, flowcharts, and storyboards.
  • Critical and Creative Thinking: The ability to explore various solutions for design problems, evaluating the pros and cons of different approaches.
  • Information Architecture Structuring: The ability to structure information architecture and navigation mechanisms, using the appropriate tools for each situation.
  • Agile Methodology: Knowledge of agile development methodologies, including their terminology and objectives.
  • Fluency in English: Good command of English, both spoken and written, to facilitate communication in an international environment.
  • Willingness to Learn: A proactive attitude, critical thinking, and attention to detail.
  • Teamwork: Being dynamic, proactive, and able to collaborate effectively in a team.

Languages:

  • Portuguese
  • English

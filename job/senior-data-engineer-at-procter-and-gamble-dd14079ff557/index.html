About the job

Job Location

WARSAW DOWNTOWN OFFICE

Job Description

We are seeking a Senior Data Engineer to design, build, and operate scalable, secure, and reliable cloud data platforms and pipelines for critical Finance analytics and AI products. The role combines hands-on engineering with technical leadership, translating business needs into governed data architectures while strengthening quality, observability, performance, automation, and operational excellence. Working across Azure, data platforms, enterprise systems, and reporting tools, the engineer partners with Product, Architecture, Analytics, Security, Operations, Software Engineering, and supplier teams to deliver supportable data solutions and continuously reduce engineering toil.

Responsibilities

  • Data Architecture and Solution Design: Translate business objectives into scalable, secure, high-performing, and cost-efficient data architectures, technical roadmaps, and reusable engineering patterns.
  • Data Platform and Pipeline Engineering: Build, test, deploy, and optimize resilient batch and streaming ETL/ELT pipelines across cloud platforms, enterprise systems, APIs, and external sources.
  • Data Modeling and Integration: Develop governed lakehouse, warehouse, and curated data products using modern modeling and integration approaches that enable analytics, AI, and self-service use cases.
  • Reliability and Operational Excellence: Own critical data solutions through production; define service expectations, monitoring, data-quality controls, reconciliation, alerting, performance tuning, and incident prevention with Operations teams.
  • Automation and Engineering Practices: Reduce manual work through reusable code, automated testing, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, AI-enabled engineering, and standardized deployment and support processes.
  • Governance and Security: Apply standards for access, privacy, lineage, documentation, observability, lifecycle management, and responsible AI across data products and platforms.
  • Technical Guidance and Collaboration: Lead architecture and code reviews, mentor engineers, and advise Product, Architecture, Analytics, Security, Operations, and Software Engineering teams on scalability, supportability, and efficiency.
  • Delivery and Continuous Improvement: Own solutions from discovery through release and adoption, contribute to product roadmaps, and use operational feedback to improve reliability, performance, and business value.

Job Qualifications

Soft skills:

  • Strong communication and collaboration skills across business, product, architecture, analytics, security, operations, engineering, and supplier teams.
  • Structured problem-solving and sound technical judgment when resolving complex data, performance, and production issues.
  • Ability to prioritize architecture, delivery, operational work, and continuous improvement across multiple products.
  • Attention to detail in code quality, testing, documentation, governance, and production readiness.
  • Ability to mentor engineers, facilitate technical decisions, and explain complex topics to senior stakeholders.

Business skills:

  • Product-oriented mindset focused on trusted data, reliability, scalability, customer value, and measurable business outcomes.
  • Ability to translate Finance and enterprise requirements into data architectures, roadmaps, service expectations, and delivery priorities.
  • Understanding of the software development lifecycle, Agile/Scrum, DevOps, production support, and service management practices.
  • Ability to assess data quality, performance, availability, security, and cost drivers and recommend improvements across application and platform layers.
  • Ownership of data solutions from design and delivery through adoption, monitoring, support, and lifecycle management.

Technical skills:

  • Data Engineering: 5+ years delivering large-scale cloud data platforms and pipelines, with expert SQL and strong Python or PySpark skills.
  • Cloud and Distributed Processing: Deep hands-on experience with Azure preferred and/or GCP, including lakehouse, warehouse, orchestration, storage, streaming, and Spark-based services.
  • Data Architecture: Strong knowledge of dimensional modeling, data warehousing, lakehouse, data vault, medallion architecture, data products, and data mesh concepts.
  • Integration: Experience integrating APIs, enterprise systems, message queues, event streams, external services, and data transfer protocols.
  • Engineering Practices: Experience with Git, pull requests, code reviews, automated testing, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and production deployment of data solutions.
  • Operations and Observability: Experience with service expectations, monitoring, diagnostics, validation, lineage, data quality, incident response, performance tuning, and cost optimization.
  • AI: Practical experience applying generative and agentic AI to engineering workflows, with knowledge of integration patterns, privacy, security, evaluation, and responsible AI.
  • Analytics: Familiarity with Power BI dashboards, semantic models, and self-service analytics that turn governed data into actionable insights.

Nice to have certifications and experience:

  • Professional Azure, Databricks, and/or Power BI certification.
  • Hands-on experience with Azure Databricks, Synapse, Data Factory, Event Hubs, Data Lake Storage, Kafka, Spark Streaming, Flink, NoSQL, or graph databases.
  • Experience with metadata, master data management, lineage, access controls, privacy, and secure data handling.
  • Knowledge of software design patterns, clean code, APIs, microservices, and production AI engineering.
  • Understanding of Finance data, systems, processes, and controls in a large international organization.

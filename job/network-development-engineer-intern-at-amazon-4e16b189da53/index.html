About the job

**This is a 12-week internship role starting in June/July 2027**
Amazon Web Services (AWS), the largest consumer cloud offering in the world, is looking for Network Development Engineer Interns to join, learn and grow with our Dublin engineering team!
AWS is the world leader in providing a highly reliable, scalable, low-cost infrastructure platform in the cloud that powers hundreds of thousands of businesses around the world! This is your opportunity to work directly on the platform that impacts millions of users worldwide, all from the comfort of our Dublin offices.
As a Network Development Engineer intern in the AWS team, you will be building, growing and maintaining Amazon’s Retail and Web Services network, one of the most complex and largest scale networks of its type, leveraging the latest in high scale networking hardware, multi-tier topologies and advanced tooling so that it can match Amazon's rapid growth. Members of this team play a key role in furthering these technologies, delivering ever expanding network capacity, connectivity whilst ensuring a safe and reliable operating environment, often through development of automated systems and tooling for network operation.
You will gain exposure in a number of different roles across our Network Engineering, Network Scaling and Deployment and Network Operations teams, where you will also interact with a variety of internal customer, product and software development teams to expand your knowledge and hands on skills in the latest network technologies, practices and operating environments, preparing you for your ongoing career in AWS networking. You will work with a mentor throughout the program and have real impact on the future of our platform whilst honing your networking and software development skills in a true production environment.

- Enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Information Systems, Management Information Systems, Telecommunications or similar program. Graduation in 2028
- Experience working in a Linux/Unix environment
- Experience scripting in languages such as Python, Perl or Shell
- CCNA or equivalent level networking certification
- Network troubleshooting or support experience
- Knowledge of IP Networking fundamentals and Routing Protocols such as BGP, OSPF, R&S
- Knowledge of network hardware and packet forwarding architectures

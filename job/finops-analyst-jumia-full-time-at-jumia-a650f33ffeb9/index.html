About the job

About Jumia:

Jumia is a leading pan-African e-commerce platform. Its mission is to improve the quality of everyday life in Africa by leveraging technology to deliver innovative, convenient and affordable online services to customers, while helping businesses grow as they use Jumia's platform to better reach and serve customers.

Jumia is built around a marketplace and supported by a proprietary logistics business [Jumia Logistics] and a digital payment and fintech platform [Jumia Pay]. With over 3,000 employees, Jumia is led by top talented leaders offering a great mix of local and international talents and is backed by very high-profile shareholders. Jumia is committed to creating a sustainable impact in Africa.

In April 2019, Jumia was the first Tech company in Africa to be listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

Why this role matters

Finance Operations sits between the business, Finance and the systems that run Jumia in eight African markets. We make sure that every commercial model (marketplace, retail, advertising, payments, B2B) is billed, collected, paid and booked correctly, and we tell the business what the numbers say.

The team works AI-first. Each analyst owns an operations stream and uses AI agents every day to query data, build reports and controls, and automate recurring work. You will spend less time producing numbers and more time questioning them and acting on them.

What you will do

  • Own an operations stream end to end: Take one of the flows that make Jumia run and know it better than anyone: how the money and the goods move, which systems carry them, and where it breaks. Examples are fulfillment (logistics partners, inventory, delivery agents) and payments (payment providers, customer refunds, seller and partner payouts).
  • Find where the money leaks: Follow the money across systems, from the order to the bank. Spot what does not match, find the root cause, and get it fixed with the countries. Tell country CFOs and operations leaders, in plain words, what happened and what to do about it.
  • Build reports and controls with AI: Use AI agents and SQL on Jumia's operational and financial data to build and maintain the dashboards, control towers and alerts that the countries and management use. Check what the AI produces before it reaches a reader.
  • Automate: Turn manual, repetitive tasks into scheduled routines, and document them so that anyone in the team can run them.
  • Shape processes and systems: Translate business needs into processes, financial treatment and specifications for system owners. Test new developments and train the countries.
  • Review new business models: Give the Finance view on new partners, payment methods and commercial models before they go live: money flow, risks and what must be in place.
  • Support controls and audit: Operate SOX controls for your stream and prepare the evidence for internal and external auditors.
  • Support strategic projects: Bring analysis and practical recommendations to management, and follow action items until they are closed.

What we are looking for

Must have

  • At least 5 years of relevant experience in finance or business operations, AI operations or automation, audit, controlling, consulting or a similar analytical role
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort with large data sets, ideally with working knowledge of SQL
  • Hands-on use of AI tools in your work, and the judgement to check their output
  • A habit of validating sources before trusting a number
  • Clear and simple communication, written and spoken, with stakeholders at all levels and in different countries
  • A habit of working out loud: work-in-progress is visible and open to feedback
  • Ownership and drive: you close topics and do not wait to be chased
  • Fluent English

Nice to have

  • Experience in e-commerce, marketplace, logistics, payments or a fast-paced tech company
  • Big 4 or consulting background; exposure to SOX or internal controls
  • Experience with an ERP
  • Basic scripting (Python, JavaScript or Google Apps Script)
  • French

We Offer:

  • A unique experience in an entrepreneurial, yet structured environment
  • A unique opportunity of having strong impact in building the African e-commerce sector
  • The opportunity to become part of a highly professional and dynamic team
  • An unparalleled personal and professional growth as our longer-term objective is to train the next generation of leaders

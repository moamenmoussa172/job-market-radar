About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Ability to work East Coast hours required.
Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

Are you bready* for a change?

The Support AI team is building voice and chat systems that handle customer support at scale—helping restaurants resolve issues quickly, often before they ever reach a human.

This is high-impact, real-world AI. When these systems work well, customers get unblocked instantly. When they don’t, it directly affects a restaurant’s ability to operate. Accuracy, speed, and reliability aren’t nice-to-haves—they’re critical.

We’re early in building these capabilities. Voice AI is launching, chat AI is improving, and we’re learning from real customer interactions as we build.

You’ll work in an environment where shipping fast, learning from real interactions, and building systems that scale all have to happen at once. You’ll put things into production to see what works, measure real customer outcomes, and iterate quickly—making pragmatic calls about when to move fast and when to invest for the long term.

As a Software Engineer II on Support AI, you’ll own features and components within these systems, from technical design through implementation, rollout, and ongoing improvements to our evolving voice and chat systems. You’ll work with product, support operations, and AI engineering teammates to clarify requirements, break work into manageable steps, and make technical decisions within your area. You’ll collaborate with senior engineers on broader architecture and decisions that affect the platform.

If you’re motivated by building AI systems that work in the real world and want to see your work directly improve how restaurants get help when they need it most, this is a role where you can have immediate, visible impact.

About this roll* (Responsibilities)

  • Own delivery of features and components within our voice and chat AI systems, including technical design, implementation, testing, rollout, and iteration based on customer and operational outcomes.
  • Build and maintain integrations with third-party AI platforms and internal Toast systems, accounting for API behavior, latency, reliability, and vendor constraints.
  • Improve observability, monitoring, and outcome tracking for the components you own, using production data to identify issues and measure improvements.
  • Break component-level work into sequenced tasks, communicate progress, and identify dependencies and blockers early.
  • Partner with product, support operations, and AI/ML engineering teams to clarify requirements, identify edge cases, and translate support workflows into maintainable software.
  • Make and explain technical tradeoffs within your area, and contribute to design discussions with teammates on changes that affect the broader system.
  • Support teammates through thoughtful code reviews, pairing, documentation, and knowledge sharing.

Do you have the right ingredients*? (Requirements)

  • 2+ years of experience in software engineering, with a strong background building and maintaining production systems using Java, Kotlin, or other object-oriented languages.
  • Demonstrated ability to own a feature or component from design through production, including breaking down work, testing changes, and supporting them after release.
  • Ability to clarify incomplete requirements, evaluate technical options, and explain tradeoffs with customer needs and operational constraints in mind.
  • Strong collaborator who can work effectively across product, operations, and technical teams to deliver customer and business outcomes.
  • Proficient with AI tooling such as Cursor, Claude, and MCP servers
  • An ownership mindset: you investigate problems, identify blockers early, and seek input when needed to keep work moving.

We are looking for an Information Systems Security Engineer (ISSE) who will help maintain our systems security posture and protect our cloud landscapes. This is a hands-on role to assist in the protection of our systems, identification of threats/vulnerabilities, and the mitigation and remediation of said vulnerabilities to ensure security compliance.

What you will bring

  • Bachelor's degree, ideally in Cyber Security, Information Security, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology
  • Minimum 3 years of practical experience in a Security Engineer role working with cloud technologies
  • Knowledge of cloud security, vulnerability management, major security compliance and development frameworks and principles
  • Solid working knowledge of ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification requirements (v3.2 preferred), RGS, PGSSI-S, and ISO 27001/27017/27018
  • Familiarity with NIS2 transposition into French law and its operational implications for OSE/OIV
  • Understanding of PASSI audit methodology and ability to produce audit-ready evidence packages
  • Mastery in at least one of the SecDevOps practices, such as Security as Code, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, Secure Coding Practices, Cloud Governance and Compliance…
  • Hands-on experience with cloud-native security platforms: CNAPP, CWPP, CSPM, CIEM — on GCP, Azure, or AWS, with awareness of French-sovereign hosting constraints (e.g. S3NS, Bleu)
  • In-depth technical knowledge of cloud platforms – GCP, Azure, AWS
  • Previous work experience with the various phases of security incident response
  • Experience working in a dynamic environment requiring collaboration across multiple organizations
  • Strong analytical and reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making skills
  • Excellent written, oral communication, and documentation skills
  • A keen curiosity about all aspects of cyber security (especially in the Cloud)
  • Use industry sources to stay current with known Cloud security issues
  • Personable, reliable team player, who’s also capable of working independently

What you will built:

  • Configure, maintain, administer, and deploy cybersecurity technologies (IDS/IPS, EDR/XDR, SIEM, SOAR, cloud vulnerability assessment, CSPM, CNAPP, CASB, CIEM) in compliance with SecNumCloud v3.2 control families and ANSSI hardening guides
  • Own technical security architecture decisions for the sovereign cloud boundary — ensuring no data path, identity flow, or API surface violates SecNumCloud compartmentalization requirement.
  • Provide technical leadership in the architecture, design, deployment, management, configuration and use of Cyber Security technologies
  • Develop configuration controls and automations to identify compliance gaps
  • Identify gaps in our current processes, workflows, designs, and controls
  • Solve complex problems using data driven recommendations for changes and improvements while considering people, processes, and technology
  • Provide mentoring and contribute to the overall growth and development of the team
  • Drive threat modelling and attack-surface analysis against the LPM/NIS2 threat model; translate findings into actionable remediation roadmaps
  • Respond to and lead post-incident reviews; feed lessons learned back into PSSI and control automation
  • Mentor team members on ANSSI frameworks and SecNumCloud operational requirements
  • Native or C2 French (mandatory — ANSSI interactions and PSSI documentation are in French)

Security clearance (Habilitation): This position involves access to potentially classified information. Employment is conditional on obtaining a French government security clearance at the appropriate level. The selected candidate must submit to the corresponding SGDSN/ANS investigation process prior to onboarding.

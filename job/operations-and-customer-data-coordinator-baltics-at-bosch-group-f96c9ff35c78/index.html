About the job

Are you a details-oriented communicator who loves turning messy data into clear, actionable insights?

At Bosch Home Comfort, we are looking for a proactive Operations & Customer Data Coordinator to join our team in Jüri (Harjumaa). In this role, you will play a vital part in ensuring the quality and safety of our installed hot water preparation systems across the Baltic region. You will bridge the gap between end-user databases and real-world safety updates, reaching out directly to customers while keeping our regional and factory teams aligned.

Your key responsibilities:

  • Data analysis & management: Collect, clean, and analyze information regarding installed hot water systems. You will work with end-user data which is often incomplete, identifying missing details to prepare systems for rebuilds or safety equipment (CO detector) deliveries.
  • Proactive communication: Reach out to end-users (private individuals) to verify technical configurations, update missing customer details, and coordinate safety solutions.
  • Reporting: Prepare regular progress and operations reports for our manufacturing factory.
  • Stakeholder alignment: Communicate smoothly with Bosch Home Comfort colleagues across the Baltics and international offices.
  • External coordination: Act as the main contact point for external partners, including hotlines, support services, and technician networks.
  • Information management: Prepare and update operational information to be published on company websites and portals.
  • Excel savvy: Strong skills working with Excel and managing databases. You should feel comfortable sorting, filtering, and organizing messy data.
  • Excellent communication: Strong interpersonal skills with a friendly, professional approach to speaking with different types of people (from end-users to corporate colleagues).
  • Languages: Fluency in Estonian and English (written and verbal) is required.
  • Quality focus: A highly responsible, thorough, and structured approach to your work.
  • Technical mindset: You do not need deep technical engineering knowledge. However, a general understanding of gas heating systems is a great advantage!
  • Salary: €1,500 – €1,900 gross/monthly (depending on your experience and skills).
  • Employment contract until 01.08.2027 with outlook for extension.
  • Office in Jüri (Harjumaa) with flexible opportunities to work from home.
  • Personal and professional development within international working environment

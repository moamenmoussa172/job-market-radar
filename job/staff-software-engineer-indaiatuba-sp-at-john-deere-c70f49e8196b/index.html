Short Descriptio

As a Staff Software Engineer at John Deere in Indaiatuba/SP, you will help shape the future of our customer and dealer digital experiences by building the platforms that power them. We are looking for a hands-on Senior Platform Engineer to design, implement, and evolve cloud-native infrastructure and platform capabilities that enable rapid software delivery, operational excellence, and world-class reliability. You will work closely with development teams to create scalable, secure, and resilient solutions that support mission-critical applications and drive business value globally.

Main Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and implement platform capabilities that enhance developer productivity, improve infrastructure resilience, strengthen observability, and increase automation across the software delivery lifecycle.
  • Build, support, and continuously improve Kubernetes-based platforms that power customer-facing and dealer-facing digital products and services.
  • Develop, maintain, and optimize CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, enabling secure, efficient, and reliable software delivery.
  • Provide production support for critical applications and platforms, including incident management, root cause analysis, troubleshooting, performance tuning, and continuous reliability improvements.
  • Design and implement monitoring, logging, alerting, self-healing mechanisms, disaster recovery strategies, and multi-region failover capabilities to ensure high availability and operational excellence.
  • Collaborate closely with software engineering teams to promote and implement DevOps, DevSecOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and cloud-native best practices.
  • Drive platform standards, automation frameworks, and operational processes that improve scalability, security, and maintainability across the technology landscape.
  • Participate actively in Agile ceremonies, technical planning sessions, architecture discussions, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Leverage AI-powered tools and emerging technologies to accelerate software delivery, improve engineering efficiency, and enhance platform operations.
  • Mentor engineers and contribute to a culture of technical excellence, innovation, knowledge sharing, and continuous learning.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or equivalent.
  • Advanced English with the ability to influence decisions, lead technical discussions, present complex concepts to technical and non-technical audiences, and collaborate effectively within global teams.
  • Proven experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), or DevSecOps environments.
  • Strong hands-on experience designing, deploying, and supporting Kubernetes-based container platforms in production environments.
  • Experience working with public cloud platforms, preferably AWS, including cloud-native services and infrastructure management.
  • Proficiency in building, maintaining, and optimizing CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar automation tools.
  • Experience supporting highly available, scalable, and resilient platforms, including disaster recovery, fault tolerance, and infrastructure reliability practices.
  • Software development and scripting experience using languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, or Node.js.
  • Strong understanding of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices and automation tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or equivalent.
  • Experience implementing monitoring, observability, logging, and alerting solutions for cloud-native applications and platforms.
  • Knowledge of cloud security principles, DevSecOps practices, identity and access management, and secure software delivery.
  • Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to identify root causes and implement sustainable solutions.
  • Experience working in Agile environments and collaborating effectively with cross-functional engineering teams.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Advanced expertise in Platform Engineering, DevOps, SRE, or DevSecOps practices.
  • Strong experience managing and scaling Kubernetes-based platforms in production environments.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS cloud services and cloud-native architectures.
  • Proficiency in CI/CD automation using GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar tools.
  • Experience designing highly available, resilient, and scalable systems.
  • Strong programming and automation skills using Python, Java, Node.js, or similar languages.
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, or equivalent).
  • Ability to drive technical excellence, influence platform strategy, and collaborate with global teams.

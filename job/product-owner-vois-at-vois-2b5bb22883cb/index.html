About this Role

We are seeking a Technical Product Owner to work closely with a data engineering team, translating business and stakeholder needs into clear, testable technical requirements. You will connect technical and non-technical stakeholders, ensuring that data products address the right business problems and deliver measurable value.

The role requires sufficient technical knowledge to read SQL, understand relational data models, follow data pipeline logic and assess engineers’ query approaches. You will own and prioritise the product backlog and roadmap, define acceptance criteria and support data initiatives throughout the Agile delivery lifecycle

What you’ll do

  • Conduct structured interviews and workshops with business stakeholders, data engineers and other technical teams to understand requirements.
  • Translate business needs into clear and testable business requirements documents, epics, user stories, functional specifications and acceptance criteria.
  • Own, refine and prioritise the product backlog according to business value, product direction, dependencies and technical feasibility.
  • Develop and maintain roadmaps and release plans for data initiatives and demands.
  • Map current-state and future-state processes, identify gaps and assess the potential impact of proposed features.
  • Define data quality requirements, validation criteria and test cases for data deliverables.
  • Review completed work against agreed acceptance criteria and provide product sign-off.
  • Participate in Agile and Scrum activities, including backlog refinement, sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives.
  • Work collaboratively with onshore and offshore teams throughout day-to-day product delivery.
  • Act as a communication bridge between data engineers and non-technical stakeholders, translating information accurately in both directions.
  • Maintain current documentation covering requirements, decisions, processes and data definitions.
  • Present product requirements, decisions and findings to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Ask constructive questions and challenge technical or business assumptions when further clarification is needed.
  • Build trust across the product team and influence decisions through collaboration rather than formal authority.

Who you are

  • You have approximately four years of relevant professional experience.
  • Experience with Data Engineering is a must
  • V.good English
  • You hold a qualification in computer science, engineering or a related discipline, or possess an equivalent combination of education and professional experience.
  • You have strong SQL knowledge, including joins, common table expressions, window functions and aggregations, and can read and validate query logic.
  • You understand relational data modelling and can interpret entity-relationship diagrams.
  • You have working knowledge of ETL and ELT concepts, pipeline or DAG logic, and end-to-end data flows.
  • You have experience gathering, documenting and managing technical and non-technical requirements.
  • You can write clear user stories, acceptance criteria and supporting requirements documentation.
  • You understand Agile software development and have experience participating in Scrum ceremonies.
  • You have strong research, analytical, stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
  • You are committed to user-centred product design and development.
  • You can make informed product decisions at squad level and prioritise requirements according to value and feasibility.
  • You are comfortable communicating with data engineers and asking detailed questions without requiring extensive technical support.
  • You can present complex requirements clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • You maintain accurate documentation and ensure that requirements, decisions and data definitions remain current.

Experience working directly within or alongside a data engineering team, exposure to data governance or data catalogue platforms such as Collibra or Alation, and relevant industry knowledge would be beneficial.

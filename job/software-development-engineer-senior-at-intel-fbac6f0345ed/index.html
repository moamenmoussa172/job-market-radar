About the job

Job Details:

Job Description:

Intel's Graphics Software Engineering group is seeking an experienced and passionate Software Development Engineer to contribute to the development and enablement of Intel GPUs through innovative software solutions.

This position is suited for an experienced/senior full-stack software engineer, preferably with a background in building data-intensive systems and data pipelines, including AI-powered solutions. We are looking for an engineer who will help develop internal software platforms used daily by graphics software and hardware engineers.

You will participate in all phases of platform development, including requirements analysis, design, and implementation, and will work closely with graphics domain experts on Intel's latest graphics products.

We strongly support Agile values and a DevOps culture and continuously strive for improvement. Your work will directly impact Intel's ability to deliver high-quality graphics solutions that optimise gaming experiences and enable advanced computing applications.

This role offers the opportunity to collaborate across geographies and teams while driving innovation and delivering meaningful advancements in the graphics and GPU software domain.

Qualifications:

Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field with 6+ years of industry experience in research, development, product development, and/or validation, or a Master's Degree with 4+ years of relevant experience, or a PhD with 2+ years of relevant experience.
- Excellent knowledge of C#/.NET and SQL.
- Hands-on experience designing and building scalable, distributed systems.
- Working knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines.
- Strong analytical problem solving and troubleshooting skills.
- Strong technical communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Strong ability to work independently on complex problems.
Preferred Qualifications

- Hands-on experience developing AI-based solutions, including deep neural networks (DNNs), computer vision, and large language models (LLMs).
- Experience developing data ingestion, integration, and ETL solutions.
We are excited to welcome talented professionals to join our mission of delivering exceptional graphics solutions and driving innovation across the industry. Apply today and be a part of our journey to redefine the future of computing.

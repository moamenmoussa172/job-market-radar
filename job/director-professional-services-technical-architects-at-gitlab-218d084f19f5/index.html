An overview of this role

As the Director, Technical Architects on GitLab's AMER Professional Services team, you oversee one of the most technically credible and customer-facing groups in the organization. This team helps turn customer priorities into real adoption and measurable outcomes by guiding complex implementations, shaping new service patterns, and bringing emerging GitLab capabilities into production environments.

You'll own the Global Technical Architecture practice across both presales and delivery, managing a team of AMER Technical Architects who work on complex customer needs, early-stage offerings, and strategic engagements. This role is well suited for someone who can coach senior technical talent, bring structure to ambiguity, and keep the team focused on customer outcomes instead of task completion.

In your first year, you'll focus on stabilizing and growing the team, tightening the link between presales scoping and delivery, and turning field work into repeatable offerings. You'll work alongside architects in areas including AI, security, and broader platform architecture, while helping the team turn what they learn in the field into repeatable methods that can scale across Professional Services.

Some examples of our projects:

  • Running technical discovery, scoping, and delivery for complex Professional Services engagements in AMER
  • Turning early customer work on new GitLab capabilities into repeatable delivery kits, standards, and service patterns

What you’ll do

  • Hire, manage, and develop a growing AMER Technical Architecture team, including Intermediate, Senior, and Staff Technical Architects working across platform, AI, and security-related customer engagements.
  • Lead the global Technical Architecture practice, aligning architects in AMER, EMEA, and APJ on shared standards for scoping, statement of work review, service offerings and delivery leadership.
  • Manage the practice end to end, covering presales collaboration, engagement scoping, technical delivery oversight, staffing, utilization, and forecast alignment, with accountability for improving utilization, delivery quality, and forecast accuracy.
  • Coach architects to grow from hands-on implementers into technical leaders and credible advisors on most of their engagements, with a focus on customer outcomes, strong technical judgment, and consultative communication.
  • Partner on complex presales motions with Engagement Managers on estimation, solution framing, technical objection handling, statement of work assumptions, and technical risk to build customer confidence, improve scoping accuracy, and strengthen delivery readiness.
  • Join strategic customer conversations where senior technical expertise helps build credibility, clarify ambiguity, and improve the path to successful delivery.
  • Guide the team through first-mover delivery of new GitLab capabilities, helping shape how emerging offerings are tested, delivered, and improved in real customer environments to shorten time to production and increase successful adoption.
  • Turn lessons from strategic engagements into reusable templates, architecture guidance, migration patterns, and implementation standards that increase reuse and improve delivery consistency across the broader Professional Services organization.
  • Represent the Technical Architecture practice in AMER Professional Services management discussions and partner with adjacent functions to improve coordination, service evolution, and delivery consistency.

What you’ll bring

  • Proven experience managing senior technical professionals in a services, consulting, or Professional Services environment, with a strong track record of coaching, performance management, and career development.
  • Working knowledge of GitLab, software development, site reliability and infrastructure engineering, security, cloud architecture, and agile delivery, with enough depth to guide technical direction and build credibility with highly experienced architects.
  • Experience running consultative engagements by linking technical work to business goals and keeping teams focused on customer value and realized outcomes.
  • Experience with technically complex presales work, including discovery, scoping, estimation, solution definition, and risk assessment.
  • Experience running a billable team against utilization targets, with a clear grasp of how utilization, margin, and forecast accuracy drive Professional Services revenue.
  • Demonstrated ability to operate effectively in fast-moving environments where offerings, priorities, and organizational needs continue to evolve.
  • Experience building or improving repeatable delivery methods such as playbooks, standards, service kits, architecture patterns, or implementation guidance.
  • A servant-leadership style built on empathy and continuous improvement: you remove blockers for your team, act on what retrospectives surface, and build processes that make it easy to do the right thing.
  • Experience leading customer conversations at every level, from platform engineers to the CTO, giving clear feedback, and aligning cross-functional stakeholders around delivery direction.
  • Familiarity with GitLab-relevant domains such as AI-powered developer workflows, security, DevSecOps, or enterprise platform adoption is helpful, along with experience ramping up quickly in adjacent areas where needed.
  • Experience using AI to scale how your team scopes, documents, and delivers work.
  • Ability to travel up to 25%.

About the team

The Professional Services team helps customers adopt GitLab in ways that create lasting value. Within that organization, the Technical Architecture team handles some of the most complex and ambiguous work. This includes shaping early delivery approaches for new capabilities, contributing to high-impact customer engagements, and creating the patterns that help the wider organization scale successful services.

You'll manage a growing AMER team of Technical Architects, lead the global Technical Architecture practice, and work closely with Engagement Management, Professional Services Operations, Customer Experience Engineering, and other Professional Services colleagues. We play an important role in setting the technical standard for delivery while also helping define how new services should be scoped, delivered, and improved over time.

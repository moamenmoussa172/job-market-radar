Job Description:

Parsons is looking for an amazingly talented ABAD Senior Systems Engineer to join our team! In this role you will serve as an Engineering lead for operational sustainment of large-scale air surveillance system. **This position is located at Ramstein Air Base, Germany (International Assignment) **NO REMOTE WORK**

What You'll Be Doing:

  • Manage a technical baseline of requirements, compliance, and policy for a theater-wide Air Domain Control and Communications System
  • Lead efforts associated with network design and maintenance, fault isolation, performance monitoring, configuration management standards, principles, and techniques
  • Support hardware, software, and network systems maintenance and sustainment
  • Perform system engineering, special studies, analyses, and evaluations
  • Conduct systems modernization, integration, analysis, and sustainment, and enterprise architecture process evaluation
  • Maintaining information system compliance for an integrated communication network
  • Briefing and generating briefing information for military leadership and partner military leadership
  • Support field service representatives at sites throughout Europe

What Required Skills You'll Bring:

  • Bachelor’s degree in engineering or similar technical field plus 8+ years of relevant experience
  • Experience with technical baseline of requirements, compliance, and policy for a theater-wide Air Domain Control and Communications System
  • Experience with data & network infrastructure supporting an operational air defense system and integrated communication network
  • Familiarity with data analysis scripting tools such as python or MATLAB and compiled software languages such as Java or C
  • Secret Clearance is required
  • **This position is located at Ramstein Air Base, Germany (International Assignment) **NO REMOTE WORK**

What Desired Skills You'll Bring:

  • Familiarization with type II hypervisors
  • Familiarization with Joint All Domain Command and Control methods and architecture
  • Familiarization with U.S. and NATO Military Air Operations and Joint Air Battle Management processes
  • Familiarization with Cross Domain Solutions (CDS) and communications networks
  • IAT level II or IAM Level I+ certifications
  • Familiarity with secure cloud computing and centralized networks
  • Familiarity with failover server architecture and bugout Concept of Operations (CONOPS

Security Clearance Requirement:

An active Secret security clearance is required for this position.​

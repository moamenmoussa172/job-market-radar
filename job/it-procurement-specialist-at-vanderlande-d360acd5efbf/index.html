About the job

Job Title

IT Procurement Specialist

Job Description

The Company and Department

Vanderlande empowers the world’s most demanding airports and distribution operations to move faster, smarter, and more sustainably. As a trusted long-term partner, Vanderlande designs, delivers, and manages end-to-end logistics automation — combining best-in-class mechanical systems with cyber-secure operational software and data-driven services that keep mission-critical infrastructure performing at its peak.

The IT License Specialist is part of the IT Asset Management team, which is part of the Enterprise Architecture and Governance team within ICT. This is a dynamic team that plays a central role in areas such as Cyber Security, Enterprise Architecture, Data Analytics, and IT Asset Management.

About the Role

To successfully execute Vanderlande projects and processes, Vanderlande relies on hundreds of applications and software packages worldwide. The software request, purchase, registration and compliance are managed by the ICT Asset Management team.

The team operates in an informal and collegial atmosphere. We support each other by sharing responsibilities, ensuring that we can provide the best possible service to all Vanderlande colleagues and customers, even with a small team.

The Position

As an IT License Specialist, you will handle operational requests for new software, license renewals, expansions or changes, as well as consultancy and hardware. You will assess, process and follow up on these requests to help ensure alignment with Vanderlande policies and applicable legislation.

Depending on the request, you will contact suppliers for quotations and review these with the relevant stakeholders. Once agreement and approvals are in place, you will initiate and complete the purchase order process in the enterprise resource planning (ERP) system. After the software, hardware or service has been delivered, you will notify the requester and coordinate the information needed for invoice processing. In this way, you contribute to an effective and reliable ICT procurement process.

This role suits someone with an interest in ICT and the analytical skills to assess complex requests. Knowledge of, or a willingness to learn about, procurement processes and financial administration is important.

Responsibilities and Tasks

IT Procurement for Software, Hardware and Consultancy

• Independently create and manage Purchase Orders (POs) for ICT, in line with agreed processes and approval requirements.

• Maintain accurate procurement master data and support data quality across relevant systems.

• Monitor purchase orders and report on progress, working with suppliers and internal stakeholders to resolve questions or issues relating to POs, invoices and quality.

• Provide input in software tenders regarding software and license metrics.

• Place call-off orders under framework agreements for ICT based on input from Procurement and Product Owner.

• Identify recurring issues in ordering and other supply chain processes, and contribute to practical, sustainable improvements.

License Management Support

  • Assist in the execution of software license management strategies in collaboration with IT, Procurement, and Finance teams.
  • Support the procurement of software licenses by gathering requirements, requesting quotes, and reviewing offers with stakeholders.
  • Help monitor software usage and ensure alignment with license agreements and internal policies.

License Desk Operations

  • Operate, maintain and improve the Global Software License Desk as the central point for software license requests and inquiries.
  • Follow and actively monitor procedures for license registration, renewals, and updates, and clearly communicate the outcomes to all relevant stakeholders.
  • Ensure accurate documentation and communication of license-related changes to relevant teams.
  • Support compliance with licensing terms and internal governance processes.

Audit Preparation & Support

  • Assist in preparing for internal and external software license audits.
  • Help reconcile software usage with entitlements and gather necessary documentation.
  • Support the implementation of corrective actions based on audit findings.

Communication & Awareness

  • Provide guidance to end users and departments on license request procedures and best practices.
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of licensing rules and vendor-specific terms.
  • Help communicate changes in licensing processes or tools to relevant stakeholders.

Contract & Procurement Support

  • Collaborate with Procurement and other stakeholders to support license renewals and contract updates.
  • Assist in reviewing vendor proposals and tracking contract terms and expiration dates.
  • Maintain accurate records of license agreements and related documentation.

Reporting & Data Accuracy

  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date license records in the Software Asset Management system.
  • Support reporting on license usage, compliance status, and potential risks.
  • Ensure data completeness and reliability in license management tools.

Your qualifications and skills

  • Accurate and detail-oriented with strong analytical skills
  • Curious, willing to learn and comfortable suggesting improvements
  • Able to work independently, take ownership and seek support when appropriate
  • Collaborative and willing to share knowledge, ask questions and support colleagues
  • Able to communicate clearly with colleagues across departments
  • Skilled in using MS Office applications such as Word and Excel
  • Experience with systems like TOPdesk or JD Edwards is a plus
  • Able to translate vendor license terms into clear internal procedures

What You Bring

  • A relevant vocational or higher professional education level (MBO+ /HBO level), or an equivalent combination of relevant education and practical experience
  • Relevant experience in software license administration, procurement or IT asset management; experience gained through different career paths is welcome
  • Familiarity with Software Asset Management principles, tools, and processes
  • A demonstrable interest in ICT, particularly software and licensing
  • Experience in a technical or engineering environment is an advantage, but not required
  • Good understanding of common license models and vendor terms
  • Able to communicate effectively in English and/or Dutch, both verbally and in writing
  • Familiarity with systems like TOPdesk and JD Edwards is a plus

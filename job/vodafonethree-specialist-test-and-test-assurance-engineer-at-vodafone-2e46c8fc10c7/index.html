What you’ll do
As a Test Engineer, you’ll play a key role in ensuring that new and existing services are delivered into the network with confidence, quality, and reliability. Working closely with design, operations, vendors, and delivery partners, you’ll help shape and validate innovative telecom solutions before they reach customers. You’ll take ownership of test programmes from planning through execution, while helping to maintain robust test environments and supporting the continuous evolution of testing capabilities across the team.
You will:

  • Lead the analysis, design, execution, and reporting of testing activities across a range of network and technology programmes.
  • Drive the successful delivery of test programmes under your responsibility, ensuring quality outcomes are achieved within agreed timelines.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders, partners, and third-party suppliers to identify, manage, and resolve defects, issues, and risks.
  • Validate complex telecom solutions, services, and products to ensure they meet business, customer, and operational requirements.
  • Apply Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC) methodologies to technical projects and programmes.
  • Continuously improve test cases, documentation, procedures, and testing approaches to enhance quality and efficiency.
  • Support, maintain, and optimise test tools, platforms, systems, and services within your remit.
  • Use test management and automation tools to streamline delivery and improve testing effectiveness.
  • Provide technical guidance, coaching, and leadership to fellow engineers, fostering a collaborative and supportive team environment.
  • Build trusted relationships with internal stakeholders and external partners, balancing challenge and support to achieve shared goals.
  • Contribute to the stability, integrity, and operational performance of critical testing environments and services.
  • Help ensure technology changes are delivered with the right balance of quality, speed, cost-effectiveness, and customer focus.

Who you are

  • You will bring proven experience within a technical telecommunications environment.
  • You will have strong knowledge of 4G and 5G architectures, call flows, protocols, standards, and supporting systems.
  • You will have a solid understanding of the Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC) and experience applying it to technical programmes.
  • You will be confident using test management tools such as HP ALM and working with test automation tools and/or simulators.
  • You will hold an ISTQB certification or equivalent testing qualification.
  • You will be an effective communicator who can build strong partnerships across teams, stakeholders, and third-party organisations.
  • You will be comfortable analysing issues, troubleshooting complex environments, and driving resolutions through to completion.
  • You will be able to balance analytical thinking with practical decision-making to deliver high-quality outcomes.
  • You will demonstrate excellent prioritisation skills and a focus on delivering value for customers and the business.
  • You will enjoy working collaboratively while also taking ownership and accountability for your deliverables.
  • You will bring experience of testing complex projects within the telecommunications sector.
  • You may have experience in one or more of the following areas: IMS, Messaging, Billing, MVNO, Subscriber Data Management, Service Layer platforms, or Cloud-based technology delivery.
  • You will be passionate about technology, continuous improvement, and creating reliable solutions that make a real impact.
  • You will thrive in an environment that values teamwork, curiosity, innovation, and inclusive collaboration.

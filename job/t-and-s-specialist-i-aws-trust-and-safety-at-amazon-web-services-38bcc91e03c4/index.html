About the job

The T&S Specialist I is a specialist role in AWS Trust & Safety covering customer account investigations, service-related case resolution, and abuse mitigation across AWS infrastructure and services. You will investigate customer problems with their Amazon services accounts — for cases that may be more time-sensitive and where SOPs or technical documentation may not be well-defined. You will act as a developing subject matter expert (SME) in your domain area, identifying root causes, flagging known risks, and providing escalation support to peers and cross-functional partners.
You will collaborate across T&S and with internal stakeholders (Policy, Legal, Product and Engineering Teams) to drive high-quality business support for AWS customers. The role is both operational and strategic. Operationally, you will dive into technical details to diagnose issues, determine resolution for high-impact scenarios, guide customers toward self-service resources, and escalate appropriately when issues are large, critical, or complex. Strategically, you will identify opportunities and risks with customers' technical decisions that may have financial or data security impact, contribute to knowledge base improvements, and help shape more effective SOPs and customer-facing documentation.
A defining feature of this role is its relationship to continuous improvement. You will not only resolve cases — you will work with your team and management to improve how customer issues are identified, documented, and resolved, using case outcomes and process insights to drive efficiency over time. You will also contribute to team development by helping train new peers, participating in the interview process, and submitting suggestions that make knowledge base articles and user documentation more effective. As a technically proficient member of the team with 2+ years of experience and working knowledge across security, account management, and Amazon services, you will also act in a mentoring capacity whenever possible, helping ensure the continued growth and success of the AWS Trust & Safety team.
Key job responsibilities
- Become a subject matter expert (SME) across complex, legally sensitive abuse types — including child safety and exploitation, image-based sexual abuse, terrorist and violent extremist content, AI-generated content and all types of abuse outlined in the AWS Acceptable Use Policy — spanning multiple AWS services, providing guidance, escalation support, and deep-dive analysis for internal and external stakeholders.
- Assess and own escalated AWS customer issues in order to provide accurate support. Act as a high-judgement decision maker for escalated situations where there may be no established SOP.
- Act as the Voice of the Customer by reporting and acting on observed areas for improvement while actively seeking solutions to customer needs, communicating trends to leadership, and suggesting innovative solutions on behalf of the customer experience.
- Drive projects that improve support-related functions and processes. Define project goals and objectives with cross-functional teams; create detailed project plans (timelines, milestones, resource allocation); and manage timelines to ensure on-time delivery.
- Assume responsibility for developing detailed knowledge about AWS specific services and then using that knowledge to identify gaps.
- Be a team player with the ability to work in large groups and independently
- Due to the 24x7 operations of the business, must be able to work a flexible work schedule that may include nights, weekends, holidays, overtime, etc.
About the team
You'll be joining the AWS Trust & Safety (T&S) Team, a global unit dedicated to protecting AWS services from abuse while maintaining customer trust. The team acts as the first line of defense for AWS, analyzing trends and reporting findings to service teams. Your role will involve working closely with various stakeholders — including Policy, Legal, and AWS Account teams — to develop scalable and sustainable solutions that protect against and mitigate abuse. The T&S team is crucial to ensuring that Amazon Web Services remains the most customer-centric company on Earth.

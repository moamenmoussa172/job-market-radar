About the job

At Synechron, we harness the power of digital transformation to drive business success. As a global consulting firm, we combine creativity with advanced technology to deliver innovative solutions across industries. Having been recognized with multiple employer awards, we are committed to building talented teams and creating a dynamic work environment.
We are hiring a Web Intern
Novi Sad Office

Key Responsibilities:

  • Work on a full-stack development project, with Java as the core backend technology, under the guidance of a dedicated mentor
  • Learn and apply best practices in development
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to development discussions
  • Assist with bug fixing and code optimization
  • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code

Skills:

  • Strong understanding of Java and its programming concepts
  • Knowledge of Java development tools such as Eclipse or IntelliJ
  • Experience with relational databases and SQL
  • Ability to write clean and efficient code
  • Good understanding of software development life cycle (SDLC)
  • Familiarity with microservices architecture
  • Knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React or Angular

Your profile:

  • No prior professional experience required
  • Currently enrolled in the final year of a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Computer Science or a related field
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Positive attitude and strong work ethic
  • Good problem-solving skills and ability to take initiative
  • Commitment to excellence and attention to detail

For any additional information or questions, feel free to reach out to us at Recruitment-Serbia@synechron.com.

We appreciate the interest of all applicants. Please note that only those whose qualifications align closely with the position requirements will be contacted for the next steps in the selection process.

All applications will be handled with confidentiality.

We are looking forward to meeting you!

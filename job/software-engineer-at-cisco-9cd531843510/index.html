About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Hungary.

Meet the Team

Outshift by Cisco is an innovation incubator building the next generation of businesses for Cisco. We explore new markets, test bold ideas, and turn early-stage technologies—such as Agentic AI and Quantum Networking—into growth opportunities. We operate as a startup within one of the world's most trusted companies, moving fast and thriving in ambiguity.

Learn more at https://outshift.cisco.com.

Your Impact As a Developer in the Outshift marketing team, you will shape how our brand shows up across the web. You will work with designers, marketers, and researchers to maintain and innovate our marketing web properties and data dashboards. This role allows you to lead the technical aspects of high-visibility projects while working at the frontier of technology.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain high-performance marketing websites using Next.js and headless CMS platforms.
  • Drive technical decision-making, establish coding standards, and promote best practices for scalable, maintainable code.
  • Ensure website responsiveness across all device types and conduct performance testing to optimize for speed and reliability.
  • Integrate with databases (e.g., MySQL, SQL, RDS) to store and retrieve data.
  • Partner with collaborators to translate requirements into functional, interaction-first web experiences.
  • Define project scope, deliverables, and timelines while providing accurate estimations.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience as a web developer.
  • Proficiency in Next.js, headless CMS platforms, JavaScript, React.js, and Node.js.
  • Experience with cloud technologies including Lambda, EC2, Docker, and API Gateway.
  • Demonstrated knowledge of analytics integrations.
  • Understanding of software engineering principles, design patterns, and development methodologies (Agile, Scrum).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Good interpersonal skills with the ability to clearly communicate technical concepts and resolve conflicts.
  • Ability to work effectively and independently across different global time zones.

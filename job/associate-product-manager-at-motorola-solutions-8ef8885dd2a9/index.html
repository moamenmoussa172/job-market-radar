We have a fantastic city center office in the heart of Edinburgh, as well as locations in central London and Uxbridge, and fully embrace a hybrid home/office working model.

Role description

We require an Associate Product Manager to play crucial role within our agile development teams, working directly with engineering teams day-to-day as part of the Product Management group. This individual will be responsible for assisting in defining and prioritizing the product backlog, ensuring that the development team understands the vision and goals of the product as defined by the broader Product Management team. They will drive the product's success by making informed decisions and ensuring alignment with the overall product strategy.

What this role involves:

  • Develop and maintain a prioritized product backlog, ensuring it reflects stakeholder needs and business value.
  • Work closely with the development teams to ensure the delivery of valuable product increments.
  • Collaborate with the Product Management team to define the product vision, roadmap, and strategy.
  • Partner with your Senior Product Manager to refine the roadmap and break down features.
  • Collaborating with engineering, product management, UX, and other Motorola teams.
  • Communicating product updates, progress, and risks to stakeholders.

What we are looking for in you:

  • Experience working in an agile environment as a Product Manager or Product Owner.
  • High degree of organization, attention to detail, initiative and personal accountability.
  • Ability to prioritize and make rapid decisions in a fast-paced environment.
  • Creative and innovative thinker with strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Self-motivated, effective leader who repeatedly goes the extra mile.
  • Ability to communicate requirements from both the user and technical perspectives.
  • Ability to engage, influence and inspire stakeholders to drive collaboration and alignment.
  • Ability to inspire others towards a shared product vision, strategy, business goals and objectives.


Basic Requirements

  • Minimum 2 years of experience as a Product Manager, Product Manager, or similar role
  • Minimum 4 years of experience in an Agile software development organization
  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field or equivalent experience

About the job

Data & AI Intern - Intelligent Operations

Description -

Before applying please note that, to be eligible for this position, you must be currently enrolled at a university in Spain, remain enrolled until the end of the internship, and be eligible to sign an internship agreement with your university. You must also be available to start the internship in February 2027 and continue participating in the program until June–July 2027.

We are looking for a motivated student to help us explore how data, analytics and AI can transform the way we run business operations and make decisions.

Today, many business reviews still require significant manual effort to collect information from different sources, consolidate data, understand performance and identify the insights that require attention.

The objective of this project is to explore a different approach: connecting data, analytics and AI to build the foundations for Intelligent Operations.

What you will work on

You will work on a real business use case, starting with the automation and augmentation of business reviews.

Together with the team, you will:

  • Connect and integrate data from multiple business and operational sources.
  • Structure and prepare data so it can be reliably used for analytics and AI.
  • Develop analytics to understand performance, trends and key business drivers.
  • Explore how AI can automatically identify relevant trends, deviations and areas requiring attention.
  • Use Generative AI to generate insights and help users interact naturally with business data.
  • Prototype automated and AI-assisted business reviews.
  • Explore how these capabilities could evolve towards more proactive Intelligent Operations, where data and AI help identify issues and opportunities and support faster decision-making.

The project will combine data engineering, analytics, Generative AI and business understanding, with an emphasis on experimentation and building tangible prototypes.

What we are looking for

We are looking for a student in Data Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Engineering, Data Science or a related field, with:

  • Knowledge of Python and SQL.
  • Experience or interest in data analytics and data modelling.
  • Familiarity with Power BI or similar visualization tools.
  • Interest in Generative AI, LLMs and AI agents.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Curiosity to understand the business context behind the data.
  • An experimental mindset and willingness to turn ideas into working prototypes.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.

What you will learn

This internship provides the opportunity to work at the intersection of business, data and AI, applying emerging technologies to real operational challenges.

You will gain hands-on experience across the journey from data integration and analytics to AI-generated insights and automation, while exploring how AI can fundamentally change the way business operations are managed.

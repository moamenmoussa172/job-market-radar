Location: this is a hybrid position based from our New York City office located in the Hudson Yards.

What you'll build

As a Forward Deployed AI Engineer, you will independently build AI-native enterprise applications and work directly with customers to solve complex business problems. You will own technical workstreams, design production-grade AI solutions, and collaborate with architects to deliver scalable, secure, enterprise-ready AI applications. This role is designed for engineers with 3–7 years of experience who are passionate about Generative AI, cloud-native engineering, and customer success.

In this role, you will:

  • Design and implement production-grade AI applications using modern software engineering practices.
  • Develop AI agents using LLMs, RAG, MCP, tool calling, structured outputs, and multi-agent orchestration.
  • Build integrations with SAP products, enterprise applications, APIs, databases, and external services.
  • Deploy and operate AI applications on SAP AI Core, SAP BTP, Kubernetes, and public cloud platforms.
  • Own customer-facing technical workstreams and translate business requirements into working AI solutions.
  • Develop reusable frameworks, accelerators, and reference implementations.
  • Perform code reviews and mentor Associate AI Developers.
  • Optimize AI systems for performance, reliability, observability, and cost.
  • Collaborate with architects, product engineering, and customer stakeholders.
  • Drive innovation through prototypes and emerging AI technologies.

What you bring

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
  • 3–7 years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Strong Python programming skills with experience in Java, TypeScript, or JavaScript.
  • Hands-on experience with LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, or similar frameworks.
  • Strong understanding of LLMs, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, MCP, and agentic AI.
  • Experience building REST APIs, microservices, and cloud-native applications.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Git, CI/CD, and AWS, Azure, or GCP.
  • Experience integrating enterprise applications and databases.
  • Exposure to SAP BTP, SAP AI Core, Joule, or SAP Business AI services is highly desirable.
  • Excellent communication, analytical thinking, ownership, and customer engagement skills.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Knowledge Graphs, GraphRAG, AI evaluation, and AI observability.
  • Experience delivering enterprise AI projects in customer-facing roles.
  • Open-source contributions, hackathons, or technical community involvement.
  • Experience using GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, or similar AI-assisted engineering tools.

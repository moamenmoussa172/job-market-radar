Location: Singapore

Expected start and end date: Jan to May/June 2027

Working hours: Full time (5days/week) availability

Internship Type: University Credit-bearing Internship

SAP is a global market leader for business software and thus contributes a considerable part of the world's economic power grid. At SAP you get your chance to put your ideas into action with maximum impact. SAP AI Business Services Singapore is an innovation arm of SAP. The group significantly contributes to SAP's product portfolio and extends its leading position in the market by identifying and shaping emerging IT trends and generating breakthrough technologies through AI.

SAP AI teams combine the creativity and agility of a start-up with the backbone of a world market leader in business software. The focus of the team is to embed and develop Machine Learning solutions into business enterprise solutions. In collaboration with leading universities, partners, customers, and SAP product groups, SAP drives the development of promising ideas and prototypes into market-ready software for maximum customer value.

EXPECTATIONS AND TASKS

You will work as a student intern in fast-paced research projects and apply software engineering principles and concepts to help create new and innovative research prototypes. This internship gives you the unique opportunity to work on both research – developing novel Machine Learning models, and production – deploying said models for large-scale commercial use. Your work in this internship will directly impact the millions of people currently using SAP solutions. The duration of the internship is 3-6 months full-time.

SKILLS AND COMPETENCIES

  • You are currently studying for a degree in: Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Information Technology, Information Systems, or related fields
  • You are an independent learner with a strong interest in machine/deep learning and related frameworks (Tensorflow, Keras, etc.)
  • You have excellent communication skills: oral, written, and in presentations
  • You have hands-on experience in programming languages such as Python
  • Background in basic machine learning techniques is a plus

To be considered for this internship, your set of application documents should contain a cover letter, a CV, copies of your obtained degrees and certifications.

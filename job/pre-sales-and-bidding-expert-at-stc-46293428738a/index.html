Pre-Sales and Bidding Expert

stc · SA, Saudi Arabia

Onsite · Pre-sales / Sales Engineer · Posted 24 November 2026

About the job

Job Purpose:

The role holder is responsible for leading and supporting pre-sales and bidding activities within the Business-Critical segment, including solution design, proposal development, and bid management. The role focuses on analyzing customer requirements, developing technically and commercially sound proposals, and coordinating with internal and external stakeholders to ensure successful bid submissions and improved win rates, in alignment with Specialized’s business objectives and approved procedures.

Key Functional Accountability:

  • Identify and support qualification of new business opportunities with enterprise and government clients within the BusinessCritical segment.
  • Develop strong understanding of ICT solutions (infrastructure, IT, and digital services) to effectively address Business-Critical client requirements.
  • Analyze RFPs/RFQs from technical and commercial perspectives and define appropriate solution approaches.
  • Prepare and deliver high-quality proposals, presentations, and solution documents aligned with customer needs and value proposition.
  • Lead the development of end-to-end technical and commercial proposals, including solution architecture, specifications, and BoQs.
  • Provide technical and commercial inputs to bids in coordination with Business Operations and relevant stakeholders.
  • Coordinate with Technology, Sales, and Product teams to ensure feasibility and alignment of proposed solutions with client requirements.
  • Identify and mitigate risks within the presales and bidding lifecycle to support successful deal closure.
  • Evaluate bid opportunities and contribute to go/no-go decisions based on defined assessment criteria.
  • Support the development and improvement of bidding strategies, templates, and proposal frameworks.
  • Coordinate cross-functional inputs (Sales, Customer Care, Technology, Legal, Regulatory) to ensure alignment of proposals from technical, commercial, and compliance perspectives.
  • Participate in vendor evaluation and selection processes to support bid requirements.
  • Ensure alignment of proposal timelines with internal approvals and customer deadlines.
  • Maintain and update bid documentation, templates, and proposal repositories to improve efficiency.
  • Analyze win/loss outcomes and support continuous improvement of bidding strategies and success rates.
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to develop integrated, end-to-end solutions in coordination with relevant business units.

Job Requirement:

Educational Level: Bachelor's Degree

Minimum Years of Relevant Experience: 3 to 5 years

Most Preferred Education Field: Business Administration specialized in Sales (preferred), Engineering or any related field

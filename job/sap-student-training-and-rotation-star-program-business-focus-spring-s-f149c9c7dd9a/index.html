About the SAP STAR Program

The SAP Student Training and Rotation (STAR) Program is a multi-year undergraduate rotational program that prepares students for full-time employment and long-term success at SAP or within our vast ecosystem of customers and partners after graduation. The STAR program allows students to get hands-on experience and rotate between multiple lines of business such as SAP’s Product & Engineering, Customer Services and Delivery, and Customer Success board areas. Selected students will be eligible to complete an international rotation at their choice of one of over 200 worldwide office locations. Visit our Careers Page to learn more.

What You’ll Build

Position title: SAP Student Training and Rotation (STAR) Program-Business Focus Spring/Summer 2027[Newtown Square]

Location: Hybrid - Newtown Square, PA

Expected start date to end date:

Semester School Students: May 17, 2027 – July 31, 2029/2030
Quarter School Students: March 29, 2027 – July 31, 2029/2030

Working hours: 10-20 hours per week during the School Year / 40 hours per week during Summer Break

STAR students first receive a full range of formal trainings on SAP’s core technology stack and soft skills prior to entering their specific department and throughout their rotations. Students are expected to dedicate to full-time employment when on break from their university and part-time during their academic terms (10 hours per week minimum during part-time).

Top performers who successfully graduate from university will be well positioned for full-time employment at SAP. Graduates of the program will likely find their ideal job in the areas of customer success, strategy & operations, solution advisory, and functional consulting.

About this Role

We are seeking an M365 Email Security Specialist to support, administer and optimise Microsoft 365 services, with a particular focus on Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Email Security. The role will help protect business communications from phishing, spam, malware, spoofing and other email-based threats while maintaining service availability across VOIS global locations.

What you’ll do

  • Resolve end-user issues relating to Microsoft 365 services.
  • Administer, optimise and govern Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Email Security.
  • Monitor service performance and health, implementing corrective action where required.
  • Collaborate with IT teams to support seamless integration and operation of Microsoft 365 services.
  • Document support processes, solutions and best practices.
  • Stay informed about Microsoft 365 capabilities, updates and industry developments.
  • Provide guidance and training to L1 support colleagues and end users.
  • Participate in an on-call rotation for support outside standard working hours.

Who you are

  • You have working knowledge of the Microsoft 365 suite, Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID.
  • You have experience with Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Email Security services.
  • You can analyse service issues and identify practical solutions.
  • You collaborate effectively in a changing technical environment.
  • You communicate technical information clearly and maintain accurate documentation.
  • You are committed to continuous learning and service improvement.

What's in it for you

  • Exposure to Microsoft 365 services in a global enterprise environment.
  • Opportunities to collaborate with multidisciplinary IT teams.
  • Experience supporting business-critical email security services.
  • Participation in service optimisation, governance and continuous improvement.
  • Opportunities to guide colleagues and strengthen knowledge-sharing capabilities.

What skills you will learn

  • Advanced administration of Microsoft Defender for Office 365.
  • Email threat detection, investigation and mitigation.
  • Microsoft 365 service health monitoring and optimisation.
  • Identity and access integration using Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID.
  • Technical documentation, stakeholder communication and support-process improvement.

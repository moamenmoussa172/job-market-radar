Your impact


Mollie is evolving from a payment processor into a full financial services provider, and our infrastructure needs to be as ambitious as our vision.

As part of our Developer Enablement domain, you'll play a crucial role in shaping the CI/CD infrastructure that drives our continuous integration, developer workflows and deployment pipelines, providing a foundation for rapid and reliable software delivery.

Join us as a Platform Engineer within the Build team, where you’ll be at the forefront of maintaining and optimising our CI/CD tools, ensuring stability, scalability, and efficiency across our platforms. Your work will empower our engineering teams to deliver high-quality software with confidence, enabling our Engineers to build products that will eliminate the financial bureaucracy for our customers.

What you'll ship

  • Oversee the health, stability and scalability of CI/CD infrastructure to support Mollie's product delivery.
  • Monitor and respond to outages in those systems to maintain high availability, reliability and operational standards.
  • Develop, scale and implement strategies, guidelines, and governance of CI/CD systems while future-proofing their architecture for security, compliance and resilience concerns.
  • Optimize and balance CI/CD compute and storage usage towards efficient resource usage while keeping developer experience in mind.
  • Investigate, diagnose, and resolve technical issues related to CI/CD tooling with internal customers, working collaboratively with other engineering teams to minimize to maintain seamless workflows.

What you'll bring

  • Good grasp of CI Systems and hands-on experience with Gitlab or similar (such as Jenkins, Buildkite, etc.), with a strong focus on self-hosted operations.
  • Some experience with cloud-native Deployment and GitOps solutions such as Gitlab, ArgoCD or similar(Flux, GoCD, Spinnaker, Harness, etc), with strong focus on self-hosted operations.
  • Prior experience with at least one major cloud-based provider as well as Infrastructure-as-Code tools such as Terraform.
  • Comfortable running, monitoring and developing systems on Kubernetes and using tools like Helm. You're also familiar with cloud-native container tools and building optimized images.
  • Good problem-solving and investigative skills, with a creative approach to tackling challenges using observability and debugging tools.
  • The ability to work cross-functionally, communicate effectively, and maintain clear documentation while having a customer empathy mindset towards product and application engineers.

Nice to have

  • Previous experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Previous experience building and operating continuous integration with Gitlab.
  • Previous experience building and operating deployment platforms based on ArgoCD.

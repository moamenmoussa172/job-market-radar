About the job

Job Description & Summary

We’re a team that transforms vision into tech-enabled reality. We look for those who see technology not just as tools or platforms, but as the invisible infrastructure behind strategy, performance, and growth. We turn bold ideas into scalable, secure, and human-centered solutions.

SHAPING TOMORROW

Make a real impact, today, and tomorrow, through work that matters. Here, you will tackle today’s toughest challenges with a future-focused mindset; so our clients turn to us first when it matters most.

Make a real impact

In this dynamic environment, we are looking for an ambitious and self-driven Business Analyst to join our Data Engineering team in Athens.

Work closely with a talented team of data engineers, visualization experts and app developers to solve important and challenging problems for clients and internal teams within Greece and abroad. As a Business Analyst, you will be the bridge between business and technical teams, translating complex requirements into innovative data solutions.

Your Key Responsibilities

  • Facilitate requirement-gathering workshops and manage conflicting stakeholder needs across business and technical teams
  • Document and communicate your findings as well as train colleagues and clients on best practices
  • Effectively communicate with business and technical teams at all levels
  • Contribute to the design of logical data solutions with clear trade-off analysis
  • Translate business requirements into technical specifications, data mappings, and logical data flow diagrams in collaboration with data architects
  • Conduct current state vs. future state analysis and gap assessment
  • Create and maintain high-quality documentation (BRD, FDD, data dictionaries) with full traceability to technical specs
  • Preparation and execution of SIT, QA, and UAT scenarios in collaboration with technical teams and client
  • Support end-to-end data solution delivery from source to target state

What you bring matters

Should have

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems Engineering or any other relevant field
  • 2-3 years of proven experience in Business & Functional Analysis on data projects (Data Warehouse, Lakehouse, Data Migration, BI implementations)
  • Knowledge of SQL and Python or similar scripting language
  • Knowledge of data modeling concepts (Star Schema, Snowflake, Normalization)
  • Ability to think through end-to-end data flows and design trade-offs (complexity vs. usability, cost vs. performance)
  • Experience in defining test scenarios and supporting testing phases
  • Excellent planning skills and a project management mindset, with experience in scope prioritization
  • Strong facilitation skills to lead workshops and build consensus with multiple stakeholders
  • Strong oral and written communication skills in Greek and English
  • Analytical thinking and creativity to solve complex problems
  • Self-motivation, commitment to quality, and a collaborative mindset in multidisciplinary teams
  • Commitment to providing excellent quality work and building client relationships
  • Passion to work in multidisciplinary teams and projects

Nice to have

  • Experience with BI/Visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker)
  • Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies
  • Experience with project management tools (Jira, Azure DevOps) for requirement tracking and backlog management
  • Experience with cloud data and analytics platforms, such as Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, or Azure data services

About the job

About BlaBlaCar
BlaBlaCar is the world’s leading community-based travel app enabling 27 million members a year to carpool or travel by bus in 21 countries. Our team of 800 employees counts over 50 nationalities and is spread across our 5 global offices, 30% working fully remotely.

Your Mission

We are looking for an intern to join the Strategy & Business Analyst - Western Europe team at BlaBlaCar starting from January 2027. The team’s mission is to support strategic decision-making of our different business units in Western Europe, including Carpooling, BlaBlaCar Buses, and more recently Train! We do so by delivering reports, producing analyses, building dashboards and running financial models.

We also help steer the company’s strategy more broadly, in particular by supporting the Sustainability and Marketing department.

Your role as an intern will be to deliver strategic analyses and reporting tools to help the teams improve both performance and user experience.

Your responsibilities

  • Building a strong understanding of the key levers impacting our carpool & train performance and recommend actions to improve our operations
  • Building positive and trustful relationship with business teams to identify their needs, challenges and opportunities
  • Improving our current reporting framework and deliver reports using Tableau software
  • Supporting the Western Europe team on ad-hoc analyses
  • Contributing to develop our ESG analytical tools to support the Sustainability manager

Your qualifications

  • You are currently enrolled in a Business or Engineering School seeking a 6-month internship starting January 2027
  • 1 to 2 previous internship experiences in consulting, finance, business intelligence or other relevant analytical position
  • Strong analytical and problem solving skills, you love numbers and like to think “outside the box”
  • Excellent communication skills: ability/experience in synthesizing and communicating data clearly
  • Solid knowledge of spreadsheets and presentation programs (e.g. Google Slides)
  • Thorough attention to details
  • Full working proficiency in English, knowledge of French is nice to have
  • Fit with our BlaBlaPrinciples
  • Nice to have: proficiency in data visualization tools (Tableau) and SQL (or eager to learn)
  • Having an internship agreement from your school or university is mandatory. We won’t be able to proceed with your application otherwise.

What we have to offer

  • Salary : 1,600 euros gross/ month
  • Hybrid working mode
  • One holiday per month
  • Local meal plan policy (Swile card)
  • 50% transportation paid (Forfait Mobilité Durable)
  • Free unlimited carpooling & bus rides
  • Personal growth via trainings, mentorship, and internal mobility programs
  • Regular team building events
  • Mental health support through Moka.care
  • 1 day off per year to test our product
  • 1 day per year for social engagements with non-profits

Interested in joining the ride?

  • a 30-mins video-call with your Talent Acquisition Manager to get to know you and answer your questions
  • a 45-mins video call with Robin Passaga, Strategy & Business Analyst, to get to know you, assess your soft skills and present the team
  • a home assignment to evaluate your technical skills followed by a 60-min video-call with Robin and another Strategy & Business analytics team member

Our hiring process lasts on average 25-30 days, offers usually come within 48 hours.

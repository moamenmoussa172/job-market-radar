Our mission is simple: safeguard the digital world and empower people to work securely and confidently. Join us in our pursuit to defend data and protect people.

The Role

We are looking for a Software Engineer to join our ITE (Inbound Threat Engineering) backend services team, providing the core inbound email scanning engine. Our stack comprises services written in Python and deployed in AWS across the world, with some new development planned for Go. AI tools feature heavily in our development and support processes, and we continue to explore how to best utilise the technology. At our team level we work closely with other parts of the business so we can provide maximum efficacy and protection for our users.

Responsibilities

In our Engineering teams, we deeply appreciate DevOps culture, as we build and run our services. Our day to day involves working with Product Management to provide increased efficacy and implement new features, optimising our infrastructure for cost and reliability and integrating with other Proofpoint systems to expand our product offering.

You’ll have the opportunity to:

  • Design and build reliable distributed systems that handle volumes of data with low latency.
  • Maintain and support business-critical systems where scale and latency are front-of-mind.
  • Contribute code to production regularly using our CI/CD pipeline
  • Address challenges related to processing highly sensitive data in a secure and privacy-conscious way.
  • Learn to build with security in mind.
  • Provide technical leadership, mentor peers in a supportive way, contribute to design reviews, and collaborate on solving complex challenges.
  • Make your mark on ambitious projects.

What You Bring to the Team

Essential Experience:

  • Experience with Python or Go for backend development
  • Knowledge of AWS (or other Cloud Provider) Services
  • Demonstrated ability to plan, coordinate, and execute engineering projects

Beneficial Experience:

  • High volume NoSQL and SQL databases such as Dynamo DB, Postgres
  • Terraform built environments
  • Experience working with data and infrastructure at scale
  • Event driven architecture such as Kafka/Kinesis

Our standards around security, quality, and service availability can only be the highest, but at the same time we foster psychological safety, as we are comfortable with the unknowns and love to experiment to find the right solution, which get things done and keep the eyes on results.

We use data to validate our decisions, measuring our progress and success. Data informs our decision making, our goals, and our roadmap. We are sure you will love joining us if you share our focus on outcomes rather than outputs and leverage factual evidence to justify your arguments and influence the direction of the team.

Our main challenge ahead is scaling, both vertically (size of individual tenants) and horizontally (number of total tenants). We will consider experience working with data and infrastructure at scale as a bonus. If you are not sure that you exactly fit all the above criteria, please do get in touch anyway. We are always willing to speak to great candidates even if you do not feel you meet every criteria listed!

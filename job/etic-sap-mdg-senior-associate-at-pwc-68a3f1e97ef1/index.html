Job Description & Summary

We are seeking a highly motivated and technically proficient ABAP Consultant to join our team as a Senior Associate. The ideal candidate will bring 2.5 to 7 years of hands-on experience in SAP ABAP development, with a strong understanding of business processes and the ability to translate functional requirements into robust technical solutions.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop, test, and deploy custom ABAP programs, reports, interfaces, enhancements, and forms.
  • Collaborate with functional consultants to understand business requirements and deliver effective solutions.
  • Perform code reviews and ensure adherence to SAP best practices and coding standards.
  • Support SAP modules such as SD, MM, FI, and HR through technical development and troubleshooting.
  • Optimize existing ABAP code for performance and scalability.
  • Participate in SAP upgrade and migration projects.
  • Provide technical documentation and user training as needed.

Required Skills & Qualifications:

  • 2.5 to 7 years of experience in SAP ABAP development.
  • Strong expertise in classical and object-oriented ABAP programming.
  • Hands-on experience with SmartForms, SAPScripts, ALV Reports, BAPIs, BADIs, User Exits, and Enhancements.
  • Familiarity with SAP Fiori/UI5, CDS Views, and OData services is a plus.
  • Good understanding of SAP functional modules and integration points.
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field.

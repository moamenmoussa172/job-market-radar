Senior Software Engineer I

Samsara · Remote

Fully remote · Senior · Software Engineer · Posted 19 November 2026 · Visa / relocation · $88k–$114k a year (converted to USD)

Apply on Samsara's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Who we are

Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale.

Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term.

About the role:

As a Senior Software Engineer on Safety Core, you’ll build software that helps customers identify and reduce risk across vehicles, equipment, workers, and customer sites. You’ll develop the systems and product capabilities that turn signals from connected devices and operational workflows into timely, trustworthy safety insights. Initial opportunities may support Safety Operated Equipment or Safety Installations, with the flexibility to contribute to other Safety Core initiatives as priorities evolve. You’ll take ambiguous customer and operational problems from discovery through technical design, production delivery, and measurement. This role is a strong fit for an engineer who enjoys defining both the problem and the approach, making sound trade-offs, and connecting engineering work to customer outcomes.

This is a position open to candidates based in or close to Toronto, Canada. Relocation assistance will not be provided for this role.

You should apply if:

  • You want to impact the industries that run our world: The software, firmware, and hardware you build will result in real-world impact – helping to keep the lights on, get food into grocery stores, and most importantly, ensure workers return home safely.
  • You want to build for scale: With over 2.3 million IoT devices deployed to our global customers, you will work on a range of new and mature technologies driving scalable innovation for customers across industries driving the world's physical operations.
  • You are a life-long learner: We have ambitious goals. Every Samsarian has a growth mindset as we work with a wide range of technologies, challenges, and customers that push us to learn on the go.
  • You believe customers are more than a number: Samsara engineers enjoy a rare closeness to the end user and you will have the opportunity to participate in customer interviews, collaborate with customer success and product managers, and use metrics to ensure our work is translating into better customer outcomes.
  • You are a team player: Working on our Samsara Engineering teams requires a mix of independent effort and collaboration. Motivated by our mission, we’re all racing toward our connected operations vision, and we intend to win – together.

In this role, you will:

  • Unlock new customer-facing Safety product capabilities, not just maintain existing systems. Pursue the best solution to the problem - even when that means working beyond a single service or discipline and partnering across application, platform, data, and ML.
  • Translate customer and operational needs into useful, maintainable capabilities, initially supporting Safety Operated Equipment or Safety Installations, with the flexibility to contribute to other Safety Core priorities.
  • Own outcomes, not just project delivery: start from the real-world safety or customer problem, help define how success will be measured, and stay accountable for whether the product meaningfully improves that measure. Frame ambiguous problems, identify what is needed, align partners, deliver, measure impact in production, and iterate until the intended outcome is achieved. Be resourceful and move quickly while maintaining sound engineering judgment.
  • Build and operate reliable software across the parts of the stack needed to deliver the outcome, making pragmatic trade-offs across customer value, reliability, scalability, delivery speed, and long-term maintainability.
  • Use AI tools as a core part of the engineering workflow to accelerate discovery, design, implementation, testing, debugging, and iteration; validate outputs and remain accountable for the quality of what ships.
  • Partner closely across functions - including product, design, platform, data, and ML—to turn customer needs and real-world feedback into clear technical plans and shipped improvements.
  • Use production data, customer feedback, and operational signals to understand whether the real-world problem is improving - not just whether the engineering solution shipped—and use that evidence to guide iteration.
  • Raise the engineering bar through strong technical designs, thoughtful reviews, clear communication, mentoring, and improvements to how the team builds and operates software.
  • Automatically included - do not run through Bias Decoder:
  • All Roles: Champion, role model, and embed Samsara’s cultural principles (Focus on Customer Success, Build for the Long Term, Adopt a Growth Mindset, Be Inclusive, Win as a Team) as we scale globally and across new offices
  • People Manager Roles: Hire, develop and lead an inclusive, engaged, and high performing team

Minimum requirements for the role:

  • 4+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Experience shipping and supporting production software that solves customer problems.
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals and experience building production systems in at least one modern programming language.
  • Experience designing or operating backend, distributed, data-intensive, or full-stack systems, with depth in at least one of these areas.
  • Demonstrated ability to take an ambiguous customer or real-world problem, define a practical approach and meaningful measure of success, and work with cross-functional partners to deliver and validate an outcome—not just complete a scoped engineering project.
  • Demonstrated, regular use of AI tools across the software development lifecycle to improve engineering speed and quality, with the judgment to validate AI-generated work and maintain ownership of correctness, security, reliability, and maintainability.
  • Ability to connect engineering decisions to customer or operational outcomes and use data and customer feedback to determine whether the underlying problem is getting better.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to lead technical discussions, explain trade-offs, and align partners across functions.

An ideal candidate also has:

  • Experience building products involving connected devices, physical operations, safety, or other real-world workflows.
  • Experience owning product or engineering work through discovery, delivery, rollout, and iteration, with a focus on achieving measurable customer or operational impact rather than shipping to scope alone.
  • Experience developing customer-facing product capabilities across backend services and user-facing applications.
  • Experience working with event-driven, distributed, or workflow-orchestration systems.
  • Experience partnering with data, AI, or ML teams, or building products that use data-driven or model-powered capabilities. This is useful context, not a requirement for every Safety Core project.
  • A track record of improving engineering quality, mentoring teammates, and helping a team navigate ambiguity.
  • Curiosity about how software can make complex real-world operations safer and more effective.

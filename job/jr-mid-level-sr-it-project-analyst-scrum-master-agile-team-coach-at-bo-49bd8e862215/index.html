About the job

You will join the BD/SPB-APA3 team, with the mission of supporting and/or leading projects and squads throughout their lifecycle, ensuring the successful delivery of high-quality IT products, solutions, and services to our customers.

You will work on local, regional, and international projects, applying both traditional project management methodologies, such as PMI/Waterfall, and Agile methodologies, such as Scrum and Kanban.

The position level will be determined according to the candidate's experience, technical knowledge, certifications, project complexity, and level of autonomy.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan, coordinate, monitor, and implement IT and organizational projects of different levels of complexity;
  • Support and/or lead projects throughout their lifecycle, from planning and scope definition to execution, monitoring, implementation, and closure;
  • Understand business and customer requirements and translate them into structured project plans and actions;
  • Support and/or lead project teams and squads using traditional and Agile methodologies;
  • Monitor schedules, deliverables, KPIs, risks, resources, and priorities, taking corrective actions when necessary;
  • Facilitate Agile ceremonies and practices, fostering collaboration, continuous improvement, and team effectiveness;
  • Manage stakeholders and ensure clear and structured communication regarding project status, risks, priorities, and results;
  • Contribute to the alignment of projects with business strategy, IT architecture, and Bosch procedures and guidelines;
  • Support the resolution of issues and impediments throughout the project lifecycle;
  • At more senior levels, act as an Agile Team Coach and project management reference, coaching and supporting teams and less experienced project professionals.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor's degree;
  • Knowledge and/or experience in IT Project Management;
  • Knowledge of traditional project management methodologies, such as PMI/Waterfall, as well as Agile methodologies, including Scrum and Kanban;
  • Ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary teams and different stakeholders;
  • General understanding of IT systems, processes, and organizational structures;
  • Knowledge of project management and collaboration tools, such as Jira, Miro, and Microsoft Office;
  • Knowledge in areas such as ERP/SAP, Web/App/Mobile Applications, and/or IT Infrastructure is considered a plus;
  • Project Management and Agile certifications, such as PMP, PMI-related certifications, or Scrum Master, are considered a plus and may vary according to the position level;
  • Advanced/fluent English is mandatory;
  • Spanish and/or German are considered a plus.

Seniority Level: The position may be filled at the Junior, Mid-Level, or Senior level, depending primarily on:

  • Previous experience in IT Project Management;
  • Complexity and scope of projects previously managed;
  • Level of autonomy and ownership;
  • Experience with traditional and Agile methodologies;
  • Experience working with local, regional, and/or global stakeholders and teams;
  • Project Management and/or Agile certifications;
  • For Senior profiles, experience in coaching, team development, and acting as a reference in Project Management and Agile practices.

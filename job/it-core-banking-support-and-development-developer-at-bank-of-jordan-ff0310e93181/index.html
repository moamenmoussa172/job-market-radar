About the job

Job Purpose

To perform the development, enhancement, and maintenance of banking software and systems within the team's scope of responsibility. This includes implementing code modifications in accordance with approved requirements and technical designs, contributing to testing activities, providing technical support, and resolving issues to ensure system efficiency, stability, and alignment with the Bank's business needs.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Perform the development, enhancement, and maintenance of banking software, modules, and services in accordance with approved requirements, technical designs, and standards, utilizing the programming languages and frameworks adopted within the banking systems environment.
  • Implement software modifications, functionalities, and components; utilize databases, SQL, services, and required APIs; and contribute to integration activities, system connectivity, and data exchange between banking systems and applications.
  • Conduct Unit Testing and participate in Integration Testing, System Testing, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT). Address identified defects and observations and verify the integrity of modifications following their resolution.
  • Analyze and troubleshoot software and technical issues within the scope of responsibility. Contribute to root cause analysis of recurring or high-impact issues, monitor system performance and stability, and address related problems.
  • Comply with code management, version control, change management, and deployment procedures. Participate in release preparation and deployment to testing and production environments in coordination with DevOps teams and relevant technical stakeholders, in accordance with approved authorities and procedures.
  • Adhere to information security standards, data protection requirements, and Secure Coding practices. Contribute to addressing security observations and vulnerabilities while ensuring the protection of software applications, source code, and access rights in accordance with approved policies and authorization levels.
  • Collaborate with technical teams and relevant stakeholders, adhere to assigned priorities and deadlines, escalate technical risks, dependencies, and issues, and contribute to preparing and updating technical documentation and providing the information required for governance, audit, and control purposes.
  • Serve as a primary or alternate member of the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and formally designated emergency response teams, carrying out all assigned responsibilities under the plan to minimize the impact of crises and restore operations as quickly as possible.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or any related field.
  • Professional certifications in at least one programming language or technology, such as JavaScript, SQL, HTML, Oracle, etc.
  • Minimum of three (3) years of relevant work experience.
  • Excellent command of the English language.
  • Good knowledge of the policies and procedures governing the work and their updates (internal and external).
  • Good verbal communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong concentration and quick thinking.

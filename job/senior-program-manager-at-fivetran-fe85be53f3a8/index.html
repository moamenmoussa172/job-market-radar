About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Senior Program Manager to join the Product Team. In this role, you will drive the successful execution of multiple product initiatives from conception through delivery and adoption. You will collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure alignment of business objectives and customer needs.

This full-time position (Oakland or Denver) and reports to the Manager of Product Program Management.

What You’ll Do

  • Coordinate and program manage multiple concurrent product initiatives.
  • Develop and manage detailed program plans, including scope, objectives, deliverables, timelines, and resource requirements across departments.
  • Serve as the central point of contact for all stakeholders, facilitating cross-functional collaboration and maintaining clear communication.
  • Work closely with product management, engineering, UX/UI design, marketing, sales, and support to launch and drive the adoption of new features.
  • Facilitate regular team meetings to track progress, address roadblocks, and align on next steps.
  • Identify, assess, and manage program risks and issues, ensuring proactive mitigation strategies are in place.
  • Prepare and present regular program updates to senior management, highlighting key achievements, challenges, and risks.
  • Identify opportunities to improve program management processes, tools, and methodologies to enhance efficiency and team collaboration.

What You’ll Bring

  • 5+ years of program management experience.
  • Prior experience overseeing cloud-based software products from concept to launch.
  • Knowledge of market trends, SaaS business models, and financial implications of product decisions.
  • Strong critical thinking and data analysis skills; experience with market research and competitive analysis.
  • An understanding of the needs, preferences, and behaviors of users throughout the product development process.
  • Mastery of Program Management methodologies, tools, and best practices.
  • Excels at prioritization, organization, and multi-tasking.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Thrives and excels in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment with a willingness to adapt to changing priorities.
  • PMP, PMI-ACP, or other relevant project management certifications.

Bonus Skills​

-Experience in data processing (ETL, ELT) and/or cloud-based platforms

-PMP Certified

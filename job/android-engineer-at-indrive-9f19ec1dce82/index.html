About the job

Grocery Storefront is a new team at inDrive. The team builds a native grocery and food shopping experience inside the inDrive super app: the iOS and Android storefront and the mobile backend-for-frontend (BFF).
As an Android Engineer, you will build the Android storefront from its first version. You will work in a small team of iOS, Android, backend and QA engineers.

  • Develop storefront screens and features in the Android app, from the catalog to the checkout
  • Work with backend engineers on API contracts for the mobile BFF.
  • Run A/B experiments, manage feature flags, and implement analytics events
  • Do code reviews, write tests, and co-design solutions with backend and iOS engineers
  • Build with AI tools (Claude Code, Copilot) as part of an AI-First approach
  • 3+ years of commercial Android development with Kotlin
  • Good knowledge of Coroutines, Flow, MVVM/MVI and DI (Dagger/Hilt)
  • Experience with Jetpack Compose
  • Experience with multi-module projects and Gradle
  • Understanding of networking, caching and unstable connections
  • Experience with unit testing
  • English level: B1+

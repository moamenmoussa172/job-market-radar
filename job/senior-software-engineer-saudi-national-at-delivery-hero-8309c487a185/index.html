About the job

We are looking for a highly talented Senior Software Engineer to join our Engineering team in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. If you are looking for an opportunity to work on scalable systems, solve complex technical challenges, and make a meaningful impact, this may be the place for you!

The ideal candidate will be a strong individual contributor with a proven track record in backend development, software architecture, and building reliable, high-performance systems. As a senior member of the team, you will collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, and other cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software and drive technical excellence across the organization.

You Will Be Working On

  • Contributing to architectural decisions and designing scalable, reliable, and secure backend systems that support millions of users.
  • Actively participating in the evolution of our backend architecture, including migrating monolithic applications into microservices.
  • Designing, developing, and maintaining high-quality backend services and APIs to support business and technical requirements.
  • Building and optimizing systems to handle increasing scale, performance, and reliability requirements.
  • Working with Docker containers, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure on Google Cloud Platform.
  • Applying software engineering best practices, including Design-Driven Development, SOLID principles, Domain-Driven Design, and Event-Driven Architecture.
  • Collaborating with cross-functional and agile teams throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • Contributing to continuous improvements in test coverage, code quality, refactoring, and technical documentation.
  • Demonstrating strong code review, debugging, and investigation skills to ensure the quality and reliability of our systems.
  • Supporting and mentoring other engineers, sharing technical knowledge, and contributing to the overall growth of the engineering team.
  • 5+ years of experience in backend software development using Go, Ruby on Rails (RoR), Java, or Python.
  • Strong database expertise with PostgreSQL and NoSQL databases, including a solid understanding of normalization, ACID principles, isolation levels, indexing, query optimization, and caching solutions such as Redis or Memcached.
  • Experience with monitoring, alerting, and logging tools such as Grafana.
  • Strong understanding of software architecture, including Domain-Driven Design (DDD), microservices, Kubernetes, and containerization.
  • Hands-on experience with monolithic application migration and modernizing backend architectures.
  • Strong testing mindset, with experience writing unit and integration tests to ensure code quality and reliability.
  • Solid understanding of algorithms, data structures, and software design principles.
  • Strong adaptability and problem-solving skills, with the ability to navigate ambiguity, work in a dynamic environment, and make informed technical decisions.
  • Excellent collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across engineering and cross-functional teams.

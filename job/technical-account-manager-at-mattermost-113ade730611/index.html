Technical Account Manager (TAM) – Canada (Security Cleared)

Mattermost is the leading collaborative workflow platform for defense, intelligence, security, and critical infrastructure. Our platform runs on-premises, in private clouds and in air-gapped networks, and is trusted by defense, government and enterprise customers with strict security and compliance requirements.

We are hiring a Technical Account Manager (TAM) in Ottawa to deploy, integrate and secure Mattermost for Canadian public sector, defense and regulated customers. You will be the customer's trusted technical advisor, combining deep expertise in infrastructure, cybersecurity and DevSecOps with hands-on AI expertise. Customers in regulated and classified settings want AI-assisted collaboration on terms they can govern and audit, and you will design and deploy it for them, including in air-gapped environments.

A Canadian federal security clearance at the Top-Secret level is a significant advantage for this position. We also welcome technically strong candidates who meet the eligibility requirements to obtain a clearance.

Key Responsibilities

Customer Success & Secure Solution Delivery

  • Lead technical onboarding, configuration and production deployment for strategic enterprise and public sector customers.
  • Design, validate and implement secure, scalable Mattermost architectures across hybrid and air-gapped environments.
  • Align deployments with required frameworks, such as ITSG-33, NIST 800-53, FedRAMP and ISO 27001, and support customer assessment and authorization evidence.
  • Integrate Mattermost with identity (SSO, SAML, OAuth), DevOps pipelines and monitoring systems.
  • Give proactive guidance that prevents incidents, reduces risk and keeps operations stable.
  • Architect and deploy Mattermost AI and agent capabilities with customer-approved models, including self-hosted and air-gapped LLM options, so that no data leaves the customer's boundary unless they approve it.
  • Design retrieval-augmented and agent workflows over customer data, carrying access controls, data classification and audit logging through to the AI layer.
  • Integrate AI tooling with customer systems through APIs, webhooks and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, applying least-privilege access.
  • Help customers assess AI risk and write usage policy, mapped to frameworks such as the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 and to Government of Canada guidance on responsible AI use.
  • Define how AI features are evaluated before go-live: accuracy, prompt-injection and data-leakage testing, and cost and performance baselines.

Technical Leadership & Advocacy

  • Act as the primary technical point of contact for your accounts.
  • Work with Product, Engineering and Support to resolve complex issues and advocate for customer needs.
  • Capture reusable scripts, playbooks and documentation, including AI-assisted assets, that speed up secure deployments.
  • Use AI in your own work to analyze support and telemetry data, summarize escalations and flag account risk early, always validating outputs before acting on them.
  • Share field findings on AI adoption, model behaviour and customer requirements with Product and Engineering.
  • Contribute to reviews of onboarding methods and to continuous improvement.

Communication & Stakeholder Management

  • Explain complex technical and AI risk topics to executives and engineers, tailored to each audience.
  • Build long-term relationships that support adoption, expansion and advocacy.
  • Keep clear records of deployment decisions, architecture designs, AI configuration choices and customer feedback.

Required Qualifications

  • Proven experience in Technical Account Management, Sales Engineering, Customer Success Engineering, or a comparable technical enablement role.
  • Deep knowledge of Linux administration, networking, containers (Docker, Kubernetes) and cloud infrastructure.
  • Hands-on scripting or development experience in Python, Go, Node.js or similar.
  • Practical experience deploying or integrating AI or LLM-based systems, such as model hosting or API integration, retrieval-augmented generation, or agent and tool-calling workflows.
  • Working understanding of AI-specific security risks, including prompt injection, data leakage, model access control and supply chain risk, and how to mitigate them.
  • Strong grasp of cybersecurity principles and secure system design.
  • Familiarity with DevSecOps methods and tools, such as GitHub, Jenkins, Jira and Terraform.
  • Proven ability to manage large enterprise accounts and align technical delivery with business and security goals.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to influence across functions.
  • Held or eligible to obtain Top Secret with Special Access (NATO/COSMIC) clearance.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with public sector, defence or other regulated industries.
  • Experience running models in disconnected or air-gapped environments, for example with vLLM, Ollama or similar serving stacks and GPU infrastructure.
  • Familiarity with AI governance and risk frameworks, such as the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.
  • Familiarity with compliance standards such as FedRAMP, ITSG-33, NIST, SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
  • Knowledge of IT service management (ITSM) frameworks.
  • Open-source contributions or experience with secure collaboration tools.
  • Proficiency in Golang, React, or mobile and MDM platforms (Intune, Workspace ONE, MobileIron).
  • Experience in a startup or fast-paced SaaS environment.

About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Mission

Valeo is hiring a Senior SRE Engineer for the currently-growing GISACC department.

He/She will be responsible for provisioning, operating & the administration of the infrastructure of Valeo tools and applications used by R&D Engineers across all Valeo worldwide sites (either hosted on-prem or on AWS).

He/She will be also responsible for implementing & configuring the monitoring solutions, required for monitoring the previously mentioned tools and applications.

Responsibilities

  • PI Planning & Alignment: Collaborate in Program Increment (PI) planning and sprint standups to align infrastructure roadmaps and SRE initiatives with delivery goals while mitigating cross-team dependencies.
  • Proactive Monitoring & Observability: Oversee full-stack telemetry and proactive monitoring strategies, leveraging distributed tracing, infrastructure metrics, and real-time failure detection to mitigate risks early.
  • Capacity Management: Optimize resource utilization across AWS and on-premises environments through intelligent right-sizing and telemetry-driven capacity planning.
  • Advanced CI/CD & Progressive Delivery: Implement continuous verification, automated canary deployments, and automated checks via robust CI/CD pipelines to guarantee functional correctness in production.
  • FinOps & Cost Governance: Drive automated cloud cost attribution, tagging strategies, and resource governance across all workloads.
  • Infrastructure as Code & GitOps: Provision, fine-tune, and scale infrastructure using declarative IaC and GitOps workflows.
  • Reliability Engineering & Bottleneck Resolution: Identify architectural bottlenecks, reliability risks, and security vulnerabilities within operated services, engineering resilient and automated solutions.
  • Incident Response & SRE: Lead automated incident response, blameless root-cause analysis, and self-healing workflows for production incidents.
  • Shift-Left DevSecOps & Compliance: Enforce automated security policies (SAST, DAST, container scanning, and SCA) across all pipeline stages.
  • ITSM Incident Management: Manage operational incidents via enterprise ticketing platforms, delivering rapid resolution within strict SLAs.
  • On-Call & Maintenance Operations: Manage rotational on-call duties for emergency outages and planned, high-impact maintenance windows, ensuring seamless change execution.
  • Engineering Metrics & KPIs: Track and report platform health and engineering performance using DORA metrics and availability KPIs.
  • Continuous Improvement & Mentorship: Champion a culture of automation and psychological safety by submitting enhancement proposals, eliminating toil, and providing technical mentorship and peer reviews.

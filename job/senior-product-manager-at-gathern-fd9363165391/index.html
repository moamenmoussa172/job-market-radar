Senior Product Manager

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 22 November 2026

About the job

JOB PURPOSE:

Own a high-impact product area at Gathern, turning guest and host needs into simple, scalable experiences that improve conversion, revenue, and customer satisfaction. Lead evidence-based discovery, experimentation, and delivery with Engineering, Design, and Data to achieve measurable business outcomes.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES:

  • Own business outcomes for the assigned product area, with clear primary KPIs (such as search-to-reservation conversion, web conversion, or revenue per search) and guardrail metrics.
  • Define and maintain a prioritized, evidence-backed roadmap aligned with Gathern’s growth objectives; assess opportunities using customer value, business impact, effort, and trade-offs.
  • Lead guest and host discovery using interviews, funnel analysis, behavioral data, and market insights; validate problems and identify the highest-impact journey friction.
  • Build testable hypotheses and run structured A/B experiments; interpret results and recommend whether to launch, iterate, or stop based on evidence.
  • Lead the full product lifecycle from problem definition and PRD through design, development, rollout, and post-launch performance measurement.
  • Partner closely with Engineering, Design, Analytics, Marketing, and Operations to align priorities, manage dependencies, and deliver quarterly commitments.
  • Monitor product performance, share concise post-launch readouts, and continuously improve conversion, revenue, and guest/host experiences.
  • Ensure each initiative has a clearly defined problem, success metric, target impact, scope, and acceptance criteria before development.

Requirements

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE:

Education:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

Experience:

  • 5+ years in product management, including independent ownership of digital product outcomes and roadmaps.
  • Proven record of improving measurable conversion, engagement, or revenue KPIs through discovery, experimentation, and cross-functional delivery.

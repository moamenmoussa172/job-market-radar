About the job

Discover Graduate Programme is all set and designed with fantastic opportunities to grab and build upon!
Are YOU up for the challenge?

In just 2 years, you’ll explore how AI works in real-life Technology environments, gain mentorship, hands-on training, and develop a 360° understanding of the business.

From day one, you’ll be treated like a full-time employee: valued, recognized, and rewarded as a true specialist in the making. 🚀 You won’t just learn - you’ll make a real impact, working on major AI initiatives alongside experienced professionals and leadership teams.

And the best part? At the end of the programme, you’ll step into a destination role within Vodafone, setting you up for long-term professional success.

Bring your curiosity, your can-do mindset, and get ready to grow.

Still here? Good. Let’s break it down.

What’s in it for you – FLOW:

• FLEXIBILITY – A tailored program designed to feed your curiosity & hunger for growth.
• LEARNING – Get exposure and hands-on experience through different business units
• ORGANISATION – Be part of a fast-paced innovative tech company, with a diverse & multicultural environment
• WORK TOGETHER – Engage with Vodafone leaders who are eager to support and help you unlock your full potential

What are we looking for:

  • Motivated young talent with an AI mindset, high ambition, and the drive to help shape the future of technology.
  • Recent graduates (within the last 2 years) or Master's students in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Information Security, Engineering, Technology, Electronics, or related fields.
  • Strong understanding or hands-on experience with coding, cloud computing, software engineering, and modern development practices.
  • Exposure to or a good understanding of AI technologies, including Generative AI, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, and conversational AI.
  • Basic knowledge of project management principles.
  • Genuine interest in connectivity, networking, enterprise technology, and digital innovation.
  • Ability to design end-to-end AI solutions, from concept to implementation.
  • Capability to translate business challenges into digital and AI-powered products, including conversational experiences such as VoiceBots and ChatBots.
  • Passion for technology and curiosity to understand all aspects of the business.
  • An imaginative and creative spirit, with the courage to bring bold and unconventional ideas to life.
  • A proactive, self-driven, and growth-oriented mindset, with a strong appetite for continuous learning.
  • Strong communication, collaboration, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work effectively across multiple functions.
  • Excellent communication skills in English, both written and spoken.
  • A pragmatic and enthusiastic approach, balancing innovation with execution to turn ideas into impact.

Discover your true calling
Worried that you don’t meet all the desired criteria exactly? At Vodafone we believe in Inclusion for All and creating a workplace where everyone can thrive, no matter their background. So, if you’re excited about this role, have a genuine interest in Technology, but don’t check every requirement box, we still encourage you to apply!

Our selection process:

We carefully review all applications to find future Discovers who have the passion, motivation, and potential to make a real impact. And don’t worry - we’re here to guide you every step of the way so if you have any questions, just reach out!

1st step: Apply with your Resume in English.

2nd step: Show us your strengths by completing our online challenges:
• Spirit Personality & Cognitive Assessment

3rd step: Show us your true potential during Focus Group time - this is when you get to meet us in person at our Bucharest HQ, live the Vodafone life for a couple of hours, and engage in both group and individual exercises.

• Insurance for hospitalization and surgical interventions
• Life insurance
• Insurance modules and additional medical packages for you and your family
• Meal vouchers
• Annual voucher for eyeglasses
• Bookster subscription
• Preferential prices for gym membership
• Participation in development programs and challenging projects in the leadership area
• Access to internal Wellbeing & Recognition events

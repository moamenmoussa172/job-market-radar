About the job

For the Compute Performance business unit in North America, we are seeking a Technical Project Manager – ADAS Platform.
In this role, you will lead a cross-functional team to deliver an ADAS platform into series production. You will serve as the technical lead for the project and the primary customer interface for technical topics, with ownership from acquisition through start of production and beyond.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and mentor a cross-functional, globally distributed engineering team—including hardware/software component managers, system architects, developers, and validation engineers—to deliver ADAS compute platforms into series production.
  • Own the technical project lifecycle from initial RFQ/acquisition through A/B/C sample deliveries, Start of Production (SOP), and post-launch maintenance.
  • Act as the primary project interface for OEM customers, leading design reviews, aligning on system architecture, and negotiating technical scope, specifications, and delivery timelines.
  • Define, schedule, and track work packages, technical milestones, and critical-path dependencies across internal teams and external Tier-2/Tier-3 suppliers.
  • Proactively identify technical and schedule risks, driving root-cause analysis and escalation resolution to prevent launch delays.
  • Manage OEM engineering change requests (ECRs), evaluating technical feasibility, resource impacts, and Bill of Materials (BOM) cost implications.
  • Ensure all development, testing, and release activities strictly adhere to automotive quality and compliance frameworks, including Functional Safety (ISO 26262), Cybersecurity (ISO/SAE 21434), and Automotive SPICE (ASPICE).
  • Drive executive governance by delivering monthly project status reports, KPI tracking, and technical escalation briefings to senior leadership and OEM stakeholders.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Systems Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • 5+ years of experience in technical project management or engineering project leadership specifically for ADAS, autonomous driving, or central compute platforms.
  • 5+ years of industry experience within the automotive, motorsport, or heavy equipment sectors, including direct experience taking a product from acquisition/RFQ through series production (SOP).
  • 3+ years of experience leading cross-functional, globally distributed engineering teams (hardware, software, systems, and validation) and managing external Tier-2/Tier-3 suppliers.
  • Proven working knowledge of automotive development standards and compliance frameworks, specifically Functional Safety (ISO 26262) and ASPICE.
  • Demonstrated experience acting as the primary technical interface for OEM customers, including leading technical reviews, negotiating scope/timing, and managing engineering design change requests.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master’s degree in an engineering discipline or technical management, or active PMP / Agile (SAFe) certification.
  • Hands-on technical background in modern automotive centralized E/E architectures, domain controllers, or high-performance computing (HPC) platforms.
  • Working familiarity with modern automotive Systems-on-Chip (SoCs), on-chip peripherals, hardware-software interfaces, and hypervisor/virtualization technologies.
  • Experience with ADAS sensor modalities (camera, radar, LiDAR) and middleware frameworks such as Classic/Adaptive AUTOSAR or ROS.
  • Familiarity with automotive cybersecurity standards (ISO/SAE 21434) and requirements management tools (e.g., DOORS, Polarion, or Jira).
  • Exceptional executive presence with a strong ability to translate complex system-level trade-offs into clear technical concepts for both internal leadership and OEM stakeholders.

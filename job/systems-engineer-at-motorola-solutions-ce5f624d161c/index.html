Department Overview

At Motorola Solutions, everything starts with our people. We are a global, close-knit community united by our purpose of helping people be their best in the moments that matter, and our mission of "Solving for safer." Within this mission, the Pronto Team serves as the UK's leading provider of mobile policing applications, supporting 50% of the country's 45 police forces and over 70,000 active officers every single day. The Hosted Applications Team takes primary responsibility for overseeing the Pronto application across our hosted environments. Our dedicated specialists remotely manage these critical instances for all hosted customers, directly supporting platform stability, driving optimal performance, and maintaining continuous operational oversight.


Job Description

Operating primarily remotely within Motorola Solutions’ Hosted Applications Team (with occasional travel to offices and customer sites), the Systems Engineer holds a highly technical role combining application lifecycle engineering with direct client-facing responsibilities. Leveraging your proven engineering expertise, you will share accountability for managing and sustaining the operational resilience of the mission-critical Pronto Digital Policing application for frontline emergency services and police forces across the UK. We value an inclusive culture built on collaboration, continuous learning, and equivalent practical experience—welcoming candidates who bring strong diagnostic curiosity and clear communication to high-stakes environments.

In this position, you will assist with key deployment initiatives for standard product enhancements and customised client features. You will act as a vital technical bridge between our Software Engineering and Shared Services teams while maintaining direct engagement with police force stakeholders. Key activities involve assisting in analysing software issues, supporting patch rollouts and release cycles, and channeling customer feedback into future application enhancements. Serving as a technical point of contact, you will help align infrastructure and application workflows, participate in technical discussions during weekly service reviews, and observe platform health metrics to uphold strict Service Level Agreement (SLA) standards. You will also help maintain enterprise database clusters housing essential Pronto data, supporting data security and reliable operational processes tailored to the daily needs of emergency service teams.

Key Responsibilities

Platform Resilience & Operational Oversight

  • Observe and analyse platform health and key performance metrics against agreed contractual SLAs, supporting team efforts to minimise downtime and prevent service-impacting incidents
  • Support the Incident Management Team in investigating and resolving complex system issues, helping implement effective solutions and preventative measures
  • Help maintain enterprise relational database clusters, supporting data security, system reliability, and standard operational processes aligned with frontline policing needs

Release Management & Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Act as an operational liaison for the core Pronto Software Engineering Team, gathering operational insights and supporting release management activities including application hotfixes, feature rollouts, and platform deployments
  • Collaborate closely with the internal Shared Services Team to align application demands with technical specifications and assist in submitting change requests
  • Engage in active knowledge sharing within the team, participating in peer learning and documenting technical workflows to foster continuous team development

Client Engagement & Technical Documentation

  • Participate in regular customer service reviews and weekly meetings with key police force stakeholders to present system performance, address operational concerns, and drive customer satisfaction
  • Contribute to and maintain customer-facing Interface Control Documents (ICDs) detailing technical specifications and protocols required for external system integration with the Pronto platform
  • Partner with Project Managers to resolve technical challenges for customers and translate frontline feedback into actionable product enhancements

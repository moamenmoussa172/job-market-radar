The Team

Pinterest's Infrastructure organization builds and operates the platforms that every engineer at Pinterest uses to build, test, ship and run software. Our CI and deployment platforms handle the journey of every change from commit to production. We're investing heavily in making that journey faster, more efficient and safe by default, especially as AI-assisted development sharply increases how much we ship. We work closely with engineering teams across Pinterest to make sure the platform scales with the business.

What You'll Do

  • Lead cross-team programs that make production changes safe by default, driving adoption of progressive rollouts, automated canary analysis and fast rollback across code, config, data and ML model deployments.
  • Drive the CI platform roadmap to reduce build cost and wait times, improve fleet utilization and migrate CI workloads to our next-generation compute platform.
  • Define and track the delivery and reliability metrics that measure success, such as change failure rate, recovery time, lead time for change and build performance, and build dashboards that surface regressions early.
  • Manage dependencies and migrations across CI, artifact, deployment and compute platforms and the many service teams that depend on them.
  • Influence engineering teams across Pinterest to adopt new standards and paved paths, managing timelines, pushback and risk.
  • Communicate technical tradeoffs among velocity, safety, cost and reliability clearly to audiences from individual engineers to senior leadership.
  • Use GenAI as the default operating model for program execution: produce AI-assisted first drafts of core program artifacts, turn high-toil workflows into AI-first mechanisms (for example, intake triage, status synthesis, action and decision extraction, and risk and dependency tracking), and synthesize signals to surface risks, tradeoffs and escalation paths early.
  • Prototype solutions that improve decisions with data (dashboards, analysis) or simplify processes (workflow helpers, internal tools) using AI coding assistants ("vibe coding").
  • Follow Pinterest AI guidance for risk, governance and safety by design: handle sensitive data appropriately, validate AI-generated outputs, document assumptions and limits, and make sure AI-assisted workflows meet policy and compliance expectations before broad adoption.

What We're Looking For

  • 5+ years of TPM experience leading complex, cross-functional technical programs in infrastructure, platform engineering, developer productivity or SRE.
  • A solid understanding of CI/CD systems, build infrastructure and safe-deployment practices (canaries, progressive delivery, automated rollback), and the tradeoffs between velocity and safety.
  • A track record of driving platform or standards adoption across many engineering teams without direct authority.
  • Strong analytical skills, with experience defining delivery and reliability metrics (for example, DORA metrics) and using data to drive decisions.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, including giving clear, candid status and risk updates to senior leadership.
  • Comfort with ambiguity: you can take a broad mandate, define the plan and measures of success, and drive it to outcomes.
  • Experience with infrastructure efficiency, compute cost or capacity planning is a plus.
  • Workflow design, AI fluency, and a data-and-insights orientation: experience turning repeatable program work into durable, low-toil mechanisms and improving decisions using GenAI (for example, strong prompting, vibe coding lightweight scripts or tools, dashboards, data analysis and using agents where appropriate).
  • Safety-by-design AI fluency: experience operating within AI governance expectations (risk assessment, data handling, output validation, auditability) and recognizing where AI use isn't appropriate or needs extra controls.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, a related field or equivalent experience.

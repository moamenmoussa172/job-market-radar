About the job

Role: Senior SOC Analyst - Cheltenham

Location: Cheltenham (hybrid)

We are looking for 2x Analysts: 1x for shift based work and 1x for day only (M-F)

Please Note: Any offer of employment is subject to satisfactory BPSS and SC security clearance which typically requires 5 years continuous UK address history usually including no periods of 30 consecutive days or more spent outside of the UK and declaration of being a British passport holder with no dual nationalism at the point of application. The above information relates to a specific client requirement.

In our team you will learn:

The role involves monitoring and responding to alerts raised by a range of toolsets as part of an ongoing managed security monitoring service. You will also analyse data sets gathered from Incident Response investigations and help the wider team deliver positive outcomes on breach investigations.

As a SOC Analyst you will:

  • Work a combination of day and night shifts.
  • This is a hybrid role, and some office attendance is required.
  • Respond to alerts.
  • Work closely with client organisation analysts.
  • Perform detailed analysis of the data captured by monitoring systems.
  • Escalate incidents where necessary and act as a point of contact throughout
  • Review and identify alerts for further tuning.
  • Support client engagements and/or service meetings.

The following skills and experience are required for this role:

  • A good understanding of computer networks (including cloud), operating systems, software, hardware and security.
  • An understanding of cyber security risks associated with various technologies and ways to manage them.
  • A good working knowledge of various security technologies such as network and application firewalls, host intrusion prevention and antivirus.
  • Any relevant academic or industry specific training.
  • At least 1 year of experience in a similar role
  • Experience of common SOC tools such as Sentinel, Splunk and Defender

Set yourself apart:

  • Ability to seek out new ways of working more efficiently.
  • Sustain a high level of focus, effort, and energy.
  • Proven history developing innovative & creative solutions.
  • Strives to learn new skills, applications, and technologies through focused effort.

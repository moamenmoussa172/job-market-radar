About the job

Are you a technically curious problem-solver who thrives on helping customers achieve meaningful outcomes in the cloud? Amazon Web Services (AWS) Professional Services is looking for a Delivery Consultant to join our team and work directly with customers to design, implement, and optimize cloud solutions that address real business challenges. In this role, you will combine your technical depth with a consultative mindset to guide customers through their cloud journey, from solution design through delivery. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, assess customer requirements, and build solutions that account for complexity, scale, and performance. Your ability to translate technical concepts into business value will be key as you advise customer stakeholders and contribute to long-term technology strategies. If you are motivated by impact, energized by working across technologies, and ready to grow your career alongside some of the most innovative organizations in the world, we want to hear from you.
221100
Key job responsibilities
- Design and implement cloud-based solutions tailored to customer requirements, applying your expertise in system architecture, migration, and cloud computing to deliver outcomes that are operationally ready and built for scale.
- Assess customer current-state maturity and desired business and technical outcomes, then define solution approaches that address complex technical challenges while identifying and mitigating risks throughout the engagement lifecycle.
- Collaborate with internal cross-functional teams and partner organizations to develop technical solutions, contribute to presales activities, and support smooth sales-to-delivery handoffs with detailed skill requirements and governance structures.
- Create and share reusable technical content such as reference architectures, whitepapers, and code artifacts that drive efficiency and innovation across customer engagements.
- Provide prescriptive guidance and mentorship to customer teams and colleagues, helping to build cloud capabilities while contributing to the recruitment and onboarding of new team members.

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics or equivalent
- 3+ years of cloud architecture and solution implementation experience (AWS or equivalent), including systems, networking and operating systems (Linux, Windows)
- 3+ years of hands-on experience in application migration and modernization, including replatforming to containers, managed databases and managed services (e.g., Amazon ECS/EKS, RDS, Aurora)
- Experience with migration strategy and planning (7Rs), application discovery, dependency mapping and cutover execution
- Hands-on experience with migration tools (e.g., AWS MGN, AWS DMS, AWS Transform)
- 3+ years of customer-facing experience delivering complex, large-scale projects
- Fluent English, written and spoken
- Experience with database replatforming (e.g., Oracle or SQL Server to Aurora PostgreSQL)
- Experience with infrastructure as code, CI/CD and scripting (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation, Python, PowerShell)
- Experience using AI-powered and agentic tools (e.g., AWS Transform, Kiro) to speed up delivery
- Experience designing risk mitigation, rollback and legacy decommissioning plans
- Knowledge of cloud security and governance during migrations (e.g., IAM, encryption, DORA)
- AWS Professional level certifications (e.g., Solutions Architect Professional)
- Experience in financial services, ideally in large-scale migration programmes
- Strong communication skills with technical and executive audiences
- Professional working proficiency in Spanish is a strong advantage

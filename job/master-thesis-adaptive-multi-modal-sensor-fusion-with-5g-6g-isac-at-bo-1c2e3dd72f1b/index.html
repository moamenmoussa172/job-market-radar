About the job

Reliable environment perception is a cornerstone of automated driving and advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS). Today’s systems rely on complementary sensing modalities, such as radar, lidar, as well as cameras. With the emergence of 5G/6G technologies, Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) introduces a new paradigm: sensing is not viewed as an isolated function, but rather as an integral part of the wireless infrastructure. This thesis explores how ISAC can move beyond a supporting role, as well as actively shape adaptive multi-modal sensor fusion strategies in dynamic automotive environments. Rather than addressing a fixed, narrowly scoped problem, this work is designed as a flexible research space.

  • Depending on your interests and strengths, your thesis can emphasize system modeling, algorithm design or performance evaluation - all grounded in a unified simulation framework. You will analyze the role, as well as limitations of ISAC sensing in automotive perception.
  • You will develop and/or extend a modular, system-level simulator (e.g., MATLAB-based) for use in multi-sensor scenarios.
  • In addition, you will design and implement adaptive sensor fusion strategies, such as modality weighting, selection or switching.
  • Furthermore, you will compare radar-only, classical multi-sensor and ISAC-assisted perception pipelines, as well as develop simulation experiments under varying environmental and system conditions.
  • Last but not least, you will interpret your results with respect to robustness, reliability and system-level trade-offs. The exact scope, as well as focus will be jointly defined to ensure both scientific depth and alignment with your interests.
  • Education: Master studies in the field of Electrical Engineering, Communications Engineering, Robotics, Computer Science or comparable
  • Experience and Knowledge:
  • strong foundation in signal processing and communications or sensor fusion
  • experience with MATLAB, Python or comparable simulation tools
  • understanding of wireless systems (5G/6G), radar or perception systems
  • familiarity with Machine Learning is advantageous
  • Personality and Working Practice: you are a reliable and self-motivated individual with a curiosity to explore open research questions beyond well-defined boundaries
  • Work Routine: partially mobile working is possible, with regular in-person meetings
  • Enthusiasm: genuine interest in next-generation sensing, 6G technologies and intelligent mobility systems
  • Languages: very good in English

Start: according to prior agreement
Duration: 6 months

Requirement for this thesis is the enrollment at university. Please attach your CV, transcript of records, examination regulations, references to previous projects (e.g., GitHub, publications or coursework), if available and if indicated a valid work and residence permit.

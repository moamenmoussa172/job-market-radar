About the job

At HelloFresh, the goal is to change the way people eat forever by delivering high-quality food across 18 countries and 3 continents. As a global food solutions group and the world's leading meal kit provider, the portfolio includes HelloFresh, Green Chef, EveryPlate, Chefs Plate, Factor_, and Youfoodz.

Engaging with HelloFresh in Warsaw means collaborating with HelloTech-a global technology division of over 1,000 people powering an end-to-end food ecosystem. From digital personalization, pricing, and forecasting to supply chain optimization and food waste reduction, technology drives every step of this direct-to-consumer model.

About the Role: What's in the Box

HelloTech operates in autonomous, cross-functional alliances, each holding accountability for a specific product domain end to end. In this role, you will join an assigned squad, with the flexibility to transition between teams and projects as technical objectives and business priorities evolve.

Several high-impact machine learning domains are actively hiring, and your project and team placement will be aligned based on your individual strengths, interests, and expertise during the interview process. Key domains include:

  • Risk, Fraud & Payments (Growth Alliance): You will own production ML systems that protect HelloFresh from checkout fraud and intelligently recover failed recurring payments (such as Smart Payment Retry) via low-latency scoring on Databricks.
  • Personalization & Conversions (Growth Alliance): You will build real-time decisioning systems, customer win-back models, and scalable GenAI content pipelines that tailor journeys and messaging across visitor and customer funnels.
  • Dynamic Pricing & Customer Value Optimization (Growth Alliance): You will architect and scale the core Pricing Engine to drive pricing optimization and forecasting across 8 brands, 34 markets, and all weekly menus.

Across all domains, you will work on scalable, high-availability production ML systems serving global architectures. Please note that this is an individual contributor role and does not include people management responsibilities.

What You’ll Do: The Recipe

At HelloFresh, we are moving away from a model where engineers simply execute tickets toward one where product engineers are trusted to own customer problems. As a Product Engineer, you will take a problem, form a point of view, validate it with customers and data, and ship it using AI as a force multiplier.

  • Build and operate data products and machine-learning systems, taking them from experimentation through production and owning their real-world performance.
  • Architect and optimize scalable batch and streaming data pipelines (Python, SQL, PySpark on Databricks, Kafka), feature stores, and low-latency serving infrastructure.
  • Improve inference performance, reliability, and compute cost by profiling bottlenecks, right-sizing infrastructure, and maintaining tight latency targets with dependable fallback mechanisms.
  • Establish and raise modern MLOps standards across teams, including CI/CD automation, model registry versioning, infrastructure as code (Terraform), automated validation gates, and drift detection.
  • Design and execute rigorous experimentation frameworks from A/B tests and holdouts to off-policy evaluation-to credibly measure model impact on core business metrics.
  • Build and scale domain-specific applied AI solutions, ranging from real-time scoring and contextual bandits to production GenAI pipelines with automated quality gates.
  • Collaborate closely with data scientists, software engineers, product managers, and business stakeholders to pressure-test models for production readiness, mentor teammates, and leverage AI coding agents (such as Claude Code or Cursor) to accelerate engineering delivery.

What You’ll Bring: The Ingredients

  • 5+ years building and operating live ML systems in production, paired with the statistical literacy to design sound experiments and interpret their results.
  • Hands-on experience building product-facing ML solutions directly tied to measurable business metrics (such as conversion, retention, revenue, or risk reduction).
  • Deep command of Python, SQL, and large-scale data processing (PySpark on Databricks), paired with solid software engineering practices (testing, clean code, version control) and familiarity with low-latency serving or backend languages like Go.
  • Hands-on background with cloud environments (AWS), containerization, automated CI/CD, and modern ML or orchestration tooling such as Databricks Model Serving, MLflow, SageMaker, Airflow, Kafka, or Terraform.
  • Practical, day-to-day fluency with AI coding tools (like Claude Code, Cursor, or Copilot) beyond casual experimentation—knowing how to structure context and set clear boundaries on AI-generated work.
  • A quality-first mindset around model evaluation, drift monitoring, and A/B testing pitfalls for ML systems to ensure bad data or outputs never reach customers.
  • A bias to ship ("finishing the last twenty percent"), strong product sense backed by data, and clear communication skills across international, multi-disciplinary teams.

Nice to have:

  • A track record of optimizing data and ML pipelines for performance and cost at scale.
  • Experience with contextual bandits and off-policy evaluation.
  • Hands-on experience building and running production LLM pipelines.
  • Background in churn prediction and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) modeling.
  • Prior experience in high-growth consumer tech or subscription-based businesses.

What We Offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at global scale, supporting millions of customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to workspace at Warsaw Centre Point: The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

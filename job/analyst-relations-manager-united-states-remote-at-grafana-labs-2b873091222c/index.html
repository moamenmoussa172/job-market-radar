Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. The Opportunity

Grafana Labs is looking for an Analyst Relations (AR) Manager to own our relationships with the industry analyst community, including Gartner, Forrester, IDC, GigaOm, and others, and to run the programs that turn those relationships into visibility, credibility, and pipeline influence. You'll manage the full analyst lifecycle: building and nurturing analyst relationships, running Analyst Days, driving our participation in Magic Quadrants and other evaluations, managing commissioned research, and partnering with analyst firms on joint surveys and reports. This role sits at the intersection of product marketing, communications, and competitive intelligence, and reports to the Director of AR and PR.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Analyst Relationship Management
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for a defined portfolio of analysts across Gartner, Forrester, IDC, GigaOm, and other relevant firms, building long-term, trusted relationships.
  • Maintain a regular cadence of proactive briefings, inquiry calls, and informal check-ins to keep analysts current on Grafana Labs' roadmap, customer momentum, and market position.
  • Plan and run Analyst Day(s), including agenda design, executive and SME participation, content development, and post-event follow-up.
  • Track analyst coverage, sentiment, and points of view on Grafana Labs and the observability market, and share that intelligence internally.
  • Evaluations & Commissioned Research
  • Own end-to-end submission management for Magic Quadrants, Forrester Waves, GigaOm Radars, and comparable evaluations, tracking deadlines, coordinating inputs, and managing the submission and rebuttal process.
  • Manage development of commissioned/sponsored reports (e.g., Total Economic Impact studies, custom research), including scoping, vendor selection, budget, and stakeholder review.
  • Design and field surveys and joint research initiatives in partnership with analyst firms, from questionnaire design through report publication and internal/external distribution.
  • Maintain a shared calendar of all evaluation cycles, submission windows, and research deliverables across the AR program.
  • Content, Enablement & Cross-Functional Coordination
  • Prepare executives and subject-matter experts for analyst briefings and inquiries, including briefing decks, talking points, and Q&A prep.
  • Build and maintain the AR content library: positioning documents, fact sheets, RFI/questionnaire responses, and reusable proof points.
  • Partner with product marketing, competitive intelligence, PR/comms, and the executive team to align analyst messaging with the broader company narrative and launch timing.
  • Program Measurement & Operations
  • Define and report on AR program KPIs, including evaluation positioning trends, share of voice, analyst-influenced pipeline, and stakeholder satisfaction.
  • Own the AR budget, including analyst firm subscriptions/contracts and commissioned research spend; manage vendor relationships and renewals.
  • Monitor for and coordinate rapid response to analyst commentary that could affect brand or competitive positioning.
  • Synthesize and distribute analyst research and competitive insight internally (e.g., briefing summaries, an internal analyst newsletter) to keep sales, product, and leadership informed.

What Makes You a Great Fit

  • 6+ years of experience in analyst relations, communications, product marketing, or competitive intelligence, ideally in B2B SaaS or infrastructure/observability/monitoring software.
  • Direct experience managing Magic Quadrant, Wave, or similar analyst evaluation submissions.
  • Proven experience using AI to support workflows and build automations/applications.
  • Established familiarity with major analyst firms (Gartner, Forrester, IDC, GigaOm) and their methodologies.
  • Experience commissioning or managing third-party research (e.g., TEI studies, custom surveys).
  • Strong project management skills; comfortable owning multiple concurrent deadlines across teams.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the polish to prep and coach executives.
  • Data-literate: comfortable scoping surveys, interpreting research results, and translating findings into narrative.
  • Collaborative, cross-functional operator who can influence without direct authority.
  • Willingness to travel for analyst events and briefings (~15–20%).

Bonus Points For

  • Experience in observability, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, or open-source software markets.
  • Prior in-house AR leadership role (owned the function, not just supported it).

About the job

At Amazon we're working to be the most customer-centric company on earth. To get there, we need exceptionally talented, bright, and driven people. This is an exciting opportunity to work in a highly visible space and be part of a fast-growing business.
We are looking for a dynamic, organized self-starter to join our Worldwide Food Safety and Compliance team as a Food Regulatory Manager.
This position will focus on managing all regulatory aspects relating to the launch of new own label food products for Amazon own label products, ensuring they exceed customer expectations. The role will be involved in all stages from new product concept, through to the launch of the product and post launch product regulatory review.
The individual will bring experience and knowledge of a number of food categories, to the team and support food regulatory decisions. This role requires in-depth knowledge and expertise in EU food regulation. The individual will work directly with suppliers and internal cross-functional stakeholders to facilitate successful site expansion and product launches.
The individual must be detail oriented and have excellent organizational skills, a high bar for quality, strong customer obsession, and a track record of operating in a dynamic environment. The successful candidate will be comfortable with ambiguity, have strong attention to detail, and be motivated by a fast-paced start up environment.
Key job responsibilities
• Work with the team to ensure product selection meet EU regulatory requirements
• Work with wider team to ensure product selection is appropriate and will meet or exceed customer expectations
• Work with manufacturing partners to ensure products have the correct regulatory and technical documentation
• Attend pre-production / first production runs ensuring product and process match that defined in the specification, where required
• Keep abreast of new and upcoming EU regulation and emerging issues in food safety and quality
• Support the wider food safety team by contributing to shared process and policy development
• Support the Amazon site launch, registration and permitting
• Continuously identify and communicate improvement opportunities
• Leverage technology to manage compliance processes
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent
- Experience in compliance program management, legal, governance, audit, risk/loss prevention, or equivalent
- Experience handling confidential information
- Professional auditing qualification, or similar risk or compliance credentials
- Experience with SQL and Excel
- Experience in program requirements definition, together with data and metrics leveraging to drive improvements

Pinterest is looking for an Engineering Manager II, to lead the Personalization team within the Performance Ad Formats organization. In this role, you will guide a multidisciplinary team building personalized, high-performing ad experiences that help Pinners move from intent to action while improving click quality, conversion outcomes, and advertiser value.

What you’ll do:

  • Lead, coach, and grow a multidisciplinary engineering team spanning iOS, Android, Web, and Backend, creating a strong culture of ownership, technical excellence, and inclusive collaboration.
  • Drive execution for a portfolio of lower-funnel ad format investments, including personalized product and shopping experience.
  • Partner closely with Product, Design, Data Science, PMM, and peer engineering teams to shape strategy, prioritize roadmap investments, and deliver measurable business impact.
  • Establish strong engineering practices across technical design, planning, experimentation, launch quality, and production health to help the team move quickly and reliably.
  • Use data, experimentation, and sound judgment to improve key outcomes such as good click volume, click quality, and conversion performance while maintaining a high-quality Pinner experience.
  • Navigate technical and organizational complexity across multiple partner teams, clarifying tradeoffs and aligning stakeholders around goals, sequencing, and success metrics.
  • Use AI to accelerate analysis, iteration, and prototyping across product and engineering workflows, while applying strong judgment and verification to ensure quality, safety, and correctness.
  • Use AI and automation to streamline repeatable team workflows such as documentation, reporting, and quality checks so the team can focus more time on high-impact problem solving.

What we’re looking for:

  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • 8+ years of industry experience in software engineering, with 4+ years of people management experience.
  • History of managing teams of at least 8+ engineers across multiple technical domains, including client and backend systems.
  • Experience leading and managing high-performing software engineering teams across multiple technical domains, including client and backend systems.
  • Strong product orientation with a track record of translating business goals and user needs into execution plans that drive measurable outcomes.
  • Experience leading complex cross-functional initiatives across Product, Design, Data Science, PMM, and partner engineering teams.
  • Experience in ads, monetization, personalization, recommendation systems, experimentation platforms, or other data-driven product environments at scale.
  • Demonstrated ability to use AI to improve speed and quality in day-to-day workflows such as analysis, prototyping, synthesis, planning, or operational execution.
  • Strong track record of critically evaluating and verifying AI-assisted work through testing, source-checking, data validation, experimentation, and peer review.
  • High integrity and ownership: you protect sensitive data, avoid over-reliance on AI, and remain accountable for final decisions, people leadership, and delivered outcomes.

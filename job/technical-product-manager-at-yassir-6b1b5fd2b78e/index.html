About the job

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced Technical Product Manager to join our Mobile Platform & Shared Services team at Yassir, responsible for driving the evolution, reliability, and scalability of our Super App platform. The role involves working closely with engineering, product, QA, DevOps, security, analytics, marketing, and external partners to manage platform-level capabilities, oversee mobile application releases, drive technical integrations, and deliver reusable services consumed by multiple domain squads. The ideal candidate combines strong technical product management skills with excellent communication, stakeholder management, and delivery ownership.

Key Responsibilities

1. Product & Delivery Management

  • Own and maintain the product roadmap, product backlog, sprint backlog, and delivery priorities.
  • Oversee day-to-day squad activities and facilitate Agile/Scrum ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Drive delivery execution using Agile/Scrum and SAFe practices, managing timelines, dependencies, risks, and stakeholder expectations.
  • Coordinate cross-functional initiatives and delivery dependencies across multiple decentralized development squads.

2. Mobile Release Management

  • Oversee the end-to-end mobile release lifecycle across Android and iOS, including release planning, testing, store submissions, progressive rollouts, and post-release monitoring.
  • Coordinate release readiness, timelines, and dependencies across development teams.
  • Manage communication and follow-ups with Google Play and Apple App Store teams, including submission feedback and review-related queries.
  • Continuously improve release processes, governance, reporting, and automation.

3. Application Performance & Reliability

  • Drive improvements in application stability, startup time, loading performance, crashes, and ANRs/App hangs.
  • Monitor application health using tools such as Firebase Crashlytics, Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty, and other performance monitoring solutions.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to investigate issues, identify root causes, and prioritize performance improvements with zero regressions.

4. Technical Integrations, APIs & Security

  • Manage third-party integrations, mobile SDKs, and platform-level technical dependencies.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams on API requirements, specifications, integration documentation, and technical design reviews.
  • Drive mobile application security initiatives involving solutions such as Firebase App Check, RASP, and Google Play Integrity.
  • Evaluate technical solutions, SDK updates, compatibility, and their impact on application performance and security.

5. Analytics & Customer Engagement

  • Manage centralized analytics capabilities and shared analytics libraries across the Super App.
  • Drive consistent analytics instrumentation, event tracking, and conversion measurement.
  • Work with tools such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, CleverTap, Adjust, and other product analytics and customer engagement platforms.
  • Enable marketing and product teams through reliable campaign tracking, attribution, push notifications, and customer lifecycle management capabilities.

6. Shared Services & Reusable Modules

  • Own the roadmap and adoption of reusable platform capabilities consumed by multiple product development squads.
  • Define integration requirements, configuration options, and usage guidelines to enable seamless adoption across teams.

7. Cross-functional Communication & Stakeholder Management

  • Act as a central point of coordination between engineering, product, QA, DevOps, security, analytics, marketing, and decentralized development squads.
  • Proactively communicate release plans, timelines, dependencies, risks, blockers, and delivery updates across relevant teams.
  • Drive alignment and collaboration across squads to ensure timely execution of platform initiatives, shared services, and adoption of reusable capabilities.
  • Facilitate technical and product discussions, requirement walkthroughs, stakeholder reviews, and cross-team coordination meetings.
  • Manage external communication and follow-ups with third-party vendors, technology partners, Google Play Store, and Apple App Store representatives.
  • Clearly communicate technical requirements, integration expectations, platform changes, and operational guidelines to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Ensure effective documentation and timely dissemination of decisions, action items, escalations, and project updates.

8. AI, Automation & Continuous Improvement

  • Identify opportunities to leverage AI and automation to improve squad productivity and operational efficiency.
  • Drive automation initiatives across release management, reporting, documentation, impact assessments, and routine monitoring.
  • Explore AI-powered tools and workflows to reduce manual effort and improve delivery processes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
  • 3+ years of experience in Technical Product Management, Product Ownership, or software delivery.
  • Strong understanding of mobile application ecosystems, particularly Android and iOS development and release lifecycles.
  • Good understanding of REST APIs, API specifications, third-party integrations, and mobile SDKs.
  • Familiarity with application performance monitoring, crash reporting, analytics, and mobile application security concepts.
  • Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies and familiarity with SAFe practices.
  • Proficiency in Jira, Confluence, and product/project management practices.
  • Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, with proven experience managing multiple stakeholders, facilitating cross-team discussions, and driving initiatives through influence and collaboration.
  • Strong technical documentation, analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Nice to Have

  • Experience working with large-scale consumer applications, Super Apps, or shared mobile platforms.
  • Familiarity with Kotlin, Swift, Firebase, CleverTap, Adjust, Google Analytics, and similar technologies.
  • Exposure to CI/CD pipelines, mobile application security solutions, and performance optimization.
  • Experience driving AI-powered workflows, process automation, and operational improvements.

What We're Looking For

Someone who can bridge the gap between product management and engineering, take ownership of technical platform initiatives, effectively manage squad delivery, communicate and align multiple internal and external stakeholders, and continuously improve the reliability, scalability, and capabilities of a large-scale mobile application ecosystem.

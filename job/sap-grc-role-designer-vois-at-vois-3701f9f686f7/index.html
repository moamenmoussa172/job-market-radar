About this Role

We are seeking an experienced SAP Security Role Design Specialist to play a critical role in governing and designing secure access within Vodafone’s EVO SAP landscape. Based in Pune, this role sits within the Global Roles, Governance & Compliance (RGC) team and focuses on ensuring SAP roles are designed and delivered in line with Vodafone’s Core Business Model, compliance standards, and global security policies. The role involves close collaboration with system integrators, project managers, and global stakeholders to support end-to-end demand delivery, from design through testing and go-live.

What you will do

Analyse SAP security solutions received from system integrators to ensure all user access and security requirements are met.Design, build, and test consistent global SAP roles for EVO systems, aligned to SAP best practices.Ensure all designed roles are accurately maintained within the EVO SAP Roles Catalogue.Govern user access processes in line with Global IT controls, SOX requirements, and compliance policies.Act as the RGC representative within the EVO demand process, supporting change requests and enhancements.Collaborate with global project managers, system integrators, and process leads to ensure security aspects are delivered within agreed timelines.Support testing and go-live activities for SAP role changes and new role implementations.Identify opportunities for continuous improvement and drive enhancements in SAP security role design and access management.Serve as a point of contact for global teams on SAP security role design, issue resolution, and process optimisation.

Who you are

An individual with strong experience translating business requirements into effective SAP security role designs.Skilled in understanding authorisation impacts across complex SAP landscapes.A collaborative professional with excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.Comfortable working in a global, multi-cultural environment with competing priorities and deadlines.Detail-oriented, with a strong governance mindset and a focus on quality and compliance.Experienced in SAP implementation and support, with broad exposure to modules such as SAP R/3, HANA, BI, HR, EP, MDM, SRM, CRM, PI, and SuccessFactors.Knowledgeable in GRC concepts, Segregation of Duties (SoD), SOX, and IT controls.Fluent in written and spoken English.

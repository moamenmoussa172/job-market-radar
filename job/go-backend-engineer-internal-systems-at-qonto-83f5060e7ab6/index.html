About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

[OUR MISSION AND CUSTOMERS] We are creating the freedom for SMEs to succeed by delivering Europe's leading finance workspace with banking at its core, augmented by financial tools. We are proud to be rated 4.7 on Trustpilot,based on 60,000 reviews. Our cultureputs customer satisfaction at the core of what we do (more about our culture here).

[OUR JOURNEY] Founded in 2016 and launched in 2017 byAlexandre and Steve, Qonto has grown to 1,700 Qontoers serving over 750,000 customers across 8 European countries. We have been profitable since 2023, and we are just getting started.

[OUR BELIEFS] We hire for skills and potential. With 80+ nationalities, 45% women, and 56% of women in our leadership team, diversity isn't a program; It's who we are. We've built a discrimination-free hiring process because the best teams are built on merit.

[AI AT QONTO] AI is deeply embedded in how we work (here) - Every Qontoer gets unlimited access to the best AI tools. We want people who experiment without waiting for permission, push AI beyond the obvious, know when to trust it, and when to question it.

Location: This is a fully remote position. You can work from anywhere in the United Kingdom.

Mission: Join us as a Backend Engineer in our Internal Systems team to build the platform our customer care agents, legal and operations teams rely on every day to support customers and act on their accounts: secure, reliable, auditable tools sitting on top of real money, with zero room for mistakes.

➡️ What you'll do

  • Design, build, deploy, and maintain the backend services behind our new backoffice platform, rebuilt from scratch with deep AI integration at the core of the product, owning reliability in production, not just at merge time
  • Help build our new backoffice platform from scratch in Go, replacing our legacy internal tooling
  • Design resilient APIs and workflows on a demanding multi-country platform with strong compliance constraints, letting internal teams act safely on customer data with the right permissions and audit trails
  • Work directly with internal customers (customer care, legal, operations, design) to turn their day-to-day needs into robust, well-scoped solutions
  • Own service architecture, resilience, and scalability with respect to Domain-Driven Design principles
  • Drive various continuous improvement topics, from small code fixes to significant improvements propagating across multiple teams

Our primary languages are Go and Ruby: the new platform is built in Go, and we use Python for some supporting projects. Our platform runs on a Kubernetes cluster hosted on AWS, with PostgreSQL as our database of choice. We're also using Kafka for event-driven architecture and ELK for logging and auditing, among many other tools and services.

➡️ What we're looking for

  • Technical expertise: Strong backend engineering experience with exposure to large-scale platform architecture, distributed systems, and real production constraints
  • Large-scale platform experience: You've built or run backend systems for platforms with a large user base (marketplaces, e-commerce, high-traffic SaaS or consumer products); experience with backoffice or internal tools is a plus
  • User-centric: You care about the people using your product, internal or external, as much as the code behind it
  • Stack fluency: Go is a plus, but you don't need to know our exact stack: you transfer fast and are comfortable with similar technologies and with code migration work
  • Quality-driven: You write tests, set up monitoring, handle incidents methodically, and do root cause analysis
  • AI-native engineering: You build with AI, not just use it. You've designed or shipped agentic flows (tool use, orchestration, evals, guardrails), and you're excited to build AI factories on top of our internal platform, with the control and auditability that real money demands
  • Ownership mindset: You track impact after release, flag regressions, and improve what's in your scope without being asked
  • Team player: Comfortable working with PMs, Designers, and non-technical stakeholders, and with long alignment phases before heavy coding begins; fluent in English

➡️ What we can offer you

  • We value upfront technical design, team reviews, and smart slicing, quality drives the velocity our customers need
  • Our org assigns one team lead per project across stacks, giving your team a clear mission and full autonomy on how it operates
  • Our lean toolbox, Kaizen, PDCA, lets you monitor and drive continuous improvement collaboratively with your peers and manager
  • Each engineer gets access to Cursor or Copilot, and we want people who use AI to raise the bar, not just to go faster

➡️ Your future team

  • As a Backend Engineer at Qonto, you'll join the Internal Systems team, a mission-based team working on the platform that powers our internal operations. Your internal users are your customers: you'll collaborate with them closely to understand their needs and measure the impact of what you ship.
  • You'll work alongside backend and frontend engineers, as well as Product Managers, in a team that is growing and building a large-scale platform from the ground up.
  • Your team owns a clear mission end-to-end. You'll contribute to discovery, make technical decisions, build and release solutions, monitor production performance, and steadily raise quality standards, following our "you build it, you own it" approach.
  • Beyond your immediate team, you'll stay connected with Qonto's wider Backend community through our Backend Guild, where engineers share knowledge, shape technical standards, and improve developer experience across the organisation.

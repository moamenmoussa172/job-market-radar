Senior Engineering Manager, Grafana Data Visualization

The Opportunity:

Grafana is an open-source observability product and a platform that teams and plugin developers build on. The Frontend group shapes its user experience (from dashboards and visualisation to navigation, search and sharing) and the foundations that support engineering teams and community contributors across the Grafana ecosystem.

As Senior Engineering Manager, you’ll lead the DataViz squad, owning its performance, delivery and customer outcomes. The squad owns a range of Grafana visualisations, their underlying libraries, configuration experiences and interactions, spanning core Grafana and standalone plugins. You’ll help shape how these capabilities enable users to understand their data and take action.
You’ll develop senior engineers, partner with Product and Design to shape direction, and improve how the squad works, including through AI and automation. We use Cloud-first rollout and experimentation to learn quickly, bringing relevant improvements to OSS and Enterprise.

AI Notice: We invest heavily in developer productivity. You can use modern AI coding assistants as part of your daily workflow (your choice of tools, within security guidelines), backed by a company-funded usage budget so you can iterate quickly without unnecessary friction. We encourage pragmatic AI-assisted development: faster prototyping, test generation, refactors, documentation, and incident follow-ups—always with strong code review and quality standards. You'll also have access to the latest frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.

What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Be accountable for the performance of a distributed engineering squad: set clear expectations, delegate effectively, coach and develop each team member, give timely feedback, address performance gaps and build a culture of trust and accountability.
  • Own hiring and team planning, aligning team capacity and skills with product priorities and longer-term needs.
  • Lead discovery with your squad: engage directly with customers, community members and internal users, combine their feedback with research and usage evidence, and use those insights to shape roadmaps and priorities.
  • Develop an informed point of view on product direction with Product, Design and senior engineers. Keep priorities explicit, assess new requests against business impact and capacity, and make clear decisions about what to stop or defer, balancing customer and community needs with long-term platform investments and operational demands.
  • Own delivery from discovery through rollout and measurement. Help the squad scope small, valuable increments, learn through short feedback loops and complete the most important work. Build the team’s ability to surface risks early and adapt plans as evidence and priorities change.
  • Use AI regularly in your management work to synthesise research and feedback, inform roadmap and resourcing decisions, and streamline coordination, reporting and follow-through.
  • Improve how the squad plans, makes decisions and delivers, including limits on work in progress. Lead the adoption of AI and automation across engineering and operational workflows, evaluating their impact on delivery speed, quality and time spent on repetitive work.
  • Set engineering direction with senior engineers for the squad’s visualisations, rendering libraries, panel configuration and interactions. Balance user experience, accessibility, performance, reliability and security with the evolution of shared foundations and the plugin ecosystem.
  • Build shared ownership so engineers recognise delivery risks, help one another and rally around the highest-priority outcomes. Coach the squad to make decisions together and shift effort across projects when needed.
  • Set and uphold engineering standards for quality, security and operability, including how teams evaluate and validate AI-assisted work.
  • Lead improvements across Grafana and R&D that increase engineering productivity and improve business outcomes.
  • Participate in the engineering management on-call rota, coordinate responses and drive improvements that reduce recurring disruption. Regularly use Grafana’s AI capabilities for observability, incident investigation and management, bringing that experience into product feedback and roadmap decisions.

What Makes You a Great Fit

  • Significant experience managing senior engineers, building healthy teams and developing technical leaders. You give clear feedback and address performance gaps directly and fairly.
  • A strong technical foundation in modern web or frontend systems, enabling you to evaluate technical trade-offs and shape architectural and engineering-quality decisions with senior engineers.
  • A track record of turning ambiguous problems into clear priorities and measurable results, delivering in small increments and adapting commitments as business needs and evidence change.
  • Strong product and customer judgement, with the curiosity to understand users’ workflows, develop a point of view and help teams make tough trade-offs between product needs and platform investments.
  • Clear, concise written communication and the ability to work effectively in a remote, globally distributed organisation.
  • An appreciation for open-source development and the judgement to balance Cloud-first delivery with the needs of customers, community members and internal teams.
  • Practical experience using AI and automation in your own leadership work, and leading teams to adopt them effectively.

Bonus Points For

  • Experience with React, TypeScript or comparable large-scale frontend ecosystems.
  • Experience leading teams that balance shared technical foundations with user-facing product development, including plugins or extensions.
  • Experience with data visualisation, interactive analytical tools or workflows that help users understand data and take action.
  • Experience leading major library or platform migrations while protecting compatibility and the user experience.
  • Experience working on a widely adopted open-source product.

Compensation & Rewards:

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Build something people love

Wealthsimple is Canada’s leading financial innovator. The company offers a full suite of simple, sophisticated financial products across managed investing, do-it-yourself trading, cryptocurrency, tax filing, spending and saving. Wealthsimple currently serves more than 4 million Canadians and holds over $169 billion in assets under administration. The company was founded in 2014 by a team of financial experts and technology entrepreneurs, and is headquartered in Toronto, Canada.

We're proud of what we've built — and we're just getting started. Read our Culture Manual and learn more about how we work.

About the role

This role turns people data into the decisions that shape how Wealthsimple hires, grows, and keeps its people. You will build the models, the analysis, and the data products that show how talent moves through Wealthsimple, where it concentrates, who is at risk of leaving, and what the workforce looks like a year out. Reporting to the Director, People Analytics, you will own that work end to end, from the question through to the thing people actually use. Success looks like recurring people decisions getting made from what you built.

About the team

People Analytics sits within People and Communications. We are growing the people domain and want to get deeper into our data, through the models, data products, and analysis that leaders across the company use to make calls about talent. We are a small team, building like a startup inside the People team. Analytics developers own the data foundation, analysts handle day-to-day reporting, and software engineers build the products that put insights in front of managers. As our first data scientist, you will help set what best in class looks like for people analytics at Wealthsimple.

What you’ll do

  • Own the model building and the deeper analysis on people data, including attrition risk, workforce forecasting, and how talent moves and develops across the company.
  • Build people data products that leaders keep coming back to, working with our software engineers to put them in front of managers across Wealthsimple.
  • Move up and down the data stack as the question demands, from raw data through to the model and the surface it lands on.
  • Turn open questions from leaders and partners across the company into analysis they can act on, and be clear about what the data can and cannot support.
  • Bring statistical rigour to how we understand talent density and performance management, forming hypotheses and testing them so people decisions are data informed.
  • Set the analytical standard for the function and raise the level of craft around you, through how we test, document, and review our work.

Skills you bring

  • 5+ years in data science or a related quantitative role, owning ambiguous problems end to end.
  • Advanced SQL and Python, applied to work you have taken all the way to production rather than left in a notebook.
  • Applied statistics and causal inference. You can pick a method, defend it, and explain why a correlation is not enough for the decision in front of you.
  • Experience building and evaluating predictive models on messy operational data, including the judgment to say when a model is not the right answer.
  • Experience turning analysis into recommendations that senior leaders act on, including pushing back when the question itself is wrong. You lead with the outcome, then explain how you got there.
  • Comfort working close to the data layer, with modelled datasets, point-in-time history, and a cloud data platform such as Snowflake.

Nice to have

  • HR data literacy: employee lifecycle events, job hierarchies, headcount types, and compensation structures.
  • Workforce planning or headcount forecasting.
  • Experience with survey and engagement data.
  • Experience measuring people performance, such as review ratings, calibration outcomes, or goal attainment.

People analytics experience helps, but it is not required. If you have done this kind of work in product, marketing, growth, or customer analytics and you are curious about people data, we want to hear from you.

Who you are

  • You are energised by building, and you do not need a paved road to do good work.
  • You think about privacy the way a good engineer thinks about security, as a design constraint rather than an obstacle.
  • You would rather ship a useful answer and improve it than hold a perfect one back.
  • You are direct about uncertainty and say what you do not know before anyone asks.
  • You move between technical and non-technical conversations naturally.
  • You are pragmatic about AI tools and know where they speed you up and where they do not.

Why you’ll love this role

  • Ground-floor impact: Most of this work hasn’t been done yet, so you’ll shape how it gets done.
  • Visibility: Your work goes in front of leadership and shapes real decisions about talent.
  • Builders around you: Your models ship into products managers use, not slide decks

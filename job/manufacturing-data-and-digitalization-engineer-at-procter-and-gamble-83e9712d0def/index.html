Job Description

Are you passionate about programming, data, and digital technologies and ready to apply your technical expertise in a real manufacturing environment? At P&G, you can make a meaningful impact by developing digital solutions that connect production equipment, data, and technology. You will work hands-on with manufacturing data, industrial systems, visualization tools, and emerging AI technologies while helping shape the future of digital manufacturing at our Budapest Plant. 🎯

🌍ABOUT US:

At Procter & Gamble, we have been working for more than 180 years to make everyday life easier for millions of people around the world through our trusted and well-known consumer brands. As a global company, we are present in around 70 countries, and our portfolio includes internationally recognized brands such as Always®, Ariel®, Gillette®, Head & Shoulders®, Pampers®, Pantene®, Tampax®, Naturella® and Discreet®. Our Budapest Plant in Csömör has been part of P&G’s international manufacturing network since 1991. In our facility, we produce feminine hygiene products on modern, automated production lines, including products related to the Always®, Naturella®, Discreet® and Tampax® brands. In our work, we place strong emphasis on safety, quality, collaboration and continuous improvement. Our manufacturing processes are supported by automated technologies, technical systems and development projects. Our warehouse and material handling teams ensure that the necessary raw materials reach the right areas on time and safely. We work in an environment where safety, quality and continuous improvement are at the center of everything we do.

📋WHAT YOU WILL DO:

  • Develop and support manufacturing and operational data collection systems
  • Build and maintain connections to PLCs, industrial controllers, and other manufacturing equipment
  • Design, implement, and program network communication and data connections between manufacturing systems
  • Develop Grafana dashboards and other data visualization solutions to make manufacturing data accessible and actionable
  • Process and analyze manufacturing data to support operational and business decisions
  • Explore, develop, and implement AI, Machine Learning, and other advanced digital solutions in a manufacturing environment
  • Develop scalable technical solutions tailored to manufacturing and business needs
  • Collaborate with Operations, Engineering, IT, and other cross-functional teams to deliver digital solutions

🔑WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR?

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or another relevant STEM field
  • Relevant professional or project background in software development, data engineering, industrial digitalization, automation, or a related technical field
  • Strong programming skills, with practical knowledge of Python, SQL, .NET, or similar technologies
  • Working knowledge of databases, data processing, and data modeling
  • Good understanding of networks, system connectivity, and data communication
  • Proficiency in Hungarian and English
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently and take ownership of technical solutions

➕NICE TO HAVE:

  • Familiarity with PLC systems, industrial controllers, or industrial communication protocols
  • Hands-on knowledge of Grafana or similar data visualization platforms
  • Understanding of manufacturing data acquisition, industrial networks, or OT/IT connectivity
  • Exposure to AI, Machine Learning, or Computer Vision projects
  • Background in a manufacturing, industrial automation, or digitalization environment
  • Proactive, hands-on mindset and ability to collaborate effectively across Operations, Engineering, and IT teams

Job description & summary

Are you an experienced data professional ready to take ownership of complex analytical challenges and help shape how clients use their data? Join our team and play a leading role in delivering modern data solutions that drive real business impact.

As a Senior Data Analyst, you will:

  • Design and build data models that support scalable and reliable analytics.
  • Define data transformation logic and business rules across complex data pipelines.
  • Create and maintain reports, dashboards, and visualizations that support decision-making.
  • Prepare functional and technical solution designs for data and analytics projects.
  • Work with large datasets, identify business insights, ensure data quality, and drive process improvements.
  • Collaborate directly with client stakeholders and translate business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Lead and mentor junior analysts and support their professional development.
  • Deliver projects end-to-end, from analysis and design through implementation and handover.
  • Promote data quality, governance, and continuous improvement across engagements.
  • Bridge the gap between business and technology by turning data into actionable recommendations.

What matters to us

  • 3+ years of hands-on experience in data analytics, data management, BI, or a related field.
  • Strong knowledge of SQL, ETL/ELT processes, data warehousing, data quality, and data governance principles.
  • Hands-on experience with analytical and reporting tools such as Power BI, Tableau, Python, R, or similar.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain complex insights to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Client-facing mindset with strong stakeholder management capabilities.

What will help you stand out

  • Experience with Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, or cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP).
  • Understanding of modern data architecture concepts such as Data Lakehouse or Data Mesh.
  • Experience with master data management and data governance initiatives.
  • Proven delivery across the full project lifecycle, from requirements gathering to implementation.
  • Experience leading, coaching, and developing junior team members.
  • Background in banking, financial services, or insurance.

Why you’ll love working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.
  • A flexiblebenefit programme with 45,000 points to spend on what matters most to you.
  • 30 days of paid time off, including three well-being days and two additional days off at the end of the year.
  • An opportunity to give back to the community with one paid volunteering day every year.
  • The chance to work from one of PwC’s international offices (available from the Senior Associate level).
  • We support your learning and development journey: Benefit from ongoing training and certifications in consulting, business, and technology skills, including Agile and project management, data analytics (Power BI, Python, Alteryx, Tableau), cloud and Microsoft technologies (Azure, Power Platform), business process analysis, and consulting essentials, such as stakeholder management, communication, and presentation skills.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.
  • Social events and Away Days.

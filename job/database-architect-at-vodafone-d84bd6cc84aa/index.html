What you’ll do

• Design and define architecture standards for open-source database platforms such as PostgreSQL, Casandra, MongoDB, Redis, and other related technologies.
• Lead the development of highly available (HA), disaster recovery (DR), and business continuity solutions for mission-critical database environments.
• Architect and enhance database platforms across on-premises and cloud infrastructures. Enhancing the scalability and performance of existing database architecture.
• Developing database structures and features according to organizational needs.
• Supervising and mentoring opensource database teams.
• Architect and enhance database platforms across on-premises and cloud infrastructures.
• Drive performance optimization, capacity planning, scalability assessments, and technology lifecycle management.
• Define and govern database architectures running on Kubernetes, OpenShift, and containerized environments.
• Develop Database-as-a-Service (DBaaS) strategies and automation frameworks.
• Establish best practices for database security, encryption, access control, compliance, and governance.
• Evaluate emerging technologies and provide architectural recommendations aligned with business and technology objectives.
• Act as a technical leader, mentor, and trusted advisor for database engineering and operations teams.
• Collaborate closely with application, infrastructure, cloud, security, and DevOps teams to deliver end-to-end data platform solutions.

Who you are

• At least 8 years database management experiences
• At least 4 years experience in Opensource , Bigdata or DWH DB Management
• In-depth knowledge of database technologies, architecture, and data security
• Advanced analytical, leadership, and problem-solving skills
• Strong stakeholder engagement skills
• Strong people leadership skills with a focus on developing operational capacity and taking accountability for service delivery and operational excellence.
• Demonstrate a strong ability to build and maintain productive relationships with customers, subordinates, peers and top management.
• Effective people management skills
• Clear and concise communication with customers and team
• Ability to collaboratively solve complex problems
• Ability to work under pressure
• Advanced English

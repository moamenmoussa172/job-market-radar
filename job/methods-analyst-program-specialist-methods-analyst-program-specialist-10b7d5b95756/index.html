Job Duties

  • Under general supervision, supports manufacturing operations by creating complex manufacturing sequences whereby raw materials are converted and assembled into quality finished products
  • Analyzes engineering prints and other specified requirements of items to be manufactured and determines the most efficient and cost effective method when creating process plans/routings, instructions, methods, procedures, tooling, etc. required to manufacture finished products
  • Specifies machines to be utilized, routings and stages of operation, new tools, component parts and/or requests purchase or design of additional tooling as required
  • Develops preliminary cost estimates to support studies of product feasibility and assists in establishing selling prices
  • Creates complex numerical control programs to support specified machining operations
  • Validates accuracy of programs prior to release to shop floor
  • Discusses and resolves issues with planning and/or programming with other departments as required
  • Analyzes and provides direction for manufacturability for product and process improvement or development
  • Utilizes software packages used in the manufacturing process such as SAP, MATRIX, Factory Planner, Solidworks, AutoCAD, ESPRIT/UniGraphics, etc
  • Job tasks, correct performed, impact indirectly on cost containment, efficiency, profitability or operationsManufacturing process development and implementation
  • Design for Manufacturability (DFM) and protoype-to-production transition.
  • Manufacturing routing, process planning, and work center development
  • Develops preliminary cost estimates to support studies of product feasibility and assists in establishing selling price
  • CNC machining processes, NC programming, and production methods analysis
  • Engineering drawing and complex print interpretation
  • Production planning and manufacturing capacity analysis
  • Lean manufacturing and operational excellence practices
  • Cost reduction, productivity improvement, and process standardization
  • Cross-functional coordination with Engineering, Operations, Supply Chain, and Technology teams

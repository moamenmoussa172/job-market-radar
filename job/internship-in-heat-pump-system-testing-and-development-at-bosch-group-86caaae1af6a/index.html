Internship in Heat Pump System Testing and Development

Bosch Group · Wernau (Neckar), Germany

Onsite · Intern / Student · QA / Test Engineer · Posted 29 October 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Join our international, cross-functional product development team and contribute to the development of innovative heat pumps, one of the key technologies of the energy transition.

  • During your assignment, you will contribute to the development of DHW features for our next-generation heat pumps.
  • You will investigate market, regulatory and technical needs and derive DHW technical requirements for heat pump applications across our key target markets.
  • Additionally, you will develop and improve experimental procedures and methodologies for operation and performance evaluation.
  • You will support the execution and evaluation of experimental and virtual tests to assess and optimize DHW performance.
  • Finally, you will analyze results and help identify potential improvements in efficiency, performance and system behavior.
  • Education: studies in the field of Mechanical Engineering, Energy and Environmental Technology, Process Engineering or comparable, with good grades
  • Experience and Knowledge:
  • excellent understanding of thermodynamics, heat transfer and fluid mechanics
  • pronounced skills in modeling and simulation
  • proficiency with MATLAB Simulink
  • preferably prior familiarity with building technology
  • Personality and Working Practice: you are a responsible and independent person who approaches complex tasks in a structured and careful manner while enjoying working as part of a team
  • Work Routine: your on-site presence is required
  • Languages: fluent in English and very good in German

Start: according to prior agreement
Duration: 4 - 6 months

Requirement for this internship is the enrollment at university. Please attach your CV, transcript of records, enrollment certificate, examination regulations and if indicated a valid work and residence permit.

Diversity and inclusion are not just trends for us but are firmly anchored in our corporate culture. Therefore, we welcome all applications, regardless of gender, age, disability, religion, ethnic origin or sexual identity.

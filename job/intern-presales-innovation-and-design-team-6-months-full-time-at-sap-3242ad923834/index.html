About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

This is an entry-level position. Candidates should have recently completed, or be due to complete within the next 1-2 months, a relevant degree or qualification. Relocation support is not available for this role. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the UAE at the time of application.

Meet the Team:

· You will be part of the amazing team of Customer Advisory and Presales in Middle East South, working with colleagues from UAE, Qatar, Oman and many other countries

· You will work closely with our innovation team which is involved in various projects spanning across industries

· You will be exposed to a large set of roles within SAP

What you’ll do:

· Support the team in developing storylines for customer presentations

· Research industry trends and futuristic trends that will support customer engagements

· Participate in team events and call meetings

· Support in facilitating Design Thinking workshops

· Get exposed to the energetic life of working in a customer facing team of one of the biggest software companies in the world.

What you bring:

· Proficiency and passion for Graphic Design OR Video Editing

· An eye for design & detail

· An interest in developing your design skills

· A keen interest in innovation and new technologies

· High energy and willingness to work in a fast paced environment

· Exposure to UX/UI mockups software (Adobe XD, Figma, or other)

Bring out your best
SAP innovations help more than four hundred thousand customers worldwide work together more efficiently and use business insight more effectively. Originally known for leadership in enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, SAP has evolved to become a market leader in end-to-end business application software and related services for database, analytics, intelligent technologies, and experience management. As a cloud company with two hundred million users and more than one hundred thousand employees worldwide, we are purpose-driven and future-focused, with a highly collaborative team ethic and commitment to personal development. Whether connecting global industries, people, or platforms, we help ensure every challenge gets the solution it deserves. At SAP, you can bring out your best.

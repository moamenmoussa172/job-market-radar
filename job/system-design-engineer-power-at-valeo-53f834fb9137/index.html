Mission & Responsibilities :

The System Engineer is responsible for the development of the different system functions in the power electronics related products (Inverters, On Board Chargers and DC/DC converters) according to state of the art know-how, ASPICE and ISO 26262. The responsibilities of the system engineer are summarized below:

  • Contribute to the RFQ phase for the requirements assessment and the customer discussions in the project
  • Communicate professionally and autonomously with the customer and the team members.
  • Design, Analyze and Review detailed system requirements.
  • Simulate Electromagnetic behavior of synchronous machines (PMSM,EESM) and the associated power electronics equipment (Inverters, DC/DC converters etc...)
  • Create all the necessary technical documents, data sheets and descriptions, worst case analysis
  • Work with the system architect regarding the interfaces, and the system requirements in close coordination with the HW / SW / MD architects
  • Support the different stakeholders from SW, Sys, HW, Testing
  • Conduct reviews for the SW Reqs, SW Architecture, System integration test cases, System Test cases
  • Support the construction team in creating an efficient cross-project system architecture and ensuring compatibility with the project-specific architecture
  • Follow defined project processes/guidelines/checklists and ensure process conformance.
  • Report work progress continuously through attendance of daily & weekly project internal meetings.
  • Contribute effectively in the continuous improvement of his/her project, team, and work environment by submitting improvement proposals whenever possible.
  • Raise flags proactively for any issue that can impact his/her project or team.
  • Perform all the activities using the agreed internal/external configuration management tool

Requirements:

  • Bachelor degree in Electrical, Mechatronics Engineering
  • Masters in Power electronics/Motor Control or Control theory is a plus
  • Excellent command of English language is a must.
  • German language is a plus.
  • +1.5 years of relevant experience.
  • Strong Power Electronics Knowledge and foundations are a must
  • Notes:
  • MATLAB scripting and Simulink modelling are a must, Pspice modelling is a plus
  • Good knowledge in Electrical drives and Control of electrical machines (FOC, Pulse width modulation techniques, current controller design, etc..)
  • OR Good knowledge of DC/DC converters (Boost, LLC, etc..) DC/DC Converter Modelling (Switched Model, Average Model )
  • Controller Design for Dc/Dc converters (Voltage Control Mode, Current Control Mode)
  • Good knowledge in Control theory and Signal processing techniques
  • Good knowledge in Embedded systems concepts and Micro-controllers, Knowledge of the Aurix Mc is a plus
  • Autosar and BSW knowledge is a plus
  • Good knowledge about software engineering (requirement, design, development, Architecture, verification and validation) is a plus
  • Automotive SPICE is a Plus
  • Knowledge of the Safety ISO 26262 is a plus
  • Masters in Electrical Engineering is a plus.
  • Previous system engineering experience is not a must.
  • Notes:
  • At least a graduation project focus on Power electronics is needed for this position
  • Having Either the knowledge of DC/DC converters or Inverters is ok for this position

About the job

About the Team

The Collective Alignment (User Signals) team is a post-training research team working at the intersection of data science, human feedback, and reinforcement learning. We develop methods for learning from real-world user interactions to build AI systems that deliver lasting value, helping people learn, grow, and accomplish increasingly complex goals with ChatGPT.

Learning from user feedback presents fundamental research challenges: signals are often noisy, biased, and difficult to interpret, and improvements in offline metrics do not always translate into better experiences for users. Our team develops scientific approaches to understanding these signals and translating them into improvements in model behavior through evaluations, reward modeling, and post-training.

About the Role

As a Research Engineer or Research Scientist, you will investigate how AI systems can learn from human feedback and real-world usage to better serve users over time.

You will develop new approaches to extracting meaningful learning signals from complex user interactions and incorporating those signals into model training.

This role requires strong deep learning research skills, scientific rigor, and curiosity about how people interact with AI. You will design controlled experiments and ablations, critically examine the relationship between offline evaluations and online model behavior, and work closely with research, product, and data science teams to understand whether improvements translate into meaningful value for users.

In this role, you will:

  • Develop novel methods for learning from human feedback and user interaction signals.
  • Research approaches to incorporating feedback into reward modeling, reinforcement learning, and post-training.
  • Design rigorous experiments and ablation studies to isolate the effects of new data, objectives, and training methods.
  • Develop offline evaluations and investigate their relationship with online experiments and real-world user outcomes.
  • Analyze unexpected results, investigate discrepancies between evaluation metrics and user behavior, and develop hypotheses to guide further research.
  • Evaluate tradeoffs and regressions to improve targeted model behaviors while preserving broader capabilities.
  • Collaborate with research, alignment, product, and data science teams.
  • Implement and debug experiments across large-scale deep learning research systems.

You might thrive in this role if you:

  • Have a strong background in deep learning research through academic or industry experience.
  • Have demonstrated research ability through publications or substantive research contributions in deep learning or LLM post-training.
  • Have experience developing new machine learning methods and evaluating them through rigorous, hypothesis-driven experimentation.
  • Think critically about experimental design, measurement, and what evaluation results do and do not establish about model behavior.
  • Are comfortable designing ablations, investigating surprising results, and reasoning about the relationship between offline metrics and real-world outcomes.
  • Have strong programming skills and experience working with complex ML codebases.
  • Are curious about how people interact with AI and how those interactions can inform better models.

Nice to have:

  • Experience with RLHF, reinforcement learning, or modern LLM post-training.
  • Research or demonstrated interest in AI alignment, human feedback, or user signals.
  • Experience with online experimentation, evaluation methodology, or learning from noisy and biased feedback.
  • Experience connecting machine learning research with real-world product behavior.

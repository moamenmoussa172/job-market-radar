About the job

Job Description:

Airbus Commercial Aircraft is looking for Vibration Support, Digital Integration Engineer (f/m) to join our Flight Physics Centre of Competence based in Toulouse, France.

You will join the Loads and Aeroelastics domain within our transnational Engineering organization, analyzing aircraft dynamic stability and resolving in-service and production vibration events. In this role, you will act as a digital catalyst, modernizing analytical capabilities through Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence while coordinating customer support requests across airline, delivery, and flight test interfaces.

Your working environment:

Global capital of aeronautics and European capital for space research, Toulouse is a dynamic city in the southwest of France served by an international airport. Ideally located between the Mediterranean sea and the Atlantic ocean and close to the Pyrenees mountains, it offers plenty of options for outdoor activities!

How we care for you:

  • Financial rewards: Attractive salary, agreements on success and profit sharing schemes, employee savings plan abounded by Airbus and employee stock purchase plan on a voluntary basis.
  • Work / Life Balance: Extra days-off for special occasions, holiday transfer option, a Staff council offering many social, cultural and sport activities and other services.
  • Wellbeing / Health: Complementary health insurance coverage (disability, invalidity, death). Depending on the site: health services center, concierge services, gym, carpooling application.
  • Individual development: Great upskilling opportunities and development prospects with unlimited access to +10.000 e-learning courses to develop your employability, certifications, expert career path, accelerated development programmes, national and international mobility.

At Airbus, we support you to work, connect and collaborate more easily and flexibly. Wherever possible, we foster flexible working arrangements to stimulate innovative thinking.

Your challenges:

  • Spearhead the digitalization of the vibration team by deploying ML and AI applications to accelerate troubleshooting workflows.
  • Support aircraft ramp-up by optimizing diagnostic processes and reducing vibration-related reflight rates.
  • Deliver predictive and preventive maintenance strategies through advanced data analytics models to secure on-time operations.
  • Provide operational feedback and statistical in-service reporting to the design office for continuous improvement and certification.
  • Upgrade dynamic stability calculation methods, digital toolsets, and engineering processes to meet industry standards.

Your boarding pass:

  • Background in aeronautics, structural engineering, or structural dynamics.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in an engineering or industrial environment.
  • Proven technical proficiency in applied Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and data analytics.
  • Practical knowledge of aircraft maintenance, Customer Service, or MAP operations.
  • Full professional proficiency in English (negotiation level); French language skills or willingness to learn.

Not a 100% match? No worries! Airbus supports your personal growth with customized development solutions.

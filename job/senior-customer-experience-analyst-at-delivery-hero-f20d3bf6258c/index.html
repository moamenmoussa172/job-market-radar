About the job

Ready to turn customer voice into strategic action? We operate in a fast-paced environment where data, agile methodologies, and great teams come together to tackle unique challenges every day.

As a Senior CX Insights Analyst, you will provide a 360° view of our customer experience, transforming qualitative & quantitative feedback into powerful business insights. If you are analytical, story-driven, and passionate about elevating CX across cross-functional teams, join us and shape the landscape of customer experience!

What you ‘ll do:

Insights & Analytics

  • Provide a 360° view of customer experience by collecting, cleaning, and synthesizing qualitative and quantitative data from diverse sources (CSAT, NPS, QA reporting, support cases, app reviews, UnitQ, Qualtrics).
  • Apply advanced analytical techniques (e.g., statistical analysis, regression, clustering, segmentation) to identify root causes, correlations, and predictive patterns across personas and customer journey stages.
  • Partner closely with BI team to design, build, and maintain robust CX dashboards and automated reporting solutions.
  • Serve as the guardian of key CX metrics, establishing robust tracking to define, interpret, and measure business and operational impact (e.g., churn risk indicators, operational efficiency).

Customer Experience Programs & Storytelling

  • Drive the data-driven design, execution, and continuous optimization of customer feedback loops.
  • Craft compelling, data-rich storytelling reports (monthly, quarterly, ad-hoc) that inform leadership, highlight measurable impact, and deliver actionable recommendations.
  • Champion and quantitatively amplify the customer voice in product and operational roadmap decisions alongside CX DH and excellence teams.

Stakeholder Collaboration & Action

  • Work closely with Operations, Product, Marketing, QA, and Training teams to ensure analytical insights are translated into concrete action and measurable improvements.
  • Lead regular "Insights to Action" syncs, presenting key findings and facilitating data-driven discussions with critical teams to embed a customer-first mindset.
  • Proven experience as a CX Analyst, BI Analyst, Data Analyst, or similar role focused on Customer Experience.
  • Strong expertise in synthesizing qualitative and quantitative feedback (CSAT, NPS, CES, QA scores).
  • Proficiency with data analysis tools and CX platforms (e.g. Power BI/Tableau, Qualtrics, UnitQ, Playvox).
  • Demonstrated ability in data storytelling and translating complex analytics into executive-level recommendations.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills with a collaborative, cross-functional mindset.

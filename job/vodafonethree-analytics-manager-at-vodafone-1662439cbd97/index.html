Be a part of our Consumer team where creating and developing products, services and propositions is at the forefront. From the way we interact with our customers, to how we communicate in our campaigns and create data-driven propositions, this is where some of our best ideas are brought to life.

What you’ll do

  • Own the end-to-end analysis approach for allocated squad priorities, translating business questions into clear insight, recommendations and actions that support commercial strategy.
  • Partner with Chapter Leads, Senior Analytics Managers and commercial stakeholders to identify opportunities to drive the Consumer Strategic Plan. Including proposition adoption and performance, customer satisfaction, value, retention and channel performance.
  • Deliver robust analysis using high-quality data, clear assumptions and best-practice coding standards, ensuring outputs are accurate, repeatable and easy to explain.
  • Combine customer, commercial, channel, usage and revenue data with wider insight sources, including market research, to build a joined-up view of customer behaviour and business performance.
  • Quality assure data, methodology and outputs, proactively identifying risks, gaps or inconsistencies and taking action to strengthen confidence in the insight.
  • Communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences, using effective storytelling to influence decisions and support measurable business action.
  • Collaborate across Consumer, Enterprise and wider analytics teams to share best practice, align insight approaches and avoid duplication of effort

Who you are

  • Proven experience delivering customer or commercial insight from large datasets.
  • Strong SQL skills, with experience of Python, R or SAS desirable.
  • Experience using data visualisation tools such as Power BI, Qlik or Tableau.
  • Strong analytical judgement, including the ability to quality assure data, identify trends and explain drivers of performance.
  • Able to translate complex analysis into clear, practical recommendations for senior stakeholders.
  • Commercially curious, with the ability to connect customer behaviour, channel performance, revenue and retention outcomes.
  • Comfortable working collaboratively across analytics, commercial, research and operational teams.
  • Degree educated or equivalent relevant experience.

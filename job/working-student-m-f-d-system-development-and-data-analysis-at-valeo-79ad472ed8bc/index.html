About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

We are looking for YOU as a Working Student – System Development & Data Analysis (m/f/d) for our location in Braunschweig.

These challenges await you:

  • Support the development and improvement of system development and engineering tools
  • Develop solutions for data processing, analysis, visualization, and automation
  • Support the analysis of measurement, test, and system data
  • Develop and evaluate data-driven and machine-learning-based solutions for engineering applications
  • Automate repetitive engineering and analysis activities to improve efficiency and process quality.
  • Support the integration and processing of different data sources, datasets, documents, and system information
  • Work closely with system, software, and application engineers on technical topics

You inspire us with that:

  • Currently studying Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electrical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, or a comparable field
  • Good programming skills in C++, Python, or other programming languages, with strong willingness to learn new technologies
  • Experience with Python-based data processing and automation is an advantage
  • Experience with image processing, computer vision, or signal processing
  • Basic knowledge of Machine Learning / AI
  • Experience with scripting, APIs, file/document processing, or automation is an advantage
  • Familiarity with libraries and frameworks such as NumPy, Pandas, OpenCV, Matplotlib, SciPy, or similar
  • Analytical and structured approach to problem solving
  • Interest in automotive systems and software-based engineering solutions is an advantage.
  • Good communication skills in English

About the job

Would you like to be part of a team focused on increasing adoption of Amazon Web Services in UK EdTech? Do you have the business savvy and the technical background necessary to help establish Amazon as a key technology platform provider? As Account Manager in EdTech, you will have the exciting opportunity to help promote the growth and shape the future of educational technology. Your responsibility will be to increase the market penetration of AWS in EdTech accounts. The ideal candidate will possess both a business and technical background that enables them to engage an account at CXO levels, as well as with product managers, software developers and IT architects. He/she should also be a self-starter who is prepared to develop and execute against a territory coverage plan.
Key job responsibilities
- Promote AWS services in a defined territory or industry vertical.
- Develop and execute against a comprehensive account/territory plan.
- Create & articulate compelling value propositions around AWS services.
- Facilitate customer adoption.
- Maintain a robust projects pipeline.
- Work with partners to extend reach & promote adoption.
- Contribute to positive customer experience
- Experience building strategic relationships with stakeholders, including communicating and collaborating across teams and functions
- Experience with pipeline management skills to include utilization of Salesforce or other CRM tools
- Experience in Sales/Business Development or Account Management roles
- Experience in business development including complex agreements with integrators and ISVs
- Experience owning sales process from sourcing to close on deals that are 12-18 month sales cycles
- Experience creating and implementing long-term transformational account strategies in a customer-facing role or equivalent
- Experience converting insights into strategy and communicating complex ideas clearly and logically in written communication
- Experience and track record of exceeding sales goals
- Experience in direct sales or business development in software, cloud or SaaS markets selling to C-level executives
- Experience making strategic business decisions and managing internal relationships, or experience using financial models, KPIs, and data analysis to support business decisions
- Knowledge of existing and developing technologies
- Familiarity with generative AI services and EdTech use cases

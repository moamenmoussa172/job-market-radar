About the job

Distributor Support & Enablement

  • Be present side by side with distributors across GCC — responding to technical inquiries and supporting equipment selections for VRF and DX mega project opportunities.
  • Handle and resolve complex Hitachi VRF and DX system selection inquiries — reducing lead times on mega-project bids and ensuring distributors submit technically accurate proposals.
  • Review technical submittals, compliance statements, and technical documentation for active mega project pursuits across UAE and GCC.
  • Evaluate the technical competency and capability of distributor application teams — design and deliver structured training programs to elevate their skills on a quarterly basis.
  • Conduct product selection training sessions, installation training for contractors, and technical workshops to improve distributor capability and market effectiveness with coordination with product team and after sales department.
  • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering or equivalent technical discipline.
  • 5-8 years of experience in the HVAC industry — specifically in pre-sales, business development, or technical sales support for VRF and light commercial AC products.
  • Strong technical knowledge of VRF and DX system design, equipment selection, heat load calculations, and troubleshooting.
  • Proven track record of working with distributors, MEP consultants, contractors, and developers across UAE and GCC.
  • Experience preparing technical submittals, compliance statements, and techno-commercial proposals.
  • Strong presentation and communication skills in English and Arabic is a plus.
  • The candidate must hold a valid UAE driving license and maintain it in good standing for the duration of employment.

Why Join Us?
Fast-Paced & Dynamic: Work in a high-energy environment where adaptability, and strategic thinking are key.

What’s in It for You?
Career Growth: Access to professional development opportunities and leadership exposure.

About the job

MongoDB Professional Services' exists to support the development of our customers’ vision, accelerate customers’ time to value, and drive a multitude of customer adoption scenarios - from building new solutions to modernizing legacy applications or migrating to cloud. Our consulting engagements ensure that our some of the most results-oriented and complex global organizations successfully deliver on their critical initiatives and get the best out of MongoDB.

Help us take our business to the next level of excellence in delivery and governance. You will have abundant opportunities to significantly impact the growth of MongoDB and collaborate with a team of technologists, sales and delivery professionals who are among the best in their fields.

This role will be based remotely in Taiwan

Position Expectations

  • Accountable for project planning and execution, customer experience, and ensuring MongoDB delivery meets committed scope and budget
  • Employ project management excellence to navigate with agility the complex organizations of large enterprises, building mindshare to plan and execute programs likely to have ambiguous risks and challenges
  • Maintain excellence of project governance with internal and customer stakeholders, managing day-to-day project planning and execution, project scope, expectations and risks, and ensuring timelines are met and quality results produced
  • Lend support to pre-sale engagements to assist with scoping of solution and project scope definition
  • Adopt and contribute to the continuous improvement of MongoDB's engagement and project delivery methodologies
  • Confidently lead project meetings, owning meeting agenda and meeting outcomes
  • Lead the management of scope, change controls, customer expectations and timelines

Measures of Success

  • Collaborate directly with customers and other internal teams to lead and deliver high-quality, reference-able engagements
  • Of paramount importance is predictability: of forecasts for resource needs and revenue; of internal & customer stakeholder management; of outcomes; of risk management; of customer expectations and experience
  • Demonstrate eye for business through robust control of the internal and external budget, project margin, resource utilization and billability across your entire project portfolio

Candidate Profile

  • University degree or relevant work experience
  • 5+ years proven experience with enterprise-scale software solutions, large scale software engineering or consulting services delivery experience preferred
  • Demonstrated leadership skills within complex, transformative projects, with direct responsibility for leading project teams, delivering high quality results on schedule and within budget
  • Comfortable creating plans and priorities for both project delivery and customer resources
  • Demonstrated belief in the power of stakeholder management and communications; track record of affecting change through stakeholder engagement
  • Outstanding and effective interpersonal skills; strong verbal and written communication abilities
  • Consulting and delivery experience serving banking and securities clients in Taiwan is preferred
  • Certification or deep experience with standard frameworks such as the PMI PMBOK, or Scrum methodologies would be beneficial

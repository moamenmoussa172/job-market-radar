About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Ireland, Serbia, United Kingdom, United States.

Enterprises are rolling Perplexity out to thousands of employees and connecting it to their most sensitive internal data. Their security teams want to know how to do that safely, and they want to hear it from someone who has done real security work.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We're looking for a Cybersecurity Solutions Architect to be that person. You'll be the security expert our enterprise customers work with as they deploy Perplexity: running architecture and hardening sessions with their security leads, reviewing how they're configuring identity, data access, and admin controls, and helping them get to a setup they're confident in. You'll sit on the Security team and work closely with our enterprise sales, customer success, and product teams, and you'll bring what you learn from customers back into how we build and secure the product.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Lead security architecture workshops and hardening reviews with enterprise customers' security and IT teams as they deploy Perplexity. This covers identity and SSO/SCIM, connectors to internal data, access controls, data retention, admin and audit settings, and managed browser and endpoint policy.
  • Act as the technical security voice in strategic enterprise deals. Answer deep security reviews and questionnaires, and represent our security posture to CISOs and security architects.
  • Build and maintain the materials customers rely on: hardening guides, reference architectures, configuration baselines, and security FAQs.
  • Help customers configure enterprise controls in their own environments, such as identity providers, MDM and device management tools like Microsoft Intune, and data source permissions. Troubleshoot when something doesn't apply as expected.
  • Turn recurring customer security requests into product feedback. Work with product and engineering to prioritize the controls enterprises need.
  • Partner with account executives and customer success on customer-specific security asks, and know when to bring in Security Engineering or Legal.
  • Track common customer concerns and gaps, and help shape how we communicate security and trust externally.

QUALIFICATIONS

  • 5+ years in security engineering, security architecture, or a customer-facing security role such as solutions architect, sales engineer, or security consultant.
  • Hands-on depth in enterprise identity and access (SSO, SAML/OIDC, SCIM, conditional access) and in SaaS security configuration.
  • Working knowledge of the Microsoft ecosystem (Entra ID, Intune, M365, Purview) and at least one other major enterprise stack, such as Google Workspace or Okta.
  • Comfortable leading technical conversations with CISOs and security architects. Able to say no, explain trade-offs plainly, and earn trust in the room.
  • Familiarity with compliance frameworks enterprise customers ask about, such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and FedRAMP, and with how they map to product controls.
  • Clear written communication. You can turn a messy customer environment into a crisp set of recommendations.
  • Bonus: experience securing AI or LLM products, agent integrations, or data connectors in enterprise environments.
  • Bonus: background in browser security, endpoint management, or data loss prevention.

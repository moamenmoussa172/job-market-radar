About the job

Description

GSS Tech Group is hiring a Senior Developer, Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM, for a leading government financial institution in Abu Dhabi. You will design, build and maintain Dynamics 365 CRM solutions that support critical customer service and complaint management processes in a highly regulated environment.

This is a hands on senior role. You will write code daily, lead the technical design of CRM solutions, and guide junior developers.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead the design, configuration, customization and implementation of Dynamics 365 CRM solutions aligned with business requirements
  • Develop, test and deploy custom solutions using C#/.NET plugins, custom workflow activities, JavaScript and the Dynamics 365 SDK, covering both server side and client side development
  • Build forms, views, dashboards, business rules, workflows and business process flows to automate processes and improve user experience
  • Integrate Dynamics 365 with enterprise systems using REST/SOAP APIs, web services, Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions and Azure Service Bus
  • Lead solution architecture for CRM projects in line with enterprise architecture standards
  • Configure security roles, business unit hierarchies and data management, and tune system performance
  • Support system upgrades, patching and migration activities
  • Conduct code reviews, manage version control and enforce development standards
  • Prepare technical documentation, including solution designs, system documentation and release notes
  • Mentor junior team members and work closely with business analysts, functional teams, project managers and Microsoft partners

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering or a related field
  • Minimum 5 to 7 years of hands on experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM / Customer Engagement (online and/or on premises)
  • Proven experience delivering at least one full lifecycle Dynamics 365 CRM implementation
  • Strong proficiency in C#/.NET, JavaScript and Visual Studio, including plugin development and the Dynamics 365 SDK
  • Hands on experience with Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions, Azure Service Bus and Azure DevOps
  • Strong knowledge of REST/SOAP APIs, web services and enterprise integration patterns
  • Proficiency in SQL Server, T-SQL and database design
  • Experience with Git/Azure DevOps, CI/CD pipelines and release management
  • Knowledge of Agile/Scrum and ITIL
  • Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Functional Consultant Associate, Dynamics 365 Application Developer Associate or Power Platform Developer Associate (PL-400), or equivalent
  • Experience in banking, financial services or a government or regulatory environment is strongly preferred
  • Experience with Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI) is an advantage
  • Excellent written and spoken English

Please note: this role is for Dynamics 365 CRM / Customer Engagement only. Profiles focused on Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, AX or Business Central will not be considered.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on a high profile CRM platform for a leading government financial institution
  • Competitive monthly compensation
  • UAE employment visa and medical insurance
  • Long term engagement with growth opportunities through GSS Tech Group

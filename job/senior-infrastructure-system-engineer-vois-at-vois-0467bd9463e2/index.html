About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Infrastructure System Engineer to manage and optimise Active Directory and Entra ID services within a large-scale enterprise environment. This role focuses on ensuring service reliability, resolving complex technical issues through root cause analysis, supporting project delivery, and driving continuous service improvements while collaborating with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.

What you’ll do

  • Manage and oversee Office IT infrastructure services, particularly Active Directory and Entra ID environments.
  • Coordinate, assess, and implement change requests to ensure service stability and continuous improvement.
  • Investigate incidents, perform root cause analysis (RCA), and provide long-term solutions.
  • Act as a technical escalation point for service desks and specialist teams.
  • Monitor service performance, availability, and quality against agreed standards.
  • Contribute to project planning and execution, providing operational and technical assessments.
  • Develop scripts and automation solutions to enhance efficiency and reduce manual work.
  • Maintain and update technical documentation, operational guides, and user instructions.
  • Support capacity planning and reporting to ensure future scalability.
  • Drive Continuous Service Improvement (CSI) initiatives and support service review meetings.

Who you are

  • Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Proven experience working with Entra ID, including SSO integrations, conditional access, MFA, and modern authentication protocols.
  • Strong knowledge of Azure administration and Active Directory environments.
  • Experience in Windows Server environments and system integration.
  • Hands-on experience with PowerShell scripting and data reporting tools such as Power BI.
  • Familiarity with user lifecycle management and provisioning processes.
  • Understanding of ITIL practices and Agile methodologies.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities with a structured approach to troubleshooting.
  • Effective communication and collaboration skills with the ability to engage stakeholders.
  • Ability to work in complex, high-availability environments with a focus on service quality.

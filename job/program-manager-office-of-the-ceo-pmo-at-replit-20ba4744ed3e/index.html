Program Manager, Office of the CEO (PMO)

Replit · Remote

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications using natural language. With millions of users worldwide, Replit is democratizing software development by removing traditional barriers to application creation.


About the Role

We are looking for a hands-on Program Manager to operationalize the CEO's external presence and growth work: founder-led marketing, founder-led sales, investor relations, and strategic go-to-market (GTM) efforts around the world. Reporting to the Chief of Staff and working on strategic special projects, you will turn the CEO's priorities into repeatable systems, clear workplans, and completed actions.
This is an execution-heavy role. You should be equally comfortable building a founder-led sales playbook, preparing an investor update, and following up on an overdue deliverable. Success requires strong judgment, attention to detail, discretion, and a willingness to take on both high-impact initiatives and the routine work that keeps them moving.


Key Responsibilities


Founder-led marketing

  • Build and run the operating system for the CEO's marketing presence: content calendar, speaking and event schedule, media and podcast opportunities, and social channels.
  • Turn the CEO's ideas, conversations, and public moments into repeatable content, working with Brand, Comms, and Content teams.
  • Set up intake, approval, and review workflows so the CEO's time goes to the highest-leverage appearances and posts.
  • Track reach, engagement, and pipeline influence, and report what is working.


Founder-led sales

  • Operationalize the CEO's role in the sales motion: target account lists, executive outreach, customer and partner meetings, and follow-up.
  • Partner with Sales, Partnerships, and RevOps to prepare briefs, talking points, and next steps for every CEO-involved deal.
  • Maintain a tracker of CEO-sourced and CEO-assisted opportunities with owners, stages, and commitments.
  • Document the playbook so founder involvement scales without adding burden to the CEO.


Investor relations support

  • Support the preparation of investor updates, board materials, and fundraising and diligence materials.
  • Maintain the investor and stakeholder tracker: relationships, meetings, asks, and follow-ups.
  • Coordinate data requests across Finance, Legal, and functional leaders, and check inputs for accuracy and consistency before review.
  • Prepare briefing documents for investor meetings and other external engagements.


Strategic GTM, globally

  • Support GTM strategy and execution across regions, including market entry, partnerships, government and ecosystem engagement, and regional events.
  • Coordinate with regional teams and partners across time zones, local requirements, and travel logistics.
  • Translate initiatives into workplans with defined scope, milestones, owners, dependencies, and success measures.
  • Surface cross-regional insights and delivery risks early, with clear recommendations.


Strategic special projects and operating cadence

  • Own time-sensitive special projects assigned by the Chief of Staff, from scoping through close-out.
  • Run project check-ins, maintain issue and decision logs, and escalate missed commitments or unresolved dependencies.
  • Build agendas, collect pre-reads, and document decisions and action items for recurring leadership and planning meetings.
  • Maintain trackers, dashboards, and scorecards for priorities, programs, and milestones, reporting to the Chief of Staff.
  • Document standard operating procedures, and replace repetitive manual work with templates, tools, and AI-enabled automation.


Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in program management, business operations, chief of staff or executive operations, GTM operations, or a comparable execution-focused role.
  • Demonstrated experience managing cross-functional projects and recurring operational processes.
  • Strong organizational skills, with the ability to maintain detailed trackers and manage competing deadlines.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to turn complex inputs into concise executive materials.
  • Proficiency with AI. Ability to work independently, adapt quickly, and make practical decisions with incomplete information.
  • Discretion with confidential investor, customer, and company information.
  • Willingness to perform hands-on administrative and logistical work alongside program management responsibilities.


Preferred experience

  • Experience supporting a founder, CEO, or Chief of Staff.
  • Experience in marketing, sales, or investor relations at a fast-paced or high-growth company.
  • Experience working across regions or with international teams and partners.
  • Experience preparing board and investor materials.
  • Experience improving workflows through automation and AI-enabled tools.


What success looks like

  • The CEO's marketing and sales activity runs on clear systems, with consistent output and few last-minute scrambles.
  • Every founder-involved deal has a brief, an owner, and tracked follow-up.
  • Investor materials are accurate, on time, and consistent across audiences.
  • Global GTM initiatives have clear ownership and steady progress against agreed milestones.
  • The Chief of Staff has a clear, current view of priorities, risks, and decisions needed.
  • Routine requests and operational issues are resolved with minimal CEO involvement.

This is a full-time role that can be held from our Foster City, CA office. The role has an in-office requirement of Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

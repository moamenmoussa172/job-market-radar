SAP P2S rollout consultant

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

  • Drive fit-to-standard analysis, harmonization of logistics processes, and strategic alignment with global SAP templates and organization policies.
  • Ensure the successful transition from legacy systems to S/4HANA, including data migration, process reengineering, and change management activities.
  • Work with customer business teams & project teams effectively during pilot & rollout projects - discovery phase until go-live & stabilization
  • Guide the entities which are being rolled out to handle the local tasks ex. Data cleansing, process harmonization, authorization, training etc.
  • Support central template design and localization requirements for logistics operations, including inbound, outbound, and transportation processes.
  • Ensure alignment with SAP Activate methodology, including milestones such as Explore, Realize, Deploy, and Run.
  • Establish & maintain effective relationships with cross functional team & ability to work in a collaborative team environment
  • Set up change management with local organization for ERP transformation
  • Provide hypercare support, issue resolution, and continuous improvement recommendations post go-live.
  • Drive formal signoffs on deliverables, ensuring alignment with compliance, security, and audit frameworks.
  • Contribute to knowledge transfer, training documentation, and global template evolution. Reads, understands and applies the procedures from area of responsibility and other interdepartmental procedures, applicable within the company;
  • Fulfils any other tasks related to area of responsibility, as requested by direct superior.
  • Language: English
  • Strong experience of consulting & rollout experience as well as user training & support with some years of domain experience
  • Proven experience with of End-to-end Implementation experience including migration & cutover activity
  • Knowledge in integrated process chains and S/4HANA functional implementation.
  • Strong expertise in SAP PP (Production Planning) including MRP, Work Center, Routing, Production Orders, and Capacity Planning
  • Experience with SAP P2S end-to-end processes in discrete and/or process manufacturing environments
  • Hands-on knowledge in SAP S/4HANA (PP module) and understanding of integration with MM, SD, QM, and WM/EWM
  • Proficiency in data migration, master data setup (BOM, routing, production versions), and cutover activities.
  • Experience in SAP PP template rollouts, fit-gap analysis, localization, and customizing
  • Ability to write/validate functional specifications for WRICEF objects (Workflow, Reports, Interfaces, Conversions, Enhancements, Forms)
  • Familiarity with Solution Manager/Activate methodology, Signavio, Jira and tools for documentation, testing, and change management
  • Familiar with global rollout governance, change control, and regulatory alignment across business units.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex logistics requirements into technical specifications.
  • Deep understanding of SAP S/4 HANA architecture, integration points, and best practices in rollout and template localization.
  • Effective time management and strong interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment and independently with minimal supervision.
  • Agile certification or experience working in agile projects is preferred.
  • Readiness to travel internationally (EU).

About the job

Your contribution to something big:

  • Shape AI architecture: Define solution architectures, agentic interaction patterns, and engineering standards for Generative AI solutions, including tool calling, APIs, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and robust fallback mechanisms.
  • Enable scalable AI integration: Establish reusable patterns for integrating AI capabilities with enterprise platforms, applications, data, and user interfaces.
  • Drive standardization and governance: Develop pragmatic standards and production-readiness criteria that help AI product teams move efficiently from prototypes to reliable, scalable solutions.
  • Connect technology and security: Collaborate with IT and security functions to align AI architectures with approved enterprise platforms, identity and access management (IAM), cybersecurity, and compliance requirements.
  • Be a technical sparring partner: Support developers, architects, and use case owners as a technical authority and trusted partner throughout the AI solution lifecycle.
  • Evaluate what comes next: Assess emerging AI models, agent frameworks, and external technologies with regard to scalability, security, performance, and cost efficiency.
  • Prototype new approaches: Build and validate prototypes to explore new architectural patterns and accelerate the adoption of promising AI technologies.

What distinguishes you:

Education: Degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a comparable field.

Experience:

  • Several years of experience in software, solution, or AI architecture.
  • Proven experience designing and bringing production-grade AI or Generative AI solutions into operation.
  • Experience working across multiple teams, products, or international environments is an advantage.

Know-how:

  • Strong hands-on experience with AI and Generative AI platforms, architectures, or applications.
  • Solid understanding of enterprise software architecture, APIs, data architectures, and cybersecurity.
  • Practical knowledge of agentic AI, tool calling, AI integration patterns, or Model Context Protocol (MCP).
  • Ability to translate privacy, security, and compliance requirements into pragmatic engineering standards.
  • Experience evaluating emerging AI technologies and balancing technical potential with scalability and cost efficiency.

Working Style and Methods:

  • You enjoy turning complex requirements into simple, scalable, and reusable solutions.
  • You work collaboratively across engineering, IT, security, and business functions.
  • You are comfortable navigating a fast-moving AI landscape and making pragmatic decisions despite evolving technologies and requirements.
  • You communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Personality:

  • You are curious about emerging AI technologies and motivated to turn innovation into tangible business value.
  • You take ownership, challenge the status quo constructively, and enjoy working with diverse teams.
  • You are a trusted technical partner who combines analytical thinking with a pragmatic, solution-oriented mindset.

Languages: Fluent in English.

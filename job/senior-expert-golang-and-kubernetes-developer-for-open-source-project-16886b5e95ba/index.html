Gardener powers SAP’s global landscape and is trusted as the foundation for countless enterprise workloads at SAP and outside. We adopt the Kubernetes open-source system for automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized SAP solutions and business applications. We provide solutions to run and orchestrate Kubernetes clusters on public cloud, hybrid or SAP-owned infrastructures for a variety of enterprise use cases inside and outside of SAP.

Our Security and Agent Sandbox Development Team is contributing to the Gardener and other community projects (e.g. OpenShell) with a variety of topics around Kubernetes, Linux and Container Security Hardening, including networking, Linux Нamespaces & Cgroups, SecComp, eBPF, Landlock, etc.

We’re building ApeiroRA, an open-source reference architecture that strengthens Europe’s digital sovereignty. This initiative is part of a broader movement to ensure security, sustainability, and compliance for cloud-to-edge computing. By joining us, you’ll contribute to a project that’s not just technical, it’s strategic for Europe’s future.

What you'll do

  • Contribute (design and implement enhancements) in Gardener and AI agent sandbox solutions like OpenShell.
  • Implement Kubernetes controllers in Go.
  • Implement Gardener Extensions, write Gardener Enhancement and Security Enhancement Proposals (for seniors and experts).
  • Assess and address security vulnerabilities, attend Threat Modeling Workshops, maintain Gardener Security posture and compliance.
  • Continuously learn and apply new technologies and best practices to keep us ahead of the curve.

What we're looking for

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical capabilities, with a collaborative, proactive mindset and a commitment to continuous improvement.
  • Ability to clearly communicate and present complex technical concepts.
  • Good understanding of Kubernetes, cloud, and container technologies.
  • Understanding of Linux SecComp, Landlock, Namespaces and Cgroups, HTTP CONNECT Proxy and Agent workflows is a plus.
  • Hands-on experience in at least one programming language, Go preferred, Rust is a plus.
  • A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent work experience.
  • Profound background in agile software engineering.
  • Ability to positively inspire as well as build trust, seed confidence and align people on common goals.
  • Good communication skills in English, both written and verbal.

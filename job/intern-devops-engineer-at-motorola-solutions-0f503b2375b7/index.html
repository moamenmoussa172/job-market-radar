Job Description

We are looking for an Intern DevOps Engineer to join the ASTRO DevOps team, supporting mission-critical ASTRO Public Safety Systems. In this role, your core focus will be infrastructure deployment and automation using Ansible, bridging software delivery with reliable system provisioning across hybrid environments.

Working alongside experienced engineers, you will help design, build, and maintain automated infrastructure deployments of on-premises environments. You will use Ansible to turn manual setup into reliable, repeatable code, while integrating these deployments into our broader Jenkins and release pipelines.

This is an ideal growth position for a student or junior technologist looking to build practical, real-world DevOps and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) skills at enterprise scale.


Basic Requirements

Technologies/frameworks

  • Main focus: Ansible automation and configuration support
  • Automation and scripting: Python, Groovy, Bash
  • CI/CD and SCM: Jenkins, Git, Bitbucket, Artifactory
  • Platforms: Linux (Red Hat), Windows, KVM

Basic Requirements

  • Basic knowledge of scripting or programming, preferably Python, Groovy or Bash
  • Basic knowledge of Git and interest in Jenkins-based CI/CD tooling
  • Experience with Linux and confidence using the command line
  • Basic experience with Ansible, shared platform support, and operational troubleshooting
  • Ability to work in a Scrum team and communicate clearly in English
  • Availability 32 hours per week (minimum)
  • Availability to work 2 days per week in our office in Kraków (Czerwone Maki 82)
  • At least one year of cooperation

In addition, we would welcome:

  • One year of experience in DevOps, CI/CD support, system administration, or software engineering
  • Exposure to Linux, KVM, or AWS environments
  • Interest in build/release engineering and internal developer tools

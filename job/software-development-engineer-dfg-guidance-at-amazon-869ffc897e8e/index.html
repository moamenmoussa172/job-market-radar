About the job

Are you excited about building ML-powered systems that help advertisers succeed? Do you want to work at the intersection of machine learning, real-time systems, and user-facing product development?
The Forecasting Insights and Guidance (FIG) team within Amazon Ads is looking for a Software Development Engineer to help build and scale our Advertising Guidance platform, Amazon DSP's ML-powered recommendations engine that helps advertisers optimize campaign pacing and performance through personalized recommendations and actionable insights.
The platform combines historical and real-time campaign data with machine learning models to surface relevant optimization opportunities across the campaign lifecycle, from pre-flight setup through to in-flight management. Our recommendations are personalized to each individual campaign, guiding advertisers to make configuration changes that advance their delivery and performance goals. Customers encounter guidance in many forms including alerts, one-click-to-action recommendations, smart defaults for settings, and opportunity cards.
As an SDE on the team, you will design and build the services and data pipelines that power recommendation generation, delivery, and measurement. You will work across the full stack, from real-time event-driven systems that react to campaign changes, to offline batch pipelines that monitor all running campaigns, to the APIs that serve recommendations to millions of advertisers worldwide. Your systems will meet remarkably high standards of performance and reliability and will operate at massive scale, 24x7.
You will collaborate closely with scientists, product managers, and UX designers to bring new recommendation content and workflow from concept to production. You will have strong ownership of the systems you build and the autonomy to make impactful technical decisions.
Key job responsibilities
Design, build, and operate distributed services that generate, store, and serve campaign recommendations at scale
Build real-time event-driven systems that detect optimization opportunities as campaigns change
Develop and maintain ML inference pipelines, working with scientists to bring models from training to production
Build APIs and data models that power the recommendation experience across ADSP, partner networks, and programmatic interfaces
Instrument systems for comprehensive measurement of recommendation engagement, adoption, and business impact
Participate in operational excellence — on-call, monitoring, alarming, and incident response
A day in the life
You will own features end-to-end, from design through deployment and operation
You will dive deep into campaign performance data to understand customer problems and validate solutions
You will participate in code reviews, design reviews, and operational reviews with your team
You will work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment alongside Product, Science, UX, and Account teams
About the team
The Demand Forecasting and Guidance (DFG) team builds towards industry-leading solutions for campaign health management, budget replanning, and inventory insights. Our products improve campaign outcomes (budget utilisation and KPI performance) by using campaign goals and settings to guide traders to decisions that advance their outcomes. Forecasting aids customers in budget allocation and refinement, and Guidance directs customers to make configuration changes that will further their delivery and performance goals. Forecasting provides impression, spend, reach and performance forecasting for Demand Tech products, including ADSP, SD, and ST. We also supply reach and performance curves for Ads Planner, Reach Authority Group (RAG), and forecasting analytics for partner teams.

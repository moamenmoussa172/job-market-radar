About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

At Lucid Motors, we’re building the future of mobility, and our Sales Strategy, Planning & Analytics team plays a critical role in helping our European Sales organization make informed decisions, plan effectively, and continuously improve performance.

We’re looking for an enthusiastic Sales Strategy, Planning & Analytics Intern to join our European team. This is a great opportunity for a student who enjoys working with data, solving problems, and understanding what drives commercial performance. You will contribute to analytical and improvement projects aimed at strengthening sales performance, while also supporting recurring planning, reporting, and operational activities.

What you’ll do

As a Sales Strategy, Planning & Analytics Intern, you will support the team across analysis, business planning, reporting, and improvement initiatives, including:

  • Analyze the sales funnel, sales performance metrics and other relevant data to identify opportunities to improve commercial performance
  • Support the quarterly sales incentives and campaigns process, including planning, analysis, tracking, and performance evaluation
  • Support ad-hoc analysis and business questions across the European Sales organization
  • Support the preparation and coordination of monthly business reviews, quarterly workshops, and other key planning activities
  • Maintain & help automate regular sales reports, dashboards, and performance trackers, ensuring information is accurate and delivered on time

What we’re looking for

We’re looking for someone who is curious, analytical, and eager to learn, with a structured approach and an interest in understanding how data can help improve business performance.

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program (HBO/WO) in Business, Economics, Finance, Data Analytics or a related field
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken
  • Comfortable working with data, with a strong problem-solving mindset and the ability to translate analysis into clear insights
  • Detail-oriented, well-organized, proactive, and comfortable taking ownership of tasks
  • Strong communication skills and comfortable working with different stakeholders

Nice to have

  • Previous internship, part-time job, or coursework involving data analysis, business intelligence, sales, or other commercial topics
  • Experience with, or interest in, tools such as Excel, Tableau, Power BI, Power Query, SQL or other analytics and automation tools
  • Interest in the automotive industry, sales performance, and/or commercial strategy

What you’ll gain

This internship is designed to provide hands-on experience across both the analytical and operational sides of a European Sales organization.

  • Practical experience working with real-world sales and performance data
  • Exposure to sales strategy, planning, forecasting, incentives, campaigns, and performance management across European markets
  • Experience turning data into actionable insights while developing skills in analytics, automation, and process improvement
  • Exposure to business reviews, quarterly planning, and cross-functional decision-making across a European Sales organization

About the job

Change the way the world travels

Join the GetYourGuide journey to connect people with unforgettable travel experiences around the world. Millions look to us for unique activities they can trust, and it’s all powered by our commitment to make every single journey extraordinary - including yours.

Ready to unlock your potential with a community of fellow explorers? Find your next role at our Berlin HQ or one of our local offices around the globe, from New York to Bangkok. Head to getyourguide.careers to take the first step.

Team mission

Own an agentic platform allowing teams to create agentic workflows by abstracting the underlying infrastructure.

Your mission

  • Build and maintain an opinionated platform for agentic workflows, allowing mission teams to focus on delivering business value
  • Be responsible for all hiring and performance management topics on the Engineering side, helping your engineers reach their full potential
  • Partner closely with your peers and stakeholders to shape the roadmap and portfolio allocation, and drive high-impact delivery

Your toolkit

  • You have a strong sense of ownership, ensuring that technical and project plans are aligned with your stakeholders & team members for delivery
  • You approach the domain of agentic workflows with a product mindset, considering Engineers, Product Managers, and Designers as your customers.
  • Enjoy complex topics with an open mind and eye for simplicity.
  • Have a passion for people and support and coach your engineers without going into micromanagement mode.
  • Proven technical background in building secure, high-impact internal tools and LLM workflows

Extras that give you an edge

  • Hands-on experience with modern AI agent frameworks (e.g., Codex or Claude) and evaluation platforms (e.g., Braintrust).
  • Background in establishing agent safety, sandboxing execution environments, or managing identity and access controls for autonomous AI workflows.
  • Familiarity with developer experience benchmarking, observability tools, and LLM cost and routing optimization.

How we’ll make your career journey extraordinary

  • Annual personal growth budget and mentorship programs for continuous learning and development
  • Work from anywhere in the world for 30 days per year
  • A hybrid working approach with three days of in office collaboration (Mon, Tue, Thur) and two days of optional at home focus time.
  • Opportunities to collaborate and socialize with a quarterly team event budget
  • Access to leading AI tools plus the resources and budget to get the most out of them
  • Monthly transportation and fitness budget
  • Discounts for you, your friends, and family on GetYourGuide activities
  • Language reimbursement program
  • Health and wellness benefits

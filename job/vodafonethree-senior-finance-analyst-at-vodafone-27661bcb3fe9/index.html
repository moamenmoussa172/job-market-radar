What to do

Play a key role in helping the business understand financial performance and make informed decisions. Combine your analytical, business partnering and presentation skills to turn complex financial information into clear insights, while leading key elements of budgeting, forecasting and long-range planning. Working closely with senior finance and business stakeholders, you’ll have the opportunity to influence decisions, identify opportunities and continuously improve how the team works.

You will:

  • Deliver accurate and timely financial reporting across month-end, forecasts and other key reporting cycles.
  • Analyse monthly results against budgets and forecasts, identifying the key drivers behind performance, risks and opportunities.
  • Own elements of the long-range planning process, building and managing financial models and helping shape future plans with senior finance leaders.
  • Coordinate financial submissions across teams and systems, ensuring stakeholders remain aligned and key deadlines are achieved.
  • Create clear, engaging presentations for forecasts and planning cycles, translating financial information into meaningful business insights.
  • Partner with senior stakeholders across Finance and the wider business, offering constructive challenge, recommendations and solutions to support better decisions.
  • Track key initiatives and performance trends, using your insights to influence positive change and improve business performance.
  • Champion continuous improvement, using tools, data and new ways of working to make reporting, forecasting and analysis more efficient.

Who you are

  • Fully qualified accountant through ACA, ACCA or CIMA, with around 1 to 2 years of post-qualification experience.
  • Bring strong financial accounting, analysis and reporting skills, with a keen eye for accuracy and detail.
  • Demonstrable experience using Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables, lookups, filters and charts, to analyse data and communicate insights.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and delivering high-quality work within demanding reporting and forecasting timelines.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders and communicate financial information clearly to both finance and non-finance audiences.
  • Bring the confidence to challenge thinking, influence decisions and offer practical, evidence-based recommendations.
  • Take ownership of your work, identifying what needs to be done independently while seeking guidance and collaborating when it adds value.
  • Bring a continuous improvement mindset, looking for smarter and more efficient ways to deliver meaningful outcomes.

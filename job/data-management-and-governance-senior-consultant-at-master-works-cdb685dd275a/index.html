About the job

Master Works is a Saudi technology company driving digital transformation through emerging technologies and innovative digital solutions. We empower organizations with expertise across data management, AI, software development, and digital strategy, contributing to the digital economy in line with Saudi Vision 2030.

Master Works is looking for a Data Management and Governance, Senior Consultant to lead data management and AI-enablement initiatives, ensuring compliance with NDMO, NDI, and PDPL requirements. The role focuses on data strategy, governance, quality, and AI readiness, while leading client engagements, supporting business development, and ensuring high-quality project delivery.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead data management initiatives, including data strategy, governance, data quality, master data, and metadata management.
  • Conduct data maturity assessments and gap analyses against NDMO, NDI, and PDPL requirements.
  • Develop data governance frameworks, policies, standards, and implementation roadmaps.
  • Enable AI readiness and translate business requirements into data and AI use cases using tools such as Informatica.
  • Prepare technical and financial proposals, support pre-sales activities, and contribute to RFP responses.
  • Develop consulting methodologies, review deliverables, and ensure quality standards.
  • Lead and mentor consulting teams, providing technical guidance and knowledge transfer.
  • Manage client relationships, project scope, risks, timelines, and budgets.
  • Prepare progress reports and present recommendations to senior stakeholders.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Management, or a related field.
  • CDMP certification is required; PMP, relevant master's degree, and Informatica certifications are preferred.
  • 15–20 years of professional experience, including at least 6 years in data management and governance.
  • Proven experience in consulting, data maturity assessments, governance frameworks, and regulatory compliance with NDMO, NDI, and PDPL.
  • Hands-on experience with Informatica and data/AI use cases.
  • Experience in proposal development, pre-sales, project management, and team leadership.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, presentation, and analytical skills.
  • Fluency in Arabic and English.

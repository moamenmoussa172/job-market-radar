About the job

Bosch Cross-Domain Computing & ADAS (XC-CP) Team develops high-quality, state-of-the-art Automotive Head Unit and Home Appliance Linux/Android-based systems.

Together with other Bosch locations worldwide, we provide software solutions for navigation, vehicle functions, autonomous driving, camera, and IoT features to the car marker. Our team also contributes to Bosch smart home & IoT solutions through SW delivery to Home Appliance products.

We are looking for talented NEO Vehicle Autosar Embedded SW Engineers with 2 to 5 years of experience to join our development team.

Key Responsible:

  • Collaborate with third-party vendors (e.g., Vector, EB, MCAL) to incorporate any third-party software
  • Create and maintain technical specification of components. If necessary, work in close collaboration with architects, function developers, product owner and other stakeholders to clarify and understand requirements
  • You design and develop system- and hardware-related software components in C in compliance with current guidelines and standards, carry out analyses and reviews as well as the implementation of fully automation.
  • Strong embedded programming experience in C
  • Experience with embedded systems and microcontrollers.
  • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.
  • Skilled in AUTOSAR / Non-AUTOSAR architecture and configuration (NVM, COM & DIAG)
  • Experience in Driver development (CAN, LIN, Flexray, SPI, ADC, Timer, Watchdog, Interrupt, DMA, Memory)
  • Good knowledge in MISRA Rules and Compliances
  • Knowledge on CAN, ISO14229, J1939 & UDS standards
  • Basic Python knowledge
  • Language Skills: Very good English skills.
  • Education: A completed degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a comparable field.

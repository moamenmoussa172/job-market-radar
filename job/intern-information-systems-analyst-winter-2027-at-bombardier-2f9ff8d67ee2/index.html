What are your contributions to the team?

  • Analyze operational and system data to identify inconsistencies, duplicates, gaps and opportunities for improvement using powerBI.
  • Support data cleanup and remediation activities across business and IT systems.
  • Develop and maintain Power-Automated workflows to reduce manual effort, improve accuracy and streamline recurring activities.
  • Use and combine tools such as ServiceNow, Power Automate, Power BI, Excel and scripting technologies to automate processes and transform data.
  • Create dashboards, reports and performance indicators that provide visibility into data quality, progress and operational results.
  • Collect, structure, validate and reconcile data from multiple sources to support reliable analysis and decision-making.
  • Work with process owners and technical teams to understand business needs and translate them into practical automation and reporting solutions.
  • Document automated workflows, data-cleanup rules, procedures and troubleshooting guidance.
  • Test and validate solutions to ensure data integrity, expected results and alignment with business requirements.
  • Identify and propose new tools, automation opportunities and process improvements that simplify daily work.
  • Contribute, when required, to application monitoring, operational resilience and continuous-improvement activities.

During your internship, you will learn:

  • How to analyze, cleanse and validate data from different business and technology systems.
  • How to identify root causes of data-quality issues and support structured remediation activities.
  • How to design and implement automated workflows using Microsoft Power Platform tools and other appropriate technologies.
  • How to build dashboards and reports that communicate trends, exceptions, progress and key performance indicators.
  • How to translate operational needs into clear data, automation and process-improvement requirements.
  • Best practices for solution testing, documentation, knowledge management and continuous improvement.
  • The broader role of information systems in supporting data integrity, efficient operations and informed decision-making.
  • Foundational concepts related to application reliability and monitoring as a complementary area of exposure.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You are enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree in Information Systems, Business Analytics, Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field.
  • You have strong analytical skills and are comfortable working with structured data, identifying patterns and investigating inconsistencies.
  • You are familiar with Microsoft Office, particularly Excel; knowledge of Power BI, Power Automate or Power Apps would be considered a strong asset.
  • You have knowledge of programming, scripting, SQL or data-transformation concepts; this would be considered an asset.
  • You have a good command of French and English, both spoken and written.
  • You demonstrate curiosity, attention to detail and a structured approach to problem-solving.
  • You communicate effectively, collaborate well with multidisciplinary teams and are comfortable documenting your work.
  • You are proactive, organized and motivated to learn new tools and technologies.

Boarding Information:

  • Location: Administrative Centre (CA)
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.

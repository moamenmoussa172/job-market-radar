About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

1Password is growing. We’ve surpassed $500M in ARR and we’re continuing to accelerate, earning a spot on the Forbes Cloud 100 for four years in a row and teaming up with iconic partners like Oracle Red Bull Racing.

About 1Password

At 1Password, we’re building the foundation for a safe, productive digital future. Our mission is to unleash employee productivity without compromising security by ensuring every identity is authentic, every application sign-in is secure, and every device is trusted. We innovated the market-leading enterprise password manager and pioneered Unified Access Management, a new cybersecurity category built for the way people and AI agents work today. As one of the most loved brands in cybersecurity, we take a human-centric approach in everything from product strategy to user experience. Over 180,000 businesses, from Fortune 100 leaders to the world’s most innovative AI companies, trust 1Password to help their teams securely adopt the SaaS and AI tools they need to do their best work.

If you're excited about the opportunity to contribute to the digital safety of millions, to work alongside a team of curious, driven individuals, and to solve hard problems in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, then we want to hear from you. Come join us and help shape a safer, simpler digital future.

As a Finance Systems Analyst focused on integrations and automation, you’ll support the Finance Systems team in building, maintaining, and testing Application Programming Interface (API) integrations across core financial applications. You’ll work closely with senior Finance Systems team members to keep financial platforms communicating reliably, ensure automations run as expected, and document integration testing thoroughly in this hands-on, technically oriented role at the intersection of finance operations and systems engineering. By improving the reliability of financial data flows, you’ll contribute to 1Password’s mission of making it safer to use technology every day.

How we’re using AI today
On the Finance team, we’re using AI to drive productivity, scale insight, and deliver better decision-making across 1Password. AI helps us move faster and sharpen our thinking, from summarizing context and pressure-testing assumptions to turning complex inputs into clear executive-level narratives. We bring AI into our work thoughtfully, improving repeatable workflows while staying accountable for the accuracy, confidentiality, and controls behind every output, creating space to focus on judgement, strategic partnership, and meaningful business outcomes.

This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US.

What we're looking for:

• Build a foundation in technical work involving Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), integrations, automation, or related systems.

• Work with Representational State Transfer (REST) APIs and JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) data.

• Write or adapt scripts in Python, JavaScript, or another programming language, and apply sound technical logic across different tools and platforms.

• Create and execute test cases for integrations and automated workflows, including edge cases, error handling, and retry logic.

• Learn unfamiliar systems and technologies independently, using research and available tools to solve problems effectively.

• Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical partners to understand requirements and explain solutions.

• Document integrations, test results, and workflows clearly; experience with financial systems, NetSuite, Workato, Postman, or similar tools is helpful.

What you can expect:

• Support the design, development, maintenance, and release of integrations across financial applications such as NetSuite, Salesforce, Zip, Ramp, UKG, Workato, and Stripe.

• Write and maintain scripts that automate data flows, transformations, and system-to-system synchronization.

• Build and refine Workato recipes and integration workflows using APIs, JSON payloads, and webhooks.

• Assist with SuiteScript customizations within NetSuite and develop small utilities that reduce manual work.

• Design and run integration tests, validate data accuracy from end to end, and document results and follow-up actions.

• Troubleshoot integration failures and data discrepancies across connected systems.

• Collaborate with finance, accounting, procurement, data, legal, and other partner teams to understand needs, clarify requirements, and deliver reliable solutions.

USA-based roles only: The annual base salary for this role is between $83,000 USD and $116,000 USD, plus immediate participation in 1Password's benefits program (health, dental, 401k and many others), utilization of our generous paid time off, an equity grant and, where applicable, participation in our incentive programs.

Canada-based roles only: The annual base salary for this role is between $78,000 CAD and $109,000 CAD, plus immediate participation in 1Password’s generous benefits program (health, dental, RRSP and many others), utilization of our generous paid time off, an equity grant and, where applicable, participation in our incentive programs.

At 1Password, we approach each individual's compensation with a promise of fair market value and internal equity commensurate with experience and specific skill set.

Our culture
At 1Password, we prioritize collaboration, clear and transparent communication, receptiveness to feedback, and alignment with our core values: keep it simple, lead with honesty, and put people first.
You’ll be part of a team that challenges the status quo, and is excited to experiment and iterate in search of the best solution. That said, 1Password is not for everyone. Our work is demanding, we strive for excellence, and the pace is fast. We need people who are keen to take on challenging problems, who seek feedback to grow, and who are driven to make an impact. If you're looking for a place where you can settle into a comfortable routine, this might not be the right fit for you. We’re looking for individuals who are proven experts in their fields, as well as those who are highly adaptable, can thrive in ambiguity and through change, are curious, and above all deliver results.
How we work with AI
We are committed to leveraging cutting-edge technology—including AI—to achieve our mission. We also understand that thinking critically about AI in its current forms will help us create better solutions for our customers and ourselves with its future forms, which will help us continue to close the gap between security and privacy and achieve our mission. We want team members at all levels to take the approach of actively learning AI best practices, identifying opportunities to apply AI in meaningful ways, and driving innovative solutions in their daily work. Embracing the future of AI isn't just encouraged—it's an essential part of how we will be successful at 1Password.

IT System Administrator Production (f/m/x)

AUTO1 Group · Germany

Onsite · IT Support / Systems Admin · Posted 22 October 2026

Apply on AUTO1 Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Provide business-critical in-house IT support to end-users in our car production centers
  • Perform onboarding of new starters and offboarding of leavers
  • Handle hardware and software configurations across multiple platforms
  • Duly document all incidents and requests and manage a key ticketing system
  • Work with your colleagues on the development of the Corporate IT infrastructure
  • Act as the on-site IT contact at our production centers (Ketzin/Hemau), helping local teams stay productive and ensuring reliable day-to-day IT support
  • Train and guide colleagues on IT systems, tools, and best practices
  • Set up, maintain, and troubleshoot IT equipment and infrastructure at our production centers
  • Identify and implement improvements to IT processes, working with local teams and Corporate IT to support the wider organization
  • Juniors and mid-level are welcome to apply
  • Experience with Apple devices and Microsoft Windows Operating Systems.
  • Familiarity with cloud tools like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or other SaaS platforms.
  • Being service oriented and a team player
  • You are self-reliant and operate with confidence and thrive when working independently. You maintain the overview and high standards even in fast-paced environments.
  • Proficiency in English and German
  • German drivers license

Apply by uploading your CV with a note of salary expectations.

AUTO1 Group is Europe's leading digital automotive platform. As a dynamic tech company, we are revolutionizing the automotive industry with our brands wirkaufendeinauto.de, AUTO1.com and Autohero. Our strong team of 5000 people is dedicated to making Europe-wide car trading and transport as fast and stress-free as possible for our customers. Grow personally and shape the future of car trading with us.

Product Manager, Financial Connections

Stripe · Canada, United States

Onsite · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 17 November 2026

Apply on Stripe's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

Financial Connections helps people securely share their financial data with the businesses they choose. Developers use it to build products that help people understand and manage their money. We’re expanding what’s possible with that data as AI creates new ways for people to interact with their finances, from making sense of everyday spending to getting personalized answers to financial questions.

We bring together Stripe’s financial infrastructure and developer ecosystem with Link, our consumer network, to help businesses build the next generation of financial products.

What you’ll do

You’ll build and grow Financial Connections’ next generation of financial data products, with a focus on AI-powered experiences. This is an opportunity to shape an emerging business at Stripe: identify the customer problems worth solving, turn early ideas into products, and build demand for them.

You’ll work directly with customers, from fintech companies to businesses building with AI, and own the journey from the first product conversations through launch and sustained adoption. You’ll have room to define the direction, experiment, and build, with Stripe’s infrastructure and customer relationships behind you. We’re looking for someone who is as excited to win an early customer as they are to shape a long-term product vision.

Responsibilities

  • Set the vision and strategy for new financial data products, identifying where Stripe can solve valuable customer problems and build a differentiated business.
  • Spend time with developers and customers to understand their needs, test ideas, and decide which opportunities to pursue.
  • Partner with Engineering and Design to turn ambiguous opportunities into simple, high-quality products, moving quickly while earning users’ trust.
  • Work directly with early customers to help them launch, learn what makes the product valuable, and use that feedback to improve it.
  • Collaborate with Sales, Marketing, and Partnerships to bring products to market and turn early customer successes into broader adoption.
  • Define success and use customer feedback, usage, and retention to decide where to invest, iterate, or change direction.

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 6+ years of product management or product-focused founder experience
  • A track record of launching and growing new products for businesses, with ownership from customer discovery through adoption.
  • Strong product judgment and creativity, with the ability to turn an open-ended problem into a clear direction and make progress with incomplete information.
  • A hands-on approach to building a business. You’re comfortable pitching early customers, working through obstacles, and changing your approach as you learn.
  • Experience partnering closely with engineers on technical products, with a high bar for product quality and the ability to make complex experiences feel simple.
  • Strong analytical skills and clear written and verbal communication. You can use data and customer insights to make decisions and explain your reasoning.
  • Experience leading work across teams and external partners, with the autonomy, persistence, and attention to detail to see it through.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with financial products, data platforms, or products that use AI.
  • Experience building in an early-stage environment and scaling products within a larger organization.
  • Experience developing products that serve both businesses and their end users.

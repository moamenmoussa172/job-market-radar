What you'll builld

As a People & Culture Data Management Associate, you will be the backbone of our People operations. You will own the execution of administrative processes and system transactions, ensuring our employee data remains accurate and reliable. From onboarding through to offboarding, you will play a vital role in supporting the entire employee lifecycle as a trusted partner to our team.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Data Maintenance: You will own the end-to-end lifecycle of employee profiles in our HR system, serving as the guardian of data accuracy across all stages, from hiring to offboarding.
  • Payroll Support: You will be the engine behind our monthly payroll, ensuring all personal, organizational, and compensation data is precise, up-to-date, and ready for seamless salary processing.
  • Document Management: You will manage employee experience by preparing accurate contracts and amendments, ensuring every document is fully compliant with local legislation.
  • Customer Service: You will act as the go-to partner for employees and managers, providing fast, professional, and reliable support for questions related to their personal data, HR systems, and processes.
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: You will collaborate closely with Recruiters, Payroll and P&C Local Services to ensure data flows smoothly and our compliance standards remain high.
  • Service Excellence: You will take ownership of your tasks by meeting deadlines with ease, while proactively identifying ways to improve our processes and documentation.
    Note: The specific scope of responsibilities will be tailored upon joining the team.

What you bring

  • Professional fluency (written and spoken) in English
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy in data handling
  • Customer-focused mindset with clear and professional communication skills
  • High level of discretion when working with confidential information
  • Proven ability to meet strict deadlines
  • Quick learner with a proactive attitude
  • Confidence using MS Office; SAP HR systems knowledge is a plus

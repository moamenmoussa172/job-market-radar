About the job

P-932

At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started.

The Notebook Foundations team owns the service and platform that powers agentic data science on Databricks. The team's mission is to be the best platform for interactive and agentic code authoring and execution, supporting products like Genie Code. As agents increasingly write code and run analyses alongside people, the platform must scale to serve an order of magnitude more traffic while maintaining speed and reliability. This role leads the managers and engineers building the foundation where people and agents write and run code together.

The Impact You Will Have

  • Lead and grow the engineering team that owns the platform behind agentic data science on Databricks.
  • Set the technical strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for scaling the platform to an order of magnitude more traffic as agents do more of the work
  • Drive architecture decisions for horizontal sharding, admission control, load management, and real-time connections.
  • Proactively identify and resolve performance bottlenecks through load testing, capacity planning, and optimization work.
  • Ensure high reliability across the platform, including maintaining SLAs, managing on-call rotations, conducting postmortems, and minimizing regressions.
  • Make the platform easy for new products and agents to build on, and lead efforts that span multiple teams across the Workspace org.
  • Partner with product, design, and adjacent engineering teams to align priorities and remove blockers.
  • Hire, develop, and mentor engineers, including growing technical leads and senior engineers.

What We Look For

  • 10+ years of software engineering experience.
  • 4+ years managing engineering teams, including experience leading managers.
  • Experience leading teams that scaled a high-traffic backend service or a platform other teams build on.
  • Depth in distributed systems at scale: sharding, load management, performance and capacity planning.
  • Interest in how agents are changing software and data work; experience shipping AI or agent features is a plus.
  • Experience building and leading teams responsible for high-quality, reliable infrastructure services in complex technical domains.
  • Track record of attracting, hiring, and coaching engineers who meet high standards, including upleveling teams through senior talent acquisition and leadership development.
  • Experience managing distributed teams and guiding career growth across multiple levels.
  • Existing experience building and running cloud platforms or demonstrated ability to quickly learn new concepts in the SaaS space.
  • Comfort working cross-functionally with product management and directly with customers, with ability to deeply understand product and customer personas.
  • B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related quantitative field, or equivalent practical experience.

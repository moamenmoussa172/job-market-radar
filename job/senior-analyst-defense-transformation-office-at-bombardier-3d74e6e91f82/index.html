What are your contributions to the team?

Transformation & Change Leadership

· Lead and support key transformation initiatives across Bombardier Defense aligned with strategic objectives.

· Drive implementation of critical business processes, systems, governance frameworks, and operating models.

· Facilitate organizational change and ensure adoption of new ways of working.

Stakeholder Alignment & Collaboration

· Partner with leaders and stakeholders across multiple functions to build alignment and drive decisions.

· Facilitate workshops, working sessions, and governance forums to reconcile differing viewpoints and priorities.

· Act as a trusted partner supporting business leaders through transformation activities.

Business Process Transformation & Continuous Improvement

· Analyze, design, and optimize business processes to improve efficiency, effectiveness, and compliance.

· Drive continuous improvement initiatives and identify opportunities to eliminate inefficiencies.

· Support the definition and transformation of roles, responsibilities, and organizational interfaces.

Defense Systems, Tools & Compliance

· Develop and enhance defense-specific tools, frameworks, dashboards, and management systems.

· Support compliance with defense, customer, and regulatory requirements.

· Promote digital solutions and process standardization across the organization.

Project & Program Management

· Lead project planning, execution, monitoring, and reporting activities for strategic initiatives.

· Coordinate multiple workstreams, manage risks, dependencies, schedules, and deliverables.

· Apply program and project management best practices to ensure successful delivery.

Capture Management & Proposal Support

· Support capture planning and business development activities for defense opportunities.

· Contribute to proposal development, compliance reviews, executive reviews, and customer deliverables.

· Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop competitive and compliant proposals.

Data Analytics & Performance Management

· Develop KPIs, dashboards, and performance metrics to support decision-making.

· Perform analysis and provide actionable recommendations to leadership.

· Support governance reviews and executive reporting activities.

How to thrive in this role?

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Operations, or a related discipline.
  • 5-8+ years of experience in business transformation, continuous improvement, project management, program management, consulting, or business operations.
  • Strong business acumen and analytical problem-solving capabilities.
  • Experience leading cross-functional initiatives and stakeholder alignment activities.
  • Project Management and Program Management experience.
  • Experience supporting capture management and proposal development activities is considered an asset.
  • Knowledge of aerospace, defense, or regulated industries is considered an asset.
  • Excellent communication, facilitation, and presentation skills.
  • Passionate team player with strong collaboration and continuous improvement mindset.

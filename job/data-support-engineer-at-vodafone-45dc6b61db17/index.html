What you’ll do

You will be responsible for ensuring the reliable and efficient operation of applications across the Data Domain.

More specifically you will:

  • Operate, administer, and maintain assigned systems or services in terms of Maintenance & administration, Incident management, Problem management, Change & Release management, all with necessary strong Vendor management
  • Perform day-to-day L1&2 support activities, incident and service request management, problem analysis and resolution, configuration changes, health check & housekeeping activities.
  • Triage tickets to support team, identify bug fixes enhancements and minor code product issues and escalate to appropriate teams, fault logging tracking and resolution, batch processing, interface management, extend support during release and deployment
  • Perform fault detection, analysis, isolation, rectification, and verification activities accompanied by a close cooperation with vendor and other operational teams to supply the defined quality of assigned systems in terms of an adequate Incident repair times and system/service availability

Who you are

  • Bachelor's degree in computer science or engineering or other relative field
  • 1-3 years of experience in Business Intelligence, Data Warehouse or Data Analytics area
  • Good experience in below technologies
  • Oracle SQL/PLSQL
  • Unix/Linux Shell scripting
  • Experience with the below will be considered a plus:
  • Python
  • ETL – ELT Tools
  • Data Visualisation tools
  • You will have the opportunity to expand your skills and gain hands-on experience with new technologies, including Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Strong knowledge of IT operations systems and procedures
  • Excellent analytical, incident & problem-management skills
  • Self – motivated with strong time-management skills and the ability to juggle multiple balls
  • Ability to influence, mentor, and collaborate with agile development teams
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, in both Greek & English and with both technical and business audiences
  • Experience in Telco industry will be an asset

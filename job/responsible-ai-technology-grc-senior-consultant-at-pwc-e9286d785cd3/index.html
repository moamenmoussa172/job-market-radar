About the job

Job Description & Summary

About the team

Our Data and AI team is a dynamic group of Data and AI experts, leading the way in delivering AI trust, governance, design, strategy, and development services. We operate in a flat culture with a start-up mindset, fostering open and transparent communication. Our clients include both local and cross-border organisations, and we collaborate closely with multidisciplinary teams across PwC and the global PwC network.

About your manager

You’ll work closely with experienced leaders and specialists across our Data and AI practice. They will support you in navigating complex client engagements, developing your expertise, and taking increasing ownership of projects. You’ll also collaborate with colleagues across different PwC teams and countries.

Job description & summary

We are looking for a Responsible AI / Technology GRC (Senior) Consultant to support clients in navigating AI governance, risk, compliance, and regulatory change. You will help clients interpret new legislative, regulatory, ethical, and governance frameworks related to data and AI, including the EU AI Act, and translate them into practical business and technical solutions.

In this role, you will:

  • Deliver projects related to AI governance, technology risk, and compliance.
  • Support clients in implementing AI-related regulatory and governance requirements.
  • Translate legal, regulatory, and standard requirements into practical guidance, processes, and controls.
  • Help clients design operating models, risk frameworks, governance processes, and data strategies.
  • Support AI use-case discovery, implementation, and adoption.
  • Monitor developments in AI, emerging technology, and regulation.
  • Prepare analyses, presentations, proposals, and client materials.
  • Coordinate project workstreams and collaborate across PwC teams and client organisations.
  • Support business development and new service offering development.
  • Build strong stakeholder relationships and contribute proactively to team growth.

What matters to us

  • A university degree in a relevant field such as IT, business, risk, data science, public policy, law, information management, or a related discipline.
  • 3+ years of experience in technology risk, AI governance, compliance, regulatory advisory, audit, data governance, or related areas, together with knowledge of frameworks and standards such as the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, or NIST AI RMF.
  • The ability to turn complex requirements into practical business solutions, supported by strong analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills, professional fluency in English, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

What will help you stand out

  • Certifications such as AIGP or ISO/IEC 42001.
  • Experience in consulting, advisory, or business development.
  • Experience designing operating models, processes, controls, or data governance strategies.
  • Knowledge of sector-specific regulations.
  • Practical use of AI tools and digital technologies in your daily work.

Why you’ll enjoy working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.
  • A flexible benefits programme with 45,000 points to spend on what matters most to you.
  • 30 days of paid time off, including three well-being days and two additional days off at the end of the year.
  • An opportunity to give back to the community with one paid volunteering day every year.
  • The chance to work from one of PwC’s international offices (available from the Senior Associate level).
  • We support your learning and development journey: Benefit from training and certifications in business and digital skills aligned with your area of expertise, as well as soft-skills development covering presentation skills, coaching, effective communication, and more. We cover the costs of professional certifications.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.
  • Social events and Away Days.

At PwC, we help clients solve today’s and tomorrow’s challenges across audit, consulting, tax, legal, technology, and data. We create an environment where people can learn, grow, and build a career in their own way. We believe the best results come from diverse experiences and perspectives. That’s why we foster an inclusive culture where everyone can be themselves, build on their strengths, and contribute to work that makes a real impact.

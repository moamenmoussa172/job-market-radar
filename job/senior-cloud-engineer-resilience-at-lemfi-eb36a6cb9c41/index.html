About the role

LemFi moves money across 30+ countries for people who cannot afford downtime. Our infrastructure has to be as resilient as our customers are determined, and right now we need 1 person to own that end to end. This role sits inside the core engineering team and is the single point of accountability for how we plan for failure, test for it deliberately, recover from it fast, and prove with data that we are getting better every quarter.

You will work across cloud infrastructure, databases, and platform tooling to build systems that bend without breaking. If you are the kind of engineer who would rather break things in a controlled blast radius on a Tuesday afternoon than be paged at 03:00 on a Saturday, this is the job for you.

What you'll build and own

  • Own the disaster recovery programme end to end: design runbooks, run drills, close gaps, and report hard RTO/RPO numbers to the business.
  • Build and mature a chaos engineering practice, designing experiments that surface real weaknesses before customers find them.
  • Design and operate database monitoring across the stack so degradation, replication lag, or backup failures surface immediately, not after the fact.
  • Define and track resilience benchmarks and SLOs that give engineering leadership a clear, honest picture of system health, not a vanity dashboard.
  • Own backup strategy and integrity: automate, test restores regularly, and treat an untested backup as no backup at all.
  • Use AI tooling to accelerate incident analysis, pattern detection in observability data, and runbook generation so the team moves faster on every incident.
  • Partner with product and platform teams to embed resilience requirements early in design, so reliability is never a last-minute retrofit.

Your experience

  • 5 or more years in cloud infrastructure or site reliability engineering, with clear ownership of resilience or availability outcomes.
  • Hands-on experience designing and executing disaster recovery plans and chaos experiments in a production cloud environment.
  • Strong working knowledge of database operations: replication, backup, restore, monitoring, and failover across at least 1 major engine (PostgreSQL, MySQL, or similar).
  • Comfortable with infrastructure-as-code and the tooling that keeps cloud environments observable and controllable.
  • Experience defining SLOs, error budgets, or resilience benchmarks that the wider engineering org actually uses.
  • Able to translate resilience data into plain language for non-technical stakeholders, because a metric nobody understands changes nothing.
  • Bias for action when systems are degraded: you investigate, communicate clearly, and drive resolution without waiting to be told.

Nice to have

  • Experience in fintech, payments, or any regulated environment where data integrity is non-negotiable.
  • Familiarity with multi-region active-active or active-passive architectures at scale.
  • Contributions to chaos engineering frameworks or open-source observability tooling.

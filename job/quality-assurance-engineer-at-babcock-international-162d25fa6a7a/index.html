At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Quality Assurance Engineer at our Devonport Royal Dockyard site.

The role

As a Quality Assurance Engineer, you’ll help ensure that critical infrastructure, facilities, and assets supporting the maintenance of submarines and warships are delivered to the highest standards.

Day-to-day, you’ll have an exciting opportunity to work at the heart of major engineering and infrastructure projects, providing assurance that systems, equipment and facilities are safe, compliant, and fit for purpose. You'll collaborate with project teams, suppliers and stakeholders, supporting quality throughout the project lifecycle while developing your own expertise within a complex and highly regulated engineering environment.

  • Providing guidance, advice and support to project stakeholders on quality planning, delivery, installation, commissioning and handover activities.
  • Supporting the development and review of Quality Management Plans and Inspection & Test Plans during project planning and delivery phases.
  • Assuring compliance with quality requirements through oversight of project and supply chain activities, documentation and records.
  • Ensuring Lifetime Quality Records provide robust evidence that design intent and regulatory requirements have been achieved.
  • Supporting senior quality professionals with project lifecycle quality assurance activities across complex engineering programmes.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at Devonport Royal Dockyard.

Essential experience of the Quality Assurance Engineer

  • Experience of Quality Assurance and Quality Control techniques within an engineering, manufacturing, infrastructure or regulated industry environment.
  • Knowledge of supply chain quality assurance processes and practices.
  • Experience of change management, document management and continuous improvement activities.
  • Understanding of quality management principles and assurance methodologies.
  • Appreciation of national, international, statutory and regulatory requirements relating to quality management and assurance.

Qualifications for the Quality Assurance Engineer

Essential:

  • NVQ Level 3, or equivalent, in an engineering or quality-related discipline.
  • Completion of a recognised engineering apprenticeship.
  • Knowledge or experience of ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System requirements.

Desirable:

  • Additional quality, engineering or auditing qualifications.
  • Membership of a relevant professional body.

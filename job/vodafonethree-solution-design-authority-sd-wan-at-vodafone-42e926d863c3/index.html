What you’ll do

Responsible for the detailed design and development of complex SD-WAN and WAN customer solutions. A senior client facing role and owner of the low-level SD-WAN design, providing technical leadership and support to a virtual team of highly skilled professionals.

Key accountabilities and decision ownership:

  • Technical accountability for the detailed design and delivery of customer SD-WAN deployments.
  • Working alongside the Programme Manager providing assurance and governance for technical components of the solution.

Who you are

  • Proven record of producing enterprise customer SD-WAN low level designs (Fortinet, Cisco, Velocloud).
  • In depth understanding of complex customer SD-WAN deployments, and how these integrate with LAN/WAN and cloud environments.
  • Strong understanding of networking principles, protocols and products.
  • Proven track record for being the Design lead on large scale, complex projects for IT/Telco customer solutions.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Currently SC Cleared or be SC Clearable.

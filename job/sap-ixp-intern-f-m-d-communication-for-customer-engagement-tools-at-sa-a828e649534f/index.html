Position title: SAP iXp Intern (f/m/d) – Communication for Customer Engagement Tools

Location: Walldorf/Sankt Leon-Rot, Germany

Internship duration: 6 months

  • Project scope and deliverables

In this role you will support the team in marketing our achievements in Success Stories and publishing valuable updates on the organization’s project websites. You’ll be responsible for delivering visually appealing and professional presentations as a basis for decision-making, communicating changes, etc

  • Team environment and collaboration

You will work with the Product Owners of the development team as well as with business stakeholders

What You’ll Bring

We’re looking for someone who takes the initiative, perseveres, and stays curious. You like to work on meaningful innovative projects and are energized by lifelong learning.
Student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences

  • Preferred fields of study: computer science, linguistics, design
  • Experience level and background: Marketing, Public Relations, Social Media, Event Organization
  • Required Computer skills: MS Office, SharePoint, Wordpress
  • Beneficial Computer skills: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Figma
  • Soft skills: Team player, willing to take over responsibility
  • Fluent in English and German, skills in additional languages are a plus
  • Working from Walldorf/St.Leon Rot office is required. If you are applying from outside the area, please mention your relocation plans or preferences in your CV (Relocation costs are not reimbursed by the manager/employer)
  • To truly connect to the team, it is beneficial to be in the office at least once every one or two weeks. When we have events 3-4 days would be required.

Pinterest is seeking an Engineering Manager to lead the team responsible for the reliability and modernization of our campaign database and unified indexing platform. This team builds highly available distributed systems that support Ads and Consumer experiences, with a focus on scaling storage and indexing, reducing index build time, and making production changes safer.

You’ll lead engineers working on consequential infrastructure challenges, from campaign storage on TiDB and vector indexing to fleet upgrades and platform modernization. You’ll partner across engineering to improve reliability and performance while helping teams deliver new capabilities with confidence.

What you’ll do:

  • Build and lead an inclusive, high-performing engineering team through hiring, coaching, performance management, and career development.
  • Set the technical direction for database and indexer reliability, scalability, and modernization across Ads and Consumer platforms.
  • Improve the availability, latency, and operational resilience of campaign storage and large-scale indexing systems.
  • Develop safeguards for production changes, including canary rollouts, configuration verification, automated testing, and launch-readiness checks.
  • Drive adoption of infrastructure improvements, including fleet upgrades, continuous profiling, and modern compute platforms.
  • Partner with cross-functional teams to improve index build times, expand indexing capabilities, reduce operational toil, and increase developer velocity.
  • Make thoughtful tradeoffs across reliability, performance, scalability, and cost; establish clear measures of progress.
  • Use AI to accelerate engineering workflows while applying rigorous review, testing, and validation.

What we’re looking for:

  • Minimum 7 years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years managing engineers.
  • Experience leading teams that build or operate high-throughput, low-latency distributed databases, indexing systems, or comparable platform infrastructure.
  • A track record of hiring, developing, and supporting engineers while fostering an inclusive team culture.
  • Strong technical judgment in distributed systems design, reliability engineering, and production operations.
  • Experience improving availability and performance through observability, incident response, safe deployments, and automation.
  • Ability to work across teams and communicate technical priorities and tradeoffs clearly.
  • Proficiency in a programming language such as Java, C++, Go, or Python.
  • A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with distributed storage, TiDB, vector indexing, search or retrieval infrastructure, or large-scale data pipelines.
  • Experience modernizing infrastructure or leading platform migrations and fleet upgrades.
  • Experience improving compute efficiency and developer workflows for systems operating at scale.

What You’ll Build

The SAP Internship Experience Program is SAP’s global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers. This is more than an internship, it’s the foundation for a career built on connection, creativity, and impact.

Position Title: SAP iXp Intern - Personalization, Digital Platforms & AI

Location: Newtown Square, PA

Anticipated Start Date: October 2026

Contract Duration: 12 months

Work Hours: Full-time – 40 hours per week

As a Digital Platforms Personalization intern at SAP, you’ll play a hands-on role in advancing the company’s personalization strategy and digital transformation. You will coordinate and maintain the Personalization roadmaps. You will collaborate with Digital Platforms associates to align timelines and dependencies. You will also support the roll-out of key Adobe technologies. These include Experience Cloud, Experience Platform, Customer Journey Optimizer, and AJO B2B. You will work on strategic initiatives. These initiatives involve machine learning, omnichannel transformation, and cloud migration for EDS/AEM. You will also research new tools. In addition, you will explore how AI agents can create customized experiences.

Key Product Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and maintain Personalization roadmaps. Provide frequent updates. Collaborate with Digital Platforms associates. Align project timelines and identify project dependencies.
  • Digital Transformation: Support SAP in rolling out important Marketing technologies. These include Adobe Cloud, Adobe Enterprise Platform, Customer Journey Optimizer, and Adobe Journey Optimizer B2B.
  • Aid in tool discovery and vendor coordination, helping research emerging technologies and maintain productive partnerships to enhance platform capabilities.
  • Support strategic personalization initiatives. This includes machine learning and omnichannel transformation. It also involves go-to-market strategies and EDS/AEM cloud migration. Use North Star road-mapping to guide these efforts.
  • Explore the future of personalization, researching how AI agents can tailor content and experiences to individual preferences while identifying key challenges to overcome.
  • Troubleshoot and monitor website performance, resolving issues related to layout, functionality, and integrations while maintaining site updates.
  • Create and maintain technical documentation for integrations, program implementations, and Adobe Target workflows.
  • Help to educate and build partnerships by training internal staff and alignment with departments to optimize the tech stack that fuels personalization.
  • AI Operations Support: Assist in deploying and monitoring SAP Joule and Adobe AI Assistant across the digital ecosystem to drive innovation and help upskill members of the Digital Platforms team.

What You’ll Bring

A curious and agile mindset, with an interest for learning and the tenacity to adapt in a dynamic, evolving environment. Well-developed problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and successfully. Expressive intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and mindful stakeholder interaction. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies — especially artificial intelligence — paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take action.

Education: In pursuit of a degree in Marketing Tech, Data Science, Computer Science, or Digital Transformation.

Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, or recently graduated (start date must be within 6 months of graduation date) from a coding academy/bootcamp, apprenticeship, associate, bachelor’s, master’s or JD/PhD program.

Core Skills & Qualifications

  • AI Fluency: A solid desire to learn the mechanics of Large Language Models (LLMs), Vector Databases, and Agentic Orchestration.
  • Platform Literacy: Knowledge with the Adobe Experience Cloud (AEP, AEM, Target, or Analytics) or similar enterprise-grade CMS/MAP.
  • Technical Tools: Proficiency in Jira/Confluence for workflow management and Figma for reviewing AI-prototype UX designs.
  • Communication: Ability to translate business concepts into clear technical requirements working with diverse stakeholder groups.

We are looking for an intern who brings more than just basic office skills:

  • Digital mindset, Innovative thinker, and Technically Savey
  • Drive for knowledge and relationships building
  • Proactive and ready to jump in
  • Remarkable organizational and planning skills and a proven ability to multi-task
  • Great communication, customer service, and collaboration skills
  • Attention to detail with an emphasis on accuracy and quality
  • Ability to prioritize successfully to manage multiple projects, including numerous deliverables
  • Experience working in a team environment as well as individually
  • Ability to collaborate across teams globally
  • Ability to complete tasks in a timely manner
  • Strong desire to learn along with professional drive
  • Willingness to acquire new skills to meet evolving needs
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office programs, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
  • Basic knowledge of design principles and commonly used tools
  • Basic comprehension of Web production tools and content management systems
  • Cognitive Empathy: Understanding how AI agents interact with humans and the ability to map those "conversational" journeys.
  • Prompt Engineering: The ability to structure queries for GenAI tools to produce high-quality, brand-compliant business outputs.
  • Data Literacy: Moving beyond "reporting" to "analysis"—understanding metrics like AI Confidence Scores, Conversion Lift, and Pipeline Velocity.

Where You Belong

The Digital Platforms team consists of elite technologists devoted to making SAP.com and all managed digital properties the world’s most advanced B2B platform. We manage the interaction points and technologies for SAP.com, Search.sap.com, Partner.sap.com, Support.sap.com, Developer.sap.com, and campaign and microsite environments. We also guide the transformation of these platforms.

We are "Customer Zero" for SAP’s own Business AI. By joining us, you aren't just observing the future of AI—you are helping to build, run, and prove it to the world.

You’ll have endless learning resources at your fingertips and gain future-ready skills from a variety of virtual, in-person, and hybrid learning sessions, cultivated just for you, and aligned with our learning approach.

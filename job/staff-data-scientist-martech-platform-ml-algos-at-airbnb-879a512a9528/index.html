About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

Marketing Data Science Measurement empowers Airbnb to optimize marketing ROI through data-informed decision-making. We lead the way in defining and advancing best practices for measuring and optimizing marketing impact. We collaborate with Marketing, Finance, Engineering, and Product to provide actionable recommendations and tools based on effective, timely, and granular measurements.

The Difference You Will Make

As a Data Scientist working on algorithms on the Marketing Data Science team, you will develop the models and measurement behind Airbnb’s AI-driven marketing system where AI agents run campaigns within guardrails set by marketers. These systems shape how Airbnb acquires, retains, and re-engages its guest and host community across owned and paid channels. Your models will inform targeting, creative and spend across channels, audiences, and time.

We are seeking a senior technical leader in ML-driven marketing systems to serve as the data science technical lead across these efforts. You will guide the measurement and optimization approaches for these systems, help develop the models that power them, set the scientific bar, and raise the capability of the team and our partners.

  • Define with Marketing the objectives these systems optimize and the guardrails they operate within, so automated decisions on bids, budgets, audiences, content, and timing target incremental value rather than proxy metrics.
  • Lead the measurement strategy for these programs, combining channel, program, and campaign experiment results into a meta-analytic view of incremental impact.
  • Define how predictive models and causal measurement work together so budget decisions rest on both.
  • Drive large, ambiguous, cross-functional initiatives from problem framing through production.
  • Mentor and set technical standards for data scientists across the team.

A Typical Day:

  • Identify and prioritize the highest-value modeling and optimization opportunities across marketing, translating ambiguous business problems into multi-quarter scientific roadmaps.
  • Partner with Marketing, Finance, Engineering, and Product to align on metrics, methodology, and investment decisions.
  • Architect and review models and pipelines for batch and real-time decisions at scale, such as bidding and message selection; stay hands-on where it matters.
  • Define how automated decisions are evaluated before and after launch and the evidence a channel needs to move from marketer-approved proposals to autonomous operation.
  • Influence senior leadership with clear recommendations that include uncertainty and trade-offs.
  • Mentor DS teammates through design reviews, code reviews, and project scoping.
  • Use AI and agentic coding to improve the team’s workflows and velocity.

Your Expertise:

  • 6+ years of relevant industry experience and a PhD in a quantitative field.
  • Track record of building and deploying machine learning models to optimize advertising outcomes that drive automated decisions at scale.
  • Experience designing experiments or incrementality measurement (e.g., holdouts, geo tests) for ML-driven decision systems.
  • Deep expertise in the ML lifecycle and algorithms: gradient boosted trees, probabilistic and Bayesian approaches, deep learning, optimization.
  • Strong fluency in Python and SQL; experience with modern ML and data infrastructure.
  • Fluency in marketing economics (CAC, LTV/CAC, payback, ROAS) and how models connect to budget allocation.
  • Demonstrated technical leadership: setting direction across teams, mentoring senior and junior DS, and influencing leadership.
  • Clear communication with audiences from engineers to executives.

Preferred Expertise:

  • Causal ML, uplift modeling, and integrating predictive models with experimentation and incrementality measurement.
  • Contextual bandits, reinforcement learning, or other sequential decision methods for bidding, budget pacing, or next-best-action.
  • Hands-on experience with paid media platforms (e.g., Amazon, Google Ads, Meta), including value-based bidding and conversion signals, or with lifecycle channels (email, push).
  • Marketplace, travel, subscription, or e-commerce domain experience.
  • LLM and agentic AI applications in marketing or measurement, including evaluation of agent outputs.
  • Publications, patents, or external presentations that show thought leadership in quantitative marketing and related fields.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

What you’ll build

Position title: Intern (f/m/d) – Experience Center Walldorf: Engagement Planner

Work Area: Marketing Location: Walldorf

Expected start date: December 2026/January 2027

In this role, you’ll:

Face an exciting work environment at the SAP Experience Center Walldorf and be part of our extensive student community. We are looking for a very structured, self-driven and highly motivated intern. Your role is crucial for smooth and successful planning of the visits at the Experience Center:

  • Schedule and orchestrate impactful visit experiences for customers, partners, universities, and internal teams at the SAP Experience Center.
  • Own stakeholder communication from start to finish, aligning expectations and ensuring every visit runs smoothly.
  • Lead the weekly coordination of our student team, creating a supportive, high-performing environment behind the Experience Center scenes.
  • Take initiative to identify and run potential projects that shape the future of how we plan and engage with visitors at the Experience Center

Join our team and start an exciting career at SAP – while you are still studying. You’ll get great training on the job with plenty of learning opportunities and create a strong network within SAP and beyond.

What you bring

You are a student (f/m/d) at a university or a university of applied sciences. We’re looking for someone who takes initiative and stays curious.

The job is for you if you are/have:

  • An organizational talent who likes dynamic situations.
  • Structured and solution-oriented in your thinking and actions. · Someone who enjoys taking on responsibility.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills (German and English).
  • A team player who likes collaborating with others.
  • Flexible and able to adapt well to new situations.
  • Ideally, you already have some practical experience, for example, through internships or student work roles.

We are looking for an intern who supports us for 6 months and can be onsite in Walldorf. It is mandatory that you are enrolled at a university during the internship.

Where you belong

Experience Centers are innovation hubs delivering an interactive and immersive experience to visitors. SAP Experience Centers make SAP’s brand, products and innovations tangible and deliver a memorable customer experience. Our team is responsible for the business operations at SAP’s Experience Center in Walldorf. We are planning and organizing an outstanding customer experience for customer visits and events. As the flagship center at SAP’s headquarters, we are pioneering innovations and scaling best practices to other Experience Center locations around the world.

About the SAP Internship Experience Program:

The SAP Internship Experience Program is SAP’s global, strategic, paid internship program that provides university students with opportunities to find purpose in their careers.

Three reasons to intern at SAP:

1. Culture of collaboration: meet with mentors, make new friends across the globe and create a thriving personal network.

2. Project-driven experience: gain cross-functional skills from our virtual and in-person learning sessions, diverse subject matter experts, and project deliverables.

3. Gain visibility: with SAP Internship Experience Program in your title, you’ll have a global network of SAP leaders, entrepreneurs and career development opportunities at your fingertips.

About the job

About the Role

We are looking for a Google SecOps Security Engineer to implement, operate, and optimize Google SecOps SIEM capabilities to strengthen security monitoring, detection engineering, investigation, and incident response.

Key Responsibilities

  • Configure and optimize Google SecOps SIEM.
  • Onboard and validate log sources.
  • Develop and tune detection rules, correlation logic, dashboards, searches, and use cases.
  • Analyze incidents.
  • Identify monitoring gaps.
  • Maintain operational documentation and reporting.

Required Qualifications

  • Saudi Nationals Only.
  • At least 5 years of experience in security monitoring, detection engineering, incident investigation, or SIEM engineering, including direct experience with Google SecOps or comparable enterprise SIEM platforms.
  • Direct experience developing security use cases, onboarding log sources, tuning detection rules, investigating alerts, and improving security-monitoring coverage within Google SecOps.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.

Key Technologies: Google SecOps SIEM, Google Security Operations, YARA-L, Unified Data Model, log-source integrations, detection rules, dashboards, threat intelligence, security monitoring and investigation capabilities.

Key Skills: Google SecOps SIEM, Google Security Operations, Detection Engineering, Security Monitoring, Log Onboarding, Parsing and Normalization, UDM, YARA-L, Threat Hunting, Incident Investigation, Dashboard Development, Detection Tuning

About the job

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager, Agentic AI Ad Solutions to join our team and play a key role in shaping the future of neuro-contextual advertising at a global scale.

Who We Are

Seedtag is the leading Neuro-Contextual Advertising Company.
We are redefining how advertising works in a privacy-first world by moving beyond keywords and cookies, towards understanding human emotion, intent, and context.

Powered by advanced AI and neuroscience, our proprietary technology, Liz, delivers a deep, human-like understanding of content and attention. This enables brands to connect with audiences at the right moment, in the right mindset, with ads that are relevant, engaging, and respectful of the user experience.

Founded in 2014 by two ex-Googlers, Seedtag has grown into a global company with 500+ Seedtaggers across 17 countries, backed by €250M in funding, and expanding rapidly across the open web and Connected TV.

We’re not just building technology, we’re rewriting the rules of advertising.

Your Challenge

As Senior Product Manager, Agentic AI Ad Solutions, you will:

Product Vision & Strategy

  • Define and own the roadmap for AI-powered advertising applications, with a focus on agentic systems that automate and optimise advertiser workflows.
  • Identify market opportunities by analysing trends in AI, LLMs, reinforcement learning, and ad tech.

Execution & Delivery

  • Collaborate with engineering and AI research teams to design, build, and launch scalable, agent-driven applications.
  • Define and prioritise requirements, user stories, and success metrics.
  • Ensure product quality and usability through continuous iteration and experimentation.

Cross-functional Leadership

  • Partner with data scientists, AI engineers, and UX teams to create intuitive, outcome-driven experiences.
  • Work with commercial and client-facing teams to ensure product-market fit and drive adoption.

Innovation & Experimentation

  • Champion rapid prototyping and experimentation with AI capabilities—e.g., autonomous media planning agents, optimisation bots, and creative generation systems.
  • Stay ahead of emerging AI frameworks, orchestration tools, and APIs relevant to advertising.

What You’ll Need to Succeed

  • 5+ years of product management experience, with at least 2 years in AI-driven products or complex SaaS platforms.
  • Strong understanding of agentic application design (multi-agent systems, orchestration frameworks, LLM integration).
  • Proven track record of shipping innovative, data-driven products from concept to adoption.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills; able to explain AI capabilities to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Analytical mindset with experience in defining and tracking product KPIs.

It would be a plus

  • Experience in ad tech—knowledge of programmatic advertising, SSP/DSP ecosystems, campaign optimisation, and attribution.
  • Hands-on exposure to AI development tools (LangChain, Vercel AI SDK, OpenAI API, Hugging Face, etc.).
  • Understanding of ad operations workflows, creative production, and media planning.

Why Join Seedtag?

This is a defining moment to join Seedtag, a company that combines scale, ambition, and soul.

Growth & Opportunity: Accelerated, hands-on development with real ownership, global projects, and internal mobility.

Flexibility: We trust you to balance work and life with flexible schedules and a hybrid model; work from home, the office, or wherever you do your best work.

Learning & Development: Access to online learning, continuous training, and optional English or Spanish group classes.

Culture that Cares: Well-being is part of how we work, supported by initiatives like SeedDay, Digital Disconnection, and team-building moments.

Connection & Fun: Global events, offsites, celebrations, and a strong sense of belonging across all Seedtag locations.

Additional Perks

  • Flexible benefits plan (restaurant, transportation, kindergarten vouchers)
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Gympass access to wellness and fitness programs
  • Seedsave Program with exclusive discounts

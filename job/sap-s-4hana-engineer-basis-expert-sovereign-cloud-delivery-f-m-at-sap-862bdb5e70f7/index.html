What You'll Build

As an SAP S/4HANA (Basis) Engineer within the Global Sovereign Cloud Delivery & Operations team, you will contribute to the daily operations and continuous evolution of SAP's Sovereign Cloud environments. You will work across the service delivery lifecycle, helping ensure the reliability, security, performance, and availability of SAP S/4HANA landscapes in a highly regulated cloud environment.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Designing, implementing, monitoring, maintaining, and optimizing SAP S/4HANA systems within the SAP Sovereign Cloud environment.
  • Supporting the setup of multi-system landscape architectures and integrations across heterogeneous technologies.
  • Assisting with system implementations, upgrades, patching activities, refreshes, and ongoing operational support.
  • Investigating incidents, contributing to problem resolution, and supporting root cause analysis activities.
  • Providing on-call support for incidents and performance degradations as part of a rotational support model.
  • Contributing to the continuous improvement of technical documentation and operational procedures.
  • Partnering with engineering teams to enhance cloud operations processes and increase automation.
  • Supporting SAP environments running on Linux, Windows, and SAP HANA platforms.

What You Bring

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • SAP experience, including hands-on exposure to SAP S/4HANA.
  • Practical experience in the implementation, upgrade, or support of SAP S/4HANA systems.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Initial exposure to or involvement in Disaster Recovery (DR) planning and testing activities.
  • A problem-solving mindset with foundational troubleshooting and root cause analysis skills.
  • Practical experience supporting SAP systems running on Windows, Linux, and SAP HANA environments.
  • Willingness to work in a 24/7 operational environment, including participation in shift rotations and on-call duties.
  • Commitment to maintaining a regular office presence to support effective team collaboration.
  • Fluent French (C2 or equivalent) is required, as the position involves direct interactions with ANSSI and management of security and compliance documentation available exclusively in French.

Due to the potentially classified nature of our work, you must be willing to undergo a governmental security clearance process.

Nice to Have

  • Knowledge of public cloud platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Experience with infrastructure automation and configuration management tools such as Terraform and Ansible.

Where You'll Belong

The Global Sovereign Cloud Delivery & Operations organization is a newly established business unit within SAP Product Engineering, dedicated to delivering secure, scalable, and compliant cloud solutions for the public sector and highly regulated industries.

As part of this team, you will collaborate with colleagues across SAP to build and operate a harmonized Sovereign Cloud portfolio. You will help deliver standardized, transparent, and trusted cloud services while supporting customers with some of the most demanding security, compliance, and operational requirements.

This is an exciting opportunity to develop your expertise at the intersection of SAP technology, cloud operations, security, and innovation, while contributing to the future of SAP Sovereign Cloud services.

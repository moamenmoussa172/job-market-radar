Start date: February 2027
Duration: 6 months
Location: Paris
Salesforce is the Customer Company, shaping the future of business with AI, Data, and CRM. Guided by our core values, we help organizations across all industries innovate and build stronger customer relationships. We empower you to be a Trailblazer—driving growth, exploring new ideas, and making a positive impact.
Join us as a Strategy & Operations Analyst Intern to directly support our Head of Strategy & Operations, the COO Office, and our team of analysts. At Salesforce, you will work in a fast-paced, high-standard environment with the latest innovations, including AI-powered analytics and forecasting tools.
Key Responsibilities:
The intern will focus on supporting business analysis, data extraction, and strategic initiatives across four core pillars:

  • Data Extraction & Management: Extract, clean, and process business data from internal ecosystems (including Snowflake and Org62) to ensure reliable datasets for decision-making.
  • Tools Building & Maintenance: Build, maintain, and refresh analytical assets, forecasting models, and reporting tools—with a strong emphasis on leveraging AI-powered solutions.
  • Reporting Generation: Create and deliver recurring dashboards and ad-hoc business analyses to track performance and support commercial teams.
  • Strategic Projects & Market Analysis Support: Assist in coordinating strategic projects and collaborate closely with the COO Office and Analysts to develop in-depth market analyses.

In the short term, you will ramp up on our data stack (Snowflake, Org62) and own tool refresh routines. In the medium term, you will take greater ownership in building new analytical assets and driving strategic deliverables. This role offers direct exposure to cross-functional stakeholders (Sales Programs, Enablement) and the Executive Leadership Team (COO, CEO, Head of Sales, CECO, CCO).
Profile:

  • Current student pursuing a Master’s degree in .
  • Strong data-oriented background with hands-on experience or familiarity with data extraction and analysis tools (SQL, Snowflake, CRM/Org62).
  • Proven skills or a strong, demonstrated interest in Artificial Intelligence tools and their business applications.
  • Curious, autonomous, and self-driven—capable of independent research and fast self-learning.
  • Strong business acumen with a keen interest in market research, sales support, and executive-level strategic topics.
  • Adaptable, resilient, and eager to thrive in a highly challenging, fast-paced environment with high standards.
  • Fluent in English and French.

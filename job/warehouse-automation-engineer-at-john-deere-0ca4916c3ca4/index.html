Your Responsibilities

As a Warehouse Automation Engineer for John Deere at the newly constructed A&CS Hebron Distribution Center located in Hebron, IN, you will:
• Provides input into overall direction, development and integration of warehouse control systems, warehouse automation (AS/RS), and automated guided vehicles
• Serves as Subject Matter Expert for assigned systems in the areas of: Integration, Warehouse Control Systems, Material Handling Equipment, AS/RS, and Automated-Guided Vehicle integration to Business Systems (SAP-EWM)
• Facilitate and manage capital projects from inception through execution, including functional specifications, scope development, RFQs, design coordination, and successful delivery against timeline, cost, and performance objectives
• Lead system integration, commissioning, and startup activities including I/O checkout, networking, FAT/SAT, and final system validation
• Work with cross-functional warehousing teams to improve and support current and future robotics and automation initiatives to increase reliability, throughput, and quality
• Provide troubleshooting and technical support for automation systems, including controls, networking, and process equipment, both on-site and remotely
• Support the daily needs of maintaining warehouse performance by prioritizing and providing engineering support to warehouse maintenance and operations as needed
• Develop and maintain technical documentation including electrical drawings, control narratives, specifications, SOPs, and project documentation to assist maintenance and operations

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

• Experience supporting automation engineering projects in a factory, warehouse, or industrial environment
• 3+ years of experience working with various types of warehouse automation
• 3+ years of experience with AMRs/AGVs or ASRS or material handling equipment
• Strong communication and collaboration skills, with experience bringing cross functional teams (including shop floor employees) together to solve business problems
• Ability to travel up to 25% or more based on business needs for the first 6 months

What Makes You Stand Out

• Experience with automated packaging equipment
• Experience with SAP EWM or other warehouse management or warehouse control systems
• Demonstrated ability to identify and implement process improvements that drive measurable gains in safety, quality, productivity, or storage density
• Solid understanding of warehouse operations, fulfillment processes, and industry terminology
• Experience managing vendors, including scope alignment, performance oversight, schedule coordination, deliverable tracking, and issue resolution
• Project management experience in a factory, warehouse, or industrial setting
• Experience building and presenting business cases to support project approvals or capital investments
• Background in automation, industrial equipment, or facility infrastructure

Education

Ideally you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Industrial, Manufacturing, Mechanical or Electrical), Analytics, Computer Science, or a related quantitative discipline or equivalent related work experience

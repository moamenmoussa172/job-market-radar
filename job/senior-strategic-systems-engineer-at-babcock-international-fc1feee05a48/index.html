The Role

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a Senior Strategic Systems Engineer at Babcock's Submarine Support Engineering team, based at Bristol or Clyde.

As a Senior Strategic Systems Engineer, you'll take on a highly influential role supporting some of the UK's most critical defence programmes. Working within the Submarine Support Engineering team, you'll help deliver engineering management and technical support services that ensure Royal Navy submarines remain available, capable and mission-ready.

What you’ll do:

Day-to-day you'll be required to:

  • Supporting the Strategic Weapons Waterfront Optimisation Team to improve overhaul, upgrade, defect resolution and recommissioning activities.
  • Conducting technical investigations into equipment reliability, failures and incidents, identifying opportunities for engineering improvements.
  • Undertaking engineering studies, feasibility assessments and major maintenance investigations to support programme delivery.
  • Building strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders, providing technical advice, peer reviews and engineering expertise.
  • Collaborating with multidisciplinary teams and industry partners to enhance submarine strategic weapons systems capability.

What you’ll need:

Essential experience of the Senior Strategic Systems Engineer

  • Knowledge and experience of submarine engineering systems.
  • Experience delivering engineering projects, programmes or maintenance activities.
  • Strong stakeholder management and relationship-building skills across customers, partners and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Experience supporting engineering policies, procedures, technologies, equipment or operational improvements.
  • Proven problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify risks, resolve issues and drive continuous improvement.

Qualifications for the Senior Strategic Systems Engineer

  • Working towards, or holding, Incorporated Engineer (IEng) or Chartered Engineer (CEng) status with the Engineering Council, or able to demonstrate a commitment to achieving professional registration.

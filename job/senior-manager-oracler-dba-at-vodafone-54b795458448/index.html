About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Oracle Application DBA Lead to join ITO in SP Infocity, Pune. The role requires 10 to 15 years of application database administration experience, with hands-on expertise across multiple Oracle database versions.

You will support operational and critical database activities while contributing to end-to-end service stability, availability and continual improvement. The role combines Oracle database administration, performance optimisation, automation, monitoring and observability with service governance and collaboration with application teams.

You will also contribute to Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices and AI-driven optimisation initiatives across CRM and end-to-end operations, helping improve operational efficiency.

What you will do

  • Administer and support production Oracle databases and associated operational activities.
  • Analyse database and application performance bottlenecks, including scripts, code and user-generated queries that may cause application slowness.
  • Perform SQL and database performance tuning and support DML operations.
  • Support database upgrades and migrations, including integrations with other RDBMS technologies.
  • Apply knowledge of database architecture, memory architecture, storage architecture, clustering and high-availability solutions.
  • Manage database backup and recovery activities.Support user access management and security audits.
  • Monitor and participate in auditing and SOX compliance activities.
  • Use monitoring and observability solutions such as Enterprise Manager, Grafana, Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Kibana, Splunk or Zabbix.
  • Perform log analysis, alert tuning, dashboard management and proactive monitoring.
  • Develop and maintain environment support and maintenance scripts to automate BAU operational activities and improve system stability.
  • Use advanced UNIX skills to manage jobs and batch processes and to tune or create processes where required.Work closely with application teams to support database operations.
  • Use ticketing tools to manage operational activities and incidents.Provide on-call support for high-priority database issues.
  • Contribute to proactive monitoring, automation, reconciliation and continual service improvement.
  • Support service governance and end-to-end service stability and availability.
  • Apply SRE practices and contribute to AI-driven optimisation initiatives across CRM and end-to-end operations.

Who you are

  • You have 10 to 15 years of experience in application database support and administration.
  • You have hands-on experience administering Oracle databases across multiple versions.
  • You have practical expertise in database and SQL performance tuning.
  • You understand Oracle and non-Oracle database architecture.
  • You are experienced in configuring databases and providing operational support.
  • You understand memory, storage and cluster architecture as well as high-availability approaches.
  • You have advanced knowledge of UNIX, shell scripting and at least one scripting language, such as Perl.
  • You have experience with proactive monitoring, operational automation, reconciliation and continual service improvement.
  • You understand database upgrades, migrations, backup and recovery, security and user access management.
  • You have experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Enterprise Manager, Grafana, Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Kibana, Splunk or Zabbix.You bring analytical, communication, stakeholder-management and technical-leadership capabilities.
  • You have a bachelor’s degree or equivalent relevant professional experience.
  • Telecom industry knowledge would be advantageous.ITIL certification is desirable.

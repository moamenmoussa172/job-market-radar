About the job

There are over 8 billion people on this planet. And by 2050, there will be 2 billion more... many moving into urban centers at an unprecedented rate. Making sure there is enough food, fiber and infrastructure for our rapidly growing world is what we're all about at John Deere. And it's why we're investing in our people and our technology like never before! Here the world's brightest minds are tackling the world's biggest challenges. If you believe one person can make the world a better place, we'll put you to work. RIGHT NOW.

Due to the development of John Deere Business Service Center, we are currently seeking candidates for the position of:

Full Stack Software Engineer - IT Order Fullfilment

Poznań, Poland

Your responsibilities:

  • Build features across frontend and backend (React, Node.js or Python).
  • Help automate manual business processes, such as request handling and approvals, to make them faster and simpler for employees.
  • Look for opportunities to reduce repetitive work, both in business processes and in the team's daily engineering work.
  • Write automated tests and documentation for your code.
  • Take part in code reviews, refinement, and other Agile/Scrum ceremonies.
  • Work closely with the Product Manager, Delivery Lead, UX, and the business operations team to understand needs and refine requirements.
  • Learn the order fulfillment process (requests, approvals, tracking) and how our solutions support employees.
  • Support the team in fixing bugs and solving production issue.

Our requirements:

  • Hands-on experience with JavaScript/TypeScript and React, and with Git/GitHub.
  • Knowledge of Node.js or Python, REST APIs, and databases (SQL or NoSQL).
  • Knowledge of AWS or a similar cloud platform (e.g. Azure, GCP).
  • Understanding of secure coding basics, such as authentication, input validation, and protecting secrets and sensitive data.
  • Eagerness to continuously learn, expand your knowledge, and go beyond your own scope, as well as to adapt to new tools, technologies, and changing priorities.
  • English language proficiency at B2 level or higher, with good communication skills.

Nice to have:

  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines (e.g. GitHub Actions).
  • Experience with AI and agentic coding tools (e.g. GitHub Copilot) to speed up your daily work.
  • Familiarity with automated testing frameworks.
  • Interest in workflow automation, business process automation, or AI agents.

What we offer:

  • Employment contract.
  • Strong support for work-life balance, including flexible working hours (start time between 7:00 and 10:00 AM) and a hybrid work model at Business Garden in Poznań.
  • Opportunities for professional development within the company, with strong support for internal career growth.
  • Employee referral program.
  • Additional vacation days based on length of employment.
  • Holiday allowance when taking at least 2 weeks off.
  • Daily use of a foreign language in a friendly international working environment.
  • Private medical care, life insurance, sports card, meal allowance, language courses, a social fund budget, and various additional fun events in our office.
  • Free access to our e-learning platform, featuring over 1,000 courses.

Please attach an English version of your CV.

About the job

Are you a curious, analytical problem-solver who enjoys turning messy data into clear decisions? The Retail Supply Chain Initiatives (RSI) team is looking for a Business Intelligence Engineer to help shape how supply chain decisions are made across EU retail operations.
You will design, build, and automate the data models and pipelines behind how Amazon works with its vendors in Europe, from how products are shipped to us to how inventory reaches customers. You will analyse program performance, find the root causes of efficiency gaps and operational defects, and turn complex data into clear recommendations for product managers, operations leaders, and technical teams.
What we're looking for:
• Strong data mining, statistical modelling, and quantitative analysis skills
• Experience building end-to-end analytical solutions — from concept through to production
• Ability to dive deep into large datasets to identify root causes and implement scalable, long-term solutions
• Comfort working in ambiguity: structuring undefined problems, prioritising independently, and driving projects to completion
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present findings to senior stakeholders and constructively challenge assumptions with data
• A self-starter mentality with a hands-on approach, capable of supporting multiple initiatives simultaneously across a cross-functional environment
Key job responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain data models, ETL pipelines, and automated reporting for EU retail supply chain programs
- Build dashboards and visualisations in Amazon QuickSight that surface key performance indicators to business and operations teams
- Run deep-dive root cause analyses on program performance, operational defects, and cost or efficiency gaps, and present clear findings and recommendations
- Translate business questions into structured analytical frameworks, working with product managers, operations, and engineering teams
- Prepare datasets and metadata so AI tools and agents can answer business questions accurately, and contribute to the team's AI-assisted analytics tooling
- Contribute to data architecture and design decisions, mentor peers on BI best practices, and help with scoping and prioritisation
Manage multiple concurrent projects, prioritising effectively in a fast-paced environment
A day in the life
Your morning might start with reviewing overnight pipeline outputs and catching data quality issues before they reach downstream users. You then move into analytical work, such as building or refining a data model behind a dashboard used across EU retail. You will meet regularly with product managers and operations leads to plan upcoming analytical needs, with focused time in between for SQL development, root cause analysis, and documentation. You will also take part in design reviews and share findings with business partners to guide their next steps.
About the team
The Retail Supply Chain Initiatives (RSI) team's vision is to evolve EU Retail inbound into the most cost-efficient and selling partner-friendly integrated supply chain. The BI team within RSI is the analytical backbone of this mission. It delivers insights through dashboards, financial reporting, and data products used by 3,000+ people across the EU stores organisation. We value collaboration, curiosity, and practical problem-solving, and we keep looking for better ways for data to serve the business.
- Experience in analyzing and interpreting data with Redshift, Oracle, NoSQL etc.
- Experience with data visualization using Tableau, Quicksight, or similar tools
- Experience with data modeling, warehousing and building ETL pipelines
- Experience using SQL to pull data from a database or data warehouse and scripting experience (Python) to process data for modeling
- Experience with SQL
- Experience in the data/BI space
- Experience with AWS solutions such as EC2, DynamoDB, S3, and Redshift
- Experience in data mining, ETL, etc. and using databases in a business environment with large-scale, complex datasets
- Master's degree, or Advanced technical degree

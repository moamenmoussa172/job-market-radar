About the job

This is Adyen

Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition.

For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. We are motivated individuals who tackle unique technical challenges at scale and solve them as a team. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster.

The opportunity
Every time a shopper reaches an Adyen checkout, our models decide in milliseconds how to tailor that checkout to them. The Personalize Intelligence team builds those models. They power Adyen Checkout optimization and are the core of Adyen Uplift, our AI product suite that optimizes for conversion, fraud and cost and is already used by many of Adyen’s customers.
We’re looking for a science leader to run this team. You have built ML models yourself, you now lead the people who build them, and you care whether a model really moved conversion. You’ll lead a team of machine learning scientists and data engineers, set the modelling direction, and own the results in production, in close partnership with product and merchants.
This is a science-led role. The team owns the models, experiments and metrics, and works with Adyen’s engineering and ML platform teams to run them at real-time scale.
What you’ll do

  • Lead and grow a team of ML scientists and data engineers: hire, coach and develop them.
  • Own the modelling strategy for checkout and payments, from problem framing and model choice to the trade-offs between conversion, fraud and cost.
  • Set the bar for scientific rigour: experiment design, online A/B testing, offline-to-online evaluation and honest measurement of uplift.
  • Stay hands-on: review models and experiments, unblock hard problems and guide technical decisions.
  • Turn merchant and product needs into ML problems, and shape the roadmap with the product manager.
  • Partner with engineering, ML platform and data teams to take models to production and keep them performing.
  • Explain results and trade-offs clearly to non-technical stakeholders and senior leadership.
  • Build a team culture of ownership, autonomy, open feedback and impact.

Who you are

  • You come from a data science or applied ML science background, and you’ve built and shipped models yourself, for example in ranking, recommendations, personalization, pricing, fraud or propensity and uplift modelling.
  • You lead data scientists today and stay close enough to the work to challenge a model or an experiment design.
  • You know how to prove a model worked: experimentation, causal thinking and the business metrics that matter.
  • You’ve taken models into production where latency, reliability and scale matter.
  • You build alignment across teams and work with senior leaders on strategy and roadmaps.
  • You explain technical ideas clearly to people who aren’t scientists or engineers.

Minimum qualifications

  • 7+ years in applied science or machine learning, including hands-on development of models that shipped to production.
  • 2+ years of people management experience leading data scientists or ML scientists whose models run in production
  • Experience with real-time decisioning, recommender systems, contextual bandits or uplift modelling.

Nice to have

  • Experience in payments, fintech, e-commerce or marketplaces.

Why Adyen?

This is an exceptional opportunity to join a solid, established company with a startup mindset, characterized by small teams, direct communication, and work on important challenges. You’ll have direct access to massive global datasets (e.g., payments, identity data) and the ability to see your team's work have an immediate impact at scale. We offer an environment of ownership and speed, where a focused research team can make a significant difference. You will be at the forefront of bridging green and brownfield opportunities, shaping how financial products expand the next era of Adyen.

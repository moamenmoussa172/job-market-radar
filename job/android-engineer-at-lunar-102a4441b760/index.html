About the job

Banking hasn’t changed much in decades. We’re changing that. One product at a time.

Lunar is a Nordic challenger bank building the future of financial life with AI at the core. Our engineers, designers and product people work on problems that actually matter: making money simpler, smarter and more rewarding for a million registered users across the Nordics.

Fully licensed and founded in 2015, with offices in Copenhagen, Aarhus and Stockholm.

We move fast, we build with purpose, and we’re just getting started.

Northstar is the squad that owns how people and businesses join Lunar, and how their information stays up to date for as long as they bank with us. That covers everything from the first application to yearly reviews and closing an account.

We’re adding an Android engineer to the squad in Copenhagen, so Northstar can build more of these journeys itself. You’ll report to Vitaliy, the squad’s engineering manager.

What will you do?

  • Spend your first week with the squad in Copenhagen, then a week with our Android engineers in Aarhus, getting to know the app and how it’s built. We pay for travel and hotel.
  • Build and ship the onboarding journey: logging in with MitID or BankID, signing, answering our questions, uploading documents such as a passport, residence permit or payslip, and keeping people informed while their application is reviewed.
  • Build the yearly review that asks customers to confirm their details are still current, and the flows that start when a business changes, for example when the national registry shows it has moved into a new industry.
  • Build account closure journeys for both private and business customers.
  • Work with product using Lunar’s design system, bringing in a designer when it’s needed, and with our Financial Crime Prevention and Operations colleagues to understand the checks behind each step, so customers can get it right first time.
  • Work in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with Coroutines and Flow, Hilt, Room and Retrofit, in a modular codebase alongside iOS, Android, web and backend engineers and product. You build it and you run it.

What are we looking for in you?

  • Android engineering: At least 4 years of professional Android engineering in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, shipping features to production.
  • Product and UX judgement: You build clear, polished journeys from a design system, working with product without a dedicated designer.
  • Cross-functional teams: You’ve worked with mobile, web, backend and product colleagues, and you bring questions and ideas rather than waiting to be told what to build.
  • Building within what’s there: You’re happy to work in an established codebase and follow a senior platform team’s conventions, rather than pushing to rebuild.
  • Working independently: You’re comfortable working day to day away from the rest of our Android engineers, with regular trips to Aarhus.
  • Nice to have: Banking or fintech experience, identity, KYC or document capture flows (such as MitID, BankID or document upload), accessibility and animation craft, or a GitHub profile, side project or app you can show us.

What you get.

  • 4 days in the office. We’re a team that builds together, thinks together and moves together. Showing up is part of how we work. Core hours are 9:00 to 15:00, with flexibility around them, and you can work from abroad for up to 20 working days a year. If you live in Malmö or Lund, the commute to Copenhagen works and you’d be on a Danish contract.
  • A well-equipped workspace. A modern computer, two monitors, keyboard and mouse. We want you to have the tools you need to do your best work.
  • Pension, health insurance and enhanced parental leave. Lunar pays 8% into your pension and you pay 4%. From your financial future to your family moments, we’ve got you covered.
  • Time off and lunch. Five weeks’ holiday plus six extra days, and a lunch scheme.

How we hire.

A first conversation with Katherine at Scede, who’s helping Lunar hire for this role, then an interview with Vitaliy, the squad’s engineering manager. Finally, a coding challenge to prepare at home, followed by a remote coding interview with Android engineers from our platform team.

About the job

We are looking for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join our Technology Engineering team supporting enterprise Technology Platforms & Compute initiatives. The ideal candidate should have strong expertise in DevOps tooling administration, CI/CD platform engineering, infrastructure automation, container technologies, and cloud platforms.

The role involves managing enterprise DevOps platforms, implementing secure CI/CD pipelines, enabling application onboarding through automation, and working closely with Agile product teams to deliver highly available, scalable, and secure engineering platforms.

This is a hands-on engineering role requiring strong experience across DevOps tooling, Kubernetes/OpenShift, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), cloud platforms, automation, monitoring, and platform operations.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities

• Install, configure, administer, and maintain enterprise DevOps tooling and platform services.

• Design, build, implement, and maintain enterprise-grade CI/CD pipelines supporting application delivery across multiple environments.

• Automate infrastructure provisioning, deployment processes, and configuration management to ensure secure, scalable, and consistent environments.

• Support Agile delivery teams by enabling application onboarding through self-service automation and platform engineering.

• Collaborate closely with Development, Infrastructure, Security, Product, and Operations teams throughout the software delivery lifecycle.

• Implement and maintain DevOps and DevSecOps best practices across engineering teams.

• Build reusable Jenkins Shared Libraries, pipeline automation, and deployment workflows using Groovy and Python scripting.

• Integrate security and quality scanning tools into CI/CD pipelines.

• Develop and integrate APIs required for pipeline automation and platform services.

• Troubleshoot infrastructure, application, deployment, and platform issues while supporting production and operational environments.

• Implement monitoring, logging, observability, and performance management solutions across enterprise platforms.

• Ensure compliance with infrastructure security standards and organizational engineering policies.

• Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, backlog refinement, stand-ups, and retrospectives.

• Continuously evaluate emerging DevOps technologies, automation practices, and platform engineering improvements.

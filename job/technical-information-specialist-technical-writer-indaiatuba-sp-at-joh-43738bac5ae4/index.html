About the job

We work to help life advance, and we are always looking for innovative people who can contribute to our mission. How about becoming part of our team?

Location: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Function: Customer Experience (CA)
Title: Technical Information Specialist (Technical Writer) - Indaiatuba/SP - 125673

As a Technical Information Specialist for the Enterprise Technical Information Development (ETID) organization located in Indaiatuba-SP, you will provide technical and analytical leadership in the development of operational, maintenance, repair, installation, onscreen help, and diagnostic information by understanding product purposes, functions, and user requirements for John Deere products.

Your responsibilities

  • Lead and contribute to enterprise innovation & strategy initiatives supporting standards, automations & AI, and advanced diagnostic solutions;
  • Work in partnership with product engineering and customer support representatives to identify and define solutions improving product troubleshooting pain-points, technician efficiency, and customer uptime;
  • Collaborate with product engineering and customer support organizations to define hardware, software, and technical information requirements minimizing time to diagnose and repair;
  • Support project leaders, lead technical information specialists, and training development specialists to create and execute content development plans that meets quality, efficiency, and economic requirements;
  • Lead and conduct technical content reviews supporting quality and accuracy of content;
  • Provide work direction, mentoring, and training to local and remote authoring resources.

What Skills You Need

  • Complete degree in Engineering, Technologist, Ag/Ag Sciences, or other technical fields;
  • Advanced English: broad vocabulary, good grammar, conducts meetings, negotiates and provides training;
  • Thorough knowledge in engineering principles, theory, and practice;
  • Demonstrated technical experience troubleshooting systems, functions, and components;
  • Technical and analytical skills to detect, diagnose, and resolve complex machine issues, particularly with hydraulic, mechanical, electrical, and electronic systems;
  • Proven organization skills, decision making, and ability to manage competing priorities.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Cross-functional experience across one or more areas including product design, manufacturing, quality, and/or product & customer support;
  • Experience and knowledge of Product Development and Customer & Product Support processes;
  • A team player who helps to build a highly engaged and energized work environment;
  • Experience developing and integrating process improvements through application of digital dexterity tools, such as automations, AI, data & analytics;
  • Experience and knowledge with technical information development systems, processes, and tools;
  • Experience working with local and global teams.

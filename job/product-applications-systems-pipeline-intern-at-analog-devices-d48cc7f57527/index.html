Product & Systems Applications Intern

About the Opportunity:

ADI Trinamic, located in Hamburg, Germany, develops industry-leading motor and motion control solutions at the IC, module, and system level for robotics and industrial automation applications.

We welcome curious, motivated, and hands-on engineering students to join our Hardware & Applications team as a Product & Systems Applications Intern. In this role, you will work alongside experienced engineers on real-world projects involving motion control, robotics, and automation technologies. You will gain valuable insight into product development, customer applications, and the engineering challenges behind next-generation intelligent machines.

This internship offers a unique opportunity to apply your academic knowledge, build practical engineering skills, and contribute to products used by customers around the world.

What You'll Do

  • Support the design, development, and testing of development boards, reference designs, and engineering demos.
  • Contribute to the evaluation, validation, and characterization of motion control and motor drive products.
  • Create technical documentation, including user guides, application notes, and demonstration materials.
  • Assist with technical investigations and customer application support.
  • Perform laboratory measurements, debugging, and verification of electronic hardware.
  • Collaborate with engineers across a global development organization to bring innovative products to market.

What We're Looking For

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, Automation Technology, or a related field.
  • Understanding of analog, digital, and mixed-signal electronics fundamentals.
  • Basic PCB design knowledge and electronics hardware experience.
  • Programming experience in C/C++ and/or Python.
  • Hands-on experience with common laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, digital multimeters, and soldering stations.
  • Demonstrated interest in solving technical challenges and learning new technologies.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills in English.
  • Curiosity, initiative, and a passion for technology and innovation.

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on experience with cutting-edge motion control and robotics technologies.
  • Mentorship from experienced engineers and technical experts.
  • Exposure to the full product development lifecycle, from concept through validation and customer support.
  • The opportunity to make meaningful contributions to real engineering projects.
  • Experience working in a global technology company and collaborating with international teams.
  • A strong foundation for a future career in robotics, automation, and semiconductor technology.

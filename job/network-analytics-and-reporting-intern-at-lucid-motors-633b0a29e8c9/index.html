About the job

About Lucid

At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience.

We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production happening in-house across our global offices and manufacturing facilities. Our teams come from industries around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence. By refusing to settle, you can help redefine what’s possible and shape the future of mobility.

The Network Development team is central to scaling the Lucid brand across Europe, building and expanding the partner network that enables sales, service, and long‑term customer confidence in each market. At the intersection of data, planning, and execution, the team translates strategy into action. As an intern, you will support this effort through data, reporting, and project coordination, contributing to a network that grows in a structured, scalable, and effective way.

What you’ll do

  • Support the Network Development team in building and scaling Lucid’s partner network across European markets
  • Maintain and update structured datasets related to network coverage, demand, locations, and partner inputs
  • Support recurring reporting and status updates used for market rollout and decision‑making
  • Assist with partner onboarding activities by tracking inputs, updates, and documentation relevant to network planning
  • Support ongoing projects by organizing inputs, maintaining project documentation, monitoring timelines, and facilitating communication between internal stakeholders
  • Help prepare materials and presentations used for internal alignment

What we’re looking for

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program (HBO/WO), preferably in (International) Business Administration, International Business, Business Analytics/Data or a related field
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills, with high attention to detail
  • Comfortable working with structured data and performing recurring refinement tasks
  • Ability to bring clarity and structure to incomplete or messy inputs
  • Reliable and proactive, with the ability to manage multiple tasks in parallel
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Comfortable with Excel; confident creating structured trackers and summaries (PowerPoint is a plus)

Nice to have

  • Experience with basic reporting tools and/or dashboards
  • Exposure to data visualization or analytics tools (e.g. Power BI)

What you’ll gain

  • Hands‑on experience supporting real partner onboarding and market rollout activities
  • Exposure to how a premium automotive brand scales its sales and service network across Europe
  • Practical experience working with operational data, reporting, and project coordination
  • The opportunity to take on increasing ownership as you build familiarity with the network and processes
  • Insight into how strategy is translated into execution in a fast‑growing, innovative organization

Senior Sensor Systems Engineer, RIVR

About the job

Help us build legged robots that use physical AI to carry packages all the way to the customer's front door. We need a sensor systems expert to make sure they perceive the world reliably in rain, fog and changing light.
Our mission is to make robotic doorstep delivery a commercial reality, taking our robots from prototypes to a product that delights both customers and the operators who run it. As Senior Sensor Systems Engineer, you will make sure the robot has the right sensors to do its job, and keep improving its performance and safety as the fleet grows. You will define the sensor suite, work with suppliers to deliver it, and analyze field data with our autonomy teams to find and fix what limits the robot. This is a hands-on technical leadership role based in Zurich, Switzerland. You will lead through influence across hardware, software, autonomy, test and supplier teams, and travel to test sites and suppliers about once a quarter.
Key job responsibilities
- Own the sensor architecture: translate the robot's mission and autonomy needs into a sensor suite (cameras, stereo, LiDAR, radar)
- Select sensors and manage suppliers: evaluate sensor, lens and supplier options, and drive changes when off-the-shelf solutions are not enough
- Integrate sensors into the robot: work with electrical and mechanical engineers on data interfaces, time synchronization, calibration, thermal management and sealing
- Ensure validation is sound: review the V&V team's sensor test plans for coverage of the robot's operating domain, set acceptance criteria from the sensor requirements, and support the analysis of the results
- Monitor and improve field performance: analyze data from deployed robots with the autonomy teams, root-cause perception and sensing failures whether the cause is in hardware, optics, firmware or software, and drive fixes into the next design iteration
- Support robot safety: make sure the sensing system supports the safety concept and meets its safety requirements
- Lead technically: write design and decision documents, present trade-offs to leadership, review other engineers' work, and shape the sensing roadmap for future robot generations
- Bachelor's degree in Optical Engineering, Imaging Science, Physics, Mechatronics, or Electrical/Mechanical Engineering with a focus on optics, imaging or sensor systems
- 7+ years of development experience for sensing, imaging, robotics or automotive products
- Experience selecting camera (image sensor and lens), LiDAR or radar (including imaging radar) sensors and designing them into products for outdoor applications
- Experience leading or co-leading sensor design, customization and validation with external sensor suppliers
- Experience leading root cause analysis of perception and sensing failures in deployed systems, whether the cause is in the sensor hardware, optics, integration, firmware or software
- Experience with sensor interface protocols (e.g., MIPI CSI-2, GMSL, Automotive Ethernet) and time synchronization methods (e.g., PTP/gPTP, hardware triggering)
- Track record of translating system-level needs into a sensor architecture and sensor requirements, and ensuring the resulting solution is properly verified against them across the operating domain
- Master's degree or PhD in Optical Engineering, Imaging Science, Physics, Mechatronics, or Electrical/Mechanical Engineering with a focus on optics, imaging or sensor systems
- Understanding of AI/ML-based perception pipelines, including traditional and learned stereo, and the sensing and compute requirements they set
- Experience designing stereo camera hardware, including baseline selection, synchronization and calibration
- Understanding of how camera ISP and HDR settings affect image quality and perception performance, especially in low-light and high-contrast conditions
- Knowledge of environmental testing, EMC compliance and reliability qualification for sensors
- Knowledge of functional safety principles (e.g., IEC 61508, IEC 62998)
- Experience as technical lead on a multidisciplinary program

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Ireland, Portugal, Spain, United Kingdom.

Join our expanding engineering team!

We're hiring a permanent Senior Backend Engineer to join our Asset Sales group as we scale one of the most important parts of M-KOPA's FinTech platform. Build distributed, event-driven microservices and help shape what AI-native engineering looks like at M-KOPA.

The Impact 💚

Your code will directly power the systems that put smartphones and e-motorbikes into the hands of Every Day Earners across Africa. We've already helped over 10 million customers access more than $2 billion in credit. It's your chance to be part of something that's literally transforming lives across an entire continent 🌍

The Opportunity

🎯 Mission-driven engineering: Every service you build helps expand financial inclusion for under-banked populations

🏆 Global recognition: Join a company named by the Financial Times as one of Africa's fastest-growing companies for five consecutive years (2022–2026)

🚀 Scale challenges: Build backend systems serving millions across multiple African markets

🤖 AI-native from day one: We're actively embracing AI as part of the engineering craft, not bolting it on

🌍 Fully distributed: Work remotely with diverse teams across UK, Europe, and Africa

What You'll Do

At M-KOPA, you'll build and operate distributed C# and .NET microservices on Azure, working within an event-driven architecture at significant scale. This is an outcomes-driven role: we care far more about the value you create than where you sit or how many hours you're online. You'll own what you build end-to-end — from idea, through implementation and deployment, into production and iteration.

Technical Environment 💻

  • Modern C# stack: Distributed C# and .NET microservices
  • Cloud & orchestration: Hosted on Azure using Kubernetes
  • Architecture: Event-driven, supporting products used at significant scale
  • Observability: Grafana, Azure Application Insights, logs, traces, and metrics
  • AI tooling: Claude and other AI tools used throughout the engineering workflow — design exploration, code generation and review, test writing, incident investigation, and technical communication

Our Engineering Approach 🚀

  • AI-native engineering: We want engineers who tinker — experimenting with new tools, agents, and workflows, and sharing what works
  • Guardrails as we accelerate: Automated tests, SLOs, alerting, observability, and deployment safeguards around everything we ship
  • Own it beyond the pull request: Design for idempotency, retries, out-of-order events, and failure modes, and know how your services behave in production
  • The Campfire Rule: Leave the systems you touch a little better than you found them
  • On-call as feedback, not heroics: Engineers who build our systems also help operate them, using incidents to make the platform more resilient

What You Need

We're looking for a senior engineer who's curious, autonomous, and highly trusted. You're comfortable owning the entire software stack — including supporting infrastructure — throughout its lifecycle, and you see production data, not just deployment, as the real measure of "done."

Required Experience:

  • Strong grasp of C# and .NET development
  • Experience with event-driven systems - We use Azure Service Bus, but would love to hear from you if you have experience with similar messaging technology (Kafka, RabbitMQ, etc.)
  • Microservices architecture experience, ideally on Azure and Kubernetes
  • Experience using observability tooling (e.g. Grafana, Application Insights) to understand production behaviour
  • Comfort using AI tools as part of a daily engineering workflow (design, code review, testing, incident investigation)
  • DevOps culture mindset, including on-call/production ownership
  • Proven ability to collaborate effectively in distributed teams across time zones
  • Continuous learning mindset and openness to feedback

Location & Benefits

  • Fully remote role, collaborating during core GMT working hours
  • Benefits package, which includes Bonus, Growth Shares & On-Call Allowance
  • Work with diverse teams distributed across UK, Europe, and Africa
  • Personalised training budget to support your development

  • Family-friendly policies and flexible working arrangements
  • Well-being support and career growth opportunities

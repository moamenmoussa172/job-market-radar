About the job

Join Truecaller – The place where innovation meets impact!

Truecaller's mission is to build trust in communication by making it safer, smarter, and more efficient. Born in Sweden, trusted by the world, and here’s why we stand out:

  • We are trusted by over 450 million active users every month across 190+ countries
  • We identify over 15 billion calls daily, helping users avoid spam and scams
  • We are powered by a team of 450+ employees from 45+ nationalities

We always look for people who take initiative, own their work, and keep raising the bar. An entrepreneurial mindset matters here, especially when it turns bold ideas into real actions. We stay collaborative and focused, always searching for smarter paths forward. If you want to make an impact and grow with a team that inspires millions, you’ll fit right in.

The Truecaller for Business at Truecaller focuses on providing value to medium / large enterprises by improving the way they communicate with their customers. Our brand identity management solutions help businesses achieve more by enhancing their presence on Truecaller as a Verified Business. We also help improve call productivity and efficiency and message-response-rates by adding trust, context & safety to communication.

Our team is a fantastic blend of young Turks and industry veterans from the top tech companies globally and we count many of India’s top enterprises and startups as our delighted customers.

As part of our aggressive growth plans, we are looking for young, energetic and customer obsessed professionals to join our Enterprise Sales Team in the Go-To-Market organization

As an Senior Account Manager, you are expected to leverage your existing relationships as well as build new relationships across our enterprise accounts to help position and drive adoption of our communication suite of enterprise products.

What you’ll do:

  • Identify and engage with high-potential enterprise customers for our enterprise products.
  • Build and maintain relationships with existing clients: act as the primary point of contact, understand their evolving needs, address issues and ensure ongoing satisfaction.
  • Develop and implement retention programmes to minimise churn and maximise lifetime value of clients.
  • Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities within the existing client base to drive revenue growth.
  • Prospect and acquire new clients by understanding their needs, positioning our solutions and closing sales.
  • Collaborate with internal teams (e.g., marketing, product, operations, customer support) to deliver value and seamless service to clients.
  • Develop and execute territory strategies for acquisition, including outreach, networking, proposals and negotiation.
  • Track, analyse and report on key sales and retention metrics (e.g., new accounts, churn rate, contract renewals, upsell revenue) and use insights to refine strategies.
  • Build an active deals pipeline with a high velocity conversion rate
  • Accurately forecasting and meeting monthly revenue targets.
  • Help build success stories with our enterprise customers.

What you bring in:

  • 9+ years of solid experience in value-based selling of enterprise products.
  • Consistent track record of exceeding revenue goals.
  • Executed end-to-end, complex, and multi-threaded deals with large enterprises.
  • Experienced in selling SaaS/CPaaS and software products work.
  • Ability to collaborate with cross functional teams and reseller partners for deal closures.
  • Trusted partner to enterprises navigating solution selling,value-based pitches, commercial negotiations, contracting, and stakeholder management.
  • Excellent at relationship building at CXO level.
  • Self-driven and highly motivated individual.

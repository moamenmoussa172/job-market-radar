POSITION

Quality plays a huge role in how we build products. Here at Codeway, QA starts long before the final checkpoint. It means building quality in from the first idea and owning a product’s success as it reaches millions of users around the world. Sounds exciting? Keep reading!

We are looking for a Quality Assurance (QA) Engineer to develop and execute exploratory and automated tests to ensure the quality of our mobile products in a multitude of categories.

You will work with product managers and developers from the start of every feature. You shape requirements before development starts, protect critical user journeys with reliable automation and give the QA sign-off behind every release.

We work AI-first. You will use AI across your testing work and test the AI features our apps ship.

If you have an engineering mindset and enjoy solving software quality problems end to end, we’d like to meet you.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Join feature planning with product managers and developers, turn requirements into testable acceptance criteria and flag risks before development starts.
  • Review specifications and technical designs, and ask “how will this be tested?” while the answer can still change the design.
  • Estimate, prioritize and plan testing by user impact and product risk.
  • Design focused test scenarios and run exploratory sessions that find what scripted tests miss.
  • Build and maintain reliable automated tests for critical user journeys, running in CI and on real devices.
  • Use AI agents to explore builds and draft tests, verify what they produce and keep the tests worth keeping.
  • Evaluate the AI features our apps ship: the accuracy, consistency and safety of their outputs.
  • Write bug reports a developer can reproduce without calling you and trace them to a root cause.
  • Give the QA sign-off for each release: what was tested, what wasn’t and what risk remains.
  • Follow quality after release through crash, ANR and analytics signals, and turn every escaped bug into a lasting test.
  • Stay up to date with new testing and AI tools, and share what works with the other app teams.


WHAT YOU’LL BRING

  • Experience testing both iOS and Android apps end to end.
  • Strong exploratory testing and risk-based test design skills, with clear, concise test cases where they add value.
  • Hands-on experience with a test automation framework: ideally Maestro or Playwright, or comparable tools such as Appium, XCUITest or Espresso.
  • Proficiency in at least one programming or scripting language (e.g. JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Kotlin or Java), enough to write, review and debug test code, and comfort with Git.
  • Experience with CI pipelines such as GitLab CI or GitHub Actions, and with device clouds such as BrowserStack.
  • SQL and BigQuery skills for validating analytics events and data.
  • Experience testing subscriptions, paywalls and in-app purchases.
  • Hands-on use of AI tools in your daily testing work, and the habit of verifying what they produce.
  • Experience evaluating AI or LLM features.
  • Solid knowledge of software QA methodologies, tools and processes, including white-box and black-box testing.
  • Clear written communication: bug reports and sign-offs that a developer and a product manager can act on.
  • Experience working in an Agile/Scrum/Kanban team alongside developers and product managers.
  • BS/MS degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related subject, or equivalent practical experience.

NICE TO HAVE

  • Previous work at an app studio or a similar company shipping several mobile apps.
  • Exposure to performance, security or accessibility testing.
  • Background in testing Flutter apps.
  • API testing skills with tools such as Postman.
  • Ability to use network debugging tools such as Proxyman to inspect app and backend traffic.
  • Familiarity with test management tools such as Xray, Qase or TestRail.

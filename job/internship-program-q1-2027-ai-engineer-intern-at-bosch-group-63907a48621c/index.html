About the job

Internship Duration: 04 Jan 2027 - 25 Jun 2027

Internship Model: Full-time or at least 4 days/week

Application Period: 1 Nov – 16 Dec 2027

Interview date: 10, 15 & 17 Dec 2027

  • Research and prototype GenAI applications for digital learning, simulation, and content generation.
  • Support the development of interactive modules for Digital Academy platforms (e.g., e-learning, virtual labs, AI tutors).
  • Support the design, training, and evaluation of AI/ML models.
  • Assist in collecting, cleaning, preprocessing, and analyzing data for model development.
  • Work with data pipelines and datasets under guidance from senior engineers or data scientists.
  • Support the development and testing of AI-powered features, tools, or prototypes.
  • Collaborate with team members on AI-related tasks and experiments.
  • Learn and stay updated on AI/ML concepts, tools, and industry trends.
  • Experience with prompt engineering or fine-tuning AI models.
  • 3rd or Final-year student in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or a related field.
  • Official university recommendation letter with the university stamp (mandatory). This document will be required after you successfully pass Bosch's interview process.
    Exception: For students from HCM University of Science and HCM University of Social Sciences and Humanities, the university will send the recommendation letter directly to Bosch. You do not need to obtain a separate copy yourself.
  • Commitment: full-time or at least 4 days/week.
  • Good knowledge in Python or C++.
  • Good knowledge in AI/ML frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch.
  • Core understanding of AI (ML, Deep Learning, and Generative AI).
  • Have knowledge in data structures, algorithms and some of library.
  • Fair English communication skill

Important note:

  • Graduates or applicants who cannot submit a university recommendation letter (with official stamp from university) are not eligible to apply.
  • Due to the high number of applications received, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. Thank you for your understanding.

WHY BOSCH?

  • 1 day of paid leave per month
  • Good benefits, team building and company trip.
  • Opportunity to work in global projects of fast developing company and being a part of innovation team contributing initiative ideas to the hi-tech world
  • Engage in our diverse training programs which surely help strengthen both your personal and professional skills

About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Everpure is fundamentally reshaping the data storage industry for the AI era and we’re looking for a high-impact AI Channel Ecosystem Manager to help lead that transformation.

This is an incubation role - you will help define our AI channel strategy in EMEA and build repeatable co-sell motions from the ground up.

As enterprises move from AI experimentation to production at scale, storage infrastructure has emerged as one of the biggest constraints to performance, cost and speed. This role sits at the center of solving that problem through a focused set of high-impact partners.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Strategic Partner Leadership: Own the end-to-end relationship and revenue growth for select AI-focused partners.
  • GTM Motion: Design and execute co-sell motions (workshops, joint account targeting, campaigns). Pilot, test, repeat, scale successful sales plays and GTM motions.
  • FlashBlade//EXA Evangelism: Drive the adoption of Everpure’s AI portfolio, with a focus on FlashBlade//EXA and Data Stream and our collaboration with NVIDIA.
  • Pipeline Generation: Personally drive partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline. This includes direct engagement with partner AEs, joint account mapping and hands-on deal progression.
  • Enablement & Training: Drive and facilitate deep-dive sales and technical enablement sessions to ensure partner teams can effectively articulate Pure’s value proposition in the complex AI/ML landscape.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Everpure’s Scale AI and Enterprise Sales and SE teams to map accounts and close complex, large-scale deals.
  • Market Intelligence: Stay ahead of the curve on AI frameworks, GPU-accelerated computing, and the competitive landscape to keep Everpure as the partner of choice.

WHAT YOU BRING TO THE TEAM

  • Experience: 7+ years of experience in Channel Management or Sales within the enterprise storage, networking, or AI infrastructure industries.
  • AI Domain Expertise: A proven track record of selling into the AI, Deep Learning, or HPC markets. You should understand the nuances of the AI data pipeline (Ingest, Clean, Train, Inference).
  • Partner Network: Existing, deep-rooted relationships with key AI focused EMEA partners like WWT, Penguin Solutions, Eplus, PNY, Bull Computing
  • Product Knowledge: Familiarity with scale-out file and object storage (NAS) and its role in supporting GPU-heavy workloads. Experience with the FlashBlade architecture is a plus.
  • Communication: Exceptional presentation skills with the ability to simplify complex technical architectures for executive-level stakeholders.

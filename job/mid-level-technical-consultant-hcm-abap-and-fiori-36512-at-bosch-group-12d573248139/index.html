About the job

  • Lead HR technical analysis, design, configuration, customization and delivery; develop and maintain compliant ABAP/Fiori apps, including payroll inquiries and Mexican CFDI.
  • Deliver new international HR solutions with global/local architects, consultants and stakeholders; follow customer, IT architect, PSO and Solution Manager coding standards.
  • Execute unit tests/test plans, debug and fix defects for design compliance; provide tier 3 support and incident resolution, including non-executing active enhancements.
  • Assess and deliver upgrades, patches and improvements; ensure service-transition continuity with product/solution and infrastructure operations.
  • Document specifications, help create support manuals and design/develop proof-of-concept and prototype environments; maintain versioned HR repositories with Product Development Managers.
  • Manage tasks, estimates and progress; finalize technical business requirements with HR customers.
  • Position Context Key projects/challenges: Support and enhance global SAP HCM, ABAP and Fiori solutions. #LI-MM1
  • Bachelor’s in Computer Science/Engineering, IT, Information Systems or related field.
  • Strong expertise in ABAP development, including BAdIs, BAPIs, RFCs, and workflow creation and modification.
  • Hands-on experience in SAP implementation projects and HR/HCM ABAP development.
  • Practical experience with SAP Fiori, including standard applications, extensions, and custom app development.
  • Proficiency in SAP Fiori Launchpad, repositories, and SAP Business Application Studio.
  • Advanced SAPUI5, HTML5, JavaScript, Gateway and OData; hands-on UI5 development and strong OOP principles.
  • ABAP debugging, enhancement/BAdI execution and root-cause analysis; HCM/Fiori integration, infotype creation/modification, LDBs and clusters.
  • Hands-on migration/interfaces: EDI, ALE, IDocs, HCP, SuccessFactors and on-premise; RICEF: reports, interfaces, BDCs, enhancements/exits and Smart Forms.
  • Fluent English; good communication and presentation skills.
  • Preferred Qualifications
  • SAP HCM/HR, payroll inquiry/support and international projects; global/virtual teamwork.
  • SAP BTP/mobility knowledge and Workday exposure.
  • Intermediate to advanced Spanish.
  • SAP ABAP, Fiori or SAP BTP certifications.
  • Preferred Certifications: SAP ABAP, Fiori or SAP BTP certifications.

Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English

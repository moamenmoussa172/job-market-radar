We're hiring a Senior Software Engineer to join the retail spot trading engineering team within the Consumer & Business group. This team owns the systems that power spot trading in the main Coinbase app, executing orders across centralized exchange (CEX) venues and onchain (DEX) venues so customers get one experience regardless of where an asset settles. You'll own retail spot order flow end to end and set the bar for execution speed, fill quality, and asset coverage. Prior blockchain or smart contract experience is welcome but not required.

What you'll do:

  • Own retail spot order flow on Aeron, the in-memory high throughput (2M+ TPS) sequencer behind CEX and DEX trading, from market data intake through order placement, execution, and settlement.
  • Integrate new blockchains, decentralized exchanges, and launchpads into the main Coinbase app, expanding the assets and trading capabilities retail customers can reach.
  • Scale real-time market data pipelines to 100K+ messages per second at millisecond latency across centralized and onchain venues.
  • Ship end-to-end retail spot trading features alongside product, design, and client engineering, from technical design through production rollout.
  • Improve execution quality and reliability, holding the stack to explicit targets for fill quality, latency, and asset coverage.
  • Advance agentic-first development practices, building tooling and self-improving systems that raise team velocity.

Required Skills and Experience:

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience in backend or full stack roles, with at least half of your work focused on backend systems.
  • Demonstrated success building and operating production data systems or pipelines, including ownership of their reliability and on-call response.
  • Hands-on experience designing data-intensive systems on infrastructure such as Kafka, ClickHouse, StarRocks, Temporal, or Redis.
  • Experience integrating external venues or onchain systems, such as DEX aggregation, smart contract interfaces, or EVM and Solana tooling.
  • Demonstrated ability to build, iterate on, and improve agentic-first repositories and software systems in production environments.
  • Track record of improving latency, throughput, or reliability in a high-volume production system, measured against targets you helped set.
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

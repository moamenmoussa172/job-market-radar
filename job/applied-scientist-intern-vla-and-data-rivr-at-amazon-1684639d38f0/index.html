As an Applied Science Intern, you will have access to large datasets with billions of images and video to build large-scale machine learning systems. Are enrolled in a Bachelor's degree or above in computer science, machine learning, engineering, or related fields
High Degree of Proficiency in any programming language such as Python, Java, C++, SQL, Python, or similar scripting and programming languages

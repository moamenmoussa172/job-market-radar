Commissioning Engineer FLS Systems, Commissioning

About the job

Job Advert Cx Engineer.
Amazon Web Services (AWS) provides a highly reliable, scalable, low-cost infrastructure platform in the cloud that powers hundreds of thousands of businesses in 190 countries around the world. To meet the growing demand for AWS cloud computing capacity in EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa) we are now looking for a Fire, Life ad Safety Systems Commissioning Engineer to join the team.
Are you experienced in Critical Infrastructure Commissioning? As a Fire, Life and Safety systems Commissioning Engineer (CxE), you will be part of highly creative, efficient team tasked with tackling fascinating and challenging problems building and supporting the operations of AWS owned data centres, Colocation facilities and Substations The AWS Commissioning team (ACx) understands, evaluates, defines and manages the expectations of construction quality in accordance to engineering designs to meet the customer requirements.
Key job responsibilities
You will be responsible for supporting vendors, contractors and other teams involved in design, construction and commissioning.
• reviewing concept designs ensuring design standards and local code requirements are met.
• Develop and execute a FLS quality control and commissioning program.
• Coordinate with on-site construction managers to audit the general contractor’s FLS quality control program.
• Supervise FLS vendors, contractors and other teams in the construction and commis-sioning of FLS systems
• Coordinating, planning and scheduling of FLS commissioning activities.
• Travel to sites for quality control checks, FLS systems audits, startup testing, and commissioning.
• Witness required FLS testing, review FLS integrated systems testing and commission-ing documents to ensure compliance with the basis of design, specifications and standards of the project.
• Develop metrics to analyse and view FLS quality control and commissioning im-provement. Communicate status updates and metrics to stakeholders
The CxE shall provide multi-discipline input into the scheduling, test script development, designing, and planning of projects. In addition, the CxE will be required to review component submittal packages, lead Factory Witness Testing (FWT), perform system level and integrated system testing. During commissioning, the CxE will continuously audit activities to ensure compliance with environmental, quality, and safety requirements. Additionally, the individual will be responsible for comprehensive weekly reporting of status of all ongoing projects and lessons learned to continually improve the quality of our program. This position will include coverage of multiple inflight projects.
About the team
The AWS Commissioning Team (ACx) is a wholly independent business within the DCGS organization. This team of veteran professional Electrical Engineers, Mechanical Engineers, Controls Engineers, Architectural Engineers, Project Managers, Operators and seasoned Amazonians are uniquely positioned to provide deeply detailed engineering and operational review and testing of equipment designs, operational functionality and construction practices. We are focused on ensuring the rapid delivery of a quality product while maintaining full transparency, recording metrics of manufacturer/contractor/equipment issues pre-PFHO, and identifying opportunities for all our peers and customers.

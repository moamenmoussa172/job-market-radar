About the job

Overview:

We’re looking for participants for an in-person research study testing a new interactive technology. Your feedback and participation help improve how the product works in everyday situations.

Project Details

  • Job Title: In-Person VR Data Collection Opportunity
  • Location: Onsite (Burlingame, CA Location)
  • During: 2 hours from our onsite location
  • Support Type: Study
  • Participant Pay: $50 per 2 hour session (active participation for 1-1.5 hours, remaining time will be setup and instruction walkthroughs)

What You’ll Do:

You'll be invited to our research office where you'll conduct hands-on testing with our new interactive technology in a casual indoor environment, you will provide general user feedback and be paid for your time.

We are looking for individuals who are...

  • Fluent in English.
  • Comfortable wearing AR/VR headset devices.
  • Analytical and curious about emerging technology.

Why This Role:

This is an opportunity to play a direct role in how future AR/VR systems are evaluated and improved.

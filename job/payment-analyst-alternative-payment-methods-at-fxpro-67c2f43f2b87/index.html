About the job

Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.
As a Payment Analyst (Alternative Payment Methods), you will play a crucial role in analyzing and optimizing the performance of our payment solutions, turning transaction data into actionable insights that improve checkout conversion, reduce processing costs, and detect fraud patterns early.
Responsibilities

  • Assess market trends and client transaction data to identify and prioritise new APMs that improve checkout conversion
  • Track daily, weekly, and monthly deposit and withdrawal metrics, such as approval rates, processing times, volumes in local currency and USD, and average transaction values, by country, provider, and method
  • Categorise decline codes and failure reasons to identify and resolve recurring issues
  • Benchmark provider performance by country to recommend routing changes, provider switches, and method additions or removals, and work with Business Development on checkout sorting and method branding
  • Build and maintain payment dashboards by country and provider in Excel, Google Sheets, or BI tools, with automated alerts when performance falls below target
  • Prepare weekly performance reports and monthly management summaries that explain what changed, why, and the recommended next steps, and present the findings to the team and other departments
  • Track processing fees and cost per transaction across providers and methods, and provide cost analyses to finance and business development departments for fee reviews and contract negotiations
  • Monitor transaction flows for signs of fraud, abuse, chargeback spikes, or technical outages, and escalate issues promptly to the relevant teams
  • Compare internal system data with provider reports to identify discrepancies and missing transactions, and support Finance in investigating settlement issues, rolling reserves, and payout delays
  • Prepare and present data for monthly provider meetings, raising issues and recommending improvements where needed
  • Deliver ad-hoc analyses for Management, Finance, and Marketing, and support cashier A/B tests to measure their effect on conversion
  • Document report definitions, formulas, and data sources, and create templates and standard operating procedures so recurring reports can be easily reproduced

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of experience as a Payment Analyst within fintech, Forex and CFD, or iGaming
  • University degree, preferably in finance or economics
  • Strong analytical and critical thinking skills
  • Advanced knowledge of Excel and Google Sheets and experience with BI tools such as Power BI and Amplitude
  • Practical knowledge of payment systems, gateways, and alternative payment methods
  • Understanding of fraud and dispute management, settlement reporting, KYC, and AML
  • High attention to detail when reviewing transactions, charges, and fees
  • Clear communication skills, with confidence in explaining payment metrics and technical issues to finance, customer support, and management
  • Excellent time management and prioritisation skills
  • Experience in handling confidential and sensitive information with discretion
  • Strong problem-solving skills, with a good understanding of how payments affect the business
  • Professional fluency in English. Knowledge of an additional language is considered a plus

Interview steps

  • Screening call with Recruiter (30 minutes)
  • Onsite technical interview with Hiring Team (~60 minutes)

About the job

About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

As part of ADI's HR Operations organization, the Senior HRIS & Analytics Analyst will work closely with Global HR colleagues and ADI's Finance and IT Organization, building on ADI's established suite of key metrics dashboards that encompass the full employee lifecycle. This would include all key Talent Acquisition Metrics, Internal Movement, Performance and Goal Management, Succession, etc.

The role will also lead the build-out of ADI's HR predictive analytics capability, moving beyond reporting on what has happened to applying statistical and machine-learning modelling techniques that anticipate workforce trends and inform HR and business decisions. The Analyst will effectively collaborate with HR and IS colleagues, located in different countries, to configure reports and dashboards, synthesize and analyze data, and produce easy-to-understand data visualizations and insights that drive data-informed decision-making.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Advise and participate in the creation of ADI's long and short-term analytics roadmap, and the technologies required to support each.
  • Extend and enhance ADI's existing suite of Workforce Dashboards covering Talent Acquisition, Talent Management, Internal Movement, Succession Planning, etc., adding new dashboards where needed and integrating predictive model outputs and new data sources into the current reporting environment.
  • Design, build, validate and maintain predictive models on HR data using supervised learning methods (logistic regression, gradient boosting, survival analysis) and time-series forecasting.
  • Prepare model-ready datasets from Workday, ServiceNow and other HR sources: define target variables and point-in-time snapshots, engineer features, and handle class imbalance and missing data.
  • Evaluate models with appropriate metrics (AUC, precision/recall, calibration, forecast error), explain results to HR partners using feature-importance and explainability techniques such as SHAP, and test models for bias and fairness across employee groups before use.
  • Support the implementation and ongoing analytics for ServiceNow HR Service Delivery, including case and service metrics reporting and data integration with Workday.
  • Identify and assess data quality review opportunities. Design and implement meaningful reporting metrics and dashboards, and proactively monitor, govern, and drive continuous improvement of data integrity, accuracy, and compliance across HRIS platforms and processes.
  • Identify opportunities to integrate AI into HRIS and analytics solutions, driving automation, improving data accessibility, and enhancing the overall end-user experience through intelligent and intuitive capabilities.
  • Introduce new ideas, methods, and process improvements; and keep up to date with new system functionality releases.
  • Engage and collaborate with colleagues, internal stakeholders and external thought partners through regular meetings, involvement with customer community, and benchmarking with other companies.
  • Research data science methodologies and apply those methodologies to enhance and facilitate data-driven decision-making processes.
  • Create data visualizations and narratives to effectively communicate the understanding and interpretation of complex data sets.

Job Requirements

  • A minimum of 3-5 years related data analytics or HR systems experience in global organizations, preferably with Workday.
  • A bachelor's degree in Statistics, Data Science, Analytics, Economics, Industrial-Organizational Psychology, Business, HR or a related quantitative discipline. A master's degree in a quantitative field is a plus.
  • Proficiency in data analysis, visualization tools such as Power BI, Dataiku, SQL etc.
  • Hands-on experience building and validating statistical or machine-learning models in Python (pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels, XGBoost or LightGBM) or R, including regression, classification, time-series forecasting, and hypothesis testing. Experience with survival analysis or causal inference methods is a plus.
  • Applied knowledge of model evaluation and validation: train/test and time-based splits, cross-validation, AUC and precision/recall, calibration, and monitoring for model drift.
  • Experience applying predictive modelling to HR or people data is highly desirable.
  • Experience working with Snowflake or similar cloud data platforms (e.g., building queries, models, or data pipelines for HR or business reporting) is a strong plus.
  • Experience with ServiceNow, particularly HR Service Delivery (HRSD), reporting, or integrations with HR systems such as Workday, is desirable.
  • Understanding of responsible AI principles as applied to employee data, including fairness testing, explainability, and data privacy requirements (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
  • Able to handle confidential information in an appropriate manner.
  • Strong analytical and Excel skills; ability to mine and interpret data.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • An energetic, proactive, resourceful and self-motivated approach to their work.

About the job

About Wolt

At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe.
Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life.

Join us in shaping the experience that millions of customers land on every time they open DoorDash, Wolt, or Deliveroo. As part of our Core Consumer organisation, our Homepage team owns one of the single biggest levers we have on what customers find and one of the hardest surfaces in the app to get right.

Working at the intersection of ranking, personalisation, and AI/ML, you'll help decide what each customer sees and why, across three brands and dozens of markets. You'll be building on a global stack, and your roadmap will be a mix: global platform problems that apply worldwide, global capabilities we're accelerating into EMEA, and EMEA-specific problems worth solving properly rather than working around.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to join our Homepage team. In this role, you'll shape how customers discover restaurants, grocery and retail venues, dishes and products — and help define what discovery looks like as AI-driven and agentic experiences change the way people decide what to order.

What You'll Be Doing

You'll be joining a team working on one of the most-used surfaces in the app.

Here's what your day-to-day might look like:

  • Building new end-to-end features and user experiences on Homepage that shape how customers discover restaurants, grocery and retail venues, dishes, and products.
  • Working on ranking, personalisation, and AI/ML problems in the Homepage domain, including how we decide what each customer sees and why.
  • Exploring where AI-driven and agentic experiences change the way customers discover what to order.
  • Making discovery work across three brands and many markets, without flattening into one-size-fits-all.

What You'll Need to Thrive

Our ideal candidate will bring strong product judgment and curiosity to grow into the rest:

  • Proven experience as a Product Manager on consumer-facing products - discovery, homepage, feed, or ranking-driven experiences are especially relevant.
  • You are a product manager more than a feature owner. Someone who finds the customer problems that matter most and makes the case for solving them.
  • You work backwards from the customer problem, and weigh it against what the business needs most right now.
  • You're comfortable holding several business objectives at once and making a call when they pull in different directions.
  • You work well with a wide range of stakeholders and build trust with people whose priorities differ from your own.
  • You think strategically and can move between long-term direction and what we should ship next.
  • You're comfortable with ambiguity and genuinely flexible (this industry is changing fast, AI most of all).
  • You're curious about what AI actually changes for customers, and can tell real value from hype.

Next steps

The position will be filled as soon as we find the right person, so make sure to apply as soon as you realize you really, really want to join us!

Our Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion

We’re committed to growing and empowering a more inclusive community within our company, industry, and cities. That’s why we hire and cultivate diverse teams of people from all backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. We believe that true innovation happens when everyone has room at the table and the tools, resources, and opportunity to excel.

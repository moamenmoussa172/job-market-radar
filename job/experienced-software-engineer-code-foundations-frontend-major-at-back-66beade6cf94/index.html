About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Spain.

Hi, we’re Back Market.

We’re here to help make tech reliable, affordable, and better than new. We're a global marketplace for refurbished devices, helping lower our collective environmental impact by providing trustworthy, affordable tech with 92% less carbon emissions than new.

Yep, you read that right. Turns out refurbished tech is way better for the planet than new. In fact, With every device purchased on Back Market, our positive impact on the planet grows. From our Customer Care representatives to our software engineer, every individual at Back Market cuts the planet — and consumers — a break. Our mission is simple: to do more with what we already have.

Are you ready to join us?

About the role

We're looking for a versatile Software Engineer with strong frontend fundamentals and a healthy curiosity for everything around them.

You might work on frontend architecture one day, improve CI or local development the next, and then dive into performance, observability, deployment, or platform tooling. You enjoy understanding how things work beyond the browser, and you're comfortable reasoning across the edge, CDN, server, application, and data layers.

This is a build-it-and-run-it environment. We own what we build, operate it in production, and improve it when reality inevitably gives us feedback.

What you'll do

- Build and improve platform capabilities and Golden Paths for frontend engineers.

- Make frontend delivery more consistent, reliable, and self-service.

- Improve the developer experience across local development, testing, CI/CD, releases, and deployments.

- Work on complex frontend platform topics such as caching, performance, configuration, observability, and application architecture.

- Help define sensible standards without creating unnecessary ceremony.

What we're looking for

- Strong professional experience with JavaScript and TypeScript.

- Solid frontend fundamentals, including browser behaviour, HTTP, SSR, APIs, performance, and application architecture.

- Experience with Vue.js and Nuxt, or a comparable frontend ecosystem.

- A good understanding of caching and performance trade-offs. You can reason about cache keys, invalidation, freshness, consistency, and privacy.

- Experience with CI/CD, automated testing, and production software delivery.

- Interest in the broader stack, including backend services, infrastructure, observability, and developer tooling.

- Strong ownership and a pragmatic approach to solving problems.

- The ability to navigate ambiguity, explain complex topics clearly, and work well with others.

- Good written and spoken English.

You don't need to know every tool in our stack. We certainly don't expect you to. What matters is that you can learn quickly, ask good questions, and make complex things simpler for the engineers using your work.

Our technical environment

- Frontend: Vue.js, TypeScript, Nuxt, Storybook, Tailwind

- Backend: Python, FastAPI, Go

- Data and messaging: PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, RabbitMQ, BigQuery

- Infrastructure: GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, ArgoCD, CircleCI, GitHub, BackStage, Grafana, Open Telemetry

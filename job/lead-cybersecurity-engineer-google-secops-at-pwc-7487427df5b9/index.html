About the job

Job Description & Summary

About the team

A career in our Cyber Security practice, within Risk Assurance Compliance and Analytics services, will provide you with the opportunity to assist clients in developing analytics and technology solutions that help them detect, monitor, and predict risk. Our team helps business leaders protect and manage their risk related to information regarding technology, people, systems, processes, culture, and physical surroundings. We help clients’ understand their current capability and develop a plan to target cyber security investment, helping to respond to actual cyber incidents, and advising on legal issues related to breaches, data privacy, and protection.

About your manager

Lubomír has over 10 years of cyber security experience with different projects related to mainly cyber security managed services, building and operating Security Operation Centers (SOC), Threat Hunting & Intelligence and Vulnerability Management. Lubomír is also responsible for team leading, project management, designing of cyber security managed services, creation and development of training plans and is a proven coach to train other team members or client staff. Outside of work, he is involved in teaching, science, and research at the University of Hradec Králové, where he guarantees and teaches the course Surveillance Systems and Forensic Analysis.

Job description & summary

  • Own the technical vision, engineering standards and delivery methodology for Google SecOps services and implementations.
  • Act as the technical lead for complex SIEM, EDR and Threat Detection & Response transformation programmes.
  • Define reference architectures, onboarding frameworks, engineering accelerators and reusable deployment patterns.
  • Lead complex Google SecOps implementation, migration and managed services onboarding projects.
  • Drive adoption of Infrastructure-as-Code, CI/CD and automation practices across security engineering teams.
  • Review and approve technical designs, implementation approaches and engineering deliverables.
  • Mentor and develop security engineers through technical leadership and knowledge sharing.
  • Serve as a technical escalation point for Google SecOps-related challenges and strategic client engagements.
  • Support recruitment, capability development and growth of the Google SecOps engineering team.
  • Collaborate with technology vendors, internal stakeholders and clients to shape future service offerings.

What matters to us

  • 6+ years of experience delivering cybersecurity engineering, implementation or consulting projects, supported by an educational background in Information Technology, Cybersecurity or a related technical field.
  • Proven experience leading complex SIEM implementations, migrations or security transformation programmes.
  • Hands-on experience with Google SecOps and practical knowledge of other enterprise SIEM platforms such as Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, QRadar or Elastic.
  • Experience designing enterprise-scale security monitoring architectures and working with Infrastructure-as-Code and DevOps tooling such as Terraform, GitHub, GitHub Actions or Azure DevOps or similar platforms.
  • Experience building reusable engineering frameworks, CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment processes, as well as leading engineering teams or technical workstreams.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management and presentation skills, with English at C1 level or higher.

What will help you stand out

  • Experience building or scaling a Google SecOps practice or security engineering capability.
  • Google Cloud, Google SecOps or other industry-recognized cybersecurity certifications.
  • Experience working directly with Google or other strategic technology partners.
  • Experience supporting presales activities, solution shaping and large-scale proposals.
  • Experience defining engineering governance, technical standards and quality assurance processes.
  • Experience managing or mentoring multiple engineers across several concurrent engagements.

Why you’ll enjoy working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.
  • A flexible benefits programme with 55,000 points to spend on what matters most to you.
  • 30 days of paid time of, including three well-being days and two additional days off at the end of the year.
  • An opportunity to give back to the community with one paid volunteering day every year.
  • The chance to work from one of PwC’s international offices (available from the Senior Associate level).
  • We support your learning and development journey: We offer training in business skills (such as presentation and communication skills, assertiveness, and giving feedback) as well as digital skills (including Alteryx, Power BI, and AI). Our technical training will help you understand the full deals transaction lifecycle and how to present our findings to clients in a clear and impactful way. We also cover the cost of professional certifications, such as GIAC/SANS, Microsoft, Splunk, Google, OffSec, ISC2).
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.
  • Social events and Away Days.

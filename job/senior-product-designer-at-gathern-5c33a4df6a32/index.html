About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

About us:

At Gathern, we’re not just a platform, we’re the homegrown Saudi success story that built and leads the alternative hospitality sector across the Kingdom. As the largest peer-to-peer vacation rental marketplace in Saudi Arabia, Gathern enables travelers to explore the country through authentic stays hosted by local residents while directly supporting Saudi Vision 2030 by boosting tourism, empowering communities, and expanding accommodation supply.

Backed by our SAR 270 million Series B funding round, led by Sanabil Investments (PIF-owned) and valuing Gathern at over SAR 1 billion! We’re entering an exciting new chapter of growth, innovation, and regional expansion as we prepare for a future Tadawul listing.

With 5M+ users, guests from 150+ nationalities, a network of 72,000+ hospitality units, and more than SAR 2 billion paid to 33,000+ Saudi hosts, Gathern stands as one of the fastest-growing tech companies in the Kingdom, holding a 44% national market share and 53% in Riyadh!

This is your opportunity to join a company that’s redefining travel and shaping the future of tourism in Saudi Arabia!

We are looking for an experienced Senior Product Designer with a strong ability to uncover complex user problems and translate them into simple, intuitive, and visually engaging digital experiences. The ideal candidate combines strong user research, product thinking, and design craftsmanship with a data-driven approach to improving customer journeys and delivering measurable business outcomes.

You will play a key role in shaping the digital experiences of our guests and hosts, contributing to our Arabic-first design system, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to build products that deliver meaningful and lasting value.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Own the end-to-end product design process, from problem discovery and user research to prototyping, validation, and implementation.
  • Conduct user research through interviews, field studies, behavioral analysis, and customer feedback to uncover unmet needs and identify product opportunities.
  • Translate research findings and customer insights into actionable design solutions and evidence-based product decisions.
  • Design intuitive, visually engaging, and accessible experiences that simplify complex user journeys and improve customer satisfaction.
  • Optimize key product journeys, including search, listing pages, and checkout, to improve conversion rates and reduce friction.
  • Develop and maintain scalable design systems, ensuring consistency, accessibility, and reusability across products and teams.
  • Contribute to Arabic-first product experiences, ensuring natural Arabic content, effective right-to-left (RTL) layouts, and culturally relevant design patterns.
  • Collaborate with Product Managers, Engineers, and Product Analytics teams to define success metrics, validate design hypotheses, and measure the impact of design decisions through A/B testing and experimentation.
  • Design experiences that encourage customer engagement, retention, and repeat product usage.
  • Leverage AI-powered tools throughout the design process to improve research, ideation, prototyping, and validation without compromising design quality or user trust.
  • Mentor and support other Product Designers through constructive feedback, design critiques, and knowledge-sharing.
  • Continuously evaluate product performance and identify opportunities to improve usability, design quality, and overall customer experience.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 4+ years of professional experience in Product Design, UX Design, or related roles, with demonstrated ownership of end-to-end digital product experiences.
  • A strong portfolio showcasing problem-solving capabilities, user-centered design, design craftsmanship, and measurable product outcomes.
  • Proven experience conducting user research, identifying underlying customer needs, and translating insights into effective product solutions.
  • Strong expertise in interaction design, information architecture, user flows, wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing.
  • Advanced proficiency in Figma or equivalent product design and prototyping tools.
  • Experience building, maintaining, and scaling design systems, including reusable components, design patterns, and accessibility standards.
  • Strong understanding of Arabic-first product design, Arabic UX writing, localization, and right-to-left (RTL) interfaces.
  • Experience leveraging product analytics, A/B testing, and experimentation to validate design decisions and improve product performance.
  • Practical experience integrating AI-powered tools into product design workflows.
  • Strong understanding of customer journeys, conversion optimization, and user engagement.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to articulate design decisions and influence cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Experience mentoring designers and contributing to a collaborative, high-quality design culture.

Benefits

  • USD salary — stable, competitive compensation regardless of currency fluctuations.
  • Comprehensive medical insurance provided to support your wellbeing.
  • Fully remote work from Egypt with flexible hours — build your routine your way.
  • Fast career growth in a company scaling rapidly across Saudi Arabia and the region.

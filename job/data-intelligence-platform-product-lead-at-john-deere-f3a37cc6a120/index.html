Your Responsibilities

As a Data Intelligence Platform Product Lead for JD World Headquarters located in Moline, IL, you will be:

  • Own the product roadmap for a geospatial data platform that ingests satellite, aerial, and drone imagery, 3D terrain, hydrology, soils, and weather data, and fuses it with proprietary machine-generated ground truth.
  • Evaluate and manage a portfolio of commercial and government imagery/data vendors — running competitive bake-offs, and continuously improving the cost/quality tradeoff as the remote-sensing and sensor market evolves (e.g., new spectral bands, radar, hyperspectral, space-borne LiDAR).
  • Lead migration of teams currently running their own bespoke pipelines onto the shared platform — balancing platform reliability guarantees with the pace of onboarding new consumers.
  • Partner with engineering, data science, and remote-sensing specialists to translate platform capabilities into services that both internal builders and downstream product teams can consume with production-grade reliability.
  • Manage dependencies with adjacent data platforms (e.g., a machine-data/records platform and a farm-record/analytics service) whose roadmaps depend on this platform's outputs, and vice versa.
  • Define and track platform adoption, reliability, and cost metrics; represent the platform in cross-functional planning with the product teams it powers.
  • Develop the long-term vision for the platform as a shared utility used in both experimentation/R&D contexts and live production products.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT Available for this position.

What Skills You Need

  • Significant product management experience, including platform or infrastructure product management (not only feature/app-level PM experience).
  • Demonstrated experience managing external data or technology vendor relationships, including contract negotiation and vendor performance management.
  • Working fluency in geospatial or remote-sensing concepts (satellite/aerial imagery, terrain models, weather data integration) sufficient to make roadmap and vendor tradeoff decisions — deep technical specialization is not required, but credibility with technical specialists is.
  • Track record of driving migration/consolidation of fragmented internal systems onto a shared platform, including stakeholder alignment and change management.
  • Strong cross-functional collaboration skills — this role sits at the intersection of multiple dependent teams and must manage roadmap interlocks without direct authority over those teams.
  • Experience defining and operating against platform reliability/SLA-style commitments rather than one-off project delivery.

What Makes You Standout

  • Experience in agriculture, construction, or another physical/outdoor-industry technology context.
  • Familiarity with geospatial data standards and tooling (e.g., hexagonal spatial indexing systems, raster/vector pipelines).
  • Experience supporting AI/agentic products that consume platform data via APIs or MCP-style interfaces.
  • Background partnering with data science or remote-sensing engineering teams.

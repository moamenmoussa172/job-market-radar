Product Sales Engineer - Direct Electric Drives

SAP S/4HANA

About the job

The Product Sales Engineer is responsible for representing and supporting the commercial/technical introduction and promotional efforts of the Thunder Direct Electric Drive product line and services to our customers. The Product Sales Engineer supports field sales engineers and aftermarket sales associates by focusing on engagement with OEM & end user accounts to define correct application requirements and best technical solution to meet or exceed customer expectations. The Product Sales Engineer will also collaborate with counterparts in Sweden, Germany, and China to ensure a common global sales approach is done in a consistent and agreed method. The Product Sales Engineer will define target market segments to focus on, key application niches (e.g., bulk material handling, mining, pulp and paper, marine, recycling, shredders, and processing machinery), and focus OEM and end-user target accounts to target together with the US sales team.

The Product Sales Engineer should have strong communication skills and the desire to present the value of the Hägglunds Direct Electric Drive solutions to customers, both technically and commercially. This role will have regular interactions with customers during in person visits, at trade exhibitions, and during meetings at our Grove City, OH & Houston, TX facilities.

Requires ability to travel up to 30% domestically with occasional international trips, and visit customer locations which may require safety screening and wearing of PPE to gain access.

Product Sales Support:

  • Develop and implement best technical sales strategies to support sales targets and expand the customer base in order to achieve above market profitable growth for the Hägglunds Thunder product range.
  • Identify new business opportunities and develop plans to capitalize on them.
  • Monitor competitive market trends and activities to identify new opportunities and threats together with US Sales team.
  • Analyze sales data and performance metrics to assess the effectiveness of sales strategies and identify areas for improvement.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with key customers to ensure outstanding customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  • Collaborate with internal departments to align sales efforts with overall business goals.

Customer Experience:

  • Continuously work to improve customer journey and experience with Hägglunds Thunder Direct Drive products and support team.
  • Ensure customer requirements are fulfilled through supplying of value adding application presentations and clear technical information.

Collaborate and Coordinate:

  • Collaborate and Coordinate with local Hägglunds Management team & Hägglunds global team to optimize product portfolio and build application use cases to meet agreed sales targets.

Leadership:

  • Provide technical and application guidance, training, and support to the outside sales team to support revenue and market share growth.
  • Highlight areas for improvement in product design which could leverage additional sales growth.
  • Support innovation culture that facilitates conversion of ideas into solutions and products.

Education:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Industrial Technology, or a related technical discipline. Equivalent relevant industry experience will also be considered.

Experience:

  • 5 or more years of progressive experience in business development, technical sales, or application engineering within industrial automation, heavy industrial equipment, or power transmission.
  • Demonstrated track record of introducing new industrial products or technologies to the US market with successful sales outcomes.
  • In-depth knowledge of electric drive technology (variable frequency drives and motors), i.e. design, application, market, industry and customer base.
  • Specific experience in application of permanent magnet (PM) synchronous motors, would be considered a plus
  • Familiarity with heavy industrial applications (e.g., feeders, conveyors, kilns, crushers, mixers, winches) and severe-duty environment requirements.
  • Ability to develop and drive short- and long-term strategies to achieve growth objectives.
  • Strong negotiation and problem solving skills required.
  • Demonstrated experience understanding the customers unmet needs to develop a winning strategy with innovative solutions.
  • Demonstrated ability to build multi-level customer relationships (from plant maintenance and mechanical engineers to C-level decision-makers) using a value-based, solution-selling methodology.
  • Excellent presentation skills required – deliver professional content with ability to explain complex electrical and mechanical concepts simply and persuasively.
  • Must be highly organized and able to handle numerous projects, initiatives and issues simultaneously.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Experience in use of CRM system, and tracking progress of opportunities towards achieving sales targets
  • Experience with SAP or similar ERP system considered a plus
  • Experience with selling products via distribution partners or systems integrators considered a plus

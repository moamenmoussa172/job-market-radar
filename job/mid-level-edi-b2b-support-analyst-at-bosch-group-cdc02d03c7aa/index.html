MID-LEVEL EDI B2B SUPPORT ANALYST

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

About the job

  • Provide technical expertise and consulting support for Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) and other B2B communication processes.
    Ensure business continuity and high-quality service for both internal stakeholders and external trading partners.
  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Act as the primary point of contact for B2B customers, suppliers, and Bosch plants, ensuring support and stability of EDI processes.
  • Provide technical support, perform testing, monitor integrations, and manage Trading Partners.
  • Manage data interfaces, identify and resolve incidents, and ensure operational continuity.
  • Create and maintain documentation, procedures, training materials, and guidelines related to EDI processes.
  • Deliver training and provide support to suppliers and business partners through multiple service channels.
  • Collaborate with global teams, supporting projects, go-live activities, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Contribute to innovation, process automation, and the adoption of Artificial Intelligence solutions, with a strong focus on service excellence and customer satisfaction.

#LI-MM1

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent higher education qualification.
  • Advanced English proficiency (required).
  • Hands-on experience providing EDI support, including testing, monitoring, and troubleshooting system issues.
  • Experience in Trading Partner management, including partner setup, configuration, integration, and maintenance.
  • Experience supporting EDI systems, including incident resolution, documentation maintenance, and ensuring compliance with established standards, guidelines, and procedures.
  • Mandatory Skills
  • Knowledge of Jira.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Familiarity with Artificial Intelligence tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or similar solutions.
  • Knowledge of SAP R/3 IDoc structures and formats.
  • Knowledge of EDI Standards ANSI X12, EDIFACT, ODETTE e RND
  • Knowledge of communication and integration protocols, including SFTP, AS2, OFTP2, RESTful APIs, and others.
  • Knowledge of additional languages, such as Spanish, German, or Japanese.
  • Behavioral Competencies
  • Ability to work effectively in a global B2B environment, collaborating with teams and business partners across different countries and cultures.
  • Strong teamwork and collaboration skills, with a cooperative and supportive mindset.
  • Strong negotiation skills to effectively engage with suppliers and align EDI-related requirements.
  • Organized, analytical, and detail-oriented, with a structured approach to executing tasks and solving problems.

Applicants must submit their resume/CV in English!

About the job

We are hiring a Security Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Manager to lead the team that keeps Canonical's security and compliance credible to the Enterprises, Clouds, Telcos and Governments that run on Ubuntu, and to bring our controls and risk management to the standard a public company requires.

Location: EMEA or Americas — fully remote (home-based), with the option to work from our London office.

About our Team

Canonical's customers trust Ubuntu with critical systems. That trust rests on evidence: our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certifications, how we manage risk, and how we respond as security regulation such as the EU Cyber Resilience Act reaches open source software. The Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) team produces that evidence and makes it real across the company. As Canonical prepares to list on a public exchange, the team is also central to the controls and risk reporting our Audit Committee and Board rely on.

The team is part of Operations and reports to the Chief Operating Officer. It covers both sides of the discipline: keeping us certified and compliant today, and looking ahead at risk across corporate IT, infrastructure and products. It works closely with Engineering, Security Operations, Information Systems, Legal, Finance and Sales.

You will lead the team, set its priorities and operating rhythm, and be the first escalation point for security and compliance decisions.

What you'll do

  • Lead and develop a team of GRC engineers: hire, coach and set priorities, and run a roadmap that balances planned work with operational requests, so the team delivers predictably and reports progress clearly to leadership. You own the team’s budget, vendors and tooling.
  • Own our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certifications end to end: audit readiness, evidence collection, auditor relationships and fieldwork, so certifications are renewed on time with clean outcomes.
  • Run the risk management framework across corporate IT, infrastructure and products — asset classification, risk assessments, the risk register and key risk indicators — so leadership and the Board can see and decide on the company’s most important risks.
  • Own the information security policy framework — annual reviews, new policies, exceptions and risk acceptance — and make the call, or escalate it, when a policy and a business need conflict.
  • Partner with Finance on IT General Controls and internal controls over financial reporting — remediation, control execution and Audit Committee reporting — to make Canonical ready for a public listing.
  • Turn security regulation and standards — the EU Cyber Resilience Act, NIS2, DORA, the EU AI Act and IEC 62443 — into practical programmes with Legal and Engineering, including our secure software development lifecycle and penetration testing, so our products meet their obligations as they ship.
  • Build business continuity and disaster recovery with Information Systems and business owners — impact analysis, plans and tested recovery — so critical services can be restored when it matters.
  • Oversee third-party risk, access reviews and the governance of new tools and AI usage, and support customers and colleagues when it counts — security questionnaires, RFPs, our Trust Center, contract security terms and incident response — alongside Sales, Legal and Security Operations.

What we are looking for in you

  • Hands-on ownership of ISO 27001 and/or SOC 2 certification through external audit cycles
  • Experience designing or running a risk management framework (such as ISO 27005, NIST CSF or ISO 31000), including a risk register and reporting to senior leadership
  • Experience with IT General Controls supporting financial audits, under SOX or an equivalent regime
  • Experience leading a governance, risk and compliance, security compliance or IT risk team in a software or technology company
  • Experience turning regulatory and technical requirements into controls that engineering and business teams you don’t manage actually adopt
  • Sound judgement on risk acceptance and policy exceptions, and the confidence to hold a position with senior stakeholders
  • Passion for technology evidenced by personal projects and initiatives
  • Exceptional academic track record from both high school and university
  • Professional written and spoken English with excellent presentation skills
  • Ability to travel internationally twice a year for company events up to two weeks long

Nice-to-have skills

  • Experience preparing a company for a public listing
  • Experience with product security compliance for a software vendor (EU Cyber Resilience Act, IEC 62443 or NIST SSDF)
  • Business continuity and disaster recovery programme experience
  • Professional certifications such as CISSP, CISM, CISA or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor or Lead Implementer

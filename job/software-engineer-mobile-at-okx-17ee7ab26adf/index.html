About the job

OKX will be prioritising applicants who have a current right to work in Hong Kong, and do not require OKX's sponsorship of a visa. Please note that Hong Kong is a group-level service hub, and OKX does not carry on a business of operating a virtual asset trading platform in Hong Kong.

Who We Are

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er.

OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

About The Opportunity

We are looking for talented, impact-driven Mobile Engineers to join our engineering organization in Hong Kong. In this role, you will build and scale high-performance, resilient native mobile applications that power OKX’s core product ecosystems—ranging from high-concurrency payment and trading engines to user acquisition, engagement, and product growth systems. You will work on cross-functional engineering challenges at global scale, partnering closely with product design, backend architecture, security, and regional teams.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Build & Scale Native Mobile Apps: Design, develop, and maintain modular, reliable native mobile features (iOS or Android) used by millions of global users.
  • Architect Core Systems: Drive technical decision-making on mobile architecture, API design, performance optimization, and offline/network resiliency.
  • Collaborate Cross-Functionally: Partner closely with Product Management, Design, and Backend Engineering to translate complex user and commercial requirements into intuitive, reliable mobile experiences.
  • Ensure Quality & Rigor: Write clean, testable, and maintainable code while maintaining high engineering standards for code reviews, testing, security, and monitoring.
  • Integrate AI Workflows: Apply AI coding tools actively in your workflow—not only for code generation, but also to build and maintain repeatable personal AI development workflows.
  • Modernize Engineering Tooling: Leverage modern development tools, automated CI/CD pipelines, and productivity workflows to maintain rapid iteration speeds.

What We Look For In You

  • Solid software engineering fundamentals with hands-on experience building, shipping, or maintaining production native mobile applications (iOS with Swift/Objective-C or Android with Kotlin/Java).
  • Strong understanding of mobile system design, clean architecture, memory management, testing, and performance tuning.
  • Ability to actively apply AI coding tools in your workflow and continuously optimize personal engineering productivity.
  • Problem-solving mindset with the ability to take ownership of end-to-end features and drive execution independently.

Nice-To-Haves

  • Experience in fintech, crypto, digital payments, high-concurrency consumer apps, or growth systems.
  • Familiarity with mobile architecture patterns, CI/CD pipelines, experimentation frameworks, or attribution tools.

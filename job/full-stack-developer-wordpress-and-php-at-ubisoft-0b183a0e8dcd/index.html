About the job

Develop a range of internal products with an emphasis on user interface design, interactivity, and backend operations. Design, maintain, and enhance software applications and systems while ensuring code quality, robust performance, thorough documentation, and ongoing maintenance.

What you'll do

  • Contribute to coding and delivering feature components while actively participating in team efforts.
  • Provide clear and structured reporting to project and team leads, planning tasks to meet requirements for performance, maintenance, modularity, scalability, and compatibility.
  • Complete assignments within project quality standards and deadlines, participating in peer reviews, testing, debugging, profiling, and maintaining existing code.
  • Share knowledge and expertise with team members to foster collective growth.
  • Collaborate with professionals from various disciplines, such as product owners, team and tech leads, business partners, and UX teams to achieve shared development goals.
  • Proactively raise and address issues, contributing to the resolution of challenges that may arise during the development process.
  • Work within an Agile (scrum) methodology.
  • Suggest improvements to applications, processes, and technologies.
  • Continuously seek opportunities to expand your skills and knowledge.
  • Adhere to and enhance current development processes.

Education:

  • Strong proficiency in PHP, with:
  • 3+ years of hands‑on experience building object‑oriented applications (PHP7+)
  • Solid understanding of MVC concepts (Laravel, Symfony, or similar frameworks is a plus)
  • Front-end expertise:
  • Advanced HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript knowledge
  • Ability to create mobile‑responsive, accessible, high‑quality UI
  • Experience with Angular, including component design and state management; React (ReactJS) is a plus
  • Familiarity with modern design tools such as Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD, including interpreting design specs and ensuring accurate implementation
  • Familiarity WordPress development, including:
  • Custom theme and plugin development
  • Experience ensuring accessibility, UI responsiveness, and performance
  • Knowledge of WordPress ecosystem, codex, and best practices
  • Experience developing and consuming RESTful APIs
  • Strong understanding of MySQL (query optimization)
  • Good understanding of web performance, security, SEO basics, caching strategies, and accessibility standards (WCAG)
  • Experience with version control systems (GIT) - nice to have
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and modern DevOps practices – nice to have
  • Experience with unit testing and code quality tools is a plus
  • Ability to take full ownership of features—from architecture to deployment
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem‑solving capabilities
  • Solid understanding of product development lifecycle
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken
  • Familiarity with Scrum/sprint structure and proficiency in using communication tools.
  • Awareness of the multi-disciplinary environment and project milestone dependencies.
  • Ability to comprehend team and project dynamics and contribute effectively (Experience collaborating with product managers, project managers, and designers).

Work experience required

  • Experience as Full stack developer (WordPress + PHP + modern JS frameworks)
  • A strong knowledge of the IT product development lifecycle and experience of agile project management methodologies
  • A proficient ability to use various IT software, such as Jira, Confluence, Microsoft, Miro
  • Fluency in English both written and oral. [French is not required, but a plus.]

Personal skills

  • Creative, curious, and innovative: suggest improvements and offer alternative solutions.
  • Asks in-depth questions, detects unstated assumptions, and resolves conflicts.
  • Demonstrates attention to detail and strong time-management skills; able to detect hidden problems, prioritize, track, and manage resolution of multiple issues and risks.
  • Communicates effectively and works well within a team with a positive approach.
  • Enthusiastic about learning and adopting new technologies.
  • Completes duties on time while maintaining a high-quality service.
  • Exhibits creativity, proactivity, analytical thinking, and strong problem-solving skills.
  • Adapts easily to changes and proposals, leading with diplomacy.
  • Functions effectively in a fast-paced environment and manages multiple concurrent projects.
  • Maintains a customer-centric and results-oriented mindset.
  • Persevering, autonomous, takes initiative, and is self-motivated.

About the job

Meet the Team

Join the field of Network Technology within our R&D department which contributes to the evolution of the next generation of leading networking products. We are building firmware, SDK, simulators, and compilers for Cisco Silicon One™ architecture and work in developing pioneering technology.

The team is working on the new generation of silicon, which will be part of all Cisco device families and drive the future of the Internet.

This is an opportunity to work with worldwide distributed Cisco R&D centers and learn from the best engineers in the field.

We are looking for people who are in love with technology and engineering to help us push the limits of what is possible through innovation, determination, and partnership.

Your Impact

  • Collect, process, and analyze large-scale datasets to drive data-informed decision-making.
  • Apply advanced statistical methodologies to interpret complex data, identify trends, and uncover actionable patterns.
  • Architect and maintain data pipelines to ensure high-quality data availability.
  • Develop intuitive visual representations, dashboards, and automated reports to communicate complex insights to multi-functional collaborators.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate data analytics into development workflows.

Minimum Qualifications

  • BSc/MSc in Computer Science, Mathematics or equivalent
  • 4+ years of professional experience in data engineering or advanced analytics
  • Advanced SQL skills
  • Ability to quickly learn new data and data systems
  • Experience in working with multiple data sources, defining data collection and creating dashboards
  • Experience in data visualization: Grafana, Tableau or similar tools
  • Coding skills in any of the following – Python, R, C/C++ or Java

Preferred Qualifications

  • Software development experience
  • Experience with log-based analytics tools
  • Experience on No-SQL systems.
  • Experience with Jira and Jira plugins
  • Experience with AI-driven analytics

Deputy Manager_eAxle System Architect

Bosch Group · Bengaluru, India

Onsite · Senior · Software / Solutions Architect · Posted 24 November 2026

System design

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Responsible for eAxle system architecture, system layout and technical coordination of customer and internal requirements across power electronics, e-machine, transmission, cooling, software and vehicle interfaces
  • Develop and maintain comprehensive system architecture documentation, including functional decomposition, interface definitions and system specifications
  • Translate customer needs and business requirements into detailed system and subsystem requirements with full traceability
  • Define system architecture concepts, functional decomposition strategies and interface management frameworks
  • Coordinate cross-functional technical decisions across all subsystems and conduct maturity reviews to ensure programme alignment
  • Perform trade-off analyses, change-impact assessments and technical-risk evaluations to support decision-making
  • Create technical customer document (TCD), interface matrices and system verification strategies
  • Establish and oversee system verification and validation plans, ensuring compliance with V-model and ASPICE methodologies
  • Communicate complex system topics effectively with global teams, internal stakeholders and OEM customers
  • Manage stakeholder engagement and requirement traceability throughout the product development lifecycle
  • Provide technical guidance to suppliers and vendors on system integration and interface requirements
  • Demonstrate advanced knowledge of eAxle architecture and system cause-effect relationships
  • Apply advanced knowledge of V-model, ASPICE, requirements engineering and system verification practices
  • Maintain strong knowledge of power electronics, e-machine, transmission, thermal systems and vehicle interfaces
  • Apply working knowledge of functional safety, cybersecurity, EMC and NVH considerations in system design

**Required Qualifications:**

  • Bachelor or Master degree in Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics or Automotive Engineering
  • More than 10 years of experience in automotive system development
  • More than 5 years of experience in electrified powertrain or eAxle system engineering
  • Demonstrated expertise in system architecture design and functional decomposition
  • Proven experience with requirements engineering, traceability management and system specifications
  • Strong knowledge of V-model and ASPICE methodologies in automotive development
  • Experience leading cross-functional technical teams and managing stakeholder engagement
  • Proficiency in creating technical coordination documents, interface matrices and system verification strategies

**Preferred Qualifications:**

  • Advanced knowledge of power electronics, electric machine design, transmission systems and thermal management
  • Experience with functional safety (ISO 26262) and cybersecurity standards in automotive applications
  • Familiarity with electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and noise, vibration and harshness (NVH) considerations
  • Track record of managing supplier and vendor technical integration on complex automotive programmes
  • Experience with vehicle interface requirements and system-level integration challenges
  • Excellent communication skills in presenting complex technical concepts to global teams and OEM customers
  • Background in change-impact assessment and technical risk evaluation methodologies

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

About the Role

As an Engineer, Enterprise Platforms, you will be a hands-on owner of the collaboration tools our employees use all day. You will administer, automate, and improve them, working alongside identity, endpoints, and the wider SaaS stack.

This is a role for someone who sees a problem, figures out the fix, and ships it. Our team is small and the scope is broad. You will have real ownership early, a lot of autonomy, and few layers between you and the people you support. You will turn policies, runbooks, and employee needs into reliable configurations, scripts, and self-service experiences. We use AI tools where they make us faster or more accurate, and we expect good judgment about when to trust them.

This role reflects Abnormal VOICE values. You move with velocity, design for long-term ownership and governance, use data with intellectual honesty, stay close to customer needs, and aim for excellence in reliability and simplicity.

What You Will Do

  • Administer Google Workspace: users, groups, org units, shared drives, Drive sharing and DLP, Gmail routing and policies, Calendar and room resources, Meet, and audit and security investigation tooling.
  • Administer Slack (Enterprise Grid): workspaces, channels, user groups, permissions, app approvals, Slack Connect, retention, export and eDiscovery, workflows, and audit logs.
  • Build and maintain automation for joiner, mover, and leaver flows, access requests, deprovisioning, and common employee needs, using SCIM, APIs, webhooks, Apps Script, and scripting.
  • Work with identity (Okta SSO, MFA, group-based access) so Google and Slack access follows least privilege and birthright rules.
  • Support endpoints and other SaaS platforms as needed, including macOS device management and long-tail SaaS administration.
  • Create self-service experiences, runbooks, and enablement content so employees can resolve common needs without escalation.
  • Participate in Tier 2/3 incidents, perform root cause analysis, and feed what you learn back into automation, runbooks, and standards.
  • Add logging, dashboards, and reporting so changes are monitored and audit questions can be answered quickly.
  • Use AI tools where they help (documentation, troubleshooting, request triage, analysis), with human review and appropriate access controls.
  • Partner with Security, GRC, Legal, People, Finance, and IT Service Delivery to deliver changes safely.
  • Triage incoming requests and incidents by impact and urgency, route them to the right owner or queue, and resolve or escalate within agreed response targets.
  • Turn vague or incomplete requests into clear requirements: ask clarifying questions, identify stakeholders and constraints, and confirm the desired outcome before building.
  • Set clear expectations with employees and stakeholders about scope, timelines, and tradeoffs. Give status updates proactively, and flag risks or delays early.

Governance and Compliance

  • Define and enforce standards for external sharing, Slack Connect, guest access, and third-party and OAuth app approval in Google Workspace and Slack.
  • Run regular access and license reviews, using systems and audit logs to find unused, orphaned, or over-privileged access.
  • Keep configuration baselines documented, changes tracked in ServiceNow, and evidence ready for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and FedRAMP audits.
  • Review audit logs and investigate suspicious activity with Security.
  • Document requirements, risks, and approvals for changes so they pass change review and can be audited later.

Scripting and Automation

  • Automate first. If a task happens more than a couple of times, you script it, build a workflow, or turn it into self-service.
  • Write scripts and tools (Python, Apps Script, and shell) against the Google Workspace, Slack, and Okta APIs.
  • Build and maintain integrations and workflows in Workato, connecting identity, HR, collaboration, ticketing, and SaaS systems.
  • Use Torii workflows to automate app discovery, onboarding and offboarding, and license reclamation.
  • Build ServiceNow catalog items and flows so requests are routed, approved, and fulfilled with minimal manual touch.
  • Keep scripts and configuration in version control, with logging, error handling, and clear documentation so others can run and maintain them.

Our Tooling

  • Collaboration: Google Workspace, Slack (Enterprise Grid)
  • Identity: Okta
  • Automation and integration: Workato, Torii, Python, Apps Script
  • Service management: ServiceNow
  • Endpoints and other: Jamf, Zoom, and a long tail of SaaS applications

You don't need experience with every tool. Strong experience with comparable platforms, and a record of learning new ones quickly, is what matters.

How We Work

  • Automation-first: We remove repetitive work rather than absorbing it, and we measure success by manual touches eliminated.
  • Customer-first: Employees are our customers. We respond with urgency, communicate clearly, and fix root causes so the same problem doesn't come back. We do what we say we'll do, or we tell people early when that changes.
  • Fast-paced: Priorities shift quickly and requests arrive from every direction. We move fast, ship in small steps, and iterate.
  • Time management and triage: You juggle incidents, requests, and project work. You sort what comes in by impact and urgency, handle what's urgent first, and protect focused time for project work and for the root-cause fixes that prevent tomorrow's tickets.

What We're Looking For

  • You find the problem before someone assigns it, and you follow through to a documented, working fix.
  • You are comfortable with ambiguity: unclear requirements, incomplete documentation, and shifting priorities don't stop you.
  • You prioritize well when everything feels urgent. You set expectations early and honestly: you say no or "not yet" clearly and kindly, and you explain the reason.
  • You learn new platforms quickly and teach what you learn.
  • You ask the right questions up front: who needs this, what problem it solves, what 'done' looks like, and what could go wrong. You propose a recommended approach rather than waiting to be told.
  • You communicate proactively: status updates, change notices, and honest post-incident write-ups.

Must Haves

  • 2+ years of experience in IT engineering, systems administration, or a similar role supporting business-critical technology.
  • Hands-on administration of Google Workspace (Admin console, groups, Drive and sharing controls, Gmail, Calendar).
  • Hands-on administration of Slack (workspace and channel management, user groups, app management, permissions). Enterprise Grid experience is a strong plus.
  • Working knowledge of identity and access concepts: SSO, MFA, least privilege, directory and group management, and SCIM or standards-based provisioning.
  • Ability to script and integrate systems using APIs, webhooks, Apps Script, Python, or similar tooling.
  • Experience with incident and change management, troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and documented follow-through.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, including documenting technical processes and explaining complex systems to non-technical audiences.
  • A track record of working independently and taking ownership without close direction.
  • Experience writing scripts or building workflows that automate repetitive IT work, and a habit of automating before doing something manually a third time.
  • A customer-first approach to supporting employees: responsive, clear, and focused on fixing root causes.
  • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and manage your time across incidents, requests, and project work, prioritizing by impact.
  • Ability to set and manage expectations with stakeholders, including saying no or negotiating scope and timelines while keeping trust.

About this Role

We are seeking a Connectivity Product Manager to own and evolve strategic connectivity platforms within Vodafone Cloud & Infrastructure. The role focuses on driving the product strategy, roadmap and lifecycle management of FlowDB and GIANT, ensuring alignment with connectivity strategy, customer requirements and technology priorities. Working closely with architecture, engineering, operations and delivery teams, you will translate business and technical needs into prioritised product outcomes, support investment decisions, and help deliver scalable, automated and customer-centric platform capabilities. This role offers the opportunity to influence product direction across an international environment while driving adoption, value realisation and continuous improvement.

What you’ll do

  • Own the product strategy, roadmap and lifecycle management for FlowDB and GIANT products within the connectivity portfolio.
  • Translate connectivity strategy, customer requirements and business needs into clearly prioritised product initiatives and investment opportunities.
  • Manage product demand, backlog prioritisation and roadmap delivery across multiple stakeholders and teams.
  • Collaborate with product owners, network architects, engineering, operations and delivery teams to define and enhance product capabilities.
  • Support business case creation, funding approvals, go-to-market activities and communication plans for product initiatives.
  • Drive simplification, standardisation and automation initiatives across the product lifecycle.
  • Monitor product performance, adoption, customer feedback and value delivery through measurable business and technical KPIs.
  • Manage product risks, dependencies and lifecycle decisions while supporting Agile and SAFe ways of working.

Who you are

  • Experience working within Product Management in connectivity, telecommunications, network infrastructure, cloud infrastructure or technology platform environments.
  • Strong knowledge of product roadmap development, demand management, prioritisation and portfolio management.
  • Experience working in Agile environments, including Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) practices
  • Good understanding of telecom and network services, cloud technologies and supporting infrastructure platforms.
  • Ability to translate technical requirements into customer and business-focused product outcomes.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement, communication and influencing skills with experience collaborating across international teams and markets.
  • Experience working with architecture, engineering, operations and delivery functions in complex technology environments.

About Landmark

Landmark, a Halliburton business line, provides the industry’s most comprehensive suite of digital solutions for exploration, drilling, and production optimization. Its software and data platforms empower customers to model subsurface assets, manage drilling risk, and accelerate decision-making through cloud, AI, and advanced analytics.

About the Role

You will lead small projects that turn exploration and production (E&P) data into models that energy teams can use. You will work independently to develop and evaluate models, from deciding what data you need to putting a model into use.

You will work with domain experts to interpret data, with developers to integrate models into software, and with business stakeholders to explain what the results mean. This is a technical leadership role without direct reports.

Key responsibilities
• Lead technical tasks and small projects from problem definition through model deployment. Work with domain experts to define the question, the data needed and how the results will be used.
• Acquire, clean, integrate and validate structured and unstructured data from multiple sources.
• Maintain definitions, metadata and data dictionaries so others can trace and reuse the data.
• Explore datasets and develop features that capture patterns relevant to the problem.
• Independently build, evaluate and validate statistical, machine-learning or other data-driven models.
• Deploy models using established platforms or off-the-shelf software. Partner with developers when integration requires additional support.
• Explain model assumptions, limitations and business implications to technical and nontechnical audiences.
• Document pipelines, modeling methods and deployment steps so teams can reproduce and maintain the work.
• Improve team workflows and help shape technical priorities. Share findings through publications, patent activities and industry events.

Qualifications

Required
• Undergraduate degree in a science, technology, engineering or mathematics (STEM) discipline.
• Four or more years of related experience in data science, data engineering, analytics, machine learning or technical modeling.
• Experience independently developing and evaluating data-driven, statistical or machine-learning models.
• Demonstrated experience acquiring, cleaning, integrating and organizing data from multiple sources.
• Ability to lead technical tasks or small projects independently while collaborating with domain experts, developers and other stakeholders.
• Experience implementing or supporting analytical model deployment using established platforms or software solutions.

Preferred
• Certification in agentic tool development.
• Petroleum engineering experience or experience with energy, E&P, subsurface, drilling, well or production data.
• Master’s degree in a STEM discipline.
• Experience with Python, SQL, data-processing libraries, statistical modeling or machine-learning frameworks.
• Familiarity with cloud data platforms, model lifecycle management, version control or continuous integration and delivery (CI/CD).
• Experience preparing technical publications, invention disclosures, patent materials or industry presentations.

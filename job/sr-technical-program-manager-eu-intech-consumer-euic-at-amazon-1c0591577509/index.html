About the job

At Amazon, we are committed to being the Earth’s most customer-centric company. The International Technology group (INTech) leads the enhancement and delivery of Amazon’s state of the art engineering to all the varied customers and cultures of the world. We do this through a combination of partnerships with other Amazon technical teams and our own innovative new projects.
The team, mainly located in Madrid Technical Hub comprises Software Developer and ML Engineers, Applied Scientists, Product Managers, Technical Product Managers and UX Designers who are experts on several areas of ranking, computer vision, recommendations systems, Search as well as CX. Are you interested on how the experiences that fuel Catalog and Search are built to scale to customers WW? Are interesting on how we use state of the art AI to generate and provide the most relevant content?
A Sr Technical Program Manager (TPM) will partner with software engineers, applied scientists, Product Managers, and senior management to help create world-class solutions. You will own the cross-team plan for a portfolio of fast-moving GenAI programs: driving roadmap and dependency management, running experiments, and keeping many parallel stakeholders aligned across the stores, partner tech orgs, and multiple regions. We're looking for people who are passionate about innovating on behalf of customers, demonstrate a high degree of product and technical ownership, and want to have fun while they make history. The ideal TPM is a creative, demonstrated leader with analytical abilities and an expeditious drive to deliver results. This opportunity requires deep technical, problem-solving, and communication skills, including the ability to understand ML and GenAI systems well enough to challenge trade-offs with scientists and engineers.
Key job responsibilities
Engineering and science execution: Drive crisp, timely execution of GenAI and media content programs, prioritized by customer impact and business opportunity.
Priority management: Manage diverse requests and dependencies from stores and partner tech teams worldwide. Help leadership make explicit trade-offs.
Process improvements: Define, implement, and continuously improve how the team delivers and operates. This includes planning, mechanisms, experiments tracking, and efficiency of GenAI workloads.
Stakeholder management: Interface with and influence stakeholders across engineering, applied science, product, and store teams. Balance business needs against technical capabilities, and bring clarity to ambiguous situations.
Operational excellence: Monitor metrics and program health, anticipate and clear blockers, and manage escalations.
- Experience in technical product or program management
- Experience working directly with engineering teams
- Experience in software development
- Experience in technical program management working directly with software engineering teams
- Experience managing programs across cross functional teams, building processes and coordinating release schedules
- Experience in project management disciplines including scope, schedule, budget, quality, along with risk and critical path management
- Experience managing projects across cross functional teams, building sustainable processes and coordinating release schedules
- Experience defining KPI's/SLA's used to drive multi-million dollar businesses and reporting to senior leadership

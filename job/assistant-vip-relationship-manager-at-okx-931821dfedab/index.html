Assistant VIP Relationship Manager

OKX · Remote

Fully remote · Account Manager · Posted 26 November 2026

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Who We Are

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er. OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

About the Opportunity

We are looking for a proactive, highly organised, commercially minded and client-focused Assistant VIP Relationship Manager to join our Poland team.

In this role, you will support the VIP Relationship Management team in delivering an exceptional experience to high-value clients, including active traders, high-net-worth individuals, family offices, proprietary trading desks and corporate clients.

Beyond day-to-day client servicing, we are looking for someone who can recognise business opportunities through conversations with VIP clients, understand their needs and identify ways to grow their relationship with OKX.

You should be naturally curious, exceptionally organised, take ownership of client requests and understand how strong relationships can translate into long-term business growth.

What You’ll Be Doing

Client Support and Relationship Management

  • Support VIP Relationship Managers in managing, developing and retaining a portfolio of VIP clients.
  • Build strong relationships with clients and develop an understanding of their trading behaviour, needs and preferences.
  • Assist with onboarding, KYC documentation, account setup and ongoing client servicing.
  • Respond promptly to client queries, take ownership of issues and coordinate with internal teams until resolution.
  • Maintain accurate and well-organised client records, interaction notes and follow-up actions.
  • Proactively engage with clients rather than simply responding to incoming requests.
  • Ensure client requests and commitments are followed up on in a timely manner.

Commercial Opportunities and Client Growth

  • Recognise commercial opportunities through everyday conversations with VIP clients.
  • Identify opportunities to increase trading activity, Assets Under Management (AUM) and adoption of OKX products based on client needs.
  • Understand where clients trade outside OKX, which products they use and what could encourage them to consolidate more of their activity with us.
  • Identify potential VIP prospects, referral opportunities and new business opportunities.
  • Translate client conversations and feedback into actionable commercial insights.
  • Maintain a structured overview of commercial opportunities and ensure consistent follow-up.
  • Support the execution of acquisition, engagement, retention and reactivation initiatives.
  • Participate in local VIP events, client meetings and partnership activities.

Product and Market Knowledge

  • Develop a strong understanding of OKX products, including spot trading, derivatives, Earn, staking and fiat products.
  • Understand how different products can address the needs of different types of VIP clients.
  • Gather meaningful client feedback and identify opportunities for product improvements.
  • Stay informed about market developments, competitor offerings and industry trends relevant to VIP clients in Poland and the wider CEE region.

Compliance and Operations

  • Support adherence to internal policies and regulatory requirements, including KYC, AML and client due diligence processes.
  • Escalate potential compliance, operational or client-risk matters promptly.
  • Work closely with operations, compliance, legal and product teams to ensure a smooth client experience.
  • Take ownership of operational tasks and ensure consistent follow-up and high-quality client support.
  • Manage multiple ongoing cases efficiently, ensuring no client request or outstanding action is overlooked.

What We Look For In You

Core Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Finance or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Fluency in Polish and English; additional CEE languages are an advantage.
  • Strong interest in cryptocurrency, financial markets, trading and fintech.
  • Living in Poland is preferred, but not a requirement.
  • A strong understanding of the Polish market and willingness to travel for client meetings and events.

Experience and Skills

  • 1–3 years of experience in client services, account management, business development, sales or operations within crypto, fintech, trading, banking or wealth management.
  • Experience working with high-value or professional clients is preferred.
  • Strong commercial awareness and the ability to identify business opportunities naturally during client conversations.
  • Ability to ask the right questions, understand client needs and turn insights into actionable opportunities.
  • Exceptional organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities, client requests and deadlines simultaneously.
  • Ability to work in a structured and disciplined manner, ensuring consistent follow-ups without needing reminders.
  • Good understanding of cryptocurrency products and trading platforms, or the ability to develop this knowledge quickly.
  • Comfortable working with data, CRM systems and performance dashboards.

Personal Attributes

  • Exceptionally organised, structured and detail-oriented.
  • Naturally curious, proactive and commercially minded.
  • Strong communication, active listening and relationship-building skills.
  • A strong sense of ownership, accountability and responsibility.
  • Able to prioritise tasks based on urgency, client needs and business impact.
  • Able to balance operational responsibilities with commercial opportunities.
  • Comfortable initiating conversations and identifying opportunities without being overly sales-driven.
  • Self-motivated and able to work independently in a fast-paced, remote environment.
  • High standards of integrity and a strong awareness of compliance requirements.

Nice to Haves

  • Our Ideal Candidate We are looking for someone who is exceptionally organised, commercially minded and proactive. Someone who sees opportunities where others might see a simple client request, understands the bigger picture and consistently follows through on commitments without needing to be reminded. Someone who does not simply wait for instructions but takes initiative, identifies what needs to be done and constantly looks for ways to deliver more value to both our VIP clients and OKX.

Perks & Benefits

  • Competitive total compensation package
  • Various team building programs and company events
  • More that we love to tell you along the process!

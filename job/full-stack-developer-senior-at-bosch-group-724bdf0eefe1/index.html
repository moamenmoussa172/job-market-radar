About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

About Us

We develop digital platforms, web applications, and intelligent software solutions that support users and business processes across the Bosch Group. As part of an entrepreneurial and globally operating team, we combine modern software engineering, cloud-native technologies, user-centric design, and AI capabilities to create innovative products with real business impact.

Our products range from scalable business platforms and workflow applications to digital marketplaces, collaboration tools, and customer-facing solutions used across a variety of business domains.

We are continuously expanding our portfolio and looking for talented engineers who enjoy building high-quality software end-to-end and shaping the next generation of digital solutions.

Your Mission

You will help shape the next generation of digital products by designing and developing scalable web applications, digital services, platform capabilities, and AI-powered solutions. From architecture and software engineering to user experience and system integration, you will contribute to products used across the Bosch Group.

Your Responsibilities

  • Plan, develop, and operate modern web platforms, digital services, and software products
  • Build scalable frontend and backend applications using modern technologies and engineering practices
  • Develop reusable, responsive, and accessible user interfaces
  • Design modular, distributed, non-blocking, and event-driven system architectures
  • Develop AI-powered features, applications, and Agentic AI capabilities
  • Build intelligent workflows and automation solutions that create measurable value for users
  • Develop dashboards, workflow applications, and intuitive user experiences
  • Develop APIs and integration layers for internal and external systems
  • Integrate databases, message-driven systems, cloud services, and AI capabilities
  • Design and optimize data models, search capabilities, and retrieval solutions
  • Implement reliable state management, observability, logging, and error handling
  • Ensure performance, scalability, maintainability, security, and software quality
  • Collaborate with software engineers, product owners, designers, and AI specialists
  • Contribute to architecture decisions, technical concepts, code reviews, and continuous product improvement

Your Profile

  • 5+ years of professional experience in full-stack software development
  • Experience with TypeScript and Python is mandatory for the role
  • Experience building and operating production-grade web applications and digital platforms
  • Strong expertise in frontend and backend development
  • Experience designing scalable and maintainable software architectures
  • Solid understanding of databases, APIs, and distributed systems
  • Experience with cloud-native technologies and modern software engineering practices
  • Experience with AI-powered applications, intelligent workflows, or LLM-based solutions is a plus
  • Strong analytical, conceptual, and problem-solving skills
  • High attention to detail and software quality
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • Strong documentation habits and experience working in distributed teams
  • Independent, structured, and responsible working style
  • Fluent English; German is a plus

Technology Landscape

You will work with a modern technology landscape that includes technologies such as:

  • TypeScript, React, Next.js, and modern UI frameworks
  • Node.js, Python, REST APIs, GraphQL, oRPC, and modern integration patterns
  • PostgreSQL, Redis, OpenSearch, and event-driven architectures
  • NATS and asynchronous communication patterns
  • Docker, Kubernetes, Microsoft Azure, and Infrastructure as Code
  • Microsoft AI Foundry, LLM technologies, and intelligent automation solutions

Engineering & Collaboration

  • Strong engineering mindset with a focus on maintainability, documentation, code reviews, and continuous improvement
  • Ability to balance long-term architecture with pragmatic delivery
  • Experience working effectively in collaborative and distributed teams

Education

  • Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a comparable field
  • Equivalent professional experience is also welcome

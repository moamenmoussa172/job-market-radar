About the job

Synopsis

The Product Owner is responsible for delivering customer-facing digital experiences across Etihad’s website and digital products, translating product strategy and customer/business needs into a prioritized product backlog. The role leads day-to-day delivery across key digital journeys such as booking, manage booking, check-in, ancillaries, servicing and customer accounts, while coordinating business, UX, engineering, architecture and analytics teams. Strong web/digital product experience, ideally within airline, travel, aviation or e-commerce, is essential.

Accountabilities

  • Own and prioritize the backlog against strategy and roadmap.
  • Define features, user stories, functional requirements, and acceptance criteria.
  • Align priorities, KPIs and outcomes with Product Managers.
  • Lead refinement ; participate in PI Planning and Scrum ceremonies.
  • Partner with UX, engineering, architecture, and business on scalable experiences.
  • Deliver end-to-end website and airline digital journeys.
  • Clarify requirements and priorities as the delivery team’s product contact.
  • Accept functionality against requirements and Definition of Done; support UAT.
  • Manage dependencies, risks, and cross-team impacts; escalate blockers.
  • Use feedback, analytics, operational insights and KPIs to optimize journeys.
  • Ensure consistent journeys across channels and platforms.
  • Support releases, monitor performance and prioritize defects and improvements.

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor’s in Computing, Engineering, Information Systems, Business, Digital or related field.
  • 5+ years’ relevant experience ; 3+ in Product Owner, Digital Product, or similar roles.
  • Proven delivery of large-scale transactional websites or e-commerce journeys.
  • Airline, aviation, travel, hospitality, or loyalty experience strongly preferred.
  • Strong backlog, user story, acceptance criteria, and value-based prioritization skills.
  • Understanding of APIs, web platforms, CMS, analytics, and third-party integrations.
  • Hands-on Jira, Confluence, SAFe and Scrum experience.
  • Analytical, customer-focused, and data-led decision-making skills.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and cross-functional collaboration.
  • CSPO, PSPO, SAFe PO/PM or equivalent certification advantageous.

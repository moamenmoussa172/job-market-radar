About the job

About Ledger

We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger.

At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries.

The team:

As a Staff Product Manager on Ledger Wallet, you own a full product domain end to end, for example Earn or Swap/Buy/Sell. You are accountable for its vision, roadmap, adoption and business results.

We are looking for a strong, autonomous PM. You will turn a complex and regulated space into simple, safe experiences that users trust and come back to, and that grow Ledger's revenue.

You lead a cross-functional squad of engineers, designers and a delivery manager, working closely with Data, Business Development, Legal, Compliance, Marketing and external partners.

What you’ll be doing:

  • Own your domain's vision and roadmap. Define where the domain goes next and turn it into a clear, prioritized quarterly plan that balances growth, platform scalability and user trust.
  • Grow adoption and revenue. Understand your funnel inside out, remove friction, and move the metrics that matter: conversion, retention, volume and revenue.
  • Decide with data. Use Mixpanel and our analytics stack to define KPIs, find drop-offs, run experiments and prioritize on evidence, not opinion.
  • Know your users and market. Stay close to users through research and feedback, and track competitors so Ledger stays the most relevant and safest option.
  • Ship end to end. Write sharp PRDs, take initiatives through our product gates within quarterly planning, and lead your squad from discovery to launch and iteration.
  • Communicate with clarity. Produce decision-ready roadmaps, vision docs and dashboards that leadership and partners can act on without further editing.
  • Work with partners. Evaluate, integrate and manage third-party providers, and shape commercial and rev-share models with Business Development.
  • Navigate regulation with Legal and Compliance. Build products that are compliant by design across markets, and turn constraints into clear product decisions.
  • Shape strategy beyond your domain. Contribute to the Ledger Wallet vision and annual roadmap, and connect your domain to the rest of the product: onboarding, engagement, device and money movement.
  • Lead cross-squad initiatives. Drive complex, multi-team bets end to end, aligning dependencies, trade-offs and timelines across squads and business lines.

What we’re looking for:

  • Data-driven. You are at ease with data analysis and bring an approach that is both business- and user-centric: numbers guide your decisions, and you never lose sight of the user behind them.
  • Problem solving. You understand a problem in depth before solving it. You dig into root causes, challenge assumptions and frame the right question before jumping to solutions.
  • Leadership. You bring teams with you, act proactively and focus on impact. You know how to defend a product vision, including in front of business stakeholders.
  • Autonomy from discovery to delivery. You own your scope end to end, from framing the opportunity to shipping and iterating, without hand-holding.
  • At least 8 years of product management experience, ideally on consumer products at a scale-up or high-growth tech company.
  • Proven staff-level impact: you have led cross-team initiatives and influenced strategy beyond your own scope.
  • A track record of owning a product domain end to end, with measurable impact on adoption, retention or revenue.
  • Experience on transactional or financial products (fintech, payments, trading, banking or crypto) is a strong plus.
  • Comfort working with external partners and in regulated environments.
  • Fluent English; French is a plus.

We’re committed to building an inclusive hiring process. If you need any adjustments or accommodations, just let us know, we’ll do our best to support you.

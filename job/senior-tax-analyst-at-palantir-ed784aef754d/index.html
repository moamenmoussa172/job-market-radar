About the job

A World-Changing Company

Palantir builds the world’s leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. By bringing the right data to the people who need it, our platforms empower our partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more.

The Role

Join our dynamic tax department where we pride ourselves on tackling unique and complex tax situations that provide unparalleled growth and experience. The candidate will work alongside a talented tax team facing diverse tax challenges across various jurisdictions, enabling you to expand your expertise and advance your career in an environment that fosters creativity and continuous learning. The ideal candidate will enjoy this challenging environment that provides the opportunity for exposure to the countless interactions of a global finance team operating in today’s rapidly changing environment.

Core Responsibilities

  • Prepare quarterly and annual tax provision calculations including supporting workpapers
  • Work closely with accounting, operational, and other groups to obtain and validate information for income tax provision and return preparation
  • Maintain ONESOURCE Tax Provision module
  • Review stock-based compensation related tax analyses
  • Prepare documentation supporting SOX 4o4 compliance
  • Prepare calculations related to Pillar 2 tax liabilities
  • Assist with annual R&D tax credit study
  • Support tax projects as needed that will be related but not limited to income tax provision, tax returns, and process improvement
  • Effectively communicate with management on complex tax issues as necessary, including impact of any tax reform legislation and/or internal business changes
  • Identify opportunities to improve efficiency, automation, and data quality within tax processes
  • What We Value
  • Strong organizational and time management skills with ability to drive tasks to completion autonomously
  • Strong accounting skills, including ability to collect, prepare and analyze data and information to reconstruct, detect, support, or refute a position and ensure that the explanation provided reconciles with the underlying data
  • Excellent interpersonal, oral, and written communication skills
  • Proficient in Excel and comfortable with data for analysis
  • Experience with OneSource Tax Provision and OneSource Income Tax software is preferred
  • What We Require
  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in accounting or finance; CPA preferred
  • 4+ years of corporate tax experience with a public accounting firm and/or a tax department of a multi-national corporation which involved complex entity structures and tax issues
  • Strong Knowledge and understanding of ASC 740
  • Ability to work onsite at least twice a week

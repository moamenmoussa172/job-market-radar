About the job

The Application Engineer is responsible for airbag electronic control unit (ECU) algorithm application, calibration, validation, and vehicle integration.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Obtain vehicle crash-test data recorded by sensors in a laboratory environment, analyze the data, and prepare it for inclusion in a crash database used for simulation.
  • Configure and calibrate software parameters to achieve airbag deployment performance that meets documented customer and regulatory requirements while evaluating algorithm behavior and technical trade-offs.
  • Perform calibration using crash-simulation tools such as MATLAB and Simulink.
  • Validate calibration results through simulation and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing.
  • Present calibration and deployment-performance results to customers and internal stakeholders.
  • Maintain calibration performance for series production in accordance with applicable customer, safety, quality, and governmental requirements.
  • Collaborate with customers to determine suitable locations for the airbag ECU and peripheral sensors based on signal dynamics, packaging, and environmental considerations.
  • Lead customer-specific feature and algorithm development activities through the V-model in coordination with local and global teams.
  • Develop, execute, and analyze test cases in algorithm requirements and system test plans to verify that requirements are met.
  • Follow applicable standards and procedures, including requirements associated with the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS).
  • Use and support the continued development of engineering tools; working knowledge of MATLAB and Excel macros is required.
  • Serve as the technical interface for calibration activities with customers and internal project teams, including evaluating and discussing customer requirements, participating in meetings, and providing ongoing technical support.
  • Support project estimates during the acquisition phase.
  • Support planning and management of application resources for customer projects.
  • Create and maintain project timing plans, track deliverables, and complete project documentation in accordance with established global processes and methods.
  • Collaborate effectively within an international network of cross-functional team members.
  • Travel domestically or internationally on an occasional basis, as required to perform the essential functions of the position.
  • Produce accurate, high-quality work in a safety-critical environment and support continuous improvement through global collaboration and standardized processes.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, automotive engineering, mechatronics engineering, computer science, physics, or a related technical field.
  • At least 3 years of technical experience in the automotive industry.
  • At least 1 year of experience in signal processing or data analysis, software development, or systems calibration.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Demonstrated ability to organize and analyze technical information and produce accurate, high-quality work.
  • Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams.
  • Effective written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical information and support customer discussions.
  • Experience using MATLAB and Excel macros.
  • Experience supporting automotive original equipment manufacturer (OEM) customers.
  • Experience with algorithm development processes.
  • Ability to interpret system-level functional requirements and expected system behavior.
  • Knowledge of physical systems and sensing principles.

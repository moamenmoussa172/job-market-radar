About the job

Amazon's Global Procurement Organization is on a mission to transform how procurement works at Amazon scale. We build the technology that powers billions of dollars in purchasing decisions. We're looking for an Data Engineer to deliver data driven solutions focused on procurement of non-inventory supplies across Amazon's global operations. This is a high-impact role at the intersection of technology, operations, and business strategy — where your work directly influences how Amazon procures and manages supplies at scale.
Data is at the heart of procurement. Do you want to make that data AI-ready — powering not just insight but autonomous decision-making and action at a global scale? As a Data Engineer on the Global Procurement Technology team, you will build the data foundation that drives AI transformation across Amazon's procurement organization. You will design and deliver data solutions end to end: aggregate contract and spend data, creating entity relationships with suppliers and contract, map core entities to taxonomies to allow for insight and recommendations worldwide.
Your work will directly shape how procurement teams make decisions and how AI applications consume and act on trusted supplier data. You will partner with software engineers, scientists, business intelligence engineers, and business stakeholders to turn complex, multi-source datasets into reliable data products. If you enjoy solving ambiguous data problems and want your solutions to influence procurement strategy globally, we want to hear from you.
- Experience with data modeling, warehousing and building ETL pipelines
- Experience with SQL
- Experience as a Data Engineer or in a similar role
- Experience with AWS technologies like Redshift, S3, AWS Glue, EMR, Kinesis, FireHose, Lambda, and IAM roles and permissions
- Experience with non-relational databases / data stores (object storage, document or key-value stores, graph databases, column-family databases)

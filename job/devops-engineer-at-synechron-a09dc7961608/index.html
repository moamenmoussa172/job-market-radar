About the job

At Synechron, we harness the power of digital transformation to drive business success. As a global consulting firm, we combine creativity with advanced technology to deliver innovative solutions across industries. Recognized with multiple employer awards, we are committed to building strong expert teams and a collaborative, high-impact work environment.
We are hiring a DevOps Engineer
Hybrid working model – Novi Sad or Belgrade

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, deploy, and operate scalable cloud infrastructure for business-critical applications
  • Own and continuously improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes using tools such as Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and related technologies
  • Build, maintain, and optimize Kubernetes environments supporting both internal platforms and client-facing applications
  • Develop and manage Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and DevOps best practices
  • Create automation tools and scripts using Bash and Python to improve platform efficiency and reliability
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize infrastructure, applications, and distributed systems across multiple environments
  • Ensure high availability, scalability, security, and performance of cloud platforms and services
  • Lead root cause analysis and resolution of complex production incidents
  • Collaborate closely with Development, Security, Architecture, and Infrastructure teams to deliver robust solutions
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives related to platform engineering, automation, standardization, and operational excellence
  • Participate in infrastructure design decisions and contribute to long-term cloud and platform strategy

Your Profile

  • 5+ years of experience in DevOps
  • Strong hands-on experience with cloud services such as AWS or Azure and cloud-native architecture principles
  • Advanced experience with Infrastructure as Code, particularly Terraform
  • Extensive experience deploying and managing Kubernetes-based environments
  • Strong containerization expertise using Docker
  • Proven experience designing, implementing, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
  • Strong scripting and automation skills using Bash and Python
  • Experience with monitoring, logging, observability, and alerting solutions
  • Solid understanding of Linux systems administration and networking fundamentals
  • Experience supporting production environments and mission-critical systems
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills across distributed systems
  • Ability to take ownership of infrastructure and platform services from design through production operations
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills in cross-functional and international teams

Nice to Have

  • Experience with GitHub Actions, ArgoCD, or similar deployment tools
  • Exposure to security best practices and DevSecOps methodologies
  • Experience with multi-account AWS environments and large-scale cloud platforms
  • Knowledge of database administration, performance tuning, and disaster recovery concepts
  • Experience mentoring engineers and promoting DevOps best practices across teams

What’s in it for you:

  • Competitive compensation package based on experience and skills
  • Flexible hours and hybrid work options, allowing you to achieve a work-life balance that suits your needs
  • Private health insurance plan
  • Access to continuous training, accredited certifications, and a range of resources to help you grow professionally
  • Personalized leadership training program
  • FitPass membership to support your well-being and mental health support programs
  • Comprehensive Referral Rewards Program

For any additional information or questions, feel free to reach out to us at Recruitment-Serbia@synechron.com.

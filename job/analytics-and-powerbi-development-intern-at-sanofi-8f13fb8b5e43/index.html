About the job

Job title: Analytics and Power BI Development Intern

  • Location: Prague, Czech Republic
  • Contract: Dohoda o pracovní činnostii (Minimum 20hrs per week)
  • Internship Duration: One-year program with the possibility of extension for a second year
  • Role Type: Hybrid Role


About the job

As an Analytics & Power BI Development Intern within Engineering, Manufacturing & Supply (EM&S), you will design and develop Power BI reports and dashboards that serve the business community — connecting directly to the Snowflake EM&S Data Foundation as the single source of truth. Your work will help build a unified, scalable analytics ecosystem that drives data-informed decision-making across global manufacturing and supply operations.
About Sanofi:
We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives

Main responsibilities:
PowerBI Report Design & Development

  • Design, build, and publish interactive dashboards and reports tailored to business needs
  • Translate business requirements into clear, user-friendly, and visually compelling data visualizations
  • Ensure reports follow established design standards, branding guidelines, and usability best practices

Snowflake Integration & Data Harmonization

  • Connect Power BI reports to the Snowflake Data Foundation, ensuring data accuracy, consistency, and traceability
  • Collaborate with data engineers and architects to understand data models and leverage available datasets effectively
  • Support the definition and rollout of standardized reporting templates across EM&S functions and sites, identifying opportunities to consolidate existing reports and eliminate redundancies
  • Ensure alignment with global KPIs and metrics frameworks
  • Contribute to documentation of data sources, transformations, and report logic

Stakeholder Collaboration

  • Engage with business stakeholders to gather requirements, present prototypes, and incorporate feedback
  • Participate in team meetings, sprint reviews, and project updates
  • Support change management and adoption of new reports within the team

About you

Education:

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Data Science, Business Intelligence, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field
  • Available for a minimum 1 year internship

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency or strong interest in Microsoft Power BI (report building, DAX, data modeling)
  • Familiarity with SQL for data querying and manipulation
  • Understanding of data visualization principles and best practices
  • Knowledge of or exposure to cloud data platforms (Snowflake, Azure, or similar) is a plus

Soft Skills:

  • Strong analytical mindset with attention to detail
  • Excellent communication skills — ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences
  • Proactive, autonomous, and solution-oriented attitude
  • Collaborative team player, comfortable working in a multicultural environment
  • Fluent in English (working language of the team)

Desired Profile:

We are looking for someone who:

  • Aims Higher — is not satisfied with the status quo and always looks for ways to improve and innovate
  • Acts for Patients — understands that behind every data point is a patient whose life we are working to improve
  • Is Bold — is not afraid to propose new ideas, challenge existing approaches, and take initiative
  • Leads Together — thrives in collaborative environments and builds strong relationships across teams and functions

You are curious, driven, and eager to make a tangible contribution. You see data not just as numbers, but as a powerful tool to drive better decisions and better outcomes.

Why choose us?

  • A meaningful mission with real impact on Sanofi's global manufacturing and supply operations
  • A dynamic and supportive team environment within a world-leading healthcare company
  • Exposure to cutting-edge data and analytics technologies used at enterprise scale
  • The opportunity to work in Prague, one of Europe's most vibrant and culturally rich cities
  • A competitive internship package in line with Sanofi's internship policy
  • The chance to build a professional foundation that could open doors to future opportunities within Sanofi

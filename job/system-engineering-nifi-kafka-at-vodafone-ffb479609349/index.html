About this Role

We are seeking a Big Data System Specialist to design, build and maintain reliable, scalable data movement solutions across batch and real-time streaming environments. This role focuses on ensuring high availability, performance and security of data pipelines, connectors and supporting infrastructure, while providing advanced operational support and guidance to service users across the organisation.

What you’ll do

  • Provide Level 2 and Level 3 support for data movement application clusters, instances and dataflows, managing incidents and problems.
  • Define technical requirements and design, configure and implement data movement solutions at application, operating system and cluster levels, aligned with agreed KPIs and best practices.
  • Develop and maintain data streaming pipelines for data ingestion and output across multiple sources and destinations.
  • Design, configure and implement data connectors in line with business needs.
  • Configure, monitor and maintain infrastructure to improve performance, security, robustness and availability, using proactive monitoring, alerts and notifications.
  • Perform technical investigations and root-cause analysis, ensuring transparency and proposing sustainable solutions.
  • Actively support transformation and technology refresh programmes to improve service availability, performance and quality.
  • Contribute to automation, standardisation and continuous improvement initiatives to simplify recurring tasks.
  • Share knowledge and maintain accurate, detailed documentation and operational runbooks.
  • Participate in on-call and exceptional support activities when required by business needs.
  • Deliver additional business or technical requests as assigned by the direct superior.

Who you are

  • Motivated to learn and continuously develop new technical skills.
  • Comfortable working in remote and distributed teams. Fluent in written and spoken English.
  • Experienced in creating clear, detailed technical documentation.

Technical knowledge and experience:

  • Advanced knowledge of installing, configuring and operating Apache Kafka.
  • Previous experience with UNIX system administration (RHEL or similar).
  • Nice to have previous experience Apache Flink or Apache NiFi.
  • Good understanding of office systems, IT equipment, hardware and software.
  • Knowledge of common cloud services and general security principles.
  • Experience with scripting languages and database is a plus.
  • Familiarity with container orchestration, preferably Kubernetes (nice to have).

Qualifications (nice to have):

  • CCAAK, CTAK or equivalent Apache Kafka certification.

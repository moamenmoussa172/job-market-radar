Your Responsibilities

As a UX Lead for Deere & Company located in Chicago, IL or Moline, IL, you will be:

  • Lead the strategic evolution of Deere's enterprise design system by introducing industry-leading patterns, standards, and best practices.
  • Drive adoption and expansion of a unified design ecosystem across product teams, business capabilities, and enterprise platforms.
  • Champion the responsible adoption and implementation of AI-powered design capabilities that accelerate workflows, improve quality, and maximize business value.
  • Partner closely with researchers, content strategists, designers, software engineers, product managers, and business stakeholders to define scalable, systematic solutions and deliver exceptional experiences.
  • Apply deep expertise in human-centered design, responsive design, systems thinking, visual design, typography, iconography, color theory, accessibility, and layout design.
  • Develop and maintain design libraries, component documentation, governance models, contribution processes, and design standards.
  • Advocate for accessibility, usability, and consistency across all digital experiences while ensuring alignment with Deere Brand Standards.
  • Communicate design rationale effectively through presentations, documentation, workshops, and executive-level discussions.
  • Build strong relationships and credibility across product teams and leadership groups by combining customer insights, industry best practices, and data-driven decision making.
  • Serve as a visible advocate for UX both internally and externally, helping elevate Deere's design maturity and industry presence.
  • Collaborate closely with design system engineering and framework teams to define, prioritize, and implement new capabilities, components, and functionality.
  • Contribute to enterprise-wide UX governance, design reviews, accessibility initiatives, and cross-functional design communities.
  • Support a hybrid work environment and travel as necessary to collaborate with product teams, stakeholders, and customers.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT Available for this position.

What Skills You Need

  • 6 or more years of professional experience establishing, evolving, and maintaining enterprise design systems across multiple products and teams.
  • Portfolio demonstrating excellence in user experience and visual design with a strong emphasis on web and mobile experiences.
  • Demonstrated expertise developing sketches, wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, prototypes, specifications, and supporting documentation.
  • Strong understanding of human-centered design methodologies and modern UX processes.
  • Proven ability to organize complex information into intuitive and scalable design solutions.
  • Demonstrated ability to build trust, foster collaboration, and establish partnerships across multiple teams and organizations.
  • Strong written, verbal, and presentation communication skills with the ability to effectively articulate design rationale to diverse audiences.
  • Experience influencing UX consistency among designers, software engineers, product managers, business stakeholders, and executive leadership.
  • Highly skilled at incorporating AI tools, methods, and technologies into daily design and product workflows.

What Makes You Standout

  • Deep understanding of AI capabilities, limitations, and emerging trends, particularly as they relate to human-centered and machine-centered design practices.
  • Deep understanding of WCAG accessibility standards and inclusive design methodologies.
  • Advanced expertise with atomic design systems, including component architecture, libraries, variables, design tokens, properties, branching, and governance.
  • Experience leading enterprise-wide design system adoption initiatives.
  • Experience presenting at conferences, industry events, design communities, or executive leadership forums.
  • Strong understanding of front-end technologies and close collaboration workflows between design and engineering teams.
  • Experience developing design governance strategies and measuring design system adoption and effectiveness through data and analytics.

Education

Ideally you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

  • Bachelor's degree in Product Design, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Interaction Design, User Experience Design, Graphic Design, or a related field.
  • Master's degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Product Design, Design Strategy, or a related field strongly preferred.

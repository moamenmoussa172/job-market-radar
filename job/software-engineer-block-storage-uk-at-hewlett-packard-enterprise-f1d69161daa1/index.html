Job Description:

Hewlett Packard Enterprise advance the way people live and work. We bring together curious minds to create breakthrough technology solutions, helping our customers make their mark on the world.

The Bristol team are a key part of the global HPE R+D organisation. We work across multiple areas of technology, we create cutting-edge software products, and we want you to be a part of our team.

Our engineers love what they do. They love problem solving and seeking out innovative solutions to a wide variety of problems. Our engineers are great software developers. We are currently in a search for Software Developers to work on our next generation of products.

You’ll be working on some incredibly exciting tech, taking responsibility for design, development, troubleshooting, programming, and integration of some of our cutting-edge products.

What we can offer you:

  • Start-up mentality in our day to day work, coupled with the benefits and opportunities of a leading global technology company
  • The chance to work on our cutting edge technology, making a tangible difference to the products you’re working on
  • Participation in the shared values and purpose that make Hewlett Packard Enterprise one of the world´s most attractive employers

What you’ll bring:

  • Experience in programming languages such as Java, C, C++, Python, or Go
  • Understanding of Software development processes and practice
  • A desire to embrace latest AI technology and apply it to enhance our development lifecycle
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • An innate curiosity, and thirst for knowledge
  • Ability to work effectively as an individual, as part of a local team, and as part of a global development community
  • Experience of Linux systems an advantage

Education and Experience Required:

  • Bachelors or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent.
  • This role will suit individuals from new graduate or up to 2 years of experience

This is a fantastic opportunity to engage in a positive and creative work environment that offers excellent benefits and a flexible work schedule.

If you have a track record in and a passion for software engineering, want to be part of a business that is growing—with great opportunities, and are ready for your next role, then please apply today.

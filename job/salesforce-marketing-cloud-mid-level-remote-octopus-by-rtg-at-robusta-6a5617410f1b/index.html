About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in India.

We are looking for a Salesforce Marketing Cloud Specialist with 3+ years of hands-on experience working with Salesforce Marketing Cloud. The ideal candidate should have strong experience building customer journeys, creating dynamic content, integrating SFMC with third-party systems, and working within Agile environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and build customer journeys using Journey Builder and Automation Studio.
  • Develop and manage automated marketing campaigns and customer journeys.
  • Create dynamic and interactive content to deliver personalized customer experiences.
  • Integrate Salesforce Marketing Cloud with third-party systems and external platforms.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver scalable marketing solutions.
  • Participate in Agile practices, including Scrum and Scaled Agile methodologies.
  • Troubleshoot issues and ensure the reliability and effectiveness of Marketing Cloud solutions.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience working with Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
  • Hands-on experience with Marketing Cloud modules and capabilities.
  • Strong experience with Journey Builder and Automation Studio.
  • Experience creating dynamic and interactive content.
  • Experience integrating SFMC with third-party systems.
  • Experience working in Agile environments, including Scrum and Scaled Agile.

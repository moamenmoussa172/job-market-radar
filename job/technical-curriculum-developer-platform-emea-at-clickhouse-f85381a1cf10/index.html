About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France, Germany, Netherlands, Spain, United Kingdom and Europe.

The Learning Team at ClickHouse is growing fast as more and more users discover the power of ClickHouse and its diverse use cases. As part of that growth, we want to ensure our hands-on training experience is best in class.

We are looking for someone to accelerate the way we build technical content and to bring AI-powered tools and agents directly into the learning experience for our students. You will own the infrastructure that powers releases, certification exams, hands-on courses, and learning environments. You will also lead GenAI initiatives to create content more effectively and to build a broader AI-powered user experience.

A typical day as a ClickHouse Technical Curriculum Developer, Platform:

  • Manage the infrastructure that supports our Content as Code
  • New content PRs are thoroughly tested and easy to preview
  • Merged PRs are automatically released
  • Courses are periodically checked to ensure they are working properly
  • Courses are easily updated with new features
  • Lead the Learning team’s adoption of AI and GenAI:
  • build tools, prompts, and workflows to go from topic to course faster
  • Design and build AI-powered agents and tools for students
  • Evaluate new AI/GenAI capabilities and models as they emerge
  • Write and maintain provisioning and grading scripts for hands-on certification exams
  • Debugging issues that affect learners, instructors, and exam candidates
  • Building and running observability for everything learning: usage, failures, catalog health, dashboards, and alerts
  • Building and running analytics for user activity across all learning tools
  • Design and maintain hands-on lab environments across all training formats

Requirements:

  • 5+ years of experience with technical curriculum development on databases and/or distributed systems
  • 3+ years of experience in a technical education infrastructure or DevOps role (e.g. building and operating lab or sandbox environments)
  • Experience with machine learning applications and/or GenAI focused on content generation, developer tooling, and AI agents
  • 3+ years of experience building certification infrastructure including grading scripts for hands-on assessments
  • Strong scripting and automation skills, including experience with infrastructure as code, CI/CD pipelines, and GitHub
  • A self-motivated attitude that provides you with the desire and determination to complete projects in a fast-paced startup environment
  • Ability to learn new technologies quickly and effectively
  • Excellent communication skills, including the ability to write clear runbooks and technical documentation
  • You have a high level of responsibility, ownership, and accountability

Bonus points:

  • Experience with SQL
  • Experience with ClickHouse database
  • Experience with one or more Cloud Service Providers such as AWS, GCP, or Azure
  • Ability to work cross-functionally amongst engineering, support, product, and development teams
  • Technical degree (e.g., Computer Science or related field)

You'll love this role if:

  • You thrive working at the intersection of engineering, AI, and education, building systems that directly impact how people learn
  • You are self-driven, curious, and eager to continuously learn and grow
  • You possess a mindset of teamwork, global engagement, empathy, and a drive for solving challenging problems

Perks

  • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
  • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
  • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity.
  • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries.
  • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee.
  • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

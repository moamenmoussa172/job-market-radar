About the job

Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet.

The Team:

Our team, Agentic Customer Journey, is part of the Chief Customer Officer (CCO) organization. We are rebuilding how Celonis wins, delivers and grows customer relationships with AI agents. We design the methods and frameworks behind our value journey, from first discovery to realized value. Then we turn them into AI solutions that our Value Engineering and Services teams use every day. We work closely with Product, Engineering and senior leadership. We are expert generalists: equally comfortable building an agent workflow, structuring a value framework and presenting to executives.

The Role:

You will build AI solutions that automate and improve steps of our customer journey, plus the methodology that makes them work. You will take a use case from problem to working prototype: understand how teams work today, define the target process, build the solution, test it with real users and measure the impact. You will work with go-to-market (GTM) teams (Sales, Value Engineering, Services) and Product & Engineering.

The work you’ll do:

  • Design, build and improve AI agents and assistants (LLM-based workflows, prompt chains, tool integrations). They will automate steps such as account research, meeting preparation, business case creation and value tracking.
  • Connect these solutions to our business systems (e.g. Salesforce, Slack, Google Workspace, Monday.com) through APIs and integrations.
  • Test solutions with end users, collect feedback, and measure adoption and time saved.
  • Turn our value methodologies (e.g. Discover & Build, value selling, value realization) into structured, repeatable workflows that AI can run.
  • Develop and refine methods, frameworks and playbooks along the value journey, and find where AI adds the most value.
  • Run return-on-investment (ROI) analyses and build business cases, both for customers and for our own AI solutions.
  • Document solutions and roll them out globally, including training materials for users.
  • Analyze business metrics and present insights to leadership.

The qualifications you need:

  • Student of computer science, business informatics / information systems, data science, industrial engineering, business administration, economics or a comparable program.
  • Hands-on experience building with large language models (LLMs), such as prompt engineering, AI agents, LLM APIs or agent frameworks. You can show it through projects, coursework or a portfolio.
  • Programming skills in Python (or similar) and knowledge of SQL (Structured Query Language).
  • Ability to break unclear business problems down into clear processes and frameworks.
  • Business and process understanding plus financial literacy (e.g. you have built a business case).
  • Fluent in written and spoken English (any additional language is a plus).
  • Preferred: experience with APIs, workflow automation tools (e.g. n8n, Zapier, Make) or low-code platforms.
  • Preferred: first experience in consulting, value engineering, product management or a startup.
  • Available from January 2027 for at least 5 months, full-time.

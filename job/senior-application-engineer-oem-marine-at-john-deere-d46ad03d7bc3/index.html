Your Responsibilities

As a Senior Application Engineer, OEM/Marine for JD Waterloo Product Engineering Center located in Waterloo, IA, you will be:

  • Support the integration of John Deere engines into industrial and marine OEM applications by developing and maintaining Application Guidelines, technical requirements, and review criteria for distributors, customers, and internal teams.
  • Review customer applications, evaluate technical risk, interpret engine and application data, and recommend solutions that support reliable performance and proper engine installation.
  • Develop application-specific requirements and technical guidance for areas such as engine performance, reliability ratings, engine braking, cooling, intake and exhaust systems, aftertreatment, and controls.
  • Provide technical support to distributors, dealers, and OEM customers, including field testing, sea trials, application reviews, and resolution of complex product or installation issues.
  • Work across engineering, sales, product support, and customer-facing teams to resolve issues, improve application guidance, and capture reusable technical knowledge.

VISA Sponsorship is NOT Available for this position.

What Skills You Need

  • Experience in engineering or technical roles involving diesel engines, vehicle applications, marine applications, or off-highway equipment
  • Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret technical data, assess risk, and develop practical solutions
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to create technical requirements, guidelines, or procedures
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively across engineering, sales, distributor, and customer teams

What Skills Make You Standout

  • Experience with engine application engineering, application reviews, technical publications, or customer/distributor support
  • Experience integrating diesel engines, aftertreatment, cooling, intake/exhaust, controls, or related systems into industrial or marine equipment
  • Experience with field testing, data analysis, validation, reliability, durability, or Continuous Improvement

Education

Ideally you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

  • 4 year Bachelor Engineering degree or related fields.

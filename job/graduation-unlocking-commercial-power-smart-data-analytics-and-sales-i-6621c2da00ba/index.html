About the job

Job Title

Graduation: Unlocking commercial power, smart data analytics & sales insights

Job Description

Assignment type: Graduation

Start date: February 2027

Assignment duration: 6 months

Location: Veghel

Education level: Bachelor / Master

Desired study: Data science, business analytics, industrial engineering, business IT & management, technical business administration, computer science

Language: Dutch / English

Description of assignment

Would you like to play a key role in strengthening our global sales organization through structured knowledge management, automation, and data-driven insights? At our headquarters in Veghel, we are looking for a proactive Data Analyst intern to sit at the heart of our worldwide sales operations.

In today’s competitive market, Sales Engineers must quickly identify relevant reference projects to prove technical feasibility, speed, and credibility to prospective clients. In this strategic role, you will take ownership of the Sales Reference Portal within the Distribution domain. You will integrate fragmented project databases into one intuitive environment, develop automated workflows via Power Automate, and build dynamic Power BI dashboards. By bridging the gap between sales support, data analytics, AI, and commercial process optimization, your work will directly boost the speed and winning chance of global sales proposals.

Department description

You will join the Sales Support Office, centrally positioned within the global sales organization for the Distribution domain at our Veghel headquarters.

Our department enables the global sales community with aligned content, commercial data calculations, Life Cycle assessments, AI, and interactive visualization tools. You will collaborate closely with international colleagues based in North America, India, and Barcelona. The primary focus of your assignment is leveraging Power BI, AI features, and workflow automation to optimize commercial decision-making.

Tasks/responsibilities

  • Build and expand a centralized, searchable Sales Reference Portal for the global Distribution domain
  • Consolidate and structure existing project databases into a unified data landscape
  • Create automated workflows (e.g., using Power Automate) to automatically capture data when new projects are secured
  • Design advanced filtering logic and interactive Power BI dashboards based on parameters like country, solution type, throughput, and equipment
  • Explore AI capabilities to optimize data accessibility and search experience for sales teams worldwide
  • Act as process owner and data moderator, ensuring high data quality and seamless alignment between technical and commercial teams

Your profile

  • Analytical mindset with affinity for structured data handling, process optimization, and database architecture
  • Experience with or strong interest in Power BI, Power Automate, SharePoint, or low-code automation platforms
  • Proactive personality who enjoys collaborating with cross-cultural teams across the globe
  • Good communication skills in English (Dutch is a plus)
  • *Mandatory enrolment to a Dutch Education System & resident of The Netherlands

What we offer

  • A monthly internship allowance (based on a 40-hour work week):
    Bachelor: €500 per month
    Master: €550 per month
  • Full commuting allowance, including public transport, depending on whether you have access to free public transport or not
  • On-site health center with gym
  • Active employee communities and initiatives
  • Excellent company restaurant and barista coffee bar

Contact

Do you recognize yourself in this challenging profile? Are you looking for an internship in our organization? Please fill out the application form and upload your resume and cover letter. For more information, contact us by e-mail: internship@vanderlande.com.

About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

In this role, you will play a key part in refining the Sales Compensation Systems to better serve our growing global sales team. We are eager to have someone who can enhance our processes and reporting to ensure everything runs smoothly and accurately while providing excellent support to our sales leaders. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and enjoy working on automation and scalability, this position could offer you the challenge and collaboration you’re looking for.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Implement data validation processes that ensure the integrity of sales metrics. Work closely with IT during Xactly User Acceptance Testing to identify and address any data discrepancies.
  • Maintain thorough documentation for data sources and integrity protocols, and regularly test compensation plans to guarantee accuracy.
  • Find opportunities for system improvements and work closely with IT and key partners to implement technologies that enhance compensation processes
  • Develop and maintain reporting that tracks and analyzes commission payouts and quota attainment to align sales behavior with company revenue goals

What You Bring

  • 5+ years with sales compensation analysis
  • 3+ years experience with Xactly Incent and Analytics commission software or similar commission applications, plus a working experience with Workday, ServiceNow, and SalesForce
  • Proficiency in data validation processes
  • Knowledge of compensation plan testing methodologies and experience performing UAT
  • Ability to maintain detailed documentation for data sources
  • Experience with automation tools for process enhancement
  • Skills in reporting and analytics related to sales compensation
  • Executed multiple compensation plan tests with a high degree of accuracy and reliability
  • Enhanced scalability of sales compensation systems to support a growing global team
  • Strong proficiency with Excel and attention to detail

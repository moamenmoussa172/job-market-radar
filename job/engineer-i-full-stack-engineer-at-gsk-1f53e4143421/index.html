About the job

About the role

GSK’s Enterprise AI team builds artificial intelligence (AI) products that combine advanced modelling with applications people can use in their day-to-day work.

As a Full Stack Engineer, you will help build AI products from end to end. You will work across data, machine learning models, application programming interfaces (APIs), platforms and user-facing applications.

You will use AI coding and research assistants as part of your everyday engineering workflow. These tools will support development, testing and evaluation, while you remain responsible for reviewing their output and ensuring what we build meets our engineering standards.

Working with guidance from senior engineers, you will build and ship production AI features, operate what you create and develop towards greater independent ownership.

Key responsibilities

In this role, you will:

  • Build and ship AI features across data, modelling, backend services and user-facing applications.
  • Run modelling and analysis workflows, including preparing data, developing models, evaluating results and translating findings into product improvements.
  • Use AI coding and research assistants to support development, experimentation and testing, while reviewing their output for quality and accuracy.
  • Build and maintain RESTful APIs with clear contracts, authentication, versioning and effective error handling.
  • Integrate large language models (LLMs) into production applications, including context management, retrieval augmentation, streaming and tool use.
  • Connect AI applications to relevant data sources and GSK’s governed context layer.
  • Build accessible, intuitive interfaces based on user needs and feedback.
  • Monitor production AI features for performance, reliability, latency, errors and usage.
  • Support incident response and improvements to services you have helped develop.
  • Write clean, testable and maintainable code and contribute constructively to code reviews.
  • Develop reusable tools, libraries, templates and components that improve engineering efficiency.
  • Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders about the capabilities and limitations of AI solutions.

Basic qualifications

We are looking for professionals with these required skills to achieve our goals:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Applied Mathematics or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Experience combining applied machine learning, data science and software engineering, with evidence of delivering production work or strong experience in a closely related field.
  • Strong Python programming skills and experience with relevant data or machine learning libraries, such as pandas, scikit-learn or PyTorch.
  • Experience with a modern web development stack, such as React or Next.js, alongside FastAPI or Node.js.
  • Hands-on experience designing, developing and evaluating models or analytical workflows in a product or research environment.
  • Experience integrating LLMs into applications, including context management, retrieval augmentation, tool use or evaluation.
  • Experience building and deploying APIs, including authentication and error handling.
  • Experience using AI coding assistants as part of a regular software development workflow.

Preferred qualifications

If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Experience delivering a substantial AI feature that combines modelling with a user-facing application.
  • Experience building or extending agents that automate research, modelling or engineering workflows.
  • Experience with AI evaluation methods, prompt iteration frameworks or LLM observability.
  • Experience monitoring and operating AI-powered applications in production.
  • Experience developing reusable engineering tools, libraries or shared components.
  • Experience working in a fast-moving environment where priorities and requirements can evolve.
  • Experience working in a regulated or high-stakes environment, such as life sciences.

Why join us?

This is an opportunity to help define how modern data engineering operates in an AI-enabled organisation.

You will work on some of the foundational capabilities that allow AI systems to access and use data with the appropriate context, quality, permissions and governance. You will have the opportunity to influence engineering standards, shape the adoption of AI-enabled development practices and build capabilities that can be reused across GSK.

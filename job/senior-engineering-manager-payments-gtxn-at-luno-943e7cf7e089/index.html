About the job

About us:

Luno has spent over a decade making digital assets safe to own. Since 2013, we’ve helped millions of people around the world buy, store and invest in crypto, working with regulators in every market and holding all customer crypto 1:1. That discipline built the licences, banking relationships, custody and compliance machinery that the next era of this market will run on. The financial system is moving on-chain. Stablecoins are the only digital asset class operating at institutional scale today, and while 98% of supply is dollar-pegged, roughly half of global payment flows are not. That gap is the opportunity, and it is largest in exactly the markets Luno has spent twelve years building in.

About the team

You will lead the Engineering team building GTXN, Luno's global settlement business. GTXN focuses on institutional payments: the collections, pay-outs, virtual account and conversion products that let businesses and financial institutions move money in and out of emerging markets through one integration.

You will lead GTXN's engineering pods (Payments and OTC), and sit in the GTXN leadership trio alongside the Group Product Manager and the UX Manager. Lead Engineers and a Portfolio Engineer report to you, and you work closely with shared platform teams such as Exchange, Liquidity, Fiat Platform and Transaction Monitoring. You will report to the VP of Engineering for the Luno Group and be a peer to the other Engineering Managers across the organisation.

GTXN has the ambition to operate like a startup, so this role needs a balance between speed and control: you will build engineering teams and payment systems that move fast and that customers, regulators and partners can trust.

The role in a nutshell

You will be accountable for engineering as a whole in GTXN. You will lead and grow the people who build GTXN's payment products, turn the GTXN strategy into an engineering plan that is realistic and delivered, and make sure the systems that move our customers' money are reliable, operable and controlled.

Your mission will be

  • Lead and grow GTXN engineering: Line manage the Lead Engineers and Portfolio Engineer in GTXN. Own hiring, onboarding, performance, calibration and promotions, and build a bench of leaders who can run the pods without depending on you.
  • Own engineering delivery for GTXN: Make sure GTXN's engineering commitments are realistic and get met. Own capacity across the pods, anticipate delivery risk before it materialises, and manage dependencies on the shared platform teams GTXN relies on.
  • Build payment systems that are reliable, operable and controlled: Set the engineering bar for routing, reconciliation, ledger integrity, failure handling, retries and provider redundancy. Work with the Portfolio Engineer and Architecture to make sound design trade-offs as GTXN scales to new corridors and currencies.
  • Own operational excellence: Hold the standard for Launch Plans and Acceptance Into Service across GTXN. Be accountable for incident response, from triage to post-mortem, including payment provider and banking incidents, and learn from them at a systemic level.
  • Partner in the GTXN leadership trio: Work with the Group Product Manager, the UX Manager and the GTXN business leadership to turn strategy into an engineering plan. Be the single engineering voice for GTXN, including pushing back on feasibility, risk or sequencing when needed.
  • Make every corridor faster than the last: Turn bank and payment provider integrations into repeatable engineering capabilities and playbooks, so each new corridor, currency or customer integration takes less effort than the one before.
  • Shape how GTXN engineering works: Design team structure and ways of working for startup pace, raise engineering quality without slowing the team down, and lead the adoption of AI in how the team builds and operates.

A little about you

  • You are a Senior Engineering Manager or engineering leader who has led multiple teams, including managing tech leads or other managers.
  • You have a strong engineering background, and you are a credible technical partner to senior and staff engineers on system design, architecture and trade-offs.
  • You have payments experience: either you have led engineering teams building payment systems, or you built payment systems as an engineer before moving into management. Ideally this includes bank or payment provider integrations, settlement, reconciliation and ledgers.
  • You understand what it takes to run money movement in production: idempotency, retries, failure and return handling, reconciliation breaks and provider outages, and what each costs when it goes wrong.
  • You have run production systems in a regulated environment, and you can balance speed with the controls, auditability and compliance that payments require.
  • You develop people deliberately: you have grown engineers into leads, handled difficult performance conversations directly, and built teams that hold together through change.
  • You are comfortable with ambiguity and pace. You take unclear problems, create structure and ownership around them, and keep the team moving.
  • You communicate clearly with engineers, product, commercial leaders and executives, and you influence outcomes through credibility and well-reasoned positions.
  • Experience with payments in emerging markets, in Africa or Southeast Asia, or with multi-currency, cross-border or stablecoin settlement is a strong advantage.
  • You use AI as part of how you and your teams work, and can show where it has changed your output, not just your tooling.

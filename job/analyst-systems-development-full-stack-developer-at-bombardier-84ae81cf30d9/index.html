We have been successful in setting the highest standards by putting our people at the heart of it all, and defining excellence, together.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Design, build, test, deploy, and support secure applications and APIs from proof of concept through production.
  • Develop responsive, accessible user interfaces and reliable back-end services.
  • Integrate solutions with incumbent enterprise systems and approved platforms, including Azure, Databricks, SAP S/4HANA, SharePoint, Microsoft Graph, Power BI, and related APIs or services.
  • Productize forecasting, optimization, NLP, RAG, and classification models through reliable interfaces and workflows.
  • Implement identity, authorization, secrets management, encryption, input validation, audit logging, backup, and recovery controls.
  • Establish automated delivery pipelines, infrastructure as code, testing, vulnerability scanning, and release-management practices.
  • Implement application and infrastructure monitoring, incident response, performance tuning, and maintenance plans.
  • Partner with cross-functional business, technology, data, governance, and delivery stakeholders across the enterprise.
  • Translate business requirements into maintainable technical designs and acceptance criteria.
  • Capture usage and user feedback to improve product adoption, accessibility, and performance.
  • Maintain architecture, operational, security, and support documentation.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You hold a bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field.
  • You have 3+ years of experience in cloud-native software development.
  • You are proficient in TypeScript/JavaScript and Python.
  • You possess programming experience with React or an equivalent modern front-end framework.
  • You are hands-on in Azure development using App Service, Azure Functions, containers, storage, queues, identity, and monitoring,
  • You possess experience integrating applications with Databricks, including SQL, notebooks or jobs, model-serving endpoints, Lakehouse patterns, and Unity Catalog concepts.
  • You possess Enterprise integration experience involving incumbent business systems, SAP S/4HANA, SharePoint, Microsoft Graph, Power BI, APIs, or event-based integrations.
  • You possess strong knowledge of enterprise application-security practices, including authentication, authorization, secure configuration, data protection, validation controls, and audit logging.
  • You possess strong collaboration and communication skills.
  • You are fluent in English and French (spoken and written), with the ability to work effectively in a bilingual environment.

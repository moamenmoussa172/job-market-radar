About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Oracle Billing and Revenue Management (BRM) professional to provide senior technical and operational leadership across business-critical billing operations.

You will be responsible for the stability, availability and performance of the Oracle BRM ecosystem, covering rating, billing, invoicing, payments, collections and associated revenue-critical processes.

The role combines deep Oracle BRM expertise with billing assurance, Dynatrace-led observability, Oracle Database performance management, telecom system integration, incident and problem management, automation, service transition and continual service improvement

What you’ll do

  • Own the stability, availability and performance of the Oracle BRM platform in a 24x7 production environment.Administer and troubleshoot Oracle BRM Connection Manager (CM), Data Manager (DM) and associated BRM processes.
  • Monitor rating, billing, invoicing, collections, payments, General Ledger (GL) and scheduled activities.Manage BRM configuration, including pin.conf, configuration objects and multi-schema environments.
  • Troubleshoot opcode, PCM and process-level failures.Govern pre-bill checks, bill-run execution and post-bill validation.Investigate failed transactions, billing exceptions and discrepancies that could create revenue leakage.
  • Perform reconciliation across CRM, middleware, BRM and downstream platforms.Support custom C/C++ or Java opcode integrations and troubleshooting.Support Oracle BRM patchsets, interim patches and associated database or schema changes.
  • Establish and use Dynatrace as the primary observability platform across the BRM estate.Manage Dynatrace OneAgent across Linux/VM infrastructure and configure monitoring for CM, DM, rating, billing and related processes.Correlate Dynatrace telemetry with BRM pinlogs, application logs, billing and batch results, Oracle AWR/ASH data, SQL execution plans, database waits and locking information.
  • Build and maintain operational dashboards covering BRM availability, host health, critical processes, resource utilisation and dependent services.Use OpenTelemetry or compatible custom metrics and log collection where additional operational or business-level telemetry is required.Troubleshoot SQL/PL-SQL and analyse long-running SQL, locking, database waits and performance issues.Work with DBA teams on indexing, partitioning and database performance optimisation.
  • Diagnose end-to-end telecom integration issues across Siebel → FMW/AIA → BRM and OSM → BRM.
  • Lead P1/P2 technical recovery for critical billing incidents, coordinating cross-domain troubleshooting, root cause analysis and corrective or preventive actions.Build automation using Shell/Bash, Perl/Python and SQL/PL-SQL for sanity checks, process monitoring, batch validation, reconciliation and recurring operational activities.
  • Lead technical knowledge transfer, shadow/reverse-shadow activities, transition readiness and early-life support.Mentor L2/L3 engineers and collaborate with Billing, Revenue Assurance, CRM, Middleware, Database and Infrastructure teams.

  • CDR/EDR processing,Oracle Database,SQL/PL-SQL,AWR/ASH,Linux/Unix,Shell/Bash,Dynatrace

Additional relevant skills:

About the job

Turn customer data into decisions that shape the Etihad guest experience

Every journey with Etihad creates data: booking, the call to our contact centre, the meal on board, the Wi‑Fi connection at 38,000 feet and the post on social media after landing.

We're looking for a commercially minded data science leader who can connect all of it. You'll explain what our guests feel and why, and predict what they'll need next as a Manager – Data Science & Customer Value Optimization, leading advanced analytics, AI/ML, and decision science capabilities across Customer Care, Guest Relations, Contact Centre, Digital, and Customer Experience functions.

The role will transform structured and unstructured customer and operational data into actionable insights that enhance customer satisfaction, improve operational efficiency, optimize resources, and support strategic decision-making. The position will be responsible for developing predictive and prescriptive analytics solutions, driving NPS, CSAT, and CES monetization by linking customer experience improvements to commercial outcomes such as loyalty, retention, customer lifetime value, revenue growth, and brand advocacy.

The role will also identify opportunities for automation, cost optimization, productivity improvement, and operational excellence through data-driven and AI-enabled insights.

Accountabilities

  • Lead advanced analytics, statistical modelling, and predictive solutions to identify business drivers, uncover customer insights, and support strategic decision-making.
  • Measure and optimize customer experience performance by linking structured and unstructured data, including sentiment, social media, NPS, CSAT, transactional CSAT, loyalty, and retention metrics to tangible business and financial outcomes.
  • Drive the adoption of AI, machine learning, NLP, and Generative AI solutions to enhance customer experience, operational effectiveness, and business innovation.
  • Design and execute testing and optimization initiatives, including A/B testing and controlled experiments, to improve customer journeys and business performance.
  • Partner with operational teams to improve productivity, workforce planning, forecasting accuracy, resource utilization, and overall service performance.
  • Develop frameworks and dashboards to quantify revenue impact, cost savings, productivity gains, and the value delivered through analytics initiatives.
  • Act as a trusted advisor to senior leaders, delivering data-driven recommendations, influencing strategic decisions, and fostering a culture of analytical excellence.

Education & Experience

• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Analytics, Statistics, Data Science, Applied Mathematics, or Engineering.
• 7–10 years of experience in statistics, data science, advanced analytics, decision science, or customer analytics, with 1–3 years in the airline, travel, or transportation sector preferred.
• Proven experience delivering measurable business outcomes through advanced analytics and data science.
• Experience working with large-scale customer and operational datasets.
• Demonstrated experience supporting senior stakeholders and translating complex insights into clear business recommendations.
• Strong understanding of airline business drivers is an advantage.
• Excellent communication and presentation skills.
• Collaborative team player who thrives in a matrix, fast-paced environment.
• Proactive, solution-oriented, and accountable for delivery.

• Technical Skills
• Strong analytical mindset with expertise in structured and unstructured data, regression analysis, supervised and unsupervised models, forecasting models, hypothesis testing, statistical modelling, experimental design, segmentation, and clustering.
• Experimentation, testing, and optimization, including A/B testing, multivariate testing, design of experiments, and evidence-based decision making.
• Data science and machine learning, including predictive analytics, classification and regression, machine learning algorithms, NLP, text analytics, sentiment analytics, model validation, and performance monitoring.
• Data and visualization tools such as Python, R, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Databricks, Azure Machine Learning, and Advanced Excel.

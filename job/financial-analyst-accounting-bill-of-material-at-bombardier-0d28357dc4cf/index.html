What are your contributions to the team?

  • Work within the Finance team, with a focus on Material Cost Accounting, supporting the accurate reporting, analysis and understanding of material costs
  • Monitor and analyze material costs and variances to identify key drivers and support financial decision-making
  • Work closely with the Logistics group on monthly governance (unit cost, BTG and senior management governance)
  • Participate and contribute to the risk & opportunity process
  • Analyze the cost reduction program (UCR)
  • Participate in the annual budget process as well as the quarterly forecast process
  • Respond to ad hoc requests from managers and prepare analysis reports.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You hold a bachelor’s degree in accounting.
  • You hold an accounting title (CPA) or in the process to obtain it.
  • You have five years of experience.
  • You are known to be autonomous and resourceful. You are a team player ready to support your colleagues.
  • You possess skills to use MS Office. Knowledge of SAP and PBI are an asset.
  • You are bilingual

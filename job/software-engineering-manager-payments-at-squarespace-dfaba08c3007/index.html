Software Engineering Manager - Payments

Squarespace · New York City, United States

About the job

Squarespace Payments is dedicated to simplifying the financial side of commerce, enabling merchants to securely and reliably collect payments from their customers with minimal effort. Our team's mission is to build and maintain systems that allow merchants to focus on what matters most: growing their brand!

As Engineering Manager for the Payments team, you will lead a talented group of engineers in building and scaling the infrastructure that powers Squarespace's payment ecosystem. You will be responsible for driving delivery against key company and department goals, ensuring compliance with evolving regulatory requirements, and providing the technical leadership needed to push critical initiatives forward. Partnering closely with cross-functional stakeholders across Product, Design, and Operations, you will set the technical direction for your team while fostering a culture of ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement. This is a high-visibility role with direct impact on Squarespace's revenue growth and the experience of merchants around the world.

You will report directly to the Engineering Director for the Financial Solutions Group. This position is a hybrid role located in our New York office.

You’ll Get To…

  • Lead the engineering team that builds payments products.
  • Work with experienced Product Manager and Design to create product strategies that impact hundreds of thousands of businesses and their customers.
  • Work with world class managers and directors to shape the organization, processes and culture of our commerce platform.
  • Mentor engineers to develop their skill and expertise in the world of software development and payments products.

Who We’re Looking For

  • 7+ years of experience in the software industry
  • 3+ years of experience launching payments products
  • Experience developing and launching FinTech products and infrastructures.
  • Experience coaching engineers with diverse specialties.
  • Collaborate with various stakeholders to keep products compliant
  • Experience scaling up teams and organizations
  • Experience working with Adyen and Stripe platform is a huge plus

About Squarespace

Squarespace is a design-driven platform helping entrepreneurs build brands and businesses online. We empower millions of customers in more than 200 countries and territories with all the tools they need to create an online presence, build an audience, monetize, and scale their business. Our suite of products range from websites, domains, ecommerce, and marketing tools, as well as tools for scheduling with Acuity and creating and managing social media presence with Bio Sites and Unfold. Our team of more than 1,700 is headquartered in New York City, with offices in Dublin, Ireland, and Aveiro, Portugal. For more information about our company, visit https://www.squarespace.com/about/careers.

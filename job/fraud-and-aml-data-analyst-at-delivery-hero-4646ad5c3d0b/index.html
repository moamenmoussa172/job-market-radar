About the job

Job Ref ID: JR0090053

1. Data Analysis & Behavioral Profiling

  • Analyze user behavior across the platform (registration, promo usage, cart addition, address changes, order frequency, and claim/refund history) using SQL, Python, or R.
  • Analyze device fingerprinting, IP addresses, GSM verification data, geolocation (GPS), BIN (Bank Identification Number), and delivery address overlaps to detect promo-hunting bot networks and fraud rings.
  • Build comprehensive datasets from consumer, courier, and vendor behavior patterns to feed real-time risk scoring models.

2. Food E-Commerce Fraud Prevention

  • Track and analyze stolen credit card usage, Chargeback rates, and root causes on a daily basis.
  • Develop rule sets against Account Takeover (ATO), first-order coupon abuse via multi-accounting, referral code manipulation, and non-delivery/missing item claims (claim & refund fraud).
  • Continuously optimize rules within the Fraud Engine; manage real-time checks to avoid impacting order dispatch times to vendors and couriers, reducing manual review queues while lowering false negative rates.
  • Support data-driven strategies for 3D Secure, Non-3D Secure, and dynamic Risk-Based Authentication (RBA).

3. Marketplace AML & Vendors Risks

  • Analyze revenue spikes, unusual order frequencies, and menu/price mismatches (e.g., pricing a soup or toast menu at 10,000 TL) among vendors in the marketplace model to detect money laundering risks.
  • Develop algorithms and anomaly scans to detect collusion and fictitious transactions (collusion / fake ghost orders) among buyers, couriers, and vendors where no actual food delivery takes place.
  • Monitor transfers via e-wallets/food card infrastructures and restaurant payouts in compliance with MASAK regulations; report suspicious transaction patterns (structuring/smurfing, dormant restaurants suddenly becoming active).
  • Screen new restaurant onboardings (KYB - Know Your Business) against international sanctions lists (OFAC, UN, etc.) and local watchlists.

4. Reporting & Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Prepare daily/weekly dashboards (Tableau, Power BI, Looker, etc.) tracking chargeback rates, promo abuse costs, order approval rates, declined order volumes, and courier/vendor abuse metrics.
  • Collaborate with BI teams to monitor the performance of Machine Learning (ML) models that generate sub-second risk scores at checkout, recommending new features (e.g., order frequency from different accounts to the same address).
  • Work in close coordination with Compliance, Commercial, Operations, Logistics, Product, and CX to balance security measures with order velocity and customer satisfaction.

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Computer, Industrial, Software), Statistics, Mathematics, or related fields.
  • 2-4 years of experience in Risk, Fraud, Trust & Safety, or Data Analytics within Food E-Commerce, Quick-Commerce (Q-Commerce), E-Commerce Marketplaces, Payment Gateways, or FinTech platforms.

Technical Skills

  • Database & Querying: Advanced SQL skills; ability to write complex queries and anomaly detection scripts on big data platforms (BigQuery, Snowflake, AWS Redshift, etc.).
  • Analytical Tools: Proficiency in data analysis and manipulation using Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn) or R.
  • Visualization: Data storytelling skills and hands-on experience with BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker).
  • Risk Systems: Experience working with real-time rule engines and risk software (Sift, Forter, Signifyd, Stripe Radar, or in-house proprietary systems).

Domain Knowledge

  • In-depth understanding of promo/voucher fraud schemes specific to food e-commerce, ghost kitchen/restaurant dynamics, and fake order patterns.
  • Knowledge of VISA/Mastercard Chargeback rules and dispute processes.
  • Expertise in on-demand / quick-delivery payment ecosystems (Virtual POS, BKM Express, E-Wallets, Food Cards).
  • Awareness of KVKK/GDPR, electronic money, and marketplace regulations (Laws No. 6563 and 6493, MASAK E-Commerce Intermediary Service Provider guidelines).

Soft Skills

  • Agility and fast decision-making skills to match the operational pace of real-time food delivery.
  • A skeptical and investigative mindset; ability to spot complex anomalies across the courier-restaurant-buyer triangle like a detective.
  • A balanced vision to maintain the equilibrium between security and conversion/order fulfillment, prioritizing customer and vendor satisfaction.

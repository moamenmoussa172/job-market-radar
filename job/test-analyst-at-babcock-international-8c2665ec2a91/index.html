About the job

Job Title: Test Analyst

Location: Plymouth, Bristol or Leicester + Hybrid Working Arrangements

Compensation: £33,900 - £42,400 Depending on Experience + Benefits

Role Type: Full time / Permanent

Role ID: SF76630

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Test Analyst at our Devonport Royal Dockyard, Babcock Technology Centre or Leicester sites.

The role

As a Test Analyst, you’ll join our Digital Solutions team, where you'll play a pivotal role in maximising the value of data-driven systems that support critical defence programmes. Your work will help ensure the quality, reliability and performance of digital solutions used to support submarine operations, contributing directly to the safety of submarines and submariners.

Day-to-day, you’ll have an exciting opportunity to work within a collaborative digital and engineering environment, applying both manual and automated testing techniques while developing your expertise in modern testing tools, agile delivery and software quality assurance.

  • Analysing requirements and design documentation to produce test estimates, test plans, test cases and test scripts.
  • Designing and executing functional and non-functional testing activities to ensure quality and system performance.
  • Identifying, recording and managing defects through to resolution while working closely with developers and stakeholders.
  • Producing and communicating test progress, quality metrics and test outcome reports to project stakeholders.
  • Contributing to continuous improvement initiatives and utilising tools such as Azure DevOps, Playwright and Selenium.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements with a minimum of 2 days in the office/onsite.

Essential experience of the Test Analyst

  • Experience producing test estimates, test plans, test cases and test scripts from business and technical requirements.
  • Experience collaborating with developers, business analysts and stakeholders within agile delivery teams.
  • Experience managing defects throughout their lifecycle, including prioritisation and resolution tracking.
  • Hands-on experience with test automation tools such as Playwright and Selenium, alongside API and backend testing.
  • Experience using SQL and communicating quality metrics, risks and delivery status to stakeholders.

Qualifications for the Test Analyst

Essential

  • ISTQB Foundation Certificate.

Desirable

  • Additional testing, automation or software quality qualifications.
  • Experience working in a regulated, defence or engineering environment.

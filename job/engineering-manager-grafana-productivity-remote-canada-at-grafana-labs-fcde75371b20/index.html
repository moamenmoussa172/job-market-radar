About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. Customers, including Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce, rely on Grafana Labs. We are a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries, backed by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital, GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, and Lead Edge Capital. Learn more at grafana.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X.

We’re scaling fast and staying true to what makes us different: an open-source legacy, a global collaborative culture, and a passion for meaningful work. Our team thrives in an innovation-driven environment where transparency, autonomy, and trust fuel everything we do.

You may not meet every requirement, and that’s okay. If this role excites you, we’d love you to raise your hand for what could be a truly career-defining opportunity.

This is a remote position and we're considering candidates in the US & Canada (residents of Québec are not eligible for this position).

The Opportunity

About our platform

Grafana Cloud moves millions of metrics, log lines, and traces per second from our customers' environments into a highly available, low-latency stack that processes and stores this data, and serves it to dashboards and alerting tools. We aim to grow this to hundreds of millions per second, and it's critical that as we grow, we improve our performance, increase our reliability, and do it efficiently and securely.

The Foundations department owns the Internal Engineering Platform (IEP): the Kubernetes clusters, cloud service provider resources (CSP), hardware, identity, SDLC, security and observability systems that Grafana Labs's application engineers use to build, deploy, and run their workloads.

About the Productivity group

We're hiring an engineering manager for the Foundations Productivity group, which owns SDLC tooling, application security and observability, of the resources, systems and workloads used to build and run Grafana Labs products. The group enables product development through the provision of performant and feature-rich SDLC tooling, governing the use and configuration of that tooling. We are responsible for guiding the product teams in application security good practices, reviewing product changes for security compliance and enabling security functions through self-service automation. We also own and maintain the internal observability platform, using our own products to give visibility into cloud resource cost, usage and sustainability, and vulnerability management.

You'll lead one of the squads of platform engineers that comprise the group.

We're platform engineers, and our application teams are our users. Our job is to give them leverage: self-service building blocks that take care of the hard parts of infrastructure, so they can focus on what they're building. We treat the platform as a product and value the experience of the people who use it. When a change is well communicated and understood, teams welcome it, and it lands as an improvement that makes the right way the easy way. You'll lead a squad that makes that happen.

How we work

Foundations is a global team that works asynchronously. The people who own a component or problem make the decisions about it, once stakeholders have had a chance to weigh in. Application teams build and run their own services; we give them a self-service platform to do it on.

Every engineer is on-call for the components their squad provides. We plan on-call, tactical work, and time off into every quarter, so roadmap work is roughly half of an engineer's time rather than all of it.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Hire, manage, and develop a squad of platform engineers, with regular feedback and career conversations that support each person's growth.
  • Own your squad's roadmap and quarterly objectives: help decide what the squad works on, what it defers, and why, across a broad remit where demand always exceeds capacity.
  • Take platform changes from design to fleet-wide adoption: staged rollouts, migration paths, and clear communication, so changes land well for the teams who depend on them.
  • Partner with the other Productivity squads on work that spans them, and with the teams across Foundations and the rest of Engineering whose work depends on yours.
  • Help shape how the group works as a whole, including planning, on-call, and the ownership model between squads.
  • Own the squad's intake. Requests, reviews, and support from application teams arrive continuously; keep them triaged and visible so that planned work still moves.
  • Keep on-call healthy: ensure a sustainable load and support incident response.
  • Work across team boundaries, contributing beyond your squad's scope where Foundations needs it.

We invest heavily in developer productivity. You can use modern AI coding assistants as part of your daily workflow (your choice of tools, within security guidelines), backed by a company-funded usage budget so you can iterate quickly without unnecessary friction.

We encourage pragmatic AI-assisted development: faster prototyping, test generation, refactors, documentation, and incident follow-ups, always paired with strong code review and quality standards.

What Makes You a Great Fit

  • Experience managing engineers, ideally a team that builds and operates production SDLC infrastructure and governance, security and monitoring.
  • Hands-on depth in at least one area of platform engineering, such as SDLC infrastructure and enablement, developer experience, SDLC governance and compliance, application security, or observability and cost visibility. You can challenge a design in your area on its merits, and the engineers on your team will expect you to.
  • Fluency with infrastructure as code and with running Kubernetes in production.
  • A track record of landing internal engineering/developer platform changes at scale. You've driven a migration or a rollout across many teams or many clusters, and you know what makes one stall.
  • The ability to explain platform engineering to very different audiences (platform engineers, application teams, product management and business leadership) and to make the case for what the squad should and shouldn't take on.
  • Be comfortable collaborating across time zones and cultures in a fully remote environment, using clear, written communication to build trust and psychological safety.
  • You're a motivated self-starter with a strong bias toward action and ownership, able to navigate ambiguity in a complex problem space.
  • Comfort with AI-assisted tooling. We embrace AI and agentic development, so we expect you to be curious and comfortable using AI-powered tools, and ideally to have practical experience folding them into a team's workflow.

Bonus Points For

  • Experience across more than one of the group's areas: SDLC tooling, developer experience, application security, and observability.
  • Hands-on depth in cloud infrastructure, SDLC tooling expertise, developer experience and making the right way the easy way, application security, monitoring tooling and alerting. You can challenge a threat model or a system design on its merits, and the engineers on your team will expect you to.
  • Building self-service infrastructure for application teams.
  • Running observability for a production platform at scale.
  • Operating across more than one of GCP, AWS, and Azure.
  • You've worked in or on open source, or other community-based projects. At Grafana Labs, "OSS is in our DNA"

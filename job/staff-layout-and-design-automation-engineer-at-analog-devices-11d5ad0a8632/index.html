About the job

About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

Analog Devices' Platform IP organization is seeking an experienced Senior Layout & Design Automation Engineer to join our growing team. This role combines deep expertise in analog and mixed-signal IC layout with a passion for engineering productivity, automation, and IP reuse.

The successful candidate will be responsible for delivering complex block and subsystem layouts while also driving continuous improvement of layout methodologies, automation frameworks, and reusable IP infrastructure. A key aspect of the role will be identifying challenging or repetitive analog layout problems and developing practical automation solutions that can be applied across IP, projects, and technologies.

The engineer will collaborate closely with circuit designers, layout engineers, EE teams, and technology experts to enable high-quality silicon solutions across multiple business units and process technologies.

This position is ideally suited to an engineer who is equally comfortable understanding and solving challenging physical design problems, developing automation to address those problems, and supporting other engineers in the application of those solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Technical Layout Leadership

  • Lead development of analog and mixed-signal layouts from floor-planning through tapeout.
  • Own layout execution for critical IP, subsystems, and test-chip developments on planer nodes.
  • Drive high-quality layout implementation for:
  • Data converters (ADCs/DACs)
  • Power management circuits (LDOs, references, regulators)
  • Amplifiers and signal chain circuits
  • PLLs and clock-generation circuits
  • High-voltage and mixed-voltage designs
  • Ensure designs meet performance, matching, reliability, ESD, latch-up, and manufacturability requirements.
  • Drive successful DRC, LVS, ERC, EMIR and parasitic extraction closure.
  • Develop a detailed understanding of process-specific layout rules, limitations, and physical-design trade-offs, and provide practical guidance to designers and layout engineers.

Design Automation & Productivity

  • Develop and deploy automation solutions that improve layout productivity and design quality.
  • Work directly with layout engineers and designers to identify difficult, repetitive, or time-consuming physical-design tasks that are suitable for automation.
  • Develop scripting and automation, including Cadence SKILL programming, to improve and extend Virtuoso-based analog layout workflows.
  • Explore automation of analog placement, routing, layout generation, checking, and other areas where engineering knowledge can be translated into repeatable methodologies.
  • Identify repetitive engineering tasks and implement scalable solutions.
  • Support advanced methodologies including:
  • Automated layout generation
  • Placement and routing automation
  • Layout verification automation
  • IP qualification workflows
  • Reusable analog building blocks
  • AI-assisted design and productivity initiatives
  • Develop solutions that can evolve from individual productivity improvements into reusable capabilities for wider engineering teams.
  • Partner with CAD and EDA teams to improve design flows and tool integration.

IP Development & Reuse

  • Contribute to the development of reusable Platform IP across multiple technology nodes.
  • Support IP migration, qualification, and reuse activities.
  • Apply automation techniques across a broad portfolio of analog and mixed-signal IP, including data converters, references, power management, signal-chain, clocking, high-voltage and mixed-voltage circuits.
  • Identify opportunities where automation can simplify IP migration, adaptation, verification, and deployment across different process technologies.
  • Promote best practices for containerized IP, version control, and design traceability.
  • Assist in defining scalable methodologies that enable reuse across business units and geographical teams.

Technical Leadership & Mentorship

  • Provide technical guidance to layout engineers across the organization.
  • Perform layout reviews and design audits.
  • Mentor junior and mid-level layout engineers.
  • Contribute to engineering standards, training material, and best-practice documentation.
  • Serve as a go-to technical resource for questions relating to analog layout methodology, process rules, technology limitations, and the practical application of automation.
  • Help capture experienced layout knowledge and translate it, where appropriate, into reusable methodologies, checks, scripts, and automation.
  • Act as a recognized subject matter expert in one or more technical domains.

Required Qualifications

  • BS, MS, or equivalent experience in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or related discipline.
  • 8+ years of analog or mixed-signal IC layout experience.
  • Proven experience delivering silicon in advanced and mature process technologies.
  • Expert knowledge of:
  • Cadence Virtuoso Layout XL / VXL methodologies
  • Calibre DRC, LVS, ERC and extraction flows
  • Deep understanding of:
  • Device matching
  • Parasitic effects
  • Shielding and isolation techniques
  • Reliability and ESD considerations
  • Power distribution and substrate management

Scripting & Automation Experience

  • Strong scripting/programming experience, with demonstrated ability to develop and maintain engineering automation solutions.
  • Experience with Cadence SKILL programming for Virtuoso-based layout automation, productivity utilities, design checks, or flow integration.
  • Experience with Python for engineering automation, data processing, workflow integration, or tool development.
  • Working knowledge of Shell/Bash and/or Tcl for design-flow automation and EDA tool integration.
  • Ability to translate analog layout knowledge and repetitive engineering workflows into robust, reusable automation used by other engineers.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience developing engineering automation deployed to multiple users.
  • Experience with analog layout generators and parameterized cell development.
  • Experience with, or interest in, analog placement and routing automation.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted engineering workflows.
  • Experience supporting IP libraries shared across multiple design groups.
  • Knowledge of multiple foundries and process nodes.
  • Experience working with distributed global engineering teams.
  • Previous technical leadership or project-lead responsibility.

Success Profile

A successful candidate will:

  • Consistently deliver high-quality layouts on complex projects.
  • Recognize where difficult analog layout problems can be addressed through automation rather than repeated manual effort.
  • Improve engineering productivity through automation and workflow enhancement.
  • Develop practical automation grounded in real analog layout and process knowledge.
  • Influence layout methodology across projects and teams.
  • Champion IP reuse and standardization.
  • Mentor engineers and raise the technical capability of the organization.
  • Become a trusted resource for analog layout process, rules, limitations, and automation opportunities.
  • Act as a recognized technical leader while remaining hands-on with design execution.

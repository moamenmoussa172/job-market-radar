About the job

At Amazon, we're working to be the most customer-centric company on earth. To get there, we need exceptionally talented, bright, and driven people. We are looking for an EMEA, Event Security Manager at Amazon Studios to join a team of security professionals who are responsible for broad range of support, plus delivery and enhancement of our physical event security program.
The special event security manager will support Amazon MGM Studios, Prime Video and other Media and Entertainment live events across EMEA. This position will focus on the safety, security, and privacy of our customers, vendors and associates while ensuring company event goals are accomplished. Our ideal candidate will have a hybrid of skills: the ability to participate, influence and lead within cross-functional teams, solution and action oriented with excellent communication skills.
You will be focused on how to grow, scale and optimize efforts in a high-tech, fast pace, physical environment. We are seeking a proven self-starter that is driven by results and comes to the table with innovative ideas in which the results speak for themselves. Bring your bias for action and a passion for working on a team that delivers results, continuously improves performance, and always delights customers.
Key job responsibilities
• Develop customized security plans for large scale events, as well as smaller private Studios functions.
• Implement and present safety and security plans to senior company leadership.
• Ensure the delivery of high standard, professional security service across the events.
• Collaborate proactively with Amazon Studios and third-party production key stakeholders in designing and developing physical security operations.
• Maintain compliance with Amazon Content, Legal and Risk requirements in line with Amazon Policy.
• Manage and report incidents, prepare statements and follow up on reports in accordance with Amazon Policy.
• Mange proprietary and contract security personnel, law enforcement operations, customer services and medical personnel.
• Conduct and oversee event security assessment programs and leverage external and internal partners for timely threat/risk evaluations and responses.
• Support event, marketing, and public relations departments while maintaining security standards.
• Manage budget, payment accounts and balances; initiate correspondence as necessary to ensure timely payment (in accordance with the contracts). Produce budget reporting, backup information and contracts to accounts.

Job Description:

Parsons is looking for an experienced Project Director – PMO to provide strategic leadership, governance, and oversight for the delivery of major residential, mixed-use, hospitality, and infrastructure developments.

In this role, you will lead the PMO function across a complex portfolio of projects, establishing the governance, controls, reporting, and delivery frameworks required to achieve the Client’s strategic objectives.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Lead the PMO function, providing strategic direction, governance, and delivery oversight.
  • Establish PMO frameworks, procedures, stage gates, KPIs, and reporting standards.
  • Oversee projects from master planning and design through procurement, construction, commissioning, and handover.
  • Drive integrated schedule, cost, risk, change, commercial, and performance management.
  • Monitor portfolio performance, identifying key risks, dependencies, and corrective actions.
  • Ensure effective integration across buildings, infrastructure, utilities, landscape, and public realm packages.
  • Oversee consultants, contractors, and delivery teams to ensure alignment with Client objectives.
  • Lead executive reviews and provide clear reporting on progress, cost, schedule, risk, and key decisions.
  • Drive PMO standardization, continuous improvement, digital delivery, and best practices.
  • Lead and develop high-performing multidisciplinary PMO teams.

What Required Skills You'll Bring

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Architecture, Construction Management, Project Management, or related discipline.
  • 20+ years of experience, including significant senior leadership experience on major development programs.
  • Proven experience as a Project Director, PMO Director, Program Director, or equivalent.
  • Strong experience in residential, mixed-use, hospitality, and associated infrastructure developments.
  • Proven expertise in PMO governance, project controls, cost, schedule, risk, change, commercial, and performance management.
  • Experience overseeing multiple projects, consultants, contractors, and work packages within complex programs.
  • Strong leadership, client management, stakeholder engagement, communication, and decision-making skills.

What Desired Skills You'll Bring

  • Experience delivering large-scale real estate development programs in United Arab Emirates or the Middle East.
  • Experience within a client-side, developer, PMO, or program management environment.
  • Strong knowledge of BIM, digital project management, integrated controls, and executive dashboards.
  • Professional certification such as PMP, PgMP, PE, CEng, RICS, or equivalent is preferred.

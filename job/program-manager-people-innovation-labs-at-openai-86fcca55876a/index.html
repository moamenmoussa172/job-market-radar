About the job

About the Team

At OpenAI, we are building the connective tissue between our mission and our people. People Innovation Labs is a fast-moving engineering team embedded in the People organization, focused on rethinking how we find and retain the best talent and empower everyone to do their best work.

We are not a typical internal tools team. From recruiting to culture, we design and build systems that give the People Team a significant edge by infusing OpenAI’s models and first-principles thinking into how we operate. Our work spans greenfield 0→1 products like OpenHouse (our internal knowledge hub), AI-powered automations, performance and growth systems, and scalable recruiting infrastructure. We operate in a highly cross-functional environment, partnering closely with HR, Recruiting, Finance, Legal, IT, Security, and executive stakeholders.

About the Role

We’re looking for a Program Manager to own execution, coordination, and delivery across People Innovation Labs’ most complex and cross-cutting initiatives. This role is distinct from the Product Manager role and is focused on how work gets done rather than what we build.

As a Program Manager, you will be responsible for translating product strategy into executable plans, managing dependencies across teams and systems, and ensuring that high-stakes initiatives land predictably and cleanly in a fast-growing, high-context environment. You will partner closely with Product Management, Engineering, Design, and People stakeholders to drive clarity, accountability, and momentum across the portfolio.

Because we build new products from the ground up, the work does not end when a product launches. You’ll own ongoing engagement with users, including onboarding, training, hands-on support, and creating clear ways for people to share feedback. You’ll develop a deep understanding of how our products fit into users’ day-to-day work, what is working, and where they are struggling. You’ll bring those insights back to product, design, and engineering so the team can iterate quickly toward product-market fit.

You’ll also help keep the team aligned with its broader objectives. You’ll maintain a clear view of priorities, progress, dependencies, risks, and outcomes across the portfolio and provide clear updates and recommendations to team and company leadership.

This role is ideal for someone who excels at orchestrating work across many teams, navigating ambiguity and organizational complexity, and creating the operating structure that allows product and engineering teams to move quickly without chaos.

In this role, you will:

  • Own program execution across multiple People Innovation Labs initiatives, ensuring clear plans, milestones, owners, and timelines
  • Drive cross-functional alignment across People, Recruiting, HRBPs, Finance, Legal, IT, Security, and Engineering partners
  • Manage dependencies and sequencing across systems (e.g., HRIS, ATS, permissions, data pipelines, rollout infrastructure)
  • Identify risks, bottlenecks, and tradeoffs early, and proactively surface and resolve them
  • Establish and maintain operating cadence for key initiatives, including status updates, reviews, launch readiness, and retrospectives
  • Create clear, actionable documentation such as program plans, decision logs, rollout plans, and exec updates
  • Support product launches by coordinating readiness across training, comms, support, data, and operational workflows
  • Own communication, enablement, change management, and rollout strategies for new products and workflows.
  • Lead post-launch user engagement through onboarding, training, office hours, hands-on support, and other programs that drive adoption.
  • Build trusted relationships with users and create an environment where they provide frequent, candid feedback.
  • Observe how products fit into real workflows and identify points of confusion, friction, or unmet need.
  • Combine user feedback, usage data, and operational signals to understand what is working and what needs to change.
  • Track delivery health and operational success metrics (e.g., timelines, adoption readiness, launch quality, post-launch issues)

You might thrive in this role if you:

  • Have 7+ years of experience as a Program Manager, Technical Program Manager, or in a similar execution-focused role
  • Are exceptionally strong at turning ambiguous, multi-stakeholder work into clear plans and accountable execution
  • Communicate crisply and confidently across a wide range of audiences, including senior leaders
  • Enjoy operating at the intersection of product, engineering, and business operations
  • Are comfortable managing complexity across multiple systems, teams, and timelines
  • Know how to introduce structure and rigor without creating unnecessary process or slowing teams down
  • Have experience supporting launches of operationally intensive products or platforms
  • Can identify patterns across qualitative feedback, usage data, and operational signals and turn them into actionable recommendations.
  • Are energized by fast-moving environments and motivated by helping teams deliver high-impact work reliably

Sr. Product Manager (French speaker) - Fixed Term Contract, EU Books I&I, Books Category Mgmt-FR, EU Books I&I team

Amazon · Clichy, France

Onsite · Senior · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 17 November 2026

Apply on Amazon's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Books Insights and Innovation team is looking for a Product Manager to support the FR Books business and drive some of EU Books' strategic projects and programs. The work covers both long- and short-term goals and varies in scope, from FR-specific growth projects to EU-wide programs. Every initiative involves working actively with many stakeholders across functions, including Senior Leadership.
This role suits someone who is very analytical, commercially minded and comfortable with ambiguity. You should be able to move from a deep dive to a business proposal, and then to launching it. You'll have a lot of ownership and visibility, and you'll make a direct impact on the customer experience and the business.
Key job responsibilities
- Partner with the FR Books team: lead specific FR projects, analyses and deep dives. Turn findings into clear recommendations and action plans.
- Lead strategic Books projects and innovations with a strong business development focus. This runs from finding and sizing the opportunity, through writing the business case, to launch and measuring results. -You'll work with Books, non-Books and external stakeholders.
- Drive work across teams: influence partner teams (e.g. Marketing, Retail, Tech, Finance, Operations) without direct authority, and keep everyone aligned on goals, milestones and dependencies.
- Make work scale across EU Books countries: standardize inputs reporting and share analyses, findings and solutions so they can be reused across marketplaces.
- Define and track success metrics: set up the right KPIs and mechanisms, monitor performance, and report progress to leadership through written documents and business reviews.
- Be an active member of the EU Books I&I team: work closely with PMs across marketplaces, share best practices, and help shape the team's roadmap.
About the team
The EU Books Insights & Innovation (I&I) team is a cross-functional group that drives strategic projects and programs across EU Books marketplaces. We work with country Books teams, central functions and external partners. We turn data and customer insights into business growth, new programs and scalable solutions.
Amazon Books is an exciting space; we are looking for high achievers who want to make an impact. We believe reading is essential for a healthy society; our mission is to inspire readers by making it easy to read more and get more out of reading. Everyone in our team has a meaningful impact on delighting our customers.
- Bachelor's degree, or Master's degree
- Experience working effectively on milestone-driven projects
- Experience owning program strategy, end to end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience working cross functionally with tech and non-tech teams
- Speak, write, and read fluently in French

SAP PI/PO and CPI Consultant

Bosch Group · bangalore, India

Onsite · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 26 October 2026

SAP S/4HANA

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Mandatory Skills:

  • 4 – 6 years of work experience in SAP PI/PO and CPI (implementation and support projects).
  • Can handle develop and support interfaces to replicate transactional data or master data, integrations with SAP ECC, S4Hana, MDG or any other BTP services and integrations via CPI.
  • Proficient with message mapping concepts and scripting language like groovy and XSLT.
  • Strong understanding of SAP PI/PO and CPI architecture, integration patterns, and components.
  • Proficiency in working with various adapters in both on-premises and cloud integration scenarios.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to troubleshoot complex integration issues.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills to Communicate technical solutions effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment in shifts.
  • SAP Cloud Platform Integration -certification will have added advantage

Integration Solution Design:

  • Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and analyze business requirements, translating them into effective and scalable integration processes.

Adapter Configuration:

  • Configure and implement various adapters in SAP CPI, such as SOAP, REST, OData, IDoc, SFTP, and others.
  • Ensure seamless connectivity and data flow between SAP CPI and diverse external systems through expert adapter configurations.
  • Basic understanding on networking, infra and security.

Integration Flow Development:

  • Develop, deploy, and maintain integration flows in SAP CPI, ensuring efficient data transformation, mapping, and routing.

Adapters Expertise:

  • Demonstrate advanced knowledge and expertise in working with different adapters within SAP CPI.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve adapter-related issues, ensuring the reliability and performance of integration processes.

Monitoring and Optimization:

  • Implement monitoring solutions to proactively identify and address integration issues.
  • Optimize integration processes for performance, scalability, and resource efficiency.
  • Prepare Daily monitoring Reports and attending stand up calls and shift handover, daily system health check and report to respective teams.
  • Resolve the incidents and tickets based on the SLA
  • Flexible for rotational shift hours.

Documentation:

  • Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation for adapter configurations, integration flows, and troubleshooting procedures .
  • Ensure documentation is up-to-date and easily accessible.

Upgrades and Enhancements:

  • Stay informed about updates and releases in SAP CPI, especially those related to adapters.
  • Evaluate new features and recommend system upgrades or enhancements to improve integration capabilities.

Educational qualification:

  • BE, BTech, BCA, BSc (IT) MCA, MBA (IT) and MSc(IT)

Experience :

  • 4 - 6 years’ experience

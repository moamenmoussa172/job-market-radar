General Profile

We are looking for candidates with a solid technical work experience. The ideal candidate brings hands-on expertise in system integration, testing, and commissioning within a high-tech manufacturing context.

The role is responsible for the integration of complex systems, ensuring alignment with technical specifications, quality standards, and project timelines through to final installation. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will leverage strong technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills in an international, high-tech semiconductor environment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Support full system integration to ensure compliance with technical specifications, on-time delivery, and quality standards through to final installation.
  • Perform functional, operational, and system-level testing, including calibration, verification, and troubleshooting of sub-assemblies and complete systems.
  • Diagnose and resolve system- and component-level issues across electrical, mechanical, optical, and process domains using schematics, diagrams, and technical documentation.
  • Execute in-house Customer Acceptance Tests and contribute to validation activities.
  • Travel to customer sites if needed to support installation, start-up, commissioning, and provide technical assistance.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Engineering, Manufacturing, NPI) to develop, build, and test new systems (prototype, alpha, beta units).
  • Evaluate system performance, quality, and configuration issues; perform rework, support root cause analysis, and implement corrective actions, including generating Quality Notifications.
  • Prepare and maintain accurate technical documentation, including test reports, logbooks, and procedures, ensuring compliance with company standards.
  • Follow and contribute to the improvement of operational procedures, processes, and best practices.
  • Build effective working relationships with internal stakeholders and contribute to a collaborative team environment.
  • Ensure strict adherence to health, safety, and environmental standards, maintaining a clean and safe workplace and proactively reporting risks or incidents.
  • Stay up to date with manufacturing and engineering processes, supporting continuous learning and ongoing system improvements.

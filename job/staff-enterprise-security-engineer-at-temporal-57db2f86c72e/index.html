About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Role Summary

Join our Security team as a Staff Enterprise Security Engineer, where you'll own the security of the systems our employees rely on every day: their laptops, the SaaS applications that hold our data, and the AI assistants and agents now woven into how we work. In this position, you'll set technical direction across endpoint, SaaS, and enterprise AI security, building secure-by-default controls as code and partnering closely with IT, Detection & Response, GRC, and Engineering. You'll also build AI-assisted security workflows of your own, with the right guardrails. We're looking for someone who treats security as an engineering discipline and helps the whole company move fast, safely, as a trusted partner rather than a gatekeeper.

What You'll Do

  • Own endpoint security architecture for a macOS-first fleet (plus Windows, Linux, and mobile), including hardening baselines, working with IT on MDM, EDR, patching policy, application control, and device trust.
  • Manage endpoint and SaaS security configuration as code, using Terraform, version control, code review, and automated rollouts so every control is repeatable and auditable.
  • Build and run the SaaS security program: application inventory, secure configuration standards for critical platforms (Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Salesforce), and drift detection.
  • Govern third-party integrations, OAuth grants, browser extensions, and non-human identities to prevent data leakage and over-privileged access.
  • Lead discovery and inventory of AI usage across the company, covering approved assistants, coding agents, MCP servers, plugins, and shadow AI.
  • Design and deploy guardrails for enterprise AI, including data loss prevention, agent and MCP access scoping, tool-call policies, and defenses against prompt injection.
  • Lead cross-functional security initiatives from proposal through rollout, partnering with IT, Engineering, Legal, and People teams: scoping the work, setting milestones, managing stakeholders, and reporting progress and outcomes to leadership.
  • Perform threat modeling and security reviews of new SaaS vendors, AI tools, agents, and internal integrations.
  • Stay current on emerging standards and guidance (e.g. OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI RMF, MCP security specifications) and translate these into actionable internal policy.
  • Partner with Detection & Response to ensure endpoints, identity, SaaS, and AI platforms produce the telemetry needed for detection, and automate response workflows.
  • Mentor engineers across Security and IT, and serve as the senior escalation point for complex enterprise security issues.
  • Able to participate in on-call rotation.

What You'll Bring

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • 7+ years in enterprise security, corporate security, or a related role, including technical leadership of initiatives that span multiple teams.
  • Deep hands-on experience securing endpoint fleets at scale, with MDM (e.g. Jamf, Kandji, Fleet, Intune) and EDR (e.g. CrowdStrike, SentinelOne) platforms and macOS hardening.
  • Proven ability to model SaaS threats, assess vendor and integration risk, and harden configurations across platforms such as Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, and Salesforce.
  • Strong knowledge of identity as the foundation of SaaS and device security: SSO, federation, SCIM provisioning, conditional access, and device posture enforcement.
  • Practical understanding of AI security risks, including prompt injection, data leakage, insecure tool connectors, and over-privileged agents, and experience putting guardrails around AI assistants and coding agents.
  • Ability to write production-quality code in at least one language (e.g. Python or Go), with a history of building automation and security tooling beyond simple scripting.
  • Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code and GitOps workflows (Terraform, CI/CD pipelines) and with SOAR or workflow automation platforms.
  • A track record of leading projects that span several teams outside Security, from getting them prioritized through delivery, including clear plans, measurable success criteria, and a handoff to long-term owners.
  • Ability to influence without authority, bringing along teams whose goals and constraints differ from Security’s.
  • Excellent communication and ability to explain complex security concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

Nice to Have

  • Experience administering enterprise AI platforms (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, Gemini, Copilot) and their admin, audit, and compliance controls.
  • Building MCP servers, agents, or other AI tooling for security workflows.
  • Familiarity with SSPM, enterprise browser security, and DLP or CASB tooling.
  • Supporting audits and compliance programs such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, or FedRAMP.
  • Experience in a remote-first company of similar size and growth stage.
  • Open Source contributions or automation projects.
  • Security conference talks or published research.

About the job

Big systems. Exciting challenges. Real impact. As a Software Engineer II (Android), you'll build the Android experiences that let restaurants and shops complete their onboarding on their own, without waiting for anyone. You'll ship across white-labelled brands in more than 60 countries, and you'll see the result of your work in how fast new vendors go live and start receiving orders.

The Self Onboarding squad (Tribe VSO) owns the tools that let vendors, from restaurants to shops, complete their processes on their own and become active on our platform quickly and easily. We help vendors onboard in more than 60 countries around the world.

As part of our Vendor Team, you'll be the driving force behind the success of thousands of restaurants, shops, and local businesses. Your contributions will empower vendors with advanced tools to manage their operations, boosting their visibility and reach. Every feature you help build will create growth opportunities for businesses of all sizes, strengthening Delivery Hero's ecosystem and impact.

  • Build Android features for vendor self-onboarding in Kotlin, owning multiple features within your squad.
  • Adapt features for white-labelled brands so they work across our markets.
  • Write unit, integration and screenshot tests that protect quality across brands.
  • Maintain CI/CD pipelines that release changes to vendors safely.
  • Monitor features in production and trace issues to their root cause.
  • Contribute to code reviews, design discussions and architectural decisions for the features you build.
  • Hands-on mobile development experience, with Android as your main platform.
  • Solid Kotlin skills.
  • Experience writing automated tests, including screenshot testing.
  • Familiarity with monitoring and observability for production apps.
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines for mobile releases.
  • Experience building or maintaining white-label apps.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Jetpack Compose.
  • Exposure to Flutter or other cross-platform frameworks.
  • Understanding of secure mobile coding practices (e.g. OWASP Mobile Top 10).
  • Experience with backend development in Kotlin and / or Go.

Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles.

Your role at Hostinger

Hostinger’s Finance team tracks the KPIs that run the business and turns them into decisions. Working with Product, Marketing and Revenue teams, we focus on growth and on investing it wisely.
Our database holds very detailed data, and AI tools connect to it directly, so analysis is about finding what matters rather than wrangling data. In this role you’ll see the whole business up close. Expect to help shape the next generation of our forecast model, working with the team to rebuild and continuously improve it with AI. Another part of the job is preparing Board materials on core KPI performance and its drivers, and digging into why things moved when they do.
Together we’re building infrastructure where AI agents run reconciliations and anomaly checks. Your job is to review what they flag and decide what the business needs to hear. Along the way, SaaS metrics and what drives a company’s valuation become second nature, and you become part of the team behind Hostinger’s growth.

Your day-to-day

  • Own performance KPIs. Keep KPI reporting accurate and structured so we can see right away when something is off plan or could be doing better.
  • Build agents and self-service reporting. Use AI tools that query the database directly, so manual technical work shrinks. Your time goes into asking the right questions, choosing how best to show the data and getting the message across. You’ll also keep the data consistent with how the business actually works.
  • Forecast. Make the forecast as accurate as you can, then keep improving the model by comparing it with actuals and learning from each miss.
  • Set targets. Help the business set smart quarterly and annual targets, then track progress against them together with the teams.
  • Shape your own scope. Your work can grow with you. You might partner directly with a business unit and become its finance counterpart.

Your expertise and experience

  • We care more about mindset than title. You’re commercially curious, analytically sharp and a clear communicator: someone who wants to understand the business, not just report on it.
  • You’re comfortable with data, can spot when something doesn’t add up, and know how to explain what you found in plain language.
  • You work well across teams, pick things up fast, and use AI tools to move quicker, not as a substitute for thinking.
  • You’re excited to work with AI tools and help build the data infrastructure behind our numbers.
  • You’re curious about why numbers move, not just what they are.
  • 3+ years in FP&A, financial analysis, consulting or a similar analytical role.
  • Fluent English.

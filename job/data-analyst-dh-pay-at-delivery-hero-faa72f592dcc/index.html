About the job

We are now looking for a Data Analyst who will own the data behind our payment and compliance operations: reliable datasets, reconciliations and reporting across all of DH Pay while having the opportunity to dive into the payments ecosystem of one of the biggest delivery platform ecosystems globally.

You will act as the interconnection point, bringing together data from multiple systems and turn it into clear reporting and recommendations, highlighting business areas and providing answers for senior stakeholders. You will work with Compliance, Operations, Finance, Risk, Product and Group BI teams. Reporting to the Project Office manager, the position will also provide the opportunity for the right candidate to get an overview of the innerworks of a licensed Payment Institution having large scale operations across multiple European countries. The role is based in our Athens office.

What you’ll do

  • Own and lead analysis across all DH Pay, delivering structured presentations and clear recommendations to Senior Management.
  • Build and maintain dashboards and reports (SQL, BI tools) that give stakeholders a reliable view of KYB, payment, compliance and operational KPIs across platforms and countries, while defining KPIs with stakeholders, to measure what matters.
  • Analyze payment performance, explain performance drivers, diagnose root causes and proactively identify opportunities across products and countries, and turn findings into clear recommendations, challenging assumptions where needed.
  • Reconcile data from multiple platforms, including external vendor usage reports against actual system usage, and investigate and document discrepancies (Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Looker).
  • Prepare analysis-ready datasets by resolving duplicates, inconsistent coding and missing fields, documenting assumptions and data limitations, and maintaining shared data definitions.
  • Support compliance reporting by providing data and reporting for KYB, AML and regulatory requirements to Compliance and Operations.
  • Define data requirements by specifying data needs, extraction and automated flows with Group & local BI teams and engineering, and validate data during UAT.
  • Size the impact of improvements and roadmap initiatives by quantifying costs, savings and success metrics.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Information Systems, Economics, Statistics or a related field
  • 3-5 years as a Data Analyst, BI Analyst or similar, ideally in payments, FinTech, banking or other regulated environment
  • Strong SQL and spreadsheet skills (Excel or Google Sheets), and experience with BI tools (e.g. Looker, Tableau)
  • Hands-on experience managing, reconciling and analyzing large datasets
  • Ability to define data requirements and document data definitions and assumptions
  • Presentation skills, good written and verbal communication, ability to explain findings and data limitations, adapting the message for technical and non-technical audiences
  • Structured problem-solving and high attention to detail, with the ability to take an ambiguous question, form a hypothesis and iterate to an answer without perfect requirements
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment and balance multiple priorities
  • Excellent command of Greek and English (written and spoken).

Nice to have:

  • Understanding of payment ecosystems: cards, wallets, local payment methods and settlement
  • Experience with reconciliation, payouts, or fraud/risk analytics
  • Familiarity with KYC/KYB, and/or AML and/or compliance processes and/or fraud & risk analytics
  • Python or R for analysis, including segmentation and regression

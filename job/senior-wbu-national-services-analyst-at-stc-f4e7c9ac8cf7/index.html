Senior WBU National Services Analyst

stc · SA, Saudi Arabia

Onsite · Senior · Data Analyst · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on stc's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Job Purpose

The role holder is responsible for assisting in the profitability evaluation and financial analysis of C&WC’s commercial activities, supporting performance reviews, business case assessments, and strategic decision-making. This includes analyzing financial trends, pricing strategies, product mix, and retention programs to identify opportunities for cost optimization and profitability enhancement. The role holder shall carry out his duties in accordance with the stipulated business policies and procedure.

Job Responsibility

· Assists in evaluating the profitability of C&WC’s commercial activities, supporting performance measurement outside the budgeting cycle and sharing results with management.

· Supports the analysis of financial trends across C&WC, highlighting key areas for improvement for the respective VP/CxO.

· Provides support in reviewing C&WC pricing, promotions, discounts, commissions, and agreements, and helps recommend corrective measures to improve profitability for stc Group.

· Assists in analyzing C&WC services/products mix and provides recommendations for optimizing costs from a commercial perspective for National C&WC.

Job Responsibility (cont.)

· Assists in ensuring that C&WC’s proposed retention programs are in line with prevailing policies and bring value to the company.

· Supports the use of neutral sources of information and data analytics to enhance decision quality for evaluating business cases and strategic initiatives.

· Assists in reviewing and supporting market-facing business cases during and outside the budgeting cycle for National products and services.

· Assist in conducting post-performance reviews on revenue-related key C&WC projects based on management direction for National products and services.

Years of Experience

2 - 4 Years

Nature of Experience

Prior experience in WBU National Services and specifically in the telecommunication function is strongly preferred.

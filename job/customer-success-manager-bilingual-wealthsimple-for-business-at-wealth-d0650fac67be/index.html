About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Build something people love

Wealthsimple is Canada’s leading financial innovator. The company offers a full suite of simple, sophisticated financial products across managed investing, do-it-yourself trading, cryptocurrency, tax filing, spending and saving. Wealthsimple currently serves more than 4 million Canadians and holds over $169 billion in assets under administration. The company was founded in 2014 by a team of financial experts and technology entrepreneurs, and is headquartered in Toronto, Canada.

We're proud of what we've built — and we're just getting started. Read our Culture Manual and learn more about how we work.

About Wealthsimple for Business

Wealthsimple Business (WB) is a modern group savings solution that makes it easy for companies to offer financial wellness benefits that employees will actually use. WB uses our award-winning Invest platform to deliver a fully digital experience, with low fees and more inclusive options for employees, and dedicated support for employers. Plus, our simple admin portal lets employers manage employees and plans with just a few clicks.

We have built a foundation in core areas of our business and are looking for a Client Success Manager to join our team to help us scale and grow.

What you'll do

  • Orchestrate seamless onboarding experiences for new plan sponsors, establishing strong foundations for long-term partnerships.
  • Expertly manage HRIS/payroll integration processes, ensuring accurate data flow and system compatibility.
  • Drive industry-leading employee plan adoption rates through strategic communication campaigns and engaging educational initiatives.
  • Lead complex plan transfers from legacy providers with precision and care, coordinating cross-functional teams while providing clients with transparent milestone updates.
  • Empower plan administrators through comprehensive platform training, equipping them with the knowledge to maximize their Wealthsimple tools.
  • Support clients with plan governance — ensuring that plan documentation, processes, and communications align with legislative requirements and best practices.
  • Provide insights and reporting on plan health, participation, and key success metrics.
  • Cultivate strong client relationships through proactive service, anticipating needs and delivering personalized support.
  • Resolve technical issues with urgency and ownership, transforming challenges into opportunities to demonstrate our commitment to excellence.
  • Think strategically and put our Clients at the center of your work, making sure they are well taken care of, informed, and satisfied with the platform and related services.
  • Contribute to process improvements and internal initiatives that enhance client experience and operational efficiency.

What you bring

  • 5+ years of experience managing a large book of business while providing best in class Client relationships, matching problem solving skills with a deep Client empathy.
  • Excellent presentation skills and are able to adjust communications and support to different audiences and their needs.
  • Superior time management, task prioritization, organizational and communication skills.
  • Bilingual – fluent in both writing and speaking in French.
  • Experience in Group Savings & Retirement / Pension industry is preferred but not required.

About the job

Key Responsibilities

Technical & Clinical Responsibilities

  • Partner with business stakeholders to gather and translate requirements into effective visualization and reporting solutions.
  • Design, develop, validate, and maintain dashboards and analytical solutions using clinical and operational data.
  • Profile, map, integrate, and transform complex datasets from multiple clinical systems and sources.
  • Deliver high-quality visualizations that provide actionable insights for clinical development and study oversight.
  • Apply clinical domain knowledge to ensure visual outputs accurately represent clinical trial data and support decision-making.
  • Ensure compliance with data governance, privacy, information security, and regulatory standards.
  • Communicate technical concepts, assumptions, limitations, and findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Support information lifecycle management activities across visualization platforms and reporting environments.
  • Develop expertise in therapeutic areas, clinical trial methodologies, and clinical data standards.

People Management Responsibilities

  • Provide direct line management support to team members.
  • Conduct performance management, coaching, mentoring, and career development discussions.
  • Support workforce planning, onboarding, and employee engagement activities.
  • Foster a collaborative, high-performing team culture.
  • Ensure team adherence to quality standards, timelines, and delivery expectations.
  • Support capability development and knowledge sharing across the visualization team.

Required Experience

  • Minimum 5+ years of experience in Clinical Data Visualization, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Clinical Analytics, or a related field.
  • Minimum 1+ year of direct Line Management experience, including people leadership, performance management, coaching, mentoring, and team development responsibilities.
  • Experience working within a Pharmaceutical, Biotechnology, CRO, or Clinical Research environment.
  • Strong understanding of clinical trials, clinical development processes, and clinical data.
  • Proven experience delivering data visualization and reporting solutions to support clinical studies and drug development programs.
  • Hands-on experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI and/or Tableau.
  • Programming experience in R and/or Python for data processing, analysis, and visualization.
  • Proficiency in at least two of the following technologies: Power BI, Tableau, Python, and R.
  • Experience working with cross-functional stakeholders and translating business requirements into technical solutions.
  • Familiarity with SQL is highly desirable.

Education & Background Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Bioinformatics, Statistics, Quantitative Methods, Computer Science, or a Life Sciences discipline such as Biotechnology, Molecular Biology, or a related field.
  • Fluent written and spoken English.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management, and interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking capabilities.
  • High attention to detail with a focus on quality and accuracy.
  • Ability to leverage data and analytics to address business challenges and generate meaningful insights for clinical and medical stakeholders.
  • Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment..

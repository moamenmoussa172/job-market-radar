About the job

The ADAS division develops innovative components, systems and functions in the field of vehicle safety, vehicle dynamics and driver assistance. Through the combination of systems based on cameras, radar and ultrasonic sensors, electric power steering and active or passive safety systems we not only make driving safer and more comfortable today but we are also building the basis for the autonomous driving of tomorrow.

Your contribution to something big

► Creating new software functions that contribute to a safer and more comfortable driving experience, based on our vision, industry trends, and customer requirements.

► Applying both waterfall and agile engineering methods across the full lifecycle, from requirements engineering and design to implementation and verification.

► Cooperating closely with global engineering disciplines such as systems, electronics, and mechanics.

What distinguishes you

►Active Bsc/Msc ongoing studies in computer science, automation, electronics, telecommunications, informatics, mathematics or comparable in Hungary for at least one more year

►C/C++ programming skills

►Interest in embedded technologies

►Experience with embedded software development or with microcontrollers, including university projects or thesis research is a plus

►Good problem solving skills and analytic mindset

►Initiative and creative personality

►Good English and/or German language knowledge

► Weekly minimum 20 hours

A Day in the Life

As part of the Cardiovascular Digital Twin Network (CDTnet), this position contributes to collaborative research at the intersection of cardiovascular physiology, sensing technologies, computational modeling, and artificial intelligence to enable more personalized and preventive healthcare.
This role is based in Maastricht, the Netherlands, and follows a Flex work model, combining on-site collaboration with remote work opportunities. Travel within Europe may be required to support collaboration with academic, clinical, and industrial partners across the CDTnet consortium.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Conduct multidisciplinary research in cardiovascular physiology, sensing technologies, and Digital Twin applications.
  • Design, execute, and analyze experimental studies supporting the development and validation of novel sensing approaches.
  • Develop and apply signal processing, data analysis, and computational modeling methods to physiological datasets.
  • Integrate experimental measurements with mathematical and mechanistic cardiovascular models.
  • Collaborate with academic, clinical, and industrial partners across the CDTnet research network.
  • Interpret research findings and translate results toward clinically relevant applications.
  • Present research outcomes through technical reports, scientific publications, and conference presentations.
  • Contribute to the planning and execution of research activities within cross-functional project teams.

Required Qualifications

  • Master's degree in Biomedical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Medical Physics, Computational Science, or related quantitative discipline.
  • Experience with experimental research methods, data analysis, computational modeling, programming, or signal processing through academic, research, or industry projects.
  • Knowledge of quantitative analysis techniques and scientific research methodologies.
  • Fluent written and spoken English.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of cardiovascular physiology, cardiac sensing, or cardiac pacing technologies.
  • Experience with mathematical, mechanistic, or physiological modeling.
  • Experience programming in Python, MATLAB, R, or similar scientific computing environments.
  • Experience working in multidisciplinary research collaborations involving academic and industry partners.
  • Publication, thesis, or research project experience in biomedical engineering, computational science, or cardiovascular research.

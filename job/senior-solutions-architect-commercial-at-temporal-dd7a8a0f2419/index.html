About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About Temporal

Distributed systems fail in ordinary ways: services crash, networks drop, and long-running processes get cut off halfway. Engineers end up writing retries, state tracking, and recovery code instead of building their product. Temporal removes that work. With Durable Execution, code runs to completion through any failure, so teams ship reliable applications faster.
The need is growing fastest in AI, where agents run for hours or days and depend on services that fail. That’s why OpenAI, Cursor, Lovable, Stripe, and Snap run critical workloads on Temporal, and why we raised a Series E at a $12.55B valuation. Our founders, Maxim Fateev and Samar Abbas, built AWS Simple Workflow Service and the foundation of Azure Durable Functions, then co-created Cadence at Uber before starting Temporal.

Summary

We’re hiring two Senior Solutions Architects for our Commercial team: one focused on new logo acquisition, one on growth and expansion within our existing customer base.

  • New Logo. You thrive in the early stages of a sales cycle. You’re energized by discovery, whiteboarding with skeptical engineers, and earning technical trust in competitive deals. You turn “we’ve never heard of Temporal” into “we’re building on Temporal.”
  • Growth and Expansion. You’re at your best once a customer is live. You understand their architecture, spot where Temporal can go deeper, and partner with the account team to unlock new workloads, teams, and use cases. You turn one workflow into an enterprise-wide standard.

In either role, you’re the technical lead on your deals, partnering with Account Executives and working with developers, architects, and engineering leaders at fast-moving companies. Temporal Cloud is consumption-based, so we succeed when customers grow their adoption of Temporal Cloud, and your primary goal is driving that consumption. This is a sales role for technical people: you love solving customer problems, and you want the deal to close.


What you’ll do

  • Partner with AEs to qualify opportunities and run technical evaluations, from discovery through proof of concept to a decision
  • Lead technical conversations with developers, architects, and engineering leaders to understand their systems, constraints, and goals
  • Find and win new workloads, teams, and use cases inside existing customers
  • Build focused demos and proofs of concept, including orchestration of AI and agent workloads
  • Help customer engineering teams adopt and expand their Temporal usage
  • Build tools, automations, and reference implementations that elevate the team around you
  • Keep changing how we work: automate research, notes, and follow-ups, and share what works
  • Teach AEs Temporal fundamentals and how to spot a strong opportunity
  • Bring field insight back to Product and Engineering


What you’ll bring

  • New Logo: San Francisco Bay Area or Pacific time zone preferred
  • Growth and Expansion: Open to all US time zones
  • Customer-facing engineering experience. You’ve been a solutions architect, or you’re a software engineer ready to work directly with customers
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals. You can build a working proof of concept yourself in Go, Java, TypeScript, or Python
  • Deep distributed systems knowledge. You understand failure modes, retries, idempotency, state, and observability
  • A hunger to win deals. You qualify hard and measure yourself by outcomes
  • Strong communicator and collaborator. You’re the glue between customers and our internal teams, keeping Sales, Product, Engineering, and Support aligned
  • Trusted technical advisor. You earn credibility with engineers, including telling them when Temporal may not be the right fit
  • Understanding of AI agents and LLMs. You know how agents and LLM pipelines fail in production, and why that matters for orchestration, or you can learn it fast
  • Comfort building in a hyper-growth company. Where structure is thin, you create it
  • A drive to reinvent how we work. You use AI tools to automate and streamline your own work, and you share what’s working with the team

Nice to haves

  • Experience with Temporal or other workflow and orchestration tools
  • Experience selling a commercial product with an open source alternative (eg: Redis, Confluent, or Databricks)

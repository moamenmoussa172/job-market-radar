Position Title: SAP iXp Intern - Product Management, Event Technology & Digital Platforms

Location: Newtown Square, PA

Anticipated Start Date: October 2026

Contract Duration: 6 months

Work Hours: Full-time – 40 hours per week

As a Product Management Intern within the Digital Platforms team, you will be at the forefront of how enterprise events are planned, delivered, and measured at scale. You will support the "Build & Run" operations for SAP's self-service event platform and T1 event digital experiences — from registration and content delivery to post-event analytics. This role is designed for a high-potential individual who wants to master the intersection of Event Technology, SAFe Agile delivery, and AI-driven audience engagement.

Key Product Responsibilities

Event Platform Operations: Support day-to-day run operations of SAP's self-service event platform, coordinating production schedules and ensuring digital event assets meet SAP's standards for quality, governance, and metadata accuracy.

Requirements & Roadmap Support: Translate event stakeholder needs into clear product requirements — documenting user stories, acceptance criteria, and feature specs for platform enhancements across registration, streaming, and engagement tooling.

Data-Driven Insights: Utilize event analytics to track platform performance, attendee engagement metrics, and session conversion rates — moving from reporting to actionable analysis.

AI & Personalization Integration: Assist in the deployment and monitoring of AI-driven features — such as personalized agenda recommendations and intelligent content surfacing — to improve attendee experience across T1 and self-service events.

Agile Value Delivery: Actively participate in SAFe ceremonies — sprint planning, backlog refinement, and retrospectives. Help manage the Team Backlog with a focus on user stories tied to event platform capabilities and attendee journey improvements.

Governance & Responsible AI: Assist in audits to ensure all event platform AI features adhere to SAP's Responsible AI standards, with a focus on data privacy, attendee consent, and transparency.

Digital Transformation: Support the rollout of industry-leading event technology integrations — including Adobe Experience Platform (AEP), CMS-driven event microsites, and campaign environments tied to SAP's flagship events.

What You’ll Bring

A curious and agile mindset, with a passion for learning and the resilience to adapt in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Emotional intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and thoughtful stakeholder engagement. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies — especially artificial intelligence — paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take initiative.

Education: Pursuing a degree in Marketing Technology, Business, Computer Science, Digital Transformation, or a related field.

Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, or recently graduated (start date must be within 6 months of graduation date) from a coding academy/bootcamp, apprenticeship, associate, bachelor’s, master’s or JD/PhD program.

Core Skills & Qualifications

Methodology: Basic understanding of Agile/Scrum or SAFe 6.0 and how sprints move a product from vision to launch.

Event Tech Literacy: Familiarity with event management platforms (e.g., Cvent, ON24, Hopin, or similar) or enterprise-grade CMS and marketing automation platforms.

Platform Literacy: Exposure to Adobe Experience Cloud (AEP, AEM, Analytics) or comparable enterprise MarTech stack is a plus.

Technical Tools: Proficiency in Jira/Confluence for workflow and backlog management; Figma for reviewing UX/prototype designs.

Communication: Ability to translate event business requirements into clear technical specifications when working with engineers, designers, and cross-functional stakeholders globally.

Beyond core skills, we want someone who:

  • Has a digital-first mindset and a genuine curiosity for how technology shapes live and virtual event experiences
  • Is proactive, organized, and comfortable managing multiple deliverables under real event deadlines
  • Can collaborate across global teams — from field marketing to engineering
  • Has strong attention to detail, especially around data accuracy and content quality
  • Is eager to learn and willing to grow into the evolving event tech landscape
  • Is proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) with basic knowledge of design and web production tools
  • Demonstrates Cognitive Empathy — the ability to map attendee journeys and understand how digital touchpoints shape the event experience

Where You Belong

The Digital Platforms team manages the technology and programs that power SAP's most visible digital properties and flagship events. We own the self-service event platform, T1 event digital experiences, and the underlying MarTech stack that connects events to pipeline.

We are "Customer Zero" for SAP's own Business AI — applying it first to our own event and digital platforms before it reaches the market. By joining us, you're not just supporting events — you're helping build the next generation of intelligent, data-driven event experiences.

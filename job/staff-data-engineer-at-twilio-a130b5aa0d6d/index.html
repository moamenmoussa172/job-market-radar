About the job

This position is needed to design, build, and optimize Twilio's next-generation unified data platforms and services to power strategic, data-driven business decisions.

As a senior technical leader on our Enterprise Data Engineering team, you will drive a massive, highly visible transformation to unify disparate data lakes into a single, high-performance platform. By combining classic dimensional modeling with cutting-edge AI, you will accelerate data delivery at an immense scale. This is your chance to solve complex architectural challenges, mentor rising talent, and directly influence Twilio’s strategic direction.

Responsibilities

In this role, you’ll:

  • Architect and scale high-performance data platforms to unify acquired data lakes, improving delivery speed by leveraging AI-driven workflows.
  • Design robust ETL pipelines and data integration frameworks using Spark, dbt, and AWS to support rapidly growing enterprise data needs.
  • Establish rigorous data modeling standards (Dimensional, Medallion) and implement full-stack observability to ensure data trust.
  • Mentor peer engineers, promoting technical excellence and living our We > Me value through active collaboration with architects and product teams.

Qualifications

Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply. If your career is just starting or hasn't followed a traditional path, don't let that stop you from considering Twilio. We are always looking for people who will bring something new to the table!

Required:

  • 8+ years of professional data engineering experience with progressive technical leadership.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Advanced proficiency in languages, including scripting (e.g. Python, Scala, Java) and expert-level SQL.
  • Deep hands-on experience designing AWS cloud data infrastructure and scalable ETL pipelines (using Spark, dbt, or AWS Glue).
  • Expert knowledge of data modeling (Dimensional Modeling, Lakehouse/Medallion architectures).
  • Strong experience with distributed databases and query engines (such as Snowflake, Redshift, Athena, or Presto).
  • Proven track record of peer mentoring and collaborating with cross-functional teams.

Desired:

  • Master's degree in Computer Science.
  • Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines (Buildkite, ArgoCD).
  • Hands-on experience with ingestion systems (Kafka, Debezium, Meltano) and modern data formats (Parquet, Iceberg, Hudi).
  • Familiarity with relational/OLTP databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and OLAP databases (Clickhouse, Druid).
  • Experience leveraging Generative AI (LLMs, Langchain, Bedrock) to accelerate data delivery.

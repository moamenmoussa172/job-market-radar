About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Under the functional responsibility of the CRM CoE Manager:

  • Custom Development: Develop high-quality, maintainable code using Apex Classes, Triggers, Flows, Lightning Web Components (LWC), and Visualforce.
  • Integration Management: Build and maintain secure integrations between Salesforce and third-party systems using REST/SOAP APIs and middleware tools.
  • Declarative Excellence: Utilize advanced Flow Builder logic to minimize custom code where native functionality suffices.
  • Code Review & Mentorship: Conduct rigorous code reviews with the Technical Lead to ensure quality standards
  • Environment Management: Manage the CI/CD pipeline and deployment processes (using tools like SFDX, Copado, or Gearset) across various sandboxes.
  • Provides training to the users to help them using the CRM tool in a continuous improvement mindset
  • Give insights and proposals for evolution of the application based on new features proposed by the editor or by users experience/feedbacks

Education/Training

Required: Bachelor degree and/or Master degree in computer science or engineering.

Salesforce Administration Certification (Min ADM201 - Ideally ADX201)

ProfessionalExperience

Experience: Minimum of 4 years of professional experience in Salesforce Development.

Technical Skills: Mastery of Apex, LWC, SOQL,...

Certifications: Salesforce Platform Developer I & II are highly preferred.

Soft Skills: Excellent communication skills in English, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

Any other Salesforce certification will be appreciated

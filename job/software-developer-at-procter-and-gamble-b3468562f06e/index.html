About the job

Job Location

COBALT PARK GO

Job Description

Do you want to further your tech career with a company consistently recognised as one of the UK’s leading graduate employers? Do you have a strong interest in Software Engineering? Then this Software Developer role is the perfect opportunity for you!

Procter & Gamble is looking to develop the next generation of leaders in the world of technology. We are a team of IT professionals who cultivate and apply exceptional expertise to deliver ground-breaking capabilities. We are currently looking for Software Developers to develop and support solutions for our Global Business Services organisation in Cobalt Park, Newcastle.

Key Responsibilities:

P&G Information Technology is where business, technology, and innovation come together to create competitive advantage for P&G. We are focused on harnessing technology to better serve consumers, customers, and employees. An IT career in P&G gives you the chance to leverage big data and analytics, secure digital assets, and build strategic applications to grow our business with billons of consumers.

As a Software Developer you will:

  • Collaborate with key technology vendors and internal customers to deliver strategic solutions.
  • Liaise with your clients and translate their business objectives into functional and beneficial technology whilst continuously seeking areas to improve efficiencies and share best practices.
  • Be relied upon to make recommendations based on emerging trends, expansion opportunities, and information security threats.
  • Develop future facing applications focused on re-application and integration.

What We Offer You:

  • Responsibility from Day 1 – You will feel the ownership of your work from the beginning, with real projects and responsibilities to lead.
  • Continuous coaching – You will work with passionate people and receive both formal training as well as day-to-day mentoring from your manager.
  • Dynamic and respectful work environment – Employees are at the core, we value every individual and encourage initiatives, promoting agility and work/life balance.
  • We offer a competitive compensation and benefits package - P&G take a Total Rewards approach meaning on top of your salary you can expect a whole range of benefits including: Double Match Pension Scheme; Private Medical Insurance; P&G Contributed Share Programme; Flexible Working Arrangements and much more.

Job Qualifications

What We Look For:

  • Proven, hands-on experience as a Software Developer in a professional environment
  • Have good communication skills including the ability to build strong work relationships
  • Learn on the go and develop a diverse skills and wide range of technologies
  • Manage your own workload and achieve targets across multiple projects
  • Liaise with clients and translate their business requirements and objectives into functional technology that benefits their organisation
  • Develop, evaluate, and test fit-for-purpose applications
  • Support users
  • Facilitate disaster recovery and business continuity
  • Effective communication, including: Excellent verbal and written English business skills and confidence communicating with stakeholders from entry level to upper management

Preferred skills:

  • Python / Object Oriented Programming
  • Modern Web Stack
  • Experience using APIs (Soap, Rest, etc.)
  • Cl/CD Experience
  • Database Engineering

Bonus skills:

  • .NET / SQL /
  • Cloud Experience – Azure / AWS or Similar
  • UI Design
  • Experience writing VBA and TypeScript (Angular)
  • Experience using Jira / GitHub / Docker
  • Experience with Enterprise Data Systems

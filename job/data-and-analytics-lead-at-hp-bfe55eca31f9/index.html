About the job

Data & Analytics Lead

Description -

Job Summary

HP is seeking a highly skilled Data & Analytics Lead to design, develop, and modernize data and analytics solutions that support business decision-making across digital support, customer experience, and commercial operations. The role combines data analytics, data engineering, business intelligence, and business partnership to turn complex data into clear, actionable insights.

The ideal candidate will serve as a trusted partner to business stakeholders, translating complex business needs into scalable data and analytics solutions while driving reporting modernization, data quality, automation, and self-service analytics across modern data platforms.

Responsibilities

Analytics & Insights Leadership

  • Develop executive level insights, dashboards, scorecards, and performance reporting using Power BI, ThoughtSpot, and other enterprise analytics platforms.
  • Perform deep-dive analyses using SQL, Python, and other analytical tools to support strategic and operational decision-making.
  • Lead advanced analytics to identify business trends, performance drivers, opportunities, and risks, translating data into actionable insights.
  • Translate ambiguous business requirements into structured, scalable analytical solutions and data products.
  • Partner with business stakeholders to understand analytical needs, communicate findings, and drive adoption of data-driven solutions.

Data Engineering & Modern Analytics Platforms

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines, data models, and analytical datasets supporting reporting and analytics use cases.
  • Build and optimize ETL/ELT processes and data pipelines using modern cloud and analytics environments such as Databricks, Snowflake, Azure platforms.
  • Develop and maintain analytical solutions using SQL, Python/PySpark, GitHub, and CI/CD practices.
  • Support the modernization and migration of legacy reporting and data processes to scalable cloud-based architectures and modern analytics environments.
  • Driving data quality, validation, monitoring, and reliability, ensuring trusted data is available for business reporting and decision-making.

Reporting & Automation

  • Modernize reporting capabilities using Power BI, ThoughtSpot, and other enterprise business intelligence and analytics tools.
  • Automate recurring data ingestion, transformation, reporting, validation, and monitoring processes to improve efficiency and reliability.
  • Build scalable self-service analytics solutions that enable business stakeholders to access and explore trusted data independently.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify and automate manual reporting processes, improving speed, scalability, and data quality.
  • Establish sustainable reporting solutions through standardized data models, reusable analytical components, and effective documentation.

Knowledge & Skills

Required

  • Advanced experience in Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, and Data Analytics, including the development of scalable analytics and reporting solutions.
  • Strong knowledge of data modeling, ETL/ELT processes, CI/CD (Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment) practices, and data quality management.
  • Experience with Databricks, Snowflake, Azure, GCP, AWS, or comparable cloud analytics and data warehouse environments.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL and experience with enterprise analytics and reporting platforms such as Power BI and ThoughtSpot.
  • Proficiency in Python, with working knowledge of PySpark for data processing and analytics.
  • Experience developing, maintaining, and monitoring data pipelines and scheduled data-processing jobs.
  • Experience using GitHub or similar version-control platforms to manage and collaborate on analytics or data engineering solutions.
  • Experience building and maintaining scalable analytics solutions in enterprise environments.
  • Ability to translate ambiguous business requirements into structured analytical and technical solutions.
  • Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration skills.
  • Strong presentation and data storytelling skills, with the ability to communicate complex insights to technical and non-technical audiences.

Preferred

  • Experience with project management fundamentals and best practices, including leading or coordinating analytics, reporting, or data related projects.
  • Experience working in Agile delivery environments and collaborating with business and technical teams.
  • Knowledge of enterprise data lake, lakehouse, and modern cloud data architectures.
  • Experience with automation frameworks and modern data engineering development practices.
  • Relevant certifications or advanced training in data analytics, cloud data platforms, Business Intelligence, or data engineering.

Soft Skills

  • Takes ownership, shows initiative, and delivers high-quality results while adapting to changing priorities and challenges.
  • Builds strong relationships with business and technical stakeholders, aligns expectations, and collaborates to deliver effective analytics solutions.
  • Communicates complex data and insights clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Builds effective and trusted relationships with business and technical stakeholders.
  • Works collaboratively, shares knowledge, supports colleagues, and contributes to common team goals.

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or related field.
  • Typically 5+ years of experience in analytics, business intelligence, data engineering, or related disciplines.
  • Demonstrated success delivering enterprise-scale analytics and transformation initiatives.

Impact & Scope

  • Influences strategic business decisions through analytics and insights.
  • Leads complex cross-functional analytics initiatives.
  • Drives modernization of enterprise reporting and data platforms.
  • Improves operational efficiency through automation and scalable solutions.
  • Serves as a recognized subject matter expert in data, analytics, and business intelligence.

Success Metrics

  • Increased business adoption of analytics solutions.
  • Reduction in manual reporting effort through automation.
  • Improved data quality and reporting accuracy.
  • Delivery of actionable insights that influence business outcomes.
  • Successful execution of data modernization and transformation initiatives.

About the job

Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

About the team + role

Robinhood’s Credit Card & Banking Product Engineering team conceptualizes, designs and builds our entire customer-facing product: from mobile application to underlying backend systems. Team members are trusted with full ownership over our innovative features and drive them from design to launch.

As a Senior Full Stack Engineer on Credit Card & Banking, you'll be expected to operate with outsized ownership and impact, leading high-leverage projects across our mobile app, backend services, and financial partner integrations. You'll partner closely with product, design, and peer engineering teams to strengthen the systems and technical foundations that let our product scale to millions of customers. The right candidate is a force multiplier: someone who drives ambiguous initiatives, shapes architecture across the stack, and raises the bar on execution for the whole team.

What you’ll do

  • Own the technical direction for major parts of Robinhood's Credit Card & Banking product, from mobile app to backend services, and drive them from early design through launch and iteration.
  • Lead the design of scalable, reliable systems that serve millions of users and integrate with world-class financial partners.
  • Break down ambiguous, cross-functional problems into clear technical plans, and align engineers, Product, and Design around them.
  • Set the bar for engineering quality through design reviews, code reviews, and best practices in reliability, security, and performance.
  • Mentor and grow the engineers around you, and raise the technical level of the whole team.
  • Partner with Product and Design to shape the roadmap, bringing an engineering view on tradeoffs, sequencing, and long-term architecture.

What you bring

  • 6+ years of professional software engineering experience building customer-facing applications, including significant full stack work across frontend and backend.
  • Deep expertise with React / React Native, and a track record of shipping polished, high-quality mobile or web experiences at scale.
  • Strong backend and distributed systems experience: designing APIs, services, and data models that are reliable and built to grow.
  • A history of technical leadership: leading multi-engineer projects end to end, making sound architecture decisions, and delivering results in fast-moving environments.
  • Strong product sense, and comfort influencing PMs, designers, and engineering leaders.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, and experience mentoring other engineers.
  • Bonus: experience in fintech, payments, banking, or other highly regulated domains.

What we offer

  • Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career
  • Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching
  • Best-in-class benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents
  • Lifestyle wallet – a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more
  • Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits
  • Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more
  • Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces

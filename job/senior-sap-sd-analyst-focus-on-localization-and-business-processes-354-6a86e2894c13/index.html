SENIOR SAP SD ANALYST – FOCUS ON LOCALIZATION & BUSINESS PROCESSES (35459)

Onsite · Senior · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 17 November 2026

ABAPAgile / ScrumSAP S/4HANATesting practices (TDD)

About the job

Key Responsibilities:

  • Act as the regional SAP SD, providing guidance and support for business teams and junior consultants.
  • Provide advanced SAP SD expertise across Sales, Shipping, Billing, Pricing, Output Management, and
  • Integration processes, ensuring robust and scalable business solutions.
  • Lead workshops and discussions with business stakeholders to define future-state processes and identify opportunities for standardization and optimization.
  • Support SAP S/4HANA transformation initiatives, ensuring alignment between business requirements and global SAP standards.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives across Order-to-Cash (O2C) processes, identifying opportunities to increase efficiency, automation, and process compliance.
  • Ensure compliance with local legal, fiscal, and regulatory requirements, particularly for Brazilian business operations.
  • Coordinate and support system testing activities, including unit testing, integration testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), and hypercare support.
  • Provide functional specifications and collaborate with ABAP, Integration, and Basis teams during solution development and deployment.
  • Support incident resolution and root cause analysis for complex SAP SD and cross-functional issues
  • in productive environments.
  • Participate in global governance discussions and contribute to the definition of SAP standards, templates, and best practices.
  • Mentor junior consultants and key users, promoting knowledge sharing and capability development across the organization.
  • Evaluate emerging technologies, AI solutions, and SAP innovations that can enhance business processes and digital transformation initiatives.
  • Drive innovation by exploring and adopting AI-powered solutions that improve user experience, process efficiency, data analysis, and business insights across the SAP landscape.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Professional qualifications in field area relating to Information Technologies;
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA implementations, migrations, or transformation project;
  • Experience with SAP integration technologies and interfaces involving customers, suppliers, logistics providers, and third-party applications;
  • Knowledge of Brazilian localization, including taxation, electronic invoicing, and regulatory compliance requirements;
  • Experience supporting or implementing solutions related to the Brazilian Tax Reform;
  • Understanding of SAP FI, MM, WM/EWM, TM, and PP integration points within end-to-end business processes;
  • Experience working in global, multicultural environments and international projects;
  • Knowledge of Agile methodologies and project delivery frameworks;
  • Experience with SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), SAP Business AI, or other SAP innovation solutions;
  • Familiarity with Artificial Intelligence, intelligent automation, process mining, and digital transformation initiatives;
  • Basic understanding of ABAP debugging and interaction with development teams;
  • Experience in process redesign, business process optimization, and continuous improvement initiatives;
  • Fluent English;

About the job

  • Manage Automation & AI use cases end-to-end, from initial idea and requirements definition through assessment, prioritization, development, testing, implementation and rollout.
  • Plan and coordinate projects, including scope, milestones, dependencies, resources, risks and stakeholder alignment, ensuring transparent progress and timely delivery.
  • Work closely with HR business stakeholders to understand processes, pain points and requirements and translate them into clear automation and AI use cases.
  • Coordinate business and technical stakeholders, including Business Analysts, Developers, HR IT teams, external partners and solution/platform teams throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Assess automation and AI opportunities considering business value, feasibility, implementation effort, technical dependencies and scalability.
  • Translate business requirements into clear functional and technical requirements and ensure alignment between business expectations and the solution delivered.
  • Facilitate workshops and working sessions to analyze processes, clarify requirements, resolve open topics and define implementation approaches.
  • Develop a solid understanding of the HR IT landscape, systems, interfaces and dependencies relevant to each use case and ensure alignment with existing architecture and standards.
  • Support solution design and technical discussions, including automation platforms, APIs, system integrations and AI-based solutions, working closely with technical experts.
  • Coordinate proof-of-concepts, pilots, testing and implementation activities and support the transition of successful solutions into productive operation.
  • Proactively manage project risks, dependencies, issues and escalations and drive decisions with the relevant stakeholders.
  • Monitor implemented solutions and their expected business impact and identify opportunities for further optimization or scaling.

Education

  • University degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Data Science, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related technical field.

Experience

  • Several years of professional experience in Automation, AI, Software Development, Solution Design, or Business Process Optimization.
  • Proven experience in leading automation and/or AI projects from concept through implementation.
  • Experience in leading, mentoring, and developing technical teams.
  • Experience working with international stakeholders and cross-functional teams.

Know-how

  • Strong experience in business process analysis, process design, and solution development.
  • Hands-on experience with automation platforms such as UiPath, Automation Anywhere, or comparable technologies.
  • Good knowledge of AI concepts, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and their application to business processes.
  • Good knowledge of Python and experience with APIs, integrations, or related technologies.
  • Experience with solution design and translating business requirements into scalable technical solutions.
  • Understanding of AI-related risks, data privacy, security, and responsible AI practices.
  • Knowledge of HR processes and/or HR IT environments is an advantage.

Languages

  • Fluent English, written and spoken (C1 level).
  • German is an advantage.

Working Style and Methods

  • Structured and analytical approach with a strong focus on practical and scalable solutions.
  • Ability to translate complex business needs into clear technical requirements and actionable solutions.
  • Experienced in working collaboratively across business and technical teams.
  • Proactive approach to identifying automation opportunities and driving continuous improvement.
  • Comfortable working in an international environment and managing multiple priorities.
  • Able to balance innovation with feasibility, scalability, cost, security, and risk.

Personality

  • Takes ownership and drives topics independently.
  • Strong communicator who can build trust with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Collaborative team player who enjoys sharing knowledge and developing others.
  • Curious and open-minded towards new technologies and ways of working.
  • Pragmatic and solution-oriented, with the ability to stay focused in complex situations.
  • Confident in challenging existing processes and bringing forward new ideas.

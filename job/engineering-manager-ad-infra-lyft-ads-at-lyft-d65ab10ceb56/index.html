About the job

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. We aim to achieve this by cultivating a work environment where all team members belong and have the opportunity to thrive.

Lyft Ads is building the largest mobility media network. Brands use Lyft Ads to reach riders throughout their journeys, in the Lyft app, in the real world, and across the open web. Every time a rider opens the app, our systems decide in milliseconds which message they see, whether it’s a paid ad, an incentive, or a product message from another Lyft team.

The Ad Decisioning team owns that choice. We run the serving, pacing, and auction systems behind every ad on Lyft surfaces, and we are building a first-party decisioning platform that lets paid and unpaid messages compete in a single auction based on their value to riders, advertisers, and Lyft. This platform is shared infrastructure. Ads and other teams will all use it to reach riders and drivers across multiple apps and surfaces. The work spans real-time distributed systems, auction design, and close partnership with teams across Lyft that message riders and drivers.

We are looking for a hands-on Engineering Manager to lead this team. You will spend a meaningful share of your time on technical work: shaping architecture, reviewing designs and code, and building with AI tools alongside your engineers. You will report to the Engineering Manager for Ads Infrastructure and partner closely with Product, Data Science, and a Staff engineer who leads the platform’s technical direction.

Responsibilities:

  • Lead a team of backend engineers who build and operate low-latency ad serving, pacing, and auction systems.
  • Stay close to the code. Review designs and pull requests, write prototypes and tooling, and contribute directly to high-priority technical work when it unblocks the team.
  • Own the reliability of a rider-facing, high-availability system. Set latency and availability targets, take part in the on-call rotation escalation, lead incident reviews, and drive readiness for peak advertising season.
  • Partner with the Staff engineer on architecture for the first-party decisioning platform, including the unified auction, bidding, pacing, and the contracts between decisioning, audiences, and optimization models.
  • Build the decisioning platform as shared infrastructure that serves multiple teams and products. Design clear APIs, versioned contracts, self-serve onboarding, and isolation so each new team or surface can adopt it without custom work from your team.
  • Lead the migration of capabilities from third-party ad server components to first-party services, sequenced so each step unlocks business value.
  • Build agreements with partner teams in Rider, Marketing, and Growth on shared surfaces, latency budgets, and message priorities. Hold those agreements with clear owners and dates.
  • Drive informed AI adoption on your team. Partner with senior engineers to define AI-assisted, human-verified practices, keep a human in the loop on agent-generated code, and make sure engineers build real system understanding as they move faster.
  • Use AI deliberately in your own work. Automate status and tracking, use AI drafts to accelerate planning and strategy, and keep coaching, feedback, and calibration as human work.
  • Calibrate performance fairly as AI changes what productivity looks like, and coach the team to turn efficiency gains into measurable business outcomes.
  • Mentor and grow engineers, including senior engineers stepping into technical leadership. Give continuous feedback, address performance directly, and recognize strong work.
  • Work with Product to maintain a prioritized roadmap, make tradeoffs explicit, and say no to work that doesn’t serve the team’s goals.
  • Partner with recruiting to hire strong engineers from diverse backgrounds.

Experience:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 2+ years of experience managing engineering teams, and 5+ years as a hands-on backend engineer before or alongside management.
  • Deep experience architecting, building, and operating distributed systems that serve high traffic at low latency.
  • Comfort reading and writing production code in at least one backend language such as Python, Go, or Java.
  • Experience owning on-call and incident response for a high-availability service.
  • Practical experience using AI coding tools and agents in your own work, and a clear point of view on how to adopt them responsibly on a team.
  • Experience building and running platform or infrastructure services used by multiple teams or products, including API design, multi-tenant reliability, and supporting internal customers.
  • Ad tech experience is a strong plus, including ad serving, real-time bidding, auctions, pacing, yield optimization, or programmatic advertising.
  • Experience with ranking, recommendation, or marketplace systems is a plus.
  • Background in machine learning is helpful, including deploying predictive models for engagement, clicks, or conversions, or collaborating with data science and ML partners to integrate models into high-throughput serving pipelines.

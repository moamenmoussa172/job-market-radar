About this Role

We are seeking an Amdocs Order Management Java Developer with 2 to 6 years of experience in customisation and development using Amdocs Order Management System (OMS/Ordering). You will design and implement Java-based solutions for Telecom BSS applications, collaborate with product owners to translate business requirements into technical solutions, and contribute throughout the software development lifecycle.

The role requires hands-on experience in Core Java 7 or above, J2EE, Spring, SQL, Oracle, EJB, REST and SOAP Web Services, WebLogic, Jenkins and Git, alongside practical knowledge of Agile delivery and CI/CD practices.

What you’ll do

  • Design and implement Java-based solutions for Telecom BSS applications.
  • Participate throughout development and implementation, collaborating with product owners to understand business requirements.
  • Prepare high-level and low-level technical documentation, including HLD and IDD.
  • Ensure implemented solutions support business objectives and follow relevant best practices.
  • Estimate development effort for requirements and projects.
  • Work within Agile delivery models including Scrum, SAFe and Kanban.
  • Support functional and non-functional testing across relevant project phases.
  • Contribute to innovation across IT domain processes and systems in collaboration with software providers, business teams and system integrators.
  • Use technology to simplify systems and processes and improve efficiency to support business growth.
  • Deliver demonstrations to designers and share development experience and lessons learned with technical colleagues.
  • Collaborate with technical teams to plan and monitor implementation activities for successful solution delivery.

Who you are

  • You hold a BTech, BE, MCA or MTech qualification.
  • You have 2 to 4 years of experience in customisation and development using Amdocs Order Management System (OMS/Ordering).
  • You have experience with Core Java 7 or above, J2EE, Spring and SQL.
  • You have experience working with Oracle databases.
  • You understand and have worked with EJB, REST and SOAP Web Services.
  • You have experience with the WebLogic application server.
  • You have knowledge of CI/CD supporting tools, including Jenkins and Git.
  • You understand the Software Development Life Cycle, Test Driven Development, Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery.
  • You can translate business needs into practical technology solutions.
  • You have practical design and development experience and strong analytical skills.
  • You can quickly understand and apply new concepts.
  • You can organise your work effectively, manage time and prioritise competing requirements.
  • You take a proactive, solution-focused approach to improvement and innovation.

About the job

About the role

We are looking for experienced, backend-leaning product engineers to help expand the capabilities of our industry-leading AI analytics platform.

At Hex, we're deeply focused on building powerful, integrated AI features that augment and accelerate analytics workflows. The possibilities here are incredible, and we're uniquely positioned to reinvent how people think about understanding data.

In this role, you’ll work on a product team building complex workflows that increase the capabilities, reach, and ergonomics of Hex’s agentic analytics platform. We’re especially focused on features that enable Hex’s AI agent to operate across more customer segments and surface areas, including APIs, MCP, and command-line interfaces.

What you will do

Our backend product engineers work on features across the entire Hex application. Here are some example projects:

  • Build and extend Hex’s API and CLI to enable powerful, ergonomic workflows for our most sophisticated technical users
  • Design MCP server and client implementations to expand entry points into our capabilities
  • Design and implement a next-generation access control system, including role-based access control (RBAC), admin request/approval flows, and tiered admin roles that scale with complex organizations
  • Own full-stack product changes end-to-end, from backend policy enforcement to admin-facing workflows, ensuring these enterprise features are intuitive, reliable, and secure
  • Propose and implement well-architected, scalable systems in collaboration with the broader Hex team

About You

  • 5+ years of backend or full-stack software engineering experience
  • Skilled at building systems with high levels of complexity and intricate business logic
  • Deep interest and/or experience in developing state-of-the-art agentic workflows involving disparate systems and tools
  • Experience working in a remote-first team, with strong communication skills in that context
  • A passion for technical collaboration, including RFCs, code reviews, and architecture discussions
  • Hands-on familiarity with enterprise integrations: SSO, SCIM-based provisioning, audit logs, MFA, and delegated access
  • A strategic mindset that balances business and product goals with engineering excellence—you understand tradeoffs between user value and technical complexity and operate pragmatically
  • Willingness to make changes anywhere in the stack to drive impact

Our stack

Our product is a web-based notebook and app authoring platform. Our frontend is built with Typescript and React, using a combination of Apollo GraphQL and Redux for managing application state and data. On the backend, we also use Typescript to power an Express/Apollo GraphQL server that interacts with Postgres, Redis, and Kubernetes to manage our database and Python kernels. Our backend is tightly integrated with our infrastructure and CI/CD, where we use a combination of Terraform, Helm, and AWS to deploy and maintain our stack.

SAP S/4 HANA Authorization Consultant

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Romania.

Main attributions and responsibilities:

  • Design, implement and maintain SAP S/4HANA authorization concepts, with a focus on logistics processes.
  • Collaborate with customers, business stakeholders, functional consultants and technical teams to define access requirements and translate them into appropriate roles and authorizations.
  • Provide ongoing authorization support for existing customers as part of application management services.
  • Manage user role assignments in line with approved access requests and changes in job responsibilities.
  • Investigate and resolve authorization issues using diagnostic and tracing tools.
  • Review roles and access assignments to identify Segregation of Duties (SoD) conflicts and support remediation.
  • Support authorization testing, role changes and deployment activities during implementations and system updates.
  • Maintain documentation of authorization concepts, role designs and access changes.
  • Work on demanding international projects as part of SAP introductions, release changes and migrations.
  • Fulfils any other tasks related to area of responsibility, as requested by direct superior.
  • English C1/B2 proficiency (fluent)
  • Completed degree in IT-related fields or comparable
  • 3+ years of professional experience as an SAP Authorization Consultant and familiar with the development and implementation of individual authorization concepts.
  • Strong SAP S/4HANA authorization expertise across Warehouse Management (EWM/ WM), Materials Management (MM), Sales and Distribution (SD).
  • Familiar with SAP Fiori applications, including business catalogs, services and underlying authorizations.
  • Experience with SAP R/3 authorization concepts would be considered a plus.
  • Good knowledge of the development and implementation of roles as well as knowledge of module-specific authorization objects.
  • Confident in dealing with common analysis tools and in the area of SoD conflicts.
  • Familiar with SAP IDM Identity Management and S4/HANA authorizations.
  • Effective time management and strong interpersonal skills. Ability to work independently and also in collaboration with cross-functional teams.
  • Excellent presentation, people management and customer-facing communications skills.
  • Possess leadership, ownership and accountability mindset.
  • Willing to learn new things, be open to challenges and thrive on success.
  • Readiness to travel internationally (EU) at irregular intervals/individual days.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

Adree, a subsidiary of Master Works, bridges business and technology through innovative digital solutions. Its expertise spans Quality Assurance, Quality Control, Low-Code/No-Code development, application performance, and lifecycle management.

Adree is looking for a Software Engineering Manager to lead its engineering teams, oversee solution architecture, and drive the end-to-end software development lifecycle, ensuring the delivery of scalable, secure, and high-quality software solutions while fostering technical excellence and team development.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead, manage, and develop engineering teams and technical leads, overseeing recruitment, performance reviews, career development, and succession planning.
  • Define and manage the end-to-end software development lifecycle, including planning, estimation, CI/CD, release management, and development processes.
  • Establish engineering standards, code review practices, quality gates, and automated testing strategies.
  • Own solution architecture and technical design decisions, ensuring scalability, performance, availability, security, and cost efficiency.
  • Lead architecture reviews, proof of concepts (PoCs), and technology evaluations, including build-versus-buy decisions.
  • Drive technical roadmaps and innovation initiatives involving AI, GenAI, LLMs, RAG, AI Agents, and automation.
  • Oversee engineering operations, production support, incident management, monitoring, and system reliability.
  • Monitor engineering performance through DORA metrics, cycle time, and delivery KPIs.
  • Collaborate with Product, Delivery, QA, DevOps, and senior leadership to align technical priorities with business objectives.
  • Ensure compliance with relevant Saudi regulations and standards, including PDPL and NCA cybersecurity controls.
  • Represent engineering in client-facing and executive-level technical discussions.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • 12+ years of experience in software engineering, including at least 4 years in direct engineering management roles.
  • Proven experience managing multiple engineering teams and technical leads, including hiring, performance management, and career development.
  • Strong solution architecture experience, with a proven track record of designing and delivering at least two production systems end-to-end.
  • Strong knowledge of distributed systems, microservices, modular monoliths, event-driven architecture, Kafka, RabbitMQ, and API design.
  • Hands-on experience with C#/.NET, including ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, asynchronous programming, and dependency injection.
  • Working knowledge of Angular or React, and SQL Server or PostgreSQL.
  • Experience establishing code review processes, engineering standards, and automated quality practices across multiple teams.
  • Strong understanding of CI/CD pipelines, Git, Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, and Docker.
  • Experience in cloud architecture using Azure or AWS, including security and cost optimization.
  • Practical experience delivering AI/GenAI solutions or AI Agentic Development initiatives to production, including LLM integration, RAG, evaluation, and AI security.
  • Experience in production operations, incident management, SLOs, monitoring, and observability.
  • Knowledge of automated testing frameworks, SonarQube, DevSecOps practices, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code is preferred.
  • Experience with low-code/no-code platforms, application performance monitoring tools, and Python for AI/ML and automation is an advantage.
  • Strong leadership, strategic thinking, problem-solving, decision-making, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with the ability to present technical concepts to both engineering teams and executive stakeholders.
  • Relevant professional certifications such as TOGAF, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, or AWS Solutions Architect Professional are preferred.

About you and this role

As a Technical Service & Development Scientist, you will use your technical expertise, automotive industry knowledge, and customer focus to help Dow build stronger relationships with major automotive manufacturers. You will work closely with commercial colleagues and technical experts to understand customer needs, connect customers with the right technologies, and develop opportunities across Dow’s diverse portfolio of products, technologies, and chemistries, with a particular focus on battery assembly and battery cell applications and related Dow products.

This position can be based in Seneffe, Belgium, or Wiesbaden, Germany. Terneuzen, the Netherlands, and Correggio, Italy, may also be considered.

Responsibilities

  • Build strong relationships with decision-makers at major automotive manufacturers by partnering with Dow's regional Key Account Managers and expanding Dow’s connections across customer projects and opportunities.
  • Serve as the primary technical contact between Dow and customers, coordinating technical activities across Dow’s commercial and technical teams.
  • Respond to customer technical questions by providing initial guidance and connecting customers with the appropriate Dow experts for timely follow-up.
  • Coordinate with the Technical Director and Key Account Manager to connect customer needs with the appropriate teams and technologies, making it easier for customers to engage with Dow.
  • Advance customer opportunities by supporting activities that help convert potential leads into commercial opportunities.
  • Partner with commercial colleagues to support account-specific goals, including revenue growth, lead and opportunity generation, innovation events, and other initiatives aligned with account strategy.

Qualifications

  • A minimum of a bachelor’s degree in Chemistry, Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Physics, or a related field, along with at least 5 years of professional experience in an industrial environment.
  • Strong knowledge of relevant chemistries and the ability to quickly develop an understanding of Dow’s businesses, technologies, and technical capabilities.
  • Knowledge of automotive industry applications, trends, and key players.
  • Experience engaging and partnering with customers, facilitating meetings, and representing an organization externally.
  • Ability to collaborate with commercial colleagues to prepare for customer events and deliver value.
  • Willingness to travel (approximately 25%).
  • Multilingual professional, demonstrated fluency in German and English.

Your Skills

  • Product development and technology promotion: You bring proven expertise in product development and promoting technologies within the automotive industry.
  • Customer engagement: You have experience building customer relationships in technical, product development, or technology promotion roles and connecting customer needs with relevant expertise and solutions.
  • Communication and organization: You communicate complex ideas clearly and manage multiple priorities, customer requests, and stakeholders effectively.
  • Relationship building and professional networking: You listen actively, build trust, and develop connections with customer decision-makers and colleagues across the organization.
  • Analytical and solution-oriented thinking: You interpret information, anticipate needs, identify opportunities, and use insights to support effective decisions and results.

Additional Notes

  • Relocation and/or Visa-/Immigration support is not available for this position.
  • Please provide your application in English.

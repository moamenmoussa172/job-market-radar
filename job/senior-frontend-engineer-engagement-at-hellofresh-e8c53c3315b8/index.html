About the job

At HelloFresh, our mission is simple: to change the way people eat, forever. As a global digital-native FMCG company and the world's leading mealkit provider, we are transforming the traditional food supply chain through a data-driven, on-demand model that enables scalable and more sustainable food delivery. The HelloFresh Group includes eight brands serving customers across multiple meal occasions, including HelloFresh, Green Chef, EveryPlate, and Chefs Plate, as well as ready-to-eat meals from Factor and YouFoodz, meat and seafood subscriptions through Good Chop, and pet nutrition with The Pets Table. Through technology, data, and culinary innovation, we deliver personalized food experiences to millions of households worldwide.

About the Role: What's in the Box

This role is part of HelloFresh's Engagement Tribe, where we turn the HelloFresh app from a weekly subscription-management tool into a food-first daily companion. You'll work directly on the moments that make cooking easier and more fun: finding dinner inspiration, turning a TikTok or Instagram video into a recipe you can actually cook, planning the week with a ready-made grocery list, and cooking step by step with an intelligent cooking assistant at your side. Primary responsibility centers on enhancing the daily app experience to improve customer engagement and retention. You will operate with a high degree of autonomy and fast iteration while leveraging HelloFresh's global infrastructure.

As a Senior Frontend Engineer, you will contribute to key initiatives across our customer-facing mobile app and web experiences, working with a modern React Native / React / TypeScript stack (Next.js on web). You will integrate with our recipe, search, and personalization services, leverage experimentation frameworks, and utilize GenAI tools (such as Claude, Cursor, and Figma) to accelerate architecture, design, and testing workflows. Working alongside machine learning, product, and design experts, you will deliver high-quality frontend solutions, contribute to architectural improvements, and support the evolution of the platform across scalability, observability, and AI-driven workflows.

What You'll Do: The Recipe

  • Design, build, and optimize customer-facing applications using React Native, React, Next.js, and TypeScript.
  • Drive frontend architecture and technical decisions, set direction for platform scalability, and raise the bar through code reviews and mentoring.
  • Lead improvements in how we work: production monitoring, alerting, observability, testing, and release practices.
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Design, and cross-functional partners to prototype, iterate, and deliver seamless user experiences.
  • Champion modern AI tools and workflows across the team to enhance engineering efficiency, code quality, and standards.
  • Initiative Ownership: Ability to lead complex projects end-to-end—translating vision into structured implementation plans and guiding teams through execution to successful delivery.

What You'll Bring: The Ingredients

  • Frontend Mastery: Deep expertise in React and TypeScript, with hands-on React Native experience and a strong track record of delivering production-grade frontend applications, architectural design, and performance optimizations.
  • Collaboration & Ownership: Proactive, autonomous mindset with strong communication skills to explain complex technical decisions, share knowledge, and drive results across domains.
  • GenAI Expertise: Hands-on experience leveraging modern AI tools and workflows (e.g., Claude Code, AWS Bedrock) to increase development speed and elevate technical standards.
  • Observability & Monitoring: Practical familiarity with setting up and maintaining monitoring and observability frameworks in production environments.
  • Product & Design Sense: Strong product and design judgment with the ability to independently prototype and iterate on coherent, high-quality customer experiences.
  • Bonus: Hands-on experience with backend services and APIs (e.g., Node.js, GraphQL, REST, or serverless architectures) to collaborate effectively across the full stack.
  • Nice to Have: Experience with React Native native module integrations, mobile performance optimization, and mobile rendering patterns (e.g., the New Architecture).

What we offer: The Toppings

  • Global collaboration at scale: Collaborate with experienced engineers and product partners across HelloTech’s international teams, in a culture of active knowledge sharing.
  • Technology with real-world impact: Build and operate modern systems at a global scale, supporting 6+ million customers and complex supply chain operations.
  • Technical/Product/Design leadership: Drive best practices and influence architecture/design, quality, and ways of working in an autonomous, product-led setup.
  • End-to-end development/delivery: Drive decisions from problem definition to production, improving systems and enabling long-term scalability.
  • Access to the workspace at Warsaw Centre Point. The hub offers modern facilities including showers, breakout zones, outdoor space, cycle parking, and refreshments (coffee, soft drinks, and fruit).

Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.

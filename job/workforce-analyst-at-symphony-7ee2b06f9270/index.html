About the job

Symphony is an AI-first global product, design, and development company with 800+ full-time employees. Our team works across engineering offices in Southeast Europe and Latin America, business development offices in the U.S. and Western Europe, and a Remote hub that spans Latin America, Africa, and Eastern Europe. In the last 20 years, we have delivered over 400 products for the world's leading startups, enterprises, and Fortune 500 companies. We are a preferred technology partner to more than 10 of the world's biggest brands and work with 50+ private equity funds.

Our team has a proven track record in helping clients increase revenue, reduce time-to-market, improve end-user experiences, and scale. We have domain expertise spanning financial services, banking, retail, technology, healthcare, and the VC and PE ecosystem, among others.
We are looking for a Workforce Analyst to help get the right people onto the right projects at the right time. You will turn workforce and project data into clear staffing decisions, keep that data accurate, and make sure our people's resumes and capability materials are ready for leadership and clients. This is an internal role within our Workforce Management team.

What would be your day-to-day responsibilities, you ask?

  • Analyse workforce supply and project demand across portfolios, and recommend how to staff and allocate people
  • Spot capacity constraints, utilisation risks and skill gaps early, and propose how to address them
  • Build and maintain forecasting models using historical data, project demand and attrition trends
  • Keep workforce and project data accurate and complete across Jira, ERP and spreadsheets; monitor compliance with governance standards and escalate systemic issues
  • Lead recurring reporting cycles and handle ad-hoc analytics requests with minimal guidance
  • Build dashboards in Excel or Power BI that show trends, capacity gaps and utilisation issues
  • Maintain employee resumes, portfolios and capability materials, and act as the quality gate before they are shared with leadership or clients
  • Review schedules, data, forecasts and content prepared by junior team members, and guide them on corrections and best practices
  • Find inefficiencies in workforce operations, reporting and content workflows, and help fix them
  • Work with Delivery, Finance, HR and Design teams to share insights and support shared goals
  • What are we looking for?
  • 3 to 5 years in workforce management, resource planning or operations analytics
  • Advanced Excel or Google Sheets: pivot tables, advanced formulas and basic data modelling
  • Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Excel
  • Hands-on experience with Jira and an ERP system
  • Experience with capacity planning and forecasting
  • Able to combine data from several systems into one reliable dataset
  • Strong attention to detail and data discipline
  • Able to present findings to leadership and back recommendations with evidence
  • Fluent written and spoken English
  • Nice to have:
  • Experience in professional services, IT outsourcing or staff augmentation
  • Experience mentoring or reviewing the work of junior analysts
  • Experience preparing resumes or capability profiles for client proposals
  • You will easily fit into Symphony culture if you:
  • Question numbers that don't add up
  • Challenge assumptions with data, and do it respectfully
  • Handle several priorities at once and stay steady when plans change
  • Treat confidential people data with care
  • Write things down so others can follow them

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada, United States.

Webflow is the agentic web marketing platform for modern marketing teams, helping organizations build, manage, and optimize high-performing web experiences that drive predictable growth and strengthen brand trust. Building at Webflow will need grit, because we move fast, without ever sacrificing craft or quality.

Our Customer Success team works with Webflow’s most high-impact Enterprise customers, helping them launch successfully, adapt quickly, and get the full value of our AI-native website experience platform. We’re the team that stays with customers from day one through renewal, guiding their strategy, unblocking their teams, and connecting them with the right experts across Webflow.

As the primary partner for our strategic accounts, we play a central role in their long-term success — and in Webflow’s. By driving adoption, uncovering new use cases, and championing meaningful outcomes, we influence growth, retention, and the overall strength of our Enterprise business.

We’re looking for a Senior Customer Success Manager who can step into that role with confidence: the trusted advisor who sees the big picture, the operator who can bring cross-functional teams together, and the advocate who ensures customers stay with us and grow with us because of the results we help them achieve.

As a Senior Customer Success Manager you’ll:

  • Build strong, trusted relationships across your customers’ orgs, from exec sponsors to hands-on users, and guide them through every stage of their Webflow journey.
  • Get deep into your customers’ goals, workflows and tech stack so you can help them use Webflow’s platform to power more of their digital footprint.
  • Lead account strategy with clarity and confidence. Rally cross-functional partners around a shared success plan that drives adoption, impact, and measurable business outcomes.
  • Own renewals, forecasting, and account health. Spot expansion opportunities and new use cases, and partner with Sales on growth.
  • Turn usage and consumption patterns into adoption plans, forecasts, and expansion opportunities.
  • Lead discovery workshops and pilots for AI use cases. Help customers move AI agents from pilot to production and navigate rollout, adoption, and change management.
  • Define success criteria and ROI with each customer.
  • Run high-value executive conversations that surface progress, risks, and results and clearly show why Webflow is essential to the customer’s business.
  • Partner with Sales, Technical Architects, Forward Deployed Engineers, and Services Providers to steer complex accounts toward successful launches, scalable architectures and strong renewal outcomes.
  • Bring the customer’s voice back into Webflow with crisp insights that shape product direction, improve our processes, and help us deliver an even better Enterprise experience.
  • Travel up to 15% (1-2x per quarter)

About you:

Requirements:

  • BS / BA college degree or relevant experience
  • 5+ years of relevant experience advising Enterprise customers in a SaaS or digital experience environment, ideally in a role where you owned adoption, renewal, uncovering expansion and long-term customer health.
  • Experience managing usage, consumption or credit-based accounts. This includes tracking utilization against commitments, managing burn-down and forecasting renewals and expansion from usage trends.
  • Technical aptitude across codebases, deployment pipelines, integrations, permissions and security, with the ability to partner credibly with developers. Production coding is not required.
  • Experience in at least one of the following:
  • Applied AI: building real AI workflows or agents, evaluating their output and setting realistic expectations.
  • Digital transformation: leading complex enterprise programs involving integrated systems, workflow automation or platform migrations.

You’ll thrive as a Senior Customer Success Manager if you:

  • Dig into a customer’s business using financial filings, industry research, and internal data to spot risks, opportunities, and growth paths.
  • Have hands-on AI fluency: you have worked with agents, connectors, or MCP-style integrations, and you can explain AI tools to executives and technical teams in terms of business outcomes.
  • Read product usage data, spot early signs of churn or underuse (such as slowing consumption, dormant users, and stalled workflows), and turn them into an action plan before renewal.
  • Stay on top of details, keep cross-functional partners aligned, and maintain momentum in a fast-moving environment through strong organizational and project management habits.
  • Stay curious and open to growth — demonstrating a proactive embrace of AI, and actively building and applying fluency in emerging technologies to elevate how we work, drive faster outcomes, and expand collective impact.

Our Core Behaviors

  • Build lasting customer trust. We build trust by taking action that puts customer trust first.
  • Win together. We play to win, and we win as one team. Success at Webflow isn't a solo act.
  • Reinvent ourselves. We don't just improve what exists, we imagine what's possible.
  • Own it all the way. We move fast because the moment demands it, and we hold ourselves accountable to deliver outcomes — for each other, and for customers.

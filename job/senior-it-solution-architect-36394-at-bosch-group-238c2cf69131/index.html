About the job

As a Senior IT Commercial Architect, you will provide strategic and technical leadership to define, design, and evolve technology solutions supporting our Commercial Systems (Customer Portals and e-Commerce). Operating at the intersection of business strategy and technology execution, you will translate complex commercial requirements into scalable, secure roadmaps, acting as the key translator between North American business stakeholders, product management, and global engineering teams.

A critical initial focus of this role will be leading the technical transition and system integration of the newly acquired JCI commercial platforms into the Bosch IT network. This requires an exceptionally senior architect with a strong background in software engineering, database design, identity management (Single Sign-On/IAM), and modern integration patterns (APIs, IDocs) to connect front-end digital experiences smoothly with backend ERPs (SAP/Oracle), ensuring business continuity and robust system governance.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop and maintain technology architectures that support commercial business strategies,
operational objectives, and long-term digital transformation goals.
• Design scalable, secure, and integrated solutions across applications, data, infrastructure,
cloud platforms, and third-party technologies.
• Partner with business stakeholders, IT Product Management, vendors, and delivery teams to
evaluate requirements and define technology solutions that address business needs.
• Establish and govern architectural standards, design principles, technical roadmaps, and
solution patterns to ensure consistency and sustainability.
• Lead architectural assessments and technology evaluations, identifying opportunities to drive
innovation, improve operational efficiency, and enhance customer experiences.
• Provide technical leadership and architectural guidance throughout the solution lifecycle, from
concept and design through implementation and support.
• Ensure solutions meet enterprise standards for security, reliability, scalability, performance,
compliance, and maintainability.
• Identify and mitigate technology risks while balancing business priorities, technical
complexity, and long-term architectural objectives.
• Support large-scale business transformation initiatives, including system migrations,
organizational transitions, and platform modernization efforts.
• Foster strong relationships with business leaders, technology teams, and external partners to
align technology investments with business value.
• Lead the Commercial IT Architecture roles in Brazil

• Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Business, Engineering, or related field.
• Combined experience enterprise architecture, solution architecture, technology strategy, Development.
• Experience designing and integrating solutions across cloud platforms, enterprise applications,
data ecosystems, and third-party systems.
• Strong understanding of application architecture, systems integration, cybersecurity
principles, and modern software development practices.
• Proven ability to translate business objectives into scalable and sustainable technology
solutions.
• Excellent communication, stakeholder management, facilitation, and problem-solving skills.
• Demonstrated ability to influence decisions across technical and business organizations without
direct authority.
• Experience balancing business priorities, technical requirements, operational support needs,
and long-term architectural vision.

Preferred Qualifications
• Experience supporting enterprise portals, customer-facing platforms, or product lifecycle
management solutions.
• Experience supporting eCommerce, digital customer experience, or commercial technology
ecosystems.
• Experience working within manufacturing, product distribution, or industrial technology
environments.
• Familiarity with enterprise platforms such as SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, or other ERP and
commercial systems.

analytics solutions.

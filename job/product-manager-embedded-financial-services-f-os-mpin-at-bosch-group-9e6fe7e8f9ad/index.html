Product Manager, Embedded Financial Services (F.OS)_MPIN

Bosch Group · Bengaluru, India

Onsite · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 10 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

What the role owns

Embedded finance strategy - Own and evolve the financial services roadmap, starting with embedded credit and extending to payments and adjacent products as customer need, economics and regulatory readiness allow.

Customer and use-case discovery - Identify high-potential financing needs across fleet operators, system integrators, garages, marketplace sellers and vehicle owners. Define ideal customer profiles and prioritise use cases on need, economics and feasibility.

Lender and funding partnerships - Work with banks, NBFCs and fintech partners to match lender appetite with customer segments on Bosch and partner platforms. Build multi-lender capability so the business does not depend on one funding source.

Financial product structuring - Co-create products with lenders: credit limits, tenure, repayment constructs, transaction flows, eligibility criteria, pricing and customer propositions.

Programme and scheme design - Design financing schemes, incentives, bundles and activation programmes that drive adoption and repeat utilisation.

Platform partnerships - Work with mobility and ecosystem platforms to find transaction flows where financial products can be embedded, and agree operating and commercial models that work for both sides.

Digital product and infrastructure - Define the journeys and architecture for onboarding, consent, KYC and KYB, lender routing, credit decisioning, checkout, disbursement, settlement, repayment visibility and reconciliation.

Technology service provider management - Work with lending service and technology providers to build reusable lender integrations and embedded journeys.

Data and underwriting enablement - Convert consented mobility and transaction data into lender-consumable signals, such as fleet utilisation, running distance, garage throughput and purchase consistency. Contribute to a scoring and borrower-profiling approach together with the Identity Resolution and Consent Engine product lead.

Regulatory and governance coordination - Work with Legal, Compliance and Finance to put the right referral, DSA, LSP or other operating constructs in place, and build data protection and consent requirements into the product from the start.

Commercial ownership - Define and negotiate sustainable monetisation for Bosch across access, utilisation, transaction and outcome-based revenue.

Go-live and scale - Own pilots through user acceptance, first transaction, funnel measurement and iteration, and convert successful pilots into repeatable propositions across platforms.

How the role is measured

The role is accountable for business outcomes, not only for product documentation. Effectiveness is assessed on:

  • Financial products successfully launched
  • Number and quality of active lender and financial-service partnerships
  • Number of eligible customers and active borrowers
  • Application to sanction to disbursement conversion
  • Credit utilisation and repeat transactions
  • Financing throughput enabled on partner platforms
  • Incremental revenue for Bosch MPS
  • Time from identified opportunity to first transaction
  • Platform and customer adoption, and expansion across financial products and partner platforms

Who we are looking for

Education

  • Bachelor's degree in engineering, finance, economics or a related field; a master's degree or MBA is an advantage.

Experience

  • 7 to 12+ years in fintech, digital lending, embedded finance, NBFC or banking products, lending marketplaces or B2B financial services.
  • Has taken a lending or embedded-finance proposition from customer need through lender structuring, digital integration and first disbursement to scale. This is strongly preferred.

Domain and technical knowledge

  • Deep understanding of digital lending and embedded finance, and of underwriting, credit products and lender economics.
  • Experience working directly with banks and NBFCs and with their credit, product and partnership teams.
  • Experience designing B2B and MSME credit products and digital customer journeys.
  • Working knowledge of the Indian regulatory frame for digital lending (including lending service provider and DSA constructs), KYC and KYB, the Account Aggregator ecosystem, consent and data protection (DPDP), and lending APIs and integrations.
  • Ability to translate a business opportunity into clear technology requirements and to challenge an architecture.

Commercial skills

  • Strong commercial and partnership-negotiation capability, including structuring revenue models with regulated partners.
  • Comfortable owning a business metric (revenue, throughput, conversion) and not only a product backlog.

Personal attributes

  • High ownership and an entrepreneurial orientation; comfortable building a new proposition rather than managing a mature product.
  • Works across Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, Finance and business teams, and brings people along.
  • Clear communicator with the credibility to deal with lender leadership and platform partners.
  • Sound judgement on risk and compliance; calm and structured under regulatory and partner complexity.

Who the role works with

  • Product area leadership (MPS/PAx) and the Product Lead for Identity Resolution and the Consent Engine
  • Engineering and platform teams
  • Legal, Compliance, Data Protection and Finance
  • Regional business leadership (MPS/RBR) and the Global Partner Manager
  • Product owners of marketplace and commerce platforms where credit is embedded
  • Banks, NBFCs, technology service providers and platform partners

How to read this role

This role should not be read as a programme manager role. The ideal candidate sits at the intersection of fintech domain expert, product owner, lender and ecosystem partner, commercial builder and execution leader.

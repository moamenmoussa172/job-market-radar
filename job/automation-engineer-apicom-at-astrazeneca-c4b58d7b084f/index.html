About the job

Automation Engineer - APICOM

Location: APICOM Facility, College Park, Dublin, Ireland

Competitive salary and benefits

Introduction to role:

Are you ready to use advanced automation to launch a next-generation API facility and accelerate delivery of life-changing medicines to patients? Join our OT and Digital Solutions team at the APICOM facility in College Park, Dublin, where you will shape a highly autonomous, paperless factory powered by Emerson DeltaV and integrated digital technologies.

From day one, you will help bring a flagship site online, ensuring our automation systems are robust, compliant, and production-ready. You will own critical DeltaV configurations, partner closely with Front Line Support and Hypercare, and work across OT/IT, MES, and OEM platforms to drive performance and reliability. Can you see yourself translating complex batch strategies into resilient DeltaV designs that run flawlessly and safely?

As we scale late-stage development and early commercial supply of small-molecule medicines, you will play a central role in new product introductions, system upgrades, and continuous improvement. Your impact will be felt in the factory’s day-to-day stability and in the speed and quality with which new therapies reach patients.

Accountabilities:

  • Cross-System Collaboration: Partner across DeltaV, PAS-X, OEM, OT/IT, and Digital platforms; develop and maintain a strong working knowledge across all systems to improve uptime and performance.
  • DeltaV System Optimization: Develop, troubleshoot, and optimize Emerson DeltaV DCS code and configurations to drive reliable operations and continuous improvement.
  • Documentation and Validation: Create and maintain functional specifications, system design documents, test protocols, and change control records to support compliant system changes and project delivery.
  • Run Support and Issue Resolution: Act as a key point of contact between Automation, Front Line Support, and Hypercare teams to triage, prioritize, and resolve issues efficiently.
  • NPI and Tech Transfer: Assess automation requirements, implement required DeltaV changes, and support validation runs to introduce new products and processes.
  • Deviation and RCA: Lead and support root cause analysis, deviation investigations, and complex troubleshooting across automation systems to restore performance and prevent recurrence.
  • Change Management: Own automation change controls in Veeva Vault, coordinating assessments, actions, documentation, implementation, and closure.
  • Vendor and OEM Integration: Collaborate with PLC vendors and system integrators to ensure OEM systems are supported, maintained, and optimized.
  • Compliance and Audit Readiness: Maintain systems, documentation, and procedures in an audit-ready state aligned with site and regulatory requirements.
  • Coaching and Knowledge Sharing: Provide technical guidance and mentoring to team members to build troubleshooting capability and strengthen automation skills.
  • Industry 4.0 Scouting: Evaluate emerging digital manufacturing technologies to improve performance, efficiency, and data visibility; shape a forward-looking automation roadmap.
  • Additional Contributions: Support broader team needs and site objectives with flexibility and a problem-solving attitude.

Essential Skills/Experience:

  • Knowledge of industrial automation and control systems, hands-on experience with Emerson DeltaV DCS in a GMP-regulated manufacturing environment.
  • Familiarity with ANSI/ISA-88 standards for batch control systems.
  • Experience supporting the sustaining and optimisation of automation systems within a GMP-regulated manufacturing environment
  • Proven capability in automation change control management, deviation management and technical root-cause analysis troubleshooting.
  • Excellent team player with problem solving skills who thrives in a collaborative team environment.
  • Proven ability to manage partner relationships, coordinate cross-functional activities and bring teams together to deliver shared objectives.
  • Eagerness to learn and accept new challenges.
  • Strong understanding of data integrity principles and ALCOA+ principles.
  • GMP and regulatory knowledge/awareness.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or a related STEM subject area
  • Proven industry experience in a GMP-regulated manufacturing environment.

Desirable Skills/Experience:

  • Practical experience with OEM PLC’s & HMI’s.
  • Working knowledge of MES, particularly Koerber PAS-X and Tulip.
  • Data management and historian platforms.
  • Experience in API manufacturing.
  • Exposure and involvement with GMP regulatory Audits.

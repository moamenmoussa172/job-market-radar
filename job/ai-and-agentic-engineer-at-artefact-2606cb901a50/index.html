About the job

Join the most advanced community of AI makers.

Mission

Make AI work, beyond the prototype. You build the AI applications, platforms and data that the world's leading companies use every day, and you keep them running in production. Our clients work in retail, luxury, healthcare, financial services and manufacturing.

For example, a customer advisor gets a drafted answer with its sources. An analyst asks a question in plain words and gets the same figure as the finance team. A client team launches a new agent on a platform that already handles access rights and monitoring.

Activities & Responsibilities

Who you work with

You work in project teams with AI Product Managers, who manage the product with the client, and AI Architects, who design the end-to-end solution. Every day, you work with engineers from the other archetypes and with the client's business and IT teams.

You review code with your peers, pair on hard problems and help newer colleagues learn the job. Each archetype has its own community, with representatives who share practices across projects.

One title, four archetypes

Everyone in the chapter has the same title and the same foundation skills. Each person also has one main archetype, defined by the layer of the system they build, with skills specific to that archetype.

  • Product Engineer: Applications and agents used in business processes, for the business and its end users. Typical projects: Business applications (front end, back end, APIs); assistants embedded in a process; process automation.
  • Platform Engineer: Infrastructure and AI platforms that run products and agents at scale, for engineers and agents. Typical projects: Cloud and infrastructure as code; CI/CD, MLOps and LLMOps; agent runtime and observability; security foundations.
  • Context & Analytics Engineer: Data and knowledge that products and agents rely on, for the business and its agents. Typical projects: Ingestion pipelines and data models; data quality; semantic layer and metrics; knowledge pipelines for RAG.
  • ML & AI Applied Scientist: The LLM part of applications and agents: context, retrieval, tool use, evaluation, model choice, for the business and its end users. Typical projects: LLM assistants and agents; document processing; evaluation pipelines; fine-tuning a smaller model.

Your main archetype follows your projects of the last 12 months, so it can change when your work changes. You don't need to choose an archetype before applying: the hiring process assesses which one fits your experience.

What you do

Whatever your archetype, you practise the foundation skills:

  • Software craft: write code that is tested, reviewed and documented.
  • Production ownership: own what you ship after launch, including monitoring, incidents and fixes.
  • AI evaluation: define and run evaluations for AI features, and say when AI is not the right tool.
  • AI system security basics: manage access rights and secrets, and guard against prompt injection.
  • AI-assisted development: write the specification, delegate to coding agents, review the result and keep token costs under control.
  • Product and business sense: build for users, ship a first version, measure adoption, improve it.
  • Client relationship: explain your choices to clients and manage your part of the project.

Required Skills

Must-haves:

  • You build software in Python, TypeScript, SQL or another language, and you can explain how it works as a whole, including the parts a coding agent wrote.
  • You have built something people use in one of these areas: applications, infrastructure, data or LLM applications. Work, internships and study projects all count.
  • You treat monitoring, incidents, costs and security as part of the job.
  • You explain technical choices clearly to people who are not engineers.

Bonus, not required:

  • Experience evaluating AI or machine learning systems.
  • Client-facing experience.

You don't need to tick every box to apply. If you match most must-haves and want to learn the rest, we want to hear from you.

What we expect at your level

  • Senior: owns a workstream of their archetype end to end, splits and estimates the work, reviews code and works autonomously with the client.

What to expect / Recruitment process

The process has five interviews. You don't need to choose an archetype before applying: after the HR interview, interviews 2 to 4 are run by engineers from the archetype that fits your experience, with a case from that archetype. Each archetype's interview focus is described in its section above.

  • HR interview (45 min): your background and current situation, your fit (autonomy, client posture, teamwork) and your interest in building AI systems.
  • Technical interview (60 min) with an engineer from your archetype: the fundamentals of your archetype (live coding for Product and Context & Analytics, cloud and DevOps for Platform, maths, ML and LLM evaluation for ML & AI Applied Scientists), then a deep dive on something you built.
  • System design (60 min) with two engineers from different archetypes: design the AI system for a client use case, go deep on your part (application, platform, data and context, or model) and defend your choices.
  • Hands-on build (60 min) with an engineer from your archetype: build a working piece of the previous case with the tools of your choice, AI coding assistants included, then demo it and walk through it.
  • Fit interview (30 min) with a Director: meet the team and learn how we work.

About Artefact

Artefact is the trusted AI deployment team to the world's leading companies: 3,000 AI makers in more than 30 countries and more than 1,000 clients. In the US, you join the Engineering & Deployment department, in the AI Applied Engineer chapter.

Diversity & Inclusion

Artefact is an equal opportunity employer. We hire on skills and potential, and we welcome applicants of every background, identity and experience. If you need an adjustment at any step of the hiring process, tell your recruiter.

Why Join Artefact

At Artefact, data and AI are not abstract strategy topics. They are tools for creating business value, improving organizations, and helping people make better decisions.

You will join a global community of data and AI experts who combine consulting, engineering, data science, marketing, and technology expertise. You will work on complex, high-impact problems with leading organizations and help shape how enterprises adopt AI responsibly and effectively.

We value action, collaboration, learning, client trust, and shared knowledge. We believe that technology only matters when it is used, adopted, and translated into impact.

Our values aren't decorations on a wall — they're how we actually work:

  • There is always a way — We're builders and problem-solvers. An idea only counts if it gets executed.
  • Client trust is won in the field — We show up, sleeves rolled up, working side by side with our clients.
  • If it's not used, it's useless — We build for adoption and impact, not for slide decks.
  • If it's not shared, we're not done — Knowledge shared compounds. We invest in each other's growth.
  • We learn every day — In a field that moves this fast, standing still means falling behind. We embrace the challenge.

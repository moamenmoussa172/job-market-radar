Shape the Future of Submarine Engineering

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a Principal Submarine Systems Engineer at our HMNB Clyde Faslane site.

The role

As a Principal Submarine Systems Engineer, you’ll have a unique opportunity to join a high‑impact engineering environment where you’ll grow professionally, build specialist expertise, and contribute to work that truly matters.

Day-to-day, you’ll help safeguard the operational capability of the UK’s most advanced Royal Navy submarines. Your work will span deep maintenance periods, ensuring every system meets the required safety, performance and availability standards. You will also:

  • Act on behalf of the Chief Engineer, providing technical oversight and guidance across Platform Systems.
  • Hold full technical delegations and serve as a key interface with Design and Technical Authorities.
  • Understand and uphold design intent from work package creation through execution, testing and acceptance.
  • Work closely with internal project teams and external MOD stakeholders, contributing to long‑term engineering solutions.
  • Participate in working groups to assess engineering change, address technical issues and support continuous improvement.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at HMNB Clyde Faslane. The successful candidate will be flexible to work onsite in line with business requirements, ranging from a minimum of three days per week up to full‑time onsite attendance.

Essential experience of the Principal Submarine Systems Engineer

  • Demonstrable experience in submarine engineering.
  • Strong awareness of system engineering within mechanical or electrical engineering disciplines.
  • Practical experience conducting technical audits and navigating associated processes.
  • Strong communication skills, with experience engaging a range of internal and external stakeholders.
  • Experience identifying, managing and prioritising technical risks and associated mitigations.

Qualifications for the Principal Submarine Systems Engineer

  • Engineering degree or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Chartered Engineer status, or close to achieving it.
  • Additional technical qualifications or professional development related to naval, marine, systems or mechanical/electrical engineering - Desirable

Security Clearance

The successful candidate must be a sole UK National who is able to achieve and maintain Security Check (SC) security clearance for this role.

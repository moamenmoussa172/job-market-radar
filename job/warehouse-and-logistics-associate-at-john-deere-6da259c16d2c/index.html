About the job

Who We Are

Integrity. Quality. Humanity. Commitment. Innovation.

For over 50 years, Waratah, a John Deere company, has supported the global forestry industry with forestry and logging equipment built around quality, durability, reliability and customer support. With operations across Australia and internationally, we continue to evolve how we support our customers and the forestry industry.

Title: Warehouse & Logistics Associate
Primary Location: Melton Victoria
Wage
Hiring Manager: Joel Vithayathil

The Opportunity

We are looking for a Warehouse & Logistics Associate to join our Waratah Distribution Centre team in Melton.

This is a hands-on role where you’ll work across receiving, picking, packing, dispatch and inventory, helping make sure goods are handled safely, accurately and delivered to our customers on time.

You’ll also work with freight providers and warehouse systems, with opportunities to build broader skills across logistics, Wholegoods and shipment coordination as the role develops.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Receive, pick, pack and dispatch parts, Wholegoods and other materials.
  • Operate warehouse equipment safely and follow site safety requirements.
  • Support stock replenishment, inventory accuracy and stock counts.
  • Complete warehouse system transactions accurately.
  • Coordinate with freight and logistics providers to support on-time delivery.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement and help maintain a safe, organised workplace.

About You

We’re looking for someone who is reliable, safety focused and ready to learn.

You’ll ideally bring:

  • Previous warehouse, pick/pack or logistics experience.
  • A current forklift licence and forklift experience.
  • Good attention to detail and safe manual handling practices.
  • Computer skills and confidence learning new systems.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • A proactive approach and the ability to work with minimal supervision.

Experience working with freight providers, warehouse or inventory systems, machinery or Wholegoods would be an advantage. Most importantly, you’ll bring a strong work ethic, willingness to learn and an interest in developing your logistics skills.

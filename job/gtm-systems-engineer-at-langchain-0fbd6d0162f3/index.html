About the job

About Us

At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale.

We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry.

More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale.

About the Role

We’re hiring a GTM Systems Engineer to design, build, and scale the technical infrastructure behind our go-to-market engine.

You’ll own the design and implementation of GTM systems and integrations, connecting our CRM, marketing automation, sales tools, finance and product systems, and data warehouse. You’ll turn manual processes into reliable, automated workflows and help teams trust the data behind the sales funnel.

You’ll partner with Revenue Operations and Sales Operations on business processes, and with Finance, Product, Data Engineering, and IT on connected systems and data. This is a hands-on builder role for a systems thinker who can design and ship technical solutions, make sound architectural decisions, communicate tradeoffs, and own problems end to end in a fast-moving environment.

What You’ll Do

Build and connect the GTM stack

  • Architect and build applications, automations, and integrations across the GTM stack, including Salesforce, CPQ, marketing automation, and sales tools.
  • Design, build, and own integrations and data flows between GTM systems, the finance and product technology stacks, and the data warehouse using APIs, webhooks, and reverse ETL.
  • Partner with Sales Operations to build and maintain automations for data hygiene, pipeline management, deal desk processes, and other lead-to-cash workflows.
  • Prototype and productionize AI-assisted and agent-based workflows that reduce manual work or surface timely account signals, with appropriate human review.

Create reliable data and systems

  • Design and implement data models, deduplication, validation rules, and monitoring to improve data integrity across systems.
  • Build the data flows and infrastructure that power reporting, dashboards, and forecasting in partnership with Revenue Operations and Data Engineering.
  • Build reliable, maintainable solutions using appropriate testing, deployment and release practices, monitoring, documentation, and change management. Troubleshoot integrations and maintain them as connected systems change.
  • Build or implement solutions to close gaps in the GTM stack, and evaluate and retire tools as the business scales.

Partner across the business

  • Work with Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Finance, Product, IT, and Data Engineering to understand needs and build effective solutions.
  • Communicate technical concepts, tradeoffs, and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Prioritize and deliver engineering initiatives and enhancements while resolving issues in production systems.

What You’ll Bring

  • 8+ years of experience building and integrating business or GTM systems, including in Business Systems, GTM Engineering, Revenue Operations, or a technical Sales or Marketing Operations role at a high-growth company.
  • Deep hands-on experience building and customizing Salesforce solutions, including applications, objects, flows, permissions, and integrations.
  • Skills in Apex, Lightning components, Salesforce APIs, SOAP- or REST-based web services, and SOQL.
  • Strong SQL skills and experience working with a data warehouse.
  • Experience with Workato, Zapier, MuleSoft, Boomi, or another integration platform.
  • Experience with CPQ and end-to-end lead-to-cash flows and integrations.
  • Hands-on experience building AI-powered or agent-based workflows to solve practical business problems.
  • A systems-thinking mindset: you design for scale, document your work, and care about clean data.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, sound judgment in prioritizing competing work, and clear communication across teams.
  • Comfort in a high-intensity, high-autonomy environment and a desire to own problems end to end.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Snowflake or BigQuery.
  • Experience building and maintaining billing systems.
  • Scripting experience in Python or JavaScript.

How We’ll Measure Success

Over the first 6–12 months, success could include:

  • High-priority Sales Operations and lead-to-cash workflows are automated or simplified, reducing manual work.
  • Critical integrations are documented, monitored, and maintained, with clear ownership and a path for resolving failures.
  • Revenue Operations and Data Engineering partners see agreed-upon improvements in data quality and confidence in funnel reporting.
  • AI-assisted workflows help GTM teams act on useful signals or spend less time on manual tasks.
  • Cross-functional partners understand the solutions built and how they address business needs.

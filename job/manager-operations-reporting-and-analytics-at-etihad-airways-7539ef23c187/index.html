About the job

Join one of the world's fastest-growing airlines and use analytics to turn complex operational data into clear insights, helping Etihad optimize performance and shape the future of aviation.

As a Manager Operations Reporting & Analytics, you will be accountable to manage the identification and visualization of data insights and descriptive analysis in Etihad’s operations division and manage cross-departmental data projects that contribute to a cost-efficient and optimal operation. You will conduct deep-dive analysis covering all aspects of the operation and presented findings to relevant business units and assist in defining and implementing action plans to improve on operational KPIs.

Accountabilities

  • Manage, coach and mentor a team of data analysts
  • Manage data reporting and analytics within Etihad’s operations division with focus on generating business insights relevant to the division’s key performance indicators, such as OTP, NPS, MHB, customer satisfaction and fuel efficiency
  • Responsible for creation of new and upkeep of existing reports & dashboards with maintaining a repository of all reports under the remit of Operations Analytics unit
  • Conduct data exploration and wrangling of raw datasets and unstructured data using schema-on-read based data platforms
  • Prepare and present analysis outcomes to various business units in the operations division and collaborate closely with other analytical functions within the company
  • Develop analysts in using and being familiar with MS Power BI visualizations and provide further guidance on use of programming languages, such as R and Python subject to business use-case
  • Be familiar with season schedule block analysis, review of historic guest and baggage connectivity information and airport movement compliance review

As an ideal candidate, you must have:

  • Minimum of 8 years experience leading a business intelligence team
  • Experience defining a reporting and analytics strategy and execution framework
  • Hands-on analytics experience using data analysis and visualization tools
  • Ability to lead a team and engage closely with senior level stakeholders
  • Excellent communication skills

Preferred:

  • Experience working in aviation or aviation adjacent experience, ideally in Operations
  • Familiarity with operational systems such as those used for Flight Schedules, Passenger and Bag movement, Airports related systems, etc.
  • Experience in building & managing the data, transformation and semantic layers leveraged by LLMs

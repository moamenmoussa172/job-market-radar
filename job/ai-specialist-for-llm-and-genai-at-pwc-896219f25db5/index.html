About the job

Job Description & Summary

About the team

Our team is a technology-focused group within the AI Center of Excellence for PwC Czech. We are dedicated to applying technological expertise to address practical challenges inside PwC as well as for our clients. By leveraging state-of-the-art advancements in AI and machine learning, we aim to create impactful solutions that align with real-world needs. Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and its application, enabling businesses to harness the full potential of AI-driven innovations.

Therefore, we are seeking an ambitious and technically skilled AI Specialist to join us. As the AI Specialist, you will be at the forefront of innovation, exploring cutting-edge technologies, and integrating them to create novel solutions that drive the client’s success. This role is ideal for someone who possesses a deep passion for technology, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven technologies and enjoys the challenge of applying them in real-world scenarios.

About your manager

Karel is a senior manager in PwC Czech Republic’s AI team, where he focuses on agentic automation, GenAI and LLM solutions. He works with clients and colleagues to turn emerging technologies into practical solutions with measurable business value. Outside of work, Karel enjoys sports, music, and spending time with family and friends, exploring new ideas, and experimenting with the latest technology.

Job description & summary

  • Technology Exploration: Stay up to date with the latest advancements in AI, machine learning, computer vision, and other related fields. Constantly evaluate emerging technologies, frameworks, and tools to identify their potential applications in Industry.
  • Integration of Technologies: Integrate AI technologies with existing software and machinery to enhance their capabilities, efficiency, and performance.
  • Prototyping and Experimentation: Rapidly prototype and experiment with different AI algorithms and models to identify the most effective and efficient solutions.
  • Continuous Improvement: Actively contribute to the continuous improvement of AI-based products and services. Conduct research, propose enhancements, and provide recommendations to optimize existing systems.
  • Collaborative Environment: Work collaboratively with other teams to understand their requirements and provide technical solutions and support when needed.

What matters to us

  • Ability to adapt quickly to new technologies and experiment with diverse AI approaches.
  • Good understanding of current state of the art LLM and GenAI technologies.
  • Experience integrating LLM and GenAI services into processes and workflows.
  • Understanding statistical concepts and machine learning algorithms.
  • Knowledge of Git.
  • Experience developing code in a team.
  • Any experience with the Industrial environment.
  • Ability to adapt quickly to new technologies and experiment with diverse AI approaches.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.

What will help you stand out

  • Knowledge of Python, any additional programming language would be a plus.
  • Any experience in developing AI-based solutions and applications is a plus.
  • Experience with working with Docker and Azure environment would be a plus.

Why you’ll enjoy working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.
  • A flexible benefits programme with 45,000 points to spend on what matters most to you.
  • 30 days of paid time off, including three well-being days and two additional days off at the end of the year.
  • An opportunity to give back to the community with one paid volunteering day every year.
  • The chance to work from one of PwC’s international offices (available from the Senior Associate level).
  • We support your learning and development journey: Benefit from training and certifications in business and digital skills aligned with your area of expertise, as well as soft-skills development covering presentation skills, coaching, effective communication, and more. We cover the costs of professional certifications.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.
  • Social events and Away Days.

At PwC, we help clients solve today’s and tomorrow’s challenges across audit, consulting, tax, legal, technology, and data. We create an environment where people can learn, grow, and build a career in their own way. We believe the best results come from diverse experiences and perspectives. That’s why we foster an inclusive culture where everyone can be themselves, build on their strengths, and contribute to work that makes a real impact.

Interested in joining us? We’d love to hear from you and tell you more about this opportunity.

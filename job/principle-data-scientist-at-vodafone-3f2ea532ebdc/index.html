Role Purpose/Business Unit:

The Principal Data Scientist is the most senior practitioner on the Data Squad and is accountable for shaping the organization’s applied AI and advanced analytics strategy, translating it into measurable business outcomes. As squad lead, this role owns the direction, delivery governance, and technical standards of a cross-functional team comprising Senior Data Scientists, Data Scientists, Machine Learning Engineers, and Data Engineers.

This role partners with product, engineering, security, and business stakeholders to identify high-value opportunities, develop differentiated customer and operational journeys, and build decisioning capabilities that improve growth, efficiency, risk outcomes, and customer experience.

The Principal Data Scientist combines deep statistical and machine learning expertise with strong software engineering practices to deliver production-grade AI — spanning classical ML, Generative AI (LLM applications, RAG, fine-tuning), and agentic systems that plan, use tools, and execute workflows safely under well-defined guardrails.

Your responsibilities will include:

Strategy, Leadership & Stakeholder Management

  • Act as the senior subject matter expert and thought leader across applied ML, GenAI, and decision intelligence for the organization.
  • Lead and manage the functional Data Squad — setting direction, unblocking the team, and driving delivery across Senior Data Scientists, Data Scientists, MLEs, and Data Engineers.
  • Partner with product, engineering, and business leaders to prioritise AI opportunities, define success metrics, and shape roadmaps.
  • Translate complex analysis into clear narratives and recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences, including C-suite and executive stakeholders.
  • Set and uphold standards for experimentation, measurement, documentation, and responsible AI practices across the squad.
  • Foster a high-performance team culture through structured rituals (sprint planning, retrospectives, knowledge-sharing), psychological safety, and continuous improvement.

Applied Machine Learning & Advanced Analytics

  • Lead the end-to-end lifecycle of ML solutions: problem framing, feature engineering, training, validation, deployment, monitoring, and iteration.
  • Develop and review high-quality code for modelling and data pipelines (Python, SQL, distributed compute) and champion software engineering best practices across the squad.
  • Define evaluation approaches (offline/online), run A/B and champion-challenger tests, and assess post-deployment performance and drift.
  • Design reusable feature stores, modelling templates, and evaluation harnesses to accelerate squad delivery velocity.
  • Apply statistical rigour to hypothesis testing, causal inference, and business impact quantification.

Generative AI, LLMOps & Agentic Systems

  • Design and deliver GenAI solutions including RAG, prompt orchestration, tool/function calling, and model selection (open/closed) aligned to risk and cost.
  • Build agentic workflows (planning, routing, memory, tools) with strong guardrails: safety filters, permissions, human-in-the-loop controls, and full auditability.
  • Establish LLM evaluation and monitoring frameworks: quality benchmarks, hallucination testing, grounding checks, latency/cost tracking, and incident response protocols.
  • Collaborate with security, privacy, and legal teams to ensure compliant handling of sensitive data, prompt/response logging, and model governance.
  • Evaluate and select third-party GenAI tooling, platforms, and foundation models appropriate to organisational risk appetite and total cost of ownership.

Team Enablement & Technical Governance

  • Mentor and coach data scientists, MLEs, and data engineers across the squad; lead by example through code reviews, pairing sessions, and technical guidance.
  • Define and enforce data quality, model documentation, and deployment standards.
  • Conduct structured performance feedback and capability development planning for direct reports where applicable.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, and retention of data talent in partnership with HR and leadership.
  • Contribute to reusable assets, internal communities of practice, and cross-team knowledge sharing.

Delivery & Operational Excellence

  • Own squad delivery governance: sprint planning, backlog prioritisation, dependency management, and risk escalation.
  • Design, build, test, and deploy machine learning models to production with appropriate monitoring and rollback capabilities.
  • Analyse use cases and products, then advise on enhancements and deprecation strategies aligned to business priorities.
  • Contribute to organisational AI and data strategy, architecture decisions, and platform investment cases.

The ideal candidate for this role will have:

  • Postgraduate qualification in a quantitative field (Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, Operations Research, Data Science, or Artificial Intelligence) is essential.
  • Master's degree or higher is strongly advantageous.
  • Relevant professional certifications (AWS/Azure/GCP ML Specialty, Databricks Certified) are a plus.
  • 8+ years relevant experience in applied data science / machine learning, including production deployment and measurable business impact.
  • Demonstrated experience leading or managing data science teams, squads, or cross-functional delivery pods.
  • Strong experience in data manipulation and modelling using SQL and Python, including large-scale or distributed processing (e.g., Spark, Databricks).
  • Experience with modern ML frameworks (scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow, XGBoost/LightGBM) and solid statistical foundations.
  • Hands-on GenAI experience: LLM application development, RAG patterns, prompt engineering, and evaluation methodologies; fine-tuning experience advantageous.
  • Experience designing or operating agentic systems with guardrails, monitoring, and human-in-the-loop controls.
  • Experience with MLOps/LLMOps practices (CI/CD, experiment tracking, model versioning, monitoring, governance) and cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP).
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management, and mentorship skills; demonstrated experience leading or coaching other data scientists is expected.

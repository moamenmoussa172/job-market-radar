About the job

Your contribution to something big:

During your internship, your main goal will be the research and development of iOS applications for connected IoT appliances, as part of a multidisciplinary mobile team working with agile methodologies (Scrum). You will contribute to Dashboard App, the codebase behind the HomeCom Easy (Bosch), MyBuderus (Buderus), IVT Anywhere II (IVT) and Vulcano Connect (Vulcano) apps.

These apps won the Red Dot Design Award and are available across Europe. Your main tasks will be:

• Feature Development: Implement new features in Swift, from requirements analysis to delivery, in collaboration with Product Owners, UX, QA and the Android team.

• Improvements and Fixes: Develop improvements and fix bugs, contributing to the stability and quality of App Store releases.

• Testing: Develop tests for the developed code.

• Software Quality: Gain knowledge of the software quality processes of our products (code review, continuous integration, static analysis).

• Technology Research: Analyse and evaluate new mobile development frameworks and tools.

• Cross-functional Collaboration: Work in a multidisciplinary team (iOS, Android, Backend, UX and Testing), supporting and contributing to the mobile development team.

What distinguishes you:

• Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Informatics Engineering or a similar field.
• Swift: Good knowledge of the Swift programming language.
• iOS Development: Familiarity with Xcode, UIKit and/or SwiftUI, and the main iOS architecture
patterns (MVVM, MVC or other).
• Reactive Programming and Concurrency: Knowledge of reactive programming concepts
(Combine, RxSwift or other) and concurrency (async/await, GCD).
• Continuous Integration and Quality: Knowledge of continuous integration environments (for
example, Jenkins), quality tools (for example, Sonar) and Git.
• Testing: Familiarity with XCTest or other testing frameworks.
• Nice to have: Experience with REST APIs or publishing apps on the App Store. Interest in
Artificial Intelligence and basic experience with AI coding assistants (for example, GitHub
Copilot).
• Languages: Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Portuguese and English.
• Attitude: Willingness to learn and share knowledge about mobile apps and iOS, proactivity,
attention to detail and team spirit.

Duration: 12 months

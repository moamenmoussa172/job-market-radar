Working Student (f/m/d) Embedded Software Development

About the job

Are you studying Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or a related field and interested in embedded software and secure microcontroller applications?

Our department develops an embedded, secure operating system for ARM/8051 secure microcontrollers, primarily used in mobile transaction applications. As a Working Student, you will support our experienced development team in day-to-day software development activities and gain practical experience in embedded systems, development automation and hardware-related tasks.

Your Responsibilities

  • Support our embedded software development team with various software implementation tasks in Java and C
  • Contribute to the automation and continuous improvement of development activities
  • Develop supporting tools and perform performance measurements
  • Support the maintenance and continuous improvement of our development infrastructure
  • Contribute to daily Farm Support activities and SW Lab maintenance
  • Update hardware devices, such as readers, with new software
  • Support the setup and management of hardware peripherals, including readers and FPGAs
  • Work closely with experienced engineers on embedded software development topics

Your Profile

  • Currently enrolled in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or a comparable field of study
  • Programming skills in Java and C
  • Familiarity with scripting languages such as Shell or Batch
  • Interest in embedded microcontroller software development
  • Interest in cryptographic security mechanisms
  • Ideally, first hands-on experience with embedded devices such as Arduino or Raspberry Pi
  • Good communication skills in English and/or German
  • A collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively as part of a team
  • Availability to work up to 18 hours per week

What NXP Offers

  • An attractive hourly wage of €19.40, increasing to €20.00 from the 7th month
  • 30 vacation days per year on a full-year basis
  • Flexible working hours
  • The possibility to work in a hybrid setup
  • Access to an on-site cafeteria
  • Networking initiatives and Employee Resource Groups such as Young Community, No eXtra Planet, NXP Equal, Women in NXP, and more, fostering both professional and personal exchange

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security-related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications; therefore, a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Egypt.

About the Role

We are looking for a Mid-Level Full-Stack Developer to join our team and contribute to building scalable, reliable, and user-friendly web applications.

The ideal candidate has 3+ years of professional experience in full-stack development, with strong hands-on experience in Node.js and React. You will work across both the frontend and backend, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and contribute to the full software development lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop, maintain, and enhance scalable web applications across both frontend and backend.
  • Build and maintain backend services and APIs using Node.js.
  • Develop responsive and user-friendly interfaces using React.js.
  • Design and integrate RESTful APIs and work with databases.
  • Write clean, maintainable, reusable, and well-tested code.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues and improve application performance.
  • Collaborate with product, design, QA, and other engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to technical discussions and best practices.
  • Contribute to system design, technical documentation, and continuous improvement of development processes.
  • Stay up to date with modern technologies and development practices.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of professional experience in Full-Stack Web Development.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Node.js and backend development.
  • Strong experience with React.js and modern frontend development.
  • Good understanding of JavaScript/TypeScript and modern development practices.
  • Experience building and integrating RESTful APIs.
  • Good knowledge of relational and/or NoSQL databases.
  • Understanding of software development principles, clean code, and design patterns.
  • Experience working with Git and version control systems.
  • Good understanding of testing and debugging practices.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to work independently while also contributing effectively within a team.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with TypeScript.
  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
  • Familiarity with Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Experience with microservices or distributed systems.
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies.
  • Experience working in a product-based or fast-paced engineering environment.

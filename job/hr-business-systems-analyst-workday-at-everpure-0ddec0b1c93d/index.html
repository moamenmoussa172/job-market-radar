About the job

Everpure (NYSE: P) has evolved from storage pioneer to data platform, closing fiscal 2026 with $3.7 billion in revenue, its first billion-dollar quarter, and accelerating growth into FY27. Our strategic agenda spans the companies defining the next era of technology - hyperscalers, AI labs, the AI hardware supply chain, data platform providers, and the broader AI ecosystem.

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

Architect scalable Workday HCM solutions that power Everpure’s global people strategy and eliminate single points of failure across our HR technology ecosystem. Sitting at the intersection of Human Resources and our Digital Technology Group (DTG), you will serve as the US-hours business solution architect, translating complex organizational needs into credible, high-impact system designs. You will co-own our Workday portfolio alongside a Prague-based counterpart, ensuring continuous global coverage while partnering directly with Lehi-based HR Operations, Centers of Excellence, and engineering teams to turn strategic priorities into measurable business outcomes.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Co-own the global Workday HCM project portfolio with our Prague-based Business Systems Analyst, driving end-to-end functional design, project delivery, and cross-regional coverage so critical HR initiatives never stall.
  • Bridge business strategy and technical execution by pressure-testing HR requirements, architecting scalable solutions, and partnering with DTG engineering teams during quarterly Program Increment (PI) planning to deliver stable, high-impact enhancements.
  • Safeguard enterprise data integrity and employee experience by mapping upstream and downstream impacts across Workday, Greenhouse, ServiceNow, and global payroll and benefits vendors, while leading semi-annual Workday release readiness and UAT.
  • Elevate HR operational efficiency by identifying opportunities to simplify, standardize, and automate workflows—including leveraging AI-enabled capabilities—and tracking realized value across adoption, error reduction, and time saved.
  • Expand team agility by developing a secondary domain focus (such as ServiceNow HRSD, talent systems, or HR analytics) and contributing to shared playbooks that scale our PMO and BSA delivery model.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Proven expertise architecting and deploying enterprise Workday HCM solutions across core business processes—including hire, job change, compensation, absence, and security—with demonstrated ownership through full enhancement lifecycles and semi-annual releases.
  • Strong analytical judgment to translate ambiguous business challenges into clear user stories, solution designs, and test plans, coupled with the confidence to recommend process improvements over unnecessary system configuration.
  • Demonstrated success influencing cross-functional engineering teams in Agile or PI-based delivery models, seamlessly aligning HR stakeholders and technical developers across global time zones (including regular morning overlap with European colleagues).

About the job

Global Pricing is a team of around ten people based in our Berlin Hero Hub. We work closely with local pricing teams, Data Science, Commercial, and Product & Engineering to improve pricing performance and build better pricing tools.

Join the team driving brand growth and customer engagement across our markets. As part of our Marketing Team, you’ll help shape strategies that build customer loyalty and optimize the effectiveness of our marketing investments. With each campaign and initiative, you’ll play a key role in creating connections with millions of customers, positioning Delivery Hero as a leader in the market.

Your mission:

  • Work with your Pricing Manager and the local Korean Pricing team to analyse the market and pricing, and turn the findings into actionable recommendations that improve pricing performance across Korea.
  • Plan A/B tests and run them end to end, from implementation through analysis to communicating results.
  • Monitor the performance of Woowa’s pricing initiatives across Korea and act early on opportunities that improve the P&L.
  • Work with the Data Science team to implement models that improve pricing performance.
  • Build and maintain close relationships with the Korea Local Pricing team, with regular knowledge exchange.
  • Work with experts in Commercial, Data, and Product & Engineering to support the development of a dynamic pricing tool.

What you need to be successful

  • 2–4 years of professional experience in an analytical role, with strong SQL and Excel skills.
  • Hands-on experience with A/B testing and experimentation best practices.
  • Fluent Korean, plus experience working with Korean stakeholders or in the Korean market.
  • Strong analytical skills to assess pricing performance quickly and develop recommendations.
  • A pragmatic approach to problem solving that delivers quick, efficient solutions.
  • Clear communication skills to explain complex pricing topics and gain buy-in from local and central teams.

Nice to have

  • Python and Tableau experience.
  • Experience rolling out new pricing models and tools in Asian or European markets.
  • Experience in a fast-paced environment, ideally in Pricing or Revenue Management.

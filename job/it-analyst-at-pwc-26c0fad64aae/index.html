About the job

Job Description & Summary

About the team

Join PwC Technology Consulting, where business and technology come together to create meaningful impact. Our teams deliver transformation projects across Europe, working with a wide range of industries, technologies, and company cultures. Beyond client work, you’ll be part of six thriving knowledge communities covering areas such as New Technologies, Digital Strategy, Data & Analytics, and more. At PwC, growth is not just encouraged, it's expected.

About your manager

Martin Kolesár helps leading financial institutions successfully navigate large-scale transformation programmes, from post-merger integrations and core banking modernisation to enterprise-wide rollouts and complex data migrations. With more than 15 years of experience in the banking industry, he combines a structured delivery mindset with a pragmatic approach to solving business challenges. You can expect a manager who values collaboration, ownership, and high-quality outcomes.

Job description & summary

As an IT Analyst, you will play a key role in bridging business needs and technology solutions within large-scale banking transformation projects. You will be responsible for:

  • Analysing business environments and mapping processes in corporate lending and collateral management.
  • Gathering, structuring, and documenting business requirements, including impact analysis on IT systems.
  • Identifying opportunities for optimisation across business and technology functions.
  • Designing technical solutions, interfaces, and integration architectures.
  • Coordinating data mapping and transformation activities between systems.
  • Ensuring compliance with technical standards, business requirements, and regulatory expectations.
  • Collaborating with business stakeholders, architects, vendors, and delivery teams.
  • Preparing analytical documentation, use cases, data models, and integration scenarios.
  • Facilitating workshops and steering discussions with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Actively participating in Agile delivery practices such as Scrum and Kanban.

What matters to us

  • University degree in IT, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • At least 5 years of experience in IT analysis within the banking sector.
  • Strong knowledge of integration technologies and APIs (REST, SOAP, XML, JSON).
  • Experience with requirements gathering, system analysis, and solution design.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Fluency in both Czech and English.

What will help you stand out

  • Experience in corporate lending and collateral management domains.
  • Knowledge of collateral-related regulations (CRR, Basel III, etc.).
  • Experience with data migration and transformation initiatives.
  • Understanding of enterprise integration and service orchestration.
  • Hands-on experience with Jira, Confluence, or similar collaboration tools.
  • Experience working in Agile environments and large-scale transformation programmes.

Why you’ll love working here

  • Professional growth that matches your ambitions and pace. Gain in months the kind of experience that can take years to build elsewhere.
  • Fair pay with no gaps. We are among the few companies in the Czech Republic certified for Equal Pay.

  • We support your learning and development journey: Benefit from ongoing training and certifications in consulting, business, and technology skills, including Agile and project management, data analytics (Power BI, Python, Alteryx, Tableau), cloud and Microsoft technologies (Azure, Power Platform), business process analysis, and consulting essentials, such as stakeholder management, communication, and presentation skills.
  • A buddy programme, regular feedback, and access to a coach who can support your professional development and career path.
  • Extensive well-being support and initiatives promoting a healthy lifestyle, from Dr. Digital and Human Dynamic programmes to workplace yoga and running events.
  • An ultrabook and an iPhone with unlimited data.

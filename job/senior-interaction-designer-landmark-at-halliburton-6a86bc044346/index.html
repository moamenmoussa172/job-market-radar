About the Role

Halliburton is seeking a Senior Product Designer to help design and scale user experiences across a portfolio of enterprise applications, while contributing to a unified design system and platform. This role operates at the intersection of product design and system development. You will work directly within product workflows to design high-quality user experiences, while also identifying opportunities to create reusable patterns, components, and interaction models that scale across teams. You will also contribute to the evolution of next-generation user experiences, including AI-assisted and adaptive interfaces, ensuring they are grounded in real user needs and production constraints.

Job Duties

  • Design end-to-end user experiences across complex product workflows
  • Translate product requirements into clear, usable, and scalable interface designs
  • Identify and contribute reusable patterns and components to the design system
  • Ensure alignment between design specifications and implemented experiences
  • Collaborate closely with engineering to support high-quality delivery
  • Contribute to the evolution of interaction models and UX patterns across products
  • Support the adoption and consistent use of the design system within product teams
  • Help define how emerging capabilities—such as AI-assisted interfaces and dynamic UI—are applied in real product scenarios
  • Provide feedback and insights to improve system usability and coverage based on real-world usage

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or related field—or equivalent practical experience
  • 5–8+ years of experience in product design
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end product design work
  • Experience working on complex workflows or enterprise applications
  • Strong interaction design and problem-solving skills
  • Experience collaborating closely with engineering teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience contributing to or working within a design system
  • Experience designing across multiple products or platforms
  • Familiarity with component-based design approaches
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills

AI & Emerging Technology Experience

  • Interest in how AI is shaping user experience and product design
  • Experience designing for or experimenting with:
  • AI-assisted workflows (co-pilot experiences)
  • Dynamic or adaptive interfaces
  • Familiarity with emerging tools in AI-assisted design and prototyping
  • Ability to evaluate and refine AI-influenced designs for usability and consistency

What Sets You Apart

  • Ability to balance product-specific needs with system-level thinking
  • Strong attention to detail and craft, with a focus on usability
  • Curiosity and willingness to explore new interaction models and technologies
  • Ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams

Additional Information

This role is critical in connecting real product needs with system evolution. You will help ensure that the design system and platform are grounded in practical use and continue to evolve based on real-world application.

Mandatory Internship AI Agent for Automated Machine Diagnostics and Root-Cause Analysis

Bosch Group · Elchingen, Germany

LangChain / LlamaIndexPython

About the job

  • During your internship, you will build a reliable agent that analyzes a user's machine behavior directly from CAN measurements and derives solutions across calibrations, software bugs, or application/hardware issues.
  • You will create the agent harness, custom skills, and necessary tools or MCP servers to enable it (e.g. with LangChain).
  • Furthermore, you will set up automated signal and event detection routines to capture anomalies such as limit violations, oscillations, derating, and error states.
  • You will prepare appropriate context for the agent, integrating relevant documentation, parameters, and domain knowledge.
  • Additionally, you will benchmark the agent against established reference cases to assess its accuracy and reproducibility.
  • Moreover, you will automate the simulation loop by replaying measurements within a virtual test bench (e.g., TPT) to cross-compare simulated dynamics with measured behavior.
  • Finally, you will document the developed tool and present your findings and methodology to the team.
  • Education: studies in the field of Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Computer Science or comparable
  • Experience and Knowledge: inPython development, LangChain, Agentic Workflows; basic knowledge of control systems and signal analysis
  • Personality and Working Practice: you excel at analyzing challenges critically, driving innovative solutions, and learning continuously while communicating openly with others
  • Work Routine: your on-site presence is required
  • Enthusiasm: for AI workflows, Agents and Applications
  • Languages: fluent in English and very good in German

Start: February 2027
Duration: 3 - 6 months (confirmation of mandatory internship required)

Requirement for this internship is the enrollment at university. Please attach your CV, transcript of records, enrollment certificate, examination regulations and if indicated a valid work and residence permit.

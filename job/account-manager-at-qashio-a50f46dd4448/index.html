Account Manager

Qashio · Dublin, Ireland

Onsite · Account Manager · Posted 3 November 2026

Apply on Qashio's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Qashio is a global fintech company, dual-headquartered in Dubai and Dublin, helping businesses simplify corporate spending, payments and financial operations.

We're growing quickly across Europe and looking for ambitious, talented people who want to be part of our journey.

The Role

We're looking for a commercially driven Account Manager to own, retain and grow Qashio's existing customer portfolio in Europe.

This is much more than a traditional Customer Success role. You'll take full commercial ownership of your accounts, building strategic relationships, identifying growth opportunities and maximising the value of every customer relationship.

You'll be responsible for driving customer adoption, increasing spend volumes, identifying upselling and cross-selling opportunities, and ensuring Qashio becomes an integral part of our customers' financial operations.

We're looking for someone who is naturally curious, commercially sharp and proactive. Someone who understands their customers' businesses, anticipates their needs and consistently looks for opportunities to grow accounts rather than simply maintain them.

What you'll be doing

  • Own your portfolio: Take full responsibility for the customer lifecycle, from onboarding and implementation through to retention, expansion and long-term growth.
  • Drive revenue growth: Identify and convert upselling and cross-selling opportunities, increase customer spend volumes and maximise account profitability.
  • Drive retention: Proactively identify at-risk accounts, address challenges and develop strategies to minimise churn.
  • Build strategic relationships: Develop strong relationships with key decision-makers, including CFOs, finance leaders and senior stakeholders.
  • Maximise product adoption: Ensure customers are fully leveraging Qashio's products, features, integrations and rewards.
  • Develop account growth plans: Create tailored commercial strategies for each account, identifying untapped opportunities across departments, entities and markets.
  • Lead commercial conversations: Conduct regular business reviews, demonstrate ROI and position additional solutions that support customers' evolving needs.
  • Own your numbers: Track and forecast account performance, retention, expansion revenue, spend volumes and customer health.
  • Be a trusted advisor: Develop a deep understanding of customers' financial operations, challenges and business objectives.
  • Collaborate internally: Work closely with Sales, Product, Support and Operations to deliver an exceptional customer experience and remove barriers to growth.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years of experience in B2B Account Management, Commercial Customer Success or a similar revenue-generating role within SaaS, fintech or payments.
  • A proven track record of growing existing customer accounts and achieving commercial targets.
  • Demonstrated experience in upselling, cross-selling, retention and managing expansion pipelines.
  • Strong commercial acumen with the ability to identify opportunities and turn them into tangible business results.
  • Experience building relationships with senior decision-makers and managing complex customer accounts.
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret customer data and translate insights into commercial opportunities.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation and presentation skills.
  • A proactive, entrepreneurial mindset with a strong sense of ownership and accountability.
  • An understanding of financial operations, corporate payments, accounting software or expense management would be highly advantageous.

What success looks like

  • Consistently achieving retention and account growth targets.
  • Increasing customer spend volumes and product adoption.
  • Building a healthy, predictable pipeline of expansion opportunities.
  • Minimising customer churn and revenue contraction.
  • Developing strong, long-term relationships with key customers.

Why join Qashio?

  • Be part of a rapidly growing global fintech as we scale across Europe.
  • Play a meaningful role in shaping our European account management function.
  • Work alongside an ambitious, high-performing international team.
  • Take real ownership of your portfolio and have a direct impact on business growth.
  • Grow your career with a business that values ambition, initiative and results.

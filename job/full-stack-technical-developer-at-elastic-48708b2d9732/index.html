About the job

Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.

What is The Role

The Marketing Innovation team at Elastic is responsible for developing and maintaining innovative internal applications that improve marketing efficiency and execution. Our flagship app is a homegrown platform crafted to integrate campaign planning, performance reporting, automation, and audience management.

We’re seeking a Full Stack Technical Developer with a backend focus to join our team as a proficient contributor, working closely with Technical Program Managers (TPMs) and users to help bring our roadmap to life.

What You Will Be Doing

  • Build and maintain backend services, RESTful APIs, and database structures that power our internal
    marketing applications.
    • Translate business requirements from marketers and TPMs into reliable, intuitive full-stack features, applying sound judgment within established team practices and procedures.
    • Contribute to the technical implementation of features from the database up to the API layer, while working in the frontend codebase (React/TypeScript).
    • Collaborate with frontend-focused engineers and infrastructure teams to ensure smooth system integration and development lifecycle.
    • Participate in technical planning and estimation, providing input on impact, feasibility, and dependencies.
    • Participate in code reviews, giving and receiving feedback and sharing best practices with your peers.
    • Help us continue to consolidate marketing tools, reduce SaaS dependencies, and deliver custom-built, natively integrated applications that meet unique business needs

What You Bring

  • 2+ years of experience as a full stack or backend-leaning developer, with solid experience in server-side
    development (e.g., Node.js, Python, or Go).
  • Working proficiency in frontend development using React and TypeScript
  • Good understanding of API design, data modeling, and backend performance, with a growing grasp of
    system architecture.
  • Experience delivering technical features and collaborating across functions (PM, TPM, Design, etc.).
  • Familiarity with cloud environments (ideally Google Cloud) and the principles behind resilient distributed systems.
  • Experience working with CI/CD pipelines and GitOps-style development workflows.
  • Strong interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate with non-technical team members and translate business needs into technical solutions.
  • Passion for continuous improvement, creative problem solving, and delivering delightful internal tools.

Bonus Points

  • Familiarity with Google Cloud services (e.g., Cloud Functions, BigQuery)
  • Experience with NoSQL databases, specifically Elasticsearch
  • Exposure to modern web application frameworks like Next.js
  • Experience building data pipelines, analytics dashboards, or data visualizations
  • Experience integrating AI capabilities or LLMs into enterprise applications
  • Hands-on work with no-code/low-code integration tools (e.g., Zapier) or automation platforms

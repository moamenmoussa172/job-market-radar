About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

About this team and role:

Mozilla is seeking a Staff Compensation Analyst with expert-level Excel skills and deep expertise in data analysis and analytics to help develop and deliver compensation programs for our global workforce. You will work with complex datasets, build analytical models, and turn findings into clear recommendations that inform compensation decisions.

As a member of a small compensation team, you will independently lead assigned programs and projects while providing support and backup across other areas. Your work will span market pricing, salary structures, annual compensation planning, executive compensation, sales incentives, and consultation with People Business Partners and Talent Acquisition.

This is an individual contributor role requiring strong analytical judgment, substantial program ownership, and the ability to improve how we use data and deliver our work.

What you’ll do:

  • Lead compensation analysis and modeling. Combine employee, market, and financial data to assess competitiveness, internal equity, pay distribution, and program effectiveness. Build models that help leaders evaluate options, costs, and employee impact.
  • Build and maintain advanced Excel tools. Develop accurate, scalable models and reporting tools using advanced formulas, lookup functions, pivot tables, and data validation. Structure work so that calculations are traceable, assumptions are clear, and outputs are easy to maintain.
  • Strengthen data quality and reporting. Extract, reconcile, and analyze data from Workday and other sources. Investigate discrepancies, establish validation checks, and improve the consistency and reliability of compensation reporting.
  • Own compensation programs and projects. Manage assigned work from planning through implementation, including analysis, recommendations, stakeholder coordination, communications, and ongoing administration.
  • Lead market analysis and job pricing. Evaluate roles, match jobs to market surveys, assess competitive positioning, and recommend compensation ranges. Apply a consistent approach to job levels and internal comparisons across functions and countries.
  • Maintain salary structures. Analyze market movement and employee positioning, model potential changes, and recommend updates that balance competitiveness, internal equity, and cost.
  • Deliver annual compensation planning. Develop planning tools and guidelines, validate employee data, model budgets and outcomes, support decision makers, and coordinate accurate implementation with People Operations and Finance.
  • Support executive compensation. Conduct benchmarking, model compensation scenarios, prepare materials for leadership and Compensation Committee review, and administer assigned executive compensation processes.
  • Support sales compensation. Partner with business leaders, Sales Operations, and Finance on incentive plan design, modeling, documentation, and administration. Help resolve questions about plan application and payout calculations.
  • Advise business partners. Provide practical recommendations on offers, promotions, role changes, retention needs, and other compensation decisions. Explain the analysis, tradeoffs, and connection to our compensation philosophy.
  • Apply AI to improve how we work. Identify and implement practical uses of approved AI tools to support analysis, streamline recurring tasks, and improve documentation and communications, while protecting confidential data and validating outputs.
  • Improve processes and build shared capability. Automate recurring analysis, simplify workflows, document processes, share knowledge, and serve as a reliable backup for programs owned by other team members.

What you’ll bring:

  • Expert-level Excel proficiency. Demonstrated ability to build and maintain complex analytical models using advanced formulas, lookup functions, pivot tables, and data validation. You can troubleshoot calculations, reconcile multiple data sources, and create tools others can use confidently.
  • Deep expertise in data analysis and analytics. Ability to frame a business question, identify the right data and analytical approach, evaluate patterns and outliers, test assumptions, and translate findings into actionable recommendations.
  • Experience working with large, complex datasets. Strong practices for data cleaning, reconciliation, quality assurance, and documentation, with exceptional attention to accuracy.
  • Familiarity with Workday, including navigating employee and compensation data, using reports and exports, and partnering with HR systems teams to resolve data or reporting issues.
  • Significant compensation experience, typically gained through six or more years of progressively responsible work, including independent ownership of programs or complex projects.
  • Strong experience in market pricing, job evaluation, salary structures, and annual compensation planning.
  • Experience in both executive compensation and sales compensation, including benchmarking and modeling executive pay and supporting sales incentive design and administration.
  • Experience supporting compensation across multiple countries.
  • Experience applying AI tools to analytical or operational work, with sound judgment about appropriate uses, data confidentiality, and the need to independently verify results.
  • The ability to communicate complex analysis clearly, including presenting findings through effective charts, concise summaries, and practical recommendations.
  • Sound judgment when working with incomplete information, competing priorities, and sensitive employee data.
  • Strong project management skills and a record of delivering accurate work across multiple priorities.
  • A collaborative approach and the confidence to question assumptions, identify risks, and recommend practical solutions.
  • A degree in a relevant field or equivalent professional experience.

Additional experience that would be helpful:

  • Workday Advanced Compensation and report development.
  • Aon Radford market data and compensation survey participation.
  • Technology industry compensation practices.
  • Cash-based long-term incentive programs.
  • Certified Compensation Professional or similar professional development.

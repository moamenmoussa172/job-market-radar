About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way.

The Community You Will Join:

The AirCover Insurance Platform team builds the systems behind AirCover, which includes Host liability insurance, Experiences liability insurance and Host damage protection. We are expanding insurance products across global markets and building the experimentation capabilities that help us understand and increase the share of Hosts and guests who are covered. You will join a fast-growing team with greenfield opportunities and a multi-year platform roadmap.

The Difference You Will Make:

  • Contribute to backend services and APIs for policy, coverage, enrollment and regional rules that scale to new markets.
  • Translate product and regulatory requirements into clean, well-tested code and configurable platform behavior.
  • Implement experimentation hooks, logging and metrics so Product and Data Science can test offers and measure attach rate with confidence.
  • Partner with Product, Design, Data Science, Legal and other engineering teams to deliver high-impact work.
  • Use data to find gaps in conversion and reliability, and drive fixes and improvements.
  • Participate in code reviews, technical design discussions and operational improvements across the team.

A Typical Day:

  • Design, build and operate distributed backend services in a service-oriented architecture, with reliable monitoring, logging and alerting.
  • Write and review code and technical designs, and apply sound engineering judgment within your scope.
  • Build data pipelines and events that support analytics and experiments.
  • Collaborate with infrastructure, security and privacy teams to meet high standards for data protection across regions.
  • Take part in on-call rotations and help improve system reliability.

Your Expertise:

  • 3+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a focus on backend services.
  • Proficiency in at least one backend language such as Java, Kotlin, Python or Go.
  • Experience building and operating services and APIs, with working knowledge of relational databases and SQL.
  • Bachelor's and/or Master's degree, preferably in Computer Science, or equivalent experience.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with distributed systems and service-oriented architecture in a production environment.
  • Familiarity with A/B testing or experimentation systems, and comfort using data to inform product decisions.
  • Experience in insurance, payments, fintech or other regulated domains.
  • Experience with multi-region or configurable, rules-driven platforms.
  • Ability to work through ambiguity and collaborate deeply with cross-functional teams.
  • Strong communication skills, able to explain technical topics to technical and non-technical audiences.

A Note on Recruiting Scams: Scammers sometimes pose as Airbnb recruiters to get money or personal information from candidates. A few things that will always be true for Airbnb’s hiring process: our open roles are posted on Airbnb’s Career’s Page at careers.airbnb.com, and our recruiters correspond only from @airbnb.com or @ext.airbnb.com email addresses. Our recruiters will never ask for your Social Security number, bank account details, passport, or payment app information while you are interviewing. We’ll also never ask you to pay a fee, send money, deposit or cash a check, or purchase work-related equipment (such as a company laptop) during the interview process. We encourage candidates to remain vigilant of these recruiting scams and not share sensitive information if you do not believe an individual is actually affiliated with Airbnb.

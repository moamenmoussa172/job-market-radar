About the job

AWS Applied AI Solutions (AAIS) is building toward a future where every business innovates with Amazon AI teammates. To get there, we build AI solutions that improve human capabilities and transform entire business functions. We create end-to-end products that surprise and delight out-of-the-box, making complex things easy and hard things possible, with no cloud experience required. We start with customers who embrace the future and build bridges to meet the rest where they are. We pursue ambitious opportunities with conviction, and we are looking for builders who share that mindset.
Would you like to join one of the fastest-growing organizations within Amazon Web Services (AWS) and help our largest enterprise customers maximize the value and benefits of AWS Services?
As a Senior Cloud Operations Architect (or TAM) in the AWS Enterprise Support Enterprise segment team you will have a direct impact in helping our customers gain the most value from cloud technology. TAMs actively engage at the account level providing recommendations and proactive advice throughout the entire cloud adoption life cycle.
The Senior Cloud Operations Architect is the customer's trusted advisor and operational excellence expert for our Enterprise Support customers. The close relationships developed with your customers across all levels of their business will allow you to understand their business and operational needs and technical challenges and help them achieve the greatest value from cloud technologies. You will provide advocacy and strategic technical guidance to help plan and build solutions using best practices and proactively keep your customers' AWS environments operationally healthy.
As a Senior Cloud Operations Architect you'll craft and execute strategies with senior customer stakeholders up to C-level to drive our customers' adoption and use of AWS services. This support extends to addressing strategy-related queries aiding in project and launch planning and resolving operational challenges. You will work with large customer engineering organizations and advise management on complex cloud architecture decisions.
This is a post-sales advisory role within our Enterprise Support team where you can further develop your technical leadership and consulting skills while working with organizations facing sophisticated operational challenges across the Nordics region. You'll leverage best practices including the AWS Well-Architected Framework, AWS Trusted Advisor, and operational reviews to help customers maximize the value they derive from AWS.
The AWS Enterprise Support team interacts with leading companies and believes that world-class support is critical to customer success. AWS Enterprise Support also partners with a global list of customers that are building mission-critical applications on top of AWS services.
Join us in helping customers build operationally excellent cloud environments that enable innovation while optimizing performance, security, and cost.
Key job responsibilities
• Provide customers with deep technical expertise across multiple technology domains to achieve operational excellence in security, resilience, and efficiency.
• Act as the customer's trusted advisor, developing strategies with senior stakeholders up to C-level to drive AWS adoption and operational maturity.
• Collaborate with Solutions Architects, Account Managers, Customer Solutions Managers, and other internal teams to ideate around your customers' most challenging business problems.
• Own end-to-end customer technical support working with internal and external engineering teams to design long-term operational strategies.
• Lead architectural reviews and workshops to advance your customers' technical objectives and ensure solutions are designed for successful deployment in the cloud.
• Proactively identify and mitigate risks driving efficiencies and simplification in customer operations plans and product adoption.
• Act as a thought leader sharing best practices through forums such as AWS blogs, whitepapers, reference architectures, and public-speaking events such as AWS Summit, AWS re:Invent, etc.
• Provide detailed reviews of service disruptions, monthly and quarterly metrics, and detailed pre-launch planning.
• Mentor and develop other TAMs, actively participate in the hiring process, and contribute to raising the bar for the team.
• Provide data and anecdotes on what is working and what is not back to the larger TAM community and product teams.
A day in the life
In this role you'll leverage your extensive technical knowledge to ensure our customers' cloud environments are secure, resilient, and optimized on the AWS platform. As a trusted advisor you'll play a pivotal role in ensuring customer success as they navigate complex cloud transformations.
Your day might include leading an architectural review with a customer's engineering leadership to plan a critical migration followed by a proactive risk assessment using AWS Trusted Advisor and Health dashboards. You'll guide customers through trade-off decisions between performance, cost, and resilience and help them implement best practices at scale.
You will develop and deliver enablement sessions such as Well-Architected workshops, Game Days, and Immersion Days to build customer capability. When critical incidents occur you'll act as the escalation point and coordinate with AWS service teams to drive rapid resolution while minimizing business impact.
Throughout it all you'll balance strategic advisory work with tactical execution building deep trust and delivering measurable outcomes for your customers across the Nordics enterprise landscape.

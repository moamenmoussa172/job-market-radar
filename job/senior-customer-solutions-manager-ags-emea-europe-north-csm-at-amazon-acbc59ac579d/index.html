About the job

As an AWS Senior Customer Solutions Manager you will be responsible for helping guide AWS customers along their journey to the cloud. In this role you will ensure that all AWS teams are working together effectively and efficiently to deliver outcomes for the customer.
Key job responsibilities
You will be a trusted partner to our customers, leveraging your experience with cloud transformations to help shepherd them through their stages of AWS adoption. You will guide customers through the operational, educational, and governance aspects of a successful cloud journey, translating technical and business requirements into adoption roadmaps that deliver measurable value.
You will interface with customer and AWS leadership, driving collaboration between the core groups (Sales, Support, Solutions Architecture, and Professional Services), product/engineering teams, and customer teams, planning and supporting major workload initiatives. You will translate strategic initiatives into executable actions, working backwards from your customers’ key milestones. The execution of education plans, roadmap to business outcome alignments, Executive Briefing Sessions, and go-live events and transitions are a few items owned by the CSM that are critical to the success of our customers’ cloud journey.
You will drive customer value realization, building business cases and ensuring adoption leads to measurable outcomes such as efficiency gains, cost savings, or growth. You will leverage emerging technologies, including AI, to accelerate delivery, identify risks, and build mechanisms that scale execution across your engagements.
At AWS, you are encouraged to think big, invent and take ownership on customer challenges. You will not only bring the best of AWS and Amazon to our customers, you will proactively help solve the customer’s challenges through new ideas, tools, and mechanisms.
Successful candidates will have a strong delivery and change management background, be detail oriented, have excellent problem-solving abilities, and be exemplary communicators at the project and management level. You will be a peer leader, have the ability to gain stakeholder buy-in, negotiate and lead virtual teams. Your technical knowledge and operational excellence will influence the team’s decisions, provide insight, and help drive secure and robust solutions.
You will evangelize AWS services and influence customers to adopt them. You should be passionate about delivering a great customer experience by deploying AWS solutions and inspiring innovation. You will be obsessed with contributing to the day-to-day management of your customers’ successful adoption of AWS.
Do you look around corners for ways to engage and service customers? Are you passionate about using technology to solve business problems that have big customer impact?
Come build the future with us.
A day in the life
Your day might include participating in strategic planning sessions with customers, coordinating with internal AWS teams, reviewing adoption progress, and identifying opportunities for innovation. You'll balance relationship building with technical guidance while ensuring customer success.
- Experience leading customer initiatives with clear business objectives and technical requirements across multiple workstreams
- Working knowledge in multiple relevant technology domains (compute, networking, data, security, AI/ML)
- Ability to participate in customer technical discussions and provide guidance on best practices and technical solutions
- Proficiency in solution architecture concepts across multiple cloud service categories
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present to senior managers and cross-functional stakeholders
- 7+ years of experience in customer-facing technology roles involving cloud adoption, digital transformation, or IT program delivery
- Engineering, information technology, or program management background (e.g., CIS, CS, CE, EE degree, or PMP, Agile certification, or equivalent experience)
- AWS Solutions Architect Associate and AWS AI Practitioner certifications (required within 6 months of hire)
- Experience building business cases that connect technical adoption to measurable business outcomes
- Demonstrated understanding of industry-specific technical challenges and solutions
- Experience using AI and automation tools to accelerate delivery and reduce manual effort

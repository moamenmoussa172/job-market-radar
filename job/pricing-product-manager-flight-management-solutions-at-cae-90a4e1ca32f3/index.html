About the job

⠀

As a Business/Pricing Product Manager, Flight Management Solutions, you will help shape the commercial strategy of CAE’s Flight Operations Solutions platform, Flightscape, with a primary focus on the Flight Plan Manager product.

This is a business-focused product management role. You will analyze customer contracts, pricing models, service-level agreements, product usage, revenue, and profitability to generate insights that support product and sales decisions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze customer pricing, product usage, revenue, costs, and profitability to identify trends, risks, and commercial opportunities.
  • Develop data-driven pricing insights and guidance to support Sales when evaluating opportunities with prospective customers.
  • Conduct statistical and financial analyses of customer, contract, and product data.
  • Translate customer insights and operational pain points into clear business and functional requirements.
  • Participate in backlog grooming and sprint planning; write user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Support discovery activities such as interviews, demos, and data analysis to validate problem statements.
  • Collaborate with SCRUM teams to design and deliver functionality that adds measurable value for airline operations.
  • Prepare and run internal/external demos; capture feedback and convert it into actionable backlog items.
  • Maintain feature documentation and contribute to release notes.

Location & Travel

  • Kraków, Poland (Hybrid working approach: 3 days in the office).
  • Approximately 20% travel (domestic and international) for customer workshops and industry events.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 1–2 years of experience in Business Analysis.
  • Experience or relevant knowledge of pricing, P&L or profitability
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to distill complex or ambiguous information into actionable requirements.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills across cultures and time zones.
  • Demonstrated ability to work in cross-functional teams and deliver results.
  • Full professional fluency in English.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics or related field.
  • Must have legal authorization to work in Poland.

Your Responsibilities

As a Inventory Analyst for Hebron Parts Distribution Center located in Hebron, IN you will:

  • Searches for trends and identifies problems in the inventory control and develops inventory metrics.
  • Determines root cause of problems using six sigma problem solving methodology.
  • Develops corrective action plans and revises metrics as necessary.
  • Monitors corrective action plan results.
  • Pursues ongoing system improvements, evaluating new technology as potential solutions to existing problems.
  • Facilitates change management for the inventory control process.

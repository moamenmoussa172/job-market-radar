About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in France.

Hi, we’re Back Market.

We’re here to help make tech reliable, affordable, and better than new. We're a global marketplace for refurbished devices, helping lower our collective environmental impact by providing trustworthy, affordable tech with 92% less carbon emissions than new.

Yep, you read that right. Turns out refurbished tech is way better for the planet than new. In fact, With every device purchased on Back Market, our positive impact on the planet grows. From our Customer Care representatives to our software engineer, every individual at Back Market cuts the planet — and consumers — a break. Our mission is simple: to do more with what we already have.

Are you ready to join us?

Join our Customer Experience Analytics team and help build the data foundation behind a better customer journey at Back Market. You'll be working with a crew of analysts to serve data-driven decisions across the customer experience. We collect trustworthy behavioural data, build analyses, and help teams use data in their day-to-day decisions.

As a Customer Experience Analytics Engineer, you will turn messy data into reliable, scalable models that drive real business decisions. You'll partner closely with our customer journey, engagement and service team to make sure they can answer important questions about customer behaviour, engagement, retention and the impact of product and CRM initiatives.

This role combines hands-on data modelling with a strong understanding of business needs. You will help define the metrics, datasets and tools that allow the Customer Experience teams to move quickly while keeping a high level of trust in the data.

Your mission

You will own and develop the analytics engineering foundations for the Customer Experience vertical.

Build trusted data foundations

  • Build, maintain and improve production-grade data models using dbt and BigQuery.
  • Transform data from Amplitude, customer systems, experimentation tools and Braze into clean, reusable datasets.
  • Define reliable sources of truth for customer behaviour, engagement, retention, CRM performance and experiment results.
  • Improve data model performance, documentation, testing and cost efficiency.

Support product and service innovation

  • Partner with product, business, operations and engineering teams to assess new product and service opportunities.
  • Help teams define the right metrics, data requirements and success criteria before a project starts.
  • Support the launch and measurement of new services and partnerships, such as a potential partnership with a telecom operator.
  • Build the datasets and reporting needed to monitor adoption, customer behaviour, operational performance and business impact after launch.
  • Use data to identify opportunities to improve existing products and services.

Enable experimentation and customer engagement analytics

  • Build reliable datasets for product and business experiments, including experiment populations, exposure data, treatment groups and results.
  • Partner with analysts and product teams to improve behavioural tracking and experiment analysis.
  • Build and maintain data models for CRM and Braze performance.
  • Support customer segmentation, lifecycle analysis and campaign measurement.
  • Help teams understand how product and CRM initiatives affect engagement, conversion, retention and customer experience.

Make analytics accessible and reliable

  • Build trusted datasets and reusable components in OMNI.
  • Define clear metrics and dimensions that stakeholders can use independently.
  • Implement tests, monitoring and alerts for critical data models.
  • Document model logic, metric definitions, ownership and known limitations.
  • Support analysts and business stakeholders in using the right data for their decisions.
  • Contribute to analytics engineering standards, data governance and best practices across the team.

You should apply if

You have:

  • 3+ years of experience in analytics engineering, data analysis engineering, business intelligence engineering or a similar role.
  • Strong expertise with dbt, BigQuery, Python, and SQL—you're comfortable building production-grade data models
  • Track record of translating business needs into reliable data infrastructure
  • Excellent communication skills in English; French is a plus but not required

Nice to have:

  • Experience designing and scaling data pipelines with orchestration tools like Airflow
  • Experience working with event-based or behavioural data.
  • Experience working with external data sources or data enrichment

Familiarity with data quality frameworks and testing

About the job

Join Us

At Vodafone, we’re not just shaping the future of connectivity for our customers – we’re shaping the future for everyone who joins our team. When you work with us, you’re part of a global mission to connect people, solve complex challenges, and create a sustainable and more inclusive world. If you want to grow your career whilst finding the perfect balance between work and life, Vodafone offers the opportunities to help you belong and make a real impact.

What you’ll do

The jobholder will perform Presales Analysis and present Solutions for Vodafone’s Networking & Fixed Telephony solutions to Private Corporate customers. Will have the opportunity to develop expertise in new technologies/services (ex. SDWAN, SASE, Cloud Telephony etc) and work closely with a wide network of key accounts.

• Perform daily visits (physical or digital) to investigate & analyse Corporate/SME Customer needs so as to propose technical solutions that meets customer requirements
• Lead the technical and commercial discussion both with customers and internal organization. Be responsible for the preparation and presentation of Solution Propositions and RFP responses
• Liaise thoroughly with Internal and External business Partners, in order to deliver flawless Technical Solutions
• Achieve sales Targets by co-operating with Account Managers
• Earn Customer Loyalty by becoming the long-term contact of choice for the customer’s Digital Transformation journey and IT solutions lifecycle.

Who you are

• Previous working experience in a similar position in a Telecommunications or IT company
• Ability to understand the customer’s IT operation model and propose solutions for WAN/LAN networking (SDWAN/SDLAN), Security (SASE) as well as Network Security (NGFW, DDoS), Cloud and Microsoft Teams Telephony
• Experience in technical writing for the above, both in Greek and English
• Ability and interest to learn about the latest business services and products.
• Very good interpersonal skills and ability of fluent communication with customers and partners.
• Strong results orientation, attention to detail & flexibility to work under pressure & tight timelines

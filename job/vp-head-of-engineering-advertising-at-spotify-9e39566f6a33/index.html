VP, Head of Engineering - Advertising

About the job

Our mission on the Advertising Product & Technology team is to build a next generation advertising platform that aligns with our unique value proposition for audio and video. We work to scale the user experience for hundreds of millions of fans and hundreds of thousands of advertisers. This scale brings unique challenges as well as tremendous opportunities for our artists and creators.

Spotify is building the next generation of advertising, investing in the technology and capabilities that enable advertisers to reach audiences, deliver relevant experiences, and achieve measurable business outcomes across audio, video, and beyond.

As our advertising business evolves, the scale, intelligence, reliability, and efficiency of our technology platform will be fundamental to our ability to compete and grow.

We are seeking a VP, Head of Engineering, Advertising to define and lead our global advertising engineering strategy, strengthen and scale our technology platform, and shape the architecture and engineering capabilities required for Spotify Advertising's future.

Reporting directly to the SVP of Advertising, you will be a member of the global Advertising leadership team, operating within a business with end-to-end P&L accountability. You will lead a large, multidisciplinary organization spanning Advertising Engineering and Insights, working closely with Product, Data, and Commercial leadership.

This is an opportunity to shape the future of a sophisticated advertising technology platform, advancing real-time decisioning, machine learning, AI, and engineering excellence while building the technical foundation for a significantly larger business.

We are looking for an exceptional engineering executive who combines deep technical and architectural judgment, experience leading at scale, strong commercial instincts, and the ability to build outstanding engineering organizations.

What You'll Do

  • Set the engineering strategy and architecture. Define the long-term technical vision, investment priorities, and target architecture for Spotify’s advertising platform — balancing near-term delivery with future scale. Own build-versus-buy decisions, technical debt prioritization, and the transformation required to get from where we are to where we need to be.
  • Scale our advertising technology. Lead engineering across Ads Manager, Spotify Ad Exchange, ad serving, programmatic integrations, data infrastructure, and machine learning-based optimization.
  • Raise the bar on platform reliability. Advance standards across availability, latency, delivery accuracy, data quality, observability, incident management, and production resilience.
  • Advance intelligent advertising. Develop capabilities across real-time decisioning, auctions, bidding, targeting, relevance, pacing, pricing, and optimization to improve advertiser outcomes, monetization, and listener experience.
  • Fundamentally rethink how we develop and operate technology through AI. Redesign engineering workflows — not just adopt tools into existing processes — to deliver measurable improvements in productivity, quality, and speed across development, testing, debugging, and operations.
  • Partner across the Advertising leadership team. Work closely with Product, Data Science, and Commercial leadership to connect technical investments to customer value and business growth. Bring engineering perspective to major business decisions.
  • Build and develop the engineering organization. Attract exceptional technical leaders, strengthen succession, and foster a culture of ownership, accountability, and continuous improvement.
  • Who You Are
  • You have 15+ years of engineering experience, including 5+ years leading large, multilayered engineering organizations (200+ engineers) within global technology businesses.
  • You have a deep expertise in advertising technology and large-scale real-time systems, including ad serving, auctions, bidding, targeting, optimization, and measurement.
  • You have a track record of designing, scaling, and materially improving high-volume, low-latency, data-intensive platforms, with strong architectural judgment and operational discipline across reliability, observability, and incident management.
  • You have experience with large-scale ML systems and their application to real-time decisioning, pricing, optimization, and marketplace performance. You have personally led the use of AI to fundamentally reshape how engineering teams build and operate — not just introduced tools, but changed the work itself.
  • You have demonstrated experience personally leading significant technology and organizational transformations — re-platforming, restructuring, or modernizing at scale — with clear before-and-after outcomes you can point to.
  • You have the ability to connect engineering decisions to business outcomes — advertiser value, platform economics, speed to market, and revenue growth.
  • You have experience building high-performing engineering organizations: attracting senior technical talent, developing leaders, and establishing succession pipelines.
  • You are a collaborative leader with the conviction to challenge assumptions across Product, Data, and Commercial teams while building alignment.
  • You combine long-term strategic vision with a pragmatic, results-oriented approach to execution.
  • Where You'll Be
  • New York is the preferred location for this role.
  • We are open to considering exceptional candidates based elsewhere, with the expectation of 30% travel to support leadership collaboration, customer engagement, and business priorities.
  • The United States base range for this position is $384,952 to $549,932, plus equity. The benefits available for this position include health insurance, six-month paid parental leave, 401(k) retirement plan, monthly meal allowance, 23 paid days off, 13 paid flexible holidays, paid sick leave. These ranges may be modified in the future.

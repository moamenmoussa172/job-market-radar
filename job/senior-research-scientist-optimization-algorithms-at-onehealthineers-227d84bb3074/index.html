About the job

Join us in pioneering breakthroughs in healthcare. For everyone. Everywhere. Sustainably.
Our inspiring and caring environment forms a global community that celebrates diversity and individuality. We encourage you to step beyond your comfort zone, offering resources and flexibility to foster your professional and personal growth, all while valuing your unique contributions.

As Senior Research Scientist in the Optimization Algorithms team in Helsinki, you will contribute to the development of high-performance algorithms used by clinicians around the world to plan advanced radiotherapy treatments.

This position offers the opportunity to solve challenging problems at the intersection of medical physics, mathematics, optimization, and software engineering while contributing to products that help improve cancer treatment.

We are looking for someone with experience in algorithm development and numerical methods who can quickly take ownership of complex technical challenges. In addition to technical expertise, we value strong collaboration skills.

We recognize that talent comes from many different paths and encourage applicants to apply even if their experience does not align perfectly with every qualification listed.

This opportunity could be for you if you:

  • Enjoy taking ownership and delivering results.
  • Have a collaborative and innovative problem-solving mindset.
  • Enjoy working closely with colleagues and sharing knowledge across disciplines.

Your role:

  • Develop and improve radiotherapy treatment planning algorithms.
  • Research, design, implement, and evaluate new optimization methods and algorithmic solutions.
  • Contribute to the full software development lifecycle, from concept and design to implementation, testing, and delivery.
  • Collaborate closely with software engineers, physicists, researchers, product managers, and clinical users.
  • Support successful product releases by collaborating with teams and contributing to development activities as needed.
  • Participate in technical discussions, innovation initiatives, and the continuous improvement of our treatment planning solutions.
  • Contribute to technical decision-making and help shape the future direction of our solutions.

Experience and Qualifications:

  • MSc or PhD in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Strong programming skills in modern C++.
  • Solid theoretical and practical experience in algorithm design, numerical methods, optimization, scientific computing, or related areas.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Fluency in English language, both written and spoken.

Beneficial Skills & Experience:

  • Medical imaging, radiotherapy, medical device software, or other regulated software environments.
  • CUDA / GPU programming.
  • Machine learning.
  • Quick prototyping and scripting languages such as Python.
  • Scientific research and publication activities.

What We Can Offer:

  • Inspiring work atmosphere and a supportive work community.
  • Meaningful work that contributes to improving cancer treatment.
  • Opportunities for continuous learning and professional growth.
  • International and cross-cultural working environment.
  • Hybrid working model.
  • Office located in the heart of Helsinki.

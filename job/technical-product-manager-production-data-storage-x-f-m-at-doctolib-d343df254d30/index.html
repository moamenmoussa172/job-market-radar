About the job

Set a new pulse for healthcare!

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to join the DODO team in the Platform domain.

Your mission will be to build and maintain the common building blocks of Doctolib's system with a "Platform as a Product" mindset, abstracting and simplifying complex, critical data infrastructure so that product teams can focus on what matters: building faster, better products for healthcare professionals and patients. You will work closely with 600+ engineers across 70+ teams, owning the vision and strategy for data management products that form the foundation of everything we build.

Working in the product team at Doctolib means building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. We operate in feature teams within an agile environment, collaborating closely with engineering, design, and business teams.

What you'll do

Your responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  • Own and align the 2-year vision for data storage at Doctolib, defining which datastore products we build, which we source off the shelf, and how they serve both the monolith and the growing ecosystem of services around it
  • Define the product strategy and roadmap for data management products used by 600+ engineers, spanning multiple datastores (PostgreSQL/Aurora, Kafka, Debezium, Couchbase), file storage, data access, and data lifecycle solutions
  • Drive reliability, recoverability, and cost transparency across teams, including SLOs, alert ownership, backup and restore testing, and per-team cost visibility
  • Define the experience for managing production data for services outside the monolith and for new AI product storage needs
  • Partner with Staff and Senior Staff Engineers to assess the impact of complex architectural decisions, facilitate consensus across diverse viewpoints, and align stakeholders on contested trade-offs
  • Measure and improve engineer satisfaction with data management products, leading projects end-to-end, from problem definition and specs writing to development validation, rollout, and impact follow-up

Who you are

Before you read on: if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply.

You'll be a great fit if you:

  • Have at least 5 years of experience in Product Management with direct product ownership, working with developers, leading rituals, and writing specifications
  • Have extensive experience with technical products and platforms, and a passion for solving complex, large-scale infrastructure challenges
  • Can drive change across large engineering organizations, you're comfortable influencing 600+ engineers across more than 70 teams
  • Have proven experience building and launching products in a large-scale, international B2B environment, where moving fast without slowing down feature development is a constant challenge
  • Are fluent in both English and French (C1 level minimum) and are comfortable working in an international environment
  • Have an entrepreneurial mindset and the ambition to transform healthcare

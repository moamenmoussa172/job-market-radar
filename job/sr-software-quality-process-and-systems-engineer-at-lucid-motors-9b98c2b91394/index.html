Sr. Software Quality Process & Systems Engineer

Mission

Drive software quality excellence through scalable quality processes, governance frameworks, and data-driven systems that improve development efficiency, strengthen release readiness, and enable predictable software quality across vehicle programs.

The Role

  • Define and maintain software quality processes, governance frameworks, and operational standards across development and validation organizations.
  • Drive improvement of defect management processes, including issue lifecycle management, root-cause analysis, corrective actions, and closure effectiveness.
  • Develop and maintain quality dashboards, KPIs, scorecards, and executive reporting to provide visibility into software maturity, program health, and release readiness.
  • Lead enhancements to JIRA, QUAL, PDD, and related quality systems through workflow optimization, automation, and process standardization.
  • Partner with Engineering, Validation, Program Management, Product Quality, Functional Safety, and Cybersecurity teams to improve process compliance and execution.
  • Support ASPICE initiatives, process audits, governance reviews, and continuous improvement activities.
  • Drive PDD governance, deliverable tracking, quality reviews, and risk management across vehicle programs.
  • Create quality procedures, standards, training materials, and best practices.
  • Present quality metrics, risks, and improvement recommendations to leadership and executive stakeholders.

What Success Looks Like

  • Consistent adoption of software quality processes across engineering organizations.
  • Improved traceability, accountability, and effectiveness of defect management.
  • Clear visibility into software quality, program risks, and release readiness through KPI reporting.
  • Reduced recurring quality issues through effective RCA and corrective-action processes.
  • Improved process compliance, audit readiness, and software development predictability.

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field required.
  • Master's degree preferred.
  • 5+ years of experience in software quality, software engineering, software validation, quality systems, process improvement, or related technical disciplines.
  • 5+ years of experience driving quality governance, defect management, KPI reporting, and cross-functional quality initiatives.
  • Proven track record of implementing scalable quality processes, governance frameworks, quality systems, and executive reporting mechanisms.
  • Experience collaborating with Engineering, Validation, Program Management, Product Quality, Functional Safety, Cybersecurity, and Compliance organizations.
  • Experience in automotive, autonomous driving, embedded software, or other regulated industries preferred.

Qualifications

  • Strong understanding of Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), release management, validation methodologies, and quality systems.
  • Experience with ASPICE, automotive quality processes, audits, and compliance programs.
  • Hands-on experience with JIRA, Confluence, defect tracking systems, KPI reporting, and workflow administration.
  • Knowledge of root-cause analysis, corrective-action management, continuous improvement, and quality governance.
  • Familiarity with Functional Safety (ISO 26262), Cybersecurity, Homologation, and regulatory compliance processes.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder-management, and cross-functional leadership skills.
  • Ability to influence teams through data-driven decision making and process governance.

Preferred

  • 8+ years of experience in software quality, process excellence, quality systems, or governance leadership roles.
  • Automotive software quality, validation, or systems engineering experience.
  • Experience with process automation, workflow optimization, and quality analytics.
  • Experience supporting multiple vehicle programs and large-scale cross-functional organizations

Preferred Qualifications

  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • 10+ years of experience in software quality, program quality, quality operations, technical program management, or engineering operations.
  • Experience supporting executive reviews, release-readiness reviews, and quality governance boards.
  • Experience in automotive, autonomous driving, embedded software, connected vehicle systems, or another complex product-development environment.
  • Knowledge of Functional Safety, Cybersecurity, Homologation, and automotive quality processes.
  • Experience with software quality metrics, data analytics, reporting automation, and business intelligence tools.

Work Requirements

  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities across concurrent vehicle programs and software releases.
  • Ability to support time-sensitive quality reviews, escalations, and release-readiness activities as business needs require.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with geographically distributed and cross-functional teams.

The Experience

Are you looking to fast-track your career in the cloud tech space? We are seeking a dynamic, highly organized Alliances Senior Administrator to join our growing team in UAE.

In this role, you will report to the Regional Vice President for Alliances Middle East managing a large volume of small to medium sized system integrators. This is a unique opportunity for someone with 3+ years of tech selling experience or channel management to make a huge impact in a booming market.

What You'll Actually Be Doing

  • Driving an ACV number via partner pipeline generation within the assigned network of system integrators.
  • Internal Liaison: Act as the central bridge connecting external partners with internal Salesforce teams, including Direct Sales, Business Development, and Customer Success.
  • Operations : Assist with logistics related to partner training, marketing events, and ensuring partners maintain correct certifications and pipeline tracking in our tools
  • Enablement: Utilising the internal tools available to ensure partners increase certification numbers and implementation skills.

You're Our Person If...

  • 3+ years of tech selling or channel management experience
  • Extensive knowledge of the local market

Even Better If...

  • Experience managing (strategic) partner relationships in technology industry (e.g.; SaaS) Or in direct sales, or combination of the above.

About the job

About the Role

Our client is looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to join its Technology Chapter. You will report to the Head of Technology Chapter and work day to day inside an Agile squad. You will build and run the application pipelines that onboard teams and applications onto the bank’s container platform through self-service and automation.

You will also act as a platform advocate. That means teaching product and platform teams how to use new services and features so they can move onto the platform smoothly. The team works with Agile, DevOps and Product Management methods, and delivers work in priority order from the backlog.

Key Responsibilities

Platform Engineering and Automation

  • Design, build and manage CI/CD pipelines for deployments on Native Kubernetes, OpenShift and public cloud (Azure, AWS or OCI)
  • Build Jenkins pipelines with Shared Libraries, Pipeline as Code, and Groovy or Python scripting
  • Implement and maintain GitHub Actions workflows and GitOps deployments using ArgoCD
  • Provision and manage environments with Terraform, Ansible and other IaC tools
  • Automate deployment and configuration management so environments stay consistent and can scale
  • Set up and manage service mesh capabilities across microservice workloads
  • Configure logging, monitoring and observability with ELK, Prometheus and Grafana

Security and Integration

  • Implement microservice authentication and authorisation patterns, including OAuth 2.0
  • Build and integrate REST and SOAP API consumers and publishers
  • Apply sound infrastructure and security practices across every environment

Delivery and Operations

  • Own projects from inception through to production delivery
  • Work with development and operations teams to troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure and application issues
  • Cover both delivery and operational support, in line with DevOps and SRE practices
  • Onboard applications and customers onto the platform through self-service tooling

Stakeholder Management

  • Communicate clearly and openly with internal teams and external stakeholders
  • Manage client expectations and deliver under pressure
  • Build trusted working relationships with key stakeholders and support the Group’s values across the team

Requirements

Must Have

  • 5+ years in DevOps, platform or cloud engineering, ideally in banking or another regulated enterprise
  • Hands-on production experience with Kubernetes and/or OpenShift
  • Strong Jenkins CI/CD experience, including Shared Libraries and Groovy or Python scripting
  • Practical experience with GitHub Actions and service mesh (Istio, Linkerd or similar)
  • Proven IaC skills with Terraform and Ansible
  • Working knowledge of at least one public cloud: Azure, AWS or OCI
  • Experience with ELK, Prometheus and Grafana
  • Understanding of OAuth 2.0 and microservice auth patterns

Good to Have

  • GitOps experience with ArgoCD
  • REST and SOAP API integration experience
  • Solid grasp of SRE practices and 12 Factor App principles
  • Certifications such as CKA, CKAD, Red Hat OpenShift, Azure DevOps Engineer, AWS DevOps Professional or HashiCorp Terraform Associate

Soft Skills

  • Pragmatic, structured approach to problem solving
  • Clear communicator who can teach and enable other teams
  • Comfortable working in a matrix structure inside Agile squads

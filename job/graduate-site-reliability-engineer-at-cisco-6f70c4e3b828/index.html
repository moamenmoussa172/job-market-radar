Role Overview

As a Site Reliability Engineer at Cisco, you will apply software engineering principles to optimize the scalability, availability, performance, and resiliency of our global production cloud services. Operating as a Developing Professional within an agile SRE pod, you will write automation software, support production reliability tooling, participate in operational on-call rotations, and partner with senior engineers to diagnose complex incidents, resolve service bottlenecks, and systematically eliminate operational toil through code.


What You'll Do

• Software Delivery & Automation: Write and maintain production-grade software (in Java, Python, or Go) to build automated remediation workflows, self-healing scripts, and reliable CI/CD release tools. Develop API endpoints and utility tooling that provide automated visibility into platform health, availability, and latency.

• Telemetry & Production Monitoring: Support real-time data ingestion and processing systems that power production health dashboards. Monitor service health metrics and alerting thresholds to meet internal Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and customer-facing Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

• Infrastructure as Code & Service Deployment: Deploy, configure, and maintain containerized workloads across hybrid cloud and multi-region infrastructure. Code automated solutions to improve service reliability and adapt configurations across global regions.

• Incident Management & On-Call Participation: Serve as an active on-call SRE supporting 24x7 production services; troubleshoot and debug complex issues across infrastructure and platform services. Participate in blameless post-mortems and author comprehensive Root Cause Analysis (RCA) documents.

• Scale, Resiliency & Disaster Recovery: Execute load tests and participate in Disaster Recovery (DR) drills to identify bottlenecks and test High Availability (HA) measures. Identify potential failure points and make recommendations to minimize single points of failure (SPOFs).

• Security & Compliance: Troubleshoot security controls, access policies, and token authentication mechanisms; contribute to vulnerability remediation and cluster hardening.

Minimum Qualifications

• Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline with 2+ years of related experience, OR Master’s degree with 0+ years of related experience, OR 6 years of equivalent related industry experience.

• Software Development: Hands-on professional software development experience using Java (or Python / Go) in a production or distributed software environment.

• Foundational Engineering Skills: Solid understanding of core software engineering fundamentals, data structures, algorithms, debugging, automated testing, and version control.

• Problem Solving & Collaboration: Proven ability to analyze technical issues, follow standard operational procedures, and collaborate effectively within an agile engineering team.

Preferred Qualifications

• Streaming & Data Infrastructure: Working knowledge of designing, operating, or consuming from distributed streaming systems (Apache Kafka, Kafka Streams, Apache Flink, or ClickHouse).

• Containers & Orchestration: Practical hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes (K8s), Git, and modern CI/CD automation pipelines (Jenkins, Harness, or GitLab CI).

• Observability: Experience configuring and interpreting metrics, logs, and traces using Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, or Splunk.

• Certifications: Associate-level certification (e.g., CCNA, Certified Kubernetes Administrator - CKA) or equivalent practical experience.

• Datastores: Familiarity with relational, document-based, or vector datastores.

• AIOps & Tooling: Familiarity with AI-assisted software engineering tools (e.g., Claude, Kosmos, Codex) or building automated diagnostic agents.

• Networking Protocols: Understanding of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, HTTP/2, gRPC, SIP, DNS, and TLS termination).

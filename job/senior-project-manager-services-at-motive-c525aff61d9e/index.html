About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Pakistan.

Who we are:

Motive empowers the people who run physical operations with tools to make their work safer, more productive, and more profitable. For the first time ever, safety, operations and finance teams can manage their drivers, vehicles, equipment, and fleet related spend in a single system. Combined with industry leading AI, the Motive platform gives you complete visibility and control, and significantly reduces manual workloads by automating and simplifying tasks.

Motive serves nearly 100,000 customers – from Fortune 500 enterprises to small businesses – across a wide range of industries, including transportation and logistics, construction, energy, field service, manufacturing, agriculture, food and beverage, retail, and the public sector.

Visit gomotive.com to learn more.

About the Role:

As a Senior Project Manager on the Transformation & Project Management team, you will lead complex, cross-functional strategic projects that directly support our GTM teams including Professional Installation, Customer Implementation, Trial Success, Sales Engineering, Sales & Account Management teams. You will be responsible for turning strategy into execution across programs that span process, policy, data, and technology.

You will manage a portfolio of initiatives that drive critical improvements across processes, workflows, systems, tools, which enhance customer experience, enable scalability, and improve operational efficiencies.

This role is highly visible and requires frequent interaction with senior leaders across Operations, Product, Sales, Order Management, Customer Support, Finance, and IT.

We are looking for a seasoned Senior Project Manager who can operate at both strategic and tactical levels: framing problems, defining success metrics, setting up governance and operating rhythms, and driving successful projects through to measurable results and calculated impact.

What You’ll Do:

Program & Project Leadership

  • Own and lead a portfolio of mid- to large-scale, cross-functional projects supporting GTM teams from discovery, deployment and impact measurement.
  • Develop and maintain project artifacts including project charters, detailed project plans, action items, risk & mitigation plans, RACI, and weekly status updates that keep stakeholders aligned and accountable.
  • Define project scope, objectives, deliverables, critical path, and success metrics in partnership with business stakeholders and functional leaders.
  • Manage timelines, budgets, and resources across multiple concurrent projects, proactively addressing variances to keep initiatives on schedule and within budget.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Partner closely with leaders across GTM teams to understand operational pain points, define future-state processes, and prioritize initiatives that drive scalability, quality, and profitability.
  • Drive end-to-end readiness for new or enhanced workflows and systems by coordinating with Product, Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, Order Management, Customer Support, Finance, Customer Success, and IT
  • Facilitate effective decision-making through structured governance, clear mitigation planning, data based decision making and providing concise executive-ready updates.

Execution, Risk & Change Management

  • Identify, manage, and mitigate risks, issues, and dependencies across teams and systems; ensure clear owners, due dates, and resolution paths.
  • Implement and refine project management frameworks appropriate to each initiative and ensure consistent application of best practices.
  • Lead change management for Implementation and Installation programs, including impact assessments, ROI, business justifications, communications, training and enablement plans, and adoption tracking.
  • Ensure supporting project documentation, SOPs, and playbooks are comprehensive, current, and easily consumable by operational teams.

Data, Reporting & Continuous Improvement

  • Use tools such as Salesforce, and other BI/reporting platforms to size problems, establish baselines, monitor KPIs, and track realized benefits with measurable impact
  • Provide clear, data-backed status and performance reporting to stakeholders and senior leadership, highlighting progress, risks, and recommended actions.
  • Conduct project retrospectives and leverage insights to improve project intake, prioritization, planning, and execution standards across the GTM organization.
  • Leverage AI-enabled tools and automation to improve project-management efficiency, ranging from project intake, prioritization, project-planning, measuring impact, identifying risks and dependencies, tracking action items, providing weekly updates, and capturing reusable project knowledge while applying appropriate human oversight and data-governance practices.

Leadership & Coaching

  • Model best-in-class project management practices and mentor other Project Managers and project leads on methodology, communication, stakeholder management, and risk management.
  • Contribute to the evolution of GTM Operations, Transformation & Strategy standards, templates, tooling, and training to raise delivery quality and consistency across the Project Management portfolio.

What We’re Looking For:

  • 4+ years of project management experience working on Business facing, strategic cross functional initiatives with a proven track record of leading complex initiatives from discovery through execution.
  • Project Management Professional (PMP) certification required
  • Background in process improvement or operational excellence (e.g., Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen) and comfortable designing and implementing new workflows
  • Experience introducing or scaling project portfolio management practices, including intake, prioritization, and capacity planning.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple concurrent projects and portfolios, balancing scope, schedule, resources, and quality while delivering against defined success metrics.
  • Exceptional organizational and problem-solving abilities, with keen attention to detail
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, including experience presenting to and influencing senior leaders and cross-functional partners.
  • Deep familiarity with project management methodologies and tools (e.g., Jira, Asana, Smartsheet, Microsoft Project, or similar) and comfort establishing governance, cadences, and working agreements.
  • Data-driven mindset with hands-on experience using BI tools, AI tools etc. to analyze trends, build dashboards, and support decision-making.
  • Proven experience in risk management and mitigation planning, including dependency mapping and contingency planning for multi-team initiatives.
  • High degree of ownership, accountability, and follow-through, with the ability to drive clarity in ambiguous environments and keep projects moving forward.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working in fleet management, telematics, logistics, field service management, or other hardware/software deployment environments.
  • Experience applying AI and automation to streamline project-management processes, reduce manual work, improve decision-making, and increase delivery visibility.

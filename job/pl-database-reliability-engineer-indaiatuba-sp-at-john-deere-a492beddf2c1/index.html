About the job

Trabalhamos para que a vida possa avançar e estamos sempre à procura de pessoas inovadoras que possam contribuir com a nossa missão. Que tal fazer parte do nosso time?

Local Principal: Brasil (BR) - São Paulo - Indaiatuba
Função: Technology (CA)
Título: PL Database Reliability Engineer - Indaiatuba/SP - 119254

We are redefining the traditional DBRE role into a modern Data Platform Engineering function, focused on automation, reliability, cloud-native operations, and scalable patterns for database infrastructure across the company. Our team partners with multiple product and platform squads to provide a robust database platform on AWS, primarily using RDS with PostgreSQL, while also supporting Oracle and SQL Server workloads.

You will play a key role in executing our modernization journey, participating in initiatives such as migrating legacy workloads to AWS, implementing Infrastructure as Code (IaC), enhancing observability, and building automation. This is a global team.

Main Responsibilities

  • Implement and maintain highly reliable, secure, and automated database environments on AWS;
  • Provision, monitor, and operate AWS RDS instances (primarily PostgreSQL; Oracle/SQL Server as secondary);
  • Utilize and contribute to reusable cloud/database standards, Terraform modules, and Flyway pipelines;
  • Support and execute data migrations between different databases (including using AWS DMS);
  • Apply and maintain security, governance, and compliance policies in database environments;
  • Collaborate with development teams on SQL tuning, schema modeling, and troubleshooting;
  • Develop and maintain automations (Terraform pipelines, Bash/Python scripts);
  • Produce and update technical documentation, runbooks, and share knowledge with the team.

Skills You Need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Systems Analysis, or related fields (or equivalent practical experience);
  • Advanced English: Ability to participate in meetings, write clear documentation, and collaborate effectively with global teams;
  • Solid experience with AWS, specifically RDS (PostgreSQL is required);
  • Hands-on database administration experience (backup/restore, replication, troubleshooting, and tuning);
  • Practical experience with Terraform (IaC) for database provisioning;
  • Experience with schema versioning/migration tools (such as Flyway or similar);
  • Experience with scripting and automation (Bash, Python);
  • Strong SQL skills and query optimization;
  • Availability to work in a hybrid model in Indaiatuba/SP.

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Prior experience with migrations using AWS DMS;
  • Knowledge of SRE practices (SLIs, SLOs, and observability tools like Datadog, Prometheus, etc.);
  • Basic understanding of DB2 (z/OS) or mainframe environments;
  • AWS certifications (Database Specialty or Solutions Architect).

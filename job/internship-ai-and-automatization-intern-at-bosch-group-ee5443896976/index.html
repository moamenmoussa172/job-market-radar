About the job

  • Drive the development and validation of AI image classification models to support warranty process digitalization and enhance customer experience
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including internal quality management specialists and external development partners on AI model development, testing, and deployment activities
  • Analyze, report, and optimize data management workflows, dashboard performance, and business intelligence metrics to support data-driven decision-making
  • Ensure compliance with quality management standards and best practices throughout all project phases and analytical processes
  • Contribute to continuous improvement and digitalization initiatives by supporting automation efforts, documentation, and process optimization across the team
  • Assist with data collection, organization, and evaluation of model performance to validate AI solution effectiveness and accuracy
  • Maintain comprehensive documentation of project developments, test results, and process improvements throughout the project lifecycle

1. Education

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or related field
  • Able to provide Letter of recommendation from university

2. Work Experience

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
  • Experience in cross-functional or international team collaboration

3. Languages

  • Mandatory: English
  • Optional: Chinese, Vietnamese

4. Hardware / Software Skills

  • Proficiency in Python, SQL (Oracle, MSSQL), Power BI, and Excel
  • Familiarity with machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch), Azure, or Computer Vision
  • Knowledge of KNIME or RPA tools (e.g., Automation Anywhere)

5. Personality & Soft Skills

  • Eagerness to learn emerging technologies and drive continuous improvement
  • Excellent communication abilities
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality

6. Work Arrangement

  • Full-time availability required
  • Be entrusted with your own area of responsibility
  • Receive competitive internship allowances
  • Join department’s outing activities
  • Having 01 paid leave per month
  • Lunch allowance, parking allowance

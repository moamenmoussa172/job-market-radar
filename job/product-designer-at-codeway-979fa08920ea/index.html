Small teams move faster than big ones. But they need big resources to win at scale. Nobody starts from zero. The problem decides the solution, not us.

POSITION

Are you passionate about design? We’re looking for Product Designers across different experience levels, from junior to senior, who will help us deliver some of the most innovative designs in the industry.

At Codeway, you'll transform high-level concepts and creative ideas into engaging, intuitive, and functional experiences for our mobile products.

If you're passionate about design, have a strong creative mindset, and enjoy exploring new ideas and approaches, we'd love for you to join our team and help us build world-class products used by millions of people.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Contribute to the end-to-end UI/UX design process, from ideation to final implementation.
  • Create sketches, wireframes, prototypes, animations, and polished UI designs for our mobile products.
  • Explore original design concepts and turn creative ideas into intuitive, engaging user experiences.
  • Collaborate with Designers, Product Managers, and Developers to bring innovative ideas to life.
  • Stay up to date with emerging design trends, tools, and technologies.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • Hands-on experience designing mobile or web applications through professional work, internships, or personal projects.
  • Strong visual design skills, creative thinking, and a portfolio demonstrating your design approach.
  • Understanding of mobile user behavior, UX/UI principles, and platform design guidelines.
  • Curiosity across the design process, from research and benchmarking to prototyping, animation, and usability testing.
  • Proficiency in Figma and a passion for exploring new tools and creative approaches.
  • Experience with AI-powered products or incorporating AI tools into your design workflow.
  • Strong visual taste and an interest in emerging design trends, animations, and micro-interactions.

WHY THIS ROLE IS DIFFERENT

  • Design consumer products used by millions of people worldwide.
  • Explore emerging technologies across AI, creativity, learning, and productivity.
  • Work in a fast-moving environment where creative ideas turn into real products and meaningful impact.

THE RECRUITING PROCESS

We are committed to keeping our recruitment process short and transparent. Here’s how it looks like:

  • Application: Send us your CV or LinkedIn profile. You can also just write a few words about yourself.
  • Talent & Culture Interview: Let’s talk about your experience & expectations and see what we can achieve together.
  • Case Study: We might send you a task to solve.
  • Technical Interview: You will meet with future team lead to go over the case and your technical capabilities in detail.
  • Final Interview: A final interview with one of our senior leaders.
  • Welcome Aboard! You are now a part of the team.

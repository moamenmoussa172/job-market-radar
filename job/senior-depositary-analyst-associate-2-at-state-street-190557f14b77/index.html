Senior Depositary Analyst - Associate 2

State Street · Gdansk, Poland

Onsite · Senior · Data Analyst · Posted 28 October 2026

About the job

Who We Are

Interested in how investment funds operate behind the scenes? Our Depositary team helps ensure that fund assets are protected and that important controls are in place. It's a great opportunity to build expertise in investment funds, financial markets, and the broader banking industry while working in an international environment.

What You Will Do

As a Senior Depositary Analyst, you will:

  • Perform depositary controls related to asset safekeeping, cash flow monitoring, and oversight activities
  • Review and analyze data to identify exceptions, discrepancies, and potential risks
  • Ensure controls are completed in line with regulatory requirements and internal standards
  • Investigate issues and work with internal teams and external partners to resolve them
  • Support process improvements and help enhance the efficiency and quality of our operations
  • Develop expertise in investment funds, financial markets, and regulatory frameworks

What we value:

  • Analytical skills and attention to details
  • Team skills, collaboration & listening
  • Communication skills - verbal and written in English
  • Good organizational skills and effective time management
  • Experience using Microsoft Office, especially Excel
  • Familiarity with UCITS V and AIFMD
  • Curiosity and willingness to learn
  • Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Economics / Finance or related; other educational background acceptable
  • Professional Experience (1-2 years) in finance, banking, IT, custody or depositary.

Why Join Us

  • Build expertise in a specialized and growing area of financial services
  • Gain exposure to international markets and investment funds
  • Work with experienced professionals in a collaborative environment
  • Access learning and development opportunities to support your career growth
  • Develop analytical, regulatory, and problem-solving skills that are highly valued across the financial industry

Note: The role requires a minimum of three days a week working from office.

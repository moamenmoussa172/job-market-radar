What are your contributions to the team?

As Data Analyst, you will play a key role in modernizing Bombardier’s pricing infrastructure and enabling the future of data-driven pricing decisions. Working closely with the Pricing team, you will develop scalable data solutions, establish robust analytics foundations, and support the evolution of advanced pricing capabilities, including artificial intelligence and dynamic pricing. You will:

· Extract, cleanse, transform, and validate data from SAP tables, Microsoft Access databases, and other business systems.

· Develop, maintain, and optimize data pipelines, datasets, and analytics solutions within Databricks.

· Support the enhancement, automation, and long-term sustainability of pricing and rebilled pricing tools.

· Create reports, dashboards, and data models that provide actionable business insights and support strategic pricing decisions.

· Collaborate with Pricing Management Specialists to ensure that pricing business rules and strategies are accurately reflected within analytics solutions and tools.

· Document data flows, business processes, pricing logic, and tool functionality to support knowledge retention and business continuity.

· Develop user guides, training materials, and best practices for internally developed pricing tools and analytics solutions.

· Train and support Pricing team members, enabling them to independently use, maintain, and enhance analytical tools and processes.

· Facilitate knowledge transfer and establish governance practices that allow the team to manage future updates, enhancements, and development initiatives autonomously.

· Contribute to the development of the data foundation required for future AI, predictive analytics, machine learning, and dynamic pricing initiatives.

How to thrive in this role?

· University degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business Analytics, or a related field. Based on role requirements.

· Experience working with SAP tables and ERP data structures.

· Strong knowledge of Microsoft Access databases, SQL, and data extraction methodologies.

· Hands-on expertise in Databricks, including SQL, Python/PySpark, notebook development, and data modeling.

· Experience with ETL processes, data integration, data architecture, and analytics best practices.

· Ability to translate business requirements into scalable, sustainable data solutions.

· Strong documentation, communication, presentation, and training skills.

· Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with high attention to detail and data quality.

· Experience supporting pricing, finance, aerospace, manufacturing, or aftermarket business functions is considered an asset.

· Bilingual proficiency in English and French (spoken and written).

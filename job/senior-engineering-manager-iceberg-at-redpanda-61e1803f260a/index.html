About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Poland.

Redpanda is the first runtime and control plane for agent-data interaction — a unified platform that combines streaming, SQL analytics, and intelligent connectivity with the governance layer enterprise AI agents need in production.

Built on infrastructure already trusted by Fortune 500 companies and fast-growing startups, Redpanda enforces governance entirely outside the agent's data path, controlling what agents see, limiting what they do, and capturing a tamper-proof record of everything they touch, so organizations never have to choose between innovation and control.

About the Role:

Apache Iceberg is where streaming meets the lakehouse, and our customers ask for it more than almost anything else on our platform. Today Redpanda writes to Iceberg from three places: the broker (Iceberg Topics, in C++), Redpanda Connect (in Go), and Redpanda SQL. Schema evolution, type mapping, write coordination, catalog interaction and bad-record handling are each built more than once, and the differences between those paths cause bugs that customers see.

We are forming a new team to fix that, and we are hiring the Engineering Manager who will build and run it.

The team’s mission is to make Redpanda the easiest way to get from a stream to a well-maintained, queryable Iceberg table. That means one table engine and one catalog, with three entry points that hand off to it: topic configuration, Connect pipelines, and SQL. Engineering and product leadership have already set and agreed on the strategy and architecture. Your job is to turn that into a team and a shipped product, and to own the design decisions inside it.

The work covers:

  • The write path. Turning a stream of records into Iceberg tables that other engines can actually read. That means mapping schemas and types onto the table, handling schema changes and bad records, writing Parquet files sized and laid out so reads are fast, not just correct, and committing them as table snapshots into either our catalog or the customer’s.
  • CDC and deletes. Accepting keyed upserts and deletes at streaming speed, while external readers only ever see deletion vectors.
  • Read performance and search over Iceberg. Redpanda SQL has to be fast enough over Iceberg data that customers stop reaching for a separate analytics database. That includes implementing analytical benchmarks, keeping hot and cold queries across topics and tables interactive, and extending the engine into point lookups, full-text search and eventually semantic search.
  • Our own Iceberg REST catalog. Optimistic concurrency control, atomic multi-table commits, the upstream conformance suite in CI, and the commit-pipeline work needed to go from tens of commits per second to more than a thousand on any object store.
  • Table maintenance as a product feature. Compaction, snapshot expiration, converting equality deletes to deletion vectors, orphan cleanup, and index calculation, with a clear ownership switch for tables that someone else maintains. Nobody demos this work, but neglecting it quietly kills read performance, and search capability depends on it.
  • External catalog and bucket interoperability. First-class commits into Glue, Unity and Polaris, the vendor auth matrix (SigV4, OAuth2 client credentials), credential vending short-lived, downscoped credentials, and the bring-your-own-bucket setup our largest customers are asking for.
  • Migration and parity. Keeping the Iceberg Topics experience exactly the same while the implementation changes underneath it, so existing customers get an upgrade, not a sunset.

You will start with a small group of engineers who already work on Iceberg in Redpanda SQL and Streaming, and you will hire to fill the gaps.

We are looking for someone who cares about building world-class systems and data software, and about the value they give customers. You should be curious about lakehouse and open table formats and willing to read the specification yourself. You should also be accountable, self-driven, and happy to roll up your sleeves in a fast-growing startup.
This is a hybrid role based in our central Warsaw office, with a minimum of 3 days a week on-site.

You Will:

  • Build and run a new engineering team that owns Redpanda’s Iceberg table engine, catalog, and table services
  • Own end-to-end delivery of your team’s roadmap: scoping, prioritization, execution, and on-time, high-quality launches
  • Drive the RFC process for your team’s foundational design decisions: translation engine, CDC write path, table maintenance, catalog commit pipeline, commit targets, and the handoff contracts with the broker and Connect
  • Hire, onboard, and retain top engineers, and take an active part in interview loops and calibration
  • Keep a high bar for correctness and operations: exactly-once semantics, commit safety, on-call health, and the maintenance hygiene that stops write and read performance from degrading over time
  • Own the migration and parity checklist that must be complete before the existing broker translation path is retired
  • Work with the Streaming, Connect, Cloud and Redpanda SQL teams to keep interfaces clean, so that one writer sits behind three user-facing surfaces
  • Partner with Product to shape the roadmap, and raise risks, dependencies and trade-offs early
  • Give frequent, actionable feedback, run performance reviews, and guide your team’s career development
  • Build an environment where the team is aligned with company goals, decisions are made together, and people feel a strong sense of ownership and belonging
  • Work with Customer Success and Solutions Engineering to unblock customers and bring their real constraints back into the roadmap
  • Raise systemic challenges and blockers with your manager

You Have:

  • 7+ years in software engineering, building and operating production data or distributed systems
  • 2+ years managing engineers, with a track record of delivery
  • Hands-on depth in at least one of: analytical query engines, distributed storage, or streaming data systems, enough to judge a design document on its merits
  • Familiarity with Apache Iceberg or a similar open table format (Delta Lake, Hudi), including table and snapshot metadata, manifests, schema evolution, partition transforms, and how the delete-file strategies differ in practice. You can build depth on the job, but you can’t start from zero
  • A reasonably current understanding of C++, Rust, Go, or another systems language, including profiling and benchmarking. Our codebases are C++ (Redpanda SQL and the broker) and Go (Connect and cloud services)
  • Experience with cloud object storage (S3, GCS, or Azure Blob) and the IAM and credential models around it
  • Experience turning a roadmap into clear engineering plans and delivering against them predictably
  • Comfort working with a distributed engineering team that collaborates in the open on GitHub. Our engineers are in Poland, the UK, and North America
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills. Much of this role happens in writing: RFCs, parity checklists, and status updates other teams can act on

Nice to Have:

  • Direct experience with Iceberg v3, including deletion vectors and migrating off positional and equality delete files
  • Experience building or operating a streaming-to-lakehouse write path with exactly-once semantics, or a CDC pipeline that consumes the Debezium envelope
  • Experience building an Iceberg REST catalog, or running Glue, Unity Catalog, Polaris, BigLake or Lakekeeper in production
  • Experience with Parquet internals, columnar scan performance, predicate pushdown, or compaction and clustering strategy
  • Contributions to Apache Iceberg or another open table format community
  • Experience helping a team adopt AI coding tools in a way that measurably improved velocity without hurting quality
  • Experience as the first manager of a newly formed team

This is a B2B contract role in Warsaw. The rate is 475,000 - 575,000 PLN per year (net + VAT). Final compensation will depend on your skills, experience, and interview performance.

About this Role

We are seeking an experienced SAP FICO Consultant to join the Corporate Application team based in Pune (EON). This role is ideal for professionals with 4 to 6 years of SAP FICO consulting and implementation experience who are passionate about delivering high-quality finance solutions in a complex global SAP environment. The successful candidate will support solution design, business requirement analysis, implementation, testing, user acceptance support, and operational excellence across finance processes. Experience in SAP Finance modules, stakeholder engagement, and end-to-end system understanding will be key to success.

What you’ll do

  • Support SAP FICO application operations across key finance domains including:
  • General Ledger (including New GL)
  • ccounts Receivable
  • Accounts Payable
  • Real Estate (RE-FX)
  • Investment Management
  • Project Systems
  • Cost Centre Accounting
  • Profit Centre Accounting
  • Asset Accounting
  • Banking
  • Tax and Withholding Tax
  • Treasury
  • Intercompany Accounting
  • Budgeting and Planning
  • Support the Functional Operations team in solution design and architecture activities.
  • Analyse business requirements and translate them into effective SAP solutions.
  • Design, configure, test, and implement SAP FICO solutions.
  • Support testing activities and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
  • Identify integration issues and develop appropriate resolutions.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, subject matter experts, and technical teams.
  • Clarify business processes and system functionality to end users.
  • Provide transactional information and documentation to internal and external auditors when required.
  • Assess solution feasibility and provide guidance on alternative approaches.
  • Build effective relationships with stakeholders across Vodafone operating companies.
  • Work with service integration partners to resolve FICO-related development and operational issues.
  • Collaborate with finance and data owners to address data quality issues and responsibility assignments.
  • Produce and manage information in a timely and accurate manner.
  • Participate in shadow and shift-based support arrangements where required.

Who you are

  • Graduate or Postgraduate in a technical or business discipline, preferably Engineering, MBA Finance, CA, ICWA, or CFA.
  • 4 to 6 years of SAP FICO consulting and implementation experience.
  • Strong knowledge of SAP FICO processes and finance operations.
  • Understanding of the complete software lifecycle, including testing and support.
  • Experience working with large global organisations.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Ability to gather, assess, and diagnose business and system issues.
  • Effective communication and facilitation skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively within diverse teams.
  • Strong understanding of complex system landscapes and end-to-end process integration.
  • Knowledge of ITIL processes, preferably ITIL v3.
  • Fluent written and spoken English.
  • Willingness to work in shadow or shift-based support environments when required.

Desirable

  • Experience with niche SAP FICO modules including:
  • In-House Cash (IHC)
  • Travel & Expenses (T&E)
  • Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)
  • Knowledge of third-party tax calculation tools such as Sabrix.
  • International project exposure or experience working outside India.
  • Experience within telecommunications or manufacturing organisations using SAP FICO.

About the job

The Team

We are a globally distributed team inside Adobe Developer Platforms. We own the observability platform Adobe's engineering ecosystem runs on – one of the largest logging deployments in the industry, spanning 50,000+ nodes, billions of OTel events daily, and an auth service we built in Go that handles 15,000 req/sec. Every Adobe engineer depends on what we ship.
We build it, we ship it, we run it.


The Opportunity

  • Compose, build, and run Adobe's observability platform at scale
  • Write Go and Python: extend OpenTelemetry Collector receivers, processors, and exporters, contribute to open-source, and build the internal services, APIs, and tooling around them
  • Drive cost optimisation: analyse pipeline data, build guardrails, and engage engineering teams
  • Build developer-facing tooling and services, including AI-powered insight from high-volume telemetry and observability for AI and agent-based systems
  • Own SLOs and SLIs for a platform thousands of engineers rely on every day
  • Own on-call, lead incident response for high-severity issues, and make every shift better than the last


What you need to succeed

  • Strong, hands-on Go and/or Python. Our platform is predominantly Go — the OpenTelemetry Collector, Cortex, Prometheus, and Kubernetes all are — and Python carries much of our tooling. You should be able to contribute production code in at least one, and read the other
  • 5–8+ years building and owning production software at scale, ideally platform or infrastructure products used by other engineers
  • Build, code review, and automated testing coordinated into how you ship, not a separate phase
  • Familiarity with OpenTelemetry — not just configuring collectors, but extending them and instrumenting applications with the SDKs
  • Working knowledge of large-scale logging infrastructure — Splunk, ClickHouse, New Relic, Prometheus, LangSmith
  • Experience developing, deploying, and running distributed applications on cloud platforms; experience with container and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Comfortable owning on-call across a multi-tool observability stack, including leading Sev 1/2 incident response


Nice to have

  • Experience evaluating and prototyping alternative Observability platforms, both commercial and open-source
  • Experience with other Observability tooling like Grafana, Cortex, ElasticSearch, DataDog, VictoriaMetrics, Mimir
  • TypeScript/React — we ship an observability plugin in Adobe's Developer Home (based on Backstage)
  • Experience instrumenting AI/LLM or agent-based systems, nice to have

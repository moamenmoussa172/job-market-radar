About the job

About Vercel:

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience.

Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

About the role

As a Senior Deal Desk Analyst, EMEA, you’ll lead quote-to-contract execution for our global sales team, working with Sales, Finance, Legal, and Product to structure, price, and close deals into signed revenue. You’ll also help build the Deal Desk within Revenue Operations as the company grows quickly: the systems, processes, and playbooks that bring structure to ambiguity and modernize how deals get done. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in London.

What you’ll do

  • Own deal review and closing end to end, including approvals, deal structuring, and quote configuration in CPQ, so every deal is executed accurately and on time
  • Design, build, and automate the deal processes that bring new pricing, packaging, and product launches to market, working with Finance, Legal, go-to-market (GTM), Partnerships, Product, and Pricing, and using AI to scale
  • Advise Sales on complex, non-standard deals and develop practical structures where no standard terms apply
  • Keep opportunity data accurate and audit-ready, partnering with GTM and Finance on audits for revenue recognition, financial reporting, and forecasting
  • Analyze deal performance and market trends to build deal levers, sales motions, and training materials that increase sales velocity and reduce business risk, with the Pricing and Field Enablement teams

What you need

  • 4+ years in deal desk, pricing, quote-to-cash, contract management, or a related Revenue Operations or Sales Operations function in the technology sector
  • Moved complex deals forward in partnership with Sales and cross-functional teams
  • Hands-on project management experience
  • Hands-on process improvement experience

Bonus if you

  • Have hands-on experience with Salesforce CPQ or a comparable CPQ platform
  • Have used AI or automation to improve an operational workflow

Benefits & Compensation:

  • Competitive compensation package, including equity.
  • Inclusive Healthcare Package.

The London base pay range for this role is £65,000 - £85,000. Actual salary will be based on job-related skills, experience, and location.

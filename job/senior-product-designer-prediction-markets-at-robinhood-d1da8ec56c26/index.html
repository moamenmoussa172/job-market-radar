About the job

Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

About the team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers, problem-solvers, and builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Prediction Markets team is building a new asset class that enables customers to engage with real-world events in dynamic and meaningful ways. This team operates at the forefront of product development, partnering closely with engineering, product, and operations to launch new experiences across multiple verticals. You will help define how users interact with fast-evolving markets while contributing to one of the fastest-growing and most strategic areas of the business!

As a Senior Product Designer, you will own end-to-end design for a 0→1 product area within Prediction Markets. You’ll shape user experiences across trading, engagement, and platform expansion, working closely with product managers, engineers, and operations partners. This role requires navigating ambiguity, incorporating leadership feedback, and delivering high-quality design solutions tied to business outcomes. You’ll have the opportunity to influence the direction of a new business line and contribute to high-priority initiatives with visibility at the executive level!

This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

  • Own end-to-end design for key product areas within Prediction Markets, from concept through launch
  • Design and deliver user experiences for trading flows, engagement features, and web platform expansion
  • Partner with product managers, engineers, and operations teams to define requirements and execute on high-priority initiatives
  • Translate complex, ambiguous problem spaces into clear, intuitive product experiences
  • Incorporate feedback from senior leadership and stakeholders into design iterations while maintaining product quality

What you bring

  • 5+ years of experience as a product designer with a strong visual design portfolio
  • Experience designing end-to-end product experiences, including launching new or early-stage products
  • Comfortable working in ambiguous environments and can independently drive projects forward
  • Collaborate effectively with product, engineering, and operational partners to deliver results
  • Synthesize feedback from multiple stakeholders and apply it to improve product outcomes
  • Proficient in design tools such as Figma and open to incorporating AI tools into your workflow

What we offer

  • Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career
  • Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching
  • Top Tier benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents
  • Access to the Robinhood Employee Fund that gives eligible US employees the opportunity to invest in a private employee fund that provides exposure to Robinhood Ventures funds.
  • Access to the best AI tools on the market and continuous AI skill-building for every employee, technical or not.
  • Lifestyle wallet - a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more
  • Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits
  • Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more!
  • Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces.

About the job

Yassir is the leading super App in the Maghreb region set to changing the way daily services are provided. It currently operates in 45 cities across Algeria, Morocco and Tunisia with recent expansions into France, Canada and Sub-Saharan Africa. It is backed (~$200M in funding) by VCs from Silicon Valley, Europe and other parts of the world.

We offer on-demand services such as ride-hailing and last-mile delivery. Building on this infrastructure, we are now introducing financial services to help our users pay, save and borrow digitally.

Helping usher the continent into a digital economy era. We’re not just about serving people - we’re about creating a marketplace to bring people what they need while infusing social values.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the Superapp Financial Experience — our consumer wallet and the financial products that sit inside the Yassir super app. This is a B2C role at the centre of our financial services strategy.
Your core mandate is adoption: getting millions of Yassir users to fund, hold, and transact with the wallet — first inside the super app (rides, deliveries, top-ups), and then beyond it (peer-to-peer transfers, bill payments, merchant payments in the real world). You will own the full consumer journey from first wallet activation to habitual, everyday use.
You will work across engineering, design, growth, and the wider super app teams to make the wallet the default way Yassir users move money — turning an existing, trusted user base into active financial services customers.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Own the consumer wallet and superapp financial experience end-to-end, with adoption and active usage as your primary success metrics.
  • Define and execute the strategy to drive wallet activation, funding, and repeat transactions — both within the super app (rides, deliveries) and in external use cases (P2P, bill payments, merchant payments).
  • Design acquisition and activation funnels in partnership with growth and marketing; identify and remove the friction points that stop users from funding and using the wallet.
  • Deeply understand the Algerian consumer — cash habits, trust barriers, and the behavioural shifts required to move people from cash to digital money.
  • Run discovery: customer interviews, journey mapping, prototyping, and experiments to validate what will actually drive adoption at scale.
  • Translate customer insights into a prioritised roadmap of features and experiments, tested with real users in market.
  • Partner with engineering and design to ship high-quality consumer experiences — onboarding, KYC, top-up, send/receive, and transaction history — that feel effortless and trustworthy.
  • REQUIREMENTS
  • Minimum 5 years of product management experience, with a strong track record in B2C consumer products — ideally fintech, wallets, or payments.
  • Proven experience driving consumer adoption and engagement of a financial product — you have moved activation, retention, or transaction metrics, not just shipped features.
  • A track record of launching and scaling consumer-facing digital products (B2C).
  • Strong grasp of acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue — and how to design a product that grows across that funnel.
  • Experience with growth mechanics: onboarding optimisation, funnel analysis, experimentation, and retention loops.
  • Understanding of the technical aspects of digital product development, with the ability to work closely with engineers and get the best out of them.
  • Ability to create and run a customer discovery process — interviews, journey mapping, and ideation workshops — to surface real user pains and opportunities.
  • Strong understanding of value proposition design and what makes a consumer product succeed.
  • Ability to integrate analytics into a product to generate actionable insight, and to define the right metrics for a consumer wallet.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication, with the ability to influence stakeholders at all levels; presentation and storytelling skills.
  • Strong design sense and the ability to collaborate with designers to produce great consumer experiences.
  • Knowledge of modern development practices like agile and scrum.
  • Dependable, enthusiastic, curious, with great energy and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Based in Cairo, Egypt (required).
  • Native Arabic speaker with fluent English; French is a huge plus.
  • NICE TO HAVE
  • Experience taking a wallet or consumer fintech product from low to mass adoption in an emerging or cash-heavy market.
  • Understanding of behavioural psychology and how to design for high engagement and stickiness.
  • Knowledge of lean startup techniques and what a good MVP looks like for consumer products.
  • Experience working with super apps, marketplaces, or multi-product ecosystems.
  • Prior experience in a scale-up environment.

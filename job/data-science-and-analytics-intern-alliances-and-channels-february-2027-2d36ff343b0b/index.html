The intern will focus on improving and automating systems and processes, with a strong emphasis on leveraging Artificial Intelligence techniques. Excellent communication and teamwork skills are required. This internship offers exposure to cross-functional collaboration and real-world business impact.
Profile:

  • Passionate about technology’s impact.
  • Creative problem-solver, curious, and self-driven.
  • Adaptable and eager to learn.
  • Fluent in English and French.

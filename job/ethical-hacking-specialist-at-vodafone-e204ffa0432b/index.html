About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Ethical Hacking Specialist to enhance Vodafone’s cyber security posture through deliberate, controlled security testing that mirrors real-world attack techniques. This role focuses on designing, executing, and assuring the effectiveness of penetration tests, evaluating security controls against Vodafone standards, and collaborating closely with technical and business stakeholders. The individual will play a key role in identifying vulnerabilities, translating technical findings into business risk, and supporting remediation across complex technology environments.

What you’ll do

  • Execute advanced security testing activities using recognised MITRE tactics and techniques to identify vulnerabilities across Vodafone systems.
  • Design, develop, and maintain comprehensive security testing and penetration testing plans.
  • Lead and deliver specialised security testing engagements, including defining scope, prerequisites, and testing approaches.
  • Collaborate with testers, managers, and business functions to ensure coordinated and effective test execution.
  • Produce high-quality technical reports detailing identified vulnerabilities, associated technical and business risks, and clear remediation guidance.
  • Provide detailed exploit reproduction steps, retesting scripts, and support remediation activities with technical teams.
  • Develop solutions and code to automate security testing and document reusable test cases.
  • Act as a security consultant for international teams, supporting complex IT project situations.
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of emerging technologies, tools, and information security trends to continuously improve testing practices.

Who you are

  • You have 2–3 years of experience in penetration testing, with exposure to coordinating or leading testing activities.
  • You bring proven experience in vulnerability discovery, including CVE registration, security research, or participation in recognised cybersecurity competitions.
  • You hold relevant professional certifications such as CPTS, OSEP, OSWE, CWEE, eCPTX, or equivalent.
  • You have a university degree in an IT-related field, with Cybersecurity preferred.
  • You demonstrate applied knowledge of Windows and Linux/Unix environments, virtualisation technologies, and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
  • You have in-depth understanding of application development processes and hands-on experience with at least one scripting or programming
  • language (e.g. Java, Python, PowerShell, PHP).
  • You are confident working with CI/CD automation tools and/or SOAR platforms.
  • You communicate clearly, with strong documentation skills and the ability to translate complex technical issues into practical insights.

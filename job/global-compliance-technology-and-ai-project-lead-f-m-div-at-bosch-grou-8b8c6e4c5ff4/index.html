Global Compliance Technology & AI Project Lead (f/m/div.)

Bosch Group · Lisboa, Portugal

Hybrid · Lead / Principal · Data Leadership · Posted 23 October 2026

About the job

Your contribution to something big:

Compliance is evolving rapidly through AI, automation and data-driven technologies. As Global Compliance Technology & AI Project Lead, you will play a key role in shaping this transformation at Bosch. You will turn strategic priorities into executable global programs, scalable technology solutions and measurable business outcomes.

Your responsibilities include:

  • Lead strategic global projects transforming Compliance processes through technology, AI and automation.
  • Manage project resources, external delivery partners and approved project budgets.
  • Translate the Compliance digital transformation agenda into executable programs, project roadmaps and measurable business outcomes.
  • Lead interdisciplinary and international project teams across Compliance, Business, IT, Enterprise Architecture and external partners.
  • Ensure effective project governance, risk and dependency management, resource planning and change management.
  • Drive the implementation and adoption of technology-enabled Compliance solutions across regions and Compliance domains.
  • Ensure the successful transition of implemented solutions into sustainable global operations.
  • Promote global standardization, operational excellence and continuous improvement.
  • Ensure sustainable implementation while balancing global standards with country-specific requirements.
  • Manage senior stakeholders, align expectations and drive decisions across functions and regions.
  • Support the successful integration of AI, automation, data-driven technologies and digital platforms into Compliance processes.

What distinguishes you:

Education: Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Information Systems, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science or a comparable field.
Training or certification in project or program management is advantageous.

Experience:

  • Several years of experience leading complex global technology, AI or digital transformation projects, ideally in an international or regulated environment.
  • Proven experience translating strategic objectives into programs, project roadmaps and measurable outcomes.
  • Experience leading interdisciplinary and international teams and managing senior stakeholders.
  • Solid experience in project governance, risk and dependency management, resource planning and change management.
  • Experience with technology-enabled Compliance solutions is advantageous.

Know-how:

  • Strong knowledge of technology and digital transformation, AI and automation.
  • Expertise in global project/program management, governance and stakeholder management.
  • Understanding of Compliance processes, governance and regulatory requirements.
  • Experience with system integration, organizational change and implementation.

Working Style and Methods:

  • Strategic, analytical and structured, with a strong focus on execution and measurable results.
  • Able to manage complexity, competing priorities and international stakeholders.
  • Skilled at balancing global standards with local requirements and driving sustainable implementation.
  • Collaborative approach that values diverse perspectives and enables effective teamwork across cultures and functions.

Personality:

  • Takes ownership and accountability for complex initiatives.
  • Strong communication, influencing and negotiation skills.
  • Builds trusted relationships with senior stakeholders and diverse teams.
  • Pragmatic, resilient, adaptable and solution-oriented.
  • Demonstrates sound judgment, integrity and curiosity for emerging technologies.

Languages: Fluent English.

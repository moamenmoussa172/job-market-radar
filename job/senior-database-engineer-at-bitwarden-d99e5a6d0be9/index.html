About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United States.

Bitwarden is the trusted identity security leader for millions of users worldwide, empowering enterprises, developers, and individuals to securely manage and share sensitive information anywhere. Bitwarden makes it easy for all users to extend robust security across their devices with password management, secrets management, and passwordless and passkey innovations. The company is headquartered in Santa Barbara, California. Learn more at bitwarden.com.

As a Senior Database Engineer at Bitwarden you will work with our Engineering teams to lead the design, development, and continual tuning and maintenance of our database systems. You will serve as a technical leader for database query design and the optimization of our existing queries, scripts, and programs. You will support the software development process and contribute directly to the culture of quality within Bitwarden through process improvements, team outreach, and mentoring. To ensure success as a Senior Database Engineer, you should exhibit knowledge of best practices in database management and experience in a similar role. A top-notch Senior Database Engineer will be someone whose database management skills translate into the efficient flow of information throughout an organization.

This is an all-remote team and we are looking for someone located in the U.S. We do not offer visa sponsorship at this time.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Maintain and enhance the performance of existing database scripts, queries, and other database objects
  • Assist engineering teams in designing new database implementations that meet the organization's data security, storage, and performance needs
  • Tune existing database scripts and queries for optimal performance and data security
  • Establish database observability practices and monitor databases and related systems to ensure optimized performance alongside operations staff
  • Partner with other engineers to develop new scripts and queries that support company growth
  • Review database and user reports as well as system information
  • Perform debugging procedures on database scripts and systems, including as a part of incident response
  • Mentor engineers and provide them with technical support
  • Adhere to best practices in securely accessing, storing, backing up, and archiving data
  • Document processes related to database design, configuration, and performance
  • Keep abreast of developments and best practices in database engineering
  • Participate in code reviews and learn and spread technical knowledge amongst the engineering organization

WHAT YOU BRING TO BITWARDEN

Must haves:

  • Expert knowledge of Microsoft SQL Server and T-SQL: execution plans, indexing, locking and blocking, and query tuning on very large tables
  • Experience reviewing other engineers’ database code (schema changes, stored procedures, queries, migrations) and giving clear, constructive feedback that developers act on
  • Experience making schema changes safely on live systems: backward-compatible migrations, zero-downtime deployments, and knowing which changes block
  • Experience operating a large, busy cloud-hosted database in production (Azure SQL preferred; equivalent experience on another managed platform is welcome)
  • Ability to diagnose performance problems with evidence, using tools such as Query Store, execution plans, and wait and blocking analysis
  • Excellent problem-solving skills: you might not know all the answers, but you know how to find and communicate the solution
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, including explaining database findings to developers, SRE and support
  • Proficiency using source control and working through pull requests
  • Around 5 years of experience in database engineering, or equivalent depth on a large production system

Good to have:

  • Ability to read and review C# data-access code (.NET, Dapper, Entity Framework migrations)
  • Working knowledge of at least one other RDBMS, such as PostgreSQL or MySQL
  • Experience with observability tooling, such as Datadog and a telemetry query language like KQL
  • Scripting for automation in PowerShell or Python
  • Experience with production incident response and carrying out data fixes under a controlled process
  • Familiarity with Docker for local and test environments
  • Sound practice in data security: least-privilege access and safe handling of production data and secrets

WHAT TO EXPECT IN THE INTERVIEW PROCESS

  • Meeting with Talent Acquisition
  • Interview with the Hiring Manager
  • Interview with fellow database engineers

A FEW REASONS TO WORK WITH US

  • Our user community loves us and we love them. Come to work each day with a sense of purpose as we bring a more secure internet experience to everyone from our friends and family to the world’s largest organizations.
  • Become an expert. You’ll get immersed in the prominent technology markets of security and open source software.
  • We are dedicated to building a diverse and talented team. Work remotely with motivated and supportive team members across the world.
  • Learn and grow. Take on new challenges with the support of your team, and join our #growth-club to continue personal and professional development.

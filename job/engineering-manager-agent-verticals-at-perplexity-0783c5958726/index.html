About the job

We are hiring a hands-on engineering leader to lead and grow Perplexity's Agent Verticals team.

In 2026, we launched Computer, the defining product for the new era of agentic AI. We've scaled beyond the millions of people using Perplexity every day for research, shopping, investing and curiosity into a new paradigm of using AI to transform knowledge into action.

Agent Verticals builds the systems and products that connect Computer to the data and workflows people rely on in specific domains, starting with Finance, GTM, and Coding. We work close to the customer and the domain: build capabilities, bundle them into a domain offering, and expand. The best of what we build gets generalized into capabilities for every Computer user, and each vertical could grow into a separate team.

As the Engineering Manager for Agent Verticals, you'll lead a small, fast-growing team of full-stack engineers building things like:

  • Personal CFO, an always-on financial agent built on user-connected financial data
  • An agentic sales engineer that supports GTM teams end to end
  • 1st Party Agent Messaging

What you'll do:

  • Set technical and product direction across multiple verticals, and help the team make sharp tradeoffs on what to build, bundle, and generalize.
  • Work across the stack (backend, frontend, agentic engineering, data) and with nearly every team at Perplexity.
  • Stay hands-on: write code, review PRs, and own system design for critical parts of the stack.
  • Drive reliability, quality, and evaluation standards for products that customers depend on for real work.
  • Lead, hire, and grow a team of engineers, partnering closely with recruiting to build a high-caliber pipeline.
  • Ship fast: we build and dogfood quickly, and ramp to all users within weeks.

Qualifications:

  • 8+ years of engineering experience, including 3+ years as an engineering manager.
  • Strong hands-on engineering skills. You still enjoy coding and system design discussions.
  • Full-stack experience. Our work is roughly 70% backend and 30% frontend.
  • Strong product sense and a real interest in the user experience and customer feedback.
  • Comfort with ambiguity and the ability to set direction in a fast-moving environment.

Nice to have:

  • Experience building 0 to 1 products or teams, taking something from idea to launch.
  • Experience shipping LLM-powered or agentic products to users.

About the job

General & stakeholder management

  • Reporting on team performance, service status and key outcomes to internal and external customers;
  • Ensuring the team complies with Bosch internal rules and policies, as well as applicable external regulations and standards;
  • Communicating functional and departmental goals clearly and making sure the team understands them and is working toward them;
  • Leading remote and distributed teams, and acting on feedback from functional peers and stakeholders;
  • Supporting sales and Product Management processes related to the team’s activities.

Technical oversight (accountable for delivery through the team)

  • Overseeing the design, implementation and maintenance of CI/CD pipelines for fast, reliable and automated software delivery;
  • Owning the direction for cloud infrastructure management, and ensuring the team maintains it effectively with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform, Ansible and Helm;
  • Steering containerization and orchestration efforts with Docker and Kubernetes, and ensuring services meet scalability, availability and performance requirements;
  • Being accountable for the monitoring, logging and alerting solutions (e.g. Elastic Stack) that keep systems healthy and allow a fast response to incidents;
  • Championing DevSecOps best practices across the team, including vulnerability management, security scanning and compliance with organizational security policies;
  • Working with development and operations teams to define and enforce SLAs, SLOs and error budgets, building a culture of reliability and continuous improvement
  • 2 years of previous experience as a Team Leader;
  • Knowledge of and experience with key development frameworks:
  • Knowledge of key DevOps technologies would be a plus : Ansible, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Azure, Elastic, Terraform and others
  • Several years of experience in IT service management;
  • Bachelors degree in related field, or equivalent work experience;
  • Internally motivated, able to work proficiently both independently and in a team environment;
  • Exceptional communication skills with both internal team members and external business stakeholders;
  • Proactivity in finding solutions;
  • Creativity and analytical skills, able to work with deadlines and in pressing situations;
  • Entrepreneurship mindset and ability to work with stakeholders from different areas and hierarchical levels;
  • A candidate for this position must be a result oriented individual, be self-motivated, be proactive going beyond the call of duty, be a creative and strategic thinker, work comfortably in a constantly evolving environment;

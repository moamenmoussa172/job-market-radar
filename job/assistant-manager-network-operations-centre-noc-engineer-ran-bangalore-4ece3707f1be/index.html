About this Role

We are seeking a Network Operations Centre (NOC) Engineer - RAN to support the first line of network operations and service assurance activities within a 24x7 operational environment. This role is responsible for monitoring network performance, managing incidents, analysing faults, coordinating restoration activities, and ensuring service availability across radio access and transmission networks. The successful candidate will work closely with internal teams, field engineers, suppliers, and stakeholders to ensure timely incident resolution and achievement of agreed service levels.

What you’ll do

•Monitor and correlate network alarms and events across GSM, UMTS, LTE and associated network platforms.
•Detect, register, track, troubleshoot and resolve incidents in line with defined SLAs and operational procedures.
•Perform fault analysis, diagnosis and remote restoration activities, escalating incidents where required.
•Coordinate with technical teams, suppliers and field engineers to support network recovery and service restoration.
•Ensure correct incident categorisation, prioritisation, dispatching and escalation.
•Support shift operations by executing defined operational routines and maintaining service continuity.
•Contribute to proactive monitoring activities to identify and mitigate potential network issues before customer impact.
•Produce accurate shift reports highlighting critical incidents, risks and service impacts.
•Support major incident management activities and provide timely communications to stakeholders.
•Assist in the execution of operational and maintenance activities for network infrastructure.
•Provide out-of-hours standby and call-out support where necessary.
•Promote continuous improvement, process optimisation and operational excellence within the NOC environment.
•Contribute to automation initiatives through scripting or programming where applicable.
•Maintain compliance with incident management processes aligned to ITIL best practices.
•Support the achievement of team KPIs relating to monitoring, incident handling, escalation and service restoration.

Who you are

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Telecommunications, Electronics, Information Technology or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
  • 5-7 years of experience in telecommunications operations, network monitoring or network support environments.
  • Strong knowledge of GSM, UMTS and LTE technologies.
  • Good understanding of Radio Base Stations (RBS), BSC, RNC, site infrastructure and transmission technologies including PDH, SDH, Metro Ethernet and IP networks.
  • Experience working within an ITIL Incident Management framework.
  • Ability to analyse complex technical issues and coordinate effective resolution activities.
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to explain technical information to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Experience working in a shift-based, 24x7 operational environment.
  • Ability to remain effective under pressure and during major service incidents.
  • Strong stakeholder management, collaboration and coordination skills.
  • Exposure to network automation and scripting/programming concepts is desirable.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively within multidisciplinary teams and achieve operational objectives.

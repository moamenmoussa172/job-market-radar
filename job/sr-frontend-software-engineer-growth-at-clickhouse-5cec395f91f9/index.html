About the job

About the Team

ClickHouse is hiring a talented Senior Frontend Engineers for our Unified Console - Growth Team.

This team focuses on great user experience, A/B testing, signup and authentication funnel, onboarding, customer activation and product analytics instrumentation.

If you’re excited about implementing seamless UX, have a genuine interest in business metrics and customers behaviour, this team could be a great fit.

What you will do

  • Implement new onboarding flows, A/B experiments and upgrade funnels to improve conversion and retention
  • Partner cross-functionally with product and design
  • Contribute to our growth infrastructure: attribution system and experiments framework
  • Help maintain code quality, organization, and automation
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to a culture of continuous improvement

What we are looking for:

  • 6+ years experience building customer-facing products at a high quality software company
  • Strong React, Typescript and web fundamentals
  • Track record of driving complex, end-to-end features
  • A strong product and UX sensibility
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills

Perks

  • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
  • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
  • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity.
  • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries.
  • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee.
  • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

Culture - We All Shape It

As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture.

Are you interested in finding out more about our culture? Learn more about our values here. Check out our blog posts or follow us on LinkedIn to find out more about what’s happening at ClickHouse.

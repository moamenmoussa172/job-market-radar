About this Role

We are seeking a Network Project Engineer to design, implement, migrate, and transform enterprise network infrastructure across data centre, campus, WAN, cloud, and security environments. This role is ideal for professionals with experience delivering network transformation, infrastructure modernisation, technology refresh, and migration programmes. The successful candidate will combine strong technical expertise with project delivery capabilities to execute complex deployments while maintaining service stability and minimising business disruption

What you’ll do

  • Lead the technical delivery of enterprise network transformation, migration, and infrastructure modernisation projects.
  • Execute network build, configuration, deployment, integration, and commissioning activities.
  • Develop and validate implementation plans, Methods of Procedure (MOPs), test plans, and rollback strategies.
  • Perform technical assessments and provide implementation recommendations for transformation initiatives.
  • Coordinate and execute complex production changes in accordance with established change management processes.
  • Conduct post-implementation validation, troubleshooting, and operational handover activities.
  • Support office relocations, WAN migrations, technology refresh programmes, and cloud connectivity projects.
  • Support cloud-managed networking solutions, including Cisco Meraki environments.

Who you are

  • 4 to 8 years of experience in enterprise network build, consolidation, and migration activities.
  • Hands-on experience with Cisco networking technologies, SD-WAN, Cisco solutions, Cisco Meraki, and Zscaler.
  • Practical experience working with Fortinet Firewalls.
  • Knowledge of switching and routing technologies including Fabric Path, OSPF, BGP, BFD, Segment Routing, Ethernet, and VXLAN.
  • Proven track record delivering network migration, transformation, and infrastructure refresh programmes.
  • Experience implementing and managing complex changes within production environments.
  • Experience creating MOPs, implementation runbooks, test plans, and rollback procedures.
  • Professional knowledge of Software Defined Networking technologies such as SD-WAN, Cisco ACI, VMware NSX and/or Cloud Computing platforms.
  • A collaborative, solution-oriented approach with effective communication and problem-solving capabilities.

About the job

S'more about the team

We are hiring a Senior Data Scientist in our Customer Value Forecasting Squad inside the Customer Value Optimization Tribe to help build scalable machine learning models and predictive frameworks that drive value forecasting products at HelloFresh. As a Senior Data Scientist, your work will be pivotal in enabling our team to derive actionable insights, develop production-grade predictive models, and advance our customer valuation capabilities. Additionally, you will master the design and development of robust machine learning solutions capable of processing massive datasets and supporting critical business workflows.

Lettuce share what this role will be responsible for

  • Collaborate with engineers and product managers in a cross-functional team to improve HelloFresh’s customer value forecasting accuracy and efficiency.
  • Take ownership of the end-to-end machine learning lifecycle, including research, feature formulation, model development, validation, and monitoring for production-level inference.
  • Mentor junior team members and promote best practices in statistical modeling, experiment design, and data science methodology across the team.
  • Stay up-to-date on the latest advancements in data science and machine learning. Propose and implement methodological improvements or advanced algorithms to enhance predictive modeling accuracy and team efficiency.
  • Design and refine predictive frameworks to determine optimal personalized recommendations and sequential customer interventions in real-time.
  • All other duties, as assigned

Sound a-peeling? Here's what we're looking for

  • A passion for solving complex business problems with advanced machine learning and statistical modeling within a fast-paced environment. Experience with customer lifetime value modeling or personalization is a plus.
  • Strong programming skills in Python and SQL, with expertise in data science libraries (e.g., pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn) and ML frameworks (e.g., CatBoost, LightGBM, PyTorch/TensorFlow). Experience processing large-scale data and working with Spark is a plus.
  • Hands-on experience designing, training, evaluating, and deploying large-scale predictive models and machine learning pipelines for production use cases.
  • Pick up new technologies and modeling methodologies quickly while solving actual business problems.
  • Interest in working with modern data science tooling such as cloud platforms, MLOps platforms, experimentation tools, version control, and model monitoring frameworks.
  • Ability to communicate complex technical findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • An AI-native mindset, continuously leveraging generative AI tools and workflows to enhance productivity, code quality, and problem-solving efficiency.

Flexible Hybrid Approach

At HelloFresh, we know that flexible work arrangements are essential in enabling you to do your best work, while balancing your personal and life needs. Offering remote work flexibility, along with the opportunity to interact and collaborate in the office are all a part of creating a great employee experience.

To meet these needs, we are pleased to provide Flexible Hybrid work. Flexible Hybrid is a people-first approach that is based on choice, trust, personalization, and empowers teams to choose when and how often they work from the office and work from home, in addition to team days and company days. This means a minimum of 2 days in office per week, with most teams in office between 2-3 days a week.

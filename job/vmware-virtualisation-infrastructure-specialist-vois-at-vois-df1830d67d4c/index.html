About this Role

We are seeking a VMware Virtualisation Infrastructure Specialist with advanced VMware administration and troubleshooting expertise to support complex virtualisation environments. The role focuses on VMware vSphere 7.x and 8.x, VCF 9.0, VxRail, vCenter and ESXi, alongside disaster recovery and infrastructure operations. The successful candidate will also bring Windows Server administration capabilities and work collaboratively on critical incidents, service requests, changes, escalations and continuous service improvements. The role operates within a 24x7 operational environment, including night shifts.

What you’ll do

  • Administer and troubleshoot VMware vSphere 7.x and 8.x, VCF 9.0 and VxRail environments.
  • Support VMware SRM (Site Recovery Manager) 8.1 and VMware Aria administration and troubleshooting.
  • Diagnose and resolve critical VMware ESXi and vCenter issues, and support vCenter and ESXi upgrades.
  • Work with DRS, HA, vSwitch, dvSwitch, ESXCLI and ESXTOP.
  • Extract and analyse vCenter reports and produce performance-analysis reports across virtualisation components.
  • Troubleshoot hardware issues involving HP ProLiant and Dell servers and work with HP OneView.
  • Support disaster-recovery operations, including production failover to alternate data centres and third-party sites.
  • Manage incidents, service requests, changes, problems and escalated technical issues, engaging vendors where required.
  • Administer Windows Server environments, Active Directory, DNS, DHCP and Group Policy Objects (GPOs).
  • Support Windows Server upgrades and understand Microsoft Windows Server patching lifecycle activities.
  • Use PowerCLI and PowerShell scripting to support infrastructure administration and operational activities.
  • Identify continuous-improvement opportunities and contribute to improvements in service delivery.
  • Create clear technical documentation and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

Who you are

  • You have advanced administration and troubleshooting experience with VMware vSphere 7.x/8.x, VCF 9.0 and VxRail.
  • You have working knowledge of VMware SRM, VMware Aria, HP OneView, vCenter, ESXi, DRS, HA, vSwitch and dvSwitch.
  • You understand virtualisation performance analysis and can use command-line tools including ESXCLI and ESXTOP.
  • You have experience supporting disaster-recovery operations and infrastructure incident, change and problem management.
  • You have Windows administration experience covering Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, GPOs, server upgrades and patching.
  • You have experience with Windows PowerCLI and PowerShell scripting. Knowledge of TrueSight BladeLogic and VMware vRA is advantageous.
  • You communicate clearly, collaborate effectively across teams and can produce detailed technical documentation.
  • You can handle critical and complex issues while working collaboratively under the guidance of a delivery lead.
  • You are comfortable operating within ambitious timelines in a dynamic environment while maintaining a customer-focused approach.
  • You hold a graduation qualification (10+2+3/4).
  • You are able to work in a 24x7 operational environment, including night shifts.

About the job

It is imperative to ensure customers and other team members have a positive experience, while maintaining our safety, quality, and other policies and standards.

  • As a part of the branch team positively engage in our Zero Harm culture. Zero Harm is our vision that aims to eliminate all incidents, accidents, injuries, and harm in the workplace and to the environment. It is a commitment to ensure the safety and well-being of employees, contractors, and all individuals involved in our operations.
  • Strive for zero safety or property damage incidents.
  • Report any safety incidents, near miss, or unsafe conditions in a proper and timely manner. Assist in any safety investigations to determine root causes and minimize future incidents.
  • Ensure your PPE compliance and as a member of a team, help other do the same.
  • Ensure that material handling equipment are operated in a safe manner in accordance with company policy, guidelines, and practices, and in compliance with government regulations. This includes but no limited to:
  • Ensuring proper pre-trip and post-inspections are conducted.
  • That licenses and certifications are current and valid.
  • Speed limits are obeyed.
  • As a part of branch team delight our customers by providing quality products and services which meet or exceed their requirements and evolving expectations. This is achieved through a commitment to continuous improvement and the pursuit of a zero-defect culture.
  • Manage customer relationships and enhance the customer experience.
  • Communicates in professional and courteous manner, daily with customers via face-to-face interaction, phone, e-mail, or other means necessary to provide best in class service.
  • Engage in supporting and maintaining the efficient operation of the branch in accordance with current guidelines, policies, practices, and procedures.
  • Proactively engage in the safe efficient operation of the warehouse:
  • Shipping and receiving activities as required to assure that products are handled and stored in a safe and efficient manner.
  • Proper picking of orders including managing first in, first out (FIFO) compiance.
  • Receive returned product from customers – inspecting for damage, check packing slip for product accuracy, and securing returning product to ensure safe and undamaged return.
  • Enhance and improve inventory management.
  • Other duties as directed.
  • 2-4 years of delivery driver experience of a commercial vehicle weighing 10k-26k pounds.
  • High School diploma or equivalent combined education and experience.
  • Strong interpersonal, customer relationship and decision-making skills.
  • Effective and professional communication and organization skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as a member of a team in a high-volume fast-paced environment.
  • Strong analytical skills.
  • Proficiency in Windows and Microsoft Office software is a plus.
  • HVAC industry experience preferred including warehouse and forklift operations.
  • Describe reading, corresponding, and language requirements
  • Include presentation or training skills if needed

Note the above is representative of the work performed and are not intended to be all inclusive of actions within a position.

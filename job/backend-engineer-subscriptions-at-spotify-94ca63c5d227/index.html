About the job

At Spotify, our Subscriptions mission helps make the subscription experience seamless and reliable for millions of listeners around the world. We're building the technology that supports subscribers throughout their journey, from their first purchase to renewals, plan changes, and beyond.

We're looking for a Backend Engineer to join Rover, the team responsible for Spotify's core subscription system. Our services manage the lifecycle of every Spotify subscription, including activation, renewals, plan changes, grace periods, and cancellations.

You'll help build and improve the backend services and data systems that millions of subscribers depend on. Whether you're growing your engineering experience or looking for a new technical challenge, you'll have opportunities to learn, collaborate, and make a meaningful impact on Spotify's subscription experience.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build, and improve backend services that power Spotify's core subscription system.
  • Develop and deliver solutions that support subscription journeys, from activation and renewals to plan changes and cancellations.
  • Work with complex, large-scale systems to improve reliability, performance, and scalability.
  • Collaborate with engineers and teams across Spotify to solve technical challenges and deliver meaningful improvements for subscribers.
  • Contribute to technical discussions, share ideas, and help shape engineering decisions.
  • Work with large-scale data systems and pipelines, building your knowledge while contributing to their ongoing development.
  • Share feedback, learn from your colleagues, and contribute to a supportive and collaborative engineering culture.
  • Who You Are
  • You have experience developing backend services using Python or Java and working with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform or similar technologies.
  • You're familiar with database technologies such as PostgreSQL and interested in building reliable, scalable systems.
  • You have an understanding of data systems or pipelines, or an interest in developing your skills in this area.
  • You enjoy solving complex technical problems and finding practical ways to improve existing systems.
  • You communicate clearly and feel comfortable sharing technical ideas, contributing to discussions, and explaining your work to different audiences.
  • You take ownership of your work, seek feedback, and enjoy collaborating with others to achieve shared goals.
  • You bring curiosity, adaptability, and a willingness to learn as technologies and challenges evolve.
  • You may have worked with subscriptions, billing, payments, e-commerce, or financial systems, or you're interested in learning more about these areas.
  • Where You'll Be
  • This role is based in London or Stockholm
  • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.

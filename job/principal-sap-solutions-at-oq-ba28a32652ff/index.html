Job purpose

The Principal, SAP Solutions is OQEP’s senior subject-matter authority for the SAP landscape. The role sets SAP solution design standards, provides expert assurance over designs, changes, and vendor deliverables across the full SAP lifecycle, from business demand and solution design through implementation, operations, and continuous improvement, and advises management on SAP options, risks, and roadmap priorities, working in partnership with the SAP service provider.

The principal provides deep functional and technical expertise, evaluates and challenges vendor and implementation partner recommendations, and assures that SAP solutions are aligned with SAP standards and best practices, secure, supportable, and consistent with business priorities.

The position will act in accordance with OQEP’s Mission, Vision, Values & Strategies, as well as policies, guidelines, and international standards, supported by an IT Technology platform, QHSE & Energy Management System requirements and standards, Omani government regulations and other legal requirements, and best international practices in consonance with national objectives.

Main Tasks and Responsibilities

  • SAP Governance & Solution Authority
  • Act as the functional and technical assurance authority for all SAP-related demands within OQEP, ensuring appropriate assessment of end-to-end process, cross-module, integration, data, security and architecture impacts, while leveraging relevant functional and technical specialists where detailed module expertise is required.
  • Define and assure SAP design standards and solution integrity across OQEP’s SAP landscape, including OQEP-specific configuration, modules, integrations, enhancements, controls, and documentation, in line with the architecture standards.
  • Act as OQEP’s SAP solution design authority: review and endorse functional and technical designs for OQEP requirements before implementation, challenge unnecessary customization, and promote standard SAP capabilities.
  • Define and maintain SAP governance structures, decision rights, escalation paths, and a RACI matrix between OQEP, the service provider, and vendors for key SAP services, demands, and projects.
  • Act as OQEP’s primary SAP focal point for business stakeholders, IDS/IT management, vendors, implementation partners, and auditors.
  • Demand, Portfolio & Delivery
  • Assess and coordinate SAP demands from requirements assessment through prioritization, delivery, and closure, providing expert evaluation of business value, complexity, risks, and cross-module impacts.
  • Maintain technical visibility of the SAP portfolio (demands, projects, enhancements, and upgrades), including scope, dependencies, and technical risks, and support the Manager Digital Delivery in tracking budget, schedule, and resources.
  • Provide expert design and quality assurance across SAP implementations, upgrades, and enhancements, ensuring clear scope, acceptance criteria, testing, UAT coordination, cutover, go-live stabilization, and operational handover.
  • Advise the Manager Digital Delivery on SAP portfolio performance, risks, dependencies, and decisions required; escalate material risks and scope changes in a timely manner.
  • Collaborate with Business Process Owners to ensure business requirements, process decisions, UAT, and final business acceptance are formally owned and approved by the business.
  • Functional &Technical Expertise
  • Provide expert guidance to, and coordinate, functional and technical specialists (internal, service provider, and vendor) across the SAP solutions used by OQEP, such as S/4HANA, FI/CO, JVA, MM, PM/EAM, PS, SuccessFactors, Fiori, reporting and analytics, and other solutions within the SAP roadmap.
  • Assure functional and technical consistency, integration, and solution integrity across SAP modules, resolving complex cross-functional and technical issues.
  • Provide objective recommendations to management and business stakeholders, outlining solution options, business impact, risks, costs, and dependencies.
  • Operations &Support
  • Monitor SAP application performance, availability, security, and agreed service levels in coordination with the service provider and IT Operations, driving corrective actions where performance falls short.
  • Provide expert oversight of SAP application incidents, service requests, and problems, driving timely resolution, root-cause analysis, and preventive actions for complex or recurring issues.
  • Assure SAP application changes, releases, and transport management across environments, in alignment with established IT change-management processes.
  • Integration, Data& Security
  • Assure the design and integrity of SAP integrations with enterprise, operational, and third-party systems, ensuring interfaces are properly designed, documented, tested, monitored, and supported.
  • Define SAP master-data standards and monitor data-quality controls, in partnership with business data owners who retain ownership and accountability.
  • Define OQEP’s SAP role and authorization requirements and monitor Segregation of Duties (SoD) and least-privilege access controls in coordination with the service provider, Cybersecurity, and Internal Controls; support audits and the timely closure of agreed findings.
  • Vendor, Commercial &Licensing Management
  • Monitor and assess the performance of the service provider’s services, SAP vendors, system integrators, and Application Management Services (AMS) providers engaged by OQEP against agreed contracts, service agreements, Statements of Work (SOWs), SLAs, milestones, and quality expectations.
  • Review and validate vendor proposals, effort estimates, deliverables, and change requests, assessing technical justification, business impact, and cost, and provide recommendations to approvers in line with the Delegation of Authority.
  • Support SAP licensing, subscription, and maintenance optimization while maintaining visibility of OQEP’s commercial commitments and renewals in coordination with the service provider, Procurement, Finance, and Legal.
  • Drive effective knowledge transfer from vendors to internal and national resources, building in-house SAP capability and reducing dependency on external providers.
  • Strategy & Capability Development
  • Develop and maintain the SAP technical roadmap, for endorsement by the Manager Digital Delivery, aligned with business priorities, digital strategy, and enterprise architecture, incorporating technical debt, lifecycle management, and modernization planning.
  • Maximize the value of existing SAP capabilities by identifying opportunities to simplify, standardize, optimize, and automate business processes before introducing new systems.
  • Coach and develop internal SAP resources and key users(without direct line management), strengthening technical and functional capabilities, succession planning, and knowledge transfer.

Key interactions

Internal: Manager Digital Delivery and IDS & CI leadership, OQ SAOC, IT Operations, Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Enterprise Architecture, Internal Control, Internal Audit, Procurement, Finance, Legal, and Business Process Owners and users across OQEP functions and streams.

External: SAP and platform vendors, system integrators, Application Management Services providers, specialist consultants, and external auditors.

Notable Working Conditions. Office environment, intensive computer screen use.

Education Requirements

Required: Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, Business Information Systems or a related field.

Preferred: SAP certification (S/4HANA advantageous); PMP, PRINCE2, ITIL or SAP Activate.

Language

Excellent written and spoken English (required); Arabic is an advantage.

Department Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we are committed to "Solving for safer" by building and connecting technologies that help protect people, property, and places. Our Infrastructure Operations team stands behind the mission-critical platforms that emergency services rely on when every second counts, including Guardian, our 999 and 101 call-taking and dispatching solution, and Pronto, our digital notebook service empowering frontline police officers across the UK and Europe. Working across specialized networking, virtualisation, and infrastructure streams, we collaborate to keep our hosted environments resilient, secure, and ready for the moments that matter.
Join our Shared Platform services team as a Network Engineer, where you will be instrumental in the planning, designing and implementation of new networking solutions all whilst providing critical support for existing infrastructure. These solutions have multiple products deemed as critical national infrastructure and operate on an Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) platform.
About the products you will support:
Guardian is a call taking & dispatching platform used by emergency service operators across the UK & Europe. We help 999 or 101 operators answer calls from the public and dispatch out nearby responders, enabling the right response when every second counts, and ultimately helping our customers save lives.
Pronto is a digital notebook service providing front line police officers with access to national systems to obtain information quickly and efficiently when working within their communities. Pronto also allows for digital evidence collection which can be used as evidence in juridical processes, location services for fast dispatching of officers, and a range of many other tools to aid in an officers day to day activities


Job Description

Everything starts with our people, and as a Network Engineer, you will join a people-first culture where your technical expertise directly impacts community safety. You will play a vital role in a fast-paced environment undergoing major infrastructure evolution over the next year, balancing innovative transformation projects with the demanding Service Level Agreements of our live public safety solutions. We value diverse backgrounds and equivalent practical experience, empowering you to pursue your passion, embrace continuous learning, and grow your career alongside a supportive, close-knit team. Whether working remotely or traveling to UK and international sites, you will bring strong communication skills and collaborative energy to connect those in need with those who can help.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and support end-to-end networking operations across multiple Motorola Solutions sites and remote customer locations to help deliver high availability for mission-critical emergency services.
  • Administer, review, and maintain firewall rulesets, routing policies, NAT configurations, and security objects across diverse multi-vendor environments.
  • Design, update, and maintain comprehensive network documentation and topology diagrams to support operational clarity and rapid incident resolution.
  • Collaborate with third-party vendors and internal Incident Management teams to troubleshoot and resolve circuit faults, outages, and complex routing issues.
  • Conduct capacity planning, performance tuning, and network optimization while participating in an on-call rotation (beginning after six months of service).
  • Help to provide strict compliance with public sector security policies and regulatory frameworks, including ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials, and NCSC security best practices.
  • Travel to internal and customer sites across the UK (with occasional international travel) as required to support infrastructure deployments and maintenance.


Basic Requirements

Must hold or have the ability to attain NPPV3 and SC clearance (requires a minimum of 5 years of UK residency).

  • Solid hands-on experience in a network engineering role supporting enterprise or multi-site infrastructures.
  • Full professional fluency in English (written and spoken) to collaborate across teams and document technical architectures.

Network Infrastructure

  • Layer 2 and Layer 3 networking expertise (VLANs, subnetting, STP, OSPF, and BGP) to design, maintain, and troubleshoot multi-site routing topologies, routing loops, asymmetric routing, and route redistribution.
  • Enterprise switching and WAN technologies experience (Aruba, Cisco, HP, or Juniper switches, private circuits, DIA, IPsec over internet, and EWLs) to manage reliable site connectivity.
  • Multi-site firewall and VPN administration proficiency (Fortinet or Check Point, static/dynamic NAT, PAT, UTM, IDS/IPS, IPsec, IKEv2, and route-based VPNs) to uphold operational security standards.
  • Packet capture and network observation tools experience (Wireshark, Grafana, Prometheus, SolarWinds, or FortiAnalyzer) to analyze traffic, diagnose connectivity issues, and apply security best practices.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Experience configuring and managing SD-WAN solutions (specifically Fortinet SD-WAN), including overlay/underlay architecture, path selection, and link monitoring.
  • Hands-on familiarity with SIP technologies to support voice and critical communications traffic.
  • Experience working with Software-Defined Networking (SDN) platforms such as VMware NSX, Cisco ACI, or OVN.
  • Exposure to Red Hat OpenShift environments and containerized infrastructure networking.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to articulate complex network concepts clearly to technical peers, non-technical stakeholders, and public sector customers.
  • Adaptability and eagerness to learn emerging technologies while maintaining high standards under strict SLA pressures.

About the job

Join Truecaller – The place where innovation meets impact!

Truecaller's mission is to build trust in communication by making it safer, smarter, and more efficient. Born in Sweden, trusted by the world, and here’s why we stand out:

  • We are trusted by over 500 million active users every month across 190+ countries
  • We identify over 15 billion calls daily, helping users avoid spam and scams
  • We are powered by a team of 400+ employees from 45+ nationalities

We always look for people who take initiative, own their work, and keep raising the bar. An entrepreneurial mindset matters here, especially when it turns bold ideas into real actions. We stay collaborative and focused, always searching for smarter paths forward. If you want to make an impact and grow with a team that inspires millions, you’ll fit right in.

The role

The Security team at Truecaller is small and works in four areas: cloud security, threat detection and incident response, application security, and workforce security, including the security of our AI tools. We work as engineers. Most of what we do ends up as code, configuration, or a design. We run on GCP and use Wiz, CrowdStrike, Panther, GitHub, and Claude. Our automations run on our own GCP infrastructure that we maintain.

As a Staff Security Engineer, you drive the technical direction of our largest security initiatives and make the key architecture decisions in them. Your home ground is cloud security on Google Cloud (GCP), and you are expected to work across the other areas as well. The role is hands-on. You build, configure, and fix things yourself, and what you build makes the whole organization more secure.

What you'll do

  • Lead security initiatives that span several squads and several quarters, from defining the problem to running the result in production.
  • Make the key security architecture decisions for our GCP platform: identity, network perimeters, GKE, and the paths data can take out of the environment.
  • Remove whole classes of risk with platform controls and secure defaults, so the same finding does not have to be fixed squad by squad.
  • Build security into how we ship software: design reviews, threat modeling, and guardrails in CI/CD and the supply chain.
  • Define how AI assistants and agents get access to internal data and systems, and turn that into a pattern squads can adopt on their own.
  • Mentor the security engineers and set the bar for our designs, reviews, and code.
  • Own parts of the security roadmap together with the engineering manager. You bring the technical direction and the risk-based priorities.

What you bring in

  • Several years in a senior security engineering role, with deep hands-on experience securing GCP (IAM, VPC Service Controls, GKE).
  • You are comfortable with code. You read it, review it, and can take responsibility for what goes into production, and you work through pull requests, infrastructure as code, and CI/CD like any other engineer.
  • Solid knowledge of at least one more of our areas besides cloud security.
  • You have led technical work across several teams and got decisions made without formal authority.
  • Good judgment about risk. You can say which risks matter to the business and which can wait, and explain why to people outside security.
  • You have mentored experienced engineers, and you like doing it

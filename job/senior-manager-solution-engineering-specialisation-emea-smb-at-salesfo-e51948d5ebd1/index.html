About the Role

As Senior Manager of Specialist Solution Engineering (SMB EMEA), you will lead a team of elite technical experts driving technical wins and transformative solutions across the Salesforce ecosystem.

You will build, mentor and scale a world-class team of Solution Specialists who translate complex business challenges into elegant Salesforce architectures, directly impacting regional Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).

Sitting at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and business growth, your team will serve as the technical foundation for our most strategic SMB relationships across EMEA, enabling growing mid-market enterprises to unlock unprecedented value and scale their operations through Salesforce applications and AI innovation.

Key Responsibilities & Day-to-Day Impact

1. Team Leadership & Development

  • Build, mentor and scale a team of 10+ senior Solution Engineers and Architects specialising in AI, Cloud and Enterprise solutions.
  • Establish technical standards, playbooks and best practices for solution design and customer engagement.
  • Conduct regular 1:1s, performance reviews and career development planning with direct reports.
  • Drive recruitment efforts to attract top-tier technical talent and build diverse, high-performing teams.

2. Strategic Customer Engagement

  • Partner directly with C-level executives and technical leaders at strategic accounts to understand business objectives and architect transformative solutions.
  • Lead complex proof-of-concept initiatives and technical evaluations for deals valued at $1M+ ARR.
  • Deliver executive-level presentations and technical demonstrations that articulate business value and ROI.
  • Act as a trusted advisor to customers on their AI and digital transformation strategies and roadmaps.

3. Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Collaborate with Product, Engineering, Marketing and Research teams to create concept-ready demonstrations for clients, enablement materials for wider EMEA teams and thought leadership presentations at conferences and events.
  • Partner with Sales leadership to develop account strategies, qualify opportunities and accelerate deal velocity.
  • Work with Customer Success and Forward Deployment Engineering teams to ensure smooth handovers and drive product adoption post-sale.
  • Contribute to company-wide go-to-market strategy and positioning.

4. Technical Excellence & Innovation

  • Stay current on industry trends, the competitive landscape and emerging technologies in AI/ML, cloud infrastructure and enterprise architecture.
  • Develop reusable solution patterns, reference architectures and technical assets that accelerate customer outcomes.
  • Represent Salesforce at industry conferences, customer events and technical forums.
  • Drive thought leadership through white papers, technical blogs and speaking engagements.

Attendance & Travel Requirements

Collaboration-Focused Hybrid Working Policy
This role requires a minimum of 3 days per week in our Dublin hub office.

In-office days are designed to maximise team collaboration, customer meetings and cross-functional alignment. Specific in-office days may be adjusted based on customer meeting schedules and team requirements.

Customer-Driven Flexibility

  • Customer meeting schedules may require adjustments to standard in-office days.
  • Executive customer engagements may necessitate specific office presence for on-site meetings.
  • Proof-of-concept periods may require extended on-site or customer-site presence.

Team Collaboration Needs

  • Weekly team meetings, 1:1s and planning sessions scheduled on core in-office days.
  • Cross-functional collaboration sessions with Product and Engineering coordinated around office days.
  • New-hire onboarding includes increased in-office presence during the first 90 days (4–5 days per week recommended).

Travel Requirements

  • Domestic Travel: 20–30% for regional customer meetings and internal events.
  • International Travel: 5–15% for global accounts and international conferences.
  • Peak Periods: Increased travel during fiscal quarter-end, major product launches and industry conference season.

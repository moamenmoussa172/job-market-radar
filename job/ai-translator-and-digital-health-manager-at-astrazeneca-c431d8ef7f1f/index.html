About the job

Introduction to role:

Are you ready to turn AI and Digital Health ideas into scaled solutions that deliver measurable value for patients and the business? Do you thrive at the intersection of business, data and technology, orchestrating different teams to move from concept to impact?

In this role, you will help shape and execute the AI and Digital Health strategy across key brands and customer journeys, translating business needs into deployable solutions and integrating them into omnichannel ecosystems. Better experiences, faster decisions, stronger performance and compliant-by-design operations.

Accountabilities:
- Translate business needs into AI- and Digital–enabled solutions, coordinating with data scientists, IT and external partners to deliver value.
- Oversee end-to-end delivery of prioritized AI and Digital use cases, from ideation through pilot to scale.
- Supervise governance of digital assets (websites, apps, social, platforms) to ensure performance, UX, accessibility and brand consistency.
- Coach and mentor Managers and Designers, fostering AI fluency and digital capabilities across the team.
- Interact with global teams, localising Digital & AI solutions and sharing best practices
- Ensure compliance-by-design across all digital & AI initiatives (AI Act, GDPR, MDR) in close partnership with Legal, Compliance and Medical

- Drive integration of AI into key customer journeys and omnichannel ecosystems.

- Support implementation of personalisation strategies across CRM and digital platforms

- Enable standardisation of data models across web, social and CRM for unified measurement, reporting and optimisation.
- Work in cross functional teams, spanning Marketing, Medical, Market Access, External Communications, IT and Customer Engagement, driving execution excellence.
- Monitor initiative performance via KPIs, dashboards and ROI tracking, and act decisively on insights.

Essential Skills/Experience:
- 4-6+ years of experience in Digital, Digital Marketing, AI or Digital Health roles
- Proven track record in delivering digital/AI projects at scale
- Proven track record in developing web and social media projects, SEO & GEO strategy, digital media buying
- Experience working with AI use cases (LLMs, RAG, automation, AI agents, classification, recommendation systems)

- Strong understanding of ecosystem such as CRM, CMS, new channels, analytics, personalization tools
- Strong program management and stakeholder management skills

- Knowledge of data governance, privacy and compliance
- Stakeholder influence and alignment

- Experience coordinating cross-functional teams and external vendors
- Execution excellence and delivery mindset
- Data-driven decision making
- Fluent in Italian and English

Desirable Skills/Experience:
- Advanced Degree (MBA);
- Agile working certification (PSM, PSPO, etc.);
- Human center design experience;
- Familiarity with advanced AI topics ;
- Passionate about continuous learning in the AI field, with an entrepreneurial approach to innovation and problem-solving;
- Strong leadership presence with the ability to influence senior stakeholders;
- Familiarity with cybersecurity and risk management principles applied to digital health solutions.

We carefully consider all applications, including those from candidates eligible under Italian Protected Categories legislation (Art. 1 and Art. 18, L. 68/99).

Why AstraZeneca:
Here, technology is a force-multiplier for real-world impact—helping us get medicines to patients faster, more affordably and with greater sustainability. We embed digital capabilities end to end, from automated workflows and smart production to data-driven decisions and machine learning that improves quality and speed. You will join a diverse, multi-generational community that prizes curiosity, shares learnings and scales winning ideas quickly. Expect unexpected teams in the same room unleashing bold thinking, with leaders who invest in modern tools and back you to push boundaries. We value kindness alongside ambition, and we connect your contribution directly to outcomes that matter for people, society and the planet.

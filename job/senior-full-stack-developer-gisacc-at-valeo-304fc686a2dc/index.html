About the job

Valeo is a tech global company, designing breakthrough solutions to reinvent the mobility. We are an automotive supplier partner to automakers and new mobility actors worldwide. Our vision? Invent a greener and more secured mobility, thanks to solutions focusing on intuitive driving and reducing CO2 emissions. We are leader on our businesses, and recognized as one of the largest global innovative companies.

Mission

GISACC is Valeo holding group IS application center in Cairo, where the company's digital transformation is taken to a new level. We aim to build scalable and serverless applications that incrementally add value everyday. Our Senior Full Stack developers engineers are a very crucial part in achieving this where they play a big role in modifying and improving existing systems. This role requires deep technical expertise, and communication abilities to facilitate the communication and alignment between different technical and business stakeholders.

Responsibilities

  • Spec, design, develop, test, deploy, maintain and improve software
  • Contribute to system architecture: Design scalable, secure, and high-performance systems.
  • Work closely with project managers and business owners to set project priorities, deadlines and deliverables
  • Collaborate with product owners, tech-leads and software architects on user experience and application development roadmap
  • Provide support to other team members across multiple software applications.
  • Responsible for full stack feature implementation (backend and frontend)
  • End to end and close collaboration with software/infrastructure architects on complex features
  • Identify and establish patterns and best practices for building excellent software applications

Education/Training

  • BSc degree in computer engineering, computer science or any similar technical field of study.

ProfessionalExperience and Key Competences

  • 3 to 6 years of relevant experience.
  • Good knowledge in Agile Scrum
  • Business processes understanding
  • Excellent problem-solving skills
  • Excellent Troubleshooting and debugging skills
  • Team spirit and excellent communication abilities
  • Good Client management skills and organizational skills
  • GCP & Cloud Operations related knowledge is a must
  • Good DevOps knowledge is a plus
  • Good knowledge in monitoring or log analysis tools (ex. Cloud Logging, Cloud Monitoring, Error Reporting, …)
  • Good knowledge in Terraform and Cloud automation is a plus
  • Azure, AWS, or GCP certifications are a plus.

Languages

  • Excellent command of English language is a must (Written and Verbal)
  • French is a plus.

Technical Skills

  • Software development experience in Java and Spring framework
  • Strong fundamentals of distributed systems and highly scalable applications on cloud platforms
  • Excellent proficiency of developing applications using SQL or NoSQL databases
  • Solid concepts grasp of OOP, design patterns and REST APIs
  • Excellent foundations of the latest web standards, including HTML5 and CSS3
  • Knowledge of web libraries and frameworks such as Angular, VueJS or React
  • Knowledge of backend web libraries and frameworks such as Spring
  • Configuration management proficiency using Git
  • Experience with CI/CD concepts and pipelines
  • Experience with any Cloud Platform is a must (GCP/AWS/Azure)
  • Highly analytical mindset and problem solving skills
  • Excellent troubleshooting and debugging skills
  • Working proficiency and communication skills in verbal and written English
  • High sense of ownership and responsibility
  • Familiarity with Google Cloud Platform or AWS is a plus

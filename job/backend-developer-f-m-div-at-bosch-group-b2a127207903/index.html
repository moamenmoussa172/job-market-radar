About the job

Your contribution to something big:

Help shape the future of automotive diagnostics by developing modern, cloud-based software solutions. As part of an international and agile Scrum team, you will contribute to the design, implementation and evolution of software products that support innovative diagnostic solutions.

  • Shape the future: Design and develop innovative software solutions for automotive diagnostic products and services.
  • Design with impact: Create modern software architectures and contribute to technical decisions across a variety of products.
  • Develop end-to-end solutions: Implement scalable microservices and front-end components using modern software engineering practices.
  • Take ownership: Work independently within a self-organized Scrum team and take responsibility for delivering high-quality software.
  • Think holistically: Contribute your expertise while keeping the overall product, architecture and customer needs in mind.
  • Build quality in: Apply test-driven development, automation and modern CI/CD principles to create reliable and maintainable software.
  • Collaborate globally: Work closely with international colleagues, share your knowledge and learn from different perspectives and areas of expertise.
  • Explore new technologies: Evaluate and introduce technologies and approaches that can improve our products and development processes.

Education: Completed degree in computer science or similar major.

Personality:

  • Motivated, responsible, enjoying the use of technologies, knowledge sharing and exploration of new technologies.

Working style:

  • Self-reliant and mindful working style following test-driven development, team collaboration based on open feedback culture and agile principles (Scrum).

Know-how:

  • Deep knowledge regarding design, implementation and documentation of software.
  • Strong expertise in Java/Kotlin (C/C++ is a plus), confidence in development environments based on IntelliJ, Git, GitHub, Gradle, Docker, Kubernetes, knowledge of REST, Spring Boot, microservice architecture.

Experience:

  • At least 3–5 years professional experience in development of cloud software solutions and microservice architecture.
  • Experience with RDBMS and NoSQL databases, solid understanding of CI/CD principles (experience with Bitbucket, Jenkins, SonarQube, JFrog, FossID is a plus), automotive domain knowledge including ECU diagnosis and vehicle communication (e.g. CAN, K-Line, DoIP).

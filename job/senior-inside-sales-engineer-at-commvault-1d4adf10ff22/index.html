About the job

Recruitment Fraud Alert

We’ve learned that scammers are impersonating Commvault team members—including HR and leadership—via email or text. These bad actors may conduct fake interviews and ask for personal information, such as your social security number.

What to know:

  • Commvault does not conduct interviews by email or text.
  • We will never ask you to submit sensitive documents (including banking information, SSN, etc) before your first day.
  • Commvault recruiters may contact prospective candidates through LinkedIn and other professional networking platforms regarding career opportunities. If you are unsure whether a recruiting communication is legitimate, review the profile to ensure they have a badge verifying them as a Commvault employee.

If you suspect a recruiting scam, please contact us at wwrecruitingteam@commvault.com

About Commvault

Commvault (NASDAQ: CVLT) delivers continuous recovery for the AI era. We help organizations stay ready for constant disruption by returning critical data, applications, identities, and AI systems to a clean, trusted state. Our purpose-built recovery solutions span cloud-native and hybrid environments, leveraging AI to enable resilience at enterprise scale.

Commvault is a worldwide leader in delivering backup and recovery for the progressive enterprise. Commvault enables you to protect, manage, and use your data, while managing it in a consistent, compliant way. Our automated solutions work on-prem and in the cloud, and work with the digital tools and procedures you’re already using. Keep your data accessible and actionable with a single solution that ensures your data is always available — no matter what.

Position Responsibilities:

  • This role will be to remotely support various Sales Engineering Managers and Sales Engineers in the US market with pre-sales/sales engagements, primarily focussed on POC’s and Demos and support other pre-sales activities such as documentation, TODs, ALDs, RFI/RFPs, discovery& requirement gathering, technical research etc.

Position Requirements:

  • 8:10 years of hands-on experience in Technical Support and/or experience in data recovery, backup & recovery solutions with a focus on any one or more Cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS and GCP. Strong understanding of technology & cybersecurity basics, computer networking fundamentals, OSI layers, OSI layer protocols, Active directory& DNS basics, TCP, UDP etc.
  • Hands-on Configuration & troubleshooting level Knowledge on:

Networking, Storage Systems, Hypervisors and container solutions such as VMware, Hyper-V, Nutanix AHV, Kubernetes, OpenStack, OpenShift etc.

Cloud Platforms such as Azure and AWS (SaaS/PaaS/IaaS Infra exposure)

Databases such as MSSQL, MYSQL, Oracle, SAP, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, etc

  • Prior experience in Customer facing role to demonstrate, delivering a pilot / POC, Technical Demos of cybersecurity solutions with strong interpersonal skills. [For AMER COE hires: Prior experience with US based Customers is preferred & flexibility to work in permanent night shifts].
  • Excellent communication skills (English) both written and verbal. Proficient in building & articulating PowerPoint presentations.

Good to have:

o Proficiency in on-boarding CRM apps like Salesforce, D365 etc.

o Prior Hands-on exposure on Commvault/ Rubrik/ Veeam/Cohesity, Dell, NetApp, Pure Storage Technologies.

o Relevant Industry certification(s) in cloud (AWS, Azure etc) & AI.

o Additional languages spoken like French, German, Spanish etc.

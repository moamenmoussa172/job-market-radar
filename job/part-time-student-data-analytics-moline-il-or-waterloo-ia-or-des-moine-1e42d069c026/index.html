Your Responsibilities

As a Part-Time Student – Data Analytics for Deere & Company World Headquarters located in Moline, IL or Waterloo, IA or Des Moines, IA you will:

• Support analytics projects that deliver insights to end-users and help advance product support goals and improve the customer experience.
• Explore and analyze data to assess project opportunities and answer business questions.
• Help develop, test, and document data products and pipelines that support analytical insights.
• Assist with validating data and analytical outputs for accuracy, reliability, and quality.
• Contribute to the support, maintenance, and continuous improvement of existing analytics projects.
• Collaborate with teammates and stakeholders, and communicate findings through reports, presentations, and discussions.
• Identify opportunities to improve analytics processes, tools, and products.

This position is not available to students on immigration visas

What Skills You Need

• Currently pursuing a degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.

• Must be registered as a full-time student at a U.S. accredited college/university
• Graduation date of December 2027 or later
• Cumulative GPA of 2.8 or above
• Available to work during the academic year 16-20 hours/weekly during the business hours of 6 AM to 6 PM
• Available to work during the summer semester 30-40 hours/weekly during the business hours of 6 AM to 6 PM

• Experience with or coursework in Python and SQL.
• Familiarity with data analysis and working with structured datasets.
• Strong communication and interpersonal skills, including the ability to explain findings to non-technical audiences.
• Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
• Availability to work part-time alongside your academic schedule.

• Must be able to commute to the work location in Moline, IL or Waterloo, IA or Des Moines, IA. NOTE: Relocation assistance is not provided

What Makes You Stand Out

• Experience with PySpark or other tools for working with large datasets.
• Familiarity with GitHub, Databricks, VSCode, or similar analytics platforms.
• Exposure to machine learning, generative AI, or other advanced analytical techniques.
• Interest in exploring new tools and technologies to solve business problems.
• Demonstrated curiosity, learning agility, and a desire to innovate.

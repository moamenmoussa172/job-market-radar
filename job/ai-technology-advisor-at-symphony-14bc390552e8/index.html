About the job

AI Technology Advisor

Location: Southeast Europe or Latin America, with a daily overlap with US Eastern time

About the job

Symphony is a global design and development software company headquartered in San Francisco, with 700+ technologists across engineering offices in Southeast Europe and Latin America, business development offices in the US and Western Europe, and a Remote hub engaging employees from Latin America to Africa and Asia. Over the last 19 years, we have launched over 300 products for the world's leading startups, enterprises, Private Equity firms, and Fortune 500 companies. We have deep domain expertise across financial services, banking, retail, technology, healthcare, and the VC and PE ecosystem.

Every portfolio company now has an AI roadmap. Far fewer have the architecture, data and engineering practices to carry it. Their CTOs need someone who has put agentic systems into production and will tell them plainly what will work.

As an AI Technology Advisor at Symphony, you are that person. You are the senior technical voice with CTOs of PE-backed companies and enterprises. You design the systems we propose and lead them into production with our engineering teams. You work side by side with an AI Strategist, who owns the business case and the relationship, while you own the technical answer.

What would be your responsibilities if you join us?

  • Be the technical counterpart to client CTOs and engineering leaders, from the first architecture conversation to the steering committee.
  • Assess a client's architecture, data, engineering practices and security, and lay out what has to change for AI to work at scale.
  • Design agentic systems end to end: orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, guardrails, observability, and the harness that lets AI agents work safely inside a real codebase.
  • Lead the technical side of our AI-native software delivery and modernisation work, from measuring how a team builds today to designing how it will build with AI.
  • Put your name to the estimates that let us sell fixed-price work with confidence.
  • Stay through delivery as the technical authority. Set the architecture, review the work, and make sure what ships matches what was sold.
  • Spot the next technical problem on your accounts and turn it into the next engagement with your strategist.
  • Turn what you build into reference architectures and accelerators the whole company reuses.

What will make you stand out

  • 10+ years in software engineering, including 4+ years as a solution or enterprise architect on client-facing work.
  • You have taken LLM-based and agentic systems past the prototype and into production.
  • Depth in at least one cloud (AWS, Azure or GCP), plus working knowledge of data platforms such as Databricks or Snowflake, integration patterns and application security.
  • You have led engineering teams and you know where projects go wrong.
  • You can run a workshop, write a recommendation an executive can act on, and explain a trade-off in business terms.
  • Strong business English.
  • Technical due diligence for investors, or work in PE-backed companies, is a plus.

You'll recognise yourself here if you've

  • Moved from engineering into roles that ran teams or client relationships, and still review designs and code.
  • Told a CTO that their architecture would not support the plan, and shown them the path that would.
  • Estimated a fixed-price build and delivered it within the estimate.
  • Brought AI coding agents into an engineering team and measured what changed.

Why Symphony

  • You work on agentic systems across many companies and industries, so you see more patterns in a year than most architects see in several.
  • You hold a senior seat in a small advisory team, with a large engineering organisation to build what you design.
  • You stay close to the code and close to the boardroom.

How we'll assess you

A technical interview, including the design of an agentic system, and a short business case study. We are looking for people who are as comfortable with a CFO as with a codebase.

Ready to join us?

We're excited to hear from you. We review applications as they come in and reach out promptly to candidates chosen to move forward. If you have any questions, our Talent Acquisition team is at ta@symphony.is.

About the job

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is looking for a Senior Solutions Architect to join our Commercial Sector Enterprise team in the UAE. You will work directly with some of the region's largest enterprises across Financial Services, Retail & Consumer Goods, Travel & Hospitality, Media & Entertainment, and other industries. These are established AWS customers with clear technology strategies, and you will help them execute and evolve those strategies at scale — accelerating modernization, expanding into new workloads, and unlocking new value with data, analytics, and generative AI.
This is a builder role. You will go into customer environments, understand their hardest problems, and design solutions that work, from whiteboard architecture through proof of concept to production. You will bring deep expertise across cloud infrastructure, modern application development, data and analytics, and increasingly, generative AI and machine learning. You will earn trust by shipping things that matter, not by presenting slides.
You will be part of a collaborative, supportive team that values curiosity, ownership, and impact. We learn from each other, develop each other, and build an environment where every voice is heard.
Key job responsibilities
As a Senior Solutions Architect, you will own the technical relationship with customers end-to-end. This means:
- Partnering with enterprise customers across multiple industries to understand their business objectives, assess their current technology landscape, and architect cloud solutions that deliver real outcomes in cost optimization, operational efficiency, security, reliability, and innovation.
- Translating industry-specific challenges into AWS architectures. Examples include modernizing core banking and payments platforms while meeting regulatory and data residency requirements; building omnichannel commerce, personalization, and supply chain solutions for retailers.
- Building hands-on: prototypes, proofs of concept, reference architectures, and production-ready designs. You lead by doing, not just advising.
- Helping customers adopt and build with generative AI and machine learning: identifying high-value use cases, designing architectures for foundation models and intelligent applications, and guiding teams from experimentation to production.
- Partnering closely with Account Managers to build technical account plans and turn technical wins into lasting customer adoption.
- Acting as the customer's technical advocate inside AWS, translating field insights into clear, prioritized feedback that shapes the roadmap for AWS services and features.
- Creating and sharing technical content that scales your impact: workshops, immersion days, blog posts, code samples, reference architectures, and public talks at meetups, user groups, and conferences.
A day in the life
No two days look the same. You might spend the morning whiteboarding a multi-account landing zone architecture with a bank's platform team, working through security, compliance, and data residency requirements. Then you might pivot to a hands-on working session helping a retail customer's developers integrate a foundation model into their recommendation engine. In the afternoon you might design a data platform for a hospitality group that wants a single view of its guests, review a colleague's architecture proposal, draft a blog post on a pattern you discovered in the field, or hop on a call with an AWS service team to advocate for a feature your customer needs. You balance depth with breadth, strategic thinking with hands-on execution, and customer urgency with long-term technical direction.
About the team
AWS Solutions Architects in MENAT are a tight-knit team of builders and technologists who are passionate about helping customers in one of the world's fastest-growing cloud markets. We work across diverse industries and cultures, which demands adaptability, curiosity, and genuine respect for our customers' contexts. We support each other through knowledge sharing, peer reviews, and a culture of continuous learning. We believe the best ideas come from diverse perspectives, and we actively foster an inclusive environment where everyone can do their best work.
- 10+ years of IT development or implementation/consulting in the software or Internet industries experience
- 10+ years of technology domain areas (e.g., software development, cloud computing, AI/ML, systems engineering, infrastructure, security, networking, data & analytics) experience
- Cloud Technology Certification (such as Solutions Architecture, Cloud Security Professional or Cloud DevOps Engineering)
- Knowledge of AWS services, market segments, customer base and industry verticals
- 4+ years of technology/software sales experience
- Experience working with end user or developer communities
- Experience architecting infrastructure solutions using both Linux/Unix and Windows with specific recommendations on server, load balancing, HA/DR, & storage architectures
- Experience communicating across technical and non-technical audiences, including executive level stakeholders or clients
- Experience migrating or transforming legacy customer solutions to the cloud

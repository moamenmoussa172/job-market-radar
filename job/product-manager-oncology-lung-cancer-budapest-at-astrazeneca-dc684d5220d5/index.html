About the job

Product Manager – Oncology (Lung Cancer) – Budapest

Diverse Minds. Bold Disruptors. Meaningful Impact.

AstraZeneca is a global, science-led, patient-focused biopharmaceutical company that focuses on the discovery, development and commercialization of prescription medicines for some of the world’s most serious disease. But we’re more than one of the world’s leading pharmaceutical companies.
It’s our patients that come first, every decision we make and action we take is centered around how we can better respond to their evolving needs, as well as those of healthcare professionals and partners. It’s why we are shaping patient care for the future, working along the whole experience to give hope to billions of lives across the world.

What you’ll do

AstraZeneca is looking for a passionate Product Manager to manage our key products and play a pivotal role in shaping the future of our Oncology Franchise. In this role, you will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to design and deliver impactful business plans, working alongside an experienced Product Manager and under the guidance of the Franchise Lead. As Product Manager, you will be at the forefront of executing brand strategies—driving marketing initiatives and campaigns, ensuring successful product launches, and securing the best possible positioning for our brands.

This is a fantastic opportunity to join the Lung Cancer Team where we have a strong pipeline, including new indications and new molecules.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and shape the marketing strategy for assigned products, consolidating market insights and competitor intelligence to inform robust business plans.
  • Collaborate across functions—including sales, public relations, and market access—to develop comprehensive plans at the disease, operational, and campaign levels.
  • You will play a direct role in achieving sales and marketing objectives while ensuring compliance with all regulatory requirements
  • Develop and prepare promotional materials and omnichannel campaigns in line with company approval processes, working closely with agencies, x-functional team on supporting Key Account Managers through tailored training initiatives. You will also organize and manage marketing events, while partnering with the x-functional team to evaluate and optimize promotional activities.
  • Monitor product performance regularly, analyzing data and market trends to drive continuous improvement and inform strategic decisions.
  • Build and maintain strong partnerships with Key External Experts and Opinion Leaders in the therapeutic area. In addition, you will prepare sales forecasts in collaboration with the logistics team and engage with international colleagues across AstraZeneca’s global network to align strategies and share best practices.

Your profile

· Minimum 3 years experience in Marketing/Brand/Product Management position, ideally in an innovative pharmaceutical company, ideally with experience in oncology;

· Leadership experience or aspiring Leader and potential of rapid growth

· A blend of creativity and commercial acumen, paired with excellent communication skills

· Strong strategic and analytical thinking to guide impactful business decisions

· The ability to build trusted, collaborative relationships with a wide range of stakeholders

· A proactive, independent, and flexible approach, thriving in dynamic settings

· Advanced project management capabilities to ensure seamless planning, execution, and delivery of initiatives

· A strong sense of accountability and ownership, always seeking high-quality, win–win solutions

· Fluency in English and Hungarian, essential

· Degree qualified

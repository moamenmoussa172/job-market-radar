About the job

Job Title

Project Leader Engineering

Job Description

Vanderlande is a global market leader in airport automation, delivering innovative passenger processing and security solutions to airports worldwide. To support the continued growth of our PAX business, we are looking for a Project Leader Engineering (PLE) to join our PAX Module Deployment Group (MDG).

As Project Leader Engineering, you are responsible for coordinating and leading the multidisciplinary engineering team that delivers PAX solutions across international customer projects. You ensure engineering activities are executed efficiently, risks are proactively managed, and technical solutions meet customer expectations while remaining aligned with Vanderlande standards and product strategy. You act as the key engineering counterpart to Project Managers, Integration Managers, Solution Engineers, Technology teams, and Customer Centre stakeholders. Through your leadership, engineering resources are effectively deployed, technical decisions are coordinated, and projects are delivered safely, on time, and within budget.

This position requires a strong combination of engineering leadership, stakeholder management, project execution skills, and the ability to align multiple disciplines towards a common goal.

Your Responsibilities

Engineering Leadership & Coordination

  • Lead and coordinate multidisciplinary engineering teams across mechanical, electrical, controls, and installation disciplines.
  • Establish clear engineering priorities, deliverables, and responsibilities throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Drive collaboration between Product Development, Module Deployment, Customer Centres, Supply Chain, Service, and external partners.
  • Facilitate technical decision-making and ensure alignment across all engineering disciplines.

Project Engineering Management

  • Set up the engineering team and connect the disciplines with the rest of the engineering organization.
  • Own the engineering scope, planning, budget, risks, and resource allocation for multiple PAX deployment projects.
  • Develop and maintain engineering execution plans, ensuring deliverables are achieved on schedule and within budget.
  • Monitor engineering progress and proactively manage issues, dependencies, and escalation paths.
  • Ensure engineering resources are effectively utilized across multiple simultaneous projects.

Technical Governance

  • Ensure technical solutions comply with customer requirements, Vanderlande standards, safety regulations, and operational needs.
  • Coordinate design reviews, requirement management, change management, and verification activities.
  • Support successful execution of FAT, SAT, commissioning, and operational handover activities.
  • Maintain focus on serviceability, maintainability, and lifecycle performance of deployed solutions.

Stakeholder Management

  • Serve as the primary engineering contact for Project Managers, Sales, Customers, Integration Managers, and Technology stakeholders.
  • Present engineering status, risks, and mitigation plans to project and management teams.
  • Build strong relationships with customers and internal stakeholders to ensure successful project outcomes.
  • Support sales activities, tenders, and customer engagements with engineering expertise.

Continuous Improvement

  • Drive lessons learned and continuous improvement initiatives within the PAX Module Deployment Group.
  • Support industrialization and standardization of PAX deployment processes.
  • Contribute to the development of engineering best practices, methodologies, and team capabilities.

What You Bring

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • 5+ years of experience in engineering project delivery within automation, logistics, airport systems, industrial equipment, or other complex technical environments.
  • Proven experience leading multidisciplinary engineering teams and coordinating technical resources across multiple projects.
  • Strong knowledge of systems integration, project engineering, risk management, and stakeholder management.
  • Experience working in customer-facing international project environments.
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and influencing skills.
  • Excellent English
  • Ability to balance technical, operational, and commercial priorities.
  • Passion for developing teams, driving collaboration, and delivering high-quality engineering solutions.
  • Able and willingness to travel

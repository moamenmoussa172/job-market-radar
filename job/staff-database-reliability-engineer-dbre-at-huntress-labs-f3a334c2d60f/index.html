About the job

What We Do:

Cybercrime is growing, and more businesses are getting hit by threats that used to target only the biggest organizations. That pushes defenders like us to operate at the highest level, and it deepens our need for good people who want to make a meaningful impact.

Founded in 2015 by former NSA cyber operators, Huntress is a remote-first team working to make enterprise-grade cybersecurity accessible to businesses of all sizes. We work closely with security teams and service providers protecting complex environments, often without the time or headcount to handle it all. That’s why we build our technology in-house and back it with a 24/7 human-led Security Operations Center (SOC). As a result, our platform is never disconnected from the experts who manage it, ensuring our customers' protection.

Every day, you can see that commitment in how we stand with our customers and how we show up for each other.

Title: Staff Database Reliability Engineer

Reports to: Engineering Manager

Location: Remote US

Compensation Range: $215,000 to $245,000 base plus bonus and equity

What You’ll Do:

Huntress is growing our SRE Engineering team and is looking for an experienced engineer who is passionate about stability, resilience, and scalability. You’ll be joining a high-performing team responsible for proactively building, monitoring, and implementing the databases that are a core part of the Huntress Security Platform and supporting our millions of installed endpoint agents.

Responsibilities:

  • Continuously review and fine-tune critical PostgreSQL configuration parameters (e.g., shared_buffers, work_mem, autovacuum settings) to maximize resource utilization and match the current workload profile.
  • Forecast future resource needs based on application growth and agent usage trends, and propose scaling solutions.
  • Design, implement, and manage comprehensive monitoring dashboards and alerting systems to track key performance indicators (KPIs) like latency, throughput, connection count, and cache hit ratio.
  • Design, implement, and maintain optimal indexing strategies to support application requirements, and systematically identify and remove redundant or underutilized indexes that degrade write performance.
  • Implement and manage advanced scaling techniques such as database partitioning for large tables and, where necessary, coordinate efforts for database sharding.
  • Partner with SRE team to optimize PostgreSQL deployments in AWS/Azure, ensuring efficient use of cloud-specific storage and networking.
  • Provide technical guidance to application teams on their query/schema/index designs.

What You Bring To The Team:

  • 5+ years experience with scaling, configuration, maintenance and management of PostgreSQL clusters in production environments.
  • Expertise in designing, implementing, and supporting replication (e.g., streaming replication, logical replication), failover mechanisms, and robust backup/recovery strategies
  • Deep understanding of PostgreSQL's core architecture, including the WAL, transaction management, locking mechanisms, and the role of background processes like autovacuum.
  • Extensive hands-on experience managing and maintaining large-scale (Terabyte-level or high-transaction volume) PostgreSQL databases in a live, 24/7 production environment.
  • Experience with observability tools like DataDog, NewRelic, etc. (any tooling is fine)
  • Deep experience of tuning critical performance parameters

Bonus points if you have:

  • Understanding of NoSQL data models (e.g., document schemas for MongoDB, key-value structures for Redis, or column families for Cassandra) to optimize for specific access patterns
  • Understanding of when to use an RDB and when not to
  • Experience with IaC (infrastructure as code)

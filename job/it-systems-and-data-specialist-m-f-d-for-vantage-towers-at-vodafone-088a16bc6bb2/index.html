About the job

At Vantage Towers, we’re on a mission to power Europe’s sustainable digital transformation. As a leading tower company, we’re ushering in an era of technology-driven advances to help connect people, businesses, and internet-enabled devices like never before. We combine the scale, stability and quality of our tower network with the agility, optimism and energy of a start-up.

As a young TowerCo, we have already achieved strong results with a continued focus on accelerating growth and a special emphasis put on sustainability. As part of our team, you’ll work in a dynamic and multicultural environment that embraces open communication, collaboration and teamwork.

If you’re ready to take responsibility and shape the future of telco infrastructure with us, then let’s level up in your career and reach the top, together.

Your Responsibilities

  • TIMS System Administration & Ownership: You act as Vantage Greece’s Single Point of Contact and System Administrator for TIMS, taking primary ownership of data quality, user support, process alignment, training coordination, group-wide best practice sharing, and system adoption in line with local strategy.
  • Operational Bridge & Business Alignment: You serve as the vital link between Local Technology business functions and digital solutions by translating operational requirements across Site Acquisition, Deployment, Energy, Operations, and Field Maintenance into system and reporting capabilities.
  • IT Tool & Project Coordination: You coordinate end-to-end activities and projects aimed at developing, modifying, and transforming technical data, IT tools, databases, and applications to support Infrastructure & Operations needs.
  • Data Analytics & Reporting: You support business requirements by implementing impactful data analytics and reporting solutions, with a focus on Energy and Spend Management needs.
  • Independent & Collaborative Execution: You function autonomously within tight timelines while fostering a team-oriented, positive attitude, working closely on-site with local business owners and collaborating seamlessly across multicultural environments.

Your Profile

  • Educational Background: Degree in Engineering or Information Technology.
  • Professional Experience: Proven experience in software tools administration, support, or implementation teams, including experience coordinating technical activities; a project management certification is considered a plus.
  • Technical Skills: Solid understanding of infrastructure or asset lifecycles (such as in telecommunications, energy, utilities, engineering, or construction) alongside advanced expertise in MS Excel and BI reporting tools; hands-on experience with Sitetracker, SAP, or similar platforms is a strong advantage.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent ability to work independently with minimal supervision, deliver under tight deadlines, and collaborate effectively within multi-cultural teams and face-to-face with local stakeholders.
  • Language Proficiency: Excellent spoken and written communication skills in both Greek and English.

At Vantage Towers we operate in 8 different European countries. This role will be based in Athens.

What we offer – Flexibility & Growth Opportunities

  • Flexible work: We offer a hybrid working model, which allows you to work 3 days per week from our Athens office and 2 days remote for more flexibility and close collaboration with our local business owners.
  • Top Equipment: You receive a new laptop as well as a mobile device and plan. We also offer parking options at the office.
  • Salary & Vacation: A competitive salary package based on your experience and external/internal benchmarking. Additionally, we offer a meal allowance and 2 extra days of annual leave.
  • Learning & Growth: Access to LinkedIn Learning, individual support for your professional and personal development, as well as international career opportunities across our European footprint.
  • Health & Social Care: Profit from options for private healthcare, extra health and life insurances, and pension benefits (contributions depending on your role).
  • Team & Culture: Work in a diverse, multicultural team where you can grow, contribute, and make an impact—committed to the values of Accountability, Respect, Teamwork, and Trust, shaping our organization together as OneVT.

Are you ready to create the future of digital telco infrastructure together with us? Then we are happy to receive your CV. Depending on the role, we generally invite you to two-three rounds of interviews before you receive an offer.

We are committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where everyone can thrive. Applications from all individuals, regardless of background, identity, or ability are more than welcome. If you require any reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process, please contact us so we can support you.

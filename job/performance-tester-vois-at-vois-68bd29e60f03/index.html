About this Role

We are seeking a Performance Tester to define and deliver performance and non-functional testing (NFT) strategies across telecom technology environments, including Cloud, CRM and Middleware domains. The role involves performance test planning, scripting, execution, analysis and reporting, alongside collaboration with product owners, developers, IT Operations and multiple vendors.

You will drive testing efficiency, automation, reusable test assets and continuous improvement while ensuring high-quality test deliverables, effective risk management and adherence to agreed testing standards.

What you’ll do

  • Define and implement the QA vision, performance testing strategy and appropriate testing approaches across Cloud, CRM and Middleware environments.
  • Develop and maintain the NFT test strategy, updating it in line with changes to project risks, schedules and testing processes.
  • Work with business stakeholders to gather requirements and establish performance testing scenarios and scripts.
  • Plan, execute and coordinate performance tests, analyse results and communicate findings to technical and business stakeholders.
  • Collaborate with product owners and developers to define performance-testing requirements and acceptance criteria.
  • Define testing metrics, practices, processes, reporting mechanisms and tools.
  • Drive increased levels of test automation and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Develop reusable performance testing assets, including scripts and input data sets.
  • Work with application teams to investigate and resolve performance issues.
  • Ensure test deliverables meet agreed quality standards and defect management processes.
  • Recommend testing best practices and incorporate lessons learnt into future delivery.
  • Provide input into resource planning and recruitment activities supporting the test roadmap.
  • Mentor team members and support knowledge development across relevant technologies.
  • Develop effort estimates and budgets for performance testing projects.
  • Communicate project risks and issues to Test Leads, Test Managers and relevant stakeholders.
  • Provide regular and ad-hoc testing status reports in line with agreed milestones and service levels.

Who you are

  • Experience testing custom services using load-generation tools such as LoadRunner, JMeter and SoapUI across multiple protocols, including Web HTTP and Siebel.
  • Hands-on experience in performance test scripting and test execution.
  • Experience working within CI/CD environments, cloud infrastructure and cloud-based applications.
  • Ability to analyse non-functional requirements (NFRs) and conduct volumetric analysis.
  • Working knowledge of C, Java, VB macros, SQL and shell scripting for script customisation and test automation.
  • Understanding of Java/JVM architecture, including garbage collection, class loading and thread-dump analysis.
  • Knowledge of database performance analysis, including Oracle and SQL Server.
  • Experience using application monitoring and profiling/APM tools such as HP Diagnostics, CA Wily, Dynatrace or AppDynamics.
  • Ability to analyse server and application metrics and identify performance bottlenecks.
  • Ability to communicate performance test findings clearly in both technical and business language.
  • Knowledge of supporting diagnostic tools such as Fiddler, Firebug, HAR and Lighthouse is advantageous.
  • Understanding of relevant industry standards and certifications, including ISO 9000.
  • Experience with defect management processes and delivery of testing artefacts in accordance with agreed standards.
  • Ability to estimate performance-testing effort and contribute to project budgeting and resource planning.
  • Strong collaboration skills for working with product owners, developers, IT Operations, testing teams and multiple vendors.

About Landmark

Landmark, a Halliburton business line, provides the industry’s most comprehensive suite of digital solutions for exploration, drilling, and production optimization. Its software and data platforms empower customers to model subsurface assets, manage drilling risk, and accelerate decision-making through cloud, AI, and advanced analytics.

About the Role

Use computational methods to turn technical data into models for Landmark’s energy software. Depending on your background and business needs, this individual contributor role may be filled at the Data Scientist or Senior Data Scientist level.

You’ll work with data scientists, software engineers, domain experts, and business stakeholders to understand the problem a model needs to solve and how to judge its results. Under general supervision, your assignment may focus on data engineering, modeling, machine learning, or helping a team deploy a model into software.

Key responsibilities
• Work with domain experts and business stakeholders to define the question, identify available data, and agree on the expected outcome.
• Combine structured and unstructured data from relevant sources; clean and validate it before analysis.
• Maintain data dictionaries so teammates can understand the meaning and limits of each field.
• Explore datasets to identify patterns, anomalies, and quality issues that could affect a model’s results.
• Develop features and build statistical or machine-learning models under guidance. Test their performance and interpret the results.
• Help software engineers integrate tested models into software, cloud, or operational environments.
• Investigate unexpected results with data scientists and domain experts to distinguish data problems from model problems.
• Explain methods, assumptions, and limitations to technical and nontechnical colleagues. Follow established practices for data governance, software development, documentation, and model management.

Qualifications

Required
• Undergraduate degree in a science, technology, engineering, or mathematics (STEM) discipline.
• At least one year of related professional, internship, academic research, or applied project experience.
• Foundational understanding of data preparation, analysis, statistical modeling, machine learning, or related data science methods; ability to obtain, clean, organize, and document data from multiple sources.
• Ability to build models under guidance and explain the basic steps needed to deploy a model.
• Ability to work with technical stakeholders and clearly communicate data methods and results.
• Demonstrated experience using artificial intelligence (AI) tools or techniques to improve the efficiency or quality of work, analysis, education, or technical projects, including validating AI-generated information before relying on it.

Preferred
• Experience applying computational methods to energy-industry problems. Experience with engineering, geoscience, industrial, or other technical datasets is also relevant.
• Master’s degree in a STEM discipline.
• Experience using a programming or analytical language for data science, and exposure to databases, cloud environments, data pipelines, or model deployment.
• Experience contributing to a multidisciplinary product, software, engineering, or research team.
• Research publications or membership in the Society of Exploration Geophysicists (SEG) or the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE).

Candidates who possess qualifications exceeding the minimum job requirements may be considered for higher-level positions based on experience, additional qualifications, demonstrated capabilities, and current business needs. Depending on education, experience, and skill level, candidates may be eligible for roles ranging from Data Scientist to Data Scientist Sr.

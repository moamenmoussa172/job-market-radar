Job Title: Lead Verification Engineer - Embedded Systems

Location: Oujda (Morocco)

Reports to: Sr Design Engineering Manager

Job Overview:

In the Embedded Systems domain, covering both RTL and software verification, you ensure the quality and reliability of our products (soft core IPs) by defining and executing effective verification strategies on microcontrollers (FPGA prototypes) and/or RTL simulators. You drive the technical steering of verification activities within a team of verification engineers in charge of running system-level tests, and you contribute to planning, organizing, and delivering their work. This is a steering role: your hands-on experience must allow you to perform verification tasks yourself when needed, and to train and coach team members. You also oversee internal tools, collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive continuous improvement across the verification process, and regularly report progress, risks, and results to your manager. The role requires strong adaptability and the ability to steer other verification domains, such as embedded C, RTL, FPGA, and server verification, as the team covers a broad verification scope across the product portfolio.

Job Responsibilities:

  • Technically steer and coordinate verification activities, working with the team of verification engineers: planning, execution, review, and delivery of mixed Hardware / Software soft core IPs (Integrated secure elements with embedded CPU, standalone IPs)
  • Define the system verification strategy: review design specifications and write, or supervise the writing of, system test plans aligned with product requirements
  • Drive technical “Packages reviews” and project meetings with Hardware (RTL), Software (C), Project Management, and Integration teams to ensure alignment and technical coherence
  • Supervise the development, execution, and documentation of system test cases (FPGA boards and simulation testbenches) to maximize functional coverage and code coverage, and contribute hands-on whenever needed
  • Steer the analysis of coverage metrics, identify verification and quality gaps, report non-conformities, and ensure corrective actions are tracked through the ticketing system
  • Define the needs for, and oversee the build and maintenance of, internal tools related to system verification (including CI/CD pipelines, traceability tools, packaging scripts, verification scripts, and test frameworks, etc.)
  • Drive the team’s participation in roadmap projects, customer/pre-sales support activities through the ticketing system and meetings
  • Ensure the production and maintenance of internal documentation, including knowledge-sharing content, presentations, and wiki pages
  • Report progress, risks, and results regularly to your manager, through JIRA, timesheets, and weekly reports
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing verification methods, packaging workflows, product quality processes
  • Prepare and facilitate meetings, drive decisions, assign clear owners and deadlines, and follow up on action items.

Job Qualifications:

Required qualifications:

  • Education:
  • Master’s degree (Master 2 or equivalent) in Computer Science, Electronics, or Microelectronics.
  • Experience:
  • Minimum of 5 years of relevant professional experience in embedded systems or system verification, in Embedded C (microcontrollers) or in the VLSI domain (ASIC, FPGA)
  • Proven hands-on experience in low-level system verification and in testing on prototyping platforms (FPGA boards or Microcontrollers)
  • Experience in technical leadership or coordination of engineers (previous team lead experience is a plus), with the technical depth to perform the verification tasks yourself and to coach team members
  • Technical skills:
  • The candidate shall master at least the following technical environment:
  • V-cycle
  • Python
  • Linux
  • C language or VHDL / Verilog / SystemVerilog
  • C emulators or RTL simulation tools (Questasim, Xcelium)
  • Bare-metal programming on microcontroller or FPGA targets
  • Low-level system verification: firmware, drivers, registers, and hardware/software interfaces
  • Test and validation on prototyping platforms (FPGA boards)
  • Microcontroller programming, debugging, and test tools (toolchains, JTAG/SWD debuggers, IDEs, flashing and tracing tools)
  • The candidate shall master technical steering activities:
  • Project plan definition (scope, priorities, risks)
  • Task breakdown (WBS), allocation and delegation
  • Workload estimation (effort, capacity)
  • Gantt planning (schedule, milestones, dependencies)
  • Efficient reporting for progress
  • Process and quality compliance
  • Soft skills expected:
  • Excellent written and oral communication (essential in this role, as your manager and the other team leads work remotely)
  • Rigor, organization, and prioritization
  • Autonomy and proactivity
  • Leadership
  • Coaching and knowledge sharing
  • Adaptability
  • Languages:
  • French: native or bilingual proficiency, both written and spoken
  • English: bilingual level is not required, but you must be comfortable expressing yourself in English, both written and spoken, with colleagues across the company and with customers

Additional Skills/Preferences:

  • Preferred additional skills:
  • Usage of ALM tools for traceability (JAMA, Polarion)
  • Experience in safety-critical or aeronautics projects, such as automotive (ISO 26262, ISO 21434) or aeronautics (DO-254, DO-178)
  • Experience with cryptographic algorithms (symmetric, asymmetric, and hashing)

Additional Information:

  • The candidate will join a team of several people based in the Oujda, Morocco office and will interact regularly with other team members, including their manager located in France
  • Clear, proactive, and regular communication is essential in this position, as your manager and the other team leads are located remotely.
  • This position is a technical leadership role with the potential to evolve toward broader team coordination and leadership responsibilities.

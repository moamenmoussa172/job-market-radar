About the job

Join CAE’s Simulator team in Vienna and work with some of the most advanced simulation technology in the world. With a diverse range of training devices at our Vienna Training Centre, you’ll gain hands-on experience across a variety of simulator systems and equipment, while continuously expanding your technical expertise.

Location: CAE Vienna Training Services | Parzgasse 2, 2320 , Schwechat, Austria

In this role, the Simulator Systems Engineer will be expected to ensure that the simulators and other training devices are kept operational and manage new updates and installs. This position is at the supervisory level of technical staff and the incumbent is expected to exercise judgment and initiative in planning, coordination, and execution of work assignments in line with the existing policies and procedures and by effectively allocating time and resources to meet business and budget objectives in a timely manner.

Key responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  • Debugging avionics and aircraft system deficiencies
  • Tuning subjective observations raised by instructors debugging and tuning QTGs
  • Solving basic software issues and integrate them into software loads.
  • Identify and debug recurring and intermittent issues.
  • Plan and prepare for annual Recurrent Qualifications of in-service devices.
  • Coordinate new simulator projects and upgrades and subsequently provide the project engineer functions to oversee their installation, testing & qualification.
  • Provide regulatory guidance and support simulator requalification’s.
  • Support the shift and work in conjunction with the Team Leader Simulator Technician and provide cover as required during low shift numbers.

Our ideal candidate has:

  • Knowledge of CAE software and applications, including CAELIB, GitHub, Strive Framework, Visual Systems, SIM XXI & 7000XR system architecture, Simfinity, Launchpad, and ITMS.
  • Knowledge of C, C++, and FORTRAN programming languages, as well as scripting languages such as Python.
  • Strong computer literacy, including PC and/or computer mainframe architecture.
  • Ideally 5–8 years of relevant professional experience.
  • A college diploma or associate degree, ideally in a relevant technical discipline.
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office tools and applications.
  • Fluency in English.

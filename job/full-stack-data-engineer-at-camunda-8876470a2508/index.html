About the Role:

Camunda runs on trusted data for people and agents. The Holistic Data Platform (HDP) is how we get there: a data warehouse, consumption layer, and a context layer that agents use to answer questions about pipeline, bookings, ARR and product usage. We are hiring a Data Engineer who builds that platform and also cares whether the numbers are right, whether the business uses them, and whether an agent gives a correct answer on top of them. You will work directly with Corporate Strategy, Finance, Sales, Product, and Marketing leaders.
What you'll be doing:

  • Data Engineering:
  • Agent reliability. Build evals for the agents that sit on HDP. Review their answers, find the wrong ones, and fix the cause in the data, the docs, or the prompt context.
  • Pipelines and platform. Ingestion, orchestration, dbt, CI/CD, and cost control.
  • Machine Learning:
  • Help us figure out where customers are getting stuck and how we can better support them.
  • Analytics Engineering
  • Product Telemetry: Working with our product team to make sense of telemetry and build a better product for our customers.
  • Data quality as a product. Define what “correct” means for each important metric, test for it, monitor it, and tell us fast when it breaks. Reconcile HDP against data sources.
  • Enablement. Make it easy for analysts and business owners to get answers without you.

What you bring:

  • We’re looking for an experienced data engineer with ML/AI experience who’s comfortable working in an AI-native environment
  • Ability and/or willingness to use Camunda’s product
  • 5+ years of Data Engineering or ML experience
  • Strong data modeling skills and several years of experience with dbt.
  • Ownership mindset: we’re a small team and proud of our accomplishments
  • Experience in deploying, operating, and evolving data pipelines in the cloud (batching and streaming)
  • Knowledge of Infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform
  • Strong communication skills, enjoys sharing knowledge and learning collaboratively
  • Passion for clean code, adept at writing automated tests to maintain codebase quality and product excellence

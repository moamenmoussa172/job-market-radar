Associate Business Systems Analyst (Saudi Arabia)

Onsite · Systems / IT Analyst · Posted 19 October 2026

ExcelPMP / PRINCE2

About the job

To process and analyze large datasets, prepare reports, and provide meaningful insights to support business decision-making and operational improvements.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Preferred major: bachelor degree in Management Information Systems (MIS).
  • Bachelor's degree from a reputable university; distance-learning and online degrees are not accepted.
  • 8 - 10 years of relevant work experience.
  • Excellent English communication skills.

Key Responsibilities

  • Process and analyze large datasets using Microsoft Excel and professional PDF tools.
  • Extract information, interpret results, and provide conclusions to support decision-making.
  • Prepare reports and presentations using Microsoft Office applications.
  • Collaborate effectively with stakeholders and cross-functional teams.
  • Support reporting and data analysis activities, preferably within Learning & Development.

Required Skills

  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and professional PDF tools.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
  • Excellent teamwork, attention to detail, and reporting capabilities.

Preferred Certifications

  • HR-related certification.
  • Project Management Professional (PMP).

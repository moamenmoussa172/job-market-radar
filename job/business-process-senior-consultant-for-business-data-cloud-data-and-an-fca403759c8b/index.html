What you´ll do

We are looking for new employees (m/w/d) for the SAP Global Delivery Hub (GDH) who work with us to shape the constant technical progress and the digital transformation of the largest SAP customers in the area of Analytics & Data Management.

In the GDH, we help our customers implement SAP software and ensure that they can successfully plan, implement, integrate, and operate their software, including quality management, problem resolution, and knowledge transfer.

Kickstart your career at SAP service, no matter if your preference is on technical- or functional side of Analytics & Data Management. Within our team of business process consultants, we deal with both topics and this with tools and technologies around the SAP Data Warehouse (BW/4), SAP Datasphere, Extraction-, Transformation-, and Loading (ETL) technologies such as SAP Data Service or SAP Data Intelligence. Aside the technical foundation with the products and tools from above we also deal with Content and Business Content in Analytics incl. the topics of Sustainability-Reporting, Group Reporting inside analytics solutions and help our customers to build in and use Business AI wherever possible in the context of Data Management and Analytics to make their business run better and with state-of-the-art technologies.

At GDH you are working on:

  • Holistic support for SAP implementation, digital transformation and cloud transition projects
  • Creation of business process design, architecture, scalability, security, and operations concepts for SAP solutions
  • Knowledge transfer as part of training and project support from customers and partners
  • Analyze critical customer situations including performance, scalability, custom-code and implementation design

What you bring

  • Preferable a Master-Degree in natural, economics, or engineering sciences, e.g. computer science, business informatics, industrial engineering, mathematics, physics
  • Customer orientation with a strong focus on quality
  • Very good analytical and logical skills for solving complex problems; a structured, reliable, and independent way of working, especially in fast-changing IT landscapes and innovative technologies at our customers.
  • Good programming skills and experience in at least one programming language
  • Very good English language skills, both spoken and written, for communication in constructive and open style within team and customer-facing environments
  • Fluency in any other European Language, especially German, is nice to have.
  • First experiences with SAP applications in analytics and data management from internship, study, thesis is a plus.

Meet your Team

You will work in a highly motivated and international team consisting of both young and experienced colleagues. Our team primarily focuses on a 360-degree assessment our customers’ SAP solutions: This includes functional and technical design including integration of different solutions, performance of critical processes, assessment of implementation and upgrade plans, and monitoring during testing and Go Live.

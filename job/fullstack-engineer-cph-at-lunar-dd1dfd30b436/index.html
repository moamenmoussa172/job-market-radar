About the job

Banking hasn’t changed much in decades. We’re changing that. One product at a time.

Lunar is a Nordic challenger bank building the future of financial life with AI at the core. Our engineers, designers and product people work on problems that actually matter: making money simpler, smarter and more rewarding for a million registered users across the Nordics.

Fully licensed and founded in 2015, with offices in Copenhagen, Aarhus and Stockholm.

We move fast, we build with purpose, and we’re just getting started.

Northstar’s mission is to help more people and businesses become Lunar customers, with less friction and strong protection against financial crime. The squad owns customer due diligence throughout the relationship, from the first application to ongoing reviews and account closure, turning complex requirements into journeys that feel clear and intuitive.

As our new Fullstack Engineer, you’ll join Northstar in Copenhagen, reporting to Vitaliy, the squad’s engineering manager. This is a backend-leaning role: you’ll bring strong backend experience and be comfortable building customer-facing web experiences too, working across services, integrations and our web application to solve problems end to end. You’ll also work closely with our Web Guild in Aarhus, with regular trips there to spend time with the other front-end engineers.

What will you do?

  • Design and deliver reliable backend systems, APIs and integrations that support onboarding and ongoing customer reviews.
  • Build and improve customer-facing web journeys in TypeScript, React and Next.js, working with Design and our in-house Matter design system to make complex requirements feel straightforward.
  • Own solutions from discovery and technical design through to production and ongoing improvement. At Lunar, you build it and you run it.
  • Solve data aggregation and integration challenges across domains, spotting technical risks and dependencies before they become blockers.
  • Work with Product, Design, Data, Financial Crime Prevention and Operations to understand customer needs and the checks behind each journey.
  • Lead technical discussions, make thoughtful architecture trade-offs, and help other engineers grow through feedback and mentoring.
  • Use AI tools responsibly and effectively, and help the team explore how they can improve the way we build and ship.

What are we looking for in you?

  • Backend engineering: A track record of designing and delivering reliable backend systems and integrations.
  • Distributed systems: Experience with distributed systems and event-driven architecture, balancing reliability, performance and complexity.
  • Frontend in production: You’ve built production-grade frontend applications with modern JavaScript or TypeScript frameworks, ideally React and Next.js.
  • APIs: Experience designing and integrating REST APIs and working across the boundary between frontend and backend.
  • Cloud and production ownership: Experience with cloud technologies and taking responsibility for software in production.
  • Judgement: You can navigate ambiguity, spot risks and drive work forward with minimal support.
  • Collaboration: You work well across teams, disciplines and domains, and communicate clearly.
  • AI: A strong interest in AI and familiarity with tools such as OpenAI.
  • Nice to have: Experience with Go, building or maintaining a design system, frontend testing tools such as Vitest and Playwright, type-safe API clients and integration patterns, or accessible journeys involving complex forms or document collection.

What you get.

  • 4 days in the office. We’re a team that builds together, thinks together and moves together. Showing up is part of how we work.
  • A well-equipped workspace. A modern computer and a comfortable desk setup, including two monitors, a mouse and a keyboard. We want you to have the tools you need to do your best work.
  • Pension, health insurance and enhanced parental leave. From your financial future to your family moments, we’ve got you covered.

About the job

Internship Details

Location: London

Duration: 13 months

Start date: 1st July 2027

End date: 31st July 2028

Eligibility: You’re currently an undergraduate in your penultimate year of study, looking for a placement/sandwich year, and graduating in 2029.

Hybrid: 3 days per week in the office, 2 days per week work from home

Annual Pay: £32,000

About the team

Adobe's Digital Strategy Group (DSG) partners with senior leaders at some of Adobe's largest enterprise customers across EMEA. We help them transform customer experiences and explore how AI, including generative and agentic AI, can drive meaningful business outcomes.

Our projects typically run for two to six weeks and combine industry research, digital benchmarking, customer journey mapping, technology assessments and business value modelling to develop clear recommendations.

You'll also learn how Adobe technology - including Adobe Firefly, GenStudio, Adobe Experience Platform, Real-Time CDP and Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker - helps customers create the next generation of digital experiences. Prior Adobe product knowledge isn't required.

What you'll do

As a placement intern, you'll be a working member of the team, contributing to strategy projects with senior customer stakeholders and supporting internal initiatives. You'll research industry trends, develop customer insights and help shape recommendations presented to senior executives.

You'll build consulting and technology skills, learn how leading brands approach digital and AI transformation, and have support from a dedicated manager and the wider EMEA team throughout your placement.

You'll have the opportunity to contribute in one or more of these areas - you don't need experience across all of them:

  • Experience Design: Help map customer journeys and develop ideas for web, app and AI-enabled experiences.
  • Experience Architecture: Explore customers' technology and data environments and how Adobe solutions can help.
  • Analytics and Insights: Analyse performance data and identify opportunities for improvement using a range of tools, including AI-assisted analysis.
  • Business Value Analysis: Research industry metrics and help build ROI models and business cases.
  • AI and Personalisation: Explore how generative and agentic AI can improve customer experiences, content workflows and marketing.

What you need to succeed

  • Genuine interest in digital marketing, customer experience, AI and/or enterprise technology
  • Comfortable using AI tools to research, synthesise and prototype, while applying your own judgement to check and improve the output
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an interest in data and research
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills
  • A collaborative, curious mindset and willingness to learn
  • Self-motivation and a professional approach

Prior Adobe experience is not required.

Helpful, but not essential

  • Experience with web analytics, A/B testing, financial modelling or digital marketing tools
  • Experience using generative AI tools, including through a university, personal or work project
  • Understanding of UX/UI principles or design thinking
  • Fluency in a second European or Middle Eastern language, e.g. French, Spanish, German or Arabic

What to expect from the recruitment process

All applications will be reviewed after the close date, so please don't worry if you don't hear from us straight away.

Our selection process typically consists of the stages as follows.

  • Assessment Day (Role-Dependent) - For certain roles, candidates may be invited to an in-person Assessment Day.

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, United Kingdom.

We’re looking for an MLOps Engineer to join our tech team and help build the future of neuro-contextual advertising at global scale.

Who We Are

At Seedtag, our mission is to transform advertising by proving that effectiveness and user privacy can truly coexist.

As the leading Neuro-Contextual Advertising Company, we combine Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and neuroscience to understand not only what content is about, but how it makes people feel and what they intend to do next.

Our proprietary AI, Liz, enables brands to connect with audiences across the open web and Connected TV without cookies or user tracking. Founded in 2014 by two ex-Googlers, Seedtag has grown to 500+ Seedtaggers in 17 countries, backed by €250M in funding, and operates today as a global ad-tech leader.

If you enjoy solving complex engineering challenges and building AI-driven systems at scale, you’ll feel right at home here.

Your Challenge

As our MLOps Engineer, you will help scale and maintain the infrastructure behind Liz, Seedtag's contextual AI, ensuring reliable, efficient, and low-latency ML model deployment across the open web and CTV.

Your main responsibilities will include:

  • ML Model Deployment: Deploy, maintain, and optimise ML models in production, ensuring performance, reliability, and latency standards.
  • Data Pipelines: Build and improve pipelines for dataset generation, model training, validation, and performance analysis.
  • Model Observability: Implement monitoring, dashboards, drift detection, and alerting to ensure model quality and stability.
  • MLOps Infrastructure: Improve CI/CD pipelines, model versioning, reproducibility, and infrastructure automation.
  • Performance & Cost Optimisation: Identify opportunities to reduce serving costs and improve efficiency without compromising model quality.
  • AI Automation: Develop internal automations and agentic integrations to streamline ML workflows.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Data, Platform, and Backend teams to troubleshoot production issues and improve ML systems.

Tech Stack

You'll work with the technologies powering LizCore, our contextual AI infrastructure, supporting real-time ML model serving across the open web and CTV.

Our stack includes:

  • Languages: Python, Go.
  • Cloud & Infrastructure: GCP, Vertex AI, Kubernetes, Terraform.
  • MLOps & Monitoring: MLflow, Weights & Biases, Evidently, Prometheus, Grafana.
  • Data & Messaging: Kafka, Redis, Bigtable, MongoDB.
  • AI & Machine Learning: NLP, embeddings, ranking models, MCP, agentic workflows.
  • Engineering Practices: CI/CD, model versioning, automated testing, reproducibility.

What You’ll Need to Succeed

  • 2–4 years of experience building, deploying, and maintaining ML systems in production.
  • Strong Python skills, including APIs, asynchronous programming, testing, and clean architecture.
  • Hands-on experience with ML model development and production deployment.
  • Solid understanding of MLOps practices, including CI/CD, model versioning, reproducibility, and monitoring.
  • Experience with ML observability, including performance metrics, dashboards, and alerting.
  • Knowledge of distributed systems, microservices, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Strong troubleshooting skills across models, data pipelines, APIs, and infrastructure.
  • Proactive, curious, and autonomous, with the ability to own technical components end to end.

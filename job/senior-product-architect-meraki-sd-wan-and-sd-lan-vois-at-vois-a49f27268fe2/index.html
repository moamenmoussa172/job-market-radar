About this Role

We are seeking a Meraki SD-WAN Product Architect to help shape, design and deliver next-generation connectivity products for business customers across Vodafone’s global footprint. This role sits within the GSL function and provides the opportunity to work across Product Management, Commercial, Technology, Architecture and Vendor teams to deliver innovative SD-WAN and SD-LAN solutions.

The successful candidate will act as the technical authority for end-to-end product architecture, ensuring solutions meet functional and non-functional requirements while balancing quality, compliance, delivery timelines and cost efficiency. This role is ideal for an individual with deep expertise in Meraki SD-WAN technologies, strong stakeholder engagement skills, and experience leading complex telecommunications solution design activities in a global environment.

What you’ll do

Define and own the end-to-end technical architecture for Meraki SD-WAN and SD-LAN products.Collaborate with Commercial, Product Management and Technology Product Owners to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions.Develop and maintain technical product collateral, including Technical Service Descriptions, High-Level Designs (HLDs), technical options papers and solution documentation.Assess functional and non-functional requirements and provide impact assessments, delivery estimates and cost evaluations.Ensure solutions align with security, privacy, legal, regulatory and operational requirements.Act as the technical design authority throughout the product lifecycle, supporting design reviews, service models and implementation activities.Review supplier technical documentation and provide guidance to delivery and operational teams.Contribute to RFx creation, solution evaluation and vendor selection activities.Drive technical engagement with vendors, influence technical priorities and support continuous product improvement.Support the development and refinement of end-to-end operating and service models.Contribute to product lifecycle management, including enhancement, optimisation and end-of-life planning.Support the overall connectivity services strategy through architecture, integration and innovation initiatives.Simplify complex technical concepts and communicate effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences.Ensure products are delivered on time, within budget and in line with business and customer expectations.

Who you are

  • Demonstrated expertise in Meraki SD-WAN and SD-LAN technologies.
  • Strong understanding of network infrastructure, software-defined networking (SDN), network functions virtualisation (NFV) and associated enabling systems and tools.
  • Experience designing, implementing and launching telecommunications services within global or multinational environments.
  • Proven ability to work collaboratively across geographically distributed teams, stakeholders and external partners.
  • Excellent communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills, including engagement with senior leadership.
  • Experience managing multiple priorities and delivering outcomes within demanding timelines.
  • Strong capability to analyse complex technical and operational challenges and develop practical solutions.
  • Experience supporting product lifecycle activities and service evolution.
  • Understanding of enterprise technology market trends and customer requirements.
  • Relevant degree in Networking, Computer Science or a related field.

Desirable Experience

  • Knowledge of both mobile and fixed network technologies.
  • Experience working within Agile delivery environments.
  • Experience leading telecommunications solution design within multinational service provider organisations.
  • Knowledge of cloud and cloud-native technologies including AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), containers and Kubernetes.
  • Understanding of software development, DevOps practices, APIs and API gateway technologies.
  • Exposure to next-generation orchestration and assurance platforms that support digital transformation initiatives.
  • Relevant cloud certifications or significant cloud implementation experience.
  • English proficiency at B2 level or above.

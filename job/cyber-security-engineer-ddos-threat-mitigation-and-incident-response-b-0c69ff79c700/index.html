About this Role

We are seeking a Cyber Security Engineer to join our DDoS Security team in Bangalore or Pune. This role is focused on real-time cyber threat detection, DDoS attack analysis, incident response, and mitigation activities across customer and organisational infrastructure. The successful candidate will collaborate with global teams, customers, vendors, and internal stakeholders to analyse security alerts, make informed mitigation decisions, optimise defence strategies, and support a resilient security posture. This is a shift-based position requiring flexibility to support 24x7 operations.

What you’ll do

  • Monitor, analyse, and respond to security alerts to identify and mitigate genuine cyber threats and DDoS attacks.
  • Perform incident response activities in line with defined organisational SLAs.
  • Analyse attack patterns, vectors, and traffic behaviour through packet analysis and deep packet inspection.
  • Configure, fine-tune, and optimise security countermeasures and filtering policies to improve mitigation effectiveness.
  • Support mitigation of both volumetric and application-layer DDoS attacks.
  • Provide technical consultation and incident troubleshooting support to customers during cyber security events.
  • Advise customers on threat reduction strategies and security best practices.
  • Onboard new customers onto DDoS protection platforms based on business and technical requirements.
  • Partner with pre-sales teams to provide technical expertise and solution consultations for prospective customers.
  • Manage platform configurations and support configuration lifecycle management activities.
  • Execute security-related changes while maintaining agreed service levels.
  • Contribute to major incident management and problem management activities when required.
  • Work closely with global security teams, internal stakeholders, and vendors to strengthen security capabilities.
  • Identify opportunities for automation and operational efficiency improvements.
  • Create, maintain, and improve technical documentation, operational procedures, and work instructions.

Who you are

•2-7 years of experience in cyber security incident response and threat mitigation.
•Hands-on experience in cyber incident response and real-time threat mitigation.
•Strong understanding of DDoS attack detection, analysis, and mitigation techniques.
•Experience differentiating malicious traffic from legitimate traffic through packet analysis.
•Knowledge of deep packet inspection methodologies.
•Understanding of application-layer attack protection and mitigation strategies.
•Knowledge of NIST Incident Response processes and the MITRE ATT&CK Framework.
•Experience configuring and managing security filters and mitigation controls.
•Familiarity with one or more of the following technologies:
oRadware DefencePro
oRadware DefenceFlow
oArbor DDoS Protection
•Strong analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
•Ability to collaborate effectively in a globally distributed environment.
•Relevant certifications are advantageous, including:
oCertified Ethical Hacker (CEH)
oISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)
oCompTIA Security+

About the Role

We at Salesforce are looking for a Senior Applied Solution Engineer who combines deep technical building skills with the customer empathy and business acumen of a trusted advisor. You'll work at the intersection of engineering and go-to-market — translating customer problems into working solutions, shipping prototypes that become production assets, and shaping our product through field-driven feedback.
This is not a demo-and-slide role. You will use the latest vibe coding tools and applications, architect real systems, and drive technical and value outcomes for our SMB customers in EMEA Central - across Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
Join an international and growing SMB EMEA hub based out of Dublin.

What You'll Do

  • Coordinate and lead the entire solution cycle through close collaboration with other teams - bridge technical and the business needs.
  • Attain quarterly and annual objectives assigned by management.
  • Maintain operational hygiene by documenting contribution
  • Build. Working prototypes, POC ready, and have reference architectures hands-on. Something that the customers can extend and operate — not throwaway demos.
  • Advise. Serve as the technical trusted advisor for strategic accounts. Translate business problems into solution architectures. Earn and keep C-level trust through technical credibility.
  • Architect. Design scalable, multi-tier solutions spanning frontend, backend, data, and AI layers. Make sound trade-offs across performance, security, cost, and time-to-value.
  • Deploy AI. Build and deploy LLM-powered agents, RAG pipelines, evaluation frameworks, and AI-native workflows. Bring production-grade thinking to AI — safety, governance, reliability, and observability.
  • Scale knowledge. Codify working patterns into reusable blueprints, tools, playbooks, and enablement assets. Turn one customer's win into every customer's accelerator.
  • Stay up to date. Participate in all appropriate product, sales, and procedural training and certifications to acquire and maintain the knowledge vital to be effective in the position.
  • Shape product. Feed structured field feedback to Product and Engineering. You see what breaks first — make that insight count.

What You Bring

Required

  • Fluency in German and secondary in English at C2 level or native
  • 5+ years in a customer-facing technical role — Solution Engineer, Solutions Architect, Forward Deployed Engineer, Technical Account Manager, or Software Engineer with customer exposure.
  • Bachelor in Computer Science or equivalent technical accreditations and work experience
  • Builder identity. You've shipped real software. You have technical taste and care about the craft of what you build. You're as comfortable in a codebase as in a customer meeting.
  • Strong coding ability in Python (required) and at least one of JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, or Node.js. You ship prototypes regularly and can work in real codebases, not just notebooks.
  • Full-stack literacy. Comfortable across relational databases (SQL), web technologies (HTML/CSS/JS), REST/GraphQL APIs, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Everyday use of AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, or equivalent). You leverage agentic engineering tools to build faster and iterate rapidly.
  • Executive communication. You can explain complex architectures to a CTO and translate business pain into a technical roadmap for an engineering team.

Preferred

  • AI/LLM fluency. Hands-on experience building with large language models, including prompt engineering, RAG/grounding, agent frameworks, evaluation design, and production safety/governance.
  • Experience with platform-specific development — Apex/LWC (Salesforce), OpenAI API, Claude API, or equivalent platform SDKs
  • Proficiency with data platforms — Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, or Data Cloud
  • Experience with data visualization — Tableau, Looker, Power BI
  • Background in enterprise integrations — pub/sub, CDC, middleware, ETL/ELT pipelines
  • Contributions to open-source projects or published technical content
  • Former technical founder or early-stage startup engineering experience
  • Familiarity with schema modeling, SOQL/SOSL, and CRM data architectures

Attendance Policies

Collaboration-Focused - Hybrid in Office Policy

This role requires a minimum of 3 days per week in our Dublin hub office. In-office days are designed to maximize team collaboration, customer meetings, and cross-functional alignment. Specific in-office days may be adjusted based on customer meeting schedules and team needs.

Customer-Driven Flexibility:

  • Customer meeting schedules may require adjustments to standard in-office days
  • Executive customer engagements may necessitate specific office presence for on-site meetings
  • Proof-of-concept periods may require extended on-site or customer-site presence

Team Collaboration Needs:

  • Weekly team meetings, 1:1s, and planning sessions scheduled on core in-office days
  • Cross-functional collaboration sessions (Product, Engineering) coordinated around office days
  • New hire onboarding includes increased in-office presence during first 90 days (4-5 days/week recommended)

Travel Requirements:

  • Domestic Travel: 20-30% for regional customer meetings and internal events
  • International Travel: 5-15% for global accounts and international conferences
  • Peak Periods: Increased travel during fiscal quarter-end, major product launches, and industry conference season

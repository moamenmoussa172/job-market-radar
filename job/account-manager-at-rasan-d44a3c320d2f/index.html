About the job

Job Description

Position: Account Manager – Leasing
Department: Business Development
Location: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Job Summary

The Account Manager – Leasing is responsible for managing and developing relationships with banks, leasing companies, financing institutions, and corporate clients. The role focuses on driving B2B insurance business, managing key accounts, identifying growth opportunities, and ensuring the successful delivery of insurance solutions for leasing and financing portfolios.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain relationships with banks, leasing companies, financial institutions, and corporate clients.
  • Develop and grow existing B2B insurance accounts while identifying new business opportunities.
  • Act as the main point of contact for assigned clients, ensuring strong relationships and customer satisfaction.
  • Coordinate with insurance providers to deliver competitive motor, fleet, and leasing insurance solutions.
  • Support client acquisition, commercial negotiations, renewals, and contract discussions.
  • Identify cross-selling and upselling opportunities within existing accounts.
  • Work closely with Underwriting, Operations, and Claims teams to ensure smooth service delivery.
  • Monitor account performance, renewal rates, and revenue targets.
  • Prepare business proposals, quotations, and account performance reports.
  • Identify market trends and business opportunities within the banking and leasing sectors.
  • Ensure timely resolution of client issues and maintain long-term business partnerships.

Qualifications And Experience

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Insurance, or a related field.
  • 3–5 years of experience in Account Management, B2B Insurance Sales, or Corporate Relationship Management.
  • Experience in the insurance industry, preferably in B2B or corporate insurance.
  • Experience managing relationships with banks, leasing companies, or financing institutions is highly preferred.
  • Good understanding of motor insurance, fleet insurance, and leasing-related insurance products.
  • Experience in insurance brokerage or insurance companies is preferred.
  • Proven ability to manage corporate accounts and develop long-term client relationships.

Key Skills And Competencies

  • B2B Insurance & Corporate Account Management
  • Banking & Leasing Relationships
  • Key Account Management
  • Client Relationship Management
  • Insurance Products & Solutions
  • Commercial Negotiation
  • Business Development & Revenue Growth
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Problem-Solving & Customer Service

Preferred Candidate Profile

We are looking for an Account Manager with a strong background in B2B insurance and corporate account management, preferably with experience handling banking, leasing, or financing clients.

Candidates from insurance companies or brokerage firms with exposure to financial institutions are highly preferred.

Direct leasing experience is an advantage but not mandatory, provided the candidate has relevant B2B insurance experience and strong relationship management capabilities.

Title: Tactical Marketing Analyst - MTY

Primary Location: SAN PEDRO GARZA GARCÍA

Function: Marketing and Sales (CA)

John Deere is a foremost leader in the ag equipment business, and we continue to put a solid stamp on other industries. For over 185 years, the company’s portfolio of groundbreaking products has grown to include industries related to and outside of agriculture, including Turf, Construction and Forestry and Wind Technologies. The image of John Deere is no longer that of a signature green tractor meandering across a field; it’s been replaced with an image of acceleration, swift progress, and innovative leaping toward the company’s 200th year. The product line alone serves as proof with advances in machine performance, equipment automation, fleet management, and John Deere’s ongoing investment in emerging markets.

Note: All candidates should have their tax situation certificate (Constancia de Siituación Fiscal) up to date with the salary regime to be eligible for the hiring process.

Main Responsabilities

o Develop and execute tactical marketing plans for the construction equipment segment, translating business priorities, market opportunities and commercial objectives into actionable initiatives.

o Analyze market trends, industry performance, competitive dynamics, market share, customer behavior and commercial performance to identify growth opportunities by customer segment, application, product and territory.

o Partner with Sales, Product Marketing and the dealer network to develop market plans and demand generation strategies for construction equipment.

o Develop strategies and initiatives for priority customer segments and applications, including heavy construction, infrastructure, earthmoving, roadbuilding, aggregates and other relevant construction equipment applications.

o Identify opportunities by customer profile, application and territory, translating market intelligence into campaigns, commercial activations and market development initiatives.

o Support go-to-market strategies and product launches, ensuring that positioning, customer segmentation, demand generation and commercial activation are aligned with market needs and business priorities.

o Develop demand generation and lead management initiatives across the commercial funnel, monitoring leads, opportunities, pipeline, conversion and sales performance.

o Partner with the dealer network to strengthen marketing and sales capabilities, improve CRM adoption and increase the effectiveness of local commercial initiatives.

o Monitor the effectiveness of marketing plans and investments through market and commercial KPIs, identifying performance gaps and recommending adjustments or reallocation of efforts.

o Lead cross-functional projects involving Sales, Product Marketing, dealers and other key stakeholders, ensuring alignment between strategy and execution.

What Skills You Need

o Experience in Marketing, Sales, Market Development, Commercial Strategy or related business functions, preferably within B2B capital equipment industries.

o Strong business acumen and ability to understand the commercial dynamics of the construction equipment market.

o Ability to translate market data, customer needs and commercial priorities into tactical plans and demand generation initiatives.

o Strong analytical mindset, with the ability to work with industry data, market share, sales performance, pipeline, conversion and marketing effectiveness indicators.

o Experience developing and executing market plans, commercial campaigns or market development initiatives.

o Ability to understand and address different customer segments, applications, territories and sales channels.

o Strong communication, collaboration and influencing skills, with the ability to work effectively with Sales, dealers and cross-functional stakeholders.

o Ability to lead projects and influence decisions without direct authority.

o Strong customer, market and results orientation.

o Intermediate English

o Portuguese will be considered a plus.

What Makes You Stand Out

o Experience in the construction equipment / yellow iron industry, including excavators, wheel loaders, backhoe loaders, motor graders, dozers or similar heavy equipment.

o Knowledge of key construction equipment customer segments and applications, including heavy construction, infrastructure, earthmoving, roadbuilding and aggregates.

o Experience working with dealer networks, indirect sales channels or business models with strong territorial coverage.

o Understanding of construction equipment commercial dynamics, including customer segmentation, applications, competitive positioning, purchasing cycles and opportunity development.

o Experience with market intelligence, customer segmentation, market potential analysis and identification of growth opportunities.

o Experience with demand generation, lead management, CRM, pipeline development and sales enablement initiatives.

o Experience developing and executing go-to-market strategies, product launches, equipment demonstrations and commercial activation programs.

o Experience using market and commercial data to measure performance, identify opportunities and support business decisions.

o Experience working in regional or multicultural environments across Latin America.

o Demonstrated ability to connect strategy with execution and mobilize multiple stakeholders to convert market opportunities into commercial results.

Education

Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Engineering, Economics or a related field, or equivalent relevant work experience.

This position will be posted for a minimum of 10 business days and unposted when a qualified pool of candidates is achieved.

Candidates must have the necessary work permits to work in the country.

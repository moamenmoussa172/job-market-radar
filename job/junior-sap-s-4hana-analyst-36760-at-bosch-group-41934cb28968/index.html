JUNIOR SAP S/4HANA ANALYST - 36760

Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

Hybrid · Junior · ERP / CRM Consultant · Posted 25 November 2026

ABAPAgile / ScrumSAP S/4HANATesting practices (TDD)

About the job

  • Support SAP S/4HANA transformation projects, participating in solution design, development, integration, and implementation activities.
  • Assist in gathering and understanding business requirements, contributing to their translation into technical specifications and system solutions.
  • Develop and support custom solutions, enhancements, and interfaces within the SAP S/4HANA environment, following technical standards and best practices.
  • Participate in integration testing and validation activities for implemented solutions.
  • Support troubleshooting, debugging, and continuous improvement activities.
  • Collaborate with functional and technical teams to ensure system integration and the quality of delivered solutions.
  • Work with multidisciplinary and international teams in a collaborative and agile environment.
  • Support activities related to the Planning to Fulfill process.
  • Bachelor's degree completed or in progress in Engineering, Information Technology, Computer Science, or related fields.
  • Basic to intermediate knowledge of SAP S/4HANA architecture, applications, and technical capabilities.
  • At least 1 year of professional experience in SAP development or technical consulting.
  • Knowledge of ABAP, including basic development, debugging, and troubleshooting skills.
  • Familiarity with developing custom solutions, enhancements, and interfaces in SAP environments.
  • Knowledge of business requirements gathering and technical specification documentation.
  • Advanced or fluent English proficiency, with the ability to communicate and collaborate daily with international teams.
  • Strong analytical and logical thinking skills, with problem-solving abilities.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with different teams and stakeholders.

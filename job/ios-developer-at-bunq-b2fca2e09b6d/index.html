About the job

At bunq, we're not just building a banking app; we're reshaping how people around the world experience financial freedom. As a (Senior) iOS Developer, you’ll be instrumental in crafting and maintaining cutting-edge mobile applications that elevate our users' banking journeys. Collaborate with our dynamic team to deliver a seamless, innovative, and user-friendly experience on the iOS platform.

Take Ownership

  • Develop and maintain scalable iOS architecture, ensuring efficiency and a seamless user experience using Swift and iOS SDK.
  • Integrate and optimize REST APIs, managing authentication, error handling, and data flow for smooth performance.
  • Enhance CI/CD and testing automation, maintaining fast release cycles and ensuring robust app quality.
  • Develop new features and fix bugs proactively, keeping performance sharp and delivering a seamless experience for our user.
  • Proficiency in Swift, Objective-C, and SwiftUI to build intuitive, high-performance interfaces.
  • Experience in designing scalable and secure network layers using REST APIs & authentication.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and automated testing for implementing continuous testing strategies.
  • Expertise in state management and UI frameworks to create fluid, responsive applications using UIKit or SwiftUI.
  • Fluency in English to effectively communicate in a global team and ensure collaboration and clarity across all project stages.

All new hires are subject to Pre-employment Screening (PES), which includes checks conducted by our third-party partner, Zinc. This is part of our commitment to a secure and trustworthy workplace

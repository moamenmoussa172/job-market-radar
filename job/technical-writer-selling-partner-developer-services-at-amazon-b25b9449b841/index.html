About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Ireland.

Amazon Selling Partner Developer Services is seeking a customer-obsessed, detail-oriented technical writer to join a team that is redefining how developer content is created, maintained, and scaled. In this role, you will have the opportunity to make a meaningful difference by working with a talented technical team to advocate for the needs of developers, architect scalable content solutions, and deliver high-quality technical documentation for Amazon Stores.
The ideal candidate has excellent communication skills and a technical background that enables them to collaborate effectively with Software Engineers, Solutions Architects, Product Managers, and Technical Program Managers. As AI tooling reshapes how API documentation is created, reviewed, and maintained, we are looking for a writer who is curious about this shift and eager to develop skills in AI-augmented content workflows.
Key job responsibilities
- Develop technical documentation through close collaboration with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) across engineering, product, and solutions architecture
- Flexibly alternate between content creation, planning, and editing tasks across a variety of technical content solutions
- Author content in accordance with established style guides and identify improvements to those standards based on evolving best practices and AI capabilities
- Manage a weekly work queue of documentation requests, prioritizing based on business impact and developer experience outcomes
- Proactively identify and implement process improvements and collaborating with the team on implementation
- Test and use AI-assisted content workflows to improve documentation efficiency and quality, and suggest tool improvements
- Advocate for improvements to the customer experience and documentation quality standards
- Identify and appropriately escalate issues that require leadership or cross-team attention
About the team
The vision of the Selling Partner Developer Services Documentation team is to externalize quality, global API data products with speed and scale via a self-service experience supported by comprehensive, accessible multi-language documentation to simplify building, migrating to, or integrating with Amazon APIs.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, English, Technical Writing, or a related field
- Experience designing and delivering developer-oriented documentation
- Experience using tools to create, manage, and publish documentation
- Experience managing work and priorities through a ticketing system
- Experience designing, planning, and delivering content projects
- Experience working with engineers and subject matter experts to document complex technical topics
- Experience working with Agile project management methodologies
- Experience working with Git or an equivalent distributed version control system
- Experience planning, managing, and delivering customer-focused documentation across multiple teams
- Experience as a technical editor or writer of technical documentation working with style guides
- Experience with programming languages such as Python, Java, C++
- Subject-matter expertise in API documentation, developer tools, or related technical domains
- Familiarity with OpenAPI and Swagger specifications
- Experience with prompt engineering and generative AI platforms
- Demonstrated ability to evaluate, adopt, and advocate for AI tooling in a documentation or content workflow context

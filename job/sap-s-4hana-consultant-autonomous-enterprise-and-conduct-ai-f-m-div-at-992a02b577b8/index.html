About the job

Your contribution to something big:

Be part of shaping the future of Bosch Home Comfort! As an SAP S/4HANA Consultant – Autonomous Enterprise & Conduct.AI, you will drive the adoption of AI-powered solutions and help transform the way our teams collaborate, innovate, and deliver business value.

Your responsibilities include:

  • Drive AI implementation and adoption: Lead the implementation and adoption of SAP Joule Work, Joule Autonomous Assistants & Agents, Joule Studio 2.0, and SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) capabilities.
  • Identify and implement AI use cases: Identify, design, and implement high-value AI use cases that enhance productivity, collaboration, and delivery effectiveness across the SAP S/4HANA environment.
  • Design AI-powered solutions: Develop and configure solutions leveraging SAP Joule and SAP AI capabilities, translating emerging technologies into practical business applications.
  • Drive Conduct.AI adoption: Lead the rollout, adoption, and continuous development of Conduct.AI across Bosch Home Comfort, establishing scalable enablement concepts, governance frameworks, and adoption strategies.
  • Enable business transformation: Support the integration of AI capabilities into end-to-end business processes, accelerating Bosch Home Comfort's transformation towards an Autonomous Enterprise.
  • Measure business impact: Define and monitor KPIs, measurement frameworks, and management reporting to evaluate AI adoption, productivity improvements, business value realization, and ROI.
  • Establish best practices: Develop reusable implementation patterns, best practices, and governance approaches to ensure sustainable and scalable AI adoption.
  • Collaborate across teams: Work closely with Bosch Home Comfort, CPT, RBW, and SAP expert communities to exchange knowledge, align initiatives, and accelerate AI adoption across the organization.

What distinguishes you:

Education: Degree in Business Administration, Business Informatics, Process Management, Computer Science, or a related field, or an equivalent qualification.

Experience

  • Experience in international transformation, rollout, change management, or enablement initiatives within SAP Consulting, Enterprise Applications, Digital Transformation, or IT Solution Delivery.
  • Practical experience working with SAP S/4HANA and related business processes.
  • Experience applying Generative AI and Prompt Engineering to improve business processes and ways of working.
  • Experience in consulting, stakeholder management, and facilitating collaboration across international teams.

Know-how

  • Good understanding of SAP S/4HANA, particularly SAP S/4HANA Sales.
  • Knowledge of the SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) ecosystem, including SAP Joule Work, Joule Autonomous Assistants & Agents, Joule Studio 2.0, and SAP Integration Suite.
  • Understanding of Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, API integrations, and Enterprise AI adoption and change management principles.
  • Practical experience with AI and automation tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Copilot, n8n, or comparable platforms.
  • Ability to translate technological innovations into scalable business solutions with measurable impact.

Working Style and Methods

  • Structured, analytical, and solution-oriented approach to complex challenges.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into practical technological solutions.
  • Proactive mindset with the ability to drive initiatives independently.
  • Strong collaboration and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in an international and cross-functional environment.

Personality

  • Enthusiastic about emerging technologies, Artificial Intelligence, and digital transformation.
  • Curious and eager to continuously learn and explore new technological possibilities.
  • Strong communication and facilitation skills, with the ability to engage stakeholders at different organizational levels.
  • Collaborative mindset and passion for driving innovation and creating business value.

Languages: Fluent English skills.

Additional Information

  • Willingness to travel internationally, approximately 20%.

About the role:

We design, build and operate the digital and technology capabilities that power Vodafone's next-generation products and services. We provide the cloud-native engineering foundations, reusable platform capabilities, APIs, integrations and secure digital services that enable rapid, scalable delivery across multiple customer journeys, propositions and markets.

The team provides trusted data foundations, reusable platform capabilities, data products, insight and governed AI that enable scalable customer propositions and reliable operations across markets and partners. Our teams use AI-assisted engineering practices to improve delivery speed and quality while maintaining clear human accountability, security, privacy, traceability and production discipline.
The Data Platform Lead owns the reusable, secure and reliable Data Platform as an internal product. The role leads the team providing ingestion, storage, processing, orchestration, observability and self-service capabilities used by the Data Products & Insights Team and other authorised consumers.

The role is accountable for platform outcomes and people leadership while implementing the cross-domain architecture and guardrails agreed with the Lead Data Architect.

Given the responsibilities outlined above, the role includes:

Owning the Data Platform vision, roadmap, services and consumer experience.

Leading Senior Data Engineers and any assigned partner capacity.

Ensuring reliable, secure, cost-efficient and self-service platform operation.

Driving knowledge transfer and sustainable Vodafone ownership.

What you’ll do

  • Own the Data Platform vision, roadmap, service catalogue and backlog, prioritised against consumer needs, operational risk and customer outcomes.
  • Lead design, build and operation of reusable ingestion, storage, processing, orchestration, serving and self-service capabilities on the approved cloud platform.
  • Own reliability, observability, incident response, capacity, resilience, disaster recovery, performance and cost optimisation for the platform.
  • Ensure data contracts, quality gates, metadata, lineage, privacy, residency, access and multi-tenant controls are implemented, evidenced and operated.
  • Lead people, suppliers and knowledge transfer, setting clear objectives, engineering standards, succession and accountability for sustainable Vodafone operation.

Who you are

Core competencies, knowledge and experience:

  • Platform Product Leadership - Proven accountability for a cloud data platform, roadmap, internal consumers and measurable service outcomes.
  • Production Data Engineering - Strong understanding of Google Cloud Platform or equivalent, Python, SQL, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, testing and observability.
  • Operational Accountability - Experience with service-level objectives, incident management, resilience, recovery, capacity and cost control.
  • Security and Governance - Experience implementing privacy, residency, lineage, access and multi-tenant controls in regulated environments.
  • People and Supplier Leadership - Ability to manage senior engineers, workforce capability, delivery partners and technical risk.

Must have technical / professional qualifications:

  • Significant experience leading cloud data-platform or data-engineering teams in production environments.
  • Hands-on understanding of BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Dataproc, Cloud Storage, Cloud Composer and Identity and Access Management, or equivalent.
  • Demonstrable accountability for highly available data services and operational support.
  • Experience implementing secure self-service platform capabilities and reusable engineering patterns.
  • Strong people leadership, supplier management and cost-management capability.

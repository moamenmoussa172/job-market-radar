About this role

We are seeking a Technical Architect – Data Engineering & Ab Initio to join the Software Engineering – D&A team in Pune, India. The role focuses on architecting and designing enterprise ETL and data solutions using Ab Initio and Teradata, alongside data architecture, data management and data analysis.

The role requires hands-on expertise across the Ab Initio product suite, Teradata BTEQ performance tuning and utilities, data warehousing and data modelling, with an understanding of data governance practices and cloud technologies, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

The successful candidate will contribute across impact assessment, application scoping, planning, development and delivery while supporting process, resource and tooling improvements.

What you’ll do

  • Perform ETL technical design, data architecture, data management and data analysis.
  • Architect and design ETL solutions using Ab Initio, applying appropriate data governance practices.
  • Work with Ab Initio GDE and associated products and frameworks, including Control Center, Express>IT, Data Quality Management, Metadata Hub and Conduct>IT.
  • Work with Teradata BTEQ, including performance tuning, Teradata Client Utilities and backup/migration tools such as NetVault, ARCMAIN, DSA and Data Mover.
  • Take accountability for impact assessments, application scoping, planning, development and delivery while ensuring defined quality standards are met.
  • Contribute to process adoption, resource optimisation and effective use of tools to improve efficiency and quality.

Who you are

  • 10–14 years of overall professional experience, with 5–10 years of relevant experience.
  • Hands-on experience designing and delivering ETL solutions using the Ab Initio suite of products and Teradata.
  • Strong understanding of Teradata BTEQ performance tuning and Teradata utilities.
  • Knowledge of data warehousing, data modelling and data profiling.
  • Exposure to data modelling tools such as SAP PowerDesigner or erwin Data Modeler is preferred.
  • A good understanding of cloud technologies, with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) preferred.
  • Experience with Ab Initio products and frameworks such as GDE, Control Center, Express>IT, Data Quality Management, Metadata Hub and Conduct>IT.
  • Experience with relevant Teradata and data backup/movement utilities, including NetVault, ARCMAIN, DSA and Data Mover.
  • A B.E./B.Tech, BCA/MCA, or BSc/MSc in Computer Science or a relevant discipline.

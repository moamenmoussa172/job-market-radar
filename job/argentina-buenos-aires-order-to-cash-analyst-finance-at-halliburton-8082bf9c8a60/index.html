We are looking for the right people — people who want to innovate, achieve, grow and lead. Experience the challenges, rewards and opportunity of working for one of the world’s largest providers of products and services to the global energy industry.

Job Duties

Under direct supervision, supports Order to Cash (OTC) activities with a focus on the review and processing of supporting documentation for accurate revenue and cost recognition. Performs account analysis and reconciliations, including P&L and Balance Sheet accounts, and assists in the preparation of financial reports. Reviews contractual terms to identify relevant accounting and financial implications. Uses SAP, Microsoft Excel, and reporting tools to process, analyze, reconcile, and report financial information. Works closely with other team members to ensure data accuracy, completeness, and compliance with established processes and requirements. The role requires strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to effectively manage financial and accounting information.

Qualifications

  • Completed university degree in Accounting (Public Accountant).
  • Experience with account analysis and reconciliations, including P&L and Balance Sheet accounts.
  • Experience working with SAP in an accounting or financial environment.
  • Strong analytical skills and ability to interpret contractual terms.
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience with reporting tools.
  • Experience preparing financial reports.
  • English proficiency at B2 level or above, with the ability to communicate effectively in a professional environment.
  • Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and focus on data accuracy.
  • Position based in Buenos Aires (CABA).

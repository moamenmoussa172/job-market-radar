About the job

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

Your boarding pass will include…

Several conferences, including:

  • Meet An Executive
  • Women Taking Flight

Learning more about Bombardier, including:

  • Bombardier Products conference
  • Visits of the Bombardier sites
  • Bombardier Academy of Learning

Many social/networking opportunities, including:

  • Volunteering
  • Networking for Success
  • 5 à 7, Potluck, and much more!

What are your contributions to the team?

• Participate in the design, development, and documentation of web applications.
• Contribute to the enhancement and maintenance of existing applications (web and desktop).
• Collaborate with users and team members to understand business needs and help develop appropriate solutions.
• Assist in the extraction, import, processing, and documentation of data.
• Support the team in the development, testing, and deployment of new tools and features.
• Contribute to the team's continuous improvement initiatives.
• Participate in providing technical support to system users and help resolve application issues.
• Perform testing activities and help ensure the quality of developed solutions.

How to thrive in this role?

• You are currently enrolled in a college or university program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
• You have a strong interest in web application development and maintenance.
• You possess basic knowledge of web development technologies:
o Front-end: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, or similar technologies.
o Back-end: C#, .NET or .NET Core, and relational databases (SQL Server or equivalent).
• You are eager to learn new programming languages, tools, and technologies.
• You have strong analytical skills and a focus on delivering high-quality results.
• You are recognized as a team player who is curious, open to learning, and enjoys collaborating with others.
• You demonstrate initiative, autonomy, and attention to detail in the execution of your tasks.
• Knowledge of SharePoint, Microsoft Power Platform, or Azure services is considered an asset.
• You are able to communicate effectively in both French and English, verbally and in writing.

​
Boarding Information:

  • Location: Administrative Centre (CA)
  • Duration: 8 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.

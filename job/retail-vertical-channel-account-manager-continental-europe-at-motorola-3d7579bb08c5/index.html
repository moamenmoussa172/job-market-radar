Job Description

We are seeking a high-performing commercial specialist to join our Video Security & Access Control enterprise sales team. In this role, you will work in a people-first culture and find a platform to pursue your passion and potential as we expand our market footprint across Continental Europe. You will take strategic ownership of driving corporate-level adoption of our video security and physical access control ecosystem within tier-1 hypermarket, supermarket, and discount retail networks.

This high-visibility position empowers you to innovate what's next by standardizing advanced physical security architectures across massive multi-site store footprints and logistics hubs. Operating as an individual contributor, you will act as a collaborative partner to both internal regional teams and third-party technology providers, helping to shape commercial frameworks that can deliver on the promise of a safer world.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Account Engagement & Retail Penetration

  • Establish strategic business initiatives with a targeted portfolio of tier-1 European grocery and discount retail accounts to drive enterprise market adoption
  • Lead complex commercial procurement cycles and establish corporate framework agreements across multinational operating footprints
  • Standardize the deployment of our integrated video security and access control portfolio across multi-site retail store networks and critical logistics centers

Ecosystem Integration & Strategic Collaboration

  • Partner with third-party mass-retail technology providers, including point-of-sale infrastructure and checkout analytics, to help to provide unified software integrations
  • Collaborate with regional field resources and authorized channel partners to coordinate multi-country solution rollouts
  • Guide strategic partners to expand retail market reach and shape overarching commercial frameworks across Continental Europe

Commercial Strategy & Pipeline Operations

  • Oversee large-scale sales pipeline management, accurate demand forecasting, and deployment milestone tracking utilizing Salesforce.com
  • Guide channel partners on navigating complex enterprise procurement structures and optimizing multi-national forecasting models
  • Analyze high-volume retail operational trends to identify technology requirements and synthesize feedback for Product Management regarding cloud scalability and regional data privacy standards


Basic Requirements

  • Solid professional enterprise sales, business development, or vertical channel management experience in the European commercial retail sector
  • Legal authorization to work in the United Kingdom, Spain, or France indefinitely is required depending on the chosen employment hub; employer work permit sponsorship is not available for this position

Job Requirements

Technical Expertise

  • Video Security Infrastructure to articulate the business value of AI-assisted video analytics and scalable enterprise surveillance architectures
  • Access Control Solutions to integrate physical asset security software across commercial retail properties
  • Ecosystem Software Integration to align third-party retail systems and point-of-sale platforms with centralized command capabilities
  • Multilingual Capability with an excellent command of English and professional working proficiency in at least one additional European language (French, Spanish, Italian, or German) to facilitate multinational commercial negotiations

Professional Experience

  • Multinational Procurement Management to navigate commercial agreements and compliance mapping for enterprise retail conglomerates
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration to mobilize internal technical resources, authorized channel partners, and enterprise stakeholders across international territories
  • Data Privacy Compliance to analyze customer operational requirements against regional European cloud and data security standards

Preferred Qualifications (Optional)

  • Established executive relationships with major European retail conglomerates such as Carrefour, Ahold Delhaize, or Schwarz Gruppe
  • Proven track record of executing strategic accounts or channel partner frameworks across Western Europe

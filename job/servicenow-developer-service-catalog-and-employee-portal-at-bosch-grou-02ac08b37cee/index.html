About the job

Roles & Responsibilities :
Job Description

Develop and enhance ServiceNow Service Catalog and employee-facing portal experiences, with reliable request fulfillment using Flow Designer and enterprise APIs. Own end-to-end technical delivery from requirements through production support, focusing on usability, maintainability, role-based access, security, and performance.

Key Responsibilities

Catalog and fulfillment development

  • Catalog Configuration: Configure and develop catalog items, record producers, variables/variable sets, catalog UI policies, client scripts, approvals and request fulfillment workflows.
  • Workflow Automation: Build reusable Flow Designer/Workflow Studio flows, subflows and custom actions; implement exception handling, notifications, auditability and safe retries.
  • Self-Service Journeys: Translate service offerings and acceptance criteria into user-friendly self-service journeys with clear fulfillment and status tracking.

Portal and user-interface engineering

  • Portal Development: Develop and maintain Service Portal widgets or Employee Center portal experiences using JavaScript, HTML and CSS, following the technology pattern used in each deployment.
  • User Experience: Create accessible, responsive forms and pages; troubleshoot client/server interactions and optimize load and search performance.
  • Portal Integration & Security: Integrate portal components with the ServiceNow data model and catalog APIs without bypassing platform security.

Integration, quality and production support

  • Enterprise APIs: Build REST and Scripted REST integrations, IntegrationHub spokes/actions, and reusable workflow components; implement end-to-end orchestration through Workflow Studio, flows, subflows, approvals, and automated fulfillment.
  • Platform Security & Delivery: Apply ServiceNow server-side JavaScript, Glide APIs, access controls (ACLs) and role-based testing; use source control and change/release processes.
  • Testing & Production Support: Write automated or repeatable tests (including ATF where suitable), investigate production defects and maintain technical/runbook documentation.

Qualifications

Experience & Qualifications:

  • Experience: 7–12 years overall; substantial hands-on ServiceNow catalog and portal development experience.
  • Service Catalog: Strong ServiceNow Service Catalog development and flow-based request fulfillment experience.
  • Portal & Employee Center: Practical knowledge of Enterprise Hub / Employee Center and/or Service Portal customization.
  • ITSM & Request Management: Strong understanding of ServiceNow ITSM processes, with hands-on experience designing scalable request and fulfillment workflows.
  • JavaScript & UI Technologies: Hands-on JavaScript, HTML, CSS, ServiceNow scripting and debugging.
  • APIs & Security: Experience with REST APIs, JSON, authentication, ACLs and enterprise integration patterns.
  • Delivery & Operations: Experience delivering changes through peer review, testing, deployment and L2/L3 defect resolution.

Additional / Preferred Experience:

  • AI-Enabled Self-Service: Experience with AI-enabled search, Now Assist or conversational catalog requests.
  • UI Builder & Agile: Exposure to UI Builder, Experience Builder and Agile/DevOps ways of working.
  • ITOM/CMDB: Exposure to Discovery, Service Mapping, MID Server, AIOps, and CMDB integrations.
  • ITAM: Knowledge of HAM/SAM and asset fulfillment workflows.

Certifications:

  • ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) – mandatory.
  • ServiceNow Certified Application Developer (CAD) – preferred.
  • ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) - ITSM – preferred.

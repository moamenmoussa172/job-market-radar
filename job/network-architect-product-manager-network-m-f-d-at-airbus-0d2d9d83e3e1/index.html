About the job

Job Description:

A vacancy for a Network Architect / Product Manager Network has arisen within Airbus Defence and Space.

You will be part of the international IT Network Products Team.

We deliver the expertise, technology and equipment for Airbus Space and Aerospace products – We make it fly!

Our digital department is transforming and evolving but we need even more talented people who can make a real difference to how we support, enable and deliver a diverse range of technological solutions and services to our customers. As part of our transformation the new operating model is oriented around multi-functional, agile teams working with the business and developing our digital capabilities. What does this mean for you? A role in DIGITAL is an opportunity for you to grow and to develop your career in a progressive and innovative environment. Our people the teams they work in and the values embedded in DIGITAL, are driving this change and you could be part of that too.

Are you passionate for digital topics and interested in working in a diverse and international environment? Then join us as a Network Architect / Product Manager Network !

As a Network Architect / Product Manager Network you will be responsible for technology development, integration testing and deployment, it’s lifecycle, as well developing technical solutions.

You will work closely with Project- / Program managers, Service Owners, external service providers, Airbus Security and other IT teams to keep the current service infrastructure running, improve it by innovative and new capabilities and to manage quickly and professionally operational issues.

You will need to be a creative thinker able to propose and support new ways to increase the overall service quality. Other responsibilities will include the support of digitalization and innovation projects/programs.

Your location

Our site is just a stone's throw away from Munich, the beautiful capital of Bavaria. Are you into sports and other outdoor activities? The Alps and Lake Starnberg are within an hour’s reach, offering a multitude of recreational options.

Our Benefits for You

  • Fair Compensation & Extras: Attractive remuneration and individual additional benefits, such as company pension schemes, mobility offers (e.g., bike leasing), or corporate discounts with partner companies in accordance with our current guidelines.
  • Time for You (and Your Loved Ones): 30 days of annual leave based on our collective agreement (35-hour week); work-life balance through flexible working hours (flextime), mobile working, part-time options, job sharing, and sabbatical opportunities.
  • Growth Made Easy: Excellent professional development opportunities and international, group-wide career perspectives.
  • Feel Good at Work: On-site company doctor; comprehensive health programs (sports courses, preventive care, etc.), canteen and cafeteria, and local childcare facilities at selected locations.
  • Diversity Connects: Work in a diverse environment with more than 140 nationalities, where exchange, mutual support, and inclusion are part of our DNA.

Your duties and responsibilities

  • Design robust network and network‑security solutions that meet the requirements of Airbus Defence & Space’s business units in an international setting.
  • Advise internal customers on our network standards and possible solutions.
  • Coordinate with neighbouring IT‑infrastructure areas to develop comprehensive solutions.
  • Plan the technical implementation and take part in the relevant projects.
  • Transition solutions and new standards into routine operation and provide ongoing support.
  • Support product management in developing new standards and maintaining existing products.
  • Estimate effort and costs and ensure appropriate internal charging.
  • Collaborate with central functions such as Governance, Finance and Procurement.
  • Potentially take part in on‑call duty.

Desired skills and qualifications

  • IT‑related education or degree, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Experience in network and network‑security roles within a large organisation, an IT service provider or a regulated environment.
  • Basic knowledge of information‑security frameworks, for example the German BSI IT‑Grundschutz.
  • Project‑management knowledge.
  • Experience with one or more of the following technology areas: campus networks, data‑centre networks, wide‑area networks, network security.
  • ITIL knowledge and experience in service management.
  • Experience with one or more of the following vendors’ products: Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, Efficient IP (certifications are a plus).
  • Familiarity with Windows Server, Linux Server, virtualisation, storage and identity‑access management is an advantage.
  • Fluent German and English (business‑level proficiency).

Not a 100% match? No worries! Airbus supports your personal growth with customized development solutions.

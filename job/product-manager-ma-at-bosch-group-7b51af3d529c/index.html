About the job

The Product Manager (PM) is responsible for

  • Define and implement product strategy, incl. product positioning, product roadmap, long term target
  • Identifying and prioritizing business opportunities and drive business growth
  • Proactive Product Life Cycle Management (PLCM, product PI and PO)
  • Work closely together with OE business unit and sales team to ensure healthy business model
  • Project management for new product launch
  • Follow up evolving governmental guidelines, technology trend, market dynamics, pricing environment and competition landscape, working closely with regional organizations to respond strategically

Professional:

  • Economic or engineering academic education, 985/ 211 school required
  • Job experience (3-5 years) within automotive industry, gasoline system/powertrain system product area is preferred
  • Good data analysis and presentation skills
  • Digital experience
  • Fluent English language skills

Personality

  • Flexibility and creativity
  • Be determined and energetic
  • Entrepreneurial thinking
  • Strong personal initiative
  • Team player
  • Work collaboratively with different functions and cross-regions.
  • Communicate proactively and effectively at all levels, both externally and internally

By choice, we are committed to a diverse workforce and are an equal opportunity employer. Robert Bosch welcomes and encourages applications from people with disabilities. Accommodations are available on request for candidates taking part in the selection process.

About the job

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

What are your contributions to the team?

Support Systems Engineering team in investigating technical issues & governance;
Support engineering deliverables & governance by managing database and following up on outstanding actions;
Support Bombardier Operational Excellence System by tracking Key Performance Indicators (KPI) governance;
Facilitate meetings, create presentation packages, issue meeting minutes in a timely manner;
Develop good working relationships with various departments within

How to thrive in this role?

You are a student in aerospace, mechanical, electrical, computer or software engineering;
You have good oral and technical communication skills in English (French is an asset);
You are proficient in MS Office suite, especially Office365; PowerBI and Excel;
You possess working knowledge of CATIA V5; MATLAB; Python or SciLab scripts;
You possess good interpersonal skills in order to work well with the team and other groups;
You are able to work well independently and demonstrate drive and initiative to achieve the goals established;
You have previous work experience or have completed an internship;
Occassional support outside of normal working hours.

Boarding Information:

  • Location: Global 7500/8000
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. The base salary for this position is expected to range from CAD $17.95 to CAD $31.00 per hour. The applicable base salary will be determined based on various criteria, at Bombardier’s sole discretion, such as the candidate’s skills, experience and qualifications. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.

About the job

We are expanding our Guidewire practice and looking for experienced professionals across PolicyCenter, BillingCenter, ClaimCenter and Integration domains.

Whether your expertise is in business analysis, solution design, architecture or implementation, you will have an opportunity to work on large-scale transformation programmes for leading insurance carriers across Europe.

WHO WE ARE:

Accenture is a leading global professional services company that helps the world’s leading businesses, governments and other organizations build their digital core, optimize their operations, accelerate revenue growth and enhance citizen services.

THE WORK:

  • Participate in large-scale Guidewire transformation programmes for leading insurance carriers across Europe.
  • Support business and technology teams in the delivery of Guidewire PolicyCenter, BillingCenter, ClaimCenter and Integration solutions.
  • Gather, analyze and document business requirements and translate them into solution designs.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, architects, developers and testing teams throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Drive business process transformation and modernization initiatives within core insurance systems.

Flexible: We are delivering our work mostly remotely but knowing that way of working is also based on traveling and serving advisory, typically working closely with our Clients in their offices, willingness and ability to work at client’s locations mostly across Europe, but also other locations, for short or long term is still required.

HERE’S WHAT YOU’LL NEED:

  • Minimum bachelor’s degree
  • Experience in Guidewire PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter or BillingCenter solution
  • Or other experience related to IT systems implementation including systems integration, data migration, digital solutions, AI, Automation
  • Experience as Business Analyst or Tester.
  • Strong communicator with fluency in English.
  • Digitally savvy, forward-thinking, and growth-driven, with good time management skills and ability to manage multiple commitments/projects simultaneously.
  • Natural leader: proactive in skill enhancement and easily establishes relationships with clients and colleagues.

Research indicates that some candidates, especially the most diverse ones, may hesitate to apply for positions if they don't meet all requirements. If you believe you possess the necessary skills, even if not meeting every requirement, we wholeheartedly encourage you to submit your application.

BONUS POINTS IF YOU ARE AND HAVE:

  • Knowledge and certification in any Insurance Platform like Guidewire, Salesforce, EIS etc.
  • Knowledge of German, French, Italian, Spanish or other European languages.

WHAT WE OFFER:

  • Employment contract (Umowa o pracę)
  • A wide training package (soft, technical, and language training offer, access to the e-learning platforms, Gallup test, GenAI training, possibility of co-financing courses, and certification).
  • Paid employee referral program.
  • Private medical care, life insurance.
  • Individual support of a People Lead and a specific path of professional development, as well as the possibility of a session with a Coach.
  • Accenture employees eligible for the Employee share purchase plan automatically become eligible for quarterly dividends if they own company shares.
  • Access to the WorkSmile platform (possibility of using a wide range of products and services, including the Multisport card).

All internals must have manager’s approval to transfer.

Responsibilities

  • Develop analytical views with both S4/HANA CDS views and HANA calculation views
  • Evaluate source data systems for variances, anomalies, and efficiency
  • Create reusable data models (S4/HANA CDS and HANA calculated views) that scale across the enterprise
  • Optimize logical and metadata models
  • Performance tuning of long running queries/procedures
  • Collaborate with data owners and data scientists to understand relationships in enterprise source data and help create an analytical reporting roadmap

Desired Qualifications

  • Strong understanding of HANA and S/4HANA architecture, other business intelligence suites (COGNOS, Informatica, etc)
  • Understanding of Attribute Based Access Control
  • Ability to analyze business needs and translate into long term solution data models
  • Understand relational, dimensional, cube based relationships
  • Understand conceptual, physical, logical data models
  • Experience with enterprise scale relational databases
  • Experience with building queries in SAP HANA studio and SAP ADT using BW/4HANA Objects.
  • Experience with reporting tools – Business Objects, Microsoft PowerBI, Tableau, Jupyter Notebooks
  • Experience with developing SAP S4/HANA CDS Views.
  • Experience with large data volume, performance tuning
  • Experience in ABAP programming skills and SQL Skills.
  • Experience with SDLC methodologies: Agile, Waterfall
  • Experience in Smart Data Access and Smart data Integration will be an added advantage.
  • Exposure to Cloud tools/integration will be an added advantage.

Education Requirements:

  • Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Information Systems or related field.
  • 7-10 years of experience
  • Professional certification(s) desired.

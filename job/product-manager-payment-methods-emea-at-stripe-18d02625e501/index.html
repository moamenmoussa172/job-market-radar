Product Manager, Payment Methods, EMEA

Stripe · London, United Kingdom

Onsite · Product Manager / Owner · Posted 20 November 2026

About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team

The payments landscape is changing rapidly and our team is at the forefront of this change. Depending on where you live, you may be used to paying with a credit card or bank direct debit (United States), iDEAL (Netherlands) or UPI (India), amongst hundreds of other global payment methods. It turns out how you pay is increasingly dependent on where you are and what business you're buying from.
The Core Payments team makes it easy for businesses global and domestic to offer familiar local payment experiences wherever their customers choose to pay. We build bank payment methods and local payment experiences, alongside the capabilities that make these methods easier to discover, integrate, and use across Stripe. Responsible for building Stripe's payment platform that allows us to build and distribute payment methods at scale. The team works across product, engineering, design, data science, partnerships, risk, and go-to-market teams to help businesses offer locally relevant payment experiences at global scale.

What you’ll do

As a Product Manager, you’ll own relationships with some of the world’s largest payment providers and build products that drive billions in payment volume growth. You’ll help merchants offer locally relevant payment methods online and in person, improve payment performance, and expand acceptance across borders and into use cases such as recurring and agentic payments.
You’ll work directly with merchants to understand their payment needs, then partner with partnership managers and Stripe’s and financial partners’ product and engineering teams to define joint roadmaps and ship solutions. You’ll prioritize investments that grow adoption and improve approval rates while balancing revenue, margins, cost, and risk.

Responsibilities

  • Own the product vision and multi-quarter roadmap for a portfolio of strategic payment methods, using merchant feedback, partner capabilities, payment data, and market trends to set priorities.
  • Work directly with merchants and financial partners to identify gaps in payment workflows and translate them into product requirements.
  • Build joint roadmaps with partnership managers and partners’ product and engineering teams, securing alignment on investments and resolving competing priorities and dependencies.
  • Make the case for new investments and work with engineering teams to ship improvements, balancing adoption, approval rates, revenue, margins, cost, and risk.
  • Grow payment method adoption with go-to-market teams by expanding cross-border acceptance and supporting use cases such as recurring and agentic payments.
  • Partner with Risk, Legal, and Compliance to implement controls and enforcement strategies that protect Stripe and financial partners while maximizing approval rates and minimizing losses.
  • Work with product marketing and go-to-market teams to turn roadmap priorities into clear launch and adoption plans.
  • Become the product expert for your payment partners, providing insights and support to help them succeed on Stripe.

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 6+ years of experience in building, launching, and scaling products that deliver meaningful user and business results.
  • Experience independently defining product strategy and executing multi-quarter roadmaps, bringing clarity and direction to ambiguous problems.
  • Strong cross-functional leadership, with the ability to influence product outcome, navigate competing interests, and drive decisions across teams and external partners.
  • Experience working on products that require aligning multiple stakeholders, developing joint roadmaps, and navigating competing priorities between internal goals and external partner objectives.
  • Demonstrated experience partnering closely with engineers, designers, and external partners to build products and complex systems at scale.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with a knack for precise and concise articulation of user problems
  • Strong analytical capabilities with experience defining and tracking success metrics, running experiments (e.g., holdback tests), and using data to measure product impact on revenue, conversion, and margins.
  • Deep user empathy and sound product judgment, with experience translating complex user needs into intuitive experiences and technical capabilities.
  • Ability to thrive in a dynamic and fast-paced environment with significant autonomy and responsibility.
  • Strong business acumen and comfort with complex ecosystem and platform-level problems.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with bank payments, online, local and instore payments methods with deep understanding of the conversion, costs, and risk trade-offs.
  • Experience working with financial partners navigating risk, legal, and compliance requirements and users from various geographies and cultures.
  • Experience building platform products and driving product adoption across multiple markets.

Lead Systems Engineering delivery across the Dreadnought programme

At Babcock, we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together. If you join us, you can play your part as a Principal Systems Engineer supporting the Dreadnought submarine programme.

The role

As a Principal Systems Engineer, you’ll lead the team responsible for requirements and acceptance, configuration management and key customer deliverables across the Dreadnought Weapon Handling and Launch System (WHLS) and Submerged Signal Ejector (SSE).

Working across engineering disciplines and with the customer, you’ll ensure that complex engineering information and evidence are controlled, integrated, and accepted. This is an opportunity for an experienced Principal Systems Engineer to take on a key leadership role, reduce programme technical risk, improve delivery performance and develop the capability of the Systems Engineering team

Day-to-day, you’ll be required to:

  • Lead, manage and develop the Systems Team, creating a supportive and accountable environment that enables engineers to perform effectively and reduce programme technical risk.
  • Plan and prioritise the team’s work to meet contractual commitments, balancing programme needs with available capacity, individual experience, and development requirements.
  • Lead requirements management, verification, and acceptance across WHLS and SSE, ensuring requirements, compliance statements and supporting evidence are maintained in IBM DOORS and delivered through the Requirements Acceptance Matrix.
  • Lead configuration management across the Dreadnought programme, ensuring the Configuration Management Plan and associated processes are maintained and overseeing the production of Configuration Status Records and Certificates of Design.
  • Manage the integration and delivery of top-level customer documentation, including associated Document Review Forms, and drive outstanding comments and complex technical discussions to closure.
  • Collaborate with design teams, Subject Matter Experts, Chief Engineers, Project Management, Quality and the customer to resolve requirements, acceptance, configuration and deviation issues.

This role is full time, 37 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements. The expected balance of onsite and home working will be discussed at interview.

Essential experience of the Principal Systems Engineer

  • Substantial Systems Engineering experience across the engineering lifecycle on a large, complex or high-integrity programme.
  • Experience leading and developing engineers, managing performance, and creating an accountable and supportive team environment.
  • Experience of requirements management, verification, and acceptance, including the review and control of requirements evidence using a tool such as IBM DOORS.
  • Experience working directly with customers and multidisciplinary stakeholders to resolve complex technical, contractual and acceptance issues.
  • Experience planning and prioritising team activities to deliver agreed outcomes to time, cost and quality expectations.

Qualifications for the Principal Systems Engineer

  • An engineering degree, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Membership of an appropriate professional engineering institution.
  • Chartered Engineer status, or evidence of working towards professional registration, would be advantageous.

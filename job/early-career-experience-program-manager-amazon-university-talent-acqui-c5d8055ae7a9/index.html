About the job

Within Amazon University Talent Acquisition (AUTA), the Experience (AUTA-X) team provides a best-in-class internship experience (onboarding, early career development, and offboarding) to engage and retain top diverse early career talent. AUTA-X is seeking a Program Manager to support the day-to-day operations across the internship lifecycle globally. This role will be instrumental in shaping the future of our high touch programming agenda, driving operational excellence, and ensuring the success of our early talent initiatives.
This opportunity calls for a candidate willing to work effectively at scale, thrive in ambiguous situations, and deliver multiple projects while contributing to a collaborative team environment. They possess strong communication skills and ability to manage team priorities against tight timelines. A proven track record of effective prioritization and taking initiative is important. Candidates should be comfortable with ambiguity and possess strong customer obsession. The position will leverage tools, analytics, and communications to improve the education, engagement, and experience of field and corporate interns.
Key job responsibilities
- Single-threaded intern experience operations oversight for one or more global specialized programming tracks, aligned to AUTA priorities.
- Design and scale tailored operational excellence mechanisms to enable intern experience, manager experience, and conversion goals.
- Partner with other Program Managers to force-multiply specialized programming innovations.
- Lead escalation deep dives through to resolution for specialized intern, manager, and mentor populations.
- Monitor performance metrics and survey responses to surface early indicators, then identify and execute path-to-green resolutions across the end-to-end internship lifecycle.
- Propose and implement innovative solutions to enhance experience and program outcomes.
- Gain operational efficiencies through AI-powered chatbots, machine learning (ML), and AI-assisted content generation while maintaining program quality.
- Stay abreast of industry best practices and emerging trends in university programs.
- Conduct in-depth analysis of program metrics and provide actionable insights to stakeholders.
- Build and maintain strong relationships with internal and external teams, including but not limited to Recruiting Business Partners, Recruiters, AUTA teams, Field Operations PxT partners, and Global Talent Solutions.
- Provide customer and programming insights for regular rhythm-of-business mechanisms, including but not limited to 2x2s, quarterly business reviews (QBRs), and reflection documents.
A day in the life
This role requires the ability to work across multiple time zones, including AMER, EMEA, APJC, and LATAM, which may result in starting your day earlier or ending your working day later on a regular basis to support the global team.
About the team
At Amazon University Talent Acquisition (AUTA), we're revolutionizing how the world's most innovative company attracts and develops its next generation of leaders. As Amazon's early career pipeline, we manage global university recruiting across internships, full-time roles, and apprenticeships through leading-edge AI technology and strategic partnerships with top universities worldwide. Our team operates at massive scale while maintaining a steady focus on finding the best talent, driving innovation in candidate experience through personalized journeys, and identifying emerging talent in fields like Generative AI and Robotics that will shape the world's future.

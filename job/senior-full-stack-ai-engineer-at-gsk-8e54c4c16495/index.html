About the job

Help us prove, build and scale AI solutions that deliver real value

GSK’s Enterprise AI team turns high-value AI opportunities into working solutions that can scale across the business.

As a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), you will work directly with business teams to understand complex problems and rapidly determine whether AI can solve them. You will take ideas from problem definition to working proof of concept (PoC), proof of value (PoV) or minimum viable product (MVP), often within days or weeks.

This is a hands-on engineering role. You will combine strong software engineering skills with generative AI, agent-based approaches and AI-assisted development. You will work closely with product owners, engineering leads and business stakeholders to build working software, validate value and determine which solutions should progress to scale.

In this role, you will

  • Lead high-priority AI use cases from problem framing through to working prototypes and MVPs, providing evidence-based recommendations on whether they should progress.
  • Build rapidly using AI coding assistants, agent-based workflows and modern software engineering practices, applying your own judgement to validate and improve AI-generated outputs.
  • Embed with business teams to understand real workflows, data and user needs, translating complex challenges into practical AI solutions.
  • Design and test generative AI solutions using approaches such as AI agents, multi-agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), prompt engineering and evaluation.
  • Prepare successful solutions for scale, providing working code, architecture decisions, identified risks and clear handover to engineering teams.
  • Build responsible AI and appropriate governance into solutions from the outset, while contributing reusable agents, templates, engineering patterns and reference architectures for future use.

Basic Qualifications

We are looking for professionals with these required skills to achieve our goals:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Significant experience in full-stack software engineering, AI/ML engineering or a related engineering discipline, including experience delivering production systems.
  • Demonstrated experience taking complex ideas from initial problem definition to working software at pace.
  • Strong programming skills in Python, alongside experience with a modern language such as TypeScript/JavaScript, Go or equivalent.
  • Hands-on experience building generative AI solutions using areas such as AI agents, multi-agent workflows, RAG, prompt engineering and evaluation.
  • Experience using AI-assisted software development tools or coding agents as part of the development lifecycle.

Preferred Qualifications

If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Experience in a Forward Deployed Engineer, Customer Engineer, Solutions Architect or similarly hands-on customer-facing engineering role.
  • Experience building AI solutions in a regulated environment such as pharmaceuticals, healthcare or financial services.
  • Familiarity with relevant governance or regulatory considerations, such as GxP, GDPR or SOX.
  • Knowledge of model fine-tuning approaches and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.
  • Experience designing and orchestrating agent-based or multi-agent systems, including determining when human oversight is required.
  • A track record of creating reusable engineering patterns, frameworks, tooling or reference architectures that improve delivery across multiple teams.

Why join Enterprise AI?

Enterprise AI brings engineering expertise together with scientific, clinical and business teams to turn advances in artificial intelligence into practical solutions.

This is an opportunity to work at the point where emerging AI technology meets real business problems. You will have the autonomy to explore ideas quickly, challenge assumptions and build working solutions, while helping teams adopt new AI-augmented ways of working.

Success means more than delivering a prototype. You will help determine where AI can create genuine value, establish the foundations for solutions to scale and leave behind reusable engineering capabilities that make future delivery faster and stronger. #AGTech #hybrid

Your Responsibilities

As a Engineer, Software for John Deere Research & Development Center located in East Moline, IL, you will:

  • Develop Gen5 display software using C++ and Qt/QML in an embedded Linux environment
  • Design, develop, and maintain Model-Based Software Design (MBSD) solutions
    utilizing MATLAB/Simulink for automation and embedded functionalities based on
    established architecture and design specifications.
  • Implement features and resolve defects following defined requirements
  • Participate in code reviews, testing, and software integration
  • Collaborate with systems, PV&V, and senior engineers

VISA Sponsorship is NOT available for this position

What Skills You Need

  • Experience with C or C++, or MATLAB/Simulink/Stateflow.
  • Exposure to embedded Linux or real-time systems
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills

What Makes You Stand Out

  • Experience with embedded Linux, real-time systems, or distributed systems
  • Proficiency in Qt/QML, Python, or MATLAB/Simulink
  • Knowledge of version control systems such as Git or equivalent
  • Internship or EDP experience in embedded software development

Education

Ideally you will have a degree or equivalent related work experience in the following:

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or related field

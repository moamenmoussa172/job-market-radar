About the job

By combining international reach with US expertise, we build diverse and high-performing teams that are equipped to thrive in today’s competitive marketplace.

  • A solid and innovative company with a strong market presence

This position will lead the development and evolution of the Finance D&A data foundation. This role is responsible for defining the target-state data architecture, establishing scalable and governed data products, and enabling reliable access to trusted financial and operational data.

The Director will partner across Finance, Actuarial, Product, Claims, Distribution, Technology, and Enterprise Data to translate business priorities into sustainable data capabilities supporting conversion, financial reporting, forecasting, profitability analysis, planning, and advanced analytics. This leader will combine strong insurance industry knowledge, technical expertise, and strategic leadership to establish the data and technology foundation required to scale Finance D&A.

Key Responsibilities

•Define and execute the target-state data architecture and technology roadmap for Finance D&A.

•Lead the design and delivery of scalable data pipelines, curated data products, dimensional models, and semantic layers.

•Establish consistent definitions, business rules, hierarchies, and data models for critical Finance and insurance metrics.

•Establish Finance data governance standards covering data ownership, quality, lineage, metadata, access, controls, and appropriate use.

•Implement development and engineering standards for documentation, testing, deployment, reconciliation, monitoring, and production support.

•Improve integration of quotes, policy, premium, claims and financial data to enable trusted Finance analytics.

•Reduce reliance on manual data preparation, fragmented reporting solutions, and duplicated business logic.

•Partner with Enterprise Data and Technology teams to improve Finance access to enterprise data sources, platforms, and capabilities.

•Ensure solutions comply with enterprise architecture, security, privacy, governance, and financial control requirements.

•Lead complex, cross-functional data initiatives from architecture and planning through implementation and adoption.

•Build and lead a high-performing team across data architecture, engineering, modeling, governance, and technology enablement.

•Communicate architecture strategy, priorities, delivery progress, risks, and trade-offs to senior and executive leadership.

Requirements

  • 15+ years of experience in data architecture, data engineering, analytics engineering, business intelligence, data governance, or related disciplines.
  • Full English Fluency
  • Significant professional experience within the insurance industry required. (MUST)
  • Strong knowledge of insurance data and business processes, including policy, premium, claims, losses, pricing, actuarial, distribution, and financial data. Data transformation projects, multiple experiences
  • 10+ years of leadership experience managing data, architecture, engineering, analytics technology, or related teams.

Technical & Business Skills

  • Strong knowledge of modern data architecture, data engineering, integration, transformation, modeling, and analytical consumption patterns.
  • Strong understanding of insurance financial and operational data, including relationships across policies, premiums, exposures, claims, losses, expenses, profitability, and financial reporting.
  • Datalake experience (creating new data lake projects) MUST
  • Extensive experience in data management (ETLs, Data Pipelines) MUST
  • Experience with modern cloud data and analytics technologies such as Snowflake, (PLUS)
  • Azure, Power BI, SQL, Python, R, Git, and pipeline orchestration tools. Programming experience (MUST)
  • Strong understanding of data governance, quality, metadata, lineage, privacy, security, access management, and financial controls.
  • Transform Data, get it into good shape

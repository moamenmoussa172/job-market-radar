THE ROLE

At Everpure, we are empowering global enterprises to protect, govern, and scale petabyte-to-exabyte unstructured data estates. As Principal Product Manager for Data Protection, you will define the multi-year strategy and architecture for our enterprise file and object protection portfolio, solving critical scale challenges around rapid backup, snapshotting, and disaster recovery. Operating with wide latitude and strategic autonomy, you will partner closely with Systems Engineering, Solutions Architecture, and executive leadership to ensure modern data-intensive organizations can safeguard their most critical unstructured data assets reliably across distributed environments.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Pioneer Unstructured Data Protection Strategy: Define and execute the multi-year product roadmap for large-scale unstructured data protection, delivering enterprise capabilities across distributed snapshots, rapid recovery, asynchronous replication, and automated retention for petabyte-scale file and object systems.
  • Drive Portfolio Lifecycle & Operational Benchmarks: Steer data protection solutions from inception through global delivery, establishing rigorous Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), Recovery Point Objectives (RPO), and storage-efficiency benchmarks that deliver measurable business value and operational simplicity to customers.
  • Align Cross-Functional Delivery: Partner closely with Core Systems Engineering, User Experience, and Product Marketing to translate complex enterprise data workflows into clear technical requirements, ensuring seamless feature delivery and consistent platform performance.
  • Cultivate Strategic Ecosystem & Customer Alliances: Collaborate directly with enterprise infrastructure architects, strategic alliance partners, and customer advisory councils, serving as an authoritative product voice to shape technical positioning and incorporate real-world customer insights into future product generations.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Unstructured Data Protection Expertise: Comprehensive technical grounding in enterprise unstructured data storage (file and object protocols), modern backup and recovery workflows, snapshot and replication architectures, and lifecycle retention management.
  • Product Strategy & Portfolio Lifecycle: Demonstrated capability in guiding complex, high-scale enterprise infrastructure products from initial concept to commercial adoption, balancing immediate customer commitments with long-term platform investments.
  • Collaborative Leadership & Influence: Proven track record of building consensus, aligning multi-functional technical teams, and engaging executive stakeholders around a shared product vision and strategic direction.
  • Customer-Centric Technical Communication: Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to articulate technical architectures, distill complex trade-offs, and represent product priorities effectively to global customers and industry partners.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, CA office in compliance with Everpure’s policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

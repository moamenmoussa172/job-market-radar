Job Description

We are seeking a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to ensure the reliability, performance, security, and operational excellence of critical Product Supply Finance solutions. The role combines hands-on engineering with service management, translating business expectations into measurable SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs while strengthening observability, automation, incident response, and continuous improvement. Working across Azure, data platforms, reporting tools, and enterprise integrations, the engineer partners with Product, Platform, Data, Security, Software Engineering, Operations, and supplier teams to deliver supportable products and reduce operational toil.

Responsibilities:

  • Incident Management and Reliability: Monitor alerts, lead incident response, meet service objectives, conduct root-cause analyses and postmortems, and drive corrective and preventive actions.
  • Observability and Performance: Implement metrics, logging, diagnostics, and alerting that improve early issue detection, system performance, availability, and reliability.
  • Automation and Continuous Improvement: Automate repetitive tasks and operational runbooks, standardize support processes, and use engineering and AI-enabled automation to reduce toil and prevent recurring incidents.
  • Service and Operational Readiness: Define and manage SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, monitoring, support models, release readiness, change controls, documentation, and Day 1 operational processes for new and evolving products.
  • Infrastructure and Platform Operations: Administer cloud and platform infrastructure, support transformations and migrations, manage capacity and performance, and improve security, scalability, and cost efficiency.
  • Security and Access Management: Protect highly restricted Finance data through secure authentication, authorization, encryption, role-based access, compliance processes, and controlled change execution.
  • Technical Guidance and Collaboration: Advise Product, Platform, Data, Security, and Software Engineering teams on supportability, resilience, scalability, and efficiency; represent Operations in pilots and product roadmaps.

Job Qualifications

Soft skills:

  • Strong communication and collaboration across business, product, platform, data, security, engineering, operations, and supplier teams.
  • Structured problem solving and sound judgment during incidents and complex operational issues.
  • Ability to prioritize incident response, operational work, engineering improvements, and product readiness.
  • Attention to detail in documentation, incident records, access controls, and change execution.

Business skills:

  • Service-oriented mindset focused on reliability, availability, security, customer experience, and measurable business outcomes.
  • Ability to define, interpret, and improve SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, operational KPIs, and support models.
  • Understanding of the software development lifecycle, Product Management, Agile/Scrum, DevOps, and production support.
  • Knowledge of ITIL practices, including Service Level, Release, Event, Incident, Problem, Capacity, and Performance Management.
  • Ability to assess reliability, performance, security, and cost drivers and recommend improvements across application and infrastructure layers.

Technical skills:

  • Automation and Scripting: Proficiency in Python, Bash, or PowerShell, plus SQL for diagnostics and data management.
  • Cloud and Infrastructure: Hands-on experience with cloud platforms, preferably Azure, and Linux/Unix system administration.
  • IT Operations: Experience with ServiceNow or comparable ticketing platforms, task management, change management, and incident-response methods.
  • Monitoring and Observability: Experience with system metrics, diagnostics, logging, Prometheus, Grafana, or comparable tools.
  • Networking and Databases: Understanding of network components and protocols, database management, and incident-prevention techniques.
  • Engineering Practices: Familiarity with Visual Studio Code, GitHub Copilot, Git, CI/CD, configuration management, and infrastructure as code.
  • Security: Knowledge of authentication, authorization, encryption, role-based access, and controls for highly restricted data.
  • Communication: Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Preferred Experience

  • Experience with Power Query, DAX, TMDL, field parameters, calculation groups, semantic models, deployment, and enterprise security.
  • Terraform or comparable configuration and infrastructure automation tools.
  • Performance and cost optimization across code, applications, and cloud infrastructure.
  • SRE, test-driven development, chaos engineering, and AI-enabled operations practices.
  • ITIL 4 Foundation, SRE Fundamentals or Practitioner, Azure, or comparable certifications.
  • Finance domain knowledge and experience supporting critical products in a large international organization.

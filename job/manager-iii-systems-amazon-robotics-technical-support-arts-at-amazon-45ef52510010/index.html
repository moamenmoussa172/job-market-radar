About the job

Do you thrive at the intersection of technology, innovation, and people leadership? Are you passionate about solving complex technical challenges, developing high-performing teams, and driving operational excellence on a global scale? If so, you’ll feel right at home at Amazon Robotics.
At Amazon Robotics, we are a dynamic team of passionate engineers and innovators applying state-of-the-art robotics and software technologies to solve real-world challenges. Every day, we push the boundaries of innovation to transform customer experiences worldwide. We don’t just build technology—we invent new possibilities. With the right tools, resources, and support, you’ll have the opportunity to lead, innovate, and make a meaningful impact.
Our team is a global organization of talented engineers responsible for maintaining the availability, reliability, and performance of Amazon’s robotic systems, services, applications, and platforms. We are seeking an experienced, technically proficient, and customer-obsessed manager to lead, develop, and scale our technical operations in this region.
Your team is responsible for frontline technical support, incident management, complex troubleshooting, and the resolution of safety, reliability, and performance issues. You will drive operational excellence by strengthening incident response mechanisms, improving standard operating procedures, establishing meaningful performance metrics, and identifying opportunities for automation and scalability.
As a manager, you will combine strong people leadership with technical depth, developing your engineers while guiding them through complex technical investigations and business-critical incidents. You will be expected to dive deep into technical problems, challenge assumptions, drive structured troubleshooting, and make sound decisions under pressure. You will lead incident response efforts, coordinate cross-functional teams, and communicate technical challenges, business impact, and recovery strategies effectively to stakeholders at all levels. In partnership with global Software Development and Engineering teams, you will drive root cause analysis, permanent corrective actions, and continuous improvements to system reliability and resilience.
You will be expected to operate independently, navigate ambiguity, and balance competing priorities in a complex, fast-paced, and high-throughput environment. Your ability to combine technical judgment, effective communication, and strong managerial ownership will be critical to your team’s success.
If you are not sure that every qualification on the list below describes you exactly, we’d still love to hear from you! At Amazon, we value people with diverse backgrounds, experiences, and skill sets. If you are passionate about technology, developing people, and solving complex challenges that make an impact on a global scale, we encourage you to apply!
Key job responsibilities
- Lead, recruit, interview, hire, coach, and develop a high-performing team of technical engineers.
- Ensure direct reports adhere to company policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements while meeting established operational, quality, and productivity targets.
- Empower, motivate, and develop direct reports through regular 1:1 meetings, actionable feedback, clear performance expectations, and career development plans.
- Foster a positive, inclusive, and collaborative work culture that promotes ownership, accountability, innovation, and continuous learning.
- Establish and execute departmental goals and operational strategies aligned with organizational priorities to deliver measurable business outcomes.
- Identify technical and operational gaps, guide structured troubleshooting, and drive sustainable solutions to complex and recurring problems.
- Evaluate individual and team performance, analyze operational metrics and system reliability trends, and drive continuous improvement initiatives.
- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering and business stakeholders, communicating technical challenges, operational risks, and improvement opportunities effectively.
- Serve as the primary escalation point and incident manager during high-severity incidents, providing technical direction, coordinating response efforts, driving service restoration, and ensuring timely stakeholder communication.
About the team
ARTS is a global team that works 24/7, 365 day a year to support our operational and engineering teams in warehousing around the world.
We use a follow the sun model to ensure our customers have continuous support no matter their location and time of day.
Our team deal with a broad range of hardware and software issue impacting singular robotic workcells through to large scale incidents.
This is a fast paced, dynamic and highly technical environment for engineers and managers alike.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related technical discipline
- Experience in written and verbal communication with the ability to present complex technical information in a clear and concise manner to executives and non-technical leaders
- Experience driving projects to improve operations and support-related processes
- Experience leading technical support teams
- Experience in technical support work, engineering, or an operations environment
- 4+ years of management or direct team leader experience in a similarly complex technical support environment (6+ FTEs).
- Proven experience leading high severity active incident restoration efforts, as a technical authority or Incident Manager.
- 6+ years of relevant technical support and systems management or team leader experience in a diverse complex technical environment.
- 3+ years of relevant engineer technical support and systems hands-on experience.
- Relevant industry certification(s) in any broad technical domain (Networking, Telecommunications, Cybersecurity, Cloud Technologies etc.)
- Relevant Service/Project Management Certifications may present an advantage (ITIL etc)

About the job

As a Senior Platform Product Manager for FinOps, you will drive the vision, roadmap, and product outcomes for how Datadog understands and acts on its infrastructure costs. Your customers are Datadog's engineers, Finance team and product managers. You will make complex cost data useful in their daily decisions: what changed, why it changed, what happens next, and what to do about it. You will enable intuitive cost allocation, explanation, and forecasting experiences, and connect internal needs with Datadog's Cloud Cost Management product.
At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them.
What You’ll Do:

  • Own discovery, strategy, and prioritization for internal FinOps products. Understand the distinct decisions Engineering, Finance, executives, and PMs need to make, and validate which problems are most valuable to solve.
  • Make cost allocation understandable and actionable, including shared-platform costs, service consumption, and product and customer margins. Embed cost views into the core platforms, dashboards, and workflows teams already use.
  • Improve the cost-explanation experience so teams can quickly understand cost changes, identify their causes, distinguish ongoing costs from temporary changes, and project timelines for expected cost corrections. Where current processes depend on a mandate, build the product that teams will choose to use, then retire the mandate.
  • Make the underlying metrics self-serve so teams track their own costs, and reduce the review to the decisions that need a room.
  • Partner with engineering on timely detection, insights, monitors, and alerts, and support the Engineering-ownership program that puts cost explanation on Engineering leaders.
  • Shape forecasting tools with Finance and FinOps around shared data, visible assumptions, and traceable scenarios. Help teams use cost data for capacity, efficiency, and resource-planning decisions.
  • Bridge FinOps and CCM product roadmaps by 1) running joint roadmap reviews with the CCM product team to identify overlapping builds before work starts, and 2) deciding what to reuse, where internal needs justify new capabilities, and which validated internal solutions could benefit external customers.
  • Lead pilots with representative users, test usability, and use feedback and adoption evidence to guide broader rollouts.
  • Define baselines and success measures for adoption, time to explain cost changes, manual effort, allocation coverage and trust, and adoption of forecast tooling, with assumptions traceable to source. Use these measures to prioritize work and show how the product improves decisions.

Who You Are:

  • You have prior product management experience owning discovery, prioritization, and shipped outcomes, plus substantial hands-on FinOps or cloud cost-management experience.
  • You understand cost allocation, shared infrastructure economics, forecasting, and the different questions engineering and finance teams ask of the same cost data. You can reason about efficiency, usage growth, and product or customer margins.
  • You can turn complex data and technical systems into clear product experiences. You've worked directly with users, tested your assumptions, and improved adoption or reduced effort through what you shipped.
  • You bring strong analytical and systems thinking. You can interrogate cost and usage data, challenge assumptions, evaluate trade-offs, and define measures that help teams decide what to do next.
  • You communicate clearly with engineers, analysts, Finance, and executives, adapting the level of detail to the decision. You earn trust across teams and form evidence-backed recommendations when priorities conflict.
  • You're curious about how AI is changing infrastructure economics and product work. You continuously evaluate and use the latest tools to learn and test ideas quickly, and apply judgment to data quality, attribution, and the reliability of automated outputs.

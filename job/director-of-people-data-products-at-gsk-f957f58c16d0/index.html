About the job

Director of People Data Products

The posting period will end on the 2nd of December in 2026

GSK’s People Data & Analytics (PDA) team combines science and technology to drive better talent and workforce decisions. Our ambition is for every workforce decision to be informed by trusted, timely insight, and we are now bringing Agentic AI to people data at scale to achieve this. As Director of People Data Products, reporting to the Head of PDA, you will lead a global team across London and Bangalore that owns our data lake, PIE, and our analytics platform, Visier, delivering scaled insight solutions for HR and senior leaders. You will also shape how PDA scales and manages Agentic AI for end-users in a trusted, secure way, turning bold ambition into products that accelerate how GSK supports its people.

Key Responsibilities:

Strategy and stakeholder partnership

  • Build trusted relationships with senior HR and technology partners, acting as the bridge between HR needs and technical delivery, translating strategic direction and market best-practice into clearly scoped, practical deliverables
  • Work with the Head of PDA and the Behavioural Data Science team to plan and deliver the PDA roadmap, balancing vendor and in-house solutions
  • Collaborate with PDA Portfolio Manager, Behavioural and Data Science, and the Communications (CGA) team to embed change management and adoption

Team leadership

  • Lead and develop a team of 6 across London and Bangalore; analytics product developers, data engineers and data governance leads

Platform ownership: Scaled analytics platform and people analytics data lake

  • Own People Insights Ecosystem (PIE), our Azure-based cloud data store, working with your team to optimise integrations, governance, modelling, architecture, security and access
  • Own Visier, our primary end-user analytics platform, and the strategic vendor relationship, driving roadmap alignment and value reviews

Scaling Agentic AI end-user solutions

  • Lead the building of a new approach for PDA to manage scaled Agentic AI solutions, including both in-house built solutions and MCP connectors with vendor platforms, as well as making PIE MCP ready, to give AI agents secure access to governed, high-quality people data, working hand-in-hand with the PDA AI engineering lead and our Tech partners

Data quality and governance

  • Drive data quality improvements and oversight, including owning Ataccama data quality tool for HR data, overseeing data quality reporting and issue detection, building quality in by design and escalating issues to business or technology owners
  • Oversee the team to maintain strong operational governance and standards, including requirements and data documentation, privacy and approvals, planning, progress reporting, security, and management of risks and issues

Why you?

Basic qualifications:

We are looking for professionals with these required skills to achieve our goals

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
  • Experience leading and developing technical teams (such as data engineers, developers and data governance) in people analytics, HR data or data products, within a complex, multinational organisation
  • Leading projects that take data analytics products from exploration through development, testing, launch and lifecycle management
  • Stakeholder management with senior HR and business leaders
  • Proven ability to translate high-level strategy and end-user use cases into practical roadmaps, projects and deliverables
  • Data governance and oversight of cloud data platforms, including documentation, quality assurance, testing and data quality reporting
  • Knowledge of privacy, security, and data protection controls for sensitive employee data
  • Solid understanding of how AI is applied to analytics products, including LLMs, Agentic AI and Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors

Preferred Qualifications:

If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus:

  • Master’s degree in a quantitative, technical or analytics-related field
  • Experience with Visier, including managing vendor relationship
  • Experience with Azure data services (e.g. Data Factory, Synapse, Fabric or Databricks) and Azure AI services (e.g. Azure OpenAI or AI Foundry)
  • Hands-on experience building or managing AI agents, LLM-based applications or MCP connectors in production
  • Experience with data quality tools, ideally Ataccama, and with data cataloguing or lineage
  • Familiarity with HR system data structures (ideally Workday) and integrating them into analytics platforms
  • Experience in pharmaceuticals, life sciences or another regulated industry

Why GSK?

Uniting science, technology and talent to get ahead of disease together.

GSK is a global biopharma company with a purpose to unite science, technology and talent to get ahead of disease together. We aim to positively impact the health of 2.5 billion people by the end of the decade, as a successful, growing company where people can thrive.

We get ahead of disease by preventing and treating it with innovation in specialty medicines and vaccines. We focus on four therapeutic areas: respiratory, immunology and inflammation; oncology; HIV; and infectious diseases – to impact health at scale.

People and patients around the world count on the medicines and vaccines we make, so we’re committed to creating an environment where our people can thrive and focus on what matters most. Our culture of being ambitious for patients, accountable for impact and doing the right thing is the foundation for how, together, we deliver for patients, shareholders and our people.

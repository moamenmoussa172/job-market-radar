About the job

Synopsis

The Group Analyst is responsible for managing and evaluating all group requests received from travel agents through the PROS Group Sales Optimizer (GSO) system. The role focuses on maximizing potential revenue from group demand by assessing each request against availability, demand outlook, route performance, and commercial priorities. Working closely with the Market Managers, the Group Analyst ensures that group pricing and acceptance decisions are aligned with route strategy, inventory position, and overall Revenue Management objectives.

Accountabilities

• Group Request Evaluation: Accountable for reviewing and evaluating group requests submitted by travel agents through PROS GSO. Assess requested itineraries, group size, travel dates, demand outlook, fare levels, materialisation risk, and expected revenue contribution to determine the most appropriate group offer, counteroffer, or rejection decision.
• Revenue Optimization: Maximize revenue from group demand by balancing group acceptance against individual passenger demand, forecast performance, and seat availability. Use system recommendations, market inputs, and revenue management judgment to protect high-demand flights while capturing incremental group revenue where commercially justified.
• Market Alignment & Reporting: Work closely with the Market Management team to align group decisions with route strategy, seasonal priorities, tactical sales activity, and market performance. Monitor group request trends, conversion, acceptance levels, and revenue outcomes, providing clear insights to support continuous improvement of group policies, system settings, and commercial approach.
• Group Booking Policy Management: Manage and apply group booking policies consistently across accepted group requests, including deposit requirements, ticketing time limits, name submission deadlines, date change conditions, and related commercial controls. Ensure policy decisions are commercially sound, aligned with market strategy, and clearly communicated to Sales and travel agents to support conversion while protecting revenue integrity.

Education & Experience

• Educated to university degree level in Economics, Business, Statistics, Aviation Management, or another numerate discipline, or equivalent relevant experience in Revenue Management, Groups, Sales, Reservations, or airline commercial operations.
• A minimum of 4 years’ experience in the travel or airline industry, preferably within Revenue Management, Group Sales, Commercial Sales, Reservations, or an analytical environment. Previous experience with PROS GSO, Revenue Management systems, GDS, inventory tools, or group booking processes would be beneficial.

Recruitment Process

The recruitment process for this position will involve the following steps:

  • Shortlisted applicants will be invited to complete a recorded video interview via our third-party platform HireVue. This interview will allow you to record your responses to a range of questions related to the position and will allow you to demonstrate your knowledge and experience in a way that just your CV alone would not be able to. It also ensures fairness for you and all candidates, as all our shortlisted candidates are asked the same questions and assessed on their responses.
  • After a review of completed video interviews, shortlisted candidates will be invited to an initial technical interview with the hiring manager, which may require completion of a case-study.
  • Finalists may be invited to a follow-up interview with leadership team, if required

All candidates will be notified as to the status of their application as soon as is possible

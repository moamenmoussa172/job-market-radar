About the job

What you will be responsible for

As a Cyber Fusion Operations Analyst, you will:

  • Collect, validate and analyze operational security metrics, identifying trends, outliers and data quality issues across multiple source systems
  • Build and maintain dashboards (e.g., Power BI) and recurring reporting, translating data into clear findings and recommendations for technical and senior non-technical audiences
  • Support automation of data collection, pipelines and reporting to improve accuracy, timeliness and repeatability
  • Build and maintain the data models and sources behind our reporting, so metrics are consistent and self-service where possible
  • Work with team leads to document metric definitions, data sources and owners, and keep them consistent
  • Contribute to the team's service catalog, documenting services, owners, processes and the metrics each service is measured against
  • Evaluate and apply new analytics capabilities, including AI-assisted tools, to improve how we analyze and present data, and validate outputs for accuracy and alignment with internal policies
  • Create and maintain documentation such as data dictionaries, reporting procedures, standard operating procedures and the knowledge base
  • Provide data and reporting support to Fusion teams (Cyber Threat Intelligence, Advanced Threat, and Data Loss Prevention/Data Protection) and to cross-functional task forces
  • Be cross-trained on the full scope of Fusion Operations work, including the cyber tabletop exercise (TTX) program (scenario development, exercise delivery, after-action reports and tracking lessons learned to closure), and contribute as needed

What we value

  • Strong Excel skills and hands-on experience building dashboards and data models in modern analytics tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau)
  • Interest in emerging analytics and AI-enabled reporting, and the judgment to validate what those tools produce
  • Attention to detail and a habit of validating data, definitions and sources before reporting them
  • Ability to develop reports and present findings and recommendations clearly, both in person and in writing
  • Ability to follow processes and contribute to continuous improvement
  • Understanding of current security threats and frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK
  • Familiarity with common enterprise security tools, such as SIEM (Splunk), EDR (CrowdStrike, CarbonBlack, or Tanium) and Vulnerability Management (Qualys, Tenable, or Rapid7), and the data they produce

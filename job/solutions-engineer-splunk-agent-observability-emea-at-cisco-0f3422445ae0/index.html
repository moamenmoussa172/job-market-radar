About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in United Kingdom.

Meet the team

Splunk, a Cisco company, is building a resilient digital world with an end-to-end platform designed for hybrid, multi-cloud environments. We approach our work with kindness, valuing the unique talents, passion, and joy that each team member brings to our collaborative culture. Our team is dedicated to helping organizations reach their best by solving complex business challenges at scale. We foster an environment of inclusiveness, disruption, and fun, ensuring that every member feels supported as they reach new heights.

What you will do

  • Lead comprehensive architectural discovery sessions with enterprise AI architects, engineering leaders, and data scientists across EMEA to evaluate existing Generative AI stacks and identify observability gaps.
  • Deliver deep-dive technical demonstrations and customized reference architectures to showcase real-time LLM benchmarking, multi-agent evaluation, and cost optimization.
  • Own the end-to-end technical scoping, milestone definition, and execution of customer Proof of Concepts (PoCs) to secure definitive technical wins and accelerate pre-sales deal velocity.
  • Act as a strategic liaison between enterprise field engagements and Cisco/Splunk Product and Engineering teams to synthesize customer feedback and influence product roadmaps.
  • Author reusable EMEA customer reference architectures and demo assets to scale our technical sales efforts.
  • Conduct technical enablement sessions for core Sales Engineers to elevate the team's overall AI and machine learning expertise.

Minimum qualifications

  • 3+ years of professional experience in technical pre-sales engineering, solutions architecture, or customer-facing technical consulting within AI/ML, data analytics, or enterprise software.
  • Experience building, deploying, or architecting workflows using modern agentic orchestration frameworks (such as LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI, AutoGen, or equivalent).
  • Applied experience with Generative AI architectures (including Retrieval-Augmented Generation / RAG, tool calling, and multi-agent coordination) and mitigating failure modes (such as hallucinations, bias, prompt injection, and data drift).
  • 2+ years of experience scoping, instrumenting, and implementing technical Proof of Concepts (PoCs) to achieve defined technical wins aligned with customer business outcomes.
  • Ability to travel 15% to 25% across the EMEA region for customer workshops, Executive Briefing Centers (EBCs), and regional events.

Preferred qualifications

  • Coding proficiency in Python or TypeScript, with practical experience using AI libraries such as Streamlit, Pandas, LangChain, HuggingFace, or the OpenAI SDK.
  • Strong ability to prototype customized demo environments, mock agentic applications, and API integrations during fast-paced pre-sales cycles.
  • Prior professional background working as a Software Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, or Data Scientist before transitioning into technical pre-sales.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to translate complex AI concepts into clear business value for executive and technical audiences alike.

What You’ll Do

As a Fresher Embedded Software Engineer, you will:

  • Develop, integrate, and test embedded software using C/C++.
  • Analyze software requirements and support software design.
  • Perform software integration, testing, debugging, and issue analysis.
  • Document development and test results.
  • Work with experienced engineers and global development teams throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • Build your technical expertise in embedded systems and automotive software.

Who can apply:

  • Fresh graduates with a background in Electronics, Telecommunications, Mechatronics, Automation, Computer Engineering, Automotive Engineering, or related fields.

Technical Skills:

  • Good knowledge of C/C++ programming.
  • Basic understanding of microcontrollers and embedded systems.
  • Knowledge of Python, Perl, or other scripting languages is a plus.
  • Basic knowledge of communication protocols and/or sensors is an advantage.

Other Skills:

  • Good problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Willingness to learn and develop technical expertise in the automotive domain.
  • Able to communicate in English, both verbally and in writing.

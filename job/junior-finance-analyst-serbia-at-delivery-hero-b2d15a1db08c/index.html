About the job

Job Ref ID: JR0089187

YOUR MISSION

Based in Belgrade, you will report directly to the Finance Manager. Your core mission is to become the person who knows Serbia's numbers better than anyone in the building when speaking about EBITDA, Free Cash Flow (FCF), or General & Administrative (G&A) expenses.

THE JOURNEY

  • Ramp-Up & Ownership: Deep dive into G&A mechanics and costs, learn the fundamental FCF and P&L structure, and transition to autonomously managing deep dives and preparing the Best Estimate (BE) forecast.
  • Forecasting & Impact: Evolve from supporting the forecast to driving real change. You will lead projects that fundamentally improve how the finance team or the broader business operates around forecasting.
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: You will interact directly with local department heads (Ops, Commercial, Marketing, Legal), the ITSEE Regional Finance & Strategy team, and HQ stakeholders (Refund & Compensation, Fintech, People Controlling).
  • Tooling & Modern Finance: Leverage dashboards in BigQuery/Looker to monitor performance. You are encouraged to use AI tools (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) daily to accelerate your learning, structure your analysis, and move faster.

WHAT YOU WILL BRING TO THE RIDE

  • Experience: Up to 1 year of experience in FP&A, strategy, controlling, consulting, or a fast-paced analytical role. A strong internship in cost controlling or FP&A is highly preferred.
  • Academic Background: Degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related field.
  • Analytical Curiosity: You don't just accept numbers at face value; you want to dig into the mechanics of how costs are generated and how they impact the bottom line.
  • Communication: Ability to clearly explain financial realities to non-finance stakeholders.
  • Language: Full professional proficiency in English is required.

About the job

About the Team

At Trendyol Tech, our mission is to create a positive impact in our ecosystem by enabling commerce through technology.

We solve complex problems with data, creativity, and agility — always driven by real outcomes. With a culture built on learning, collaboration, and ownership, we grow together while building what’s next.

About the Role

Our primary focus will be on powering our Core E-commerce domain. Building a seamless and personalized shopping experience designed to serve millions of customers. As a Data Scientist, you'll play a critical role in driving data-informed decisions; from optimizing ranking and recommendation algorithms to user behavior modeling and improving the efficiency of the systems
We solve complex problems with data, creativity, and agility — always driven by real outcomes. With a culture built on learning, collaboration, and ownership, we grow together while building what’s next.

Responsibilities

  • Work at the intersection of product, technology, and machine learning; developing models that power seller & user experience, personalization, Growth, and other core E-commerce features
  • Enhancing a scalable, fast, and reliable discovery infrastructure designed to support the future of digital commerce. As a Data Scientist, you'll play a critical role in driving data-informed decisions; from user intent modeling and ranking optimization to improving recommendation relevance and system reliability
  • Analyze large-scale structured and unstructured data from multiple sources across the Trendyol ecosystem
  • Apply best practices for working with ML-Ops infrastructure, improve models and infrastructure to optimize model performance and deployment cycles
  • Execute A/B experiments, reach conclusions statistically and build upon learnings continously
  • Collaborate with product, engineering, and risk teams to translate business challenges into data science problems.
  • Work with stakeholders throughout the organization to identify opportunities for leveraging company data to drive business solutions
  • Synthesize methodology and data into actionable business strategy and communicate findings to engineering and management teams as driving business impact
  • Expected Qualifications
  • Experience in applied data science and a wide range of data science stack of algorithms and models. Knowledge about recommendation systems, search ranking, or personalization is a big plus
  • Good knowledge of python or other programming language. Experience in one or more of the libraries such as scikit-learn, Pytorch and TensorFlow is a plus.
  • Good knowledge of SQL and familiarity with best practices
  • Strong analytical skills with a data driven approach
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Experience with e-commerce or self-service business models is a big plus
  • The experience range for this position is suitable for specialist/senior specialist roles

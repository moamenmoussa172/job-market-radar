EU VAT Analyst, Indirect Tax Planning

Amazon · Warsaw, Poland

Onsite · Data Analyst · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Amazon's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Amazon is looking for a VAT Analyst based in Warsaw to support VAT planning and compliance initiatives for both new initiatives and on-going business activities. Specifically this role will assist with VAT affairs for Amazon’s Stores business in Poland while also supporting wider Pan-EU Stores Business projects. In this role, you’ll work closely with local business partners and members of the tax team, delivering expert VAT technical advice to support our VAT legislative, compliance, and on-going tax controversy requirements. The role will be part of a wider EMEA focused tax planning team, focused mainly on EU, UK and Turkey. We offer a fast paced, exciting tax environment with plenty of interaction with business and finance leaders, plus other key support functions such as legal.
The ideal candidate will be self-confident, highly motivated and enjoy a diverse environment where ideas are shared, opinions challenged and nothing ever stands still. You’ll be operating at the forefront of tax and international business. The role will be based in Warsaw but is likely to require periodic international travel.
Key job responsibilities
The candidate must possess:
- The ability to think strategically and communicate effectively with key Amazon stakeholders.
- Give practical VAT technical advice to local business partners and the tax team
- Support VAT planning and compliance for new initiatives and existing activities in Poland
- Contribute to Pan-EU Stores VAT projects
- Support VAT legislative, compliance and tax controversy requirements
- Work with business, finance, Legal and other key support teams.
- Communicate confidently in both Polish & English.
- Bachelor's degree
- Experience tax advisory (excluding compliance)
- Experience in Polish and/or EU VAT law
- Relevant law degree

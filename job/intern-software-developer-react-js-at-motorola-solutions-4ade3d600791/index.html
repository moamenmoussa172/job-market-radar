About the job

Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future.


Department Overview

Looking to make a visible impact? In our Digital Transformation team, you won't just write code - you'll reinvent how millions of customers interact with Motorola across our core web platforms, e-commerce engines, and support ecosystems.
We give our engineers true ownership. You’ll participate in every phase: ideation, architecture, implementation, and release, working shoulder-to-shoulder with senior engineers and product leaders to solve tough problems and build meaningful products.


Job Description

We are looking for a passionate Front-End Software Engineer to join our Digital eCommerce Team. You’ll work alongside our Product Owner and UI/UX Designers to build high-impact, customer-facing features while maintaining clean, quality code.

We’re looking for a hands-on engineer who thrives across the entire product lifecycle, from pitching ideas and writing code to launching and maintaining scalable features.

Our technology stack:

  • Oracle Commerce Cloud
  • JavaScript (ES6)
  • React
  • Node.js
  • HTML5, CSS3
  • Git, CI/CD
  • Python


Basic Requirements

What You Bring:

  • Tech Skills: Solid proficiency in JavaScript and React.
  • Engineering Mindset: A strong commitment to clean code, active code reviews, and industry best practices.
  • Communication: Fluent English skills to collaborate effectively across teams.
  • Git Version Control: Experienced in managing code and workflows using Git.

Nice-to-Have Skills:

  • Agile Collaboration: Comfortable working in a Scrum environment within a self-organizing team alongside UI/UX designers, backend engineers, and QA.

What We Offer:

  • Continuous Growth: Dedicated time for self-development, training, and skill-building.
  • Competitive Compensation: Market-leading salary package plus an Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP).
  • Comprehensive Benefits: Private medical insurance, life insurance, and a robust Employee Pension Plan (EPP).
  • Premium Office Experience: Modern office space with private parking for when you choose to work on-site.
  • Availability 32 hours per week or Full Time
  • 15 months contract (Contract of Employment)
  • 1 or 2 days a week from the Kraków office (Czerwone Maki 82)

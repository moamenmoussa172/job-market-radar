What You'll Be Doing & Why It Matters
We're hiring systems and database engineers across experience levels to build Database.com and advance Hyper, Salesforce's database engine for interactive query processing.
Hyper powers analytics in Tableau and query processing in Salesforce Data 360. With Database.com, we're building on that foundation to give developers and AI agents direct access to a database for application data, transactions, analytics, and retrieval across cloud and local environments.
Depending on your interests and experience, you'll contribute to areas such as:
Core Responsibilities & Scope

  • Query optimization, query compilation, code generation, and parallel execution.
  • Storage engines, indexing, caching, compression, and efficient I/O.
  • Transactions, concurrency control, recovery, and concurrent read/write workloads.
  • Streaming ingestion, lakehouse writes, compaction, and open formats such as Apache Iceberg and Parquet as well as internal optimized storage formats.
  • Distributed systems, federated query processing, and serverless scheduling and resource isolation.
  • Local and embedded database execution, including WebAssembly.
  • Define the long-term architecture and technical strategy for Database.com and Hyper, align multiple teams around it, and act as the technical authority on the hardest design decisions.

Minimum Requirements

  • 15+ years of experience building systems software or database technology, with a track record of architecting large-scale systems and influencing technical strategy across organizations.
  • Systems programming experience in C++, Rust, or a comparable language, and a willingness to work extensively in C++.
  • A solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, memory management, and concurrency.
  • Experience in one or more relevant areas: databases, compilers, operating systems, storage, distributed systems, networking, or performance engineering.
  • Strong product judgment and the ability to turn customer needs into clear priorities, practical requirements, and useful capabilities.
  • An ability to turn a difficult problem into a testable hypothesis and use measurements to guide implementation.
  • Clear technical communication and care in reviewing other people's work.
  • Degree or equivalent relevant experience required. Experience will be evaluated based on the core competencies for the role (e.g. extracurricular leadership roles, military experience, volunteer roles, work experience, etc.)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with LLVM, assembly, SIMD, modern CPU architectures, asynchronous I/O, or cloud storage would be useful.
  • So would work on SQL semantics, query optimizers, transaction processing, or reproducible systems benchmarks.

We expect candidates to bring strengths in some of these areas and develop others on the team.
You'll contribute throughout design, implementation, testing, performance evaluation, and production use. That includes reading unfamiliar code, investigating regressions, explaining tradeoffs, and using real workloads to decide what to improve. We care about correctness, predictable latency, throughput, and resource efficiency.
Engineers on this team are also expected to take on product responsibilities. You'll work with users to understand their needs and identify the problems worth solving. You'll maintain a strong overview of database and systems research, competing products, and industry developments, and use that knowledge to guide product decisions. You'll shape requirements, set priorities, make scope and delivery tradeoffs, and use feedback and usage data to evaluate results. We expect you to relentlessly drive the product forward, turning ideas into shipped capabilities and following through until they solve the intended problem.
We expect every engineer to be an effective manager of AI agents. You'll translate product goals into well-defined tasks, delegate substantial engineering work to agents, coordinate parallel efforts, and review and integrate their results. Managing agents is a core engineering responsibility on this team. You'll be expected to continually improve these workflows to deliver faster and more efficiently, while owning the technical decisions and the quality of what ships.
Scope and ownership will reflect your experience. Early-career candidates can demonstrate their abilities through coursework, research, internships, open-source contributions, or substantial independent projects. For senior candidates, we look for experience delivering complex systems and guiding technical and product decisions.
We're looking for engineers with:
Familiarity with database internals is valuable; engineers from adjacent systems fields are also welcome.
Hyper began as a database research project at the Technical University of Munich. The team continues to combine original systems work with the demands of a production database. You'll have the opportunity to develop new techniques, examine their behavior in detail, and ship them to users.
If you enjoy understanding how a system works all the way down—and making it work better—we'd like to hear from you.

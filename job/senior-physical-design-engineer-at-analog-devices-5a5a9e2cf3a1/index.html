Senior Physical Design Engineer

Role Overview

The Senior Physical Design Engineer supports the end-to-end physical implementation and signoff of digital blocks and SoCs. Working as part of a collaborative and potentially geographically distributed engineering team, the role applies practical physical design knowledge to deliver high-quality implementation results while developing technical depth across the RTL-to-GDSII flow.

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute assigned RTL-to-GDSII implementation activities, including floor-planning, CTS, routing, equivalence checking, timing closure, and physical verification.
  • Use Cadence or Synopsys based physical design flows to support implementation, optimisation, and signoff activities.
  • Apply a good working knowledge of synthesis and timing constraints to review implementation results and provide informed feedback to the design team.
  • Analyse and resolve timing, power, IR/EM, DRC, and LVS issues with guidance from senior engineers where required.
  • Strong communication, and teamwork skills, with a willingness to learn and take ownership of assigned implementation tasks.

Qualifications

  • B.Tech., M.Tech., or equivalent qualification in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Approximately 3–7 years of relevant physical design or digital implementation experience.
  • Hands-on experience with industry-standard physical design and signoff tools, preferably within a Cadence or Synopsys environment.

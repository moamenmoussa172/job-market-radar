About the job

Job Title

Integration Engineer

Job Description

Reports to: Senior Integration Engineer

Location: London - Heathrow

Contract Type: Permanent

Introduction to role

Vanderlande is the global market leader in baggage handling systems for airports, and sorting systems for parcel and postal services. Working within the Integration team the Integration Engineer will be responsible for solving unique but definite problems and working systematically by applying well known methods, techniques and tooling to achieve concrete and known results.

Role Responsibilities

Your responsibilities and activities will include:

  • Assist with the delivery of Integrated Systems that conform to an agreed scope and acceptance criteria.
  • Support the development and execution of tests linked to the validation and acceptance of a project.
  • Support and follow change and configuration management processes.
  • Support and contribute to integration planning and highlighting any dependencies between key technical variables
  • Support and manage existing systems of Change Management.
  • Create effective and good working relationships with all departments to ensure that customer expectations are exceeded
  • Support and contribute to the definition and implementation of the Handover Strategy. (Internal and external)
  • Contributing to co-ordination, development, implementation and management of customer requirements/ expectations
  • Customer liaison – developing and maintaining client relationships throughout the project life cycle.
  • Ensure all requests are dealt with efficiently, ensuring employees have everything required to carry out their daily duties
  • Working as part of a team to ensure a smooth running of the department and business
  • Sharing accountability for system delivery
  • Contribute to and support the development of Integration Strategies and Plans
  • Responsible for the continuous improvement of policies and procedures as well as learning continuously and developing professional potential and ability
  • Support with delivering Integrated systems within budget and on schedule
  • Support a safe working environment within business development through championing and encouragement of a safety-first culture
  • Recognising and highlighting risks, be they technical or to delivery schedule/requirements
  • Support a safe working environment within the Integration Team through championing and encouragement of a safety-first culture

Role Qualification and Skills

  • System Integration experience, preferably integrating High Level (IT), Low Level (PLC) and Electro-Mechanical (EM) Systems
  • Presentation skills at all levels
  • Experienced in Change Control
  • Configuration Management Processes
  • Experience working with the V model for requirements and testing
  • Any experience working with HP QC, Synergy, Oracle SQL & SUSE Linux would be advantageous.

What we offer

  • 28 days annual leave (excluding public holidays)
  • Aviva Medical Cover
  • YuLife – Wellbeing membership with fast access to GP appointments, promotion of health and wellbeing along with daily quests to gain Yucoins that can be swapped for shopping vouchers
  • A challenging work environment with lots of opportunities of career progression.
  • Cycle to work scheme
  • Yellow Nest is a salary exchange scheme that reduces childcare costs for parents and employers
  • Pension with Aviva
  • Access to Achievers an award-winning recognition platform that inspires to recognise your coworkers Where points are awarded that can be exchanged for a range of goods and discounts.

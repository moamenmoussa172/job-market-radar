About the job

About Paystack

Paystack’s mission is to power African ambition. Over 300K businesses across Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and Kenya use Paystack’s modern payments gateway, including Qatar Airways, MTN, Burger King, UPS, Africa World Airlines, AXA Mansard Insurance, FairMoney, PiggyVest, Crocs, Under Armour, Richemont Lifestyle Group, and many others.

Over the last 10 years, we’ve built products that have helped shape online payments in Africa, from automated recurring payments to direct bank payments and automated chargebacks. Today, Paystack is part of The Stack Group (TSG), a family of technology brands building modern infrastructure across payments, banking, consumer products, and emerging technologies.

At Paystack, we hire talented people, treat them with genuine respect, and give them the space, resources, and support to do the best work of their lives. We’d love your help.

About the Senior Product Manager role

Our team is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the merchant journey at Paystack, from a merchant's first visit to everyday use.

The role brings onboarding and merchant experience under one owner. Today that journey is shared across many teams and run largely through operations. This role makes it product-led.

You will own onboarding, from signup through KYB decisioning and risk scoring to activation. You will also own the Dashboard, the Merchant App, notifications, webhooks, developer experience, and merchant support and self-serve, across every Paystack market. You will set the standard for what a good merchant experience looks like. Any change that touches merchants, whether it comes from compliance, trust and safety or settlement, passes through this role.

The job is to build systems, not workarounds. A merchant who submits the right information is approved by the next business day, and gets the same good experience every time after that.

The role reports to the Head of Product. It is strongly preferred if the candidate is based in Lagos, Nigeria.

We’ll trust you to

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for merchant onboarding, the Dashboard, the Merchant App, notifications, webhooks, developer experience and merchant self-serve.
  • Own activation end to end, from a merchant's first visit until they are fully using the products they need.
  • Own KYB decisioning and risk scoring at signup, and the rules that decide who is approved, who is reviewed and who is declined.
  • Get every merchant who submits complete, correct information approved by the next business day at the latest.
  • Stop bad submissions at the point of entry by validating documents and data as merchants submit, so at least 98% of submissions need no human fix.
  • Take over the onboarding review automation and extend it, so AI agents and reviewers work in one flow and manual review becomes the exception.
  • Pull verified data from identity, registry and partner sources by API, with the merchant's permission, instead of asking merchants to upload it.
  • Build onboarding as shared infrastructure, with APIs that other Paystack businesses can build their own front ends on.
  • Reactivate dormant merchants, and re-verify merchants whose business or identity details change significantly.
  • Work with Compliance to turn regulatory requirements in each market into rules the system enforces, and let market teams tailor the last mile.
  • Define what good looks like for merchants and hold every team that touches merchants to that standard.
  • Decide how and when merchants hear from Paystack when something happens to their account, money or integration, and build the system that delivers it.
  • Build tooling that plugs into compliance, trust and safety, and settlement systems, and replace manual, case-by-case fixes with self-serve product.
  • Make building on Paystack simple, with clear docs, reliable webhooks, useful logs and a fast path from test to live.
  • Drive adoption of Paystack products with nudges and recommendations, and work with Growth so a better experience turns into more revenue from existing merchants.
  • Track the onboarding funnel and what merchants do in the product, and fix the biggest leaks first.
  • Define and track the metrics: time to approval, first-time-right submission rate, activation rate, and a primary measure of merchant happiness supported by CSAT and NPS.

You’ll thrive in this role if you

  • 7+ years of product management experience shipping customer-facing products, ideally B2B or developer products
  • Experience owning onboarding, KYC/KYB, identity verification or activation products at a payments company, bank or fintech
  • A track record of cutting time to approval or growing activation, with the numbers to show it
  • A strong eye for great experiences, in both product UX and customer experience
  • A sharp front-end and pixel eye, and the discipline to make complex things simple for self-serve users
  • A working understanding of KYB and AML onboarding requirements for payment companies, and how regulators such as the CBN apply them
  • Experience designing decision engines or rules, and the review tooling that sits behind them
  • Strong analytical skills, including funnel analysis and SQL, and the habit of tracking merchant behaviour closely
  • Experience with product-led growth, in-product nudges, recommendations or lifecycle messaging
  • Systems thinking and a habit of fixing the class of problem rather than the single case
  • Technical fluency with APIs, webhooks and developer tools, and the ability to design APIs other teams build on
  • Experience building products for more than one market
  • Sound judgement on the trade-off between speed, conversion and compliance risk, and the confidence to push back on changes that would hurt merchants
  • Prior experience in payments, fintech or merchant platforms, which is a strong asset

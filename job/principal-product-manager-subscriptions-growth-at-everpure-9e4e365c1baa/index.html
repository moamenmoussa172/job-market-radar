About the job

This type of work—work that changes the world—is what the tech industry was founded on. So, if you're ready to seize the endless opportunities and leave your mark, come join us.

THE ROLE

As the Principal Product Manager, Evergreen Subscriptions, you will own the product direction for subscription pricing and the renewal experience. You will translate customer value, market context, business strategy, and unit economics into clear product choices across pricing architecture, packaging, terms, expansion, and renewal policies.

You will set direction across a broad, cross-functional surface area—from commercial policy and workflow automation to customer and partner experiences. You will bring structure to ambiguity, make trade-offs explicit, and align stakeholders on priorities and execution.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Pricing Strategy & Product Direction: Develop and evolve pricing, packaging, and renewal constructs for subscription offers. Translate customer value, market context, business strategy, and unit economics into choices on price architecture, terms, bundles, expansions, and renewal policies.
  • Scalable Pricing Capabilities: Define pricing rules, guardrails, approval thresholds, and exception paths with Finance and commercial partners. Make pricing explainable, consistently applied, and adaptable as offers and go-to-market needs evolve.
  • Renewal Lifecycle Transformation: Set product strategy for renewal capabilities, including opportunity and quote readiness, asset and subscription validation, contract changes, co-terming, approvals, partner and customer communications, and renewal completion.
  • Workflow Automation & Exception Management: Partner with Engineering and business systems teams to automate repeatable workflows across quoting, CRM, subscription, and asset data. Identify failure modes, prevent avoidable rework, and make exceptions visible, actionable, and owned.
  • Data-Informed Growth: Define and own KPIs for adoption, expansion, and churn reduction. Synthesize market intelligence and competitive signals into actionable product requirements and performance dashboards.
  • Customer & Partner Experience: Use customer, partner, and field research to improve pricing transparency, quote comprehension, renewal visibility, and decision-making. Prioritize changes that reduce friction without obscuring commercial terms or customer value.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Align Engineering, Finance, Sales, Deal Desk, Renewals Operations, Customer Success, and other stakeholders on priorities, policy decisions, dependencies, and launch readiness. Drive decisions through ambiguity and communicate trade-offs clearly, including to executive audiences.
  • We are primarily an in-office environment and therefore, you will be expected to work from the Santa Clara, CA office in compliance with Everpure's policies, unless you are on PTO, or work travel, or other approved leave.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Product Leadership: Significant product management experience leading complex, cross-functional initiatives from strategy through delivery.
  • Pricing & Subscription Acumen: Experience with subscription pricing, packaging, renewals, commercial policies, or related monetization and lifecycle capabilities.
  • Systems Thinking: Ability to translate business rules and operational workflows into scalable product capabilities, including automation and exception handling.
  • Data-Informed Decision Making: Experience defining product KPIs, using quantitative and qualitative signals, and turning insights into roadmap decisions.
  • Cross-Functional Influence: Proven ability to align technical and business stakeholders, navigate ambiguity, communicate trade-offs, and drive decisions with senior audiences.
  • Customer Orientation: Experience using customer, partner, or field insights to improve complex commercial experiences while maintaining clarity and trust.

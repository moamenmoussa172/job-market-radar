Department Overview

Motorola Solutions is a global leader in mission-critical communications and public safety solutions. Within our Defense & Tactical Communications division, we equip armed forces and security agencies with high-security, interoperable command, control, and communication systems (TETRA, tactical LTE/5G, C4ISR). For our complex defense procurement and modernization projects supporting the German Navy (surface and subsurface vessels, as well as naval shore installations), we are seeking an experienced and dedicated Systems Engineer (Technical Lead) (m/f/d)


Job Description

In this senior leadership role, you will hold end-to-end technical authority and systems engineering responsibility for naval communication and tactical data transmission systems—from early concept development and bidding through detailed architectural design to final commissioning and trials at sea:

  • Overall Technical Leadership & Architecture: Lead and mentor multi-disciplinary engineering teams (RF/tactical radio, IP networking, cybersecurity, mechanical/hardware, and software); develop and maintain the overall system architecture and technical specifications in alignment with V-Modell XT Bund.
  • Requirements Engineering & Traceability: Analyze and decompose complex customer requirement baselines (e.g., German Defence system specifications / Lastenheft) into clear functional and non-functional subsystem requirements; maintain strict end-to-end traceability down to test cases using tools such as IBM DOORS or Polarion.
  • Naval Platform Integration & Environmental Standards: Design and adapt systems to withstand harsh maritime environments (shock, vibration, and climatic qualifications per German VG standards and MIL-STD), ensuring strict compliance with electromagnetic compatibility and emission security (EMC / TEMPEST / BSI zoning).
  • Interface Management: Define, coordinate, and control mechanical, electrical, and data interfaces to third-party shipboard systems (such as Combat Management Systems / CMS, navigation aids, and tactical intercoms) in close collaboration with shipyard partners and naval system integrators.
  • Verification, Validation & Acceptance: Direct the overall test, verification, and qualification program; lead Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT), Harbor Acceptance Tests (HAT), and Sea Acceptance Trials (SAT) in direct coordination with Defence, Technical Centers (e.g., WTD 71/81), and military quality assurance authorities.
  • Collaboration with CM & ILS: Partner closely with Configuration Management (evaluating technical changes in the Change Control Board / CCB, approving baselines) and ILS Management to guarantee long-term maintainability (RAMS), safety, and life cycle cost optimization.


Basic Requirements

  • Education: Master’s degree or Diploma in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Systems Engineering, Computer Science, or a related engineering discipline.
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of demonstrable track record in systems engineering or lead systems architecture for complex technical platforms—ideally within the defense, naval electronics, or aerospace industry.
  • Technical Acumen: Deep architectural understanding of mission-critical tactical communications (TETRA, software-defined radio / SDR, military HF/VHF/UHF, tactical data links, secure IP routing, cryptographic subsystems, and defense IT security).
  • Methods & Standards: Thorough proficiency in Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE / INCOSE principles) and V-Modell XT, combined with working familiarity with German Bundeswehr and NATO standards (e.g., AQAP 2110, ACMP, MIL-STD-461/810).
  • Soft Skills: Outstanding technical leadership and communication capabilities, negotiation strength in dealing with defense procurement agencies and naval shipyards, and a structured, delivery-focused mindset.
  • Languages: Professional business proficiency in German (for customer negotiations and local naval documentation) and fluent English (written and spoken).
  • Due to the specific requirements of the project, candidates interested in this position will be required to undergo a background check in accordance with applicable legal requirements. German or EU citizenship may be required, depending on the applicable requirements.

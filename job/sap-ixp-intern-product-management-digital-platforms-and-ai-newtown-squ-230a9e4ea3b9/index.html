Position Title: SAP iXp Intern - Product Management, Digital Platforms & AI

Location: Newtown Square, PA

Anticipated Start Date: October 2026

Contract Duration: 6 months

Work Hours: Full-time internship – 40 hours per week

As a Product Management Intern within the Digital Platforms team, you will be at the forefront of the Agentic AI revolution. You will support the "Build & Run" operations for SAP’s premier digital properties—including SAP.com. You will support our teams as we transform our digital platforms to AI-driven, autonomous experiences. This role is designed for a high-potential individual who wants to master the intersection of Enterprise MarTech, SAFe Agile delivery, and Generative AI integration.

What You’ll Bring

A curious and agile mindset, with a passion for learning and the resilience to adapt in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong problem-solving and creative thinking skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams and communicate ideas clearly and effectively. Emotional intelligence and cultural awareness that support inclusive teamwork and thoughtful stakeholder engagement. A growing foundation in business acumen and emerging technologies — especially artificial intelligence — paired with a results-oriented approach and the courage to take initiative.

Education: In pursuit of a degree in Marketing Tech, Data Science, Computer Science, or Digital Transformation.

Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, or recently graduated (start date must be within 6 months of graduation date) from a coding academy/bootcamp, apprenticeship, associate, bachelor’s, master’s or JD/PhD program.

Key Product Responsibilities

  • Prompt & Experience Engineering: Support the refinement of RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) workflows by documenting client needs and helping technical teams tune AI responses for accuracy and brand voice.
  • Digital Platform Orchestration: Coordinate production schedules ensuring assets meet SAP’s high standards for quality, governance, and metadata accuracy.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Utilize Analytics to track the performance of new capabilities
  • Support technical readiness and enablement for new technologies
  • AI Operations Support: Assist in the deployment and monitoring of SAP Joule and Adobe AI Assistant across the digital ecosystem to automate B2B buyer journeys and content supply chains.
  • Agile Value Delivery: Actively participate in SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) ceremonies. Help manage the Team Backlog, focusing on user stories related to AI-driven personalization and "segmentation-of-one" strategies.
  • Responsible AI Governance: Assist in audits to ensure all digital AI features adhere to SAP’s Responsible AI global standards, focusing on data privacy, transparency, and bias monitoring.
  • Digital Transformation: Support SAP’s roll out of industry leading Marketing technologies including the Adobe Suite (AEP)

Core Skills & Qualifications

  • Methodology: Basic understanding of Agile/Scrum or SAFe 6.0. Knowledge of how "Sprints" move a product from vision to reality.
  • AI Fluency: A strong desire to learn the mechanics of Large Language Models (LLMs), Vector Databases, and Agentic Orchestration.
  • Platform Literacy: Familiarity with the Adobe Experience Cloud (AEP, AEM, Target, or Analytics) or similar enterprise-grade CMS/MAP.
  • Technical Tools: Proficiency in Jira/Confluence for workflow management and Figma for reviewing AI-prototype UX designs.
  • Communication: Ability to translate business concepts into clear technical requirements working with diverse stakeholder groups.

We are looking for an intern who brings more than just basic office skills:

Digital mindset, Innovative thinker, and Technically savvy.

Drive for knowledge and relationships building.

Proactive and eager to jump in.

Exceptional organizational and planning skills and a proven ability to multi-task.

Excellent communication, customer service, and collaboration skills.

Attention to detail with an emphasis on accuracy and quality.

Ability to prioritize effectively to manage multiple projects, including numerous deliverables.

Experience working in a team environment as well as independently.

Ability to collaborate across teams globally.

Ability to complete tasks in a timely manner.

Strong desire to learn along with professional drive.

Willingness to acquire new skills to meet evolving needs.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office programs, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

Basic knowledge of design principles and commonly used tools.

Basic understanding of Web production tools and content management systems.

Cognitive Empathy: Understanding how AI agents interact with humans and the ability to map those "conversational" journeys.

Prompt Engineering: The ability to structure queries for GenAI tools to produce high-quality, brand-compliant business outputs.

Data Literacy: Moving beyond "reporting" to "analysis"—understanding metrics like AI Confidence Scores, Conversion Lift, and Pipeline Velocity.

Where You Belong

The Digital Platforms team consists of elite technologists dedicated to making SAP.com and all managed digital properties the world’s most advanced B2B platform. We manage the touchpoints, underlying technologies, and program driving transformation for SAP.com, Search.sap.com, Partner.sap.com, Support.sap.com, Developer.sap.com, campaign and microsite environments.

We are "Customer Zero" for SAP’s own Business AI. By joining us, you aren't just observing the future of AI—you are helping to build, run, and prove it to the world.

We are hiring an AI Architect

Hybrid working model – Novi Sad or Belgrade

Role Description

We are seeking an experienced AI Architect to define, govern, and drive enterprise AI architecture initiatives across large-scale AI ecosystems, platforms, and transformational programs. In this role, you will lead the architectural strategy for Generative AI, RAG, AI Agents, Multi-Agent Systems, and Enterprise AI Platforms, ensuring alignment with business objectives, security requirements, and enterprise technology standards.

As a key technical leader, you will establish architectural principles, technology roadmaps, governance frameworks, and implementation standards while working closely with engineering teams to deliver scalable, secure, and production-ready AI solutions. You will act as a trusted advisor to both business and technology stakeholders, guiding architectural decisions, evaluating emerging technologies, and driving enterprise adoption of AI capabilities.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Define and drive enterprise architecture for AI, Generative AI, RAG, AI agents, and multi-agent ecosystems.
  • Establish AI platform strategies, technology roadmaps, architectural standards, and governance frameworks.
  • Design scalable, secure, and cloud-native AI solutions leveraging Azure AI services and modern architecture patterns.
  • Translate business requirements into solution architectures and implementation roadmaps.
  • Guide engineering teams on architecture decisions, performance optimization, technical risks, and production readiness.
  • Review solution designs, proofs of concept, integrations, data architectures, and MLOps practices to ensure alignment with enterprise standards.
  • Drive adoption of AI best practices, including LLM architecture, prompt engineering, orchestration frameworks, and Responsible AI principles.
  • Collaborate with engineering, DevOps, security, and business teams to ensure successful delivery of enterprise AI solutions.
  • Lead architecture reviews, technology evaluations, and stakeholder discussions while mentoring architects and engineers.

Must Have Requirements:

  • 8+ years of experience in Enterprise Architecture, Solution Architecture, AI Architecture, Cloud Architecture, Software Engineering, or related disciplines.
  • Proven experience designing and delivering large-scale enterprise technology or transformation initiatives.
  • Strong expertise in Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, multi-agent systems, prompt engineering, and modern AI architectures.
  • Hands-on experience designing enterprise AI platforms and cloud-native solutions on Azure.
  • Strong knowledge of Azure AI services, APIs, microservices, distributed systems, integrations, and modern architecture patterns.
  • Experience with MLOps, observability, security, resilience, and operational best practices.
  • Understanding of Responsible AI, governance, cybersecurity, privacy, compliance, and risk management principles.
  • Experience leading architectural decisions and collaborating with senior business and technology stakeholders.
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, AI, Data Science, or a related field.

Nice to Have:

  • Experience in banking, financial services, fintech, capital markets, or other regulated industries.
  • Experience with LangGraph, LangChain, Semantic Kernel, or similar AI orchestration frameworks.
  • Experience with Azure Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Apache Spark, Azure Data Factory, or modern data platforms.
  • Experience with vector databases, graph databases, knowledge graphs, and advanced retrieval architectures.
  • Experience leading architecture governance initiatives and enterprise technology strategy programs.
  • Azure, TOGAF, Kubernetes, Databricks, or Generative AI related certifications.

  • Flexible hours and hybrid work options, allowing you to achieve a work-life balance that suits your needs

  • Access to continuous training, accredited certifications, and a range of resources to help you grow professionally

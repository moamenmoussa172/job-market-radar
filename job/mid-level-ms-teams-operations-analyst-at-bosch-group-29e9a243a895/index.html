Bosch Group · Campinas, Brazil

About the job

  • Administer and manage Microsoft Teams, ensuring integration with Microsoft 365 services
  • Operate and support Microsoft Teams platform including TCQ, Whiteboard, Operator Connect
  • Provide Tier 3 support for Teams telephony, including voice policies, meetings, chat, call quality and collaboration features.
  • Perform day-to-day operational activities such as user provisioning, policy management, and service monitoring for global users.
  • Proactively identify and resolve issues impacting service performance and user experience.
  • Implement and maintain governance, security, and compliance controls within Microsoft Teams.
  • Develop automation using PowerShell and Power Automate to improve operational efficiency.
  • Maintain technical documentation, SOPs, and knowledge base articles
  • Support feature rollouts, updates, and platform enhancements for Teams and associated devices

#LI-MM1

Bachelor’s degree

English fluency

Microsoft 365 Certified: Teams Administrator Associate (preferred)

Experience in Microsoft Teams and UC platforms

Strong experience in troubleshooting Teams issues (meetings, chat, calling, collaboration)

Proven experience in enterprise-level support environments

Experience with PowerShell scripting / Power Automate (similar tools) for automation and administration

Familiarity with support processes, SLA/KPI information, and global support environments

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills

Ability to manage multiple priorities in a collaborative team environment

Proactive mindset and collaborative in working with global team

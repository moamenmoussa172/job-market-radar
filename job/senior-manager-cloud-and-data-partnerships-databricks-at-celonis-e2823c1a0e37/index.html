About the job

Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet.

The Team:

The Cloud & AI Partnerships team is central to Celonis’ growth strategy as we build out our Go-To-Market (GTM) with the major Hyperscalers, AI and Tech companies. Key for that is to effectively enable and activate our partnerships and work together with our field teams to drive joint opportunities, marketing events and sales programs.

For that role we seek a Senior Manager, Cloud & Data Partnerships, based in New York join our team and to manage our global Go-To-Market activities with Databricks.

So come aboard the next generational SaaS company as a Celonaut!

The Role:

  • Define the Joint Business Plan, articulating a clear "better together" value proposition that aligns Celonis’ revenue goals with Partner's consumption targets.
  • Define the GTM strategy to achieve joint goals, including building joint sales motions and programs with Celonis and partner teams.
  • Direct the creation of sales assets and enablement programs. Ensure regional teams have the right battle cards, pitch decks, and use cases to independently identify and drive joint opportunities.
  • Drive Regional Execution: Work with regional Sales Leaders and Partner Managers to execute the strategy locally.
  • Own and orchestrate the relationships with Senior Leadership at both Celonis and their partners.
  • Personally support strategic customer pursuits in collaboration with the Celonis field teams.

The qualifications you need:

  • You have a sales mindset and you are Team-oriented & Entrepreneurial.
  • Combine the ability to develop a holistic strategy and work on operational day to day tasks to execute that strategy and grow our partnerships.
  • Being able to translate and articulate a joint product value proposition to business stakeholders.
  • 5-10 years of relevant work experience in the area of partner management, sales or business development, ideally with Databricks.
  • The ability to excite others and build strong relationships.
  • Studies in Business Administration / Economics / Information Systems is a preference
  • Strong communication skills, verbally & written in English (Native level).

Visa sponsorship is not offered for this role.

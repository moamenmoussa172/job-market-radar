Project Management Degree Apprentice - 42 month Fixed Term Contract

Amazon · London, United Kingdom

Onsite · Intern / Student · Other tech role · Posted 20 November 2026

Apply on Amazon's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

Do you enjoy working collaboratively to solve complex challenges and deliver meaningful change? Are you ready to help lead projects that make a real impact across one of the world's most dynamic organisations? If so, we have an exciting opportunity for you to join Amazon as a Project Management Degree Apprentice.
As a Project Management Apprentice, you'll help deliver real projects that improve processes, enhance customer experience, and drive innovation across Amazon's diverse business lines - all while earning your fully funded BSc (Hons) Degree in Project Management. You'll work alongside experienced project professionals, using recognised methodologies and quality frameworks to bring ideas to life.
Throughout your apprenticeship you'll build a broad, adaptable skillset - stakeholder engagement, governance, financial control, and risk management - and grow from supporting projects to helping lead them. We move fast, fail fast, and fix faster, so you'll be encouraged to contribute ideas and take ownership early.
A day in the life can involve:
- Participating in planning sessions to align on project goals, milestones, and priorities
- Coordinating resources and timelines to keep deliverables on track
- Identifying risks early and collaborating on practical ways to mitigate them
- Tracking progress with data and reporting tools, and recommending improvements
- Presenting updates to stakeholders using dashboards, metrics, and reports
- Building relationships with colleagues and senior leaders across the business
- Supporting process improvement initiatives to simplify workflows and boost efficiency
- Learning directly from experienced project and programme managers through mentorship
We welcome individuals from all backgrounds who bring curiosity, adaptability, and a genuine passion for learning. You'll thrive here if you enjoy solving problems and juggling multiple priorities, communicate clearly with a range of people, and bring strong organisational and analytical skills - able to think strategically while staying on top of the day-to-day detail. You don't need previous project management experience; we're looking for motivated people eager to learn, grow, and make a difference.
Here at Amazon, it's in our nature to learn and be curious. Within this apprenticeship you'll put newfound knowledge into practice every day, supported by first-class coaching, tailored development, and rewarding work that will empower you to reach new heights and build the future you envision.
You'll gain a vast range of experiences and skills while working towards the BSc (Hons) Degree in Project Management. As a working student, you'll spend 80% of your time working in your business area and 20% on dedicated study - developing the skills, knowledge, and behaviours to work towards successful graduation, with goals set by your training provider and manager to support you.
You'll build your skills over a 42-month period, starting in September 2027. Your apprenticeship will combine on-the-job learning at your Amazon base location with structured study, online learning, and mentoring from experienced team members.
About the team
Our Project Management Apprentices work across a diverse range of Amazon teams - from HR and Operations to Technology and Entertainment. You could find yourself supporting people programmes, driving innovation in tech, optimising operational processes, or contributing to projects in our entertainment businesses. We aim to match placements to your skills and interests, but team allocation is based on business need - so flexibility and curiosity are key. Wherever you're based, you'll play an active role in delivering projects that make a real difference.
We value different perspectives and experiences, and we're dedicated to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive. Our employee-led affinity groups – including communities for women in tech, LGBTQ+ employees, and underrepresented minorities – provide community, mentorship, and a platform for your voice to be heard. We're committed to building a workforce that reflects the customers and communities we serve.
We're also continuously raising our performance bar as we strive to become Earth's Best Employer, so you'll have the resources and support to develop into a well-rounded professional.
Your career isn't a straight path – it's a journey you build as you go. A universe of opportunity awaits, and your next adventure starts here.
Please note: as part of your 20% off-the-job learning, you may be required to travel to a training centre, which may differ from your primary work base. Candidates should be prepared for occasional travel as part of the programme.
- To qualify for this programme, you must:
- Be 18 years or over before the start date (September 2027)
- Have the right to live and work in England at the point of enrolment (June 2027). If you are on a visa, this must enable you to legally work and reside in England for the entire duration of the programme from enrolment.
- Have lived in the UK or in the European Economic Area (EEA) for the last 3 years prior to the start date
- Not be registered to study on a UK government funded course ending August 2027 or later
- Be prepared to travel for external/mandatory training as part of the apprenticeship
- Hold 5 GCSEs at grade 4-9 (A-C) or above (or equivalent) including Maths and English and 3 A Levels or equivalent qualifications at grade C or above
- Please note, if you have received educational qualifications outside of the UK then we will require a Statement of Comparability to confirm your eligibility for the scheme. Please ensure you obtain one during the application process, through UK ENIC: https://www.enic.org.uk/
- Previous qualifications will be reviewed for funding eligibility and offers are subject to funding approval
- PLEASE NOTE: This position is a 42 month fixed-term contract. You must NOT already have a qualification in a similar subject at the same or higher level than this apprenticeship.
- This Apprenticeship assumes the candidate has some or little knowledge/experience of the occupation
- A genuine interest in how projects are planned, delivered, and improved
- Excellent organisational skills, with the ability to juggle multiple priorities and deadlines
- A logical, analytical mindset and enjoyment of solving problems
- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage a range of stakeholders
- A collaborative, team-first approach
- Comfortable working with data to track progress and inform decisions
- The ability to think strategically while staying on top of day-to-day detail
- A growth mindset - adaptable, open to feedback, and eager to learn new tools and ways of working
- Evidence of leading or organising something - e.g. a group project, event, society, team, or community initiative

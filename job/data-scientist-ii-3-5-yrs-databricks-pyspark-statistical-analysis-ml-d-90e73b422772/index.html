About the job

What you'll do

In the Reporting and Data Analytics Team of the Maps Platform and Operations department you will use a modern data architecture to:

  • Analyze data from various internal sources and define metrics
  • Design, develop and maintain data processing pipelines to use these data sources individually or combined
  • Develop processes and tools to monitor and analyze model and system performance and data accuracy
  • Create and or improve descriptive, predictive, and potentially prescriptive analytics
  • Provide outcome in a clear and concise manner to stakeholders

What you'll need

  • 3+ years of proven working experience as a data scientist
  • Thorough knowledge of modern data platforms
  • Experience in designing, developing, and monitoring stable and performant software with minimum guidance
  • Knowledge of statistics and experience using statistical packages for analyzing large data sets and applying predictive analytics
  • Experience with Scala, Python, SQL
  • Background in mathematics, economics, computer science, engineering, information management or statistics
  • Effective English written and verbal communication skills
  • Agentic coding

What’s nice to have

  • Experience using BI tools
  • operational management background
  • operational research
  • Prior experience with working with geospatial and map data

About the job

Role Summary
The Senior POS Integrations Support Specialist, EU is responsible for ensuring the seamless operation and troubleshooting of food and beverage (F&B) point-of-sale systems and integration interfaces across the European region. The role involves managing and prioritizing support tickets in Salesforce, escalating complex issues to Level 2 (L2) teams, and actively utilizing REST APIs and telemetry tools (Grafana, Postman) to identify and resolve system defects.
Key Responsibilities

  • Ticket Management and Prioritization: Handle, monitor, and resolve incoming support tickets in Salesforce while strictly adhering to SLAs, escalating issues to L2 teams when necessary.
  • POS Integration Troubleshooting: Perform technical diagnostics and resolve connectivity and data exchange issues between F&B POS terminals, kitchen systems, and back-end applications.
  • API Investigation and Testing: Identify network and integration issues via REST API endpoints, send test requests, and verify response codes using Postman.
  • Vendor and Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with internal product development teams and local F&B operations managers to ensure continuous business operations.

Requirements

  • Experience in supporting or configuring POS systems within an F&B (restaurant, hospitality, or hotel) operational environment.
  • Solid understanding of REST API architectures, with hands-on experience in API testing and log analysis using Postman.
  • Experience with ticketing and CRM platforms, specifically daily hands-on use of Salesforce (Service Cloud/Cases).
  • Working knowledge of Grafana (or a similar monitoring platform) for tracking system metrics and errors.
  • Excellent communication, prioritization, and problem-solving skills in a multi-market, international environment.
  • Professional working proficiency in English, both written and spoken.

Your Benefits

  • Modern office in Budapest’s Mill Park – easy to reach, even by bike!
  • International good vibe + strong corporate background = a secure & inspiring place to grow.
  • From day one: life, accident & health insurance (MetLife).
  • SZÉP-card & Medicare health package.
  • foodora PRO & vouchers – tasty meals, even in the home office.
  • Mental health support & relaxing office massages.
  • In-house English lessons + LinkedIn Learning access.
  • Extra days off, AYCM partnership & all tools provided.
  • Social impact matters: we support 6 charities and you can join in!

About the job

We are looking for an experienced SAP Security & GRC Lead to lead all aspects of SAP Security, User Access Management, and Governance across the SAP Finance Platform landscape.

The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on expertise in SAP GRC and extensive experience designing, managing, and governing user access across multiple SAP platforms and modules. This role will be responsible for both day-to-day SAP Security operations and security-related projects, while ensuring appropriate access controls, compliance, and governance standards are maintained across the SAP environment.

Requirements

  • Lead all aspects of SAP Security, User Access Management, and Access Governance across the SAP Finance Platform.
  • Own and manage security and user-access activities across a diverse SAP landscape, including:
  • SAP S/4HANA
  • SAP Datasphere
  • SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)
  • SAP Profitability and Performance Management (PaPM)
  • SAP Group Reporting
  • SAP FS-RI
  • SAP Master Data Governance (MDG)
  • Other integrated SAP applications and modules
  • Provide functional and technical leadership for the SAP Security team, covering both operational support and project delivery.
  • Manage user provisioning, de-provisioning, role assignments, access changes, and periodic access reviews.
  • Design, maintain, and govern SAP roles and authorization concepts, including business and technical roles.
  • Lead the implementation, configuration, administration, and ongoing management of SAP GRC capabilities.
  • Manage Segregation of Duties (SoD) risks, access-risk analysis, mitigating controls, and remediation activities.
  • Support SAP GRC Access Control processes such as access requests, approvals, risk analysis, and emergency/privileged access.
  • Establish and maintain consistent access governance standards and controls across SAP platforms.
  • Work closely with Finance, IT, Internal Audit, Risk, Compliance, application teams, and business stakeholders on security requirements.
  • Support internal and external audits by providing appropriate access-control documentation and evidence.
  • Identify security and access-control gaps and drive remediation plans.
  • Lead SAP Security activities for implementations, upgrades, integrations, enhancements, and transformation projects.
  • Manage security-related incidents, operational issues, and escalations.
  • Ensure SAP security processes and controls are properly documented and consistently followed.

Required Skills & Experience

  • Strong hands-on experience in SAP Security and Authorization.
  • Advanced knowledge and practical experience with SAP GRC, particularly SAP GRC Access Control.
  • Strong understanding of:
  • User Access Management
  • Identity and Access Governance
  • Role Design and Administration
  • Segregation of Duties (SoD)
  • Access Risk Analysis
  • Mitigating Controls
  • Emergency/Privileged Access
  • Access Reviews and Auditing
  • Experience managing security across multiple SAP applications and cloud/on-premises environments.
  • SAP S/4HANA security and authorization experience.
  • Experience with security/access models for platforms such as Datasphere, SAC, PaPM, Group Reporting, FS-RI, and MDG.
  • Experience managing both SAP Security operations and project implementation activities.
  • Strong understanding of security controls, audit requirements, and governance processes.
  • Ability to work with business, technical, audit, risk, and compliance stakeholders.
  • Strong troubleshooting, analytical, communication, and leadership skills.
  • Experience leading or mentoring SAP Security professionals.

Preferred / Good-to-Have Skills

  • Experience with large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformation programs.
  • Experience integrating SAP GRC with multiple SAP systems.
  • Knowledge of SAP Fiori security and authorization concepts.
  • Experience with identity-management or provisioning solutions integrated with SAP.
  • Finance-domain exposure and understanding of financial access-control requirements.
  • Experience supporting security audits and regulatory/compliance requirements.

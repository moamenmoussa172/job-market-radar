About the job

About the role
The Ring Blink ASIC team is looking for a Design Verification Intern to be part of a silicon development for the wide range of AI powered camera products used by millions of users worldwide.
This is an opportunity to work alongside experienced engineers and learn how a chip goes from idea to silicon. You'll get an exposure to product specifications, architecture and how designs are built and verified before they ship in real products. Along the way you'll get hands-on experience with SystemVerilog-based verification, verification methodologies, simulation environments, testbench development and functional sign off.
The role suits students exploring a career in ASIC/SoC development.
Key job responsibilities
-Develop design verification solutions such as testbench components, checkers and test sequences, using SystemVerilog, UVM, formal or similar methods
-Use AI agents to automate and reinvent verification workflows.
-Support automation flows and continuous regression infrastructure
- - Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering or a related field
- - Programming experience
- • Enrolled in a Master’s degree program in Electrical Engineering or Computer Engineering • Experience implementing computer architecture concepts and testing Verilog designs • Proficiency with a scripting language (Python or Perl) • Experience testing Verilog designs • Programming experience in C or C++

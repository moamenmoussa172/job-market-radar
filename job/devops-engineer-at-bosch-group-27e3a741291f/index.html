About the job

We are seeking a highly motivated individual to join us as a DevOps Engineer. In this role, you will build and continuously improve the infrastructure, tooling, and automated CI/CD pipelines that enable secure, reliable, and efficient software delivery. Working closely with developers and engineers, you will drive infrastructure as code, integrate automated testing and security best practices, and resolve infrastructure and deployment challenges. This position combines strong technical expertise with a collaborative mindset and a passion for automation and continuous improvement.

  • Build, set up, and maintain the infrastructure and tooling required for software development.
  • Design, develop, and improve automated CI/CD pipelines for development and release processes.
  • Implement and enforce security best practices to ensure systems are safe and secure against cybersecurity threats.
  • Enable and automate testing processes within the pipeline to ensure code quality and reliability.
  • Identify, troubleshoot, and apply fixes to technical problems within the infrastructure and deployment pipelines.
  • Work closely with software developers and engineers to ensure that development follows established processes and works as intended.
  • Manage source code, automate infrastructure provisioning, and drive infrastructure as code (IaC) principles.
  • Participate in project planning to share knowledge of system options, risks, and potential improvements.
  • Define and communicate technical requirements for infrastructure and deployment processes
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Informatics, or equivalent.
  • 3+ years of experience as a DevOps Engineer.
  • Advanced knowledge and hands-on experience with AWS or Azure.
  • Proficiency in building and managing CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins.
  • In-depth knowledge of configuration management (Ansible) and monitoring stacks (Prometheus, Grafana).
  • Advanced knowledge of containers (Docker, Podman) and orchestration (Kubernetes).
  • Good understanding of search technologies like OpenSearch or Elasticsearch.
  • Experience managing and deploying SQL and NoSQL databases in a cloud environment.
  • Working proficiency with build tools (Maven, npm) and version control (Git).
  • Experience with REST API integration.
  • Deep understanding of microservices architecture and continuous delivery principles.
  • Experience breaking down complex requirements into technical tasks and providing estimates.
  • Ability to effectively communicate technical challenges to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking skills.

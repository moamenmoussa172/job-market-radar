About the job

Every product capability in Marketplace Experience shapes how millions of customers, riders, and vendors get support and resolve live issues — directly driving Glovo’s contact ratio, unit economics, and user retention. Over the next 12 to 18 months, you will operate as the right hand to the Senior Director of Marketplace Experience and the central bridge uniting Customer, Rider, and Vendor Experience with Glovo Product, Delivery Hero Product, and Global Service Ops. You will own how MX sizes, challenges, and prioritizes product investments across local and global tech stacks while running our executive operating rhythm and leading our highest-impact transversal programs.

THE JOURNEY

  • Orchestrate Product Demand Across MX & Tech: Structure, challenge, and consolidate product requests from Customer Experience, Rider Experience, and Vendor Experience into crisp, impact-sized requirements in partnership with Glovo Product and Delivery Hero (DH) Product.
  • Own DH Product Prioritization for Glovo MX: Build rigorous business cases (sizing contact ratio, cost per order, self-serve automation, satisfaction, and fraud/compensation impact) to make explicit cross-vertical trade-offs and secure Glovo MX priorities on DH Product roadmaps.
  • Serve as Primary POC with Global Service Ops (GSO): Partner closely with DH’s Global Service Ops domain experts to align global service tooling, self-service flows, and capabilities with Glovo’s market needs, driving smooth rollouts and measurable adoption.
  • Run the MX Operating Rhythm & Executive Narratives: Act as thought partner and right hand to the MX Senior Director — owning WBR, MBR, and Masterplan cycles, challenging KPI performance across verticals, spotting gaps early, and writing sharp executive narratives for senior leadership.
  • Drive High-Impact Transversal Initiatives: Personally scope and lead 2–3 strategic cross-MX and product-operations programs per quarter end-to-end, from ambiguous problem framing and analytical deep dives to cross-market launch.
  • Raise the Analytical & Governance Bar: Design scalable ROI frameworks, KPI trees, and post-launch impact tracking so engineering and operational capacity consistently goes to the highest-return bets.

WHAT YOU WILL BRING TO THE RIDE

Must have

  • 5+ years of experience in Product Operations, Program/Project Management (partnering closely with Product), Strategy & BizOps, Product Management, or strategy consulting within tech or multi-sided marketplaces.
  • Outstanding analytical and business-modeling skills: you independently size P&L and operational trade-offs in Google Sheets/Excel, interrogate BI dashboards, and turn messy data into clear prioritization calls.
  • Proven track record of coordinating product roadmaps, requirements, and delivery across Product/Engineering and multiple business or operational teams without formal authority.
  • Crisp executive communication and synthesis: you distill complex, multi-stakeholder topics into answer-first memos and presentations ready for senior leadership.
  • High ownership, structured thinking, comfort pushing back constructively with data, and bias for action in fast-paced environments.
  • Full professional proficiency in English.

Nice to have

  • Experience in multi-sided marketplaces (food delivery, Q-commerce, mobility, e-commerce) or customer/rider/partner support operations and service tooling.
  • Experience navigating multi-entity or group/platform product organizations (bridging local market operations with global product teams).
  • Working knowledge of SQL for ad-hoc data validation, or hands-on experience building AI/automation workflows to streamline operations.
  • Proficiency in Spanish.

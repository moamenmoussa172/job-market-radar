About the job

Job Ref ID: JR0090010

As the Security Operations Analyst within the Security function, you will manage security compliance obligations for Yemeksepeti in Turkey, including Turkish cybersecurity, data protection and internet regulation, and coordinate ongoing communication with the relevant public authorities.

You will be based in Istanbul and report to the Regional CISO.

You will work effectively with Legal, Procurement and IT teams, and coordinate with teams abroad in English.

This role will cover areas such as:

  • Compliance with Cybersecurity Law No. 7545, KVKK and Law No. 5651
  • Information security policies and procedures
  • Information systems inventory, vulnerability and incident tracking
  • Log correlation and cyber incident detection as part of the corporate CSIRT (SOME)
  • Monitoring of sectoral CSIRT and Cybersecurity Directorate announcements
  • Supplier and procurement security controls

The ideal candidate combines strong reporting, communication and analytical thinking skills with a solid grounding in network, security and log analysis, and a strong awareness of confidentiality.

  • At least 3 years cyber security experience.
  • Meet the knowledge, skills and competencies defined under the Vocational Qualifications Authority (MYK) Cybersecurity Specialist (Level 5) National Occupational Standard (19UMS0740-5) and National Qualification (23UY0544-5), and comply with any updates to the standard and qualification
  • Knowledge of network and security devices, operating systems and log analysis
  • Knowledge of vulnerability, risk and incident management
  • Good command of English: able to handle correspondence, meetings and coordination with teams abroad
  • Ability to work effectively with Legal, Procurement and IT teams
  • Knowledge of the Cybersecurity Law, KVKK and ISO 27001
  • Reporting, communication and analytical thinking skills; strong awareness of confidentiality

Preferred Qualifications (Although not essential)

  • MYK Vocational Qualification Certificate
  • CCNA, CCNP, NSE 4-7, CCSA, CCSE or similar certifications
  • ISO 27001 Lead Implementer/Auditor
  • Relevant degree and SOC/compliance experience

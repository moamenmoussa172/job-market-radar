Position Overview

We are looking for an experienced SAP Basis Automation & AI Engineer with experience in SAP Basis administration, automation, and Linux/Unix environments. The ideal candidate combines strong hands-on SAP Basis expertise with a passion for automation and emerging technologies. This role will focus on automating repetitive SAP Basis activities, reducing manual intervention, improving operational efficiency, and leveraging Ansible, Python, Linux shell scripting, and AI tools to modernize SAP operations. The successful candidate will also be a strong team player who enjoys sharing knowledge, collaborating with others, and helping team members develop their technical skills.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, implement, and maintain automation solutions for SAP Basis operations.
  • Identify repetitive, manual, and error-prone SAP Basis tasks and develop automation to minimize human intervention.
  • Develop and maintain Ansible playbooks, roles, modules, and automation workflows for SAP environments.
  • Develop robust Linux/Unix shell scripts to automate SAP administration, system health checks, monitoring, maintenance, deployments, housekeeping, and operational activities.
  • Use Python to develop automation tools, scripts, utilities, integrations, and APIs.
  • Make extensive use of Linux shell scripting such as Bash to automate complex SAP Basis and operating-system-level tasks.
  • Design reusable shell scripts that can be integrated with Ansible and other enterprise automation frameworks.
  • Troubleshoot and optimize Linux-based automation and scripting solutions.
  • Explore and apply AI and Generative AI tools to improve SAP operations, troubleshooting, monitoring, documentation, scripting, and automation.
  • Investigate opportunities to use AI-assisted solutions to reduce operational effort and improve the speed and consistency of SAP support.
  • Automate activities such as SAP system health checks; System monitoring and validation; Start/stop procedures; Log analysis; Housekeeping; File-system checks and management; SAP parameter and configuration checks; Routine maintenance activities; System availability checks; Reporting and operational dashboards; and Repetitive troubleshooting and remediation activities
  • Work closely with SAP Basis engineers and other IT teams to identify and prioritize automation opportunities.
  • Develop reusable automation components and establish automation standards and best practices.
  • Maintain source code and automation assets usingGit or other version-control systems.
  • Troubleshoot SAP, Linux, scripting, and automation-related issues.
  • Document automation solutions, scripts, processes, and operational procedures.
  • Actively share technical knowledge and best practices with team members.
  • Mentor and assist colleagues in learning SAP Basis, Linux scripting, Ansible, Python, and automation technologies.
  • Participate in continuous improvement initiatives and identify new opportunities to reduce manual operational effort.

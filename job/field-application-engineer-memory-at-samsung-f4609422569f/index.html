About the job

Position Summary

Samsung Memory is a leading force in the semiconductor industry, showcasing its unwavering commitment to innovation and technological excellence. From the volatile DRAM that supports modern computing to the non-volatile NAND that enables data storage, Samsung's memory solutions are at the core of our data-driven world. With next-generation high-performance, high-capacity, and low-power products, Samsung is pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the semiconductor industry.
As a member of the Technical Marketing Team in the Field Application Engineer role, you will analyze the EMEA market, understand customer expectations, and identify opportunities to drive business growth. You will deliver technical support and guidance, providing prompt solutions to technical issues to maximize orders for the company's products and services across various B2B markets, including Data Centre, Servers, PC, Networking, and Embedded.

Role and Responsibilities

  • Understand customers' requirements, short-, mid-, and long-term objectives and challenges via regular customer calls and on-site meetings
  • Promote new products, technical developments, and provide guidance to customers
  • Support all technical activities until mass production, including answering technical questions, handling documentation, samples, conducting regular technical meetings with customers, tracking progress and milestones, driving internal development and quality teams to fulfill customers' requirements, and managing troubleshooting with HQ.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with key decision-makers within the customer's organization.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including sales, technical/product marketing, and executive management, to ensure customer needs are met and a trusted partnership is established.
  • Identify and report future trends, new demands, and competition
  • Follow-up and attend industry events

Key Stakeholders:

  • Memory Marketing and Sales team
  • Technical marketing and QA team
  • Samsung Semiconductor Korea Technical Marketing Team
  • Major European Customers & Distributor

Skills and Qualifications

  • A Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Electronics or related disciplines
  • Demonstrated technical expertise in the semiconductor industry and computing systems such as Server, PC, and Embedded systems
  • In-depth knowledge of DRAM and NAND technologies is required
  • Comprehensive understanding of the IT industry (Server and DC markets), particularly in the memory sector
  • Minimum 7 years of experience in Field Application Engineer (FAE) or similar positions in Sales and Marketing functions
  • Strong communication and presentation skills, both written and verbal
  • Strong technical marketing and business development capabilities, along with commercial sense
  • Effective interpersonal and negotiation skills
  • Welcomes working in a multi-cultural and high intensity environment

What we offer
Samsung is a global leader in technology, opening new possibilities for people everywhere. In our center you will be part of a dynamic team, in an international work environment. Being one of the best in the industry comes with hard work, but we also make it rewarding through:

  • Compensation: attractive salary and performance bonus
  • Free breakfasts and subsidized lunches in our Munich office
  • Flexible working hours/ telecommuting opportunity
  • Health & wellbeing: wellness program, e.g. gym subscription
  • Quarterly team events and various team activities

About Us
SAMSUNG Semiconductor Europe, a wholly owned subsidiary of SAMSUNG Electronics Co. Ltd., has it's headquarter in Munich, Germany and operates offices all over Europe, Middle East & Africa (EMEA). The European headquarter is handling the marketing and sales activities of Samsung Electronics' component business units including Memory, System LSI, Foundry, LED and Display in EMEA for various applications including servers, data centers, mobile, industrial / automotive.

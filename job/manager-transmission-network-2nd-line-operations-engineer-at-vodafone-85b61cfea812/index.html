About this Role

We are seeking an experienced Transmission Network Operations professional to provide second-line NOC support across multi-vendor Access Transmission networks supporting Mobile (UK) and Enterprise (Ireland) services.

The role focuses on advanced network troubleshooting, incident and fault resolution, change management, network performance and service restoration across IP and transmission technologies including BGP, IS-IS, MPLS, SDH, DWDM, OTN and Microwave. You will work across equipment from vendors including Cisco, Nokia, Ericsson, SIAE, ADVA, Huawei, Tellabs, Alcatel and Marconi, while collaborating with first-line operations, field teams, network planning, design, provisioning and equipment vendors.

What you’ll do

  • Provide second-line NOC operational support for Access Transmission networks in a multi-vendor environment covering Mobile (UK) and Enterprise (Ireland) services.
  • Perform advanced troubleshooting of complex transmission network faults and incidents, supporting resolution within agreed SLAs.
  • Manage incidents, faults and network changes, including detailed analysis and service restoration.
  • Support networks using BGP, IS-IS, MPLS, SDH, DWDM, OTN and Microwave technologies.
  • Operate and troubleshoot equipment across vendors including Cisco, Nokia, Ericsson, SIAE Microwave, ADVA, Huawei, Tellabs, Alcatel, Marconi and Timepictra.
  • Collaborate with design teams on Line-of-Sight blocked cases, tree-lopping requirements and network re-routing scenarios.
  • Test new software releases in a laboratory environment before deployment to the live network.
  • Support FSI, SSI and new vendor/product onboarding, including ADVA, Ericsson Microwave, Cisco 540 and Nokia IXR routers.
  • Prepare technical Methods of Procedure (MOPs) for network configurations and operational processes, and provide knowledge transfer and training to first-line Transmission Operations teams.
  • Work with equipment vendors including Cisco, SIAE, Ericsson, Chronos and Penta on critical faults and software or hardware issues, supporting escalation and Root Cause Analysis (RCA).
  • Support legacy network decommissioning involving Marconi, Alcatel, Tellabs, Tarana, Timepictra and Siemens Microwave equipment, alongside the IP-fication of sites.
  • Carry out software upgrades, hardware changes, configuration changes and configuration restoration across network infrastructure.
  • Manage new network elements within EMS/NMS platforms and troubleshoot Microwave-related faults.
  • Maintain configuration backups for Cisco, Tellabs, Alcatel, ADVA and Nokia equipment.
  • Monitor the performance of network elements and circuits for errors, alarms and service outages, implementing appropriate preventive and corrective actions.
  • Analyse logs and technical evidence to determine root causes for critical network outages.
  • Plan network change activities relating to configuration and commissioning of telecommunications equipment.
  • Collaborate with Network Planning teams on project roll-outs, capacity enhancements, circuit resiliency and new-site integrations.
  • Provide technical support to field engineers during service-restoration activities.
  • Conduct network health checks and produce operational reports to identify potential or emerging faults before they impact services.
  • Develop and improve procedures for alarm analysis and fault elimination in collaboration with first-line Operations teams.
  • Work with Cramer and provisioning teams to maintain accurate network data.
  • Participate in regular customer and equipment-vendor reviews covering critical cases and network outages.

Who you are

You have practical experience in telecommunications network operations, ideally within a second-line NOC or Transmission Operations environment, with the ability to investigate and resolve complex network incidents.

You bring relevant knowledge or experience across:

  • IP networking: BGP, IS-IS and MPLS.
  • Optical and transmission technologies: SDH, DWDM and OTN.
  • Microwave transmission networks and associated troubleshooting.
  • Multi-vendor network equipment, particularly technologies from Cisco, Nokia, Ericsson, SIAE, ADVA, Huawei and Tellabs.
  • Advanced second-line troubleshooting, fault isolation and service restoration.
  • Incident, fault and change management processes within an operational network environment.
  • Network performance monitoring, alarm analysis and preventive fault management.
  • Root Cause Analysis through network logs, alarms and technical evidence.
  • Software upgrades, hardware changes, configuration management and restoration.
  • EMS/NMS management and network configuration backup processes.
  • Creation and execution of technical MOPs.
  • Collaboration with first-line NOC teams, field engineers, design, network planning, provisioning, customers and technology vendors.

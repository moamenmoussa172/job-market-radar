About the Role

Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies. We’re looking for a Senior Systems Engineer, Customer Support Systems who will play a key role in driving our Customer Support technology.

As part of our Systems Engineering team, you will build and maintain the integrations, automations, and custom applications that power our Customer Support Systems, anchored by Zendesk. You’ll write code to solve support workflow problems, administer and configure the platform when needed, and primarily work closely with Customer Support, but also on occasion with Product Operations, RevOps, Legal, and other partner teams.

You’ll join a small team and work alongside a senior systems engineer to learn from and collaborate with, sharing ownership of our Zendesk environment. You’ll be a backup administrator, so support operations stay covered when either of you is out.

Technologies You’ll Use

Zendesk (including Knowledge and Community), BigQuery, Salesforce, Jira, Slack, and Okta are systems you’ll use day-to-day. You’ll write code in languages such as Python and TypeScript, work with REST APIs and webhooks, and use Git and code review as part of your normal workflow.

What You’ll Do

  • Design, build, and maintain custom integrations, automations, and applications between Zendesk and other systems using REST APIs and webhooks
  • Write tested, documented, version-controlled code, and take part in code review
  • Monitor and troubleshoot integrations and Zendesk in production, finding root causes and implementing timely fixes, with error handling, retries, and alerting
  • Administer and configure Zendesk, including users, roles, groups, and licenses, applying least-privilege access in coordination with IT and Okta/SSO
  • Assess how changes to fields, forms, and picklists affect downstream systems (BigQuery, Salesforce, Slack, internal apps), and coordinate with the owning teams before release
  • Author technical design documents and maintain documentation for larger changes, migrations, and system configuration
  • Enable and govern AI agent integrations with Zendesk, including the tools and data pipelines that support them
  • Triage and prioritize requests through Jira and sprint planning, delivering changes in line with Fivetran’s Feature Development Life Cycle

Skills We’re Looking For

  • 3+ years of professional software development experience in Python, JavaScript, or a similar language, including building and supporting integrations or internal tools
  • Experience with REST APIs and webhooks, including authentication (OAuth, API tokens) and rate limiting
  • Experience with Zendesk or a comparable support platform, including configuration and app or integration development
  • Familiarity with testing, CI/CD, and deploying small services or functions to a cloud platform
  • SQL and general data literacy
  • Disciplined change management: impact analysis, sandbox testing, and staged rollouts
  • Experience administering user access and permissions in a SaaS platform, including SSO
  • Ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions, with strong written communication for a globally distributed team

Bonus Skills

  • Prior Customer Support experience. This is not a user-facing role, but it helps when building solutions for Support teams
  • Experience with AI to enhance support, such as routing, triage, and language model-based helpers
  • Experience building tools or agents that use large language models
  • Salesforce experience
  • Public company experience and a working understanding of system and process controls, such as access reviews

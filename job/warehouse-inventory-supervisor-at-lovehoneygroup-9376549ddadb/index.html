We are currently looking for a Warehouse Supervisor who will be integral to driving high-level performance across Lovehoney Group. Partnering closely with the Warehouse leadership team, your focus will be on supervising day-to-day warehouse operations across Goods In, Returns and Inventory, ensuring work is completed safely, accurately and on time. In this role, you will combine your people leadership and operational skills with your ability to plan resources, manage performance and maintain a positive and professional working environment.

What you will do

  • Oversee daily Warehouse operations across Goods In, Returns and Inventory, allocating resources and prioritising workload to meet operational and service requirements.
  • Monitor productivity, quality, service and inventory performance, using operational data to identify issues, implement corrective actions and support continuous improvement.
  • Manage and develop assigned Warehouse colleagues, including probation reviews, one-to-ones, performance discussions, return-to-work meetings, attendance and holiday management, as well as supporting recruitment, onboarding and training.
  • Own shift administration and operational reporting, including KPI and productivity reporting, EOS documentation, stock investigations, inventory adjustments and workforce planning, ensuring the Warehouse remains audit ready.
  • Ensure compliance with Company Policies, Site Rules, HSE requirements and local legislation, promoting a positive safety culture while supporting operational escalations and acting as Key Holder, First Aider or Fire Marshal where required.
  • What you should bring
  • Experience working within a Warehouse, Distribution or Fulfilment environment, with proven experience leading, coaching or developing individuals or teams in a fast-paced operational setting.
  • Strong understanding of Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), ERP systems and Microsoft Office, including Excel; NetSuite experience is preferred.
  • Ability to plan, prioritise and coordinate people, workload and operational activities, while analysing performance data to improve productivity, quality and service.
  • Experience with people management activities such as performance reviews, probation meetings, return-to-work discussions or employee investigations is desirable.
  • Excellent communication, organisation and problem-solving skills, with a positive and professional approach to leadership, accountability and managing competing priorities.

About the job

We help the world run better
At SAP, we keep it simple: you bring your best to us, and we'll bring out the best in you. We're builders touching over 20 industries and 80% of global commerce, and we need your unique talents to help shape what's next. The work is challenging – but it matters. You'll find a place where you can be yourself, prioritize your wellbeing, and truly belong. What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.

You bring deep expertise in SAP engagement delivery and governance strategy, ready to operate as a senior delivery authority and lead programmes to lasting customer value. This role is for you.

What you’ll bring

  • 12+ years leading complex SAP service engagements, navigating commercial realities and aligning delivery to customer objectives.
  • Deep customer success capability spanning the full engagement lifecycle, with a focus on retention, growth, and satisfaction.
  • Proven C-level influencing skills, establishing advisory relationships, managing escalations, and sustaining strategic partnerships.
  • Value management discipline, quantifying SAP business impact through co-developed scorecards for senior stakeholders.
  • Time-boxed engagement orchestration: 90-day cycles with clear milestones, fixed scope, and a focus on rapid value delivery.
  • Fluency across SAP BTP, S/4HANA, and adjacent landscapes, guiding customers through complex integration and architecture decisions.
  • Emotional intelligence and sound problem-solving, reading dynamics, de-escalating tension, and resolving issues before they affect outcomes.
  • Demonstrated Core AI Skills: Agentic AI, Context Engineering, AI-Assisted Automation, AI Adoption Capability, and AI Output Quality Assurance, assessed via a Codility take-home assignment.

What you’ll build

You lead high-stakes SAP service engagements across a strategic portfolio, governing delivery, guiding adoption, and keeping programmes on track.

  • Design and execute governance models that keep SAP programmes aligned to customer objectives and focused on innovation.
  • Lead SAP solution adoption across your portfolio, managing timelines, mitigating risk, and resolving critical issues before they affect outcomes.
  • Serve as the trusted advisor to C-level stakeholders, reinforcing partnership through consistent value communication.
  • Communicate value realisation at every stage, making the SAP investment case tangible and credible.
  • Orchestrate time-boxed engagements, maintaining milestone discipline and scope integrity across complex programmes.
  • Apply integration expertise to accelerate time-to-value on SAP BTP and S/4HANA, guiding customers through complexity.
  • Embed AI-assisted tools and agentic workflows to raise delivery quality and pace, modelling AI adoption for the team.

Where you belong

At SAP, you join a global community of delivery professionals who believe great outcomes are built through expertise, trust, and follow-through. You work with SAP’s most strategic customers, at the intersection of technology and long-term partnership. Your impact is yours to own.

If you thrive when the stakes are high, the work is complex, and outcomes are real, you belong here.

About the job

Your Contribution to Something Big

  • Support data analysis and reporting across material management, planning, logistics, and repair processes
  • Collect, process, and analyze data to support data-driven decisions and process improvements
  • Create reports and interactive dashboards using Power BI or Tableau to monitor KPIs and operational performance
  • Contribute to process digitalization, data modeling, and workflow automation using Python and other digital tools
  • Take part in AI and machine learning projects, including predictive analytics and anomaly detection
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify improvement opportunities and implement data-driven solutions

What Distinguishes You

  • 3rd or 4th-year undergraduate or postgraduate student in Industrial Engineering, Information Systems Engineering, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or related fields
  • Strong interest in data analytics, AI, machine learning, and digitalization
  • Familiarity with Power BI or Tableau for data visualization and reporting
  • Basic knowledge of Python, SQL, and data modeling
  • Familiarity with Agentic AI concepts or applications is an asset
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Very good command of English

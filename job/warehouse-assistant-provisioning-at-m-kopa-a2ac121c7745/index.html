Warehouse Assistant - Provisioning

M-KOPA · Delta, Nigeria

Onsite · Other tech role · Posted 20 October 2026

Apply on M-KOPA's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

We are looking for a Warehouse Assistant to join our Warehouse team; as we scale up and drive digital and financial inclusion across our markets.


The role

As Warehouse Assistant, you receive and process incoming stock and materials, carry out stock counts, oversee provisioning officers and manage their SLAs, and resolve provisioning issues. The role sits in Warehouse & Logistics within Supply Chain, and the preferred candidate should be based in Asaba, Delta State.

About Us

M-KOPA is a pan-African fintech providing asset financing and credit services across Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa. We have reached 10 million customers, extended $2B+ in credit, and work with 2,300+ employees and 35,000+ field agents. 86% of our customers are satisfied, and 55% are accessing formal financial services for the first time.

This is a on-site role, you would be working from our offices in Nigeria, partnering with a diverse group of employees from our different markets and locations across the UK, Europe and Africa. You will be reporting to the Warehouse Lead.

What You'll Do

  • Receive OEM deliveries, verify them against the delivery note, and flag damage or shortages.
  • Label and store goods, update bin cards, and maintain FIFO on the floor.
  • Work with IT and the App testing team to run flashing tools (Odin/HMD Toolkit) and app installation (Cygwin) on all provisioning systems for device enrolment.
  • Support serial verification and keep provisioning desktops updated within the 24-hour firmware SLA.
  • Package devices (QSG, casing, screen protector, seal) and complete final QC checks.
  • Oversee provisioning officers, manage their SLAs, and resolve provisioning issues.
  • Produce daily, weekly and monthly throughput and stock count reports.
  • Report to the Warehouse Lead and jointly verify materials with Handlers.
  • Hit the standards: a 24-hour firmware update SLA, 300 devices per operator per day, and zero hand-over discrepancy.

What You'll Need

  • A Diploma in Store/Warehouse Management or Procurement
  • 2 years’ work experience in a similar position
  • Hands-on experience with an inventory management system, and working knowledge of Microsoft Office tools
  • Strong stock management, problem solving, time management and multitasking skills, along with the conflict management, negotiation and collaboration needed to keep a busy floor working together.

The honest part

This role is demanding. The SLAs are tight, the daily targets are real, and there is little room for error between a delivery note and a customer’s device. You will be managing people and pressure at the same time. If you take pride in getting the count right every time, you are probably ready for exactly this.

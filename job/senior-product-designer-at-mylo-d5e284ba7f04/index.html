About the job

mylo is a fintech platform dedicated to helping millions of people and businesses thrive by providing accessible and responsible financial solutions. Whether you’re purchasing a mobile phone, a new jacket, a flight ticket, a comfy couch, or even covering school tuition, mylo enables you to buy now and pay later at thousands of points of sale across Egypt. Born out of B.TECH—Egypt’s leading electronics and appliances retailer with over 27 years of experience in offering buy now, pay later solutions—mylo brings a legacy of trust and innovation to the fintech space. All mylo products are fully Sharia-compliant, ensuring ethical and inclusive financial practices.
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to lead the end-to-end design strategy.

You’ll be responsible for creating intuitive, elegant, and user-centric experiences that directly impact product success and customer satisfaction.

As a Senior Product Designer, you'll collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, researchers, and key stakeholders to define and execute design solutions that align with business goals. You'll take ownership of the full design lifecycle—from discovery and ideation to prototyping, user testing, and final delivery. You'll continuously explore new ways to improve our products through research, interaction design, and visual polish.

We're looking for someone who thrives in a fast-paced, iterative environment and has a proven track record of solving complex UX challenges while balancing user needs with technical constraints and business objectives.

If you're a strategic thinker who’s passionate about user experience and product design—and you're ready to make a significant impact—we’d love to hear from you.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience as a Senior Product Designer, with a portfolio showcasing your work on complex projects.
  • Advanced proficiency in design software and deep knowledge of interaction design principles.
  • Proven ability to solve complex design problems.
  • Outstanding communication skills, with an ability to drive design conversations at a strategic level.
  • Robust experience in conducting comprehensive user research and usability testing.

Benefits

Office environment: When you come to our mylo office, you'll find creative workspaces, a well-equipped kitchen, and an open design to foster collaboration between teams.

Flexibility: You know best whether you want to work from home or in the office. However, if you want to join your teammates onsite, we will subsidize the costs for lunch to foster the mylo office community

Equipment: From "Day 1" you will receive all the equipment you need be successful at work. Therefore, you can choose your laptop and get all the tools you need to stay on top of your game.

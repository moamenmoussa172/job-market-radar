About the job

Your primary objective as our Game Security Programmer is to develop and improve new and/or existing security solutions in order to secure Ubisoft portfolio of multiplayer games.

What you will do:

  • Work in a dedicated team of passionate engineers with experienced leadership
  • Develop game security software to ensure fair environments for Ubisoft's players
  • Acquire a strong knowledge of existing game security solutions
  • Collaborate with other teams to stay on top your challenges
  • Collaborate with game production teams to ensure the quality of our service
  • Act as a source of subject matter advice towards the organisation

What you bring:

  • Proficient in C/C++, C# and Python, expert in one or more
  • Experience working with Windows Internals
  • Experienced in game development, engine development, anti-tampering-, anti-cheat- or anti-malware software
  • Passionate about software security, anti-cheat or anti-malware technologies
  • Excellent in oral and written English
  • Knowledge of common constraints and limitations of multiplayer/online games is a plus
  • Knowledge of common vulnerabilities and exploitation methods of multiplayer/online games is a plus
  • Skills in reverse engineering, decompiling, tracing and debugging is a plus
  • Experience delivering services utilising Docker Containers & Kubernetes framework is a plus
  • Experience in monitoring the health of SaaS offerings, site reliability engineering is a plus
  • Experience working effectively in a distributed team across time zones is a plus
  • Experience in Windows Driver development is a plus

What to send our way:

  • Your CV, highlighting your education, experience and skills
  • A cover letter including your earliest starting date, expected salary and why you would like to join us

What we offer:

  • Relocation support: We offer financial support in form of a cash allowance and assistance with accommodation search and settle-in support via our relocation partner, for international candidates we offer visa assistance
  • Ubisoft Blue Byte Academy: We offer multiple opportunities for you to discover your full potential and develop yourself in various areas. Guest speakers from the industry, workshops, and access to our training and development platform as well as to our in-house library
  • Free games: Free Ubisoft day one release copies and free games on Ubisoft Connect PC
  • Monthly Mobility Budget: 80€ per month that can be used on bicycle lease, parking spot rental or public transportation ticket
  • Up to 350€ childcare support: We offer up to 350€ net per child for preschool child day care costs
  • Company Pension Scheme: We offer an attractive company pension scheme
  • Gym subsidy: We contribute 50% (up to a maximum amount of 25€) towards the monthly cost of a gym membership of your choice
  • 26 days paid vacation per year
  • Corporate Benefits web portal: Employee discount program for affiliated retailers, shops, and service providers

Ubisoft offers the same job opportunities to all, without any distinction of gender, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, social status, disability, or age. Ubisoft ensures the development of an inclusive work environment which mirrors the diversity of our players’ community.

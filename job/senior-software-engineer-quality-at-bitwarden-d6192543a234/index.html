About the job

Bitwarden is the trusted identity security leader for millions of users worldwide, empowering enterprises, developers, and individuals to securely manage and share sensitive information anywhere. Bitwarden makes it easy for all users to extend robust security across their devices with password management, secrets management, and passwordless and passkey innovations. The company is headquartered in Santa Barbara, California with team members located across the globe. Learn more at bitwarden.com.

As we establish our India Engineering Center of Excellence (COE), we are looking for an experienced Senior Engineer in Quality at Bitwarden, you will contribute directly to the future of the Bitwarden product across mobile, web and server components while working with a great team and amazing open source community. We’re looking for someone who can help our team grow confidently by creating a suite of automated tests, help facilitate a total quality approach to software development and delivery, as well as establish guidelines and processes around the testing of community and internal product contributions.

WHAT WILL YOU DO

  • Develop and maintain automated test scripts for web applications using C# and Selenium/Appium frameworks.
  • Design and implement robust test automation frameworks to support various application types, ensuring scalability, reusability, and maintainability.
  • Collaborate with development and QA teams to understand requirements, identify test scenarios, and automate test cases to ensure comprehensive test coverage.
  • Conduct code reviews of test automation scripts to ensure adherence to coding standards, best practices, and maintainability.
  • Execute automated tests and analyze results to identify defects, performance issues, and areas for improvement.
  • Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies to provide updates on test automation progress, discuss blockers, and plan testing activities for upcoming sprints.
  • Identify and implement opportunities for test automation to improve efficiency, reduce manual testing efforts, and increase test coverage.
  • Contribute to the continuous integration and delivery (CI/CD) pipeline by integrating automated tests and ensuring their successful execution in build pipelines.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to troubleshoot and resolve automation-related issues in different application environments.
  • Stay updated with the latest automation trends and technologies to continuously improve test automation strategies and practices.
  • Document test automation frameworks and processes to facilitate knowledge sharing and ensure maintainability.
  • Provide mentorship and guidance to junior team members on test automation best practices, coding standards, and frameworks.

WHAT YOU BRING TO BITWARDEN

  • Strong proficiency in C# programming
  • Experience setting up and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
  • Experience with remote testing using tools such as Sauce Labs, Browser Stack, etc
  • Solid understanding of software testing principles and methodologies of 4-6 years
  • Experience with AI and machine learning (nice to have)
  • Collaborative and adaptable mindset
  • Openness and authenticity combined with excellent communication skills
  • Excitement and enthusiasm for open source and for better internet security
  • Excellent problem-solving skills – you might not know all the answers, but you know how to find and communicate the solution

WHY WORK WITH US

  • Our user community loves us and we love them. Come to work each day with a sense of purpose as we bring a more secure internet experience to everyone from our friends and family to the world’s largest organizations.
  • Become an expert. You’ll get immersed in the prominent technology markets of security and open source software.
  • We are dedicated to building a diverse and talented team. Work remotely with motivated and supportive team members across the world.
  • Learn and grow. Take on new challenges with the support of your team, and join our #growth-club to continue personal and professional development.

Equal Opportunity Employer

We are committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where everyone can do their best work. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds and make employment decisions based on skills, experience, qualifications, and business needs.

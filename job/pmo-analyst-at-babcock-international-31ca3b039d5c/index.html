About the job

Job Title: PMO Analyst

Location: Plymouth, Devon

Compensation: £33,900 - £42,400 Depending on Experience + Benefits

Role Type: Full time / Permanent

Role ID: SF77037

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as a PMO (Project Management Office) Analyst at our Devonport Royal Dockyard site.

The role

As a PMO Analyst, you’ll be at the heart of a diverse portfolio of Data & Digital Infrastructure projects. Working alongside Project Managers, Technical Leads and key stakeholders, you'll help ensure projects are delivered safely, efficiently and in line with business objectives.

Day-to-day, you’ll have an excellent opportunity to develop your career within project controls, governance and portfolio management while contributing to projects that support operational excellence across one of the UK's most important defence environments. You'll transform project data into meaningful insights, helping teams make informed decisions and maintain effective project delivery.

  • Supporting project governance, reporting and assurance activities across the Data & Digital Infrastructure portfolio.
  • Producing and analysing project reports, dashboards and management information to support decision-making.
  • Maintaining project schedules, milestones and risk, issue, action and dependency registers.
  • Coordinating project meetings, governance reviews and stakeholder forums, tracking actions through to completion.
  • Supporting financial tracking, forecasting, resource planning and continuous improvement of PMO processes.

This role is full time, 35 hours per week and is based on site at Devonport Royal Dockyard.

Essential experience of the PMO Analyst

  • Experience supporting projects, programmes or Project Management Office functions.
  • Experience producing reports, dashboards and management information with a strong focus on data accuracy.
  • Knowledge of project governance, controls and reporting processes.
  • Experience maintaining risk, issue, action and dependency registers.
  • Strong analytical, organisational and stakeholder management skills, including proficiency in Microsoft Excel.

Qualifications for the PMO Analyst

Essential

  • Relevant experience within a Project Management Office, project support, business support or project controls environment.

Desirable

  • Experience within infrastructure, engineering, defence, information technology or digital project environments.

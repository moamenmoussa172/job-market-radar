About the job

About Us:
AI needs a new infrastructure layer. We're building it at Modal.

Every era of computing brought new workloads that previous infrastructure couldn't support: mainframes, databases, and the cloud. Each time, the company that rebuilt the layer underneath defined the decade. AI is no different, except it touches everything instead of one slice, and the window to build the layer underneath it is open right now.

Our customers include category-defining companies like Ramp, Cognition, DoorDash, and Suno. They rely on Modal for instant GPU access, sub-second container starts, and native storage, so it's simple to serve low-latency inference, fine-tune models, and access production-ready sandboxes at scale.

We've passed $1B in annualized run rate, up 15x from a year ago. In October 2026 we raised a $750M Series D led by Accel at a $15.75B valuation.

Our team includes creators of popular open-source projects (e.g., Seaborn, Luigi), academic researchers, international olympiad medalists, and experienced engineering and product leaders with decades of experience.

The Role:

We’re looking for a Security Field Engineer to own how we represent and defend our security posture externally. This role sits at the intersection of security, engineering, and go-to-market, and is critical to building customer trust and accelerating deals.

You will work directly with customer security teams, translate our architecture and controls into clear, credible narratives, and ensure that what we present externally reflects how our systems actually operate.

This is a hands-on, technical role, not just documentation. You will identify patterns in customer requirements and drive improvements in both our product and security posture.

What You’ll Work On:

Customer security engagement

  • Lead technical security conversations with customers and prospects
  • Respond to security reviews, deep-dive calls, and architecture discussions
  • Clearly explain our systems, controls, and tradeoffs to sophisticated security teams

Deal enablement and trust

  • Partner with Sales and GTM to unblock deals and accelerate security approvals
  • Own responses to complex security questionnaires and RFPs
  • Build credibility with customer security stakeholders through clear, consistent communication

Security representation

  • Own and evolve customer-facing security materials (trust center, whitepapers, architecture docs)
  • Ensure external messaging accurately reflects internal systems and controls
  • Continuously improve how we communicate security to reduce friction and ambiguity

Engineering partnership

  • Identify recurring customer requirements and gaps in our security posture
  • Work with engineering to prioritize and implement meaningful improvements
  • Translate customer expectations into concrete, actionable technical changes

Tooling and process

  • Improve how we handle inbound security reviews (automation, knowledge base, workflows)
  • Evaluate and implement tools to scale customer security interactions

You May Be a Fit If:

  • You have experience in security engineering, security GRC, or solutions engineering in a technical product company.
  • You have strong experience engaging with customer security teams or supporting enterprise sales.
  • You have a solid understanding of modern cloud architectures (AWS/GCP/Azure), APIs, and distributed systems.
  • You can explain security controls (auth, isolation, encryption, logging, and so on) in real systems.
  • You’re comfortable going deep with engineers and equally comfortable explaining concepts to customers.
  • You’re highly responsive and action-oriented, able to operate on deal-critical timelines.
  • You identify patterns and drive improvements, not just one-off answers.
  • You have strong written and verbal communication skills.

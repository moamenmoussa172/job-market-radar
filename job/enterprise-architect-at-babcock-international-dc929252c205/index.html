About the job

Job Title: Enterprise Architect

At Babcock we’re working to create a safe and secure world, together, and if you join us, you can play your part as an Enterprise Architect at any of our UK sites.

The role

As an Enterprise Architect, you’ll play a pivotal role in shaping the strategic technology direction of a complex, international organisation. This is an exciting opportunity to influence enterprise-wide decision-making, working closely with senior leaders to align technology investments with business objectives. You'll help define the future technology landscape, driving innovation, efficiency, resilience, and long-term value across the organisation.

Day-to-day, you’ll have the opportunity to contribute to projects that support critical national infrastructure and defence capabilities, while developing your own leadership, strategic planning, and architecture expertise in a collaborative and forward-thinking environment.

  • Represent enterprise architecture in executive forums, providing insight and recommendations that influence strategic decisions.
  • Creating and presenting enterprise architecture roadmaps that align technology capabilities to business goals.
  • Establishing and maintaining architecture principles, standards, and governance across technology domains.
  • Contributing architectural insight to the prioritisation of technology projects, programmes, and investments.
  • Identifying opportunities to improve, simplify, standardise, and modernise the technology landscape.

This role is full-time 37.5 hours per week and provides hybrid working arrangements.

Essential experience of the Enterprise Architect

  • Demonstrable experience in enterprise architecture, systems architecture, and architecture modelling tools.
  • Experience developing strategic technology plans and translating them into actionable roadmaps.
  • Ability to transform complex business requirements into scalable, sustainable architecture solutions.
  • Strong understanding of business strategy and aligning technology architecture to organisational objectives.
  • Knowledge of modern technology environments including microservices, APIs, data platforms, and cybersecurity principles.

Qualifications for the Enterprise Architect

  • Bachelor’s degree in information technology, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
  • Professional architecture or technical certifications such as TOGAF, Microsoft Certified Solutions Expert, Cisco Certified Network Associate, or equivalent - Desirable

Security Clearance

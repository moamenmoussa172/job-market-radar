What you'll do

As an Expert DevOps Engineer, you will be responsible for:

  • Leading the technical governance of large-scale data center network infrastructure — owning architectural decisions, defining delivery standards, and ensuring engineering quality across EVPN/VXLAN fabric (Cisco Nexus 9k/NX-OS, ACI), core routing (ASR9k/NCS, IOS-XR), network security (FortiGate), Neutron routing (C8500, IOS-XE), load balancing (F5 R-Series, F5OS-A), and high-security modules (Thales/Utimaco)
  • Driving end-to-end delivery of complex network infrastructure workstreams — from design through operational handover — within an agile delivery model
  • Acting as the primary technical authority for network infrastructure in cross-organizational engagements with SAP Product Engineering, Platform Engineering, and external stakeholders
  • Shaping and continuously improving the operating model for network delivery, including tooling, automation, and AI-assisted engineering practices
  • Ensuring compliance with sovereign cloud and security frameworks, including SecNumCloud requirements for French cloud regions

In this role, you will also:

  • Provide technical mentorship and guidance to DevOps Engineers and network engineering teams, raising the overall capability bar
  • Lead engagement with third-party managed service providers — defining expectations, reviewing deliverables, and holding providers accountable to quality and timeline commitments
  • Drive post-incident analysis and translate findings into architectural improvements and updated delivery standards
  • Represent SAP Cloud Infrastructure Network Delivery in cross-organizational and cross-program technical forums
  • Champion AI-assisted development, automation, and prototyping practices within the team, accelerating SAP's Autonomous Enterprise ambitions

What you bring

  • 8+ years of hands-on experience in data center network engineering with deep expertise in EVPN/VXLAN fabric (Cisco Nexus 9k/NX-OS, ACI), core routing (IOS-XR), network security (FortiGate), Neutron routing (C8500, IOS-XE), load balancing (F5 R-Series, F5OS-A), and high-security modules (Thales/Utimaco)
  • Proven experience as a technical authority — owning architectural decisions, defining standards, and driving delivery outcomes independently across complex, multi-team programs
  • Strong technical project management skills with deep practical experience in agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, Scrumban) and backlog-driven delivery at program scale
  • Experience leading third-party managed service provider engagements — setting expectations, managing performance, and ensuring quality outcomes
  • Ability to represent technical positions credibly in cross-organizational and executive-level forums
  • AI adoption mindset — active use of AI-assisted tools in engineering and delivery practice
  • SecNumCloud or equivalent sovereign cloud security framework knowledge required for French cloud region work
  • Fluent English and French required

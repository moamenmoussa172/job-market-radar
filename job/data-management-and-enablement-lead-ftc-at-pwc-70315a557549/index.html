Job Description & Summary

Why Join PwC Ireland?
At PwC Ireland, you’ll be part of a purpose-driven organisation dedicated to building trust in society and solving our clients’ most complex challenges. Here, diverse teams collaborate across sectors and geographies to deliver innovative solutions that make a real difference.
We invest in your growth through continuous learning, leadership development, and access to cutting-edge technology, empowering you to shape your career and impact the future of business and society.

We are seeking a proactive and strategic Manager to join the Chief Data Office (CDO) team on a fixed term contract for 12 months. In this role, you will lead data management and enablement initiatives delivering to the CDO data strategy.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead data strategy, management and enablement initiatives with LoS and business teams, translating priorities into practical roadmaps and measurable outcomes.
  • Lead the implementation and adoption of the firm's enterprise data catalog.
  • Define metadata standards, business glossaries, taxonomies and minimum metadata requirements to improve data consistency and discoverability.
  • Partner with LoS and business teams to identify, prioritise and onboard data assets and use cases into the enterprise data catalog.
  • Develop project plans and manage scope, milestones, dependencies, risks, issues and reporting across concurrent CDO initiatives.
  • Embed data governance and lifecycle practices that support responsible data access, reuse, analytics and GenAI.
  • Implement data policies and standards agreed by the Data Governance Board and promote alignment with global and network requirements.
  • Collaborate across business, Technology, Risk, Legal, Information Security and global teams to deliver sustainable data capabilities.
  • Design, implement and continuously improve data operating models, including ownership, stewardship, decision rights, governance forums, workflows, service and support arrangements.
  • Advocate for a data-driven culture and promote good data management and stewardship practices across the firm.

Your Profile:

  • 6+ years of experience in data management, data governance, data strategy, technology transformation or a related role.
  • Experience leading the implementation and adoption of Collibra or a comparable enterprise data catalogue or data intelligence platform
  • Experience in metadata management, taxonomy development, data ownership and data asset onboarding.
  • Experience leading cross-functional projects and managing plans, risks, dependencies, governance and reporting.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into practical data management processes and technology-enabled solutions.
  • Experience engaging senior stakeholders and working across business, technology, risk and global teams.
  • Understanding of the data foundations required for analytics, GenAI and responsible data reuse.
  • Experience delivering change, communications, training and adoption activities for new data capabilities.
  • Ability to work independently and lead cross-functional initiatives.
  • Ability to handle multi-tasking environment, meet deadlines, and work with a wide range stakeholders
  • Excellent analytical and problem solving skills
  • Excellent communication skills both verbal and written, with storytelling
  • Demonstrates enthusiasm & commitment to work
  • Ability to develop business relationships internally and externally
  • Demonstrates a commitment to quality and integrity
  • Proactive work approach and ability to demonstrate good judgement

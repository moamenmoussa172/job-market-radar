About the job

The role

You’ll lead the delivery of security services for clients in Kuwait, with a focus on SOC planning, design, and build-out. You’ll draw on Accenture’s global delivery network (methods, tooling, accelerators, talent) and tailor it to Kuwaiti clients, whether that’s a bank standing up a new SOC, an energy company monitoring critical infrastructure, or a government entity working toward national cyber security requirements. You’ll manage the delivery team day to day and make the calls that keep programs on track.

What you’ll do

  • Lead delivery of SOC and security operations engagements, from planning and design through to steady-state operations
  • Manage and guide your team, making decisions on priorities, resourcing, and approach
  • Track progress against plan and make sure delivery stays aligned with client goals and compliance requirements
  • Spot risks early and put mitigation in place before they hit timelines or service quality
  • Solve problems that cut across teams and workstreams, working closely with other delivery leads
  • Bring fresh ideas to how we deliver, using automation, better tooling, and proven approaches from our global teams
  • Run regular team check-ins and knowledge-sharing sessions so learning spreads fast
  • Gather client feedback and use it to sharpen our services

What you bring

  • Strong, hands-on experience planning and building SOCs, including operating models, use case development, and onboarding of log sources
  • Solid working experience with Splunk SIEM operations
  • Good grasp of delivery governance, including reporting, risk management, and quality control
  • Experience leading and developing delivery teams
  • Ability to communicate clearly with technical teams and client stakeholders alike
  • Experience in the GCC market and familiarity with regional regulatory expectations

Preferred certifications

  • CISSP, CISM, or GCIH (at least one)
  • Splunk Core Certified Power User or Splunk Enterprise Security Certified Admin
  • PMP or equivalent project management certification
  • Nice to have: CISA, CRISC, or GIAC SOC-related certifications

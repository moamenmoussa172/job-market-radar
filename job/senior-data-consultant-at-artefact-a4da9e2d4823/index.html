About the job

Senior Consultant

Hybrid working pattern

Who we are

Artefact is a new generation of a data service provider, specialising in data consulting and data-driven digital marketing, dedicated to transforming data into business impact across the entire value chain of organisations. We are proud to say we’re enjoying skyrocketing growth.

Our broad range of data-driven solutions in data consulting and digital marketing are designed to meet our clients’ specific needs, always conceived with a business-centric approach and delivered with tangible results. Our data-driven services are built upon the deep AI expertise we’ve acquired with our 1000+ client base around the globe.

We have over 2000 employees across 36 offices who are focused on accelerating digital transformation. Thanks to a unique mix of company assets: State of the art data technologies, lean AI agile methodologies for fast delivery, and cohesive teams of the finest business consultants, data analysts, data scientists, data engineers, and digital experts, all dedicated to bringing extra value to every client.

Key responsibilities

As a Senior Consultant, you are a team leader - you will receive the coaching of the most senior people at Artefact and you will be in constant contact with our clients to drive success. You will take the responsibility and ownership of the quality and timeliness of the project delivery while contributing to the vision and organising the next steps.

To succeed in this role, the Senior Consultant needs to be a true problem-solver and project manager, relying on the expertise of others (data scientists, data engineers, or their peers and clients) and getting a very detailed understanding of what great looks like for their clients. They will be able to adapt to a vast diversity of missions while acquiring a fine knowledge of the complex organisations to make data go beyond technology and drive real business impact. Leveraging the right and smart data to improve the business outcome for our clients is the DNA of a Senior Consultant at Artefact.

As a Senior Consultant, your missions will include:

  • Understanding clients needs and build your convictions to create success in your missions
  • Formulating strategies, roadmaps, action plans and communicate them efficiently in writing and orally to the clients and other team members
  • Organising the delivery of projects end-to-end, from briefing and managing your teams to driving clients in the right direction
  • Inspiring and contribute to deliverables with a desire to lead by example, while your philosophy is to create legitimacy on the ground
  • Driving the commercial roadmaps with your managers and partners and helping to build knowledge for Artefact as you build industry / topic expertise project after project.

What we are looking for

  • Individuals who align with our values; We value potential over existing knowledge. We value ideas over hierarchy. We value actual work over nice talks. We value people over experience.
  • We love individuals who are curious and problem-solving.
  • We have a strong appetite for those who want to discover new things even when it pushes them out of their comfort zone.
  • We would appreciate people who are as passionate as us in all things digital, data and tech.
  • The ability to demonstrate autonomy, high-quality deliverables, strong attention to detail and the ability to build long-term customer relationships is a must, you will also have a strong appetite to grow in the world of Data.

Why you should join us

  • Artefact is the place to be: come and build the future of marketing, join a community, we have a team of experts and professionals worldwide that will always be willing to help
  • Get immersed into the digital and data industry: meet the most innovative partners, deliver cutting-edge projects, and see the industry from backstage. Sooner than you think, you’ll talk about Machine Learning, DevOps and AI like a pro.
  • Progress: Every day offers new challenges and new opportunities to learn. At Artefact, we take training very seriously, and you’ll be coached and mentored on your technical, consulting, and interpersonal skills on a regular basis.
  • Culture: join an diverse, collaborative, and action-oriented team
  • Entrepreneurship: you will be joining a team of driven entrepreneurs, come and help industry leaders transform through data, we won’t give up until we make a huge dent in this industry!

About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Canada.

Role Description

As a Financial Reporting & Technical Accounting Sr. Analyst on the Finance team, you will play a key role in supporting quarterly and annual external reporting, SEC filing coordination, and the broader close process. You will independently manage recurring reporting workstreams, help keep critical timelines on track, and contribute to accurate, thoughtful, and audit-ready external reporting.

This role sits at the intersection of financial reporting and technical accounting. In addition to coordinating core reporting deliverables, you will partner across Accounting, Legal, FP&A, Equity Ops, Tax, Investor Relations, senior Finance leadership, and external auditors to support disclosure processes, governance materials, and reporting requirements tied to complex accounting matters.

This is a strong opportunity for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complex processes, thrives in fast-paced environments, and wants to deepen their experience in both SEC reporting and technical accounting. You will gain exposure to new accounting standard adoption, disclosure analysis, and transaction-related workstreams, including M&A and other strategic projects.

Responsibilities

  • Independently manage recurring financial reporting and close workstreams, including journal entry preparation, reconciliations, and financial analysis, with a high degree of ownership and accountability.
  • Support quarterly and annual SEC reporting activities, including preparation and coordination for Forms 10-Q and 10-K.
  • Coordinate timelines, stakeholder feedback, and sign-offs across Accounting, Legal, FP&A, Equity Ops, Tax, Investor Relations, senior Finance leadership, and external auditors.
  • Prepare and maintain reporting support, disclosure checklists, sign-off packages, and audit-ready documentation.
  • Support materials for Disclosure Committee, Audit Committee, earnings-related deliverables, and other reporting governance processes.
  • Partner on technical accounting matters that have disclosure or external reporting implications, including adoption of new accounting standards such as ASU 2024-03, Disaggregation of Income Statement Expenses (“DISE”).
  • Contribute to technical accounting and special projects, including M&A, strategic transactions, and other non-routine accounting matters.

Requirements

  • 4-6 years of relevant experience in financial reporting, technical accounting, audit, or corporate accounting.
  • Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP and financial statement reporting.
  • Experience supporting SEC reporting processes, including 10-Q and 10-K preparation and review.
  • Experience working in reporting at a publicly traded company.
  • Strong project management and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple deadlines and stakeholders.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across cross-functional partners.
  • High attention to detail, sound judgment, and a strong sense of ownership.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance.
  • CPA preferred.
  • Experience with technical accounting topics, including new accounting standard adoption, complex accounting issues, and disclosure analysis.
  • Experience preparing or supporting technical accounting memoranda, reporting support, or external reporting disclosures.
  • Experience supporting M&A, strategic transactions, or other special accounting projects.

About the job

Tasks and responsibilitiesCapture requirements from global and local factories and Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) partners. Consolidate them into traceable system requirements and assess where local deviations are justified.Own the reusable architecture connecting product and electronics platforms with factory test systems, production lines, and IT backends. Define system boundaries, components, interfaces, and end-to-end data flows.Standardize production workflows for firmware flashing, bootloader interaction, automated calibration, testing, and production data exchange. Evaluate alternatives against cost, complexity, scalability, and business value.Model the architecture and interfaces in CAMEO using UML/SysML, maintain requirements traceability in Polarion, and document constraints, dependencies, and Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).Align factory, product, platform, IT, and supplier teams. Guide implementation, integration, verification, and production ramp-up across factories and EMS partners.Provide third-line support for production issues, analyse manufacturing and test data to identify root causes, and incorporate lessons learned into the standard architecture.Mandatory skill requirements: Wireless & RF Expertise: Deep expertise in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, Thread/Matter, NFC, and RF fundamentals, including link budget calculations.Connectivity Security & Quality: Strong knowledge of cybersecurity-by-design, cryptographic principles, robust connectivityWireless Connectivity Architecture & Modeling : Strong experience designing end-to-end wireless connectivity architectures across embedded electronics, software, edge gateways, and cloud services.Wireless Technology Evaluation & Prototyping: Hands-on experience evaluating and prototyping wireless technologies through PoCs and analysing trade-offs between power, range, performance, and cost.Expertise in interface design, requirements engineering, and architecture modelling using UML, SysML, and C4.Hands-on experience with CAMEO for systems modelling and Polarion for requirements management and design traceability.Experience defining ADRs, reusable connectivity interfaces, and architectural guidelines that support security compliance and platform reuse.Ability to design modular, resilient, and resource-efficient connectivity solutions for long-lifecycle appliances.Understanding of connected-appliance ecosystems, cloud platforms, and global smart-home standards.Strong systems thinking, technical ownership, and communication skills to align multidisciplinary teams and translate complex technical decisions into actionable requirements.

Bachelor's/Master’s degree in Electronics Engineering, or related field.

8 to 14 years

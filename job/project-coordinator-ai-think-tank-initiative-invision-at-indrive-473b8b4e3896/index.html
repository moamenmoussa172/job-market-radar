About the job

This is a fully remote job, open to people in Kazakhstan.

We are seeking a Project Coordinator (AI think tank initiative) to coordinate a comprehensive project assessing the impact of artificial intelligence on the labor market and key economic sectors of Cyprus. The project spans the entire lifecycle: from multidisciplinary research and data collection to building a unified predictive model, executing a public campaign and deploying B2G and B2C digital products. The implementation horizon is 2025–2030, starting in Cyprus with subsequent international expansion to other markets.

  • Coordinate five research tracks: occupation mapping and AI substitution risk studies (ESCO, MOT, OECD), national statistics analysis (CYSTAT, Eurostat), expert scenario modeling, academic validation and AI agent deployment.
  • Engage with the Expert Advisory Board, bringing together top analysts, economists, political scientists, NGO leaders, academic partners and Cypriot government stakeholders.
  • Organize the preparation of analytical deliverables, including the publication of a White Paper, and hold roundtables and workshops with stakeholders and government ministries.
  • Support communication and PR/GR initiatives, special projects with HR platforms, public awareness campaigns and digital preventive qualification services.
  • Assist in the development and rollout of tech solutions: a decision-support dashboard monitoring unemployment risks for government bodies and a mobile career navigator app for citizens.
  • Oversee the pilot phase in 1–2 economic sectors in Cyprus, integrating the predictive model into state structures and preparing for international scalability.

Experience: Proven experience coordinating or managing multidisciplinary, product or research projects in B2G, B2C, data analytics or AI

Knowledge and skills:

  • Labor market research methodologies
  • Open data analysis
  • Practical AI applications
  • Structuring and maintaining project documentation

Languages: English (Advanced, C1).

Key Competencies:

Communication with stakeholders at all levels, including government ministries, academic institutions, international organizations and expert communities
Exceptional organization and ability to manage multiple parallel work streams
Structured approach to project management and documentation.

Quality Analyst (BMW NA)

Bosch Group · Taguig, Philippines

Onsite · Data Analyst · Posted 6 November 2026

Apply on Bosch Group's site →Open in Radarak

About the job

  • Conducts quality evaluations of calls and transactions to ensure compliance with established processes, standards, and customer experience expectations.
  • Supports the OPM in monitoring and achieving Quality targets and KPIs across the assigned LOB.
  • Identifies, analyzes, and escalates quality gaps, recurring errors, compliance risks, and performance trends to the OPM and Team Leaders.
  • Prepares accurate and timely internal and external Quality reports, including evaluation results, trends, and key findings.
  • Partners with Team Leaders to drive the continuous improvement of call and transaction quality.
  • Supports Team Leaders in developing and implementing quality improvement initiatives and corrective actions.
  • Provides Team Leaders with actionable feedback on individual and team Quality performance, including identified strengths and improvement areas.
  • Identifies training and coaching opportunities based on evaluation results, error trends, and root-cause analysis.
  • Support Team Leaders in conducting training needs analysis and prioritizing interventions based on Quality findings.
  • Collaborates with Team Leaders in planning and delivering targeted coaching and refresher sessions for identified performance gaps.
  • Develops and implements SMART action plans for recurring or significant Quality issues and monitor progress against agreed actions.
  • Participates in calibration sessions to promote evaluation consistency and alignment across Quality, Operations, and relevant stakeholders.
  • Collaborates with the Global Quality Team on Quality standards, initiatives, and continuous improvement activities.
  • Reviews and supports the resolution of customer complaints and Quality-related escalations, including root-cause identification and recommended corrective actions.
  • Provides agents with structured coaching and feedback on call and transaction handling, focusing on process adherence, accuracy, compliance, customer experience, and improvement opportunities.
  • Maintains required phone time and live call-handling exposure to ensure continuous process retention, firsthand understanding of customer interactions, and familiarity with current operational procedures.
  • Handles live customer calls as scheduled to maintain process knowledge, system proficiency, and practical understanding of agent-level challenges.
  • Applies insights gained from call handling to strengthen quality evaluations, coaching, root-cause analysis, and improvement recommendations.
  • Demonstrates the ability to provide constructive, objective, and actionable feedback.
  • Maintains a positive, professional, and solutions-oriented attitude.
  • Serves as a positive role model and ambassador for Quality and Operations.
  • Maintains a good attendance, punctuality, and conduct record.
  • Must have no active corrective or disciplinary action at the time of application/appointment.
  • Demonstrates a consistent productivity and performance record.
  • Maintains a strong customer and client-focused mindset.
  • Demonstrates strong analytical skills and attention to detail, particularly in identifying Quality trends, recurring errors, and improvement opportunities.
  • Possesses effective communication, coaching, and interpersonal skills.
  • Demonstrates initiative, accountability, and ownership in driving actions through completion.
  • Able to work collaboratively with agents, Team Leaders, Operations, Training, and other Quality stakeholders.
  • Demonstrates flexibility to support varying schedules and business requirements, including early or late shifts when required.
  • Willing to maintain the required phone time and live call-handling exposure to support process retention and operational proficiency.
  • Willing to provide additional operational support when reasonably required and in accordance with applicable company policies.

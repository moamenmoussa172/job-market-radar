Job Description:

The Opportunity

At Hitachi Energy, we are advancing a sustainable energy future through innovation, digitalization, and operational excellence. As a Site IT Service Delivery Manager, you will serve as the primary IT partner for the factory or business site, ensuring reliable, secure, and business-focused IT services that enable operational success.

In this role, you will lead local IT service delivery, act as the key interface between business stakeholders and global IT teams, and ensure the effective delivery of infrastructure, applications, cybersecurity, and digital solutions. You will drive business value by aligning IT services with operational requirements, optimizing service performance, and supporting continuous improvement across the site.

How You'll Make an Impact

  • Lead and develop the local Site IT team, ensuring the effective delivery of IT services and support aligned with business objectives.
  • Act as the primary point of contact between the site, global IT teams, shared service organizations, and external suppliers for all IT-related matters.
  • Own ticket management, prioritization, escalation, and resolution processes to ensure high levels of service performance and end-user satisfaction.
  • Ensure reliable delivery and support of site infrastructure, network services, workplace technologies, and IT operations.
  • Maintain compliance with Hitachi Energy governance, cybersecurity, and information security standards.
  • Partner with business leaders to understand operational requirements, collect demand for new solutions and services, and prioritize business needs.
  • Lead local IT projects and support the deployment of global digital initiatives at the site.
  • Provide governance and first-line support for business applications while coordinating with application owners and global support teams.
  • Support operational management of IT/OT environments, including cybersecurity controls, risk mitigation, and compliance activities.
  • Coordinate and lead the local response to cybersecurity incidents and critical IT service disruptions.
  • Plan, manage, and monitor the site IT budget, ensuring effective cost control and resource utilization.
  • Ensure machine and production-system connectivity, network availability, and coordination of supporting vendors and service providers.
  • Manage relationships with software, infrastructure, and network vendors to ensure successful service delivery.
  • Ensure users have appropriate access to systems and applications in accordance with corporate policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance service quality, operational efficiency, and business value.
  • Ensure the team is appropriately structured, staffed, trained, and developed to meet current and future business needs.
  • Foster a collaborative, high-performing, and customer-focused team culture.

Your Background

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related discipline. Equivalent experience may also be considered.
  • Minimum 8 years of professional experience in IT service delivery, infrastructure management, or a related IT leadership role.
  • Experience leading IT support teams within a manufacturing, industrial, or production environment.
  • Proven experience managing complex stakeholder relationships across business and technology functions.
  • Strong understanding of IT infrastructure, networking, workplace technologies, cybersecurity, and application support.
  • Experience managing IT projects and delivering technology solutions in complex operational environments.
  • Knowledge of process improvement methodologies, business process management, and service optimization practices.
  • Experience supporting manufacturing operations, factory systems, or operational technology (OT) environments is highly desirable.
  • Strong analytical, communication, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced and highly operational environment.
  • Fluency in English and proficiency in the local language relevant to the country of responsibility.

Preferred Technical Expertise

  • Microsoft 365 ecosystem including Excel, Word, Teams, Outlook, and SharePoint.
  • ServiceNow or similar IT Service Management (ITSM) platforms.
  • Microsoft Azure and Azure DevOps.
  • SAP S/4HANA and related enterprise applications.
  • Data Analytics, Reporting, and Business Intelligence solutions.
  • Infrastructure, networking, endpoint management, and cybersecurity technologies.
  • IT/OT integration and industrial connectivity environments.

Preferred Certifications

  • ITIL® 4 Foundation or higher.
  • Project Management certification (PMP®, PRINCE2®, Agile, Scrum, or equivalent).
  • Lean Six Sigma certification.
  • COBIT knowledge or certification.

About the job

Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

What you’ll do

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and maintain user-facing experiences, services, APIs, and systems.
  • Make effective tradeoffs that consider business priorities, user experiences, and a sustainable technical foundation.
  • Scrutinize, reason clearly, and make decisions about the technology and architecture choices we make in building these products.
  • Discuss and learn from users and startup founders during their initial company start.
  • Debug production issues across services and multiple levels of the stack.

Who you are

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's Degree or foreign equivalent degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field, plus two (2) years of related work experience in Software Engineering.

Must also have two (2) years of experience in each of the following:

  • Owning full product lifecycle from requirements through production including defining product requirements, designing system architecture, implementing features, running rollouts, and measuring results;
  • Designing APIs, implementing backend and frontend changes, coordinating QA, and deploying with observability and rollback strategies;
  • Designing and executing production rollouts using feature flags and exit/rollback criteria;
  • Translating product hypotheses into experiments and measurable success metrics and analyzing results to drive product decisions;
  • Maintaining backlog in Jira, participating in sprint planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives;
  • Designing and implementing RESTful APIs (JSON), SOAP-based services, and designing API contracts, versioning, and backward compatibility strategies; and
  • Implementing production monitoring, logging, tracing, including: Prometheus, Grafana and set SLOs/SLIs.

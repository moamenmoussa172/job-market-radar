Imagine being the trusted partner who transforms business strategy into data-driven decisions across Europe. As Associate Director, Data & Analytics – Delivery Region Europe, you will sit at the intersection of business, analytics, and technology, shaping high-impact data products that enable smarter decisions and measurable outcomes. You’ll collaborate with senior stakeholders, influence strategic priorities, and lead the end-to-end delivery of trusted, governed data solutions—from concept to adoption. If you’re passionate about turning complex data challenges into business value and thrive in a highly visible role where your expertise directly impacts organizational success, we’d love to hear from you.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner with business leaders to translate strategic priorities into high-impact data and analytics initiatives.
  • Shape and prioritize a regional data and analytics roadmap aligned with business goals and measurable outcomes.
  • Lead end-to-end delivery of data products from concept, development, and deployment through adoption.
  • Transform complex business requirements into actionable product backlogs, epics, and user stories.
  • Drive implementation of scalable data pipelines, transformations, and analytics solutions across enterprise platforms.
  • Ensure data products meet quality, governance, security, and compliance standards throughout the lifecycle.
  • Coordinate cross-functional teams across business, product, engineering, and governance functions.
  • Lead stakeholder communications, business readiness activities, and user adoption initiatives.
  • Monitor solution performance, service reliability, and value realization after deployment.
  • Champion continuous improvement in delivery efficiency, data quality, and business engagement.

Essential Requirements

  • Proven experience partnering with senior stakeholders to translate business needs into data-driven solutions.
  • Strong business analysis and requirements management skills in complex, cross-functional environments.
  • Advanced expertise in Structured Query Language and modern cloud-based data platforms.
  • Hands-on experience delivering scalable data pipelines, transformation workflows, and analytics products.
  • Strong knowledge of data governance, metadata management, lineage, and data quality practices.
  • Experience with Snowflake and enterprise data orchestration technologies.
  • Proficiency in Python for automation, scripting, and testing activities.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to influence technical and non-technical audiences.

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience delivering data and analytics solutions within pharmaceutical, life sciences, or other regulated industries.
  • Exposure to enterprise data governance and policy management platforms in large-scale environments.
